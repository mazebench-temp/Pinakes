summary: Odysseus' host after the Cyclops. On his floating island he makes Odysseus welcome for a month and questions him about Troy, then binds the winds for him in an oxhide skin and lets the West Wind alone blow him home; when the storm brings the ships back, he will not help a man hated by the gods and drives him away (10.1–79).

**The island.** It is the first land Odysseus reaches after the Cyclops: “And we came to the island of Aeolia; and there lived / Aeolus, son of Hippotes, dear to the immortal gods, / on a floating island” (10.1–3; see [[aeolia]] and the translation's note on 10.1–3). W. W. Merry notes that the island must lie to the west of Ithaca, since Aeolus means to send Odysseus straight home by shutting up every wind but the West Wind (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 10.1). The house is full of feasting: his six sons and six daughters, married to one another, feast by day beside their father and mother, “and the house, full of the savor of meat, resounds round about in the court”, and at night they sleep beside their wives (10.5–12; see the entry, [[house]] and the translation's note on 10.10).

**The welcome.** “And so we came to their city and their fine house. / And for a whole month he made me welcome and questioned me about each thing, / Ilios and the ships of the Argives and the homecoming of the Achaeans; / and I told him all of it fair and right” (10.13–16; see [[xenia]] and [[nostos]]). τῶν, “their”, takes in the whole family, but Aeolus, as head of the household, is the one who “made me welcome” (Merry on 10.13). When Odysseus “in my turn” asks to be on his way, after Aeolus has done with his questions, “he did not refuse at all, but made ready my escort” (10.17–18; Merry on 10.17; see [[pompe]]).

**The gift.** The escort is the winds themselves:

{{quote:dc309778-a54a-5075-b557-38ec8e6baa78}}

ἐκδείρας, “that he had flayed”, tells how he had come by the skin, not when he gave it (Merry on 10.19; see [[skin-of-the-winds]]). He binds it in the ship with a cord of silver drawn so fine, Merry thinks, that it could be plaited, and tied so tight that not a breath could slip past (Merry on 10.23). The office that the son of Cronus gave him, ταμίης ἀνέμων, “dispenser of the winds”, makes him the manager of the winds by leave of Zeus, not their king (Merry on 10.21; see [[dispenser-of-the-winds]] and [[zeus]]), and the wind he lets blow is the West Wind (see [[zephyrus]] and [[the-winds]]). The companions believe that the skin holds gold and silver, “gifts from Aeolus, great-hearted son of Hippotes”, “a favor done in friendship, / by Aeolus” (10.35–36, 10.43–44; see [[gold-and-silver]] and the translation's note on 10.43–44), and when they loose it the winds carry the ships back to his island (10.46–55).

**Off with you.** Odysseus goes with a herald and one companion to “the glorious house of Aeolus”, and finds him “feasting beside his wife and his children” (10.59–61). They are amazed and ask: “How have you come, Odysseus? What evil god has attacked you? / Surely we sent you off with care, so that you might reach / your native land and your house, or wherever it is your pleasure” (10.63–66; see [[daimon]]). Odysseus blames his companions and sleep and begs them to make it good; they fall silent, and the father answers:

{{quote:9e85fb10-74df-579e-a0de-c3c0aa8b4193}}

ἔρρε is “go away to your ruin”, as a scholiast glosses it, and θᾶσσον, “quickly”, is “as speedily as possible” (Merry on 10.72; see the translation's key words). The host who “made ready my escort” refuses it now as unlawful, for a man hated by the gods (see [[themis]] and [[xenia]]), and the verb of the first sending, ἀπεπέμπομεν, “we sent you off” (10.65), returns in his refusal, οὐδʼ ἀποπέμπειν, “or to send on his way” (10.73), and in the dismissal: “So saying, he sent me from the house, groaning heavily” (ἀπέπεμπε, 10.76; see [[groaning-heavily]]). The ships row on, “since no escort showed itself any longer” (10.79): πομπή here is the wind, the means of their journey (Merry on 10.79; see [[odysseus-and-aeolus]] and [[odysseus]]).
