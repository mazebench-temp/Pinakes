summary: Odysseus tells his answers with “So he spoke, and I answered him and said” (10.270), “So she spoke, and I answered her and said” (10.336, 10.382) and “then at last I answered her with words and said” (10.500); and “the father answered me” with his dismissal (10.71).
---

ἀμειβόμενος προσέειπον, the first-person verse of the tale (as 4.375; see the Odyssey translation's formulas): to Eurylochus' plea (10.270), to Circe's invitation to her bed and her question at the meal (10.336, 10.382), and after his weeping at her news of the journey to the dead (10.496–500). πατὴρ δʼ ἠμείβετο μύθῳ gives Aeolus no name, only his part as father of the household that had questioned Odysseus (10.63–71; [[aeolus]]).
