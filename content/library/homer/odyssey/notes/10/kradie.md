---
summary: Going to Circe's house with Hermes' drug, Odysseus says, “my heart heaved with many thoughts as I went” (10.309).
---

Hermes has gone away to Olympus, and Odysseus goes on alone to the house of Circe, “and my heart heaved with many thoughts as I went” (πολλὰ δέ μοι κραδίη πόρφυρε κιόντι, 10.309; see [[hermes]] and [[circe]]). The verse is Menelaus' twice, as he goes back to the ships on Pharos after Eidothea's counsel and again after Proteus' prophecy (4.427, 4.572), and the Iliad has its first half for Agenor waiting for Achilles: “and his heart heaved with many thoughts as he waited” (*Iliad* 21.551).
