---
summary: Circe walks to and fro at her loom, “such as are / the fine and lovely and splendid works of goddesses” (10.222–223).
---

ἀγλαὰ ἔργα, the goddesses' handiwork; in later books ἀγλαὰ ἔργα ἰδυῖα describes a woman skilled in such work, Athena in disguise and the Phoenician nurse of Eumaeus (13.289, 15.418, 16.158; [[loom]], [[circe]]).
