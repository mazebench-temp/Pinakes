---
summary: The companions find Circe's house “built / of polished stones, in a place with a view all round” (10.210–211), and Eurylochus says the same at the ship (10.252–253).
---

Merry takes περισκέπτῳ here as “open ground,” since the men had come down from the lookout and the island lies low (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 10.211; see the translation's note on 10.211). 10.253 is lacking in most manuscripts and in Eustathius, and Apollonius Dyscolus gives 10.212 in its place (see the translation's note on 10.253; [[polished]], [[circe]], [[house]]).
