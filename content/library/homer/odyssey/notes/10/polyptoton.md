---
summary: In the land of the Laestrygonians, “where herdsman hails herdsman / as he drives his flock in, and the other answers as he drives his herd out” (10.82–83).
grc: ["ποιμένα ποιμὴν"]
en: [herdsman hails herdsman]
---

ποιμένα ποιμήν, the noun in two cases side by side, sets the herdsman coming home against the one going out, for the paths of night and of day are close together (10.82–86; see the translation's note on 10.81–86 and the Odyssey translation's key words, ποιμήν; [[telepylus]]).
