---
summary: Odysseus sends scouts to learn “what men they might be who eat bread upon the earth” (10.101).
---

10.100–102 repeat the verses of the scouts sent to the Lotus-eaters (9.88–90); the bread-eaters this time are the Laestrygonians, whose king makes a meal of one of the scouts (10.116; [[laestrygonians]], [[spies-and-scouts]]).
