---
summary: At Circe's news of the journey to the dead, “my heart / no longer wished to live and see the light of the sun” (10.497–498).
---

ὁρᾶν φάος ἠελίοιο, life measured by sight, said by a man who is sent alive to the dead; 10.496–498 are the verses of Menelaus' despair at Proteus' news of Agamemnon's death (4.538–540; see the Odyssey translation's formulas; [[nostos]]).
