---
summary: Circe bids Odysseus beach his ship by Oceanus “and go yourself into the dank house of Hades” (10.512).
---

Ἀίδεω … δόμον εὐρώεντα, the dwellings that Hades feared Poseidon's earthquake would lay bare (*Iliad* 20.61–65), are reached here by a voyage across Oceanus, not by a descent beneath the earth: Merry notes that in the next book Odysseus stays on the earth's surface, on the shore beyond Oceanus, and never goes down into the depths (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 10.512).
