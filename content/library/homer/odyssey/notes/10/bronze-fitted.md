κυνέῃ χαλκήρεϊ, the helmet in which the lots are shaken for the duel of Paris and Menelaus and for the archery contest in the Iliad (*Iliad* 3.316, 23.861). Merry takes the lots for pebbles, the one that leapt out first deciding the lot (*Iliad* 3.325, 23.353), and compares Sophocles' lines on the lot that was to be the first to leap from the plumed helmet (*Ajax* 1285–1287, as Merry quotes them; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 10.207; [[lots]], [[helmet]]).
