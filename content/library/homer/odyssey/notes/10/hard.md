---
summary: Odysseus to Aeolus: “My evil companions led me into blind folly, and with them sleep, / cruel sleep” (10.68–69).
---

Merry: σχέτλιος is used here exactly like Latin *improbus*, in such phrases as *improbus anser* and *improbus labor* (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 10.69). The translation keeps “cruel” for things (see the Odyssey translation's key words, σχέτλιος). Sleep is blamed with the companions for the opening of the skin (10.31–55; [[ate]], [[hypnos]], [[sweet-sleep]]).
