---
summary: Circe is “own sister of baleful Aeetes”, both children of the Sun and of Perse (10.137–139).
---

Circe's kin are named when the ship comes to Aeaea: “Circe of the lovely tresses, a dread goddess with human voice, / own sister of baleful Aeetes; / both were born of the Sun, who brings light to mortals, / and of their mother Perse, whom Oceanus begot as his child” (αὐτοκασιγνήτη ὀλοόφρονος Αἰήταο, 10.136–139; see [[circe]], [[aeetes]], [[helios]] and [[perse]]). αὐτοκασιγνήτη is “own sister”, of the same father and mother, as the Iliad's αὐτοκασίγνητος is “own brother” (*Iliad* 2.706; see the translation's table of key words).
