summary: Circe knows “all the sorrows you suffered on the fish-filled sea” (10.458), and Teiresias will tell him “the way and the measures of your path / and your homecoming, how you shall go over the fish-filled sea” (10.539–540).
grc: [ἰχθυόεντι]
---

10.539–540 are Eidothea's promise to Menelaus that Proteus will tell him the way home (4.389–390), given to Teiresias (see the Odyssey translation's formulas). The dative ἐν πόντῳ … ἰχθυόεντι (10.458) is new to the entry; the Iliad has it of the sea and of the river Hyllus (*Iliad* 16.746, 20.392; [[sea]], [[nostos]]).
