---
summary: One of Circe's handmaids sets golden baskets, for the bread, on the silver tables drawn up before the chairs (10.354–355).
---

“Another drew up tables before the chairs, / silver ones, and set golden baskets on them” (10.354–355; see [[table]], [[handmaids]] and [[gold-and-silver]]). The κάνεια are the baskets in which bread is set out at a meal, as when slave women heap bread beside the suitors in baskets (1.147; see the translation's row δρήστειρα; λῖτα; κάνεια); in Circe's house they are of gold, like the cups (10.357; see [[cup]]).
