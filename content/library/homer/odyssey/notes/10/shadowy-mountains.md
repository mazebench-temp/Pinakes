---
summary: After the last day of feasting the companions “lay down to sleep throughout the shadowy halls” (10.479).
---

κατὰ μέγαρα σκιόεντα, the halls of Circe's house; Odysseus does not sleep, but goes up to her bed to ask for his release (10.479–486). The verse is one of those that three manuscripts and Eustathius lack and that Wolf and Bekker reject (see the translation's note on 10.475–479, 482; [[house]]).
