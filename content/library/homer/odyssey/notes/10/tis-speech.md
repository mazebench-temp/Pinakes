The Iliad's verse of the crowd's verdict (*Iliad* 2.271; again 8.328), given to the crew of one ship, whose nameless voice speaks the evil counsel: “But come, quickly, let us see what these are, / how much gold and silver is in the skin” (10.44–45; [[o-popoi]], [[hetairos]], [[gold-and-silver]]).
