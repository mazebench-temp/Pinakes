---
summary: The companions at the ship, seeing Odysseus come back, “poured around me in tears” (10.415).
grc: [δακρυόεντες]
en: [in tears]
---

δακρυόεντες ἔχυντο, the end of the simile of the calves (10.410–417); Merry takes ἐμέ with ἔχυντο, “poured around me,” rather than with ἴδον (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 10.414; [[of-the-fields]], [[shedding-tears]]).
