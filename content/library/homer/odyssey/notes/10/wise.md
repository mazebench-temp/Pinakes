---
summary: Persephone has given Teiresias a mind, “that he alone should be wise; but the others flit as shadows” (10.495).
grc: ["οἴῳ πεπνῦσθαι"]
---

οἴῳ πεπνῦσθαι, the perfect infinitive whose participle is the epithet (see the general article). Merry notes that οἴῳ is attracted into the dative of τῷ, “to him,” from the usual accusative with the infinitive (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 10.495; [[teiresias]], [[the-blind-seer]]).
