---
summary: The bristles fall from the companions' limbs, “that had been made to grow before / by the ruinous drug that lady Circe had given them” (10.393–394).
grc: [οὐλόμενον]
---

οὐλόμενος, the participle of ὄλλυμι that the Iliad's second verse gives to the wrath, “that ruinous wrath” (*Iliad* 1.2), is here the drug that turned men into swine; another drug undoes it (10.391–396; [[drugs]], [[circe]]).
