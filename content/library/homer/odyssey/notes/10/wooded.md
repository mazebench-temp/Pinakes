---
summary: “Then Hermes went away to high Olympus / across the wooded island” (10.307–308).
grc: [ὑλήεσσαν]
---

Aeaea is wooded throughout the book: the smoke of Circe's halls rises “through the thick brush and the woods,” and her house stands in the glens (10.150, 10.197, 10.210; [[aeaea]], [[high-olympus]]).
