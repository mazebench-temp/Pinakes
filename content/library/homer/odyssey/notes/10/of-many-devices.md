---
summary: “Son of Laertes, sprung from Zeus, Odysseus of many devices” (10.401, 10.488, 10.504), three times in Circe's mouth.
---

Each time the verse opens a command, and the last two send him on a journey that no man has made: “No one has ever yet come to the house of Hades in a black ship” (10.502). Circe had named him first with the other epithet of his wits, “Odysseus of many turns” (10.330; [[of-many-turns]], [[son-of-laertes]], [[sprung-from-zeus]]).
