---
summary: Circe addresses Odysseus three times with the full verse, “Son of Laertes, sprung from Zeus, Odysseus of many devices” (10.401, 10.488, 10.504).
---

The verse of Calypso's farewell question (5.203), and of Athena's in the Iliad (*Iliad* 2.173), opens each of Circe's orders: to bring the ship to land and fetch the companions (10.401–405), to leave her house for the house of Hades (10.488–495), and to go there without a pilot, by the North Wind (10.504–540). In Book 11 the same verse is spoken by Elpenor, Teiresias, Agamemnon, Achilles and Heracles (11.60, 11.92, 11.405, 11.473, 11.617). See [[sprung-from-zeus]], [[of-many-devices]] and [[laertes]].
