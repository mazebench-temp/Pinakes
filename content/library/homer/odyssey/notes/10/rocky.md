---
summary: Three times Odysseus climbs “to a rugged lookout”: on the coast of the Laestrygonians (10.97), and twice on Aeaea, when he sees the smoke of Circe's halls and when he tells his companions of it (10.148, 10.194).
grc: [παιπαλόεσσαν]
en: [rugged]
---

σκοπιὴν ἐς παιπαλόεσσαν ἀνελθών is a verse of the book (see the Odyssey translation's key words), with the adjective that the translation renders “rocky” of islands and paths and “rugged” here. Merry connects it with πάλλω, reduplicated, of the rugged lines of upheaved rock (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 3.170; [[spies-and-scouts]]).
