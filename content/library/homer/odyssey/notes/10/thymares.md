---
summary: Odysseus is bathed with water from the great tripod, “mixing it to suit my heart” (10.362).
grc: [θυμῆρες]
en: [to suit my heart]
---

θυμῆρες κεράσασα, of the bath water mixed to the right warmth (10.358–363). Merry takes the word predicatively with κεράσασα, and doubts whether θυμήρης is the same word as θυμαρής, since the accent seems against it: he refers θυμαρής to the stem of ἀραρίσκω and takes θυμήρης for an adjective formed from θυμός (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 10.362; see the Odyssey translation's key words, θυμῆρες; [[bathing]]).
