---
summary: Aeolus' dismissal: “So saying, he sent me from the house, groaning heavily” (10.76).
---

βαρέα στενάχοντα, the phrase of men carried over the sea by storms in Books 4 and 5 (4.516, 5.420), is here Odysseus driven from the house of the host who had once sent him on his way. The summary of his wanderings that he gives Penelope gives the phrase to the storm that carried him back from Aeolus' island (23.314–317; [[aeolus]], [[xenia]]).
