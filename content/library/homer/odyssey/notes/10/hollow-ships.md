summary: Aeolus binds the skin fast “in the hollow ship” (10.23); Odysseus tells Eurylochus he may stay “eating and drinking beside the hollow black ship” (10.272), and later “Nor was Eurylochus left behind beside the hollow ship” (10.447).
grc: ["νηὶ δʼ ἐνὶ γλαφυρῇ"]
---

γλαφυρῇ at 10.23 is the other word for the hollow ship, as at 9.99 and 9.548. κοίλῃ παρὰ νηί frames Eurylochus' part: offered the safety of the ship, he does not stay there after all, for fear of Odysseus' rebuke (10.271–273, 10.447–448; [[eurylochus]]).
