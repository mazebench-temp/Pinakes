# πόσις at 10.176 is "drink" (βρῶσίς τε πόσις τε, "food and drink"), as at 1.191 and 6.209, 6.246, 6.248; again 12.320.
except: [10.176]
---

The scouts find the king's wife “as huge as a mountain peak”, and she calls from the place of assembly “famous Antiphates, / her husband, who devised a grim destruction for them” (ὃν πόσιν, 10.113–115; see [[antiphates-the-laestrygonian]] and [[marriage]]). The πόσις of 10.176, the drink still left in the ship, is another word (see [[fasting]]).
