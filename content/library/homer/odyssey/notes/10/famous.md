κλυτὰ δώματα unelided, as the Iliad's house of Poseidon (*Iliad* 13.21; elided at 5.381). The famous harbor and the famous king are the trap and its master (10.87–132). Merry takes κλυτά of the dead as more than an ornamental epithet, since most of those who will appear are heroes or famous women (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 10.526; see the Odyssey translation's key words, κλυτός; [[aeolus]], [[the-tribes-of-the-dead]]).
