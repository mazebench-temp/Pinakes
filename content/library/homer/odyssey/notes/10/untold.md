κρέα τʼ ἄσπετα: the stag on the first day (10.184), every day of the year on Aeaea (10.467–468), and the last day before Odysseus asks to go (10.476–477), in the verses of the feasts of Book 9 (9.161–162; see the Odyssey translation's formulas; [[feast-type-scene]], [[sunset-lines]]).
