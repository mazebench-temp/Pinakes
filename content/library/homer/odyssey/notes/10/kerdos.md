---
summary: On the lookout of Aeaea Odysseus weighs whether to go toward the smoke himself, and “this seemed to me the better way”: first to feed his men and send scouts (10.151–155).
---

Having seen the smoke from Circe's halls, Odysseus ponders whether to go and find out, “And as I pondered, this seemed to me the better way: / first to go to the swift ship and the shore of the sea, / and give my companions their meal, and send some of them to find out” (ὧδε δέ μοι φρονέοντι δοάσσατο κέρδιον εἶναι, 10.153–155; see [[decision-scene]] and [[spies-and-scouts]]). The verse closes the deliberation of the decision scene with the course that seemed the better, as at 5.474 and *Iliad* 13.458 (see the translation's table of formulas).
