---
summary: On the morning of Odysseus' departure Circe puts on her great silver-white mantle and casts a fine golden girdle around her waist (10.543–545), in the verses of Calypso's dressing.
---

“And the nymph herself put on a great silver-white mantle, / fine and lovely, and around her waist she cast a girdle, / fine and golden, and on her head she set a head covering” (10.543–545; see [[circe]], [[cloak]] and [[veil]]). The verses are Calypso's on the morning she shows Odysseus the trees for his raft (5.230–232; see [[calypso]]): the two goddesses who kept him for a year and for seven years dress alike to send him on his way.
