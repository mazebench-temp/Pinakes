---
summary: Four times: Odysseus obeys Circe, “So she spoke, and my proud spirit was persuaded” (10.406); they all yield to her, “So she spoke, and our proud spirit was persuaded” (10.466); after a year he yields to his companions, “So they spoke, and my proud spirit was persuaded” (10.475); and they to him, “So I spoke, and their proud spirit was persuaded” (10.550).
---

10.466 is the verse of Antinous' story of the web (2.103). The four verses mark the stay on Aeaea from the first welcome to the departure, and the third ends the year, after the companions' “Strange one, think now at last of your native land” (10.472–475; [[strange-one]], [[proud]], [[peitho]]).
