---
summary: At Circe's house “the strong-clawed wolves and lions fawned / around these men” (10.218–219).
grc: [κρατερώνυχες]
en: [strong-clawed]
---

The epithet of horses and mules is given here to beasts of prey, and the translation renders it “strong-clawed” (see the Odyssey translation's key words, κρατερῶνυξ). The wolves and lions fawn like dogs on their master, for Circe has bewitched them (10.212–219; [[circe]]).
