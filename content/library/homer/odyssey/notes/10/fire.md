---
summary: Within sight of Ithaca Odysseus' men can see the men of the island tending their fires (10.30). On Aeaea the smoke of Circe's halls shows him that the island is inhabited (10.149–152), and in her house the fourth handmaid kindles a great fire under the tripod for his bath (10.358).
# πυρπολέοντας, "men tending fires" (10.30), found only here in Homer; it applies in the Odyssey only.
grc: [πυρπολέοντας]
---

**The fires of Ithaca.** On the tenth day from Aeolus' island “our native fields were already coming into sight, / and already we could see men tending fires, we were so near” (10.29–30; see [[ithaca]] and [[skin-of-the-winds]]). W. W. Merry sets beside the word the Iliad's simile of the fire that sailors see burning high in the mountains while the storm carries them away from their friends (*Iliad* 19.375–378; see [[the-fire-seen-by-sailors]]); the fires are usually taken for the watch fires of shepherds, but since the day had dawned he prefers to think that the ship had been sighted and a fire lit to guide her in, or else that it was a fire for some work of the farm, put into the picture to show how near they had come to home (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 10.30; see the translation's row πυρπολέω). Then Odysseus falls asleep and the winds are let out.

**The smoke of Aeaea.** From the lookout on Aeaea Odysseus sees “smoke … rising from the wide-wayed earth, / in the halls of Circe, through the thick brush and the woods” (10.149–150), and ponders “whether to go and find out, since I had seen the fiery smoke” (10.152; see [[circe]] and [[decision-scene]]). αἴθοπα καπνόν is probably smoke lit by the fire beneath it, glowing red, Merry explains; αἶθοψ is said elsewhere of bronze and of wine (on 10.152; see the translation's row αἶθοψ). In the land of the Laestrygonians too he had seen only smoke rising from the land (10.99).

**The fire of the bath.** “And the fourth brought water and kindled a great fire / under a great tripod; and the water grew warm” (10.358–359; see [[tripod]] and [[bathing]]).
