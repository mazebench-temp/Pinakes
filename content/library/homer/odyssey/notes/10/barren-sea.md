---
summary: The companions, uncovering their heads, “along the shore of the barren sea / they gazed at the stag” (10.179–180).
---

They had muffled their heads in despair, as Odysseus did on the ship when the winds broke loose (10.53); Merry compares Odysseus weeping at the song of Demodocus (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 10.179, citing 8.85; [[deer]], [[sea]]).
