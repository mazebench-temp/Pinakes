summary: The meals of the book: on Aeolus' island the companions take their meal by the ships (10.56–58); on Aeaea the feast of the stag lasts till sunset (10.180–186); at Circe's house a meal is set before Odysseus that he will not eat (10.366–387); the second band feasts in the halls (10.449–452); and a year of feasting follows (10.460–479).
refs: [10.56-58, 10.180-186, 10.366-387, 10.449-452, 10.460-479]
---

The verses of the scene are all here: the washing of hands and “a glorious feast,” the seated guest and the five verses of the water for the hands and the bread, and the day's feasting until the sun goes down (10.182–186, 10.366–372, 10.476–477). The meal at Circe's is refused: Odysseus will not taste food or drink until he has seen his companions freed (10.373–387; [[untold]], [[water-for-the-hands]], [[sunset-lines]], [[xenia]]).
