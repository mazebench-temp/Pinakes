summary: Circe bids Odysseus “set up your mast and spread the white sails” (10.506); in the Laestrygonians' harbor “there was a white calm all around” (10.94); and “white barley meal” is sprinkled over the pit (10.520).
grc: ["λευκὴ δʼ ἦν ἀμφὶ γαλήνη", "ἄλφιτα λευκὰ"]
---

λευκὴ γαλήνη is the still water of the enclosed harbor, where “no wave ever swelled” (10.93–94; see the Odyssey translation's key words). The barley meal is white as in Hecamede's mixed drink (*Iliad* 11.640) and in the rite as Odysseus performs it (11.28; [[barley-meal]], [[harbor]], [[mast]]).
