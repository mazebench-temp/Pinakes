---
summary: Hermes shows Odysseus the herb: “moly the gods call it; and it is hard to dig up / for mortal men; but the gods can do all things” (10.305–306).
---

The gods' name stands alone: men have none, for they cannot dig the plant up, and the verse turns from the name to the gods' power. Merry thinks the moly most likely the poet's own creation (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 10.305; see the translation's note on 10.305; [[moly]], [[hermes]]).
