---
summary: “the ruinous drug that lady Circe had given them” (10.394), and Odysseus to his sleeping companions, “for lady Circe has shown me the way” (10.549).
---

πότνια Κίρκη, the title by which the goddess was first named in the poem (8.448; again 12.36; see the Odyssey translation's formulas). At 10.394 a scholion records a variant δῖα θεάων (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, critical note on 10.394), and at 10.549 two Vienna manuscripts read θέσφατα for πότνια, which Bekker would adopt (see the translation's note on 10.549; [[circe]]).
