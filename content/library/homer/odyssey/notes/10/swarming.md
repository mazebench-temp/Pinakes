---
summary: In the simile of the calves, “lowing without ceasing they run around / their mothers” (10.413–414).
grc: ["ἁδινὸν μυκώμεναι"]
---

ἁδινόν of sound, “without ceasing,” as in the laments of the Iliad and of Penelope (4.721). Merry joins it with μυκώμεναι, not with ἀμφιθέουσι, on the analogy of ἁδινόν or ἁδινὰ στενάχειν and κλαίειν (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 10.413; [[of-the-fields]], [[cattle]]).
