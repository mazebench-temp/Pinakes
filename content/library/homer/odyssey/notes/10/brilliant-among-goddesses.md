---
summary: Circe four times: “And she came and stood near me and spoke, brilliant among goddesses” (10.400 = 10.455), and “So I spoke, and at once she answered, brilliant among goddesses” (10.487, 10.503).
---

The first verse is the Odyssey's own, after Eidothea's “She came and stood near me, and spoke and said” (4.370); the second is Eidothea's answer to Menelaus (4.382, 4.398; see the Odyssey translation's formulas). Each time Circe brings an order or an answer: she sends Odysseus to fetch his companions from the ship (10.400–405), stops the lament when the two bands meet (10.455–465), and answers his two pleas, to be sent home and to be told who will guide them to the house of Hades (10.487–495, 10.503–540). In Book 9 Odysseus gave the formula to Calypso (9.29; [[circe]], [[then-answered]]).
