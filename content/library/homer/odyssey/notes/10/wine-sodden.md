---
summary: Elpenor, “longing for the cool air, had lain down to sleep, sodden with wine” (10.555).
---

οἰνοβαρείων, the Cyclops' word (9.374), is here the youngest companion's, asleep apart on Circe's roof (10.552–560). His soul blames “an evil fate from a god … and endless wine” (11.61, a plain rendering; [[elpenor]], [[wine]]).
