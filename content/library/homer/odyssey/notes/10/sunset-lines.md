summary: The feast of the stag ends “So all day long, until the sun went down, / we sat feasting on untold meat and sweet wine; / when the sun went down and darkness came on, / and then we lay down to sleep where the sea breaks upon the shore” (10.183–186); and the last day of the year on Aeaea ends in the same verses, with “they lay down to sleep throughout the shadowy halls” (10.476–479).
grc: ["οἱ μὲν κοιμήσαντο"]
---

10.183–186 repeat the feasts of Book 9 (9.161–162, 9.168–169); at 10.476–479 the companions sleep in Circe's halls, and Odysseus goes up to her bed (10.480; see the Odyssey translation's formulas; [[untold]], [[feast-type-scene]], [[shadowy-mountains]]).
