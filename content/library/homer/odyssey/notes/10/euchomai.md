---
summary: At the pit Odysseus is to beg “the glorious tribes of the dead with prayers” (εὐχῇσι, 10.526).
---

The noun of εὔχομαι comes once in the book, in Circe's instructions: “But when you have begged the glorious tribes of the dead with prayers” (εὐχῇσι λίσῃ, 10.526), prayers made to the dead, not to the gods (see [[prayer]] and [[the-offering-to-the-dead]]). When Odysseus does it in Book 11, the prayers are joined with vows, εὐχωλῇσι λιτῇσί τε, “with vows and prayers” (11.34).
