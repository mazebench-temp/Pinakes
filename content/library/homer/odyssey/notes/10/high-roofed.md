---
summary: The Laestrygonian girl points out “the high-roofed house of her father” (10.111), and the companions remind Odysseus of “your high-roofed house and your native land” (10.474).
---

ὑψερεφὲς δῶ is the giant king's house, where his wife waits, “as huge as a mountain peak” (10.111–113). At 10.474 Merry's text has οἶκον ἐυκτίμενον, “your well-built house” (6.315), where Murray prints οἶκον ἐς ὑψόροφον (7.77; see the translation's note on 10.474; [[house]], [[dear-native-land]]).
