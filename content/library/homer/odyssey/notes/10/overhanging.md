---
summary: “Gladly out to the open sea, away from the overhanging rocks, fled / my ship” (10.131–132).
grc: [ἐπηρεφέας]
---

Merry takes these “beetling rocks” as the cliffs at the entrance of the harbor (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 10.131, referring to 10.89–90). The same adjective returns of the rocks against which Amphitrite's wave roars (12.59–60; see the Odyssey translation's key words, ἐπηρεφής; [[harbor]], [[lofty]]).
