---
summary: Circe tells Odysseus that he needs no guide to the house of Hades: “let no longing for a guide trouble you beside your ship” (10.505).
---

When Odysseus asks who will guide him to the house of Hades, Circe answers: “let no longing for a guide trouble you beside your ship, / but set up your mast and spread the white sails, / and sit; and the breath of the North Wind will carry her” (μή τί τοι ἡγεμόνος γε ποθὴ παρὰ νηὶ μελέσθω, 10.505–507; see [[hegemon]], [[pompe]] and [[boreas]]). παρὰ νηί goes best with μελέσθω, as though Odysseus might be hanging about the ship waiting for a pilot (W. W. Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 10.505).
