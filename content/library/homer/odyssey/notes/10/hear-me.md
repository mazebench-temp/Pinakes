---
summary: Odysseus to his companions on Aeaea: “Hear my words, my companions, though you are suffering evils” (10.189); and twice “the goddess heard my voice” (10.311, 10.481).
grc: ["κέκλυτέ μευ μύθων", "μευ ἔκλυεν αὐδῆς"]
en: [Hear my words, heard my voice]
---

κέκλυτέ μευ μύθων opens a speech to a gathering, a verse of the Odyssey's (again 12.271, 12.340; see the Odyssey translation's formulas). θεὰ δέ μευ ἔκλυεν αὐδῆς closes a call that is heard, as a prayer is: Circe hears Odysseus' shout at her door and his entreaty by her knees (10.310–311, 10.480–481; [[assembly-type-scene]], [[supplication-type-scene]]).
