---
summary: “But when you have begged the glorious tribes of the dead with prayers” (10.526).
---

Merry takes κλυτά as more than an ornamental epithet here, since most of those who are about to appear are heroes or famous women (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 10.526); the prayers are the vows of the verses before (10.521–525; [[famous]], [[the-strengthless-heads]]).
