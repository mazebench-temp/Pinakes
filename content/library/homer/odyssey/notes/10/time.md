# τίμιος (10.38), only here.
grc: [τίμιος]
en: [honored]
---

The companions measure their leader's honor by his gifts: “Ah, how dear and honored this man is / by all men, to whatever people's city and land he comes! / Many fine treasures he is bringing from Troy / out of the spoil, while we, who have gone the same road to its end, / are going home, all of us, with empty hands” (φίλος καὶ τίμιος, 10.38–42; see [[doron]], [[gold-and-silver]] and [[hetairos]]). For τίμιος a scholium records the reading τιμῇς, that is τιμήεις (W. W. Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, his critical note on 10.38).
