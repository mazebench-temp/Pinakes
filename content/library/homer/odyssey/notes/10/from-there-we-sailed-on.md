---
summary: The refrain twice more: after Aeolus has driven Odysseus from his island, the first verse alone, “From there we sailed on, grieving at heart” (10.77); and after the Laestrygonians, the whole couplet for the last time, “From there we sailed on, grieving at heart, / glad to have escaped death, though we had lost our dear companions” (10.133–134).
---

No companion was lost on Aeolus' island, but the escort and the sight of home were (10.28–55, 10.78–79); among the Laestrygonians every ship but Odysseus' own perished with its crew (9.159; 10.121–132). The next verse brings the one ship to Aeaea, and the refrain is not heard again (10.135; see the Odyssey translation's formulas; [[aeolus]], [[laestrygonians]]).
