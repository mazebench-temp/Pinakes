---
summary: Circe makes Odysseus' companions a mixture of cheese, barley meal and pale honey with Pramnian wine, and mixes her drugs into it (10.234–236).
---

“And for them she made a mixture of cheese and barley meal and pale honey / with Pramnian wine; and she mixed into the food / grim drugs” (10.234–236; see [[kykeon]], [[barley-meal]], [[honey]] and [[drugs]]). It is the drink that Hecamede made for Nestor and Machaon, with goat's cheese grated into Pramnian wine and barley meal sprinkled over it (*Iliad* 11.638–640), with honey stirred in, which Hecamede set beside the drink rather than in it (W. W. Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 10.234).
