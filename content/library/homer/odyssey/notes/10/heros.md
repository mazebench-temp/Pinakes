---
summary: Circe addresses Odysseus as “hero” when she tells him where to dig the pit for the dead (10.516).
---

In her instructions for the journey to the house of Hades, Circe turns to Odysseus at the decisive point: “there then, hero, draw close, as I bid you, / and dig a pit about a cubit's length this way and that” (ἔνθα δʼ ἔπειθʼ, ἥρως, 10.516–517; see [[the-offering-to-the-dead]] and [[circe]]). The word is the address of the living warriors of the Iliad, and here a goddess gives it to the man she sends among the dead.
