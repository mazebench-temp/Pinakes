---
summary: Three times the companions are “shedding big tears” (10.201, 10.409, 10.570), and Circe bids them “Rouse this swelling lament no longer” (10.457).
en: [swelling]
---

θαλερὸν δάκρυ, the “big tear” of the Iliad's weeping heroes, and θαλερὸς γόος, “swelling lament” (see the Odyssey translation's key words). Merry quotes Eustathius, who explains θαλερόν of the lament by its likeness to θαλερὸν δάκρυ, as unceasing and ever fresh, and compares θαλερὴ φωνή (4.705; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 10.457; [[lament]], [[shedding-tears]]).
