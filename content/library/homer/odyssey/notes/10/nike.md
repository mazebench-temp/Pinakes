---
summary: The only victory in the book is that of a bad counsel: “the evil counsel of my companions prevailed” (10.46).
en: [prevailed]
---

“So they spoke, and the evil counsel of my companions prevailed” (βουλὴ δὲ κακὴ νίκησεν ἑταίρων, 10.46): νίκησεν is “carried the day”, as when one of the suitors complains that there will be no pleasure in the feast, since the worse prevails (ἐπεὶ τὰ χερείονα νικᾷ, 18.404, a plain rendering; W. W. Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 10.46; see [[boule]] and [[hetairos]]).
