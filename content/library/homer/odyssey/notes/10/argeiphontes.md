---

The two titles of Hermes stand together only at 10.331, in Circe's recollection of his prophecy; in Odysseus' own narrative he is “Hermes of the golden wand” when he comes and “the slayer of Argus” when he gives the moly (10.277, 10.302; [[of-the-golden-wand]], [[moly]], [[hermes]]).
