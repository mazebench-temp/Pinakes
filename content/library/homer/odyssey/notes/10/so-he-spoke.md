summary: Twenty-nine speeches close with the formula in the book, ten of them with Odysseus' “So I spoke”; the companions' envy ends “So they spoke, and the evil counsel of my companions prevailed” (10.46), and the companions at the ship leave with “So saying, they went up from the ship and the sea” (10.446).
grc: ["ὣς ἔφασαν", "ὣς ἔφαν", "ὣς φάμενοι"]
---

ὣς ἔφασαν, ὣς ἔφαν and ὣς φάμενοι are plural closes new to the entry (10.46, 10.422, 10.446, 10.475); ὣς φάσαν closes the questions of Aeolus' household (10.67), and ὣς ἄρʼ ἐφώνησεν Polites' words at the door (10.229). Every close in the book is Odysseus' own, since the whole book is his speech ([[the-tale-to-alcinous]], [[answering-him]]).
