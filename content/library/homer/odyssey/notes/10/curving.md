---
summary: In the Laestrygonians' harbor “there they all steered their curving ships inside” (10.91), and on Aeaea Odysseus is “close to the curving ship” when a god sends him the stag (10.156).
---

The plural is the fleet that went in, all of which perished there “in a body” (10.91–92, 10.132); the singular is the one ship left (10.156–158; [[ships]], [[harbor]]).
