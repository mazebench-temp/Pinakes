---
summary: Eurylochus' report begins “We went, as you bade us, through the brush, shining Odysseus” (10.251).
grc: [φαίδιμʼ]
---

φαίδιμʼ Ὀδυσσεῦ, the address that Teiresias will use in the next book (11.100). Merry takes the abrupt clauses that follow, “we went … we found,” as the broken speech of the excited messenger (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 10.251; [[eurylochus]]).
