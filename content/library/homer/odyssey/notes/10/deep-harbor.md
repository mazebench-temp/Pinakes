λιμένος πολυβενθέος ἐντός, the half-verse of the harbor of Ithaca in Book 16 (16.324, 16.352). The Laestrygonians' “famous harbor,” closed by cliffs, with a narrow entrance and no wave ever swelling in it, is a trap for the ships moored inside (10.87–94, 10.121–125; [[harbor]], [[lofty]], [[overhanging]]).
