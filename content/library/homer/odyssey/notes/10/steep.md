---
summary: “and on the seventh we came to the steep citadel of Lamus” (10.81).
---

αἰπὺ πτολίεθρον is the half-verse of Dium in the Catalogue (*Iliad* 2.538) and of Pylos (3.485, 15.193). Some took Lamus for the name of the town; Merry, with the scholia, takes him for the king (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 10.81; see the translation's note on 10.81–86; [[telepylus]]).
