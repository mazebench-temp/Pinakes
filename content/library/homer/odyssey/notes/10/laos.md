---
summary: Circe calls Odysseus “leader of the people” when she promises him that Teiresias will come to the pit at once (10.538).
---

The book's one λαός is a title: “Then the seer will come to you at once, leader of the people” (ὄρχαμε λαῶν, 10.538; see [[leader-of-men]] and [[teiresias]]). The people Odysseus leads are by now the crew of a single ship (see [[hetairos]]).
