---
summary: Aeolus sends Odysseus away as “most despised of the living” (10.72).
---

When the storm brings Odysseus back to the floating island, Aeolus drives him off with the superlative of disgrace: “Off with you from the island, quickly, most despised of the living” (ἐλέγχιστε ζωόντων, 10.72), for a man who comes back so must be “hated by the blessed gods” (10.74; see [[themis]], [[aeolus]] and [[xenia]]). ἔρρε is, in the scholium Merry quotes, “begone to your ruin” (W. W. Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 10.72).
