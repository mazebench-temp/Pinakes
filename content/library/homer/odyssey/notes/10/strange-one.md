---
summary: After a year on Aeaea the companions call Odysseus out: “Strange one, think now at last of your native land” (10.472).
grc: [δαιμόνιʼ]
---

Merry: δαιμόνιος always implies that a man is “possessed,” under the influence of something superhuman, bad or good, and so it is said when his behavior or intentions excite wonder (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 10.472). The companions' reproach ends the year, and Odysseus' “proud spirit was persuaded” (10.475; [[our-proud-spirit-was-persuaded]], [[nostos]]).
