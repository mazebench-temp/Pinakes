---
summary: “then I went up into the beautiful bed of Circe” (10.347), and a year later, “But I went up into the beautiful bed of Circe / and entreated her by her knees” (10.480–481).
---

The verse comes first after the oath, as Hermes had bidden him not to refuse the bed of the goddess (10.296–301, 10.343–347), and again when he asks her to keep her promise and send him home (10.480–486; [[bed]], [[circe]], [[supplication]]).
