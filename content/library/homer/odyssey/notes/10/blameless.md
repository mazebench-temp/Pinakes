---
summary: Odysseus wakes to the storm “and pondered in my noble heart / whether I should fall from the ship and perish in the sea, / or endure in silence and remain still among the living” (10.50–52).
---

κατὰ θυμὸν ἀμύμονα, the phrase of Ajax's knowledge that the gods were against him (*Iliad* 16.119), and of Odysseus' pondering again in Book 16 (16.237). The noble heart chooses to endure (10.53; [[decision-scene]], [[pondering-two-ways]], [[thymos]]).
