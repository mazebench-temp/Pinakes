---
summary: Elpenor, “not very / valiant in war nor sound in his wits” (10.552–553).
---

ἄλκιμος ἐν πολέμῳ, the warrior's praise, given with a negative: Elpenor is introduced by what he was not ([[litotes]]). Merry renders φρεσὶν ᾗσιν ἀρηρώς “steady in mind,” like the later φρενήρης (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 10.553; [[elpenor]]).
