# "ἄλκιμος ἐν πολέμῳ" (10.553), only here.
grc: ["ἄλκιμος ἐν πολέμῳ"]
en: [valiant in war]
---

The companion who dies on Aeaea is introduced by what he lacked: “There was one Elpenor, the youngest, not very / valiant in war nor sound in his wits” (οὔτε τι λίην / ἄλκιμος ἐν πολέμῳ οὔτε φρεσὶν ᾗσιν ἀρηρώς, 10.552–553; see [[elpenor]] and [[phrenes]]). The understatement, “not very”, denies him both courage in war and good sense (see [[litotes]]).
