---
summary: Eurylochus to the companions: “Ah, wretched men, where are we going? Why do you long for these evils” (10.431).
---

ἆ δειλοί, the address of pity for men who do not see what lies ahead, spoken by the one who saw the others vanish into Circe's house (10.259–260, 10.429–437); Merry renders πόσʼ ἴμεν “Whither are we going?” (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 10.431, citing 6.199; [[eurylochus]]).
