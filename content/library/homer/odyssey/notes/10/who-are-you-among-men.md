---
summary: Circe, at Odysseus' knees: “Who are you among men, and from where? Where are your city and your parents?” (10.325).
---

The question put to strangers, asked here by the hostess who has failed to change her guest, and answered by herself from a prophecy (10.330–332; see the Odyssey translation's formulas). The text prints εἰς, as at 1.170, where the same verse in later books has εἶς (see the translation's note on 10.325; [[of-many-turns]], [[circe]]).
