---
summary: From the lookout on Aeaea “smoke appeared to me rising from the wide-wayed earth, / in the halls of Circe, through the thick brush and the woods” (10.149–150).
---

ἀπὸ χθονὸς εὐρυοδείης, the verse-end of the din over Sarpedon (*Iliad* 16.635), here of the smoke that leads the men to Circe's house; in the next book Elpenor's body is not yet buried beneath the same earth (11.52; [[aeaea]], [[circe]]).
