---
summary: Circe's instructions at the pit: “And earnestly implore the strengthless heads of the dead” (10.521), and “do not let the strengthless heads of the dead / come near the blood until you have questioned Teiresias” (10.536–537).
---

With the first goes the vow: a barren heifer for all the dead when he comes home, and a black sheep for Teiresias alone, to be sacrificed apart, so that the offering to the seer should not be confounded with the offering to the strengthless heads (10.521–525; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 10.524). The second keeps the dead from the blood until the seer has come (10.535–540; [[teiresias]], [[sacrifice]]).
