summary: “Circe of the lovely tresses, a dread goddess with human voice, / own sister of baleful Aeetes” (10.136–137).
---

Circe's first verses in the tale give her the whole formula, her family and her island together (10.135–139). The verse-end δεινὴ θεὸς αὐδήεσσα comes back when she sends the following wind (11.8, 12.150), and once of Calypso (12.449); the shorter ἐυπλόκαμος, δεινὴ θεός was Athena's and Calypso's in Book 7 (7.41, 7.246, 7.255; see the Odyssey translation's formulas; [[with-lovely-tresses]], [[with-human-voice]], [[oloophron]], [[circe]]).
