---
summary: “Nor did Ares of the golden reins keep a blind watch, / when he saw Hephaestus, famed for his craft, going away” (8.285–286).
---

ἀλαοσκοπιήν, “a blind watch” (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 8.285; see the translation's note on 8.285, with Aristarchus' and Zenodotus' readings). χρυσήνιος is said elsewhere in Homer only of Artemis, “and Artemis of the golden reins killed his daughter in her anger” (*Iliad* 6.205; [[ares]], [[the-song-of-ares-and-aphrodite]]).
