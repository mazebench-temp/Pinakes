# φῦλον (8.481; again 14.68, 14.181).
grc: [φῦλον]
en: [tribe]
---

φῦλον is a stock or kind, and singers are one, as the gods are a φῦλον beside men in Apollo's warning to Diomedes, “never alike is the race / of immortal gods and of men who walk on the earth” (*Iliad* 5.441–442; see [[gods-and-men-not-alike]]). The tribe of singers is loved by the Muse and taught by her, and so honored among all men (8.479–481; see [[singers]] and [[time]]).
