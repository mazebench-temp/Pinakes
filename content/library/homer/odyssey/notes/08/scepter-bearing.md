---
summary: Alcinous bids “the scepter-bearing kings” come to his house to make the stranger welcome (8.41), and they follow him there (8.47).
grc: [σκηπτοῦχοι]
---

They are the twelve kings who rule the land with him, “and I myself am the thirteenth” (8.390–391), and each of them gives the stranger a mantle, a tunic and a talent of gold (8.392–393; [[kings]], [[guest-gifts]]).
