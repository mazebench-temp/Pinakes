---
summary: “and young men stood up, many and noble” (8.110) for the games.
grc: ["πολλοί τε καὶ ἐσθλοί"]
en: [many and noble]
---

The pair is the Iliad's for warriors, “many and brave” (*Iliad* 5.624, 13.709), and the Odyssey's for the suitors, “many and noble” (6.284; see the Odyssey translation's formulas; [[the-games-of-the-phaeacians]]).
