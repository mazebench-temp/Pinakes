---
summary: Alcinous to the stranger: “So now you too must not hide with crafty thoughts / whatever I ask you; it is better for you to speak” (8.548–549), and “And tell me why you weep and mourn within your heart” (8.577).
refs: [8.548-549, 8.577]
---

The parts of the formula are here, the bidding not to hide, μηδὲ σὺ κεῦθε, and the question why the listener weeps, but spread through a long speech and turned to a host's request (see the general article). The answer is the tale of Books 9–12 ([[who-are-you-among-men]], [[odysseus-weeps-at-the-feast]]).
