summary: Hephaestus: “how Aphrodite, the daughter of Zeus, because I am lame, / always dishonors me” (8.308–309); and Odysseus to Demodocus: “Either the Muse, the child of Zeus, has taught you, or Apollo” (8.488).
grc: ["Διὸς πάϊς"]
---

Διὸς θυγάτηρ Ἀφροδίτη is the Iliad's verse-end (see the general article). The Muse is Διὸς πάϊς only here in Homer; the Iliad has the words once, in Hector's wish that he were the son of aegis-bearing Zeus (*Iliad* 13.825; [[aphrodite]], [[muses]]).
