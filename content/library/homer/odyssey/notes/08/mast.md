---
summary: The young men of the escort set up the mast of the Phaeacian ship as they launch her (8.52), in the verse of the suitors' launching (4.781). The loom, which ἱστός also names, excepts the line.
---

“And set the mast and the sails in the black ship” (8.52 = 4.781; see [[sails]], [[ships]] and the Book 4 note): the Phaeacian ship is made ready for the stranger's voyage on the morning of the games, and anchored out in the water (8.51–55). ἱστόν here is the mast, not the [[loom]], whose entry excepts the line.
