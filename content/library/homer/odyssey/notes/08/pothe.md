---
summary: The stranger's blessing on Euryalus: “And may no longing for this sword ever come upon you hereafter, / which you have given me, making amends with your words” (8.414–415).
---

ποθή is the longing for what is missing. The stranger wishes that the giver may never miss the gift, the sword with its silver hilt and ivory sheath (8.403–405, 8.414–415; see [[amends-and-reconciliation]], [[sword]] and [[euryalus-the-phaeacian]]). The wish answers Euryalus' own, that the stranger may see his wife and reach his native land (8.410–411; see [[nostos]]).
