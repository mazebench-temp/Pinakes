---
summary: The Phaeacians “came and sat down on the polished stones / near together” (8.6–7) at their place of assembly; and Odysseus: “I know well how to handle the polished bow” (8.215).
---

Nestor sits at dawn on such stones before his doors (3.406–407), and the elders who give judgment in the assembly on the shield of Achilles sit “on polished stones in the sacred circle” (*Iliad* 18.504; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 8.6, comparing *Iliad* 18.497–504; [[assembly]], [[bow]]).
