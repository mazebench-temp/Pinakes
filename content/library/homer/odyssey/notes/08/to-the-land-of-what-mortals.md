---
summary: Alcinous asks about the lands the stranger has seen, “both those who are harsh and savage and unjust, / and those who are kind to strangers, with a mind that fears the gods” (8.575–576).
grc: ["χαλεποί τε καὶ ἄγριοι οὐδὲ δίκαιοι"]
en: [harsh and savage and unjust]
---

These are the questions Odysseus asked himself when he woke on Scheria, with χαλεποί, “harsh,” in place of ὑβρισταί, “arrogant” (6.120–121); the host asks them of the lands his guest has seen, and the tale that answers them begins with the Cyclopes, of whom he asks them again (9.175–176; see the Odyssey translation's formulas; [[who-are-you-among-men]], [[xenia]]).
