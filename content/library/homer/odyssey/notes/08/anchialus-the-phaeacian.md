He is named in the middle of the second verse of the list, among names of the sea and of ships: “Nauteus and Prymneus and Anchialus and Eretmeus” (8.112; see [[nauteus]] and [[the-games-of-the-phaeacians]]). He wins none of the five contests (8.120–130).
