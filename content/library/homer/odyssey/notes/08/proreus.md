---
summary: One of the young men who rise for the games (8.113); he wins none of the contests and is not named again.
---

“Ponteus and Proreus, Thoon and Anabesineus” (8.113; see [[ponteus]] and [[the-games-of-the-phaeacians]]). His name, the look-out man at the prow, stands where Prymneus, the stern, stands in the verse before (8.112; see [[prymneus]]). On the text's spelling Πρωρεύς, without the iota subscript, see the translation's note on 8.113. He wins none of the five contests (8.120–130).
