---
summary: Euryalus likens the stranger not to an athlete but “to one who goes to and fro with a many-benched ship, / a leader of sailors who are traders” (8.161–162).
grc: [πολυκλήιδι]
---

Merry takes the merchant of the taunt as the captain of a crew of traders, in charge of a freight or watching over a home cargo (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 8.161; see the translation's note on 8.161–164, which records the variant θαμά for θʼ ἅμα; [[trade]], [[ships]], [[euryalus-the-phaeacian]]).
