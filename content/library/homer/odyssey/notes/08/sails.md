---
summary: The young men of the escort set the sails in the Phaeacian ship and spread them white (8.52–54), in the verses of the suitors' launching (4.781–783).
---

At the launching of the escort ship the young men “set the mast and the sails in the black ship, / and fitted the oars in their leather straps, / all in good order, and spread the white sails” (8.52–54, as 4.781–783; see [[mast]], [[oars]], [[white]] and the Book 4 note). The sails are spread while the ship rides at anchor, ready for the voyage (8.55; see [[ships]]).
