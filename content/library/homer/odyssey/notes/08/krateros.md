---
summary: Laodamas: “there is nothing else worse than the sea / to unstring a man, however strong he may be” (8.138–139); Apollo asks Hermes whether he would be willing, “held fast in strong bonds, / to sleep in bed beside golden Aphrodite” (8.336–337); and the lovers are loosed “from the bonds, strong as they were” (8.360).
grc: [κρατεροῖσι, κρατεροῦ]
---

καρτερός (8.139) is the other form of the word (see the general article). The strength of the bonds is the point of the song: Hephaestus forged them “unbreakable, not to be loosed” (8.275; [[privative-adjectives]], [[the-song-of-ares-and-aphrodite]], [[sea]]).
