---
summary: The herald sets a basket, κάνεον, with the table and the cup beside the blind singer Demodocus (8.69).
---

“And beside him he set a basket and a beautiful table, / and beside it a cup of wine” (8.69–70; see [[demodocus]], [[pontonous]], [[table]] and [[cup]]). The κάνεον is the bread basket of the meal, as in the hall of Odysseus, where the slave women heap bread beside the suitors in baskets (1.147; see the entry and [[feast]]).
