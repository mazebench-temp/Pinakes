---
summary: “there came the great helper / Hermes” (8.322–323).
---

Merry notes that ἐριούνης is used in this form only here and in *Iliad* 20.34, where again Poseidon the earth-holder and Hermes come together, and that the usual word is ἐριούνιος (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 8.322). Apollo calls him “giver of blessings” in the same scene (8.335; [[givers-of-blessings]], [[hermes]]).
