---
summary: Odysseus takes his great purple mantle “in his strong hands” and draws it over his head (8.84), and lets the discus fly “from his strong hand” (8.189).
grc: ["χερσὶ στιβαρῇσι", "στιβαρῆς ἀπὸ χειρός"]
---

στιβαρῆς ἀπὸ χειρός is the Iliad's half-verse for a spear leaving the hand (*Iliad* 13.505, 16.615); the hands that hide his tears are the hands that will throw (8.84–86, 8.186–193; [[discus]], [[purple]]).
