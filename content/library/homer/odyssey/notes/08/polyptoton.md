---
summary: Hephaestus refuses Poseidon's surety: “pledges for the worthless are worthless things even to accept” (8.351).
grc: ["δειλαί τοι δειλῶν"]
en: [for the worthless are worthless]
---

δειλαί τοι δειλῶν γε καὶ ἐγγύαι ἐγγυάασθαι: the adjective is set against itself in two cases, and the noun beside its own verb. Merry, after one of the scholia, takes δειλός as “sorry,” not, with Nitzsch, as “weak,” and cites Eustathius for the middle voice as the act of the one who accepts the pledge (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 8.351; see the translation's note on 8.351; [[pledges-for-the-worthless]], [[the-song-of-ares-and-aphrodite]]).
