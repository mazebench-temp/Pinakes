---
summary: Hephaestus' bonds could not be seen, “not even one of the blessed gods; for they were made exceedingly cunning” (8.281).
grc: [δολόεντα]
en: [cunning]
---

Merry joins περί with δολόεντα, “exceeding cunning” (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 8.281). The translation keeps “wily” for the goddesses and gives the bonds “cunning,” apart from τεχνήεις, “skillfully made,” the word of the same bonds at 8.297 (see the Odyssey translation's key words, δολόεις and τεχνήεις). The whole contrivance is a δόλος, “snare” (8.276, 8.282; [[dolos]], [[the-song-of-ares-and-aphrodite]]).
