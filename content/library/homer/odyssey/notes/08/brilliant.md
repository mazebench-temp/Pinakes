summary: Odysseus is “brilliant” four times in the book: “much-enduring brilliant Odysseus” (8.199, 8.446), “Then brilliant Odysseus spoke to Alcinous” (8.381), and in the song of the horse, “which once brilliant Odysseus brought up into the citadel as a snare” (8.494).
---

The singer gives the epithet to the Odysseus of the war, and the guest who hears it is the same man (8.492–495; [[much-enduring]], [[odysseus]]).
