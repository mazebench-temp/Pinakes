---
summary: “Then they made trial of painful wrestling” (8.126); and Odysseus has endured much, “passing through the wars of men and the grievous waves” (8.183).
grc: [ἀλεγεινά]
---

παλαιμοσύνης ἀλεγεινῆς is the wrestling of the Iliad's games (*Iliad* 23.701, where the text spells παλαισμοσύνης; see the translation's note on 8.103, 8.126). 8.183 is Achilles' verse for all he endured with Patroclus (*Iliad* 24.8), and the Odyssey says it of Odysseus twice more in Book 13 (13.91, 13.264; [[algos]], [[sea]]).
