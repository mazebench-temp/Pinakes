ἦλθε … ἦλθʼ … ἦλθεν: each god arrives in his own clause with his own epithet, and the next verse sets the goddesses apart, who stay at home (8.324; [[earth-holder]], [[great-helper]], [[far-worker]], [[womenfolk]], [[olympians]]).
