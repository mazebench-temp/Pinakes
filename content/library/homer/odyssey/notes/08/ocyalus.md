---
summary: One of the young men who rise for the games (8.111); he wins none of the contests and is not named again.
---

He is named second in the list, between Acroneus and Elatreus (8.111; see [[acroneus]], [[elatreus]] and [[the-games-of-the-phaeacians]]). His name, an epithet of ships, suits a people who say of themselves that they “are the best with ships” (8.247; see [[phaeacians]]). He wins none of the five contests (8.120–130).
