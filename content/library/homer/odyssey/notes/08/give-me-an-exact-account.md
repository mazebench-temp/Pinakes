---
summary: Alcinous' last question: “But come, tell me this, and give me an exact account: / where you were driven in your wanderings, and to what lands / of men you came” (8.572–574).
---

8.572 is Telemachus' verse to the goddess as Mentes (1.169; see the Odyssey translation's formulas). The question is answered by the tale of Books 9–12 (see [[the-tale-to-alcinous]], [[who-are-you-among-men]] and [[to-the-land-of-what-mortals]]).
