summary: In the ball dance “one of them would fling it toward the shadowy clouds, / bending back” (8.374–375).

νέφεα σκιόεντα are the Iliad's clouds driven by the winds (*Iliad* 5.525, 12.157). The verse is said again of Tantalus' fruit, which the wind flings toward the shadowy clouds whenever he reaches for it (11.592; [[ball]], [[dance]]).
