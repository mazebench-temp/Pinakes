---
summary: Odysseus sends Demodocus his portion: “For among all men upon the earth singers / have their share of honor and respect” (8.479–480).
grc: [ἐπιχθονίοισιν]
en: [upon the earth]
---

Men on the earth are set against the Muse who teaches the singers their lays (8.480–481). In the last book the gods will make a song for men upon the earth of Penelope's faithfulness (24.196–198; [[singers]], [[oime]], [[muses]]).
