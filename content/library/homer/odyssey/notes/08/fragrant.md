---
summary: Aphrodite goes “to Paphos, where she has a precinct and a fragrant altar” (8.363).
grc: [θυήεις]
---

The half-verse is the Iliad's for Zeus' precinct on Gargarus and for the Spercheius' at its springs (*Iliad* 8.48, 23.148; see the Odyssey translation's formulas). The *Hymn to Aphrodite* has the whole verse of Paphos with θυώδης in place of θυήεις (*Hymn to Aphrodite* 59; [[paphos]], [[gargarus]], [[altar]]).
