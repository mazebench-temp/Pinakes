---
summary: “Answering him, Odysseus of many wiles said” introduces his answers to Laodamas' invitation (8.152) and to Euryalus' amends (8.412), and “Answering her” his farewell to Nausicaa (8.463); “Looking at him darkly, Odysseus of many wiles said” his answer to Euryalus' taunt (8.165); and at supper he speaks to the herald and to Demodocus (8.474, 8.486).
---

Each speech is one of measure: he declines the contests for his sorrows, blesses the young man who has made amends, promises the girl his prayers all his days, and honors the singer above all mortals (8.153–157, 8.413–415, 8.464–468, 8.477–481, 8.487–498; [[answering-him]], [[looking-darkly]], [[odysseus]]).
