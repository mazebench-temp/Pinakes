---
summary: One of the young men who rise for the games (8.112); he wins none of the contests and is not named again.
---

“Nauteus and Prymneus and Anchialus and Eretmeus” (8.112; see [[nauteus]] and [[the-games-of-the-phaeacians]]). The verse begins with a pair joined by τε … τε, Ναυτεύς τε Πρυμνεύς τε, and the next verse begins in the same way, Ποντεύς τε Πρωρεύς τε, “Ponteus and Proreus” (8.113), so that the stern of the one verse stands in the place of the prow in the other (see [[ponteus]] and [[proreus]]). He wins none of the five contests (8.120–130).
