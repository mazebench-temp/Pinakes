# κλισίῃσι (8.501), first met in the Odyssey here and its only hut there; the Iliad's are in its Book 12 note. κλισίη in
# the Odyssey is also a chair (4.123, excepted by the entry; later 19.55).
grc: [κλισίῃσι]
---

The last song begins “from where they had gone on board their well-benched ships / and were sailing away, having thrown fire on their huts, / the Argives” (8.500–502; see [[fire]], [[the-wooden-horse]] and [[the-fall-of-troy]]). The camp of the Iliad, the leaders' huts beside the beached ships, ends in this verse. The huts were built of wood, W. W. Merry notes, as Achilles' was of fir timbers that the Myrmidons cut for their lord, and canvas tents were a later invention (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 8.501; *Iliad* 24.449–450).
