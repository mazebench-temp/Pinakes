---
summary: Odysseus to Nausicaa: “so may Zeus now grant it, the loud-thundering husband of Hera, / that I may come home and see the day of my homecoming” (8.465–466).
---

The verse-end of oaths and wishes in the Iliad (*Iliad* 7.411, 10.329); in the Odyssey it closes farewell wishes, here and twice at Sparta in Book 15 (15.112, 15.180; see the Odyssey translation's formulas; [[loud-thundering]], [[zeus]]).
