---
summary: The Muse set the singer on to sing “from a lay whose fame had at that time reached the wide sky” (8.74).
---

The fame that reaches the sky is in the Iliad Nestor's shield (*Iliad* 8.192); in the Odyssey Odysseus names himself to the Phaeacians with it in his first words of Book 9 (9.19–20), and he gives it to Penelope in Book 19 (19.108; see the translation's note on 8.74–82; [[kleos]], [[song-and-fame]], [[sky]]).
