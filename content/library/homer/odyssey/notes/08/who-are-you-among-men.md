The question is the host's of the pattern, put at length and late: the guest has eaten, been promised his escort, received his gifts and heard three songs before he is asked who he is, as Arete's question came only in the evening of Book 7 (7.237–239). Alcinous gives reasons the formula does not: no one is nameless, for parents give names to all when they are born (8.552–554), and the Phaeacian ships, which have no helmsmen, must know where they are going (8.556–563). The answer, his name, opens Book 9 (9.19; [[no-one-is-nameless]], [[give-me-an-exact-account]], [[to-the-land-of-what-mortals]], [[xenia]]).
