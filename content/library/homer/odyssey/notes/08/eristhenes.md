---
summary: “She had just come from her father, the mighty son of Cronus, / and had sat down” (8.289–290), when Ares comes to Aphrodite.
---

ἐρισθενής names Zeus as a father, as it always does in Homer (see the general article). The father matters to the song: Hephaestus will keep the lovers in the bonds “until her father gives me back the whole bride-price” (8.318; [[son-of-cronus]], [[daughter-of-zeus]], [[bride-price]]).
