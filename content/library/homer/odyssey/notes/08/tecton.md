---
summary: Named only in his son's patronymic, when his grandson Amphialus rises for the games: “son of Polyneus, the son of Tecton” (8.114).
---

The list of the young men gives a father to Amphialus alone, and a grandfather with him (8.114; see [[amphialus]], [[polyneus]] and [[the-games-of-the-phaeacians]]). Tecton is named in no other way.
