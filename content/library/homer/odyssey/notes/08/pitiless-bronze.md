---
summary: The first of the Trojans' three counsels about the horse: “either to split the hollow timber with the pitiless bronze” (8.507).
---

The bronze that kills men and beasts is turned on the timber of the horse, and the counsel is rejected (8.505–510; see the translation's note on 8.507, where Zenodotus may have read διατμῆξαι; [[decision-scene]], [[bronze]]).
