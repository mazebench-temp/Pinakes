---
summary: Athena goes through the city “devising a homecoming for great-hearted Odysseus” (8.9); Odysseus answers “Nausicaa, daughter of great-hearted Alcinous” (8.464); and Demodocus sings that Odysseus conquered at the house of Deiphobus “through great-hearted Athena” (8.520).
---

8.9 is the verse of Athena's journey to Nausicaa's bed (6.14), and 8.464 the formula of the daughter (6.17, 6.196, 6.213; see the Odyssey translation's formulas). The goddess is μεγάθυμος only here and in the same half-verse in Book 13, where the gifts of the Phaeacians were given him through her (13.121; [[athena]], [[nausicaa]], [[alcinous]]).
