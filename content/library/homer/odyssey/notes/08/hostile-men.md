---
summary: Odysseus: “I would be the first to shoot and strike my man in a throng / of hostile men, even if very many companions / stood near beside me and were shooting at the men” (8.216–218).
---

Merry takes the boast as one of speed and aim, picking off a man in the thick of the press while all his comrades are shooting (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 8.216; [[bow]], [[odysseus]]).
