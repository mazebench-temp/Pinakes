# ἀτασθάλῳ (8.166).
grc: [ἀτασθάλῳ]
en: [a reckless man]
---

ἀτάσθαλος is the man whose folly brings his own ruin. Euryalus has likened the stranger to a merchant, not an athlete; the stranger likens him in return, with the same verb of likeness (ἔοικας, as οὐδʼ ἀθλητῆρι ἔοικας, 8.164), to a reckless man (8.164–166; see [[euryalus-the-phaeacian]] and [[different-gifts]]). The young man is reckless in speech, not in deeds, and he makes amends with a sword (8.396–415; see [[amends-and-reconciliation]]).
