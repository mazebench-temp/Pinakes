---
summary: Alcinous: “The stranger seems to me to be a very wise man” (8.388); and in his last question, “Since in no way inferior to a brother / is a companion who has wise understanding” (8.585–586).
grc: ["πεπνυμένα εἰδῇ"]
en: [wise understanding]
---

The king's judgment follows the stranger's praise of the dancers, and leads to the gifts (8.381–395). At 8.586 a variant has κεχαρισμένα for πεπνυμένα (see the translation's note on 8.586; [[a-companion-no-worse-than-a-brother]], [[alcinous]]).
