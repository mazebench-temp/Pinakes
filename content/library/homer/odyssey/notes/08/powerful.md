---
summary: “Powerful Alcinous, preeminent among all the people” (8.382, 8.401): the stranger's address after the ball dance, and Euryalus' as he makes amends.
grc: [κρεῖον]
---

κρεῖον is the vocative of the Iliad's title of Agamemnon, κρείων. The whole verse of address comes four times more in Books 9–13, the first time to open the stranger's tale (9.2, 11.355, 11.378, 13.38; see the Odyssey translation's formulas; [[preeminent]], [[alcinous]]).
