---
summary: Demodocus is “honored by the people” (λαοῖσι τετιμένον, 8.472); Alcinous is “preeminent among all the people” (8.382, 8.401); Clytoneus runs ahead and reaches “the people” watching at the end of the course (8.125); and in the simile the warrior falls “before his city and his people” (8.524).
---

λαοί are the people of a city and its gathered crowd. Clytoneus wins the footrace “so far did he run out ahead and reach the people” (λαοὺς ἵκεθʼ, 8.125), the spectators at the end of the course (see [[clytoneus]] and [[the-games-of-the-phaeacians]]). Alcinous is “Powerful Alcinous, preeminent among all the people” (8.382 = 8.401; see [[preeminent]]); the singer is “honored by the people” (8.472; see [[time]]). In the simile of the third song the husband “has fallen before his city and his people, / keeping off the pitiless day from his town and his children” (8.524–525; see [[the-woman-weeping-over-her-husband]]).
