“Nauteus and Prymneus and Anchialus and Eretmeus” (8.112): the second verse of the list is made of four names of the sea and of ships, the sailor, the stern, the seaside and the oar (see [[prymneus]], [[anchialus-the-phaeacian]], [[eretmeus]] and [[the-games-of-the-phaeacians]]). Nauteus wins none of the five contests (8.120–130).
