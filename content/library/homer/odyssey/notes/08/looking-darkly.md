---
summary: “Looking at him darkly, Odysseus of many wiles said” (8.165), in answer to Euryalus' taunt.
---

The verse is Odysseus' answer to Agamemnon in the Iliad, to his rebuke at the review and to his proposal to launch the ships (*Iliad* 4.349, 14.82). Here it answers a young man of his host's people, and the speech that follows is his lesson on the gods' gifts (8.166–185; [[different-gifts]], [[euryalus-the-phaeacian]]).
