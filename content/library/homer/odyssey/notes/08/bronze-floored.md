---
summary: “So he spoke, and the gods gathered to the bronze-floored house” (8.321).
---

In the Iliad the bronze-floored house is the house of Zeus (*Iliad* 1.426, 14.173, 21.438, 21.505); here it is Hephaestus' own, the builder of the gods' houses (*Iliad* 1.606–608), and in Book 13 Alcinous calls his palace so (13.4; [[hephaestus]], [[olympians]]).
