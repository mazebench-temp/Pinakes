---
summary: Athena “poured a wondrous grace, upon his head and shoulders” (8.19); and Odysseus will tell all men “how the god has readily granted you wondrous song” (8.498).
grc: [θέσπιν]
---

θεσπεσίην … χάριν is Athena's gift to Telemachus before the assembly of Ithaca (2.12), as here before the assembly of the Phaeacians (8.17–20; [[gold-poured-around-silver]]). θέσπις is the shorter word, said of song: Penelope heard Phemius' “wondrous song” from her upper room (1.328), and Odysseus gives the word to Demodocus' gift when he asks for the song of the wooden horse (see the Odyssey translation's key words, θέσπις; [[demodocus]], [[divine]]).
