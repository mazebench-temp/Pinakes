---
summary: “and lord Apollo, son of Zeus, spoke to Hermes” (8.334), and he begins, “Hermes, son of Zeus, guide, giver of blessings” (8.335).
grc: ["Διὸς υἱέ"]
---

The title passes from the speaker to the god he speaks to: Apollo is ἄναξ Διὸς υἱός, as in his speeches to Athena in *Iliad* 7 (*Iliad* 7.23, 7.37), and Hermes is Διὸς υἱέ, a vocative found only here in Homer ([[apollo]], [[hermes]], [[guide]]).
