---
summary: Apollo: “Hermes, son of Zeus, guide, giver of blessings” (8.335); and “Then the guide, the slayer of Argus, answered him” (8.338).
grc: [διάκτορε]
---

διάκτορος ἀργεϊφόντης is the formula of Hermes' flight to Calypso in Book 5 and of his words to Leto in the Iliad (*Iliad* 21.497; see the Odyssey translation's formulas); the vocative διάκτορε is found only here in Homer ([[argeiphontes]], [[givers-of-blessings]], [[hermes]]).
