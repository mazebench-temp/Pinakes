---
summary: Nausithous' prophecy: “He said that one day a well-made ship of the Phaeacian men, / as it came back from an escort on the misty sea, / he would shatter” (8.567–569).
grc: [ἐυεργέα]
---

ἐυεργέα νῆα, “a well-made ship,” is the Odyssey's verse-end for a ship from here on (9.279, 11.70, 11.106, 11.159, 12.305), as Hermes' νηῦς εὐεργής in the Iliad (*Iliad* 24.396; see the Odyssey translation's formulas; [[ships]], [[the-migration-of-the-phaeacians]]).
