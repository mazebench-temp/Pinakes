summary: “Then the guide, the slayer of Argus, answered him” (8.338), and Hephaestus answers Poseidon's offer: “Then the renowned god, lame in both legs, answered him” (8.357).
---

8.357 is the Iliad's verse for his answers to Thetis, with “her” (*Iliad* 18.393, 18.462), and his answer is the verse with which Aphrodite grants Hera's request in the Iliad, “It is not possible, nor would it be fitting, to refuse your word” (8.358 = *Iliad* 14.212; [[answering-him]], [[lame-in-both-legs]]).
