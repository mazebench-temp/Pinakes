---
summary: Hephaestus “made as if to go to Lemnos, the well-built citadel” (8.283); and Nausicaa “stood by the doorpost of the strongly made hall” (8.458).
---

8.458 is the verse with which Penelope comes down among the suitors and stands by the doorpost (1.333; again 16.415, 18.209, 21.64); the girl stands where the queen of Ithaca will stand (see the Odyssey translation's key words, πύκα ποιητός; [[lemnos]], [[nausicaa]]).
