---
summary: Peleus is named only in his son's patronymic, Πηλεΐδεω Ἀχιλῆος, “Achilles, son of Peleus”, in Demodocus' song of the quarrel of Odysseus and Achilles (8.75).
---

νεῖκος Ὀδυσσῆος καὶ Πηλεΐδεω Ἀχιλῆος, “the quarrel of Odysseus and Achilles, son of Peleus” (8.75; see [[achilles]] and [[the-quarrel-of-odysseus-and-achilles]]). The phrase Πηλεΐδεω Ἀχιλῆος opens three verses of the Iliad (*Iliad* 15.64, 17.195, 20.85); the longer Πηληϊάδεω Ἀχιλῆος ends the first verse of the Iliad and the verse in which the shade of Achilles comes to Odysseus among the dead (*Iliad* 1.1; 11.467, Greek only).
