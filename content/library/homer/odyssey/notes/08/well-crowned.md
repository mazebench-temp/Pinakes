---
summary: Demodocus sings “about the love of Ares and well-crowned Aphrodite” (8.267), and Ares goes to Hephaestus' house “eager for the love of well-crowned Cytherea” (8.288).
grc: [ἐυστεφάνου]
---

The epithet opens the song and comes back when the lover comes to the goddess. The second verse-end returns in the nominative in Book 18, of the ambrosial beauty with which Cytherea anoints herself for the dance of the Graces (18.193; [[cytherea]], [[aphrodite]]).
