---
summary: Ares goes to the house of renowned Hephaestus, “eager for the love of well-crowned Cytherea” (8.288).
---

The first of the two uses of the name in Homer. A variant read Ἀφροδίτης for Κυθερείης here (see the translation's note on 8.288). The song that names the goddess by Cythera ends with her going to her other island, “to Cyprus, / to Paphos, where she has a precinct and a fragrant altar” (8.362–363; [[well-crowned]], [[cyprus]], [[paphos]], [[the-song-of-ares-and-aphrodite]]).
