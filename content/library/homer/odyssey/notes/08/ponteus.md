“Ponteus and Proreus, Thoon and Anabesineus” (8.113; see [[proreus]], [[thoon-the-phaeacian]], [[anabesineus]] and [[the-games-of-the-phaeacians]]). The verse begins as the one before it does, with a pair joined by τε … τε (see [[prymneus]]). He wins none of the five contests (8.120–130).
