---
summary: Apollo had told Agamemnon in prophecy “in sacred Pytho, when he crossed the stone threshold / to consult the oracle” (8.80–81).
---

Πυθοῖ ἐν ἠγαθέῃ: the stone threshold is the one Achilles names among the riches he would not take for his life, “the stone threshold of the Archer, / Phoebus Apollo, in rocky Pytho” (*Iliad* 9.404–405; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 8.80; see the translation's note on 8.80; [[pytho]], [[aphetor]]).
