---
summary: The stranger's blessing on Euryalus, who has given him a sword in amends: “And you too, my friend, fare very well, and may the gods grant you prosperity” (θεοὶ δέ τοι ὄλβια δοῖεν, 8.413).
---

Euryalus has wished him a homecoming, to see his wife and reach his native land (8.408–411); the stranger returns the wish with prosperity, the gods' gift, and with a wish that he may never miss the sword he has given (8.413–415; see [[amends-and-reconciliation]] and [[euryalus-the-phaeacian]]). ὄλβια δοῖεν is the blessing of a guest to his host's people (see the entry and [[xenia]]).
