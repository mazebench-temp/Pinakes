---
summary: The Phaeacians flay and dress the victims “and made ready a lovely feast” (8.61); and at Paphos the Graces clothe Aphrodite “in lovely garments, a wonder to see” (8.366).
grc: [ἐπήρατα]
---

ἐπήρατος, “lovely,” is a word of the same root, which the Iliad gives to a feast and a citadel (*Iliad* 9.228, 18.512, 22.121) and the Odyssey to Ithaca (4.606); here the garments complete the bath and anointing of the goddess at her altar (8.364–366; [[the-adornment-of-a-goddess]], [[feast]]).
