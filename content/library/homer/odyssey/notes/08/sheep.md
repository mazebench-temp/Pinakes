“And for them Alcinous sacrificed twelve sheep, / and eight white-tusked hogs, and two shambling cattle; / these they flayed and dressed, and made ready a lovely feast” (8.59–61; see [[sacrifice]], [[boar]], [[cattle]] and [[feast]]). The feast is for the scepter-bearing kings whom he has bidden to his house and for the fifty-two young men of the escort, who come up from the ship (8.38–41, 8.56–58; see [[kings]]).
