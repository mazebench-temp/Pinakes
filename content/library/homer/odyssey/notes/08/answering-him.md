summary: The answers of the book: “Then Euryalus answered him in turn and said” (8.140, 8.400), and once “Then Euryalus answered him in turn and rebuked him to his face” (8.158); “Answering him, Odysseus of many wiles said” (8.152, 8.412), and “Answering her” to Nausicaa (8.463); “But Alcinous alone, answering him, said” (8.235); and in the song, “Then the renowned god, lame in both legs, spoke to him in turn” (8.349) and “Then Poseidon the earth-shaker spoke to him in turn” (8.354).
grc: ["ἀπαμείβετο νείκεσέ"]
---

8.140 is Alcinous' verse of Book 7 with another name (7.298; see the Odyssey translation's formulas); at 8.158 the scholia read φώνησέν τε for νείκεσέ τʼ ἄντην (see the translation's note on 8.158). 8.235 is Antinous' verse in the assembly of Ithaca, “but Antinous alone, answering him, said” (2.84), and the text ends 8.463 with a full stop, a slip (see the translation's note on 8.463; [[of-many-wiles]], [[then-answered]]).
