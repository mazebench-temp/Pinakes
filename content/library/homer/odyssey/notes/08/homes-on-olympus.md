---
summary: The gods say that Hephaestus, slow as he is, has caught Ares, “though he is the swiftest of the gods who hold Olympus” (8.331).
grc: ["οἳ Ὄλυμπον ἔχουσιν"]
en: [who hold Olympus]
---

θεῶν οἳ Ὄλυμπον ἔχουσιν is the shorter verse-end of the formula, the Iliad's in Dione's and Zeus' words (*Iliad* 5.404, 5.890) and Nausicaa's in Book 6 (6.240; [[olympus]], [[olympians]]).
