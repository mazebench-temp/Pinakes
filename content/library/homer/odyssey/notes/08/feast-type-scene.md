8.71–72 and 8.484–485 are the verses of Telemachus' feast in Book 1 (1.149–150), and both times the singer follows (8.73, 8.486–498). The morning feast ends when Alcinous calls the games: “Already we have had our hearts' fill of our equal share of the feast / and of the lyre, which is the companion of the rich feast” (8.98–99; [[equal-feast]], [[rich-feast]]). At supper the guest honors the singer from his own portion of honor (8.474–483; [[white-tusked]], [[desire-for-drink-and-food]], [[feast]], [[demodocus]]).
