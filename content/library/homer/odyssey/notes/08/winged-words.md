---
summary: Four speeches of asking and giving begin with the verse: Poseidon's plea to set Ares free, “and he spoke to him, addressing him with winged words” (8.346); Euryalus' farewell as he gives his sword (8.407); Arete's instruction about the knot on the chest, “and she spoke to him, addressing him with winged words” (8.442); and Nausicaa's farewell at the doorpost (8.460).
---

The verse is the Odyssey's from Telemachus' welcome of the stranger (1.122) and the goddess's word to him at the shore (2.269; see the Odyssey translation's formulas; [[so-he-spoke]], [[nausicaa]], [[euryalus-the-phaeacian]]).
