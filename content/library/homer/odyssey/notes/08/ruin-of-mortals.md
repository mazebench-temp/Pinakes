summary: “and up rose Euryalus too, the equal of Ares, ruin of mortals” (8.115), among the young Phaeacians at the games.
grc: [βροτολοιγῷ]
---

βροτολοιγῷ ἶσος Ἄρηϊ is the Iliad's verse-end for Hector leading the Trojans, “Hector, son of Priam, the equal of Ares, ruin of mortals” (*Iliad* 11.295, 13.802), and for Leonteus and Achilles in the accusative (*Iliad* 12.130, 20.46). Here the warrior's title goes to the athlete who wins the wrestling and taunts the stranger (8.127, 8.158–164; [[equal-of-ares]], [[euryalus-the-phaeacian]], [[ares]]).
