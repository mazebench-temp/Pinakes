---
summary: Apollo asks Hermes whether he would be willing, held fast in strong bonds, “to sleep in bed beside golden Aphrodite?” (8.337), and Hermes answers, “but let me sleep beside golden Aphrodite” (8.342).
grc: ["χρυσέῃ Ἀφροδίτῃ"]
---

Question and answer end with the same words. “Golden Aphrodite” is the Iliad's measure of a woman's beauty (see the general article); here the goddess herself is the prize for which Hermes would take three times as many bonds, and the gods laugh (8.339–343; [[aphrodite]], [[hermes]]).
