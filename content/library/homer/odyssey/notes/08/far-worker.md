---
summary: “and far-working lord Apollo came” (8.323).
---

Merry gives the usual sense, “working,” that is shooting, “from afar,” equivalent to ἑκηβόλος, and reports another comparison, with Latin *averruncus*, from ἑκάς and εἴργειν (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 8.323). ἄναξ ἑκάεργος Ἀπόλλων is the Iliad's verse-end (*Iliad* 15.253; see the Odyssey translation's formulas; [[apollo]], [[hekatebolos]]).
