---
summary: Demodocus takes up the song of the horse “from where they had gone on board their well-benched ships / and were sailing away, having thrown fire on their huts, / the Argives” (8.500–502).
grc: [ἐυσσέλμων]
---

ἔνθεν ἑλών, “taking it up from where”: the singer begins at a point of his choosing, as the Muse is asked to tell the story “beginning where you will” in the proem (1.10; see the translation's note on 1.10, which compares 8.500; [[ships]], [[sinon-and-the-wooden-horse]]).
