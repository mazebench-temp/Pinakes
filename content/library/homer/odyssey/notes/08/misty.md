---
summary: Nausithous used to say that Poseidon would one day shatter a Phaeacian ship “as it came back from an escort on the misty sea” (8.568).
---

The verse is said twice more in Book 13, when the prophecy is fulfilled (13.150, 13.176; see the Odyssey translation's formulas; [[sea]], [[the-migration-of-the-phaeacians]]).
