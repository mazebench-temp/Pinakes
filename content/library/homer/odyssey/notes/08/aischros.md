# ᾔσχυνε (8.269).
grc: [ᾔσχυνε]
en: [shamed]
---

αἰσχύνω is to disfigure and to disgrace, the verb of the shameful (see the entry). Ares gave Aphrodite many gifts to win her consent (W. W. Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 8.269) and dishonored her husband's bed (8.268–270; see [[the-song-of-ares-and-aphrodite]] and [[marriage]]). The shame falls on the husband who is wronged, and he calls the gods to see it: “deeds that are laughable, and hard to bear” (8.307; see [[laughter-of-the-gods]]). The goddesses, for their part, stay at home “for shame” (αἰδοῖ, 8.324; see [[aidos]]).
