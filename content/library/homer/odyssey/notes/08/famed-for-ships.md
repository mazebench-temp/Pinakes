---
summary: “the Phaeacians of the long oars, men famed for ships” (8.191 = 8.369).
---

The verse calls them seafarers when they cower before the stranger's discus and when they sit delighted at the song (8.190–192, 8.367–369; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 8.191; [[long-oared]], [[phaeacians]]).
