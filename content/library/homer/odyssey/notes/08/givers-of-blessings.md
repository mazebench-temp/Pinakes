---
summary: “And the gods, the givers of blessings, stood in the doorway” (8.325); and Apollo begins his question, “Hermes, son of Zeus, guide, giver of blessings” (8.335).
---

The plural stands where the gods gather in Hephaestus' doorway to see the lovers caught, and the next verse is their laughter (8.325–327); the singular is given to Hermes in the question that invites him to wish himself in Ares' place (8.334–337; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 8.325; see the translation's note on 8.325; [[olympians]], [[hermes]]).
