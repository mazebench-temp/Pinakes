---
summary: Alcinous sacrifices “eight white-tusked hogs” for the feast (8.60), and at supper Odysseus cuts for Demodocus a portion of the chine “of a white-tusked boar, and there was rich lard on either side” (8.476).
grc: [ἀργιόδοντας, ἀργιόδοντος]
---

The chine had been served to Odysseus as a mark of honor (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 8.475), as the long chine was given to Ajax after his duel with Hector (*Iliad* 7.321), and he shares it with the singer (8.474–483; [[plato-chine-of-ajax]], [[flourishing]], [[demodocus]]).
