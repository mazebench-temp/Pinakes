---
summary: Nausithous settled the Phaeacians in Scheria, “far from toiling men” (6.8).
---

The epithet names ordinary mortals against a people set apart; Nausicaa says it in her own words, “we live far apart in the sea of many waves, / the farthest of men” (6.204–205; [[of-many-waves]], [[phaeacians]], [[the-migration-of-the-phaeacians]]).
