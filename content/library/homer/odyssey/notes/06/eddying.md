---
summary: The girls drive the mules “along the eddying river / to graze on the honey-sweet grass” (6.89–90).
grc: [δινήεντα]
---

ποταμὸν πάρα δινήεντα ends the verse as πὰρ ποταμὸν … δινήεντα does of the Xanthus in the Iliad (*Iliad* 21.206). It is the river into whose “deep eddy,” δίνῃ, Nausicaa's ball will fall (6.116; [[the-river-of-scheria]], [[honey-sweet]]).
