Φαίηκες ἀγαυοί returns twice in Book 13, of the Phaeacians who gave Odysseus the treasures that are put ashore with him on Ithaca and that Athena helps him hide (13.120, 13.304). See [[phaeacians]], [[famous]] and [[boule]].
