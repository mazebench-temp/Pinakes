---
summary: Washing in the river, Odysseus wipes from his head “the scurf of the barren sea” (6.226).
---

The phrase ἁλὸς ἀτρυγέτοιο, which ends the verse “along the shore of the barren sea” (*Iliad* 1.316, 1.327), is divided here by χνόον, “the scurf,” a word found only here in Homer (see the Odyssey translation's key words; [[bathing]]).
