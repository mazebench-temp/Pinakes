---
summary: Artemis' nymphs are “daughters of aegis-bearing Zeus” (6.105), and Athena is “child of aegis-bearing Zeus” in Odysseus' prayer (6.324).
---

Both times the epithet belongs to a title of Zeus' daughters, the nymphs of the simile and the goddess of the prayer ([[child-of-aegis-bearing-zeus]], [[nymphs]], [[athena]]).
