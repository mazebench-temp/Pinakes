---
summary: “And at once Dawn of the fair throne came, and she woke her, / well-robed Nausicaa” (6.48–49).
---

It is the dawn, ἥ μιν ἔγειρε, that wakes the girl, the dawn the goddess named in the dream when she bade her go to wash “when dawn appears” and urge her father “in the early dawn” (6.31, 6.36; [[dawn-line]], [[eos]], [[well-robed]]). The nominative returns when Telemachus lands on Ithaca (15.495).
