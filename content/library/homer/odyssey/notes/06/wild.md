---
summary: In the simile, the lion goes after cattle or sheep “or after the wild deer” (6.133).
---

ἀγροτέρας ἐλάφους is the Iliad's phrase for the deer that Hera bids Artemis hunt on the mountains (*Iliad* 21.485–486), and the lion's quarry is the goddess's in the simile a few verses before, “delighting in boars and swift deer” (6.104; [[deer]], [[lion]], [[artemis]]).
