---
summary: In the simile of Nausicaa at play, “Artemis of the showering arrows goes over the mountains” (6.102).
---

The archer goddess of the simile hunts, “delighting in boars and swift deer” (6.104); elsewhere in the Odyssey her arrows are the sudden death of women (11.172–173, 15.478). Odysseus likens Nausicaa to her when he speaks (6.151). See [[artemis]] and [[artemis-among-her-nymphs]].
