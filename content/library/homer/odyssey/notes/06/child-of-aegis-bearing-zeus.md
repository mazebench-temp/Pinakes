summary: The nymphs of Artemis' company are “daughters of aegis-bearing Zeus” (6.105), and Odysseus' prayer begins “Hear me, child of aegis-bearing Zeus, Atrytone” (6.324).
grc: ["κοῦραι Διὸς αἰγιόχοιο"]
en: [daughters]
---

κοῦραι Διὸς αἰγιόχοιο is the title of the mountain nymphs who planted elms around Eetion's tomb (*Iliad* 6.420) and of the nymphs who rouse the goats for Odysseus' men (9.154; [[nymphs]]). The verse of the prayer is Penelope's (4.762), and both prayers are heard (4.767, 6.328; [[hear-me]], [[atrytone]]).
