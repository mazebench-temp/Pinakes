---
summary: The Phaeacians care for “masts and the oars of ships and balanced ships, / in which they glory as they cross the gray sea” (6.271–272).
---

The words are those of Odysseus' refusal of the raft in Book 5, where “not even balanced ships / cross, swift-sailing, glorying in the wind of Zeus” over the great gulf of the sea (5.174–176): there the ships glory, here the Phaeacians glory in their ships (see the Odyssey translation's key words, ἀγάλλομαι; [[gray-sea]], [[ships]], [[phaeacians]]).
