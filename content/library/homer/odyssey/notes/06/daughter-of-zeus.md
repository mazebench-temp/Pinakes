summary: Odysseus likens Nausicaa to “Artemis, the daughter of great Zeus” (6.151); “Athena, child of Zeus” makes him taller (6.229); and he prays “to the daughter of great Zeus” (6.323).
grc: ["Διὸς κούρῃ μεγάλοιο"]
---

Διὸς ἐκγεγαυῖα is otherwise Helen's title, in both poems (*Iliad* 3.199, 3.418; *Odyssey* 4.184, 4.219, 23.218); only here is it given to Athena. The dative verse-end Διὸς κούρῃ μεγάλοιο names Athena in the prayers of the Iliad (*Iliad* 6.304, 10.296) and Artemis once (*Iliad* 9.536); in this book it names both (6.151, 6.323; [[artemis]], [[athena]]).
