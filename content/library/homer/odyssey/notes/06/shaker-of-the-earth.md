---
summary: Odysseus reproaches Athena that she did not hear him “when the famous shaker of the earth wrecked me” (6.326).
---

κλυτὸς ἐννοσίγαιος is the phrase of Book 5 for the god who wrecked him (5.423); in the last verses of the book he is still unnamed, Athena's “father's brother” (6.329–331; [[poseidon]], [[famous]]).
