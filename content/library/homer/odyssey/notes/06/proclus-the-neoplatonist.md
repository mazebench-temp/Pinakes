---
summary: Proclus takes the last verse of the book's Olympus, where the blessed gods take their delight all their days, for the poet's own proof that there is no strife among the gods.
---

- There is neither faction nor any mortal division among the gods, but peace and a life without harm, as the poet himself shows when he says of Olympus that “in it the blessed gods take their delight all their days” (*On the Republic* I 87 Kroll, quoting 6.46), so that the battle of the gods must be read as a figure: [[proclus-on-the-battle-of-the-gods]] and [[the-seat-of-the-gods-in-later-writers]].
