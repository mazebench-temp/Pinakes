summary: “And white-armed Nausicaa answered him face to face” (6.186); and Alcinous, “But he understood everything, and answered her” (6.67).
grc: ["ἀμείβετο μύθῳ"]
en: [answered her]
---

τὸν δʼ αὖ … ἀντίον ηὔδα is the Odyssey's verse of a reply, as of Telemachus' (1.213), here with Nausicaa's name and epithet (see the Odyssey translation's formulas; [[white-armed]]). ἀμείβετο μύθῳ closes the Iliad's verses for the answers of Hecuba and Priam (*Iliad* 24.200, 24.424); at 6.67 it follows the father's silent understanding of what his daughter has not said ([[alcinous]], [[aidos]]).
