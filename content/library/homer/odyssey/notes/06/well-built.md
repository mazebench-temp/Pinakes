---
summary: If the queen thinks of him in friendship, “then there is hope that you will see your friends and reach / your well-built house and your native land” (6.313–315).
---

The verses are Proteus' to Menelaus (4.475–476), and Athena repeats them in her directions at the palace, with “high-roofed” for “well-built” (7.75–77); several manuscripts lack them here (see the Odyssey translation's formulas and the note on 6.313–315).
