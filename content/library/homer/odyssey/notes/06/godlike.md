θεοειδής is given to the founder of the Phaeacians' city, who is dead before the story begins (6.7–11; [[nausithous]], [[the-migration-of-the-phaeacians]]). The Odyssey gives it most often to Telemachus and to the seer Theoclymenus (1.113, 15.271 and after) and to suitors (4.628), and in Book 7 to Nausithous' son Alcinous (7.231).
