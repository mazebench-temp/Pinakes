---
summary: The cry of the girls is like that of nymphs, “who hold the steep peaks of the mountains” (6.123).
---

ὀρέων αἰπεινὰ κάρηνα is the Iliad's phrase for the mountain peaks that Poseidon shakes in the battle of the gods (*Iliad* 20.58); the next verse is *Iliad* 20.9 (see the translation's note on 6.122–125; [[grassy]], [[nymphs]]).
