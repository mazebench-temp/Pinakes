---
summary: Nausicaa meets her father on his way "to join the famous kings / at the council" of the Phaeacians (6.54–55).
---

Alcinous is not the only king of Scheria. Nausicaa meets him at the door "on his way to join the famous kings / at the council, to which the noble Phaeacians had called him" (6.54–55; see [[alcinous]] and [[boule]]). βασιλῆες are here the chief men of the land, the fathers of the suitors whom Athena calls "the best men in the land … of all the Phaeacians" (6.34–35; see [[phaeacians]]). In *Odyssey* 8 Alcinous counts them: twelve kings rule among the people, and he himself is the thirteenth (8.390–391, a plain rendering).
