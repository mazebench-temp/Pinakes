---
summary: Nausicaa's mother sits at the hearth “in the light of the fire”, spinning, where the stranger is to find her (6.305).
---

“and she sits at the hearth in the light of the fire, / turning the sea-purple yarn, a wonder to see” (6.305–306; see [[hearth]] and [[distaff]]). The fire on the hearth is the center of the hall, as the great fire on the hearth is the first thing in the description of Calypso's cave (5.59; see the Book 5 note). In the next book Odysseus, when he has clasped the queen's knees, sits down at the hearth in the ashes, beside the fire (7.153–154; see [[supplication]]).
