---
summary: The Phaeacians “once lived in Hypereia with its wide dancing floors” (6.4).
---

Eustathius, as Merry reports him, explained the word in Homer as a place “where there is room to dance,” and in later writers as merely “spacious” (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 6.4; see the translation's note on 6.4). The epithet is given to the town the Phaeacians left for Scheria ([[hypereia-of-the-phaeacians]], [[the-migration-of-the-phaeacians]]).
