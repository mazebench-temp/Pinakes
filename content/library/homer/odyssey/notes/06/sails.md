# σπεῖρα, the sails (6.269); at 4.245 the same word is the wretched rags of Helen's tale, which the line excepts. The
# raft's σπεῖρον (5.318) is in the Book 5 note; σπείρων, the wrapping of the washing (6.179), is not a sail.
grc: [σπεῖρα]
except: [4.245]
---

“There they take care of the tackle of the black ships, / cables and sails, and they taper the oars” (6.268–269; see [[ships]], [[stern-cables]] and [[oars]]). σπεῖρα is any large piece of cloth, the sails here, as σπεῖρον was the sail of the raft (5.318; see the Book 5 note); the same word is the rags in which Odysseus slipped into Troy (4.245) and the wrapping in which the washing was packed (6.179; see [[cloak]]). For σπεῖρα Eustathius gives a variant σπείρας, warping cables, which a scholium explains as the cables by which the ships are hauled (W. W. Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, critical note on 6.269; see the translation's note on 6.269).
