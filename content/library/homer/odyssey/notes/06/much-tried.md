---
summary: Nausicaa promises the stranger that he will not lack clothing, “nor anything else / that is fitting for a much-tried suppliant who has come our way” (6.192–193).
---

With ὧν ἐπέοιχʼ the sense “not to lack” is carried over from 6.192, “which a much-tried suppliant should not lack” (see the translation's note on 6.193). Her promise answers his request for a city and a rag to wear (6.178–179; [[supplication-type-scene]], [[xenia]]).
