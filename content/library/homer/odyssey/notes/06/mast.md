# ἱστοί, the masts (6.271); at 13.107 the same word is the stone looms of the nymphs, to except with Book 13.
grc: [ἱστοὶ]
en: [masts]
---

“For the Phaeacians care nothing for bow or quiver, / but for masts and the oars of ships and balanced ships, / in which they glory as they cross the gray sea” (6.270–272; see [[ships]], [[oars]], [[bow]] and [[phaeacians]]). The masts are named with the tackle that the Phaeacians look after at their place of assembly, the cables and the sails (6.268–269; see [[sails]] and [[stern-cables]]).
