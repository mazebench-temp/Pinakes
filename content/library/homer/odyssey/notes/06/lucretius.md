---
summary: Lucretius gives the Odyssey's Olympus, unshaken by winds, unwet by rain and untouched by snow under a cloudless sky, to the quiet seats of Epicurus' gods, whose peace nothing disturbs.
---

- “The majesty of the gods appears, and their quiet seats, which no winds shake, nor clouds sprinkle with rain, nor snow, congealed with sharp frost, falling white, violates; but an ever cloudless sky covers them and laughs with light spread wide” (*On the Nature of Things* 3.18–22, a plain rendering, after 6.42–46; Merry on 6.42): [[the-seat-of-the-gods-in-later-writers]] and [[olympus]].
