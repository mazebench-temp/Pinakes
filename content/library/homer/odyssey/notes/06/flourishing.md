θαλερὸς γάμος is found again only in Penelope's prayer, where Aphrodite goes to Zeus to ask for the daughters of Pandareus the fulfillment of blooming marriage (20.73–74). Nausicaa speaks of her brothers' clothes for the dance, not of her own wedding, and her father understands everything (6.62–67; [[aidos]], [[marriage]]).
