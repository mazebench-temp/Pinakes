---
summary: Nausicaa asks her father for the wagon, “high, with good wheels” (6.57–58), and he answers in her words, “the slaves will make ready the wagon for you, / high, with good wheels” (6.69–70).
en: [with good wheels]
---

The father gives back her verse and adds a part of his own, “fitted with a frame above” (6.70; see the translation's note on 6.70). Of a wagon ἐύκυκλος is “with good wheels” (see the Odyssey translation's formulas); a few verses later the narrator calls it “well-wheeled,” ἐύτροχος (6.72; [[well-wheeled]], [[wagon]]).
