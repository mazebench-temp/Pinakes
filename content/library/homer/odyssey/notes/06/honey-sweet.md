---
summary: The mules graze “on the honey-sweet grass” by the river (6.90).
grc: [μελιηδέα]
---

μελιηδής is elsewhere the epithet of wine, of fruit and of life itself (*Iliad* 6.258, 18.568, 10.495), and of the wheat that Diomedes' horses eat at their manger (*Iliad* 10.568–569); here it is the grass of the mules turned out to graze while the girls wash (6.88–90; [[mules]], [[eddying]]).
