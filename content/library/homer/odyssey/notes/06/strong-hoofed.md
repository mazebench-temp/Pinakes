---
summary: For the drive back to the city Nausicaa “yoked the strong-hoofed mules, and mounted herself” (6.253).
---

The half-verse is the Iliad's for the mules that draw Priam's wagon, ζεῦξαν δʼ ἡμιόνους κρατερώνυχας (*Iliad* 24.277), with the verb in the singular: in the morning the slaves yoked them (6.73), now the girl yokes them herself ([[mules]], [[wagon]], [[chariot-journey]]).
