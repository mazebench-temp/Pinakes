---
summary: Artemis goes “along towering Taygetus or Erymanthus”, delighting in boars and swift deer, in the simile of Nausicaa among her handmaids (6.102–104).
---

The second of the two mountains of the simile (6.103; see [[taygetus]] and [[artemis-among-her-nymphs]]). Taygetus is in Laconia and Erymanthus between Arcadia and Elis, so that the goddess ranges over the mountains of the Peloponnese, “delighting in boars and swift deer” (6.104; see [[artemis]], [[boar]] and [[deer]]). Merry thought the boars especially apt on Erymanthus, the haunt of the great boar of Heracles' labors (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 6.102; see [[heracles]]).
