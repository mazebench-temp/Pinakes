---
summary: “And it is Zeus himself, the Olympian, who deals out prosperity to men” (6.188).
---

Merry finds the point of the verses in ὅπως ἐθέλῃσιν, “as he wishes”: the Olympian's dispensation of good and evil is arbitrary (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 6.188–189; [[zeus-gives-good-and-evil]], [[olbos]], [[zeus]]).
