summary: The slaves make ready “the well-wheeled mule wagon” (6.72).
---

ἄμαξαν ἐύτροχον ἡμιονείην is the Iliad's verse-end for the wagon on which Priam takes the ransom to Achilles (*Iliad* 24.189, 24.266). In the speeches the same wagon is an ἀπήνη, “high, with good wheels” (6.57–58, 6.69–70; [[well-rounded]], [[wagon]], [[mules]]).
