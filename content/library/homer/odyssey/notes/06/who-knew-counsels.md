---
summary: “But he had already been overcome by his doom and gone to the house of Hades, / and Alcinous was ruling then, who knew counsels from the gods” (6.11–12).
---

The verse brings in the king after the founder: Nausithous led the people to Scheria and built the city, and Alcinous rules it with counsels from the gods (6.7–12; [[nausithous]], [[alcinous]]). The Phaeacians are the people Zeus calls “near kin to the gods” (5.35; [[near-kin-to-the-gods]]), and Nausicaa speaks of her “wise-hearted father” (6.256; [[wise-hearted]]).
