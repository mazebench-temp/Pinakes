---
summary: Nausithous, who led the Phaeacians to Scheria, is dead: “But he had already been overcome by his doom and gone to the house of Hades” (6.11).
---

The verse is the one with which Nestor's father Neleus was put out of the story at Pylos (3.410), and here it closes the history of the founder, Nausithous, who settled the Phaeacians in Scheria, walled the city, built houses and temples and divided the fields (6.7–11; see [[nausithous]], [[the-migration-of-the-phaeacians]] and [[hades]]). The English keeps the formula of Book 3 (see the translation's table of formulas).
