---
summary: Athena goes “to the intricately worked chamber, in which a girl / was sleeping” (6.15–16).
grc: [πολυδαίδαλον]
---

The Iliad gives the epithet to corselets, a shield and a chair, and to the Sidonian craftsmen; only here is it given to a room, the girl's chamber with its shining doors (6.15–19; [[phaeinos]], [[nausicaa]]).
