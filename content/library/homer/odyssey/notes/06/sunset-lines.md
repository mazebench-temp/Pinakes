summary: “And the sun set, and they came to the famous grove, / sacred to Athena” (6.321–322).
grc: ["δύσετό τʼ ἠέλιος"]
---

The half-verse of the Odyssey's sunset (2.388) brings the travelers to the grove where, as Nausicaa bade him, Odysseus is to wait while she goes on to the city (6.295–299, 6.321–322; [[the-grove-of-athena]], [[hieros]]).
