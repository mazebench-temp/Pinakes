---
summary: “Brilliant Odysseus” seven times: asleep (6.1), waking (6.117), coming out from under the bushes (6.127), speaking to the handmaids (6.217), washing off the brine (6.224), eating (6.249), and sitting down in the grove of Athena (6.322).
---

δῖος Ὀδυσσεύς ends the verse each time. ὁ δʼ ἔγρετο δῖος Ὀδυσσεύς, “and brilliant Odysseus woke” (6.117), returns when he wakes on Ithaca (13.187), and both times he asks the same question ([[to-the-land-of-what-mortals]]). See [[much-enduring]] and [[odysseus]].
