summary: Odysseus ends his plea to Nausicaa, “But pity me, queen” (6.175), the words of his prayer to the river with “queen” for “lord”; and in Athena's grove he prays, “Grant that I come to the Phaeacians as one welcomed and pitied” (6.327), the verse of Priam's prayer before he goes to Achilles.
# ἐλεεινόν "pitied" (6.327); again, of a pitiful tear, 8.531, 16.219.
grc: [ἐλεεινόν]
---

**Pity me, queen.** The request comes after the praise, the fear and the tale of the sea: “But pity me, queen; for it is to you, after toiling through many evils, / that I have come first, and of the others I know no one / of the people who hold this city and land” (6.175–177). ἀλλʼ ἐλέαιρε, ἄνασσα is the river prayer's ἀλλʼ ἐλέαιρε, ἄναξ, “But pity me, lord” (5.450; see the translation's table of formulas, [[supplication]] and [[anax]]), and it rests on the same ground, that the man who asks has come far and suffered much. σέ … ἐς πρώτην, “to you first”, is set in emphatic place to balance “of the others” (W. W. Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 6.175). Nausicaa does not speak of pity in her answer; she gives what pity would give, clothing and all that a much-tried suppliant should have (6.191–193; see [[xenia]]).

**Welcomed and pitied.** At sunset, in the grove of Athena, Odysseus prays: “Grant that I come to the Phaeacians as one welcomed and pitied” (δός μʼ ἐς Φαίηκας φίλον ἐλθεῖν ἠδʼ ἐλεεινόν, 6.327; see [[prayer]] and [[athena]]). It is the verse of Priam's prayer to Zeus as he sets out for the hut of the man who killed his son, “grant that I come to Achilles as one welcomed and pitied” (*Iliad* 24.309), with the Phaeacians in the place of Achilles, and the English is the Iliad's (see [[philos]]). Aristotle quotes the verse as well said, for a speaker must aim at these two things, to be liked and to be pitied (*Rhetoric* 1415b26–28; see [[welcomed-and-pitied]] and the translation's note on 6.327). The adjective comes again of the tears Odysseus sheds at Demodocus' song of Troy, ἐλεεινὸν … δάκρυον, “a pitiful tear” (8.531).
