---
summary: “And we live far apart in the sea of many waves, / the farthest of men,” Nausicaa tells her handmaids (6.204–205).
---

Merry notes that ἐνὶ πόντῳ does not show that Scheria is an island, only that the Phaeacians lived far across the sea (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 6.204; see the translation's note on 6.204). The verse-end sets them apart as Nausithous did when he settled them “far from toiling men” (6.8; [[toiling-men]], [[phaeacians]], [[scheria]]).
