summary: At the opening of On the Laws Cicero's brother Quintus sets the palm that Homer's Ulysses said he saw at Delos beside the oak of Cicero's Marius and the everlasting olive of the Athenian citadel, among the things that last longer in remembrance than they could stand by nature.
---
