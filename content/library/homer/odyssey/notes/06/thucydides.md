---
summary: In the Peloponnesian War Corcyra still had a precinct of Alcinous, as Merry notes on the king's domain beside the grove of Athena.
---

- The vine-stakes cut at Corcyra “from the precinct of Zeus and that of Alcinous” (Thucydides 3.70.5, as Merry quotes it on 6.293), which Merry takes as a memory of the royal domain that Nausicaa names beside the grove of Athena, “my father's domain and his flourishing orchard” (6.293): [[the-grove-of-athena]], [[alcinous]] and [[scheria]].
