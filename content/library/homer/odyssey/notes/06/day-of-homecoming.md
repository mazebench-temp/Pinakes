summary: Nausicaa bids him clasp her mother's knees, “so that you may see the day of your homecoming / rejoicing, and quickly” (6.311–312).
en: [day of your homecoming]
---

Odysseus had told Calypso that he wished “to come home and see the day of my homecoming” (5.220); now a girl of the Phaeacians tells him how he may see it, by the queen's favor (6.310–315; [[supplication-type-scene]], [[nostos]]).
