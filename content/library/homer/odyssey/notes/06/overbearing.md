---
summary: Nausicaa fears the talk of the town, “for there are very overbearing men in the land” (6.274).
---

The suitors' epithet in Ithaca is given to Phaeacians, whose gossip she shuns and whose words she imagines in the next verses (6.273–285; [[the-talk-of-the-people]], [[tis-speech]], [[nemesis]], [[oneidos]]).
