---
summary: The craftsman whom “Hephaestus and Pallas Athena have taught / every kind of craft” (6.233–234), and “So he spoke in prayer, and Pallas Athena heard him” (6.328).
---

6.328 is the verse with which Athena heard Nestor's prayer at Pylos (3.385); this time the goddess who hears does not yet appear to him face to face, out of regard for her father's brother (6.329–331; [[prayer-type-scene]], [[poseidon]]). In the simile she is named with Hephaestus as the teacher of the goldsmith's skill (6.232–235; [[gold-poured-around-silver]], [[hephaestus]]).
