---
summary: Athenaeus reports that Agallis, a woman grammarian of Corcyra, gave Nausicaa the invention of the game of ball, as a favor to her countrywoman.
---

- There are dances in Homer, Athenaeus says, some of tumblers and some with the ball, the invention of which Agallis, the Corcyraean woman grammarian, ascribes to Nausicaa, gratifying her fellow citizen (Athenaeus 1.25, as Merry quotes him on 6.101, on the ball game and the song and dance of 6.100–101). The Suda gives the same notice under the name Anagallis (Suda α 1817, σ 1720, ο 671): [[ball]] and [[nausicaa]].
