---
summary: Ready to drive back to the city, Nausicaa “urged Odysseus on, and spoke to him, and called him by name” (6.254).
---

She does not know his name, and the speech begins ξεῖνε, “stranger” (6.255): the half-verse is the common frame of a close and urgent address, and what goes before it is not a touch of the hand but ὤτρυνεν, “urged on.” See [[nausicaa]].
