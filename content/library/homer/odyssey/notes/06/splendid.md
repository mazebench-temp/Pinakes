---
summary: “You will find a splendid grove of Athena near the road, / of poplars” (6.291–292).
---

ἀγλαὸν ἄλσος is the Iliad's phrase for Poseidon's grove at Onchestus, Ποσιδήϊον ἀγλαὸν ἄλσος (*Iliad* 2.506); in Nausicaa's city the precinct of Poseidon stands by the place of assembly (6.266), and the splendid grove outside is Athena's (see the Odyssey translation's key words, Ποσιδήιον; [[the-grove-of-athena]], [[the-city-of-the-phaeacians]], [[onchestus]]).
