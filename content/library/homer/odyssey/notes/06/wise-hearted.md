---
summary: Nausicaa bids the stranger come “to the house of my wise-hearted father” (6.256).
---

The Odyssey gives δαΐφρων to Alcinous three times more in Book 8, in the verses of Athena's disguise as his herald and of the stranger and the Phaeacians coming to his house (8.8, 8.13, 8.56). The sense “wise” suits the king “who knew counsels from the gods” (6.12; [[who-knew-counsels]], [[alcinous]]).
