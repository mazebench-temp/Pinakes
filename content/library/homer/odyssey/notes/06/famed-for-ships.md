---
summary: Athena comes to Nausicaa “in the likeness of the daughter of Dymas, famed for ships” (6.22).
---

The epithet of the friend's father suits a people whose city, as Nausicaa describes it, is a city of harbors, ships and slips, who “care nothing for bow or quiver” (6.262–272; [[phaeacians]], [[dymas-the-phaeacian]], [[dream-scene]]).
