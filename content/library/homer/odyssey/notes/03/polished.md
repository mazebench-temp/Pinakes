---
summary: Nestor goes out at dawn “and sat down on the polished stones / which stood before his high doors” (3.406–407).
---

ἐπὶ ξεστοῖσι λίθοισιν, the seats “white, shining with oil” where Neleus used to sit before him (3.408–409; [[white]], [[neleus]], [[house]]), as the elders on the Iliad's shield “sat on polished stones in the sacred circle” (*Iliad* 18.504). The Phaeacian leaders sit on polished stones at their place of assembly (8.6).
