---
summary: The Myrmidons came home led by “the shining son of great-hearted Achilles” (3.189).
---

φαίδιμος υἱός is Neoptolemus, whom the verse does not name (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 3.189; [[neoptolemus]], [[great-hearted]], [[myrmidons]]); in Book 2 the same words were Noemon, “the shining son of Phronius” (2.386).
