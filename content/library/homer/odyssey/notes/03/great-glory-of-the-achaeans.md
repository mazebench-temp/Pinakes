---
summary: Telemachus twice addresses Nestor with the whole verse, “Nestor, son of Neleus, great glory of the Achaeans” (3.79, 3.202), the address of the Iliad.
---

ὦ Νέστορ Νηληϊάδη, μέγα κῦδος Ἀχαιῶν is the verse with which Agamemnon, Odysseus and Idomeneus address Nestor in the Iliad (*Iliad* 10.87, 10.555, 11.511, 14.42), and the young man speaks to the old king as his peers did. Merry renders κῦδος Ἀχαιῶν “of whom the Achaeans are proud,” and notes that Odysseus too is so addressed (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 3.79). The third time Telemachus begins the verse he ends it with a demand, “Nestor, son of Neleus, tell me the truth” (3.247; [[son-of-neleus]]). See [[nestor]].
