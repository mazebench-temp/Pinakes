εὐρύοπα Ζεύς begins the storm that splits Menelaus' fleet, with the breath of shrill winds and waves like mountains (3.288–290; [[hateful]], [[shrill]], [[huge]], [[the-wanderings-of-menelaus]]). See [[zeus]].
