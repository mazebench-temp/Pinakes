---
summary: Named once, in the adjective of his son's title: Philoctetes came home safely, “the splendid son of Poias” (3.190).
---

Ποιάντιον ἀγλαὸν υἱόν, “the splendid son of Poias” (3.190; see [[philoctetes]] and [[splendid]]), closes the verse that brings Philoctetes home among the men who came back safely from Troy (3.188–190; see [[the-returns-of-the-achaeans]]). The adjective Ποιάντιος does the work of a genitive, as Merry says of such Aeolic patronymics, and is the only mention of Poias in Homer (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 3.190).
