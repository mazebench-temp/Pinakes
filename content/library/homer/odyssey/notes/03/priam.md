---
summary: Troy is Priam's city in Nestor's memory: the war was fought “around the great city of lord Priam”, and the returns began when “we had sacked the steep city of Priam” (3.107, 3.130).
---

Nestor names the king only in the city's name: “and all the battles around the great city of lord Priam / that we fought” (3.107–108), and “But when we had sacked the steep city of Priam” (3.130; see [[troy]], [[steep]] and [[the-fall-of-troy]]). The sack is the point at which the returns begin (3.130–136; see [[the-returns-of-the-achaeans]]).
