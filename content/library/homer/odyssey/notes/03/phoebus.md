---
summary: At Sunium “Phoebus Apollo came upon Menelaus' helmsman / with his gentle arrows and killed him” (3.279–280).
---

Φοῖβος Ἀπόλλων, the formula of the name, brings the sudden death of Phrontis, son of Onetor, the best of helmsmen ([[gentle-arrows]], [[phrontis-son-of-onetor]], [[apollo]]).
