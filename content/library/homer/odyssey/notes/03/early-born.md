---
summary: “Early-born Dawn” begins the verse of both mornings in the book, at Pylos (3.404) and at Pherae (3.491).
---

Each dawn begins a day of the journey: Nestor rises and goes out to sit on the polished stones before his doors (3.404–406), and Telemachus and Peisistratus yoke the horses at the house of Diocles (3.491–492; [[dawn-line]], [[rosy-fingered]], [[eos]]).
