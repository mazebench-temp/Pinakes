---
summary: The sun rises “into the brazen sky” (3.2) as the ship reaches Pylos.
---

οὐρανὸν ἐς πολύχαλκον. Merry reports that the older commentators and Eustathius took the epithet as “solid” or “firm,” comparing the Iliad's χάλκεος οὐρανός (*Iliad* 17.425), and others as “bright-flashing”; he finds most picturesque the sky as a vault of burnished metal (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 3.2). The verse opens the book with the sunrise ([[helios]], [[sky]]).
