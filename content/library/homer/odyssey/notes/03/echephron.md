---
summary: Echephron is named first of the sons who come from their chambers at dawn to sit around Nestor on the polished stones (3.413), and with Stratius he leads the heifer by the horns at the sacrifice to Athena (3.439).
# Capitalized only: the adjective ἐχέφρων "sensible" has the same letters (Od. 4.111 and often).
grc: ["^Ἐχέφρων"]
---

“And around him his sons gathered in a body, / coming from their chambers, Echephron and Stratius / and Perseus and Aretus and godlike Thrasymedes” (3.412–414; see [[nestor]], [[stratius]], [[perseus-son-of-nestor]], [[aretus-son-of-nestor]] and [[thrasymedes]]). When the heifer has come from the plain and the smith has gilded her horns, “Stratius and brilliant Echephron led the heifer by the horns” (3.439; see [[brilliant]] and [[the-sacrifice-to-athena]]). The verb is a dual, ἀγέτην, “the two led”.
