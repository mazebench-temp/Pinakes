---
summary: Telemachus' ship is “the balanced ship” as it puts in at Pylos (3.10) and as the crew come from it to the sacrifice (3.431); and “the balanced ships” of Diomedes' companions are moored at Argos on the fourth day (3.180).
---

The book adds the genitive νηὸς ἐίσης (3.10, 3.431) to the plural νῆας ἐίσας (3.180). At 3.431 the ship has both its epithets, θοῆς παρὰ νηὸς ἐίσης, “from the swift balanced ship” ([[swift-ships]]). See [[ships]] and [[sailing-type-scene]].
