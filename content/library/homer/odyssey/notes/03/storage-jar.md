---
summary: The wine that Nestor mixes for Athena is in its eleventh year when the housekeeper opens it and loosens its cover (3.390–392).
refs: [3.391-392]
---

Nestor mixes a bowl “of wine sweet to drink, which in its eleventh year / the housekeeper opened, and loosed its cover” (3.391–392; see [[wine]] and [[handmaids]]). The jar itself is not named; its κρήδεμνον, Merry explains, is not the lid but the string passed over the lid to keep it secure (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 3.392). The old wines of Odysseus' storeroom, “sweet to drink” like Nestor's, stand in their jars kept for his return (2.340–343; see [[the-storeroom-of-odysseus]]).
