---
summary: Nestor, knowing the goddess by her departure, tells Telemachus that she “also honored your brave father among the Argives” (3.379).
grc: [ἐτίμα]
---

Athena's τιμή for Odysseus is what Nestor had described as love, “for never yet have I seen the gods show love so openly” (3.221; see [[philos]] and [[athena]]): he names the goddess, “most glorious Tritogeneia” (3.378), and the honor she gave the father is his reason to hope for the son (3.375–379; see [[fathers-and-sons]]).
