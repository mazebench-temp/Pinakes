---
summary: The sacrifice on the shore is to “the dark-haired earth-shaker” (3.6), whose name the verse does not need.
---

ἐνοσίχθονι κυανοχαίτῃ: two epithets stand for Poseidon himself at the head of the book ([[dark-haired]], [[the-sacrifice-at-pylos]]). Peisistratus names him when he gives the stranger the cup: “Pray now, stranger, to lord Poseidon” (3.43). See [[poseidon]].
