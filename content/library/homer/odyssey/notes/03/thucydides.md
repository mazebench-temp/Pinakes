---
summary: Thucydides takes the question that Nestor puts to his guests, whether they sail on business or roam the sea like pirates, as evidence that the early Greeks lived by piracy and saw no shame in it.
---

- The old poets, who have men everywhere ask those who sail in whether they are pirates, as though neither those asked disowned the deed nor those asking reproached them with it (Thucydides 1.5.2, on 3.71–74): [[thucydides-and-the-pirates]] and [[piracy]].
