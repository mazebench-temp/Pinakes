---
summary: Telemachus adjures Nestor by “my brave father Odysseus” (3.98), and Nestor, when the goddess has gone, says that she “also honored your brave father among the Argives” (3.379).
---

ἐσθλὸς Ὀδυσσεύς (3.98) and πατέρʼ ἐσθλόν (3.379) are the forms of Books 1 and 2 ([[odysseus]]). The book's other ἐσθλός is “noble fame,” κλέος ἐσθλόν, which Athena wants for Telemachus and Nestor asks of her for himself (3.78, 3.380; [[kleos]]).
