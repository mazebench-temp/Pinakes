---
summary: Peisistratus is “leader of men” three times: as he sleeps beside Telemachus (3.400), as he cuts the heifer's throat (3.454), and as he mounts the chariot for Sparta (3.482).
---

ὄρχαμος ἀνδρῶν closes the verse after his name each time, and once in the accusative, ὄρχαμον ἀνδρῶν (3.400). The title of leaders goes to the youngest of Nestor's sons, still unmarried in his father's halls (3.401; [[peisistratus]], [[with-good-ash-spear]]).
