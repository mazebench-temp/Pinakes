---
summary: The storm off Malea drove “the five dark-prowed ships” of Menelaus to Egypt (3.299–300).
---

νέας κυανοπρῳρείους is the longer form of the adjective, found only here. Merry takes it as lengthened for the meter, with the ending of εὐρυόδεια, against the phrase νεὸς κυανοπρῴροιο, found thirteen times in Homer; he reports a variant κυανοπρωίρους that Cobet accepted, and compares Herodotus' story of Jason, carried from Malea to Libya by the north wind (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 3.299; [[egypt]], [[malea]], [[the-wanderings-of-menelaus]]).
