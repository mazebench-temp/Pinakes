---
summary: Athena prays Poseidon to give the Pylians “a gracious return” for their famous hecatomb (3.58–59).
grc: [χαρίεσσαν]
en: [gracious]
---

χαρίεσσαν ἀμοιβήν is the return a god makes for a gift: the hecatomb is given, and the prayer asks for χάρις in exchange (see [[reciprocity-with-the-gods]] and [[hecatomb]]). Merry joins ἀμοιβὴν ἑκατόμβης, and notes that the “hecatomb” here is not a hundred oxen but eighty-one bulls, nine in each of the nine companies, the word being used loosely of any large offering (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 3.58; see [[the-sacrifice-at-pylos]]). The goddess who asks it is the one who grants it, since “she herself was bringing it all to pass” (3.62; see [[athena]]).
