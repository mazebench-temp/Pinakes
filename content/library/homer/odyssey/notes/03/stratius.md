---
summary: Stratius is named with his brothers around Nestor on the polished stones at dawn (3.413), and with Echephron he leads the heifer by the horns at the sacrifice to Athena (3.439).
# The exact form as Murray prints it; elsewhere στράτιος is the epithet of Zeus "of armies" (Herodotus 5.119.2).
grc: [Στρατίος]
---

He comes from his chamber with Echephron, Perseus, Aretus and Thrasymedes to sit around their father (3.412–414; see [[nestor]] and [[echephron]]), and at the sacrifice the two brothers lead the victim: “Stratius and brilliant Echephron led the heifer by the horns” (3.439; see [[the-sacrifice-to-athena]]). Her horns have just been covered with gold leaf by the smith Laerces, “so that the goddess might rejoice to see the offering” (3.437–438; see [[laerces-the-goldsmith]] and the translation's note on 3.384, 3.437).
