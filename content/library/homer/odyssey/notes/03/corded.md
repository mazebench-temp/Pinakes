summary: Nestor has Telemachus sleep “on a corded bed beneath the loud-resounding colonnade” (3.399).

τρητοῖς ἐν λεχέεσσιν ὑπʼ αἰθούσῃ ἐριδούπῳ, the verse of the guest's bed, used again of Odysseus at the house of Alcinous (7.345; [[loud-resounding]], [[bed]], [[xenia]]). On the corded bed see the translation's note on 1.440.
