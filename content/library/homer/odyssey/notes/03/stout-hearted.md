summary: Telemachus seeks the report “of brilliant Odysseus the stout-hearted” (3.84).
---

δίου Ὀδυσσῆος ταλασίφρονος, the Odyssey's genitive formula, here with δῖος ([[brilliant]]), in Telemachus' first speech to Nestor, which asks for “the wide report of my father” (3.83; [[kleos]], [[odysseus]]).
