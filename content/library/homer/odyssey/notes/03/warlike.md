---
summary: “Warlike Ajax” lies dead at Troy (3.109), and “the warlike son of Tydeus” fled from Tenedos with Nestor (3.167).
---

Αἴας … ἀρήιος: Merry notes that Ajax without a patronymic always means the Telamonian (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 3.109; [[ajax]]). Τυδέος υἱὸς ἀρήιος is Diomedes, whose ships reach Argos on the fourth day (3.167, 3.180–181; [[son-of-tydeus]], [[diomedes]]).
