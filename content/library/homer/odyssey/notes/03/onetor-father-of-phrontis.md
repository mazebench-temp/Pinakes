---
summary: Named once, in the patronymic of Menelaus' helmsman, “Phrontis, son of Onetor” (3.282).
---

Ὀνητορίδην, “son of Onetor”, stands in the verse that names the helmsman Apollo killed at Sunium, Φρόντιν Ὀνητορίδην (3.282; see [[phrontis-son-of-onetor]]), and nothing else is said of the father. The Iliad's Onetor, the Trojan priest whose son Laogonus Meriones kills, is another man (*Iliad* 16.603–605; see [[onetor]]).
