summary: Nestor's verse of reply, “Then the Gerenian horseman Nestor answered him,” three times: before his tale of the war and the returns (3.102), his question about the suitors (3.210), and the story of Agamemnon's death (3.253).
---

τὸν δʼ ἠμείβετʼ ἔπειτα Γερήνιος ἱππότα Νέστωρ is the Iliad's verse (*Iliad* 10.102), and each time it brings in a long answer to the young man's questions. Two of the answers begin “My friend, since you have reminded me” (3.103, 3.211), the third “Well then, my child, I will tell you all the truth” (3.254; [[give-me-an-exact-account]], [[gerenian-horseman]], [[answering-him]]).
