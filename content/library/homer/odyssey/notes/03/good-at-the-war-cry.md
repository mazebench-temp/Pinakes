---
summary: Menelaus comes home on the day of the funeral feast: “and on the same day Menelaus, good at the war cry, came to him” (3.311).
---

βοὴν ἀγαθὸς Μενέλαος: Merry counts the epithet twenty-five times of Menelaus and twenty-one of Diomedes, and takes βοή of the captain's rallying shout rather than, with the scholia, of battle itself (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 3.311). The war cry comes home too late, with ships laden with possessions (3.311–312; [[menelaus]], [[the-wanderings-of-menelaus]]).
