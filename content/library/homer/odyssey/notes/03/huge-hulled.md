---
summary: When half of the army sailed from Troy, “a god made smooth the vast-gulfed sea” (3.158).
---

μεγακήτεα πόντον, the word's only use of the sea; the translation renders it “vast-gulfed” here and “huge in the hull” of ships (see the translation's key words). Merry renders it “gulfy,” full of deep hollows, from κῆτος, and compares the ship of *Iliad* 8.222 (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 3.158). The calm is a god's, as the wind of the next stage is (3.158, 3.183; [[sea]], [[the-departure-of-the-greeks]]).
