---
summary: Telemachus' ship is black four times: in Athena's prayer, “our swift black ship” (3.61), when she says she will go back to it and lie down “by the hollow black ship” (3.360, 3.365), and when Nestor sends to it for the crew (3.423).
---

θοῇ σὺν νηὶ μελαίνῃ (3.61) joins two epithets of the ship, as at sea in Book 2 (2.430; [[swift-ships]]), and κοίλῃ παρὰ νηὶ μελαίνῃ (3.365) joins two others ([[hollow-ships]]). The κῆρα μέλαιναν of 3.242 is black doom, not a ship ([[black-doom]]). See [[ships]].
