---
summary: At Sunium Apollo killed Menelaus' helmsman “with his gentle arrows” (3.280).
---

οἷς ἀγανοῖς βελέεσσιν ἐποιχόμενος κατέπεφνε: Phrontis, who “surpassed the tribes of men / in steering a ship whenever storms rushed on,” dies with the steering oar in his hands, and Menelaus stays to bury him (3.279–285; [[phrontis-son-of-onetor]], [[phoebus]], [[sunium]]). Merry takes the expression of a sudden death without suffering (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 3.280). See [[apollo]].
