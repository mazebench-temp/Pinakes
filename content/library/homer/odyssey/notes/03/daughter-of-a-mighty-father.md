---
summary: Many of the Achaeans met an evil fate “through the deadly wrath of the gray-eyed daughter of the mighty father” (3.135).
---

μήνιος ἐξ ὀλοῆς γλαυκώπιδος ὀβριμοπάτρης: the epithet of her arming is given to her wrath, which set strife between the two sons of Atreus (3.135–136; [[menis]], [[deadly]], [[gray-eyed]], [[the-departure-of-the-greeks]]). Merry takes the wrath as directed first against the Locrian Ajax for his outrage upon Cassandra (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 3.135; [[ajax-son-of-oileus]]).
