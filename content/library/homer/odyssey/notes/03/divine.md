---
summary: Nestor has his guest sleep in his house, “Telemachus, the dear son of divine Odysseus” (3.398).
---

φίλον υἱὸν Ὀδυσσῆος θείοιο: the genitive of 1.65 and 2.233, in apposition to the guest whom Nestor keeps from the ship and lays on a corded bed in the colonnade (3.397–399; [[xenia]], [[corded]], [[telemachus]]).
