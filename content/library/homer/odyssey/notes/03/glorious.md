summary: On the shore the Pylians and their guests “divided the portions and feasted on a glorious feast” (3.66).

ἐρικυδέα δαῖτα, the feast of the sacrifice to Poseidon, at which the strangers receive their share of the entrails and the cup (3.40–66; [[feast-type-scene]], [[the-sacrifice-at-pylos]]). Nestor's word for it, when he knows who came to it, is “the rich feast of the god” (3.420; [[rich-feast]]).
