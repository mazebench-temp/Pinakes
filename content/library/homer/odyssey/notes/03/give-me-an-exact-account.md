---
summary: Nestor begins the story of Agamemnon's death, “Well then, my child, I will tell you all the truth” (3.254).
---

The answer verse of the formula, with ἀληθέα πάντʼ, “all the truth,” in place of μάλʼ ἀτρεκέως (see the entry), after Telemachus has asked, “tell me the truth” (3.247). Eumaeus answers Telemachus with the same verse in Book 16 (16.61). See [[the-murder-of-agamemnon]].
