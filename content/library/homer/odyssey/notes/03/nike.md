summary: At Troy “brilliant Odysseus far surpassed them / in tricks of every kind” (3.121–122).
grc: [ἐνίκα]
en: [surpassed]
---

Odysseus' victory is in counsel, not in battle: no man would match himself with him “in counsel to his face”, since he prevailed over all in δόλοι (3.120–122; see [[metis]], [[dolos]] and [[odysseus]]).
