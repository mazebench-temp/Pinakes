---
summary: Athena, as Mentor, rebukes Telemachus' despair: “Telemachus, what a word has escaped the barrier of your teeth!” (3.230).
---

The verse is Zeus' to Athena in Book 1 (1.64), now hers to the young man who has said that not even the gods could bring his father home (3.226–228); her answer is a pair of sayings on the power of the gods and its limit (3.231–238; [[a-god-can-save-a-man-from-afar]], [[not-even-the-gods-can-ward-off-death]]).
