---
summary: Peisistratus seats the strangers “on soft fleeces on the sands of the sea” (3.38), and Nestor has cloaks and blankets for his guests “to sleep softly in” (3.350).
grc: [μαλακῶς]
en: [softly]
---

κώεσιν ἐν μαλακοῖσιν, the guest's seat at the feast on the shore ([[visit-scene]], [[peisistratus]]); μαλακῶς … ἐνεύδειν, the guest's night, in Nestor's protest that no guest of his will sleep on a ship's deck (3.346–355; [[xenia]], [[blankets]]).
