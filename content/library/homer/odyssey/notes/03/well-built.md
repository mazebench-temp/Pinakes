---
summary: The ship reaches “Pylos, the well-built citadel of Neleus” (3.4).
---

Νηλῆος ἐυκτίμενον πτολίεθρον, the verse-end of cities; as the chariot leaves it, the same city is Πύλου αἰπὺ πτολίεθρον, “the steep citadel of Pylos” (3.485; [[steep]], [[pylos]], [[neleus]]). On the site of Pylos see the translation's note on 3.4.
