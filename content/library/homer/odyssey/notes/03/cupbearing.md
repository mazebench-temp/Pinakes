# οἰνοχοεῦντες (3.472).
grc: [οἰνοχοεῦντες]
en: [pouring wine]
---

At the libation before the night, “the young men filled the mixing bowls brim-full with drink, / and served it out to all, first pouring drops for the libation into the cups” (3.339–340; as *Iliad* 1.470–471, 9.175–176; see [[libation]] and [[mixing-bowl]]). At the feast after the sacrifice “good men stood by to serve, / pouring wine into golden cups” (3.471–472; see [[the-sacrifice-to-athena]]). ὄροντο is commonly rendered “moved along them” as they sat, Merry reports, as ἐπῴχετο (1.143); others take it from the root of ὁράω and οὖρος, “looked after them”, the sense he thinks necessary at 14.104 (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 3.471).
