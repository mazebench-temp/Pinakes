---
summary: No one could tell all the evils of the war: “you would grow weary first and go home to your native land” (3.117).
---

σὴν πατρίδα γαῖαν ἵκοιο, the formula without φίλην. Merry explains the turn of the thought: instead of “you would set off home,” Nestor says “you would reach home,” as if impatience would not stop until it landed the listener in Ithaca (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 3.117; [[nostos]]).
