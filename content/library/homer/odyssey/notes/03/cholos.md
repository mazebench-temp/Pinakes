---
summary: Agamemnon wished to hold back the army and offer hecatombs, “so that he might heal the terrible anger of Athena” (3.145).
---

The χόλος Agamemnon hoped to heal is the goddess's μῆνις of the line before the quarrel (3.135; see [[menis]]), and the verb, ἐξακέσαιτο, “heal”, makes it a wound that sacrifice might cure. Nestor's next lines say why it could not be done in a night (3.146–147; see [[the-mind-of-the-gods-is-not-quickly-turned]] and [[hecatomb]]).
