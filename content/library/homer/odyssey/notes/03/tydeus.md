---
summary: Named only in his son's titles: Diomedes is “the warlike son of Tydeus” and “Diomedes, son of Tydeus, tamer of horses” (3.167, 3.181).
---

Τυδέος υἱὸς ἀρήιος, “the warlike son of Tydeus” (3.167), and Τυδεΐδεω … Διομήδεος, “of Diomedes, son of Tydeus” (3.181), the patronymic that counts for the father too (see [[diomedes]] and [[son-of-tydeus]]). Nothing is said of Tydeus himself.
