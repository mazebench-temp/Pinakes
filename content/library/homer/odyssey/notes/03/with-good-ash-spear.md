---
summary: Peisistratus sleeps beside Telemachus, “Peisistratus of the good ash spear, leader of men” (3.400).
---

ἐυμμελίην Πεισίστρατον: the ash, the wood fittest for spear-shafts, stands for the spear itself (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 3.400; [[leader-of-men]], [[peisistratus]]). In the Iliad the epithet goes with Priam and his people, and with Euphorbus, son of Panthous (*Iliad* 17.59).
