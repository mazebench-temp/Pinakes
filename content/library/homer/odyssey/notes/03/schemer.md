summary: “Scheming Aegisthus” three times: twice in the verse of Orestes' vengeance, “scheming Aegisthus, who had killed his famous father” (3.198, 3.308), and in Telemachus' question, “What destruction did he devise for him, / scheming Aegisthus” (3.249–250).
---

Αἴγισθον δολόμητιν, ὅ οἱ πατέρα κλυτὸν ἔκτα is the verse of 1.300, now twice in Nestor's mouth ([[famous]]). δολόμητις is made of δόλος and μῆτις, “trick” and “counsel,” of which Nestor has just said that Odysseus surpassed all men (3.118–122; [[dolos]], [[metis]]); Athena names the guile of Aegisthus, δόλῳ, in the same scene (3.235). See [[aegisthus]].
