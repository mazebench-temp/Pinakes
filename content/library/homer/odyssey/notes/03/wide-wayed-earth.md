---
summary: “Then they lifted the heifer's head from the wide-wayed earth” (3.453).
---

ἀπὸ χθονὸς εὐρυοδείης, between the stroke and the cutting of the throat: ἀνελόντες has no object, and the victim's head is lifted to bare the throat (see the translation's note on 3.453; [[sacrifice-type-scene]], [[the-sacrifice-to-athena]]).
