# Unnamed: "the shining son of great-hearted Achilles" (3.189).
refs: [3.189]
---

“whom the shining son of great-hearted Achilles led” (3.189; see [[phaidimos]], [[great-hearted]] and [[achilles]]): the son is not named, and Merry supplies the name, Neoptolemus (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 3.189). He is among the men Nestor has heard of, not seen, who came home safely (3.186–188; see [[myrmidons]] and [[the-returns-of-the-achaeans]]). The Odyssey names him first in the underworld, when Odysseus tells the shade of Achilles that he brought the boy from Scyros himself (Νεοπτολέμοιο, 11.505–509); in the next book Menelaus is sending his daughter to him as bride, to the city of the Myrmidons, over whom he ruled (4.5–9, plain renderings).
