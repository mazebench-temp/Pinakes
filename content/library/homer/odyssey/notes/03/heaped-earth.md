---
summary: Had Menelaus found Aegisthus alive, “then they would not have heaped up the loose earth over him even in death” (3.258).
---

χυτὴν ἐπὶ γαῖαν ἔχευαν, the verse-end of the mound of Patroclus (*Iliad* 23.256): instead of burial, “the dogs and the birds would have devoured him” far from the city (3.259–261; [[dogs]], [[birds]], [[aegisthus]]).
