---
summary: Telemachus asks, “how did the son of Atreus die, wide-ruling Agamemnon?” (3.248).
---

πῶς ἔθανʼ Ἀτρεΐδης εὐρὺ κρείων Ἀγαμέμνων: the Iliad's verse-end of the king in a question about his death. Merry takes πῶς as wonder at how such a man came to such an end, rather than a request for the details, since Telemachus knew the fact (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 3.248; [[the-murder-of-agamemnon]], [[son-of-atreus]], [[agamemnon]]).
