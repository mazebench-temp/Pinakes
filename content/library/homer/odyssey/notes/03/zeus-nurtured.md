---
summary: The housekeeper puts bread and wine in the chariot, “and meats, such as kings whom Zeus has nurtured eat” (3.479–480).
---

διοτρεφέες βασιλῆες, the plural of the Iliad's gatherings of kings (*Iliad* 2.445, 14.27), here for the provisions of a prince's journey; ὄψα are the meats, flesh as against bread (see the translation's key words; [[kings]], [[basileus]], [[chariot-journey]]).
