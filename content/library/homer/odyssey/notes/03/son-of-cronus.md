---
summary: “The son of Cronus” twice in Telemachus' questions and Nestor's answer: he “has made even the destruction unknown” of Odysseus (3.88), and after nine years of war he “barely brought it to an end” (3.119).
---

Κρονίων closes the verse each time ([[zeus]]). Merry renders 3.88 “even his death hath Zeus put out of the reach of all enquiry,” to say nothing of the trials that befell him (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 3.88), and notes at 3.119 the change of tense from the long labor of the imperfect, ῥάπτομεν, to the aorist of its end, ἐτέλεσσε (Merry on 3.119).
