---
summary: Zeus devised “a hateful journey” for Menelaus off Malea (3.288), and Orestes gave the funeral feast “for his hateful mother and for the coward Aegisthus” (3.310).
---

στυγερὴν ὁδόν is the storm that splits Menelaus' fleet, in a verse that the beggar's tale of his own voyage repeats (14.235; [[the-wanderings-of-menelaus]]). μητρός τε στυγερῆς is the only word the poem gives Clytemnestra at the end of the story, whose death it does not tell (see the translation's note on 3.309–310; [[clytemnestra]], [[orestes-kills-clytemnestra]]).
