---
summary: Athena's prayer opens “Hear me, Poseidon, earth-holder” (3.55), and Nestor's vow closes “and Pallas Athena heard him” (3.385).
---

κλῦθι without μευ, with the god's name and epithet in the vocative: the Cyclops opens his curse on Odysseus with the same words (9.528; [[earth-holder]]). The goddess who prays to Poseidon is the one who hears Nestor; his closing verse, ὣς ἔφατʼ εὐχόμενος, τοῦ δʼ ἔκλυε Παλλὰς Ἀθήνη, is that of Diomedes' prayer in the Iliad (*Iliad* 5.121; [[prayer-type-scene]], [[gold-poured-around-her-horns]]).
