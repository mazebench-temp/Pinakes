---
summary: At dawn Nestor sits on the polished stones before his high doors, where Neleus sat before him, and the chariot of Telemachus and Peisistratus drives out from the doorway and the loud-resounding colonnade of Diocles' house.
refs: [3.407, 3.493]
---

Nestor “went out and sat down on the polished stones / which stood before his high doors” (3.406–407; see [[nestor]] and [[neleus]]). At Pherae the young men “drove out from the doorway and the loud-resounding colonnade” (3.493, as *Iliad* 24.323; see [[chariot]] and [[pherae-in-messenia]]).
