---
summary: Athena's prayer on the shore begins, “Hear me, Poseidon, earth-holder” (3.55).
---

κλῦθι, Ποσείδαον γαιήοχε: the vocative opens the prayer of the goddess in Mentor's likeness to the god whose feast it is (3.43–44, 3.55–62; [[hear-me]], [[prayer-type-scene]]); the Cyclops begins his prayer for vengeance on Odysseus with the same words (9.528). On the meaning of γαιήοχος see the translation's note on 1.68. See [[poseidon]].
