---
summary: After the bath Telemachus “stepped out of the bathtub, like the immortals in form” (3.468).
---

δέμας ἀθανάτοισιν ὁμοῖος, a verse of the Odyssey's bath, said again of Odysseus when he is bathed before Penelope's test (23.163; [[bathing]], [[polycaste]]); Athena, as a herald, gives the same words to the stranger among the Phaeacians (8.14).
