---
summary: The last of Nestor's sons named at dawn is “godlike Thrasymedes” (3.414).
---

ἀντίθεος Θρασυμήδης is the Iliad's verse-end for the same man, as he strikes down Maris, who has sprung at his brother Antilochus (*Iliad* 16.319–322). At Pylos he is the son who strikes the heifer (3.442–449; [[thrasymedes]], [[steadfast-in-battle]], [[high-hearted]]).
