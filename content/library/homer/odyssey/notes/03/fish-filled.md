---
summary: With a fair wind Nestor's ships “ran over the fish-filled ways” to Geraestus (3.177).
---

ἰχθυόεντα κέλευθα is a variant of the sea's “watery ways” (3.71; [[watery-ways]]). Merry reports that most modern commentators render the epithet “monster-teeming,” since fish were no part of the heroic diet except in hunger, but notes that it is also the epithet of a river (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 3.177, comparing *Iliad* 20.392). See [[geraestus]] and [[sea]].
