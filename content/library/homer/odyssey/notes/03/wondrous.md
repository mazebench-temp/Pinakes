ἠχῇ θεσπεσίῃ: Merry notes that θεσπέσιος goes with ἠχή more often than with any other word in Homer, and with words of shouting may keep something of its origin, what a god might utter (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 3.150; [[the-departure-of-the-greeks]]).
