---
summary: Nestor is “tamer of horses” in Athena's first command, “go straight to Nestor, tamer of horses” (3.17), and Diomedes in Nestor's tale of the return (3.181).
---

Merry counts the epithet three times in the Odyssey, of Nestor, of Diomedes and of Castor (11.300), against forty-five times in the Iliad, generally of the Trojans (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 3.17). See [[nestor]] and [[diomedes]].
