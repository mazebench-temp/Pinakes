summary: “Fair-haired Menelaus” three times: as he overtakes Nestor and Diomedes at Lesbos (3.168), in the return that would have found Aegisthus alive (3.257), and in Lacedaemon, where Nestor sends Telemachus (3.326).
---

ξανθὸς Μενέλαος, the Iliad's verse-end of his name, frames his part in Nestor's tale: late at Lesbos, late again in Argos, and now at home (3.168, 3.257, 3.326; [[menelaus]], [[the-wanderings-of-menelaus]]).
