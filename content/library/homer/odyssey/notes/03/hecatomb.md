---
summary: Athena, praying to Poseidon at the sacrifice on the shore, asks a gracious return for the Pylians' famous hecatomb, and at Troy Agamemnon wished to hold back the army to offer holy hecatombs to Athena.
refs: [3.59, 3.144]
---

Athena prays that Poseidon give a gracious return “to all the Pylians, for this famous hecatomb” (3.59; see [[the-sacrifice-at-pylos]] and [[reciprocity-with-the-gods]]): nine companies of five hundred men, with nine bulls each, all black (3.6–8). The other hecatombs of the book are only wished for. Agamemnon wished “to hold back the army and offer holy hecatombs” to heal Athena's anger, and the wish divided the fleet (3.144–147; see [[the-departure-of-the-greeks]]).
