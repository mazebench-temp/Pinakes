---
summary: Athena asks Poseidon for a return “for this famous hecatomb” (3.59); Orestes killed the slayer of “his famous father” (3.198, 3.308); and Nestor's is “the famous house” (3.388, 3.428).
---

Three words of the family: ἀγακλειτῆς ἑκατόμβης (3.59), πατέρα κλυτόν, the verse of 1.300 that Nestor speaks twice (3.198, 3.308; [[orestes]], [[agamemnon]]), and δώματʼ ἀγακλυτά, the house to which Nestor leads his sons and sons-in-law after the goddess' departure, and where the slave women are to prepare the feast for her (3.388, 3.428). Merry notes that the offering on the shore was not strictly a hecatomb but eighty-one bulls, the word being used loosely of any large sacrifice (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 3.58; [[the-sacrifice-at-pylos]]).
