---
summary: Half the army stayed “beside Agamemnon, son of Atreus, shepherd of the people” (3.156), and Telemachus sits down after his bath “beside Nestor, shepherd of the people” (3.469).
---

The book adds the dative ποιμένι λαῶν (3.156) to the accusative ποιμένα λαῶν (3.469). The title of kings goes to the two leaders of the book, Agamemnon at Troy, where his army was divided (3.155–156; [[the-departure-of-the-greeks]], [[agamemnon]]), and Nestor at home among his own (3.469; [[nestor]]).
