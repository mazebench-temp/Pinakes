μενεπτόλεμος Θρασυμήδης: the warrior's epithet goes to the son who strikes the victim. In the book he has three, “godlike,” “steadfast in battle” and “high-hearted” (3.414, 3.442, 3.448; [[antitheos]], [[high-hearted]], [[thrasymedes]]).
