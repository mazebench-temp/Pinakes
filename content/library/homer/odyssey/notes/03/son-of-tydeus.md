summary: Diomedes is “the warlike son of Tydeus” who fled from Tenedos with Nestor (3.167), and his companions are those “of Diomedes, son of Tydeus, tamer of horses” (3.181).
---

Τυδέος υἱὸς ἀρήιος (3.167) and Τυδεΐδεω … Διομήδεος (3.181): both forms of the patronymic, in the verses of his return to Argos on the fourth day (3.180–182; [[diomedes]], [[tydeus]], [[warlike]], [[tamer-of-horses]]).
