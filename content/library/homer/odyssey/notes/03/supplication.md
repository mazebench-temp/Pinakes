---
summary: Athena and Nestor tell Telemachus to beg the old kings for the truth: "you yourself must beg him to tell you truly" (3.19, 3.327).
---

The verb of supplication, λίσσομαι, "beg", comes twice in the book, in the same verse, of a young man's request to an old king: Athena tells Telemachus to beg Nestor himself to tell him truly (3.19), and Nestor tells him to beg Menelaus (3.327); "he will not tell a lie, for he is very wise" (3.20, 3.328; see [[nestor]], [[menelaus]] and [[telemachus]]).
