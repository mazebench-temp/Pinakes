summary: Telemachus addresses Nestor as “son of Neleus” three times (3.79, 3.202, 3.247), and Polycaste is “the youngest daughter of Nestor, son of Neleus” (3.465).
---

Νηληϊάδη, the vocative of the Iliad's address ([[great-glory-of-the-achaeans]]), and Νηληϊάδαο at the end of Polycaste's verse. Merry explains the double patronymic, Νηληϊάδης beside Νηλεΐδης (*Iliad* 23.652), from the stem's doubtful vowel, as with Πηληϊάδης and Πηλεΐδης (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 3.79). The father himself is remembered on the polished stones where he used to sit (3.408–410; [[neleus]], [[nestor]], [[polycaste]]).
