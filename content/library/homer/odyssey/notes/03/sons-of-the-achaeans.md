---
summary: Nestor speaks of “we sons of the Achaeans, uncontrollable in our might” at Troy (3.104), and of the assembly to which “the sons of the Achaeans came heavy with wine” (3.139).
---

μένος ἄσχετοι υἷες Ἀχαιῶν (3.104) gives the Achaeans at war the words of Antinous' reproach in Book 2, μένος ἄσχετε, “uncontrollable in your fury” (2.85; see the translation's key words). The heavy wine of 3.139 is the disorder of the assembly called at sunset, “idly and against all order” (3.138; [[the-departure-of-the-greeks]], [[assembly]], [[achaeans]]).
