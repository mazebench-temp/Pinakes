---
summary: The sea is misty twice: in Nestor's memory of the raids, “all that we bore with our ships over the misty sea” (3.105), and off Gortyn, where a smooth rock stands “in the misty sea” (3.294).
---

ἐπʼ ἠεροειδέα πόντον (3.105) is the accusative of 2.263; the book adds the dative, ἐν ἠεροειδέι πόντῳ (3.294), a verse-end of the Odyssey (5.281, 8.568, 12.285). See [[sea]] and [[gortyn]].
