summary: “And when the black blood had flowed from her, and the spirit had left her bones” (3.455), the heifer is jointed.
---

τῆς δʼ ἐπεὶ ἐκ μέλαν αἷμα ῥύη stands between Peisistratus' stroke and the jointing (3.454–456; [[sacrifice-type-scene]], [[the-sacrifice-to-athena]]). Perseus held the vessel for it, “the bowl for the blood,” ἀμνίον, a word found only here in Homer (3.444; [[bowl-for-the-blood]]).
