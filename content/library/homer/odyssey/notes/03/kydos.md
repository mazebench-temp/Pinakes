---
summary: Athena prays to Poseidon, “To Nestor first of all and to his sons grant glory” (3.57), and Telemachus twice addresses Nestor as “great glory of the Achaeans” (3.79, 3.202).
---

The goddess asks of Poseidon the κῦδος that gods give, for the house of the man whose feast she shares (3.57; see [[kleos]] and [[reciprocity-with-the-gods]]). μέγα κῦδος Ἀχαιῶν, which Merry renders “of whom the Achaeans are proud” (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 3.79), is Nestor's title in the Iliad too (*Iliad* 10.87, 10.555, 11.511, 14.42), and Odysseus' at Troy and in the song of the Sirens (*Iliad* 9.673, 10.544; 12.184; see [[great-glory-of-the-achaeans]]).
