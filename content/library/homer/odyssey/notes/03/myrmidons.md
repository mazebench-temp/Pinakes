# Unnamed: "whom the shining son of great-hearted Achilles led" (3.189).
refs: [3.189]
---

“They say that the Myrmidons, fighters with the spear, came home safely, / whom the shining son of great-hearted Achilles led” (3.188–189; see [[neoptolemus]] and [[achilles]]). φάσʼ, “they say”, marks it as hearsay, among the homecomings Nestor did not see (3.186–187; see [[the-returns-of-the-achaeans]]). ἐγχεσίμωροι, “fighters with the spear”, is an epithet whose second half was disputed: Merry reports derivations from the root of μόρος, a lot or share in the spear, from μωρός, of men raging with the spear, and from roots of shining and of thinking, without deciding (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 3.188; see [[fighters-with-the-spear]]). In the next book Menelaus is sending his daughter to the son of Achilles as his bride, to “the renowned city of the Myrmidons, over whom he ruled” (4.5–9).
