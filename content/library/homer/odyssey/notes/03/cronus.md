---
summary: Cronus is named in Book 3 only in his son's patronymic: the son of Cronus has made the destruction of Odysseus unknown, and barely brought the war at Troy to an end.
---

Telemachus tells Nestor that of all who fought at Troy only his father's end is unknown: “but of that man the son of Cronus has made even the destruction unknown” (3.88; see [[zeus]] and [[son-of-cronus]]). Nestor answers that for nine years the Achaeans stitched evils together for the Trojans, “and the son of Cronus barely brought it to an end” (3.119; see [[the-fall-of-troy]]).
