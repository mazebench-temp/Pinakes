summary: “The son of Nestor, high-hearted Thrasymedes,” strikes the heifer (3.448).
---

Νέστορος υἱὸς ὑπέρθυμος Θρασυμήδης: the son of the sacrifice has three epithets in the book, “godlike,” “steadfast in battle” and “high-hearted” (3.414, 3.442, 3.448; [[antitheos]], [[steadfast-in-battle]], [[thrasymedes]]). In the Iliad ὑπέρθυμος is said of Diomedes and of Heracles (*Iliad* 5.376, 14.250).
