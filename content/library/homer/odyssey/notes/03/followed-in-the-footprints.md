---
summary: Athena leads Telemachus from the ship to the Pylians: “So she spoke, and Pallas Athena led the way / swiftly; and he followed in the footprints of the goddess” (3.29–30).
---

The couplet of 2.405–406, which led him from the house to the ship in Ithaca, now leads him from the ship to the first of the two kings he must question, after her counsel to put away his shame (3.14–30; [[pallas]], [[the-journey-of-telemachus]]).
