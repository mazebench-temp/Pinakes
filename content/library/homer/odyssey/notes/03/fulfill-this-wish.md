---
summary: Nestor bids his sons, “Quickly, dear children, fulfill my wish” (3.418), so that he may propitiate Athena.
---

κρηήνατʼ ἐέλδωρ, the plural of the prayer's request, here an old man's order to his sons for the sacrifice (3.418–429; [[the-sacrifice-to-athena]]). Eumaeus uses the words in his prayer to the nymphs of the spring (17.240–242).
