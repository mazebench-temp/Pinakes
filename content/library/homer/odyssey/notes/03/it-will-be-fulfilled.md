---
summary: Telemachus answers Nestor's wish that Athena might love him: “Old man, I do not think this word will ever be fulfilled” (3.226).
---

οὔ πω τοῦτο ἔπος τελέεσθαι ὀίω turns the formula of prophecy into a denial; in Book 1 the stranger used it to prophesy Odysseus' return (1.200–201). The goddess at his side rebukes him and answers that a god could easily save a man even from far away (3.230–231; [[what-a-word]], [[a-god-can-save-a-man-from-afar]]).
