---
summary: The Myrmidons, “fighters with the spear, came home safely” (3.188), the first of the returns that Nestor has heard of.
---

Μυρμιδόνας … ἐγχεσιμώρους: Merry reviews the guesses at the second half of the word, from Eustathius' to Curtius' root μερ, as in μερμηρίζω (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 3.188). The Myrmidons were led home by the shining son of great-hearted Achilles, whom Nestor does not name (3.189; [[myrmidons]], [[neoptolemus]], [[the-returns-of-the-achaeans]]).
