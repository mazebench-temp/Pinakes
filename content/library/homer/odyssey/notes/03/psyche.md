---
summary: Pirates wander the sea “staking their lives” (ψυχὰς παρθέμενοι, 3.74).
---

The life that a pirate stakes is the life that Odysseus strove to win in the proem, ἀρνύμενος ἥν τε ψυχὴν καὶ νόστον ἑταίρων (1.5; see [[piracy]] and [[nostos]]); the Cyclops asks the same question in the same words (9.255).
