---
summary: When they have landed at Pylos, “The gray-eyed goddess Athena spoke to him first” (3.13).
---

τὸν προτέρη προσέειπε θεά, γλαυκῶπις Ἀθήνη, the feminine of the Iliad's verse (see the entry). She speaks first, and her words are a command: Telemachus is to put away his shame and go straight to Nestor (3.14–20; [[gray-eyed]], [[aidos]], [[he-will-not-tell-a-lie]]).
