---
summary: Telemachus fears to meet Nestor: “I am not yet practiced at all in shrewd words” (3.23).
grc: ["πεπείρημαι πυκινοῖσιν"]
---

μύθοισι πεπείρημαι πυκινοῖσιν, with the noun and its epithet at either side of the verb: Merry renders “I have not practised myself in wisely-worded address” (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 3.23). Athena answers that some things he will think of himself, and in others a god will give him counsel (3.26–28; [[some-things-you-will-think-of-yourself]], [[shame-for-a-young-man]], [[telemachus]]).
