---
summary: Nestor has heard that Idomeneus brought home to Crete all his companions who had escaped the war, and that the sea took none of them (3.191–192).
---

“And Idomeneus brought all his companions back to Crete, / all who had escaped from the war, and the sea took none of them from him” (3.191–192; see [[crete]]). He is the last of those who came home safely in Nestor's hearsay, before the one return that ended in murder (3.193–198; see [[the-returns-of-the-achaeans]] and [[the-murder-of-agamemnon]]). Crete comes into the book again a hundred verses later, where Menelaus' ships are wrecked near Gortyn (3.291–298; see [[the-wanderings-of-menelaus]]).
