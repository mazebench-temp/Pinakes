---
summary: Nestor himself sleeps “in the inner part of the high house” (3.402), while his guest sleeps in the colonnade; at dawn he sits on the polished stones “which stood before his high doors” (3.407).
grc: ["θυράων ὑψηλάων"]
en: [high doors]
---

μυχῷ δόμου ὑψηλοῖο is the verse-end of the host's bed, again for Menelaus and for Alcinous (4.304, 7.346; [[loud-resounding]], [[house]]), and θυράων ὑψηλάων the doors before which the stones stand (3.406–408; [[polished]]). Merry places the seats, after Gerlach, in the porch on either side of the door rather than outside the court (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 3.407).
