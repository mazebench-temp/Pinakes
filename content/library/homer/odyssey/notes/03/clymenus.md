---
summary: Clymenus is named once, as the father of Nestor's wife: “Eurydice, eldest of the daughters of Clymenus” (3.452).
grc: [Κλυμένοιο]
---

Νέστορος, Εὐρυδίκη, πρέσβα Κλυμένοιο θυγατρῶν, “of Nestor, Eurydice, eldest of the daughters of Clymenus” (3.452), the verse that ends the list of the women who raise the cry at the sacrifice to Athena (3.450–452; see [[eurydice]] and [[the-sacrifice-to-athena]]).
