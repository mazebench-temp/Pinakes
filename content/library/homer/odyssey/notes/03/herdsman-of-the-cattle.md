summary: Nestor orders that the heifer come from the plain, driven by “the herdsman of the cattle” (3.421–422).
---

βοῶν ἐπιβουκόλος ἀνήρ, the verse-end that will be Philoetius' (see the entry); here the herdsman is one of Nestor's men, and the heifer comes “from the plain” as he ordered (3.430–431; [[cattle]], [[the-sacrifice-to-athena]]).
