---
summary: Euripides' Electra and Orestes take up the vengeance that Nestor tells in this book: Orestes grows up in Phocis and goes to Athens only after the matricide, Aegisthus dies at a sacrifice to which he has welcomed the strangers, and Menelaus lands at Nauplia in the days after the mother's death.
---

- Orestes given to Strophius “to rear in the land of the Phocians”, and sent to Athens after the deed (*Electra* 14–18, 1250–1257; on 3.307); Aegisthus, sacrificing to the Nymphs, asking the strangers who they are and where they come from, and killed over the entrails (*Electra* 779–787, 838–843); and Menelaus, come from Troy to the harbor of Nauplia on the sixth day after the matricide (*Orestes* 39–56; on 3.311–312): [[the-murder-of-agamemnon-in-tragedy]].
