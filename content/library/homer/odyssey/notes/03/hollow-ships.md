---
summary: The ships are “hollow” three times: Menelaus sails past Malea “in the hollow ships” (3.287), and Athena and Telemachus are eager “to go back to the hollow ship” (3.344), where the goddess says she will lie down “by the hollow black ship” (3.365).
---

Both of the entry's words: ἐν νηυσὶ γλαφυρῇσι (3.287), the Iliad's phrase of the homecoming (*Iliad* 2.454, 11.14), and κοίλην ἐπὶ νῆα, κοίλῃ παρὰ νηί (3.344, 3.365). See [[ships]] and [[black-ships]].
