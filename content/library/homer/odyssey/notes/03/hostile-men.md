---
summary: No one can tell where Odysseus perished, “whether he was brought down on the mainland by hostile men / or on the open sea among the waves of Amphitrite” (3.90–91).
---

ἀνδράσι δυσμενέεσσιν: the land and the sea are the two deaths that Telemachus can imagine for his father, as in Book 1, where his bones rot on the mainland or roll in the sea (1.161–162; [[amphitrite]], [[odysseus]]).
