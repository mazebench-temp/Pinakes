γέρων ἱππηλάτα Νέστωρ, the Iliad's “old horse-driving” Peleus and Oeneus given to Nestor (*Iliad* 7.125, 9.581; see the translation's formulas). The chariot-fighter's title stands beside the old man's acts of piety ([[the-sacrifice-to-athena]], [[gerenian-horseman]], [[nestor]]).
