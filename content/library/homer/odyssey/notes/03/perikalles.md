---
summary: The sun rises “leaving the beautiful water” (3.1), and Telemachus “mounted the beautiful chariot” (3.481) for Sparta.
---

περικαλλέα λίμνην is the farther margin of the Ocean stream, beyond which the sun sets and beside which he rises; Merry explains λίμνη as “water which washes a shore” (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 3.1; see the translation's note on 3.1; [[helios]]). The book ends with the chariot, περικαλλέα … δίφρον, of the journey to Sparta ([[chariot]], [[chariot-journey]]).
