---
summary: Horace, consoling a friend, sets Nestor among the mourners who stopped: the old man who had lived out three ages did not weep for Antilochus, the son whom this book's Nestor names among the dead at Troy, all his years.
---

- *At non ter aevo functus amabilem / ploravit omnis Antilochum senex / annos*, “but the old man who had lived out three ages did not weep for lovable Antilochus all his years” (*Odes* 2.9.13–15, on 3.111–112 and 3.245): [[nestors-three-generations]] and [[antilochus]].
