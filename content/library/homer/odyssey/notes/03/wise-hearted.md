---
summary: Odysseus is “wise-hearted, of subtle wiles” in Nestor's account of the second strife at Tenedos (3.163).
---

δαΐφρονα, ποικιλομήτην, the Iliad's pair of epithets for Odysseus (*Iliad* 11.482; [[of-subtle-wiles]]). The translation takes δαΐφρων as “wise-hearted,” from δαῆναι, “learn” (see the translation's note on 1.48). See [[odysseus]].
