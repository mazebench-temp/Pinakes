---
summary: Menelaus bids the Achaeans “to think of their homecoming over the broad back of the sea” (3.142).
---

Merry joins νόστου with ἐπʼ εὐρέα νῶτα, “the homecoming over the broad back,” comparing νόστου γαίης Φαιήκων (5.344) and the Iliad's νόστου … νῆας ἐπὶ γλαφυράς (*Iliad* 10.509; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 3.142). The sea of the homecoming begins the quarrel: Menelaus wants to sail at once, Agamemnon to stay and sacrifice (3.141–147; [[the-departure-of-the-greeks]], [[nostos]]). See [[sea]].
