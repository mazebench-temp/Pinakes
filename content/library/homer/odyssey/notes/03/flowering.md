summary: Aretus brings the water for the hands “in a flowered basin” (3.440).
en: [flowered]

ἀνθεμόεντι λέβητι, a basin worked with flowers, among the vessels of the sacrifice: the water and the barley grains with which Nestor begins the rite (3.440–441, 3.445; [[hand-washing]], [[barley-grains]], [[aretus-son-of-nestor]]).
