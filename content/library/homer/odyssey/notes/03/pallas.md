---
summary: Pallas Athena four times: as she leads Telemachus to the Pylians (3.29), as Peisistratus addresses her (3.42), in Nestor's memory, “as Pallas Athena openly stood beside him” (3.222), and when she hears his vow (3.385).
---

Παλλάδʼ Ἀθηναίην (3.42) is the accusative with the long form of her name. The verse of 3.385, “So he spoke in prayer, and Pallas Athena heard him,” is that of Diomedes' prayer in *Iliad* 5 (*Iliad* 5.121), and in the plural it follows his vow of a heifer in *Iliad* 10 (*Iliad* 10.295; [[hear-me]], [[gold-poured-around-her-horns]]). See [[athena]].
