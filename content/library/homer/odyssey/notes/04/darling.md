---
summary: Megapenthes, Menelaus' son by a slave woman, “was born to him as his darling” (4.11).
grc: [τηλύγετος]
---

τηλύγετος, the child born late, is the son of Menelaus' later years: the gods gave Helen no more children after Hermione (4.10–14; [[megapenthes]], [[menelaus]]). Merry reports Buttmann's derivation from τελευταῖος γενόμενος, “last-born”, with the secondary sense “dearly loved”, and objects that it does not suit μοῦνον τηλύγετον, “an only son” (*Iliad* 9.482; 16.19), nor ἄμφω τηλυγέτω, two sons (*Iliad* 5.153) (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 4.11).
