---
summary: Menelaus: “for satiety of chilling lament comes quickly” (4.103).
grc: ["κρυεροῖο γόοιο"]
---

κρυεροῖο γόοιο is the phrase of Achilles' word to Priam, that there is no profit in chilling lament (*Iliad* 24.524). Menelaus, between comfort and weariness, takes the other side: lament comforts the heart, but its satiety comes quickly (4.100–103; [[lament]], [[satiety-of-all-things]]).
