---
summary: As the suitors fit out the ship for the ambush, “their high-hearted attendants brought them their arms” (4.784).
grc: [ὑπέρθυμοι]
---

The τεύχεα are the weapons the suitors take against Telemachus, not the ship's tackle (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 4.784; see the translation's note on 4.784). The epithet of heroes and peoples is given to the attendants who serve the ambush (4.778–786; [[sailing-type-scene]], [[the-suitors]]).
