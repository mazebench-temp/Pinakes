πολιῆς ἁλός is the sea from which the seals come at midday (4.404–405; [[seals]]); πολιὴν ἅλα τύπτον ἐρετμοῖς ends the verse of rowing in the launching of 4.577–580 ([[sailing-type-scene]], [[rowing-benches]]).
