---
summary: Helen sits in her chair “and there was a footstool under her feet” (4.136).
---

“She sat down in the chair, and there was a footstool under her feet” (4.136; see [[throne]] and [[helen]]). The words are nearly those that seat an honored guest: Telemachus' guest in Book 1 and Thetis in Hephaestus' house have “a footstool under her feet” (1.131; *Iliad* 18.390). In Helen's own house the footstool belongs to the lady of the house, as she sits with her work basket beside her (see [[basket]] and [[distaff]]).
