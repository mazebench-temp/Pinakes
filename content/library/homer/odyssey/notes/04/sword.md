---
summary: Menelaus rises at dawn and sets his sharp sword about his shoulder (4.308), in the verse of Telemachus' rising (2.3).
---

“Put on his clothes, and about his shoulder set his sharp sword” (4.308; see [[sandals]], [[sharp]] and [[dressing-scene]]): Menelaus rises to hear Telemachus' errand armed, as Telemachus rose for the assembly (2.3).
