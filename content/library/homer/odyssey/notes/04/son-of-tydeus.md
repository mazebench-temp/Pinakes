---
summary: Diomedes, “the son of Tydeus,” sits with Menelaus and Odysseus in the Horse (4.280).
---

Τυδεΐδης is the only name he has in the tale: he and Menelaus would have answered Helen's call when she used the voice of a wife, but Odysseus held them back (4.280–284; [[diomedes]], [[sinon-and-the-wooden-horse]], [[odysseus]]).
