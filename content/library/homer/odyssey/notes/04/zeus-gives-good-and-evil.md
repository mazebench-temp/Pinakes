---
summary: Helen's maxim at Sparta, as she turns the company from tears to stories (4.235–239). It stands between her greeting and her bidding, “now feast”, with her drug against grief already in the wine.
---

Helen speaks after she has cast the drug into the wine, and the maxim gives the reason for her bidding: what comes, good or evil, is Zeus' to give, so the company may feast and take delight in stories (4.219–239; see [[nepenthe]] and [[helen]]). Merry's explanation of ἀτάρ, and of ἐοικότα, “what is fitting”, is given in the article (W. W. Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 4.236, 4.239).
