---
summary: Peisistratus says of his brother Antilochus that he was “surpassingly swift at running, and a fighter” (4.202), the half-verse of Nestor's praise (3.112).
grc: ["θείειν ταχὺν", "θείειν ταχὺς"]
---

Father and son use the same half-verse, Nestor in the nominative, πέρι μὲν θείειν ταχὺς ἠδὲ μαχητής (3.112), Peisistratus in the accusative, θείειν ταχὺν ἠδὲ μαχητήν (4.202): both remember Antilochus by his speed and his fighting (3.111–112, 4.199–202; [[antilochus]], [[peisistratus]], [[nestor]]).
