---
summary: Penelope laments her “beloved son,” snatched away by the storm winds (4.727), and tells the phantom that “my beloved son has gone off in a hollow ship” (4.817).
grc: [ἀγαπητὸς]
---

The word for an only son, said of Astyanax in the Iliad (*Iliad* 6.401), comes twice in the two versions of Penelope's lament (4.724–728, 4.814–818; [[telemachus]], [[mothers-and-sons]]).
