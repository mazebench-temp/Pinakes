Menelaus' launching is told in the verses of the Odyssey's voyages: the ships dragged into the sea and the masts and sails set (4.577–578), “and they went on board themselves, and sat down at the rowing benches” (4.579 = 2.419), “and sitting in order they struck the gray sea with their oars” (4.580; again 9.104 and in Odysseus' other departures; [[rowing-benches]], [[gray-sea]]). The suitors' ship is fitted out in the verses of the Phaeacians' ship for Odysseus (4.781–783, as 8.52–54), and anchored out in the water to wait for evening (4.785–786; see the translation's note on 4.785).
