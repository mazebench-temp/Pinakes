---
summary: Menelaus' wish that nothing would have parted him and Odysseus “until the black cloud of death had enfolded us” (4.180).
grc: ["μέλαν νέφος ἀμφεκάλυψεν"]
en: [black cloud of death]
---

θανάτοιο μέλαν νέφος ἀμφεκάλυψεν, the end of a death in battle in the Iliad, “and the black cloud of death enfolded him” (*Iliad* 16.350), is here the end of a long life together that the god did not grant (4.178–182; [[thanatos]], [[odysseus]]).
