---
summary: Aegisthus' lookout watched lest Agamemnon “should pass by unseen, and remember his furious courage” (4.527).
---

θούριδος ἀλκῆς, the half-verse of the exhortation, “be men, my friends, and remember your furious courage” (*Iliad* 6.112), is turned into a fear: the courage that a leader calls up in his men is what Aegisthus must keep the king from remembering (4.524–531; [[furious]], [[aegisthus]], [[the-murder-of-agamemnon]]).
