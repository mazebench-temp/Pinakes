---
summary: The strangers' chariot is leaned “against the gleaming walls” of Menelaus' courtyard (4.42).
---

The verse is the Iliad's for the chariot of Hera, unyoked by the Hours on Olympus: “and leaned the chariot against the gleaming walls” (*Iliad* 8.435). The horses of Telemachus and Peisistratus are tended like a goddess's (4.39–42; [[chariot-journey]], [[house]]).
