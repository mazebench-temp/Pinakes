---
summary: Aegisthus' lookout was promised “as wages / two talents of gold” to watch for Agamemnon's return (4.524–526).
---

The lookout whom Aegisthus posted had been promised a μισθός for his watch, two talents of gold, and he kept it for a year, lest Agamemnon pass by unseen (4.524–527; see [[spies-and-scouts]], [[talent]] and [[the-murder-of-agamemnon]]).
