summary: Telemachus does what Nestor told him: he begs Menelaus, “I beg you” (4.328), to “tell me truly” (4.331), and Menelaus answers that he will not “swerve aside to speak of other things, nor deceive you” (4.347–348).
refs: [4.328-331, 4.347-350]
---

Nestor sent Telemachus to Sparta with the couplet of Book 3, “And you yourself must beg him to tell you truly; / he will not tell a lie, for he is very wise” (3.327–328). The plea is the one Telemachus made to Nestor (4.322–331 = 3.92–101), and Menelaus' answer keeps the promise: he will not conceal a word of what the unerring Old Man of the Sea told him (4.347–350; [[tell-me-this-truly]], [[unerring-old-man-of-the-sea]], [[menelaus]]).
