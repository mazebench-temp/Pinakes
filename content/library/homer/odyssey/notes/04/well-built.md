---
summary: Odysseus once wrestled “in well-built Lesbos” (4.342), and Proteus tells Menelaus that he will not reach “your well-built house” until he has sacrificed in Egypt (4.475–476).
grc: [ἐυκτιμένῃ]
---

At 4.476 Bekker read οἶκον ἐς ὑψόροφον, “your high-roofed house”, with four manuscripts (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 4.476; [[high-roofed]]). The wrestling match at Lesbos is told only in Menelaus' wish (4.341–346; [[lesbos]], [[philomeleides]]).
