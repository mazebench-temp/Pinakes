---
summary: Athena is “daughter of aegis-bearing Zeus” in Eurycleia's counsel and in Penelope's prayer (4.752, 4.762).
---

Eurycleia bids Penelope bathe and “pray to Athena, daughter of aegis-bearing Zeus” (4.752), and Penelope begins her prayer, “Hear me, child of aegis-bearing Zeus, Atrytone” (4.762; see [[aegis-bearing]], [[child-of-aegis-bearing-zeus]], [[athena]] and [[prayer]]).
