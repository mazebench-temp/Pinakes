# κατένευσε (4.6), first met here in the Odyssey.
grc: [κατένευσε]
---

“For in Troy he had first promised and nodded his assent / that he would give her” (4.6–7; see [[achilles]], [[neoptolemus]] and [[the-wedding-at-sparta]]). The nod binds the promise, as Zeus' nod binds his (see the general article).
