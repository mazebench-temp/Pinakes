---
summary: Cronus is named in Book 4 only in his son's patronymic: the son of Cronus spins prosperity for a man at his marriage and at his birth, and Medon prays that he may not bring the suitors' plot to pass.
---

Menelaus praises Nestor's sons as the offspring of a fortunate man: “Easily known is the offspring of a man for whom the son of Cronus / spins prosperity at his marriage and at his birth” (4.207–208; see [[zeus]], [[son-of-cronus]] and [[easily-known-is-the-offspring]]). In Ithaca the herald Medon tells Penelope of the suitors' plan to kill Telemachus, a greater evil than she fears, “which may the son of Cronus not bring to pass” (4.698–699; see [[medon-the-herald]]).
