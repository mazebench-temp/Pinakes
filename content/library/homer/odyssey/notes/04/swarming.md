μῆλʼ ἁδινά, as in Book 1 (1.92); ἁδινόν with the verb of lament is the Iliad's ἁδινοῦ ἐξῆρχε γόοιο, when Achilles “led the unceasing lamentation” (*Iliad* 18.316; [[lament]], [[penelope]]).
