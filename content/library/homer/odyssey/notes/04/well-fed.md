# ζατρεφέας (4.451), the accusative; the entry claims ζατρεφέων.
grc: [ζατρεφέας]
---

At midday the old man “came out of the sea, and found the seals, / well-fed ones, and went among them all, and counted their number” (4.450–451; see [[seals]] and [[proteus]]).
