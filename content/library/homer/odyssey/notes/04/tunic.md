# χιτῶνας (4.50), first met here in the Odyssey.
grc: [χιτῶνας]
---

“And put woolly cloaks and tunics around them” (4.50; see [[cloak]], [[bathing]] and [[thick-and-woolly]]). The cloak is named first, though the tunic is put on before it (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 4.50; see [[hysteron-proteron]]).
