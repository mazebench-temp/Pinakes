# ἐρετμά, the oars of the suitors' ship (4.782), first met here.
grc: [ἐρετμὰ]
---

Proteus tells Menelaus that Odysseus cannot reach his native land, “for he has no ships with oars and no companions” (4.559; see [[calypso]]). Menelaus' men, sitting at the rowing benches, “struck the gray sea with their oars” (4.580; see [[rowing-benches]] and [[gray-sea]]), and the suitors “fitted the oars in their leather straps” (4.782; see the translation's key-word row on τροπός, and [[sailing-type-scene]]).
