summary: The goal of every return in the book: Menelaus' and Agamemnon's (4.474–476, 4.521–522, 4.545, 4.586), Odysseus', held by Calypso (4.558), and Telemachus', against whom the suitors lie in wait (4.823); and the land that Helen left (4.262).
grc: ["σὴν ἐς πατρίδʼ", "ἣν πατρίδα", "φίλην ἐς πατρίδʼ"]
---

Agamemnon reaches it and dies: “then he set foot on his native land rejoicing, / and he touched his native land and kissed it” (4.521–522; [[the-murder-of-agamemnon]]). Menelaus reaches it last, when the immortals “quickly sent me to my dear native land” (4.586). Helen, in Troy, groaned over the blind folly that led her “away from my dear native land” (4.261–262; [[ate]]).
