summary: Eidothea answers Menelaus twice with “Well then, stranger, I will tell you very exactly” (4.383, 4.399), and Menelaus turns to his question about the Achaeans with “But come, tell me this, and give me an exact account” (4.486).
---

The formula of host and stranger in Book 1 (1.179, 1.214) is spoken by a goddess to a stranger on the shore of Pharos (4.383, 4.399; [[eidothea]]), and by Menelaus to the old man he has caught (4.486; [[proteus]]).
