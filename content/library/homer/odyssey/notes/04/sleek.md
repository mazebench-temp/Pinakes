ποσσὶ … λιπαροῖσιν, the verse of the dressing scene (4.309 = 2.4; [[dressing-scene]]). λιπαρῶς, the adverb, is the comfortable old age that Teiresias promises Odysseus, γήραι ὕπο λιπαρῷ (11.136; [[olbos]], [[nestor]]).
