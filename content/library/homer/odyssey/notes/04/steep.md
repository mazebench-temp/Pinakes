---
summary: Agamemnon's storm comes as he nears “the steep mountain of Malea” (4.514), and the suitors sail “pondering in their minds sheer slaughter for Telemachus” (4.843).
grc: ["φόνον αἰπὺν"]
---

Μαλειάων ὄρος αἰπύ is the verse-end of Menelaus' own storm in Nestor's tale (3.287; [[malea]]). φόνον αἰπύν, “sheer slaughter”, is the sense of αἰπὺς ὄλεθρος, “sheer destruction” (1.11, 1.37), turned on Telemachus ([[the-suitors]]).
