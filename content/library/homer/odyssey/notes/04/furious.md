summary: Aegisthus' lookout watched for a year “lest he should pass by unseen, and remember his furious courage” (4.527).

θούριδος ἀλκῆς, the half-verse of battle exhortation, “remember your furious courage”, here the warrior's courage that Aegisthus fears in Agamemnon (4.524–527; [[exhortation]], [[aegisthus]]).
