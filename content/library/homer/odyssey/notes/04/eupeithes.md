---
summary: Eupeithes is named in Book 4 only in his son's verse, “Antinous, son of Eupeithes” (4.641, 4.660).
---

Antinous answers Noemon, “Then Antinous, son of Eupeithes, spoke to him in turn” (4.641), the verse of 1.383, and speaks to the suitors in council, “And Antinous, son of Eupeithes, spoke among them” (4.660; see [[antinous]] and [[the-suitors]]). At 4.641 the digitized text prints the name Ἐυπείθεος (see the translation's note on 4.641).
