grc: [λαοῖσι, λαοῖσιν]
---

The formulas ποιμὴν λαῶν, “shepherd of the people”, and ὄρχαμε λαῶν, “leader of the people”, are Menelaus' (4.24, 4.156, 4.291, 4.316) and Agamemnon's (4.532), and the lookout runs with his news to Aegisthus, “the shepherd of the people” too (4.528; see [[shepherd-of-the-people]] and [[leader-of-men]]). Menelaus would have settled Odysseus in Argos “with his possessions and his son / and all his people” (4.175–176; see [[kingship-and-authority]]); Penelope hopes that Laertes may come out and mourn before the people, who are eager to destroy the offspring of Odysseus (4.739–741; see [[laertes]]).
