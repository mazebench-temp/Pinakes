---
summary: The river of Book 4 is Aegyptus, “the river fallen from Zeus”, to which Proteus sends Menelaus back to sacrifice (4.477, 4.581).
---

Proteus tells Menelaus that he will not reach home “until to the water of Aegyptus, the river fallen from Zeus, / you have gone back again, and offered holy hecatombs” (4.477–478), and Menelaus does: “And back to the water of Aegyptus, the river fallen from Zeus, / I brought my ships to a halt” (4.581–582; see [[aegyptus-river]], [[fallen-from-zeus]] and [[hecatomb]]). See the translation's note on 4.477, 4.581.
