ἔπεα πτερόεντα προσηύδα (4.25, 4.77), with ἀγόρευεν at the end of the verse for Peisistratus, “remembering him, he spoke winged words” (4.189; [[antilochus]]), and the first person προσηύδων in Menelaus' tale (4.550; [[proteus]]).
