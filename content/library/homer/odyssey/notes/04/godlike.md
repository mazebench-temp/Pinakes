---
summary: “Godlike Deiphobus” follows Helen to the Horse (4.276), and “godlike Eurymachus” sits beside Antinous among the suitors (4.628).
grc: [θεοείκελος]
---

The two epithets of looks, θεοείκελος and θεοειδής, are given to men whose part is not godlike: the Trojan who goes with Helen as she tries the voices of the Achaeans' wives (4.274–279; [[deiphobus]]), and the suitor who is one of the two “leaders of the suitors” (4.628–629; [[eurymachus]], [[the-suitors]]).
