---
summary: Noemon's twelve mares in Elis have “at their teats hard-working mules, / unbroken” (4.636–637).
grc: [ταλαεργοὶ]
---

ἡμίονοι ταλαεργοί, the mules that Noemon wants to break in; Merry finds mules particularly suited to a country like Ithaca (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 4.635; [[mules]], [[unbroken]]).
