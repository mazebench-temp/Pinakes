---
summary: Peisistratus tells Menelaus that “the Gerenian horseman Nestor sent me forth / to go with him as his escort” (4.161–162).
---

Nestor's title, in his son's mouth, is the only one he has in this book; elsewhere he is “old Nestor”, who used to say that Menelaus was wise beyond all mortals (4.190–191; [[wise]]), and the man whom Zeus has granted to grow old in ease among sons of good sense (4.209–211). See [[nestor]] and [[peisistratus]].
