---
summary: Menelaus' “heart and proud spirit / were warmed again” when Proteus tells him that he may yet find Aegisthus alive (4.548–549); and “the proud spirit” of Antinous and Eurymachus is indignant at Noemon's news (4.658).
---

θυμὸς ἀγήνωρ, in the Odyssey above all the suitors' spirit, is here Menelaus' too, revived by the hope of vengeance after his tears (4.538–549; [[the-murder-of-agamemnon]]). At 4.658 the two leaders of the suitors are meant, Antinous and Eurymachus (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 4.658; [[antinous]], [[eurymachus]]).
