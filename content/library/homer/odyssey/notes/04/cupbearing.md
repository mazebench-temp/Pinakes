# οἰνοχοῆσαι (4.233), first met here in the Odyssey.
grc: [οἰνοχοῆσαι]
en: [pour the wine]
---

“And when she had cast it in and bidden them pour the wine, / she spoke again” (4.233–234; see [[nepenthe]], [[wine]] and [[mixing-bowl]]). The drugged wine is served like any other, drawn from the bowl and carried round; Helen's command is the whole of the service in this scene. At the parting meal in Book 15 it is Menelaus' son who pours the wine (15.141, a plain rendering; see [[megapenthes]]).
