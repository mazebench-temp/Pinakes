---
summary: The suitors supposed that Telemachus was on the farm, “with the flocks or with the swineherd” (4.640), Odysseus' swineherd, who is not named in this book.
refs: [4.640]
---

The swineherd of 4.640 (συβώτῃ) is Odysseus' swineherd on the farm; the poem names him Eumaeus when Odysseus comes to his hut in Book 14 (see [[the-suitors]] and [[telemachus]]).
