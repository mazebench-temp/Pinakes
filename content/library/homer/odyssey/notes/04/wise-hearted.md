---
summary: Penelope accuses the suitors of wasting “the wealth of wise-hearted Telemachus” (4.687).
---

On the meaning of δαΐφρων, “wise-hearted” rather than “warlike”, see the translation's note on 1.48. Penelope speaks of the son's inheritance as she reminds the suitors of the father's justice (4.686–695; [[telemachus]], [[penelope]]).
