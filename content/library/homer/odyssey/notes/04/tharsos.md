---
summary: The phantom bids Penelope, θάρσει, “Take heart”, for Pallas Athena goes with her son as his escort (4.825–829).
---

θάρσει, “Take heart”, is Homer's word of encouragement, and the phantom gives its reason: “for such an escort goes with him, one whom other men too / have prayed to stand beside them, for she has the power, / Pallas Athena” (4.825–828; see [[eidolon]], [[athena]] and [[deos]]).
