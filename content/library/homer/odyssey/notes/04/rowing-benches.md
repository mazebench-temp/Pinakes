---
summary: On Pharos Menelaus' crews go on board and sit down at the rowing benches (4.579).
---

After the masts and sails are set, the crews “went on board themselves, and sat down at the rowing benches” (4.579; see [[oars]] and [[sailing-type-scene]]). The verse is 2.419; here the subject turns from “we” (4.577–578) to “they”, the crews (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 4.579).
