summary: Only two of the “leaders of the bronze-armored Achaeans” perished on the way home (4.496–497); Antinous and Eurymachus are the “leaders of the suitors” (4.629), and Noemon saw Mentor, or a god, go on board as the crew's “leader” (4.653–654).
---

ἀρχός is the man in command. Proteus counts the losses among the ἀρχοί of the Achaeans: two in their homecoming, Ajax and Agamemnon, and a third held alive on the sea (4.496–498; see [[the-returns-of-the-achaeans]]). In Ithaca the suitors have their ἀρχοί, Antinous and Eurymachus (4.628–629; the second verse again 21.187; see [[the-suitors]]), and Telemachus' crew had theirs, Mentor or a god in his likeness (4.653–656; see [[mentor]] and [[truth-and-deception]]).
