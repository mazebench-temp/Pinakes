---
summary: Peisistratus and Telemachus address Menelaus as “leader of the people” in the same verse, “Menelaus, son of Atreus, nurtured by Zeus, leader of the people” (4.156, 4.291, 4.316).
grc: ["ὄρχαμε λαῶν"]
en: [leader of the people]
---

The whole verse is the Iliad's: Euphorbus addresses Menelaus with it when he challenges him over the body of Patroclus (*Iliad* 17.12). Here it is the young men's formal opening: Peisistratus speaking for Telemachus (4.156), and Telemachus himself, asking to go to bed and, the next morning, telling his errand (4.291, 4.316). He uses it again when he asks leave to go home (15.64, 15.87). See [[son-of-atreus]], [[zeus-nurtured]] and [[menelaus]].
