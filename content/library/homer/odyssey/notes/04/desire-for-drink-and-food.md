summary: The formula ends the first meal at Sparta, and Telemachus speaks to Peisistratus with his head close (4.68–70).
---

As at Pylos, the questions wait for the end of the meal: Menelaus has said that he will ask who they are when they have eaten (4.60–62; [[visit-scene]]), but it is Telemachus' whisper that he hears first (4.69–76; [[putting-his-head-close]]).
