The tale of Proteus is told in the first person, and its verses of reply are first-person forms of the formula: ἀμειβόμενος προσέειπον for Menelaus' own answers, ὁ δέ μʼ αὐτίκʼ ἀμειβόμενος προσέειπεν for the old man's (see the translation's table of formulas; [[proteus]], [[eidothea]]). Peisistratus “answered him face to face” in the verse of his name (4.155), and so does Noemon (4.648); Medon, Eurycleia and Penelope each “spoke to her in turn” (αὖτε προσέειπε, 4.696, 4.742, 4.830). Helen and Penelope answer with words (4.234, 4.706). See [[fair-haired]], [[wise]] and [[then-answered]].
