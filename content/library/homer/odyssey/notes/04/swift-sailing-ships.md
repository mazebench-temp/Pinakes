---
summary: Penelope: Telemachus had no need “to go on board swift-sailing ships, which are horses of the sea / for men” (4.708–709).
grc: [ὠκυπόρων]
---

The epithet introduces the Odyssey's metaphor of ships as horses (ἁλὸς ἵπποι). Merry takes ἵπποι here as almost “chariots”, comparing Aeschylus' ναυτίλων ὀχήματα, “the chariots of sailors” (*Prometheus Bound* 468), as Merry quotes it (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 4.708; [[ships]], [[watery-ways]]).
