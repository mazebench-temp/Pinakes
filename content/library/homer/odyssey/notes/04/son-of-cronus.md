summary: Menelaus: “Easily known is the offspring of a man for whom the son of Cronus / spins prosperity at his marriage and at his birth” (4.207–208); and Medon prays that “the son of Cronus” may not bring the suitors' plan to pass (4.699).
---

Κρονίων spins a man's fortune in Menelaus' praise of Nestor ([[olbos]], [[nestor]]) and is the god of Medon's short prayer against the ambush (4.697–702; [[medon-the-herald]], [[zeus]]).
