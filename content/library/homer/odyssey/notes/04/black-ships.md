summary: Antinous asks whether Telemachus took “the black ship” from Noemon by force (4.646); Penelope's son “went on board the black hollow ship” (4.731); and the suitors “set the mast and the sails in the black ship” (4.781).
---

νηὶ μελαίνῃ at 4.781 is the suitors' ship for the ambush (4.778–786; [[sailing-type-scene]]). The black ripple of the West Wind's breath is not the ships' color (4.402; see [[the-ripple-of-the-west-wind]]).
