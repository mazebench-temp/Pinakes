---
summary: The phantom will not tell Penelope whether Odysseus is alive or dead, “and it is a bad thing to speak empty wind” (4.837).
---

ἀνεμώλια βάζειν, “to speak empty wind”, is to talk of what one does not know (4.836–837; see [[it-is-bad-to-speak-empty-wind]] and [[eidolon]]). The phantom that says it then slips away past the bolt “into the blasts of the winds” (4.838–839). Odysseus gives the shade of Agamemnon the same answer about Orestes (11.464).
