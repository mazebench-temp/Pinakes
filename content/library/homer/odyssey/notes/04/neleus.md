# Νηλήιον (4.639), the adjective of the name; pylos' Book 4 note claims it too.
grc: [Νηλήιον]
---

Pylos is called “Neleian” after Nestor's father, who ruled it before him (4.639; see [[pylos]], [[nestor]] and the Book 3 note), as “Neleian Pylos” in the Iliad (*Iliad* 11.682).
