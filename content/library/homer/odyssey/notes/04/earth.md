---
summary: The earth of Book 4 is above all the native land of the returns (4.262, 4.476, 4.521–523, 4.545, 4.558, 4.823), and also the grain-giving earth of Egypt (4.229), the earth over which Menelaus has wandered (4.268), the creatures that move upon it (4.417), and the limits of the earth where the Elysian plain lies (4.563).
---

**The native land.** Proteus sends Menelaus back to Egypt before he may reach “your well-built house and your native land” (4.476), and tells him that Agamemnon “set foot on his native land rejoicing” and “was glad to see his land” (4.521–523; see [[agamemnon]]); Odysseus “cannot reach his native land” (4.558); the suitors are eager to kill Telemachus “before he reaches his native land” (4.823). Helen calls Sparta “my dear native land” (4.262; see [[dear-native-land]]).

**The earth.** Helen's drugs come from Egypt, “where the grain-giving earth bears the most / drugs” (4.229–230; see [[egypt]] and [[nepenthe]]); Menelaus has “gone over much of the earth” (4.268); Proteus “will make his attempt by becoming all the things that upon the earth / creep” (4.417–418; see [[proteus]] and [[shape-changing]]); and Menelaus' end is “the Elysian plain and the limits of the earth” (4.563; see [[elysium]]).
