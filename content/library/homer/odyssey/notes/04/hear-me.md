summary: Penelope's prayer opens “Hear me, child of aegis-bearing Zeus, Atrytone” (4.762) and closes “and the goddess heard her prayer” (4.767); and her lament to her women begins “Hear me, friends” (4.722).
grc: ["κλῦτε φίλαι", "ἔκλυεν ἀρῆς"]
en: [heard her prayer]
---

κλῦτε, φίλαι is the opening of Thetis' lament among her sisters in the Iliad, κλῦτε κασίγνηται (*Iliad* 18.52), here of a mortal among her women ([[lament]], [[handmaids]]). For the close of the prayer Merry takes ἔκλυεν οἱ as “heard for her”, that is, heard her prayer, comparing the dative with δέξασθαί τινι (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 4.767; [[prayer-type-scene]], [[atrytone]]).
