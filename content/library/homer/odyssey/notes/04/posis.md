---
summary: Helen questions “her husband” about the strangers (4.137) and remembers the husband she left (4.263); Penelope mourns “my brave, lion-hearted husband” (4.724, 4.814).
---

Helen sits down and at once questions πόσιν, “her husband” (4.137), and in her tale remembers leaving “my child and my bridal chamber and my husband, / who lacked nothing, in mind or in form” (4.263–264; see [[helen]]). Penelope twice mourns πόσιν ἐσθλὸν ... θυμολέοντα, “my brave, lion-hearted husband” (4.724, 4.814; see [[lion-hearted]] and [[husbands-and-wives]]).
