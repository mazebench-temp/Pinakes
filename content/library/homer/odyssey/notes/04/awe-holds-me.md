---
summary: Telemachus, at the splendor of Menelaus' house: “awe holds me as I look at them” (4.75); and Helen, at the young stranger's likeness to Odysseus: “awe holds me as I look” (4.142).
en: [awe holds me as I look]
---

The two uses in the book are two of the wonders the formula names: a king's palace and a son's likeness to his father, as Nestor's at Pylos (3.123; [[sebas]], [[house]], [[fathers-and-sons]]).
