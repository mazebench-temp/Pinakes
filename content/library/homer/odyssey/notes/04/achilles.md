---
summary: Achilles appears in Book 4 only through his son, to whom Menelaus is sending Hermione as he promised at Troy (4.5–9).
---

**His son.** “The daughter he was sending to the son of Achilles, breaker of men; / for in Troy he had first promised and nodded his assent / that he would give her, and now the gods were bringing their wedding to pass” (4.5–7; see [[neoptolemus]], [[hermione-daughter-of-helen]], [[breaker-of-men]] and [[the-wedding-at-sparta]]). She goes with horses and chariots “to the renowned city of the Myrmidons, over whom he ruled” (4.8–9; see [[myrmidons]]). The son is not named here; the Odyssey names him Neoptolemus when Odysseus tells Achilles' shade of him in the underworld (11.506).
