θαλερὸν κατὰ δάκρυ χέοντα (4.556; [[shedding-tears]]). Penelope's θαλερὴ … φωνή is in the two verses of Antilochus when he hears of Patroclus' death (*Iliad* 17.695–696 = 4.704–705; [[silence]], [[messenger-scene]]).
