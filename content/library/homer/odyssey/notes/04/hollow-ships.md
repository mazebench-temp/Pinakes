grc: ["γλαφυρὴ νηῦς"]

γλαφυρὴ νηῦς, “as far off as a hollow ship covers in a whole day / when a shrill fair wind blows behind it” (4.356–357; [[pharos]], [[shrill]]). The two hollow ships of Penelope's laments are the same voyage told to her women and to the phantom (4.731, 4.817).
