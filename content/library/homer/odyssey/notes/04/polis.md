summary: Hermione is sent to “the renowned city of the Myrmidons” (4.9); Menelaus would have given Odysseus “a city in Argos”, emptying one of the cities he ruled (4.174–177); Odysseus slipped into the city of the Trojans as a beggar (4.246, 4.249).
---

The son of Achilles rules a city in the land of the Myrmidons, προτὶ ἄστυ περικλυτόν, “the renowned city” (4.9; see [[myrmidons]]). Menelaus' offer is a city for Odysseus, νάσσα πόλιν, emptied of its people for him (4.174–177; see [[kingship-and-authority]]). In Helen's tale Odysseus slips into “the wide-streeted city of hostile men”, “the city of the Trojans”, in a beggar's likeness (4.246–249; see [[odysseus-enters-troy-as-a-beggar]] and [[troy]]).
