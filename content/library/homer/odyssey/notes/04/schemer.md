---
summary: Proteus tells how “scheming Aegisthus” posted a lookout and promised him two talents of gold (4.524–526).
---

Αἴγισθος δολόμητις, his verse-opening in Nestor's tale (3.250), comes as the scheme itself is told: the lookout watching for a year, the twenty men in ambush, the feast (4.524–535). The next verses say it again: “at once Aegisthus devised a crafty scheme” (δολίην … τέχνην, 4.529; [[dolos]], [[aegisthus]], [[the-murder-of-agamemnon]]).
