summary: Menelaus rebukes Eteoneus, “You were no fool … before now” (4.31–32); Peisistratus calls his brother “by no means the worst / of the Argives” (4.199–200); Helen says that she left a husband “who lacked nothing, in mind or in form” (4.264); and the narrator describes Asteris as “not large” (4.846).
refs: [4.31, 4.199-200, 4.264, 4.846]
---

Each understatement says more than its words: the rebuke begins with praise of the attendant's past good sense (4.31–32; [[eteoneus]]); Antilochus, “by no means the worst”, was in fact swift and a fighter (4.199–202; [[antilochus]]); and Helen's self-reproach praises the husband she left (4.261–264; [[helen]], [[menelaus]]).
