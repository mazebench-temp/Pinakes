summary: In Helen's tale the other Trojan women wail for the men Odysseus killed, while Helen rejoices (4.259–260).
---

“Then the other Trojan women wailed shrilly; but my heart / rejoiced, since already my heart was turned to going / back home” (4.259–261; see [[helen]], [[lament]] and [[odysseus-enters-troy-as-a-beggar]]).
