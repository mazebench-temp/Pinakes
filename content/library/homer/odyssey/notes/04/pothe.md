summary: Telemachus would stay a year at Sparta, and “no longing for home or parents would seize me” (4.596); Eurycleia was to keep his voyage secret until Penelope herself “missed him” (4.748).
grc: [πόθος, ποθέσαι]
en: [missed]
---

Telemachus asks to go, though he could sit a year beside Menelaus, “and no longing for home or parents would seize me; / for listening to your stories and your words I am terribly / delighted” (4.595–598; see [[mythos]]). In Ithaca Eurycleia confesses that he made her swear not to tell his mother before the twelfth day, “or you yourself missed him and heard that he had gone” (4.747–748; see [[mothers-and-sons]]), the terms he set in Book 2 (2.373–375).
