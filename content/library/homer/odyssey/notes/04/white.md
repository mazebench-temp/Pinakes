summary: The suitors “spread the white sails” for the ambush (4.783); “white barley” is mixed for the strangers' horses (4.41); and Menelaus' plain bears “broad-eared white barley” (4.604).
grc: ["κρῖ λευκὸν"]
---

ἱστία λευκά closes the launching verses (4.780–783; [[sailing-type-scene]]); κρῖ λευκόν is the barley of Sparta's wide plain, which Telemachus sets against rocky Ithaca (4.602–608; [[barley]], [[horse-pasturing]]).
