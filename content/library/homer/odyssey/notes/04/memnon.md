Ἠοῦς … φαεινῆς ἀγλαὸς υἱός, “the splendid son of shining Dawn” (4.188), is Memnon, king of the Eastern Ethiopians, whom Achilles killed to avenge Antilochus (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 4.188; see [[antilochus]], [[eos]] and [[peisistratus]]). The poem names him only once, at 11.522.
