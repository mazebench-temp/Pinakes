---
summary: Menelaus lays Helen's coming to the Horse on “a god who meant to hold out glory to the Trojans” (4.275).
---

κῦδος is the glory of a victory, which a god gives. Had Helen drawn the men in the Horse to answer her, the Trojans would have won it; so Menelaus credits her coming to “a god who meant to hold out glory to the Trojans” (4.274–275; see [[daimon]] and [[who-is-to-blame]]), and Merry explains it so: had she tempted the heroes to reveal themselves, the Trojans would have won the glory of victory over the Greeks (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 4.274).
