---
summary: Menelaus finds the lineage of kings in his young guests: “you are of the lineage of men, of kings whom Zeus has nurtured, / scepter-bearing kings, since base men would not beget such sons as you” (4.63–64).
grc: [σκηπτούχων]
en: [scepter-bearing kings]
---

The two epithets of royal authority stand together ([[zeus-nurtured]]). Menelaus reads the strangers' birth in their looks before he asks who they are (4.60–64; [[visit-scene]]), as Eteoneus did at the door (4.27). Telemachus is the son of a king, and Peisistratus of Nestor (see [[basileus]] and [[kings]]).
