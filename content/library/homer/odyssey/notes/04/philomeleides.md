# Unnamed: "and threw him down mightily" (4.344).
refs: [4.344]
---

ἐξ ἔριδος Φιλομηλεΐδῃ ἐπάλαισεν ἀναστάς, / κὰδ δʼ ἔβαλε κρατερῶς, “when he stood up and wrestled Philomeleides out of strife, / and threw him down mightily” (4.343–344; see [[odysseus-wrestles-philomeleides]] and [[odysseus]]). The poem tells nothing more of him: not his country, though the match was on Lesbos, nor why he fought (see [[lesbos]]). The verses return when Telemachus repeats Menelaus' words to Penelope (17.133–135).
