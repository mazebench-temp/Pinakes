---
summary: Telemachus tells Menelaus that he has come for a report of his father (4.316–317), and in Ithaca Medon tells Penelope that he has gone “in search of news of his father” (4.701), to learn of his homecoming “or what fate he has met” (4.713–714).
refs: [4.316-317, 4.701, 4.712-714]
---

The purpose that Book 2 gives in the verse νόστον πευσόμενος πατρὸς δὴν οἰχομένοιο is told here in other words: Telemachus' εἴ τινά μοι κληηδόνα πατρὸς ἐνίσποις (4.317), μετὰ πατρὸς ἀκουήν, the phrase of 2.308 (4.701), and Medon's guess, ὄφρα πύθηται / πατρὸς ἑοῦ ἢ νόστον ἢ ὅν τινα πότμον ἐπέσπεν (4.713–714; [[the-journey-of-telemachus]], [[medon-the-herald]]).
