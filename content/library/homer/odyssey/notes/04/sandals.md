---
summary: Menelaus rises at dawn and binds his fine sandals under his shining feet (4.309), in the verse of Telemachus' rising (2.4).
---

At dawn Menelaus “put on his clothes, and about his shoulder set his sharp sword, / and under his shining feet he bound his fine sandals” (4.308–309; see [[sword]] and [[dressing-scene]]), the verses of Telemachus' rising for the assembly (2.3–4).
