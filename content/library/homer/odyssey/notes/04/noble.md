---
summary: Penelope's first words to Medon give the suitors their title: “Herald, why have the noble suitors sent you?” (4.681).
---

μνηστῆρες ἀγαυοί, the suitors' epithet in Telemachus' mouth (2.209), opens Penelope's bitter question, which goes on to wish that they may feast in her house for the last time (4.681–685; [[the-suitors]], [[medon-the-herald]], [[penelope]]).
