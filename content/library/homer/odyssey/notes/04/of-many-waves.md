---
summary: Menelaus begins the tale of Pharos: “Now there is an island in the sea of many waves / in front of Egypt, and they call it Pharos” (4.354–355).
---

The first of the three uses of the verse-end πολυκλύστῳ ἐνὶ πόντῳ sets the island apart in the open sea, a day's sail from Egypt (4.354–357; [[pharos]], [[sea]]).
