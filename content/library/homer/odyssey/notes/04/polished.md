ἐυξέστας ἀσαμίνθους and ξεστὴν … τράπεζαν are the furnishings of the welcome ([[bathing]], [[water-for-the-hands]]); ἵππῳ ἔνι ξεστῷ, the wooden horse of planed timber (4.272; [[sinon-and-the-wooden-horse]]); δίφρον ἐύξοον, the chariot that Telemachus will not take to Ithaca (4.590, 4.601; [[chariot]]).
