summary: The welcome at Sparta: the strangers arrive during the wedding feast, halt in the doorway, and are seen by Eteoneus, who asks whether to receive them; Menelaus rebukes him, and they are brought in, bathed, clothed and fed, and the question who they are is put off until they have eaten (4.1–67).

---

Here the host's question is answered before it is asked: Helen knows Telemachus by his likeness to his father (4.138–146), and Peisistratus confirms it (4.155–167). Menelaus puts it off in the words of the custom, “when you have tasted the meal, we will ask who you are / among men” (4.61–62; [[who-are-you-among-men]], [[xenia]], [[water-for-the-hands]]).
