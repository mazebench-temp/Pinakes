---
summary: Penelope: ships “cross over the wide waters” (4.709); and the suitors sail “over the watery ways” to their ambush (4.842).
grc: ["πουλὺν ἐφʼ ὑγρήν"]
en: [wide waters]
---

πουλὺν ἐφʼ ὑγρήν, the Iliad's phrase for the Argives who crossed the sea for Menelaus' sake (*Iliad* 10.27), is Penelope's for the sea that her son has dared (4.707–709; [[swift-sailing-ships]]). ἐπέπλεον ὑγρὰ κέλευθα takes the suitors to Asteris (4.842–847; [[asteris]]).
