summary: Antinous lays his ambush “in the strait between Ithaca and rocky Samos” (4.671), at “a rocky island in the middle of the sea, / between Ithaca and rocky Samos,” Asteris (4.844–846).
grc: [πετρήεσσα]
---

Σάμοιό τε παιπαλοέσσης ends both verses of the strait (4.671, 4.845; again 15.29; [[samos]]), and πετρήεσσα describes the island itself (4.844; [[asteris]]).
