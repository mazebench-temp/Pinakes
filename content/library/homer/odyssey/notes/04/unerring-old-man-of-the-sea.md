---
summary: Menelaus names the source of his tale: “what the unerring Old Man of the Sea told me” (4.349); Eidothea describes her father so twice (4.384, 4.401), and the title brings him back when he gives his last answer (4.542).
---

Telemachus has asked twice to be told truly, νημερτὲς ἐνίσπες (4.314, 4.331), and Menelaus answers with what the unerring one told him (4.347–350; [[tell-me-this-truly]]). The title passes from Eidothea's account of her father (4.384–386) to Menelaus' own narration of the ambush and the prophecy (4.401, 4.542; [[proteus]], [[eidothea]]). Telemachus repeats Menelaus' verse when he tells his mother of the journey (17.140).
