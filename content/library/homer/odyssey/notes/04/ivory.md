# ἐλέφαντος (4.73), first met here in the Odyssey.
grc: [ἐλέφαντος]
---

“Take note, son of Nestor, dear to my heart, / of the flash of bronze throughout the echoing halls, / and of gold and amber and silver and ivory” (4.71–73; see [[house]], [[amber]] and [[gold-and-silver]]). Ivory stands last in the list of the precious things of a king's house. In the later books it adorns a sword's scabbard, Penelope's chair and the handle of the key to the storeroom, and the false dreams come through gates of ivory (8.404, 19.56, 19.562–564, 21.7, plain renderings).
