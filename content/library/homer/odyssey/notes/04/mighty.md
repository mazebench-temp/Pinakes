---
summary: Eidothea is “the daughter of mighty Proteus, the Old Man of the Sea” (4.365).
grc: [ἰφθίμου]
---

Merry refers ἴφθιμος simply to ἶφι, “by might”, rather than to ἶφι with τιμή or with θυμός (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 4.365; [[proteus]], [[eidothea]]). The same word is the name of Penelope's sister Iphthime (4.797), and Aristarchus doubted whether it was an epithet or a name there (see the translation's note on 4.797; [[iphthime]]).
