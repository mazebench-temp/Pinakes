---
summary: Menelaus answers “deeply troubled” twice: Eteoneus' question whether to receive the strangers (4.30), and Telemachus' account of the suitors (4.332).
---

Each time the vexation is at a wrong done to hospitality: the attendant who would send guests away, rebuked with the memory of the hospitality Menelaus himself received on his wanderings (4.31–36; [[xenia]], [[eteoneus]]), and the suitors who would lie in Odysseus' bed (4.333–346; [[fierce-hearted]], [[the-suitors]]).
