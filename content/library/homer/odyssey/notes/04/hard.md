---
summary: Penelope turns on her women: “Hard-hearted women, not even you, any of you, thought in your minds / to rouse me from my bed” (4.729–730).
---

σχέτλιαι, the word of reproach for those who will not give way, is spoken by a mistress to her slave women, who knew of the voyage and said nothing (4.729–734; [[handmaids]], [[penelope]]). Eurycleia answers for them all (4.742–757; [[eurycleia]]).
