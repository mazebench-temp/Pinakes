grc: ["ἠοῖ φαινομένηφιν"]
en: [when dawn appears]

ἦμος δʼ ἠριγένεια φάνη ῥοδοδάκτυλος Ἠώς opens the one morning of the book in Sparta (4.306–310; [[dressing-scene]]) and, in Menelaus' tale, the morning of the ambush among the seals and the morning of the return to Egypt (4.431–433, 4.576–580; [[early-born]], [[rosy-fingered]]).
