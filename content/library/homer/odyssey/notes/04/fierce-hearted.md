---
summary: Menelaus on the suitors: “it was in the bed of a fierce-hearted man / that they wished to lie, weaklings as they are” (4.333–334).
---

κρατερόφρονος ἀνδρός begins the lion simile, in which the man whose bed they would take returns to his lair (4.333–340; [[lion]], [[odysseus]], [[the-suitors]]). Telemachus repeats the speech to his mother (17.124–131).
