---
summary: Aeschylus wrote a satyr play called Proteus. Its few fragments keep the name Εἰδώ for the Old Man's daughter, the short form of the Εἰδοθέη of this book.
---

- *Proteus* fr. 212 Nauck; the scholia on 4.366, as Merry reports them, say that Aeschylus called her Εἰδοθέα, and Dindorf that he called her Εἰδώ (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, critical note on 4.366): [[eidothea]], [[proteus]] and [[euripides-helen]].
