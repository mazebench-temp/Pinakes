ἐσθλὸς Ὀδυσσεύς is the form of Telemachus' plea in Book 3, and the verses of 4.322–331 are those of 3.92–101 ([[odysseus]]). Penelope's two ἐσθλόν frame her verse of his excellence, πόσιν ἐσθλὸν … ἐσθλόν, τοῦ κλέος εὐρύ (4.724–726 = 4.814–816; [[lion-hearted]], [[kleos]]).
