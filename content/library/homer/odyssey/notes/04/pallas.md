---
summary: Menelaus ends his tale of the Horse with “Pallas Athena,” who led Helen away (4.289); and the phantom names Telemachus' escort, “Pallas Athena” (4.828).
---

Παλλὰς Ἀθήνη closes the story of the voices at the Horse, and Παλλὰς Ἀθηναίη, at the start of the verse, reveals the companion of Telemachus' voyage to his sleeping mother (4.826–829; [[sinon-and-the-wooden-horse]], [[athena]]).
