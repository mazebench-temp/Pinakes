---
summary: Hermione is sent as a bride “to the son of Achilles, breaker of men” (4.5).
grc: ["Ἀχιλλῆος ῥηξήνορος"]
---

The son is not named (see [[neoptolemus]]); his father has the epithet that the Iliad gives to no one else (*Iliad* 7.228, 13.324, 16.146, 16.575). The marriage was promised at Troy, and the bride goes to the city of the Myrmidons (4.5–9; [[achilles]], [[myrmidons]]). The Phaeacian Ῥηξήνωρ, Arete's father, has the word as his name (7.63, 7.146).
