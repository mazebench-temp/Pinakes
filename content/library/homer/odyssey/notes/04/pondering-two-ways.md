4.120 is the verse of Achilles' deliberation in *Iliad* 1, “While he was pondering this in his mind and in his heart”, and there too the pondering is cut short by an arrival, Athena's (*Iliad* 1.193–194); here it is Helen's (4.120–122; [[decision-scene]], [[helen]]). The suitors, sailing to the ambush, ponder only one thing, “sheer slaughter for Telemachus” (4.843; [[steep]]).
