---
summary: When Menelaus has offered the hecatombs at the river Aegyptus he has “ended the anger of the gods who live forever” (4.583); Helen's drug is ἄχολον, against anger (4.221).
grc: [ἄχολον]
---

The gods' χόλος held Menelaus in Egypt until he went back to the river and offered perfect hecatombs, αὐτὰρ ἐπεὶ κατέπαυσα θεῶν χόλον αἰὲν ἐόντων (4.581–583; see [[reciprocity-with-the-gods]]). Helen's drug was νηπενθές τʼ ἄχολόν τε, “a drug against grief and anger” (4.221; see [[nepenthe]] and [[achos]]); ἄχολος is found only here in Homer.
