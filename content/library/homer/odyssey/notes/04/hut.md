---
summary: In Helen's tale Odysseus, disguised as a beggar in Troy, is not to be revealed until he has reached “the swift ships and the huts” of the Achaeans (4.255).
---

Helen swore “not to reveal Odysseus among the Trojans / before he had reached the swift ships and the huts” (4.254–255; see [[odysseus-enters-troy-as-a-beggar]] and [[helen]]). The huts are the Achaean camp of the Iliad (see the general article). At 4.123 κλισίη is Helen's chair at Sparta, which this entry does not claim (see [[throne]]).
