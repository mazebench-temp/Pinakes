---
summary: Before telling what Proteus told him, Menelaus promises Telemachus that he will not swerve aside to speak of other things, “nor deceive you” (4.347–348).
grc: [ἀπατήσω]
en: [deceive]
---

Telemachus has begged for the truth about his father (4.322–331), and Menelaus answers: “But as for what you ask and beg of me, I would not / swerve aside to speak of other things, nor deceive you; / but what the unerring Old Man of the Sea told me, / of that I will not conceal a word from you, or hide it” (4.347–350; see [[truth-and-deception]] and [[proteus]]). Merry takes παρακλιδόν, “aside”, as the nearer definition of παρέξ (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 4.348). When Telemachus tells his mother of Menelaus' answer, he repeats these verses (17.138–141).
