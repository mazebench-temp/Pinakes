---
summary: Horace's sixteenth Epode bids the Romans sail for the blessed fields and rich islands that Oceanus keeps, beyond the reach of the Sidonian sailors and of Ulysses' crew, the Isles of the Blessed that later writers made of this book's Elysian plain.
---
