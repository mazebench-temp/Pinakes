---
summary: The storm wind carries Agamemnon “over the fish-filled sea, groaning heavily” (4.516).
grc: ["βαρέα στενάχοντα"]
en: [groaning heavily]
---

βαρέα στενάχοντα, the Iliad's phrase for a wounded man carried from the field, is here the king driven off his course near Malea (4.514–516; [[the-murder-of-agamemnon]], [[fish-filled]]).
