---
summary: Eidothea warns that her father will try “all the things that upon the earth / creep, and water, and wondrous fire” (4.417–418).
grc: [θεσπιδαὲς]
---

θεσπιδαὲς πῦρ is the Iliad's verse-end for fire, on the wall, at the ships and in the river (*Iliad* 12.177, 15.597, 21.342). In the event the old man becomes a lion, a serpent, a leopard, a boar, water and a tree, but not fire (4.456–458; [[proteus]], [[shape-changing]]).
