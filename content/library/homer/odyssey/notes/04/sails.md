---
summary: Menelaus' crews set the sails in their ships on Pharos (4.578), and the suitors set theirs and spread them white (4.781–783).
---

On Pharos Menelaus' men “set the masts and the sails in the balanced ships” (4.578; see [[mast]]). In Ithaca the suitors launch their ship for the ambush: they “set the mast and the sails in the black ship, / and fitted the oars in their leather straps, / all in good order, and spread the white sails” (4.781–783; see [[oars]], [[white]] and [[sailing-type-scene]]).
