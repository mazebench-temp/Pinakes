# ἱστούς, the masts of Menelaus' ships (4.578), first met here.
grc: [ἱστοὺς]
---

“And set the masts and the sails in the balanced ships” (4.578), Menelaus says of his ships on Pharos, and the suitors “set the mast and the sails in the black ship” (4.781), the verse of the Phaeacians' launching (8.52; see [[sails]], [[sailing-type-scene]] and [[balanced-ships]]).
