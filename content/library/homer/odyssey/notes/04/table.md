---
summary: A handmaid draws up a polished table beside the guests at Sparta, in the verses of the welcome (4.54 = 1.138).
---

“for them to wash; and she drew up a polished table beside them” (4.54; see [[water-for-the-hands]] and [[feast-type-scene]]). The scholia report a variant χρυσῆν, “golden”, for ξεστήν, “polished” (W. W. Merry's critical note on 4.54), which would suit a house of gold and silver (see [[gold-and-silver]]).
