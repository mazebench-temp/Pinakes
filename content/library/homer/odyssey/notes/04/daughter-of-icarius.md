---
summary: Penelope wakes from the dream as “the daughter of Icarius” (4.839–840), after the phantom has come to her in the likeness of her sister Iphthime, “daughter of great-hearted Icarius” (4.797).
---

κούρη Ἰκαρίοιο closes the dream, at the moment she wakes comforted (4.839–841; [[dream-scene]]). The father's name ties the dreamer to the phantom, who wears the form of another daughter of Icarius, married far away in Pherae (4.795–798; [[iphthime]], [[icarius]], [[penelope]]).
