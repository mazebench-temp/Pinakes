---
summary: In the Horse “all the other sons of the Achaeans were silent” when Helen called to them (4.285).
---

υἷες Ἀχαιῶν closes the verse that sets the silent chiefs against Anticlus, who alone wanted to answer (4.285–286; [[anticlus]], [[sinon-and-the-wooden-horse]]). On the doubts about 4.285–289 see the translation's note on 4.285–289.
