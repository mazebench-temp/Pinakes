---
summary: Menelaus, hearing of his brother's death, had no wish “to live and see the light of the sun” (4.540); and Penelope asks the phantom whether Odysseus “still lives somewhere and looks on the light of the sun” (4.833).
grc: ["ὁρᾶν φάος ἠελίοιο", "ὁρᾷ φάος ἠελίοιο"]
en: [see the light of the sun, looks on the light of the sun]
---

To see the light is to be alive: ζώειν καὶ ὁρᾶν φάος ἠελίοιο (4.540) and ζώει καὶ ὁρᾷ φάος ἠελίοιο (4.833). The phantom will not tell Penelope whether he is alive or dead (4.836–837; [[eidolon]]).
