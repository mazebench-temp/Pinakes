summary: Telemachus and Peisistratus marvel at the gleam of Menelaus' house (4.43–46). In Ithaca the suitors are amazed to learn that Telemachus has really sailed (4.638), and Noemon marvels that he saw Mentor at home after the ship had gone (4.655–656).
grc: [ἐθάμβεον, θαυμάζω]
en: [marvel]
---

**At Sparta.** The strangers are led into the divine house, “And when they saw it, / they marveled throughout the house of the king whom Zeus had nurtured; / for there was a gleam as of the sun or of the moon” (4.43–45; see [[house]] and [[sebas]]).

**In Ithaca.** Noemon asks Antinous when Telemachus will be back with his ship, “and they were amazed in their hearts; for they did not think / that he had gone to Neleian Pylos” (4.638–639; see [[the-suitors]] and [[noemon-son-of-phronius]]). Noemon has his own wonder: he saw Mentor, or a god in his likeness, go on board as the crew's leader, “But at this I marvel: I saw brilliant Mentor here / yesterday at daybreak” (4.653–656; see [[mentor]] and [[truth-and-deception]]).
