---
summary: The gods are “the immortals, who hold the wide sky” twice in Menelaus' tale, in his own words to Eidothea and in Proteus' command (4.378, 4.479).
---

οὐρανὸν εὐρὺν ἔχουσιν closes the verse of the gods whom Menelaus has offended and must appease (4.377–380, 4.478–480; [[immortals]], [[hecatomb]]).
