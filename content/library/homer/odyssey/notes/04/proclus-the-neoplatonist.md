---
summary: Proclus answers the Republic's ban on lies about Proteus with an allegory of this book. Proteus is an angelic intellect in the lot of Poseidon, full of the forms of all things; Eidothea is a daemonic soul joined to him; the seals are rational souls, counted because they are eternal; and his changes are the shifting thoughts of the souls that look at him.
