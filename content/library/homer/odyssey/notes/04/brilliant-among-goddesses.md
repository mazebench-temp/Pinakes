---
summary: Eidothea answers Menelaus twice in the same verse: “So I spoke, and at once she answered, brilliant among goddesses” (4.382, 4.398).
---

The verse of a goddess's answer in Odysseus' tales, used again of Circe (10.487, 10.503, 12.115), is here the sea goddess who teaches Menelaus how to catch her father (4.382–424; [[eidothea]], [[proteus]]).
