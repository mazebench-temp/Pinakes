# "δοιὰ τάλαντα" (4.526), the lookout's wages, first claimed here; χρυσοῖο τάλαντα (4.129) is the entry's.
grc: ["δοιὰ τάλαντα"]
---

**Ten talents.** Polybus gave Menelaus “two silver bathtubs / and two tripods, and ten talents of gold” (4.128–129; see [[polybus-of-thebes]] and [[tripod]]), the sum Agamemnon offered Achilles among his gifts (*Iliad* 9.122; see [[the-gifts-of-agamemnon]]).

**Two talents.** Aegisthus posted a lookout to watch for Agamemnon's landing, “and promised him as wages / two talents of gold; and he had kept watch for a year” (4.525–526; see [[the-murder-of-agamemnon]], [[spies-and-scouts]] and [[misthos]]). Two talents of gold were the fourth prize in Patroclus' chariot race (*Iliad* 23.269).
