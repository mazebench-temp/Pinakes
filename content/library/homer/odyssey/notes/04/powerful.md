---
summary: Menelaus' attendant is “powerful Eteoneus” (4.22), a title that Merry takes to imply that he was of gentle birth.
---

κρείων, the title of Agamemnon, is given to the man who keeps Menelaus' door, “the ready attendant of glorious Menelaus” (4.22–23; [[ready-attendants]]). Merry takes it to show that Eteoneus was of gentle birth, and notes that he appears again in Book 15 as living near Menelaus (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 4.22; 15.95–96). See [[eteoneus]].
