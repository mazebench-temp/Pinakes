# Unnamed: "whom Nestor and I left behind when we went from Troy" (4.488).
refs: [4.488]
---

“Did all the Achaeans come home unharmed with their ships, / whom Nestor and I left behind when we went from Troy” (4.487–488; see [[the-returns-of-the-achaeans]]). Nestor told how the fleet divided after the quarrel of the sons of Atreus: half the army sailed, as Menelaus urged, and half stayed with Agamemnon to appease Athena; at Tenedos Odysseus turned back to Agamemnon, and Menelaus, coming late, overtook Nestor and Diomedes at Lesbos (3.141–169; see [[agamemnon]] and [[menelaus]]). Those whom Menelaus left behind are the men of the answer that follows, his brother, murdered on his return, and Odysseus, still held on an island (4.512–560). He does not speak of the quarrel itself.
