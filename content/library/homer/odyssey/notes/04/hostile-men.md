summary: Odysseus slips into Troy, “the wide-streeted city of hostile men” (4.246); Telemachus' house “is full of hostile men” (4.319); and Penelope fears that “many enemies are plotting against him” (4.822).
grc: ["ἀνδρῶν δυσμενέων", "δυσμενέων δʼ ἀνδρῶν", δυσμενέες]
en: [enemies]
---

The same phrase names the enemy city that Odysseus entered as a beggar and the suitors in his own house (4.244–246, 4.318–321; [[wide-streeted]], [[the-suitors]]). Penelope's δυσμενέες are the men lying in wait for her son (4.820–823).
