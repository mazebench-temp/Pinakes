---
summary: Menelaus goes out of his chamber at dawn “like a god to look upon” (4.310), in the verse with which Telemachus went out to the assembly (2.5).
---

The verse closes the dressing scene at the start of the day in Sparta, as it did the first morning in Ithaca (4.306–310 = 2.1–5, with Menelaus in place of the son of Odysseus; [[dressing-scene]], [[menelaus]]). Merry on 2.5 takes ἄντην as “in presence”, literally “if looked at in the face” (see the translation's note on 2.5).
