---
summary: Eidothea asks why Menelaus can find “no end” to his stay on Pharos, and he gives Proteus the same words (4.373–374, 4.466–467).
---

τέκμωρ here is the end that one finds, as when Apollo tells Athena that the armies will fight again “until they find the end / of Ilios” (*Iliad* 7.30–31). Eidothea asks Menelaus why he lets himself be held on the island and can find οὐδέ τι τέκμωρ, no end to it, while his companions' hearts dwindle (4.373–374); he answers Proteus in the same words (4.466–467), and the old man gives him the end: the hecatombs at the river Aegyptus (4.472–480; see [[reciprocity-with-the-gods]]).
