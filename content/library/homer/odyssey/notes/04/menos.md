# μενεήναμεν "we were eager" (4.282), the verb of μένος, as the Iliad note claims it.
grc: ["μένεʼ ἀνδρῶν", μενεήναμεν]
en: [strength]
---

Twenty days becalmed, Menelaus' men would have lost their μένεα with their provisions, “had not one of the gods pitied me and saved me” (4.360–365; see [[eidothea]] and [[fasting]]). In Ithaca μένος is anger: Antinous speaks “in distress; and his heart, dark on every side, was filled / with mighty fury, and his eyes were like blazing fire” (4.661–662, the verses of Agamemnon in *Iliad* 1.103–104; see [[achos]], [[eyes-like-fire]] and [[antinous]]).
