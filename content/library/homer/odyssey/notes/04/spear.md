# ἔγχεσιν (4.211) and αἰγανέῃσιν, the javelin (4.626), first met here in the Odyssey.
grc: [ἔγχεσιν, αἰγανέῃσιν]
---

Menelaus praises Nestor's good fortune: Zeus gave him to grow old in ease in his halls, “and his sons in turn to be men of good sense and the best with the spear” (4.210–211; see [[nestor]] and [[easily-known-is-the-offspring]]). The suitors, idling in Ithaca, amuse themselves “throwing the discus and the javelin” (4.626; see [[discus]] and [[the-suitors]]).
