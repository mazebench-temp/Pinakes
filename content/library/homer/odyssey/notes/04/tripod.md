---
summary: Polybus of Egyptian Thebes gave Menelaus two tripods, with two silver bathtubs and ten talents of gold (4.128–129).
---

“He gave Menelaus two silver bathtubs / and two tripods, and ten talents of gold” (4.128–129; see [[polybus-of-thebes]], [[guest-gifts]] and [[gold-and-silver]]). The verse is close to Agamemnon's list of gifts for Achilles, “seven tripods untouched by fire, and ten talents of gold” (*Iliad* 9.122, 9.264; see [[the-gifts-of-agamemnon]]): tripods and gold are the gifts of kings. W. W. Merry compares Helen's basket on wheels with the tripods on golden wheels that Hephaestus made, so that they might go of themselves to the assembly of the gods and back (*Iliad* 18.373–377; *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 4.131; see [[basket]]).
