πόλιν εὐρυάγυιαν, the Iliad's epithet of the city the Achaeans hoped to take, in Helen's tale of the spy who entered it disguised as a beggar (4.244–250; [[troy]], [[hostile-men]], [[odysseus-enters-troy-as-a-beggar]]).
