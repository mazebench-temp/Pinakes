---
summary: The sky is where the gods live, “the immortals, who hold the wide sky”, in Menelaus' words on Pharos and in Proteus' answer; and at noon the sun “stands astride the middle of the sky”.
---

Menelaus tells Eidothea that he must have sinned against “the immortals, who hold the wide sky” (4.378), and Proteus bids him offer hecatombs “to the immortal gods, who hold the wide sky” (4.479; see [[wide-sky]], [[immortals]] and [[olympians]]). Between the two the sky marks the hour of the old man's coming, “When the sun stands astride the middle of the sky” (4.400; see [[helios]] and [[proteus]]).
