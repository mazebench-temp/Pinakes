grc: ["ἀμείβετο δῖα θεάων"]
en: [answered]

τὴν δʼ ἠμείβετʼ ἔπειτα opens the herald's answer to Penelope's question about her son (4.711; [[medon-the-herald]]) and her own answer in the dream (4.808; [[periphron]]). ἀμείβετο δῖα θεάων is the variant with the goddess's title ([[brilliant-among-goddesses]]).
