---
summary: Eidothea and then her father leave Menelaus in the same verse, “So saying, she plunged beneath the billowing sea” (4.425, 4.570); and Ajax is carried “into the boundless billowing sea” (4.510).
---

The verse of a sea god's departure closes each of the two meetings on the shore of Pharos (4.425, 4.570; [[eidothea]], [[proteus]]); κυμαίνοντα also ends the verse of Ajax's drowning (4.510; [[boundless-sea]]).
