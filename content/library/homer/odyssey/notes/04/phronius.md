---
summary: Phronius is named in Book 4 only in his son's verse, “Noemon, son of Phronius” (4.630, 4.648), as in Book 2.
---

Noemon, who lent Telemachus his ship, comes to Antinous and the suitors to ask when it will come back from Pylos, for he needs it to fetch a mule from Elis (4.630–637; see [[noemon-son-of-phronius]] and the Book 2 note). The verse that names him and his father opens his speech, “And Noemon, son of Phronius, came near them” (4.630), and his answer, “And Noemon, son of Phronius, answered him face to face” (4.648).
