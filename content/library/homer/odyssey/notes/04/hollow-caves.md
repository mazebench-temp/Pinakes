summary: At midday the old man comes out of the sea and “lies down to sleep in the hollow caves” (4.403).

---

ὑπὸ σπέσσι γλαφυροῖσιν, the phrase of the caves of Calypso and of the nymph Thoosa (1.15, 1.73), here of the caves of Pharos where Proteus sleeps among his seals (4.400–406; [[proteus]], [[seals]]).
