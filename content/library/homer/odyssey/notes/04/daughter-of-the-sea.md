---
summary: The seals are “the brood of the lovely daughter of the sea” (4.404).
---

καλῆς ἁλοσύδνης: Merry writes the word with a small initial, as a descriptive epithet of Amphitrite rather than a name, and notes that the Iliad uses it of Thetis (*Iliad* 20.207); he refers νέποδες to the root of Latin *nepos* and renders it “brood” (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 4.404; see the translation's note on 4.404; [[seals]], [[amphitrite]]).
