---
summary: Helen recognized Odysseus in Troy and questioned him, “but by craft he evaded me” (4.251).
grc: [κερδοσύνῃ]
en: [craft]
---

In Helen's tale Odysseus slips into Troy disfigured as a beggar; she alone knows him and questions him, ὁ δὲ κερδοσύνῃ ἀλέεινεν, “but by craft he evaded me”, until she has bathed him and sworn not to betray him (4.244–256; see [[odysseus-enters-troy-as-a-beggar]] and [[truth-and-deception]]). κερδοσύνη is the craft of the κερδαλέος man; in Book 14 Odysseus, set upon by Eumaeus' dogs, sits down κερδοσύνῃ, with the same craft (14.29–31).
