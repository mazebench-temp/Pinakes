---
summary: Eidothea tells Menelaus how to hold her father: “then let your strength and your force be your concern” (4.415).
---

Once Proteus lies asleep among his seals, Menelaus and his three companions are to use κάρτος and βίη, “strength” and “force”, and hold him however he struggles to escape (4.414–416, 4.419; see [[proteus]] and [[a-god-is-hard-for-a-mortal-to-overpower]]); when he speaks in his own shape, they are to stop their force and set him free (4.420–422).
