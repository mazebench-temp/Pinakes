---
summary: Menelaus goes back to the ships “with my godlike companions” (4.571), and Penelope fears for “the offspring of godlike Odysseus” (4.741).
grc: [ἀντιθέοις, ἀντιθέοιο]
---

ἀντιθέοις ἑτάροισιν, a verse-end of the Odyssey, names the three men who lay in ambush with Menelaus among the seals, as they go back to the ships after the prophecy (4.433–434, 4.570–572; [[proteus]]). Penelope's γόνον ἀντιθέοιο ends her plea to Laertes, against the suitors who would destroy the house's line (4.739–741; [[odysseus]], [[telemachus]]).
