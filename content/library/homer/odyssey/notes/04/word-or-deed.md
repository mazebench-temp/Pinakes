summary: Peisistratus: Nestor sent him so that Menelaus might give Telemachus counsel “in some word or some deed” (4.163); and Telemachus adjures Menelaus by any “word or deed” that Odysseus promised him and fulfilled at Troy (4.328–330).
grc: ["ἔπος ἠέ τι ἔργον", "ἔπος ὑποθήσεαι ἠέ τι ἔργον"]
en: [some word or some deed, word or deed]
---

The pair of speech and action in two requests: for counsel, ἤ τι ἔπος ὑποθήσεαι ἠέ τι ἔργον (4.163), and for the memory of Odysseus' promises kept, ἢ ἔπος ἠέ τι ἔργον ὑποστὰς ἐξετέλεσσε (4.329 = 3.99; [[odysseus]], [[epos]]).
