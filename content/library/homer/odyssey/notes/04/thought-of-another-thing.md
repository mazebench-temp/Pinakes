The verse-opening that brought Athena's plans in Book 2 (2.382, 2.393) gives the new turn to both halves of the book: in Sparta the woman's remedy for grief (4.219; [[helen]], [[nepenthe]]), in Ithaca the goddess's comfort for Penelope (4.795; [[gray-eyed]], [[eidolon]]).
