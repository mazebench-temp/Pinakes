---
summary: Proteus: Menelaus ought to have sacrificed before he embarked, “so that very quickly / you might come to your native land, sailing over the wine-dark sea” (4.473–474).
---

ἐπὶ οἴνοπα πόντον is the sea of the voyage home that the gods have held back for want of hecatombs (4.472–480; [[the-wanderings-of-menelaus]], [[sea]]).
