---
summary: At Menelaus' house, as at Odysseus', a handmaid pours the water for the guests' hands from a golden pitcher over a silver basin (4.52–54).
---

“And a handmaid brought water for the hands in a pitcher and poured it, / a fine golden one, over a silver basin, / for them to wash” (4.52–54 = 1.136–138; see [[water-for-the-hands]] and [[hand-washing]]). In a house of bronze, gold, amber, silver and ivory the golden pitcher and silver basin are in their place (4.71–75; see [[gold-and-silver]] and [[house]]). Menelaus himself received two silver bathtubs from Polybus of Thebes (4.128; see [[bathing]]).
