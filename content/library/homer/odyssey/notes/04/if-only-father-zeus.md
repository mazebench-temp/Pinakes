---
summary: Menelaus wishes that Odysseus might face the suitors as he was at Lesbos: “If only, father Zeus and Athena and Apollo” (4.341).
---

The wish joins the old wrestling match, Odysseus' victory over Philomeleides, to the suitors' doom, “they would all be swift-fated” (4.341–346; [[philomeleides]], [[swift-fated]], [[father-zeus]]). Telemachus repeats it to his mother (17.132–137).
