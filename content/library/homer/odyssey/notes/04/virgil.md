---
summary: The fourth book of the Georgics retells the capture of Proteus. The nymph Cyrene sends her son Aristaeus to the seer in the Carpathian sea, who counts his seals at noon like a herdsman, turns into fire, beast and river when he is seized, and at last tells of the anger of Orpheus.
