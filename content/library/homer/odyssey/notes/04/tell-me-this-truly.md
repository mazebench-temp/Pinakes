summary: Menelaus asks Telemachus, “Is it a public matter or your own? Tell me this truly” (4.314); Telemachus ends his plea “and tell me truly” (4.331); and Antinous asks Noemon, “Tell me truly,” and then in the whole verse, “And tell me this truly, so that I may know it well” (4.642, 4.645).
grc: ["νημερτὲς ἐνίσπες", "νημερτές μοι ἔνισπε"]
en: [tell me truly]
---

νημερτὲς ἐνίσπες, “tell me truly”, is the plainer form of the request (4.331 = 3.101), and Menelaus' answer begins with the unerring source of his knowledge, “what the unerring Old Man of the Sea told me” (4.349; [[unerring-old-man-of-the-sea]]). Antinous uses both forms, short and whole, as he questions Noemon about the ship (4.642–647; [[antinous]], [[noemon-son-of-phronius]]).
