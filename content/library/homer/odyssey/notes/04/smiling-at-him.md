---
summary: Menelaus smiles at Telemachus' refusal of his horses: “So he spoke, and Menelaus, good at the war cry, smiled, / and stroked him with his hand” (4.609–610).
grc: [μείδησεν]
en: [smiled]
---

The smile answers a young man's frankness, as Agamemnon's smile answers Odysseus' angry reply in *Iliad* 4 (*Iliad* 4.356–357): Menelaus takes no offense, praises the blood that speaks in him, and changes the gift for the mixing bowl of Phaedimus (4.609–619; [[good-at-the-war-cry]], [[called-him-by-name]], [[mixing-bowl]]).
