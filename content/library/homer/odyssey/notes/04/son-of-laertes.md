---
summary: Proteus names the third of the Achaeans by his father: “It is the son of Laertes, who has his home in Ithaca” (4.555).
grc: ["υἱὸς Λαέρτεω"]
---

The old man does not name Odysseus: he gives only his father and his home, and the island where Calypso holds him (4.555–560; [[odysseus]], [[laertes]], [[calypso]]). Odysseus names himself in nearly the same words to the Cyclops (υἱὸν Λαέρτεω, Ἰθάκῃ ἔνι οἰκίʼ ἔχοντα, 9.505, 9.531).
