summary: The verse of Menelaus' question, “and tell me of my homecoming, how I shall go over the fish-filled sea,” four times between him, Eidothea and Proteus (4.381, 4.390, 4.424, 4.470); and the storm carries Agamemnon “over the fish-filled sea” (4.516).
---

The question and the promise pass back and forth in the same words: Menelaus asks Eidothea (4.379–381), she promises that her father will tell him (4.389–390) and bids him ask (4.423–424), and he asks the old man in the verses he used to her (4.468–470 = 4.379–381; [[nostos]], [[proteus]]). The same sea carries Agamemnon off his course near Malea (4.514–516; [[the-murder-of-agamemnon]]).
