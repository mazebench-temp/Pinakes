The tears of the evening at Sparta and of the tales: Telemachus' at his father's name (4.113–116; [[the-longing-for-lament]]), the honor of the dead (4.197–198; [[honoring-the-dead]]), and the drug that stops them (4.222–226; [[nepenthe]]); and in Proteus' account the tears of joy and of exile (4.521–523, 4.555–558; [[hot-tears]], [[flourishing]], [[tears]]).
