grc: ["νηυσὶ θοῇσι"]

νηυσὶ θοῇσι closes Menelaus' wish for the return he would have shared with Odysseus (4.171–173); νῆα θοήν is the ship of the ambush (4.669, 4.779; [[the-suitors]]).
