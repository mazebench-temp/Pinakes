---
summary: Menelaus' companions, kept on Pharos by the gods, were “always wandering about the island, fishing / with curved hooks, and hunger wore at their bellies” (4.368–369).
---

γναμπτοῖς ἀγκίστροισιν, “with curved hooks” (4.369), is the verse of the starving companions on Thrinacia too (12.332, claimed by the Book 12 note). γναμπτός is the word of the supple limbs of the living (11.394) and of curved things such as these hooks (see the general article and [[fish]]).
