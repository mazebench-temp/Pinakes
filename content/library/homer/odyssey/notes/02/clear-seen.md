---
summary: Halitherses warns that “for many others too there will be evil, / of us who live in clear-seen Ithaca” (2.166–167).
---

The epithet's first use in the poem comes in the old reader of birds' prophecy: the slaughter that Odysseus plants for the suitors will bring evil to many of the islanders as well, and he urges them to stop the suitors first (2.163–169; [[halitherses]], [[the-omen-of-the-eagles]]). On the meaning see the translation's note on 2.167. See [[ithaca]].
