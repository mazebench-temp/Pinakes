---
summary: Halitherses recalls the day the Argives embarked for Ilios, “and among them went Odysseus of many wiles” (2.173).
---

The epithet comes in the old seer's account of his prophecy at the sailing for Troy: that after suffering many evils and losing all his companions Odysseus would come home in the twentieth year, unknown to all (2.171–176; [[the-prophecy-of-halitherses]], [[the-return-of-odysseus]]). πολύμητις Ὀδυσσεύς closes the verse, as in the *Iliad*'s voyage to Chryse (*Iliad* 1.311, 1.440). See [[odysseus]].
