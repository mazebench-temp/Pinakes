---
summary: Telemachus refuses Antinous: “there is no way for me among you overbearing men / to feast in silence and make merry at my ease” (2.310–311).
---

The phrase answers Antinous' invitation to eat and drink with him as before (2.303–305; [[telemachus-and-the-suitors]]). In Book 1 Telemachus seated his guest apart so that he would not be among overbearing men at the feast (1.133–134); now he refuses to sit among them himself. See [[the-suitors]] and [[hybris]].
