---
summary: The periphrasis is first used when Telemachus speaks to his crew on the shore: “And among them spoke also the sacred strength of Telemachus” (2.409).
---

The first words Telemachus speaks as leader of his own crew come in a verse that names him by his strength: “Come here, friends, let us bring the provisions” (2.409–412; [[provisions]], [[sailing-type-scene]]). The verse τοῖσι δὲ καὶ μετέειπε brought in Halitherses in the assembly, “And among them spoke also the old hero Halitherses” (2.157); here the periphrasis fills the place of the name. See [[telemachus]].
