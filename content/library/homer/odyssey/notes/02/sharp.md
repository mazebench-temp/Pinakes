summary: Telemachus “about his shoulder set his sharp sword” as he dresses for the assembly (2.3).
---

εἵματα ἑσσάμενος, περὶ δὲ ξίφος ὀξὺ θέτʼ ὤμῳ is a verse of the Odyssey's dressing scene, said again of Menelaus at Sparta and of Telemachus on the morning of the contest of the bow (2.3, 4.308, 20.125; [[dressing-scene]]). See [[sword]].
