---
summary: Athena, as Mentor, says of the suitors, “nor do they know anything of death and black doom, / which is near them now, that they should all perish in a single day” (2.283–284).
---

κῆρα μέλαιναν closes the verse after θάνατον καί, “death and,” and the doom it names is the suitors' (2.281–284; [[ker-doom]], [[thanatos]]). Halitherses had said the same in the assembly, that Odysseus “plants slaughter and doom for these men” (2.165). See [[the-suitors]].
