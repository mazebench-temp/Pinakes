---
summary: Named again in the patronymic of Eurycleia's lineage, as she is named the keeper of Odysseus' storeroom (2.347).
---

Πεισηνορίδαο, “son of Peisenor” (2.347), ends the verse that names Eurycleia with her father and grandfather, the verse of 1.429, here at the end of the description of the storeroom she keeps (see [[ops]], [[eurycleia]] and [[the-storeroom-of-odysseus]]). The patronymic in -ίδαο counts for the son, Ops, and for the father, Peisenor; nothing else is said of either.
