---
summary: If Telemachus hears that his father is dead, “then I will come home to my dear native land, / and heap up a mound for him” (2.221–222).
---

Telemachus gives the assembly as his own plan, in the first person, the counsel Athena gave him in Book 1 (2.218–223; 1.287–292; [[tomb]], [[the-journey-of-telemachus]]). The formula of every return is here the son's, coming home from his journey to bury a father who has not come home. See [[nostos]].
