---
summary: The suitors caught Penelope “unraveling the splendid web” (2.109).
---

The epithet is given to the web at the moment it is undone, when one of her women has told the suitors (2.108–109; [[the-web-of-penelope]], [[loom]]). ἀγλαός, “bright, splendid,” is the word of fine things, as the entry describes.
