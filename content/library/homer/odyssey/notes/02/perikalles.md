---
summary: Antinous grants that Athena gave Penelope beyond all others “to be skilled in beautiful handiwork, and good wits, / and crafty ways” (2.116–118).
---

The verse ἔργα τʼ ἐπίστασθαι περικαλλέα καὶ φρένας ἐσθλάς is used again of the women of the Phaeacians, to whom Athena gave skill in weaving (7.110–111). Here it is the suitor's praise of the weaver whose trick he has just told (2.93–110; [[the-web-of-penelope]], [[kerdos]]). See [[penelope]].
