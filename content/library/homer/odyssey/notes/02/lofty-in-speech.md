---
summary: Antinous addresses Telemachus as “lofty in speech, uncontrollable in your fury” twice: in the assembly, after the scepter has been flung down (2.85), and in the house, when he invites him to eat and drink as before (2.303).
---

The first address opens Antinous' answer to the speech that ended in tears: “what a thing you have said / to shame us!” (2.85–86; [[what-a-word]]). The second comes with a laugh and a hand clasped in his, as Antinous tells him to let no evil be a care in his breast and promises him a ship from the Achaeans (2.301–308; [[called-him-by-name]], [[egatheos]]). The same address serves anger and mockery. See [[antinous]] and [[telemachus-and-the-suitors]].
