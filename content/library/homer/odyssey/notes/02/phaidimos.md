---
summary: Athena asks “Noemon, the shining son of Phronius,” for a swift ship (2.386).
grc: [φαίδιμον]
---

The verse-end φαίδιμος υἱός names a man by his father, here in the accusative, φαίδιμον υἱόν, as the *Iliad* names the sons of Hippolochus, Lethus, Priam and Pelegon (*Iliad* 6.144, 17.288, 21.97, 21.152). Noemon enters the poem as the man who gladly lends the ship (2.386–387; [[noemon-son-of-phronius]], [[phronius]]).
