---
summary: Eurycleia begs Telemachus to stay: “you have no need / to suffer evils on the barren sea or to wander” (2.369–370).
grc: [ἀτρύγετον]
---

The nurse's fear names the sea by its standing epithet, πόντον ἐπʼ ἀτρύγετον, in the speech in which she says that his father has perished far from his native land (2.363–370; [[eurycleia]]). See [[sea]].
