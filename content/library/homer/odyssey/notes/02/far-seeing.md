---
summary: When Telemachus has spoken, “far-seeing Zeus sent two eagles / flying from on high, from the peak of a mountain” (2.146–147).
---

The epithet names the god at the moment he answers Telemachus' call on the gods who live forever (2.143–147; [[gods-who-live-forever]]). εὐρύοπα Ζεύς closes the verse in its nominative use, and the eagles are his sign ([[the-omen-of-the-eagles]], [[eagle]]). See [[zeus]].
