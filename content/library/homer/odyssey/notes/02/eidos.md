---
summary: Athena comes to Telemachus “in the likeness of Mentor, both in form and in voice” (2.268, 2.401).
---

εἰδομένη is the participle of εἴδομαι, “appear, look like”, the word of the gods' disguises (see the entry); δέμας is the bodily form, αὐδή the voice. She also goes through the city in the likeness of Telemachus himself (Τηλεμάχῳ ἐικυῖα, 2.383). See [[athena]], [[mentor]] and [[seeing-the-gods]].
