---
summary: Eurymachus says that the suitors “strive for the sake of her excellence” (ἐριδαίνομεν, 2.206), waiting every day for Penelope's choice.
grc: [ἐριδαίνομεν]
---

The suitors' rivalry is a contest for a prize (see [[arete]] and [[the-suitors]]). In Book 18 one of them complains that, since the beggar came, they strive over beggars (νῦν δὲ περὶ πτωχῶν ἐριδαίνομεν, 18.400–404).
