---
summary: Telemachus will go “to Sparta and to sandy Pylos” (2.214, 2.359), in the verse of Athena's plan in Book 1 (1.93); and the suitors jeer that he may bring helpers “from sandy Pylos” (2.326).
---

The verse εἶμι γὰρ ἐς Σπάρτην τε καὶ ἐς Πύλον ἠμαθόεντα announces the journey twice, to the assembly and to Eurycleia, in the words with which Athena first planned it in the council of the gods, πέμψω δʼ ἐς Σπάρτην τε καὶ ἐς Πύλον ἠμαθόεντα (2.214, 2.359; 1.93; [[the-journey-of-telemachus]], [[sparta]]). The suitors take up the epithet in their jeer (2.326; [[tis-speech]]), and Antinous calls the same place “sacred Pylos” (2.308; [[egatheos]]). See [[pylos]].
