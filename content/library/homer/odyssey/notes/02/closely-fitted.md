---
summary: The storeroom has “closely fitted doors … that could be locked, / double doors” (2.344–345).
grc: ["πυκινῶς ἀραρυῖαι"]
---

The formula is in the nominative here, σανίδες πυκινῶς ἀραρυῖαι, the leaves of the double doors (δικλίδες) that keep the old wine, the oil and the gold of Odysseus' storeroom (2.337–347; [[the-storeroom-of-odysseus]], [[door]]). ἔπεσαν is from ἔπειμι, “were on it,” not from πίπτω (see the translation's note on 2.344).
