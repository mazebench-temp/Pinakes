---
summary: Eurymachus tells Halitherses that he would not “prophesy and declare so much” if he had perished with Odysseus (2.184), and that the suitors do not “heed the oracle” he pronounces (2.201).
grc: [θεοπροπίης]
---

θεοπροπίη is the word Telemachus used the evening before of the prophets his mother consults, “nor do I heed any oracle” (οὔτε θεοπροπίης ἐμπάζομαι, 1.415); Eurymachus says it of the seer of the assembly (2.201; see [[divination]], [[halitherses]] and [[many-birds-not-all-of-fate]]).
