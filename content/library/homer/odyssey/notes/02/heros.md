summary: The narrator gives the title to two old men of Ithaca, “the hero Aegyptius” (2.15) and “the old hero Halitherses” (2.157), and Penelope's shroud is for “the hero Laertes” (2.99).
grc: [ἥρωι]
---

As in Book 1, where Laertes is “the hero Laertes” (1.189), ἥρως is a title of respect for men who no longer fight (see [[geron]], [[aegyptius]] and [[halitherses]]).
