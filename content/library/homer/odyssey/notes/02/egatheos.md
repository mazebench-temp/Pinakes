summary: Antinous mocks Telemachus' journey: the Achaeans will give him a ship, so that he may come the sooner “to sacred Pylos, in search of news of your noble father” (2.306–308).
grc: [ἠγαθέην]
---

The epithet that Nestor's Pylos has in the *Iliad* (*Iliad* 1.252) is spoken by a suitor, in his promise of “a ship and choice oarsmen” (2.306–308; [[telemachus-and-the-suitors]]). Elsewhere in the book Pylos is “sandy” (2.214, 2.326, 2.359; [[sandy]]). See [[pylos]].
