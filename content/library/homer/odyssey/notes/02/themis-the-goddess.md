---
summary: Telemachus ends his first speech in the assembly by begging the people “by Olympian Zeus and by Themis, / who dissolves the assemblies of men and seats them”. It is the only place in Homer where she presides over the assemblies of men, and the assembly in which she is named is dissolved by a suitor.
passages:
  - 2.68-69 | By Olympian Zeus and by Themis
---

**By Zeus and Themis.** The first assembly held in Ithaca since Odysseus sailed (2.26–27; see [[the-assembly-of-the-ithacans]] and [[assembly]]) has heard Telemachus tell of the evil that has fallen on his house. He bids the people be indignant, feel shame before their neighbors and fear the wrath of the gods (2.64–67; see [[nemesis]], [[aidos]] and [[menis]]), and then begs them:

{{quote:e23a8275-d736-5db9-add2-1997527a1272}}

λίσσομαι takes the genitive of the powers by whom he begs, as Elpenor's shade begs Odysseus by those he left behind at home (τῶν ὄπιθεν … γουνάζομαι, 11.66), where the suppliants of the Iliad say ὑπέρ, “by” (*Iliad* 22.338; see [[supplication]]). He names Zeus by the title of his halls on Olympus, Ζηνὸς Ὀλυμπίου (as 1.27; see [[zeus]] and [[olympian]]), and with him Themis, the order of what is right made a goddess (see [[themis]]). In the Iliad she calls the gods to their own assembly for Zeus (*Iliad* 20.4–6); here she both dissolves the assemblies of men and seats them, the verbs standing in the reverse of their order in time. Θέμιστος, her genitive, is found only here (see the article above).

**The assembly dissolved.** No one answers the appeal but Antinous (2.82–84; see [[antinous]]), and later Mentor rebukes the people for sitting silent while the few suitors devour the house (2.239–241; see [[mentor]] and [[laos]]). In the end it is a suitor who does the goddess' office. Leiocritus bids the people scatter, each to his work, and “So he spoke, and quickly dissolved the assembly” (λῦσεν δʼ ἀγορήν, 2.252–257; see [[leiocritus-son-of-euenor]]), in the verse with which Achilles ends the assembly of reconciliation (*Iliad* 19.276).
