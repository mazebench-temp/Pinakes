en: [noble]

The first of the two evils that have fallen on his house is the loss of his brave father, “who once among you / here was king, and was kind, like a father” (2.46–47; [[kind-as-a-father]]); πατέρʼ ἐσθλόν is the phrase with which he first saw him in his mind among the suitors (1.115). Later in the same speech it is “my brave father Odysseus” (2.71). The goddess' test of the son is his father's quality, μένος ἠύ, with the old adjective of the entry: “if indeed your father's noble might has been instilled in you” (2.271; [[menos]], [[fathers-and-sons]]). See [[odysseus]].
