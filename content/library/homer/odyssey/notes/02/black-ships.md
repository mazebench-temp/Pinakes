---
summary: At sea Telemachus' crew make fast the tackle “throughout the swift black ship” (2.430).
---

The two epithets of the ship come together, θοὴν ἀνὰ νῆα μέλαιναν, as the voyage begins and the crew set up mixing bowls for the libation (2.430–432; [[swift-ships]], [[sailing-type-scene]]). The other μέλαιναν of the book is the suitors' black doom (2.283; [[black-doom]]). See [[ships]].
