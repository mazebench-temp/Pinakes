---
summary: Penelope has been “cheating the heart in the breasts of the Achaeans” (2.90), and “our proud spirit was persuaded” by her web (2.103); Telemachus tells the suitors that “the spirit within me grows” (2.315).
---

The θυμός is where persuasion works and where it is cheated (2.90, 2.103; see [[peitho]] and [[dolos]]), and where a man ponders what is to come (2.116, 2.156). The growth of Telemachus' θυμός is his answer to the suitors who plundered him when he was a child (2.312–315; see [[nepios]]).
