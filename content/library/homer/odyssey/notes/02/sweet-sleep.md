---
summary: Athena goes to the house of Odysseus, “and there she poured sweet sleep upon the suitors” (2.395).
grc: ["γλυκὺν ὕπνον"]
---

The goddess who cast sweet sleep on Penelope's eyelids in Book 1 (1.363–364) pours it on the suitors, so that the cups fall from their hands and they go off to sleep through the city, leaving Telemachus free to go (2.393–398; [[hypnos]], [[sleep]]).
