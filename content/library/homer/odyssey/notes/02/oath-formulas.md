---
summary: Eurycleia “swore a great oath by the gods not to tell” (2.377), and the next verse closes the oath, “But when she had sworn and completed the oath” (2.378), before she draws off the wine.
grc: ["ὄμοσέν τε τελεύτησέν τε τὸν ὅρκον"]
en: [completed the oath]
---

The great oath is sworn here by the gods, θεῶν μέγαν ὅρκον, and the verb is ἀπώμνυ, an oath not to do a thing: she swore not to tell (2.377; see the translation's note on 2.377). The next verse, αὐτὰρ ἐπεί ῥʼ ὄμοσέν τε τελεύτησέν τε τὸν ὅρκον, completes Hera's oath to Sleep in the *Iliad* (*Iliad* 14.280) and Circe's to Odysseus (10.346). See [[oath]] and [[eurycleia]].
