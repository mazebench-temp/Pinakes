---
summary: Athena puts in the borrowed ship “all the tackle that well-benched ships carry” (2.390), and the crew bring the provisions “to the well-benched ship” (2.414).
grc: [ἐύσσελμοι, ἐυσσέλμῳ]
---

The epithet goes with the ship's benches, where the crew sit to row (2.403, 2.419; [[rowing-benches]], [[sailing-type-scene]]). See [[ships]].
