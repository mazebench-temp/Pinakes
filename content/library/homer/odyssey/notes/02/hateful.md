---
summary: Telemachus will not send his mother away, since she “will call down the hateful Erinyes / as she leaves the house” (2.135–136).
grc: [στυγερὰς]
---

The epithet goes with the powers a wronged mother may call on against her son (2.130–137; [[erinyes]], [[mothers-and-sons]]). In Book 1 the same word was the marriage that Penelope can neither refuse nor bring to an end (1.249).
