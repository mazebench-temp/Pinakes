---
summary: Antinous takes Telemachus' complaint as an insult: “what a thing you have said / to shame us! You would like to fasten blame on us” (2.85–86).
en: [shame]
---

αἰσχύνω is to bring disgrace on someone, and μῶμος, “blame”, the reproach that does it. Antinous turns the blame from the suitors to Penelope in his next line (2.87–88; see [[aitios]] and [[nemesis]]).
