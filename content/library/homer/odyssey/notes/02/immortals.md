---
summary: The crew pour libations “to the immortal, everlasting gods, / and most of all to the gray-eyed daughter of Zeus” (2.432–433).
---

Two epithets of the gods' nature fill the verse after the verb, ἀθανάτοισι θεοῖς αἰειγενέτῃσιν ([[gods-who-live-forever]]), and the next verse singles out Athena ([[gray-eyed]], [[daughter-of-zeus]]). See [[libation]].
