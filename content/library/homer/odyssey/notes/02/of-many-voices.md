---
summary: The eagles wheel over “the middle of the assembly of many voices” (2.150).
---

The epithet names the assembly at the moment Zeus' sign comes over it: the two eagles fly side by side, and when they reach the middle of the gathering they wheel, shake their wings and look down on the heads of all (2.146–152; [[the-omen-of-the-eagles]], [[eagle]]). See [[assembly]].
