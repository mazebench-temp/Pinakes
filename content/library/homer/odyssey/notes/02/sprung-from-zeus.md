---
summary: Telemachus speaks of his father as “Odysseus, sprung from Zeus, escaping death and the fates” (2.352), and Eurycleia answers that “Odysseus, sprung from Zeus” has perished “among a strange people” (2.366).
---

The nurse takes the honorific from the son and turns his hope into her fear. He asks for the sweetest wine after the one she keeps “thinking of that ill-fated man, if from somewhere he might come” (2.350–352); she answers that he has perished far from his native land (2.365–366; [[eurycleia]]).
