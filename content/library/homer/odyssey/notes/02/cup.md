---
summary: Athena pours sweet sleep on the suitors in the house of Odysseus, confuses them as they drink, “and made the cups fall from their hands” (2.395–396).
---

“and there she poured sweet sleep upon the suitors, / and confused them as they drank, and made the cups fall from their hands” (2.395–396; see [[athena]], [[hypnos]] and [[the-suitors]]). κύπελλον is the drinking cup, here the suitors' cups at their evening's wine (see [[wine]]); in the first book golden cups were set beside Telemachus and his guest (1.142). Athena clears the house of the suitors before she calls Telemachus out to the ship (2.397–401).
