---
summary: Penelope asked the suitors to wait until she had finished the shroud for Laertes, “so that my threads may not be wasted and perish” (μεταμώνια, 2.98).
en: [wasted]
---

μεταμώνιος, “carried off by the wind”, is here the work that would be lost if it were left unfinished. The verse returns in Penelope's own telling and in Amphimedon's (19.143, 24.133; see [[the-web-of-penelope]]).
