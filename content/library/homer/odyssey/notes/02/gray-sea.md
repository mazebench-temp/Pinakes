---
summary: Telemachus “washed his hands in the gray sea, and prayed to Athena” (2.261).
---

The gesture of the prayer is a washing in the sea itself, πολιῆς ἁλός, when he has gone apart along the shore (2.260–261; [[prayer-type-scene]], [[hand-washing]]). In the *Iliad* Achilles sits apart on the shore of the gray sea to pray to his mother (*Iliad* 1.349–351). See [[sea]].
