---
summary: As Telemachus goes to the assembly, “Athena poured a wondrous grace upon him, / and all the people gazed at him as he came” (2.12–13).
---

The two verses come again when Telemachus goes out through the hall with his spear and his two swift dogs in Book 17 (17.61–64; [[swift-dogs]]). The grace is the goddess' gift, poured over him like a liquid (κατέχευεν), as Athena pours wondrous grace on the head and shoulders of Odysseus before the Phaeacians (8.18–19). The people gaze at the son as he takes his father's seat (2.13–14). See [[athena]] and [[charis]].
