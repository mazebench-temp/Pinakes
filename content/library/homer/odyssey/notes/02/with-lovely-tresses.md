---
summary: Antinous says that Penelope's crafty ways surpass those of all the women of old, “the Achaean women with lovely tresses, / Tyro and Alcmene and well-crowned Mycene” (2.119–120).
grc: [ἐυπλοκαμῖδες]
---

ἐϋπλοκαμίς is a form of the epithet found only in the Odyssey, both times of Achaean women: here the heroines of old, and in Penelope's dream of the geese the women who gather round her as she weeps (2.119, 19.542; [[achaean-women]]). Of the three heroines named only Mycene has an epithet of her own ([[well-crowned]], [[mycene]], [[tyro]], [[alcmene]]).
