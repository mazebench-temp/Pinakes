---
summary: Athena, as Mentor, tells Telemachus that “There are many ships / in sea-girt Ithaca, new and old” (2.292–293), and that she will pick out the best of them.
---

In Book 1 the island was “sea-girt” in the quarrel over the kingship (1.386, 1.395, 1.401). Here the epithet goes with the island's ships, in the goddess' promise to fit out a swift ship for the son and to go with him herself (2.286–295; [[swift-ships]], [[the-journey-of-telemachus]]). See [[ithaca]].
