---
summary: Athena goes on board first and sits down in the stern of the ship, with Telemachus close beside her (2.416–418).
---

“And Telemachus went on board the ship, and Athena went before him / and sat down in the stern of the ship; and close beside her / sat Telemachus” (2.416–418). The goddess, whom Noemon will describe as the crew's leader, Mentor or a god in his likeness (4.653–654; see [[mentor]]), sits in the stern, and Telemachus beside her, as the crew loose the stern-cables and take their benches (2.418–419; see [[stern-cables]] and [[rowing-benches]]). νηὶ δʼ ἐνὶ πρυμνῇ, “in the stern of the ship”, is the Iliad's phrase for the place where Odysseus stows the spoils of Dolon (*Iliad* 10.570). On the voyage home from Pylos the places change: Telemachus sits in the stern and seats Theoclymenus, the seer who has come to him as a suppliant, beside him (15.285–286).
