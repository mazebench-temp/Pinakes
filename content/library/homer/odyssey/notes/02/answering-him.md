summary: The replies of the assembly and the house: Antinous alone, “answering him” (2.84); Telemachus' verse, “And wise Telemachus answered him face to face,” four times (2.129, 2.208, 2.309, 2.371); and the suitors' verses with their fathers' names, “Then Eurymachus, son of Polybus” (2.177) and “Then Leiocritus, son of Euenor, answered him face to face” (2.242).
grc: ["ἀμειβόμενος προσέειπε"]
en: [answering him]
---

After Telemachus' speech “all the others were silent, and no one dared / to answer Telemachus with harsh words; / but Antinous alone, answering him, said” (2.82–84): the frame of reply marks the one man who breaks the silence. The suitors who answer after him are named by their fathers in verses of one shape, τὸν δʼ αὖτʼ Εὐρύμαχος Πολύβου πάϊς ἀντίον ηὔδα (2.177, as 1.399) and τὸν δʼ Εὐηνορίδης Λειώκριτος ἀντίον ηὔδα (2.242; [[eurymachus]], [[leiocritus-son-of-euenor]]). Telemachus has his own verse, as in Book 1, for his answers to Antinous, to Eurymachus, and in the storeroom to Eurycleia (2.129, 2.309; 2.208; 2.371; [[wise]]). See [[then-answered]].
