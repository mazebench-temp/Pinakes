---
summary: Antinous names the heroines of old whose devices did not equal Penelope's: “Tyro and Alcmene and well-crowned Mycene” (2.120).
grc: [ἐυστέφανος]
---

The epithet that the Odyssey gives to Aphrodite (8.267, 8.288, 18.193) is given to the heroine Mycene, not to the city Mycenae ([[mycene]]). Tyro and Alcmene are among the women Odysseus sees in the underworld (11.235, 11.266; [[tyro]], [[alcmene]], [[with-lovely-tresses]]).
