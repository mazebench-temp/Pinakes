---
summary: “He spoke thus and sat down, and among them stood up / Mentor” (2.224–225): the verse brings Mentor to his feet after Telemachus has asked for a ship.
---

The whole-line formula of the Iliad's assembly marks the change of speakers once in this assembly, when Telemachus has asked for a swift ship and twenty companions and sits down (2.212–224; [[mentor]]). The other speakers come in with other verses: Telemachus stands in the middle of the assembly and the herald puts the scepter in his hand (2.36–38), Antinous answers “alone” (2.84; [[answering-him]]), and Halitherses and Mentor are introduced with the verse of good intent (2.160, 2.228; [[with-good-intent]], [[assembly-type-scene]]).
