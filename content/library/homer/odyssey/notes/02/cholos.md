---
summary: Telemachus flings down the scepter “in anger” (χωόμενος, 2.80), and Eurymachus charges Halitherses with setting Telemachus on “in his anger” (κεχολωμένον, 2.185).
---

Eurymachus' threat is against the old man who would rouse a younger man to anger (χαλεπαίνειν, 2.188–189; see [[geron]] and [[halitherses]]).
