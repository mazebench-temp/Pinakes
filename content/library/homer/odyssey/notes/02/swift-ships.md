---
summary: “A swift ship” six times: Telemachus asks the assembly for one (2.212), Athena promises to fit one out (2.287), and at night she gathers the crew at it, asks Noemon for it and drags it down to the sea (2.385, 2.387, 2.389); at sea it is “the swift black ship” (2.430).
---

The swift ship that Telemachus asked of the assembly, and that he says the suitors thought it better he should not have (2.212, 2.319–320), is found by the goddess: she asks Noemon, son of Phronius, for it, “and he gladly promised it to her” (2.386–387; [[noemon-son-of-phronius]], [[sailing-type-scene]]).
