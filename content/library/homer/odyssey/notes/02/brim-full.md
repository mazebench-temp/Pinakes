---
summary: At sea the crew “set up mixing bowls brim-full of wine, / and poured libations to the immortal, everlasting gods” (2.431–432).
---

This is the second of the adjective's two uses in Homer. On Lemnos the bowls brim-full of wine went with the Achaeans' boasting (*Iliad* 8.232); here they serve the libation of a crew that has just put out by night (2.430–433; [[libation]], [[mixing-bowl]], [[sailing-type-scene]]). See [[wine]].
