---
summary: Telemachus is “the dear son of Odysseus” (2.2, 2.35, 2.415), and he calls the people “my friends” (2.70) and his crew “friends” (2.410). Antinous blames “your dear mother” (2.88), and “the dear nurse Eurycleia” calls Telemachus “dear child” (2.361–363).
---

φίλος names the bonds of the household: the dear son, the dear mother (2.88, 2.373), the dear father whose homecoming he seeks (2.360), the dear nurse. As an address it makes the people and the crew his own (2.70, 2.410). See [[telemachus]] and [[eurycleia]].
