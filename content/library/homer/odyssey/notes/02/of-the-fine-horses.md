---
summary: Antiphus had gone with Odysseus “to Ilios of the fine horses in the hollow ships” (2.18).
---

The verse-end Ἴλιον εἰς ἐύπωλον names Troy as the goal of a voyage from which a man did not come back: the Cyclops killed Antiphus on the way home, the last of his suppers (2.19–20; [[antiphus-son-of-aegyptius]], [[polyphemus-the-cyclops]]). In the *Iliad* the same half-verse brings the twin sons of Diocles and the Myrmidon Epeigeus to Troy shortly before their deaths (*Iliad* 5.551, 16.576). See [[troy]] and [[hollow-ships]].
