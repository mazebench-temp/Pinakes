---
summary: “And under his shining feet he bound his fine sandals” (2.4): the sandal verse dresses Telemachus for the assembly.
---

The whole-line formula is the one with which Agamemnon dresses on the morning of the Dream (*Iliad* 2.44), and the Odyssey uses it again of Menelaus at Sparta and of Telemachus on the morning of the contest of the bow (4.309, 20.126; [[dressing-scene]], [[sandals]]). The feet are λιπαροί, “shining,” as an anointed body is.
