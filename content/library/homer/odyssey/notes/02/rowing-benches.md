---
summary: Telemachus' companions loose the stern-cables, go on board and sit down at the rowing benches (2.418–419).
---

With Athena seated in the stern and Telemachus beside her, the crew “loosed the stern-cables, / and went on board themselves, and sat down at the rowing benches” (2.418–419; see [[stern-cables]] and [[stern]]). The verse ἂν δὲ καὶ αὐτοὶ βάντες ἐπὶ κληῖσι καθῖζον is used once more, when Menelaus tells how he and his men put out from Pharos to go back to the river of Egypt; there the next verse has them row (4.577–582). Telemachus' crew are not said to row: Athena sends them a following wind, and they set up the mast and hoist the sails instead (2.420–426; see [[mast]], [[sails]] and [[sailing-type-scene]]).
