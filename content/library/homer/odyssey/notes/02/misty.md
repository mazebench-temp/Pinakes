---
summary: Telemachus reminds the god that he was bidden to go “in a ship over the misty sea” (2.263).
grc: [ἠεροειδέα]
en: [misty]
---

ἐπʼ ἠεροειδέα πόντον is the sea a voyage must cross, here in the prayer for the journey that has not yet begun (2.262–264; [[prayer-type-scene]], [[the-journey-of-telemachus]]). See [[sea]].
