---
summary: The suitors joke that if Telemachus perishes at sea, “So he would increase our toil even more” (2.334): the labor of dividing his possessions.
---

The joke is in the word. πόνος, the labor of war and of the field (see the entry), is for the suitors the trouble of sharing out another man's goods and giving his house to his mother and her new husband (2.335–336; see [[possessions]] and [[the-suitors]]).
