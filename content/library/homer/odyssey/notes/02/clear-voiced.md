---
summary: Telemachus orders “the clear-voiced heralds / to summon the long-haired Achaeans to the assembly” (2.6–7), the only use of the epithet in the Odyssey.
---

The order comes in the three verses with which Agamemnon has the army called to its assembly in *Iliad* 2, with αἶψα, “at once,” for αὐτὰρ ὅ and ἀγορήνδε for ἀγορὴν δέ (2.6–8; *Iliad* 2.50–52; [[heralds]], [[long-haired]]). The summons calls the first assembly of Ithaca since Odysseus sailed (2.26–27; [[assembly-type-scene]], [[the-assembly-of-the-ithacans]]).
