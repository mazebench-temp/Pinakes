---
summary: The savage Cyclops killed Aegyptius' son Antiphus “in his hollow cave, and made ready of him the last of his suppers” (2.19–20).
---

The phrase ἐν σπῆι γλαφυρῷ names the same cave in Odysseus' own story, where the Cyclops shuts in and eats his companions (9.476, 12.209–210; [[polyphemus-the-cyclops]]). Here the narrator gives it in a father's grief, before the old man speaks (2.15–24; [[aegyptius]], [[antiphus-son-of-aegyptius]]).
