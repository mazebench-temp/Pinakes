grc: ["=ὄσσαν"]

If no mortal can tell him of his father, Telemachus may “hear a rumor / from Zeus, which most of all brings report to men” (2.216–217), as Athena counseled him (1.282–283; see [[the-journey-of-telemachus]] and [[kleos]]). The rumor that comes from Zeus is the Voice that in the Iliad blazes through the army, “the messenger of Zeus” (*Iliad* 2.93–94).
