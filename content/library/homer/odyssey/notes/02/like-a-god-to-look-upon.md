---
summary: Telemachus “went out of his chamber, like a god to look upon” (2.5), on his way to the assembly.
---

The phrase closes the dressing scene with the young man's appearance, and a few verses later the narrative gives its cause: “Athena poured a wondrous grace upon him, / and all the people gazed at him as he came” (2.12–13; [[wondrous]], [[dressing-scene]]). See [[telemachus]].
