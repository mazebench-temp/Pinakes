---
summary: A mother sent from her house would call down the Erinyes on her son (2.135).
refs: [2.135]
---

Telemachus will not send his mother away: “my mother will call down the hateful Erinyes / as she leaves the house” (2.135–136), the parent's curse that the powers below fulfill, as Amyntor “called on the hateful Erinyes” against his son (*Iliad* 9.454) and Althaea's curse was heard by “the Erinys who walks in darkness” (*Iliad* 9.571; see [[erinyes]] and [[mothers-and-sons]]).
