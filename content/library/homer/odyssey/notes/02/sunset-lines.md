---
summary: “And the sun set, and all the streets grew dark” (2.388): the Odyssey's sunset verse, as Athena drags the borrowed ship down to the sea.
grc: ["δύσετό τʼ ἠέλιος σκιόωντό τε πᾶσαι ἀγυιαί"]
en: [the sun set, and all the streets grew dark]
---

The verse divides the goddess' work in the town from the launching. By day she has gathered the crew and asked Noemon for the ship; when the streets grow dark she drags it down to the sea and fits it out (2.382–392; [[sailing-type-scene]]). The Odyssey uses it six times more, each time on a journey by land or by sea (3.487, 3.497, 11.12, 15.185, 15.296, 15.471).
