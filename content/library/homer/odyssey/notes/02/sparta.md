---
summary: Telemachus names Sparta first among the goals of his journey (2.214, 2.359), and the young suitors jeer that he may bring helpers “even from Sparta” (2.327).
---

Telemachus' verse keeps the order of Athena's in the council: “For I will go to Sparta and to sandy Pylos” (2.214, and to Eurycleia at 2.359; as 1.93; see [[pylos]]). Her counsel to him had put Pylos first and Sparta second, the house of Menelaus, who came home last of the Achaeans (1.284–286; see [[menelaus]]), and it is in that order that he will travel. The young suitors name the two as places where he might find men to fight for him: “Either he will bring some helpers from sandy Pylos, / or even from Sparta, since he is so terribly eager” (2.326–327). Σπάρτηθεν, “from Sparta”, is found again only when Menelaus brings a bride from Sparta for his son Megapenthes (4.10–11; see [[lacedaemon]]).
