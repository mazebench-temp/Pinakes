---
summary: If Telemachus hears that his father is dead, he will “heap up a mound for him, and give him funeral rites” (2.222), as Athena told him (1.291).
---

Athena's counsel of the first book becomes Telemachus' own words to the assembly: “then I will come home to my dear native land, / and heap up a mound for him, and give him funeral rites, / very many, as many as are fitting, and give my mother to a husband” (2.221–223, as 1.290–292; see [[honoring-the-dead]] and [[the-journey-of-telemachus]]).
