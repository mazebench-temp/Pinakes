---
summary: Telemachus bids the people “fear the wrath of the gods” (ὑποδείσατε, 2.66), and Eurymachus says that the suitors “fear no one”, not even Telemachus for all his many words (2.199–200).
grc: [ὑποδείσατε, δείδιμεν]
---

The suitors' boast answers Telemachus' appeal: the people should fear the gods, and the suitors fear no man (see [[menis]] and [[the-suitors]]). At the end Odysseus tells them that they feared neither the gods nor any indignation of men (22.39–40; see [[nemesis]]).
