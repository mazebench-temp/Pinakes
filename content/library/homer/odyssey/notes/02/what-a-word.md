---
summary: “What a thing you have said”: Antinous to Telemachus (2.85), and Leiocritus to Mentor (2.243), each after the epithets of his address.
grc: ["ποῖον ἔειπες"]
---

The shorter form of the exclamation closes two insulting addresses: “Telemachus, lofty in speech, uncontrollable in your fury, what a thing you have said / to shame us!” (2.85–86; [[lofty-in-speech]]), and “Harsh Mentor, crazed in your mind, what a thing you have said, / urging them to stop us!” (2.243–244; [[mentor]]). See [[antinous]] and [[leiocritus-son-of-euenor]].
