summary: The eagles dart “across the houses and the city of the people” (2.154); Athena, in the likeness of Telemachus, goes “everywhere through the city” to gather a crew (2.383), and the suitors rise to go to sleep “through the city” (2.397).

Telemachus would press the people for repayment “throughout the city” (κατὰ ἄστυ, 2.77; see [[poine]]). See [[ithaca]].
