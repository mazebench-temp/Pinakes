---
summary: Mentor is introduced as “the companion of noble Odysseus” (2.225).
---

Ὀδυσῆος ἀμύμονος … ἑταῖρος names the man to whom Odysseus entrusted his house when he sailed (2.225–227; [[mentor]]). In Book 1 Zeus gave the same genitive to Aegisthus (1.29), and the translation renders it “noble” of both. See [[odysseus]].
