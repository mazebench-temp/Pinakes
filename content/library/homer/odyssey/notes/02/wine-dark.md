---
summary: Athena sends “a fresh West Wind, sounding over the wine-dark sea” (2.421), as Telemachus sets out.
---

The traveler's phrase ἐπὶ οἴνοπα πόντον comes with the wind of the first voyage that the poem narrates (2.420–421; [[zephyrus]], [[sailing-type-scene]]). In Book 1 it belonged to the trading voyage that the goddess invented for Mentes (1.183). See [[sea]].
