The narrator names the goddess by her father at the moment Telemachus leaves, “once he had heard the voice of the goddess” (2.296–297), and the crew single her out among the gods to whom they pour (2.432–433; [[gray-eyed]], [[libation]]). See [[athena]] and [[zeus]].
