summary: “And Athena poured a wondrous grace upon him” (2.12): the grace the goddess sheds on Telemachus as he goes to the assembly. Icarius is to give his daughter to the man “who came pleasing to him” (κεχαρισμένος, 2.54).
grc: [κεχαρισμένος]
---

The grace is what makes the people gaze at him (2.12–13; see [[laos]] and [[athena]]), and the two verses return when he goes to the place of assembly in Book 17 (17.63–64).
