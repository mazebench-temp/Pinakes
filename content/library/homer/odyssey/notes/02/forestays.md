---
summary: As the ship puts out from Ithaca, the crew make the mast fast with forestays (2.425).
---

κατὰ δὲ προτόνοισιν ἔδησαν, “and made it fast with forestays” (2.425; see [[mast]] and [[mast-box]]): the ropes that let the mast down into its crutch when the ship came in to Chryse (*Iliad* 1.434; see [[mast-crutch]]) hold it upright as the ship goes out. The verse returns when Telemachus sails from Pylos (15.290).
