---
summary: The sun is named twice in passing, the birds that go “under the rays of the sun” (2.181) and the sunset before the voyage (2.388).
---

“Many are the birds that go to and fro under the rays of the sun” (2.181), Eurymachus says to mock Halitherses (see [[many-birds-not-all-of-fate]]); and “the sun set, and all the streets grew dark” (2.388; see [[sunset-lines]]) when Athena launches the ship. In neither line is the sun the god himself.
