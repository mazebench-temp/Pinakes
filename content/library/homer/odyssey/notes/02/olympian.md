---
summary: Telemachus begs the Ithacans “by Olympian Zeus and by Themis, / who dissolves the assemblies of men and seats them” (2.68–69).
---

The title of Zeus stands beside the goddess of the assembly in the appeal with which Telemachus ends his speech (2.68–69; [[themis-the-goddess]], [[supplication]]). See [[zeus]] and [[olympians]].
