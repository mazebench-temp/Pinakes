---
summary: The suitors' feasting is the grievance of the assembly: Telemachus bids them provide other feasts (2.139), Leiocritus finds it hard to fight over a feast (2.245), and Telemachus will not feast in silence among them (2.311).
---

“leave my halls, and provide other feasts” (2.139, as 1.374), Telemachus tells the suitors; Leiocritus answers that “It is a hard thing / to fight over a feast with men who are more numerous, at that” (2.244–245); and when Antinous invites him to eat and drink as before, Telemachus will not “feast in silence and make merry at my ease” (2.311), while the suitors “were making ready a feast throughout the house” (2.322; see [[feast-type-scene]] and [[telemachus-and-the-suitors]]).
