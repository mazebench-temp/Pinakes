---
summary: Penelope's shroud is for Laertes, against the time when “the deadly fate of death that lays men low takes him down” (2.99–100).
grc: [ὀλοὴ]
---

μοῖρʼ ὀλοή, “the deadly fate,” is the subject of a verse that the Odyssey uses three times, each time in the story of the web (2.100, 19.145, 24.135; [[that-lays-men-low]], [[moira]], [[the-web-of-penelope]]).
