# χηλοῖσιν (2.339), the first form of the word in the Odyssey.
grc: [χηλοῖσιν]
en: [chests]
---

“where gold and bronze lay heaped, / and clothing in chests, and fragrant oil in plenty” (2.338–339; see [[the-storeroom-of-odysseus]] and [[olive]]). The chests keep the clothes of the house with its other wealth, as Priam's chests, opened in his fragrant chamber, give up the robes, cloaks and mantles of Hector's ransom (*Iliad* 24.228–231; see [[the-ransom-of-hector]]), and as chests of fragrant clothing stand in the storeroom where Penelope finds the bow (21.51–52).
