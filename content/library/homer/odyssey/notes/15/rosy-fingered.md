---
summary: “And when early-born Dawn appeared, the rosy-fingered” (15.189), the morning at Pherae on the drive back.
---

The only rosy-fingered dawn of the book: the others come with other epithets, “Dawn of the golden throne” after Peisistratus' speech (15.56) and “Dawn of the fair throne” at the landing (15.495; see [[golden-throned]] and [[of-the-fair-throne]]). The verse and its morning at Pherae, the same as on the drive out (3.491), are told under [[early-born]] and [[chariot-journey]].
