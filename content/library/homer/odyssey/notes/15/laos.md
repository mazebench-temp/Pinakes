---
summary: The people of a leader, in the formulas of address: Menelaus, “leader of the people” (15.64, 15.87, 15.167), and Nestor, “shepherd of the people” (15.151).
---

At Sparta the word lives in the titles of kings. Telemachus twice and Peisistratus once address Menelaus as “Menelaus, son of Atreus, nurtured by Zeus, leader of the people” (ὄρχαμε λαῶν, 15.64, 15.87; “Consider now, Menelaus, nurtured by Zeus, leader of the people”, 15.167; see [[zeus-nurtured]] and [[menelaus]]), and Menelaus sends his farewell to “Nestor, shepherd of the people” (Νέστορι ποιμένι λαῶν, 15.151; see [[shepherd-of-the-people]] and [[nestor]]). The titles of the war are given to kings at peace in their own houses: the war in which Nestor was “kind to me like a father” is over (15.152–153; see [[kind-as-a-father]]).
