---
summary: Peisistratus leaves Telemachus at the ship and drives home alone: “So he spoke, and drove the fine-maned horses / back to the city of the Pylians, and quickly reached the house” (15.215–216).
---

καλλίτριχας ἵππους, the horses that Nestor's sons yoked for the drive to Sparta (3.475; see the Book 3 note), end the journey of the two young men. Peisistratus bids Telemachus go aboard before he himself reaches home and tells the old man, who would come down to the ship to keep him (15.209–214; see [[peisistratus]], [[nestor]] and [[chariot-journey]]).
