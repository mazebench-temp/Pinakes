---
summary: The line of Melampus: “Antiphates begot great-hearted Oicles, / and Oicles Amphiaraus, who rouses armies” (15.243–244).
---

The Iliad's epithet of the gods of war, given here to a man: the seer who went with the Seven against Thebes, whom Zeus and Apollo loved, and who “perished at Thebes, for the sake of gifts to a woman” (15.244–247; see the general article, [[amphiaraus]], [[the-seven-against-thebes]] and [[theoclymenus]]).
