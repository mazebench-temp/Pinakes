---
summary: Amphiaraus, “whom aegis-bearing Zeus and Apollo loved with all their hearts / with every kind of love; but he did not reach the threshold of old age” (15.245–246).
---

The nominative αἰγίοχος, new to the entry in the Odyssey, is rare in Homer: the verse has the shape of Agamemnon's promise to Teucer, “if aegis-bearing Zeus and Athena grant me” (*Iliad* 8.287), with Apollo, who makes the seers of this line, in Athena's place (15.252; see [[amphiaraus]], [[apollo]] and [[zeus]]).
