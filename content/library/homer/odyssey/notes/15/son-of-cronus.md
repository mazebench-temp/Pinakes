---
summary: “but when Zeus, son of Cronus, added the seventh day” (15.477), the day on which the nurse dies at sea.
---

The verse of the seventh day on Thrinacia (12.399), after a verse of the voyage to the Laestrygonians, “For six days we sailed on, night and day alike” (15.476 = 10.80). Six days and a seventh bring the turn of a story: the departure from Thrinacia and the storm (12.397–419), the sailing from Crete in the false tale (14.249–252), and here the nurse's death (15.478–481; see [[the-kidnapping-of-eumaeus]], [[the-phoenician-nurse]] and [[zeus]]).
