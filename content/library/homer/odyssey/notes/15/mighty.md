---
summary: Eumaeus: “because she herself reared me together with Ctimene of the long robes, / her strong daughter, whom she bore the youngest of her children” (15.363–364).
---

θυγατέρʼ ἰφθίμῃ is the phrase of the Laestrygonian king's daughter at the spring, “the strong daughter of Antiphates the Laestrygonian” (10.106; see the Book 10 note); of a woman ἴφθιμος is “strong,” as of wives of rank. Here it is Ctimene, the youngest child of Laertes and Anticleia, reared with the swineherd and married into Same (15.363–367; see [[ctimene]] and [[long-robed]]). The “two mighty sons” of Melampus are κραταιώ, another word (15.242; see [[krateros]]).
