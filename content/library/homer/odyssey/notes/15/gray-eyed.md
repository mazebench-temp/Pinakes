---
summary: “Standing near him, gray-eyed Athena spoke to him” (15.9), at Sparta; and at Pylos, “And gray-eyed Athena sent them a following wind” (15.292).
---

15.9 is the verse in which Athena comes to Odysseus by the ships in *Iliad* 2 (*Iliad* 2.172); here she stands by Telemachus as he lies awake (15.7–9; see [[athena]] and [[divine-epiphany]]). 15.292 is the wind of the outward voyage, given in the same verse (2.420; see the Book 2 note and [[sailing-type-scene]]); the immortal who, she said, would send it is herself (15.34–35; see [[immortals]]).
