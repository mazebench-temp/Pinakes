ἀκαχμένον ὀξέϊ χαλκῷ, the verse-end of Athena's spear in Book 1 (1.99) and of Nestor's and Teucer's in the Iliad (*Iliad* 10.135, 14.12, 15.482), as Telemachus sets out for the hut (see [[strong-spear]] and [[dressing-scene]]).
