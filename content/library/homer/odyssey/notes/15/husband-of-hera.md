---
summary: Menelaus' farewell: “may Zeus bring it to pass for you, the loud-thundering husband of Hera” (15.112); and Telemachus' answer to Helen's reading of the omen: “So may Zeus now grant it, the loud-thundering husband of Hera; / then even there I would pray to you as to a god” (15.180–181).
---

The formula closes two wishes for the homecoming, the host's for the guest and the guest's for the prophecy (see the general article). 15.180–181 are Odysseus' farewell to Nausicaa without the verse between them (8.465–467; see the Odyssey translation's formulas and the Book 8 note): there the girl who saved him, here the woman who has read the sign is to be honored as a god (see [[helen]] and [[loud-thundering]]).
