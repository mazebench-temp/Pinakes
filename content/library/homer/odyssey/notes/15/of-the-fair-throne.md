---
summary: “and they slept, not for long, but a little while; / for soon came Dawn of the fair throne” (15.494–495).
---

Ἠὼς ἦλθεν ἐΰθρονος, the half-verse of the dawn that woke Nausicaa (6.48), here with αἶψα γάρ: it cuts short the sleep of the swineherd and his guest, who have talked through the night, and it brings Telemachus' ship to the shore of Ithaca (15.390–497; see [[dawn-line]] and [[eos]]).
