---
summary: “But when they had put away the desire for drink and food” three times, one for each place of the book: at Sparta before the parting (15.143), in the hut before Odysseus tests the swineherd (15.303), and on the shore of Ithaca before Telemachus gives his orders (15.501).
---

In the hut the verse is followed, as the night before, by the verse of the test, “Odysseus spoke among them, testing the swineherd” (15.303–304 = 14.454, 14.459; see [[peira]]). On the shore it comes after the verses of the Iliad's landing at Chryse (15.498–499 = *Iliad* 1.436–437; see [[sailing-type-scene]] and [[feast-type-scene]]).
