---
summary: In Eumaeus' story, on the seventh day at sea, “then Artemis of the showering arrows struck the woman, / and she fell into the bilge with a splash, like a sea bird” (15.478–479).
---

βάλʼ Ἄρτεμις ἰοχέαιρα, the half-verse of the death of Andromache's mother, struck by the goddess in her father's halls (*Iliad* 6.428), and in Homer only these two times: the sudden death that Artemis brings to women (11.172–173), here to the nurse who carried off the king's son (see [[artemis]], [[like-a-sea-bird]], [[the-kidnapping-of-eumaeus]] and [[the-phoenician-nurse]]).
