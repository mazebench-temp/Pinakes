# Κλυτίδη (15.540) is claimed by the entry, and by peiraeus-son-of-clytius; the alias "Clytius" takes the English.
---

The father of the companion whom Telemachus trusts most, named once, in the vocative of address, “Peiraeus, son of Clytius, you in other things most of all / obey me, of my companions who followed me to Pylos” (15.540–541; see [[peiraeus-son-of-clytius]], [[trusted-companion]] and [[telemachus]]). To name a man by his father is a courtesy: Agamemnon bids Menelaus wake the chiefs “naming each man by his father and his lineage, / and giving glory to them all” (*Iliad* 10.68–69). His house will receive the gifts of Menelaus in the next book (16.327).
