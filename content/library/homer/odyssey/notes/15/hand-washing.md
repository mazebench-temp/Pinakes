---
summary: At Sparta water is poured over the guests' hands before the meal of departure, in the verses of the first book (15.135–137 = 1.136–138).
---

The meal that Menelaus orders before the road is served in the Odyssey's verses for a guest's meal: “And a handmaid brought water for the hands in a pitcher and poured it, / a fine golden one, over a silver basin, / for them to wash; and she drew up a polished table beside them” (15.135–137 = 1.136–138, 4.52–54; see [[water-for-the-hands]], [[basin-and-pitcher]] and [[feast]]). The verses come on the morning of departure, after the gifts and before the parting cup, which Menelaus brings when his guests are already in the chariot (15.147–150; see [[libation]]).
