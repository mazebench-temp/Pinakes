περίφρονι Πηνελοπείῃ, the dative of her verse-end, after ἀγγελίην: two messages for the queen, the son's safe return and the beggar's news of her husband (15.307–316; see [[penelope]] and [[peira]]). The first is carried in the next book twice, by the swineherd at Telemachus' bidding and by a herald from the ship, in Athena's verse (16.130–131, 16.328–329). Eumaeus has said that he goes to the city only when the queen sends for him with news (14.372–374).
