summary: In Eumaeus' story one of the Phoenicians lay with the nurse “by the hollow ship” (15.420); the traders “got much substance by trading for their hollow ship” (15.456), “But when the hollow ship was laden for their going” (15.457), the man with the necklace “went off to the hollow ship” (15.464).
---

κοίλη νηῦς, the nominative (15.457), is new to the entry. The traders' hollow ship frames their year on the island and the plot with the nurse (15.415–475; see [[phoenicians]], [[trade]], [[the-kidnapping-of-eumaeus]] and [[the-phoenician-nurse]]). The entry excepts 15.289, where κοίλης is the hollow mast-box (see [[mast-box]]).
