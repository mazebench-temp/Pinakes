summary: Eumaeus bids the stranger “listen now in silence and take your delight” before he tells his own story (15.390–391); in the story the Phoenician nurse binds her lovers to silence, “Silence now, and let none of your companions speak to me” (15.440–444), and the man with the necklace gives her the signal in the queen's hall, “and he nodded to her in silence” (15.463).
# σιγῇ (15.391, 15.440) is the Book 4 note's, σιωπῇ (15.463) the Book 10 note's; "in silence" and "silence" are the
# entry's English.

**The listener's silence.** “Stranger, since you ask me this and question me, / listen now in silence and take your delight, and drink your wine / as you sit” (15.390–392; the first verse as 1.231; see [[much-sleep-is-a-vexation]] and [[delight-in-remembered-sorrows]]). The silence is a host's courtesy to his own story, and the delight goes with it, as the Phaeacians sat silent, held by a spell, when Odysseus paused in his tale (11.333–334; see [[thelgo]]). This time the master listens, in disguise, to the story of the slave his father bought.

**The conspirators' silence.** In the story the nurse makes silence the first condition of the plot, once the sailors have sworn: “Silence now, and let none of your companions speak to me, / meeting me either in the street / or perhaps at the spring; lest someone go to the house and tell / the old man, and he suspect it and bind me / in a grievous bond, and devise destruction for you” (15.440–444; see [[oath]], [[the-kidnapping-of-eumaeus]] and [[the-phoenician-nurse]]). The street and the spring are where a woman of the house would be met and talked to (see [[the-talk-of-the-people]]).

**The nod.** When the ship is laden, a man of the traders comes to the king's house with a golden necklace; while the queen and her slave women handle it and gaze at it, offering a price, “he nodded to her in silence. / And when he had nodded, he went off to the hollow ship” (15.459–464; see [[necklace]], [[trade]] and [[neuo]]). The signal is given under the eyes of those it betrays, and the nurse leads the child out of the house (15.465).
