---
summary: The young men “drove out from the doorway and the loud-resounding colonnade,” at Sparta (15.146) and at Pherae (15.191).
---

αἰθούσης ἐριδούπου is the verse of the departure from Pherae in Book 3 (3.493; see the Book 3 note), used here of the departure from Menelaus' house as well. Monro takes the πρόθυρον as the gateway of the court (D. B. Monro, *Homer's Odyssey, Books XIII–XXIV*, 1901, on 15.146; see [[house]] and [[chariot-journey]]).
