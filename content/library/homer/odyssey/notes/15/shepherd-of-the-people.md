---
summary: Menelaus' parting words: “Farewell, young men, and give my greeting to Nestor, shepherd of the people; / for truly he was kind to me like a father” (15.151–152).
---

Νέστορι ποιμένι λαῶν, the dative of the chariot race, where Antilochus warns his horses that “there will be no keep for you two with Nestor, shepherd of the people” (*Iliad* 23.411); Telemachus will tell his mother of the visit with the accusative, “We went to Pylos and to Nestor, shepherd of the people” (17.109, a plain rendering). The greeting will reach Nestor through his son alone: Telemachus promises that they will tell him all, but begs Peisistratus to leave him at the ship (15.155–156, 15.195–201; see [[nestor]], [[kind-as-a-father]] and [[sons-of-the-achaeans]]).
