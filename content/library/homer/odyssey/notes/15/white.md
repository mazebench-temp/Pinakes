---
summary: The eagle of the first omen carries “a huge white goose” (15.161); and the crew hoist “the white sails with well-twisted ox-hide ropes” (15.291).
grc: [ἀργήν]
---

ἀργὴν χῆνα: ἀργός, the adjective of the dogs (see [[swift-dogs]]), is “white” here, of a tame goose from the court (see the translation's key words, and [[geese]] and [[the-omen-of-the-eagle-and-the-goose]]). 15.291 is the verse of the launch in Book 2 (2.426), in the voyage home (15.287–292; see [[sailing-type-scene]] and [[sails]]).
