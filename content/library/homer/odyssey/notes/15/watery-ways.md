---
summary: “Then they embarked and sailed over the watery ways, / having taken us two on board; and Zeus sent a fair wind” (15.474–475).
---

The verse of the ship that took Chryseis home (*Iliad* 1.312) and of the suitors setting out for their ambush (4.842), followed by one of the Phoenicians' own: ἀναβησάμενοι, “having taken us two on board,” the nurse and the child (see [[sailing-type-scene]], [[the-kidnapping-of-eumaeus]] and [[the-phoenician-nurse]]).
