---
summary: At Pylos the crew go on board and sit down at the rowing benches (15.221), and on the coast of Ithaca they do so again to take the ship on to the city (15.549), in the Odyssey's verse of boarding.
---

At Pylos, at Telemachus' bidding, “they went on board at once and sat down at the rowing benches” (15.220–221; see [[ships]] and [[telemachus]]), and on Ithaca, at Peiraeus', “they went on board at once and sat down at the rowing benches” (15.549; see [[peiraeus-son-of-clytius]] and [[stern-cables]]): the verse of boarding of Odysseus' ships in the tale of his wanderings (9.103, 9.179, 9.471, 9.563, 11.638, 12.146; see the entry), the first time with αἶψα δʼ ἄρʼ for οἱ δʼ αἶψʼ (15.221). Neither time is it followed by the verse of rowing: the first ship sails with a wind that Athena sends, and the second pushes off for the city (15.292, 15.553; see [[oars]]).
