---
summary: Megapenthes brings “the shining mixing bowl” and sets it before Telemachus, “the silver one” (15.121–123).
---

κρητῆρα φαεινόν at the verse-end, as when the herald Idaeus brings the bowl for the oaths in *Iliad* 3 (*Iliad* 3.247). The bowl promised in Book 4 is given now, “of silver … and its rims are finished with gold, / the work of Hephaestus” (15.115–117 = 4.615–617; see [[mixing-bowl]] and [[guest-gifts]]).
