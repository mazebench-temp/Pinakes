---
summary: “Then godlike Theoclymenus spoke to him in turn” (15.271, 15.508).
---

Θεοκλύμενος θεοειδής is the seer's verse-end, here and in the later books (17.151; 20.350, 20.363), as Τηλέμαχος θεοειδής is the son's (1.113; see the Book 1 note). Both times in this book the verse brings in his answer to Telemachus: at Pylos, the killing he flees and his plea to be taken aboard (15.272–278), and at the landing, his question where he is to go (15.509–511; see [[theoclymenus]] and [[craggy]]).
