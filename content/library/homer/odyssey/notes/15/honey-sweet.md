---
summary: Menelaus at the farewell: “holding wine sweet to the heart in his right hand / in a golden cup, so that they might pour a libation before they went” (15.148–149).
---

15.148–150 are the verses of Hecuba's parting libation in *Iliad* 24, where she brings Priam and his herald wine in a golden cup before they set out for Achilles (*Iliad* 24.283–286; see the Odyssey translation's formulas). Here the host brings it, and pledges the young men as they go (15.150–153; see [[libation]], [[cup]], [[wine]] and [[menelaus]]).
