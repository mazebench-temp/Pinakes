---
summary: Melampus, “who once lived in Pylos, mother of flocks” (15.226).
---

μητέρι μήλων, the dative, is the phrase of Thrace in the Iliad (*Iliad* 11.222); it is new to the entry in the Odyssey, where it is used only here, of the land from which Melampus fled (15.226–228; see the Odyssey translation's key words, μήτηρ μήλων, [[pylos]] and [[melampus]]).
