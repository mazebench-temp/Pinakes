---
summary: On Syrie “Famine never comes upon its people, nor does any / hateful sickness besides fall upon wretched mortals” (15.407–408).
---

πέλεται δειλοῖσι βροτοῖσιν is the verse-end of Priam's plea, “this is the most pitiable thing that comes to wretched mortals” (*Iliad* 22.76), and of the Dog Star that “brings much fever to wretched mortals” (*Iliad* 22.31). In the account of the blessed island the formula of human misery names what its people are spared (see [[hateful]], [[syrie]] and [[nousos]]).
