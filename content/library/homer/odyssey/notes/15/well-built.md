---
summary: Helen's farewell: “And may you reach, rejoicing, / your well-built house and your native land” (15.128–129).
---

The verse of homecoming that Proteus spoke to Menelaus and Nausicaa to Odysseus (4.476, 6.315), here a wish for a son going home with a robe for his bride (15.125–129; see [[robe]] and [[helen]]); Penelope will say it to Odysseus when he has come home (23.259; see [[dear-native-land]]).
