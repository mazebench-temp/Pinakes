---
summary: Syrie: “Famine never comes upon its people, nor does any / hateful sickness besides fall upon wretched mortals” (15.407–408).
---

The hateful sickness is the death that Anticleia's shade said did not take her, the one that “takes the spirit from the limbs with hateful wasting” (11.200–201; see the Book 11 note). On Syrie the gentle arrows take the old instead (15.409–411; see [[gentle-arrows]] and [[syrie]]). ἄλλη νοῦσος is “disease as well,” by the familiar idiom (D. B. Monro, *Homer's Odyssey, Books XIII–XXIV*, 1901, on 15.407; see the translation's note on 15.407).
