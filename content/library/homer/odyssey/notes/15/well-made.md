---
summary: Athena's instructions for the voyage: “But keep your well-made ship far from the islands, / and sail by night as well” (15.33–34).
---

εὐεργέα νῆα, the Odyssey's verse-end for a ship (see the note on Book 8), here the ship that is to carry Telemachus past the ambush; the islands it must keep away from are told under [[the-pointed-islands]] (see also the translation's note on 15.33–34, and [[ambush]], [[ships]] and [[sailing-type-scene]]).
