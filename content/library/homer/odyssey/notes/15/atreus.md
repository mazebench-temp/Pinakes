summary: Named only in the patronymic of Menelaus, “the son of Atreus”, six times at the leave-taking at Sparta (15.52, 15.64, 15.87, 15.102, 15.121, 15.147).
# Ἀτρεΐδ* (in the entry) claims Ἀτρείδης (15.52, printed without the diaeresis), Ἀτρεΐδη (15.64, 15.87) and Ἀτρεΐδης
# (15.102, 15.121, 15.147), all of Menelaus; agamemnon excepts the six lines, since Agamemnon is not in the book.
---

All six are Menelaus: in Peisistratus' words, “the hero, the son of Atreus, Menelaus famed for his spear” (15.52), in Telemachus' address, “Menelaus, son of Atreus, nurtured by Zeus, leader of the people” (15.64 = 15.87), and in the narrative, as he takes the cup among his treasures, puts it in Telemachus' hand, and follows the chariot with the parting cup (15.102, 15.121, 15.147; see [[menelaus]], [[son-of-atreus]] and [[zeus-nurtured]]). The patronymic counts for the father each time.
