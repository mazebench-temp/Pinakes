summary: After the swineherd's account of Laertes and Anticleia, “Answering him, Odysseus of many wiles said” (15.380).
---

The verse of his two answers on the first night in the hut (14.191, 14.390; see [[answering-him]]) brings in the question that draws out the swineherd's own story. From the account of a childhood beside [[ctimene|Ctimene]] the stranger guesses that Eumaeus was taken from home as a child, and asks whether his city was sacked or raiders carried him off alone with the flocks (15.381–388; see [[give-me-an-exact-account]], [[eumaeus]] and [[the-kidnapping-of-eumaeus]]). The verse returns twenty-nine times in Books 16–24.
