---
summary: The book opens: “But Pallas Athena went to Lacedaemon with its wide dancing floors” (15.1).
---

εἰς εὐρύχορον Λακεδαίμονα … ᾤχετʼ is the goddess' own phrase for Telemachus' going, when she told his father where he was (13.414–415; see the note on Book 13): she follows the son in the words she used of him (see [[lacedaemon]], [[pallas]] and [[the-journey-of-telemachus]]).
