---
summary: The “gentle words” a host gives at parting (15.53); Telemachus' request to Peisistratus, “how might you promise and fulfill / a word of mine?” (15.195–196); and the nurse's charge to the Phoenicians, “But keep the word in your minds” (15.445); besides the formulas of speaking and answering (15.45, 15.166, 15.171, 15.434, 15.439, 15.485, 15.502).
---

**Gentle words.** Peisistratus bids Telemachus wait until Menelaus “sends us off, speaking comfortingly with gentle words” (μύθοις ἀγανοῖσι, 15.53; see [[gentle-words]] and [[pompe]]): ancient manners required some such speech from the host, and Monro finds it in Menelaus' offer of a tour through Hellas and Argos (D. B. Monro, *Homer's Odyssey, Books XIII–XXIV*, 1901, on 15.80–85; see [[xenia]]).

**A word to fulfill.** At Pylos Telemachus asks a favor: “Son of Nestor, how might you promise and fulfill / a word of mine?” (πῶς κέν μοι ὑποσχόμενος τελέσειας / μῦθον ἐμόν, 15.195–196; see [[peisistratus]]), and the word is a request: “Do not take me past the ship” (15.199). Peisistratus considers “how he might promise and fulfill it fair and right” (15.203; see [[moira]]) and does what is asked. μῦθος is here what one man asks of another and expects to be done.

**A word kept.** In Eumaeus' story the Phoenician woman, having made the sailors swear, charges them with her plan: “But keep the word in your minds, and hasten the buying of your cargo” (ἀλλʼ ἔχετʼ ἐν φρεσὶ μῦθον, 15.445; see [[phrenes]], [[oath]], [[the-kidnapping-of-eumaeus]] and [[the-phoenician-nurse]]): the word is the secret agreement, to be held and acted on. ὦνον ὁδαίων is “the buying of your freight”, and to hurry on with the price is a slightly colloquial way of saying “make haste with your bargaining” (Monro on 15.445; see [[trade]]).

**Speaking and answering.** The formulas of the book's speeches are built on the word: “and spoke to him” (πρὸς μῦθον ἔειπεν, 15.45), “began to speak” (ἤρχετο μύθων, 15.166, 15.502), “and said” (φάτο μῦθον, 15.171), and “answered” (ἀμείβετο μύθῳ, 15.434, 15.439, 15.485).
