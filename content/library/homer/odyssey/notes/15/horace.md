---
summary: Horace's gold, which breaks through guards and rocks, brought down “the house of the Argive augur”, the Amphiaraus who in this book “perished at Thebes, for the sake of gifts to a woman”. His Telemachus, who leaves Menelaus his horses because Ithaca is no place for them, is the guest whom Menelaus sends home in this book with the bowl he promised instead, after offering him a tour of Hellas and Argos for more gifts.
---

- **The Argive augur's house.** Gold loves to make its way through the midst of guards and to break through rock, more powerful than the thunderbolt's stroke; “the house of the Argive augur fell, sunk in ruin for gain”; the man of Macedon split open the gates of cities and undermined rival kings with gifts (*concidit auguris / Argivi domus ob lucrum / demersa exitio*, *Odes* 3.16.9–15, plain renderings). The poem says that Amphiaraus “perished at Thebes, for the sake of gifts to a woman” (γυναίων εἵνεκα δώρων, 15.247), and his house is the house of Melampus, who came to Argos “ruling over many Argives” (15.240): [[amphiaraus]], [[eriphyle]] and [[melampus]].
- **Gifts declined.** “Not badly did Telemachus, the son of much-enduring Ulysses: ‘Ithaca is not a place fit for horses, since it neither stretches out in level spaces nor is lavish of much grass; son of Atreus, I will leave your gifts to you, as fitter for you’” (*Haud male Telemachus, proles patientis Ulixei*, *Epistles* 1.7.40–43, plain renderings); and so, Horace says of himself, “small things suit the small” (*Parvum parva decent*, *Epistles* 1.7.44). The refusal is in *Odyssey* 4 (4.601–608). In this book Menelaus gives the bowl that he promised in place of the horses, in the verses of the promise (15.113–119 = 4.613–619), and offers to take Telemachus through “Hellas and the heart of Argos”, where each host would give them at least “a tripod of good bronze or a cauldron, / or two mules or a golden goblet”; Telemachus declines that too, “I wish to go back now to my own” (15.80–88): [[guest-gifts]], [[mixing-bowl]], [[menelaus]] and [[telemachus]].
