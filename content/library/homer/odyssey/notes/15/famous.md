---
summary: Eumaeus' story: “And the sun set, and all the streets grew dark; / and we went quickly and came to the famous harbor, / where the swift-sailing ship of the Phoenician men was” (15.471–473).
---

λιμένα κλυτόν, the Laestrygonians' “famous harbor” (10.87; see the Book 10 note), is here the harbor of Syrie, from which the traders sail away with the nurse and the child (15.471–475; see [[harbor]], [[syrie]], [[the-kidnapping-of-eumaeus]] and [[the-phoenician-nurse]]).
