---
summary: Telemachus to the suppliant seer: “I will not drive you from the balanced ship when you wish to come; / but follow me; and there you shall be welcomed with such things as we have” (15.280–281).
---

νηὸς ἐΐσης, the genitive of the ship that brought Telemachus to Pylos (3.10, 3.431; see the Book 3 note), ends his answer to Theoclymenus' plea, “But take me on board the ship, since I have come to you as a suppliant in my flight” (15.277; see [[theoclymenus]] and [[supplication]]). The welcome is promised “there,” in Ithaca; when they land, Telemachus sends him to Peiraeus (15.508–546; see [[peiraeus-son-of-clytius]] and [[xenia]]).
