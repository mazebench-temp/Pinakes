---
summary: On the coast of Ithaca the crew cast out the mooring-stones and make fast the stern-cables, in the verse of the Iliad's landing at Chryse (15.498 = *Iliad* 1.436).
---

“they cast out the mooring-stones and made fast the stern-cables, / and they themselves stepped out where the sea breaks upon the shore” (15.498–499 = *Iliad* 1.436–437; see [[stern-cables]], [[shore]] and [[sailing-type-scene]]). The ship is held at both ends, by the stones off the bow and by the cables from the stern to the land (see the entry). The verse is found only in these two places, and the Odyssey's only other mooring-stones are those that the harbor of the island off the land of the Cyclopes does not need (9.137; see the Book 9 note).
