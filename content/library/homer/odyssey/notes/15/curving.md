---
summary: Telemachus takes the seer's spear: “So he spoke, and took the bronze spear from him, / and laid it on the deck of the curving ship” (15.282–283).
---

νεὸς ἀμφιελίσσης ends the verse as when Odysseus clings to the keel of his wrecked ship (7.252) and comes near his ship on Aeaea (10.156 = 12.368; see the Book 12 note). Telemachus lays the stranger's spear on the deck before he seats him beside himself in the stern (15.282–286; see [[theoclymenus]], [[deck]] and [[spear]]).
