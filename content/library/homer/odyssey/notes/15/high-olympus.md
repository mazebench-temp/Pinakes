---
summary: “So saying, she went away to high Olympus” (15.43), when Athena has given Telemachus her warning.
---

ἀπέβη πρὸς μακρὸν Ὄλυμπον, the verse-end of Hermes' departure from Odysseus on Aeaea and from Priam (10.307; *Iliad* 24.694; see the Book 10 note and [[athena]]). The opening of the verse returns for the Phoenician nurse when she has made her bargain with the traders: “So saying, she went away to the fine house” (15.454; see [[the-phoenician-nurse]] and [[the-kidnapping-of-eumaeus]]).
