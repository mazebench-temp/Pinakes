---
summary: Menelaus remembers Nestor's kindness “while we sons of the Achaeans were fighting in Troy” (15.153).
---

The verse with which Odysseus reminded Athena that she was kind to him at Troy (13.314–315), and the Cretan of the false tale told his nine years of war, “There we sons of the Achaeans fought” (14.240): three speakers in three books count themselves in the first person among the sons of the Achaeans. Here the kindness is Nestor's, “kind to me like a father” (15.152; see [[kind-as-a-father]], [[nestor]] and [[achaeans]]).
