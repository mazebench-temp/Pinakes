---
summary: Menelaus goes for the gifts: “And he himself went down into the fragrant chamber, / not alone: with him went Helen and Megapenthes” (15.99–100).
---

15.99 is the verse in which Priam goes down to choose the ransom for Hector (*Iliad* 24.191), and Hecuba, with αὐτή, the robe for Athena (*Iliad* 6.288; see the general article); the verses that follow take up Hecuba's choice (15.104–108; see [[inlaid]], [[robe]] and [[like-a-star]]). κηώεντα is new to the entry in the Odyssey.
