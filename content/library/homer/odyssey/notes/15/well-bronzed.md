---
summary: Menelaus offers to take Telemachus through Hellas and Argos, where each host “will give us at least one thing to take with us, / either a tripod of good bronze or a cauldron, / or two mules or a golden goblet” (15.83–85).
---

The Iliad gives εὔχαλκος to weapons, a helmet, an axe and a spear; only here is it said of vessels, the tripods that come first among the gifts a host might give (see the general article, [[tripod]], [[cauldron]] and [[guest-gifts]]). Aristarchus obelized 15.78–85, the offer and the two verses before it (see the translation's note on 15.78–85, and [[hellas]]).
