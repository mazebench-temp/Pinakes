# No form of the name in the book. Unnamed: "her father" (15.16).
refs: [15.16]
---

Athena tells Telemachus at Sparta that “already her father and her brothers are bidding her / marry Eurymachus” (15.16–17; see [[penelope]] and [[eurymachus]]). Neither the father nor the brothers are named, and the brothers appear nowhere else in Homer. Earlier the suitors would not go to Icarius' house so that he might settle the bride-price himself (2.52–54), and Penelope later says that her parents urge her to marry (19.158–159). Strabo quotes these verses with 2.52–53 to argue that Icarius and his sons were then living in Acarnania, not in Lacedaemon, where Telemachus would have lodged with them and not with Menelaus (*Geography* 10.2.24; see [[the-icarius-problem]] and [[strabo]]).
