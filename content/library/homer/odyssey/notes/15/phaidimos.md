---
summary: Athena goes to Lacedaemon “to the shining son of great-hearted Odysseus” (15.2).
---

Ὀδυσσῆος μεγαθύμου φαίδιμον υἱόν names Telemachus through his father in the shape of the verse that named Neoptolemus, “the shining son of great-hearted Achilles” (3.189; see [[great-hearted]]). It is the first time the narrator gives him the title that is his in the later books (see the note on Book 14, and [[telemachus]]).
