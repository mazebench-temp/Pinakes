grc: ["βροτῶν ὄχʼ ἄριστον"]
en: [by far the best of mortals]
---

βροτῶν ὄχʼ ἄριστον gives the seer the pre-eminence of the Iliad's half-line in other words (*Iliad* 1.69, 6.76; see the general article). Like Calchas, who guided the ships “by his gift of prophecy, which Phoebus Apollo had granted him” (*Iliad* 1.72), Polypheides has his gift from Apollo, and his son is the seer of this book (15.252–256; see [[polypheides]], [[theoclymenus]] and [[divination]]).
