---
summary: “But Apollo made high-hearted Polypheides a seer, / by far the best of mortals, after Amphiaraus died” (15.252–253).
---

ὑπέρθυμον, the accusative, is new to the entry in the Odyssey. Polypheides, son of [[mantius|Mantius]], is the seer who “moved away to Hyperesia, angry with his father” (15.254), and the father of [[theoclymenus|Theoclymenus]] (15.256; see [[polypheides]], [[hyperesia]] and [[best-of-bird-seers]]).
