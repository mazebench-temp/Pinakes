# Ὀϊκλ* (in the entry) claims Ὀϊκλῆα (15.243) and Ὀϊκλείης (15.244).
---

The middle link of the elder line of Melampus: “Antiphates begot great-hearted Oicles, / and Oicles Amphiaraus, who rouses armies” (15.243–244; see [[antiphates-son-of-melampus]], [[amphiaraus]] and [[melampus]]). The two verses hand the name on, accusative at the end of one and nominative at the beginning of the next, and the line runs on through Amphiaraus to his sons (15.245–248; see [[alcmaeon]] and [[amphilochus]]). μεγάθυμος, “great-hearted”, is the epithet that the same pedigree gives to Neleus (15.229; see [[great-hearted]] and [[neleus]]).
