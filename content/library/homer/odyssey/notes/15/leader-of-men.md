---
summary: In the hut, twice “Then the swineherd, leader of men, spoke to him in turn” (15.351, 15.389); and at Sparta the young men's address to Menelaus ends “leader of the people” (15.64, 15.87, 15.167).
---

The swineherd's title brings in his two long answers to the stranger, on Laertes and Anticleia (15.352–379) and on his own childhood (15.390–484; see [[eumaeus]], the Book 14 note and [[the-kidnapping-of-eumaeus]]). The verse of address at Sparta, which ends in ὄρχαμε λαῶν as in Book 4 (see the Book 4 note), is told under [[zeus-nurtured]].
