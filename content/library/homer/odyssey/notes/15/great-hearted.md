---
summary: Athena goes “to the shining son of great-hearted Odysseus” (15.2); and in the line of Melampus, “great-hearted Neleus, the most noble of living men” (15.229) and “Antiphates begot great-hearted Oicles” (15.243).
---

Ὀδυσσῆος μεγαθύμου is used only here (15.2). The other two belong to the genealogy of Theoclymenus: Neleus, the king whom Melampus fled and then punished, and Oicles, father of Amphiaraus (15.225–244; see [[neleus]], [[oicles]], [[antiphates-son-of-melampus]] and [[theoclymenus]]).
