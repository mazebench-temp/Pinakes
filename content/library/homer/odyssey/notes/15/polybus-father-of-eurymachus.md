summary: Named in his son's patronymic when Telemachus tells the seer to go to “Eurymachus, the splendid son of wise-hearted Polybus” (15.519).
# "Πολύβοιο δαΐφρονος" (15.519) is claimed by the entry; polybus-of-thebes, whose Πολύβοιο would take the line,
# excepts it.
---

Telemachus names the suitor with his father, Εὐρύμαχον, Πολύβοιο δαΐφρονος ἀγλαὸν υἱόν, “Eurymachus, the splendid son of wise-hearted Polybus” (15.519; see [[eurymachus]], [[wise-hearted]] and [[splendid]]), where the poem elsewhere has the shorter Πολύβου πάϊς, “son of Polybus” (1.399 and later). The genitive Πολύβοιο is the same as that of the Polybus of Egyptian Thebes, who gave Menelaus gifts (4.126; see [[polybus-of-thebes]]).
