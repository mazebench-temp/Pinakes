# Βοηθοΐδης (15.95, 15.140) is claimed by the entry, and by eteoneus.
---

Eteoneus is named with his father, Βοηθοΐδης Ἐτεωνεύς, “Eteoneus, son of Boethous”, when he comes to Menelaus at dawn (15.95), and by the patronymic alone at the meal, “beside them the son of Boethous divided the meat and served out the portions” (15.140; see [[eteoneus]]), as Menelaus named him with his father when he rebuked him at the door (4.31). Nothing else is said of the father.
