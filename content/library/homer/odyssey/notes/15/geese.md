**A tame goose.** αἰετὸς ἀργὴν χῆνα φέρων ὀνύχεσσι πέλωρον, / ἥμερον ἐξ αὐλῆς: “an eagle, carrying in its talons a huge white goose, / a tame one from the court” (15.161–162; see [[eagle]] and [[the-omen-of-the-eagle-and-the-goose]]). The verses give the goose three words (see the translation's row ἀργὴ χήν; πέλωρος; ἥμερος). ἀργός is “white” here, where the Iliad's dogs are “swift” and its oxen “sleek”; πέλωρος, “huge”, is the word of the monstrous serpent that the Iliad's eagle carries in the same half-verse (*Iliad* 12.202); and ἥμερος, “tame”, is found only here in Homer. The geese of the Iliad are wild birds of the river meadows, feeding in flocks with cranes and long-necked swans and falling to the eagle and the vulture (*Iliad* 2.459–463, 15.690–692, 17.460; see [[birds-on-the-asian-meadow]] and [[the-eagle-and-the-birds]]). This one comes from the court of Menelaus' house, and its loss is a household's: “they followed, shouting, / men and women” (15.162–163).

**Reared in the house.** Helen's reading begins with the goose: “As this eagle snatched the goose that was being reared in the house, / coming from the mountain, where he was born and bred, / so Odysseus, after much suffering and much wandering, / will come home and take vengeance” (15.174–177; see [[helen]] and [[the-omen-of-the-eagle-and-the-goose]]). ἀτιταλλομένην, “being reared”, is the verb of children brought up in a house, as the Phoenician nurse says in the same book that she is rearing the child of a good man in the halls (15.450; see [[the-phoenician-nurse]]), and of beasts kept at the manger, as Priam reared his horses (*Iliad* 24.280); and it is the verb of Eumaeus' complaint, “I sit here, and rear fat hogs for others / to eat” (14.41–42; see [[boar]]). Helen does not say who the goose is. Her reading ends with the suitors, for whom Odysseus is planting evil (15.178; see [[the-suitors]]), and Penelope's dream says it outright: the eagle that kills her twenty geese in the house tells her, “The geese are the suitors, and I was the eagle” (19.535–550, a plain rendering; see [[penelope]]).
