---
summary: Telemachus, leaving the ship for the swineherd's hut, “took a strong spear, tipped with sharp bronze, / from the deck of the ship” (15.551–552).
---

The verse with which Athena armed to leave Olympus (1.99). Telemachus takes his spear in the same words on the morning of the contest of the bow (20.127), and in Book 17 with the second half of Paris' arming verse, “a strong spear that fitted his grip” (17.4, a plain rendering; *Iliad* 3.338). Whether it is his own spear or the seer's, laid on the deck at Pylos (15.282–283), is told under [[spear]] (see also [[dressing-scene]] and [[deck]]).
