---
summary: Telemachus' verse of reply, “And wise Telemachus answered him face to face,” seven times: to Menelaus (15.86, 15.154), to Helen, “answered her” (15.179), and four times to Theoclymenus (15.265, 15.279, 15.512, 15.535); and once at the landing, “among them wise Telemachus began to speak” (15.502).
---

The book's answers show him as master of his own ship and host to a stranger: he takes the suppliant aboard (15.280–281), will not bring him to his house while the suitors are there (15.513–517), and after the falcon has been read promises him friendship and many gifts (15.536–538; see [[it-will-be-fulfilled]], [[theoclymenus]], [[answering-him]] and [[the-omen-of-the-falcon]]). 15.502 is the verse of his first speech to the suitors (1.367); here it brings in his orders to the crew (15.503–507; see [[telemachus]]).
