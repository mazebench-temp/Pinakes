---
summary: The verse from which Strabo shows that Penelope's father and brothers were living is spoken in this book, by Athena at Sparta, and the book keeps the silence that gave Aristotle's critics their problem. Telemachus wakes, receives his gifts and takes his leave in the house of Menelaus, and no one there speaks of his mother's father.
refs: [15.16-18]
---

**Father and brothers.** Athena warns Telemachus at Sparta: “For already her father and her brothers are bidding her / marry Eurymachus; for he surpasses all / the suitors in his gifts, and has increased his bride-price greatly” (15.16–18; see [[icarius]], [[eurymachus]] and [[bride-price]]). It is the only verse in Homer that gives Penelope brothers. Strabo quotes it, with the suitors' fear of going to the house of Icarius (2.52–53), to show that the poet presents her father and her brothers as living, and argues that they cannot have lived in Lacedaemon, “for Telemachus would not have lodged with Menelaus when he came there” (*Geography* 10.2.24, a plain rendering; see the Book 1 note and [[strabo]]). He gives them a home in Acarnania, where Icarius stayed with a part of the land beyond the Achelous, and a mother for Penelope and her brothers, Polycaste, daughter of Lygaeus (*Geography* 10.2.24). The verse shows a father near enough to press his daughter's marriage, and a suitor who courts the family with gifts (see [[the-game-of-penelope]]).

**The silence at Sparta.** The critics whom Aristotle answers asked why Telemachus did not meet Icarius when he came to Lacedaemon (*Poetics* 1461b4–9; see the entry above). This book ends his stay there with the silence of *Odyssey* 4. He wakes in the porch of Menelaus' house, receives the gifts of Menelaus and Helen, and takes his leave of them; Menelaus sends his greeting to Nestor, who was kind to him at Troy (15.151–153; see [[nestor]]), and of the family of Telemachus' mother no one in Lacedaemon says a word (15.1–181; see [[the-title-of-odyssey-4]]). The only news of Icarius in the book is the goddess's, and it is a reason to hurry home.
