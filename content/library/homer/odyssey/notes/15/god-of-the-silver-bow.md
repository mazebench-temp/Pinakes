---
summary: “Apollo, god of the silver bow, comes with Artemis / and comes upon them with his gentle arrows and kills them” (15.410–411).
---

ἀργυρότοξος Ἀπόλλων, the Odyssey's nominative of Rhexenor's death (7.64; see the Book 7 note), here brings the death of old age on Syrie (15.403–411; see [[gentle-arrows]], [[syrie]] and [[apollo]]). Later in the book the bird of the omen is his, “the swift messenger of Apollo” (15.526; see [[the-omen-of-the-falcon]]).
