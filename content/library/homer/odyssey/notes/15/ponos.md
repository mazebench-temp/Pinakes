---
summary: Telemachus at the stern of his ship at Pylos, “busy with these things”, praying and sacrificing to Athena (15.222).
en: [was busy]
---

When the companions are aboard, “So he was busy with these things, and was praying and sacrificing to Athena / beside the stern of the ship” (ἦ τοι ὁ μὲν τὰ πονεῖτο καὶ εὔχετο, θῦε δʼ Ἀθήνῃ, 15.222–223; see [[euchomai]], [[sacrifice]] and [[athena]]). πονέομαι is to labor at a task, here the preparations for sailing and the rites before it (see [[sailing-type-scene]]), and it is at this moment that the seer comes up to him (15.223–225; see [[theoclymenus]]). The goddess who told Odysseus that she stands by him “in all your toils” (13.301; see the Book 13 note) receives the son's offering for his voyage.
