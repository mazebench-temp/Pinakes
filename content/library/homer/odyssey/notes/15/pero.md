# ^Πηρώ is not in the book. Unnamed: "the daughter of Neleus" (15.233); "for his brother he brought the woman home"
# (15.237-238).
refs: [15.233, 15.237-238]
---

The catalogue of heroines named her, “strong Pero, a marvel to mortals”, whom Neleus would give only to the man who drove off the cattle of Iphiclus (11.287–291; see [[neleus]] and [[iphiclus]]). Book 15 tells the end without her name: Melampus lay bound in the halls of Phylacus “for the sake of the daughter of Neleus and the heavy blind folly / which the goddess, the house-smiting Erinys, had put in his mind” (15.233–234; see [[melampus]], [[ate]] and [[erinyes]]), and when he had driven the cattle to Pylos and taken vengeance on Neleus, “for his brother he brought the woman / home” (15.237–238; see [[bride-price]]). γυναῖκα, “the woman”, is the bride, and the poem names neither her nor the brother.
