Ἑλένη καλλίκομος, its only use in the Odyssey, takes the English of the Iliad's Ἑλένης … ἠϋκόμοιο (*Iliad* 3.329; see the Odyssey translation's key words). καλλικόμοιο, new to the entry in the Odyssey, is the Iliad's word for the concubine of Phoenix's father, where it is “fair-haired” (*Iliad* 9.449).
