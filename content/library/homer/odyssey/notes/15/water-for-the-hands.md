---
summary: The meal before the parting at Sparta opens with the five verses of the guest's meal: “And a handmaid brought water for the hands in a pitcher and poured it, / a fine golden one, over a silver basin” (15.135–139).
---

The verses with which Telemachus served his guest in Book 1 and Menelaus his guests in Book 4 (1.136–140, 4.52–56), now at the end of the visit; after them the carving and the pouring are given to Eteoneus and Megapenthes (15.140–141; see [[feast-type-scene]] and [[basin-and-pitcher]]). 15.139 is omitted in G, P, H, X and other manuscripts (D. B. Monro, *Homer's Odyssey, Books XIII–XXIV*, 1901, critical note on 15.139; see the translation's note on 15.63, 15.74, 15.139).
