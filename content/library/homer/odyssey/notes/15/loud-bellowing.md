---
summary: Melampus at Phylace: “But he escaped his doom and drove the loud-bellowing cattle / to Pylos from Phylace” (15.235–236).
---

The cattle are those that Neleus asked of his daughter's suitors, to be driven from Phylace by force from Iphiclus (11.288–297; see [[melampus-and-the-cattle-of-iphiclus]], [[iphiclus]] and [[pero]]). The epithet, the Iliad's for the bulls of the threshing floor and the oxen of Patroclus' pyre, is used only here in the Odyssey (see the general article, [[cattle]] and [[melampus]]).
