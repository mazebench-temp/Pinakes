---
summary: Telemachus, leaving the ship, “took a strong spear, tipped with sharp bronze” (15.551), the last verse of an arming.
---

εἵλετο δʼ ἄλκιμον ἔγχος closes Paris' arming in the Iliad (*Iliad* 3.338) and Nestor's dressing in the night (*Iliad* 10.131–135); here, as for Athena in Book 1 (1.96–99), it follows the sandals and ends a dressing scene without armor (see [[dressing-scene]] and [[strong-spear]]).
