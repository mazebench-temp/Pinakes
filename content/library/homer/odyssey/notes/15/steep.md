Πύλου αἰπὺ πτολίεθρον, the citadel the young men left behind as they set out for Sparta (3.485). After the whip verse the drive out began with leaving it; the drive back ends with reaching it, in the verse with which Iris and Aphrodite reach Olympus, “And quickly they came to the seat of the gods, steep Olympus” (*Iliad* 5.366–367; see [[chariot-journey]]). That the town lay some way inland, Strabo inferred from Telemachus' plea not to be taken past the ship (15.199; see [[strabo-on-nestors-pylos]] and [[pylos]]).
