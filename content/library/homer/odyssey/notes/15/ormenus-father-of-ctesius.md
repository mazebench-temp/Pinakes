# Ὀρμενίδης (15.414) is claimed by the entry, and by ctesius.
---

The swineherd's grandfather, named once and in passing, in the verse that names the king of [[syrie|Syrie]] with his father and an epithet, as a hero is named (15.414; see [[ctesius]], [[eumaeus]] and [[like-the-immortals]]). It is the swineherd's pedigree, given to a stranger whom he takes for a beggar: Eumaeus, born a king's grandson, was bought by Laertes with his own possessions (15.483; see [[laertes]], [[slavery-and-freedom]] and [[the-kidnapping-of-eumaeus]]).
