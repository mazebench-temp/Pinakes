---
summary: Sailing from Pylos, the crew make the mast fast with forestays, in the verse of the departure from Ithaca (15.290 = 2.425).
---

“they raised and set up, and made it fast with forestays” (15.290 = 2.425; see [[mast]], [[mast-box]] and the Book 2 note). The stays that held the mast upright on the voyage out hold it again on the voyage home, until at dawn off Ithaca the crew take the mast down to bring the ship in (15.496; see [[sails]]). In the storm after Thrinacia the West Wind broke both of them, and the mast fell (12.409–410; see the Book 12 note).
