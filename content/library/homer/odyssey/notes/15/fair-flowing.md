---
summary: The voyage from Pylos: “And they went past Cruni and fair-flowing Chalcis” (15.295).
---

καλλιρέεθρος, new to the entry, is in Homer the epithet of the spring Artacia, where the scouts met the Laestrygonian girl (10.107), and of Chalcis here. The verse is in no manuscript of the Odyssey (see the translation's note on 15.295–300). Strabo quotes it with καλλιρέεθρον in his account of the voyage (*Geography* 8.3.26), and again with Χαλκίδα πετρήεσσαν, “rocky Chalcis,” among the places called Chalcis (*Geography* 10.1.9); the *Hymn to Apollo* has it with neither epithet, βῆ δὲ παρὰ Κρουνοὺς καὶ Χαλκίδα καὶ παρὰ Δύμην, “and he went past Cruni and Chalcis and past Dyme” (*Hymn to Apollo* 425, a plain rendering; see [[chalcis-in-elis]] and [[cruni]]).
