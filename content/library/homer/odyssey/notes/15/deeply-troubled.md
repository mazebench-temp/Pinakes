---
summary: The swineherd answers the stranger's plan to go and serve the suitors: “Then, deeply troubled, you said to him, swineherd Eumaeus” (15.325).
en: [deeply troubled]
---

τὸν δὲ μέγʼ ὀχθήσας προσέφη is the frame of the indignant answers of Zeus, Poseidon, Achilles and Menelaus in the Iliad, and of Menelaus at Sparta (4.30, 4.332); here it is given, in the poet's address, to the swineherd's alarm for his guest: “Surely you long to perish there altogether” (15.326–339; see [[apostrophe]] and [[peira]]). The anger is at the plan, not the man, and the speech ends in the plea to stay until Telemachus comes (15.335–339).
