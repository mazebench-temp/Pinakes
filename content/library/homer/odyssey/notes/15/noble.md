---
summary: Melampus fled his native land “and great-hearted Neleus, the most noble of living men” (15.229).
grc: [ἀγαυότατον]
---

ἀγαυότατον ζωόντων, the superlative, found only here in Homer (see the translation's key words), praises the king who held the seer's goods by force for a year and on whom Melampus took vengeance “for the shameful deed” (15.229–237; see [[neleus]] and [[melampus-and-the-cattle-of-iphiclus]]).
