---
summary: In Eumaeus' story the nurse leads him at nightfall to the harbor, “where the swift-sailing ship of the Phoenician men was” (15.473).
---

ὠκύαλος νηῦς, the verse-end of Odysseus' ship as the Sirens see it (12.182; see the note on Book 12). G and other manuscripts read ὠκύπορος, the entry's other epithet (D. B. Monro, *Homer's Odyssey, Books XIII–XXIV*, 1901, critical note on 15.473, comparing 12.182; see [[phoenicians]] and [[the-kidnapping-of-eumaeus]]).
