---
summary: Peisistratus: “Telemachus, there is no way, eager though we are for our journey, / to drive through the dark night; and soon it will be dawn” (15.49–50).
grc: [δνοφερὴν]
---

νύκτα διὰ δνοφερήν, used only here, stands where the night journeys of the Iliad and of Odysseus' voyage have νύκτα διʼ ὀρφναίην, “through the dark night” (*Iliad* 10.83; 9.143). A few lines earlier the same night is “ambrosial” for Telemachus, who lies awake through it (15.8; see [[ambrosial]]), and the dawn that Peisistratus foretells comes at once (15.56; see [[golden-throned]]).
