summary: “Of these Helen, brilliant among women, took one and carried it” (15.106); the ship runs “past shining Elis, where the Epeians rule” (15.298); “And the two of them in the hut, Odysseus and the brilliant swineherd, / were having supper” (15.301–302); and “Then much-enduring brilliant Odysseus answered him” (15.340).
---

15.106 is Hecuba's verse in *Iliad* 6, “of these Hecuba took one and carried it as a gift to Athena” (*Iliad* 6.293), with Helen and her epithet δῖα γυναικῶν in place of the gift to the goddess (see [[like-a-star]], [[robe]] and [[helen]]). Ἤλιδα δῖαν, which the entry claims from Book 13, ends the half-verse of 13.275 again (see the Book 13 note and [[elis]]); [[shining-sea]] excepts the line. 15.301 opens the night in the hut, and the same verse opens the next book (16.1; see [[eumaeus]]); for 15.340 see [[much-enduring]].
