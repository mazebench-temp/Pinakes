---
summary: Helen gives Telemachus the robe: “So saying, she put it in his hands, and he received it with joy” (15.130).
---

The verse with which Achilles hands over a prize at the games of Patroclus (*Iliad* 23.624, 23.797), here with the feminine participle. To Nestor, too old to compete, Achilles gives a prize “to be a memorial of the funeral of Patroclus” (*Iliad* 23.618–624); Helen's gift is “a remembrance of Helen's hands,” μνῆμʼ Ἑλένης χειρῶν, for the bride of the young man (15.125–128; see [[robe]], [[guest-gifts]] and [[helen]]).
