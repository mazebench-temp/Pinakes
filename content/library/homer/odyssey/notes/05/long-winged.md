---
summary: Around Calypso's cave “long-winged birds would roost, / owls and hawks and long-tongued crows / of the sea” (5.65–67).
grc: [τανυσίπτεροι]
---

τανυσίπτερος, the Odyssey's form of the epithet, is used again of the thrushes or doves caught in a net in the simile of the hanged women (22.468). See [[birds]] and [[hawk]].
