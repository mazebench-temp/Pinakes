---
summary: The South Wind is one of the four winds that clash together in Poseidon's storm (5.295), and when the raft is broken it tosses the wreck to the North Wind to carry (5.331).
---

“The East Wind and the South Wind clashed together” (5.295) at the head of the four winds that Poseidon looses on the raft (see [[poseidon]], [[eurus]], [[zephyrus]] and [[boreas]]). When the mast is gone and the raft is carried over the sea like thistles in the wind, “now the South Wind would toss it to the North Wind to carry” (5.331; see [[thistles-in-the-north-wind]]). The iterative forms προβάλεσκε, “would toss”, and εἴξασκε, “would yield” (5.332), make the tossing go on and on.
