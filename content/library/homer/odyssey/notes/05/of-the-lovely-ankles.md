---
summary: “But the daughter of Cadmus saw him, Ino of the lovely ankles, / Leucothea” (5.333–334).
grc: [καλλίσφυρος]
---

The epithet stands between her father's name and her name as a goddess: Κάδμου θυγάτηρ, καλλίσφυρος Ἰνώ, / Λευκοθέη. She “was once a mortal with human voice,” and the epithet is the one the Iliad gives to mortal women, Marpessa and Danaë (*Iliad* 9.557, 14.319); in the Odyssey it is given once more, to Hebe (11.603).
