---
summary: “There all his other noble companions perished” (5.110, 5.133).
---

The verse and the next are Hermes' account of the wreck, which Calypso repeats in her own account; Merry holds that they cannot stand in Hermes' speech, where they would make the storm Athena's (see the translation's note on 5.105–111; [[hetairos]], [[the-shipwreck-of-odysseus]]).
