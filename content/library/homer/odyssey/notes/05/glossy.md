---
summary: Calypso seats Hermes “on a shining, glossy throne” (5.86).
grc: [σιγαλόεντι]
---

Merry reports two derivations of σιγαλόεις: from σίαλος, “fat,” of a surface glossed with oil, or from a root γαλ-, “shine,” with an intensive prefix (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 5.86). The throne is the seat of honor in the goddess's house, on which Odysseus sits when Hermes has gone (5.195; [[throne]], [[phaeinos]], [[xenia]]).
