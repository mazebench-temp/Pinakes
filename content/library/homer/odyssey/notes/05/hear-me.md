---
summary: Odysseus' prayer to the river: “Hear me, lord, whoever you are; I come to you, much prayed for” (5.445).
grc: ["κλῦθι ἄναξ"]
---

κλῦθι ἄναξ opens Glaucus' prayer in the Iliad, to a god who is “somewhere in the rich land of Lycia / or in Troy” (*Iliad* 16.514–515); Odysseus does not know the god's name at all, ὅτις ἐσσί. The prayer is answered at once: “So he spoke, and at once the god stopped his stream” (5.451; [[prayer-type-scene]], [[the-river-of-scheria]]).
