---
summary: “and with levers he dragged it down into the shining sea” (5.261): the launching of the raft.
---

Merry notes that a lever lifts and pushes rather than drags, though κατερύω had become the word for getting a ship down into the water, and thinks μοχλοί may here be rollers (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 5.261; [[the-building-of-the-raft]]).
