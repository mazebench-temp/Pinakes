---
summary: Odysseus in the storm: “Now sheer destruction is sure for me” (5.305).
grc: ["αἰπὺς ὄλεθρος"]
---

The half-verse is Hector's reproach to Paris in the Iliad, “now sheer destruction is sure for you” (*Iliad* 13.769–773), and the suitors' to the beggar who has shot Antinous (22.28). Merry takes σῶς as “certain,” perhaps with an ironical tone, “safe to be destroyed” (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 5.305).
