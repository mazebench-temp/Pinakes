summary: Poseidon drives to Aegae, “where his glorious house is” (5.381), and Odysseus fears the sea monsters “such as famous Amphitrite breeds in great numbers” and the anger of “the famous shaker of the earth” (5.422–423).
grc: ["κλυτὰ δώματʼ", "κλυτὸς ἐννοσίγαιος", "κλυτὸς Ἀμφιτρίτη"]
en: [glorious]

κλυτὰ δώματα is Poseidon's house at Aegae in the Iliad too (*Iliad* 13.21); here the phrase is elided. In consecutive verses the epithet is given to the two powers of the sea, Amphitrite and Poseidon (5.422–423; [[amphitrite]], [[shaker-of-the-earth]], [[aegae]]).
