---
summary: Poseidon is “the powerful earth-shaker” twice: returning from the Ethiopians he sees Odysseus on his raft (5.282), and he sees him again when the raft is broken (5.375).
---

κρείων ἐνοσίχθων, a ruler's title given to the lord of the sea, as in the Iliad (*Iliad* 13.10, 21.435), introduces each of his two soliloquies (5.285, 5.376; [[shook-his-head]], [[earth-shaker]]). See [[poseidon]].
