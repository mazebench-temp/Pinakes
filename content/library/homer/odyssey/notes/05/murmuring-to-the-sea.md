---
summary: Odysseus lets Ino's veil go “into the river that murmurs to the sea” (5.460).
grc: [ἁλιμυρήεντα]
en: [murmurs to the sea]
---

The epithet of rivers where they meet the sea, used in the Iliad of the rivers that flow from Oceanus (*Iliad* 21.190), is here the river of Scheria, at whose mouth Odysseus has come ashore (5.441–462; [[the-river-of-scheria]], [[veil]]).
