---
summary: Zeus, and Hermes after him: it is Odysseus' fate “to see his friends and to reach / his high-roofed house and his native land” (5.41–42, 5.114–115).
---

The verses follow Proteus' to Menelaus, where the house is “well-built” (4.475–476); Hermes delivers them as the command of Zeus (5.114–115; [[moira]], [[dear-native-land]], [[house]]).
