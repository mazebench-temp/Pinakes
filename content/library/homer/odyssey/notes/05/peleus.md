---
summary: Peleus is named only in his son's patronymic, περὶ Πηλεΐωνι θανόντι, “around the dead son of Peleus” (5.310).
---

Achilles is named by his father, Πηλεΐων, “son of Peleus”, as often in both poems, in Odysseus' memory of the fight over his body (5.310; see [[achilles]] and [[the-fight-over-the-body-of-achilles]]). In the underworld the shade of Achilles asks Odysseus whether he has heard anything of noble Peleus, whether he still has honor among the Myrmidons, or is slighted in Hellas and Phthia because old age holds his hands and feet (11.494–497, a plain rendering).
