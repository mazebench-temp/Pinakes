---
summary: Zeus answers Athena (5.21), Hermes answers Calypso twice (5.96, 5.145), and Odysseus answers her offer of immortality (5.214); and Zeus “said to Hermes, his dear son, face to face” (5.28).
---

Merry notes that ἀντίον ηὔδα at 5.28 is no answer: Zeus speaks to Hermes in the presence of the others (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 5.28; see the translation's note on 5.28). Hermes, a god answering a goddess, “answered her with words and said” (5.96), and again “spoke to her in turn” (5.145). See [[cloud-gatherer]] and [[of-many-wiles]].
