---
summary: There is no city of mortals near Calypso's island, Hermes says, “men who to the gods / offer sacrifices and choice hecatombs” (5.101–102).
grc: ["ἱερά τε ῥέζουσι"]
---

Hermes names the rite by its usual phrase, ἱερὰ ῥέζειν, “offer sacrifices”, to explain why a god would not cross so much salt water of his own will (5.99–102; see [[hecatomb]], [[hermes]] and [[reciprocity-with-the-gods]]).
