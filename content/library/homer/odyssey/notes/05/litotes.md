summary: Calypso welcomes Hermes, “Before now you have not come often” (5.88); Zeus “not long without hearing of it” kills Iasion (5.127–128); Calypso calls Odysseus “a rogue, and no fool” (5.182); and Ino tells him, “you do not seem to me to be senseless” (5.342).
refs: [5.88, 5.127, 5.182, 5.342]
---

The first is the Iliad's welcome of Thetis (*Iliad* 18.386), with “not often” for “never”; οὐδὲ δὴν ἦεν ἄπυστος makes Zeus' punishment of Iasion swift. Calypso's οὐκ ἀποφώλια εἰδώς is playful (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 5.182). See [[visit-scene]].
