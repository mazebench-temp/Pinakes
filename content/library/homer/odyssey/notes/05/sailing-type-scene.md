The verse of the fair wind, “and she sent forth a fair wind, harmless and warm,” returns when Odysseus tells Arete of the voyage (5.268 = 7.266). Instead of a crew there is one man at the steering oar, who does not sleep (5.270–271; [[steering-oar]], [[the-bear]], [[pleiades]]). See [[raft]].
