---
summary: Calypso promises bread, water and red wine “to satisfy your heart” (5.165–166), and puts on board “many meats to satisfy his heart” (5.267).
grc: ["μενοεικέʼ"]
en: [to satisfy your heart, to satisfy his heart]
---

Merry takes μενοεικέα at 5.166 with all three things of the verse before (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 5.166). See [[provisions]].
