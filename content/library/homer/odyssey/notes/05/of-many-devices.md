---
summary: Calypso addresses Odysseus by his full titles: “Son of Laertes, sprung from Zeus, Odysseus of many devices, / is it so, then, that home to your dear native land / you wish to go at once, now?” (5.203–205).
---

The first two verses are Athena's to Odysseus in the Iliad, when the army runs to the ships to sail home and she sends him to stop them (*Iliad* 2.173–174); here a goddess asks the same question and lets him go (5.203–210; [[son-of-laertes]], [[sprung-from-zeus]], [[dear-native-land]]).
