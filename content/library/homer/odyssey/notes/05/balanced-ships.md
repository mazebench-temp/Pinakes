---
summary: Odysseus refuses the raft: over the great gulf of the sea “not even balanced ships / cross, swift-sailing, glorying in the wind of Zeus” (5.175–176).
grc: ["νῆες ἐῖσαι"]
---

Merry finds in ἀγαλλόμεναι the common figure of the ship as a living thing (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 5.176). See [[swift-sailing-ships]] and [[raft]].
