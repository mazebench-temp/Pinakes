summary: Hermes binds on “his fine sandals, / ambrosial, golden” (5.44–45).

The verses of the god's sandals, which carry him over the waters and the boundless earth, are his in the Iliad and Athena's in Book 1 (5.44–46 = *Iliad* 24.340–342; 1.96–98; [[sandals]], [[dressing-scene]], [[boundless-sea]]). See [[hermes]].
