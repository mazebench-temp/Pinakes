---
summary: The related phrase ἐν περιφαινομένῳ, “in a place open to view”: the clearing near the river where Odysseus finds the two bushes (5.475–477).
refs: [5.476]
en: [in a place open to view]
---

Merry takes it as “in a clearing,” ground with a clear open space round it, and compares περισκέπτῳ ἐνὶ χώρῳ of Circe's house (10.211; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 5.476; Merry on 1.425). See [[olive]].
