---
summary: Odysseus, swimming along the cliffs: “for I know how the famous shaker of the earth is angry with me” (5.423).
---

In his first speech in the storm Odysseus blamed Zeus, not knowing that the storm was Poseidon's (5.303–304; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 5.304); since then Ino has asked him why Poseidon is so angry with him (5.339–340), and he names the god by his title with her verb, ὀδώδυσται (5.423; [[cholos]], [[famous]]). See [[poseidon]].
