summary: The same half-verse twice: Odysseus “lies on an island, suffering bitter sorrows” (5.13), and the father of the simile “lies in sickness, suffering bitter pains” (5.395).
---

κεῖται κρατέρʼ ἄλγεα πάσχων: the father whose recovery is as welcome to his children as the land is to Odysseus suffers in the words Athena used of Odysseus at the council (5.13, 5.394–398; [[the-fathers-recovery]], [[algos]]).
