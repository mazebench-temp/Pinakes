---
summary: Calypso tells Hermes that she meant to make Odysseus “immortal and ageless all his days” (5.136), and Odysseus grants that she is “immortal and ageless” while Penelope is mortal (5.218).
grc: [ἀγήραον]
---

ἀγήραον at 5.136, where Aristarchus read ἀγήρων (see the translation's note on 5.136). The verse of the offer returns in Odysseus' account to Arete and in what he tells Penelope (7.257, 23.336). See [[immortal-and-ageless]] and [[immortals]].
