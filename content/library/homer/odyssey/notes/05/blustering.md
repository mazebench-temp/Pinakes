---
summary: “And as a blustering wind shakes a heap of husks, / dry ones, and scatters them this way and that, / so it scattered the long beams of the raft” (5.368–370).
grc: [ζαὴς]
---

ἄνεμος ζαής is the wind of the Iliad's simile of the stones like snow (*Iliad* 12.157); here it scatters the beams of the raft like chaff (5.366–370; see [[the-heap-of-husks]] and [[winnowing]]).
