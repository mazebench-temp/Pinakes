---
summary: Athena, and then Calypso: Odysseus has no ships and companions “who might take him over the broad back of the sea” (5.16–17, 5.141–142).
---

The verses are Proteus' (5.14–17 = 4.557–560); Calypso repeats the last two in the first person, as her reason for not sending him herself (5.140–142). See [[calypso]].
