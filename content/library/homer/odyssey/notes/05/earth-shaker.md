---
summary: Poseidon is “the earth-shaker” four times in the storm: “the powerful earth-shaker” sees Odysseus from the mountains of the Solymi and again as he leaps into the sea (5.282, 5.375), and “Poseidon the earth-shaker” is named in Ino's question and when he raises the great wave (5.339, 5.366).
---

The line-end formula Ποσειδάων ἐνοσίχθων and κρείων ἐνοσίχθων name him at each of his acts against Odysseus; Odysseus himself calls him “the famous shaker of the earth” (5.423; [[shaker-of-the-earth]], [[powerful]]). See [[poseidon]].
