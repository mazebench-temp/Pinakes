---
summary: “But Athena, daughter of Zeus, thought of another thing” (5.382).
---

The verse brings her back into the story as soon as Poseidon has gone home: she binds the paths of the other winds and rouses the North Wind to carry Odysseus to the Phaeacians (5.382–387; [[thought-of-another-thing]], [[athena]], [[boreas]]).
