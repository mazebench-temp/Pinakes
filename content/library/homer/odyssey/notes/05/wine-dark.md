summary: Zeus shattered Odysseus' ship “in the middle of the wine-dark sea” (5.132); Odysseus will endure if some god wrecks him “on the wine-dark sea” (5.221); and Ino bids him throw her veil back “into the wine-dark sea” (5.349).
grc: [οἴνοπι]
---

The dative ἐνὶ οἴνοπι πόντῳ closes the verse of the wreck, which Odysseus repeats to Arete (5.132 = 7.250; [[the-shipwreck-of-odysseus]], [[veil]]).
