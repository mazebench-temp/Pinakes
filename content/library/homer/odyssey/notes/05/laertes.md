summary: Laertes is named once, in the patronymic of the verse with which Calypso addresses Odysseus on the last evening, “Son of Laertes, sprung from Zeus, Odysseus of many devices” (5.203). It is the Iliad's verse of address (*Iliad* 2.173 and often), here used for the first time in the Odyssey (see [[son-of-laertes]] and [[odysseus]]).
# Λαερτιάδη (5.203) is claimed by the entry.
