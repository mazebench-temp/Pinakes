---
summary: “But Athena, daughter of Zeus, thought of another thing” (5.382).
grc: ["Διὸς ἄλλʼ ἐνόησεν"]
---

The verse brings Athena back as soon as Poseidon has gone home to Aegae: she binds the other winds and sends the North Wind to carry Odysseus to the Phaeacians (5.380–387; [[daughter-of-zeus]], [[athena]], [[boreas]]).
