---
summary: Odysseus on the shore of Calypso's island “would look out over the barren sea, letting tears fall” (5.84, 5.158).
grc: ["δάκρυα λείβων"]
en: [letting tears fall]
---

The verse comes twice, when Hermes arrives and when Calypso finds him (5.83–84 = 5.157–158; see the translation's note on 5.83–84); “his eyes were never / dry of tears” (5.151–152). See [[tears]] and [[lament]].
