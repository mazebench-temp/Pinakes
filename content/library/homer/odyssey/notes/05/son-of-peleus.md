---
summary: Odysseus wishes he had died “on that day when, in their greatest numbers, bronze-fitted spears / the Trojans hurled at me around the dead son of Peleus” (5.309–310).
---

The fight over the body of Achilles is told by the shade of Agamemnon in the underworld (24.36–42); Merry adds that in the struggle Ajax bore off the body while Odysseus kept the Trojans at bay (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 5.309). See [[achilles]] and [[bronze-fitted]].
