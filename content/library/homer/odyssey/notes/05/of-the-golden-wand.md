---
summary: Calypso greets Hermes: “Why, Hermes of the golden wand, have you come to me, / respected and dear? Before now you have not come often” (5.87–88).
---

The greeting is Charis' and Hephaestus' welcome of Thetis in the Iliad, with Hermes' name and title in place of “Thetis of the long robes” (5.87–88; *Iliad* 18.385–386, 18.424–425; [[visit-scene]]). The wand itself he took up for the journey (5.47; [[wand]]). See [[hermes]].
