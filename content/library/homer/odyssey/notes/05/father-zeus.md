---
summary: Athena opens her plea at the second council: “Father Zeus, and you other blessed gods who live forever” (5.7).
---

The verse addresses the gods as a body after their father; the Odyssey gives it again to Hephaestus, to Odysseus and to the Sun (8.306, 12.371, 12.377). See [[blessed-gods]], [[gods-who-live-forever]] and [[the-council-of-the-gods]].
