---
summary: Back on his raft after the great wave, Odysseus “sat down in the middle of it, avoiding the end of death” (5.326).
grc: ["τέλος θανάτου"]
---

The phrase that in the Iliad covers men as they finish speaking (*Iliad* 16.502, 16.855, 22.361) is turned aside here. Odysseus has just wished he had died at Troy and won his funeral rites and fame (5.306–312), and the verse that follows his speech is the Iliad's death verse with a wave in place of death: “As he spoke, a great wave struck him from above” (5.313; [[so-he-spoke]]).
