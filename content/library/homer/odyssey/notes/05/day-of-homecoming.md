---
summary: Odysseus to Calypso: “But even so I wish and yearn all my days / to come home and see the day of my homecoming” (5.219–220).
---

The verse is Athena's, as Mentor, in Book 3, and Odysseus says it again to Nausicaa (3.233, 8.466). Here it is his answer to the offer of immortality (5.209–220; [[immortal-and-ageless]], [[nostos]]).
