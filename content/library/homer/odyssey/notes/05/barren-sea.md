---
summary: The sea is “barren” four times: the gull hunts fish through “the dread bosom of the barren sea” (5.52), Odysseus “would look out over the barren sea, letting tears fall” (5.84, 5.158), and Calypso lets him go “over the barren sea” (5.140).
