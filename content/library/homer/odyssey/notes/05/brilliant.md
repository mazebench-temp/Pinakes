summary: “Much-enduring brilliant Odysseus” three times (5.171, 5.354, 5.486) and “brilliant Odysseus” as he spreads his sails (5.269); “brilliant Calypso” when she sends him from the island and in the verse of the garments she gave him (5.263, 5.321, 5.372); and “shining Lacedaemon” in Athena's plea (5.20).
grc: ["δῖα Καλυψώ"]
---

δῖα Καλυψώ is the short form of her title, the epithet after the name ([[brilliant-among-goddesses]]). The garments she gave him weigh him down in the sea, and he strips them off before he stretches Ino's veil under his chest (5.321, 5.372–373; [[veil]]). Λακεδαίμονα δῖαν is in Medon's verses to Penelope, which Athena repeats (5.19–20 = 4.701–702; [[lacedaemon]]). See [[much-enduring]].
