---
summary: Calypso, who drinks nectar, promises Odysseus “red wine” for his raft and puts on board a skin of dark wine (5.165, 5.265).
---

Calypso promises to put on the raft “bread and water and red wine” (οἶνον ἐρυθρόν, 5.165), and on the fifth day “the goddess put on board for him a skin of dark wine” (μέλανος οἴνοιο, 5.265; see [[provisions]] and [[calypso]]). The goddess drinks nectar, which is red too (5.93; see [[nectar]] and [[red]]), and gives the mortal his own drink. The wine is carried in an ἀσκός, a skin, as the skin of Maron's dark wine that Odysseus takes to the Cyclops' cave (9.196–197) and the goatskin of wine for the oath in the Iliad (*Iliad* 3.247).
