summary: “So she spoke, and led the way, brilliant among goddesses, / swiftly; and he followed in the footprints of the goddess” (5.192–193).

The couplet of Athena in Books 2 and 3 with Calypso in her place; it leads Odysseus from the shore to the cave, where he sits on Hermes' throne (5.192–196; [[brilliant-among-goddesses]], [[calypso]]).
