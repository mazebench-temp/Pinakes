The welcome is the one Charis and Hephaestus give Thetis, “Why … have you come to me, / respected and dear? Before now you have not come often,” with “Say what is in your mind” and “But come farther in” (5.87–91; *Iliad* 18.385–387, 18.424–427). Merry thinks 5.91, which the best manuscripts lack, interpolated from the Iliad, since Hermes is already seated (see the translation's note on 5.91). The finding has its absent man: Hermes found the nymph within, but not Odysseus, who was weeping on the shore (5.58, 5.81–84). The host asks the reason before the meal, and the guest gives it after (5.87, 5.95–97). See [[xenia]], [[of-the-golden-wand]] and [[messenger-scene]].
