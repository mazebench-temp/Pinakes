ἧος ὁ ταῦθʼ ὥρμαινε … is Achilles' verse in *Iliad* 1 and Menelaus' in Book 4, each cut short by an arrival (*Iliad* 1.193–194; 4.120–121); here what cuts it short is the sea, and the main clause begins with the wave, ὦρσε δέ (5.366; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 5.366).
