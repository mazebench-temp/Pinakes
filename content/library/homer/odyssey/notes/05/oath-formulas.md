---
summary: Odysseus asks Calypso “to swear me a great oath / that you will not plan some other evil bane against me myself” (5.178–179), and she swears by Earth, Heaven and the water of Styx: “Let Earth now be witness to this” (5.184–187).
grc: ["ἴστω νῦν"]
en: [be witness]
---

ἴστω, “let … be witness,” calls the witnesses, as in Hera's oath to Zeus, whose three verses Calypso uses (5.184–186 = *Iliad* 15.36–38), and the oath repeats the terms of his request in the second person (5.179, 5.187). Circe is asked for the same oath in the same words (10.343–344). See [[oath]], [[styx]] and [[wide-sky]].
