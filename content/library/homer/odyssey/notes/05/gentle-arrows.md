---
summary: Artemis kills Orion: she “came upon him with her gentle arrows and killed him” (5.124).
---

Merry notes that an arrow shot in anger is still called ἀγανόν, so that the epithet speaks of the painlessness of the death, not of mercy; that men are usually killed by Apollo and women by Artemis; and that here Artemis kills Orion not as a goddess of death but from jealousy (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 5.124; see the translation's note on 5.124).
