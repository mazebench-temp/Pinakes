---
summary: “and the North Wind, born of the clear sky, rolling up a great wave” (5.296).
grc: [αἰθρηγενέτης]
---

Merry takes αἰθρηγενέτης as passive, “sky-born,” comparing αἰειγενέτης (14.446) and the Iliad's αἰθρηγενής, and makes the North Wind a land wind that comes down from the heights, against the scholia's “bringing fair weather” or “producing cold”; Rhianus and Aristophanes read αἰθρηγενέης (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 5.296 and his critical note; see the translation's note on 5.296). See [[boreas]].
