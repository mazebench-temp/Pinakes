---
summary: Poseidon, seeing Odysseus near the land of the Phaeacians: “Ah, truly the gods have changed their purpose / about Odysseus, while I was among the Ethiopians” (5.286–287).
---

Merry takes πόποι as an interjection, like παπαῖ, almost always of astonishment, anger or sorrow, and reports a scholium that made it a vocative, “gods,” in the speech of the Dryopes, a use some Alexandrian poets followed (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 5.286). As in Book 4 the cry is followed by ἦ μάλα δή, “Ah, truly.” See [[shook-his-head]] and [[poseidon]].
