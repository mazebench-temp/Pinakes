---
summary: Athena addresses “you other blessed gods who live forever” (5.7), and Calypso swears by the water of Styx, “which is the greatest / and most dread oath for the blessed gods” (5.185–186).
grc: ["μακάρεσσι θεοῖσι"]
---

Calypso's oath, by Earth, Heaven and Styx, is Hera's to Zeus in the Iliad (5.184–186 = *Iliad* 15.36–38; [[styx]], [[oath-formulas]]). See [[father-zeus]].
