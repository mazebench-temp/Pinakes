summary: Odysseus sees the land near “with a sharp look ahead, as he was lifted by a great wave” (5.393), and before it “sharp crags” (5.411).

ὀξὺ … προϊδών, “with a quick look forward” in Merry's rendering (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 5.393); ὀξέες of the crags, as of weapons ([[shore]]).
