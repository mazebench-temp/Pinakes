---
summary: The gods sit down to their session, “and among them / Zeus who thunders on high, whose power is the greatest” (5.3–4).
---

The second council opens on the god's power. The half-verse οὗ τε κράτος ἐστὶ μέγιστον is said of Zeus in the Iliad, who “has brought down the heights of many cities” (*Iliad* 2.116–118 = 9.23–25), and in Book 1 of the Cyclops Polyphemus (1.70).
