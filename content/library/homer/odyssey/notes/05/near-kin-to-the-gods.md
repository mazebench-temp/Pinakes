---
summary: Zeus' command: Odysseus will come to “the land of the Phaeacians, who are near kin to the gods” (5.35).
---

The Phaeacians “will honor him with all their hearts like a god” and send him home with more than he would have carried from Troy (5.35–40; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 5.34; [[phaeacians]]).
