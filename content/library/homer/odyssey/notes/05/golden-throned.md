---
summary: Calypso: Orion was killed in Ortygia when “chaste Artemis of the golden throne / came upon him with her gentle arrows” (5.123–124).
---

The epithet that the Odyssey gives above all to Dawn is here given to the goddess who kills Dawn's lover (5.121–124); Dawn of the golden throne carries off Cleitus for his beauty in a later book (15.250–251). See [[artemis]], [[gentle-arrows]] and [[dawn-and-orion]].
