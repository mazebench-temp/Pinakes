---
summary: Poseidon's parting threat: “So now, after suffering many evils, wander over the sea, / until you come among men whom Zeus has nurtured” (5.377–378).
grc: [διοτρεφέεσσι]
---

Merry notes that the epithet is generally used of a king or chieftain, under the special protection of Zeus, and is here given to a whole people, the Phaeacians, whom Zeus called ἀγχίθεοι; a variant reads Φαιήκεσσι (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 5.378 and his critical note; [[near-kin-to-the-gods]], [[phaeacians]]).
