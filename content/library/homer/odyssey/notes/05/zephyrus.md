summary: The West Wind is the “stormy” wind among the four that clash together in Poseidon's storm (5.295), and when the raft is broken the East Wind yields it to the West Wind to chase (5.332).
# Ζεφύρῳ (5.332), the dative, is claimed by the entry.
---

“The East Wind and the South Wind clashed together, and the stormy West Wind / and the North Wind, born of the clear sky, rolling up a great wave” (5.295–296; see [[poseidon]], [[eurus]], [[notus]] and [[boreas]]). δυσαής, “ill-blowing”, “stormy”, is the West Wind's epithet in the house of Zephyrus where the winds feast in the Iliad (*Iliad* 23.200), and again in the Odyssey's storms (12.289; see [[stormy]]). Here he is the wind of the wreck, not the fair wind that Athena sent Telemachus (2.420–421) or the breeze of the Elysian plain (4.567–568; see the general article). When the raft is tossed among the winds, “now again the East Wind would yield it to the West Wind to chase” (5.332; see [[thistles-in-the-north-wind]]).
