summary: Hermes is “the slayer of Argus” six times: “the guide, the slayer of Argus” four times (5.43, 5.75, 5.94, 5.145), and “the mighty slayer of Argus” when he flies off with his wand and when he leaves Calypso (5.49, 5.148).
grc: [ἀργεϊφόντης]
---

κρατὺς ἀργεϊφόντης, the epithet of his flight in the Iliad (*Iliad* 24.345; 16.181), begins and ends his visit (5.49, 5.148). Merry compares his flight with Hera's, who also steps on Pieria (*Iliad* 14.225–229; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 5.50; [[pieria]]). See [[guide]] and [[hermes]].
