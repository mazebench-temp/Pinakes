---
summary: “Answering her, Odysseus of many wiles said” (5.214): his answer to Calypso's offer of immortality.
---

The answer shows the quality the epithet names. He grants the goddess her beauty, calls Penelope “prudent,” and speaks of his home rather than of his wife (5.215–220; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 5.216; [[periphron]], [[day-of-homecoming]], [[immortal-and-ageless]]). See [[odysseus]].
