---
summary: Poseidon's drive home in two verses: “So he spoke, and lashed the fine-maned horses, / and came to Aegae, where his glorious house is” (5.380–381).
refs: [5.380-381]
---

The house at Aegae is the one from which Poseidon drives his chariot over the sea in the Iliad (*Iliad* 13.21–31); here only the whipping and the arrival are told ([[aegae]], [[fine-maned]], [[poseidon]]).
