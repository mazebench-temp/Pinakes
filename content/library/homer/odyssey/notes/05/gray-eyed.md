---
summary: Twice on the rocks of Scheria “the gray-eyed goddess Athena” saves Odysseus: she puts it in his mind to seize the rock (5.427), and she gives him presence of mind (5.437).
---

Both times the epithet stands in the second verse of a “had not” sentence, εἰ μὴ … γλαυκῶπις Ἀθήνη, after what would have happened to him (5.426–427, 5.436–437; [[would-have-had-not]]). See [[athena]].
