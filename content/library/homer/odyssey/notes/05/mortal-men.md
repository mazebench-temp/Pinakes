---
summary: Odysseus is to go home “with the escort neither of gods nor of mortal men” (5.32).
---

Merry contrasts the escort of a god such as Telemachus had, with Athena aboard his ship (2.416–417), and explains the escort of men by the Phaeacians, who “will send him in a ship” (5.37; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 5.32). See [[raft]] and [[of-many-bindings]].
