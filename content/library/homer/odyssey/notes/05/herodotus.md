---
summary: Herodotus says that the people of the land the Lycians held were once called Solymi, the people of the mountains from which Poseidon sees the raft; and his Samians, carried by a wind beyond the Pillars of Heracles, reach Tartessus “with a divine escort”, the escort that Zeus denies Odysseus.
---

- The land the Lycians held was once called Milyas, and the Milyans “were then called Solymi” (*Histories* 1.173.2; on 5.283): [[solymi]].
- The Samian ship that an east wind carried beyond the Pillars of Heracles came to Tartessus θείῃ πομπῇ χρεώμενοι, “with a divine escort” (*Histories* 4.152.2). Merry sets the phrase beside Zeus' command that Odysseus go home “with the escort neither of gods nor of mortal men” (5.32; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 5.32): [[raft]].
