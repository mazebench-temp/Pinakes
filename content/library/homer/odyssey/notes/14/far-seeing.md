---
summary: The beggar's tale: “But when far-seeing Zeus devised that hateful journey, / which loosed the knees of many men” (14.235–236).
---

Zeus is εὐρύοπα at the end of the verse as the god who devised the war: the [[the-tale-to-eumaeus|tale]] puts the journey to Troy under his devising, as it does the evils after it (14.243; see [[counselor]] and [[hateful]]).
