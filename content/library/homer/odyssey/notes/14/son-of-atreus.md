---
summary: The tale of the cloak names both sons of Atreus: “Odysseus and Menelaus, son of Atreus, were the leaders” of the ambush (14.470), and Odysseus asks for a man to go and tell “Agamemnon, son of Atreus, shepherd of the people” (14.497).
---

Ἀτρεΐδης Μενέλαος and Ἀτρεΐδῃ Ἀγαμέμνονι, the patronymic with the name for each brother in turn, as in the Iliad (see the general article, and [[menelaus]], [[agamemnon]] and [[shepherd-of-the-people]]). Menelaus leads the ambush beside Odysseus, and the speaker is third in command (14.469–471; see [[the-tale-of-the-cloak]] and [[ambush]]).
