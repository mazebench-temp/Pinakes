---
summary: Eumaeus lives apart among the swine and does not go to the city, “unless perhaps prudent Penelope / urges me to come, when news comes from somewhere” (14.373–374).
---

The queen sends for the swineherd when there is news, as she welcomes every vagrant and questions him about her husband (14.126–130, 14.372–377; see [[vagrants-tell-lies]]); the swineherd no longer goes to hear such news, since the Aetolian deceived him (14.378–385; see [[penelope]] and [[aetolians]]).
