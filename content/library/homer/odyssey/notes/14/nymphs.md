summary: The nymphs share with Hermes the portion that Eumaeus sets aside, with a prayer, at his evening sacrifice: “one he set aside for the nymphs and for Hermes, son of Maia” (14.435–436). On Ithaca they are the herdsman's goddesses, as Book 17 shows when the swineherd prays to the nymphs of the spring for his master's return.
# νύμφῃσι (14.435) is claimed by the Book 13 note, with the entry's English "nymphs".
---

**The portion.** At the division of the boar, “one he set aside for the nymphs and for Hermes, son of Maia, / with a prayer, and the others he gave to each” (14.435–436; see [[hermes]], [[maia]], [[sacrifice]] and [[eumaeus]]). D. B. Monro calls them “the host's especial patrons”: the raw pieces from every limb were the gods' share, but in the division among the guests one share was kept for the nymphs and Hermes (*Homer's Odyssey, Books XIII–XXIV*, 1901, on 14.428). In Homer the two are joined only here; the Hymns know Hermes as the lover of the mountain nymphs and the father of Pan by a nymph (see the general article).

**The nymphs of Ithaca.** The poem has shown their worship on the island twice. At the harbor of Phorcys is the cave of the Naiads, where Odysseus used to offer them hecatombs, and he prays to them there when Athena has scattered the mist (13.347–360; see [[the-cave-of-the-naiads]] and the Book 13 note). Near the town, at the spring that Ithacus, Neritus and Polyctor made, stands “an altar / of the nymphs, where all wayfarers made offerings”, and there the swineherd will pray: “Nymphs of the spring, daughters of Zeus, if ever Odysseus / burned thigh-pieces to you, wrapping them in rich fat, / of lambs and kids, fulfill for me this wish, / that that man may come, and a god may lead him” (17.210–211, 17.240–243, a plain rendering). At his own table, before the stranger whom he does not know for his master, he gives them their share; at the sacrifice he has prayed to all the gods “that Odysseus of much wisdom should return to his own house” (14.423–424; see [[prayer]] and [[olympians]]).
