# Ὑλακίδης (14.204) is claimed by the entry, and by castor-son-of-hylax.
---

The stranger's grandfather in the tale, named once and in passing, in the patronymic of the rich Cretan whom he calls his father (14.204; see [[castor-son-of-hylax]] and [[the-tale-to-eumaeus]]). The name is heard in a book that began with Eumaeus' barking dogs, κύνες ὑλακόμωροι, rushing at the stranger (14.29; see [[dogs]]).
