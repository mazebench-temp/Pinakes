# κῆρες (14.207), first met here among the Odyssey's notes.
grc: [κῆρες]
---

The κῆρες come for the Cretan's father as death-spirits that carry a man off: “But the fates of death came and carried him away / to the house of Hades; and his livelihood was divided / by his high-hearted sons” (τὸν κῆρες ἔβαν θανάτοιο φέρουσαι, 14.207–209; see [[castor-son-of-hylax]], [[thanatos]] and [[olbos]]). The half-verse is the Iliad's, where Odysseus calls to witness “all whom the fates of death have not carried away” (*Iliad* 2.302).
