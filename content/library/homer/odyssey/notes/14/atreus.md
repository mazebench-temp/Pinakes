# Ἀτρεΐδ* (in the entry) claims Ἀτρεΐδης (14.470), of Menelaus, and Ἀτρεΐδῃ (14.497), of Agamemnon; agamemnon's
# Book 14 note excepts 14.470.
---

Both sons of Atreus have a part in the stranger's tale of the cloak, each with the patronymic: “Odysseus and Menelaus, son of Atreus, were the leaders” of the ambush (14.470; see [[menelaus]]), and Odysseus asks for someone who “would go and tell Agamemnon, son of Atreus, shepherd of the people” to send more men from the ships (14.497; see [[agamemnon]] and [[the-tale-of-the-cloak]]). The patronymic counts for the father both times (see [[son-of-atreus]]).
