---
summary: The race of Helen, which Eumaeus wishes had perished utterly (14.68), and the race of Arceisius, which the suitors' ambush would destroy (14.181–182).
en: [race]
---

φῦλον here is a stock, the descendants of one person. Eumaeus curses the cause of the war: “would that the race of Helen had perished utterly, / brought to its knees, since she loosed the knees of many men” (Ἑλένης ἀπὸ φῦλον ὀλέσθαι, 14.68–69; see [[helen]] and [[gounata]]). The same verb serves the suitors' purpose against Telemachus: “the noble suitors / lie in ambush for him on his way home, so that the race / of godlike Arceisius may perish utterly from Ithaca, nameless” (ὅπως ἀπὸ φῦλον ὄληται / νώνυμον, 14.180–182; see [[arceisius]], [[telemachus-and-the-suitors]] and [[ambush]]). The line of Arceisius is thin: each generation has had a single son, Laertes, Odysseus, Telemachus (16.117–120).
