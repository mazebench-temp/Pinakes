---
summary: “Then the swineherd, leader of men, answered him” (14.121): Eumaeus' answer to the stranger's question about his master.
---

συβώτης, ὄρχαμος ἀνδρῶν, the swineherd's title, fills the second half of the verse as a hero's name and epithet would; the half-verse ends 14.22 as well, of the man who reared the four dogs (see [[leader-of-men]] and [[eumaeus]]). The answer is the speech on vagrants who tell Penelope lies for their keep, and on the master who is surely dead (14.122–147; see [[vagrants-tell-lies]] and [[pseudos]]). The other replies of the book are in [[answering-him]].
