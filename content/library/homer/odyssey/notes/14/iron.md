---
summary: Iron “worked with much toil” is among the treasures of Odysseus that the king of the Thesprotians shows the Cretan of the beggar's tale (14.324).
---

The king “showed me the possessions that Odysseus had gathered, / bronze and gold and iron worked with much toil” (14.323–324; see [[possessions]], [[bronze]], [[worked-with-much-toil]] and [[thesprotians]]), the treasure of the Iliad's ransom offers (*Iliad* 6.48, 10.379, 11.133) and of Odysseus' own storeroom (21.10). It is iron as treasure, stored in a king's halls, as in the Iliad (see the entry).
