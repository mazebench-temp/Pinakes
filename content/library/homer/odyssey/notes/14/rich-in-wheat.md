summary: In the Cretan's tale the king of the Thesprotians sends him off on a ship “of Thesprotian men to Dulichium, rich in wheat” (14.335).

πολύπυρον is new to the entry. The verse returns when the stranger tells Penelope the same story (19.292); Dulichium is the island of the suitor Amphinomus, and has the epithet again in Book 16 (16.396; see [[dulichium]] and [[thesprotians]]).
