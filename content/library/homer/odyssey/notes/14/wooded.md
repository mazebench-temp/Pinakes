---
summary: Odysseus goes up from the harbor “by the rugged path / through the wooded country, among the heights” (14.1–2).
---

χῶρον ἀνʼ ὑλήεντα, the accusative, is new to the entry. The country above the harbor has the woods of Athena's description of Ithaca that morning, “there is forest / of every kind” (13.246–247; see [[rugged]], [[ithaca]] and [[visit-scene]]).
