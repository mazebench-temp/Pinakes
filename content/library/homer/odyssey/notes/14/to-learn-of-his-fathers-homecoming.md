---
summary: Eumaeus of Telemachus: “and he has gone in search of news of his father / to sacred Pylos” (14.179–180).
refs: [14.179-180]
---

ὁ δʼ ἔβη μετὰ πατρὸς ἀκουήν, the verse with which Medon told Penelope, and Athena told Zeus, of the young man's journey, each time with the suitors' plan to kill him “as he comes home” (4.700–702, 5.18–20); the swineherd joins it to the same plan: “and the noble suitors / lie in ambush for him on his way home” (14.180–181; see [[noble]] and [[telemachus-and-the-suitors]]). D. B. Monro compares μετὰ σὸν κλέος, Athena's phrase to Odysseus on Ithaca (13.415), and reports that 14.174–184 are obelized in one manuscript, “perhaps because Eumaeus could not know of the ambush” (*Homer's Odyssey, Books XIII–XXIV*, 1901, on 14.179, and his critical note on 14.174–184).
