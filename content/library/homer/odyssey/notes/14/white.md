summary: The swineherd serves the roast porkers: “and he sprinkled white barley meal on it” (14.77).
---

ἄλφιτα λευκὰ πάλυνεν, the verse-end of Hecamede's posset for Nestor and Machaon (*Iliad* 11.640) and of the offering at the pit of the dead (10.520, 11.28), here sprinkled on the meat at a meal (see [[barley-meal]] and [[feast]]). At the evening sacrifice the barley meal is ἀλφίτου ἀκτή, the meal of barley, sprinkled on the gods' portion (14.429; see [[sacrifice-type-scene]]).
