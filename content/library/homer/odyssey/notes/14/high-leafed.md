---
summary: Pheidon told the beggar that Odysseus “had gone to Dodona, so that from the god's / high-leafed oak he might hear the will of Zeus” (14.327–328).
---

The verses come again in the tale to Penelope (19.296–297). Here the high-leafed oak is the oak of Zeus at Dodona, from which his will is heard (see [[the-oak-of-zeus]], [[dodona]] and [[pheidon]]).
