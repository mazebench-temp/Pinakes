summary: The welcome: “and in an ivy-wood bowl he mixed honey-sweet wine” (14.78).
---

μελιηδέα οἶνον, mixed in a κισσύβιον, the bowl in which Odysseus brought the Cyclops his wine (9.346; see the translation's note on 9.346 and [[wine]]). The swineherd's meal for the stranger is two roasted porkers, white barley meal and wine (14.73–79; see [[barley-meal]] and [[white]]).
