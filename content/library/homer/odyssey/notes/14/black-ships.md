---
summary: In the Cretan tale the thunderbolt strikes the ship and the crew fall into the sea: “And like sea crows around the black ship / they were carried on the waves, and the god took away their homecoming” (14.308–309).
---

The two verses are those of the wreck after Thrinacia, where they told the end of Odysseus' last companions (12.418–419; see [[like-sea-crows]] and [[the-shipwreck-of-odysseus]]). In the tale they tell the end of the Phoenician's crew, who were taking the beggar to Libya to sell him (14.295–300; see [[the-tale-to-eumaeus]] and [[phoenicians]]).
