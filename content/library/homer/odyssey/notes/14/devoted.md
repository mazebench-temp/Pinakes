---
summary: Eumaeus: “for truly the heart in my breast / grieves whenever anyone reminds me of my devoted master” (14.169–170).
---

κεδνοῖο ἄνακτος. The word is usually connected with κήδομαι, “care for” (see the general article), and Eumaeus has just said that his master “loved me greatly and cared for me in his heart” (κήδετο θυμῷ, 14.146). He says it to the master himself, whom he does not know (see [[eumaeus]] and [[anax]]).
