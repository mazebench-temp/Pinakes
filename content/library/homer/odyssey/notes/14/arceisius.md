“And the noble suitors / lie in ambush for him on his way home, so that the race / of godlike Arceisius may perish utterly from Ithaca, nameless” (14.180–182; see [[telemachus]], [[ambush]] and [[the-suitors]]). The swineherd names the line by its founder, as Eurycleia had named it by Laertes' patronymic, “the offspring of the son of Arceisius” (4.755). Since each of its men had only one son, Telemachus' death would end it (16.117–120). Athena, in the likeness of Mentes, had told Telemachus the opposite: “The gods have not made your lineage nameless for the time to come” (1.222). ἀντιθέοιο, “godlike”, is the epithet of heroes (see [[antitheos]]).
