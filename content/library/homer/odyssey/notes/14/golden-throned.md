---
summary: The end of the tale of the cloak: “and in his clothing / I lay gladly, and Dawn of the golden throne shone forth” (14.501–502).
---

φάε δὲ χρυσόθρονος Ἠώς is found only here; the Odyssey's verse-end is “and at once Dawn of the golden throne came” (10.541, 12.142; see the general article and [[eos]]). The night of the ambush, cold enough to kill a man without a cloak (14.483–489; see [[the-tale-of-the-cloak]]), ends at dawn in the borrowed cloak (see [[crimson]] and [[cloak]]).
