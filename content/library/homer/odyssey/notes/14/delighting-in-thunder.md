---
summary: The raid on Egypt: “and the whole plain was filled with foot soldiers and horses / and the flash of bronze; and Zeus who delights in thunder / cast evil panic among my companions, and no one dared / to stand and face them” (14.267–270).
grc: [τερπικέραυνος]
---

The nominative, which the Iliad has three times (*Iliad* 8.2, 12.252, 24.529), is new to the Odyssey here, and the verses of the rout return when the beggar tells the raid again to Antinous (17.436–438; see [[the-raid-on-egypt]]). The god who sends the panic also puts into the raider's mind the thought that saves him (14.273–284; see [[zeus]]).
