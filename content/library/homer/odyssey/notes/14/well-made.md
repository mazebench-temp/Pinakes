---
summary: The Cretan gives himself up to the king of Egypt: “at once I took the well-made helmet from my head” (14.276).
---

The helmet verse of the arming scene, κρατὶ δʼ ἐπʼ ἰφθίμῳ κυνέην εὔτυκτον ἔθηκεν (*Iliad* 3.336), turned round: ἀπὸ κρατὸς κυνέην εὔτυκτον ἔθηκα, “I took … from my head” (see [[arming-scene]], [[helmet]] and [[supplication-type-scene]]).
