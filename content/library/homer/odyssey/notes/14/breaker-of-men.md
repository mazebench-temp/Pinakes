---
summary: The beggar's tale: “Truly Ares and Athena gave me daring / and the strength that breaks men” (14.216–217).
grc: [ῥηξηνορίην]
en: [the strength that breaks men]
---

ῥηξηνορίη, the noun of the epithet that Homer gives to Achilles alone, is found only here (see the general article and the Odyssey translation's key words). The Cretan claims it from the gods of war, for the ambushes in which he was always the first to leap out and kill (14.217–221; see [[ares]], [[athena]] and [[ambush]]).
