---
summary: Fed and given the cup, the stranger “spoke to him, addressing him with winged words,” and asked his first question: “My friend, who was it that bought you with his own possessions, / so very rich and mighty as you say?” (14.114–116).
---

The whole verse, καί μιν φωνήσας ἔπεα πτερόεντα προσηύδα, with which Telemachus welcomed the goddess as Mentes and Odysseus spoke to the goddess as a young herdsman on the morning of this day (1.122; 13.227, 13.253). The guest's question comes before the host's: he asks about the master who bought the swineherd, “in case I may know him,” and offers to bring news of him (14.115–120); the host asks who the guest is only at 14.185–190 (see [[who-are-you-among-men]] and [[visit-scene]]).
