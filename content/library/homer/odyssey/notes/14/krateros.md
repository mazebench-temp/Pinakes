---
summary: Eumaeus: on the minds of raiders too “falls a mighty fear of the gods' regard” (14.88); and the beggar asks who bought him, “so very rich and mighty as you say?” (14.115–116).
---

κρατερὸν δέος is the fear that falls even on raiders and not on the suitors (14.85–92; see [[hostile-men]] and [[deos]]). καρτερός, the other form of the word (see the Book 4 note), is the beggar's word for the master whom Eumaeus has praised but not yet named (14.115–117).
