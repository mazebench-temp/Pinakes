en: ["men hostile", enemy]
---

δυσμενέες καὶ ἀνάρσιοι is the pair of words with which Hermes warned Priam of the Achaeans (*Iliad* 24.365). Here they are raiders, and Eumaeus sets their fear of the gods against the fearlessness of the suitors (14.81–95; see [[piracy]], [[krateros]] and [[the-suitors]]). The beggar's tale gives the raider's side: he led such men nine times before Troy (14.229–231; see [[the-tale-to-eumaeus]] and [[the-raid-on-egypt]]).
