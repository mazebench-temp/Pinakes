---
summary: Castor, “who was honored then among the Cretans by the people like a god / for his prosperity and wealth and his glorious sons” (14.205–206).
grc: [κυδαλίμοισιν]
---

υἱάσι κυδαλίμοισιν, “his glorious sons,” is the verse-end of Nestor and his sons when Telemachus tells of his welcome at Pylos (17.113). Here they are the lawful sons who divide their father's goods and leave the bastard little (14.207–210; see [[castor-son-of-hylax]], [[honored-like-a-god]] and [[high-hearted]]).
