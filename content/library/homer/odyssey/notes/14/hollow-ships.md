summary: The storm of the Cretan tale: “then the son of Cronus set a dark cloud / above the hollow ship, and the sea grew dark beneath it” (14.303–304); and the Thesprotian sailors, who could not find the beggar, “went back again / on board the hollow ship” (14.356–357).
grc: ["νηὸς ἔπι γλαφυρῆς"]
---

14.301–304 are the verses of the storm after Thrinacia, with Crete in place of the island (12.403–406; see the Book 12 note and [[the-shipwreck-of-odysseus]]). The sailors' νηὸς ἔπι γλαφυρῆς, with the preposition after its noun, is new to the entry (see [[thesprotians]]).
