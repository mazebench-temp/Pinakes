---
summary: The men who gathered quickly for the Cretan's raid on Egypt (14.248), and Agamemnon, “shepherd of the people” (14.497).
en: [the men]
---

“Nine ships I fitted out, and the men quickly gathered” (θοῶς δʼ ἐσαγείρατο λαός, 14.248; see [[piracy]] and [[the-raid-on-egypt]]): λαός is the host that follows a leader (see the entry), here a raider's crews. In the [[the-tale-of-the-cloak|tale of the cloak]] Odysseus wishes someone would go and tell “Agamemnon, son of Atreus, shepherd of the people” to send more men from the ships (ποιμένι λαῶν, 14.497; see [[shepherd-of-the-people]] and [[agamemnon]]).
