---

παῖδες ὑπέρθυμοι are the lawful sons of Castor (14.201–202), who share out their father's goods by lot and leave the son of a bought mother little more than a house (see [[castor-son-of-hylax]], [[nothos]] and [[lots]]). The praise is given them by the brother they left with little.
