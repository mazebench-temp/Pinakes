summary: In the wreck of the Cretan tale: “Clinging to it I was borne along by the deadly winds” (14.313).
---

φερόμην ὀλοοῖς ἀνέμοισιν is the verse-end of the nine days from Malea (9.82) and of the ride on keel and mast after the storm of Book 12 (12.425; see the Book 9 and Book 12 notes). The [[the-tale-to-eumaeus|false tale]] is told in the words of the true voyages: nine days of drift, and land on the tenth night (14.314; see [[black-night]] and [[the-winds]]).
