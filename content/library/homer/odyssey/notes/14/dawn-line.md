ἅμʼ ἠοῖ φαινομένηφιν, the half-verse of Achilles' promise to Priam (*Iliad* 24.600) and of Eidothea's instructions to Menelaus (4.407), returns when the raid is told again to Antinous (17.435; see [[the-raid-on-egypt]]). φάε δὲ χρυσόθρονος Ἠώς is found only here (see [[golden-throned]] and [[eos]]). The book's own night has no dawn: it ends with the swineherd going out to sleep beside the boars (14.523–533), and the next book opens with Athena at Lacedaemon (15.1–3).
