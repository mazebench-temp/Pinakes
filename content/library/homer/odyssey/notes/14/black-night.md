---
summary: The Cretan tale: “For nine days I was borne along; and on the tenth black night / a great rolling wave brought me to the land of the Thesprotians” (14.314–315).
---

The verse is the one with which Odysseus told Arete how the gods brought him to Ogygia (7.253; see the Book 7 note); in the false tale the drift ends instead in Thesprotia (see [[the-tale-to-eumaeus]], [[the-shipwreck-of-odysseus]] and [[thesprotians]]). The wreck that comes before it is told in the verses of Book 12 (14.301–309; 12.403–419).
