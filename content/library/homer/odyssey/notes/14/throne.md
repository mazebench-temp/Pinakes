---
summary: The only throne of the book is Dawn's: as the tale of the cloak ends, “Dawn of the golden throne shone forth” (14.502).
---

“and in his clothing / I lay gladly, and Dawn of the golden throne shone forth” (14.501–502; see [[golden-throned]], [[eos]] and [[the-tale-of-the-cloak]]). χρυσόθρονος is formed from θρόνος, the gods' golden seat of honor (see the entry), and in the Odyssey it is Dawn's epithet, as at the end of both of Circe's speeches of instruction (10.541, 12.142; see the Book 10 note), and once Artemis' (5.123).
