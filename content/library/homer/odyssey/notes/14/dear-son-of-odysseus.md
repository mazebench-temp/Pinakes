---
summary: Eumaeus to the beggar: “But when the dear son of Odysseus comes, / he himself will give you a cloak and a tunic as clothing, / and will send you wherever your heart and spirit bid you” (14.515–517).
---

Nearly the same three verses stand again when Eumaeus repeats the promise in Book 15 (15.337–339), and several manuscripts omit them here (see the translation's note on 14.515–517). Telemachus is at Sparta, where Athena goes to remind him of his return as Book 15 opens (15.1–3), and when he comes home it is to the swineherd's hut (16.1–48; see [[telemachus]] and [[the-journey-of-telemachus]]).
