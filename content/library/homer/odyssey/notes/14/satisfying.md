---
summary: The Cretan's share of his raids: “From this I picked out what satisfied my heart, and much afterward / I received by lot” (14.232–233).
en: [satisfied my heart]
---

μενοεικέα, the pick of the spoil that the raider took for himself; the rest came to him by lot, ὀπίσσω, “afterwards,” which D. B. Monro explains as the regular division of the spoil after the prizes of honor had been given to the leaders (*Homer's Odyssey, Books XIII–XXIV*, 1901, on 14.232; *Iliad* 1.368; see [[geras]], [[lots]] and [[piracy]]). The Cretan of the tale told to Athena that morning paid his passage with spoil of the same word (13.273; see [[the-first-cretan-tale]]).
