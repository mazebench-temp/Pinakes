# The harbor of 14.1, unnamed (λιμένος, which harbor claims as well).
refs: [14.1]
---

“But he went up from the harbor by the rugged path / through the wooded country, among the heights, to where Athena / had shown him the brilliant swineherd” (1–3; see [[rugged]] and [[eumaeus]]). The harbor is the harbor of Phorcys, where the Phaeacians set him ashore, and where Athena told him to go first of all to the swineherd, who would be found sitting beside his swine at the Rock of Corax and the spring Arethusa, and then changed him into a beggar (13.96–125, 13.404–411, 13.429–438; see [[odysseus-set-ashore-on-ithaca]], [[odysseus-changed-into-a-beggar]] and [[the-rock-of-corax]]). The book that brought him to the harbor asleep sends him from it on foot, alone, into the island's high country.
