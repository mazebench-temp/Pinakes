---
summary: The beggar stayed a year in the Phoenician's house, “But when the months and the days were being fulfilled / as the year circled round again, and the seasons came round” (14.293–294).
---

The two verses are those of the year after which the seer of Book 11 was set free (11.294–295; see the Book 11 note). Here they end the year that the Phoenician kept the beggar before he put him on a ship for Libya, to sell him there (14.290–297; see [[phoenicians]] and [[libya]]).
