---
summary: Eumaeus draws his tunic tight with his belt before he goes to the sties for two porkers to feast his guest (14.72), the only ζωστήρ of the Odyssey.
---

“So saying, he quickly drew his tunic tight with his belt, / and went to the sties” (14.72–73; see [[eumaeus]], [[tunic]] and [[sties]]). It is the only ζωστήρ of the Odyssey, and the only belt in Homer that is not a warrior's (see the entry): the swineherd girds up his tunic for work, as a warrior girds himself to arm. The sashes, ζῶστρα, that Nausicaa takes to the washing are another word (6.38; see [[girdle]]).
