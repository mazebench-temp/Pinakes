---
summary: Eumaeus grieves for Telemachus: “and he has gone in search of news of his father / to sacred Pylos; and the noble suitors / lie in ambush for him on his way home” (14.179–181).
---

μνηστῆρες ἀγαυοί, the suitors' title in Telemachus' and Penelope's mouths (2.209, 4.681), shares its verse with the “sacred Pylos” of the son's journey (14.180; see [[egatheos]]). The ambush was planned in Book 4, and Athena has told Odysseus of it that morning (4.669–672, 4.842–847; 13.425–428; see [[the-suitors]], [[telemachus-and-the-suitors]] and [[to-learn-of-his-fathers-homecoming]]).
