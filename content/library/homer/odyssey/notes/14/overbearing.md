μνηστῆρσιν ὑπερφιάλοισιν, the suitors' formula (see the general article), in the swineherd's own household: every day the best boar goes to their table, and the herd of boars dwindles (14.17–20, 14.26–28; see [[the-suitors]] and [[well-fed]]).
