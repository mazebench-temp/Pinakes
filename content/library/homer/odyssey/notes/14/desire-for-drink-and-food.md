summary: The supper in the hut ends in the Odyssey's verses: “And they put out their hands to the good things lying ready before them. / But when they had put away the desire for drink and food, / Mesaulius took away their bread, and they hastened to bed, / full of bread and meat” (14.453–456).
---

14.453–454 are the verses of the first meal in the house of Odysseus (1.149–150), after which the suitors turn to song and dancing (1.151–152); here a slave clears the bread and the men go to bed (see [[mesaulius]] and [[feast-type-scene]]). The night that follows is the night of the [[the-tale-of-the-cloak|tale of the cloak]] (14.457–506).
