---
summary: Eumaeus: “The blessed gods do not love cruel deeds” (14.83).
---

σχέτλια ἔργα, “cruel deeds,” what Odysseus and his companions saw when the Cyclops ate their friends (9.295; see the Book 9 note), are here the deeds of the suitors, who devour the swine with no thought of the gods' regard (14.80–82; see [[the-gods-honor-justice]], [[blessed-gods]] and [[the-suitors]]). The words name the suitors' deeds again when they lie dead in the hall (22.413).
