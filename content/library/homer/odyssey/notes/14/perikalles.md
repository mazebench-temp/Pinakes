summary: The Cretan's men break his order and raid: “at once the beautiful fields of the Egyptian men / they laid waste, and carried off the women and the little children” (14.263–264).

περικαλλέας ἀγρούς, the only fields in Homer called beautiful, laid waste in the next verse; the verses return when the raid is told to Antinous (17.432–433; see [[the-raid-on-egypt]], [[egypt]] and [[piracy]]).
