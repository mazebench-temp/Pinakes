---
summary: Eumaeus on Telemachus: “and he has gone in search of news of his father / to sacred Pylos; and the noble suitors / lie in ambush for him on his way home” (14.179–181).
---

ἐς Πύλον ἠγαθέην opens the verse as when Medon told Penelope of the journey and when Athena spoke of it to Zeus (4.702, 5.20; see the Book 4 note). Telemachus has gone on from Pylos to Sparta, and Athena has just left to call him home (13.412–415, 13.439–440; see [[pylos]], [[the-journey-of-telemachus]] and [[telemachus-and-the-suitors]]).
