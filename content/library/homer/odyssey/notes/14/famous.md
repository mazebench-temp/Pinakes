---
summary: The beggar's tale: “then they bade me and far-famed Idomeneus / lead the ships to Ilios” (14.237–238).
---

ἀγακλυτὸν Ἰδομενῆα closes the verse as in Andromache's words to Hector (*Iliad* 6.436). The Cretan who in the tale to Athena would not serve under Idomeneus at Troy but led his own companions (13.265–266) here shares the command with him, since “the harsh talk of the people held us” (14.239; see [[idomeneus]], [[the-tale-to-eumaeus]], [[the-first-cretan-tale]] and [[the-talk-of-the-people]]).
