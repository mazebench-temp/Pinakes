---
summary: The Cretan before Troy: “nine times I led men and swift-sailing ships / against foreign men, and very much fell to my lot” (14.230–231).
---

The epithet's one place in the Odyssey, with νέεσσιν where the Iliad has νηυσί (*Iliad* 1.421, 1.488, 2.351, 10.442). The ships beside which Achilles sat out the war are here a raider's, and the nine raids are the making of his house (14.229–234; see [[piracy]], [[sons-of-the-achaeans]] and [[the-tale-to-eumaeus]]).
