---
summary: The Cretan's ships came to Egypt “unharmed and free of sickness,” ἀσκηθέες καὶ ἄνουσοι (14.255); and the king of the Thesprotians took care of the castaway ἀπριάτην, “without price” (14.317).
refs: [14.317]
---

ἀσκηθής, “unhurt,” and ἄνουσος, ἀν- + νοῦσος, “without sickness,” a pair joined with καί, as ἀδάκρυτος καὶ ἀπήμων (see the general article); ἄνουσος is found only here in Homer (see the Odyssey translation's key words). ἀπριάτην stands alone here, without the ἀνάποινον with which it named the return of Chryseis to her father, “unbought, unransomed” (*Iliad* 1.99; see the translation's key words, and [[pheidon]]).
