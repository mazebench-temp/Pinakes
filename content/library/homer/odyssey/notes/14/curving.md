---
summary: The raid on Egypt: “and in the river Aegyptus I brought my curving ships to a halt” (14.258).
---

The nine ships that the beggar fitted out in Crete reach Egypt on the fifth day (14.248–258; see [[aegyptus-river]] and [[the-raid-on-egypt]]). The verse returns with the raid when he tells it again to Antinous (17.427).
