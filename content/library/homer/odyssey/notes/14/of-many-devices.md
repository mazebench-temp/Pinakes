---
summary: In the tale of the cloak: “Son of Laertes, sprung from Zeus, Odysseus of many devices, / I shall no longer be among the living” (14.486–487).
---

The man who calls on Odysseus' contrivance has come out without his cloak, and the device follows at once: Odysseus “had this thought in his heart, / such a man was he at planning and at fighting,” and sends Thoas running to the ships with a false dream (14.490–499; see [[son-of-laertes]], [[metis]] and [[the-tale-of-the-cloak]]).
