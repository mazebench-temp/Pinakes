# Ἀνδραίμονος (in the entry) claims Ἀνδραίμονος (14.499).
---

Θόας, Ἀνδραίμονος υἱός, “Thoas, son of Andraemon” (14.499), is the half-verse with which the Iliad names Thoas in the Catalogue and in the council of Book 15 (*Iliad* 2.638, 15.281), and the Odyssey's only mention of the father (see [[thoas]] and [[the-tale-of-the-cloak]]).
