# Πριαμ* (in the entry) claims Πριάμου (14.241).
---

“There we sons of the Achaeans fought for nine years, / and in the tenth we sacked the city of Priam and set out / for home with the ships, and a god scattered the Achaeans” (14.240–242; see [[the-tale-to-eumaeus]], [[troy]] and [[the-fall-of-troy]]). Nine years of war and the city taken in the tenth were the reckoning of Calchas' sign at Aulis (*Iliad* 2.328–329; see [[calchas]]). The second verse ends as Nestor ended his account of the sack, and as Odysseus told it to Athena in Book 13, “and a god scattered the Achaeans” (3.130–131, 13.316–317; see [[the-returns-of-the-achaeans]]).
