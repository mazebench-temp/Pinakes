---
summary: In the tale of the cloak the speaker went out to the ambush without his cloak, “but I followed with only my shield and my shining kilt” (14.482).
---

ζῶμα φαεινόν, the kilt that the Iliad's warrior wears under his belt, with “the band that the bronze-smiths made” (*Iliad* 4.186–187), is all the speaker had for the night of frost besides his shield; D. B. Monro notes that he has a tunic on, which “is always taken for granted” (*Homer's Odyssey, Books XIII–XXIV*, 1901, on 14.482; 14.489; see [[kilt]], [[arming-scene]] and [[the-tale-of-the-cloak]]).
