---
summary: When the swineherd's dogs run at him, the beggar sits down, by craft, and the staff falls from his hand (14.31).
---

“Suddenly the barking dogs caught sight of Odysseus. / And they ran at him, baying; but Odysseus / sat down, by craft, and the staff fell from his hand” (14.29–31; see [[dogs]], [[kerdos]] and [[odysseus]]). The staff is the σκῆπτρον that Athena gave him with his beggar's dress (13.437; see the Book 13 note and the translation's row σκῆπτρον, of a beggar). The same half-verse, ἔκπεσε χειρός, comes three lines later of the swineherd, as the leather he was cutting falls from his hand when he runs out to drive off the dogs (14.34; see [[door]] and [[sandals]]).
