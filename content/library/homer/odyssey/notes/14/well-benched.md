---
summary: The Thesprotian crew put in at Ithaca: “There they bound me fast in the well-benched ship / with a well-twisted rope, firmly, and they themselves went ashore / and hastily took their supper beside the shore of the sea” (14.345–347).
---

The epithet of the ship that Telemachus' companions loaded for his voyage (2.414), here of the slavers' ship in which the captive lies bound while the crew eat on the beach; the gods undo his bonds and he slips into the sea (14.348–352; see [[the-tale-to-eumaeus]] and [[sailing-type-scene]]).
