---
summary: The stranger's oath ends with vengeance on “whoever / here dishonors his wife and his shining son” (14.163–164).
---

φαίδιμος υἱός is Telemachus' title in the later books, from the second verse of the next (15.2), and at his father's side in the hall and in the last fight (16.308; 19.31, 22.141, 24.526; see [[telemachus]]). In the swineherd's answer the son is the one he grieves for, gone to Pylos and ambushed on his way home (14.174–182; see [[noble]]).
