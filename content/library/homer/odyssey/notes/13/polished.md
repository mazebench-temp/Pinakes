---

ἐϋξέστῃ ἐνὶ χηλῷ, the chest that Arete brought out and filled with the gifts (8.424–448), which a slave woman carries down to the ship as “the close-fitted chest” (13.68; see [[chest]] and [[possessions]]). The form is new in the Odyssey; the Iliad has it of the manger of Priam's horses (*Iliad* 24.280).
