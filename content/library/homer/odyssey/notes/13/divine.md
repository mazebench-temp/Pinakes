---
summary: At the farewell feast “among them a divine singer sang / Demodocus, honored by the people” (13.27–28).
---

θεῖος ἀοιδός is the verse of the singer on the Shield and of the singer at Menelaus' wedding feast (13.27 = 4.17 = *Iliad* 18.604; see the Book 4 note and [[the-singer-on-the-shield]]). It is the last appearance of Demodocus, and the poem does not say what he sang: Odysseus is watching the sun (13.27–30; see [[demodocus]] and [[honored-like-a-god]]).
