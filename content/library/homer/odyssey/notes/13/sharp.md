ὀξέϊ χαλκῷ, the Iliad's formula of the killing blow, in a lie: the Cretan exile says that he struck Orsilochus with his spear from ambush, by night, with one companion (13.267–271; see [[bronze-fitted]], [[ambush]], [[orsilochus-son-of-idomeneus]] and [[the-first-cretan-tale]]).
