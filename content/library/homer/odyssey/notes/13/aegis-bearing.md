---
summary: Athena is “Pallas Athena, daughter of aegis-bearing Zeus” when Odysseus rejoices at the name of his land (13.252) and when she has laid away his goods in the cave and set a stone against the doors (13.371).
---

Both times the father's epithet closes the verse-end κούρη Διὸς αἰγιόχοιο (see [[child-of-aegis-bearing-zeus]] and [[daughter-of-zeus]]). At 13.252 the text prints a comma after Διός, which splits the genitive formula; there is none at 13.371 (see the translation's note on 13.252). See [[athena]] and [[zeus]].
