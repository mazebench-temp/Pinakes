---
summary: Under the sacred olive Odysseus and Athena “planned destruction for the overbearing suitors” (13.372–373).
---

μνηστῆρσιν ὑπερφιάλοισιν, the suitors' formula, here with the movable ν before ὄλεθρον; the form is new to the entry. Some manuscripts give the same words again three verses later, in Athena's first order, “consider now destruction for the overbearing suitors,” where the text has “consider how you will lay hands on the shameless suitors” (13.376; D. B. Monro, *Homer's Odyssey, Books XIII–XXIV*, 1901, critical note on 13.376). See [[the-suitors]], [[anaideia]] and [[hybris]].
