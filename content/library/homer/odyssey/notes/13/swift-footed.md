---
summary: In the false tale, the son of Idomeneus is “swift-footed Orsilochus, who in wide Crete / outran toiling men with his swift feet” (13.260–261).
grc: ["πόδας ὠκύν"]
---

πόδας ὠκύν, the accusative of Achilles' epithet, found only here in Homer: the speed of the man whom the Cretan says he killed is his whole description, and the next verse says it again, ταχέεσσι πόδεσσιν, “with his swift feet” (see [[swift]], [[toiling-men]] and [[orsilochus-son-of-idomeneus]]).
