---
summary: Fathers in the book: “father Zeus”, to whom the Phaeacians pour their libation and Poseidon makes his complaint (13.51, 13.128), and by whom Odysseus implores his daughter (13.324); Alcinous' father Nausithous, whose prophecy comes true (13.173); and Idomeneus, whose favor the Cretan of the false tale would not court (13.265).
---

**Father Zeus.** Alcinous bids the herald serve the wine “so that when we have prayed to father Zeus / we may send the stranger on his way to his native land” (Διὶ πατρί, 13.51–52; see [[father-zeus]] and [[libation]]), and Poseidon opens his complaint, “Father Zeus, no longer shall I among the immortal gods / be held in honor” (Ζεῦ πάτερ, 13.128–129; see [[poseidon]] and [[time]]). On Ithaca, when the herdsman has become Athena, “daughter of Zeus” (13.300), Odysseus implores her by him: “But now I implore you by your father” (πρὸς πατρός, 13.324; see [[gounata]]). The Naiads too are “daughters of Zeus” (13.356; see [[nymphs]]).

**The father's prophecy.** Alcinous recognizes in the ship turned to stone “the oracles of my father, who used to say that Poseidon begrudged / us, because we are escorts of all men and come to no harm” (πατρὸς ἐμοῦ, 13.172–174; see [[nausithous]] and [[thesphaton]]). Monro distinguishes ἔφασκε, “alleged”, the cause the old man gave, from φῆ, “said”, with which the prophecy itself begins (D. B. Monro, *Homer's Odyssey, Books XIII–XXIV*, 1901, on 13.173). The son lives to see what his father foretold.

**Fathers and sons.** In the false tale the Cretan killed Idomeneus' son, “because I would not court his father's favor by serving as his attendant / in the land of the Trojans” (ᾧ πατρί, 13.265–266; see [[idomeneus]] and [[charis]]). Two fathers who are gods stand behind the book's story: Poseidon, angry because Odysseus blinded “his dear son” (13.343; see [[polyphemus-the-cyclops]]), and Zeus, whose daughter stands by Odysseus (13.300–302); and Odysseus himself is a father, who prays that the goddess may make “my dear son grow” (13.360; see [[telemachus]]).
