---
summary: The false tale: “Him I struck with my bronze-fitted spear as he came down / from the country, lying in ambush near the road with one companion” (13.267–268).
---

The verse is Nestor's, in the Iliad's tale of his first kill in the war with the Epeians: “As he came on I struck him with my bronze-fitted spear” (*Iliad* 11.742), of Mulius the spearman. The Cretan of Odysseus' lie has κατιόντα, “as he came down,” for Nestor's προσιόντα, and he strikes Idomeneus' son from ambush, by night (13.267–270; see [[orsilochus-son-of-idomeneus]], [[the-first-cretan-tale]] and [[spear]]).
