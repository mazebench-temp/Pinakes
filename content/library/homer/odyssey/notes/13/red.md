---
summary: Arete sends three of her slave women to the ship with Odysseus, one with a mantle and a tunic, one with the chest, “and a third brought bread and red wine” (13.66–69).
---

σῖτόν τʼ … καὶ οἶνον ἐρυθρόν, the provisions of a voyage, as Calypso promised to put them on the raft, “bread and water and red wine” (5.165). The escorts stow them, “all the drink and food,” beside the bed they spread for the sleeper on the deck (13.71–75; see [[provisions]], [[wine]] and [[arete-queen-of-the-phaeacians]]).
