en: [mighty]

ὑπερμενέων βασιλήων, the verse-end of Agamemnon's complaint to Zeus in the Iliad (*Iliad* 8.236), and later of the cowherd's thought of fleeing to another king (20.222). The wish is that the goods had stayed among the Phaeacians (D. B. Monro, *Homer's Odyssey, Books XIII–XXIV*, 1901, on 13.204), and the kings he imagines are those who would have escorted him home, as the Phaeacians have done without his knowing it (see [[to-the-land-of-what-mortals]] and [[pompe]]).
