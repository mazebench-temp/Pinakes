---
summary: The people of the Phaeacians: Demodocus is “honored by the people” (13.28), Alcinous “preeminent among all the people” (13.38), Arete is left with her children and her people (13.62), and Zeus would have the ship turned to stone when “the people from the city” see her coming in (13.156).
---

In Scheria λαοί are the people of a city at peace, not an army. At the farewell feast the singer is “Demodocus, honored by the people” (λαοῖσι τετιμένος, 13.28; see [[demodocus]] and [[singers]]); Odysseus addresses the king in the formula of the Phaeacian books, “Powerful Alcinous, preeminent among all the people” (13.38 = 8.382, 8.401, 11.355, 11.378; see [[alcinous]] and [[preeminent]]), and bids Arete farewell, “take your delight in this house / with your children and your people and King Alcinous” (13.61–62; see [[arete-queen-of-the-phaeacians]]). The people are also the witnesses of Poseidon's punishment. Zeus advises him, “when all catch sight of her as she is driven on, / the people from the city, to turn her to stone near the land, / like a swift ship, so that all men may marvel” (λαοὶ ἀπὸ πτόλιος, 13.155–157; see [[the-migration-of-the-phaeacians]] and [[polis]]): the ship is to be a sign set before the whole people.
