νὺξ … μάλα δνοφερή is the night of the ambush by the road, in which the Cretan of Odysseus' lie strikes down Idomeneus' son with one companion beside him (13.267–270; see [[orsilochus-son-of-idomeneus]], [[the-first-cretan-tale]] and [[bronze-fitted]]). The Iliad gives the word to dark water (*Iliad* 9.15, 16.4); the Odyssey's only other δνοφερός is the night through which Peisistratus will not drive (15.50; see the general article).
