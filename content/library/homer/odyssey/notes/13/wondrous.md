summary: Athena: “But let us put the goods in the inner part of the wondrous cave / at once, now, where they may stay safe for you” (13.363–364).
---

μυχῷ ἄντρου θεσπεσίοιο, the cave of the Naiads, with its looms of stone and its door for the gods (13.103–112). The same words describe the cave of the simile in which the souls of the suitors gibber like bats (24.6–9). See [[the-cave-of-the-naiads]] and [[possessions]].
