---
summary: Athena shows Odysseus his land: “and this is the vaulted cave where you used to offer / many perfect hecatombs to the nymphs” (13.349–350).
en: [vaulted]
---

σπέος … κατηρεφές, of the cave of the Naiads, as the Cyclops' cave was “high, and overhung with laurels” (9.183) and the wave that Poseidon raised over the raft “arching over him” (5.367; see the Odyssey translation's key words). With the deictic τοῦτο, “this,” the verse is the first mention of the cave in Athena's speech, for D. B. Monro takes the two verses before it as repeated wrongly in some manuscripts from 13.103–104 (*Homer's Odyssey, Books XIII–XXIV*, 1901, on 13.347–348; see the translation's note on 13.347–348).
