---
summary: Alcinous: “Clothes for the stranger lie in the polished chest, / and intricately worked gold and all the other / gifts that the counselors of the Phaeacians brought here” (13.10–12).
---

χρυσὸς πολυδαίδαλος is the gold of the guest-gifts of Book 8, which Arete laid in the chest with the clothing, “the clothing and the gold, which the Phaeacians had given him” (8.440; see [[possessions]] and [[chest]]). The nominative is new to the entry; Book 6 gave the epithet to Nausicaa's chamber (see the Book 6 note).
