---
summary: The two headlands of the harbor of Phorcys “keep off the great wave of the stormy winds / outside” (13.99–100).
---

ἀνέμων … δυσαήων, the winds in general, where the epithet is elsewhere given to Zephyrus (5.295, 12.289); the genitive plural is found only here and is new to the entry. D. B. Monro renders ἀνέμων κῦμα “the waves raised by the winds,” comparing *Iliad* 2.396 (*Homer's Odyssey, Books XIII–XXIV*, 1901, on 13.99), and cites δυσαήων as an instance of the lengthening that the poet allows when the verse requires it (Monro on 13.194; see the translation's note on 13.98–99).
