---
summary: At the farewell feast “among them a divine singer sang / Demodocus, honored by the people” (13.27–28).
grc: ["λαοῖσι τετιμένος"]
---

The singer's own verse-end, here in the nominative; the herald led him in as Δημόδοκον λαοῖσι τετιμένον at the supper of Book 8 (8.471–472; see the Book 8 note). It is his last appearance in the poem, and the honor is given him once more at the moment he sings for the departing guest (13.24–28; see [[demodocus]], [[divine]] and [[singers]]).
