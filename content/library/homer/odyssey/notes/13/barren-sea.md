---
summary: Odysseus asks why Athena did not tell Telemachus: “Was it so that he too, wandering, might suffer sorrows / over the barren sea, while others devour his substance?” (13.418–419).
---

πόντον ἐπʼ ἀτρύγετον is the half-verse of the nurse's fear for Telemachus in Book 2, “you have no need / to suffer evils on the barren sea or to wander” (2.369–370; see the Book 2 note): the father fears for his son what the nurse feared. Athena answers that the boy has no toil, but sits at his ease in the house of Menelaus (13.421–424; see [[telemachus]], [[the-journey-of-telemachus]] and [[sea]]).
