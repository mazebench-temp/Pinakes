---
summary: In the false tale, the son of Idomeneus is “swift-footed Orsilochus, who in wide Crete / outran toiling men with his swift feet” (13.260–261).
---

ἀνέρας ἀλφηστάς, the accusative, is new to the entry. As in Book 6, where the Phaeacians live “far from toiling men” (6.8), the word sets ordinary men against one who surpasses them, here a runner (see [[swift-footed]], [[swift]], [[orsilochus-son-of-idomeneus]] and [[the-first-cretan-tale]]).
