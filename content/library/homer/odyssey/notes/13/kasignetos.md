# πατροκασιγνήτῳ (13.342), first met here among the Odyssey's notes.
grc: [πατροκασιγνήτῳ]
en: [father's brother]
---

“But I did not wish to fight with Poseidon, / my father's brother, who has laid up a grudge against you in his heart, / angry because you blinded his dear son” (πατροκασιγνήτῳ, 13.341–343; see [[poseidon]], [[kotos]], [[cholos]] and [[athena]]). The reason is the one the narrator gave in Scheria, where she did not yet appear to him face to face, “for she respected / her father's brother” (πατροκασίγνητον, 6.329–331; see the Book 6 note), and now she gives it herself, answering his reproach that after Troy he never saw her on his ship (13.316–319). Apollo refuses to fight his uncle Poseidon in the Iliad for the same reason, out of respect for his father's brother (*Iliad* 21.468–469).
