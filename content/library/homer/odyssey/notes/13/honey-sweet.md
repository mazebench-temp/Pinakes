---
summary: For the last libation, “Pontonous mixed the wine sweet to the heart, / and served it out to all, going up to each in turn” (13.53–54).
---

13.53 is the verse of the libation that received the suppliant in Book 7 (7.182; see the Book 7 note); now the wine is poured for his escort home, and the libation goes to the blessed gods (13.50–56; see [[pontonous]], [[libation]], [[wine]] and [[blessed-gods]]).
