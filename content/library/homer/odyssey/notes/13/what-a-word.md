---
summary: Zeus to Poseidon: “Ah, shaker of the earth, wide in strength, what a thing you have said!” (13.140).
grc: ["οἷον ἔειπες"]
---

οἷον ἔειπες, the shorter exclamation, in the whole verse of *Iliad* 7.455; this is its only place in the Odyssey. The protest is not a refusal: Zeus tells Poseidon that the gods do not dishonor him and leaves the Phaeacians to him (13.141–145; see [[shaker-of-the-earth]]).
