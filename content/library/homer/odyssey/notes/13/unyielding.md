---
summary: Odysseus carries the gifts to the cave, “the gold and the unyielding bronze / and the well-made clothes” (13.368–369).
---

χρυσὸν καὶ ἀτειρέα χαλκόν: the accusative is new to the entry, the bronze of the gifts set beside the gold, as Hephaestus casts “unyielding bronze and tin / and precious gold and silver” into the fire (*Iliad* 18.474–475). See [[bronze]], [[gold-and-silver]] and [[possessions]].
