summary: Athena of Telemachus, “who went to Lacedaemon with its wide dancing floors, to Menelaus, / to learn the report of you” (13.414–415).

εὐρύχορον Λακεδαίμονα, here and when the goddess goes there at the beginning of Book 15 (15.1). The narrative of Book 4 calls the land “hollow Lacedaemon with its many ravines” (4.1), and the end of this book “shining Lacedaemon” (13.440; see [[lacedaemon]], [[brilliant]] and [[to-learn-of-his-fathers-homecoming]]).
