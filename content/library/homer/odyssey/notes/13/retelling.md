---
summary: Alcinous sees his father's prophecy fulfilled and tells it again: “Ah, truly the oracles spoken long ago have come home to me, / the oracles of my father” (13.172–178).
refs: [13.172-178]
---

The verses with which he told it the day before (8.564–571) come back with the father unnamed, with περικαλλέα, “beautiful,” for ἐυεργέα, “well-made,” and with the doubt of the last verse gone: “these things the god may bring to pass, / or they may be left unfulfilled” has become “and now it is all being fulfilled” (13.178; see [[the-migration-of-the-phaeacians]], [[perikalles]] and [[so-he-spoke]]).
