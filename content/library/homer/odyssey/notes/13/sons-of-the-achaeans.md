---
summary: Odysseus to Athena: “But this I know well, that before now you were kind to me, / while we sons of the Achaeans were fighting in Troy” (13.314–315).
---

υἷες Ἀχαιῶν at the verse-end, with the verb in the first person, πολεμίζομεν, “we were fighting”: Odysseus counts himself among the sons of the Achaeans (the verse again at 15.153). After the fall of the city, he says, he did not see her again until Scheria (13.316–323; see [[steep]], [[troy]] and [[athena]]).
