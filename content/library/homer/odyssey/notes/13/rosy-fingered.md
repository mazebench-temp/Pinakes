---
summary: “And when early-born Dawn appeared, the rosy-fingered, / they hurried to the ship, and brought the bronze” (13.18–19), the last morning on Scheria.
---

The morning of the thirty-fourth day of the action of the poem, by D. B. Monro's count (*Homer's Odyssey, Books XIII–XXIV*, 1901, on 13.18). The next dawn is told without the epithet, by the star that announces it (13.93–94; see [[dawn-line]] and [[early-born]]).
