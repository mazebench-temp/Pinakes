---
summary: Odysseus' farewell: “for now all is accomplished that my dear heart desired, / an escort and gifts of friendship; and may the heavenly gods / make them prosper for me” (13.40–42).
---

θεοὶ Οὐρανίωνες closed the verse in which he told Arete, and again Alcinous, that “the heavenly gods have given me many” sorrows (7.242 = 9.15; see the Book 7 and Book 9 notes). At the end of his stay the same gods are asked to bless the escort and the gifts (see [[pompe]], [[doron]] and [[olbos]]).
