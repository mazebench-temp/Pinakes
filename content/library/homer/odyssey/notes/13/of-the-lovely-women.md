---
summary: Athena will go “to Sparta of the lovely women / to call Telemachus, your dear son, Odysseus” (13.412–413).
---

Σπάρτην ἐς καλλιγύναικα, the only place where Homer gives the epithet to Sparta; the Iliad gives it to Hellas and to Achaea (*Iliad* 2.683, 3.75; see the Odyssey translation's formulas). Telemachus is still there, a guest in the house of Menelaus and Helen (Book 4; see [[sparta]], [[helen]] and [[the-journey-of-telemachus]]).
