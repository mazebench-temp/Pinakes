---
summary: Athena: “Truly young men in a black ship lie in ambush for him, / eager to kill him before he reaches his native land” (13.425–426).
---

The ambush that the suitors laid in Book 4, when Antinous asked for a swift ship and twenty companions to watch for Telemachus in the strait between Ithaca and Samos (4.669–672, 4.842–847); 13.426 is the verse of Book 4 (4.823; see the Odyssey translation's formulas, [[ambush]], [[the-suitors]] and [[telemachus]]). Athena does not think it will succeed (13.427–428).
