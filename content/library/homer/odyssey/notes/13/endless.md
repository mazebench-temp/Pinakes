---
summary: Athena's account of Ithaca: “For in it grows endless grain, and wine / grows too” (13.244–245).
---

σῖτος ἀθέσφατος is the plenty of a rugged island that is “not good for driving horses” and “not broad” (13.242–247; see [[ithaca]]). The Odyssey's other uses of the word are the sea that Poseidon stirred up against the raft, the wine that undid Elpenor and the long night of the tale (7.273, 11.61, 11.373; see the Book 7 and Book 11 notes).
