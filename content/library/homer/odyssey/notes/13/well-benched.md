---
summary: In the harbor of Phorcys, “within, without a mooring rope, there stay / the well-benched ships, once they have reached the measure of anchorage” (13.100–101).
---

νῆες ἐΰσσελμοι, any ships that come there, in the present tense of a description: the harbor is so sheltered that it needs no cable, like the harbor of the island of goats, “where there is no need of a cable” (9.136; see [[the-harbor-of-phorcys]], [[harbor]], [[stern-cables]] and [[phorcys-the-sea-god]]). ὅρμου μέτρον is “the measure of anchorage” (see the Odyssey translation's key words).
