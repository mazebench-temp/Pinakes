---
summary: Zeus to Poseidon: “My friend, this is what seems best to my heart” (13.154).
en: [My friend]
---

πέπον, the address of comrades and brothers in the Iliad, Agamemnon's to Menelaus and Ajax's to Teucer (*Iliad* 6.55, 15.437, 15.472), is Zeus' word to his brother as he gives him his counsel about the Phaeacians' ship (13.154–158; see the translation's note on 13.158, [[cloud-gatherer]] and [[poseidon]]). The translation renders it here “My friend,” as in Ajax's words to Teucer.
