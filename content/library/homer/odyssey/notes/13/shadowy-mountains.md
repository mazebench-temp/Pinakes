summary: The tale is over: “So he spoke, and they all fell hushed in silence, / and they were held by a spell throughout the shadowy halls” (13.1–2).
---

The two verses of the pause in the tale (11.333–334) now mark its end; κατὰ μέγαρα σκιόεντα is the verse-end of halls where men fall silent or lie down to sleep (10.479, 23.299; see [[the-tale-to-alcinous]], [[silence]] and [[thelgo]]).
