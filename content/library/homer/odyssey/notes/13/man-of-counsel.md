Φαιήκων βουληφόροι are the twelve kings who rule with Alcinous, each of whom brought the stranger a mantle, a tunic and a talent of gold (8.389–395); now each is to add a great tripod and a cauldron, to be repaid by a collection among the people (13.13–15; see [[possessions]], [[tripod]] and [[cauldron]]). In the Iliad the plural is said of the Trojan counselors who hold council with Hector by the mound of Ilus (*Iliad* 10.414–415); in the Odyssey the Cyclopes have no “assemblies that bring counsel” (9.112; see the Book 9 note). βουληφόροι is new to the entry in the Odyssey.
