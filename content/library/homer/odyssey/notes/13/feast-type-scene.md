summary: The last feast in the house of Alcinous: “And they went to the house of Alcinous and made ready a feast,” with an ox for Zeus and the song of Demodocus (13.23–28).
refs: [13.23-28]
---

The feast fills the day of waiting: “delighting in it; and among them a divine singer sang” is the verse of the singer at Menelaus' wedding feast (13.27 = 4.17), and the day ends at sunset with the libation instead of sleep (13.29–56; see [[sacrifice-type-scene]], [[sunset-lines]] and [[demodocus]]). The meal itself is in [[feast]].
