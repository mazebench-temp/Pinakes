---
summary: When Athena makes Odysseus a beggar, she puts around him, over his rags, “the great skin of a swift deer, / worn bare” (13.436–437).
passages:
  - 13.429-438 | Odysseus made a beggar
---

**The deerskin.** Athena touches Odysseus with her wand, withers his skin, destroys his hair and dims his eyes, and dresses him in a vile rag and a tunic, rent and dirty; “and around him she put the great skin of a swift deer, / worn bare; and she gave him a staff and a shabby pouch” (13.429–437; see [[odysseus-changed-into-a-beggar]], [[wand]], [[clothing]] and [[scepter]]). ψιλόν is “bare”, “the wool worn off” (D. B. Monro, *Homer's Odyssey, Books XIII–XXIV*, 1901, on 13.437). The skin keeps the deer's epithet, ταχείης, “swift”, the word of the swift deer whose fawn the eagle drops at Zeus' altar and whose young the lion seizes (*Iliad* 8.248, 11.113): the animal that runs is now an old hide on a beggar's back. Two skins are put on him in one passage: first “the skin / of an aged old man” over all his limbs, ἀμφὶ δὲ δέρμα, and then the deer's, ἀμφὶ δέ μιν μέγα δέρμα (13.431–432, 13.436).

**Skins for clothing.** The heroes of the Iliad's night put skins around them when they rise to go out. Agamemnon “put around him the blood-red skin of a lion, / a great tawny lion, reaching to his feet”, and Diomedes the same, in words that the beggar's deerskin echoes, ἀμφὶ … ἑέσσατο δέρμα λέοντος / αἴθωνος μεγάλοιο (*Iliad* 10.23–24, 10.177–178; see [[lion-skin]]); Menelaus covers his back with a leopard's skin and Dolon puts on the skin of a gray wolf (*Iliad* 10.29, 10.334; see [[leopard-skin]] and [[wolf-skin]]). The lion's skin is a warrior's; the deer's, worn bare, is a beggar's wrap, and in the Iliad the deer is the beast that runs away (see the general article and [[dog-eyes-deer-heart]]). The swineherd's hut has such hides: he spreads “the hide of a shaggy wild goat, / his own bedding” for the beggar to sit on, and takes up “the fleece of a large, well-fed goat” when he goes out at night to sleep by his swine (14.49–51, 14.530, a plain rendering; see [[goats]]).
