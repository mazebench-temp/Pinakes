# The entry's Κρονίδη* claims Κρονίδῃ (13.25).
---

**The son of Cronus.** On the last day in Scheria “the sacred might of Alcinous sacrificed an ox / to Zeus, lord of the dark clouds, son of Cronus, who rules over all” (13.24–25; see [[zeus]], [[son-of-cronus]] and [[lord-of-dark-clouds]]). Ζηνὶ κελαινεφέϊ Κρονίδῃ, ὃς πᾶσιν ἀνάσσει is a whole verse of the Odyssey, the one in which Odysseus sacrificed the Cyclops' ram to a Zeus who took no heed of the offering (9.552; see the Book 9 note and the translation's formula row). It is the only time Cronus is named in the book. When Poseidon, Cronus' other son, comes to complain of the Phaeacians, he calls his brother “Father Zeus” (13.128; see [[father-zeus]] and [[poseidon]]).
