---
summary: Alcinous sacrifices an ox “to Zeus, lord of the dark clouds, son of Cronus, who rules over all” (13.25).
---

The verse of the ram that Odysseus sacrificed to Zeus on the shore of the Cyclopes' island, a sacrifice the god did not heed (9.551–555). Here it is the king's sacrifice on the morning of the departure, before the feast at which Demodocus sings (13.24–28; see [[lord-of-dark-clouds]], [[sacrifice-type-scene]] and [[zeus]]).
