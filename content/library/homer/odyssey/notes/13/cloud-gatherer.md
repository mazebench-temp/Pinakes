---
summary: Twice “Answering him, Zeus the cloud-gatherer said” (13.139, 13.153), when Zeus answers Poseidon's complaint and then gives him his counsel about the Phaeacians' ship.
---

The first answer opens with the verse with which Zeus answered Poseidon's complaint about the Achaean wall, “Ah, shaker of the earth, wide in strength, what a thing you have said!” (13.140 = *Iliad* 7.455; see [[shaker-of-the-earth]], [[the-achaean-wall]] and [[earth-shaker]]). The second, “My friend” (13.154; see [[my-dear]]), gives the counsel of the ship turned to stone near the land, so that all men may marvel (13.154–158; see the translation's note on 13.158 and [[the-migration-of-the-phaeacians]]). The god of the storm raises no storm here; the punishment is left to the god of the sea (see [[zeus]] and [[poseidon]]).
