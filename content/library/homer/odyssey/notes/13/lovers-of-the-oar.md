---
summary: At sunset “at once he spoke among the Phaeacians, lovers of the oar, / and making it known to Alcinous most of all he said” (13.36–37).
---

The verse of the king's three speeches in Book 8 (8.96, 8.386, 8.535; see the Book 8 note) is spoken here of the guest: Odysseus, who has watched the sun all day, asks the seafarers for his escort and bids them farewell (13.28–46; see [[phaeacians]], [[alcinous]] and [[pompe]]).
