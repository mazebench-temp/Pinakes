summary: In the false tale the Phoenicians leave the Cretan on the shore: “And they went aboard for well-peopled Sidon / and were gone” (13.285–286).
grc: ["εὖ ναιομένην"]
---

εὖ ναιομένην, the participle of the Iliad's well-peopled cities (*Iliad* 5.489), here of Sidon, the home of the Phoenicians (see the Odyssey translation's formulas, and [[sidon]], [[phoenicians]] and [[the-first-cretan-tale]]).
