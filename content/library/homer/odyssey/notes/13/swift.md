ταχέεσσι πόδεσσιν, the speed of Achilles in the Iliad (*Iliad* 20.189, 21.564), given in the lie to a Cretan runner (see [[swift-footed]] and [[toiling-men]]). The deer is “swift” as in the Iliad (*Iliad* 11.113); its skin, “worn bare,” is part of the dress of an old beggar (13.436–437; see [[deer]], [[dressing-scene]] and [[shape-changing]]).
