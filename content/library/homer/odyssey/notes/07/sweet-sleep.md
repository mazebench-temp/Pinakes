summary: “And the sun was declining, and sweet sleep let me go” (7.289).

The half-verse of Agamemnon's waking from his dream (*Iliad* 2.71) ends the sleep among the leaves that lasted “all night long, and into the dawn and to midday” (7.288; see the translation's note on 7.289; [[sunset-lines]], [[boundless-sea]]).
