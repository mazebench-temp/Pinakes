---
summary: When the suppliant is seated in Laodamas' chair, the five verses serve him: “And a handmaid brought water for the hands in a pitcher and poured it” (7.172–176).
---

The third of the six uses, and the only one in which the guest has come as a suppliant: the meal follows the rebuke of Echeneus and comes before the libation to Zeus, “who attends on revered suppliants” (7.159–181; [[supplication-type-scene]], [[xenia]]). A scholium marking an athetesis of 7.174, because Homer does not have tables taken away while the diners are present, belongs to 7.232, where the handmaids clear away the gear of the feast (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 7.174; see the translation's note on 7.174).
