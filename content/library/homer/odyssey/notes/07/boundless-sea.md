---
summary: Odysseus of his first night on Scheria: “and a god poured boundless sleep over me” (7.286).
---

The adjective of the sea and the earth measures the sleep poured down over him. Merry reports that some take ὕπνῳ ὕπο at 4.295 as “under the covering of sleep,” comparing 5.492 and this verse (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 4.295). In Book 5 the narrator says that Athena poured sleep on his eyes (5.491–493); Odysseus says only “a god” (7.286; [[hypnos]], [[athena]], [[daimon]]).
