---
summary: The Phaeacian lords pour libations “to the keen-sighted slayer of Argus, / to whom they would pour the last libation, whenever they thought of bed” (7.137–138).
---

The title stands with [[keen-sighted]] as in the Iliad's last book (*Iliad* 24.24, 24.109) and in the Odyssey's first (1.38). Why the last libation of the night went to Hermes is discussed in [[hermes]].
