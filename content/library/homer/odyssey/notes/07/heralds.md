**The herald of the house.** Echeneus' advice names the heralds as the men who mix the wine of a libation (7.163–164), and Alcinous gives the order to his own: “And then the might of Alcinous spoke to the herald: / ‘Pontonous, mix a bowl and serve out wine / to all throughout the hall’” (7.178–180; see [[pontonous]], [[libation]] and [[mixing-bowl]]). The same verses send Pontonous round with the wine for a libation to Zeus on the day of the escort (13.49–52), and in Book 8 he sets the singer's chair against a pillar and hangs the lyre above it (8.65–67).
