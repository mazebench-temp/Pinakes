---
summary: Poseidon is “the earth-shaker” three times: the Phaeacians cross the great gulf “since the earth-shaker granted it to them” (7.35); “Nausithous first was born to Poseidon the earth-shaker / and Periboea” (7.56–57); and he raised the storm against the raft, “much misery, which Poseidon the earth-shaker roused against me” (7.271).
---

The god who gives the Phaeacians their seafaring and fathers their royal line is the god whose anger keeps Odysseus from home (1.20, 1.68–75; [[poseidon]], [[phaeacians]], [[nausithous]]).
