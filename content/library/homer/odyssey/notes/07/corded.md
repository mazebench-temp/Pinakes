---
summary: “So he lay sleeping there, much-enduring brilliant Odysseus, / on a corded bed beneath the loud-resounding colonnade” (7.344–345).
---

7.345 is the verse of Telemachus' bed at Pylos (3.399), and the next two follow those of Nestor's house: the master sleeps in the inner part of the high house, and the lady shares his bed (3.402–403, 7.346–347; [[beds-beneath-the-colonnade]], [[bed]]).
