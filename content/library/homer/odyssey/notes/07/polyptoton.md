en: [Pear upon pear, apple upon apple, fig upon fig]
---

Each noun is set against itself in another case, as in the Iliad's battle line, shield on shield and man on man (*Iliad* 13.130–131, 16.215); here the figure pictures fruit that ripens without end, the new growing as the old grows old (7.117–121; [[the-garden-of-alcinous]], [[trees]], [[vine]]).
