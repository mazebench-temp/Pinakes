---
summary: Odysseus prays that Alcinous may keep his promise: “Then his fame over the grain-giving earth / would be unquenchable” (7.332–333).
---

Merry takes it that the king's fame would spread throughout the world by the praise that Odysseus would give him in gratitude (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 7.332; [[kleos]], [[earth]]).
