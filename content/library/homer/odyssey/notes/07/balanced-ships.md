---
summary: “And Odysseus marveled at the harbors and the balanced ships” (7.43).
---

The ships are the first sight of the city, with the places of assembly and the long walls (7.43–45; [[the-city-of-the-phaeacians]], [[harbor]], [[thambos]]). Merry notes the imperfect θαύμαζεν, the wonder renewed at each new marvel (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 7.43).
