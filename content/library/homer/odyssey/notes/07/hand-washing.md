---
summary: Raised from the ashes, the stranger washes before he eats, in the verses of the welcome of guests (7.172–174 = 1.136–138, 4.52–54).
refs: [7.172-174]
---

When Alcinous has raised the suppliant and seated him in his son's chair, the meal of welcome follows in the verses of Telemachus' and Menelaus' halls: “And a handmaid brought water for the hands in a pitcher and poured it, / a fine golden one, over a silver basin, / for them to wash; and she drew up a polished table beside them” (7.172–174 = 1.136–138, 4.52–54; see [[water-for-the-hands]], [[basin-and-pitcher]] and [[supplication]]).
