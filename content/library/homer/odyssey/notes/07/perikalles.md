---
summary: Athena has given the Phaeacian women above all others “to be skilled in beautiful handiwork, and good wits” (7.110–111).
---

The verse is Antinous' of Penelope's gifts from Athena (2.116–117); here it praises the women at the looms, as skilled as the Phaeacian men at sailing (7.108–111; [[weaving]], [[athena]]).
