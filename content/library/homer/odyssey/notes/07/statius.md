---
summary: Statius, praising the villa of Manilius Vopiscus at Tibur, will not praise the twice-bearing orchards of Alcinous.
---

- “Why should I praise the twice-bearing orchards of Alcinous, and you, branches that never rose empty into the air?” (*quid bifera Alcinoi laudem pomaria*, *Silvae* 1.3.81–82, a plain rendering), the garden whose fruit never fails, in winter or in summer (on 7.112–132): [[the-garden-of-alcinous-in-later-writers]] and [[the-garden-of-alcinous]].
