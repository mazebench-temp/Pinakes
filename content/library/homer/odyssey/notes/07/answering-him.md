en: [answered him in turn]

Alcinous' verse is the Iliad's for Aeneas' answer to Achilles (*Iliad* 20.199), and it returns for him in Books 11 and 13 (11.347, 11.362, 13.3; see the Odyssey translation's formulas). His two answers are the blame of his daughter and the offer of her hand with the promise of the escort (7.299–301, 7.309–328; [[of-many-wiles]], [[alcinous]]).
