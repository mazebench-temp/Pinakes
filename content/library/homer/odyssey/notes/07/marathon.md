# Μαραθῶνα (7.80) is claimed by the entry.
---

**On the way to Athens.** Athena “left lovely Scheria, / and came to Marathon and to wide-streeted Athens, / and entered the well-built house of Erechtheus” (7.79–81; see [[athena]], [[scheria]], [[athens]] and [[erechtheus]]). The poem names the place and says nothing of it. The scholia report that Chaeris suspected these verses, and Merry thinks they were suspected as an addition by a later rhapsode to please Athenian feeling (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, critical note on 7.79–80; see the translation's note on 7.79–80). Strabo counts Marathon among the four towns of the Attic Tetrapolis, which Xuthus founded when he married the daughter of Erechtheus (*Geography* 8.7.1; see [[strabo]]).
