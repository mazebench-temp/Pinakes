---
summary: Poseidon “stirred up the endless sea” against the raft (7.273).
---

Merry reports the gloss of Apollonius' Homeric lexicon, “great, such as not even a god could tell, for its multitude,” and Düntzer's view that the α- is intensive, which would make ἀθέσφατος the same as θεσπέσιος, “wondrous” (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 7.273; [[wondrous]]). See [[the-shipwreck-of-odysseus]] and [[poseidon]].
