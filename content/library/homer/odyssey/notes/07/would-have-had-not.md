---
summary: Odysseus to Arete: “There, as I came out onto the land, the wave would have overpowered me, / dashing me against great rocks, a joyless place; / but I drew back and swam out again” (7.278–280).
grc: ["ἔνθα κέ μʼ ἐκβαίνοντα"]
---

In Book 5 the narrator told the danger on the rocks twice with “had not” and named Athena as the one who turned it (5.426–427, 5.436–437); in Odysseus' own account the sentence has no “had not,” and the turn is his: “but I drew back and swam out again.” Merry renders βιήσατο … κε “would have crushed me as I climbed out upon the shore” (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 7.278; [[retelling]], [[the-shipwreck-of-odysseus]]).
