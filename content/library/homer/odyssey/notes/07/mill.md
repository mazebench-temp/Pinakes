# "μήλοπα καρπόν", the grain they grind (7.104), found only here; μύλῃς and ἀλετρεύουσι are in the entry.
grc: ["μήλοπα καρπόν"]
en: [yellow grain]
---

“And he has fifty slave women in the house: / some grind the yellow grain at the mills” (7.103–104; see [[handmaids]] and [[the-house-of-alcinous]]). μήλοψ is “apple-hued”, that is, yellow, W. W. Merry explains, though others connect it with μαλός, “white”, “glistering” (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 7.104; see the translation's row μύλη, μήλοψ). Merry prints μύλης, “at the mill”, with the lexicon of Apollonius, and records μύλῃς, the reading of this text, and μύλοις; in the scene of the woman at the mill in Book 20 the singular μύλην seems to him to support μύλης, and the plural μύλαι to support μύλῃς (Merry's critical note on 7.104; 20.106, 20.111; see the translation's note on 7.104). The rest of the women weave at the looms and turn the yarn (7.105–107; see [[loom]], [[distaff]] and [[weaving]]).
