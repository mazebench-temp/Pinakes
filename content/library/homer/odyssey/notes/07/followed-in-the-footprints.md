summary: “So she spoke, and Pallas Athena led the way / swiftly; and he followed in the footprints of the goddess” (7.37–38).

The fourth and last use of the couplet: Athena, in the likeness of a young girl with a water jar, leads Odysseus through the city of the Phaeacians, who cannot see him in her mist (7.19–42; [[pallas]], [[athena]], [[achlys]]).
