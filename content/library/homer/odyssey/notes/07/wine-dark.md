---
summary: Odysseus to Arete: “when my swift ship, with a gleaming thunderbolt, / Zeus struck and shattered in the middle of the wine-dark sea” (7.249–250).
---

The verses are Calypso's account of the wreck to Hermes, told in the first person (5.131–132; [[the-shipwreck-of-odysseus]], [[thunderbolt]], [[retelling]]).
