---
summary: The epithet that Nausicaa gave her father, “my wise-hearted father” (6.256), is given in this book to the suppliant: Alcinous “took wise-hearted Odysseus of subtle wiles by the hand” (7.168).
---

δαΐφρων returns to Alcinous in Book 8 (8.8, 8.13, 8.56). Beside ποικιλομήτην, “of subtle wiles,” the sense is the second of the two the general article gives, “wise” ([[of-subtle-wiles]], [[alcinous]]).
