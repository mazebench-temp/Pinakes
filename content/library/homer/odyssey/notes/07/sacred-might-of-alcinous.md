The periphrasis first names the king when he acts as king, raising the suppliant at Echeneus' word and ordering the libation to Zeus, who attends on suppliants (7.167–171, 7.178–181; see the Odyssey translation's formulas; [[alcinous]], [[supplication-type-scene]], [[pontonous]]).
