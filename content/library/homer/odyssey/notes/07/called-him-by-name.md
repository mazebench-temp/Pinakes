---
summary: Odysseus answers Alcinous' promise: “and he spoke in prayer, and spoke to him, and called him by name” (7.330).
---

In its other uses in the Odyssey the half-verse follows a touch of the hand (4.610, 5.181); here it follows εὐχόμενος, “in prayer,” and the words that follow are addressed to Zeus and name Alcinous in the third person (7.331–333). A scholium preserves two other endings of the verse, εἶπε πρὸς ὃν μεγαλήτορα θυμόν and ἰδὼν εἰς οὐρανὸν εὐρύν (see the translation's note on 7.330; [[prayer-type-scene]], [[father-zeus]]).
