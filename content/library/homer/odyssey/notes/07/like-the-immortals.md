---
summary: Nausicaa comes home, “and her brothers stood / around her, like the immortals,” and unyoked the mules (7.4–6).
---

ἀθανάτοις ἐναλίγκιοι is formed like θεοῖς ἐναλίγκιος αὐδήν, “like the gods in his voice” (1.371; see the Odyssey translation's formulas). The king's sons, whom Nausicaa counted in Book 6 (6.62–65), do the unyoking for their sister (7.4–6; [[nausicaa]], [[kasignetos]], [[mules]], [[chariot-journey]]).
