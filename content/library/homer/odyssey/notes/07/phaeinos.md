---
summary: Alcinous “seated him on a shining chair, / making his son, manly Laodamas, stand up” (7.169–170).
grc: [φαεινοῦ]
---

Echeneus had asked for “a silver-studded chair” (7.162), and the king gives his guest the chair of his own son, as Nestor rises from his shining chair to welcome Patroclus (*Iliad* 11.645; [[silver-studded]], [[throne]], [[manly]]).
