---
summary: Odysseus to Alcinous: “I, for my part, / am not like the immortals, who hold the wide sky, / in form or stature, but like mortal men” (7.208–210).
---

The verse-end of the gods, τοὶ οὐρανὸν εὐρὺν ἔχουσιν (1.67, 5.169), answers Nausicaa's words to her handmaids, “but now he is like the gods who hold the wide sky” (6.243): the stranger whom she likened to the gods denies the likeness (7.199–210; [[immortals]], [[mortal-men]], [[divine-epiphany]]).
