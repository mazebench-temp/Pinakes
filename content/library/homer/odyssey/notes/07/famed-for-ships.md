---
summary: “But the Phaeacians famed for ships did not notice him / as he went through the city among them” (7.39–40).
grc: [ναυσικλυτοί]
---

ναυσίκλυτος, the Odyssey's epithet of the Phaeacians and once of the Phoenicians (8.191, 8.369, 13.166, 16.227; 15.415), comes after Athena's account of their ships (7.34–36), and Odysseus marvels at the harbors and the ships as he passes unseen (7.43; see the Odyssey translation's key words; [[phaeacians]], [[swift-ships]]).
