---
summary: Odysseus begins his supplication, “Arete, daughter of godlike Rhexenor” (7.146).
---

He names the queen by her father, as Athena had told him her descent (7.54–66; [[rhexenor]], [[arete-queen-of-the-phaeacians]], [[supplication]]). In Book 6 the word was the Phaeacians' own, “the godlike Phaeacians” (6.241).
