---
summary: Odysseus is “brilliant” in eight verses of the book, six times in the full formula “much-enduring brilliant Odysseus” (7.1, 7.133, 7.139, 7.177, 7.329, 7.344) and twice alone, “and brilliant Odysseus questioned her” (7.21) and “but brilliant Odysseus was left behind in the hall” (7.230).
---

The book gives the epithet to no one else. Its other titles for him are πολύμητις, in the verse of his three answers (7.207, 7.240, 7.302; [[of-many-wiles]]), and once the Iliad's verse-end “wise-hearted Odysseus of subtle wiles” (7.168; [[wise-hearted]], [[of-subtle-wiles]]). See [[much-enduring]] and [[odysseus]].
