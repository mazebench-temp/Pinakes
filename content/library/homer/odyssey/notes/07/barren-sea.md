summary: “So she spoke, and gray-eyed Athena went away / over the barren sea, and left lovely Scheria” (7.78–79).
---

πόντον ἐπʼ ἀτρύγετον is the half-verse of Odysseus looking out over the sea from Calypso's island (5.84 = 5.158); here the goddess crosses it, for Marathon and Athens (7.78–81; [[athena]], [[sea]], [[wide-streeted]]).
