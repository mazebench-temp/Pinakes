---
summary: Echeneus bids the heralds mix the wine “so that to Zeus who delights in thunder / we may pour libations too, who attends on revered suppliants” (7.164–165), and Alcinous gives the order to Pontonous in the same words (7.180–181).
---

The thunderer is named here as the god of suppliants, and the libation receives the stranger under his protection (7.159–183; [[a-suppliant-is-respected-by-the-gods]], [[supplication]], [[libation]], [[zeus]]). When Alcinous orders the wine again on the evening of the departure, the verse after the order to Pontonous asks for a prayer to father Zeus for the stranger's escort instead (13.50–52).
