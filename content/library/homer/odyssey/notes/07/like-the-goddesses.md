---
summary: Odysseus to Arete and Alcinous: “And I noticed your daughter's handmaids on the shore / playing, and she herself was among them, like the goddesses” (7.290–291).
---

In his speech to her he had likened her to Artemis (6.149–152); to her parents he gives her the Iliad's verse-end of a woman's beauty, and then the praise of her good sense, “she did not fall short of good sense” (7.292; [[nausicaa]], [[litotes]]).
