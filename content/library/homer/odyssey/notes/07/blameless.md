---
summary: Athena in the likeness of a girl: “since he lives near my noble father” (7.29); and Odysseus to Alcinous: “Hero, do not rebuke the noble girl for that” (7.303).
grc: [ἀμύμονα]
---

ἀμύμων of persons is “noble” in the Odyssey's English, as in the Iliad's (see the Odyssey translation's principles). Odysseus' praise of the girl who “did not fall short of good sense” (7.292) becomes her defense against her father's blame (7.298–307; [[nausicaa]], [[litotes]]).
