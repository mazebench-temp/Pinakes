---
summary: Rhexenor, Arete's father: “Him, when he had no son, Apollo, god of the silver bow, struck down, / newly married, in his hall” (7.64–65).
grc: [ἀργυρότοξος]
---

In the Odyssey Apollo's arrows are a sudden death: Menelaus' helmsman (3.279–280), and the old men of Syria, whom he comes with Artemis to kill (15.409–411). Rhexenor dies young, leaving one daughter, and his brother Alcinous marries her (7.63–66; see the translation's note on 7.64–65; [[rhexenor]], [[apollo]], [[arete-queen-of-the-phaeacians]]).
