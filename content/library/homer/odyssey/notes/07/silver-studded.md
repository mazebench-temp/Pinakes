---
summary: Echeneus to Alcinous: “But come, seat the stranger on a silver-studded chair, / raising him up” (7.162–163).
grc: [ἀργυροήλου]
---

The chair of an honored guest: Charis seats Thetis on a silver-studded throne in Hephaestus' house (*Iliad* 18.389), and Circe will seat Odysseus on one (10.314, 10.366). The text prints ἄλλʼ ἄγε, a slip for the ἀλλʼ ἄγε, “But come,” of the formula (see the translation's note on 7.162; [[throne]], [[echeneus]], [[phaeinos]]).
