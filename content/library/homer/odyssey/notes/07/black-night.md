---
summary: “and was borne along for nine days; and on the tenth black night / the gods brought me to the island of Ogygia” (7.253–254).
---

The verses are among those that, the scholia say, were athetized because the story is told later, where they return without “black” (12.447–448; see the translation's note on 7.251–258). Odysseus uses the nine days and the tenth black night again in his Cretan tale to Eumaeus, where the wave brings him to the land of the Thesprotians (14.314–315; [[ogygia]], [[the-shipwreck-of-odysseus]]).
