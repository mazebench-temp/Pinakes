---
summary: Athena “poured a mist / over him, a heaven-sent mist, in friendship in her heart” (7.41–42).
en: [heaven-sent]
---

ἀχλὺν / θεσπεσίην divides across two verses as in the Iliad, where Poseidon scatters “the heaven-sent mist” from Achilles' eyes (*Iliad* 20.341–342; see the Odyssey translation's key words). The mist flows back when Odysseus clasps Arete's knees, “the god-sent mist” (7.143; [[achlys]], [[thesphaton]], [[athena]]).
