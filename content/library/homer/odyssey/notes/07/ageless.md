---
summary: The gold and silver dogs that Hephaestus made to guard the house of Alcinous are “immortal and ageless all their days” (7.91–94), and Calypso promised Odysseus “that she would make me immortal and ageless all my days” (7.257).
---

7.257 is Calypso's own verse in Book 5 (5.136), and the offer is told once more in the last books (23.336). The craftsman god gives his dogs what the goddess offered the man and he refused (7.255–258; [[immortal-and-ageless]], [[immortality]], [[hephaestus]]).
