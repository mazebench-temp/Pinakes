---
summary: Odysseus is “not like the immortals” but “like mortal men” (7.209–210), and Calypso lives where no one mingles with her, “neither of the gods nor of mortal men” (7.247).
---

7.247 is the verse of Hephaestus' hidden years with Thetis and Eurynome in a cave by the stream of Oceanus, which no one knew of (*Iliad* 18.402–405; [[calypso]], [[ogygia]], [[immortals]]).
