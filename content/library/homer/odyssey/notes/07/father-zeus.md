The king's wish and the guest's prayer both call on Zeus by his title of father: Alcinous would keep the stranger as a son-in-law, but not against his will or the will of Zeus (7.311–316), and Odysseus answers with a prayer that the promise of the escort be kept (7.331–333; [[if-only-father-zeus]], [[prayer-type-scene]], [[zeus]]).
