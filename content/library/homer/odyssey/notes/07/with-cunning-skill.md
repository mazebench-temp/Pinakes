---
summary: The golden and silver dogs “which Hephaestus had made with his cunning skill / to guard the house of great-hearted Alcinous” (7.92–93).
---

The formula of Hephaestus' craft in the Iliad, for the shield of Achilles and the halls of Olympus (*Iliad* 18.482, 20.12), is said here of the guardians of a mortal king's house ([[hephaestus]], [[dogs]], [[the-house-of-alcinous]]).
