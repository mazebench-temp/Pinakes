---
summary: Periboea, the mother of Nausithous by Poseidon, is “most beautiful in form of women” (7.57).
---

The Iliad's formula praises the fairest of a king's daughters (θυγατρῶν εἶδος ἀρίστη); here it is said of women, and the next verse makes her a daughter after all, “the youngest daughter of great-hearted Eurymedon” (7.58; [[periboea-mother-of-nausithous]], [[eurymedon-king-of-the-giants]]).
