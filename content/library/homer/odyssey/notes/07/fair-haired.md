---
summary: Alcinous: the Phaeacians once “carried fair-haired Rhadamanthys / to visit Tityus, the son of Earth” (7.323–324).
grc: ["ξανθὸν Ῥαδάμανθυν"]
---

Rhadamanthys has the epithet in Proteus' prophecy of the Elysian plain, where he lives (4.564; [[rhadamanthus]], [[elysium]]). Merry finds no clue to the legend of this voyage (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 7.323; see the translation's note on 7.323–324 and [[tityus]]).
