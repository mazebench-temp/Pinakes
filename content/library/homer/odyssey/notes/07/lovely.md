ἐραννός is used in the Iliad of Calydon (*Iliad* 9.531, 9.577); Merry relates it to ἐράω as Latin *amoenus* to *amare*, and explains the double ν from ἐρασ-νός (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 7.18). ἐρατεινός, the commoner word, is said of Scheria ([[scheria]], [[the-city-of-the-phaeacians]]).
