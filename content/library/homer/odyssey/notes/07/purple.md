summary: Arete has her handmaids throw fine purple blankets on the guest's bed in the colonnade (7.336–337).
# "πορφύρεʼ ἐμβαλέειν" (7.337) is in the Book 4 note; its English "purple" stands on the line before.
refs: [7.336]
---

“to set beds beneath the colonnade, and throw fine purple / blankets on them” (7.336–337; see [[blankets]], [[bed]] and [[arete-queen-of-the-phaeacians]]), the verses of the beds for the guests at Sparta and for Priam in Achilles' hut (4.297–298; *Iliad* 24.644–645; see the Book 4 note). Purple is the color of the fine cloth of a great house, as of the yarn that Arete spins at her hearth (6.53, 6.306; see the Book 6 note and [[distaff]]).
