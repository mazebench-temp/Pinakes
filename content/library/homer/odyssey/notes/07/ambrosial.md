---
summary: “And I fell out of the water, gathering my spirit, and the ambrosial night / came on” (7.283–284).
---

ἐπὶ δʼ ἀμβροσίη νὺξ / ἤλυθε divides across two verses the half-verse of Menelaus' nights on Proteus' island (4.429, 4.574); the ambrosial night brings the sleep that a god pours over him (7.286; [[sweet-sleep]], [[boundless-sea]]).
