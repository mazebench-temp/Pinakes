---
summary: Athena leaves Scheria “and came to Marathon and to wide-streeted Athens, / and entered the well-built house of Erechtheus” (7.80–81).
---

The Iliad's epithet of Troy is given here, for the only time in Homer, to Athens; the passage was suspected in antiquity, probably as an addition to please Athenian feeling (see the translation's note on 7.79–80; [[athens]], [[marathon]], [[erechtheus]], [[athena-and-erechtheus]]).
