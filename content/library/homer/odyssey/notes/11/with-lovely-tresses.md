---
summary: The wind behind the ship is sent by “Circe of the lovely tresses, a dread goddess with human voice” (11.8).
---

The verse is Circe's introduction in the last book (11.8 = 10.136). Here the text prints εὐπλόκαμος, where the same verse reads ἐυπλόκαμος at 10.136 and 12.150, and εὐπλόκαμ- occurs nowhere else in the Odyssey: a slip, left as printed (see the translation's note on 11.8, and [[dread-goddess]] and [[with-human-voice]]).
