---
summary: Honey and milk, μελίκρητον, is the first of the drink offerings that Odysseus pours for the dead around the pit (11.27).
---

“And around it poured a drink offering to all the dead, / first with honey and milk, then with sweet wine, / and the third time with water” (11.26–28; see [[the-offering-to-the-dead]], [[honey]] and [[wine]]), the order that Circe gave him (10.518–520).
