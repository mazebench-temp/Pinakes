---
summary: Iphimedeia bore “godlike Otus and far-famed Ephialtes,” the tallest and handsomest men the earth ever reared, “after famous Orion” (11.308–310).
grc: [τηλεκλειτόν, "κλυτὸν Ὠρίωνα"]
en: [far-famed]
---

τηλεκλειτός, “famed far and wide,” the Iliad's epithet of the Trojans' allies, is given to one of the giant sons of Aloeus, and κλυτός to the hunter whom Odysseus will see among the dead (11.572–575; see [[otus]], [[ephialtes]] and [[orion]]).
