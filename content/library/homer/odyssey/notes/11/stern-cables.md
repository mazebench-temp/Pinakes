---
summary: When the dead throng about him, Odysseus hurries back to the ship and orders his companions to go on board and loose the stern-cables (11.636–637).
---

αὐτίκʼ ἔπειτʼ ἐπὶ νῆα κιὼν ἐκέλευον ἑταίρους / αὐτούς τʼ ἀμβαίνειν ἀνά τε πρυμνήσια λῦσαι, “At once then I went to the ship and ordered my companions / to go on board themselves and loose the stern-cables” (11.636–637; see [[ships]] and [[rowing-benches]]), where the second verse is the order of departure from the land of the Cyclopes (9.178, 9.562). The book that began with a launching (11.1–11) ends with the ship loosed from the shore.
