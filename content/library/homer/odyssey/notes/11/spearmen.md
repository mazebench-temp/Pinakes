---
summary: Odysseus to Ajax: “and no one else / is to blame, but Zeus, who hated the army of the Danaan spearmen / terribly, and set your fate upon you” (11.558–560).
---

Merry compares the turn “no one else is to blame, but …” with Hephaestus' words about his parents (8.311–312) and the evil fate that Zeus “set” on Helen and Paris (*Iliad* 6.357; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.559; see [[aitios]] and [[far-seeing]]).
