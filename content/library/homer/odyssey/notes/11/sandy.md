---
summary: Neleus lived “in sandy Pylos” (11.257); and Agamemnon asks whether Orestes is alive “perhaps in Orchomenus, or in sandy Pylos” (11.459).
grc: [ἠμαθόεντι]
---

Πύλῳ ἠμαθόεντι, the dative of the verse-end, twice in the book: the home of Tyro's son Neleus, and the place where Agamemnon imagines his son may be, with Nestor (see [[pylos]], [[neleus]] and [[give-me-an-exact-account]]).
