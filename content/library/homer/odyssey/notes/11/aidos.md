# αἰδοιότερος (11.360).
grc: [αἰδοιότερος]
en: [more respected]
---

αἰδοῖος is the man who is owed αἰδώς, regard, and the comparative measures what gifts bring home: “and I would be more respected and dearer to men, / by all who should see me come home to Ithaca” (καί κʼ αἰδοιότερος καὶ φίλτερος ἀνδράσιν εἴην, 11.360–361; see [[shameful-to-stay-long]], [[doron]] and [[philos]]). καί introduces the second advantage he would secure by staying (W. W. Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.360).
