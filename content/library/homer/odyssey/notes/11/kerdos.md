---
summary: Odysseus tells Alcinous that if they kept him a year and gave him more gifts, “it would be far better / to come to my dear native land with a fuller hand” (11.358–359).
---

The gain is honor at home: with a fuller hand he would be “more respected and dearer to men” who saw him return to Ithaca (11.358–361; see [[shameful-to-stay-long]], [[doron]] and [[nostos]]). The apodosis is introduced by καί, as in Teiresias' “you may still reach Ithaca” (11.111), and Merry notes how bad a thing it was to come home empty-handed, as the companions complained before they opened the skin of the winds (10.42; W. W. Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.358; see [[skin-of-the-winds]]).
