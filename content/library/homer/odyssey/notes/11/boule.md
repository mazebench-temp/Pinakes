---
summary: The plans of the book: “the plan of Zeus was being fulfilled” when the seer of Pylos was freed (11.297); Oedipus suffered “through the deadly plans of the gods” (11.276), and Zeus has hated the offspring of Atreus “through the plans of women” (11.437); Odysseus asks for Penelope's “plan and her thought” (11.177), plans how to question the women (11.229–230), and came to Teiresias for “some plan” for his way home (11.479). βούλομαι is “prefer” in Achilles' “I would rather” (11.489).
---

**The plans of gods and women.** The proem's phrase closes the story of the seer and the cattle of Iphiclus: he was released “when he had told all the oracles; and the plan of Zeus was being fulfilled” (Διὸς δʼ ἐτελείετο βουλή, 11.297; see [[plan-of-zeus]] and [[melampus]]). Oedipus ruled the Cadmeans “through the deadly plans of the gods” (11.276), the phrase going with his sufferings (W. W. Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.275; see [[oedipus-kills-laius]]). Odysseus answers Agamemnon that Zeus has hated the house of Atreus “through the plans of women” (γυναικείας διὰ βουλάς, 11.437), διά with the accusative marking the means: Zeus used the intrigues of Helen and Clytemnestra to work out his enmity (Merry on 11.437; see [[there-is-no-trusting-women]]).

**The plans of men.** Odysseus asks his mother about his wife's “plan and her thought” (βουλήν τε νόον τε, 11.177; see [[penelope]] and [[noos]]); at the pit he “planned how I might question each one” of the women, and “this plan seemed to me in my heart the best” (11.229–230; see [[decision-scene]]); and he tells Achilles that he came to consult Teiresias “in case he might tell me some plan / how I might come to rocky Ithaca” (11.479–480; see [[teiresias]]). Neoptolemus spoke first “whenever we took counsel about the city of Troy” (11.510; see [[neoptolemus]]). The verb βούλομαι is a preference: Odysseus “would want” a year among the Phaeacians for more gifts (11.358), and Achilles “would rather” serve another for hire (11.489; see [[rather-a-hired-laborer]]).
