# Ἰκάρι* (in the entry) claims Ἰκαρίοιο (11.446).
---

κούρη Ἰκαρίοιο, περίφρων Πηνελόπεια, “the daughter of Icarius, prudent Penelope” (11.446), is the verse that names Penelope in the Odyssey's first book (1.329) and often after (see [[penelope]] and [[periphron]]). Here it ends Agamemnon's praise of her good sense, the one exception he makes to his warning against wives (11.444–446; see [[agamemnon]]).
