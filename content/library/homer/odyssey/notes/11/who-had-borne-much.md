---
summary: “brides and young unmarried men and old men who had borne much” (11.38), the first of the classes of the dead that gather to the pit.
---

The list of brides, young men, old men, maidens and warriors (11.38–41) was rejected by some ancient critics, since the souls afterward come one at a time (see [[slain-in-battle]]); Merry thinks the reasons hardly conclusive, and reports Bergk's proposal to move the lines to the end of the book, after the countless tribes of the dead gather (11.632; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.38–43; see the translation's note on 11.38–43).
