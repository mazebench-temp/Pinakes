---
summary: Odysseus: it would be far better “to come to my dear native land with a fuller hand” (11.359); and Agamemnon: “secretly, and not openly, to your dear native land / bring your ship to shore” (11.455–456).
---

The goal of the return, twice in the book: the profit of a later homecoming, and the danger of an open one (see [[lay-it-to-heart]] and [[nostos]]).
