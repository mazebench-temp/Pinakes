summary: Each of the women at the pit “declared her own parentage” (11.234), Tyro saying that she was “the offspring of noble Salmoneus” (11.236); and Odysseus tells Agamemnon that Zeus has hated “the offspring of Atreus” through the plans of women (11.436–437).
---

The catalogue of women is a catalogue of descent. Odysseus questions the women one at a time, “and each / declared her own parentage” (ὃν γόνον ἐξαγόρευεν, 11.233–234; see [[catalogue-of-heroines]]), and the first, Tyro, “said that she was the offspring of noble Salmoneus” (ἔκγονος, 11.236; see [[tyro]] and [[salmoneus]]). The same word names a cursed line: “far-seeing Zeus has hated the offspring of Atreus / terribly, through the plans of women” (γόνον Ἀτρέος, 11.436–437), Helen's and Clytemnestra's, which Zeus used as the means of working out his enmity (W. W. Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.437; see [[atreus]] and [[atreidae]]).
