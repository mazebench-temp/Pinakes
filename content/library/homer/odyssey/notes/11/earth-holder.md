---
summary: “And in his likeness the earth-holder, the shaker of the earth, / lay with her at the mouth of the eddying river” (11.241–242).
---

The god takes the likeness of the river Enipeus, whom Tyro loved, and the wave stands around them like a mountain to hide them (11.238–244; see [[tyro]], [[shaker-of-the-earth]] and [[eddying]]).
