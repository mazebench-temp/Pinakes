summary: Odysseus addresses the king twice in the pause: “Powerful Alcinous, preeminent among all the people” (11.355, 11.378).

The verse of address with which Odysseus began his tale (9.2), and of the games (8.382 = 11.355 = 11.378): it opens his answer about the gifts and his offer to tell of more sorrows (see [[preeminent]], [[alcinous]] and [[the-tale-to-alcinous]]).
