---
summary: Achilles to Odysseus: “hard man, what still greater deed will you devise in your mind? / How did you dare to come down to Hades” (11.474–475).
---

σχέτλιε, the reproach of a man who will not stop, here half in wonder. Merry takes it as “daring,” almost “foolhardy,” and the question as “What still mightier feat wilt thou devise?”, mightier than this descent to Hades (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.474; see [[of-many-devices]] and [[hades]]).
