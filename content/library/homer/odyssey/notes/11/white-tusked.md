---
summary: Agamemnon: “and around me my other companions / were killed unceasingly, like white-tusked hogs / that are slaughtered in the house of a rich man of great power” (11.412–414).
grc: [ἀργιόδοντες]
---

The swine of a rich man's feast, in the simile that turns the murder at the feast into the slaughter of the feast's own beasts (11.413–415; see [[the-murder-of-agamemnon]] and [[boar]]).
