---
summary: “And I saw Tityus, the son of glorious Earth” (11.576); and Odysseus wished to see “Theseus and Peirithous, glorious children of the gods” (11.631).
grc: [ἐρικυδέος]
---

ἐρικυδής, of gifts and feasts elsewhere, is given here to the mother of Tityus and to the sons of gods whom Odysseus did not see (see [[tityus]], [[earth]], [[theseus]] and [[peirithous]]). Hereas the Megarian, as Plutarch reports him, said that Peisistratus inserted 11.631 (see the translation's note on 11.631).
