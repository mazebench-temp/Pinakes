# Μενελά* (in the entry) claims Μενελάῳ (11.460).
---

“or perhaps with Menelaus in broad Sparta” (11.460; see [[sparta]], [[agamemnon]] and [[orestes]]): the dead king thinks of his brother's house as one of the places where his son might have found refuge, far from Mycenae (W. W. Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.458). He does not know that Menelaus was still wandering: he came home with his ships full of possessions only in the eighth year, on the very day that Orestes gave the funeral feast for Aegisthus (3.306–312; see [[the-wanderings-of-menelaus]]).
