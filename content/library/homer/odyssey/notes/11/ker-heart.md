# κηρόθι (11.208; again 5.284, 9.480 and after).
grc: [κηρόθι]
en: [in my heart]
---

κηρόθι μᾶλλον, “in the heart all the more”, ends the verse of Odysseus' grief at the failed embrace: “and three times she flew from my hands like a shadow or a dream, / and sharp grief rose in my heart all the more” (11.207–208; see [[achos]], [[anticleia]] and [[the-way-of-mortals]]). γενέσκετο is an iterative aorist, the grief rising at each of the three attempts (W. W. Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.208). The same ending carries anger elsewhere, Poseidon's at the raft (5.284) and the Cyclops' (9.480; see [[cholos]]).
