---
summary: Poseidon names himself to Tyro: “but I, you must know, am Poseidon the earth-shaker” (11.252).
---

He has told her to keep silent and not to name him (11.251), and then gives her the name with its epithet (see [[called-him-by-name]], [[tyro]] and [[poseidon]]). Earlier in the story the narrator calls him by his other titles, “the earth-holder, the shaker of the earth” (11.241; see [[earth-holder]]).
