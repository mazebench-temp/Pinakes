---
summary: Named among the three sons whom Tyro bore to Cretheus, “Aeson and Pheres and Amythaon” (11.259), his only mention in the Odyssey.
---

The father of Admetus, whom the Iliad names only in the patronymic of his grandson Eumelus (*Iliad* 2.763, 23.376; see [[eumelus]]), here takes his place in Tyro's family, the half-brother of Pelias, whose daughter Alcestis married his son (*Iliad* 2.713–715; see [[tyro]], [[pelias]] and [[alcestis]]).
