# ψεύδεα (11.366; again 14.296, 19.203).
grc: [ψεύδεα]
en: [lies]
---

The lies are those of travelers who tell what no one can check (ψεύδεά τʼ ἀρτύνοντας ὅθεν κέ τις οὐδὲ ἴδοιτο, 11.366; see [[eperopeuo]] and [[truth-and-deception]]). The king's compliment is pointed, since the tale it praises is itself told of a place no living man could visit, and the poem will soon show the teller “saying many lies like the truth” to his wife (19.203, a plain rendering; see [[odysseus]]).
