# δαίμονος (11.61; again 15.261).
grc: [δαίμονος]
en: [from a god]
---

Both uses are of an unnamed power. Elpenor's δαίμων is “quite vaguely, some evil power”, and not Circe (11.61; W. W. Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.61; see [[aisa]] and [[elpenor]]). In Tantalus' lake the water vanishes every time the old man stoops, the black earth appears about his feet, “and a god dried it up” (καταζήνασκε δὲ δαίμων, 11.585–587; see [[tantalus]] and the Odyssey translation's note on 11.586–588).
