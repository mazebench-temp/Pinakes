---
summary: “and she came to the limits of deep-streaming Oceanus” (11.13).
---

πείραθʼ … βαθυρρόου Ὠκεανοῖο: Merry renders “Ocean's bounding-line,” the boundary that Oceanus makes to the land of the living, not the farthest edge of Oceanus itself (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.13; see the Odyssey translation's key words, πείρατα Ὠκεανοῖο). The epithet of the river around the earth stands at the place where the voyage reaches the world of the dead (see [[oceanus]] and [[sunset-lines]]).
