---
summary: Named with his brother Amphion as the son of Antiope and Zeus who first founded Thebes and fenced it with towers (11.262–265).
---

The two brothers are named in one verse, “Amphion and Zethus”, and share every verb that follows, ἔκτισαν, πύργωσαν, ἐδύναντο, with the dual κρατερώ περ ἐόντε, “mighty though they were” (11.262–265; see [[amphion-of-thebes]], [[antiope]] and [[thebes]]).
