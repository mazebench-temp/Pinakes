---
summary: “Most glorious son of Atreus, Agamemnon, lord of men” (11.397).
---

The Iliad's verse of address to Agamemnon (*Iliad* 2.434 and often; see the Odyssey translation's formulas), spoken to his soul by the man who had used it to him at Troy, when he brought back Achilles' refusal (*Iliad* 9.677; see [[son-of-atreus]] and [[lord-of-men]]).
