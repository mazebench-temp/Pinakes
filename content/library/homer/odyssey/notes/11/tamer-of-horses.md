---
summary: “Castor, tamer of horses, and Polydeuces, good with his fists” (11.300).
grc: [ἱππόδαμον]
---

The whole verse is the one in which Helen, on the wall of Troy, looks in vain for her brothers (11.300 = *Iliad* 3.237; see [[good-with-his-fists]], [[life-giving-earth]] and [[castor]]). Merry counts the epithet three times in the Odyssey against forty-five in the Iliad (on 3.17; see the Book 3 note).
