---
summary: In the pause in the hall, “Answering him, Odysseus of many wiles said” (11.354, 11.377), twice to Alcinous.
---

The only places in the book where the poet names Odysseus, since the rest is his own tale; the verse is the one with which he answers Alcinous in Book 7 (11.354 = 11.377 = 7.207; see [[answering-him]] and [[the-tale-to-alcinous]]).
