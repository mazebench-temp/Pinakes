summary: Elpenor asks for “a mound for me on the shore of the gray sea, / the mound of an unhappy man, even for men to come to hear of” (11.75–76).

πολιῆς ἐπὶ θινὶ θαλάσσης, the sea by the shore, where the companions bury him in the next book, “where the headland jutted out farthest” (12.11; see [[tomb]], [[fame-and-the-tomb]] and [[elpenor]]).
