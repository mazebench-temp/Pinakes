summary: Twenty-two speeches close with the formula in the book, nine of them with Odysseus' “So I spoke”; the soul of Teiresias goes back “So saying” (11.150), and the narrator's own “So he spoke, and they all fell hushed in silence” ends the first part of the tale (11.333).
grc: ["ὣς φαμένη"]
---

Every close in the book but one is Odysseus' own, since he is telling the tale; the exception is the verse with which the poet takes up the narrative at the pause in the hall, the verse that fell on the Phaeacians after Odysseus' challenge in the games (11.333 = 8.234; see [[silence]] and [[the-tale-to-alcinous]]). ὣς φαμένη is new to the entry, of the soul (ψυχή) of the seer (11.150). The closes of Odysseus' own speeches to the dead are followed by their answers (see [[answering-him]]), and once by silence: “So I spoke, and he answered me nothing” (11.563), when Ajax turns away (see [[meeting-with-the-dead]]).
