---
summary: Odysseus asks whether his mother died of a long sickness, “or did Artemis of the showering arrows / come upon you with her gentle arrows and kill you?” (11.172–173), and she answers that it was not “the keen-sighted goddess of the showering arrows” (11.198).
---

The second time the goddess is not named, only her two epithets (see [[keen-sighted]], [[gentle-arrows]] and [[artemis]]).
