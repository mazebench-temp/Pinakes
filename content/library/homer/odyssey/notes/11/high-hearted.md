---
summary: Odysseus saw “Megara, the daughter of high-hearted Creon, / whom the son of Amphitryon, ever unyielding in his might, had as wife” (11.269–270).
---

Merry distinguishes this Creon, king of Thebes and father of Heracles' first wife, from the Creon of the story of Oedipus (see the translation's note on 11.269, and [[creon-of-thebes]] and [[unyielding]]).
