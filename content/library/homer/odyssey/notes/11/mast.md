---
summary: At the launching the companions set the mast and the sails in the black ship for the voyage to the limits of Oceanus (11.3).
---

ἐν δʼ ἱστὸν τιθέμεσθα καὶ ἱστία νηὶ μελαίνῃ, “and we set the mast and the sails in the black ship” (11.3; see [[sails]] and [[ships]]): Circe had told Odysseus to “set up your mast and spread the white sails” for the voyage (10.506), and he does so. The ἱστόν of this line is the mast, not the loom, which excepts it (see [[loom]]).
