---
summary: Artemis slew Ariadne “in sea-washed Dia, on the testimony of Dionysus” (11.325).
---

The fourth island of the epithet, after Ogygia and before the island of the Sun (see the general article). Merry takes Dia for the islet off Heracleion, on the north coast of Crete, which Theseus would pass on the way to Athens, and notes that this differs from the common story of Ariadne deserted on Dia, that is, Naxos (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.322; see the translation's note on 11.325, and [[ariadne]] and [[dia]]).
