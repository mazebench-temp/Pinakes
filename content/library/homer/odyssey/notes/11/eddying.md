---
summary: Poseidon “lay with her at the mouth of the eddying river” (11.242).
---

ἐν προχοῇς ποταμοῦ … δινήεντος: Merry takes προχοαί for the river's outfall, and notes that the phrase is used loosely, since the Enipeus does not run into the sea, where the sea god would come to it, but into the Peneus (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.242; see [[tyro]] and [[divine]]).
