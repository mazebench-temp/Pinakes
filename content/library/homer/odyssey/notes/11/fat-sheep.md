---
summary: “and find grazing there the cattle and fat sheep / of the Sun” (11.108–109).
---

βόας καὶ ἴφια μῆλα, the wealth of a household, here the flocks and herds of a god, which the companions must leave unharmed if they are to come home (11.110–113; see [[who-sees-all-things]], [[the-cattle-of-helios]] and [[cattle]]).
