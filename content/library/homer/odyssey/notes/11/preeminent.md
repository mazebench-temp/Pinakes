---
summary: “Powerful Alcinous, preeminent among all the people” (11.355, 11.378); and the soul of Achilles goes off “glad because I said that his son was preeminent” (11.540).
grc: [ἀριδείκετον]
---

ἀριδείκετος, “conspicuous,” twice in the formula of address to the king (see [[powerful]]), and once in Odysseus' praise of Neoptolemus, which sends Achilles' soul striding across the meadow of asphodel (11.538–540; see [[neoptolemus]] and [[achilles]]). Merry renders γηθοσύνη ὅ “rejoicing that” (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.540).
