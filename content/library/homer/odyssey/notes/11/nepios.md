---
summary: Agamemnon remembers Telemachus as “an infant” at his mother's breast when they left for the war (11.448–449).
---

“Truly we left her a young bride / when we went to the war; and she had a child at her breast, / an infant, who now, I think, sits among the number of men, / happy one!” (11.447–450; see [[telemachus]] and [[penelope]]). νήπιος here is the plain sense, a small child, set against the man he is now, and against Agamemnon's own son, whom his wife did not let him see (11.452–453; see [[orestes]]).
