summary: Anticleia: “it was not the keen-sighted goddess of the showering arrows who in my halls / came upon me with her gentle arrows and killed me” (11.198–199).
grc: [ἐύσκοπος]
---

ἐύσκοπος ἰοχέαιρα, the only place where the epithet of Hermes is given to Artemis (see the general article, and [[of-the-showering-arrows]] and [[gentle-arrows]]). Murray prints the word without the diaeresis of the Iliad's text.
