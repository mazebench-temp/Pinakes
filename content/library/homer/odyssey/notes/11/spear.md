# "δόρυ χαλκοβαρές" (11.532), a phrase, since δόρυ alone is also timber and the wooden horse (8.507); it applies in the
# Odyssey only.
grc: ["δόρυ χαλκοβαρές"]

**The dead of war.** The souls that come out of Erebus include “many stabbed by bronze-fitted spears, / men slain in battle, wearing their armor clotted with blood” (11.40–41; see [[bronze-fitted]], [[slain-in-battle]] and [[the-offering-to-the-dead]]): the dead keep the wounds and the arms of their deaths.

**Neoptolemus' spear.** Inside the wooden horse Neoptolemus “kept feeling for the hilt of his sword / and his spear heavy with bronze, and he had evil in mind for the Trojans” (11.531–532; see [[neoptolemus]], [[sword]], [[the-wooden-horse]] and [[heavy-with-bronze]]).
