---
summary: Odysseus sees huge Orion herding the beasts he had killed across the meadow of asphodel, “holding in his hands a club all of bronze, forever unbroken” (11.572–575).
---

“And after him I caught sight of huge Orion / herding together wild beasts across the meadow of asphodel, / beasts that he himself had killed on the lonely mountains, / holding in his hands a club all of bronze, forever unbroken” (11.572–575; see [[orion]], [[the-meadow-of-asphodel]] and [[bronze]]). ῥόπαλον παγχάλκεον, αἰὲν ἀαγές is a hunter's club, not the war mace of Areithous (*Iliad* 7.141), and it is all of bronze where the Cyclops' club was green olive wood (9.319–320). Like Minos, W. W. Merry notes, Orion goes on with his customary pursuits in Hades, a phantom hunter driving before him the shadows of the beasts he once hunted on the hills (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.572).
