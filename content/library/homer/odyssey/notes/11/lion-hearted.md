---
summary: “Heracles, bold in endurance, lion-hearted” (11.267).
---

The same pair of epithets as in Tlepolemus' boast (*Iliad* 5.639). Merry notes that θυμολέων is used of Odysseus himself in the Odyssey (4.724, 4.814; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.267; see [[bold-in-endurance]] and [[heracles]]).
