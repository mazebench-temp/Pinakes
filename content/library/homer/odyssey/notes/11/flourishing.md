summary: The companions go aboard “grieving, shedding big tears” (11.5); Agamemnon “wept loudly, letting big tears fall” (11.391); and Odysseus and Agamemnon stand “grieving, shedding big tears” (11.466).
---

ἀχνύμενοι θαλερὸν κατὰ δάκρυ χέοντες, the verse-end with which the companions went down to the ship at the end of the last book (10.570), opens and closes the book's grief: the departure for the dead, and the end of the talk with Agamemnon; it returns at the burial of Elpenor (12.12; see [[tears]] and [[meeting-with-the-dead]]).
