---
summary: Neleus would give Pero to no one “who did not drive the broad-browed cattle with curving horns / out of Phylace, the cattle of mighty Iphiclus” (11.289–290).
---

ἕλικας βόας εὐρυμετώπους, two epithets of cattle in one verse, as of the cattle of the Sun in the next book (12.355; see [[broad-browed]] and [[cattle]]). Merry takes ὃς μὴ … ἐλάσειε as a condition, “who did not drive,” not a statement of what no one could do (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.290; see the translation's note on 11.289–290).
