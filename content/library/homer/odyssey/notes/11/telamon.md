# The patronymic Τελαμωνιάδαο (11.543) and ^Τελαμῶνος (11.553) are claimed here, as the Iliad's Book 8 and 13 notes claim
# them in the Iliad: the capital keeps out τελαμῶνος "strap" (Il. 5.796), and in this book the lowercase τελαμών is
# the strap of Heracles' baldric (11.610, 11.614). In the Odyssey each form is found only in its line here.

οἴη δʼ Αἴαντος ψυχὴ Τελαμωνιάδαο, “Alone the soul of Ajax, son of Telamon” (11.543), and Αἶαν, παῖ Τελαμῶνος ἀμύμονος, “Ajax, son of noble Telamon” (11.553; see [[ajax]], [[telamonian]] and [[blameless]]): the father is named twice in the scene of the arms, both times to name the son, the second time in the address with which Odysseus begs the angry soul to listen. Homer tells nothing more of him in the Odyssey. The strap of Heracles' baldric, a few verses later, is the same word, τελαμών, “strap” (11.610, 11.614; see [[heracles]]).
