---
summary: Odysseus asks his mother: “And tell me of my wedded wife, her plan and her thought” (11.177).
grc: ["μνηστῆς ἀλόχου"]
---

μνηστὴ ἄλοχος, the wife won by courtship and gifts, in the last of Odysseus' questions, which his mother answers first (11.177–183; see [[penelope]] and [[hysteron-proteron]]).
