---
summary: “the broad-browed cattle with curving horns / out of Phylace, the cattle of mighty Iphiclus” (11.289–290), the bride-price that Neleus asked for Pero.
---

The cattle are “hard to drive,” and the seer who promised to drive them was fettered for a year (11.291–297; see [[with-curving-horns]], [[bride-price]] and [[iphiclus]]).
