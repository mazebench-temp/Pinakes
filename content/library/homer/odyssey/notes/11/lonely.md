---
summary: Orion herds together wild beasts, “beasts that he himself had killed on the lonely mountains” (11.574).
grc: [οἰοπόλοισιν]
---

ἐν οἰοπόλοισιν ὄρεσσι, the mountains where Niobe sits turned to stone (*Iliad* 24.614), and the hunting grounds of Orion when he was alive (see [[orion]] and [[huge]]).
