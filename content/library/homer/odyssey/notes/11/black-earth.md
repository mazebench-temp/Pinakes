---
summary: Alcinous does not judge Odysseus to be one of “the many / whom the black earth feeds, men scattered far and wide, / fashioning lies” (11.364–366); and whenever Tantalus stooped to drink, “the black earth appeared” at his feet (11.587).
---

Merry takes πολυσπερέας as a descriptive epexegesis of the many men the earth feeds (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.365). The earth that feeds the many liars appears dry under the feet of the man who thirsts in the lake (11.583–587; see [[tantalus]] and [[earth]]).
