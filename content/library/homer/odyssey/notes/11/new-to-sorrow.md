---
summary: “and tender maidens with hearts new to sorrow” (11.39), among the souls that gather to the pit.
---

The phrase is one of the five classes of the dead in the throng (11.38–41; see [[atalos]], [[who-had-borne-much]] and [[slain-in-battle]]). On Merry's reading the word shows the maidens with the marks of a fresh grief (see the general article).
