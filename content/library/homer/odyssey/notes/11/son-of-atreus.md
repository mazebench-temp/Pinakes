summary: “there came the soul of Agamemnon, son of Atreus” (11.387); Odysseus addresses him, “Most glorious son of Atreus, Agamemnon, lord of men” (11.397), and answers his question about Orestes, “Son of Atreus, why do you ask me this?” (11.463).
---

The patronymic with which Odysseus greets the dead king in the Iliad's verse of address (see [[most-glorious]] and [[lord-of-men]]); it is the house of Atreus that Zeus has hated “through the plans of women” (11.436–437; see [[atreus]] and [[o-popoi]]).
