---
summary: Named last of the three sons whom Chloris bore to Neleus, “Nestor and Chromius and proud Periclymenus” (11.286), his only mention in Homer.
---

Νέστορά τε Χρόμιόν τε Περικλύμενόν τʼ ἀγέρωχον (11.286; see [[nestor]], [[chromius-son-of-neleus]] and [[proud]]): the Hesiodic *Catalogue of Women* has the same three words for him, Περικλύμενόν τʼ ἀγέρωχον, and goes on to tell of the shapes that Poseidon gave him (see the general article and [[hesiod]]).
