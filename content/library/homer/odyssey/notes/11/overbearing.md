---
summary: Teiresias foretells that Odysseus will find bane in his house, “overbearing men, who are devouring your substance, / wooing your godlike wife and giving bride-price” (11.116–117).
---

The suitors' standing epithet comes into the prophecy before their name (μνηστῆρας, 11.119; see [[the-suitors]] and [[antitheos]]). Merry raises the chronology: when Teiresias spoke, the suitors' outrages had not yet begun (see the translation's note on 11.115–116).
