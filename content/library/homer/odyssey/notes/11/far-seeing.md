---
summary: Odysseus: “Ah, truly far-seeing Zeus has hated the offspring of Atreus / terribly, through the plans of women, / from of old” (11.436–438).
---

The epithet of Zeus in a judgment on a house (see [[o-popoi]] and [[zeus]]). Odysseus uses the same verb of Zeus' hatred when he speaks to Ajax: Zeus “hated the army of the Danaan spearmen / terribly” (11.559–560; see [[spearmen]]).
