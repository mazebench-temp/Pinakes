---
summary: “And after him I caught sight of huge Orion” (11.572); and Sisyphus was “heaving a huge stone with both hands” (11.594).
---

πελώριος, the epithet of Ajax in the Iliad, is given to the hunter among the dead and to the stone of Sisyphus. Merry sees Orion still at his customary pursuit, a phantom hunter driving the phantoms of the beasts he had killed on the lonely hills (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.572; see [[orion]], [[lonely]] and [[sisyphus]]).
