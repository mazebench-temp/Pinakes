---
summary: The wave stood around Poseidon and Tyro, “and hid the god and the mortal woman” (11.244).
grc: ["θεὸν θνητήν τε γυναῖκα"]
en: [the mortal woman]
---

The two races side by side in one half-verse, as in Achilles' wish that his mother had stayed among the immortal goddesses of the sea “and Peleus had taken a mortal wife” (*Iliad* 18.86–87; see [[tyro]], [[poseidon]] and [[gods-and-mortals]]).
