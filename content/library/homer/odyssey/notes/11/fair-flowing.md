---
summary: Tyro loved the river Enipeus, “and she used to go often to the fair streams of Enipeus” (11.240).
grc: ["καλὰ ῥέεθρα"]
en: [fair streams]
---

καλὰ ῥέεθρα, the Iliad's verse-end of the streams of the Scamander in the battle of the river (*Iliad* 21.238 and often), of the river that the narrator has just called “by far the fairest of the rivers that run upon the earth” (11.239; see [[divine]] and [[tyro]]).
