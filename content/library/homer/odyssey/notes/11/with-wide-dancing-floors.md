---
summary: “Pelias lived in Iolcus with its wide dancing floors, / rich in flocks” (11.256–257), and Amphion and Zethus walled “Thebes with its wide dancing floors” (11.265).
---

Two cities of the heroines' sons have the epithet (see [[iolcus]] and [[thebes]]); Merry compares Elis “with its wide dancing floors” in Noemon's words (4.635; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.256). The book has also the rarer καλλίχορος, “with its lovely dancing floors,” of Panopeus (11.581; see [[panopeus]]).
