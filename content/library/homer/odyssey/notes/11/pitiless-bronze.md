---
summary: Odysseus bids his companions “flay the sheep that lay there slaughtered with the pitiless bronze, / and burn them” (11.45–46).
---

The verse of Circe's instructions, told as done: κατέκειτʼ, “lay,” where the last book had the present κατάκειτʼ, “lie” (11.45 ~ 10.532; see the Book 10 note, and [[sacrifice]]).
