# Μέμνονα (11.522) is claimed by the entry.
---

κεῖνον δὴ κάλλιστον ἴδον μετὰ Μέμνονα δῖον, “He was the handsomest man I saw, after brilliant Memnon” (11.522; see [[eurypylus-son-of-telephus]] and [[brilliant]]): Odysseus measures the son of Telephus against the son of Dawn, as the Catalogue measured Nireus against Achilles (*Iliad* 2.673–674). It is the only time Homer names him; in the fourth book Peisistratus remembered him, unnamed, as “the splendid son of shining Dawn” who killed his brother Antilochus (4.187–188; see [[antilochus]] and [[eos]]), whose soul has just come to the pit with Achilles' (11.468).
