---
summary: Megara's husband was “the son of Amphitryon, ever unyielding in his might” (11.270).
---

μένος αἰὲν ἀτειρής, of Heracles. Merry renders ἀτειρής “sturdy,” literally “unwearying,” and compares Hector's heart, “always unyielding, like an axe” (*Iliad* 3.60), and the bronze (*Iliad* 5.292; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.270; see [[heracles]] and [[high-hearted]]).
