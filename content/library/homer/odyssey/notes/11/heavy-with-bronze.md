---
summary: In the wooden horse Neoptolemus “kept feeling for the hilt of his sword / and his spear heavy with bronze, and he had evil in mind for the Trojans” (11.531–532).
grc: [χαλκοβαρές]
---

δόρυ χαλκοβαρές, the neuter of the epithet that the Iliad gives to spears and arrows, of the spear of the young man who alone did not weep or turn pale in the horse (11.526–532; see [[the-wooden-horse]] and [[neoptolemus]]).
