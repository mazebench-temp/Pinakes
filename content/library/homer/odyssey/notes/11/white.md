---
summary: Odysseus sprinkles “white barley meal” over the pit (11.28); and his mother says that the fire overcomes the flesh and bones “as soon as the spirit has left the white bones” (11.221).
---

ἄλφιτα λευκά, the barley meal of Circe's instructions, now strewn (11.28 ~ 10.520; see [[barley-meal]] and [[the-offering-to-the-dead]]). λεύκʼ ὀστέα are the bones of the burned dead, as in the Iliad's funerals (see [[cremation]] and [[the-way-of-mortals]]).
