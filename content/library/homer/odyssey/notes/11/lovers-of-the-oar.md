---
summary: Alcinous grants Arete's request: “This word shall indeed be so, as surely as I / live and am lord over the Phaeacians, lovers of the oar” (11.348–349).
---

The verse-end of Athena as Mentes (1.181), in the mouth of the king of the seafaring people. Merry puts the emphasis on ζωός, “as sure as I am alive and reigning” (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.348; see [[phaeacians]] and [[alcinous]]).
