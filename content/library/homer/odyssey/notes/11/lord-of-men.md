---
summary: “Most glorious son of Atreus, Agamemnon, lord of men” (11.397).
---

ἄναξ ἀνδρῶν, the title of the commander of the army, given to his soul (see [[most-glorious]] and [[agamemnon]]). He died, he says, not in battle or at sea, but at a feast, “as one kills an ox at the manger” (11.409–411; see [[the-murder-of-agamemnon]]).
