---
summary: Agamemnon's last counsel: “And I will tell you something else, and you lay it to heart: / secretly, and not openly, to your dear native land / bring your ship to shore; for there is no trusting women any more” (11.454–456).
---

The verse with which Hera turns to her second warning to Zeus about Sarpedon (*Iliad* 16.444 = 11.454) here introduces the advice to come home in secret (see [[dear-native-land]] and [[there-is-no-trusting-women]]). The three verses do not sit easily with Agamemnon's praise of Penelope just before (11.444–446); the scholion “not in most copies, as conflicting” has been referred to them, and Merry brackets them (see the translation's note on 11.454–456).
