---
summary: Five of the dead greet Odysseus as “sprung from Zeus” in the same verse (11.60, 11.92, 11.405, 11.473, 11.617).
---

The souls give the living man the title of a king, as Circe had (10.401; see [[son-of-laertes]] and [[of-many-devices]]). Heracles adds his own descent: “I was the son of Zeus, son of Cronus” (11.620; see [[son-of-cronus]]).
