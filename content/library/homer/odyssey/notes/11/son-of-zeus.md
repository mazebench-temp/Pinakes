Three sons of Zeus in the book, and the first is not named: Apollo, known by his mother (see [[apollo]], [[leto]] and [[lovely-haired]]). Merry reports that in Pindar the Aloadae are killed by Artemis in Naxos (on 11.318). Minos is the son of Zeus by the daughter of Phoenix in the Iliad (*Iliad* 14.321–322; see [[minos]]), and Heracles names his father before his labors (11.620–622; see [[heracles]] and [[son-of-cronus]]).
