---
summary: Tyro “fell in love with a river, the divine Enipeus, / which is by far the fairest of the rivers that run upon the earth” (11.238–239).
---

ποταμοῦ … Ἐνιπῆος θείοιο, the river god whose likeness Poseidon takes to lie with her (11.241; see [[tyro]] and [[earth-holder]]). Rivers are divine in Homer and have their own gods (see [[rivers]]).
