---
summary: “Son of Laertes, sprung from Zeus, Odysseus of many devices” (11.60, 11.92, 11.405, 11.473, 11.617), the greeting of Elpenor, Teiresias, Agamemnon, Achilles and Heracles.
---

The epithet of contrivance is set beside the deed that only Odysseus has done, the voyage of the living to the dead: “hard man, what still greater deed will you devise in your mind? / How did you dare to come down to Hades” (11.474–475; see [[son-of-laertes]] and [[hard]]).
