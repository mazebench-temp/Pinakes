---
summary: Odysseus will not tell Agamemnon whether Orestes is alive or dead: “it is a bad thing to speak empty wind” (11.464).
---

The verse is the phantom's answer to Penelope (4.837), and Odysseus gives it to the soul of Agamemnon, who has asked whether his son lives (11.457–464; see [[it-is-bad-to-speak-empty-wind]] and [[orestes]]). All Odysseus means, Merry says, is that he has no certain knowledge, and so will not waste time in talk (W. W. Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.464).
