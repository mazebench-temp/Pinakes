---
summary: Five of the dead greet Odysseus with the full verse, “Son of Laertes, sprung from Zeus, Odysseus of many devices”: Elpenor, Teiresias, Agamemnon, Achilles and Heracles (11.60, 11.92, 11.405, 11.473, 11.617).
---

The verse of Circe's commands in the last book is now the greeting of the dead (10.401, 10.488, 10.504; see [[meeting-with-the-dead]]). Two of the five are doubtful: 11.60 is missing from most manuscripts, and 11.92 is found only in Eustathius and three other witnesses; both have been bracketed since Wolf, and Murray prints both. Merry ties the second to the question whether Teiresias needed the blood to know Odysseus (see the translation's note on 11.60, 11.92, and [[dark-blood]]). Achilles follows the verse with σχέτλιε, “hard man” (11.474; see [[hard]]), and Heracles with ἆ δείλʼ, “ah, wretch” (11.618; see [[wretch]]).
