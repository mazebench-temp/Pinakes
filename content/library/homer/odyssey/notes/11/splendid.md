summary: Poseidon to Tyro, “you will bear splendid children” (11.249); Chloris “bore him splendid children” (11.285); Odysseus would stay a year, if Alcinous gave “splendid gifts” (11.357); and Minos is “the splendid son of Zeus” (11.568).
---

ἀγλαὰ τέκνα twice in the catalogue of the heroines, of Pelias and Neleus, Poseidon's sons by Tyro, and of Nestor and his brothers, the sons of Neleus by Chloris (11.254, 11.286; see [[tyro]] and [[neleus]]). ἀγλαὰ δῶρα are the gifts of the Phaeacians, which Arete has asked them not to cut short (11.339–341, 11.357; see [[doron]]), and Διὸς ἀγλαὸν υἱόν the judge among the dead (see [[minos]] and [[son-of-zeus]]).
