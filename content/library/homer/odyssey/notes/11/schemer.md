summary: “Cassandra, whom scheming Clytemnestra killed / beside me” (11.422–423).
---

δολόμητις, the standing epithet of Aegisthus, given once to Clytemnestra (see the general article, and [[clytemnestra]] and [[cassandra]]). Odysseus answers that Clytemnestra was preparing a snare, δόλον, while her husband was far away (11.439; see [[dolos]]).
