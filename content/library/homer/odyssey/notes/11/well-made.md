---
summary: Elpenor knows that Odysseus “will put in with your well-made ship at the island of Aeaea” (11.70); Teiresias warns of the time “when first you bring your well-made ship / to the island of Thrinacia” (11.106–107); and Anticleia says that no one can cross Oceanus on foot, “unless he has a well-made ship” (11.159).
---

ἐυεργέα νῆα, three times in three mouths: the ship that will carry Odysseus back to Aeaea to bury Elpenor, to the island of the Sun, and across Oceanus to the dead (see [[ships]], [[aeaea]] and [[oceanus]]).
