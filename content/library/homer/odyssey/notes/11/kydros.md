---
summary: Tityus “had dragged off Leto, the glorious wife of Zeus, / as she went to Pytho” (11.580–581).
grc: [κυδρὴν]
---

Merry finds Διὸς κυδρὴν παράκοιτιν somewhat inappropriate to Leto, since it is the distinctive title of Hera (*Iliad* 18.184; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.580; see [[leto]], [[tityus]] and [[hera]]).
