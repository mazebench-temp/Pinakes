summary: “So I spoke, and at once my lady mother answered” (11.180, 11.215); and the arms of Achilles were set as a prize by “his lady mother” (11.546).
---

πότνια μήτηρ twice of Odysseus' mother in the verse of her answers (see [[answering-him]]), and once of Thetis, who set the arms of her dead son before the Achaeans (11.545–547; see [[the-judgment-of-arms]] and [[thetis]]).
