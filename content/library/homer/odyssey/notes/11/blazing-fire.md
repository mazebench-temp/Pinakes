---
summary: When a mortal dies, “the strong might of the blazing fire / overcomes” the flesh and bones (11.220–221).
---

πυρὸς κρατερὸν μένος αἰθομένοιο: the fire of the pyre, in Anticleia's account of what death does to the body (11.219–222; see [[cremation]], [[krateros]] and [[the-way-of-mortals]]).
