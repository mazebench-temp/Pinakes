---
summary: Teiresias: “when first you bring your well-made ship / to the island of Thrinacia, escaping the violet sea” (11.106–107).
---

προφυγὼν ἰοειδέα πόντον: the ship that reaches the island of the Sun has escaped the open sea, the πόντος to which Homer gives this epithet (see [[sea]]). Circe's warning in the next book repeats the verses that follow, with one small change (11.110–114 ~ 12.137–141), but not this one.
