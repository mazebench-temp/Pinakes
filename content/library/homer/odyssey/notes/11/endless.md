---
summary: Elpenor: “an evil fate from a god led me into blind folly, and endless wine” (11.61); and Alcinous: “This night is very long, endless” (11.373).
grc: [ἀθέσφατος]
---

ἀθέσφατος οἶνος is the wine that blinded Elpenor, so that he forgot the ladder and fell from Circe's roof (11.61–65), joined with the evil fate from a god (see the Odyssey translation's key words, δαίμονος αἶσα κακή, and [[elpenor]] and [[ate]]); νὺξ … ἀθέσφατος, the long night of the pause, which the king would fill with the tale (see [[the-tale-to-alcinous]] and [[dawn-line]]).
