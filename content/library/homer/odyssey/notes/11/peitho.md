---
summary: Echeneus tells the Phaeacians that Arete has spoken to the purpose, “so obey her” (11.344–345).
---

The old man's advice in the pause of the tale: the queen's words are “not wide of the mark, nor against our expectation, … so obey her”, though deed and word depend on Alcinous (11.344–346; ἀπὸ σκοποῦ is “wide of the mark”, σκοπός being the archer's mark: W. W. Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.344; see [[echeneus]], [[arete-queen-of-the-phaeacians]] and [[word-or-deed]]).
