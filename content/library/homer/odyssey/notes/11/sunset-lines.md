---
summary: The ship sails all day with full sails, “And the sun set, and all the streets grew dark; / and she came to the limits of deep-streaming Oceanus” (11.12–13).
---

The verse of the evening on Telemachus' journeys (2.388, 3.487, 3.497) brings the ship to the edge of the world at nightfall, and the next verses tell of the Cimmerians, on whom “the shining sun” never looks down (11.14–19; see [[deep-streaming]], [[oceanus]] and [[deadly]]).
