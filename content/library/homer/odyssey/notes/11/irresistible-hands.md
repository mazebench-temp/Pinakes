---
summary: Achilles: “then I would make my might and my irresistible hands hateful to any / of those who do him violence and keep him from his honor” (11.502–503).
---

μένος καὶ χεῖρας ἀάπτους, the formula of the might of great warriors, in the wish of the dead man to come back to his father's house, if only for a little while (11.498–503; see [[peleus]]). Merry renders “in that case I would make bitter my might and my invincible hands” (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.502).
