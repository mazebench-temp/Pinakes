en: [son of Telamon]
---

Αἴαντος ψυχὴ Τελαμωνιάδαο, the patronymic that distinguishes the great Ajax, as in the Iliad; Odysseus addresses him as “son of noble Telamon” (11.553; see [[ajax]], [[telamon]] and [[the-judgment-of-arms]]).
