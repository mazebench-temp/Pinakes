---
summary: In the pause of the tale Odysseus answers Alcinous twice as πολύμητις, “Odysseus of many wiles” (11.354, 11.377).
---

The epithet introduces the two answers that keep the tale going: that he would gladly stay a year for the gifts, and that there is a time for many words and a time for sleep (11.354–361, 11.377–384; see [[of-many-wiles]], [[a-time-for-many-words]] and [[odysseus]]). The book has no plan of his to call cunning; its one device is the sword over the blood, by which he chooses which of the dead may speak (11.48–50, 11.229–234; see [[the-offering-to-the-dead]]).
