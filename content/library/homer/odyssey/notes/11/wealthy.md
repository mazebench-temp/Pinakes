---
summary: Agamemnon's companions were killed “like white-tusked hogs / that are slaughtered in the house of a rich man of great power / for a wedding, or a shared feast, or a rich banquet” (11.413–415).
grc: [ἀφνειοῦ]
---

The rich man of the simile, whose feast is the image of the slaughter in Aegisthus' house (see [[white-tusked]] and [[the-murder-of-agamemnon]]).
