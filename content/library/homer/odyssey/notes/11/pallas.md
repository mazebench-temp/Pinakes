---
summary: For the arms of Achilles “the sons of the Trojans were the judges, and Pallas Athena” (11.547).
---

Aristarchus rejected the verse; Merry gives Nitzsch's reasons, and on who judged the arms he reports the *Little Iliad*, the *Aethiopis*, Pindar and Sophocles (see the translation's note on 11.547, and [[the-judgment-of-arms]] and [[athena]]).
