---
summary: “But when we had sacked the steep city of Priam” (11.533); and Epicaste hanged herself, “when she had fastened a noose hanging sheer from the high roof-beam” (11.278).
grc: ["βρόχον αἰπὺν"]
---

πόλιν … αἰπήν, of Troy; βρόχον αἰπύν, the noose “hanging sheer” (see the Odyssey translation's key words). Merry takes αἰπύν here in its simple physical sense, as a predicate with ἁψαμένη, “having fastened high a noose from the lofty beam” (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.278; see [[jocasta]] and [[troy]]).
