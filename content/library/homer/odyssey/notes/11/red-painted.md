---
summary: Teiresias: Odysseus is to go inland with an oar until he comes to men who do not know the sea, “nor do they know ships with crimson prows, / or well-fitted oars, which are wings to ships” (11.122–125).
grc: [φοινικοπαρῄους]
en: [with crimson prows]
---

φοινικοπάρῃος, “with crimson cheeks,” from φοῖνιξ, the red-purple dye (see [[purple]] and [[crimson]]), is the twin of the μιλτοπάρῃοι, “red-painted,” ships of the Iliad's Catalogue and of the Cyclopes' lack (9.125); it is found only here and in Odysseus' retelling to Penelope (23.271; see the Odyssey translation's key words). The men who do not know the sea know neither the ships nor the oars (see [[oars]] and [[ships]]).
