# πόσει (11.430; again 17.555, 19.95).
grc: [πόσει]
en: [husband]
---

Agamemnon's soul names his murder as a wife's crime against her husband: she “devised” the shameful deed, “contriving slaughter for her wedded husband” (11.429–430; see [[clytemnestra]], [[the-murder-of-agamemnon]] and [[marriage]]). κουρίδιος, “wedded”, marks the lawful marriage of husband and wife of equal station (W. W. Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.430), and the same pair of words returns when the soul of Agamemnon sets Penelope against her, who killed “her wedded husband” (κουρίδιον … πόσιν, 24.200).
