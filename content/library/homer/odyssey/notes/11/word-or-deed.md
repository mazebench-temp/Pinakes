---
summary: Echeneus, agreeing with the queen: “But on Alcinous here depend both deed and word” (11.346).
---

Merry renders “both promise and performance,” and compares the same pair in Athena's praise of Odysseus (2.272) and Nausicaa's words that on her father “the strength and the force of the Phaeacians depend” (6.197; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.346). Alcinous takes up the word at once: “This word shall indeed be so” (11.348; see [[echeneus]] and [[alcinous]]).
