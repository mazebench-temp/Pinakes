# δῆμός (11.14), accent-sensitive against δημός "fat".
grc: ["=δῆμός"]
en: [land]
---

δῆμος is both the land and the people on it. At the limits of Oceanus are “the land and the city of the Cimmerian men” (11.14), δῆμος and πόλις together, which gives a sort of reality to a country where social life is hard to imagine (W. W. Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.15; see [[cimmerians]], [[polis]] and the Odyssey translation's table of key words, Κιμμέριοι). Among the Phaeacians it is the people over whom Alcinous rules: “for mine is the power among the people” (ἐνὶ δήμῳ, 11.353; see [[kratos]] and [[alcinous]]).
