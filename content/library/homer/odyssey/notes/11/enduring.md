summary: Anticleia: “Yes, truly she stays with enduring heart / in your halls” (11.181–182).
---

τετληότι θυμῷ, the verse-end of Penelope's endurance. Merry notes that it occurs nine times in the Odyssey and never in the Iliad, and compares κραδίῃ τετληυίῃ (20.23; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.181; see [[penelope]] and [[hysteron-proteron]]).
