---
summary: Teiresias warns of the island of Thrinacia, where Odysseus will find “the cattle and fat sheep / of the Sun, who sees all things and hears all things” (11.108–109).
---

The words of the oath in the Iliad, there addressed to the Sun (*Iliad* 3.277), are here said of him: the owner of the cattle will know if they are harmed. Merry quotes the scholion: the phrase hints that no one who wronged such a god could escape his notice (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.109; see [[helios]] and [[the-cattle-of-helios]]). The verse returns when Odysseus warns his companions on the island (12.323).
