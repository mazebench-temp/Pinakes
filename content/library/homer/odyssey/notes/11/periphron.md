---
summary: Echeneus: “Friends, not wide of the mark, nor against our expectation, / is our prudent queen speaking to us; so obey her” (11.344–345); and Agamemnon praises “the daughter of Icarius, prudent Penelope” (11.446).
---

Penelope's standing epithet, given here to Arete, who is called “prudent” only in this verse (see [[arete-queen-of-the-phaeacians]] and [[echeneus]]); in the dead king's mouth it is set against the wife who killed him (11.444–446; see [[penelope]] and [[daughter-of-icarius]]).
