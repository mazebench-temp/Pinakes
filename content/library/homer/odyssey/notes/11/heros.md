# ἥρωʼ (11.520; ἥρῳ 8.483).
grc: [ἥρωʼ]
en: [hero]
---

The word is a title of rank for the dead of the heroic past and the living elders alike. The women Odysseus cannot all name are “the wives and daughters of heroes” (ἡρώων ἀλόχους … ἠδὲ θύγατρας, 11.329), where the same list began with “chieftains”, ἀριστήων (11.227; see the Odyssey translation's table of key words and [[catalogue-of-heroines]]). Among the Phaeacians it is a title of age: “the old hero Echeneus” (γέρων ἥρως, 11.342; see [[echeneus]] and [[geron]]). Eurypylus son of Telephus, killed by Neoptolemus, is “the hero Eurypylus” (11.519–520; see [[eurypylus-son-of-telephus]]), and at the end Odysseus stays at the pit “in case any more should come / of the heroes who had perished in former days”, Theseus and Peirithous among them (11.628–631; see [[theseus]] and [[peirithous]]).
