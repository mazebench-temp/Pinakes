---
summary: “And after him I caught sight of mighty Heracles, / his phantom” (11.601–602).
grc: ["βίην Ἡρακληείην"]
---

βίην Ἡρακληείην, the accusative of the periphrasis, as in the Iliad (*Iliad* 5.638, 19.98), is set against εἴδωλον: the might of Heracles is among the dead only as a phantom, while he himself is among the gods (11.601–604; see [[of-the-golden-sandals]], [[eidolon]] and [[heracles]]). Iphiclus of Phylace is named in the same way in this book (11.290, 11.296; see [[iphiclus]]).
