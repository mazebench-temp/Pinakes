---
summary: Over the Cimmerians “deadly night is spread over those wretched mortals” (11.19); Oedipus ruled in Thebes “through the deadly plans of the gods” (11.276); Agamemnon was killed “with my ruinous wife” (11.410); and Odysseus speaks to Ajax of “the ruinous armor” (11.555).
grc: [ὀλοὰς, οὐλομένῃ, οὐλομένων]
---

νὺξ ὀλοή is a phrase of this book (see the Odyssey translation's key words). θεῶν ὀλοὰς διὰ βουλάς belongs with ἄλγεα πάσχων, “suffering sorrows … through the deadly plans of the gods,” as Merry takes it (see the translation's note on 11.275–276, and [[oedipus]]). οὐλόμενος, the participle of the same verb that the last book gave to Circe's drug (10.394), describes the wife who shared in the murder (11.410; see [[clytemnestra]]) and the arms of Achilles, which cost Ajax his life (11.554–555; see [[the-judgment-of-arms]]).
