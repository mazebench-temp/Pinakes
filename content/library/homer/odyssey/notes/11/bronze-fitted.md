---
summary: Among the souls at the pit come “many stabbed by bronze-fitted spears” (11.40).
---

χαλκήρεσιν ἐγχείῃσιν: the warriors among the dead keep the wounds of the spears that killed them, and their armor is “clotted with blood” (11.40–41; see [[slain-in-battle]] and [[spear]]). Merry takes οὐτάμενοι, “stabbed,” for an aorist participle with a passive sense, “almost purely adjectival” (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.40).
