summary: “and we set the mast and the sails in the black ship” (11.3); and Odysseus to Elpenor, “You have come faster on foot than I in my black ship” (11.58).

νηὶ μελαίνῃ twice: the ship that sails to the dead, and the ship that the dead man outran on foot (see [[elpenor]] and [[misty]]).
