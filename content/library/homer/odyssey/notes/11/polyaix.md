---
summary: The sons of Aloeus “even threatened the immortals on Olympus / that they would raise the din of furious war” (11.313–314).
---

φυλόπιδα … πολυάικος πολέμοιο: Merry notes that φύλοπις, common in the Iliad, is found in the Odyssey only here and twice in later books (16.268, 24.475; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.314; see [[otus]] and [[immortals]]).
