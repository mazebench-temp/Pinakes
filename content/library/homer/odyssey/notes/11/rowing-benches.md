---
summary: The companions go on board at once and sit down at the rowing benches, and the ship is rowed down the stream of Oceanus until a fair wind takes it (11.638–640).
---

οἱ δʼ αἶψʼ εἴσβαινον καὶ ἐπὶ κληῖσι καθῖζον, “And they went on board at once and sat down at the rowing benches” (11.638), the Odyssey's verse of boarding (see [[stern-cables]] and [[ships]]). Here it is not followed by the verse of striking the sea with the oars: “the wave of the stream carried the ship down the river Oceanus, / first with rowing, and afterward a fair wind” (11.639–640; see [[oceanus]] and [[oars]]).
