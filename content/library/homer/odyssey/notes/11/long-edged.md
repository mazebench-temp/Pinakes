---
summary: To question the women one by one, Odysseus keeps them from the blood, “drawing the long-edged sword from beside my stout thigh” (11.231).
---

The verse of his anger against Eurylochus in the last book (11.231 = 10.439), here the sword that orders the souls: “I would not let them all drink the dark blood at once” (11.232; see [[sword]], [[sharp]] and [[dark-blood]]).
