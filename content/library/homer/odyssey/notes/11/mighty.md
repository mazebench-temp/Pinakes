summary: The prayer at the pit is “to mighty Hades and dread Persephone” (11.47); and Chloris bore “strong Pero, a marvel to mortals” (11.287).
grc: [ἰφθίμην]
---

ἰφθίμῳ τʼ Ἀΐδῃ καὶ ἐπαινῇ Περσεφονείῃ, the verse of Circe's instructions, now performed (11.47 = 10.534; see [[dread]] and [[hades]]). ἰφθίμην is the epithet of a woman, as in the last book of the giant's daughter, and the translation keeps “strong” (see the translation's note on 10.106); Pero is the daughter whom Neleus would give only for the cattle of Iphiclus (11.287–290; see [[neleus]]).
