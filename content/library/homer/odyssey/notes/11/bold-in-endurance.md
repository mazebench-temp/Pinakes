---
summary: Alcmene “bore Heracles, bold in endurance, lion-hearted, / having lain in love in the arms of great Zeus” (11.267–268).
---

The pair θρασυμέμνονα θυμολέοντα is the one Tlepolemus gives his father in the Iliad (*Iliad* 5.639). Merry takes θρασυμέμνων not as “bravely enduring” but as “boldly eager,” from the root of μέμονα, μέμαα (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.267; see [[lion-hearted]], [[heracles]] and [[alcmene]]).
