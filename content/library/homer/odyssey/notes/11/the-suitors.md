**The seer's warning.** If the companions harm the cattle of the Sun, Odysseus will come home late, on someone else's ship, “and find bane in your house, / overbearing men, who are devouring your substance, / wooing your godlike wife and giving bride-price. / Yet you will surely take vengeance for their violence when you come. / But when you have killed the suitors in your halls, / whether by guile or openly with the sharp bronze”, he must take an oar and go inland (11.115–121; see [[teiresias]], [[bride-price]] and [[the-return-of-odysseus]]). It is the first time Odysseus hears of them, and the prophecy names both the wrong and its end, the vengeance that Athena and Zeus had planned in the fifth book (5.23–24; see the Book 5 note). The two verses on the killing are nearly those in which Athena bade Telemachus consider “how you may kill the suitors in your halls, / whether by guile or openly” (1.295–296; see [[athena]] and [[dolos]]).

**Too soon?** W. W. Merry points out the difficulty of time. Odysseus' visit to the dead falls at the end of the third year after the fall of Troy, and the suitors' misconduct, which lasted until he came home, did not begin more than four years before his return (2.106 and following); yet Teiresias speaks as if the outrages in his house were already going on, unless κατέδουσι, “are devouring”, is taken as a prophetic present (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.115). Aristophanes read κατέδοιεν, “would devour”, to remove the difficulty, but Aristarchus, La Roche observes, must have read κατέδουσι (Merry's critical note on 11.116 and on 11.115; see the translation's note on 11.115–116). Anticleia's news, too, knows nothing of suitors: no one yet holds Odysseus' prize of honor, and Telemachus holds the domains undisturbed and feasts at the equal feasts that befit a man who gives judgment (11.184–187; see [[telemachus]]). Here the difficulty runs the other way, Merry observes, for Telemachus is spoken of as a grown man, which he may have been when his father came home, but not seven years earlier, when he could have been at most fourteen (on 11.115, 11.184).
