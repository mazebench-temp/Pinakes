---
summary: Alcinous keeps his guest one more day and gives the half-line to the escort: “and his escort will be the concern of men, / of all of them, and mine most of all; for mine is the power among the people” (11.352–353).
---

The verses are Telemachus' to his mother, “for mine is the power in this house” (1.358–359), here with πομπή, “escort”, for μῦθος, “speech”, and ἐνὶ δήμῳ, “among the people”, for ἐνὶ οἴκῳ (11.352–353; see [[pompe]], [[kratos]] and [[alcinous]]). Odysseus had left his escort to “the care of the gods and of you” (11.332); the king claims it as a man's business and his own above all, after Arete has asked for more gifts (11.336–341; see [[arete-queen-of-the-phaeacians]]).
