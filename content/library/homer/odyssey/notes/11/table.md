---
summary: Agamemnon and his companions lay dying in Aegisthus' hall “around the mixing bowl and the laden tables” (11.419).
---

ὡς ἀμφὶ κρητῆρα τραπέζας τε πληθούσας / κείμεθʼ ἐνὶ μεγάρῳ, “how around the mixing bowl and the laden tables / we lay in the hall” (11.419–420; see [[mixing-bowl]], [[agamemnon]] and [[the-murder-of-agamemnon]]). The tables are full: the guests were killed with the food still before them, as the suitors will be at their tables in Book 22.
