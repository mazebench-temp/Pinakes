---
summary: “And among them white-armed Arete began to speak” (11.335).
---

The verse is the one with which Arete first spoke to the stranger in her hall (11.335 = 7.233), and with which she now asks the Phaeacians to keep him and to add to his gifts (11.336–341; see [[arete-queen-of-the-phaeacians]]).
