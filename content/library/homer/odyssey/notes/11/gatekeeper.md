---
summary: Epicaste, the mother of Oedipus, “went to the house of Hades, the strong gatekeeper, / when she had fastened a noose hanging sheer from the high roof beam” (11.277–278).
---

The third of Homer's three uses of the epithet, each in a phrase of going down to the dead (see the general article). Merry renders πυλάρτης “the warder,” rejecting Apion's “he who is fastened to the gates” for “he who keeps the gates fast,” like the gates “fitted close and strong” in the Iliad (*Iliad* 12.454; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.277; see [[hades]], [[jocasta]] and [[krateros]]).
