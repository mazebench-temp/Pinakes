---
summary: Tyro bore to Cretheus “Aeson and Pheres and Amythaon, who fought from his chariot” (11.259).
---

Merry renders ἱππιοχάρμης “fighting from the war chariot” (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 11.259). The Iliad's only use is of Troilus, a son of Priam (see the general article and [[tyro]]).
