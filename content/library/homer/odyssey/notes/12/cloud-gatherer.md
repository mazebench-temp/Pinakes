The god of the epithet raises the storm that keeps the ship a month on Thrinacia (12.312–326), and answers the Sun's threat with the promise of the thunderbolt (12.385–388; see [[blustering]], [[thunderbolt]], [[answering-him]] and [[zeus]]).
