---
summary: Odysseus wakes to the smell of the meat: “truly to my blind folly you lulled me in pitiless sleep” (12.372).
grc: ["νηλέι ὕπνῳ"]
---

νηλέι ὕπνῳ, found only here, turns the word of the bronze and of the hard heart on sleep, the gift of the gods a few verses before (12.338; see [[sweet-sleep]], [[ate]] and [[pitiless-bronze]]).
