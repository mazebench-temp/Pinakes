summary: Aeaea is the island “where are the house of early-born Dawn / and her dancing floors, and the risings of the Sun” (12.3–4); and twice “And when early-born Dawn appeared, the rosy-fingered” (12.8, 12.316).
grc: [ἠριγενείης]
---

Ἠοῦς ἠριγενείης, the genitive, is new to the entry in the Odyssey (as *Iliad* 8.508; again 13.94). The house of Dawn puts Aeaea at the sunrise, against the course of the voyage in Book 10 (see the translation's note on 12.1–4, and [[aeaea]], [[eos]] and [[dawn-line]]).
