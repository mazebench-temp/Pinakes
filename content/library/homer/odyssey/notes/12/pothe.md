# ποθήμεναι (12.110), only here.
grc: [ποθήμεναι]
en: [miss]
---

“But be sure to draw close to Scylla's crag, and quickly / row your ship past, since it is far better / to miss six companions in your ship than all of them together” (12.108–110; see [[scylla]] and [[odysseus-passes-scylla]]). ποθέω is to long for the absent, and here the absence is a loss counted in advance: six men, one for each of Scylla's heads (12.98–100), set against the whole crew that Charybdis would take. Odysseus asks at once how he might fight Scylla instead (12.112–114; see [[polemos]] and [[alke]]), but the reckoning holds: six are taken, “who were the strongest with their hands and in strength” (12.245–246), and the companions weep for them that night (12.309–311; see [[tears]]).
