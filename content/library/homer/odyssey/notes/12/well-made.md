summary: “and meanwhile the well-made ship quickly came / to the island of the two Sirens” (12.166–167); and on Thrinacia “we moored the well-made ship in the hollow harbor” (12.305).
grc: [ἐυεργὴς]
---

νηῦς ἐυεργής, the nominative, is new to the entry in the Odyssey (again 16.322; as *Iliad* 24.396). Teiresias foretold the landing on Thrinacia with the same word (11.106–107; see [[thrinacia]] and [[sailing-type-scene]]).
