summary: The first of the two crags “reaches the broad sky / with its sharp peak” (12.73–74); and Odysseus “with the sharp bronze cut a great round of wax” (12.173).
grc: [ὀξείῃ]
---

ὀξείῃ κορυφῇ, the only ὀξείῃ in Homer, is new to the entry (see [[wide-sky]]). ὀξέι χαλκῷ, the verse-end of the bronze that wounds, cuts wax for the companions' ears (12.173–174; see [[wax]] and [[strong-hand]]).
