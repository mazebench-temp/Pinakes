---
summary: Eurylochus fears that “a storm of wind should come, / of the South Wind or the stormy West Wind, which most of all / shatter ships” (12.288–290).
---

Ζεφύροιο δυσαέος, as in the storm of Book 5 (5.295). The two winds he names bring the end of the voyage: the West Wind brings down the mast and the South Wind carries Odysseus back to Charybdis (12.408–409, 12.426–428; see [[zephyrus]] and [[notus]]).
