---
summary: On Thrinacia, after the storm: “And then I called an assembly and spoke among them” (12.319), and Odysseus warns his companions to keep away from the cattle (12.320–323).
refs: [12.319-324]
---

The verse of Books 9 and 10 (9.171, 10.188), with μετὰ μῦθον, “spoke among them,” where those have μετὰ πᾶσιν, “among them all”; a manuscript note records πᾶσιν here too (see the translation's note on 12.319, 12.325). It is again an assembly of one speaker and no answer: “So I spoke, and their proud spirit was persuaded” (12.324; see [[our-proud-spirit-was-persuaded]] and [[assembly]]).
