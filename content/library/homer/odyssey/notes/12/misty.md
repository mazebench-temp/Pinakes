---
summary: Scylla lives in “a misty cave” (12.80); Odysseus' eyes grow weary “as I glanced all around toward the misty rock” (12.233); and Eurylochus fears being “driven off course from the island on the misty sea” (12.285).
---

σπέος ἠεροειδές and ἠεροειδέα πέτρην are the cave and the rock in shadow, the cave “turned toward the darkness, toward Erebus” (12.81; see the translation's key words, σπέος ἠεροειδές, and [[scylla]] and [[erebus]]). Merry takes πρὸς ζόφον of the westward face of the cave and εἰς Ἔρεβος of its downward slope (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 12.81). The sea is the Odyssey's ἠεροειδέι πόντῳ (see the general article).
