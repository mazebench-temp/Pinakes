summary: “There we fell asleep and waited for brilliant Dawn” (12.7); Charybdis is twice “brilliant Charybdis” (12.104, 12.235); and the mother of the Sun's daughters is “brilliant Neaera” (12.133).
grc: ["δῖα Χάρυβδις", "δῖα Νέαιρα"]
---

δῖα Χάρυβδις and δῖα Νέαιρα are new to the entry. The epithet of goddesses is given to the whirlpool personified, and Merry suggests that the later story of Charybdis as a daughter of Poseidon may have grown out of it (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 12.104; see the translation's key words, δῖα Χάρυβδις, and [[charybdis]]). Neaera, “Younger,” is with her daughters “Bright” and “Shining” a fit family for the Sun (Merry on 12.132; see [[neaera]]). Ἠῶ δῖαν is the verse of the island of goats (9.151; see [[dawn-line]]); the entry for the shining sea excepts the line (see [[shining-sea]]).
