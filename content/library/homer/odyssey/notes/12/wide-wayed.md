---
summary: The ship leaves the stream of Oceanus “and had come to the wave of the wide-wayed sea” (12.2).
---

The last of the three uses of θαλάσσης εὐρυπόροιο in Homer (see the general article). Merry, on the second, sets beside it πόρους ἁλός, “the paths of the sea,” which Odysseus says he searched out (12.259; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 4.432); on this verse he notes that ἀπὸ δʼ ἵκετο is still part of the protasis (on 12.2; see [[oceanus]]).
