---
summary: The Sirens “bewitch him with their clear song” (12.44), and “they raised their clear song” (12.183).
grc: [λιγυρῇ, λιγυρὴν]
en: [clear]
---

λιγυρὴ ἀοιδή, the only λιγυρός in the Odyssey, is new to the entry in the poem; the translation renders it “clear” of song and keeps “shrill” for the Iliad's wind and whip (see the translation's key words, λιγυρὴ ἀοιδή). Merry compares a fragment of Alcman in which the Muse is called a clear-voiced Siren (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 12.44; see [[sirens]], [[thelgo]] and [[honeyed-voice]]).
