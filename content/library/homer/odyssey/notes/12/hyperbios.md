---
summary: The Sun: “punish the companions of Odysseus, son of Laertes, / who overweeningly have killed my cattle” (12.378–379).
---

ὑπέρβιον, used as an adverb, as of the suitors who devour Odysseus' goods (14.92, 14.95; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 12.379). The god uses of the companions the word that the poem keeps above all for the suitors (see the general article, and [[the-cattle-of-helios]] and [[son-of-laertes]]).
