---
summary: “Zeus the cloud-gatherer roused a blustering wind against us / with a heaven-sent storm” (12.313–314).
grc: [ζαῆν]
---

ζαῆν, new to the entry, is the third of the three uses of ζαής (see the general article). Merry explains it as an Aeolic accusative, with Aristarchus' accent (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 12.313 and his critical note; see the translation's note on 12.312–313, and [[cloud-gatherer]] and [[wondrous]]).
