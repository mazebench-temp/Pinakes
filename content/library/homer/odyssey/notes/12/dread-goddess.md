The verse-end of Circe's first appearance (10.136, 11.8) is given at the end of the tale to Calypso, in verses that take up Odysseus' account of her to Arete, where she was “she of the lovely tresses, the dread goddess” (7.246, 7.255; 12.448 = 7.254; see [[with-lovely-tresses]], [[with-human-voice]], [[circe]] and [[calypso]]).
