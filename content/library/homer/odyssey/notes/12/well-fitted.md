---
summary: The companions bury Elpenor: “we heaped up a tomb, and dragged a pillar up onto it, / and on the top of the tomb we fixed the well-fitted oar” (12.14–15).
---

The oar that Elpenor asked for among the dead (11.77–78) is fixed with the verse-end of the oar that Teiresias bids Odysseus fix in the ground at the end of his wanderings (12.15 ~ 11.129; see [[tomb]], [[oars]] and [[the-death-of-elpenor]]).
