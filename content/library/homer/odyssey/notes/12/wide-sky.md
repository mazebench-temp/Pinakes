---
summary: The first crag “reaches the broad sky / with its sharp peak” (12.73–74); and Eurylochus: “and sacrifice to the immortals, who hold the wide sky” (12.344).
en: [broad sky]
---

οὐρανὸν εὐρὺν ἱκάνει, the height of the crag, which no mortal man could climb (12.73–79; see [[sharp]]); and the half-verse of Proteus' and Teiresias' instructions for sacrifice (4.479, 11.133; see [[immortals]]). The English at 12.73 is “broad sky.”
