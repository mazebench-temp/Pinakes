---
summary: “then at once we came to the flawless island of the god” (12.261).
---

ἀμύμονα νῆσον, the island of the Sun, given the word of praise as places are given it (see the translation's key words, ἀμύμων νῆσος, and [[thrinacia]] and [[helios]]). A scholion records ἀμύμων as a variant at 12.167 too, for the “harmless” wind, οὖρος ἀπήμων, that drove the ship to the Sirens' island (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, critical note on 12.167).
