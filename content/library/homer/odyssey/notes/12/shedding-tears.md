---
summary: The companions bury Elpenor, “grieving and shedding big tears” (12.12).
---

The verse-end of the companions' grief at the departures of the last two books (10.570, 11.5, 11.466; see [[flourishing]]) closes the burial that Elpenor asked for among the dead (11.71–78; see [[the-death-of-elpenor]] and [[tears]]). Later in the book the companions weep again, for the six whom Scylla took (12.309; see [[desire-for-drink-and-food]]).
