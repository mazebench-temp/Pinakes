---
summary: Scylla's victims die “stretching out their hands to me in grim combat” (12.257).
---

The formula of the formal duel is given to a struggle that cannot be won. Merry takes ἐν αἰνῇ δηιοτῆτι here as more graphic than “destruction” or “pain,” an unavailing conflict with an overmastering power, a “death-struggle” (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 12.257; see the translation's note on 12.256–257, and [[odysseus-passes-scylla]]).
