---
summary: On Thrinacia Odysseus remembers “the word / of the blind seer, Theban Teiresias” (12.266–267).
---

Θηβαίου Τειρεσίαο, “Theban Teiresias”, ends the verse as in Circe's directions and in the book of the dead (10.492, 10.565; 11.90, 11.165; see [[teiresias]], [[the-blind-seer]] and the Book 10 note), and once more when Odysseus tells Penelope of his journey to consult him (23.323). The manuscripts read μάντιος for μάντηος, “of the seer” (Merry's critical note on 12.267, referring to 10.493).
