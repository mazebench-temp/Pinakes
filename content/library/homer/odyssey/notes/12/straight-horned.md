---
summary: Eurylochus: “But if, angry over his straight-horned cattle, / he wishes to destroy our ship” (12.348–349).
---

Merry: ὀρθοκραιράων is shortened from ὀρθοκεραιράων, and the epithet does not clash with ἕλικες a few verses later (12.355) unless that word is taken to mean “with crumpled horns” (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 12.348, referring to his note on 1.92; see [[with-curving-horns]] and [[the-cattle-of-helios]]).
