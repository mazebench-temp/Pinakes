---
summary: The day of feasting on Aeaea: “So all day long, until the sun went down, / we sat feasting on untold meat and sweet wine. / When the sun went down and darkness came on, / the others lay down to sleep beside the stern-cables of the ship” (12.29–32).
---

The verses of the feasts of Books 9 and 10 (9.161–162, 10.183–184, 10.476–478; see the Odyssey translation's formulas), with a change at the end. The half-verse of the night at Chryse (*Iliad* 1.476) puts the others to sleep by the ship, but Circe takes Odysseus by the hand, “away from my dear companions,” to hear the tale of the dead and tell him the way home (12.32–38; see [[untold]], [[feast-type-scene]], [[stern-cables]] and [[circe]]).
