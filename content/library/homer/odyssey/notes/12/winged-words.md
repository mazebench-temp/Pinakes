---
summary: “and I spoke to him, addressing him with winged words” (12.296), Odysseus' answer to Eurylochus.
---

Odysseus' own verse of the tale (10.482; 11.56, 11.209, 11.396), here once, after he has understood “that a god was devising evil” (12.295). The speech that follows gives way to the companions and asks only for an oath (12.297–302; see [[oath-formulas]], [[eurylochus]] and [[daimon]]).
