In this book the hollow ship is always γλαφυρή, and four of the phrases are new to the entry; κοῖλος is given instead to Scylla's cave (12.84, 12.93; see [[hollow-caves]]). The dark cloud over the hollow ship begins the wreck, and the verse returns in the [[the-tale-to-eumaeus|Cretan tale]] of Book 14 (12.406 = 14.304; see [[son-of-cronus]] and [[thunderbolt]]).
