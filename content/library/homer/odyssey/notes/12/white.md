summary: “for they had no white barley on board the well-benched ship” (12.358); Zeus' “gleaming thunderbolt” (12.387); and “setting up the mast and hoisting the white sails” (12.402).
---

κρῖ λευκόν, the white barley that the sacrifice lacks (see [[sacrifice-type-scene]] and [[barley]]); ἀργῆτι κεραυνῷ, the bright thunderbolt of Zeus, as when he hurls it before Diomedes' horses in the Iliad, a verse that Merry compares for the sulfur of the stroke (*Iliad* 8.133; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 12.417; see [[thunderbolt]]); and the white sails of the last departure (see [[sails]]).
