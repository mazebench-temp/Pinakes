---
summary: Odysseus cuts the wax “into small pieces, and pressed them with my strong hands” (12.174).
---

χερσὶ στιβαρῇσι, the plural of the formula (see the general article). Merry sees a conscious touch of humor in the majestic language for the kneading of a cake of wax, “since the great strength bade it / and the ray of the lord Sun, son of Hyperion” (12.175–176; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 12.175; see the translation's note on 12.175–176, and [[wax]]).
