---
summary: Scylla's voice is no louder than a newborn puppy's, “but she herself is an evil monster” (12.87).
---

πέλωρ κακόν: the noun of the Cyclops (9.428), here of Scylla. Merry finds an intentionally grotesque contrast between her vast size and the thin, shrill cry she utters (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 12.86); the scholia rejected 12.86–88 (see the translation's note on 12.85–88, and [[scylla]]).
