summary: “And at once Eurylochus answered me with a hateful word” (12.278); and Eurylochus to the companions: “All deaths are hateful to wretched mortals, / but to die of hunger and meet one's fate is the most pitiable” (12.341–342).
grc: [στυγεροὶ]
---

στυγεροὶ θάνατοι is new to the entry. The plural is “forms of death,” as Merry notes, elsewhere called κῆρες θανάτοιο (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 12.341; see [[all-deaths-are-hateful]], [[wretch]] and [[thanatos]]). The hateful word of 12.278 is the one that Merry, on 11.81, would render “lamentable” (see the Book 11 note).
