---
summary: “to avoid the island of the Sun, who gladdens mortals” (12.269, 12.274).
---

The verse is said twice: first as Odysseus, still at sea, hears the lowing of the cattle and remembers the warnings of Teiresias and Circe, and then as he repeats them to his companions (12.264–269, 12.271–274; see [[retelling]] and [[the-blind-seer]]). The island is the one where the god's cattle will be killed (12.353; see [[thrinacia]]).
