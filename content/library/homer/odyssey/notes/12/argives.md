---
summary: The Argives are named once, in the Sirens' song of the war: “all the things that in wide Troy / the Argives and Trojans toiled through by the will of the gods” (12.189–190).
---

The verse names both sides of the war as sharing its toil, as Menelaus in the Iliad would have “the Argives and the Trojans” parted, since they had suffered many evils for his quarrel (*Iliad* 3.98–100). It comes back in Book 17, of the toil both armies endured for Helen (17.118–119; see [[troy]] and [[trojans]]).
