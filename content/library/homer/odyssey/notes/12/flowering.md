---
summary: Odysseus to his companions: “First, of the wondrous Sirens, she bids us avoid / the voice and the flowering meadow” (12.158–159).
---

Circe spoke of a meadow and a heap of bones (12.45–46); in Odysseus' report the meadow is flowering and the bones are left out (see [[retelling]], [[wondrous]] and [[sirens]]).
