---
summary: Zeus to the Sun: “Sun, do you keep giving light among the immortals / and to mortal men upon the grain-giving earth” (12.385–386).
---

12.386 is the verse of the sunrise that opens Book 3 (3.3), in Zeus' answer to the Sun's threat to shine among the dead (12.383): the grain-giving earth of mortals against the house of Hades (see [[mortal-men]] and [[helios]]).
