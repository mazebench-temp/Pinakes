---
summary: “there fell upon my heart the word / of the blind seer, Theban Teiresias, / and of Circe of Aeaea” (12.266–268).
---

The second of the two uses (10.493; see the general article). The manuscripts give μάντιος here, and Merry refers to his note on 10.493 for the genitive μάντηος (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, critical note on 12.267; see the translation's note on 12.265–275, and [[teiresias]] and [[the-prophecy-of-teiresias]]).
