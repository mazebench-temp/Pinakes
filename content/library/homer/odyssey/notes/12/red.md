---
summary: Circe's handmaids bring “bread and meat in plenty and sparkling red wine” (12.19); and on Thrinacia the companions keep from the cattle “as long as they had bread and red wine” (12.327).
---

οἶνον ἐρυθρόν, the verse-end of the Odyssey's wine (see the general article): the wine that Circe's handmaids bring to the shore, and the wine of the ship's stores on Thrinacia, which lasts as long as the food “that immortal Circe gave” (12.302, 12.327–329; see [[wine]] and [[provisions]]).
