summary: The Cyclops penned them “with his mighty strength” (12.210); and Odysseus: “swear me a mighty oath” (12.298).
---

κρατερῆφι βίηφιν ends the half-verse of the Cyclops' cave (9.476; see [[hollow-caves]]), and καρτερὸν ὅρκον names the oath that the companions will break (12.298–304; see [[oath-formulas]]).
