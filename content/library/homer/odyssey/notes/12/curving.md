---
summary: Odysseus comes back from his sleep: “But when I was close to the curving ship as I went, / then the sweet fragrance of the fat came round me” (12.368–369).
---

The verse of the stag on Aeaea, when a god sent him the beast as he came near the ship (12.368 = 10.156; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 12.359): there it brought food, here the smell of the forbidden meat (12.369–370; see [[sacrifice-type-scene]]).
