---
summary: The Sirens: “Come here, much-praised Odysseus, great glory of the Achaeans” (12.184).
---

The fourth use of the epithet in Homer, in the verse of Agamemnon and Nestor (*Iliad* 9.673, 10.544; see the general article, and [[great-glory-of-the-achaeans]]). Merry notes that the dual νωιτέρην in the next verse, “the voice of us two,” confirms that Homer's Sirens are two (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 12.185; see [[sirens]]).
