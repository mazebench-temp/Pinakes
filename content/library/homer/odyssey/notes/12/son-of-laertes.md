---
summary: The Sun: “punish the companions of Odysseus, son of Laertes” (12.378).
grc: [Λαερτιάδεω]
---

Λαερτιάδεω Ὀδυσῆος, the genitive patronymic at the verse-end, is new to the entry, and frequent in the later books (16.104, 17.152 and often). The god names the leader of the men who killed his cattle with his father's name (see [[laertes]] and [[hyperbios]]).
