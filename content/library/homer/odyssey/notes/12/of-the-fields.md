---
summary: The fisherman of the simile “lets the horn of an ox of the fields down into the sea” (12.253).
---

βοὸς κέρας ἀγραύλοιο, the horn on the fishing line, as in the simile of Iris plunging into the sea (*Iliad* 24.80–82). Merry gives Aristarchus' explanation from the scholia: a little pipe of horn that protected the line where it joined the hook, so that the fish could not bite through it (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 12.251; see the translation's note on 12.251–253, and [[the-fisherman]]).
