---
summary: Neaera settled her daughters on Thrinacia to “guard their father's sheep and his cattle with curving horns” (12.136); and “the fine broad-browed cattle with curving horns were grazing” (12.355).
grc: [ἕλικες]
---

ἕλικες, the nominative, is new to the entry; the verse joins it with καλαὶ βόες εὐρυμέτωποι, as 11.289 joins ἕλικας with εὐρυμετώπους (see [[broad-browed]]). Merry: Eurylochus' ὀρθοκραιράων does not clash with it unless ἕλικες means “with crumpled horns” (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 12.348; see [[straight-horned]] and [[the-cattle-of-helios]]).
