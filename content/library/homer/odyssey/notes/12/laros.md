---
summary: Eurylochus: “on the sea-washed island we might make ready a pleasant supper” (12.283).
en: [pleasant]
---

λαρὸν … δόρπον, the supper of men “worn out by weariness and by sleep” (12.281), which Odysseus would have them forgo; the English here is “pleasant” (see [[sea-washed]], [[feast-type-scene]] and [[eurylochus]]).
