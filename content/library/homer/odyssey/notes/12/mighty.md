ἰφθίμῃ ἀλόχῳ, the strong wife of rank (as *Iliad* 5.415), is Arete, to whom Odysseus told his voyage from Ogygia on the evening he came to the palace (7.240–297; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 12.451, referring to his note on 7.244; see [[arete-queen-of-the-phaeacians]] and [[the-tale-to-alcinous]]).
