---
summary: Odysseus to the helmsman: “and to you, helmsman, I give this charge; lay it / to heart, since you handle the steering oar of the hollow ship” (12.217–218).
refs: [12.217-218]
---

ἀλλʼ ἐνὶ θυμῷ / βάλλευ, the second half of the formula with θυμῷ for φρεσί, running over the verse (see the general article). The charge is to keep the ship away from the smoke and the wave and make for the crag (12.219–221); the ship is the subject of ἐξορμήσασα, and κεῖσε, “there,” points toward the Wanderers (see the translation's note on 12.219–221, and [[steering-oar]] and [[hollow-ships]]).
