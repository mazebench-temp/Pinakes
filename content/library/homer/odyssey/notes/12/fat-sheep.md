summary: Circe: “there in great numbers / graze the cattle of the Sun and his fat sheep” (12.127–128); on the island, “and many fat sheep of Hyperion the Sun” (12.263); and Odysseus: “for these are the cattle and fat sheep of a dread god” (12.322).
grc: ["ἴφια μῆλʼ"]
---

Teiresias' pair from the last book (11.108), three times; ἴφια μῆλʼ, elided, is new to the entry. The flocks are counted with the herds, “seven herds of cattle, and as many fine flocks of sheep, / fifty in each” (12.129–130), but only the cattle are killed (12.353–355; see [[the-cattle-of-helios]], [[sheep]] and [[cattle]]).
