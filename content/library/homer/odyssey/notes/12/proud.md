---
summary: θυμὸς ἀγήνωρ three times: “So she spoke, and our proud spirit was persuaded” (12.28); “So I spoke, and their proud spirit was persuaded” (12.324); and the helmsman struck by the mast “fell from the deck, and his proud spirit left his bones” (12.414).
---

12.412–414 are the verses of the death of Epicles, whom Ajax struck on the head with a stone so that he fell from the tower “like a diver” (*Iliad* 12.384–386), with the deck for the tower and θυμὸς ἀγήνωρ for θυμός (see [[like-a-diver]]). In the other two verses the proud spirit yields, to Circe and to Odysseus (see [[our-proud-spirit-was-persuaded]] and [[thymos]]).
