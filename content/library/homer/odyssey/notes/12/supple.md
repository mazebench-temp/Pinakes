summary: On Thrinacia the starving companions hunt “fish and birds, whatever came to their hands, / with curved hooks, and hunger wore at their bellies” (12.331–332).
grc: [γναμπτοῖς]
en: [curved]
---

γναμπτοῖς ἀγκίστροισιν, the verse of Menelaus' companions on Pharos (12.332 = 4.369), is new to the entry and claimed for both places. Bekker and other editors bracketed the verse here as interpolated from Book 4, though Athenaeus quotes it in this place; it is not customary to catch sea-birds with a hook and line (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, critical note on 12.332; see the translation's note on 12.332, and [[fish]] and [[birds]]).
