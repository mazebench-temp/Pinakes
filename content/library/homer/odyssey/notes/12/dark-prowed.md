summary: Scylla takes a man with each head, “snatching him from the dark-prowed ship” (12.100); Circe sends the wind “behind our dark-prowed ship” (12.148); and the cattle graze “not far from the dark-prowed ship” (12.354).

νεὸς κυανοπρῴροιο, three times in the book; 12.148 is the verse of the voyage to the dead (11.6). Scylla's heads reach the dark-prowed ship, and the cattle graze close to it (12.98–100, 12.353–355; see [[scylla]] and [[the-cattle-of-helios]]).
