φύλλα … τέρενα, the young leaves that stand in for barley at the sacrifice (12.358; see [[high-leafed]] and [[sacrifice-type-scene]]); the Iliad has τέρενα of leaves in the simile of the falling ash (*Iliad* 13.180).
