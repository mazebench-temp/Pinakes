summary: Circe: “Only one seafaring ship has sailed past there, / the Argo, in the thoughts of all, sailing from Aeetes” (12.69–70).
grc: [ποντοπόρος]
---

ποντοπόρος νηῦς, the nominative, is new to the entry (again 13.95, 13.161, 14.339). The only ship to pass the Wanderers is named by its epithet before its name (see [[the-argo]], [[the-wanderers]] and [[would-have-had-not]]).
