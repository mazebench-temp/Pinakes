καλαὶ βόες εὐρυμέτωποι ends both verses; the second adds ἕλικες, as of the cattle of Iphiclus in the last book (11.289; see [[with-curving-horns]] and [[the-cattle-of-helios]]). The English “broad-browed” is linked here.
