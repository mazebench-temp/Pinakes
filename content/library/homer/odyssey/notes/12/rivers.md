---
summary: The book opens where the last one ended, as the ship leaves “the stream of the river Oceanus” for the sea (12.1–2).
---

ποταμοῖο … ῥόον Ὠκεανοῖο, “the stream of the river Oceanus” (12.1), takes up the end of Book 11, where “the wave of the stream carried the ship down the river Oceanus” (11.639; see [[oceanus]] and the Book 11 note). Out of it the ship comes to “the wave of the wide-wayed sea”, εὐρυπόροιο, which W. W. Merry explains as “the great and wide sea, as distinguished from the narrow Ocean-stream” (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 12.2; see [[sea]] and [[wide-wayed]]).
