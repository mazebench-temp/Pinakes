summary: Scylla “fishes, questing round the crag, / for dolphins and dogfish, and if anywhere she may catch a greater / sea monster, of which loud-moaning Amphitrite feeds countless numbers” (12.95–97).
---

The sea that roars against the Wanderers in Circe's first description is the sea that feeds the beasts of Scylla's waters in the second (12.59–60; see [[dark-eyed]], [[sea-beasts]] and [[dolphins]]).
