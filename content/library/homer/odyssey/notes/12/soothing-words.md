---
summary: Odysseus goes through the ship “with soothing words, standing beside each man” (12.207).
---

The verse of Aeaea (10.173 = 10.547), before the speech that begins “Friends, we are not without knowledge of evils at all” (12.208). Merry pictures Odysseus walking from the stern down the whole length of the ship, past the rowers, addressing each man in turn (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 12.206; see [[exhortation]] and [[not-without-knowledge-of-evils]]).
