χλωρὸν δέος ᾕρει, the verse-end of Odysseus' terror at the dead (11.43, 11.633), now of the companions, τοὺς δέ, as they watch the whirlpool, while Scylla takes six of them (12.243–246; see [[charybdis]] and [[odysseus-passes-scylla]]).
