---
summary: “but when Zeus, son of Cronus, added the seventh day” (12.399); and “then the son of Cronus set a dark cloud / above the hollow ship” (12.405–406).
---

Κρονίων at the turn from the six days of feasting to the storm; Merry joins ἐπὶ … θῆκε, “added thereto,” the seventh day to the tale of six (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 12.399). The verse of the seventh day comes again in Eumaeus' story (15.477), and the verses of the cloud in the [[the-tale-to-eumaeus|Cretan tale]] (12.405–406 = 14.303–304; see [[hollow-ships]] and [[zeus]]).
