---
summary: As Odysseus' ship comes within call, “the swift-sailing ship did not escape their notice / as it drew near” (12.182–183), the Sirens' first sight of it.
grc: ["ὠκύαλος νηῦς"]
---

ὠκύαλος νηῦς is new to the entry in the Odyssey (again 15.473, of the Phoenicians' ship). The phrase is claimed rather than the bare word, which would also catch the Phaeacian Ὠκύαλος (8.111; see [[ocyalus]]). Merry supplies νῆα with ῥίμφα διώκοντες, “driving swiftly on” (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 12.182; see [[sirens]]).
