---
summary: In the calm the rowers “made the water white with their polished oars of fir” (12.172).
grc: [ξεστῇς]
---

ξεστῇς ἐλάτῃσιν, the oars called by the fir they are made of, in a verse of this book (see the Odyssey translation's formulas, and [[oars]]). ξεστός is elsewhere the word of dressed stone (see the general article).
