---
summary: The Sirens: “and we know all that comes to pass upon the bountiful earth” (12.191).
---

The verse-end of the ground on which the Iliad's warriors stand and lay down their arms (see the general article) is here the whole world of the Sirens' knowledge, set after the war at Troy (12.189–191; see [[anaphora]] and [[sirens]]).
