---
summary: “So I spoke, and at once she answered, brilliant among goddesses” (12.115).
---

Eidothea's verse (4.382, 4.398), given to Circe for her answers in Book 10 (10.487, 10.503) and here for the last time. The answer is the rebuke that Scylla is “not mortal, but an immortal evil” and that the strongest course is to flee from her (12.116–126; see [[brilliant-among-goddesses]], [[immortals]] and [[circe]]).
