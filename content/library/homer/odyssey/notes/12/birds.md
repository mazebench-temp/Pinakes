**Winged things.** Of the Wanderers Circe says: “By that way not even winged things pass, not even the timid doves / that carry ambrosia to father Zeus, / but even of these the sheer rock always takes one away, / and the father sends in another to make up the number” (12.62–65; see [[the-wanderers]] and [[doves]]). ποτητά, “winged things”, from ποτάομαι, “fly”, is found only here; W. W. Merry renders “not even can bird pass by, no! not the timorous doves” (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 12.62; see the translation's row ποτητά; λὶς πέτρη; ἐναρίθμιος). The ship that comes there fares worse: “no ship of men that comes there has ever yet escaped” (12.66), νηῦς … ἀνδρῶν, which Merry sets against the birds (on 12.66).

**Fish and birds.** When the provisions of the ship were spent, the companions “were roaming about in quest of game, as they had to, / fish and birds, whatever came to their hands, / with curved hooks, and hunger wore at their bellies” (12.330–332; see [[fish]], [[provisions]] and [[gaster]]). The last verse is the verse of Menelaus' men on Pharos, who fished for hunger in the same way (4.369; see the Book 4 note on [[fish]]), and Bekker and other editors bracket it here as interpolated from that place, though Athenaeus quotes it as standing here: “It is not customary (though, as Eustath. says, it is possible) to catch (sea-) birds with a hook and line” (Merry's critical note on 12.332). Hunting birds is the last resort of hungry men; the Sun's cattle graze untouched beside them (12.328).

**Sea crows.** After the thunderbolt the companions fall out of the ship, “And like sea crows around the black ship / they were carried on the waves, and the god took away their homecoming” (12.418–419; see [[like-sea-crows]]). The birds are the κορῶναι εἰνάλιαι, the “crows / of the sea” of Calypso's wood, which Merry would render “cormorants” (5.66–67; Merry on 12.418, referring to his note on 5.66; see the Book 5 note). The birds of the sea that were likenesses of gods in the fifth book, the gull and the diving bird, are here a likeness of drowning men.
