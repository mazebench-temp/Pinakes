---
summary: Twice the rowers “struck the gray sea with their oars” (12.147, 12.180): leaving Aeaea, and past the Sirens.
---

The first is the boarding verse of Book 9 (12.147 = 9.104), bracketed by many editors here, since the wind made rowing needless (see the translation's note on 12.147); the second is said of the rowers with wax in their ears, as Odysseus stands bound at the mast (12.177–180; see [[sailing-type-scene]] and [[oars]]).
