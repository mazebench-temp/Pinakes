summary: Eurylochus: “And if we ever come to Ithaca, our native land, / we will at once build a rich temple to Hyperion the Sun” (12.345–346).

πατρίδα γαῖαν in the vow that promises the Sun a temple at home, near the verse of the companions' joy at Circe's ship, “as if we had come to Ithaca, our native land” (10.420; see [[ithaca]], [[hyperion]] and [[nostos]]).
