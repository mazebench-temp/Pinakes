---
summary: Circe names the rocks by their divine name alone: “the Wanderers, as the blessed gods call them” (12.61).
grc: ["θεοὶ μάκαρες καλέουσι"]
---

As with the moly of the last book, the gods' name stands without a human one (10.305). Merry takes Πλαγκταί as “the wandering” rocks, against Nitzsch (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 12.61); on the name and the later identifications of the rocks, see the translation's note on 12.61, and [[blessed-gods]].
