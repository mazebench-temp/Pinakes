---
summary: The other heroes are home, “having escaped both the war and the sea” (1.12); Telemachus would not grieve so if his father had died among his friends “when he had wound out the war” (1.238).
---

τολυπεύω is to wind wool into a ball; Merry takes the τολύπη as the ball of spun yarn, and the metaphor as our “wound up” (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 1.238).
