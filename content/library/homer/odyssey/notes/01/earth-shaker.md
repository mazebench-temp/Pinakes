---
summary: Zeus: “From that time Poseidon the earth-shaker, as for Odysseus, / does not kill him, but drives him wandering from his native land” (1.74–75).
---

Six verses after naming Poseidon “the earth-holder” ([[earth-holder]]), Zeus names him by his other title as he explains why Odysseus wanders: the god does not kill him, but keeps him from home, because of the blinding of his son the Cyclops (1.68–75; [[polyphemus-the-cyclops]]). See [[poseidon]].
