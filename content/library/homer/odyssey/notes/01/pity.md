---
summary: “And all the gods pitied him / except Poseidon” (1.19–20): the gods' pity for Odysseus in the tenth year, with Poseidon's anger as the exception, sets the council in motion.
---

Athena's plea is the same pity in words, “my heart is torn for wise-hearted Odysseus, / ill-fated man” (1.48–49; see [[etor]], [[the-council-of-the-gods]] and [[poseidon]]).
