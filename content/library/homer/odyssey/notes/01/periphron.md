---
summary: Penelope's standing epithet makes its first appearance with her: “the daughter of Icarius, prudent Penelope” (1.329).
en: [prudent]
---

The translation renders περίφρων “prudent” for Penelope, and keeps “wise” for πεπνυμένος, the word of Telemachus ([[wise]]); the Iliad's one περίφρων, of Aegialeia, is “wise” (see the translation's key words). The epithet comes in the whole verse κούρη Ἰκαρίοιο, περίφρων Πηνελόπεια ([[daughter-of-icarius]]), as she hears Phemius' song from her upper room (1.328–329). See [[penelope]].
