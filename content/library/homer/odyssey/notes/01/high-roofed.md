summary: The great house is high: Telemachus leads the stranger “inside the high house” (1.126), and his own chamber “had been built high” in the courtyard (1.426).
grc: ["δόμου ὑψηλοῖο", "ὑψηλὸς δέδμητο"]
en: [high house, built high]
---

The adjective goes with the great house twice in the book: as the stranger enters it (1.126) and as Telemachus goes to sleep in his own high chamber, “in a place with a view all round” (1.425–426; [[in-a-place-with-a-view]]). See [[house]].
