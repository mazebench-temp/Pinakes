en: [strongly made]
---

The translation renders πύκα ποιητός “strongly made,” as in the *Iliad* (*Iliad* 18.608), of the hall and the chamber (see the translation's key words). The two uses mark the two rooms of the book's end: the hall, where Penelope stands holding her veil before her cheeks (1.333–334), and the chamber, whose doors Telemachus opens to go to bed (1.436).
