summary: The verse of Telemachus' journey begins in Athena's plan, “to learn of his dear father's homecoming” (1.94), and her counsel to him, “to learn of your father, who has long been gone” (1.281).
---

Athena plans to send Telemachus to Sparta and sandy Pylos “to learn of his dear father's homecoming, if he may hear of it somewhere” (1.93–94), and in her counsel to him the half-verse returns with a new ending, “and go to learn of your father, who has long been gone” (1.281; see [[the-journey-of-telemachus]] and [[athena-visits-telemachus]]).
