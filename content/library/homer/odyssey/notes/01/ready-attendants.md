---
summary: The suitors are served by “heralds and ready attendants” (1.109).
grc: ["ὀτρηροὶ θεράποντες"]
---

The Iliad's epithet of Agamemnon's heralds is given to the men who wait on the suitors: some mix wine and water, others wash the tables with sponges, others divide the meat (1.109–112; [[heralds]], [[mixing-bowl]], [[table]]).
