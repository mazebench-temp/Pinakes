---
summary: Telemachus names the suitors by their islands, among them “all who rule over craggy Ithaca” (1.247).
grc: [κραναὴν]
---

The islands are Dulichium, Same and wooded Zacynthus, and Ithaca itself, and the best men of each woo Penelope and wear down the house (1.245–248). At the end of the book the same island is “sea-girt Ithaca” in the quarrel over the kingship ([[sea-girt]]). See [[ithaca]].
