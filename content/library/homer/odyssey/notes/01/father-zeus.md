---
summary: Athena twice addresses Zeus as father in the council, “Our father, son of Cronus, highest of lords” (1.45, 1.81).
grc: ["πάτερ ἡμέτερε"]
en: [Our father]
---

The address is a whole verse of the *Iliad*, where Athena uses it to Zeus when he has forbidden the gods to help either side (*Iliad* 8.31). Here she uses it to agree with him about Aegisthus before turning to Odysseus (1.45–48), and again to propose the plan of the poem (1.81–95). Zeus answers her as “My child” (1.64; [[what-a-word]]).
