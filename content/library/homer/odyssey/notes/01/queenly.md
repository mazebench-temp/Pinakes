---
summary: Calypso is “the lady nymph,” νύμφη πότνια, in the proem (1.14).
grc: [πότνιʼ]
---

The title of goddesses and mothers is joined to the nymph's name in the verse that keeps Odysseus from home, “the lady nymph Calypso, brilliant among goddesses, held back” (1.14; [[brilliant-among-goddesses]], [[calypso]]).
