The ranks of battle appear in the first book only in the description of Athena's spear as she arms for the journey to Ithaca (1.99–101), lines that are also the Iliad's (*Iliad* 5.746–747, 8.390–391; see [[athena]], [[spear]] and [[daughter-of-a-mighty-father]]).
