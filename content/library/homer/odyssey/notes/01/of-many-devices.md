---
summary: The stranger's prophecy: Odysseus “will devise how to return, since he is a man of many devices” (1.205).
grc: [πολυμήχανος]
---

The epithet, almost always an address in Homer, is here a reason in the third person: the man of many devices will find his way home, “not even if bonds of iron hold him” (1.204–205; [[it-will-be-fulfilled]]). See [[of-many-turns]] and [[odysseus]].
