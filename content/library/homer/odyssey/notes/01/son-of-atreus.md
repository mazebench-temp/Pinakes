---
summary: Agamemnon is twice “the son of Atreus” in Zeus' speech on Aegisthus: Aegisthus “married the wife of the son of Atreus” (1.35), and “from Orestes vengeance will come for the son of Atreus” (1.40).
---

Zeus names Agamemnon only by his father's name, and the patronymic frames the crime and its punishment. Merry takes τίσις Ἀτρεΐδαο as “vengeance for the murder of Atreus' son,” comparing ποινὴ Πατρόκλοιο (*Iliad* 21.28), and rejects joining Ὀρέσταο Ἀτρεΐδαο, since Homer uses a grandfather's name as a patronymic only for Achilles; he notes too the turn from reported to direct speech in the message of Hermes (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 1.40; see the translation's note on 1.40).
