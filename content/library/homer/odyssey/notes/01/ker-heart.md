---
summary: Telemachus would have his guest bathe and satisfy “your dear heart” before he goes (1.310); Penelope asks Phemius to stop the song that “always wears down my dear heart in my breast” (1.341).
---

Merry takes αἰεί as a sign that the song was already popular (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 1.341; see [[the-song-of-the-returns]]).
