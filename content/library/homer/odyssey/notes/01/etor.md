---
summary: Athena's plea opens with her own heart and Zeus': “my heart is torn for wise-hearted Odysseus” (1.48), and “not even your / dear heart is moved, Olympian” (1.59–60). Telemachus sits among the suitors “grieving at heart” (1.114), and the goddess puts off “the gift that your dear heart bids you give me” until her return (1.316).
