---
summary: The proem's companions are the men Odysseus could not save (1.5–6). Mentes says that he has come “with my ship and companions”, who must be chafing as they wait (1.182, 1.303–304), and Telemachus would not grieve so if his father had fallen “among his companions in the land of the Trojans” (1.237).
grc: [ἑταίρων, ἑτάρους]
---

“But even so he did not save his companions, eager though he was” (1.6): Merry renders οὐδʼ ὣς “not for all that” (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 1.6; see [[nostos]] and [[atasthalie]]).
