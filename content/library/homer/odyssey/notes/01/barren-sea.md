---
summary: The sea of Phorcys, the old god of the sea, father of the nymph Thoosa: “the daughter of Phorcys, who rules over the barren sea” (1.72).
---

Zeus gives the genealogy of the Cyclops whom Odysseus blinded: his mother was the nymph Thoosa, “the daughter of Phorcys, who rules over the barren sea” (1.71–72). The epithet stands in the half-verse ἁλὸς ἀτρυγέτοιο μέδοντος that names Phorcys by his realm. See [[phorcys-the-sea-god]], [[thoosa]] and [[sea]].
