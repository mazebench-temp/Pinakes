---
summary: Zeus “spoke his words among the immortals” (1.31) and answers Athena, “what a word has escaped the barrier of your teeth!” (1.64); Telemachus greets the stranger with winged words (1.122).
grc: ["=ἔπεʼ"]
---

Merry takes ἕρκος ὀδόντων as the teeth themselves, which look like a fence when the lips part to speak, rather than the lips (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 1.64; see the translation's note on 1.64).
