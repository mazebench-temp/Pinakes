---
summary: Athena counsels Telemachus to kill the suitors “whether by guile or openly” (1.296).
---

Guile and open force are the two ways Athena leaves open: “how you may kill the suitors in your halls, / whether by guile or openly” (1.295–296), with Orestes held up as the example (1.298–300; see [[the-suitors]] and [[orestes]]).
