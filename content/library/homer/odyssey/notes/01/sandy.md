---
summary: Athena will send Telemachus “to Sparta and to sandy Pylos” (1.93).
grc: [ἠμαθόεντα]
---

The epithet comes with the first mention of the journey that will fill Books 3 and 4, in Athena's plan in the council (1.93–95), and Pylos is named again without it when she gives Telemachus the same counsel (1.284; [[pylos]], [[the-journey-of-telemachus]]).
