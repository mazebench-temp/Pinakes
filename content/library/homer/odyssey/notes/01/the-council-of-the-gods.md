summary: While Poseidon is away among the Ethiopians, the other gods gather in the halls of Zeus. Zeus, remembering Aegisthus, complains that mortals blame the gods for the sorrows their own recklessness brings them; Athena turns the talk to Odysseus, held by Calypso, and Zeus agrees that he shall come home in spite of Poseidon's anger. Hermes is to go to Calypso, and Athena to Ithaca to rouse Telemachus.
