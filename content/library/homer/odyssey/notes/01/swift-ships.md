summary: Odysseus “had gone there too on his swift ship” to Ephyra for poison (1.260), and the stranger says she will go down to her “swift ship” (1.303).
grc: [θοῆς]
---

Both ships belong to stories told by the stranger: Odysseus' voyage to Ilus of Ephyra in search of a man-slaying drug (1.259–262; [[odysseus-seeks-poison]]), and the ship of Mentes, which she says is waiting for her at Rheithron with her companions (1.185–186, 1.303–304). See [[ships]].
