---
summary: Menelaus, says Athena, “was the last of the bronze-armored Achaeans to come home” (1.286).
---

The epithet of the army at Troy is used of the Achaeans on their way home: Menelaus came back last of them all, and so may have news of Odysseus (1.285–286; [[menelaus]], [[the-journey-of-telemachus]]). See [[achaeans]].
