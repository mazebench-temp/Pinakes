summary: On the evening of Athena's visit Telemachus speaks to the suitors for the first time. Let them feast in peace tonight, but at dawn in the assembly he will order them out of his house, and if they go on devouring it, he will call on Zeus for requital. Antinous mocks his lofty speech and prays that he may never be king; Eurymachus answers courteously and asks about the stranger, and Telemachus keeps to himself that he has known the goddess.
