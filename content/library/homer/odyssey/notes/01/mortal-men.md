---
summary: Telemachus on his father: “of him who was the most ill-starred of mortal men / they say that I was born” (1.219–220).
---

The answer to the stranger's question, whether he is truly the son of Odysseus, ends with the phrase: Telemachus wishes he were the son of some blessed man who grew old among his possessions, “But as it is, of him who was the most ill-starred of mortal men / they say that I was born” (1.217–220).
