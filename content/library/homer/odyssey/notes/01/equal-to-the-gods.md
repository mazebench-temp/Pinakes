---
summary: When Athena has gone, Telemachus goes among the suitors, “a man equal to the gods” (1.324).
---

The epithet marks the change the goddess has made. She has put might and daring in his heart, and he has understood that the stranger was a god (1.320–323); “And at once he went among the suitors, a man equal to the gods” (1.324). When the stranger came, “godlike Telemachus” was sitting among the suitors, grieving at heart (1.113–114; [[godlike]]); now he goes among them as one of the heroes.
