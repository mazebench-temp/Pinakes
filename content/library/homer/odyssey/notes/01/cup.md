# κύπελλα (1.142), claimed in the Iliad by its notes.
grc: [κύπελλα]
---

“and a carver lifted up platters of meat and set them before them, / of every kind, and set golden cups beside them” (1.141–142; see [[feast-type-scene]] and [[table]]). The two verses return at Menelaus' table in Sparta (*Odyssey* 4.57–58). The cups are gold, as the pitcher is (1.137; see [[basin-and-pitcher]] and [[gold-and-silver]]), and the herald fills them, “pouring wine for them” (1.143; see [[cupbearing]] and [[wine]]).
