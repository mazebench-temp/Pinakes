---
summary: Hermes is to tell “the nymph of the lovely tresses” the gods' unerring counsel (1.86).
grc: [ἐυπλοκάμῳ]
en: [of the lovely tresses]
---

The epithet belongs to Calypso in Athena's plan (1.84–87; [[calypso]]). Merry notes that ἐϋπλόκαμος does not mean only “fair-haired,” like ἠΰκομος, but points to the beauty of her braided hair, πλόκαμοι being ringlets or plaits (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 1.86).
