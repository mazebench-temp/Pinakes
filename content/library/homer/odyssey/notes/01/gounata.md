---
summary: The knees of the gods, on which the future "lies": Odysseus' return (1.267) and the kingship of Ithaca (1.400).
---

Twice in the book the future is said to lie on the knees of the gods, θεῶν ἐν γούνασι κεῖται: Athena, as Mentes, says it of Odysseus' return and vengeance (1.267), and Eurymachus of which of the Achaeans will be king in Ithaca (1.400; see [[on-the-knees-of-the-gods]]).
