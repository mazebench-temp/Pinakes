summary: The title is given to the men Athena's spear overpowers, “the ranks of men, / of heroes” (1.100–101), to old Laertes, “the hero Laertes” (1.189), and to the Ithacans whom Telemachus is to call to the assembly, “the Achaean heroes” (1.272).
grc: [ἥρωα, ἥρωας]
---

As in the Iliad, ἥρως is a title of respect, not the demigod of later cult: an old man on his farm is a hero, and so are the men of Ithaca in their assembly (see [[laertes]] and [[assembly]]). Merry notes that the Achaeans were found in Ithaca too, and that the name served for the whole Greek race (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 1.90; see [[achaeans]]).
