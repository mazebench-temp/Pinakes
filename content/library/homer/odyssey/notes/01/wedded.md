---
summary: Zeus: Aegisthus, against fate, “married the wife of the son of Atreus, / his wedded wife, and killed him on his return” (1.35–36).
grc: ["ἄλοχον μνηστήν"]
---

The epithet names what Aegisthus took: a lawful wife, won by courtship and gifts, ἄλοχον μνηστήν. The same root gives the suitors their name, μνηστῆρες, and the gods' warning to Aegisthus was not to woo her (μνάασθαι, 1.39), the word Telemachus uses of the suitors who woo his mother (μνῶνται, 1.248). See [[aegisthus]] and [[clytemnestra-and-the-return]].
