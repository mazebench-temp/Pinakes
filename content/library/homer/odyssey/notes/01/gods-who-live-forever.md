---
summary: Ilus would not give Odysseus poison, “since he feared the indignation of the gods who live forever” (1.263); and Telemachus will “call upon the gods who live forever” against the suitors (1.378).
grc: ["αἰὲν ἐόντας"]
---

In the Odyssey the formula is split by its verb, θεοὺς … αἰὲν ἐόντας, with the gods at the head of the phrase and their everlasting being at the end of the verse. Telemachus' threat to the suitors (1.376–380) returns word for word in the assembly of Book 2 (2.141–145). See [[nemesis]] and [[immortals]].
