---
summary: The proem names Troy by its citadel, “the sacred citadel of Troy” (1.2), that Odysseus sacked.
grc: [ἱερὸν]
---

The epithet is given to the city in the verse that names the hero's great deed, “after he had sacked the sacred citadel of Troy” (1.2; [[troy]], [[of-many-turns]]). The *Iliad* gives the same epithet to the city Achilles sacked, “Thebe, the sacred city of Eetion” (*Iliad* 1.366). See [[sacrifice]] for ἱερά as sacrifices (1.61, 1.66).
