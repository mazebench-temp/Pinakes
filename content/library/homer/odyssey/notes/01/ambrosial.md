---
summary: Athena's sandals are “ambrosial, golden” (1.97), as Hermes' are in the Iliad.
grc: [ἀμβρόσια]
---

The sandals of the gods' journeys carry the goddess over sea and land “as fast as the blasts of the wind” (1.96–98), in the verses that carry Hermes to Priam in the *Iliad* (*Iliad* 24.340–342) and to Calypso in Book 5 (5.44–46). The epithet makes them the gods' own things, like the hair of Zeus and the robe of Hera in the *Iliad*. See [[dressing-scene]] and [[boundless-sea]].
