---
summary: Telemachus sits among the suitors “seeing in his mind his brave father” (1.115).
grc: ["πατέρʼ ἐσθλὸν"]
---

The phrase is the first thing the poem tells of Telemachus' thoughts: he sees his father in his mind coming home from somewhere to scatter the suitors and take back his honor (1.113–117). The stranger appears at that moment (1.118). See [[telemachus]] and [[fathers-and-sons]].
