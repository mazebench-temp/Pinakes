---
summary: The herald puts “a beautiful lyre” in Phemius' hands (1.153), and Telemachus' chamber stands “in the beautiful courtyard” (1.425).
---

The epithet goes with the instrument of the singer who sings for the suitors under compulsion (1.153–154; [[lyre]], [[phemius]]) and with the courtyard of the house, where Telemachus' chamber was built high (1.425–426; [[in-a-place-with-a-view]]).
