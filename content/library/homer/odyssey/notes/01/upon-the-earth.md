---
summary: Telemachus: there is no comfort for him, “even if some one of the men on earth / says that he will come” (1.167–168).
---

The phrase sets the reports of men against the knowledge that Telemachus lacks: no mortal's word can give him comfort, since his father's day of homecoming is lost (1.166–168; [[day-of-homecoming]]). The stranger who hears him is not one of the men on earth. See [[telemachus]].
