---
summary: Telemachus stands the stranger's spear “inside a polished spear-rack” (1.128), and a handmaid draws up “a polished table” (1.138).
grc: [ἐυξόου, ξεστὴν]
---

The two epithets are those of the entry, ἐΰξοος of worked wood and ξεστός of a smoothed surface. The spear-rack holds the many spears of Odysseus (1.127–129), and the table is part of the verses of the guest's meal (1.138; [[water-for-the-hands]], [[table]]).
