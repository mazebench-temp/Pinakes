---
summary: The proem calls on the Muse as “goddess, daughter of Zeus” (1.10).
grc: ["θύγατερ Διός"]
---

The vocative names the Muse as Zeus' daughter, as the *Iliad* names the Muses Διὸς αἰγιόχοιο θυγατέρες before the Catalogue (*Iliad* 2.491), a parallel Merry gives (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 1.1). The Odyssey uses the same address for Artemis (20.61). See [[muses]] and [[invocation-of-the-muses]].
