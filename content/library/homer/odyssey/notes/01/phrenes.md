summary: Hermes could not persuade the φρένες of Aegisthus (1.42); Telemachus sees his father “in his mind” (1.115), perceives the god there (1.322), and ends the day planning “in his mind the journey that Athena had shown him” (1.444).
grc: [φρεσίν]
---

Merry takes ὀσσόμενος … ἐνὶ φρεσίν (1.115) as seeing in one's mind's eye (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 1.115; see [[fathers-and-sons]]).
