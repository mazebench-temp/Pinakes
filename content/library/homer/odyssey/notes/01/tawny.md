---
summary: Mentes carries “gleaming iron” to trade for bronze at Temese (1.184).
grc: ["αἴθωνα σίδηρον"]
en: [gleaming]
---

The translation renders αἴθων of iron “gleaming,” as in the *Iliad* (*Iliad* 4.485; [[iron]]). The cargo is part of the stranger's cover story, a trading voyage over the wine-dark sea to men of another tongue (1.182–184; [[temese]], [[wine-dark]]). Merry takes Temese for Tamassus in Cyprus, famous for its copper mines (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 1.184); the translation keeps χαλκός “bronze” (see the translation's note on 1.184).
