---
summary: Penelope asks Phemius to stop his song, “since upon me above all has come grief not to be forgotten” (πένθος ἄλαστον, 1.342).
---

Merry takes τοίην in the next line as taking up ἄλαστον: so noble a man has she lost that the grief for him cannot be forgotten (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 1.343; see [[the-song-of-the-returns]] and [[penelope]]).
