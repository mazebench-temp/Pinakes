# οἶνον (1.110, 1.340).
grc: [οἶνον]
en: [wine]
---

The suitors' wine is Odysseus' own. When Athena arrives, the heralds and attendants are “mixing wine and water in the mixing bowls” (1.110; see [[mixing-bowl]] and [[heralds]]); a herald goes to and fro pouring wine for Telemachus and his guest (1.143; see [[cupbearing]]); and the young men fill the bowls brim-full for the suitors (1.148). Penelope, coming down in tears, asks Phemius to sing another song, “and let them in silence / drink their wine” (1.339–340; see [[the-song-of-the-returns]]). The house's wine is kept in the storeroom, where in the next book Telemachus asks Eurycleia for the sweetest after the one she keeps for Odysseus (*Odyssey* 2.340–343, 2.349–352; see [[eurycleia]]).
