The verse in which the goddess arms is a warrior's verse: in the *Iliad* it is the spear of Nestor, roused in the night (*Iliad* 10.135), of Nestor again (*Iliad* 14.12), and of Teucer (*Iliad* 15.482), and in the Odyssey Telemachus takes up such a spear (15.551, 20.127). See [[strong-spear]], [[arming-scene]] and [[spear]].
