---
summary: Athena, as Mentes, sends Telemachus to Nestor first: “First go to Pylos and ask brilliant Nestor” (1.284). He is one of the Achaeans who came home from Troy.
---

πρῶτα μὲν ἐς Πύλον ἐλθὲ καὶ εἴρεο Νέστορα δῖον, “First go to Pylos and ask brilliant Nestor” (1.284; see [[pylos]], [[brilliant]] and [[the-journey-of-telemachus]]). In the council Athena had named the two places the other way round, “And I will send him to Sparta and to sandy Pylos” (1.93); in her counsel to Telemachus she gives the order of the journey, Pylos and then Sparta (1.284–285; see [[menelaus]]). Nestor is the only one of the Achaean leaders named in the book who came home safely and quickly; in the third book he tells Telemachus that he came home without news, and knows nothing of those who were saved or lost but what he has heard (3.165–187; see [[the-returns-of-the-achaeans]]). The rendering of the third book is a plain rendering, not the translation.
