---
summary: Telemachus defends Phemius to his mother: “why do you begrudge the trusty singer / giving delight in whatever way his mind is roused?” (1.346–347).
grc: [ἐρίηρον]
---

The epithet of companions is given to the singer as Telemachus answers Penelope, who has asked Phemius to stop his song of the Achaeans' return (1.337–347). It is not the singers who are to blame, he says, but Zeus (1.347–349; [[who-is-to-blame]], [[phemius]], [[the-song-of-the-returns]]).
