---
summary: Athena's golden sandals carry her “over the waters / and over the boundless earth” (1.97–98), in the verses that carry Hermes in the Iliad.
---

The three verses of the sandals (1.96–98) are those of Hermes setting out for Priam in the *Iliad* (24.340–342), and of Hermes again when Zeus sends him to Calypso (5.44–46). Here the boundless earth is the land Athena crosses on her way from Olympus to Ithaca (1.102–103). See [[dressing-scene]] and [[ambrosial]].
