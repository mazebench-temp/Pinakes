---
summary: Athena bids Telemachus declare his word to the assembly, “and let the gods be witnesses” (1.273).
---

The gods are called as witnesses, μάρτυροι, to what Telemachus will declare before the Achaeans (1.272–273; see [[assembly]] and [[athena-visits-telemachus]]), as Agamemnon calls them to witness the oaths before the duel, “be witnesses, and keep watch over the trusty oaths” (*Iliad* 3.280), and Hector to witness his offer to Achilles (*Iliad* 22.254–255). Zeus is ἐπιμάρτυρος, “witness”, in Hector's challenge (*Iliad* 7.76); Merry prints the word of this line as one, ἐπιμάρτυροι, with ἐπί meaning “thereto” (Merry on 1.273).
