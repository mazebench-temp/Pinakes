---
summary: Antinous first calls Telemachus “a man of lofty speech”, mocking the boldness the gods seem to be teaching him (1.385).
---

“Telemachus, surely the gods themselves are teaching you / to be a man of lofty speech and to speak boldly” (1.384–385): Antinous answers the young man's first stand against the suitors with the word he will turn into a refrain of mockery in the next book (see [[antinous]] and [[telemachus-and-the-suitors]]).
