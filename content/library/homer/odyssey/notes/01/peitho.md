summary: Hermes' warning did not persuade Aegisthus (πεῖθʼ, 1.42–43); Athena's counsel to Telemachus begins “if you will obey me” (1.279); and Telemachus tells Eurymachus that he no longer trusts in news of his father (πείθομαι, 1.414).
grc: [πεῖθʼ, πείθομαι]
en: [trust]
---

Aegisthus is the man who would not be persuaded, though a god warned him (1.37–43; see [[who-is-to-blame]] and [[aegisthus]]). Merry takes πείθομαι (1.414) not as πέποιθα, “trust”, but as “let myself be persuaded by” (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 1.414; see [[obedience-and-persuasion]]).
