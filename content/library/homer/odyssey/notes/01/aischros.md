---
summary: The stranger says that any man of good sense would be indignant “seeing so much that is shameful” at the suitors' feast (αἴσχεα, 1.229).
---

Merry renders the end of the line “who should come among them with his senses about him” (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 1.229; see [[nemesis]] and [[hybris]]).
