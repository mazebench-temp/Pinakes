summary: The housekeeper of the meal is αἰδοίη, “revered” (1.139), and the suitors are ἀναιδέες, “shameless”: the stranger wishes for Odysseus, “who would lay his hands on the shameless suitors” (1.254; see [[anaideia]]).
grc: [αἰδοίη]
---

νέμεσις, the blame of others, is what αἰδώς fears, and Merry compares αἰδῶ καὶ νέμεσιν (*Iliad* 13.122) for Ilus' fear of the gods' indignation (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 1.263; see [[nemesis]]).
