---
summary: Zeus answers Athena's plea for Odysseus: “Answering her, Zeus the cloud-gatherer said” (1.63).
---

The verse of reply ([[answering-him]]) gives Zeus his sky-god's epithet as he answers Athena's reproach, “Why are you so angry with him, Zeus?” (1.62), with his own rebuke, “My child, what a word has escaped the barrier of your teeth!” (1.64; [[what-a-word]]). See [[zeus]].
