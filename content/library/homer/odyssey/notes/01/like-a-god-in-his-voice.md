---
summary: Telemachus asks the suitors for silence: “it is a fine thing to listen to a singer / such as this one is, like the gods in his voice” (1.370–371).
grc: ["θεοῖς ἐναλίγκιος αὐδήν"]
en: [like the gods in his voice]
---

The plural of the formula praises Phemius, and the same two verses open Odysseus' tale to the Phaeacians in praise of Demodocus (9.3–4). The translation keeps the Iliad's rendering, with “gods” for the plural (see the translation's table of formulas). See [[phemius]] and [[telemachus-and-the-suitors]].
