---
summary: The stranger remembers Odysseus before he sailed for Troy, “where the others too, / the best of the Argives, went in their hollow ships” (1.210–211).
---

Mentes claims to have met Odysseus often, “before he embarked for Troy” (1.209–210), and the epithet of the ships belongs to that memory of the expedition. Since then, he says, neither has seen the other (1.212). See [[ships]].
