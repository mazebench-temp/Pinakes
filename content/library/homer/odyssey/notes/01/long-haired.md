---
summary: Athena's plan: Telemachus is to call “the long-haired Achaeans to an assembly” (1.90).
---

The Iliad's formula for the army at Troy names the men of Ithaca who will gather in Book 2 (1.88–92). When Athena gives Telemachus the same counsel, they are “the Achaean heroes” (1.272; [[heros]]). See [[achaeans]] and [[assembly]].
