---
summary: The proem's Odysseus suffered on the sea “seeking to win his own life and the homecoming of his companions” (ψυχήν, 1.5).
grc: [ψυχὴν]
en: [life]
---

Merry derives ἀρνύμενος from a root meaning “gain”, as in μίσθαρνος, and renders “seeking to win” (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 1.5). The Iliad's proem sends many strong souls of heroes to Hades (*Iliad* 1.3–4; see [[proem]]); the Odyssey's hero strives to keep his own, and to bring his companions home (see [[nostos]]).
