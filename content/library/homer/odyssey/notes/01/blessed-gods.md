---
summary: Athena takes up Zeus' word: “if this is now truly dear to the blessed gods, / that Odysseus of much wisdom should return to his own house” (1.82–83).
---

Zeus has just said that the gods must take thought for Odysseus' homecoming, and that Poseidon will not be able to strive alone against them all (1.76–79). Athena turns his words into a condition and a plan: if the blessed gods wish it, let Hermes go to Calypso and let her go to Ithaca (1.82–95; [[of-much-wisdom]], [[the-council-of-the-gods]]). See [[olympians]].
