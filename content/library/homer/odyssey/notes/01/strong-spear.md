---
summary: Athena takes up “a strong spear, tipped with sharp bronze” (1.99), and later bids Telemachus be ἄλκιμος, “valiant” (1.302).
grc: [ἄλκιμος]
en: [valiant]
---

The same word arms the goddess and is her charge to the young man. Before she leaves Olympus she takes the strong spear with which she overpowers the ranks of heroes (1.99–101; [[arming-scene]], [[daughter-of-a-mighty-father]]); in the likeness of Mentes she ends the example of Orestes with “You too, my friend … / be valiant, so that some one of those born hereafter may speak well of you” (1.301–302; [[born-hereafter]]).
