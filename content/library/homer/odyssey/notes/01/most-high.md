---
summary: Athena's address to Zeus in the council, “Our father, son of Cronus, highest of lords” (1.45, 1.81).
grc: ["ὕπατε κρειόντων"]
en: [highest of lords]
---

The verse is the address of the *Iliad* (*Iliad* 8.31), and Athena speaks it twice in the first council of the Odyssey, before her judgment on Aegisthus and before her plan for Odysseus (1.45, 1.81; [[father-zeus]], [[son-of-cronus]]). See [[zeus]].
