summary: The poem's first speech is Zeus' complaint that mortals “blame the gods” (1.32). At the end of the visit a mortal knows that a god has been with him: Telemachus “thought that it was a god” (1.323), and later “in his heart he knew the immortal goddess” (1.420).
grc: [θεάων, θεοὺς]
