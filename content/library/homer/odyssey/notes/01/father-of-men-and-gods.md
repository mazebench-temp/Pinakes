---
summary: Zeus opens the first council of the gods: “And among them the father of men and gods began to speak” (1.28).
---

The gods are gathered in the halls of Olympian Zeus, Poseidon being away among the Ethiopians (1.22–27), and the father of men and gods speaks first, of Aegisthus and of mortals who blame the gods (1.28–43; [[the-council-of-the-gods]], [[who-is-to-blame]]). The same verse brings Zeus to speech in the last book of the *Iliad* (*Iliad* 24.103). See [[zeus]].
