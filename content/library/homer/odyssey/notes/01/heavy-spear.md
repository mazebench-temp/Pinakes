The half-verse is that of Athena's spear in the *Iliad* (*Iliad* 5.746, 8.390) and of Achilles' Pelian ash (*Iliad* 16.141, 19.388). The goddess takes it to Ithaca, where Telemachus receives it at the door and stands it in the spear-rack among the spears of Odysseus (1.104, 1.121, 1.127–129; [[arming-scene]], [[daughter-of-a-mighty-father]]).
