---
summary: Zeus as the Olympian: the gods gather “in the halls of Olympian Zeus” (1.27), and Athena reproaches him by the title, “Yet not even your / dear heart is moved, Olympian” (1.59–60).
grc: [Ὀλυμπίου]
---

The title frames the council. The narrator places the gods in Zeus' halls with it (1.26–27), and Athena uses it as an address in the plea that ends with the play on Odysseus' name, “Why are you so angry with him, Zeus?” (1.59–62). See [[zeus]] and [[olympians]].
