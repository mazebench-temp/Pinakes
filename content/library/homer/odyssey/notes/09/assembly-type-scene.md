---
summary: On the island, “then I called an assembly and spoke among them all” (9.171), and Odysseus tells his companions that he will cross with his own ship to make trial of the men on the mainland (9.172–176).
refs: [9.171-177]
---

The assembly is reduced to its speech: no summons, one speaker and no answer, and the speaker goes on board as soon as he has spoken (9.177). The verse of the summons comes again on Circe's island (10.188; see the Odyssey translation's formulas; [[assembly]], [[to-the-land-of-what-mortals]]).
