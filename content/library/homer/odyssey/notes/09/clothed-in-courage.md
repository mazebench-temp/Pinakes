---
summary: Odysseus foresees “that a man would come upon us, clothed in great courage, / a savage” (9.214–215), and the Cyclops had expected “some tall and handsome man / to come here, clothed in great courage” (9.513–514).
---

The same words describe each antagonist as the other imagined him: the Cyclops in Odysseus' foreboding, and the hero of the prophecy in the Cyclops' expectation, who turned out to be “small and a nobody and feeble” (9.515; [[small-and-of-no-account]], [[proud]], [[brave]]).
