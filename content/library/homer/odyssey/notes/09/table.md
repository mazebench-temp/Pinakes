summary: In his praise of the Phaeacians' feast Odysseus tells of the banqueters sitting in order, with the tables beside them laden with bread and meat (9.7–9).
# τράπεζαι (9.8) is in the entry.
en: [tables]
---

“And the banqueters throughout the house listen to a singer, / sitting in order, and beside them the tables are laden / with bread and meat, and drawing wine from the mixing bowl / the wine-pourer carries it round and pours it into the cups” (9.7–10; see [[feast]], [[singers]], [[cupbearing]] and [[mixing-bowl]]). The tables are those of the feast at which he speaks, where a table had been set beside the blind singer (8.69; see the Book 8 note).
