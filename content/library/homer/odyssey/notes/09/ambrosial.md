---
summary: The other Cyclopes ask why Polyphemus has cried out “through the ambrosial night, and make us sleepless” (9.404).
---

νύκτα διʼ ἀμβροσίην is the Iliad's phrase of the night of the raid (*Iliad* 10.41, 10.142) and of Hermes' question to Priam (*Iliad* 24.363); the scholia record ὀρφναίην, “dark,” as a variant here (see the translation's note on 9.402–404; [[dark]], [[sleepless]]).
