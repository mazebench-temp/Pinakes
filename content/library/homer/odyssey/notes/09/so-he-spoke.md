summary: Seventeen speeches close with the formula in the tale, seven of them with the narrator's “So I spoke” (9.272, 9.287, 9.353, 9.368, 9.480, 9.506, 9.526); the Cyclopes leave with “So they spoke as they went away” (9.413), the companions' plea ends “So they spoke, but they did not persuade my proud heart” (9.500), and the prayer “So he spoke in prayer, and the dark-haired god heard him” (9.536).
grc: ["ὣς ἄρʼ ἔφαν", "ὣς φάσαν"]
except: [9.280]
---

At 9.280 ἦ καί is the disjunctive “or,” in the Cyclops' question, “whether somewhere at the far edge of the land, or near,” not the close of a speech, and the note excepts it, as Book 1 excepts 1.158, 1.175 and 1.389. ὣς φάσαν and ὣς ἄρʼ ἔφαν, “So they spoke,” are the plural closes, the second as in the Iliad (*Iliad* 3.161). At 9.371 the Cyclops' last words before his sleep end with ἦ καί, “He spoke, and leaning back he fell on his back” ([[answering-him]], [[hear-me]]).
