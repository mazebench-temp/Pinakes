κλυτὸν ἐννοσίγαιον, as in the Iliad (*Iliad* 15.173), of the father who, the Cyclops says, “himself, if he wishes, will heal me” (9.520; see the translation's note on 9.518; [[famous]], [[earth-shaker]], [[poseidon]], [[pompe]]).
