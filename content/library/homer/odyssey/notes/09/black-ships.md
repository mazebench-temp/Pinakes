---
summary: The Cyclops' club is likened “to the mast of a black ship of twenty oars, / a broad freight ship, which crosses the great gulf” (9.322–323).
grc: ["ἐεικοσόροιο μελαίνης"]
---

Merry notes that Milton imitated the simile in his description of Satan's spear (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 9.322; see the Odyssey translation's key words, φορτίς; [[mast]], [[mace]]).
