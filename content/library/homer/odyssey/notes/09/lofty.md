---
summary: The door-stone: “so lofty a rock he set against the doorway” (9.243).
---

Merry takes the use of the word for a stone block as conclusive against Apollonius' derivation from ἥλιος and βαίνειν, a rock that only the sun can tread, and prefers Buttmann's, “hard to climb” (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 9.243; see the translation's note on 9.243; [[door-stone]]).
