---
summary: Shipwrights “would also have made the island well settled for them” (9.130), and the curse's “well-built house” (9.533).
en: [well settled]
---

Merry takes ἐυκτιμένην as predicate: the craftsmen who would have built ships would also have worked the island (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 9.130; see the translation's note on 9.130; [[the-island-of-goats]], [[dear-native-land]]).
