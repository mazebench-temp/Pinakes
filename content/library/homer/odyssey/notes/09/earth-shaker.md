---
summary: Odysseus' lie to the Cyclops, “My ship Poseidon the earth-shaker shattered” (9.283), and his last words to him, “as surely as not even the earth-shaker will heal your eye” (9.525).
---

ἐνοσίχθων is “the earth-shaker,” and ἐννοσίγαιος, in the Cyclops' own words, “the famous shaker of the earth” (9.518), as the Iliad keeps the two apart (see the Odyssey translation's key words; [[shaker-of-the-earth]]). The god named in the lie is the Cyclops' father, who will hear his prayer (9.519, 9.526–536; [[poseidon]]).
