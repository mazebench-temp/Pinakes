---
summary: “As long as it was morning and the sacred day was growing” (9.56), and “when we took the sacred citadel of the Cicones” (9.165).
---

9.56 is the Iliad's verse of the morning of battle (*Iliad* 8.66, 11.84); Merry sets the day that grows before noon against the sun's descent after it (9.58; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 9.58). ἱερὸν πτολίεθρον is Troy's in the proem (1.2), here the city of the Cicones; the stop that the text prints after Κικόνων is a slip (see the translation's note on 9.165; [[cicones]], [[ismarus]], [[sunset-lines]]).
