summary: The ships sail on from each loss “grieving at heart” (9.62, 9.105, 9.565); in the cave, at the Cyclops' first words, “our dear heart was broken” (9.256).
---

The ἦτορ grieves and breaks. The refrain of departure, “From there we sailed on, grieving at heart” (ἀκαχήμενοι ἦτορ), closes the raid on the Cicones, the visit to the Lotus-eaters and the escape from the Cyclops (9.62, 9.105, 9.565; see [[from-there-we-sailed-on]] and [[achos]]). When the Cyclops asks the strangers who they are, “our dear heart was broken, / in terror of his heavy voice and of the monster himself” (κατεκλάσθη φίλον ἦτορ, 9.256–257), the half-verse of Menelaus' despair when Proteus told him he must go back to Egypt (4.481; see [[deos]] and the translation's table of formulas).
