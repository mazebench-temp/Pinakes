---
summary: “Then I planned in my proud heart / to go up close” (9.299–300), and after the first taunt, “So they spoke, but they did not persuade my proud heart” (9.500).
---

μεγαλήτορα θυμόν is “proud heart,” as in the Odyssey's verse of deliberation (5.298; see the Odyssey translation's formulas). Both times the heart is Odysseus' own, once held back by a second thought (9.302), once not (9.501–505; [[decision-scene]], [[say-that-odysseus-blinded-you]]).
