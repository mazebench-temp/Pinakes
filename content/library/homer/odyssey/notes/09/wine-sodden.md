---
summary: “and he belched, sodden with wine” (9.374).
---

οἰνοβαρείων, the participle beside the Iliad's insult οἰνοβαρές (*Iliad* 1.225), states the Cyclops' drunkenness as a fact; Merry takes the clause as giving the reason for the wine and flesh that rush from his throat (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 9.374; [[wine]], [[that-subdues-all]]).
