---
summary: Of the Cyclopes: “They have no assemblies that bring counsel, and no ordinances” (9.112).
grc: ["ἀγοραὶ βουληφόροι"]
en: [that bring counsel]
---

The epithet of the counselors who sit with a king is given here to the assemblies themselves, and the Cyclopes have none: each gives the law to his own household (9.112–115; see the Odyssey translation's key words, ἀγοραὶ βουληφόροι; [[assembly]], [[themis]], [[cyclopes]]).
