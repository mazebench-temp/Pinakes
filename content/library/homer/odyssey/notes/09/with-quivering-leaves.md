---
summary: “and on it is a mountain, / Neritum with its quivering leaves, conspicuous” (9.21–22).
---

Νήριτον εἰνοσίφυλλον is the Catalogue's mountain of Odysseus' men, “who held Ithaca and Neritum with its quivering leaves” (*Iliad* 2.632). Crates and Philoxenus read Νήιον here, the name of the mountain above the harbor in Book 1 (1.186; see the translation's note on 9.22, 9.24; [[neritum]], [[neion]], [[preeminent]]).
