---
summary: Odysseus' praise of Ithaca: “rugged, but a good nurse of young men; and I, for my part, / can see nothing else sweeter than one's own land” (9.27–28).
---

Merry compares κουροτρόφος, “a good nurse of young men,” with βωτιάνειρα, the epithet of Phthia, “nurse of men” (*Iliad* 1.155; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 9.27; see the Odyssey translation's key words, ἀγαθὴ κουροτρόφος). Helen gives the island its other epithet, [[craggy]] (*Iliad* 3.201). See [[ithaca]].
