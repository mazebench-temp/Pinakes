summary: On the island off the Cyclopes' land the men take their curved bows and javelins from the ships and hunt the wild goats in three bands (9.156–158).
# τόξα, the plural, first met in the Odyssey here (9.156; later 10.262 and Book 21 throughout, always the bow); it
# applies in the Odyssey only; the Iliad's are in its notes.
grc: [τόξα]
en: [bows]
---

When the nymphs rouse the goats of the island, “at once we took our curved bows and long-socketed javelins / from the ships, and, arranged in three bands, / we began to shoot; and at once the god gave us game to satisfy the heart” (9.156–158; see [[curved-bow]], [[spear]], [[goats]] and [[the-island-of-goats]]). διὰ … κοσμηθέντες go together, “arranged in three bands” (W. W. Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 9.157; see the translation's note on 9.157). The bow is the weapon of the hunt here; in Book 8 Odysseus boasted of it among the Phaeacians but did not shoot (see the Book 8 note).
