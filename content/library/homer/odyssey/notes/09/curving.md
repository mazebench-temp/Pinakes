---
summary: “Nor did my curving ships go on farther / until we had called three times on each of our wretched companions” (9.64–65).
---

Merry renders, “I let not my ships sail on, till we had thrice called to each one of our poor comrades” (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 9.64; see the translation's note on 9.65; [[lament]], [[wretch]], [[the-raid-on-the-cicones]]).
