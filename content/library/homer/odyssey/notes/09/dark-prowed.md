---
summary: The Cyclops' rocks fall “in front of the dark-prowed ship” (9.482) and “behind the dark-prowed ship” (9.539).
---

The verses of the two throws are the same but for προπάροιθε and μετόπισθε; Merry holds that the verse that follows the first throw, of the stone that failed to reach the tip of the steering oar, belongs only to the second (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 9.483; see the translation's note on 9.483; [[steering-oar]], [[polyphemus-the-cyclops]]).
