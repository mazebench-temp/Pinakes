The steps of the pattern are there, but none of them is a welcome. Odysseus stays against his companions' advice, “so that I might see him himself, and whether he would give me guest-gifts” (9.229); the question that Nestor puts to his guests after the meal (3.69–74), the Cyclops puts before any, and the guest-gift he promises is to be eaten last (9.252–255, 9.369–370; [[xenia]], [[guest-gifts]], [[strangers-who-are-you]], [[the-cave-of-polyphemus]]).
