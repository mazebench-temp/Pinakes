---
summary: “Meanwhile the Cicones went off and called to other Cicones” (9.47).
grc: ["Κίκονες Κικόνεσσι"]
---

The name in two cases sets the people of the coast against their neighbors of the inland, “more numerous and braver too” (9.48; see the translation's note on 9.49–50; [[cicones]], [[the-raid-on-the-cicones]]).
