---
summary: The ram is sacrificed “to Zeus, lord of the dark clouds, son of Cronus, who rules over all” (9.552), and the god takes no heed of the offering.
---

The verse returns when Alcinous sacrifices an ox on the day of Odysseus' departure (13.25; see the Odyssey translation's formulas); here Zeus “took no heed of the offering, / but was pondering how they might all perish” (9.553–554; [[son-of-cronus]], [[sacrifice-type-scene]], [[zeus]]).
