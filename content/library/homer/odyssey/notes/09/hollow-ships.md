---
summary: The men who ate the lotus are bound “in the hollow ships” (9.99), and the Cyclops' flocks are taken “out of the hollow ship” on the island (9.548).
grc: [γλαφυρῇσιν, "γλαφυρῆς ἐκ νηὸς"]
---

γλαφυρός, the epithet of caves, is also the ship's, as in the Iliad's νηυσὶ γλαφυρῇσι (see the general article and [[hollow-caves]], where the Cyclops lives ἐν σπῆι γλαφυρῷ, 9.476). Merry sets νηυσὶν ἔνι against ἐπὶ νῆας in the verse before, “when I had got them there” (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 9.99; [[ships]], [[lotus-eaters]]).
