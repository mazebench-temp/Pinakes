---
summary: In the land of the Cyclopes barley grows unsown and unplowed, with wheat and vines (9.110).
---

Barley is one of the three crops that the Cyclopes neither plant nor plow: “but all these things grow unsown and unplowed, / wheat and barley and vines” (9.109–110; see [[wheat]], [[vine]] and [[cyclopes]]). κριθαί is the plural of κριθή, which the Iliad has only in the short form κρῖ and in the genitive κριθῶν (see the entry). Odysseus' own men keep barley for sacrifice: on the island of the Sun they have none left and must use oak leaves instead (12.357–358, Greek only).
