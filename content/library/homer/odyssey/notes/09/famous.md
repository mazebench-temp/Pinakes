summary: The Cyclops' “fine flocks” (9.308), Odysseus' “famous name” (9.364), and “the famous shaker of the earth” (9.518).
grc: ["κλυτὰ μῆλα", "ὄνομα κλυτόν", "κλυτὸν ἐννοσίγαιον"]
en: [fine]
---

Merry compares κλυτὰ αἰπόλια in Sophocles' *Ajax* and accepts Eustathius' rendering of κλυτά as “fine,” for their number or their beauty (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 9.308; see the Odyssey translation's key words, κλυτός). The famous name that Odysseus gives is Nobody (9.366; [[nobody]], [[shaker-of-the-earth]]).
