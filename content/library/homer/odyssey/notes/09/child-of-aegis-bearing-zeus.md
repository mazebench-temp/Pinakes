The title of the nymphs who planted elms around Eetion's tomb (*Iliad* 6.420) and of Artemis' companions in Book 6 (6.105), here of the nymphs of the island, who rouse the goats for the hunters (9.154–158; [[nymphs]], [[mountain-dwelling]], [[goats]]).
