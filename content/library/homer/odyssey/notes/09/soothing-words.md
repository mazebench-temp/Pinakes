---
summary: Odysseus to the drunken Cyclops: “then I spoke to him with soothing words” (9.363); and the companions after the first taunt “tried to hold me back with soothing words” (9.493).
---

Both times the soft words go with a hard purpose: the false name (9.364–367), and the plea not to provoke the Cyclops, which Odysseus does not heed (9.494–501; see the Odyssey translation's formulas; [[nobody]], [[hard]]).
