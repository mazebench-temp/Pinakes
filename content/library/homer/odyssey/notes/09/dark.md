---
summary: The landing on the island in the dark: “There we sailed in, and some god guided us / through the dark night, and nothing could be seen” (9.142–143).
---

Merry takes ὀρφναίην here not as a standing epithet of night but of this misty night, when the moon was hidden in clouds (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 9.143; 9.144–145; see the translation's note on 9.143–145). The phrase νύκτα διʼ ὀρφναίην is the night raid's in the Iliad (*Iliad* 10.83, 10.276, 10.386), and the scholia record it as a variant for νύκτα διʼ ἀμβροσίην at 9.404 (see the translation's note on 9.402–404; [[ambrosial]], [[sailing-type-scene]]).
