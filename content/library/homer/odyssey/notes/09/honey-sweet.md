---
summary: The lotus, “And whoever of them ate the honey-sweet fruit of the lotus” (9.94), and Maron's wine, “And whenever they drank that honey-sweet red wine” (9.208).
---

Merry compares the epithet of the lotus with Herodotus' account of its fruit, as sweet as the date (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 9.94; see the translation's note on 9.94; [[lotus]], [[wine]], [[red]]).
