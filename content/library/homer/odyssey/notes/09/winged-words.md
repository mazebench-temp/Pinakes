---
summary: The other Cyclopes answer Polyphemus' cry: “And they answered him and spoke winged words” (9.409).
---

The plural verse of the Iliad, of men speaking to one another (*Iliad* 3.155, 24.142); the winged words are the answer that turns on μή τις, “nobody” (9.410–412; [[nobody]], [[answering-him]]).
