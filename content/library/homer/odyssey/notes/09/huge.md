en: [monster]

πέλωρ, the noun beside πελώριος, is said once in the Iliad, of Hephaestus (*Iliad* 18.410); in the Odyssey it names the Cyclops and Scylla (12.87). See [[polyphemus-the-cyclops]] and [[teras]].
