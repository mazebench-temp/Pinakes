---
summary: “For the Cyclopes have no ships with red-painted prows” (9.125).
---

μιλτοπάρῃοι is the epithet of Odysseus' own twelve ships in the Catalogue, “and with him followed twelve ships with red-painted prows” (*Iliad* 2.637; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 9.125); the Cyclopes have neither ships nor shipwrights to cross the sea to other men (9.125–130; [[cyclopes]], [[well-benched]]).
