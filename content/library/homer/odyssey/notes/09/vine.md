# ἄμπελοι (9.110, 9.133) is in the entry.
en: [vines]

In the land of the Cyclopes “all these things grow unsown and unplowed, / wheat and barley and vines, which bear / wine of great clusters, and the rain of Zeus makes it grow for them” (9.109–111; see [[wheat]], [[barley]] and [[cyclopes]]). The Cyclops repeats the verse when he tastes Maron's wine: “for the grain-giving earth bears for the Cyclopes too / wine of great clusters, and the rain of Zeus makes it grow for them; / but this is a branch of ambrosia and nectar” (9.357–359; see [[wine]] and [[polyphemus-the-cyclops]]). ἐριστάφυλος, “of great clusters”, is found only in these two verses in Homer. The empty island off the coast would bear everything in its season: it has meadows by the sea, “well-watered and soft; the vines there would be imperishable” (9.132–133; see [[the-island-of-goats]]).
