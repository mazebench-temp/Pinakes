---
summary: Tasting Maron's wine, the Cyclops calls it “a branch of ambrosia and nectar” (9.359).
---

“For the grain-giving earth bears for the Cyclopes too / wine of great clusters, and the rain of Zeus makes it grow for them; / but this is a branch of ambrosia and nectar” (9.357–359; see [[wine]], [[ambrosia]] and [[polyphemus-the-cyclops]]). Nectar is the gods' wine, poured and mixed like wine (see the entry), and the Cyclops' praise makes Maron's wine a branch of it, ἀπορρώξ, as the Iliad's Titaressus is a branch of the water of the Styx (*Iliad* 2.755; see the translation's row ἀπορρώξ and W. W. Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 9.359).
