summary: Alcinous is “preeminent among all the people” (9.2); to the Cyclops Odysseus claims to be one of “the people of Agamemnon”, who sacked a great city and “destroyed many / people” (9.263–266).
---

The λαός of Book 9 is a people under a king. Odysseus begins the tale by addressing Alcinous as “preeminent among all the people” (9.2; see [[preeminent]] and [[alcinous]]). In the cave he names his men by their king: “we claim to be the people of Agamemnon, son of Atreus, / whose fame is now the greatest under heaven; / so great a city did he sack, and destroyed many / people” (9.263–266; see [[agamemnon]] and [[kleos]]). The Cyclopes, who have no assemblies and no king, are never called a λαός (9.112–115; see [[themis]]).
