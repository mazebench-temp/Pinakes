κρατερὸς Πολύφημος, the name with its epithet, comes at the two moments when Nobody's trick works, at the neighbors' question and at the ram (9.403–412, 9.444–461; [[nobody]], [[polyphemus-the-cyclops]]). κρατερῆφι βίηφι is the Iliad's phrase (*Iliad* 21.501), and Odysseus uses it of the Cyclops again when he reminds his men of the cave before Scylla (12.209–210; [[hollow-caves]]).
