---
summary: The refrain three times in the book: after the Cicones, “From there we sailed on, grieving at heart, / glad to have escaped death, though we had lost our dear companions” (9.62–63); after the Lotus-eaters, the first verse alone (9.105); and after the Cyclops, at the end of the book (9.565–566).
---

The losses it counts are told just before it: six men of each ship at Ismarus (9.60–61), none among the Lotus-eaters, and six in the Cyclops' cave, two at each of three meals (9.289–293, 9.311, 9.344). Merry notes that ἄσμενοι ἐκ θανάτοιο is repeated at 9.566 (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 9.63; see the Odyssey translation's formulas; [[the-raid-on-the-cicones]], [[lotus-eaters]], [[polyphemus-the-cyclops]]).
