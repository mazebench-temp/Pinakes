---
summary: “For the heavenly gods have given me many sorrows” (9.15), Odysseus' reason for not knowing where to begin.
---

The verse is the one with which he began his account to Arete (7.242). Merry puts the weight on πολλά: it is the multitude of the sorrows that makes it hard to know in what order to tell them (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 9.14; [[algos]], [[olympians]]).
