μαρμάρῳ ὀκριόεντι; Merry takes μάρμαρος, from the root of μαρμαίρω, as a white or glittering stone, and cites Patroclus' πέτρος μάρμαρος (*Iliad* 16.735; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 9.499; see the translation's note on 9.499; [[polyphemus-the-cyclops]]).
