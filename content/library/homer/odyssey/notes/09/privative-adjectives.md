---
summary: The land of the Cyclopes, where “all these things grow unsown and unplowed” (9.109), and the island beyond their harbor, “unsown and unplowed all its days” (9.123).
grc: ["ἄσπαρτα καὶ ἀνήροτα", "ἄσπαρτος καὶ ἀνήροτος"]
en: [unsown and unplowed]
---

The same pair describes two lands: the mainland, where the earth gives wheat, barley and vines to people who neither plant nor plow (9.108–111), and the island, rich enough to farm, which no one farms, because the Cyclopes have no ships to reach it (9.123–135; [[cyclopes]], [[the-island-of-goats]], [[imperishable]]).
