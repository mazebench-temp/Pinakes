---
summary: The drunken Cyclops falls asleep: “and sleep / that subdues all took him” (9.372–373).
---

The phrase is the one of the sleep that would not take Achilles (*Iliad* 24.4–5); here the wine brings it, and the sleeper vomits wine and gobbets of human flesh (9.373–374; [[wine-sodden]], [[sweet-sleep]]).
