---
summary: After Malea: “From there for nine days I was borne by deadly winds / over the fish-filled sea” (9.82–83).
---

The fish-filled sea is the one of Menelaus' question about his homecoming (4.381, 4.390; see the Odyssey translation's formulas); it brings Odysseus to the land of the Lotus-eaters (9.83–84; [[deadly]], [[lotus-eaters]], [[sea]]).
