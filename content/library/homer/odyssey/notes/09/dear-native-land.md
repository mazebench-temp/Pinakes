---
summary: “And now I would have reached my native land unharmed” (9.79), and the Cyclops' curse, “But if it is his fate to see his friends and reach / his well-built house and his native land” (9.532–533).
---

9.532–533 are the verses of the gods' decree for Odysseus' return (5.41–42, 5.114–115), turned into a condition, with “his well-built house” for the high-roofed house of Book 5 (see the Odyssey translation's formulas; [[well-built]], [[curses]], [[would-have-had-not]]).
