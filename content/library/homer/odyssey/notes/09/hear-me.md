---
summary: The Cyclops' prayer opens “Hear me, Poseidon, dark-haired earth-holder” (9.528) and closes “So he spoke in prayer, and the dark-haired god heard him” (9.536).
---

The close is the verse of Nestor's and Odysseus' prayers to Athena (3.385, 6.328) with Poseidon's epithet in place of the goddess's name; here what is heard is a curse (see the Odyssey translation's formulas; [[prayer-type-scene]], [[curses]], [[earth-holder]], [[dark-haired]]).
