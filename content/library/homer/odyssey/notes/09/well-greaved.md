---
summary: “And from each ship six of my well-greaved companions / perished” (9.60–61), and “But the ram my well-greaved companions gave to me alone” (9.550).
---

The Iliad's epithet of the Achaeans is given to Odysseus' crews. Merry reports that Crates took the six from each ship as an average, seventy-two men in all from the twelve ships, and that Zoilus was offended at so exact a symmetry (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 9.60; [[hetairos]], [[the-raid-on-the-cicones]]).
