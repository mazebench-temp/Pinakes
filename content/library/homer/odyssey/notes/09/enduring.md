---
summary: Odysseus under the ram: “turning myself over, I held on unceasingly with enduring heart” (9.435).
---

τετληότι θυμῷ, the verse-end of Menelaus' ambush on Pharos (4.447, 4.459). Merry notes that the men were tied to the sheep, but no one could tie Odysseus, who had only his hands to hold on with (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 9.434; see the translation's note on 9.434–435; [[ram]]).
