---
summary: Odysseus to the Cyclops: “And we claim to be the people of Agamemnon, son of Atreus, / whose fame is now the greatest under heaven” (9.263–264).
---

The fame of the sack of Troy is offered as a claim on the host, before the appeal to the gods (9.263–271; [[under-heaven]], [[agamemnon]], [[kleos]]).
