---
summary: “Truly Calypso, brilliant among goddesses, held me back there / in her hollow caves, longing for me to be her husband” (9.29–30).
---

Odysseus names Calypso first and Circe beside her, “the wily one of Aeaea” (9.31–32; [[wily]]). 9.30 repeats the proem's verse (1.15), and most manuscripts lack it here (see the translation's note on 9.29–36; [[calypso]], [[hollow-caves]]).
