---
summary: “So all day long, until the sun went down, / we sat feasting on untold meat and sweet wine” (9.161–162 = 9.556–557).
---

κρέα τʼ ἄσπετα, the meat of the goats of the hunt and then of the Cyclops' flocks; the verse comes four times more in Books 10 and 12 (10.184, 10.468, 10.477, 12.30; see the Odyssey translation's key words, ἄσπετος; [[feast-type-scene]], [[sunset-lines]]).
