---
summary: The Cyclops remembers the prophecy: “There was once a seer here, a man tall and brave” (9.508).
---

ἠύς τε μέγας τε, the Iliad's half-verse for a fine figure of a man (*Iliad* 3.167, 3.226), is given to the seer Telemus; the Cyclops looked for “some tall and handsome man” to fulfill the prophecy (9.513; [[telemus]], [[divination]], [[small-and-of-no-account]]).
