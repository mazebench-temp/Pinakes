---
summary: Calypso kept Odysseus “in her hollow caves” (9.30); the Cyclopes “live on the peaks of high mountains / in hollow caves” (9.113–114); and Odysseus taunts the Cyclops, “it was not a weakling's companions after all that you were / to eat in your hollow cave with your mighty strength” (9.475–476).
---

The same plural serves the nymph's home and the dwellings of the Cyclopes (see the Odyssey translation's formulas, ἐν σπέσσι γλαφυροῖσι; [[calypsos-cave]], [[cyclopes]], [[the-cave-of-polyphemus]], [[hollow-ships]]).
