---
summary: “whose fame is now the greatest under heaven” (9.264), of Agamemnon.
---

Merry takes μέγιστον as predicate, “his fame, far as the heaven covers, is the greatest” (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 9.264). Odysseus has said of his own fame that it “reaches the sky” (9.20; [[kleos]], [[son-of-atreus]]).
