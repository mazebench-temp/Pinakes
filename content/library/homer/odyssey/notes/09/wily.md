Αἰαίη δολόεσσα: the epithet of Calypso in Book 7 (7.245) is given to Circe, with the name of her island, which Merry notes is called Αἰαίη too (10.135; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 9.32; [[circe]], [[aeaea]], [[brilliant-among-goddesses]]).
