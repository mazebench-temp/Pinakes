---
summary: The ram goes out last, “weighed down by his wool and by me, thinking shrewd thoughts” (9.445).
grc: ["πυκινὰ φρονέοντι"]
---

Merry finds a comic touch in the pairing, “cumbered by his wool and me” (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 9.445; see the translation's note on 9.445; [[ram]], [[metis]]).
