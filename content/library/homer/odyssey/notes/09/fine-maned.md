---
summary: “In the evening he came, herding his fine-fleeced flocks” (9.336), and Odysseus bids his men “quickly throw the many fine-fleeced sheep / on board the ship” (9.469–470).
en: [fine-fleeced]
---

καλλίτριχα μῆλα, the horses' epithet given to the flocks; the translation renders it “fine-fleeced” (see the Odyssey translation's key words, καλλίτριχα μῆλα; [[sheep]]).
