summary: The Cyclops to the ram: “Surely you miss your master's / eye, which a bad man blinded / with his wretched companions” (9.452–454).
en: [wretched]
---

λυγρός of persons is “wretched,” as in Poseidon's rebuke of the Achaeans, who says he would not quarrel with a man who held back from war “if he were a wretch” (*Iliad* 13.118–119; see the Odyssey translation's key words, δύστηνος; [[wretch]], [[my-dear]]).
