summary: The Cyclops curdles half his milk and lays it “in woven baskets” (9.247).

πλεκτοῖς ἐν ταλάροισιν, the vintage baskets of the shield of Achilles (*Iliad* 18.568). Merry identifies the ταρσοί, “racks,” of 9.219 with these baskets (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 9.219; see the translation's note on 9.219; [[basket]], [[cheese]]).
