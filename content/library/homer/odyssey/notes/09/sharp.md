---
summary: Odysseus' first plan, “drawing the sharp sword from beside my thigh” (9.300), and the stake, “sharp at the point” (9.382).
---

ὀξὺ ἐρυσσάμενος παρὰ μηροῦ is the Iliad's half-verse (*Iliad* 1.190, 21.173). The sword is held back by a second thought, and the stake does the work (9.302, 9.325–328, 9.382–388; [[sword]], [[stake]], [[decision-scene]]).
