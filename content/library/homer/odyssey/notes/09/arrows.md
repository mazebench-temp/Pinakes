---
summary: The companions call the mountain peak that the Cyclops hurls at the ship his βέλος, his “missile” (9.495).
en: [missile]
---

The general word for anything shot or thrown is used of the Cyclops' rock. When Odysseus means to taunt him a second time, his companions hold him back: “Just now he threw his missile into the sea and brought the ship / back to the land, and we thought we would perish there” (9.495–496; see [[polyphemus-the-cyclops]] and [[ships]]). The missile is the peak of a great mountain that the Cyclops broke off and hurled (9.481–486).
