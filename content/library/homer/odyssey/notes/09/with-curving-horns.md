summary: At Ismarus the men stay to feast: “and many sheep / they slaughtered beside the shore, and shambling cattle with curving horns” (9.45–46).
---

εἰλίποδας ἕλικας βοῦς, the verse-end of the suitors' feasts (1.92, 4.320) and of the Iliad (*Iliad* 9.466). The men would not obey, and the Cicones of the inland came at dawn (9.43–52; [[shambling]], [[cattle]], [[the-raid-on-the-cicones]]).
