summary: Merry finds the comparison of the Cyclops' club to the mast of a ship imitated in Milton's description of Satan's spear.
---

- **Satan's spear.** The Cyclops' club of green olive wood was, as Odysseus and his men judged it, as long and as thick as “the mast of a black ship of twenty oars, / a broad freight ship” (9.321–324). Merry finds the comparison imitated in Milton's description of Satan's spear, “To which the tallest pine, / Hewn on Norwegian hills to be the mast / Of some great Ammiral, were but a wand” (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 9.322, who quotes the lines without their place in *Paradise Lost*): [[the-mast-of-twenty-oars]] and [[mace]].
