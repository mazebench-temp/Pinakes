---
summary: The blinded Cyclops to his ram: “Dear ram, why do you hurry out through the cave like this, the last / of the flock?” (9.447–448).
---

πέπον, the address of comrades in the Iliad, is spoken to a beast. Merry notes that the Iliad's word for a ram is κτίλος, and cites Eustathius' comparison with the heroes who speak to their horses (*Iliad* 8.185, 19.400, 23.402; Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 9.447; see the translation's note on 9.447; [[ram]], [[polyphemus-the-cyclops]]).
