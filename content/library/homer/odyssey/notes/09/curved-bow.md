---
summary: “At once we took our curved bows and long-socketed javelins / from the ships” (9.156–157).
---

Merry explains δολιχαύλους as no more than “long,” the αὐλός being the socket by which the head is fixed on every spear (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 9.156; [[bow]], [[satisfying]]).
