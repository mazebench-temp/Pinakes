---
summary: After the battle with the Cicones, “against the ships Zeus the cloud-gatherer roused the North Wind / with a heaven-sent storm” (9.67–68).
---

The clouds and the night of the next verses are those of Poseidon's storm in Book 5, “and with clouds he covered / land and sea alike; and night arose from the sky” (9.68–69 = 5.293–294), and the three verses come again, with a raging wind in place of the North Wind, when Zeus raises the storm that keeps the ships on the island of the Sun (12.313–315; see the Odyssey translation's formulas; [[boreas]], [[zeus]], [[wondrous]]).
