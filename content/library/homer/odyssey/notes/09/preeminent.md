---
summary: “Powerful Alcinous, preeminent among all the people” (9.2); and on Ithaca “a mountain, / Neritum with its quivering leaves, conspicuous” (9.21–22).
en: [conspicuous]
---

Two words, two renderings: ἀριδείκετος, “preeminent,” in the address, and ἀριπρεπής, “conspicuous,” of the mountain, as the Iliad has it of things seen (*Iliad* 15.309, 23.453; see the Odyssey translation's key words, ἀριπρεπής; [[powerful]], [[neritum]], [[with-quivering-leaves]]).
