summary: The island off the land of the Cyclopes, “For it is not a poor place at all” (9.131); the host Odysseus waited for, “he was not to be a lovely sight for my companions” (9.230); and the first taunt, “it was not a weakling's companions after all that you were / to eat” (9.475–476).
refs: [9.131, 9.230, 9.475]
---

Merry joins the negative of 9.475 with ἀνάλκιδος, “He was no weakling whose comrades thou wast minded to eat” (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on 9.475; see the translation's note on 9.474–475; [[lovely]], [[say-that-odysseus-blinded-you]]).
