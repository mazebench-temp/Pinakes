summary: The Cyclops sees the strangers by the light of his fire and asks: “Strangers, who are you? From where do you sail the watery ways? / Is it on some business, or do you wander at random / over the sea, like pirates, who wander / staking their lives and bringing evil to foreigners?” (9.252–255).
en: [wander at random]
---

Nestor's four verses (3.71–74), here put by a host who has given his guests nothing: the text prints ἦ τι for the ἤ τι of 3.72 and a comma after ληιστῆρες, both left as printed, and the scholia record μηχανόωνται for ἀλόωνται (see the translation's note on 9.252–255). Odysseus answers with Troy and Agamemnon's fame (9.259–271; [[son-of-atreus]], [[piracy]], [[thucydides-and-the-pirates]], [[watery-ways]]).
