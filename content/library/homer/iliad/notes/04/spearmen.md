---
summary: Athena takes the likeness of Laodocus, son of Antenor, “a strong spearman.”
grc: [αἰχμητῇ]
---

{{quote:0ee45413-03c4-59eb-b88c-aa1354ba2845}}

The phrase κρατερὸς αἰχμητής was Helen's praise of Agamemnon in Book 3 ({{ref:Iliad 3.179}}; [[krateros]]). Here it is the disguise of a goddess. Athena takes the form of a spearman, a son of Antenor ([[laodocus]], [[son-of-antenor]]), to persuade an archer to shoot ([[pandarus]], [[bow]]).
