---
summary: Book 4 gives ἴφθιμος to the Aetolian Thoas, “big and mighty and noble,” as the Thracians push him back.
grc: [ἴφθιμον]
---

The Thracians force Thoas back from the body of Peirous, “big and mighty and noble as he was” (4.534–535; [[noble]], [[thoas]]). In Book 3 the adjective belonged to the arming of Paris, who set a helmet on his mighty head (3.336); here it describes a man whose strength is not enough to win the body. The translation also uses “mighty” for another adjective of strength, κρατερός, in the formula of Diomedes (4.401; [[krateros]]).
