summary: The Athenians, “masters of the war cry”, stand around Menestheus waiting for another column to charge when Agamemnon comes by on his review, for their men have not yet heard the war cry (4.328–335).
grc: [Ἀθηναῖοι]
refs: [4.331-335]
---

Book 4 gives the Athenians an epithet and a delay. They are μήστωρες ἀϋτῆς, “masters of the war cry” (4.328; see [[masters-of-the-war-cry]]), and three lines later “their people had not yet heard the war cry” (ἀϋτῆς, 4.331). With the Cephallenians they wait until another column of Achaeans should charge, and Agamemnon rebukes their leader for it (4.331–348; see [[menestheus]] and [[cephallenians]]). The nominative Ἀθηναῖοι is heard here for the first time; the Catalogue had only the genitive (2.551, 2.558).
