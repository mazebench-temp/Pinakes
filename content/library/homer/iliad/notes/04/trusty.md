---
summary: Idomeneus promises Agamemnon to be his “trusty companion,” as he promised at the beginning.
grc: [ἐρίηρος]
---

“Son of Atreus, I will indeed be your trusty companion, as I promised at first and nodded my assent” (4.266–267). Idomeneus answers Agamemnon's praise with a pledge of loyalty, in the singular of the formula ἐρίηρες ἑταῖροι ([[hetairos]], [[idomeneus]]). Homer does not say what the first promise was. The pledge is one of friendship between kings, and Idomeneus turns it at once into a demand: let Agamemnon urge on the others, since the Trojans have broken the oaths (4.268–271).
