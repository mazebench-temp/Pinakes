---
summary: The Cretans are the first contingent Agamemnon reaches on his review of the army. They are arming around Idomeneus, and the king rejoices to see them (4.251–256).
refs: [4.252, 4.254-255]
---

ἦλθε δʼ ἐπὶ Κρήτεσσι κιὼν ἀνὰ οὐλαμὸν ἀνδρῶν, “and he came to the Cretans, going through the crowd of men” (4.251). The Cretans are arming around Idomeneus, the king among the champions and Meriones driving on the rear (4.252–254), and Agamemnon rejoices at the sight (4.255). Idomeneus answers as “leader of the Cretans” (4.265). The same line, with another name, brings Agamemnon to the two Ajaxes (4.273). See [[idomeneus]], [[meriones]] and [[the-review-of-the-army]].
