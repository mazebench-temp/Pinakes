---
summary: Book 4 gives the epithet to the Theban Polyphontes, one of the two leaders of the ambush that Tydeus destroyed.
---

“And the son of Autophonus, Polyphontes, steadfast in battle” (4.395) is the second leader of the Theban ambush ([[polyphontes]], [[like-the-immortals]]). His name means “killer of many.” The epithet of the man who holds his ground is given to a man whom Tydeus kills, with all the rest of the fifty except Maeon (4.396–398; [[tydeus-at-thebes]]).
