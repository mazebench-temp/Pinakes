---
summary: The sword is drawn once in the first battle: Thoas pulls his spear from Peirous' chest and finishes him with his sharp sword (4.530–531).
---

The spear does the fighting of Book 4, and the sword comes out only at close quarters, to finish a man. Thoas the Aetolian has struck Peirous in the chest; he comes up close, “pulled the heavy spear out of his chest, and drew his sharp sword, and struck him full in the belly, and took away his life” (4.529–531). The spear is thrown or thrust first, and the sword is for the end, as when Menelaus, his spear spent, drew his sword against Paris (3.361; see [[spear]]). Thoas cannot strip his armor, for the Thracians close round the body with their long spears (4.532–535; see [[thoas]] and [[peirous]]).
