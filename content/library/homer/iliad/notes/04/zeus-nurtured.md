---
summary: Book 4 gives the epithet to the young men around the two Ajaxes, and to Menestheus' father in Agamemnon's rebuke.
---

The battalions around the two Ajaxes are “young men nurtured by Zeus” (4.280; [[storm-cloud]]). Agamemnon begins his rebuke of Menestheus with his father's title: “Son of Peteos, the king whom Zeus has nurtured” (4.338; the Greek genitive belongs to Peteos). The honor of the father sharpens the reproach of the son ([[menestheus]], [[peteos]]).
