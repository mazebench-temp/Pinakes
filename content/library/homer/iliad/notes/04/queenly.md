---
summary: Book 4 gives the title to Hebe as she pours the nectar for the gods, and to Hera in the formula βοῶπις πότνια Ἥρη.
---

{{quote:2490a58c-3f84-5ecf-b764-42420587d9c5}}

The book opens on the gods drinking together on the golden floor, “and among them lady Hebe poured out the nectar” (4.2–3; [[hebe]], [[nectar]]). πότνια is the title of a goddess, even one who serves at table. Hera is πότνια in her formula when she answers Zeus (4.50; [[ox-eyed]]).
