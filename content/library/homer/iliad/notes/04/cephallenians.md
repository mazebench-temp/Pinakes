summary: The ranks of the Cephallenians, “no weaklings”, stand around Odysseus beside the Athenians, waiting for another column to begin the charge, when Agamemnon rebukes their leader (4.330–335).

πὰρ δὲ Κεφαλλήνων ἀμφὶ στίχες οὐκ ἀλαπαδναί, “and beside him, all around, the ranks of the Cephallenians, no weaklings” (4.330). The understatement ([[litotes]]) speaks for them before Agamemnon speaks against their leader. Nestor has just warned his charioteers that breaking the line would make them “the weaker” (ἀλαπαδνότεροι, 4.305); the Cephallenians keep their ranks and are called “no weaklings”. With the Athenians they had not yet heard the war cry, and stood waiting for another column to charge (4.331–335; see [[athenians]] and [[odysseus]]).
