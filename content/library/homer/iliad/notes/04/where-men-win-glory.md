summary: In Book 4 the epithet is back in its usual place, with battle: Agamemnon is eager “for the battle where men win glory.”
---

{{quote:e54d1847-9556-5821-b657-133af625beab}}

In Book 1 the epithet was given to the assembly that Achilles no longer attended ({{ref:Iliad 1.490}}). Here it has its common use, and it marks the change in Agamemnon: the king who tested the army with talk of flight in Book 2 now hurries to the fighting ([[the-review]], [[brilliant]]).
