---
summary: The title goes to Bias, one of Nestor's captains, and to Agamemnon in Diomedes' defense of him.
---

Among Nestor's captains is “Bias, shepherd of the people” (4.296; [[bias]]). The other use is more pointed. Sthenelus has answered Agamemnon's rebuke, and Diomedes silences him: “I do not blame Agamemnon, shepherd of the people, for urging the well-greaved Achaeans to fight” (4.413–414). The man just rebuked gives the king his title of care and grants that the rebuke was his duty ([[kingship-and-authority]], [[diomedes]]).
