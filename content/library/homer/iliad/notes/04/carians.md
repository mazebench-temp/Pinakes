---
summary: In the simile of Menelaus' wound, a Maeonian or a Carian woman stains ivory with purple to make a cheekpiece for a king's horse (4.141–142), the only glimpse of Carian crafts in Homer.
grc: [Κάειρα]
en: [Carian]
---

Κάειρα is the feminine of Κάρ, “Carian”. The simile imagines a woman of Maeonia or of Caria who “stains ivory with purple” for a horse's cheekpiece, a thing kept in a chamber as “a delight for a king” (4.141–144). See [[ivory-stained-with-purple]] and [[maeonians]].
