---
summary: Pallas Athena darts down like a star at the beginning of the fighting and, at its end, leads an imagined man through the battle by the hand.
---

“In its likeness Pallas Athena darted down to the earth” (4.78; [[shooting-star]]). The last lines of the book imagine a man who might walk unhurt through the battle, if “Pallas Athena should lead him by the hand, and keep off the force of the missiles” (4.541–542). The goddess who brought on the fighting is the only protection the narrator can imagine within it ([[athena]]).
