---
summary: Agamemnon finds “high-hearted Diomedes” standing by his chariot and rebukes him with his father's deeds.
grc: [ὑπέρθυμον]
---

“He found the son of Tydeus, high-hearted Diomedes, standing among his horses and his well-joined chariot” (4.365–366; [[son-of-tydeus]], [[well-joined]]). The epithet of spirit introduces the man whom Agamemnon is about to accuse of cowering (4.371). Diomedes shows his spirit in another way. He says nothing, in awe of the king (4.401–402), and silences Sthenelus when he protests (4.411–418). Then he leaps down from his chariot in his armor (4.419–421; [[stout-hearted]], [[diomedes]]).
