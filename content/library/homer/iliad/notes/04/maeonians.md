---
summary: In the simile of Menelaus' wound, a Maeonian or a Carian woman stains ivory with purple to make a cheekpiece for a king's horse (4.141–142).
grc: [Μῃονὶς]
en: [Maeonian]
---

Μῃονίς is the feminine of Μῄων, “Maeonian”. The simile imagines a woman of Maeonia or of Caria who “stains ivory with purple” for a horse's cheekpiece, a thing kept in a chamber as “a delight for a king” (4.141–144). Two of Troy's allies from Asia Minor (2.864–867) are named here for a luxury craft. See [[ivory-stained-with-purple]] and [[carians]].
