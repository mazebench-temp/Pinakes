---
summary: In the review the Danaans are “of the swift horses” (4.232, 4.257), and when it is over their battalions move to war like waves breaking on a shore, in silence (4.427).
refs: [4.428-432]
---

Δαναοὶ ταχύπωλοι, “the Danaans of the swift horses”, is the name the narrator uses for the men whom Agamemnon finds eager (4.232), and the name Agamemnon uses for those above whom he honors Idomeneus (4.257; see [[of-the-swift-horses]]). It returns when the review ends, after the simile of the waves: “so then the battalions of the Danaans moved, rank upon rank, unceasingly to war” (4.427–428; see [[waves-on-the-shore]]). See [[achaeans]] and [[argives]].
