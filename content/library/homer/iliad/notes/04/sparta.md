summary: Sparta, a city of Menelaus, is one of the three cities Hera loves most, and she offers it to Zeus to be sacked whenever it becomes hateful to him.
grc: [Σπάρτη]
passages:
  - 4.50-56 | Hera's three cities
---

Hera names Sparta second of the three cities dearest to her, between Argos and “wide-streeted Mycenae” (4.51–52). In the Catalogue it is one of the towns of Lacedaemon, the land of Menelaus (2.581–582; see [[lacedaemon]] and [[menelaus]]). The war is fought over the wife of Sparta's king, and the goddess who wants Troy destroyed offers his city as part of the price. See [[hera]] and [[the-bargain-of-zeus-and-hera]].
