  - 4.1-72 | The council of the gods

Olympus is named once in Book 4. Athena “went darting down from the peaks of Olympus” (βῆ δὲ κατʼ Οὐλύμποιο καρήνων ἀΐξασα, 4.74), the line that sent her down to Odysseus at Hera's word in Book 2 (2.167). The council before it is set there without the name. The gods sit in assembly with Zeus “on the golden floor” (χρυσέῳ ἐν δαπέδῳ), drinking nectar from golden cups and looking out at the city of the Trojans (4.1–4). In Book 1 Thetis spoke of “the bronze-floored house of Zeus” (1.426); the floor on which the gods sit here is gold, like their cups. See [[olympians]] and [[houses-of-the-gods]].
