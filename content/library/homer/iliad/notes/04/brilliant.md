---
summary: Book 4 gives δῖος to Agamemnon, eager for battle, and to Ereuthalion, the champion whom the young Nestor killed.
---

“Then you would not have seen brilliant Agamemnon drowsing” (4.223; [[where-men-win-glory]]). And Nestor, wishing for the strength of his youth, remembers the day “when I killed brilliant Ereuthalion” (4.319; [[ereuthalion]], [[nestor-and-ereuthalion]]). The epithet praises the enemy whose defeat was Nestor's great deed: in Book 7 he calls Ereuthalion the tallest and strongest man he ever killed (Il. 7.155; [[old-age-and-memory]]).
