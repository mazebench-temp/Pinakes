Μηριόνης δʼ ἄρα οἱ πυμάτας ὄτρυνε φάλαγγας, “while Meriones was urging on the rearmost battalions for him” (4.254). The two Cretan leaders divide the work. Idomeneus fights in front among the champions, and Meriones drives on the rear, where the laggards are; οἱ (“for him”) makes him the king's second. Nestor, a few lines later, puts his cowards in the middle so that they will have to fight (4.299–300). See [[idomeneus]] and [[cretans]].
