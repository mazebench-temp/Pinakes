---
summary: Hera is “ox-eyed queen Hera” as she answers Zeus and makes her bargain.
---

“Then ox-eyed queen Hera answered him” (4.50) introduces her offer of Argos, Sparta and Mycenae ([[the-bargain-of-zeus-and-hera]], [[wide-streeted]]). As in Book 1, the formula belongs to Hera in confrontation with her husband; her other formula, [[white-armed]], does not occur in Book 4. Her other title in the book is [[argive]] (4.8), from the first of the cities she gives up.
