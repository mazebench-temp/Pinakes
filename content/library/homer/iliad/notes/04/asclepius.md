---
summary: Asclepius is named twice as the father of Machaon, “the son of Asclepius, the noble healer” (4.194), who heals Menelaus with the drugs that Chiron once gave Asclepius.
refs: [4.219]
---

Agamemnon identifies the healer he needs by his father: “the man who is the son of Asclepius, the noble healer” (4.194). The title belongs to the father, ἀμύμων ἰητήρ (see [[blameless]]); Talthybius calls the son simply Ἀσκληπιάδη, “son of Asclepius” (4.204). Asclepius does not appear in the Iliad. He is present in what he handed on: the drugs that came to him from [[Chiron]] “in friendship” (4.219), and the skill with which his son uses them on Menelaus' wound. See [[machaon]] and [[healing]].
