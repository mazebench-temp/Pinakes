---
summary: Agamemnon takes back his rebuke of Odysseus with the formal address Athena used in Book 2: “Son of Laertes, sprung from Zeus, Odysseus of many devices.”
---

{{quote:c6915534-a83a-59c7-9bf5-533b3ab46e6e}}

The whole-line address διογενὲς Λαερτιάδη πολυμήχανʼ Ὀδυσσεῦ was Athena's in Book 2 ({{ref:Iliad 2.173}}). Agamemnon uses it to make amends, smiling “when he saw that he was angry” (4.357). The ceremonious formula replaces the insult of a few lines before ([[mind-set-on-gain]], [[sprung-from-zeus]], [[son-of-laertes]]).
