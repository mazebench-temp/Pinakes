---
summary: Simoeisius, killed by Telamonian Ajax in the first battle, is “the young man in his prime.”
en: [in his prime]
---

“Then Telamonian Ajax struck the son of Anthemion, the young man in his prime, Simoeisius” (4.473–474). ἠΐθεος θαλερός is an unmarried young man in the bloom of youth. The word of flourishing, from θάλλω (“bloom”), prepares the simile of his death: he falls like a poplar grown by a marsh, smooth, with branches at the top, cut down by a chariot-maker and left to dry by the river (4.482–487; [[the-poplar]], [[short-lived]], [[simoeisius]]).
