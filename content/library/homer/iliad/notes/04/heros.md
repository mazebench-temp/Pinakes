  - 4.199-202 | Talthybius finds Machaon
---

ἥρως in Homer is a title for any man of the warrior class, not a mark of special greatness. Talthybius goes through the army “looking for the hero Machaon” (παπταίνων ἥρωα Μαχάονα, 4.200), and finds him standing among the shield-bearing men who had followed him from Tricca (4.201–202). The healer is a fighting man and a leader; in the Catalogue he and his brother Podalirius lead thirty ships from Tricca, Ithome and Oechalia (2.729–733). See [[machaon]].
