---
summary: Machaon's men come from “horse-pasturing Tricca” in Thessaly.
---

Talthybius finds Machaon among “the strong ranks of shield-bearing men, who had followed him from horse-pasturing Tricca” (4.201–202; [[shield-bearing]], [[machaon]]). The Catalogue names Tricca among the towns of the two sons of Asclepius ({{ref:Iliad 2.729-733}}); here the town has the epithet usually given to Argos.
