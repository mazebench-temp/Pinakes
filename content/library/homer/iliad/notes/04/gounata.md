---
summary: Agamemnon wishes that Nestor's knees could follow the spirit in his breast: old age has worn his body down.
grc: [γούναθʼ]
passages:
  - 4.311-316 | Agamemnon praises Nestor
---

“Old man, I wish that, as the spirit is in your breast, so your knees (γούνατα) might follow, and your strength be steadfast. But old age, the common lot, wears you down” (4.313–315). The knees are where a man's strength and speed sit, and they fail first in age. In battle the same failure is death, when a man's limbs are loosened (4.469; see [[loosened-his-limbs]]). Nestor keeps his spirit and loses his knees, and so he keeps to counsel and leaves the spears to the young (4.318–325). See [[old-age-and-memory]], [[nestor]] and [[thymos]].
