---
summary: Agamemnon sends Talthybius to fetch the healer Machaon to his wounded brother, and the herald finds him among his men and gives him the king's message.
refs: [4.198-201, 4.203, 4.208-210]
passages:
  - 4.192-209 | Talthybius fetches Machaon
---

To bring a healer for his brother, Agamemnon calls “Talthybius, the divine herald” (θεῖον κήρυκα, 4.192; see [[divine]]), and his order is urgent: “Talthybius, call Machaon here as fast as you can” (4.193). “The herald heard him and did not disobey” (4.198), as he did not disobey when he was sent for the lamb of the oath (3.120). He goes through the army looking for Machaon, finds him standing among his men, and delivers the message (4.199–207).

He keeps the king's words and changes only what delivery requires. The order to call Machaon becomes a summons, “Rise, son of Asclepius; powerful Agamemnon calls you” (4.204). “So that he may see” becomes “so that you may see”, and Menelaus, “the son of Atreus” in his brother's mouth (4.195), is “leader of the Achaeans” in the herald's (4.205). The rest is repeated word for word, down to “fame for him, and grief for us” (4.197 = 4.207). Then he leads Machaon back through the army (4.209–210).
