summary: Pandarus' companions hold their shields in front of him while he strings his bow; the Ajaxes' battalions come on “bristling with shields and spears”; and when the armies meet, shields of hide and bossed shields crash together (4.113, 4.282, 4.447–449).
grc: [σάκεα, σάκεσιν, ῥινούς, ἀσπίδες]
en: [shields of hide]
---

**Cover.** While Pandarus strings his bow, “his brave companions held their shields in front of him, so that the warlike sons of the Achaeans would not spring up” before Menelaus was hit (σάκεα, 4.113–115). The shield that covers a man can cover an archer beside him, as Ajax's great shield covers Teucer (8.266–272; see [[bow]]).

**Shield-bearing men.** The formula of the massed infantry, “the strong ranks of shield-bearing men” (κρατεραὶ στίχες ἀσπιστάων), describes the men around Pandarus and around Machaon (4.90, 4.201), and “the ranks of the shield-bearing Trojans” come on while Menelaus is being healed (4.221; see [[shield-bearing]]). The Ajaxes' battalions move into war dark and “bristling with shields and spears” (σάκεσίν τε καὶ ἔγχεσι πεφρικυῖαι, 4.282). See [[battle-order]] and [[storm-cloud]].

**The clash.** “They dashed together their shields of hide, and their spears, and the fury of men in bronze corselets; and the bossed shields pressed against one another” (4.447–449; see [[the-armies-clash]] and [[bossed]]). The shields of hide are ῥινοί: ῥινός is hide or skin, and the name stands for the shield made of it. The bossed shields are ἀσπίδες ὀμφαλόεσσαι. ὀμφαλόεις, “bossed”, is from ὀμφαλός, “navel”, the raised center of the shield.

**Exposed.** Elephenor dies because his shield leaves him bare. As he stoops to drag away Echepolus' body, his side is “exposed beside his shield”, and Agenor stabs him there (παρʼ ἀσπίδος ἐξεφαάνθη, 4.468; see [[elephenor]] and [[androktasia]]).
