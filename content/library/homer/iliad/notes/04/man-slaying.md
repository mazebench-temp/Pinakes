summary: Book 4 gives the epithet to Ares, as Strife is named his sister and companion.
---

{{quote:c99a1945-6921-5957-8d4d-c000d5479a6a}}

Ares and Athena rouse the two armies, “and Terror and Rout, and Strife, raging without end, the sister and companion of man-slaying Ares” (4.440–441; [[strife]], [[terror]], [[rout]], [[ares]]). The epithet that named Hector in Book 1 names the war god as the armies meet. Strife, his sister, grows from small beginnings until her head is in the sky (4.442–443).
