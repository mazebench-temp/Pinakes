summary: Book 4 gives the formula to the Theban Maeon, one of the two leaders of the ambush against Tydeus, and the one man Tydeus spared.
grc: [ἐπιείκελος ἀθανάτοισιν]
---

The Cadmeians set an ambush of fifty young men for Tydeus, with two leaders, “Maeon son of Haemon, like the immortals, and the son of Autophonus, Polyphontes, steadfast in battle” (4.394–395; [[maeon]], [[polyphontes]], [[steadfast-in-battle]]). Tydeus kills them all except Maeon, whom he sends home “obeying the portents of the gods” (4.396–398; [[tydeus-at-thebes]]). Agamemnon's story praises the enemy leaders to magnify the man who beat them. In Book 1 the same formula praised Theseus in Nestor's list of the heroes of old ({{ref:Iliad 1.265}}).
