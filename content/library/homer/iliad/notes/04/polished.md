---
summary: Pandarus uncovers his “polished bow,” made from the horns of a wild goat.
---

“At once he uncovered his polished bow, made from a leaping wild goat” (4.105). τόξον ἐΰξοον is the formula of Odysseus' bow in the Odyssey. Here it introduces the history of Pandarus' bow: the goat he shot from ambush, its horns sixteen palms long, and the horn-worker who smoothed and fitted them and set a golden tip on the bow (4.105–111; [[bow]], [[goats]], [[the-bow-of-pandarus]]).
