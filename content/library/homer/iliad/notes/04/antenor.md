---
summary: Athena goes among the Trojans in the likeness of Antenor's son Laodocus to find Pandarus and have him break the truce (4.86–88).
---

Athena comes to Pandarus as “Laodocus, son of Antenor, a strong spearman” (4.87; see [[laodocus]]). The borrowed face belongs to the house of the elder who drove beside Priam to the oath sacrifice (3.262) and who will tell the Trojans, in Book 7, that they are fighting with the oaths broken and should give Helen back (7.347–353). In Book 3 Iris took the likeness of Laodice, the wife of his son [[Helicaon]] (3.121–124). Another of his sons, [[agenor|Agenor]], kills [[Elephenor]] in the first battle (4.467–469). See [[oaths-and-their-breaking]].
