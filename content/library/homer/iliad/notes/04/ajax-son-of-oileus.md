summary: The lesser Ajax is one of “the two Ajaxes” whom Agamemnon finds arming with their battalions and praises as leaders who need no orders (4.273–291).

passages:
  - 4.273-292 | Agamemnon and the two Ajaxes
---

Book 4 never names the son of Oileus. He is present in the dual. Agamemnon comes “to the two Ajaxes” (Αἰάντεσσι, 4.273), the battalions dark as a storm cloud move into war “with the two Ajaxes” (4.280), and the king addresses them together: “Ajaxes, leaders of the bronze-armored Argives, to you two I give no orders” (4.285–286). The pair is praised as one, for its spirit and for the men it leads. See [[ajax]] and [[storm-cloud]].
