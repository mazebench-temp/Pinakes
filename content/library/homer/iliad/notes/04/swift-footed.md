---
summary: Achilles is absent from Book 4, and his formula does not occur; the adjective ὠκύς describes the mares of Democoon, a son of Priam.
grc: [ὠκειάων]
---

Achilles is named once in Book 4, by Apollo, who tells the Trojans that he is not fighting but broods over his anger by the ships (4.512–513; [[heart-grieving]]). His formula πόδας ὠκὺς Ἀχιλλεύς does not occur. The adjective ὠκύς is used only of horses. Democoon, a bastard son of Priam, “had come to him from Abydos, from his swift mares” (4.500), and Odysseus kills him in anger for Leucus (4.499–504; [[democoon]], [[abydos]], [[horses]]).
