The Pylians are the one contingent of the review that the poem shows in its order. Nestor sets his companions (ἑτάρους) around five captains, [[Pelagon]], [[Alastor]], [[Chromius]], [[Haemon]] and [[Bias]] (4.294–296), and gives his orders to the horsemen, who must keep their line (4.301–309). The Pylians' part in the war is Nestor's. In the Catalogue they are his ninety ships (2.591–602), and in his tales they are the people of his youth, at war with the Arcadians and the Epeians (7.133–156, 11.670–761).
