---
summary: Book 4 gives the epithet to the Aetolian Thoas, “big and mighty and noble,” as the Thracians push him back from the body of their leader.
grc: [ἀγαυὸν]
---

Thoas has killed Peirous, but the Thracians close around the body of their leader and push Thoas away, “big and mighty and noble as he was” (4.534–535; [[thoas]], [[topknotted]]). Three words of praise, μέγας (“big”), ἴφθιμος ([[mighty]]) and ἀγαυός, are given to the victor at the moment he must give ground, and the concessive περ (“as he was”) turns the praise into a measure of the Thracians' resistance. The fight over the body ends with the two leaders lying side by side in the dust (4.536–538; [[peirous]]).
