---
summary: Zeleia is the home to which Pandarus vows to bring Apollo a hecatomb of firstborn lambs, “when you come home to the city of sacred Zeleia” (4.103, 4.121). He never comes home.
grc: [Ζελείης]
---

The vow is Athena's suggestion, and Pandarus prays it in her words: he will offer “a glorious hecatomb of firstborn lambs” when he comes home “to the city of sacred Zeleia” (4.102–103, 4.120–121; see [[hecatomb]] and [[lambs]]). ἱερός (“sacred”) is an epithet of cities, Ilios above all, which is “sacred Ilios” in the same book (4.46, 4.164; see [[hieros]]). A vow to Apollo payable at home suggests a cult of the god at Zeleia (see [[apollo]]). The vow depends on a homecoming, οἴκαδε νοστήσας (“having come home”; see [[nostos]]). In Book 5 Diomedes kills Pandarus (5.290–296), and the hecatomb is never paid.
