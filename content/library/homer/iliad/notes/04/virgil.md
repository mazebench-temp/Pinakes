---
summary: Virgil gave the growth of Homer's Strife to his Rumor, and made the breaking of the truce in Aeneid 12 follow Pandarus' shot and the healing of Menelaus.
---

- Rumor, small at first, then walking on the ground with her head among the clouds (*Aeneid* 4.173–188): [[virgil-rumor]].
- The truce broken: Juturna in disguise, the augur's spear, the arrow from an unknown hand, and Aeneas healed (*Aeneid* 12.134–429): [[virgil-duel-for-lavinia]].
- **Discord.** On the shield of Aeneas, in the battle of Actium, Discord strides rejoicing in her torn cloak beside Mars, and Bellona follows her with a bloody scourge (*Aeneid* 8.700–703). Homer's Strife, too, goes to battle as the sister and companion of Ares (4.439–445).
