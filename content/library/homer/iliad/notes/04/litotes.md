---
summary: Book 4 uses litotes for obedience (“did not disobey”), for Agamemnon's eagerness (“you would not have seen him drowsing”), for courage (“no weaklings”), for a spear well thrown (“not in vain”), and for the fierceness of the first battle (“no man could make light of it”).
refs: [4.68, 4.198, 4.223-225, 4.330, 4.372, 4.498, 4.539]
---

**“Did not disobey.”** Zeus yields to Hera in the formula of obedience: “So she spoke, and the father of men and gods did not disobey” (4.68). The herald Talthybius “heard him and did not disobey” (4.198). See [[so-he-spoke]].

**The king eager.** The review opens with three denials and an assertion: “Then you would not have seen brilliant Agamemnon drowsing, nor cowering, nor unwilling to fight, but very eager for the battle where men win glory” (4.223–225). The last denial is itself double, οὐδʼ οὐκ ἐθέλοντα, “nor unwilling”. The king who had just lamented his brother as dead is shown by what he was not (see [[the-review]] and [[apostrophe]]).

**No weaklings.** The Cephallenians who stand around Odysseus are στίχες οὐκ ἀλαπαδναί, “ranks … no weaklings” (4.330). They are waiting, not afraid, and Agamemnon's rebuke will be unjust (see [[cephallenians]]). His rebuke of Diomedes begins with a denial of the same kind: “Tydeus was not one to cower like this” (4.372).

**Not in vain.** Odysseus comes close to avenge Leucus, and “he did not throw the shaft in vain” (οὐχ ἅλιον βέλος ἧκεν, 4.498): he killed Democoon. ἅλιος, “vain”, is a word of the book: Hera will not have her labor made vain (4.26), and the oath, says Agamemnon, is “in no way vain” (οὐ … ἅλιον, 4.158).

**The first battle.** The book ends with an onlooker: “Then no man who came into the work could make light of it any more” (4.539). The understatement measures the slaughter: even a man led through it by Athena and kept safe from the missiles would not think it small (4.539–544).

The strongest of the book's denials is Agamemnon's “These things will not go unfulfilled” (τὰ μὲν ἔσσεται οὐκ ἀτέλεστα, 4.168; see [[it-will-be-fulfilled]]).
