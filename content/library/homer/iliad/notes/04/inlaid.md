---
summary: The Achaeans march to battle in silence, and the inlaid armor gleams on them.
grc: [ποικίλʼ]
---

The Achaeans move to war rank upon rank, silent, in fear of their commanders, “and on all of them gleamed the inlaid armor that they wore as they marched in their ranks” (4.431–432; [[silence]]). The silent army is described by what it wears and how it shines. Earlier in the book Agamemnon leaves behind “his chariot, inlaid with bronze” (ἅρματα ποικίλα χαλκῷ, 4.226) to go through the ranks on foot.
