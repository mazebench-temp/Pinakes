---
summary: Athena tempts Pandarus with the “splendid gifts” Paris would give him.
---

“From him before all others you would carry off splendid gifts” (4.97). The Catalogue called Pandarus “the splendid son of Lycaon” ({{ref:Iliad 2.826}}); here splendor is what he is offered. Athena's bribe is the gratitude of Paris and his gifts ([[doron]], [[pandarus]], [[paris]]).
