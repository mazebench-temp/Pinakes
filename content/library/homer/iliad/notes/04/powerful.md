---
summary: κρείων Ἀγαμέμνων marks Agamemnon at every turn of Book 4, from his lament over Menelaus to his rebukes in the review; the title also goes to Nestor's captain Haemon and to Elephenor.
grc: [κρείοντα]
---

The formula κρείων Ἀγαμέμνων names Agamemnon in seven lines. It introduces his lament over his wounded brother, “Groaning heavily, powerful Agamemnon spoke among them” (4.153), and his answer to Menelaus (4.188), and Talthybius uses it when he summons Machaon (4.204). In the review it marks the king's reactions: he rejoices at the Ajaxes and at Nestor (4.283, 4.311), smiles at Odysseus' anger (4.356), and rebukes Diomedes (4.368; [[agamemnon]], [[the-review]]). The title also goes to lesser leaders: Haemon, one of Nestor's captains (4.296; [[haemon]]), and Elephenor, the leader of the Abantes, who dies in the first battle (4.463; [[elephenor]]).
