---
summary: Hera answers Zeus' proposal to spare Troy with her verse of protest, “Most dread son of Cronus, what a thing you have said!”
---

{{quote:e671a048-6dff-52cb-9ab8-0f4621599875}}

The verse is the one Hera spoke in Book 1, when Zeus refused to share his counsels with her ({{ref:Iliad 1.552}}). There she protested his secret agreement with Thetis; here she protests his proposal to end the war and let Troy stand ([[the-bargain-of-zeus-and-hera]]). She speaks for the labor she has spent in gathering the army, and she does not ask in vain: Zeus gives way (4.30–49).
