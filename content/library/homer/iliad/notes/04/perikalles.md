---
summary: The poplar of the simile is cut down to make a wheel rim “for a beautiful chariot.”
grc: [περικαλλέϊ]
---

Simoeisius falls like a poplar grown by a marsh, which a chariot-maker cuts “to bend a wheel rim for a beautiful chariot” (4.486; [[the-poplar]], [[chariot]]). The beautiful thing that will be made of the tree is set beside the young man's death. In Book 3 the epithet was given to Priam's chariot, in which Antenor drove to the oath (3.262, 3.312).
