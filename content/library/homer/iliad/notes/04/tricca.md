---
summary: Talthybius finds Machaon among the men who “had followed him from horse-pasturing Tricca” (4.202).
grc: [Τρίκης]
---

The Catalogue spells the name with a double consonant (Τρίκκην, 2.729); here it is Τρίκη (Τρίκης, 4.202). ἱππόβοτος (“horse-pasturing”) is the standing epithet of Argos (see [[horse-pasturing]]); Thessaly was horse country, and the best horses at Troy were the Thessalian mares of Eumelus (2.763–767). The line repeats the frame that introduced Pandarus and his men from the Aesepus (4.89–91), so that the healer is found as the archer was. See [[machaon]].
