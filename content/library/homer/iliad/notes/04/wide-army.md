---
summary: Book 4 uses the formula of the Achaean camp, of an army in a simile, and at last of the Trojan host.
grc: [στρατὸν εὐρὺν, στρατῷ εὐρέϊ]
---

Talthybius and Machaon go “through the throng, through the wide army of the Achaeans” to find Menelaus (4.209), in the formula that Book 1 used of the camp. Book 4 uses the phrase more widely. In the simile of the star, Zeus sends a portent “for sailors or for a wide army of men” (4.76; [[shooting-star]]). And when the armies advance, the wide army is the Trojans': “so the shouting of the Trojans rose through the wide army” (4.436; [[called-from-many-lands]], [[sheep-in-the-fold]]).
