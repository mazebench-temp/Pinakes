---
summary: Zeus asks Hera why she rages to sack “the well-built citadel of Ilios.”
---

“Strange one, what great evils have Priam and the sons of Priam done to you, that you rage so furiously to sack the well-built citadel of Ilios?” (4.31–33; [[strange-one]]). The epithet of building stands against the verb of destruction, ἐξαλαπάξαι. The Catalogue gave the same formula to Mycenae ({{ref:Iliad 2.569}}), and a few lines later Hera offers Mycenae to be sacked in return ([[wide-streeted]], [[the-bargain-of-zeus-and-hera]]).
