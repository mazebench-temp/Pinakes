---
summary: Machaon the healer comes to Menelaus and stands among the best men, “the man equal to the gods.”
---

When Machaon arrives, the best men are gathered in a circle around Menelaus, “and he came and stood among them, the man equal to the gods” (4.211–212; [[machaon]]). The heroic formula is given to the healer, as Idomeneus will say in Book 11 that a healer is worth many other men (Il. 11.514; [[soothing-drugs]]). In Book 3 the same line-end praised Priam as he drove home from the oath (3.310).
