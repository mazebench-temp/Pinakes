---
summary: Book 4 gives κλειτός to the hecatomb Pandarus vows to Apollo, and to the allies that Tydeus and Polynices sought at Mycenae for the war against Thebes.
grc: [κλειτὴν, κλειτοὺς]
en: [glorious]
---

The formula κλειτοὶ ἐπίκουροι usually names the Trojans' allies. In Book 4 it names allies for another war. Tydeus came to Mycenae with Polynices, gathering an army against Thebes, “and they begged hard to be given famous allies” (4.379). The men of Mycenae were willing, but Zeus turned them back with ill-omened signs (4.380–381; [[tydeus-at-thebes]]).

The adjective also describes the hecatomb of Pandarus' vow, which the translation calls “glorious”: “a glorious hecatomb of firstborn lambs” (4.102, 4.120; [[hecatomb]], [[famed-for-his-bow]]). The same line of vow returns in the archery contest of Book 23, where it is Meriones' promise to Apollo (Il. 23.872–873).
