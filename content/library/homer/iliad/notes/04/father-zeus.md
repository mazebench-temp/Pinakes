---
summary: Book 4 names father Zeus when Athena sulks at him, when Agamemnon promises he will not help liars, and in the wish formula of Book 2.
grc: [πατὴρ Ζεὺς]
---

Athena keeps silent at Zeus' proposal to end the war, “sulking at father Zeus” (4.23), and Hera speaks for both. Agamemnon uses the title as a guarantee: “father Zeus will be no helper to liars” (4.235). And in his praise of the Ajaxes he speaks again the wish formula of Book 2: “If only, father Zeus and Athena and Apollo, there were such a spirit in the breasts of all!” (4.288–289). In Book 2 the wish was for ten counselors like Nestor ({{ref:Iliad 2.371-372}}); here it is for an army with the spirit of the two Ajaxes ([[ajax]], [[ajax-son-of-oileus]]).
