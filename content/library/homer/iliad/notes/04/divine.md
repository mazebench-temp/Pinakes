---
summary: Agamemnon calls Talthybius “the divine herald” as he sends him for Machaon.
grc: [θεῖον]
---

“He spoke, and addressed Talthybius, the divine herald” (4.192). The epithet honors the herald's office: heralds are the messengers of Zeus and of men, under the gods' protection ([[heralds]], [[messengers-of-zeus]], [[talthybius]]). In Book 3 Talthybius fetched a lamb for the oath (3.118–120). In Book 4, when the oath has been broken, he fetches the healer.
