---
summary: In the first battle the epithet goes to the Abantes, and to two killers, the Trojan Agenor and the Achaean Ajax.
grc: [μεγάθυμος]
---

The verse that named Elephenor in the Catalogue returns when he dies: “son of Chalcodon, leader of the great-hearted Abantes” (4.464; {{ref:Iliad 2.541}}; [[elephenor]], [[abantes]]). He is killed by “great-hearted Agenor” as he drags off the body of Echepolus (4.467–469; [[agenor]]). A few lines later the young Simoeisius dies “by the spear of great-hearted Ajax” (4.479; [[simoeisius]], [[ajax]]). The epithet of spirit is given to a killer on each side.
