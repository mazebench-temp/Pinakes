{{quote:1115d2c5-c473-5637-a7a5-eb7acaf756eb}}

The softness of young flesh, the word used of tears and of leaves, is set beside the vultures ([[birds]]). Agamemnon's encouragement promises the fulfillment of the curse spoken over the wine at the oath in Book 3: the brains of those who first did harm were to be poured on the ground, and their wives subdued by others (3.298–301; [[oath-formulas]], [[oaths-and-their-breaking]]). Apollo later reminds the Trojans that the Argives' flesh is no harder: it “is not stone or iron” (4.510; [[flesh-cutting]]).
