---
summary: Agamemnon names Zeus “the Olympian” as the god who will fulfill the broken oath in the end.
---

“For even if the Olympian has not fulfilled it at once, he fulfills it in the end, even late, and they pay a great price” (4.160–161; [[oaths-and-their-breaking]], [[a-day-will-come]]). As in Book 2, the title alone, without the name, stresses the authority of Zeus behind the sign or the oath. The promise answers the narrator's comment at the oath in Book 3: “the son of Cronus was not yet going to fulfill their prayer” ({{ref:Iliad 3.302}}; [[son-of-cronus]]).
