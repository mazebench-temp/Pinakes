---
summary: Menelaus is “good at the war cry” as the healer tends him and the Trojan ranks advance.
grc: [βοὴν ἀγαθὸν]
---

“While they were busy about Menelaus, good at the war cry, the ranks of the shield-bearing Trojans came on” (4.220–221). The formula of the warrior's shout names a man standing wounded while the healer works on him, and the war resumes around him ([[shield-bearing]], [[menelaus]]). In Book 3 it introduced his speech proposing the oath (3.96); here the oath is broken, and he is its first casualty.
