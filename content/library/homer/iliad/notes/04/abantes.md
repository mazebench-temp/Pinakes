---
summary: The Abantes lose their leader in the first clash of the poem. Elephenor, “leader of the great-hearted Abantes”, is killed as he drags away the body of Echepolus to strip it (4.463–470).
---

The Catalogue's line for their leader returns at his death: “son of Chalcodon, leader of the great-hearted Abantes” (4.464 = 2.541; see [[great-hearted]]). The spearmen who were “eager with outstretched ash spears to tear the corselets around the chests of their enemies” (2.543–544) lose Elephenor as he hurries to strip a dead man's armor, and Agenor kills him (4.465–469). They are not named again in the Iliad. See [[elephenor]] and [[agenor]].
