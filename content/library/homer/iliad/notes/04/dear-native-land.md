In Book 2 the formula was the refrain of the flight to the ships ({{ref:Iliad 2.140}}, {{ref:Iliad 2.158}}). In Book 4 Agamemnon fears it. If Menelaus dies, “at once the Achaeans will remember their native land” (4.172), and Helen will be left to the Trojans ([[argive-helen]]). He imagines a Trojan leaping on Menelaus' tomb and saying that Agamemnon “has gone home to his dear native land with empty ships” (4.180–181; [[overweening]]). The native land is the place of his shame: he would come to it “in utter disgrace” (4.171; [[thirsty]]).
