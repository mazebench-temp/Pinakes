---
summary: Two replies of Book 4 are framed by this formula: Hera's answer to Zeus, in the line of Book 1 (4.50 = 1.551), and Nestor's answer to Agamemnon (4.317).
---

“Then ox-eyed queen Hera answered him” (4.50) is 1.551, where Hera answered Zeus' warning in the quarrel on Olympus. In Book 1 she protested and was silenced. Here she answers Zeus' offer of a bargain with her own terms, the three cities he may destroy (4.51–67; see [[hera]] and [[the-bargain-of-zeus-and-hera]]).

“Then the Gerenian horseman Nestor answered him” (4.317; see [[gerenian-horseman]]). The old man answers Agamemnon's wish that he were young with his memory of killing Ereuthalion and a maxim: “the gods never give men all things at once” (4.318–325; see [[all-things-at-once]], [[nestor]] and [[nestor-and-ereuthalion]]).
