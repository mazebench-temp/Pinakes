---
summary: Chalcodon is named again in his son's patronymic when Elephenor dies (4.464).
---

Χαλκωδοντιάδης μεγαθύμων ἀρχὸς Ἀβάντων, “son of Chalcodon, leader of the great-hearted Abantes”: the line that introduced Elephenor in the Catalogue (2.541) is repeated word for word as he falls, while he drags away the body of Echepolus (4.463–469). See [[elephenor]].
