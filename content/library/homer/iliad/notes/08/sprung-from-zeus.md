{{quote:e4baf5b5-6a70-5718-92f6-80e4a8f926cb}}

The address διογενὲς Λαερτιάδη, πολυμήχανʼ Ὀδυσσεῦ is the formal one with which Athena, Agamemnon, Achilles, Ajax and Nestor speak to Odysseus in the Iliad (2.173, 4.358, 9.308, 9.624, 10.144, 23.723). Diomedes uses all its honors and follows them with a reproach: Odysseus is fleeing, “turning your back like a coward in the throng” (8.94; [[son-of-laertes]], [[of-many-devices]]). Odysseus does not hear, or does not heed, and runs to the ships ([[much-enduring]]). See [[odysseus]].
