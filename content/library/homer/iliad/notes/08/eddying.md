---
summary: Hector holds his night assembly away from the ships, “beside the eddying river.”
---

“Then shining Hector held an assembly of the Trojans, leading them away from the ships, beside the eddying river, in a clear space, where the ground showed free of corpses” (8.489–491; [[phaidimos]], [[assembly]]). The river is not named here; the Trojan fires burn “between the ships and the streams of the Xanthus” (8.560), the Trojan river also called Scamander ([[scamander]]). The assembly meets on a patch of ground clear of the dead, beside the eddying stream. See [[trojans]].
