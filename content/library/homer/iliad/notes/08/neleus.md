Νηληϊάδης, “son of Neleus” (Νηληϊάδαο, 8.100), is the patronymic in -ιάδης. In Book 2 Nestor was named with the adjective Νηλήϊος, “of Neleus” (2.20; see [[son-of-neleus]] and [[nestor]]). The patronymic returns in the address “Nestor, son of Neleus, great glory of the Achaeans” (ὦ Νέστορ Νηληϊάδη, μέγα κῦδος Ἀχαιῶν, 10.87, 10.555, 11.511, 14.42; Od. 3.79, 3.202).
