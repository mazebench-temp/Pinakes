---
summary: The two Ajaxes follow the sons of Atreus across the ditch, “clothed in furious courage,” in the verse that brought them forward among the volunteers in Book 7.
---

“And after them the two Ajaxes, clothed in furious courage” (8.262; [[furious]]). The four verses of the leaders who follow Diomedes across the ditch (8.262–265) are the four that named the volunteers for the duel with Hector in Book 7 (7.164–167): the Ajaxes, Idomeneus and Meriones, Eurypylus ([[enyalius]], [[splendid]]). That morning the same two Ajaxes, “attendants of Ares,” had not stood their ground before Zeus' lightning (8.79; [[attendants-of-ares]]). See [[ajax]] and [[ajax-son-of-oileus]].
