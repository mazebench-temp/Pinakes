---
summary: Hector would have burned “the balanced ships” had Hera not roused Agamemnon, and Ajax and Achilles have hauled up their balanced ships at the two ends of the line.
---

“And now he would have burned the balanced ships with blazing fire, had not queen Hera put it in Agamemnon's mind” to rally the Achaeans (8.217–219; [[would-have-had-not]], [[blazing-fire]], [[queenly]]). Agamemnon goes to Odysseus' ship in the middle of the line, from which his shout reaches the huts of Ajax and of Achilles, “who had hauled up their balanced ships at the farthest ends” (8.222–226; [[huge-hulled]]). The epithet of the ships' good build stands in the verse of the threat to burn them and in the verse that lays out the camp. See [[ships]].
