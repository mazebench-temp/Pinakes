summary: Eurypylus, “the splendid son of Euaemon,” is the eighth of the leaders who follow Diomedes across the ditch, in a verse repeated from Book 7.
---

“And after them Eurypylus, the splendid son of Euaemon” (8.265 = 7.167; [[eurypylus]], [[euaemon]]). ἀγλαὸς υἱός honors a man through his father, in the last of the verses that bring the volunteers of Book 7 forward again ([[clothed-in-courage]]). Teucer comes ninth (8.266; [[back-bent]]). See [[eurypylus]].
