---
summary: κρείων describes Poseidon, “the powerful earth-shaker,” as he refuses Hera's proposal; Agamemnon, its usual bearer, is not given it in Book 8.
---

The title that the Iliad gives above all to Agamemnon, κρείων Ἀγαμέμνων, describes Poseidon in the formula κρείων ἐνοσίχθων: “Deeply troubled, the powerful earth-shaker said to her” (8.208; [[earth-shaker]], [[reckless-in-speech]]). In Book 8 Agamemnon is “lord of men” and “most glorious son of Atreus” (8.278, 8.293; [[lord-of-men]], [[most-glorious]]), but never κρείων. The participle is the same in Athena's address to Zeus, “highest of lords,” ὕπατε κρειόντων (8.31; [[most-high]]). See [[poseidon]].
