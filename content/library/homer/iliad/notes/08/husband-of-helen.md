---
summary: The shot that wounds Nestor's trace horse is by “brilliant Alexander, the husband of Helen of the lovely hair.”
---

“Only Nestor stayed … not of his own will: his horse was in pain; with an arrow brilliant Alexander, the husband of Helen of the lovely hair, had struck it on the top of the crown” (8.80–83; [[brilliant]], [[lovely-haired]]). The formula names Paris by the marriage that caused the war, in the verse in which his arrow nearly costs the Achaeans their oldest counselor ([[guardian-of-the-achaeans]]). Paris fights as an archer: in Book 11 the same formula introduces his arrow in Machaon's shoulder (11.505–507). See [[paris]] and [[helen]].
