---
summary: Ovid compares the dying Hyacinthus to flowers broken in a garden, a poppy among them, and names Aethon, one of Hector's horses in Book 8, among the four horses of the Sun.
---

- Hyacinthus (*Metamorphoses* 10.190–195): [[the-poppy-in-later-poets]].
- **Aethon.** Pyrois, Eous, Aethon and Phlegon, the four horses of the Sun, fill the air with fiery neighing and beat against the bars (*Metamorphoses* 2.153–155). Hector's team in Book 8 is also four, and one of them is Aethon (8.185; see [[the-horses-of-hector]]).
