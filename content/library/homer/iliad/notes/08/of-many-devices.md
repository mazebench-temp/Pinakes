---
summary: Odysseus “of many devices” is called to stand by Nestor, and runs past toward the ships.
---

The epithet of Odysseus' resource ends the formal address with which Diomedes calls on him (8.93; [[sprung-from-zeus]], [[son-of-laertes]]): the man of many devices is asked to help save the old man from Hector, and does not stop (8.97–98; [[much-enduring]]). In Book 11 he is the one left alone among the Trojans when the others have fled, and he stands his ground (11.401–410). See [[odysseus]].
