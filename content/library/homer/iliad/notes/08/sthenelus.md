---
summary: When Diomedes takes Nestor into his chariot, Sthenelus and Nestor's attendant Eurymedon take charge of Nestor's horses, “mighty Sthenelus and manly Eurymedon” (8.113–114).
refs: [8.109, 8.113]
relations:
  - companion: diomedes (8.109, 8.113-114)
---

Diomedes gives his own charioteer's place to Nestor and leaves Nestor's horses to “our attendants” (8.109): Sthenelus and Eurymedon, Nestor's attendant (8.113–114; see [[eurymedon-attendant-of-nestor]] and [[nestor]]). Nestor takes the reins, as Athena did when she pushed Sthenelus from the chariot in Book 5 (8.116; 5.835–841; see [[diomedes]]). ἴφθιμος, “mighty”, is Sthenelus' epithet again in the chariot race, when he takes the prize Diomedes has won (23.511).
