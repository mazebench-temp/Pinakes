**The named dead.** Zeus gives the day to the Trojans, yet every man the narrator names as killed in Book 8 is a Trojan: Eniopeus and Agelaus, killed by Diomedes (8.118–123, 8.256–260), and ten men killed by Teucer, eight in a list and Gorgythion and Archeptolemus with arrows meant for Hector (8.273–277, 8.300–315). The Achaeans fall only in the mass. Hector presses the Achaeans like a hound at a boar's heels, “always killing the hindmost” (8.342), and “many had been brought down at the hands of the Trojans” (8.344).

**Full and short.** The killings show the scene at every length. Eniopeus has his father Thebaeus, the blow “in the chest beside the nipple as he held the horses' reins”, the fall from the chariot, the shying horses, and the formula of death, “and there his soul and his strength were loosed” (8.119–123; see [[eniopeus]] and [[loosened-his-limbs]]). Agelaus is speared in the back as he turns to flee, “and his armor clattered upon him” (8.257–260; see [[agelaus]] and [[fell-with-a-thud]]). Teucer's eight are a list, introduced by the narrator's question, “Whom then did noble Teucer kill first of the Trojans?” (8.273; see [[whom-first-whom-last]]), and told in two and a half verses of names with a single father, Polyaemon, and a single epithet, “godlike Lycophontes” (8.274–277). Gorgythion, son of Priam, has the fullest scene of the book, with his mother Castianeira, her home in Aesyme, her beauty, and the simile of the poppy (8.302–308; see [[gorgythion]] and [[castianeira]]). Archeptolemus dies in the verses of Eniopeus' death (8.313–315 ≈ 8.121–123; see [[archeptolemus]]).

**Chains.** The killings are linked as the scene usually links them, by the man who was missed. Diomedes' spear and Teucer's second arrow are aimed at Hector and kill his charioteers (8.118–119, 8.309–313), and Teucer's first arrow at Hector kills Gorgythion, another son of Priam (8.300–303). The Trojan dead fall in Hector's place. Hector's answer is a stone, which fells Teucer but does not kill him (8.320–334; see [[stone-throwing]]).
