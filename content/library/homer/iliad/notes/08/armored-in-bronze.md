---
summary: The two armies meet in the verses that described their first clash in Book 4: “the fury of men in bronze corselets.”
---

“And when they had come together into one place, they dashed together their shields of hide, and their spears, and the fury of men in bronze corselets” (8.60–62). The six verses of the clash (8.60–65) are those that brought the armies together in Book 4 (4.446–451; [[the-armies-clash]], [[bossed]]). The second day of battle begins as the first did, before Zeus' scales turn it (8.66–74). See [[corselet]].
