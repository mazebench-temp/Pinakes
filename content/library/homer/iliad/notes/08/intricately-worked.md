---
summary: Hector wants to take “the intricately worked corselet that Hephaestus toiled to make” from the shoulders of Diomedes.
grc: [δαιδάλεον]
---

Hector names two prizes for his horses to win him: Nestor's shield, all of gold, and “from the shoulders of Diomedes, tamer of horses, the intricately worked corselet that Hephaestus toiled to make” (8.191–195; [[tamer-of-horses]], [[hephaestus]], [[corselet]]). δαιδάλεος, from the root of δαιδάλλω (“work with art”), is the adjective of the god's craftsmanship, used here as the entry's other words are used of richly made armor. A corselet made by Hephaestus for Diomedes is named nowhere else, and Hector does not win it. See [[diomedes]] and [[stripping-the-dead]].
