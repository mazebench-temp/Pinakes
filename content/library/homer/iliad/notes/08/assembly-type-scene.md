summary: Neither of Book 8's assemblies follows the full procedure. Zeus calls the gods and speaks alone, and the meeting ends when he drives away; Hector gathers the army on the battlefield at night, speaks leaning on his spear, and is answered by a shout.
refs: [8.2-4, 8.28-30, 8.489-496, 8.542]

  - 8.489-496 | Hector holds an assembly of the Trojans

Set against the pattern (see [[assembly-type-scene]]), the two assemblies of Book 8 keep only its frame.

1. **Summons.** Both are “held” by one man, ἀγορὴν ποιήσατο (8.2, 8.489), and no heralds are named. Zeus' gods meet on the highest peak of Olympus; Hector leads (ἀγαγών) the army away from the ships to a clear space by the river (8.490–491).
2. **Gathering.** There is no verse of gathering. The Trojans step down from their chariots to the ground and listen (8.492).
3. **Speeches.** One speaker each. After Zeus “they all fell hushed in silence”, and “at last” Athena speaks among them (8.28–30), in the verses that open the second speech after a silence in Books 7 and 9 (7.398–399, 9.29–31, 9.693–696; see [[silence]]). Zeus answers her and the debate ends (8.38–40). In Hector's assembly no one answers; the army shouts (8.542). Hector speaks with a spear in his hand instead of the scepter (8.493–496; compare 2.109; see [[spear]] and [[scepter]]).
4. **Dissolution.** Neither assembly is dissolved. Zeus yokes his horses and goes (8.41); the Trojans unyoke theirs and set about the night's work (8.543–547).
