---
summary: Hector boasts that he will kill Diomedes and “carry off his bloody spoils.”
---

“I will know whether the son of Tydeus, mighty Diomedes, will push me back from the ships to the wall, or whether I will kill him with the bronze and carry off his bloody spoils” (8.532–534; [[son-of-tydeus]], [[krateros]]). Earlier in the day Hector named the spoil he wanted from Diomedes, the corselet that Hephaestus made (8.194–195; [[intricately-worked]], [[stripping-the-dead]]). The boast looks to the next day, when Diomedes will be wounded, but not by Hector (11.369–378). See [[hector]] and [[diomedes]].
