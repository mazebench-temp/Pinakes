---
summary: Hera harnesses her horses with golden headbands, in the verse that began her preparations in Book 5, to drive out against Zeus' command.
---

“She went about harnessing the horses with golden headbands, Hera, the august goddess, daughter of great Cronus” (8.382–383; [[august-goddess]]). The two verses repeat Book 5, where Hera harnessed the same team to drive out with Zeus' leave and stop Ares (5.720–721). In Book 8 she goes against his command, and the team with the golden headbands is turned back at the gates of Olympus (8.409–437; [[storm-footed]], [[ambrosial]]). The gods' horses are adorned with gold: Zeus' own team, earlier in the day, had “flowing manes of gold” (8.42; [[swift-flying]]). See [[hera]] and [[horses]].
