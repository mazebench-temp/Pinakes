summary: The bossed shields press against one another as the armies meet, in the verses of the clash repeated from Book 4.
---

“And the bossed shields pressed against one another, and a great din arose” (8.62–63). The clash of the armies is told in the verses of Book 4 (8.60–65 = 4.446–451; [[the-armies-clash]], [[armored-in-bronze]]): hides, spears and the fury of bronze-corseleted men meet, and the bosses of the round shields jam together. See [[shield]].
