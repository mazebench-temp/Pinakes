---
summary: Hector taunts Diomedes that “the Danaans of the swift horses honored you above others,” in the words with which Agamemnon honored Idomeneus.
grc: [ταχύπωλοι]
---

{{quote:b237aad7-3e41-5baa-b04e-3451e50d490c}}

Hector's taunt turns the language of honor against Diomedes. “I honor you above the Danaans of the swift horses,” Agamemnon told Idomeneus, with a full cup always standing by him at the feast (4.257–263; [[honor-and-dishonor]]); the honors that Hector lists, “a seat of honor, with meat, and with full cups,” are those that Sarpedon names to Glaucus as the reason why the foremost must fight (12.310–321). The formula of the Danaans' horses, usually in the genitive, here makes them the honoring people, who will now dishonor him ([[wretched-doll]], [[time]]). See [[danaans]] and [[diomedes]].
