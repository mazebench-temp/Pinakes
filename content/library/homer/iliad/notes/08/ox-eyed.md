The formula of Hera's beauty stands in the address of Zeus' harshest words to her. He will be stronger still at dawn, Hector will not stop until Achilles is roused, and he does not care for her anger, not even if she goes to the lowest limits of earth and sea, where Iapetus and Cronus sit, since there is nothing more shameless than she is (8.477–483; [[queenly]], [[supreme-in-might]], [[shameless-dog]]). Hera answers with silence (8.484; [[white-armed]]). See [[hera]] and [[plan-of-zeus]].
