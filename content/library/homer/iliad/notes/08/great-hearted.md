summary: Both words of the entry occur in Book 8: μεγάθυμος of the Trojans whose wives Diomedes has widowed, μεγαλήτωρ of Eetion, Andromache's father, and of the Trojans whom Hector addresses at night.
grc: [μεγαλήτορες]
---

Nestor reminds Diomedes of what he has done: the Trojans will not believe Hector, “nor the wives of the great-hearted shield-bearing Trojans, whose husbands in their prime you have flung in the dust” (8.155–156; [[shield-bearing]], [[flourishing]]). Hector, speaking to his horses, names their keeper, “Andromache, the daughter of great-hearted Eetion” (8.187; [[eetion]], [[andromache]], [[the-horses-of-hector]]), and in his night speech he addresses his people: “Let it be as I say, great-hearted Trojans” (8.523). The epithet of the Trojans' spirit stands in their enemy's praise and their leader's command on the same day. See [[trojans]].
