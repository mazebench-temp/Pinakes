---
summary: Helen is “of the lovely hair” in the formula that names Paris as her husband, when his arrow wounds Nestor's horse.
---

“Brilliant Alexander, the husband of Helen of the lovely hair” (8.82; [[husband-of-helen]], [[brilliant]]) is Helen's only appearance in Book 8. The epithet of her beauty is given in a verse about her husband's arrow, which lames Nestor's trace horse and leaves the old man exposed to Hector (8.81–91). See [[helen]] and [[hair]].
