summary: Zeus has a precinct and a fragrant altar on Gargarus, the peak of Ida from which he watches the battle; Agamemnon claims to have burned offerings on every altar of Zeus he passed on the way to Troy; and the eagle drops its fawn beside the altar of Zeus in the camp. At 8.441 βωμός is not an altar but the stand of Zeus' chariot.

**On Gargarus.** Zeus drives to Ida, “to Gargarus, where he has a precinct and a fragrant altar” (8.48; see [[gargarus]] and [[fragrant]]). The half-verse belongs to the gods' own places: Peleus vowed sheep to the river Spercheius “at your springs, where you have a precinct and a fragrant altar” (23.148), and Aphrodite goes to Paphos, “where she has a precinct and a fragrant altar” (Od. 8.363). τέμενος, “precinct”, is land cut off (τέμνω) for a god or a king; θυήεις, “fragrant”, is said of the smoke of offerings (from θύω, “burn as an offering”). Zeus will remember the sacrifices on Ida when Hector is about to die: Hector burned many thighs of oxen for him “on the peaks of many-folded Ida, and at other times on the height of the city” (22.170–172).

**Agamemnon's altars.** In his prayer Agamemnon tells Zeus that he never passed “your beautiful altar” on the voyage to Troy without burning the fat and thighs of oxen on it (8.238–241; see [[sacrifice]] and [[prayer]]). He speaks of every altar of Zeus on the way as one.

**In the camp.** The eagle drops the fawn “beside the beautiful altar of Zeus … where the Achaeans used to sacrifice to Zeus, lord of all omens” (8.249–250; see [[teras]] and [[eagle]]). The camp's altars stood by the ships of Odysseus, at its place of assembly and judgment (11.806–808), and that is where Agamemnon has just stood to rally the army, on Odysseus' ship in the middle of the line (8.222–223; see [[ships]]).

**Not an altar.** When Zeus comes back from Ida, Poseidon sets his chariot ἂμ βωμοῖσι, “on its stand” (8.441; see [[chariot]]). βωμός is first a raised base, and the altar is the base on which offerings are burned. The form occurs nowhere else in Homer, and it is not counted here.
