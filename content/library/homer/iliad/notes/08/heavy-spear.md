---
summary: Athena takes up her spear, “heavy, huge, and massive,” in the verses of her arming repeated from Book 5.
---

The arming of Athena ends with her spear, “heavy, huge, and massive, with which she overpowers the ranks of men, of heroes, against whom she, the daughter of the mighty father, bears resentment” (8.390–391 = 5.746–747; [[daughter-of-a-mighty-father]], [[kotos]]). The verse βριθὺ μέγα στιβαρόν describes Athena's spear again in the Odyssey, as she sets out for Ithaca (Od. 1.99–100), and the Pelian ash that only Achilles can wield (16.140–144). It is the spear that Iris says Athena would dare to lift against Zeus (8.424; [[huge]]). See [[spear]] and [[athena]].
