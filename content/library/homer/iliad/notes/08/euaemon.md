---
summary: Euaemon is named only as the father of Eurypylus, “the splendid son of Euaemon”, among the leaders who follow Diomedes across the ditch (8.265).
---

Εὐαίμονος ἀγλαὸς υἱός, “the splendid son of Euaemon” (8.265), ends the verse that brings Eurypylus out, as it ended the verse of his rising for Hector's challenge (7.167; see [[eurypylus]]). In the passage of the charge five of the nine leaders are named through their fathers: Diomedes is “the son of Tydeus” (8.254), Agamemnon and Menelaus are “the sons of Atreus” (8.261), Eurypylus is Euaemon's son, and Ajax is Τελαμωνιάδης, “son of Telamon” (8.267). Euaemon is known only in this way.
