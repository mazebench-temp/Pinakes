---
summary: The two Ajaxes, “attendants of Ares,” do not stay when Zeus' lightning falls among the Achaeans.
---

{{quote:9b367cd7-0178-57da-866f-d07e2cb812c8}}

The title of the war god's servants is given to the two Ajaxes, in the dual, in the verse of their retreat; Book 10 gives it to them again in the dual (10.228), and elsewhere it is the formal address to the whole army (ὦ φίλοι ἥρωες Δαναοί, θεράποντες Ἄρηος). Zeus' thunder and lightning are beyond what the attendants of Ares can face, and only Nestor is left, against his will ([[guardian-of-the-achaeans]], [[pale-fear]]). Later in the day the two Ajaxes are back among the leaders who cross the ditch, “clothed in furious courage” (8.262; [[clothed-in-courage]], [[furious]]). See [[ajax]] and [[ajax-son-of-oileus]].
