---
summary: The fates of the Achaeans sink to “the bountiful earth” in Zeus' scales, and Teucer brings eight Trojans down to it, one after another.
---

The formula names the ground where men fall. At noon “the fates of the Achaeans settled down on the bountiful earth” (8.73; [[wide-sky]], [[the-scales-of-zeus]]). In the afternoon the same verse-end ends Teucer's run of kills: Orsilochus, Ormenus, Ophelestes, Daetor, Chromius, Lycophontes, Amopaon and Melanippus, “all of them, one after another, he brought down to the bountiful earth” (8.274–277; [[long-barbed]], [[teucer]]). On the day the scales gave the earth to the Achaeans' fates, an Achaean archer gives it eight Trojans. See [[earth]].
