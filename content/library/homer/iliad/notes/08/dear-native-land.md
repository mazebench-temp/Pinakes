---
summary: Athena wishes that Hector may lose his life “at the hands of the Argives in his native land.”
grc: [ἐν πατρίδι γαίῃ]
en: [native land]
---

“Yes, and how I wish that man would lose his strength and his life, killed at the hands of the Argives in his native land” (8.358–359). The formula of home, which elsewhere names the land the Achaeans long to return to ([[nostos]]), names the place where Athena wants Hector to die. He does: in Book 22 Achilles kills him before the walls of Troy, and in the same book Hector, dying, asks that his body be given back to his parents and his people (22.338–343). The Achaeans, for their part, die far from their own land (2.162, 2.178). See [[hector]] and [[athena]].
