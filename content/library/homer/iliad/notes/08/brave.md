---
summary: Teucer's arrow kills Gorgythion, “the brave son of Priam.”
grc: [υἱὸν ἐῢν]
---

“And he missed him, but noble Gorgythion, the brave son of Priam, he struck in the chest with the arrow” (8.302–303; [[gorgythion]], [[blameless]], [[son-of-priam]]). The old adjective ἐΰς, “good, brave,” which the entry's formulas give to sons, praises a young man as he dies. The verses that follow name his mother and compare his drooping head to a poppy weighed down by its seed and the spring rain (8.304–308; [[like-the-goddesses]], [[castianeira]]). See [[teucer]].
