---
summary: The verses that end Athena's arming in Book 5 end it again in Book 8, as she arms against her father's command.
---

{{quote:470d57b9-22f0-5f9c-90da-3daf385ce8e2}}

The eight verses repeat Athena's arming in Book 5, where she went out with Zeus' leave to stop Ares (5.733–747; [[arming-scene]], [[flaming]], [[heavy-spear]]). Here she arms against his command, and the epithet that names her by her father's strength becomes pointed: the mighty father is the one she is defying. He turns her back before she reaches the plain, and Iris asks whether she will really dare to lift her huge spear against Zeus (8.397–424; [[huge]], [[shameless-dog]]). See [[athena]].
