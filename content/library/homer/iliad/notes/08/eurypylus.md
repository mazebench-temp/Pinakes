summary: Eurypylus, “the splendid son of Euaemon”, is the eighth of the nine leaders who follow Diomedes across the ditch, just before Teucer (8.265).
---

“And after them Eurypylus, the splendid son of Euaemon” (8.265 = 7.167; see [[euaemon]] and [[splendid]]). In Book 7 he was the seventh of the nine who stood up to face Hector (7.161–169); here he is the eighth of the nine who come out against the Trojans, and Teucer follows him with his bow (8.266; see [[teucer]]). An arrow will end his fighting: in Book 11 Paris shoots him in the thigh as he strips a fallen Trojan, and Patroclus tends the wound (11.575–592, 11.806–848; see [[paris]] and [[patroclus]]).
