---
summary: Iris asks Athena whether she will really dare to lift her “huge spear” against Zeus.
---

“But you are most dread, you shameless dog, if you really dare to lift your huge spear against Zeus” (8.423–424; [[shameless-dog]], [[most-dread]]). The huge spear is the one Athena took up as she armed, “heavy, huge, and massive” (8.390; [[heavy-spear]]). The phrase πελώριον ἔγχος describes only one other spear in the Iliad: the spear of Ares, which he wielded in the front of the Trojans in Book 5 (5.592–594). The weapon fit for the war god is to be raised against the father of gods. See [[athena]].
