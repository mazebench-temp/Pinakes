“But Athena, daughter of aegis-bearing Zeus, let her soft robe fall on her father's floor, richly worked, which she herself had made and labored at with her hands” (8.384–386 = 5.733–735; [[child-of-aegis-bearing-zeus]], [[robe]]). ποικίλος, the epithet of embroidered robes and inlaid armor, describes the goddess' own weaving ([[weaving]]). The robe of peace is dropped for the tunic of Zeus and the armor of war (8.387–388; [[cloud-gatherer]], [[arming-scene]]). See [[athena]].
