---
summary: Teucer answers Agamemnon's praise with the formal “Most glorious son of Atreus, why do you urge me on when I am eager myself?”
---

“Most glorious son of Atreus, why do you urge me on when I am eager myself? I do not stop, as far as I have strength” (8.293–294; [[son-of-atreus]]). The superlative of honor, used in address to Zeus and to kings, opens a reply that turns the king's encouragement aside: Teucer needs no urging, and has already shot eight arrows into eight men ([[long-barbed]], [[dear-heart]]). See [[agamemnon]] and [[teucer]].
