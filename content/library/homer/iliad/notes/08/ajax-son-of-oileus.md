---
summary: The lesser Ajax is one of “the two Ajaxes” who run with the other leaders when Zeus thunders and come out again behind Diomedes, “clothed in furious courage” (8.79, 8.262).
refs: [8.79, 8.262]
---

He is not named alone in Book 8. With Telamonian Ajax he makes the pair whom the narrator calls θεράποντες Ἄρηος, “attendants of Ares”, in the verse that tells their flight: οὔτε δύʼ Αἴαντες μενέτην, “nor did the two Ajaxes stay” (8.79), with a dual verb for the pair (see [[ajax]]). When the eagle of Zeus has rallied the army they come out fourth and fifth, after Diomedes and the sons of Atreus, “clothed in furious courage” (8.262 = 7.164; see [[furious]]). The pair of “attendants of Ares” returns when the two volunteer to go with Diomedes into the Trojan camp at night (10.228).
