---
summary: Agamemnon weeps as he prays, and Zeus pities him: “So he spoke, and the father pitied him as he shed tears.”
grc: ["δάκρυ χέοντα"]
---

Agamemnon's tears are told only when his prayer is over: “So he spoke, and the father pitied him as he shed tears, and nodded to him that his people would be saved and not perish” (8.245–246; see [[prayer-type-scene]]). The verse returns when Ajax, lost in the mist over Patroclus' body, prays to Zeus for light, and Zeus scatters the mist at once (17.645–650). Both times the tears are part of the plea, and both times Zeus gives way to them. Agamemnon will weep again later that night, standing before the army in assembly “like a spring of dark water” (9.13–16).
