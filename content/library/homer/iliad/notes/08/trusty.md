The formula ἐρίηρες ἑταῖροι names the companions who serve in need. The same three verses bring the wounded Hypsenor back to the ships in Book 13, carried by the same two men (13.421–423; [[mecisteus-son-of-echius]], [[brilliant]]). Here they carry Teucer, whose arm is numb and whose bowstring is broken (8.324–329; [[jagged]]). See [[hetairos]] and [[teucer]].
