---
summary: Diomedes is “far the first” to kill “a helmeted man of the Trojans” when the Achaeans rally after the eagle: Agelaus, son of Phradmon.
---

“Then none of the Danaans, many as they were, could boast that he had held his swift horses ahead of the son of Tydeus, driven them out over the ditch, and fought face to face; but far the first, he killed a helmeted man of the Trojans, Agelaus, son of Phradmon” (8.253–257; [[agelaus]], [[phradmon]]). The formula of the first kill, ἄνδρα κορυστήν, gives Diomedes the first blood of the rally, as it gave Antilochus the first kill of the battle in Book 4 (4.457). Agelaus has turned his horses to flee, and Diomedes spears him in the back (8.257–260; [[fell-with-a-thud]]). See [[diomedes]].
