---
summary: Eetion is named in his daughter's title, “Andromache, the daughter of great-hearted Eetion”, when Hector reminds his horses of her care (8.187).
relations:
  - child: andromache (8.187)
---

The verse is the one that introduced Andromache in Book 6 (8.187 = 6.395; see [[andromache]] and [[the-horses-of-hector]]). μεγαλήτωρ, “great-hearted”, is his epithet in it (see [[great-hearted]]). His name stands here beside Hector's horses. Achilles' trace horse Pedasus came from his city, taken when Achilles sacked it (16.152–154), with the lyre Achilles plays in his hut (9.186–189; see [[sack-of-thebe]]).
