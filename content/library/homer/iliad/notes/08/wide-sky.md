---
summary: In Zeus' scales the fates of the Trojans are lifted “toward the wide sky,” while those of the Achaeans sink to the earth.
---

“The fates of the Achaeans settled down on the bountiful earth, and those of the Trojans were lifted toward the wide sky” (8.73–74; [[bountiful-earth]], [[that-lays-men-low]]). The pan that sinks carries death, and the one that rises is spared ([[the-scales-of-zeus]], [[ker-doom]]). The wide sky, Zeus' own share of the world, receives the side he favors for the day. See [[sky]] and [[plan-of-zeus]].
