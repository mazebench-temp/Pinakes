---
summary: Hector wants the Trojans, when he reaches the ships, to remember “consuming fire.”
---

“But when I come among the hollow ships, then let there be some remembrance of consuming fire, so that I may set fire to the ships and kill the men themselves” (8.180–182; [[hollow-ships]]). πῦρ δήϊον is fire as an enemy, the fire that destroys ships and cities ([[blazing-fire]]). Hector names the goal of his attack, which the narrator and Agamemnon name twice more before the day ends (8.217, 8.235). See [[hector]] and [[ships]].
