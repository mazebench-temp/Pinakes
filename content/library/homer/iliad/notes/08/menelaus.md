---
summary: Menelaus comes out with his brother behind Diomedes, among the nine leaders who cross the ditch when the eagle of Zeus has rallied the Achaeans (8.261).
---

“After him came the sons of Atreus, Agamemnon and Menelaus” (8.261; see [[atreidae]]). It is his only appearance in the book. In Book 7 he was the first to rise against Hector's challenge, and Agamemnon held him back, since Hector was far the stronger (7.94–122). When Nestor's rebuke then brought nine leaders to their feet, he was not among them (7.161–169). Now he is one of the nine who follow Diomedes out against the Trojans, and the narrator names him with his brother, third in the order after Diomedes and Agamemnon (8.253–266; see [[agamemnon]] and [[diomedes]]).
