---
summary: Zeus thunders three times from Ida, “giving the Trojans a sign of the victory that turns the battle,” and Hector reads the sign.
---

Diomedes ponders three times whether to turn and fight Hector, and “three times Zeus the counselor thundered from the mountains of Ida, giving the Trojans a sign of the victory that turns the battle” (8.169–171; [[counselor]], [[pondering-two-ways]]). In Book 7 Athena and Apollo had each wanted the victory for their own side (7.21–26); now Zeus gives it, and gives a sign of it. Hector understands it at once: “I know that the son of Cronus has readily nodded his promise to me of victory and great glory” (8.175–176; [[nike]], [[kydos]], [[teras]]). See [[plan-of-zeus]].
