---
summary: Agamemnon promises Teucer a gift of honor “if aegis-bearing Zeus and Athena grant me to sack the well-built citadel of Ilios.”
---

“If aegis-bearing Zeus and Athena grant me to sack the well-built citadel of Ilios, I will put into your hand, first after me, a gift of honor” (8.287–289; [[aegis-bearing]], [[geras]]). The line-end ἐϋκτίμενον πτολίεθρον names the city at its strongest in the verse of its capture, as the epithets of Troy's walls do ([[well-walled]], [[of-the-fine-towers]]). The promise is made on the day the Trojans have driven the Achaeans back to their ships. See [[troy]] and [[dear-heart]].
