summary: “The sons of Atreus, Agamemnon and Menelaus,” follow Diomedes across the ditch, and Teucer answers Agamemnon's praise with “Most glorious son of Atreus.”
---

When Diomedes has driven out across the ditch, “After him came the sons of Atreus, Agamemnon and Menelaus” (8.261; [[atreidae]], [[agamemnon]], [[menelaus]]), first of the eight leaders who follow him (8.261–266). Later Teucer answers the king's praise and promises with the formal address “Most glorious son of Atreus, why do you urge me on when I am eager myself?” (8.293–294; [[most-glorious]], [[dear-heart]]). See [[atreus]].
