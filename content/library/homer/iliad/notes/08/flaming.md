---
summary: Athena steps onto the “flaming chariot” in the verse that ends her arming, repeated from Book 5.
---

“And she stepped onto the flaming chariot, and took up her spear” (8.389 = 5.745). The verse takes Athena from her arming to Hera's chariot, and the verses that follow describe her spear ([[heavy-spear]], [[daughter-of-a-mighty-father]]). In Book 5 the chariot carried the two goddesses to the plain with Zeus' leave; in Book 8 it gets no farther than the gates of Olympus, where Iris stops it with his threat to lame the horses and smash the chariot (8.402–403; [[circling-years]]). See [[chariot]] and [[athena]].
