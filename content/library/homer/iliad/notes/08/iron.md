---
summary: The gates of Tartarus are of iron, the only gates of iron in Homer.
grc: [σιδήρειαι]
---

Zeus' Tartarus lies “where the gates are of iron and the threshold of bronze” (8.15; see [[tartarus]] and [[threshold]]). They are the only gates of iron in Homer; Hesiod gives the gates of Tartarus marble (*Theogony* 811). The adjective σιδήρειος, “of iron”, is used elsewhere of Areithous' iron mace (7.141, 7.144; see [[mace]]), of the din of battle (σιδήρειος ὀρυμαγδός, 17.424), and of a heart that nothing moves (σιδήρειόν νύ τοι ἦτορ, 24.205, 24.521).
