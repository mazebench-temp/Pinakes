---
summary: Hector, “the equal of swift Ares,” hems the Achaeans in behind their ditch.
---

θοός, “swift,” describes the war god in the formula θοῷ ἀτάλαντος Ἄρηϊ: Hector is “the equal of swift Ares” as he presses the Achaeans back into the space between their wall and ditch (8.215–216; [[equal-of-ares]]). The speed of the war god is the speed of Hector's advance, with Zeus behind him. The other adjective of the entry, ταχύς, is the word in Zeus' command “Go, swift Iris” (8.399; [[swift-iris]]). See [[ares]] and [[hector]].
