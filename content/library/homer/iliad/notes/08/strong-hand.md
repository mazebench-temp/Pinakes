---
summary: Agamemnon goes along the huts and ships “holding a great purple cloak in his strong hand.”
---

“He went along the huts and ships of the Achaeans, holding a great purple cloak in his strong hand” (8.220–221). The formula of the hand that grips a spear, a stone or a sword holds a cloak: the king goes to rally the army without a weapon, carrying the robe of his rank, and takes his stand on Odysseus' ship to be heard (8.222–227; [[cloak]], [[purple]], [[huge-hulled]]). In Book 7 the same formula described Hector's hand as he picked up a stone against Ajax (7.264; [[hector]]). See [[agamemnon]].
