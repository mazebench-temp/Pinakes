---
summary: Hector tells the Trojans he had thought to destroy the ships and go back home “to windy Ilios” before night came.
---

“I thought just now that I would destroy the ships and all the Achaeans and go back home to windy Ilios; but darkness came first” (8.498–500; [[black-night]], [[war-and-night]]). The epithet of the city on its height names the home that Hector means to return to only as a victor, and he keeps the army on the plain for the night instead (8.502–522; [[yield-to-night]]). See [[troy]].
