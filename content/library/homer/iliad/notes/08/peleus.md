---
summary: Peleus is named in Book 8 only through his son, “the swift-footed son of Peleus”, in Zeus' prophecy that Hector will not stop until Achilles is roused beside the ships (8.474).
---

πρὶν ὄρθαι παρὰ ναῦφι ποδώκεα Πηλεΐωνα, “until the swift-footed son of Peleus is roused beside the ships” (8.474; see [[achilles]], [[swift-footed]] and [[son-of-peleus]]). In a book that names Telamon through both his sons (8.224, 8.267, 8.281–285; see [[telamon]]), the father of the absent hero appears only in his son's patronymic, in Zeus' prophecy of what will bring him back to the war (see [[plan-of-zeus]]).
