---
summary: Helen is named once in Book 8, in the verse that calls Paris “the husband of Helen of the lovely hair” as he shoots Nestor's horse (8.82).
---

The verse names Paris by his marriage, as it does when he arms for the duel (3.329) and when he refuses to give her up (7.355); its last words return when his arrows wound Diomedes and Machaon (11.369, 11.505; see [[paris]] and [[husband-of-helen]]). ἠΰκομος, “of the lovely hair”, is her epithet in it (see [[lovely-haired]]).
