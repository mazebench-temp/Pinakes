---
summary: Diomedes names Odysseus by his father in the full address with which he calls on him to stand by Nestor.
---

“Son of Laertes, sprung from Zeus, Odysseus of many devices, where are you fleeing, turning your back like a coward in the throng?” (8.93–94; [[sprung-from-zeus]], [[of-many-devices]]). The patronymic opens the formal address, and the address turns into a reproach. Odysseus, “much-enduring” in the next verse, runs on to the hollow ships (8.97–98; [[much-enduring]], [[hollow-ships]]). See [[odysseus]] and [[laertes]].
