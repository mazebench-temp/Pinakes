---
summary: Meriones, Idomeneus' squire, “the equal of Enyalius, slayer of men”, is the seventh of the nine leaders who follow Diomedes across the ditch (8.263–264).
---

“And after them Idomeneus and Idomeneus' squire Meriones, the equal of Enyalius, slayer of men” (8.263–264). The verse that names him is his title in the Catalogue and among the volunteers of Book 7 (8.264 = 7.166 = 2.651; see [[idomeneus]], [[enyalius]] and [[equal-of-ares]]). Teucer comes two places after him, ninth, with his bow (8.266; see [[teucer]]). The squire and the archer meet again at Patroclus' funeral games. Teucer shoots first, makes no vow to Apollo, and misses the dove, cutting only the cord that held it; Meriones vows a hecatomb to Apollo, hits the dove in flight and takes the first prize (23.859–883; see [[contests]]).
