---
summary: Agamemnon opens his rebuke with Hera's words from Book 5: “Shame, Argives, wretched disgraces, admirable in looks!”
---

{{quote:c0105804-2e15-5519-86df-98e1bc03061a}}

The first verse is the one Hera shouted in the likeness of Stentor when the Achaeans fell back before Ares (5.787; [[aidos]], [[elenchos]]). Praise of looks is turned into reproach, as Hector turned εἶδος ἄριστε against Paris (3.39): men who look like heroes do not fight like them. Agamemnon goes on to the boasts made on Lemnos, which now prove empty (8.229–234; [[empty-boasting]]). See [[agamemnon]] and [[praise-and-blame]].
