---
summary: Hector orders a watch over Troy for the night, “so that no ambush may enter the city while the army is away”.
grc: [λόχος]
---

With the army camped on the plain, the city is left to boys, old men and women, and Hector orders “a steadfast watch, so that no ambush may enter the city while the army is away” (8.521–522; see [[night-watch]]). λόχος here is a band of men who lie in wait and strike by surprise. The ambush of the night comes from the other side and in the open plain: in Book 10 Odysseus and Diomedes lie down among the dead beside the path and let the Trojan scout Dolon run past them before they catch him (10.349–350).
