---
summary: Zeus foretells that “mighty Hector will not cease from war” until Achilles is roused beside the ships.
---

“For mighty Hector will not cease from war until the swift-footed son of Peleus is roused beside the ships” (8.473–474; [[swift-footed]], [[son-of-peleus]]). ὄβριμος, the epithet of Ares and of the heavy spear ([[heavy-spear]]), describes Hector in the prophecy of his advance, which will not end until it has brought about Patroclus' death and Achilles' return ([[plan-of-zeus]], [[the-death-of-patroclus]]). The same root gives Athena her title ὀβριμοπάτρη in this book, as she arms (8.391; [[daughter-of-a-mighty-father]]). See [[hector]].
