summary: In verses not found in the manuscripts, the gods hate “Priam, and the people of Priam of the good ash spear”; the verse is 4.47.
---

{{quote:18d63079-8ab9-5bd9-804b-224fb982d362}}

The last of the four verses known only from the Second Alcibiades (149d) is a verse of Book 4: there Zeus says that of all cities he honored most sacred Ilios, “and Priam, and the people of Priam of the good ash spear” (4.44–47; [[blessed-gods]], [[the-refused-sacrifice]]). In Book 8 the same words name the people whom the gods now hate. See [[priam]] and [[troy]].
