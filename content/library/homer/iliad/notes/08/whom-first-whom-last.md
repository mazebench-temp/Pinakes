summary: The narrator asks before Teucer's list of victims, “Whom then did noble Teucer kill first of the Trojans?”, and answers with eight names.
grc: [ἔνθα τίνα πρῶτον]
en: [Whom then]
---

{{quote:9657c756-7b23-5e3a-9b87-4e2e76cef3d0}}

The question has only its first half here, “whom first”, without “whom last”, and the answer takes up its word: “Orsilochus first” (πρῶτα, 8.274). Seven more names follow in two verses, and the list closes, “all of them, one after another, he brought down to the bountiful earth” (8.277; see [[androktasia]]). The full question, ἔνθα τίνα πρῶτον τίνα δʼ ὕστατον, is asked of Hector and Ares (5.703), and will be asked of Hector (11.299) and of Patroclus (16.692). Teucer's own count, “Eight long-barbed arrows I have let fly” (8.297), agrees with the list (see [[arrows]]).
