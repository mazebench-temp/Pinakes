---
summary: Hector strikes Teucer with “the jagged stone” by the collarbone, breaking his bowstring.
---

“There, as he took aim at him, struck him with the jagged stone, and broke his bowstring; and his hand went numb at the wrist, and he sank to his knees and stayed there, and the bow fell from his hand” (8.327–329; [[stone-throwing]], [[bitter]]). Hector has leapt from his chariot, picked up a stone, and gone straight at the archer (8.320–322; [[gleaming-all-over]]). The stone answers the arrows that missed him and killed Gorgythion and Archeptolemus (8.300–315). Ajax covers his fallen brother with his shield (8.330–334; [[trusty]]).
