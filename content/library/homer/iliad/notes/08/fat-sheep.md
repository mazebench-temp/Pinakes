---
summary: Hector orders the Trojans to bring “cattle and fat sheep” from the city for their supper on the plain, and they do.
---

“And bring from the city cattle and fat sheep, quickly, and provide yourselves with honey-sweet wine” (8.505–506), says Hector, and the narrator repeats the verses as the Trojans carry out the order (8.545–546; [[honey-sweet]], [[feast]], [[cattle]], [[sheep]]). The repetition of order and deed is the poem's way with commands ([[message-repeated]]). The army camps on the plain and feasts as though the city were already safe. See [[trojans]].
