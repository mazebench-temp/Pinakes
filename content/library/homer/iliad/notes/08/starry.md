---
summary: Zeus' horses fly “between earth and the starry sky” as he drives to Ida, in the verse that carried Hera's horses in Book 5.
---

Zeus whips up his team, “and the pair flew on not unwilling between earth and the starry sky” (8.45–46; [[bronze-footed]], [[swift-flying]]). The two verses carried Hera and Athena from Olympus to the plain in Book 5 (5.768–769). The starry sky of the morning's flight returns at night, when the Trojan watch-fires are likened to the stars around the moon (8.553–561; [[preeminent]]). See [[sky]].
