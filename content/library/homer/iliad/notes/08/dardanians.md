---
summary: Nestor tells Diomedes that “the Trojans and the Dardanians” will not believe Hector if he calls him a coward (8.154), and Hector names the Dardanians in his calls to “Trojans and Lycians and Dardanians who fight hand to hand” (8.173) and to “Trojans and Dardanians and allies” (8.497).
---

Both of Hector's addresses are formulas. The first is his call to battle, and it returns five times (8.173 = 11.286, 13.150, 15.425, 15.486, 17.184; see [[exhortation]] and [[lycians]]). The second opens a speech to the assembled army, as it did when Agamemnon demanded Helen back and when Antenor and Priam spoke in the Trojan assembly (8.497 = 3.456, 7.348, 7.368; see [[trojan-allies]] and [[hear-me]]).
