summary: Athena complains that Zeus has granted Thetis' request “to honor Achilles, sacker of cities.”
grc: [πτολίπορθον]
---

{{quote:20548812-4ac3-5546-8f95-aa59c171db4b}}

Athena retells Thetis' supplication of Book 1 in her own words: Thetis kissed Zeus' knees and took his chin, begging him to honor Achilles (8.370–372; [[thetis-supplicates-zeus]], [[supplication]]). In Book 1 she clasped his knees and took his chin, and no kiss is mentioned (1.500–502, 1.512). The epithet she gives Achilles is the epithet of the great destroyers; Achilles himself counts twelve cities sacked from the ships and eleven by land (9.328–329; [[achilles-raids]]). In the Iliad it is Achilles' for the first time here. See [[achilles]] and [[athena]].
