---
summary: Agamemnon asks Zeus to fulfill “at least this wish”: not victory, but that the Achaeans may escape alive.
en: [fulfill for me at least this wish]
---

“But, Zeus, fulfill for me at least this wish: let us at least escape and get away, and do not let the Achaeans be so beaten down by the Trojans” (8.242–244). περ, “at least”, is in both of the first two verses (τόδε πέρ, αὐτοὺς δή περ): the wish is cut down to the least that can be asked. It follows a large claim, offerings on every altar of Zeus on the voyage (8.238–241; see [[prayer-type-scene]] and [[sacrifice]]), and it is granted at once: Zeus nods “that his people would be saved and not perish” (8.246). In Book 2 Agamemnon prayed that the sun might not set before he had burned Priam's palace, and Zeus did not fulfill it (2.412–420; see [[prayer]]).
