---
summary: Hera and Athena drive their horses, “spurred on by the goad,” through the gates of heaven, in the verse repeated from Book 5.
---

Hera whips up the horses, and the gates of heaven that the Hours keep groan open of themselves: “Through them they drove the horses, spurred on by the goad” (8.392–396 = 5.748–752; [[hours]]). In Book 5 the goddesses drove on to the plain, to Zeus on the highest peak of Olympus and then down to the battle (5.753–777). In Book 8 Zeus sees them from Ida and sends Iris, who meets them at the outermost gates and turns them back (8.397–432; [[golden-winged]], [[many-folded]]). See [[horses]] and [[chariot-journey]].
