---
summary: Achilles is “the noble son of Peleus” whose horses Dolon asks for and remembers being promised.
grc: [Πηλεΐωνος]
---

Dolon asks Hector to swear that he will give him “the horses and the chariot inlaid with bronze that carry the noble son of Peleus” (10.322–323; [[blameless]], [[inlaid]]). Captured, he blames Hector, who promised him “the single-hoofed horses of the noble son of Peleus” (10.392; [[noble]], [[single-hoofed]]). The two verses give Achilles two epithets, ἀμύμονα and ἀγαυοῦ, both rendered “noble.” Achilles himself takes no part in the night; his horses are the prize ([[the-horses-of-achilles]], [[achilles]]). See [[dolon]].
