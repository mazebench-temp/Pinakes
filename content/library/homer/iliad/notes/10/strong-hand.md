---
summary: Menelaus takes up a spear “in his strong hand,” and Dolon reaches for Diomedes' chin with his.
---

Menelaus arms with a leopard's skin and a bronze helmet, “and took up a spear in his strong hand” (10.29–31; [[leopard-skin]]). At the end of his questioning Dolon “was about to take him by the chin with his strong hand and beg him,” when Diomedes struck him in the neck (10.454–456; [[supplication]]). The formula of the hand that grips a weapon describes here the hand of a suppliant, stopped before it touches. See [[dolon]] and [[menelaus]].
