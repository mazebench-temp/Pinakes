---
summary: Nestor does not yield to “grievous old age,” and he tells Diomedes that it stands on a razor's edge whether grim destruction comes on the Achaeans.
grc: [λυγρὸς]
---

Nestor's belt is the one he wears when he arms for war, “for he did not give way to grievous old age” (10.79; [[man-destroying]]). Answering Diomedes' protest, he gives the reason for his rounds: “For now it stands for all of us on the edge of a razor, whether grim destruction comes on the Achaeans, or life” (10.173–174; [[on-the-razors-edge]], [[this-night-will-shatter-or-save]]). λυγρὸς ὄλεθρος is the formula that closed the deaths of the Carian leader and of Axylus (2.873, 6.16).
