---
summary: Odysseus and Diomedes strip Dolon of “the back-bent bow” with his cap, wolfskin and spear.
---

“Then they took from his head the cap of ferret skin, and the wolfskin, and the back-bent bow, and the long spear” (10.458–459; [[long-spears]], [[stripping-the-dead]]). Dolon set out with “his curved bow about his shoulders” (10.333; [[curved-bow]]); stripped from him, it has the epithet of Teucer's bow and of Odysseus' great bow in the Odyssey. Odysseus, who carries a bow of his own on the raid (10.260), holds it up with the rest to Athena (10.460–464; [[of-the-spoil]]). See [[bow]] and [[dolon]].
