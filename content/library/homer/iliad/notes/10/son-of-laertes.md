---
summary: Nestor wakes Odysseus with the formal address “Son of Laertes, sprung from Zeus, Odysseus of many devices.”
---

“Son of Laertes, sprung from Zeus, Odysseus of many devices, do not be indignant; such grief has overpowered the Achaeans” (10.144–145; [[of-many-devices]], [[sprung-from-zeus]]). Achilles and Ajax used the same verse in the embassy a few hours earlier (9.308, 9.624). It is the book's only use of the patronymic. See [[odysseus]] and [[laertes]].
