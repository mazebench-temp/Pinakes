---
summary: Dolon is “rich in gold, rich in bronze.”
grc: [πολύχαλκος]
en: [rich in bronze]
---

“A man rich in gold, rich in bronze” (10.315; [[rich-in-gold]]). Of a man πολύχαλκος means wealth, as of Troy before the war (18.288–289), and not the bronze vault of the sky. Dolon offers some of that bronze as ransom (10.378–379; [[boundless-ransom]]). See [[dolon]].
