---
summary: Menelaus finds Agamemnon arming at night and addresses him as ἠθεῖε, “dear brother.”
grc: [ἠθεῖε]
en: [dear brother]
---

“Why are you arming like this, dear brother? Will you urge one of the companions to go and spy on the Trojans?” (10.37–38; [[spies-and-scouts]]). The address of a brother's affection and respect opens the brothers' night talk, and Agamemnon answers with Menelaus' title, “nurtured by Zeus” (10.43; [[zeus-nurtured]]). In the Iliad the word is nearly always spoken to a brother, as by Paris to Hector (6.518). See [[menelaus]] and [[agamemnon]].
