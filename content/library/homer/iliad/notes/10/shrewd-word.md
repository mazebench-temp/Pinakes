---
summary: Hector calls the Trojan leaders together and “laid out a shrewd plan,” in the verse used of Agamemnon's council in Book 2.
grc: ["πυκινὴν ἀρτύνετο βουλήν"]
---

“When he had called them together, he laid out a shrewd plan” (10.302; [[boule]]), as Agamemnon did when he called the elders to his ship after the Dream (2.55). Hector's plan is an offer: a chariot and the two best horses at the Achaean ships for a man who will go and spy (10.303–312; [[high-necked]], [[spies-and-scouts]]). It sends Dolon to his death. See [[hector]].
