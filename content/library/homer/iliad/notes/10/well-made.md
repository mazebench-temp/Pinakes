---
summary: The captured horses are tied at “the well-made hut of the son of Tydeus.”
---

“And when they came to the well-made hut of the son of Tydeus, they tied the horses with well-cut straps at the horses' manger” (10.566–568; [[well-cut]], [[hut]]). The raid ends where Nestor found Diomedes at its beginning, outside his hut (10.150–151). See [[diomedes]].
