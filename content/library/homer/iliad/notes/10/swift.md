---
summary: The Locrian Ajax is “swift Ajax” twice in Nestor's plans for the night, in the accusative Αἴαντα ταχύν.
grc: [Αἴαντα ταχὺν]
---

Nestor proposes to wake “the son of Tydeus, famed for his spear, and Odysseus, and swift Ajax, and the valiant son of Phyleus” (10.109–110; [[famed-for-his-spear]], [[strong-spear]]), and he sends Diomedes to “rouse swift Ajax and the son of Phyleus; for you are younger” (10.175–176). The adjective tells the son of Oileus apart from Telamonian Ajax, whom Menelaus has gone to call (10.53, 10.112; [[ajax-son-of-oileus]], [[ajax]], [[meges]]). See [[nestor]].
