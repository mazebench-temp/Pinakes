---
summary: The lion skin that Agamemnon and Diomedes wear is the hide of “a great tawny lion.”
grc: [αἴθωνος]
---

The same verse describes the lion skin twice: “a great tawny lion, reaching to his feet, and he took up his spear,” of Agamemnon as he dresses to go to Nestor and of Diomedes as he goes to rouse the others (10.24, 10.178; [[reaching-to-the-feet]], [[blood-red]], [[lion-skin]]). αἴθων is the color of a lion's hide, as in the simile of the herdsmen who cannot drive a hungry, tawny lion from a carcass (18.161–162). The two Achaeans set out later “like two lions” (10.297; [[two-lions]], [[lion]]). See [[agamemnon]] and [[diomedes]].
