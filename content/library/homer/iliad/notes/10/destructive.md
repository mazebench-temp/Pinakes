---
summary: Nestor hopes that a volunteer may catch a straggler “of the enemy,” and Dolon, running, knows his pursuers for “enemy men.”
grc: [δηΐων, ἄνδρας δηΐους]
en: [enemy]
---

Nestor asks for a man who will go among the Trojans, “in the hope that he might catch some straggler of the enemy, or perhaps hear some talk among the Trojans” (10.206–207; [[spies-and-scouts]]): δήϊοι, the plural used as a noun, are the enemy. Later in the night Dolon stops at the sound behind him, thinking his own companions are coming, “but when they were a spear's throw away, or even less, he knew them for enemy men” (10.357–358; [[nimble]]). See [[dolon]].
