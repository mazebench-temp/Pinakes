---
summary: The point of Diomedes' “polished spear” sticks in the ground over Dolon's shoulder, and at the end of the night the two men bathe in “polished bathtubs.”
grc: [ἐΰξου, ἐϋξέστας]
---

“Over his right shoulder the point of the polished spear stuck fast in the ground” (10.373–374; [[steep]], [[pale-fear]]): Diomedes misses on purpose. At the end of the night, washed in the sea, the two men “stepped into polished bathtubs and bathed” (10.576; [[bathing]]). The spear is ἐΰξοος, “well-planed,” of wood; the tubs are ἐΰξεστος, “well-smoothed,” in a verse found twice in the Odyssey's scenes of welcome (Od. 4.48, 17.87). See [[diomedes]] and [[odysseus]].
