---
summary: “Powerful Agamemnon” answers Menelaus that the two of them need shrewd counsel, since the mind of Zeus has turned.
---

“Answering him, powerful Agamemnon said: You and I have need, Menelaus, nurtured by Zeus, of counsel, shrewd counsel, that will protect and save the Argives and the ships, since the mind of Zeus has turned” (10.42–45; [[zeus-nurtured]], [[kerdos]], [[boule]]). The title of rule introduces a confession of need: the king who commands the army must look for a plan, and goes to Nestor for one (10.17–20). See [[agamemnon]].
