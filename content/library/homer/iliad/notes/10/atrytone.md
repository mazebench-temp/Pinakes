---
summary: Diomedes calls Athena “child of Zeus, Atrytone” as he begins his prayer for her help on the raid.
---

“Now hear me too, child of Zeus, Atrytone: go with me, as when you went with my father, brilliant Tydeus, to Thebes” (10.284–286; [[tydeus-at-thebes]], [[prayer]]). The usual verse is κλῦθί μευ αἰγιόχοιο Διὸς τέκος Ἀτρυτώνη, with which Diomedes prayed to her in Book 5 (5.115); here he prays second, after Odysseus, and the opening κέκλυθι νῦν καὶ ἐμεῖο, “now hear me too,” leaves room only for Διὸς τέκος ([[child-of-aegis-bearing-zeus]]). The prayer ends with the vow of a heifer, and Pallas Athena hears it (10.292–295; [[yearling]], [[pallas]]). See [[athena]] and [[diomedes]].
