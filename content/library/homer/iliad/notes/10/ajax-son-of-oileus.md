summary: “Swift Ajax” is one of the men Nestor would wake, and Diomedes rouses him with Meges (10.110, 10.175–179); he is one of “the two Ajaxes” who volunteer for the raid (10.228).
grc: ["Αἴαντα ταχὺν"]
refs: [10.179, 10.228]
---

Αἴαντα ταχύν, “swift Ajax”, is the son of Oileus, whose mark is speed (2.527; 14.520–522; see [[swift]]). Nestor names him with Meges, “the valiant son of Phyleus”, among the men to be woken (10.110), and sends Diomedes to rouse the two, “for you are younger” (10.175–176). “The hero went his way, and roused them from there and brought them” (10.179; see [[meges]] and [[diomedes]]). The son of Telamon is the “godlike Ajax” whose ships lie farthest off (10.112; see [[ajax]]). When Diomedes asks for a companion, “the two Ajaxes were willing, attendants of Ares” (10.228), as the pair were named when they fled before Zeus' thunder (8.79; see [[attendants-of-ares]]).
