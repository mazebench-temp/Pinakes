---
summary: “The splendid son of Nestor” goes with Meriones and the kings across the ditch to the council.
---

“And with them went Meriones and the splendid son of Nestor, for the kings themselves had called them to take counsel with them” (10.196–197). The son is Thrasymedes, who commands the sentries with Meriones (10.57–59) and later arms Diomedes for the raid (10.255–258; [[thrasymedes]], [[steadfast-in-battle]]). ἀγλαὸς υἱός is the formula's usual shape for the son of a great father. See [[nestor]].
