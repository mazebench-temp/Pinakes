“Where did you leave Hector, shepherd of the people, when you came here? Where does his armor of war lie, and where are his horses?” (10.406–407; [[shepherd-of-the-people]]). Odysseus asks where a raider might find the enemy's commander and his gear. Dolon answers with Hector's council and the allies' camp, and offers the Thracians instead (10.414–441; [[newly-come]]). See [[hector]] and [[dolon]].
