summary: Idomeneus is one of the chiefs whom Agamemnon sends Menelaus to call, and whose ships, Nestor says, lie farthest off (10.53, 10.112–113); his squire Meriones commands the sentries with Nestor's son (10.58–59).
refs: [10.113]
---

Agamemnon sends Menelaus to “call Ajax and Idomeneus” (10.53), and Nestor, who does not know it, wishes that someone would fetch “godlike Ajax and lord Idomeneus; for their ships are farthest off” (10.112–113; see [[ajax]] and [[menelaus]]). Ajax's huts are at one end of the line of ships (8.224–226). Idomeneus is not named again in the book, but his squire (ὀπάων) Meriones is one of the two captains of the sentries, goes to the kings' council and volunteers for the raid (10.58–59, 10.196, 10.229; see [[meriones]] and [[night-watch]]). ὀπάων Ἰδομενῆος, “Idomeneus' squire”, is Meriones' title in the lists of the leaders in Books 7 and 8 (7.165, 8.263).
