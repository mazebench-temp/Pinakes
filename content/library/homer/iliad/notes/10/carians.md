---
summary: Dolon places the Carians “toward the sea”, first of the allies on that side, beside the Paeonians, Leleges, Caucones and Pelasgians (10.428).
grc: [Κᾶρες]
---

Κᾶρες is the plural of Κάρ, “a Carian”, whose feminine is Κάειρα (4.142). In Dolon's account of the allies' camp the Carians lie by the sea (10.428–429; see [[trojan-allies]]). Strabo cites the verse to show that Homer kept the Carians and the Leleges apart, though some made them one people (13.1.58; see [[leleges]]).
