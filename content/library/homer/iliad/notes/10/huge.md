---
summary: Dolon tells of the golden armor of Rhesus, “huge, a wonder to see.”
grc: [πελώρια]
---

“And armor of gold, huge, a wonder to see, he brought with him; it is not fit at all for mortal men to wear, but for the immortal gods” (10.439–441; [[mortal-men]], [[immortals]], [[gold-and-silver]]). The word of uncanny size, given to Ajax and to Ares, is given to the armor of a king who will die asleep beside it. The armor is not taken: Diomedes considers carrying off the chariot “where the inlaid armor lay,” and Athena sends him back to the ships (10.503–511; [[inlaid]]). See [[rhesus]].
