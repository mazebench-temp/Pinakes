---
summary: Autolycus broke into the “well-built house” of Amyntor to steal the boar's-tusk helmet.
grc: ["πυκινὸν δόμον"]
---

“It was taken once from Eleon, from Amyntor, son of Ormenus, by Autolycus, who broke into his well-built house” (10.266–267; [[autolycus]], [[amyntor]], [[the-boars-tusk-helmet]]). The Greek is πυκινὸν δόμον, “close-built,” a house strongly joined and hard to break into, and the thief's skill is measured by it. The translation renders πυκινός “well-built” here, as it renders [[eupektos]]; of words and plans the same adjective is “shrewd” ([[shrewd-word]]). See [[eleon]].
