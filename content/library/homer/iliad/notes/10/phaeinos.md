---
summary: φαεινός describes Nestor's helmet, the rug under Diomedes' head, and the whip Odysseus forgets to take from Rhesus' chariot.
grc: [φαεινή, φαεινός]
---

Beside Nestor's bed lie his shield, two spears “and a shining helmet” (10.76; [[helmet]]). Diomedes sleeps on an oxhide, “and under his head a shining rug was stretched” (10.156; [[of-the-fields]]). Among the Thracians Odysseus drives the horses out with his bow, “since he had not thought of the shining whip, to take it in his hands from the inlaid chariot” (10.500–501; [[inlaid]]); yet on the way back Diomedes “whipped the horses” (10.530), though no whip has been taken. See [[nestor]], [[diomedes]] and [[odysseus]].
