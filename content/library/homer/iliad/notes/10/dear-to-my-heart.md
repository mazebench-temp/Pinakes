---
summary: Agamemnon addresses Diomedes as “dear to my heart” and tells him to choose the best companion, not the most kingly.
---

“Diomedes, son of Tydeus, dear to my heart, you shall choose the companion you wish, the best of those who offer, since many are eager” (10.234–236). The verse is the one with which Sthenelus and then Athena addressed Diomedes in Book 5 (5.243, 5.826). Agamemnon's affection has a purpose. He tells Diomedes not to leave the better man behind out of respect, looking to lineage, and the narrator adds that he feared for Menelaus (10.237–240; [[aidos]], [[fair-haired]]). Diomedes chooses Odysseus (10.242–247). See [[diomedes]] and [[agamemnon]].
