---
summary: Dolon asks to be left behind “with a pitiless bond” until his captors have tested his story.
grc: [νηλέϊ δεσμῷ]
---

“But as for me, take me now to the swift-sailing ships, or bind me with a pitiless bond and leave me here, until you two have gone and put me to the test, whether I have spoken to you fairly, or not” (10.442–445; [[swift-sailing-ships]], [[aisa]]). The phrase νηλέϊ δεσμῷ is shaped like the formula of the weapon, νηλέϊ χαλκῷ, “with the pitiless bronze” ([[pitiless-bronze]]): the captive asks for the bond, and Diomedes answers with the sword (10.446–457). See [[dolon]].
