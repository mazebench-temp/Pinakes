---
summary: The horses of Rhesus are tied at Diomedes' manger “with well-cut straps.”
grc: [ἐϋτμήτοισιν]
---

“They tied the horses with well-cut straps at the horses' manger, where the horses of Diomedes stood, swift-footed, eating honey-sweet wheat” (10.567–569; [[well-made]], [[honey-sweet]], [[swift-footed]]). The horses had been tied with thongs to the rail of Rhesus' chariot (10.475), and Odysseus tied them together with thongs to drive them out (10.499). See [[horses]] and [[diomedes]].
