---
summary: Agamemnon and Nestor dress in the same verse, binding their sandals under their shining feet.
---

“And under his shining feet he bound his fine sandals” (10.22, 10.132; [[dressing-scene]], [[sandals]]). The verse that dressed Agamemnon on the morning of the Dream (2.44) dresses him at night, and then Nestor. After the tunic and the sandals each takes what fits him: Agamemnon a lion's skin and a spear, Nestor a crimson cloak and a spear (10.23–24, 10.133–135; [[blood-red]], [[crimson]]). See [[agamemnon]] and [[nestor]].
