---
summary: ἄλκιμος describes Meges, “the valiant son of Phyleus,” and the strong spear that Nestor takes up.
---

Nestor proposes to wake “swift Ajax, and the valiant son of Phyleus” (10.110; [[swift]], [[meges]], [[phyleus]]). As he dresses he takes “a strong spear, tipped with sharp bronze” (10.135; [[sharp]]), in the verse that ends an arming elsewhere in both poems (14.12, 15.482; Od. 1.99). The old man takes a warrior's spear before going to wake the others. See [[nestor]].
