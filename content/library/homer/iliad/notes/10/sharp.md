“And he took a strong spear, tipped with sharp bronze” (10.135; [[strong-spear]]). The verse εἵλετο δʼ ἄλκιμον ἔγχος ἀκαχμένον ὀξέϊ χαλκῷ, in which ἀκαχμένος, “sharpened,” is from the root ἀκ-, “sharp, point” ([[two-edged]]), recurs in the Iliad and the Odyssey (14.12, 15.482; Od. 1.99, 15.551, 20.127). Dolon arms himself with “a sharp javelin” (10.335; [[curved-bow]]), which is “the long spear” when it is stripped from him (10.459; [[long-spears]]). See [[nestor]], [[dolon]] and [[spear]].
