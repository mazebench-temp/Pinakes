---
summary: Dolon is “rich in gold, rich in bronze.”
grc: [πολύχρυσος]
---

“Now there was among the Trojans one Dolon, son of Eumedes the divine herald, a man rich in gold, rich in bronze” (10.314–315; [[brazen]], [[divine]]). The pair of epithets describes Troy before the war, which all men once called rich in gold and bronze (18.288–289). The rich man offers his father's treasure as ransom (10.378–381; [[boundless-ransom]], [[worked-with-much-toil]]); his wealth does not save him. See [[dolon]].
