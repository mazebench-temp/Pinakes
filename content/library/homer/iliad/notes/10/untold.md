---
summary: “A clamor and an untold tumult” rise among the Trojans when Hippocoon wakes and cries out.
---

“And a clamor and an untold tumult rose among the Trojans as they came rushing together; and they gazed at the grim deeds the men had done” (10.523–524; [[grim-deeds]], [[tumult]]). κυδοιμός here is the common noun; in Book 5 Tumult is a god (5.593). The uproar comes too late, for the two men are already riding back to the ships (10.525–531). See [[trojans]].
