summary: ἀγαυός describes Achilles in Dolon's complaint and the Trojans in Odysseus' account of Dolon's mission.
---

Dolon says that Hector promised him “the single-hoofed horses of the noble son of Peleus” (10.392; [[son-of-peleus]], [[single-hoofed]]). At the end of the night Odysseus tells Nestor that the thirteenth man they killed was a spy, “one sent out to be a scout upon our army by Hector and the other noble Trojans” (10.562–563; [[spies-and-scouts]]). The word of praise is given to the enemy who sent Dolon out. See [[dolon]] and [[trojans]].
