---
summary: Hector swears to Dolon by “the loud-thundering husband of Hera,” an oath that will prove false.
---

“Now let Zeus himself be witness, the loud-thundering husband of Hera, that no other man shall ride behind those horses, no other Trojan, but I say that you shall glory in them forever” (10.329–331; [[husband-of-hera]], [[oath]]). The verse-end is the one with which Agamemnon closed his oath at the truce for the dead (7.411). The narrator comments at once that Hector “swore an oath that would prove false” (10.332): Dolon never comes back to claim the horses ([[dolon]], [[hector]]). See [[zeus]].
