---
summary: Menelaus fears for the Argives who came to Troy “bent on bold war” for his sake.
---

“For fear that they might suffer harm, the Argives, who for his sake had come over the wide waters to Troy, bent on bold war” (10.26–28; [[watery-ways]], [[bold]]). The verse-end names the war that was waged for Helen. Menelaus lies awake for the men who came to fight his war, and his brother, a few lines later, fears for him (10.240; [[fair-haired]]). See [[menelaus]].
