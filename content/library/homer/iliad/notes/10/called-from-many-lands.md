---
summary: Dolon tells Odysseus that the allies, “called from many lands,” sleep and leave the watch to the Trojans, since their wives and children are far away.
---

“But the allies, called from many lands, are asleep; for they leave it to the Trojans to keep watch, since their children are not near them, nor their wives” (10.420–422; [[trojan-allies]], [[night-watch]]). The epithet that names the allies by their distant homes gives the reason they do not keep watch. The newest of them, the Thracians, lie farthest out and asleep (10.434; [[newly-come]]). See [[dolon]].
