**The best plan.** The verse that tells what Agamemnon decides in his sleepless night, “And this plan seemed to him in his heart the best” (10.17), is the verse that told how Zeus, sleepless in Book 2, decided to send the Dream (2.5), and how Hera decides to beguile Zeus on Ida (14.161). The plan that seems best to the king is to go to the man whose counsel “had shown itself best before” (7.325, 9.94; see [[nestor]] and [[metis]]).

**Two councils.** Book 9 was a night of counsel in Agamemnon's hut (see the note on Book 9). Book 10 moves the council outside. The kings “who had been called to the council” (ὅσοι κεκλήατο βουλήν, 10.195) cross the ditch with Meriones and Nestor's son and sit in the open, on ground clear of corpses (10.194–202; see [[the-achaean-wall]]). At the same hour Hector calls together the leaders and rulers of the Trojans “and laid out a shrewd plan” (πυκινὴν ἀρτύνετο βουλήν, 10.302), the verse with which Agamemnon opened his council of elders in Book 2 (2.55). Dolon later tells where it was held: “Hector, with all those who are counselors, is holding council by the mound of divine Ilus, away from the din” (βουληφόροι, βουλὰς βουλεύει, 10.414–416; see [[the-tomb-of-ilus]]). Even the Thracians have their counselor, Hippocoon, whom Apollo wakes (βουληφόρον, 10.518; see [[hippocoon]] and [[man-of-counsel]]).

**Whether to flee or to fight.** Each side imagines the other's council. Nestor wants to wake one more chief, “one whom it befits to take counsel, whether to flee or to fight” (βουλὰς βουλεύειν, ἢ φευγέμεν ἠὲ μάχεσθαι, 10.146–147), and Dolon promises Hector to go as far as Agamemnon's ship, “where I suppose the chiefs are taking counsel, whether to flee or to fight” (10.326–327), in the same verse. Hector wants to know whether the Achaeans, beaten, “are planning flight among themselves” (φύξιν βουλεύουσι, 10.311), and Dolon repeats the line to his captors (10.398). The question is a fair one: twice before, Agamemnon has proposed flight to the assembled army (2.139–141, 9.26–28). This time the Achaean council plans a raid.
