---
summary: δῖος honors Nestor, Odysseus, Diomedes and his father Tydeus, Athena as “brilliant goddess,” and the Pelasgians in Dolon's list of the allies.
grc: ["=δῖοί"]
---

**Odysseus and Diomedes.** “Much-enduring brilliant Odysseus” answers Diomedes' praise (10.248; [[much-enduring]]), and “brilliant Odysseus” holds up Dolon's spoils to Athena (10.460; [[of-the-spoil]]). Diomedes is “brilliant Diomedes” when Odysseus whistles to him from the Thracian camp and when Athena comes to warn him (10.502, 10.508).

**Nestor and Tydeus.** Agamemnon goes “to brilliant Nestor” to rouse him (10.54). In his prayer Diomedes calls his father “brilliant Tydeus” and Athena, who stood by Tydeus at Thebes, “brilliant goddess” (δῖα θεά, 10.285, 10.290; [[tydeus-at-thebes]]).

**The Pelasgians.** In Dolon's account of the allies' camp the Pelasgians are δῖοι, “the brilliant Pelasgians” (10.429; [[pelasgians]], [[with-curved-bows]]). See [[odysseus]] and [[diomedes]].
