---
summary: The kings cross “the ditch they had dug” to hold their council in the clear space beyond it.
---

“And crossing over the ditch they had dug, they sat down in a clear space, where the ground showed free of corpses” (10.198–199; [[obrimos]]). The council of the night is held outside the defenses, on the ground where Hector turned back at nightfall; why the leaders cross the ditch to sit there is not said. At the end of the raid Odysseus drives the captured horses back across the ditch (10.564). See [[the-achaean-wall]].
