---
summary: Diomedes threatens Dolon with “sheer destruction.”
---

“Stand, or I will catch you with my spear; and I tell you you will not long escape sheer destruction at my hand” (10.370–371; [[krateros]]). He throws, and misses on purpose: the point sticks in the ground over Dolon's right shoulder, and Dolon stops, pale with fear (10.372–376; [[polished]], [[pale-fear]]). See [[diomedes]] and [[dolon]].
