---
summary: Dolon places the Phrygians “who fight from horses” toward Thymbre, beside the Lycians, Mysians and Maeonians (10.431).
grc: [Φρύγες]
---

Φρύγες ἱππόμαχοι, “the Phrygians who fight from horses” (10.431; see [[trojan-allies]]). ἱππόμαχος occurs only here in Homer (see [[horse-fighting]]); a Trojan of Book 12 bears it as a name, Hippomachus (12.189). The Phrygians are horsemen in Priam's memory too, the Phrygian men “with their quick horses” (3.185; see [[with-quick-horses]]).
