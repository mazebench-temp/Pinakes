---
summary: Hector holds council with “those who are counselors,” and Hippocoon, whom Apollo wakes, is “a counselor of the Thracians.”
grc: [βουληφόροι]
en: [counselors]
---

Dolon tells Odysseus that “Hector, with all those who are counselors, is holding council by the mound of divine Ilus” (10.414–415; [[boule]], [[divine]]): the Trojan leaders in council are the βουληφόροι. When Apollo comes down into the Trojan throng he wakes “Hippocoon, a counselor of the Thracians, the brave cousin of Rhesus” (10.518–519; [[brave]], [[hippocoon]]). The counselor wakes too late to save his king. See [[hector]] and [[thracians]].
