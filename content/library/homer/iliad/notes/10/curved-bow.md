---
summary: Dolon puts his curved bow about his shoulders as he arms to go out as a spy.
---

“At once he put his curved bow about his shoulders, and put on over it the skin of a gray wolf, and on his head a cap of ferret skin, and he took a sharp javelin” (10.333–335; [[gray-sea]], [[sharp]], [[wolf-skin]]). Like Paris in Book 3, Dolon goes out with a bow (3.17); when he is stripped it is “the back-bent bow” (10.459; [[back-bent]]). See [[dolon]] and [[bow]].
