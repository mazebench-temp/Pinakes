---
summary: Nestor wishes that someone would call “godlike Ajax” and lord Idomeneus, whose ships lie farthest off.
---

“And I wish someone would go and call these too, godlike Ajax and lord Idomeneus; for their ships are farthest off, and not close at all” (10.111–113). The godlike Ajax is the son of Telamon, whose ships, with Achilles', were drawn up at the ends of the line in Book 8 (8.222–226; [[ajax]]). Agamemnon has already sent Menelaus to call him and Idomeneus (10.53–54), and he tells Nestor so (10.124–125; [[idomeneus]], [[menelaus]]). See [[nestor]].
