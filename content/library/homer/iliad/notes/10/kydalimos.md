---
summary: Agamemnon's “glorious heart” groans as he looks from the Trojan fires to his own army.
grc: [κυδάλιμον]
---

“But whenever he looked at the ships and the army of the Achaeans, he tore out many hairs from his head by the roots to Zeus on high, and his glorious heart groaned greatly” (10.14–16). The epithet of glory, which belongs above all to Menelaus (Μενελάου κυδαλίμοιο), describes the heart of his brother at the moment of his despair ([[menelaus]], [[thymos]]). See [[agamemnon]].
