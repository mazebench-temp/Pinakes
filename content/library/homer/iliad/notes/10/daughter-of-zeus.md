---
summary: Odysseus and Diomedes have prayed “to the daughter of great Zeus” as they set out like two lions through the night.
---

“And when they had prayed to the daughter of great Zeus, they went like two lions through the black night” (10.296–297; [[two-lions]], [[black-night]]). The half-verse Διὸς κούρῃ μεγάλοιο follows the two prayers and Athena's hearing of them (10.277–295; [[prayer]]). It is the half-verse that closed the Trojan women's prayer in Book 6, which Athena refused (6.311–312; [[the-robe-for-athena]]). See [[athena]].
