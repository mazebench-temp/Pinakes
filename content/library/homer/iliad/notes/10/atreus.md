---
summary: Atreus is named only in his sons' patronymic: four times of Agamemnon, who names himself “Agamemnon, son of Atreus” when Nestor challenges him in the dark, and once of Menelaus, when he volunteers for the raid (10.3, 10.81, 10.88, 10.103, 10.230).
---

“The son of Atreus, Agamemnon, shepherd of the people” is the one man whom sleep does not hold (10.3). When Nestor raises himself in his bed and asks who is coming through the camp, Agamemnon answers with his father's name and his own: “you will know Agamemnon, son of Atreus” (10.88; see [[agamemnon]]). Nestor replies with the address of the night before, “Most glorious son of Atreus, Agamemnon, lord of men” (10.103; 9.96, 9.163; see [[son-of-atreus]] and [[lord-of-men]]). At 10.230 the son of Atreus is Menelaus: “the son of Atreus was willing, Menelaus famed for his spear”. His brother's next words make sure that he is not chosen (10.233–240; see [[menelaus]]).
