---
summary: Ormenus is named in his son's patronymic, Ἀμύντορος Ὀρμενίδαο, “Amyntor, son of Ormenus”, from whose house at Eleon Autolycus stole the boar's-tusk helmet (10.266).
---

The same two words end the verse in which Phoenix names the father he fled, “my father Amyntor, son of Ormenus” (9.448; 10.266). The repeated patronymic is the strongest reason for taking the two Amyntors as one man, as this index does, though the poem does not say so and places them differently, in Hellas and at Eleon (see [[amyntor]], [[eleon]] and [[hellas-or-ormenium]]).
