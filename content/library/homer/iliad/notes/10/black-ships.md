summary: Agamemnon finds Nestor beside his hut and his black ship; in four other verses of the book μέλας describes a ewe and the night, and those belong elsewhere.
except: [10.215, 10.297, 10.394, 10.468]
---

“And he found him beside his hut and his black ship, in a soft bed” (10.74–75; [[soft]]). Each chief sleeps by his ship, as Achilles sat “beside his hut and his black ship” in Book 1 (1.329). Elsewhere in the book μέλαινα describes the black ewe that every chief will give to the volunteer (10.215; [[sheep]]) and the black night (10.297, 10.394, 10.468; [[black-night]]). See [[ships]] and [[nestor]].
