summary: Dolon places the Maeonians “who fight from chariots” toward Thymbre, last of the allies on that side (10.431).
---

Μῄονες ἱπποκορυσταί, “the Maeonians who fight from chariots” (10.431; see [[trojan-allies]]). The epithet is given to the Paeonians in later books (16.287, 21.205) and to “men” in the verse that opens Book 2 (2.1; see [[chariot-fighters]]). Dolon's verse sets the horse-fighting Phrygians beside the chariot-fighting Maeonians.
