---
summary: The Achaeans are “bronze-armored” as Nestor goes along their ships, in Diomedes' story of Tydeus at Thebes, and in Athena's care that no other Achaean should strike Dolon first.
grc: [χαλκοχίτωνας]
---

Nestor dresses and “went his way along the ships of the bronze-armored Achaeans” (10.136). Diomedes tells Athena that Tydeus “left the bronze-armored Achaeans by the Asopus” when he went into Thebes as a messenger (10.287; [[tydeus-at-thebes]], [[asopus]]). And when Dolon is about to run into the sentries, Athena puts might into Diomedes, “so that none of the bronze-armored Achaeans might boast that he struck him first, and he himself come second” (10.366–368; [[menos]], [[kydos]]).
