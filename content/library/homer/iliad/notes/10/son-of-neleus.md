---
summary: Nestor is “son of Neleus” as Agamemnon resolves to go to him, and twice in the address “Nestor, son of Neleus, great glory of the Achaeans.”
grc: [Νηλήϊον]
---

Agamemnon decides that the best plan is “to go first of all men to Nestor, son of Neleus” (10.18), where the adjective Νηλήϊος, “of Neleus,” serves as the patronymic. The verse of address ὦ Νέστορ Νηληϊάδη, μέγα κῦδος Ἀχαιῶν is spoken to him twice: by Agamemnon when he wakes him (10.87) and by Odysseus when he answers Nestor's question about the horses (10.555; [[great-glory-of-the-achaeans]]).
