---
summary: Dolon places the “brilliant Pelasgians” by the sea, last of the allies on that side, after the Carians, Paeonians, Leleges and Caucones (10.429).
---

δῖοί τε Πελασγοί, “and the brilliant Pelasgians”, ends the verse (10.429; see [[brilliant]] and [[trojan-allies]]). The same half-verse ends the Odyssey's list of the peoples of Crete (*Odyssey* 19.177). In the Catalogue the Pelasgians of Larisa are “fighters with the spear” (2.840–841; see [[fighters-with-the-spear]] and [[larisa]]). Here they lie beside the Leleges and the Caucones, two peoples whom later Greeks, like the Pelasgians, counted among the old wandering peoples of the Aegean (Strabo 12.8.4; see [[leleges]] and [[caucones]]).
