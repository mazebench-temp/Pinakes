---
summary: Odysseus asks Dolon whether the allies sleep mingled with “the horse-taming Trojans” or apart.
---

“How is it, then: are they mingled with the horse-taming Trojans as they sleep, or apart? Tell me plainly, so that I may know” (10.424–425). The question about the allies' camp leads Dolon to the Thracians, newly come, and to the finest horses he has ever seen (10.433–441; [[newly-come]], [[the-horses-of-rhesus]]). In a book about the taking of horses, the Trojans' standing epithet is heard once. See [[trojans]] and [[trojan-allies]].
