---
summary: Nestor's belt is the one he puts on whenever he arms “for man-destroying war.”
---

“And beside them lay the gleaming belt with which the old man girded himself whenever he armed for man-destroying war, leading his people, for he did not give way to grievous old age” (10.77–79; [[panaiolos]], [[grim]], [[belt]]). The epithet of war comes in the description of an old man's gear, kept beside his bed and ready. See [[nestor]].
