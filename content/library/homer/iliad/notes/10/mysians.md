---
summary: Dolon places the “proud” Mysians toward Thymbre, beside the Lycians, Phrygians and Maeonians (10.430).
grc: [Μυσοί]
---

Μυσοί τʼ ἀγέρωχοι, “and the proud Mysians” (10.430; see [[trojan-allies]]). Elsewhere the Trojans (3.36, 7.343) and the Rhodians (2.654) are ἀγέρωχοι, “lordly, proud” (see [[proud]]). Their camp lies toward Thymbre (see [[thymbre]]).
