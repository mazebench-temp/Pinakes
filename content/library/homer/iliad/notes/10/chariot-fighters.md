---
summary: In Dolon's account of the allies the Maeonians are men “who fight from chariots,” beside the Phrygians “who fight from horses.”
---

“And the Phrygians who fight from horses and the Maeonians who fight from chariots” (10.431; [[horse-fighting]], [[maeonians]], [[phrygians]]). The verse ends Dolon's list of the allies' places in the camp with two compounds of ἵππος. In Book 2 the same word described all the men asleep on the night of the Dream (2.1); here it is the epithet of one people. See [[trojan-allies]].
