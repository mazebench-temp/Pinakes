# Νηλήϊον is also Neleian Pylos, the city of Neleus, at 11.682 and Od. 4.639.
grc: [Νηλήϊον]
relations:
  - child: nestor (10.18, 10.87)
---

Νέστορʼ ἔπι πρῶτον Νηλήϊον, “first of all men to Nestor, son of Neleus” (10.18): Νηλήϊον is the accusative of Νηλήϊος, “of Neleus”, the adjective that serves as a patronymic, as in the Dream's disguise, Νηληΐῳ υἷι, “the son of Neleus” (2.20; see [[son-of-neleus]]). The vocative of the patronymic in -ιάδης opens the address “Nestor, son of Neleus, great glory of the Achaeans”, with which Agamemnon answers Nestor's challenge in the dark and Odysseus answers his question when the spies come back (ὦ Νέστορ Νηληϊάδη, 10.87, 10.555; see [[great-glory-of-the-achaeans]] and [[nestor]]).
