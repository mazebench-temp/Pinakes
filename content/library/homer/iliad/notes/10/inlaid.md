---
summary: ποικίλος describes Nestor's armor and Odysseus' shield, the chariot of Achilles that Dolon wants, the chariot and armor of Rhesus, and Menelaus' dappled leopard skin.
grc: [ποικίλῃ, ποικίλου]
en: [dappled]
---

**The Achaeans' gear.** Beside Nestor's bed “lay his inlaid armor” (10.75), and Odysseus puts “his inlaid shield” about his shoulders (10.149). Menelaus' leopard skin is ποικίλη, “dappled” (10.29–30; [[leopard-skin]]): of a spotted hide the word keeps its first sense, “many-colored.”

**The chariots.** Dolon asks for the horses “and the chariot inlaid with bronze” of Achilles, and complains that Hector promised them (ἅρματα ποικίλα χαλκῷ, 10.322, 10.393; [[the-horses-of-achilles]]). Among the Thracians Odysseus forgets to take the shining whip “from the inlaid chariot” (10.500–501; [[phaeinos]]), and Diomedes wonders whether to drag away the chariot “where the inlaid armor lay” (10.504; [[pondering-two-ways]]). See [[chariot]].
