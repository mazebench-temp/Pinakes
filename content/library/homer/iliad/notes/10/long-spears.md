---
summary: Dolon's spear, stripped from him with his cap, wolfskin and bow, is “the long spear.”
---

“And the wolfskin, and the back-bent bow, and the long spear” (10.459; [[back-bent]]). Dolon set out with “a sharp javelin” (ἄκοντα, 10.335; [[sharp]]); stripped from him, it is δόρυ μακρόν. Odysseus holds it up with the rest to Athena (10.460–461; [[of-the-spoil]]). See [[dolon]] and [[spear]].
