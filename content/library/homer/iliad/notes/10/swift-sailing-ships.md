---
summary: Hector asks for a spy “to go close to the swift-sailing ships,” Dolon volunteers in the same words, and Dolon, captured, asks to be taken to the swift-sailing ships.
grc: [ὠκυπόρων]
---

Hector's offer is for a man “to go close to the swift-sailing ships and find out” whether they are guarded (10.308), and Dolon volunteers in the same words (10.320; [[spies-and-scouts]]). Captured, he asks to be taken “to the swift-sailing ships,” or left bound until his captors return (10.442–445; [[pitiless]]). Only his spoils reach the ships (10.570; [[bloody-spoils]]). See [[dolon]].
