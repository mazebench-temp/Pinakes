---
summary: Agamemnon calls the sentries “the sacred company of the sentries.”
---

Agamemnon will go to Nestor and urge him “to go to the sacred company of the sentries and give them orders” (10.55–56; [[night-watch]]). φυλάκων ἱερὸν τέλος is unusual: ἱερός of a body of men is taken as “sacred,” the watch being under the gods' care, or as “strong,” as in the Odyssey's ἱερὸς στρατὸς αἰχμητάων, “the sacred host of spearmen” (Od. 24.81). τέλος is a company, as in “the company of the Thracian men” (10.470). See [[nestor]].
