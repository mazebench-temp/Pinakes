---
summary: The spoils of Dolon are “bloody spoils” when Diomedes takes them up on the way back and when Odysseus lays them in the stern of the ship for Athena.
---

At the tamarisk where they had hung them, “the son of Tydeus leapt to the ground and put the bloody spoils into Odysseus' hands” (10.528–529; [[swift-night]]); at the ships, “in the stern of the ship the bloody spoils of Dolon Odysseus set, until they could make ready an offering to Athena” (10.570–571; [[of-the-spoil]], [[dedication-of-spoils]]). The formula of the arms taken in battle is given to the cap, wolfskin, bow and spear of a man killed as he begged for his life (10.454–459; [[supplication]], [[stripping-the-dead]]). See [[dolon]].
