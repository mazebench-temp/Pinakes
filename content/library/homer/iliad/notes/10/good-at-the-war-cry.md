---
summary: The formula introduces Menelaus twice in his night talk with Agamemnon, and Diomedes three times: as he volunteers, as he chooses Odysseus, and as he prays to Athena.
---

**Menelaus.** Finding his brother arming by his ship, “Menelaus, good at the war cry, spoke to him first” and asked whether he meant to send a spy (10.36–41); the formula introduces him again as he asks for his orders (10.60–63). **Diomedes.** After Nestor's call and the silence that follows, “Diomedes, good at the war cry” offers to go (10.218–226; [[silence]]); “again Diomedes, good at the war cry, spoke among them” and chose Odysseus (10.241–247); and “Diomedes, good at the war cry, prayed second” (10.283; [[prayer]]). On the raid itself there is no war cry: among the sleeping Thracians Odysseus signals to Diomedes with a whistle (10.502). See [[menelaus]] and [[diomedes]].
