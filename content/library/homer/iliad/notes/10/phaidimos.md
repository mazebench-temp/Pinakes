---
summary: Agamemnon tells Nestor that his “shining limbs” tremble beneath him.
---

“And my heart leaps out of my breast, and my shining limbs tremble beneath me” (10.94–95; [[kradie]], [[deos]]). The epithet that praises a hero's body, and Hector himself (φαίδιμος Ἕκτωρ), describes the limbs of a king shaking with fear. Dolon's limbs tremble too when Odysseus questions him, without the epithet (10.390). See [[agamemnon]].
