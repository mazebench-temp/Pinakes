---
summary: Dolon places the Lycians toward Thymbre, first of the allies on that side, with the Mysians, Phrygians and Maeonians (10.430).
---

πρὸς Θύμβρης δʼ ἔλαχον Λύκιοι, “and toward Thymbre the lot fell to the Lycians” (10.430; see [[thymbre]] and [[trojan-allies]]): the places in the allies' camp were drawn by lot (ἔλαχον; see [[lots]]). Strabo used the verse to place Homer's Ilium (13.1.35). In the *Rhesus* the Trojan watches are also kept by lot, and the Lycians are to take the fifth (*Rhesus* 543–545, 562–564).
