---
summary: κρατερὸς Διομήδης, “mighty Diomedes,” three times on the night of the raid, and the “strong might” Odysseus tells him to bring out among the Thracians.
---

**Mighty Diomedes.** The formula introduces his threat to Dolon, “Stand, or I will catch you with my spear” (10.369–371; [[steep]]), and his refusal of Dolon's plea, “looking at him darkly” (10.446; [[looking-darkly]], [[supplication]]); at the return Nestor hopes that it is Odysseus and mighty Diomedes whose horses he hears (10.536–537; [[single-hoofed]]).

**Strong might.** Among the sleeping Thracians Odysseus tells him, “But come now, bring out your strong might” (10.479), and Athena breathes might into him (10.482; [[menos]], [[gray-eyed]]). See [[diomedes]].
