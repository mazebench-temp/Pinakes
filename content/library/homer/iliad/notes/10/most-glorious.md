---
summary: Nestor answers Agamemnon with the full verse of address, “Most glorious son of Atreus, Agamemnon, lord of men.”
---

“Most glorious son of Atreus, Agamemnon, lord of men, surely Zeus the counselor will not fulfill for Hector all the plans that he now hopes for” (10.103–105; [[counselor]], [[lord-of-men]]). The formal address answers a king who has come to Nestor's bed at night, groaning and afraid, to ask for a plan (10.87–101). Four speakers began with the same verse in Book 9 (9.96, 9.163, 9.677, 9.697). See [[agamemnon]] and [[nestor]].
