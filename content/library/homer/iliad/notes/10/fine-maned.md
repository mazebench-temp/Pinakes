---
summary: Odysseus drags the dead aside so that “the fine-maned horses” of Rhesus may pass without shying.
grc: [καλλίτριχες]
---

“Odysseus would take him by the foot from behind and drag him aside, with this thought in his heart: that the fine-maned horses might pass through easily, and not tremble in their hearts at treading on the dead; for they were not yet used to them” (10.490–493; [[of-many-wiles]], [[the-horses-of-rhesus]]). The horses are newly come to Troy and have not yet seen a battle ([[newly-come]]). The nominative καλλίτριχες is the form here. See [[odysseus]] and [[horses]].
