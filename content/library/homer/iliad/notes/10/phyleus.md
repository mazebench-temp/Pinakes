---
summary: Phyleus is named only in his son's title, “the son of Phyleus”, when Nestor twice asks for Meges to be woken (10.110, 10.175).
grc: [Φυλέος]
---

Φυλέος is the genitive of Φυλεύς. In the Catalogue Meges is Φυλεΐδης, “son of Phyleus” (2.628); in Book 10 the father's name stands in the genitive beside υἱός, Φυλέος ἄλκιμον υἱόν, “the valiant son of Phyleus” (10.110), and Φυλέος υἱόν (10.175). Meges himself is not named in the book (see [[meges]]).
