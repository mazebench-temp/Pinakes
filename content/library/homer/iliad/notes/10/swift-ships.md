summary: θοός describes the Achaean ships in Hector's offer and Dolon's mission, in Diomedes' refusal to let Dolon go, and in the flight of the horses; the swift night of 10.394 and 10.468 belongs to another entry.
grc: [θοῇς]
except: [10.394, 10.468]
---

Hector promises the two best horses “at the swift ships of the Achaeans” and asks for a man to find out “whether the swift ships are guarded as before” (10.306, 10.309; [[high-necked]]); Dolon repeats the question as Hector's order (10.396). Diomedes refuses to release him: “surely later too you will come to the swift ships of the Achaeans, either to spy, or to fight face to face” (10.450–451). The captured horses fly “toward the swift ships of the Achaeans” (10.514). The adjective of night in θοὴν διὰ νύκτα μέλαιναν (10.394, 10.468) belongs to [[swift-night]]. See [[ships]].
