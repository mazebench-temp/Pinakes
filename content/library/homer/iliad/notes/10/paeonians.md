---
summary: Dolon places the Paeonians “with their curved bows” by the sea (10.428), with the epithet the Catalogue gave them (2.848).
grc: [Παίονες]
---

In Dolon's account of the allies' camp the Paeonians lie toward the sea, after the Carians (10.428; see [[trojan-allies]]). ἀγκυλότοξοι, “with curved bows”, is their epithet in the Catalogue too (Παίονας ἀγκυλοτόξους, 2.848; see [[with-curved-bows]]). Later they are “chariot-fighters” and spearmen (16.287, 21.155).
