---
summary: ἀμύμων describes the plan Agamemnon hopes to devise with Nestor, Nestor's sons, and Achilles in Dolon's request.
grc: [ἀμύμονες]
---

Agamemnon resolves to go to Nestor, “in the hope that he might devise with him some flawless plan that would ward off evil from all the Danaans” (10.19–20; [[metis]]). Nestor answers Diomedes' protest that younger men should run his errands: “I have noble sons, and I have people too” (10.170; [[hard]]). Dolon asks for the horses that carry “the noble son of Peleus” (10.322–323; [[son-of-peleus]]). The translation renders the word “flawless” of the plan and “noble” of persons. See [[nestor]].
