---
summary: Nestor greets Odysseus on his return from the raid with the verse Agamemnon used when he came back from Achilles.
---

“Come, tell me, much-praised Odysseus, great glory of the Achaeans: how did you two take these horses?” (10.544–545; [[great-glory-of-the-achaeans]]). The verse repeats Agamemnon's greeting on the envoys' return earlier the same night (9.673), and again it opens a question about a night's mission. This time the answer is a success: Odysseus tells of the Thracian king and his twelve companions, killed by Diomedes, and of Dolon, the thirteenth (10.555–563). See [[odysseus]] and [[nestor]].
