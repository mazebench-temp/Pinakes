---
summary: The ships are “hollow” in Odysseus' question to Dolon, in Athena's warning to Diomedes, and as the two men ride back.
---

Odysseus asks Dolon whether Hector sent him “to spy out everything at the hollow ships” (10.388–389; [[spies-and-scouts]]). Athena tells Diomedes to think of his return “to the hollow ships” (10.509–510; [[great-hearted]]). The Trojans gaze at what the men had done “before they went off to the hollow ships” (10.524–525; [[grim-deeds]]), and the horses fly on “toward the hollow ships; for there their hearts were glad to go” (10.530–531; [[chariot-journey]]).
