---
summary: Agamemnon addresses Menelaus as “nurtured by Zeus” and tells him that the two of them need shrewd counsel.
---

“You and I have need, Menelaus, nurtured by Zeus, of counsel, shrewd counsel” (10.43–44; [[powerful]], [[kerdos]]). The honorific of kings is given to the brother for whose sake the army came to Troy, as Menelaus himself has just been remembering (10.25–28; [[bold-war]]). See [[menelaus]] and [[agamemnon]].
