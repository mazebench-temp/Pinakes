---
summary: Nestor wakes Odysseus and addresses him with the full formal verse, “Son of Laertes, sprung from Zeus, Odysseus of many devices.”
---

Odysseus, woken by Nestor's call, asks why the two kings wander alone through the camp in the ambrosial night (10.141–142; [[ambrosial]]), and Nestor answers with the full verse: “Son of Laertes, sprung from Zeus, Odysseus of many devices, do not be indignant; such grief has overpowered the Achaeans” (10.144–145; [[son-of-laertes]], [[sprung-from-zeus]], [[nemesis]]). He asks him to come and help wake another man fit to take counsel (10.146–147). The address that Achilles and Ajax used in the embassy (9.308, 9.624) calls Odysseus out for the night raid.
