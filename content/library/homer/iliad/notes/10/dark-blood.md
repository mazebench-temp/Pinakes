---
summary: The two spies go through the corpses and the black blood of the day's battle on their way out and on their way to the Thracians.
---

“They went like two lions through the black night, through the slaughter, through the corpses, through the armor and the black blood” (10.297–298); after Dolon's death, “the two went on, through the armor and the black blood” (10.469; [[black-night]], [[two-lions]]). The way to the Trojan camp lies over the battlefield of the day before. The killing that follows adds its own blood: among the Thracians “the earth grew red with blood” (10.484).
