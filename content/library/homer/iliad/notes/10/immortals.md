---
summary: The immortals are named three times in the second half of the book: Achilles' immortal mother, the gods for whom Rhesus' armor is fit, and Athena, first of all the immortals on Olympus.
---

Achilles alone can master his horses, he “whom an immortal mother bore” (10.404; [[the-horses-of-achilles]]). Rhesus' golden armor is fit “for the immortal gods,” not for mortal men (10.440–441; [[huge]], [[mortal-men]]). Odysseus holds up Dolon's spoils to Athena: “for you, first on Olympus of all the immortals, we will call upon” (10.462–463; [[of-the-spoil]], [[olympus]]). See [[athena]].
