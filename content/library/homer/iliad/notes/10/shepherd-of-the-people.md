---
summary: Agamemnon is “shepherd of the people” as he lies awake and as he goes to Nestor, and Odysseus asks Dolon where he left “Hector, shepherd of the people.”
---

The sleepless king is named by his care for his people: “but the son of Atreus, Agamemnon, shepherd of the people, was not held by sweet sleep” (10.3–4; [[sweet-sleep]]). He goes “to Nestor, shepherd of the people” (10.73), who is awake like him (10.96). On the other side Odysseus asks Dolon: “where did you leave Hector, shepherd of the people, when you came here?” (10.406). Hector is at council by the mound of Ilus, and the allies sleep, leaving the watch to the Trojans (10.414–422; [[night-watch]]). See [[agamemnon]], [[nestor]] and [[hector]].
