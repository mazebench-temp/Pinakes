---
summary: Diomedes says that with Odysseus beside him they would both come home “even out of blazing fire.”
---

“If he goes with me, even out of blazing fire we would both come home, since he is wise beyond all others” (10.246–247; [[equal-of-zeus-in-counsel]], [[nostos]]). The fire is a figure for the worst danger. Odysseus answers by turning from praise to the hour (10.249–253; [[do-not-praise-me-too-much]]). See [[diomedes]] and [[odysseus]].
