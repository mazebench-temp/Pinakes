summary: In the simile of the two hounds the fawn or hare runs “through wooded country.”
grc: ["ὑλήενθʼ"]
---

“And as when two sharp-toothed hounds, skilled in the hunt, press hard on a fawn or a hare, without letting up, through wooded country, and it runs on ahead, crying out” (10.360–362; [[two-hounds]], [[sharp-toothed]], [[hare]]). The wooded ground belongs to the simile; Dolon is chased across the open plain. See [[dogs]] and [[dolon]].
