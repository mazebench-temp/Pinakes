---
summary: Nestor gives Odysseus the title in the formal address as he wakes him, and the narrator gives it to Odysseus as he sees Dolon coming.
---

Nestor calls him out of his hut: “Son of Laertes, sprung from Zeus, Odysseus of many devices” (10.144; [[son-of-laertes]], [[of-many-devices]]). Later, on the plain, Dolon “was noticed by Odysseus, sprung from Zeus, who said to Diomedes” that a man was coming from the army (10.339–341). Odysseus is the first to see him and plans the ambush: let him pass, then cut him off from his own lines (10.341–348; [[ambush]]). See [[odysseus]].
