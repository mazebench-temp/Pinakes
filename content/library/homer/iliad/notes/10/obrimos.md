---
summary: The kings sit down beyond the ditch where “mighty Hector” turned back at nightfall.
---

“And crossing over the ditch they had dug, they sat down in a clear space, where the ground showed free of corpses of the fallen; from there mighty Hector had turned back again from killing the Argives, when night covered all around” (10.198–201; [[dug]], [[the-achaean-wall]]). The council sits where the day's killing stopped, and Hector, absent, is named by his strength. The same epithet named him in Zeus' prophecy at the end of Book 8, that mighty Hector would not cease from war until Achilles rose (8.473–474). See [[hector]].
