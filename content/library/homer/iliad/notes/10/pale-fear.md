---
summary: Dolon stands “pale with fear” when Diomedes' spear strikes the ground beside him.
grc: ["χλωρὸς ὑπαὶ δείους"]
en: [pale with fear]
---

“And he stood still, terrified, stammering, and there was a chattering of teeth in his mouth, pale with fear; and the two came up, panting, and seized his hands” (10.374–377; [[deos]], [[steep]]). Here the color is the man's, χλωρός, not the fear's, as in the formula χλωρὸν δέος (7.479, 8.77). The Trojans in their rout at the start of Book 15 are “pale with fear” in the same words (χλωροὶ ὑπαὶ δείους, 15.4). See [[dolon]].
