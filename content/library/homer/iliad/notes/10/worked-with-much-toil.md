---
summary: Dolon offers his captors bronze, gold and “iron worked with much toil,” in the verse of Adrastus' plea.
---

“For there is at home bronze and gold and iron worked with much toil; from these my father would gladly give you a boundless ransom” (10.378–380; [[boundless-ransom]], [[iron]]). The verse of a rich house's treasure is the suppliant's, as it was Adrastus' (6.48) and will be the sons of Antimachus' (11.133). Dolon himself is “rich in gold, rich in bronze” (10.315; [[rich-in-gold]], [[brazen]]). See [[dolon]].
