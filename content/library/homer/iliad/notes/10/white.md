---
summary: The tusks on the helmet Meriones gives Odysseus are “the white teeth” of a boar.
grc: ["λευκοὶ ὀδόντες"]
---

“And on the outside the white teeth of a white-tusked boar were set close, this way and that” (10.263–264; [[white-tusked]], [[the-boars-tusk-helmet]]). The plain λευκός stands beside the boar's own epithet, ἀργιόδους, from the root of brightness that gives ἀργής. Later in the book the horses of Rhesus are “whiter than snow” (10.437; [[whiter-than-snow]]). See [[odysseus]].
