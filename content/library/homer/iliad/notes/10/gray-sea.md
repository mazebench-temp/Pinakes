---
summary: Dolon puts on the skin of a gray wolf.
grc: [πολιοῖο λύκοιο]
---

“And put on over it the skin of a gray wolf” (10.334; [[wolf-skin]], [[wolf]]). πολιός, the gray of the sea and of old men's hair, is the gray of a wolf's coat. When Dolon is stripped the skin is λυκέη, “the wolfskin” (10.459). See [[dolon]].
