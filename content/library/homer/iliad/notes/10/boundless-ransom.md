---
summary: Dolon offers his captors a boundless ransom in the verses Adrastus used to beg Menelaus in Book 6; like Adrastus, he is killed.
---

“Take me alive, and I will ransom myself; for there is at home bronze and gold and iron worked with much toil; from these my father would gladly give you a boundless ransom, if he heard that I was alive at the ships of the Achaeans” (10.378–381; [[worked-with-much-toil]], [[apoina]]). The last three verses are the ones Adrastus spoke when he clasped Menelaus' knees (6.48–50), and in Book 11 the sons of Antimachus speak them to Agamemnon (11.133–135). None of the three pleas is accepted ([[supplication]], [[adrastus-the-trojan]]). Odysseus answers “Take heart” and questions him; when he has told all, Diomedes kills him (10.383, 10.446–457).
