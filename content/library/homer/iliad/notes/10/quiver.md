---
summary: Meriones gives Odysseus a quiver with a bow for the night raid (10.260). Its arrows are never shot.
grc: [φαρέτρην]
---

“And Meriones gave Odysseus a bow and a quiver” (βιὸν ἠδὲ φαρέτρην, 10.260; see [[bow]]). The quivers of the Iliad belong to archers: Apollo comes down from Olympus with his “close-covered quiver” (1.45), Pandarus takes the lid off his before he shoots at Menelaus (4.116), and Teucer runs to Ajax's side with his bow in his hand and his quiver when Ajax calls for his arrows (15.440–444). Odysseus carries one only on this night, and lets nothing fly. The Odyssey gives him bow and quiver in the same half-verse when he stands on the threshold to shoot the suitors (Od. 21.233, 22.2).
