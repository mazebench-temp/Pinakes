---
summary: The Rhesus, handed down among Euripides' plays, is the one surviving tragedy whose plot is taken from the Iliad. It stages the night of Book 10 in the Trojan camp, with Dolon disguised as a wolf, Rhesus the son of a Muse and the river Strymon, and the dream of wolves given to his charioteer.
