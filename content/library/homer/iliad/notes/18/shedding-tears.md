summary: The formula follows the news of Patroclus' death through the book. Antilochus comes “shedding hot tears” and mourns “letting tears fall” (18.17, 18.32), Achilles follows the bier “shedding hot tears” (18.235), Thetis answers her son and Hephaestus “letting her tears fall” (18.94, 18.428), and Achilles promises that captive women will weep for Patroclus, “shedding tears” (18.340).
grc: ["δάκρυ χέουσαι", "δάκρυα λείβων"]
en: [letting tears fall]
---

δάκρυα λείβων, “letting tears fall” (18.32), is said also of Pylaemenes, who follows his son's body to Ilios weeping, “and no penalty was paid for his dead son” (13.658–659; see [[poine]]). Antilochus left the battle at the end of Book 17 “shedding tears” (δάκρυ χέοντα, 17.700), and he reaches Achilles “shedding hot tears” (δάκρυα θερμὰ χέων, 18.17), the words in which Achilles himself follows the bier (18.235; see [[hot-tears]], [[tears]] and [[messenger-scene]]).
