---
summary: Thetis asks Achilles what grief has come upon him, “Speak out; do not hide it” (18.74), as she did in Book 1 (1.363).
grc: ["ἐξαύδα μὴ κεῦθε"]
en: [Speak out; do not hide it]
---

The half-verse lacks the rest of the Book 1 line, νόῳ, ἵνα εἴδομεν ἄμφω, “in your mind, so that we both may know”, and goes on at once to the answer: “These things have been fulfilled for you / by Zeus” (18.74–75; see [[prophecy]] and [[mothers-and-sons]]).
