summary: On the Shield a king stands in silence among the reapers of his domain, holding his scepter, glad at heart (18.550–560), and elders holding the heralds' scepters give judgment in the place of assembly (18.503–506). On Olympus Hera reminds Zeus that he is “lord among all the immortals” (18.366); in the Trojan assembly Hector overrules the better counsel and the people cheer him (18.285–313).
