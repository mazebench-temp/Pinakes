---
summary: Thetis reminds Achilles of his prayer, “as you prayed before, lifting up your hands”, that the Achaeans be penned among the sterns (18.75–77); Zeus has fulfilled it, and its cost is Patroclus (18.79–82).
---

The prayer is that of Book 1, which Thetis carried to Zeus (1.407–412, 1.503–510; see [[aekelios]] and [[zeus-does-not-fulfill-all-plans]]). εὔχετο at 18.499 is “vowed” and is excepted (see [[the-trial-on-the-shield]]).
