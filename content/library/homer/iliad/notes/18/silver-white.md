---
summary: The cave in the depths of the sea where Thetis lives among the Nereids is “the silver-white cave.”
---

“The silver-white cave was filled with them; and all of them together / they beat their breasts, and Thetis led the lamentation” (18.50–51; [[nereids]], [[thetis]], [[lament]]). The cave is Thetis' home beside her father in the sea (18.36), and she leaves it to go to her son: “So she spoke and left the cave” (18.65). Hephaestus, too, spent nine years “in a hollow cave,” with the stream of Oceanus flowing around it, where Thetis and Eurynome kept him (18.398–405; [[fall-of-hephaestus]]).
