---
summary: Thetis tells her son that Hector wears his armor, “bronze, gleaming,” and the book ends as she carries “the gleaming armor from Hephaestus.”
---

“But your fine armor is held among the Trojans, / bronze, gleaming; Hector of the gleaming helmet / wears it on his own shoulders and glories in it” (18.130–132; [[of-the-gleaming-helmet]], [[exchange-of-armor]]). The last verse of the book has the new armor: “And she like a hawk leapt down from snowy Olympus, / carrying the gleaming armor from Hephaestus” (18.616–617; [[like-a-hawk]], [[niphoeis]], [[the-making-of-the-arms]]).
