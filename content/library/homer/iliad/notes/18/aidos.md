---
summary: Charis and Hephaestus greet Thetis as “respected and dear” (αἰδοίη τε φίλη τε, 18.386, 18.425), and Hephaestus calls her “a goddess I fear and respect” (δεινή τε καὶ αἰδοίη, 18.394). Iris' word for the shame that should rouse Achilles is σέβας (18.178).
---

Walter Leaf notes the favorite pairing of αἰδοῖος and φίλος in greetings (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 18.386; 10.114, 14.210). See [[sebas]], [[philos]] and [[visit-scene]].
