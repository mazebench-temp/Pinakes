---
summary: The ambushers on the Shield set “two lookouts” apart from the army, to watch for the sheep and cattle (18.523–524).
grc: [σκοποὶ]
en: [lookouts]
---

The lookouts watch for the herds, and the herdsmen, playing their pipes, “did not foresee the trick at all” (18.526; see [[ambush]] and [[foresight]]).
