summary: When the plowmen on the Shield turn at the end of the field, “a cup of honey-sweet wine would be put into their hands / by a man who came up to them” (18.545–546).
---

Fick would cut the lines as “curious, not to say ridiculous”, as Walter Leaf reports (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 18.545; see the translation's note on 18.541–549). See [[wine]].
