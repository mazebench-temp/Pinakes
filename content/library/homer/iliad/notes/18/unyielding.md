---
summary: Hephaestus casts “unyielding bronze” into the fire with tin, gold and silver.
---

“And he cast into the fire unyielding bronze and tin / and precious gold and silver” (18.474–475; [[forge]], [[bronze]], [[tin]], [[gold-and-silver]]). χαλκὸς ἀτειρής, bronze that does not wear down, is a phrase of armor and weapons (19.233, 20.108); here it is the metal before it is worked.
