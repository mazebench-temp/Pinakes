---
summary: Iris answers Achilles: “Hera sent me, the glorious wife of Zeus.”
---

“Hera sent me, the glorious wife of Zeus; / and the son of Cronus, throned on high, does not know, nor any other / of the immortals who dwell around snowy Olympus” (18.184–186; [[hera]], [[high-throned]], [[snowy-olympus]]). The formula of Zeus' consort is spoken as Hera acts behind his back; Zeus learns of it after the event (18.356–359). Walter Leaf suspects the line on secrecy, 18.168, and with it 18.185–186 (see the translation's note on 18.168).
