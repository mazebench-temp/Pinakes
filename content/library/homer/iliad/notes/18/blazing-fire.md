---
summary: The book opens on the verse that closes two earlier scenes of battle, “So they fought on like blazing fire”; and the tripod for Patroclus' bath is set “on the blazing fire.”
---

**The battle.** “So they fought on like blazing fire” (18.1) is the verse that rounds off the fighting at 11.596 and 13.673 ([[like-blazing-fire]]). Here it carries the fight over Patroclus forward while Antilochus runs to Achilles with the news ([[the-fight-for-the-body-of-patroclus]], [[antilochus]]).

**The bath.** Achilles tells his companions to set a great tripod over the fire to wash the blood from Patroclus: “And they set the tripod for the bath on the blazing fire” (18.344–346; [[tripod]], [[bathing]], [[patroclus]]). πυρὶ κηλέῳ, the fire that Hector carried to the ships, now heats the water for the dead.
