---
summary: On the Shield the golden vineyard has a trench “of dark blue” around it, κυανέην, and a fence of tin beyond (18.564–565).
grc: [κυανέην]
en: [of dark blue]
---

Walter Leaf takes κυανέην as naming the material, as κασσιτέρου after it shows, though κύανος is not among the metals Hephaestus casts into the fire (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 18.564; 18.474–475; see the translation's note on 18.564 and [[tin]]).
