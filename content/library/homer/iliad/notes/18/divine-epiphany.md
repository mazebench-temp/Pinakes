---
summary: Iris comes to Achilles from Olympus: she stands near him and speaks, he knows her and asks who sent her, she answers and departs (18.166–202). Thetis comes up from the sea with the Nereids and stands beside her son (18.65–72).
passages:
  - 18.166-202 | Iris rouses Achilles
---

The scene has the parts of the epiphany: arrival (18.166–167), the goddess standing near, ἀγχοῦ δʼ ἱσταμένη (18.169), recognition, “Goddess Iris” (18.182), speech and departure, “So saying, swift-footed Iris went away” (18.202; see [[iris]] and [[seeing-the-gods]]). Athena follows at once without words, with the aegis and the flame (18.203–206; see [[divine-intervention]]).
