---
summary: Iris rouses Achilles with Agamemnon's words of Book 1: “Rise, son of Peleus, most terrible of all men.”
---

“Rise, son of Peleus, most terrible of all men; / defend Patroclus, for whose sake the dread din of battle / stands before the ships” (18.170–172; [[iris]], [[son-of-peleus]], [[dread-din-of-battle]]). The address is the one Agamemnon used in anger (1.146); in the messenger's mouth Walter Leaf hears in it amazement at Achilles' incredible remissness (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 18.170).
