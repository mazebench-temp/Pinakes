**Galateia.** “Doris and Panope and famous Galateia” (18.45; [[galateia]], [[nereids]]): of all the Nereids of the catalogue, she alone has an epithet of fame.

**The glorious armor.** Thetis goes to Hephaestus “in the hope that he may be willing / to give my son glorious armor, gleaming all over” (18.143–144; [[gleaming-all-over]]), and goes “to bring glorious armor to her dear son” (18.147). Achilles knows of no other man “whose glorious armor I could put on, / unless it were the shield of Ajax, son of Telamon” (18.192–193; [[the-shield-of-ajax]]), and Iris answers, “We too know well that your glorious armor is held by them” (18.197; [[exchange-of-armor]]).

**The allies, Patroclus and the gifts.** At Achilles' shout “three times the Trojans and their famous allies were thrown into confusion” (18.229; [[trojan-allies]]). Achilles told Menoetius “that I would bring his renowned son back to Opus” (18.326; [[menoetius]], [[opus]]). Thetis tells Hephaestus that the elders begged Achilles “and named many glorious gifts” (18.449; [[the-embassy-to-achilles]]). ἀγακλειτή and περικλυτόν are new to the entry; περικλυτὸς ἀμφιγυήεις, Hephaestus' epithet, stays with [[lame-in-both-legs]].
