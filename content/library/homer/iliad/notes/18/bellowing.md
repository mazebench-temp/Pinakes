---
summary: Two lions on the shield seize “a bellowing bull” from the herd and drag it off.
---

“But two terrible lions among the foremost cows / had seized a bellowing bull; and he, lowing loudly, / was being dragged off; and the dogs and the young men went after him” (18.579–581; [[the-herd-and-the-lions]], [[lion]], [[bulls]], [[swift-dogs]]). The epithet, found only here, gives the bull the voice of its pain.
