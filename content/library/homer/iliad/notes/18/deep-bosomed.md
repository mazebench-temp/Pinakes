---
summary: The “deep-bosomed Dardanian women” will weep for the men Achilles kills, and for Patroclus.
---

**For the Trojans.** Achilles' reason for choosing to fight: “but now may I win noble fame, / and make some woman among the Trojan and the deep-bosomed Dardanian women, / with both her hands, from her soft cheeks, / wiping away the tears, groan without ceasing” (18.121–124; [[kleos]], [[trojan-women]], [[tender]]).

**For Patroclus.** Over the body: “and around you the Trojan and the deep-bosomed Dardanian women / shall weep night and day, shedding tears, / whom we ourselves won by toil with our strength and our long spear” (18.339–341; [[lament]], [[patroclus]]). The same women weep for the enemy and, as captives, for Patroclus.
