---
summary: Thetis hopes for “glorious armor, gleaming all over,” and Athena kindles above Achilles' head “a flame, gleaming all over.”
---

Thetis to the Nereids: “in the hope that he may be willing / to give my son glorious armor, gleaming all over” (18.143–144; [[famous]]). At the ditch Athena “set a cloud as a crown, brilliant among goddesses, / a golden one, and from him she kindled a flame, gleaming all over” (18.205–206; [[athena]]). The word that Thetis gives to the armor she asks for is given to the fire that Achilles wears before the armor comes.
