---
summary: Peleus is “wise-hearted” in Antilochus' first words, and Achilles “wise-hearted” as the captive women run out around him.
---

Antilochus: “Ah me, son of wise-hearted Peleus” (18.18; [[peleus]], [[son-of-peleus]]). The women whom Achilles and Patroclus had taken as spoil “cried aloud, and ran out of doors / around wise-hearted Achilles, and with their hands all of them / beat their breasts” (18.29–31; [[handmaids]], [[lament]], [[achilles]]).
