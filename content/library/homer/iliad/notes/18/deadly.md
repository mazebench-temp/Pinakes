summary: On the shield “Strife and Tumult were in the throng, and deadly Doom.”
---

“And among them Strife and Tumult were in the throng, and deadly Doom, / holding one man alive and freshly wounded, another unwounded, / and another, dead, she was dragging through the fray by the feet” (18.535–537; [[ker-doom]], [[strife]], [[tumult]]). The verses recur almost word for word in the Hesiodic *Shield of Heracles* (156–158; see the translation's note on 18.535–538).
