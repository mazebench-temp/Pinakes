summary: The book's one use of the word is the enemy's: Polydamas tells the Trojans that “As long as this man nursed his wrath against brilliant Agamemnon, / so long the Achaeans were easier to fight” (18.257–258).
---

The verb μηνίω is the poem's own word for Achilles' wrath, from the first line (1.1; see [[quarrel-of-achilles-and-agamemnon]]). When Achilles speaks of it himself he calls it χόλος, anger, which he wishes would perish (18.107–113; see [[cholos]]).
