---
summary: Hector answers Polydamas' advice “Looking at him darkly” (18.284), in the two lines with which he answered Polydamas at the Achaean wall: “Polydamas, what you say is no longer pleasing to me” (18.284–285 = 12.230–231).
---

Both times Polydamas' counsel is caution and Hector refuses it (12.210–250; see [[polydamas]], [[retreat]] and [[assembly-type-scene]]); Walter Leaf notes the repetition (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 18.284).
