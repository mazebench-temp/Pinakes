---
summary: Hector: “we will rouse sharp Ares beside the hollow ships.”
---

“And early, at daybreak, armed in our armor, / we will rouse sharp Ares beside the hollow ships” (18.303–304; [[sharp]], [[ares]], [[hector]]). Hector spoke the same two verses at nightfall in Book 8 (8.530–531), when the Trojans first camped on the plain; he repeats them now that Achilles has appeared. See [[ships]].
