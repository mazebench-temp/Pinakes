---
summary: Iris warns Achilles that if Patroclus' body comes back disfigured, “yours will be the disgrace” (σοὶ λώβη, 18.180).
---

The outrage is done to the body, but the disgrace falls on the friend who failed to protect it. Walter Leaf reports Döderlein's reading of νέκυς as an accusative plural, “if he comes disfigured to the dead”, which he thinks gives the better sense; the translation keeps the usual reading (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 18.180; see the translation's note on 18.180). See [[sebas]] and [[honoring-the-dead]].
