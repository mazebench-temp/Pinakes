---
summary: At Iris' word “Achilles, dear to Zeus, rose up,” and Athena put the aegis around him.
---

“So saying, swift-footed Iris went away; / and Achilles, dear to Zeus, rose up; and Athena around / his mighty shoulders flung the tasseled aegis” (18.202–204; [[iris]], [[athena]], [[aegis]]). The narrator gives Achilles the epithet here as he rises from the dust where he had lain since the news (18.26–27). Calchas called him “dear to Zeus” in Book 1 (Διῒ φίλε, 1.74).
