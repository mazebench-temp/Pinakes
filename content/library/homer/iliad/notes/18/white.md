**The cloak.** “and laying him on a bier they covered him with a soft linen cloth / from head to foot, and over it with a white cloak” (18.352–353; [[heanos]], [[honoring-the-dead]], [[patroclus]]).

**The sheep.** The men of the ambush “cut off the herds of cattle and the fine flocks / of white sheep, and killed the shepherds as well” (18.528–529), and Hephaestus made “a pasture / in a lovely glen, a great one, for white sheep” (18.587–588; [[sheep]], [[the-sheep-pasture]]).

**The meal.** At the harvest “the women / were sprinkling much white barley meal for the laborers' dinner” (18.559–560; [[barley-meal]], [[the-kings-harvest]]), as Hecamede sprinkled it on the drink she mixed for Nestor (11.640). The three forms are new to the entry.
