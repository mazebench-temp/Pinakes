summary: Thetis is “his lady mother” when she hears his groan and when she stands beside him (18.35, 18.70), “my mother” and “my dear mother” in Achilles' mouth (18.9, 18.79, 18.189, 18.332), whose command he heeds at the ditch (18.216); the book ends with the armor set before “the mother of Achilles” (18.615). Hephaestus speaks of “my dog-faced mother” (18.396).
