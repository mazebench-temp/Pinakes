---
summary: Hephaestus wipes his face and hands, “and his massive neck and his shaggy chest”; the book adds λαχνήεντα.
grc: [λαχνήεντα]
---

“and with a sponge he wiped his face and both his hands / and his massive neck and his shaggy chest, / and put on his tunic” (18.414–416; [[hephaestus]]). The smith comes from the forge with the body of a laborer, massive above and slender-legged below (18.411). The accusative λαχνήεντα is new to the entry.
