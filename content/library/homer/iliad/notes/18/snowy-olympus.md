---
summary: Iris says that none “of the immortals who dwell around snowy Olympus” knows that Hera sent her.
---

“and the son of Cronus, throned on high, does not know, nor any other / of the immortals who dwell around snowy Olympus” (18.185–186; [[immortals]], [[olympus]]). At the end of the book Olympus is snowy again, with the other word, νιφόεις, as Thetis leaps down from it with the armor (18.616; [[niphoeis]]).
