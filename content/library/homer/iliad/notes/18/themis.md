---
summary: On the Shield the elders “gave their judgments in turn” (δίκαζον), and two talents of gold lie in the middle “to give to the one among them who spoke the straightest judgment” (δίκην, 18.506–508).
grc: [δίκαζον]
en: [gave their judgments]
---

δικάζω is to give judgment; Leaf notes that the active always has this sense, which tells against referring the verb to the litigants (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 18.506). The straight judgment answers the crooked ordinances on which Zeus sends his storm (16.386–388; see [[the-trial-on-the-shield]] and [[istor]]).
