---
summary: Hera answers Zeus' taunt with her verse of indignation: “Most dread son of Cronus, what a thing you have said!”
---

“Most dread son of Cronus, what a thing you have said! / Surely even a mortal will accomplish his purpose against another man” (18.361–362; [[hera]], [[zeus]], [[mortal-men]]). It is the sixth time she opens a speech with the verse (1.552, 4.25, 8.462, 14.330, 16.440). Here she defends what she has done: she has roused Achilles, and will not be denied her revenge on the Trojans (18.364–367).
