---
summary: Hephaestus sets the river Oceanus around the outermost rim of “the strongly made shield”; the book adds πύκα ποιητοῖο.
grc: ["πύκα ποιητοῖο"]
en: [strongly made]
---

“And on it he set the great strength of the river Oceanus / along the outermost rim of the strongly made shield” (18.607–608; [[oceanus]], [[the-shield-of-achilles]]). ποιητός, “made,” with πύκα, “firmly, closely,” is the Odyssey's phrase for the well-made roof and chamber of a house (*Odyssey* 1.333, 1.436); here it closes the description of the shield. The phrase is new to the entry.
