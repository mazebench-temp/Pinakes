“So let us go to the city; do as I say; for this is how it will be: / now night has stopped the swift-footed son of Peleus, / the ambrosial night” (18.266–268; [[polydamas]], [[night]]). The epithet of the night that comes from the gods stands alone at the head of the next verse. The nominative ἀμβροσίη is new to the entry; the accent-sensitive pattern keeps out the dative of the noun, ἀμβροσίῃ, “with ambrosia” (14.170, 16.670, 16.680; [[ambrosia]]).
