---
summary: The companion is Patroclus: “my dear companion has perished, / Patroclus, whom I honored above all my companions, / as my own life” (18.80–82), the companion Achilles failed to defend (18.98–100), his “trusted companion” on the bier (18.235) and in Thetis' account (18.460). Polydamas, on the Trojan side, is Hector's companion, born on the same night (18.251).
---

Achilles' grief joins the one companion to the many: he was “no light at all to Patroclus, nor to my companions, / the others, the many who were brought down by brilliant Hector” (18.102–103). The companions stand weeping round the bier, and Achilles has them wash the body (18.233–234, 18.343; see [[trusted-companion]], [[patroclus]] and [[honoring-the-dead]]).
