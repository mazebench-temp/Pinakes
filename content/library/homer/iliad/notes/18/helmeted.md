---
summary: The two Ajaxes are “helmeted men” who cannot frighten Hector away from the body.
---

“so the two Ajaxes, helmeted men, could not / frighten Hector, son of Priam, away from the body” (18.163–164; [[the-two-ajaxes]], [[lion-on-a-carcass]], [[son-of-priam]]). The dual κορυστά is used of the Ajaxes in Book 13 too (13.201), and the word belongs elsewhere to the formula for the first kill of a battle.
