---
summary: Hephaestus works at the tripods “with his cunning skill,” and makes the many designs of the shield “with his cunning skill”; the book adds the form ending πραπίδεσσιν.
grc: ["ἰδυίῃσι πραπίδεσσιν"]
---

“While he was toiling at these things with his cunning skill, / the goddess silver-footed Thetis came near him” (18.380–381; [[tripod]], [[silver-footed]]); and on the shield, “and on it / he made many intricate designs with his cunning skill” (18.481–482; [[intricately-worked]], [[the-shield-of-achilles]]). The formula of the god at work, used of the houses he built the gods in Book 1 (1.608), frames his two labors in the book: the tripods Thetis finds him making, and the arms she asks for. The form with the final ν is new to the entry.
