---
summary: Thetis tells Hephaestus how “powerful Agamemnon” took Briseis back from Achilles.
---

“The girl whom the sons of the Achaeans picked out for him as his prize, / powerful Agamemnon took her back out of his hands” (18.444–445; [[agamemnon]], [[briseis]], [[sons-of-the-achaeans]]). In Thetis' short history of the wrath the old title of the king names the man who took the prize (1.182–187; [[menis]]).
