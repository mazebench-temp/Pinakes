grc: ["βαρὺ στενάχοντι"]
en: [as he groaned heavily]

The night lament surrounds the formula with other groans: the Achaeans “groaned over Patroclus”, Achilles groans “thick and fast, like a bearded lion”, and the Myrmidons groan all night (18.315–318, 18.355; see [[lament]] and [[the-lion-and-its-cubs]]).
