---
summary: Thetis grants that it is no bad thing “to ward off sheer destruction” from hard-pressed companions.
---

“Yes, my child, truly this is no bad thing, / to ward off sheer destruction from your hard-pressed companions” (18.128–129; [[thetis]]). αἰπὺς ὄλεθρος, “sheer destruction,” is a verse-end formula (6.57, 14.507, 16.283), and Hector asks the dead Patroclus, “why do you prophesy sheer destruction for me?” (16.859).
