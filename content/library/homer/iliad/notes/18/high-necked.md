---
summary: Polydamas foresees Achilles giving “his horses with high-arched necks” their fill of running beneath the walls.
---

“He will go back again to the ships, when he has given his horses with high-arched necks / their fill of running every way as he roams beneath the city; / but his heart will not let him rush in” (18.280–282; [[the-horses-of-achilles]], [[polydamas]]). The verse-end ἐριαύχενας ἵππους is the formula of chariot horses (10.305, 11.159, 17.496).
