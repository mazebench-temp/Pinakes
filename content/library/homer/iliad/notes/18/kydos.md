---
summary: Glory is Hector's, for the day. He would have dragged off Patroclus' body and “won untold glory”, had not Iris come to Achilles (18.165); he boasts that the son of Cronus has granted him “to win glory at the ships” (18.293–294); and Thetis says that Apollo killed Patroclus “and given glory to Hector” (18.456). Antilochus groans in his “glorious heart” (18.33).
---

The glory was promised by Zeus for a limited time, until Hector reaches the ships and the sun goes down (11.192–194, 17.453–455; see [[the-mind-of-zeus]]), and Hera sends the sun down early (18.239–240; see [[divine-intervention]]).
