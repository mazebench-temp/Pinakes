---
summary: Hector would have won “untold glory” had Iris not come; Achilles' shout rouses “untold tumult” among the Trojans; and the stream of Oceanus flowed “boundless” around the cave of Hephaestus.
---

**Glory.** “And now he would have dragged him off and won untold glory, / had not swift Iris, wind-footed, to the son of Peleus / come running from Olympus as a messenger” (18.165–167; [[kydos]], [[would-have-had-not]], [[wind-footed]]), the verse that told how Menelaus would have dragged off Paris (3.373).

**Tumult.** “and among the Trojans he roused untold tumult” (18.218; [[tumult]]).

**Oceanus.** “in a hollow cave; and around it the stream of Oceanus / flowed boundless, seething with foam” (18.402–403; [[oceanus]], [[fall-of-hephaestus]]).
