summary: The triple pattern, without its fourth. Three times Hector seizes Patroclus by the feet, and three times the two Ajaxes drive him back (18.155–158); three times Achilles shouts across the ditch, and three times the Trojans and their allies are thrown into confusion (18.228–229).
refs: [18.155-157, 18.228-229]
en: [three times]
---

In both scenes the third time is decisive in another way: Hector would have dragged the body off, had not Iris come to Achilles (18.165–168; see [[would-have-had-not]]), and after the third shout twelve Trojans die among their own chariots and spears (18.230–231). See [[numbers-and-counting]].
