---
summary: Achilles tells the dead Patroclus that the captive women were won “with our strength and our long spear.”
---

“and around you the Trojan and the deep-bosomed Dardanian women / shall weep night and day, shedding tears, / whom we ourselves won by toil with our strength and our long spear, / as we two sacked the rich cities of mortal men” (18.339–342; [[deep-bosomed]], [[meropes]], [[patroclus]]). The long spear is the weapon of the raids the two men made together, the cities that Achilles counted in Book 9 (9.328–329).
