---
summary: Nestor is named once, in his son's title: “the son of noble Nestor” brings Achilles the news (18.16). The plan he gave Patroclus in Book 11, to go out in Achilles' armor so that the Trojans would hold off from the war, has ended in Patroclus' death, and Iris gives Achilles the same counsel in Nestor's words (18.102, 18.197–201).
---

**His son.** ἀγαυοῦ Νέστορος υἱός, “the son of noble Nestor” (18.16; see [[antilochus]] and [[noble]]), is the only place where Nestor is called ἀγαυός; elsewhere Antilochus is the son of “great-hearted Nestor” (5.565, 13.400, 17.653, 23.541, 23.596; see [[great-hearted]]). In Book 17 it was Nestor's order that kept Antilochus fighting apart, so that he did not know that Patroclus was dead (17.377–383).

**His plan.** In Book 11 Nestor told Patroclus that if Achilles was holding back because of some oracle, he should at least send Patroclus out with the Myrmidons “in the hope that you may become a light to the Danaans”, and give him his fine armor, “in the hope that, taking you for him, they may hold off from the war” (11.796–799; see [[patroclus]]). Patroclus asked for it in the same words (16.36–45), and Book 18 tells how it ended. Patroclus is dead and Hector has the armor (18.20–21), and Achilles says of himself, “and I was no light at all to Patroclus” (18.102; see [[achilles]]). Thetis tells Hephaestus that Achilles put his own armor on Patroclus and sent him into the war, where Apollo killed him (18.451–456). Iris then gives Achilles himself Nestor's counsel: he is to show himself at the ditch, “in the hope that in fear of you they may hold off from the war, / the Trojans, and the warlike sons of the Achaeans may draw breath, / worn out as they are; and brief is the breathing space in war” (18.199–201). The last two verses are Nestor's (11.800–801); Walter Leaf thought them interpolated here, since Achilles is to be roused not by the Achaeans' weariness but by his friend's body (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 18.200; see the translation's note on 18.200–201).
