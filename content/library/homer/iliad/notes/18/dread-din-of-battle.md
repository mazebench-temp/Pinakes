---
summary: Iris tells Achilles that “the dread din of battle / stands before the ships” for the sake of Patroclus.
---

“defend Patroclus, for whose sake the dread din of battle / stands before the ships; and they are killing one another” (18.171–172; [[most-terrible-of-men]], [[the-fight-for-the-body-of-patroclus]]). At sunset the Achaeans cease “from the fierce din of battle” (φυλόπιδος κρατερῆς, 18.242; [[krateros]]).
