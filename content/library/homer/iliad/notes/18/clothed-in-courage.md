summary: Three times “the two Ajaxes, clothed in furious courage,” drive Hector back from the body.
---

“and three times the two Ajaxes, clothed in furious courage, / drove him back from the body” (18.157–158; [[the-two-ajaxes]], [[furious]]). The half-verse is the formula of the Ajaxes, used when they offer to fight Hector and when they go out in battle (7.164, 8.262). Here it marks the last stand over Patroclus.
