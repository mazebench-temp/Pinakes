---
summary: Polydamas tells the Trojans not to wait on the plain “for brilliant Dawn” (18.255). The dawn comes at the opening of Book 19, with Thetis and the armor.
---

The Trojans' plans for the morning are told in a repeated verse, “and early, at daybreak, armed in our armor” (18.277 = 18.303), in the mouths of both speakers, and Thetis promises to come back “at dawn … with the rising sun” (18.136). See [[eos]], [[war-and-night]] and [[retreat]].
