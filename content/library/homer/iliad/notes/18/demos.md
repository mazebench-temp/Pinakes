---
summary: Hector tells Polydamas to show his foolish thoughts no longer “among the people” (18.295), and orders that the man troubled about his possessions give them to the people “to consume in common” (καταδημοβορῆσαι, 18.301). On the Shield a man declares his offer “to the people” in the place of assembly (18.500).
grc: [καταδημοβορῆσαι]
en: [to consume in common]
---

καταδημοβορέω, “to consume from the common stock”, occurs only here; Walter Leaf compares δημοβόρος βασιλεύς, the king who does nothing but feast at the public cost, Achilles' taunt to Agamemnon (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 18.301; 1.231; see the translation's note on 18.300–302). See [[laos]] and [[the-trial-on-the-shield]].
