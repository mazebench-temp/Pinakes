---
summary: Hephaestus' work in Thetis' cave included necklaces (ὅρμους, 18.401).
grc: [ὅρμους]
en: [necklaces]
---

In the *Homeric Hymn to Aphrodite* the goddess wears “curved spirals and shining rosettes”, and “necklaces” around her soft neck, words taken from this line (86–89; Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 18.401). See [[brooch]].
