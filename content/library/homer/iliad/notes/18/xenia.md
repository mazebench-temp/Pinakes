---
summary: Thetis is received as a guest in the house of Hephaestus: Charis offers her good cheer (18.387), and Hephaestus has guest-gifts set before her (18.408).
---

The reception follows the welcome of the Odyssey's guests (see [[visit-scene]] and [[guest-gifts]]). Hephaestus' debt to his guest is older than hospitality: she took him in when he fell from heaven (18.394–407; see [[zoagria]]).
