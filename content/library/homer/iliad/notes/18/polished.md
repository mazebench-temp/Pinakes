**The gates.** “and the towers / and the high gates and the doors fitted on them, / long, well polished, bolted fast, will guard the city” (18.274–276; [[polydamas]], [[troy]]).

**The trial.** “and the elders / sat on polished stones in the sacred circle” (18.503–504; [[hieros]], [[the-trial-on-the-shield]]). Walter Leaf compares the place of assembly of the Phaeacians, built of stones set deep in the ground (*Odyssey* 6.266–267), where they sit ἐπὶ ξεστοῖσι λίθοισι, “on polished stones” (*Odyssey* 8.6; *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 18.504). ἐΰξεστοι and the phrase ξεστοῖσι λίθοις are new to the entry.
