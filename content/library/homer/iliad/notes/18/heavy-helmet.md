---
summary: Hephaestus makes Achilles “a heavy helmet fitted to his temples.”
---

“and he made him a heavy helmet fitted to his temples, / fine and intricately worked, and set a golden crest on it” (18.611–612; [[helmet]], [[intricately-worked]], [[the-making-of-the-arms]]). The phrase κόρυθα βριαρήν is used of the helmet of Agastrophus that Diomedes was stripping when Paris shot him (11.373–376), and of the helmet that Hector will think of putting aside before Achilles (22.111–112).
