summary: Old age is Peleus': he will not welcome his son home, “the old horse-driving Peleus” (18.331), and he lies worn out by grievous old age in his halls (18.434–435). The elders of the Argives begged Achilles in Book 9 (18.448), the elders on the Shield sit in judgment (18.503), and the men “whom old age held” guard the wall of the besieged city (18.515). Thetis' father is “aged” (18.36).
