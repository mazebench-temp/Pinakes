---
summary: The formula of “brilliant swift-footed Achilles” introduces his answer to Iris, a question: who of the gods sent her?
---

“Then brilliant swift-footed Achilles answered her” (18.181; [[iris]], [[brilliant]]), with a question: “Goddess Iris, who of the gods sent you to me as a messenger?” (18.182). The formula ποδάρκης δῖος Ἀχιλλεύς is the verse-end with which he first answered Agamemnon (1.121). See [[achilles]].
