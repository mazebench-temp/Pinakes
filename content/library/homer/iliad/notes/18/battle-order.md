# ἐπὶ στίχας (18.602): the dancers on the Shield running in lines, not ranks of soldiers.
except: [18.602]
---

The battle on the Shield begins in the words used of the Cicones' battle with Odysseus' men in the Odyssey (*Odyssey* 9.54; see [[the-two-cities]]). The dancers who run “in ranks toward one another” (18.602) take the soldiers' phrase into the dance (see [[dance]]).
