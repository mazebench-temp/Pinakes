---
summary: Hector tells the Trojans to take their supper by companies, “and remember to keep watch, and let each man stay awake” (18.298–299).
---

Polydamas had proposed to keep the army through the night in the place of assembly while the towers and gates guarded the city (18.274–276; see [[retreat]]). Hector's orders repeat those of his first night on the plain, when the Trojans burned many fires and set a watch (8.502–522; see [[war-and-night]]).
