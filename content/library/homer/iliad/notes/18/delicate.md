---
summary: The boy at the vintage sings the Linus song “in a delicate voice.”
---

“And in the middle of them a boy on a clear-toned lyre / played lovely music, and to it he sang the beautiful Linus song / in a delicate voice” (18.569–571; [[clear-toned]], [[the-linus-song]], [[the-vintage]]). Walter Leaf, following Aristarchus, takes λίνος as a kind of song, one of the ancient dirges, perhaps for the departing summer and so fitting at the vintage (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 18.570; see the translation's note on 18.570).
