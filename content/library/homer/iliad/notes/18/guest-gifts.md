---
summary: Charis offers Thetis “good cheer” (ξείνια, 18.387), and Hephaestus tells her to set “fine guest-gifts” before their guest while he puts away his tools (ξεινήϊα, 18.408).
en: [fine guest-gifts]
---

Here the ξείνια are the food and drink set before a guest, as Calypso sets a table before Hermes after the same words (*Odyssey* 5.91–94; see [[xenia]] and [[visit-scene]]).
