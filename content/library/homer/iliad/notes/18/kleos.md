---
summary: Achilles chooses fame: he will lie still when he is dead, “but now may I win noble fame”, and make some Trojan woman wipe her tears and groan without ceasing (18.121–125). The Trojans' allies are “famous” (κλειτοί, 18.229).
---

κλέος ἐσθλόν is the “noble fame” that Achilles said would be lost if he went home (9.415; see [[the-two-fates-of-achilles]]). Here he imagines it in the voices of others: the Trojan and Dardanian women will learn by their own grief that he has come back to the war (18.122–125; see [[lament]] and [[song-and-fame]]).
