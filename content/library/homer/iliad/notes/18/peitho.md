summary: Achilles tells Thetis, “you will not persuade me” (18.126). In the Trojan assembly Polydamas asks the Trojans to do as he says (18.266, 18.273), and Hector answers that none of them will obey him, and that they should all do as Hector says (18.296–297). Hector stands over the body “trusting in his courage” (18.158), and on the Shield the besieged “would not yet yield” (18.513).
