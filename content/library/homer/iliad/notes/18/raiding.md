summary: The ambushers on the Shield fall on the herds at the river: they “cut off the herds of cattle and the fine flocks / of white sheep, and killed the shepherds as well” (18.528–529). Achilles and Patroclus took their slave women in their raids (18.28), and sacked rich cities together (18.342).
grc: ["τάμνοντʼ ἀμφὶ βοῶν"]
en: [cut off the herds]
refs: [18.28, 18.342]
---

Walter Leaf takes τάμνοντʼ ἀμφί as “intercepted”, cut off on both sides of the path, and compares the cattle raid of *Odyssey* 11.402 and the *Hymn to Hermes* 74 (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 18.528). See [[ambush]], [[captive-women]] and [[achilles-raids]].
