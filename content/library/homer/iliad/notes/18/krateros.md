---
summary: Thetis bore “a son, noble and strong”; at sunset the Achaeans cease from “the fierce din of battle” and the Trojans withdraw from “the fierce battle”; and Hephaestus takes up “a mighty hammer.”
---

**Achilles.** “for I bore a son, noble and strong” (18.55; [[blameless]], [[thetis]]).

**The end of the day.** “and the sun set, and the brilliant Achaeans ceased / from the fierce din of battle and from war, common to all. / And the Trojans on the other side, away from the fierce battle, / withdrew” (18.241–244; [[dread-din-of-battle]], [[common-to-all]], [[the-third-day-of-battle]]).

**The forge.** “and took in one hand / a mighty hammer, and in the other he took the tongs” (18.476–477; [[forge]], [[hephaestus]]).
