# Unnamed: "they were in the throng" (18.539), Strife, Tumult and Doom among the fighters.

**On the shield.** In the city at war on the shield of Achilles, the defenders and the besiegers fight beside the river, and the battle has its powers:

{{quote:b47b4ea6-cee9-57e9-bb78-f31ab507c5de}}

“And they were in the throng and fought like living mortals, / and they dragged away the bodies of one another's dead” (18.539–540). ὁμίλεον, which Walter Leaf glosses “joined in the fray”, is rendered “were in the throng”, keeping “the fray” for μόθος (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 18.535; see the translation's note on 18.535–538). Leaf finds that these personified spirits of strife recall the Valkyries of Teutonic mythology, and that they cannot be called alien to epic thought, for Strife has walked the battlefield before (4.440–445, 11.3–14); 18.538 implies a human shape, but in what form the poet conceived them we cannot guess (on 18.535). Strife was set on the aegis with Courage and Onslaught (5.740; see [[aegis]], [[courage]] and [[onslaught]]), and she is “the sister and companion of man-slaying Ares” (4.441; see [[ares]]), who leads the sortie in the same scene with Athena (18.516–519). Here she is in the throng with [[tumult|Tumult]], and Doom drags the dead by the feet (see [[ker-doom]] and [[the-shield-of-achilles]]). The four verses occur almost word for word in the *Shield of Heracles* ascribed to Hesiod, where the powers “rushed about”, ἐθύνεον (*Shield* 156–159; Leaf on 18.535; see [[shield-of-heracles]]).

**The word.** Achilles' wish, as he resolves to go back to the war, uses the common word: “may strife perish from among gods and from among men, / and anger, which sets even a man of much wisdom to rage” (18.107–108; see [[eris]], [[cholos]] and [[achilles]]). Aristotle reports that Heraclitus rebuked the poet for this verse, since there would be no harmony without high and low, nor living things without female and male, which are opposites (*Eudemian Ethics* 7.1, 1235a25–29; Leaf on 18.107; see the translation's note on 18.107).
