---
summary: Achilles had promised Menoetius that Patroclus would come home “when he had sacked Ilios and won his share of the spoil” (λαχόντα τε ληΐδος αἶσαν, 18.327).
en: [share]
---

The share of the spoil is the portion each man takes when the plunder is divided (see [[division-of-spoils]]). The fate that undoes the promise is spoken in another word of allotment, πέπρωται, “it is fated” (18.329; see [[moira]] and [[zeus-does-not-fulfill-all-plans]]).
