---
summary: Athena flings “the tasseled aegis” around the shoulders of Achilles at the ditch.
---

“and Athena around / his mighty shoulders flung the tasseled aegis” (18.203–204; [[aegis]], [[athena]], [[mighty]]). Athena slung the aegis on her own shoulders when she armed for battle (5.738), and Zeus took it up to rout the Achaeans (17.593); now a goddess puts it on a man. Walter Leaf refers to his note on the aegis at 2.447 (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 18.204).
