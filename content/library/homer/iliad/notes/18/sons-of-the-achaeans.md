---
summary: Thetis recalls the prayer that “all the sons of the Achaeans” be penned in at the sterns; Iris promises that “the warlike sons of the Achaeans may draw breath”; and Thetis tells Hephaestus of the girl “the sons of the Achaeans picked out” for Achilles.
---

**The prayer.** Thetis to her son: “These things have been fulfilled for you / by Zeus, as you prayed before, lifting up your hands, / that all the sons of the Achaeans should be penned in among the sterns / for want of you, and suffer unseemly deeds” (18.74–77), the prayer of Book 1 (1.408–410; [[plan-of-zeus]]).

**Iris.** “in the hope that in fear of you they may hold off from the war, / the Trojans, and the warlike sons of the Achaeans may draw breath” (18.199–200; [[warlike]]), the words of Nestor to Patroclus and of Patroclus to Achilles (11.799–800, 16.41–42).

**Briseis.** “The girl whom the sons of the Achaeans picked out for him as his prize, / powerful Agamemnon took her back out of his hands” (18.444–445; [[briseis]], [[powerful]]), the verse with which Achilles told the story to Patroclus (16.56).
