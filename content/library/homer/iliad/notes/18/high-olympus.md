---
summary: Thetis sends the Nereids home and says, “and I will go to high Olympus.”
---

“Now go down into the broad bosom of the sea, / to see the Old Man of the Sea and our father's house, / and tell him everything; and I will go to high Olympus, / to Hephaestus, famed for his craft” (18.140–143; [[old-man-of-the-sea]], [[famed-for-his-craft]], [[olympus]]). In Book 1 she went to Olympus for her son's honor; now she goes for his armor (1.420; [[snowy-olympus]]).
