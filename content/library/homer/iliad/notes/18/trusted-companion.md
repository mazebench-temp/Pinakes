---
summary: Achilles weeps when he sees “his trusted companion” on the litter, and Thetis tells Hephaestus that “his trusted companion” lost his armor.
---

“and swift-footed Achilles followed among them / shedding hot tears, when he saw his trusted companion / lying on the litter, torn by the sharp bronze” (18.234–236; [[hot-tears]], [[patroclus]]). Thetis to Hephaestus: “for what he had, his trusted companion lost / when he was brought down by the Trojans” (18.460–461; [[exchange-of-armor]]). The verse-end formula of the companion killed in battle is used of Patroclus by the narrator and by his mother.
