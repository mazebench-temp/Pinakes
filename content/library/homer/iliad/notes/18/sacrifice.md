---
summary: On the king's domain the heralds have sacrificed a great ox for the feast of the reapers (βοῦν δʼ ἱερεύσαντες, 18.559).
grc: [ἱερεύσαντες]
en: [had sacrificed]
---

The ox is slaughtered for the meal, and Walter Leaf asks whether the feast was meant for the king and his men or for the laborers themselves (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 18.560; see [[feast]], [[heralds]] and [[barley-meal]]).
