---
summary: The Achaeans could not have drawn Patroclus “out of range of the missiles” until Achilles showed himself, and then they drew him “out from under the missiles” (18.152, 18.232).
---

The two phrases are formulas of rescue from the fighting (see the translation's formula rows; 14.130, 16.122; 4.465). See [[fighting-over-the-body]].
