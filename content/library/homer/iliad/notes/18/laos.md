summary: λαός is the army and the people. Once more “the army and the horses” of the Trojans catch up with Patroclus' body (18.153), and Achilles sent Patroclus out with “a great army” (18.452). On the Shield the people gather in the place of assembly, cheer both litigants and are held back by the heralds (18.497–503); two armies besiege the other city, the people are smaller than the gods who lead them, and two lookouts sit apart from the army (18.509, 18.519, 18.523). Hector tells the rich to give their goods to the people (18.301).
