---
summary: It is “the gray-eyed goddess Athena” who kindles the fire above the head of Achilles.
---

“And the charioteers were struck with terror when they saw the tireless fire / blazing terribly above the head of the great-hearted son of Peleus; / the gray-eyed goddess Athena kindled it” (18.225–227; [[tireless]], [[athena]]). The verse names the goddess at the end of the scene, after the fire has done its work (18.205–206). See [[pallas]].
