---
summary: The armor of Achilles was “the splendid gifts that the gods gave to Peleus”; and Achilles vows to kill “twelve / splendid children of the Trojans” at Patroclus' pyre.
---

**The armor.** “stripped off the armor, huge, a wonder to see, / and fine, the splendid gifts that the gods gave to Peleus / on the day when they put you into the bed of a mortal man” (18.83–85; [[huge]], [[the-armor-of-peleus]], [[thetis-in-the-house-of-peleus]]).

**The captives.** “and before your pyre I will cut the throats of twelve / splendid children of the Trojans, in my anger over your killing” (18.336–337; [[patroclus]]). The verses are repeated when the promise is kept (23.22–23).
