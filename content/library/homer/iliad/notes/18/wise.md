---
summary: In the Trojan assembly “wise Polydamas” speaks first, “for he alone saw before and after.”
---

“Among them wise Polydamas began to speak, / the son of Panthous; for he alone saw before and after” (18.249–250; [[polydamas]], [[panthous]], [[before-and-after]]). The epithet of sensible speakers among the Trojan elders is given to the man who “excelled far in words” (18.252), and whose good counsel no one approved (18.313).
