grc: ["=δειλή"]
---

“Ah me, wretched that I am, ah me, unhappy mother of the best of men” (18.54; [[thetis]], [[lament]]). The address δειλέ, “wretch,” is turned on the speaker herself; δυσαριστοτόκεια, “unhappy mother of the best of men,” occurs only here (see the translation's note on 18.54). The form δειλή is new to the entry; the accent-sensitive pattern keeps out δείλη, “afternoon,” which has the same letters.
