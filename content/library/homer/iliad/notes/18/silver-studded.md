---
summary: Charis seats Thetis “on a silver-studded throne.”
---

“Then she sat her down on a silver-studded throne, / fine and intricately worked; and there was a footstool under her feet” (18.389–390; [[throne]], [[intricately-worked]], [[xenia]]). Elsewhere in the Iliad the epithet belongs to the sword; the silver-studded throne for a guest is a verse of the Odyssey, where Circe seats Odysseus in the same words (*Odyssey* 10.314–315).
