---
summary: The maidens who dance on the shield are maidens “who would bring many oxen.”
---

“And on it the renowned god, lame in both legs, inlaid a dancing floor, / like the one that once in wide Cnossus / Daedalus fashioned for Ariadne of the lovely tresses. / There young men and maidens who would bring many oxen / were dancing, holding one another's hands by the wrist” (18.590–594; [[the-dancing-floor-of-ariadne]], [[dance]], [[bride-price]]). The girls of marriageable age are valued by the bride-price their suitors will pay (see the entry).
