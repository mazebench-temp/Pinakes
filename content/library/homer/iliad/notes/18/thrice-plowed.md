---
summary: The fallow field on the shield is “broad, thrice plowed,” with many plowmen turning their teams on it.
---

“And on it he set a soft fallow field, rich plowland, / broad, thrice plowed; and many plowmen on it / were wheeling their teams and driving them this way and that” (18.541–543; [[soft]], [[the-plowing]]). The plowmen are rewarded at each turn with a cup of wine, and the gold of the field grows dark behind them like plowed earth, “a wonder beyond all” (18.544–549; [[honey-sweet]]).
