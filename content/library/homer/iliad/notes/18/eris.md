---
summary: Achilles wishes that strife might perish: “may strife perish from among gods and from among men, / and anger” (18.107–108). The wish answers Agamemnon's taunt in Book 1, that strife was always dear to him (1.177).
---

“strife is always dear to you, and wars and battles” (1.177; see [[strife-is-always-dear]]). The wish is a ὡς with the optative, “how I wish that strife might perish” (see the translation's note on 18.107). Strife herself, Ἔρις, appears in the throng of the battle on the Shield (18.535; see [[strife]] and [[the-two-cities]]). See [[cholos]].
