---
summary: The house of Hephaestus is “starry,” probably adorned with starlike ornaments.
---

“imperishable, starry, standing out among the immortals” (18.370; [[imperishable]], [[house]], [[hephaestus]]). The epithet of the sky is given to the god's house; Walter Leaf takes it as adorned with star-like ornaments, as at 16.134, and compares the decoration of the great tomb at Mycenae called the Treasury of Atreus (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 18.370; see the translation's note on 18.370).
