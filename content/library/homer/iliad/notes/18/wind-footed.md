---
summary: Iris is “swift Iris, wind-footed,” as she comes from Olympus to rouse Achilles, and twice as she answers him.
---

“had not swift Iris, wind-footed, to the son of Peleus / come running from Olympus as a messenger, to arm, / in secret from Zeus and the other gods; for Hera had sent her” (18.166–168; [[iris]], [[hera]]). Twice the formula introduces her answers: “Then swift Iris, wind-footed, spoke to him in turn” (18.183, 18.196), telling him who sent her and that he must go to the ditch as he is. When she leaves she is “swift-footed Iris” (18.202; [[swift-footed]]).
