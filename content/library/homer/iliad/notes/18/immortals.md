---
summary: Achilles wishes that Thetis had stayed “among the immortal goddesses of the sea,” and will accept his death when Zeus “and the other immortal gods” will it; the house of Hephaestus stands out “among the immortals.”
---

**Achilles.** “If only you had stayed there among the immortal goddesses of the sea, / and Peleus had taken a mortal wife!” (18.86–87; [[mortal-men]], [[thetis-in-the-house-of-peleus]]); and “I will accept my doom then, whenever / Zeus is willing to bring it to pass, and the other immortal gods” (18.115–116; [[ker-doom]]).

**The gods.** Iris: “and the son of Cronus, throned on high, does not know, nor any other / of the immortals who dwell around snowy Olympus” (18.185–186; [[snowy-olympus]]). Hera to Zeus: “you are lord among all the immortals” (18.366; [[hera]]).

**Hephaestus.** His house is “imperishable, starry, standing out among the immortals” (18.370; [[standing-out]]), and his golden handmaids “are skilled in handiwork from the immortal gods” (18.420; [[the-golden-handmaids]]).
