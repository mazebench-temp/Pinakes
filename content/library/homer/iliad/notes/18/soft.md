---
summary: On the shield Hephaestus sets “a soft fallow field, rich plowland”; the book adds νειὸν μαλακήν.
grc: ["νειὸν μαλακὴν"]
---

“And on it he set a soft fallow field, rich plowland, / broad, thrice plowed; and many plowmen on it / were wheeling their teams and driving them this way and that” (18.541–543; [[thrice-plowed]], [[the-plowing]]). The soft earth of the fallow, broken by repeated plowing, is the first of the scenes of country life. The phrase is new to the entry.
