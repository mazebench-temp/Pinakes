summary: The Shield's two cities are made of households: in the city at peace brides are led from their chambers and the women watch from their doors (18.491–496), and in the city at war the wives, children and old men keep the wall while the men go out (18.514–515). Polydamas warns that Achilles will fight for the Trojans' city and their women, and Hector complains that the treasures of their houses are gone (18.265, 18.290).
