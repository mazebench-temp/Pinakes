---
summary: Patroclus is covered on his bier with a soft linen cloth “from head to foot, and over it with a white cloak” (18.352–353). On the Shield Doom wears a cloak “blood-red with the blood of men” (18.538).
grc: [φάρεϊ, "εἷμα δʼ ἔχʼ ἀμφʼ ὤμοισι"]
---

φᾶρος is the mantle of fine cloth in which the dead are wrapped, as Hector's body is wrapped in a fine cloak and a tunic before it is given back (24.588; see [[honoring-the-dead]]). Doom's garment is εἷμα, “clothing”, which the translation renders “cloak” (18.538; see [[ker-doom]]).
