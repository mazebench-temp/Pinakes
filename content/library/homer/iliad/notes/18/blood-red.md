---
summary: The cloak of Doom on the shield is “blood-red with the blood of men.”
---

“and the cloak on her shoulders was blood-red with the blood of men” (18.538; [[ker-doom]], [[deadly]], [[the-shield-of-achilles]]). The lengthened form δαφοινεόν is found only here in Homer; the verse recurs in the Hesiodic *Shield of Heracles* (159).
