summary: Breasts are beaten in mourning, by the slave women at the news (18.31) and by the Nereids in their cave (18.51), and Achilles leads the lament with his hands on the breast of his dead companion (18.317). In Achilles' wish, anger swells “in the breasts of men like smoke” (18.110), and he will tame his heart “in our breasts under compulsion” (18.113). Hephaestus sponges his shaggy chest before he greets Thetis (18.415).
