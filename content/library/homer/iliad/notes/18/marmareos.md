---
summary: The rim of the shield is “threefold, gleaming.”
---

“And first he made a shield, great and heavy, / working it intricately all over, and he set around it a shining rim, / threefold, gleaming, and from it a silver strap” (18.478–480; [[phaeinos]], [[the-shield-of-achilles]]). What the threefold rim was is not clear; Walter Leaf takes τρίπλαξ as a form of τριπλός, “threefold,” and the rim as a real part of the shield (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 18.480; see the translation's note on 18.480).
