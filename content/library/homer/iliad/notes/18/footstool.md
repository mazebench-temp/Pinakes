---
summary: Thetis is seated in the house of Hephaestus on a silver-studded throne, “and there was a footstool under her feet” (18.390).
grc: [θρῆνυς]
---

The verse is used of the honored guest in the Odyssey, of Athena as Mentes in Telemachus' house and of Helen in her own (*Odyssey* 1.131, 4.136; see [[visit-scene]] and [[throne]]).
