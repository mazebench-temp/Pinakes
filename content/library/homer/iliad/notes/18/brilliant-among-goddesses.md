---
summary: Athena, “brilliant among goddesses,” crowns Achilles with a golden cloud; Charis, “brilliant among goddesses,” leads Thetis into the house of Hephaestus.
---

**Athena.** “and around his head she set a cloud as a crown, brilliant among goddesses, / a golden one, and from him she kindled a flame, gleaming all over” (18.205–206; [[athena]], [[gleaming-all-over]]).

**Charis.** “So she spoke, and led her in, brilliant among goddesses” (18.388; [[charis-wife-of-hephaestus]]), as she welcomes Thetis as a guest ([[xenia]]). See [[brilliant]].
