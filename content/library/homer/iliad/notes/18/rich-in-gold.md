---
summary: Hector says that men once called the city of Priam “rich in gold, rich in bronze”; the book adds πολύχρυσον.
grc: [πολύχρυσον]
---

“Once all mortal men used to speak of the city of Priam / as rich in gold, rich in bronze; / but now the fine treasures of our houses are gone” (18.288–290; [[brazen]], [[meropes]], [[troy]]). The epithet of Mycenae is given to Troy as it was, before the war spent its wealth (see the translation's note on 18.288–292). The accusative πολύχρυσον is new to the entry.
