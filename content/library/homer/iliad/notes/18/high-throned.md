---
summary: Iris says that “the son of Cronus, throned on high,” does not know that Hera sent her.
---

“Hera sent me, the glorious wife of Zeus; / and the son of Cronus, throned on high, does not know” (18.184–185; [[kydros]], [[son-of-cronus]]). The god who rules from on high is, for once, kept in the dark. The epithet stood beside Κρονίδης in Book 4 (4.166) and Book 7 (7.69). See [[zeus]].
