**Polydamas.** “but his heart will not let him rush in, / nor will he ever sack it; before that the swift dogs will eat him” (18.282–283; [[dogs]], [[polydamas]]). The dogs of the battlefield are κύνες ἀργοί, as in the plague of Book 1 (1.50).

**The herdsmen.** On the shield, “and nine dogs, fleet of foot, followed them” (18.578), and when the lions seize the bull, “the herdsmen / were setting on their swift dogs and urging them, for nothing; / for the dogs shrank back from biting the lions” (18.583–585; [[the-herd-and-the-lions]], [[lion]], [[cattle]]). κύνες πόδας ἀργοί, “dogs, fleet of foot,” occurs only here. Both phrases are new to the entry.
