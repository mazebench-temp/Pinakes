---
summary: In the simile of the beacon fires the men of a besieged island town “try their strength in the hateful work of Ares” all day long.
---

“And as when smoke goes up from a city and reaches the upper air / far off, from an island that enemies are fighting around, / and all day long the men try their strength in the hateful work of Ares / from their own city” (18.207–210; [[the-beacon-fires]], [[ares]], [[destructive]]). The phrase is Agamemnon's in Book 2, “so that all day long we may try our strength in the hateful work of Ares” (2.385).
