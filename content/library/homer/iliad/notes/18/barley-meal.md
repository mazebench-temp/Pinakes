---
summary: On the king's domain the women sprinkle much white barley meal for the laborers' dinner (18.560).
---

Walter Leaf reports two explanations of πάλυνον: making porridge by sprinkling meal into water, as Hecamede thickens the drink for Nestor (11.640), or sprinkling it on the roast meat, as in the Odyssey (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 18.560; *Odyssey* 14.77, 14.429). See [[feast]].
