---
summary: The Nereids go with Thetis from the cave “in tears”; the book adds δακρυόεσσαι.
grc: [δακρυόεσσαι]
---

“So she spoke and left the cave; and they went with her / in tears, and around them the wave of the sea / broke” (18.65–67; [[nereids]], [[thetis]], [[lament]]). The plural δακρυόεσσαι is new to the entry, which has the word of Andromache, “laughing through her tears” (δακρυόεν γελάσασα, 6.484).
