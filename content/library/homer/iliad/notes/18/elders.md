---
summary: Thetis tells Hephaestus that the elders of the Argives begged Achilles to return and named many glorious gifts (18.448–449), the embassy of Book 9. On the Shield the elders of the city at peace sit in judgment (18.503–506).
refs: [18.448-449]
---

The elders on the Shield are the elders of a city, not the Achaean council: they sit on polished stones in the sacred circle, take the heralds' scepters and rise in turn to judge (see [[the-trial-on-the-shield]] and [[geron]]). See [[the-embassy-to-achilles]].
