---
summary: Achilles ends his curse on anger with Agamemnon's title: “so has Agamemnon, lord of men, angered me now.”
---

“may strife perish from among gods and from among men, / and anger … / so has Agamemnon, lord of men, angered me now” (18.107–111; [[cholos]], [[of-much-wisdom]], [[agamemnon]]). The title of the commander of the army stands beside the anger he caused. Later in the book Polydamas calls him “brilliant Agamemnon” (18.257) and Thetis “powerful Agamemnon” (18.445; [[brilliant]], [[powerful]]).
