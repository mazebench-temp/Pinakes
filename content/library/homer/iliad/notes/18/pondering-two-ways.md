---
summary: Achilles ponders the meaning of the Achaeans' flight “in his mind and in his heart”, and fears that his mother's prophecy has been fulfilled, while Antilochus comes with the news (18.4–16).
---

Here the pondering is not a choice between two courses but a foreboding: “pondering in his heart things that had now been fulfilled” (18.4; see [[prophecy]] and [[deeply-troubled]]).
