---
summary: On the Shield two herdsmen walk with their herds to the river, “taking their delight in their pipes”, and do not see the ambush (18.525–526).
grc: [σύριγξι]
en: [pipes]
---

Walter Leaf notes that the only other Homeric mention of σύριγγες as instruments is the night of Book 10 (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 18.526; 10.13). The herdsmen's music is the peace the ambushers break (see [[ambush]] and [[the-two-cities]]).
