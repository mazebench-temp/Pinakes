---
summary: Thetis leaps “like a hawk” down from “snowy Olympus” with the armor.
---

“And she like a hawk leapt down from snowy Olympus, / carrying the gleaming armor from Hephaestus” (18.616–617; [[like-a-hawk]], [[marmairon]], [[olympus]]). The last scene of the book uses νιφόεις of Olympus, where Iris spoke of ἀγάννιφος Ὄλυμπος, the other word for “snowy” (18.186; [[snowy-olympus]]).
