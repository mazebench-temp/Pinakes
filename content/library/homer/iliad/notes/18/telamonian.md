---
summary: Achilles could wear no armor but “the shield of Ajax, son of Telamon.”
---

“And I know of no other man whose glorious armor I could put on, / unless it were the shield of Ajax, son of Telamon. / But he himself, I think, is in the throng among the foremost” (18.192–194; [[the-shield-of-ajax]], [[ajax]], [[telamon]]). Walter Leaf refers it to the shield “like a tower” that Ajax carried in Book 7 (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 18.193; 7.219–223).
