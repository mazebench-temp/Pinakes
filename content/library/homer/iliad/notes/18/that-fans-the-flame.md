---
summary: The bellows of Hephaestus send out “every kind of blast that fans the flame.”
---

“And the bellows, twenty in all, blew on the crucibles, / sending out every kind of blast that fans the flame, / now to be at hand for him as he hurried, and now otherwise” (18.470–472; [[forge]], [[hephaestus]], [[the-making-of-the-arms]]). Walter Leaf takes παντοίην as of every degree of force (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 18.471).
