---
summary: Antilochus is “the son of noble Nestor” as he comes to Achilles with the news.
---

“While he was pondering this in his mind and in his heart, / the son of noble Nestor came near him, / shedding hot tears, and told his grievous news” (18.15–17; [[nestor]], [[antilochus]], [[hot-tears]], [[grievous]]). The son is named by his father, and it was the father who had urged Patroclus to go out in Achilles' armor (11.796–803).
