---
summary: Patroclus is “my dear companion” and “that dear life” (18.80, 18.114), and Heracles was “the dearest of all” to Zeus (18.118). Thetis comes to see “my dear child” (18.63), whom she cannot keep from the battle “though you love me” (18.126); Achilles rises “dear to Zeus” (18.203); Polydamas addresses the Trojans as “friends” (18.254); and Charis and Hephaestus greet Thetis as “respected and dear” (18.386, 18.425).
