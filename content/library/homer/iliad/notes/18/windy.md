---
summary: Iris tells Achilles that the Trojans strive to drag the body “to windy Ilios.”
---

“the one side defending the corpse of the dead man, / and the other side striving to drag it off to windy Ilios, / the Trojans” (18.173–175; [[troy]], [[the-fight-for-the-body-of-patroclus]]). προτὶ Ἴλιον ἠνεμόεσσαν is the verse-end formula of going back to the city (3.305, 8.499, 13.724).
