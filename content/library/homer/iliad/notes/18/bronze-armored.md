---
summary: Achilles calls himself such as none “of the bronze-armored Achaeans / in war.”
---

“but I sit idly beside the ships, a burden on the earth, / I, who am such as none of the bronze-armored Achaeans / in war, though in the assembly others too are better” (18.104–106; [[achaeans]], [[achilles]]). The scholiast defends the self-praise as the custom of all (see the translation's note on 18.105–106).
