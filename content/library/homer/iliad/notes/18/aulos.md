---
summary: At the weddings on the Shield “flutes and lyres kept up their sound” among the dancing young men (18.495).
grc: [αὐλοὶ]
en: [flutes]
---

Walter Leaf reports the ancient observation that the poet mentions auloi only here and in the night of Book 10, where Agamemnon hears “the sound of flutes and pipes” from the Trojan camp (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 18.495; 10.13). See [[lyre]], [[marriage]] and [[dance]].
