---
summary: Hector is “shining Hector” as he seizes the body of Patroclus three times by the feet, and in Iris' words, bent on dragging it away.
---

“Three times shining Hector seized him from behind by the feet, / eager to drag him off” (18.155–156), and Iris: “and above all shining Hector / is bent on dragging it; and his heart bids him fix the head / on the stakes” (18.175–177; [[tender]], [[the-fight-for-the-body-of-patroclus]]). The formula φαίδιμος Ἕκτωρ frames the most savage of Hector's intentions in the book. See [[hector]].
