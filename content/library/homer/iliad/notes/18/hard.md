---
summary: Achilles calls Patroclus “hard man!” for not coming back once he had thrust the fire from the ships.
---

“Surely now the valiant son of Menoetius is dead, / hard man! And yet I bade him, once he had thrust off the consuming fire, / to come back to the ships, and not to fight Hector in force” (18.12–14; [[patroclus]], [[destructive]]). Walter Leaf renders σχέτλιος “headstrong” here (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 18.13). The orders were given when Patroclus set out: “When you have driven them from the ships, come back” (16.87), and “turn back, once you have brought light to the ships” (16.95).
