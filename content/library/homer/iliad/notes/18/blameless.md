---
summary: Thetis laments that she bore “a son, noble and strong, / preeminent among heroes.”
---

“for I bore a son, noble and strong, / preeminent among heroes; and he shot up like a sapling” (18.55–56; [[krateros]], [[like-a-sapling]], [[thetis]], [[achilles]]). The praise of the son is the measure of the mother's grief; the next verses, of the plant reared on the slope of an orchard, she repeats to Hephaestus (18.56–57 = 18.437–438; [[mothers-and-sons]]).
