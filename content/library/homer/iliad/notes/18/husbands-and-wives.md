summary: Thetis' marriage to Peleus was forced on her: Zeus subdued her to a mortal man, and she endured his bed against her will; now he lies worn out by old age in his halls (18.85–87, 18.432–435). On Olympus Zeus taunts Hera, “his sister and wife” (18.356–367), Hephaestus' wife is Charis (18.382–383), and on the Shield the brides are led through the city and the wives guard the wall (18.491–496, 18.514).
