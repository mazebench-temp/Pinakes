---
summary: The plowmen on the shield are given “a cup of honey-sweet wine” at each turn, and at the vintage the young carry “the honey-sweet fruit” in baskets.
---

**The plowing.** “And whenever they turned and reached the end of the field, / then a cup of honey-sweet wine would be put into their hands / by a man who came up to them” (18.544–546; [[the-plowing]], [[wine]]).

**The vintage.** “And girls and young men, with light hearts, / were carrying the honey-sweet fruit in woven baskets” (18.567–568; [[the-vintage]], [[of-tender-mind]], [[woven]]). The epithet of wine is given to the grapes from which it will be made.
