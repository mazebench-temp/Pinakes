summary: Achilles promised Menoetius that Patroclus would come home “when he had sacked Ilios and won his share of the spoil” (18.327), and Thetis tells Hephaestus that Briseis was the prize the army picked out for Achilles (18.444).
grc: ["ληΐδος αἶσαν"]
en: [his share of the spoil]
---

λαχόντα τε ληΐδος αἶσαν, “won his share of the spoil” (18.327), names the share each man took when the army divided the plunder; the Odyssey uses the same words of the share Odysseus would have brought home from Troy (*Odyssey* 5.40, 13.138). On the Shield the besiegers debate whether to sack the city or to divide all its wealth in two (18.511–512; see [[the-two-cities]]). See [[aisa]], [[geras]] and [[captive-women]].
