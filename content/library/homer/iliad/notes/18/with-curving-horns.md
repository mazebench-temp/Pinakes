---
summary: The ambush on the shield waits for “the sheep and the cattle with curving horns.”
---

“And for them two lookouts sat apart from the army, / waiting for when they should see the sheep and the cattle with curving horns” (18.523–524; [[ambush]], [[cattle]], [[the-shield-of-achilles]]). ἕλικας βοῦς stands here without εἰλίποδας, its companion in the formula of the entry (9.466). The ἕλικες that Hephaestus made in the cave by Oceanus (18.401) are spirals of jewelry, not this word.
