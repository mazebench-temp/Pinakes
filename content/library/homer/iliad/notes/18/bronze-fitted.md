summary: On the shield the two sides “struck at one another with bronze-fitted spears.”
---

“And they set their battle in array and fought beside the river's banks, / and they struck at one another with bronze-fitted spears” (18.533–534; [[spear]], [[the-shield-of-achilles]]). The verse-end χαλκήρεσιν ἐγχείῃσιν returns when Aeneas challenges Achilles (20.258).
