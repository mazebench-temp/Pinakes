---
summary: Hector recalls that men called Troy “rich in gold, rich in bronze.”
---

“Once all mortal men used to speak of the city of Priam / as rich in gold, rich in bronze” (18.288–289; [[rich-in-gold]], [[troy]]). The pair of epithets describes the city's wealth in the past; Hector goes on to its treasures “sold away” to Phrygia and Maeonia (18.290–292; [[lovely]]).
