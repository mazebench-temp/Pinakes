---
summary: As Antilochus weeps, holding his hands, Achilles “groaned in his glorious heart” (18.33); on the Shield the king stands in silence by the swath, “glad at heart” (18.557).
---

Walter Leaf takes Achilles as the subject of ἔστενε, and counts the changes of subject in 18.33–35 among the reasons that led Bentley and others to reject 18.34 (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 18.34; see the translation's note on 18.34). See [[kydalimos]] and [[basileus]].
