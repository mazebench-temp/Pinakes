---
summary: Achilles has the companions heat water to wash “the clotted blood” from Patroclus.
---

“So saying, brilliant Achilles called to his companions / to set a great tripod over the fire, so that with all speed / they might wash the clotted blood from Patroclus” (18.343–345; [[clotted-blood]], [[bathing]], [[tripod]]). ἄπο βρότον αἱματόεντα is the verse-end of washing the wounded and the dead (7.425, 14.7).
