**Far from the wall.** Polydamas bids the Trojans “go now to the city, and not wait for brilliant Dawn / on the plain beside the ships; we are far from the wall” (18.255–256; see [[polydamas]]). They have spent a night on the plain once before, after their victory in Book 8, when Hector left the city to the boys and the old men, who were to keep watch from the god-built towers (8.517–522; see [[night-watch]]).

**The towers and the gates.**

{{quote:1eaa6b42-91e2-59fc-bfdb-b65d3cb2ea5c}}

Walter Leaf takes the σανίδες, “doors”, as boards, perhaps a covering fastened on the gates for extra security against assault, and notes that ἐζευγμέναι, “bolted fast”, seems to be the only place in Homer where the verb is used of anything but yoking (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 18.275, 18.276). Achilles, Polydamas goes on, may give his horses their fill of running beneath the city, but his heart will not let him rush in, “nor will he ever sack it” (18.280–283; see [[troy]]).

**Penned in.** Hector answers, “Have you not had your fill yet of being penned within the towers?” (18.287; see [[hector]]), and at dawn he will lead the army to the ships (18.303–304). The next day Polydamas' plan is carried out in flight: Priam has the gatekeepers hold the gates open for the routed army and then set back the close-fitted doors, σανίδας … ἀραρυίας, as in Polydamas' words (21.526–537; 18.275; see [[priam]]), and the Trojans rest on the battlements while Hector alone waits outside, held there by his fate before the Scaean gates (22.1–6; see [[scaean-gates]]). Achilles does fight around the wall: he chases Hector three times around the city of Priam (22.136–166).

**The stakes.** Iris tells Achilles that Hector means to fix Patroclus' head “on the stakes” (18.177). Of the explanations offered, poles to carry the head about or the palisade of the Achaean ditch, Walter Leaf prefers a palisade along the top of the Trojan wall, such as crowns the walls of the Phaeacians' town (*Odyssey* 7.45; on 18.177; see the translation's note on 18.177).
