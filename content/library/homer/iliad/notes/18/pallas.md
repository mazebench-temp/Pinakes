---
summary: “Pallas Athena” cries out from the ditch beside Achilles, takes away the Trojans' wits in their assembly, and on the shield leads one army with Ares.
---

**The ditch.** “There he stood and shouted, and apart from him Pallas Athena / cried out” (18.217–218; [[athena]]).

**The Trojan assembly.** “So Hector spoke, and the Trojans shouted in applause, / fools; for Pallas Athena had taken away their wits” (18.310–311; [[nepios]], [[polydamas]]).

**The shield.** “and the others went; and Ares led them and Pallas Athena, / both of gold, and clad in golden clothing” (18.516–517; [[ares]], [[the-shield-of-achilles]]).
