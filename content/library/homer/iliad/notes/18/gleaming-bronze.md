---
summary: The water for Patroclus' bath boils “in the gleaming bronze” of the tripod; and on the shield the ambush sits “wrapped in gleaming bronze.”
---

**The tripod.** “The fire played around the belly of the tripod, and the water grew warm; / and when the water had boiled in the gleaming bronze, / then they washed him” (18.348–350; [[tripod]], [[bathing]]). ἤνοπι χαλκῷ is the phrase of the fisherman's hook in Book 16 (16.408); here the bronze is a cauldron.

**The ambush.** “there they sat down, wrapped in gleaming bronze” (18.522; [[ambush]], [[the-shield-of-achilles]]). αἴθοπι χαλκῷ usually ends the verse of a warrior striding through the champions, κεκορυθμένος αἴθοπι χαλκῷ (4.495, 17.3).
