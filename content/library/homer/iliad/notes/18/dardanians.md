# Δαρδανίδες, "the Dardanian women", beside Τρωϊάδες and Τρῳαί, the Trojan women (trojan-women); only here in
# Homer. Δαρδανίδης, Δαρδανίδῃ (dardanus) are a man of Dardanus' line.
grc: [Δαρδανίδων, Δαρδανίδες]
---

Τρωϊάδων καὶ Δαρδανίδων βαθυκόλπων (18.122) and Τρῳαὶ καὶ Δαρδανίδες βαθύκολποι (18.339), “the Trojan and the deep-bosomed Dardanian women”: the women's pair answers the men's, “Trojans and Dardanians” (Τρῶες καὶ Δάρδανοι, 3.456, 7.348, 7.368, 8.497; see [[trojans]], [[trojan-women]] and [[deep-bosomed]]). Paired with Τρωϊάδες, the women of the Trojans, Δαρδανίδες names the women of the Dardanians; the masculine Δαρδανίδης, “descendant of Dardanus”, names Priam (3.303; see [[dardanus]]).

Achilles would win fame by making some woman among them wipe the tears from her cheeks and groan without ceasing (18.121–125; see [[achilles]] and [[kleos]]). At 18.339 they are captives, women of the Troad whom Achilles and Patroclus won by the spear when they sacked its cities, and who shall weep around Patroclus night and day (18.339–342; see [[captive-women]] and [[patroclus]]).
