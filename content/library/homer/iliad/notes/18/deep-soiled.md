---
summary: The Nereids come up out of the sea to “deep-soiled Troy.”
---

“and when they came to deep-soiled Troy / they went up onto the shore one after another” (18.67–68; [[troy]], [[nereids]]). The epithet of the rich land is given to Troy also when Achilles counts the cities he sacked on foot in the land of Troy (9.329), and in the longer form ἐριβῶλαξ in the terms of the duel (3.74, 3.257).
