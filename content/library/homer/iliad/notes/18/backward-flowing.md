---
summary: Hephaestus names Eurynome, who with Thetis saved him, “daughter of backward-flowing Oceanus.”
---

“had not Eurynome and Thetis taken me to their bosom, / Eurynome, daughter of backward-flowing Oceanus” (18.398–399; [[eurynome]], [[oceanus]], [[fall-of-hephaestus]]). Walter Leaf thinks Payne Knight right to expel the line as a gloss, since the repetition of the first of two names is very unusual (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 18.399; see the translation's note on 18.399).
