---
summary: The house of Hephaestus is “standing out among the immortals.”
---

“imperishable, starry, standing out among the immortals, / of bronze, which the crook-footed god had built for himself” (18.370–371; [[imperishable]], [[starry]], [[house]]). The bronze house of the craftsman stands out even among the houses of the gods, which he built himself (1.607–608).
