---
summary: The lions on the shield gulp down “the entrails and the black blood” of the bull.
---

“The two had torn open the hide of the great bull / and were gulping down the entrails and the black blood” (18.582–583; [[the-herd-and-the-lions]], [[lion]], [[bellowing]]). The lion of the similes gulps down “her blood and all her entrails” in the same words (11.176 = 17.64).
