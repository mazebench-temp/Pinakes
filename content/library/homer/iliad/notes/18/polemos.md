---
summary: War is what Achilles has been away from and returns to. Thetis goes to learn what grief has come to him “while he stays away from the war” (18.64); he is such as none of the Achaeans “in war” (18.106), and he wants the Trojans to know that he has long ceased from the war (18.125), as they do in their assembly (18.248). Iris hopes the Trojans may hold off from the war in fear of him, since brief is the breathing space in war (18.199–201), and Hector will not flee “out of grim-sounding war” (18.307).
