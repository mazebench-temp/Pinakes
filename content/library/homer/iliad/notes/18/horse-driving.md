---
summary: Achilles knows he will not come home to be welcomed by “the old horse-driving Peleus.”
---

“for it is fated that both of us redden the same earth / here in Troy, since I too shall not come home / to be welcomed in his halls by the old horse-driving Peleus, / nor by my mother Thetis, but the earth will hold me here” (18.329–332; [[peleus]], [[death-of-achilles-foretold]], [[nostos]]). The verse-end γέρων ἱππηλάτα Πηλεύς names the father who sent his son to Troy (9.438, 11.772) and who would groan aloud at the Achaeans' cowardice (7.125).
