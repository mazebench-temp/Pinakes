---
summary: Thetis is “silver-footed Thetis” as she answers her son, goes to Olympus, comes to the house of Hephaestus and comes near the god at his work.
---

“Then the goddess silver-footed Thetis answered him” (18.127), and “she, the goddess silver-footed Thetis, went to Olympus / to bring glorious armor to her dear son” (18.146–147). “And silver-footed Thetis came to the house of Hephaestus” (18.369), and while he worked at the tripods, “the goddess silver-footed Thetis came near him” (18.381; [[hephaestus]]). The standing epithet marks each stage of her journey for the armor ([[thetis]], [[the-making-of-the-arms]]).
