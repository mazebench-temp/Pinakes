---
summary: The men of the besieged city mount “behind their high-stepping horses” when they hear the din among the cattle; the book adds ἀερσιπόδων.
grc: [ἀερσιπόδων]
---

“And they, when they heard the great din among the cattle / as they sat in front of their places of assembly, at once mounted / behind their high-stepping horses and went after them, and quickly reached them” (18.530–532; [[horses]], [[the-shield-of-achilles]]). The genitive ἀερσιπόδων is new to the entry. Walter Leaf compares the word in explaining ἠερόφωνος, which Döderlein derived from the same ἀείρω, “lift” (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 18.505; [[far-voiced]]).
