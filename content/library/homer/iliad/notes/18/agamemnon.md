---
summary: Agamemnon takes no part in the book, but the quarrel is remembered three times. Achilles names him as the man who angered him, and lets the anger go; Polydamas tells the Trojans that while Achilles nursed his wrath against Agamemnon the Achaeans were easier to fight; and Thetis tells Hephaestus that Agamemnon took back the girl the Achaeans had given her son (18.107–113, 18.257–258, 18.444–450).
---

**The anger let go.** “so has Agamemnon, lord of men, angered me now” (18.111; see [[lord-of-men]] and [[achilles]]). The verse ends Achilles' wish that strife and anger might perish from among gods and men (18.107–110; see [[eris]] and [[cholos]]), and in the next two he puts the anger behind him: “But we will let all this be, as done and past, for all our grief, / taming the dear heart in our breasts under compulsion” (18.112–113). He will say the two verses again to the assembled Achaeans, with Agamemnon present, when he renounces his wrath (19.65–66; see [[menis]] and [[quarrel-of-achilles-and-agamemnon]]).

**In the Trojans' reckoning.** Polydamas dates the Trojans' success to the quarrel: “As long as this man nursed his wrath against brilliant Agamemnon, / so long the Achaeans were easier to fight” (18.257–258; see [[polydamas]]). μήνιε, “nursed his wrath”, is the verb of the poem's first word (see [[menis]]).

**In Thetis' story.** Thetis tells Hephaestus the quarrel in two verses that Achilles spoke to Patroclus in Book 16, with “him” where he said “me”: “The girl whom the sons of the Achaeans picked out for him as his prize, / powerful Agamemnon took her back out of his hands” (18.444–445; 16.56, 16.58; see [[briseis]], [[geras]] and [[powerful]]). Then, she says, the Trojans penned the Achaeans at the ships, and “the elders of the Argives” begged Achilles and named many glorious gifts, and he refused (18.446–450; see [[the-embassy-to-achilles]] and [[the-gifts-of-agamemnon]]). In Book 9 the gifts were Agamemnon's, named at his council of the elders, and Nestor chose the envoys (9.89–181; see [[elders]]).
