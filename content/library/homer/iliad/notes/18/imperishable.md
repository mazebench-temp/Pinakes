---
summary: The house of Hephaestus is “imperishable, starry, standing out among the immortals.”
---

“And silver-footed Thetis came to the house of Hephaestus, / imperishable, starry, standing out among the immortals, / of bronze, which the crook-footed god had built for himself” (18.369–371; [[starry]], [[standing-out]], [[crook-footed]], [[house]]). The builder of the gods' houses (1.607–608) has built his own, of bronze that does not decay.
