summary: The handles of Hephaestus' tripods, the throne on which Charis seats Thetis, and the helmet of Achilles are “intricately worked”; the book adds δαιδάλεα, δαιδαλέου and δαιδαλέην.
grc: [δαιδάλεα, δαιδαλέου, δαιδαλέην]
---

**The tripods.** “They were finished so far, but the handles were not yet / fixed on, intricately worked; these he was fitting, and forging the rivets” (18.378–379; [[tripod]], [[hephaestus]]).

**The throne.** “Then she sat her down on a silver-studded throne, / fine and intricately worked; and there was a footstool under her feet” (18.389–390; [[silver-studded]], [[throne]], [[xenia]]).

**The helmet.** “and he made him a heavy helmet fitted to his temples, / fine and intricately worked, and set a golden crest on it” (18.611–612; [[heavy-helmet]], [[helmet]]). The god's work is δαίδαλα πολλά, “many intricate designs,” on the shield and in the cave of Thetis and Eurynome (18.400, 18.482). The three forms are new to the entry.
