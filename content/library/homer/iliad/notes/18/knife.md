---
summary: On the Shield the young men of the dance have “knives / of gold, hanging from silver straps” (18.597–598).
grc: [μαχαίρας]
en: [knives]
---

The two lines were athetized, as Aristonicus reports, because Homer never calls a sword μάχαιρα and dancers should not carry knives, and they were not in Aristophanes' text; Walter Leaf finds neither argument weighty, since a knife is not a sword, and dances that act out war need arms (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 18.597; see the translation's note on 18.597–598 and [[dance]]).
