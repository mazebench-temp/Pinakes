---
summary: Hector claims that “the son of crooked-counseling Cronus” has granted him glory at the ships.
---

“But now, when the son of crooked-counseling Cronus has granted me / to win glory at the ships, and to pen the Achaeans against the sea, / fool, no longer show these thoughts among the people” (18.293–295; [[son-of-cronus]], [[cronus]], [[hector]]). Hector relies on the promise of Zeus (11.207–209), which was to last only until sunset on the day that has just ended.
