---
summary: The Achaeans flee to the ships “with a wondrous shout” before Hector.
---

“but the Achaeans, / with a wondrous shout, before man-slaying Hector, / in their flight reached the ships and the Hellespont” (18.148–150; [[man-slaying]], [[hellespont]], [[achaeans]]). θεσπέσιος, “beyond the measure of man,” gives the cry of the routed army its size.
