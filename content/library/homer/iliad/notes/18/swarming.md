**The Dardanian women.** “and make some woman among the Trojan and the deep-bosomed Dardanian women, / with both her hands, from her soft cheeks, / wiping away the tears, groan without ceasing” (18.122–124; [[deep-bosomed]], [[tender]]).

**The lament.** “And among them the son of Peleus led the unceasing lamentation, / laying his man-slaying hands on the breast of his companion” (18.316–317; [[lament]], [[son-of-peleus]], [[man-slaying]]). ἁδινός of groaning and lamentation is “unceasing” in the translation, as it was “throbbing” of the heart in Book 16 (16.481); Walter Leaf renders it “vehement” (see the translation's note on 18.316). The half-verse returns at the funeral (23.17) and in Hecuba's laments for Hector (22.430, 24.747). Both phrases are new to the entry.
