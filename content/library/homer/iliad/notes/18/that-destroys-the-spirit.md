---
summary: In the simile of the trumpet the alarm sounds “because enemies who destroy the spirit surround a city.”
en: [who destroy the spirit]
---

“And as a voice rings out clear, when a trumpet sounds / because enemies who destroy the spirit surround a city, / so clear then rang out the voice of the grandson of Aeacus” (18.219–221; [[the-trumpet]], [[destructive]], [[grandson-of-aeacus]]). The phrase δηΐων ὕπο θυμοραϊστέων is used in the simile of the spear-cast in Book 16 (16.591), and Walter Leaf, on the causal ὕπο, compares it there (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 18.220).
