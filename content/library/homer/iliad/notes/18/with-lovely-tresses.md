---
summary: The last of the named Nereids is “Amatheia of the lovely tresses.”
---

“Maera and Oreithyia and Amatheia of the lovely tresses” (18.48; [[amatheia]], [[nereids]]): the catalogue of the Nereids ends on the epithet that the Odyssey gives above all to goddesses, Calypso and Circe. In the Iliad it is otherwise given to the Trojan women and to serving women (6.380, 11.624).
