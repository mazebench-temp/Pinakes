---
summary: Achilles pours ash over his head, and “the black ash settled on his fragrant tunic”; the book adds νεκταρέῳ.
grc: [νεκταρέῳ]
---

“and taking the sooty dust in both his hands / he poured it down over his head, and fouled his handsome face, / and the black ash settled on his fragrant tunic” (18.23–25; [[tunic]], [[lament]]). νεκτάρεος, the adjective of nectar, is the word of Helen's “fragrant robe” in Book 3 (3.385; [[robe]]); a garment with the fragrance of the gods is fouled with ash. The dative νεκταρέῳ is new to the entry.
