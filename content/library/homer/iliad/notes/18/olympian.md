---
summary: Achilles tells his mother that “the Olympian” has fulfilled his prayer.
---

“My mother, these things the Olympian has indeed fulfilled for me; / but what pleasure have I in them, since my dear companion has perished, / Patroclus” (18.79–81; [[zeus]], [[thetis]]). The prayer that Thetis carried to Zeus in Book 1 has been granted (1.408–412, 18.74–77; [[plan-of-zeus]]), and its fulfillment has cost Achilles his friend.
