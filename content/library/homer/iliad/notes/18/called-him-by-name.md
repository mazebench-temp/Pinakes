---
summary: The whole verse of greeting is said twice in the house of Hephaestus: Charis, and then Hephaestus, “clung to her hand, and spoke to her, and called her by name” before asking Thetis why she has come (18.384, 18.423).
---

The repetition marks the two stages of the reception, by the mistress of the house and by its master (see [[visit-scene]], [[charis-wife-of-hephaestus]] and [[hephaestus]]).
