summary: The Shield is described by anaphora: ἐν μέν … ἐν δέ … ἐν δέ, “On it he made the earth, on it the sky, on it the sea” (18.483), and each new picture begins with ἐν δέ, “and on it” (18.490, 18.541, 18.550, 18.561, 18.573, 18.587, 18.590, 18.607). In the battle of the city at war the same word brings in Strife, Tumult and Doom: ἐν δʼ Ἔρις ἐν δὲ Κυδοιμὸς … ἐν δʼ ὀλοὴ Κήρ (18.535).
refs: [18.483, 18.535]
---

The repeated ἐν, “in it, on it”, sets each scene on the surface of the metal, and the series ends where the Shield does, with Oceanus on the rim (18.607–608; see [[the-shield-of-achilles]]).
