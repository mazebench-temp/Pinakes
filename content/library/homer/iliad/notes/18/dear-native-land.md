---
summary: Achilles, deciding to die, says that he is “not going home to my dear native land.”
---

“May I die at once, since I was not, it seems, to defend my companion / when he was killed; and he, very far from his native land, / perished … / But now, since I am not going home to my dear native land” (18.98–101; [[nostos]], [[the-two-fates-of-achilles]]). Patroclus died “very far from his native land” (τηλόθι πάτρης), and Achilles chooses to die there too. In Book 9 he set out the choice: “but if I come home to my dear native land, / my noble fame is lost, but my life will be long” (9.414–415).
