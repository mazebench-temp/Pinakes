---
summary: Patroclus is twice “the valiant son of Menoetius,” in Achilles' foreboding and in Thetis' account of his death.
---

ἄλκιμος, “valiant,” the word of the stout spear, praises the man himself: “Surely now the valiant son of Menoetius is dead” (18.12), and Thetis tells Hephaestus that Apollo killed him “after the valiant son of Menoetius had done much harm” (18.455; [[son-of-menoetius]], [[patroclus]]).
