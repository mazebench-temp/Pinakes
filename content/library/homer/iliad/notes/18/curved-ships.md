---
summary: Thetis sent her son “with the curved ships to Ilios,” in her lament and again to Hephaestus; and Patroclus is to lie “beside the curved ships” until Achilles has avenged him.
---

Thetis among the Nereids: “I reared him like a plant on the slope of an orchard, / and sent him out with the curved ships to Ilios / to fight with the Trojans” (18.57–59), the verses she repeats to Hephaestus (18.438–440; [[mothers-and-sons]]). Achilles to the dead Patroclus: “Until then you shall lie just as you are beside the curved ships” (18.338; [[patroclus]]). See [[ships]].
