---
summary: Athena flings the aegis around Achilles' “mighty shoulders”; the book adds ἰφθίμοισι.
grc: [ἰφθίμοισι]
---

“and Athena around / his mighty shoulders flung the tasseled aegis” (18.203–204; [[tasseled]], [[aegis]], [[athena]]). The dative plural ἰφθίμοισι is new to the entry, which gives ἴφθιμος to heroes and their heads; here it is the shoulders that carry the aegis of Zeus.
