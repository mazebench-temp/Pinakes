---
summary: At the vintage on the shield a boy plays “a clear-toned lyre” and sings the Linus song.
---

“And in the middle of them a boy on a clear-toned lyre / played lovely music, and to it he sang the beautiful Linus song / in a delicate voice” (18.569–571; [[lyre]], [[delicate]], [[the-linus-song]], [[the-vintage]]). The lyre of Achilles' song in Book 9 (9.186) is given here to a boy among the grape-pickers; in the Odyssey it is the lyre of the bards.
