summary: At Achilles' shout “the fine-maned horses / turned the chariots back; for they foresaw sorrows in their hearts.”
---

“and the fine-maned horses / turned the chariots back; for they foresaw sorrows in their hearts” (18.223–224; [[horses]]). The Trojan horses sense what is coming before their drivers, who are struck with terror only when they see the fire (18.225).
