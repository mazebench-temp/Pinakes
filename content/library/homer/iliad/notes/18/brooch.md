---
summary: In the cave of Thetis and Eurynome Hephaestus forged “brooches and curved spirals, rosettes and necklaces” (πόρπας, 18.401).
grc: [πόρπας]
en: [brooches]
---

Walter Leaf takes πόρπαι as probably the same as περόναι, brooches or buckles, and notes that the rest of the line is copied in the *Homeric Hymn to Aphrodite* (86–89), whose author may not have understood it either (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 18.401; see the translation's note on 18.401). See [[necklace]] and [[fall-of-hephaestus]].
