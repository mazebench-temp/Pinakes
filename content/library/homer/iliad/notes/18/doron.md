summary: Two sets of gifts lie behind the book. The armor Hector took from Patroclus was “the splendid gifts that the gods gave to Peleus / on the day when they put you into the bed of a mortal man” (18.84–85), and the elders of the Argives named “many glorious gifts” when they begged Achilles to return (18.448–449).
