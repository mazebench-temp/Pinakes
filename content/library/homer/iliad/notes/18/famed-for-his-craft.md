---
summary: Thetis goes “to Hephaestus, famed for his craft,” and Charis calls to “Hephaestus, famed for his craft” to come and meet her; the book adds κλυτοτέχνην.
grc: [κλυτοτέχνην]
---

Thetis: “and I will go to high Olympus, / to Hephaestus, famed for his craft, in the hope that he may be willing / to give my son glorious armor, gleaming all over” (18.142–144; [[high-olympus]], [[famous]]). At his house Charis seats her, “and she called to Hephaestus, famed for his craft, and spoke a word” (18.391; [[charis-wife-of-hephaestus]]). The accusative κλυτοτέχνην is new to the entry. See [[hephaestus]].
