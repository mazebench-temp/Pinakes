---
summary: Hephaestus is making twenty tripods “to stand around the wall of his well-founded hall.”
---

“in haste; for he was making tripods, twenty in all, / to stand around the wall of his well-founded hall, / and he had set golden wheels under the base of each” (18.373–375; [[tripod]], [[house]], [[hephaestus]]). The Odyssey's verse-end for the hall of Odysseus is used here of the hall of a god (see the entry).
