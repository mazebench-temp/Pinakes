---
summary: The “well-greaved Achaeans” could not have drawn Patroclus out of range of the missiles but for Iris.
---

“Nor would the well-greaved Achaeans have drawn Patroclus / out of range of the missiles, the corpse of the attendant of Achilles” (18.151–152; [[the-fight-for-the-body-of-patroclus]], [[would-have-had-not]]). The condition is completed only at 18.165–168, when Iris comes; Walter Leaf finds the reference of κε obscure, with its protasis so far off (see the translation's note on 18.151–165). See [[achaeans]].
