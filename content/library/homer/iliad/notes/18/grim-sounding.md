---
summary: Hector will not flee from Achilles “out of grim-sounding war”; Hephaestus wishes he could hide Achilles “from grim-sounding death.”
---

**Hector.** “I at least / will not flee from him out of grim-sounding war, but face to face / I will stand” (18.306–308; [[hector]], [[enyalius]]). The verse is the one in which Eurypylus said of Ajax that he would not escape from grim-sounding war (11.589–590).

**Hephaestus.** “If only I could hide him as surely from grim-sounding death, / away and apart, when dread fate comes upon him, / as he will have fine armor” (18.464–466; [[death-of-achilles-foretold]], [[hephaestus]]). θανάτοιο δυσηχέος is the death from which Zeus wished to free Sarpedon (16.442).
