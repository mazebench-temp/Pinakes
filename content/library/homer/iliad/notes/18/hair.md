---
summary: At the news of Patroclus' death Achilles pours dust over his head and lies in the dust, “and with his own hands he tore his hair and fouled it” (18.27).
grc: [κόμην]
---

The gesture belongs to mourning: Hecuba tears her hair when she sees Hector dragged (22.405–407), and Achilles will cut his own hair for Patroclus at the pyre (23.141–153; see [[lament]] and [[great-over-a-great-space]]).
