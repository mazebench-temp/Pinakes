---
summary: Hephaestus works by twenties: twenty tripods, twenty bellows (18.373, 18.470), and he forged for nine years in Thetis' cave (18.400). Twelve Trojans die at the ditch and twelve are promised to Patroclus' pyre (18.230, 18.336). On the Shield things come in pairs: two cities, two men at law, two talents, two armies, two lookouts, two herdsmen, two lions, two tumblers (18.490–606); and there are five layers to the shield, three sheaf-binders, four herdsmen and nine dogs (18.481, 18.554, 18.578).
---

The triples of the fight over the body and of Achilles' shout have no fourth (18.155–157, 18.228–229; see [[three-times-and-a-fourth]]). The ointment in Patroclus' wounds is “nine years old”, which Walter Leaf takes as a round number for full maturity (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 18.351; see [[bathing]]).
