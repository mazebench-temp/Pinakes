---
summary: The two Ajaxes are “clothed in furious courage” as they drive Hector back from the body.
---

“and three times the two Ajaxes, clothed in furious courage, / drove him back from the body” (18.157–158; [[clothed-in-courage]], [[the-two-ajaxes]]). θοῦρις, the feminine of θοῦρος, is the epithet of courage (ἀλκή) in this formula (7.164, 8.262).
