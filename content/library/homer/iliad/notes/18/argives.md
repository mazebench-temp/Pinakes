summary: The Argives are named once, in Thetis' account of the embassy to Achilles: “the elders of the Argives” begged him and named many glorious gifts (18.448–449), in the words in which Phoenix told how the elders of the Aetolians begged Meleager.
---

“and the elders begged him, / the elders of the Argives, and named many glorious gifts” (18.448–449; see [[elders]], [[the-embassy-to-achilles]] and [[the-gifts-of-agamemnon]]). The words are those of Phoenix's story of Meleager, whom “the elders begged … the elders of the Aetolians” to come out and fight (9.574–575; see [[the-wrath-of-meleager]] and [[meleager]]): Thetis tells her son's refusal in the language of the tale that Phoenix told him to change his mind. In Book 9 the envoys were Phoenix, Ajax and Odysseus, chosen by Nestor at Agamemnon's council of the elders (9.89–181).
