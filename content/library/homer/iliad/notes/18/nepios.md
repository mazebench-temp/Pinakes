---
summary: Hector calls Polydamas “fool” for advising retreat (18.295), and the narrator turns the word on the Trojans who cheer Hector: “fools; for Pallas Athena had taken away their wits” (18.311). On the Shield the “little children” guard the wall of the besieged city with the wives and old men (18.514).
---

The narrator's νήπιοι, at the head of the verse, is his judgment on the assembly (see [[retreat]], [[assembly]] and [[divine-intervention]]).
