---
summary: Hector “of the gleaming helmet” has Achilles' armor, in Antilochus' news and in Thetis' words, and answers Polydamas darkly in the Trojan assembly.
---

**The armor.** Antilochus: “Patroclus lies dead, and they are fighting around his corpse, / naked; for Hector of the gleaming helmet has his armor” (18.20–21). Thetis: “But your fine armor is held among the Trojans, / bronze, gleaming; Hector of the gleaming helmet / wears it on his own shoulders and glories in it” (18.130–132; [[marmairon]], [[exchange-of-armor]]).

**The assembly.** “Looking at him darkly, Hector of the gleaming helmet said” (18.284; [[looking-darkly]], [[polydamas]]), the verse with which he answered Polydamas at the wall (12.230). See [[hector]].
