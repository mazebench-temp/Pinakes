---
summary: Polydamas recalls the nights he spent “beside the swift ships”; the name of the Nereid Thoe, which has the letters of θοή, “swift,” is kept out of the entry.
---

Polydamas, in the Trojan assembly: “for I myself used to rejoice, spending the night beside the swift ships, / hoping that we would take the curving ships” (18.259–260; [[curving]], [[polydamas]]). The nights he means are the Trojans' bivouac on the plain after their victory in Book 8 (see the translation's note on 18.259).

In the catalogue of the Nereids, “Nesaea and Speio and Thoe and ox-eyed Halia” (18.40), Θόη is a name, not the adjective; the entry excludes it, as it excludes the name Thoas ([[thoe]]).
