---
summary: Tin is one of the four metals Hephaestus casts into the fire (18.474). On the Shield the vineyard has a fence of tin, the cows are made of gold and tin, and the greaves are “of soft tin” (18.565, 18.574, 18.613).
---

Walter Leaf reports Reichel's view that greaves of so soft a metal were guards for the shins against the edge of the shield, not against missiles (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 18.613; see the translation's note on 18.613, [[greaves]] and [[heanos]]).
