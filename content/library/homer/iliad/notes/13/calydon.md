---
summary: Calydon is “steep” in the verse that makes Thoas lord of the Aetolians “in all Pleuron and steep Calydon” (13.217).
grc: [Καλυδῶνι]
---

αἰπεινῇ Καλυδῶνι, “steep Calydon” (13.217; see [[steep]]): the Catalogue called it “rocky” (2.640) and Phoenix “lovely” (9.531, 9.577). The half-verse returns in Diomedes' account of his family, where the sons of Portheus, Oeneus among them, lived “in Pleuron and steep Calydon” (14.116; see [[pleuron]], [[oeneus]] and [[thoas]]).
