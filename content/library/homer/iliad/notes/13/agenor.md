---
summary: Agenor is one of the leaders Aeneas calls to his side in the fight over Alcathous (13.489–491), and he draws Menelaus' spear from the wounded hand of Helenus and binds it with a sling (13.598–600).
refs: [13.491, 13.599]
---

He stands with Deiphobus and Paris as one of the “leaders of the Trojans” beside Aeneas (13.489–491; see [[aeneas]]), as in Book 12 he led a company with Paris and Alcathous (12.93). When Menelaus' spear has pinned Helenus' hand to his bow, μεγάθυμος Ἀγήνωρ, “great-hearted Agenor”, pulls it out and binds the hand with a sling of twisted wool (13.598–600; see [[helenus]], [[sling]], [[healing]] and [[great-hearted]]). It is the only time in the poem that he tends a wound.
