---
summary: The Aetolians are named once in Book 13, in the description of Thoas, “who in all Pleuron and steep Calydon was lord of the Aetolians” (13.217–218), when Poseidon takes his voice.
---

Αἰτωλοῖσιν ἄνασσε, “was lord of the Aetolians” (13.218): the verse sets Thoas' rule in the two towns of the old house of Oeneus, Pleuron and Calydon (see [[thoas]], [[pleuron]], [[calydon]] and [[oeneus]]); the Catalogue named five (2.639–640). The Aetolians do not fight as a body in this book.
