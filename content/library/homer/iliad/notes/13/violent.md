---
summary: The Achaean wall was lowest by the ships of Ajax and Protesilaus, “where most of all they themselves and their horses were violent in battle.”
---

Hector holds on where he first broke in, by the ships of Ajax and Protesilaus, “and above them the wall had been built lowest, where most of all they themselves and their horses were violent in battle” (13.682–684; [[the-achaean-wall]], [[protesilaus]], [[hector]]). The subject is not named: αὐτοί τε καὶ ἵπποι may be the attackers or the men who fight there on both sides. ζαχρηής, used of winds in Book 5 and of the Lycian leaders in Book 12 (5.525; 12.347, 12.360), is here given to men and horses together, at the place where the fight for the ships is fiercest.
