---
summary: The Achaeans in front, in their intricate armor, fight “Hector armed in bronze,” while the Locrians, who have no armor, shoot from behind.
---

The Locrians have no bronze-fitted helmets, no shields and no ash spears, only bows and slings, and they shoot from behind the armed men: “So then the ones in front, in their intricate armor, / fought with the Trojans and with Hector armed in bronze, / and the others shot from behind, unseen” (13.719–721; [[locrians]], [[intricately-worked]], [[bow]], [[sling]]). The epithet is Hector's line-end formula, Ἕκτορι χαλκοκορυστῇ, and here it sets the Trojan leader in his bronze against archers without armor, whose arrows throw the Trojans into confusion (13.722). The Trojans would have been driven back from the ships, had not Polydamas come to Hector with his counsel (13.723–725; [[would-have-had-not]], [[polydamas]]). See [[hector]].
