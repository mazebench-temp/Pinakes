---
summary: Teucer's first victim is “Imbrius the spearman, son of Mentor, rich in horses.”
---

“And Teucer, son of Telamon, was the first to kill his man, Imbrius the spearman, son of Mentor, rich in horses” (13.170–171; [[teucer]], [[imbrius]], [[mentor-father-of-imbrius]], [[rich-in-horses]]). The warrior's title is given to a man of whom the narrator then tells a short life: he lived at Pedaeum, married a bastard daughter of Priam, Medesicaste, came back to Ilios when the Achaeans came, and was honored by Priam like his own children (13.172–176; [[pedaeum]], [[medesicaste]]). The archer Teucer kills him with a thrust of the spear below the ear (13.177–178). See [[androktasia]].
