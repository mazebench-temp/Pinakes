# Πολίτης returns for this Polites at 15.339; at Od. 10.224 it is Odysseus' companion, who will need an except.
grc: [Πολίτης]
refs: [13.534-535]
---

When Meriones has stabbed Deiphobus in the arm, “Polites, / his own brother, put his arms around his waist / and led him out of grim-sounding war” to the horses and chariot waiting behind the battle (13.533–537; see [[deiphobus]] and [[grim-sounding]]). αὐτοκασίγνητος, “own brother”, is the word Helen uses of Castor and Polydeuces, “whom one mother bore with me” (3.237–238; see [[kasignetos]]): a full brother, not one of Priam's many half-brothers. In Book 2 Iris took Polites' voice to warn the Trojans (2.791); here, for the first time, he acts. He kills the Achaean Echius in Book 15 (15.339).
