# Ἰφίκλοιο (13.698), the epic genitive; the Catalogue has Ἰφίκλου (2.705).
grc: [Ἰφίκλοιο]
relations:
  - child: podarces (13.698)
---

αὐτὰρ ὃ Ἰφίκλοιο πάϊς τοῦ Φυλακίδαο, “and the other was the son of Iphiclus, the son of Phylacus” (13.698), repeats the Catalogue's three generations, “son of Iphiclus, rich in flocks, the son of Phylacus” (2.705), without the flocks (see [[podarces]], [[phylacus]] and [[rich-in-flocks]]). The son of Iphiclus leads the Phthians with Medon beside the ships of Iphiclus' elder son, Protesilaus, who died on the day of the landing (13.681, 13.693; see [[protesilaus]] and [[phthians]]).
