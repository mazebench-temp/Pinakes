---
summary: Oileus, the father of Medon and of the swift Ajax, is “divine Oileus” in the verses that tell of Medon's exile.
---

Medon leads the Phthians with Podarces: “One was the bastard son of divine Oileus, / Medon, the brother of Ajax; but he lived / in Phylace, away from his native land, since he had killed a man, / a kinsman of his stepmother Eriopis, whom Oileus had as wife” (13.693–697; [[medon]], [[oileus]], [[eriopis]], [[nothos]]). Elsewhere in the book Oileus is named only as the father of the swift Ajax (13.66, 13.701). The verses return when Aeneas kills Medon (15.333–336). The epithet of honor is given to the father in the story of a bastard son's exile ([[exile-and-refuge]]). See [[ajax-son-of-oileus]].
