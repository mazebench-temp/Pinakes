---
summary: Phylacus is named in Book 13 only through the patronymic of his son Iphiclus, in Podarces' descent, “the son of Iphiclus, the son of Phylacus” (13.698).
---

Ἰφίκλοιο πάϊς τοῦ Φυλακίδαο, “the son of Iphiclus, the son of Phylacus” (13.698): the line of the house of Phylace is given again as in the Catalogue (2.705), when its youngest member leads the Phthians at the ships (see [[podarces]], [[iphiclus]] and [[phylace]]). Φυλακίδης, like every patronymic, counts as a mention of the father.
