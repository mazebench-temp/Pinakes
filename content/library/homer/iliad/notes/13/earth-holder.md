---
summary: Poseidon is “the earth-holder” five times in the book, three times in the formula “the earth-holder, the shaker of the earth”; the book adds the nominative γαιήοχος.
grc: [γαιήοχος]
---

Book 13 gives Poseidon this title more often than any other book of the Iliad, always in the nominative and always of the god at work among the Achaeans ([[poseidon]], [[poseidon-rallies-the-achaeans]]). “But Poseidon, the earth-holder, the shaker of the earth, / urged on the Argives” in the likeness of Calchas (13.43–45); “He spoke, and with his staff the earth-holder, the shaker of the earth, / struck them both” (13.59–60); “the earth-holder roused the Achaeans in the rear” (13.83), and roused them with his orders (13.125); and on the left the glory would soon have been the Achaeans', “so hard did the earth-holder, the shaker of the earth, / urge on the Argives” (13.676–678). Three of the five are the full formula γαιήοχος ἐννοσίγαιος, which Book 9 had in the dative (9.183; [[shaker-of-the-earth]], [[earth-shaker]]). The nominative γαιήοχος is new to the entry.
