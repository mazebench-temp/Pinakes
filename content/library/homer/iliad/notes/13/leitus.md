---
summary: Leitus is the second of the leaders whom Poseidon rouses among the weary men in the rear (13.91).
grc: [Λήϊτον]
---

“First he came to Teucer and Leitus, giving orders” (13.91; see [[poseidon]] and [[teucer]]). Poseidon's list goes on with Peneleos, Leitus' fellow commander of the Boeotians (2.494), and with Thoas, Deipyrus, Meriones and Antilochus (13.92–93; see [[peneleos]]). Leitus has no other part in the book.
