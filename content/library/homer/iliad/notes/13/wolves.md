# λύκων will also catch the Trojan Λύκων, whom Peneleos kills (16.335, 16.337): add "!^Λύκων" with Book 16.
grc: [λύκων]

θώων παρδαλίων τε λύκων τʼ ἤϊα πέλονται: “are the prey of jackals and leopards and wolves” (13.103). The deer are the Trojans of the past, before they dared to fight at the ships (13.101–106; see [[timid-deer]]). The wolves that kill a stag return in the simile of the Myrmidons arming, who go out like a pack of wolves that have torn a great horned stag in the mountains (16.156–163). See also [[jackal]] and [[leopard]].
