---
summary: The Paphlagonians carry the dead Harpalion to “sacred Ilios.”
---

Harpalion has been shot by Meriones as he drew back, and “The great-hearted Paphlagonians tended him, / and lifting him into a chariot they took him to sacred Ilios, / grieving; and his father went with them, letting tears fall” (13.656–658; [[harpalion]], [[paphlagonians]], [[pylaemenes]]). Ἴλιον ἱρήν at the end of the verse is the city's common formula ([[troy]]). The holy city receives the body of an ally whose death goes unpaid: “and no penalty was paid for his dead son” (13.659; [[poine]], [[pylaemenes-dead-and-alive]]).
