  - child: thoas (13.216)

Ἀνδραίμονος υἷϊ Θόαντι, “Thoas, son of Andraemon” (13.216): the father's name in full, as in the Catalogue and in Book 15 (2.638, 15.281), where Book 7 had the patronymic Ἀνδραιμονίδης (7.168; see [[thoas]] and [[poseidon]]).
