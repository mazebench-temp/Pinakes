summary: Thoas rules the Aetolians “in all Pleuron and steep Calydon” (13.217), in the description of the man whose voice Poseidon takes.
grc: ["^Πλευρῶνι"]
---

πάσῃ Πλευρῶνι, “in all Pleuron” (13.217), a dative of place (see [[thoas]] and [[aetolians]]). The same pair of towns is the home of the sons of Portheus, Oeneus among them, in Diomedes' account of his family: “they lived in Pleuron and steep Calydon” (14.116; see [[calydon]] and [[oeneus]]).
