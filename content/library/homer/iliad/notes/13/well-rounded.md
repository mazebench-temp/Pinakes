---
summary: The Locrians who follow the son of Oileus have no bronze helmets, “nor … well-rounded shields and ash spears,” but fight with bows and slings.
---

The Locrians are described by what they lack: “for they had no bronze-fitted helmets with horsehair crests, nor had they well-rounded shields and ash spears, but trusting in bows and in well-twisted sheep's wool they had followed him to Ilios” (13.714–717; [[locrians]], [[bronze-fitted]], [[horsehair-crested]], [[ash-spear]], [[sling]], [[bow]]). The round shield, which the Trojans and Achaeans hacked at across the battlements in Book 12 (12.426), belongs to the men in front; the Locrians shoot from behind them, unseen (13.719–722; [[ajax-son-of-oileus]]).
