**The will of Zeus.** The narrator states it plainly: “Zeus wished for victory for the Trojans and for Hector, / to give glory to Achilles, swift of foot; but he did not at all / wish the Achaean army to perish before Ilios” (βούλετο νίκην, 13.347–349; see [[plan-of-zeus]] and [[kydos]]). The verb of the will has two objects, and the second limits the first: Hector's victories are not to become the Achaeans' destruction. Poseidon, vexed to see the Achaeans beaten down, helps them in secret (13.351–357; see [[poseidon-rallies-the-achaeans]]).

**Held back by his counsels.** When Ascalaphus falls, his father Ares does not know it, for he sits on the peak of Olympus beneath golden clouds, “held back by the counsels of Zeus, where the other / immortal gods were also, shut out from the war” (Διὸς βουλῇσιν ἐελμένος, 13.523–525; see [[ares]] and [[bereaved-parents]]). The dative plural βουλῇσιν is found only here, and the counsels are the ban that Zeus laid on the gods in Book 8, that none should help either side (8.5–27; see [[divine-intervention]]). Poseidon is the one god who breaks it.

**Counsel among men.** Polydamas tells Hector that because a god has given him the works of war beyond others, “you want also in counsel to know more than others” (καὶ βουλῇ ἐθέλεις περιίδμεναι ἄλλων, 13.728; see [[polydamas]] and [[different-gifts]]); he cannot have everything at once (13.729; see [[all-things-at-once]]). His own advice is a council: draw back and call all the best men here, “and then we might consider every plan, / whether we should fall upon the many-benched ships, / if a god is willing to give us the power, or after that / come away from the ships unharmed” (πᾶσαν ἐπιφρασσαίμεθα βουλὴν, 13.740–744; see [[kratos]]). Hector accepts it, “and his safe counsel pleased Hector” (13.748). The title of counselor goes in this book to Idomeneus, whom Poseidon and Meriones both address as “counselor of the Cretans”, and to Aeneas, whom Deiphobus calls “counselor of the Trojans” (βουληφόρε, 13.219, 13.255, 13.463; see [[man-of-counsel]]).
