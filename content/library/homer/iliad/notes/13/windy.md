---
summary: The Trojans would have retreated to “windy Ilios,” had not Polydamas given Hector his counsel.
---

“Then wretchedly from the ships and the huts the Trojans would have retreated to windy Ilios, had not Polydamas come and stood beside bold Hector and said” (13.723–725; [[polydamas]], [[would-have-had-not]]). The city's formula names the place of retreat that does not come. Polydamas advises not a retreat but a council of the best men at the ships, and Hector agrees (13.726–753; [[boule]]). The retreat to the city is the counsel he will give after Achilles' return, and Hector will refuse it (18.254–313). See [[troy]].
