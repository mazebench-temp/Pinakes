---
summary: Peteos is named in Book 13 only in his son's title, when “Menestheus, son of Peteos”, leads the picked men of the Athenians at the ships (13.690).
---

ἦρχʼ υἱὸς Πετεῶο Μενεσθεύς, “Menestheus, son of Peteos, led” (13.690): the half-verse with which the Catalogue introduced the Athenian leader (2.552) and Book 12 named him at his tower (12.331), here as he leads the Athenians against Hector (see [[menestheus]] and [[athenians]]).
