---
summary: Idomeneus draws his “long-shadowed spear” from the body of Oenomaus but is too slow to strip the armor.
---

Idomeneus kills Oenomaus with a spear through the belly. “As for Idomeneus, his long-shadowed spear from the corpse / he drew out; but he could not take the rest of the beautiful armor / from his shoulders; for he was hard pressed by missiles” (13.506–511; [[oenomaus-the-trojan]], [[idomeneus]], [[stripping-the-dead]]). The narrator gives the reason: his legs are no longer steady, and he gives ground step by step (13.512–516; [[half-gray]]). The epithet of the spear in the formal duels here names the one thing Idomeneus can take back from the body.
