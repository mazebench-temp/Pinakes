summary: Poseidon's cave lies “in the depths of the deep sea,” and he comes to the Argives “up out of the deep sea”; the book adds the genitive βαθείης in two phrases.
grc: ["βαθείης βένθεσι λίμνης", "βαθείης ἐξ ἁλὸς"]
---

“There is a wide cave in the depths of the deep sea, / between Tenedos and rocky Imbros” (13.32–33), where Poseidon leaves his horses ([[poseidon]], [[ambrosial]]); and he “urged on the Argives, coming up out of the deep sea / in the likeness of Calchas” (13.44–45; [[divine-epiphany]]). The sea is λίμνη in the first phrase, as in the verse of Poseidon's golden house at Aegae, “in the depths of the sea” (βένθεσι λίμνης, 13.21; [[aegae]]). The genitive βαθείης is new to the entry. See [[sea]].
