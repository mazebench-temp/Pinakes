---
summary: Hector holds on where he first broke into the Achaean camp, “breaking the close ranks of the shield-bearing Danaans.”
---

While the Achaeans are winning on the left, “Hector held on where he had first leapt in at the gates and the wall, breaking the close ranks of the shield-bearing Danaans, where the ships of Ajax and of Protesilaus were” (13.679–681; [[hector]], [[the-gates-of-the-wall]], [[the-achaean-wall]]). The phrase στίχες ἀσπιστάων, used in Book 4 of the ranks around Pandarus and Machaon and of the Trojan ranks (4.90, 4.201, 4.221), names here the Achaean line that Hector broke at the end of Book 12 (12.437–471). It is the only time the epithet stands with the name of the Danaans. See [[danaans]] and [[battle-order]].
