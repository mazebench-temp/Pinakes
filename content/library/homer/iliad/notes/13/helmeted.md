---
summary: The two Ajaxes, “helmeted men,” hold up the body of Imbrius like two lions with a goat; the book adds the dual κορυστά.
grc: [κορυστὰ]
---

The two Ajaxes carry off Imbrius like two lions that have snatched a goat from the dogs, “so the two Ajaxes, helmeted men, held him high / and stripped his armor” (13.198–202; [[two-lions-and-a-goat]], [[the-two-ajaxes]], [[imbrius]], [[stripping-the-dead]]). Elsewhere κορυστής is a man in the formula of the first kill, ἄνδρα κορυστήν (4.457, 8.256); here, in the dual, it is an epithet of the pair, as it will be again when the two Ajaxes cannot drive Hector away from Patroclus' body (18.163). The dual κορυστά is new to the entry.
