---
summary: Meriones asks Idomeneus where to enter the throng, since nowhere else are “the long-haired Achaeans so lacking in war.”
---

“Son of Deucalion, where are you eager to enter the throng? / On the right of the whole army, or through the middle, / or on the left? For nowhere else, I think, / are the long-haired Achaeans so lacking in war” (13.307–310; [[meriones]], [[idomeneus]]). It is the only use of the formula in Book 13, in the accusative, κάρη κομόωντας Ἀχαιούς. Idomeneus chooses the left, since the two Ajaxes and Teucer are holding the middle against Hector (13.312–327; [[the-two-ajaxes]], [[teucer]]), and there Idomeneus leaps among the Trojans and routs them (13.361–362).
