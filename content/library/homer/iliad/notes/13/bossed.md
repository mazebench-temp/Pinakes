---
summary: Idomeneus keeps in his hut the spoils of the Trojans he has killed: spears, “bossed shields,” helmets and corselets.
---

Meriones has broken his spear on Deiphobus' shield, and Idomeneus offers him one of his own: “Spears, if you want them, you will find, one or twenty, / standing in my hut against the gleaming walls, / Trojan spears, which I take from the men I kill; for I do not mean / to fight standing far off from hostile men. / So I have spears and bossed shields / and helmets and corselets shining brightly” (13.260–265; [[idomeneus]], [[stripping-the-dead]], [[hostile-men]], [[gleaming-all-over]]). The bossed shields are trophies, taken from men he has fought at close quarters. Meriones answers that his own spoils lie by his hut and his black ship, too far off to fetch (13.267–268; [[black-ships]]). See [[shield]].
