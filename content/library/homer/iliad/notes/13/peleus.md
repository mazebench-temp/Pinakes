---
summary: Peleus is named in Book 13 only through his son, “the swift-footed son of Peleus”, whom, Poseidon says in Calchas' likeness, Agamemnon dishonored (13.113).
---

οὕνεκʼ ἀπητίμησε ποδώκεα Πηλεΐωνα, “because he dishonored the swift-footed son of Peleus” (13.113; see [[achilles]], [[son-of-peleus]] and [[swift-footed]]). The verse-end ποδώκεα Πηλεΐωνα is the one in Zeus' prophecy that Hector will not stop fighting “until the swift-footed son of Peleus is roused beside the ships” (8.474; see [[plan-of-zeus]]). In the same speech the god grants that Agamemnon may be to blame for the quarrel (13.111–114; see [[agamemnon]]).
