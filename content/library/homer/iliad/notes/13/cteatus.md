# Ἀκτορίωνος (13.185) agrees with Κτεάτου: Cteatus, son of Actor. The patronymic counts for both.
grc: [Ἀκτορίωνος]
relations:
  - child: amphimachus (13.185)
  - father: poseidon (13.206-207)
---

Ἀμφίμαχον Κτεάτου υἷʼ Ἀκτορίωνος, “Amphimachus, son of Cteatus, of Actor's line” (13.185): Cteatus is named, as in the Catalogue, only as the father of the Epeian leader whom Hector kills (2.621; see [[amphimachus]]). Ἀκτορίωνος, a singular patronymic agreeing with Κτεάτου, makes him a son of Actor, as the dual Ἀκτορίωνε names the twins in Nestor's tales (11.750; see [[actor-of-elis]]). Twenty verses later Poseidon is angry “for his grandson” (13.207). υἱωνός is a son's son, as Tlepolemus, son of Heracles, is the υἱωνός of Zeus (5.631) and Telemachus of Laertes (Od. 24.515), and so the father of Cteatus is the god, as Nestor said when he told how Poseidon saved the Moliones from his spear (11.750–752; see [[the-moliones]] and [[poseidon]]).
