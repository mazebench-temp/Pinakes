Hector looks for him among the champions and finds him dead at the sterns of the ships.
# Ἀσιάδης, "son of Asius" (13.771), with asius (the patronymic rule).
grc: [Ἀσιάδης]
refs: [13.562-575, 13.761-763, 13.780]
---

He dies after his father, in the fight around Antilochus. Poseidon, guarding Nestor's son among the missiles, makes the point of Adamas' spear powerless, since the god “begrudged him the other's life” (13.563): half the spear stays in the shield “like a fire-hardened stake”, and half lies on the ground (13.554–565; see [[poseidon]], [[antilochus]] and [[dark-haired]]). Meriones follows him as he draws back and strikes him “where most of all / Ares is painful for miserable mortals”, and he writhes around the spear “as an ox writhes that herdsmen in the mountains / have bound by force with twisted ropes” (13.567–573; see [[meriones]] and [[the-roped-ox]]). Hector asks for “Adamas, son of Asius, and Asius, son of Hyrtacus” together, and finds them dead (13.759–763, 13.771; see [[asius]] and [[hector]]).
