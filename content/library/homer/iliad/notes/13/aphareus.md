# Καλητορίδην (13.541), "son of Caletor", counts for Aphareus and for his father (caletor-father-of-aphareus).
grc: [Καλητορίδην]
refs: [13.542-544]
relations:

---

He is one of the five companions whom Idomeneus calls to his side when Aeneas comes at him (13.477–479; see [[idomeneus]] and [[aeneas]]). Aeneas, come to defend Alcathous, kills him:

{{quote:7b758356-6e9c-5dcc-a6b3-1967e92f4786}}

Καλητορίδην, “son of Caletor”, is all the poem tells of his father (see [[caletor-father-of-aphareus]]). ἑάφθη (13.543) is obscure: the shield and helmet “clung” to the falling man, or went down with him (see the translation's note on 13.543). θάνατος … θυμοραϊστής, “death that destroys the spirit” (13.544), returns twice in Book 16 (16.414, 16.580). Aphareus is the second of the three captains of the night watch (9.83) who die in this book (see [[ascalaphus]] and [[deipyrus]]).
