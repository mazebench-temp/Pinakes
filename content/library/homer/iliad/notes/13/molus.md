“Meriones, son of Molus, swift of foot, dearest of my companions” (13.249; see [[meriones]] and [[idomeneus]]). The other mention is the history of the boar's-tusk helmet, which Molus received as a guest-gift and gave to his son (10.269–270; see [[the-boars-tusk-helmet]]). Homer does not say whether Molus was kin to Idomeneus (for Apollodorus' answer, see “Outside Homer” in the entry).
