---
summary: Echius is named as the father of Mecisteus in the verse that brings Hypsenor's bearers (13.422 = 8.333).
---

Μηκιστεὺς Ἐχίοιο πάϊς, “Mecisteus, son of Echius” (13.422), in the block of verses that carried Teucer from the field in Book 8 and carries Hypsenor here (13.420–423 = 8.331–334; see [[mecisteus-son-of-echius]] and [[alastor-who-carries-teucer]]). The name is all the poem gives of him.
