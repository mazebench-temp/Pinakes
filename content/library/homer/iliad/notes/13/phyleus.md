---
summary: Phyleus is named in Book 13 only in his son's patronymic, when Meges is counted among the leaders of the Epeians at the ships (13.692).
---

Φυλεΐδης τε Μέγης, “Meges, son of Phyleus” (13.692): the son of the Elean prince who went off to live on Dulichium, angry with his father (2.628–629), is named here among the leaders of the Epeians, his father's people (see [[meges]], [[epeians]] and [[phyleus-and-augeas]]).
