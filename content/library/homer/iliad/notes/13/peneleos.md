---
summary: Poseidon rouses “the hero Peneleos” among the weary men in the rear (13.92).
grc: [Πηνέλεών]
---

Πηνέλεών θʼ ἥρωα, “and to the hero Peneleos” (13.92; see [[heros]] and [[poseidon]]), named after his fellow Boeotian Leitus (13.91; see [[leitus]]). He has no other part in Book 13. In Book 14 he kills Ilioneus and holds up the head on his spear (14.487–505).
