# Ἀκτορίωνος (13.185), singular, of Cteatus; the patronymic counts for the father.
grc: [Ἀκτορίωνος]
---

Ἀκτορίωνος, “of Actor's line” (13.185), is the only singular of this patronymic in Homer; the dual Ἀκτορίωνε names the twins themselves in Nestor's tales (11.750, 23.638) and, in the Catalogue, their two sons (2.621). It agrees with Κτεάτου: Cteatus, father of the Amphimachus whom Hector kills, is a son of Actor (see [[cteatus]] and [[amphimachus]]). In the same scene Poseidon is angry for his grandson (13.206–207), so the line has two fathers, as in Nestor's tale, where the twins are “the two sons of Actor, the Moliones”, and Poseidon is “their father” (11.750–751; see [[the-moliones]] and [[poseidon]]).
