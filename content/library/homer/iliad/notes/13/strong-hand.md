---
summary: Aeneas' spear goes quivering into the earth, “since it had sped in vain from his strong hand”; the book adds the phrase στιβαρῆς ἀπὸ χειρός.
grc: [στιβαρῆς ἀπὸ χειρὸς]
---

Aeneas throws first at Idomeneus, who sees the spear coming and avoids it, “and Aeneas' spear went quivering into the earth, since it had sped in vain from his strong hand” (13.503–505; [[aeneas]], [[idomeneus]]). στιβαρός, “firm, sturdy,” is the adjective of Sarpedon's “strong hands” at the battlement (χερσὶ στιβαρῇσιν, 12.397), added to the entry in Book 12. The three verses return in Book 16, when Aeneas throws at Meriones and misses again (16.610, 16.614–615). See [[spear]].
