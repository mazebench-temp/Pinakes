---
summary: Ajax and Menelaus speak of “the grievous news” of Patroclus' death that must be carried to Achilles; the book adds λυγρῆς.
grc: [λυγρῆς]
---

Ajax wishes for a companion to take the news to Achilles, “since I do not think he has even heard / the grievous news, that his dear companion has perished” (17.641–642; [[son-of-peleus]], [[ajax]]). Menelaus tells Antilochus: “Antilochus, come here, nurtured by Zeus, so that you may hear / grievous news, which I wish had never been” (17.685–686; [[antilochus]], [[zeus-nurtured]]). The verse-end λυγρῆς ἀγγελίης is the same in both places (see the translation's table of formulas), and Antilochus opens his message to Achilles with the same words in Book 18 (18.18–19). The genitive λυγρῆς is new to the entry. See [[achilles]].
