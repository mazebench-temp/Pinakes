---
summary: The best men around the body in the mist “were worn down by the pitiless bronze.”
---

“But those in the middle suffered pains / from the mist and the war, and were worn down by the pitiless bronze, / all who were the best” (17.375–377; [[fighting-over-the-body]]). The contrast is with the rest of the field, where men fight at their ease under the clear sky, avoiding one another's missiles (17.370–375; [[groaning]]). See [[bronze]].
