summary: “Father Zeus” is called on in the first speech and the last prayer of the book: Menelaus' exclamation at Euphorbus' boast (17.19) and Ajax's prayer for light (17.645), and Zeus as “the father” pities Ajax's tears (17.648). Among men the book's father is Peleus, who gave his son the armor the gods gave him (17.195–197; see [[fathers-and-sons]]).
