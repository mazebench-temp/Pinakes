---
summary: “The gray-eyed goddess Athena” is glad that Menelaus prayed to her first of all the gods, and fills him with the daring of a fly.
---

“So he spoke, and the gray-eyed goddess Athena was glad / that he had prayed to her first of all the gods. / And she put strength into his shoulders and his knees, / and put in his breast the daring of a fly” (17.567–570; [[menelaus]]). Menelaus has spoken to her in the likeness of Phoenix, wishing that Athena would give him strength (17.561–562; [[old-father]], [[phoenix]]), and the goddess hears her own name in the prayer. See [[athena]].
