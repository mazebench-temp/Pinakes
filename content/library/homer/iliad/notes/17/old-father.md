---
summary: Menelaus addresses Athena, in the likeness of Phoenix, as “Phoenix, old father, aged one.”
---

“Phoenix, old father, aged one, if only Athena / would give me strength, and keep off the force of the missiles!” (17.561–562; [[phoenix]], [[athena]], [[menelaus]]). Menelaus uses the opening of Achilles' answer to Phoenix in the embassy (9.607), with παλαιγενές, “aged one,” in place of διοτρεφές (see the translation's note on 9.607). He prays to Athena by name without knowing that he is speaking to her, and she is glad that he prayed to her first of all the gods (17.567–568; [[gray-eyed]]).
