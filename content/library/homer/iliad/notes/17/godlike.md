---
summary: Aretus and Chromius, who go with Hector and Aeneas against Automedon, are “godlike.”
---

“And with them went Chromius and godlike Aretus, / both of them; and their hearts had high hopes / of killing the two men and driving off the horses with high-arched necks” (17.494–496; [[aretus]], [[high-necked]]). Automedon's spear kills Aretus (17.516–524), and the others draw back: “Hector and Aeneas and godlike Chromius” (17.534; [[the-two-ajaxes]]). See [[automedon]].
