---
summary: The verse “He went through the champions, armored in gleaming bronze” is used three times: of Menelaus as he takes his stand over the body, and twice of Hector, as he comes to take it and as he goes back into the fight grieving for Podes.
---

“He went through the champions, armored in gleaming bronze” (17.3) brings Menelaus to the body of Patroclus ([[menelaus]]); the same verse brings Hector to the body of Euphorbus, “shouting shrilly, like the flame of Hephaestus” (17.87–88; [[like-a-flame]], [[hector]]); and Hector again, when a black cloud of grief has covered him for Podes (17.591–592; [[podes]]). The whole verse is used in Books 4, 5 and 20 of other heroes going into the front (4.495, 5.562, 5.681, 20.111). Book 17 uses it more than any other book. See [[bronze]] and [[battle-order]].
