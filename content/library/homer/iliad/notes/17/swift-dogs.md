---
summary: Athena warns Menelaus of the shame if Patroclus “is torn by swift dogs under the wall of the Trojans”; the book adds ταχέες κύνες.
grc: ["ταχέες κύνες"]
---

“To you, Menelaus, it will be a shame and a reproach / if the trusted companion of noble Achilles / is torn by swift dogs under the wall of the Trojans” (17.556–558; [[trusted-companion]], [[noble]], [[oneidos]], [[dogs]]). The threat of the dogs runs through the book, from Hector's purpose to give the head to “the Trojan dogs” (17.127) to Menelaus' call to the leaders (17.255). The phrase ταχέες κύνες is new to the entry. See [[phoenix]] and [[athena]].
