---
summary: Apollo rouses Hector in the likeness of Mentes, “the leader of the Cicones” (17.73), though the Catalogue gives them another leader, Euphemus.
---

ἀνέρι εἰσάμενος Κικόνων ἡγήτορι Μέντῃ, “in the likeness of a man, Mentes, the leader of the Cicones” (17.73; see [[mentes]] and [[apollo]]). The Catalogue names Euphemus as their leader (2.846–847; see [[euphemus]]), and Walter Leaf, noting it, reports that some therefore wrote Πείρῳ for Μέντῃ, the name of a Thracian leader (4.520; *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 17.73; see [[peirous]]). The Cicones themselves are not named again in the Iliad.
