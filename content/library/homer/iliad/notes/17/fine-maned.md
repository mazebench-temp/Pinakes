---
summary: Automedon fears that Hector will mount behind “the fine-maned horses of Achilles,” and Idomeneus lashes “the fine-maned horses” toward the ships; the book adds the dual καλλίτριχε.
grc: [καλλίτριχε]
---

“for I do not think / that Hector, son of Priam, will hold back from his fury / before he mounts behind the fine-maned horses of Achilles” (17.502–504; [[son-of-priam]], [[the-horses-of-achilles]], [[automedon]]); Zeus has sworn that he will not (17.448–449). Later, “Idomeneus lashed the fine-maned horses / toward the hollow ships” (17.624–625; [[hollow-ships]], [[idomeneus]]). The dual καλλίτριχε, of the pair, is new to the entry. See [[horses]].
