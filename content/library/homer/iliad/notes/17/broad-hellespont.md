---
summary: The horses of Achilles will not go back “to the ships by the broad Hellespont”; the book adds the accusative πλατὺν Ἑλλήσποντον.
grc: [πλατὺν Ἑλλήσποντον]
---

“but the two would neither go back to the ships by the broad Hellespont / nor into the battle among the Achaeans, / but as a pillar stays fixed that stands on the tomb / of a man who has died, or of a woman, / so they stood unmoving” (17.432–436; [[the-horses-of-achilles]], [[tomb]], [[hellespont]]). The accusative phrase is new to the entry. In its other two uses the broad Hellespont is the place of a tomb (7.86; *Odyssey* 24.82); here the tomb comes in the simile two verses later.
