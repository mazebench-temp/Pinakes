---
summary: Oileus is named once, in his son's title, when the son of Oileus is the first to answer Menelaus' call to the leaders: “swift Ajax, son of Oileus, heard him clearly” (17.256).
---

ὀξὺ δʼ ἄκουσεν Ὀϊλῆος ταχὺς Αἴας, “swift Ajax, son of Oileus, heard him clearly” (17.256; see [[ajax-son-of-oileus]] and [[swift]]): he is the first to run through the combat to Menelaus when he calls the leaders to the body (17.246–257; see [[menelaus]]). Ὀϊλῆος ταχὺς Αἴας ends the verse as it did in the Catalogue (2.527) and in Books 13 and 14 (13.66, 14.442), and will again at the funeral games (23.473, 23.488, 23.754). For the rest of the book the son of Oileus is one of “the two Ajaxes” (see [[the-two-ajaxes]]).
