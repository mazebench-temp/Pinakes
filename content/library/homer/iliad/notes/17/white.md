---
summary: The olive sapling of Euphorbus' simile “teems with white blossom.”
grc: ["ἄνθεϊ λευκῷ"]
---

“a fine, thriving tree; and the breezes of all the winds / shake it, and it teems with white blossom” (17.55–56; [[euphorbus]]). The blossoming tree is the young man whose hair was “like the Graces'” a few verses before (17.51; [[graces]]). The phrase ἄνθεϊ λευκῷ is new to the entry.
