---
summary: Zeus promises the Trojans glory to kill “until they come to the well-benched ships,” in the words of his promise to Hector in Book 11.
---

“for I will still hold out glory to them / to kill, until they come to the well-benched ships, / and the sun goes down and sacred darkness comes on” (17.453–455; [[hieros]], [[kydos]]). The promise is the one Zeus sent to Hector by Iris in Book 11 (11.192–194, 11.207–209; see the translation's note on 11.192–194). Here he speaks it to the horses of Achilles, as he pities them ([[the-horses-of-achilles]]). See [[zeus]] and [[ships]].
