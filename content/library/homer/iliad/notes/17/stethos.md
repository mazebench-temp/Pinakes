summary: The breast holds what men feel in the fight over the body: Menelaus stands over it “nursing great grief in his breast” (17.139), and Athena puts “the daring of a fly” in his breast (17.570; see [[the-daring-of-a-fly]]). Idomeneus' spear strikes Hector's corselet “on the chest beside the nipple” and snaps (17.606).
