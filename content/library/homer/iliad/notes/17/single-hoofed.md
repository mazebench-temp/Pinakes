---
summary: Antilochus gives his armor to Laodocus, “who was wheeling his single-hoofed horses close by.”
---

“but set off running, and gave his armor to his noble companion / Laodocus, who was wheeling his single-hoofed horses close by” (17.698–699; [[blameless]], [[laodocus-companion-of-antilochus]], [[antilochus]]). Antilochus leaves armor and chariot and runs on foot to Achilles with the news. See [[horses]].
