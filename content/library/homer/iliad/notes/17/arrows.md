---
summary: βέλος in this book is any missile. Outside the mist the armies fight apart, “avoiding one another's groaning missiles” (17.374); Menelaus wishes Athena would “keep off the force of the missiles” (17.562); and Ajax reads Zeus' will in the missiles: the Trojans' all strike home, the Achaeans' fall idly to the ground (17.631–633; see [[even-a-fool-can-see]]).
