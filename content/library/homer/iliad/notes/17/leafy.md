summary: The hare of Menelaus' eagle simile crouches “under a leafy bush,” and the eagle sees it all the same.
---

{{quote:897fdfdc-6094-59ba-a947-7b3daee0d498}}

The hare crouches “under a leafy bush” (17.677; [[hare]], [[eagle]], [[swift]]). The bush hides the hare from everything but the sharpest sight of the birds (17.675; [[sharp]], [[under-heaven]]), and so Menelaus' eyes search the crowd for Antilochus (17.679–681; [[phaeinos]]). See [[menelaus]].
