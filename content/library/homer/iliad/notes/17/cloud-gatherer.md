---
summary: “Zeus the cloud-gatherer” sees Hector putting on the armor of Achilles and speaks to his own heart.
---

“And when Zeus the cloud-gatherer saw him from afar / arming himself in the armor of the divine son of Peleus, / he shook his head and spoke to his own heart” (17.198–200; [[divine]], [[son-of-peleus]], [[exchange-of-armor]]). The soliloquy that follows pities Hector, who does not see his death, and promises him great power in recompense (17.201–208; [[wretch]], [[hector]]). The god of the clouds will later pour a mist over the body and take it away at Ajax's prayer (17.268–273, 17.645–650). See [[zeus]].
