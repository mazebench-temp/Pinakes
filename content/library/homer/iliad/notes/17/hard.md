---
summary: Glaucus calls Hector “hard man” for leaving Sarpedon's body to the Argives.
---

“How would you save a lesser man in the throng, / hard man, when you left Sarpedon, at once your guest-friend and your companion, / to become the prey and spoil of the Argives” (17.149–151; [[sarpedon]], [[xenia]], [[hetairos]], [[glaucus]]). The vocative σχέτλιε stands at the head of the verse, as in Book 16 (16.203). Glaucus does not know that Apollo has already carried the body to Lycia (see the translation's note on 17.163). See [[hector]].
