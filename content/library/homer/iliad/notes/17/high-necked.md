---
summary: Hector, Aeneas, Chromius and Aretus hope to drive off the horses of Achilles, “with high-arched necks.”
---

“and their hearts had high hopes / of killing the two men and driving off the horses with high-arched necks, / the fools; they were not to come back without bloodshed / from Automedon” (17.495–498; [[the-horses-of-achilles]], [[nepios]], [[automedon]]). In Book 10 Hector promised “two horses with high-arched necks” to the man who would spy on the ships (10.305), and Dolon asked for the horses of Achilles (10.322–323); now Hector goes after the team himself. See [[horses]].
