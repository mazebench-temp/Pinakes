---
summary: Menelaus leaves the body “turning back again and again, like a bearded lion” driven from the farmyard.
---

“And he gave ground backward, and left the body, / turning back again and again, like a bearded lion / that dogs and men drive from the steading / with spears and shouting” (17.108–111; [[lion-driven-from-the-farmyard]], [[menelaus]]). The bearded lion appeared in Book 15, when it scattered the hunters of the stag (15.275), and will return in Book 18, in the simile of Achilles' lament (18.318). See [[lion]].
