summary: The book ends on the word: “and there was no respite from the war” (πολέμου δʼ οὐ γίγνετʼ ἐρωή, 17.761 = 16.302). Hector is “a cloud of war” that covers everything (17.243), the strife of war has blazed up so that Menelaus cannot pick out the leaders (17.253), and Hector calls the choice to perish or be saved “the intimate talk of war” (17.228; see [[oaristys]]).
