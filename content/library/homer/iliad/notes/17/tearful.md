summary: Hector changes his armor “apart from the tearful battle,” Automedon sees Hector and Aeneas bearing down “through the tearful war,” and the battle is stretched again, “grievous and tearful”; the book adds πολυδακρύου and πολύδακρυς.
grc: [πολυδακρύου, πολύδακρυς]
---

“And standing apart from the tearful battle he changed his armor” (17.192; [[exchange-of-armor]], [[hector]]); “for here they are bearing down through the tearful war, / Hector and Aeneas” (17.512–513; [[automedon]]); and “again over Patroclus the fierce battle was stretched tight, / grievous and tearful” (17.543–544; [[krateros]]). The genitive πολυδακρύου and the nominative πολύδακρυς are new to the entry. See [[polemos]] and [[tears]].
