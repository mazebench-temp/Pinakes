---
summary: Hector calls on the Trojans to fight while he puts on “the fine armor I stripped from mighty Patroclus when I killed him.”
---

“be men, my friends, and remember your furious courage, / while I put on the armor of noble Achilles, / the fine armor I stripped from mighty Patroclus when I killed him” (17.185–187; [[furious]], [[blameless]], [[exchange-of-armor]], [[hector]]). The periphrasis honors the dead man in the boast of his killer; the same verse-end describes the armor in which Achilles must find an opening in Book 22 (22.323). See [[patroclus]].
