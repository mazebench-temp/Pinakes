summary: The houses of the book are those that the dead leave: Hyperenor's widow in her new bridal chamber (17.36), Schedius' home in Panopeus, where he ruled over many men (17.307–308), and the home of Phaenops at Abydos, whose likeness Apollo takes (17.584). In the simile of the fire the houses of a city dwindle in the blaze (17.737–739).
