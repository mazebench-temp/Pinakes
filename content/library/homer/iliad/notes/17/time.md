summary: Patroclus lies dead “to win satisfaction for me”, Menelaus says (17.92), the satisfaction owed him for Helen. A god “honors” Hector, so Menelaus may give way (17.99); “from Zeus come honor and glory” to the leaders (17.251; see [[feast]]); and Hector “honored” Podes most of all the people (17.576).
---

Menelaus' τιμή is the cause of the war, the satisfaction that the army came to win from the Trojans for him and Agamemnon (1.159; see the translation's table of renderings). Patroclus has died fighting in Menelaus' quarrel, and so Menelaus fears the blame of the Danaans if he leaves him (17.91–93; see [[decision-scene]]).
