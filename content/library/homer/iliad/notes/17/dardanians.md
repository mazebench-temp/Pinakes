---
summary: The Dardanians are named once, in Hector's rallying cry to the “Trojans and Lycians and Dardanians who fight hand to hand” (17.184), as he goes to put on the armor of Achilles.
---

“Trojans and Lycians and Dardanians who fight hand to hand, / be men, my friends, and remember your furious courage” (17.184–185; see [[hector]] and [[lycians]]): the cry with which Hector has rallied his army before (8.173–174, 11.286–287, 15.486–487; 13.150 and 15.425 begin the same way). He gives it before he runs to put on the armor of Achilles (17.186–188). The Dardanians' leader Aeneas is roused by Apollo in this book and fights beside Hector to the end of it (17.319–345, 17.483–534, 17.753–759; see [[aeneas]]).
