summary: The first simile is a mother's: Menelaus stands over Patroclus “as a mother stands over her calf, / one that has borne her first” (17.4–5). Achilles is the son “whom an immortal mother bore” (17.78), and his mother had often told him the purpose of Zeus in secret, but not that Patroclus would die (17.408–411; see [[mothers-and-sons]]).
