---
summary: In the simile of the lion driven from the farmyard, “thick the javelins / dart against him from bold hands.”
---

“charges straight on, but gains nothing; for thick the javelins / dart against him from bold hands, / and blazing torches, which he dreads, eager though he is” (17.661–663; [[lion-driven-from-the-farmyard]], [[menelaus]]). The simile repeats the one of Ajax's retreat in Book 11 (11.548–555 = 17.657–664). See [[lion]].
