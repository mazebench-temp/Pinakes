# Λύκτου (17.611) occurs only here; Λύκτον (2.647) is claimed by the entry.
grc: [Λύκτου]

Κοίρανον, ὅς ῥʼ ἐκ Λύκτου ἐϋκτιμένης ἕπετʼ αὐτῷ, “Coeranus, who had followed him from well-built Lyctus” (17.611; see [[coeranus-of-lyctus]] and [[meriones]]). In the Catalogue Lyctus is one of the Cretan towns that sent ships with Idomeneus and Meriones (2.647; see [[cretans]]); here it is the home of the charioteer who drives up to save Idomeneus and dies in his place (17.608–619; see [[idomeneus]]). ἐϋκτίμενος, “well-built”, is its epithet only here (see [[well-built]]).
