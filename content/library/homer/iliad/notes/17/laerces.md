---
summary: Laerces is named a second time when his son Alcimedon sees Automedon fighting alone from the chariot, and is himself given a patronymic: “Alcimedon, son of Laerces, son of Haemon” (17.467).
relations:
  - father: haemon-father-of-laerces (17.467)
---

ὀψὲ δὲ δή μιν ἑταῖρος ἀνὴρ ἴδεν ὀφθαλμοῖσιν / Ἀλκιμέδων υἱὸς Λαέρκεος Αἱμονίδαο, “But at last a companion saw him with his eyes, / Alcimedon, son of Laerces, son of Haemon” (17.466–467; see [[alcimedon]] and [[automedon]]). In Book 16 Alcimedon was “the noble son of Laerces” (16.197); here the narrator adds the father's father. The genitive Αἱμονίδαο agrees with Λαέρκεος, so the second patronymic belongs to Laerces: he is the son of Haemon (see [[haemon-father-of-laerces]]). Phoenix names his own father in the same way, with the father's patronymic, Ἀμύντορος Ὀρμενίδαο, “Amyntor, son of Ormenus” (9.448; see [[phoenix]]).
