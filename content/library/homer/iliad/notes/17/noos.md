summary: The mind of Zeus is always stronger, Hector tells Glaucus (17.176; see [[the-mind-of-zeus]]); once it is said to have turned, when Zeus sends Athena to rouse the Danaans (17.546), in a couplet Walter Leaf judges an interpolation (see the translation's note on 17.545–546). The verb is seeing: Menelaus sees Ajax and later Antilochus “on the left of all the battle” (17.116, 17.682), and Hector sees Automedon get down from the chariot (17.483).
