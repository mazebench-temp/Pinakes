---
summary: Automedon calls Alcimedon, “his trusted companion,” to keep the horses close; Athena names Patroclus “the trusted companion of noble Achilles”; and Apollo tells Hector that Menelaus has killed Podes, “your trusted companion.”
---

**Alcimedon.** “and at once he spoke to Alcimedon, his trusted companion” (17.500; [[alcimedon]], [[automedon]]), bidding him keep the horses breathing on his back.

**Patroclus.** Athena, in the likeness of Phoenix, warns Menelaus of the shame “if the trusted companion of noble Achilles / is torn by swift dogs under the wall of the Trojans” (17.557–558; [[noble]], [[swift-dogs]], [[phoenix]]).

**Podes.** Apollo, in the likeness of Phaenops, tells Hector that Menelaus “has killed your trusted companion, / brave among the champions, Podes, son of Eetion” (17.589–590; [[podes]], [[brave]], [[phaenops-son-of-asius]]). The narrator has called Podes Hector's “dear companion at the feast” (17.577). Grief covers Hector, and he goes back into the fight (17.591–592). See [[hetairos]].
