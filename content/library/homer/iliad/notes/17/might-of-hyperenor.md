---
summary: Menelaus recalls “mighty Hyperenor, tamer of horses,” whom he killed, as a warning to his brother Euphorbus.
---

{{quote:ebd330c3-f011-5af4-95a5-21cd25769709}}

Menelaus warns Euphorbus with the fate of his brother, “mighty Hyperenor, tamer of horses” (17.24; [[tamer-of-horses]], [[hyperenor]], [[menelaus]]). The killing was told in Book 14, where the verse names only “the son of Atreus” (14.516; [[son-of-atreus]]). The periphrasis gives the dead man his strength in the speech of the man who killed him. See [[euphorbus]].
