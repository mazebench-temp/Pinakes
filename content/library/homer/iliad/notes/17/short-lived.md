---
summary: Hippothous, killed by Ajax, “did not repay / his dear parents for his rearing, and short was his life,” in the words of the death of Simoeisius.
---

{{quote:c52a9d41-a9ae-5b85-adae-0df1d2404b99}}

Hippothous dies far from home, “and short was his life” (17.302; [[deep-soiled]], [[larisa]], [[great-hearted]], [[hippothous]]). The three verses end the death of Simoeisius in Book 4, also killed by Ajax (4.477–479; [[simoeisius]]). Simoeisius was named for the river beside which his mother bore him (4.474–477); Hippothous dies “far from deep-soiled Larisa.” See [[ajax]].
