---
summary: Apollo tells Hector that Menelaus has stopped Euphorbus “from his furious courage,” and Hector calls on the Trojans and allies to “remember your furious courage.”
---

“Euphorbus, son of Panthous, and stopped him from his furious courage” (17.81; [[euphorbus]], [[mentes]]); and Hector to the Trojans, Lycians and Dardanians: “be men, my friends, and remember your furious courage, / while I put on the armor of noble Achilles” (17.185–186; [[exhortation]], [[exchange-of-armor]]). The second is the whole verse of exhortation that Hector, Ajax and Patroclus use in earlier books (6.112, 8.174, 11.287, 15.487, 15.734, 16.270). See [[alke]].
