summary: Menelaus' news gives the title to the dead Patroclus: “the best of the Achaeans has been slain, / Patroclus” (ὤριστος Ἀχαιῶν, 17.689–690). Glaucus calls Achilles “by far the best / of the Argives beside the ships” in the words of Book 16 (17.164–165 = 16.271–272), and the narrator ranks Ajax first of the Danaans in looks and in deeds, “after the noble son of Peleus” (17.279–280; see [[aristos]]).
