---
summary: A note in the scholia on 24.15, collected among the fragments of the Homeric Problems, defends Achilles' dragging of Hector from Book 17: Hector had dragged Patroclus first, meaning to cut off his head, and both sides had pulled the body about like a hide; Aristotle, the note adds, explained the dragging by a custom still kept in Thessaly.
