---
summary: Hippothous' helmet splits “struck by the great spear and the strong hand” of Ajax.
---

“and the horsehair-crested helmet split around the point of the spear, / struck by the great spear and the strong hand” (17.295–296; [[horsehair-crested]], [[ajax]], [[hippothous]]). The formula χειρὶ παχείῃ, usually the hand that grips or throws, is here the force of the blow. See [[spear]].
