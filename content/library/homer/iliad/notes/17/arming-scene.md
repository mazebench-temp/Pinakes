summary: Hector's arming in Achilles' armor is not told as an arming scene. He stands apart from the battle, changes his armor, sends his own to the city, and puts on the immortal armor in one verse (17.192–197); then Zeus fits it to his body, Ares enters him, and he goes among the allies flashing in it (17.209–214; see [[exchange-of-armor]] and [[the-armor-of-peleus]]).
