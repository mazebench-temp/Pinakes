---
summary: Hector's spear sticks in the ground behind Automedon, and “mighty Ares let its might go,” as in Books 13 and 16.
---

“for he bent forward, and the long spear behind him / stuck in the ground, and the butt quivered / of the spear; and then mighty Ares let its might go” (17.527–529; [[long-spears]], [[automedon]]). The verse ends a picture of a quivering spear in Book 13 and Book 16 (13.444, 16.613). In Book 13 the spear was fixed in a beating heart; here, as in Book 16, it has missed and stuck in the ground, and the might that Ares lets go is the spear's (see the translation's note on 13.443–444).
