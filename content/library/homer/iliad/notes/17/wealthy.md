Podes, son of Eetion, was “rich and brave,” and Hector's dear companion at the feast (17.576–577; [[brave]], [[podes]], [[eetion-father-of-podes]]). The words “rich and brave” were said of Euchenor, son of Polyidus the seer, killed by Paris (13.663–664). Here the loss is Hector's, and Apollo uses it to send him back into the fight (17.586–592).
