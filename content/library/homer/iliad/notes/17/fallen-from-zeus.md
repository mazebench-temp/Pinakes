The Trojans come on with a shout like a wave “at the mouth of a river fallen from Zeus” (17.263; [[sea]], [[trojans]]). The river fed by the rain of Zeus meets the sea at its mouth, and the noise of the two is the noise of the Trojan charge. The verse-end διιπετέος ποταμοῖο is the one of the Spercheius in Book 16 (16.174; see the translation's note on 16.174).
