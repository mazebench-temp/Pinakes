---
summary: Iphitus is named as the father of Schedius when Hector's spear kills him: “Schedius, son of great-hearted Iphitus” (17.306).
---

Σχεδίον μεγαθύμου Ἰφίτου υἱὸν, “Schedius, son of great-hearted Iphitus” (17.306; see [[schedius]]): the son's death recalls the father's one title, “great-hearted”, from the Catalogue, which called both leaders of the Phocians υἷες Ἰφίτου μεγαθύμου, “sons of great-hearted Iphitus” (2.518; see [[great-hearted]] and [[phocians]]). The father's name sets this Schedius apart from the Phocian of the same name, the son of Perimedes, whom Hector killed in Book 15 (15.515–516; see [[schedius-son-of-perimedes]]). Hector's spear was meant for Ajax (17.304–311; see [[hector]]).
