---
summary: Aeneas tells Hector that a god has told him that “Zeus, the highest counselor,” is still the Trojans' helper.
---

“But one of the gods has come and stood close beside me, and says / that Zeus, the highest counselor, is still our helper in the battle” (17.338–339; [[apollo]], [[aeneas]]). The words Ζῆνʼ ὕπατον μήστωρα were Zeus' own in Book 8, when he challenged the gods to drag him from the sky with a golden rope, “Zeus, the highest counselor” (8.21–22; [[the-golden-rope]]). Aeneas has recognized Apollo in the herald Periphas (17.333–334; [[hekatebolos]]). See [[zeus]].
