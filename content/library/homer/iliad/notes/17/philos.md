summary: The book's φίλτατος, “dearest”, is Patroclus: Thetis did not tell Achilles that “by far the dearest of his companions had perished”, and Ajax sends that news (17.411, 17.655; see [[hetairos]]). Phaenops was the dearest of Hector's guest-friends, and Podes his dear companion at the feast (17.584, 17.577).
