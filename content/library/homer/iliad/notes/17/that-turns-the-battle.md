---
summary: Ajax and Menelaus see that Zeus is giving the Trojans “the victory that turns the battle.”
---

“Nor did it escape great-hearted Ajax and Menelaus / that Zeus was now giving the Trojans the victory that turns the battle” (17.626–627; [[great-hearted]], [[ajax]], [[menelaus]]). Zeus has just thundered from Ida with the aegis (17.593–596; [[tasseled]]), and the Achaean leaders read the sign, as in Book 8 his thunder was a sign of victory to the Trojans (8.170–171). See [[zeus]] and [[nike]].
