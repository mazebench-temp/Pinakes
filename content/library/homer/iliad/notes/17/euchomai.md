summary: The verb's two senses meet in the first exchange of the book. Menelaus tells Zeus that it is no fine thing “to boast so overweeningly” (17.19), Euphorbus answers that Menelaus boasts of killing his brother (17.35), and Menelaus kills him “praying to father Zeus” (17.46). Automedon prays before his kill and speaks “in triumph” after it (17.498, 17.537), and Zeus says that Hector “boasts” of Achilles' armor (17.450).
grc: [ἐπευχόμενος]
