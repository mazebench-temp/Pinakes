---
summary: Longing follows the dead and the absent. The horses weep “in longing for their charioteer” (17.439); Menelaus tells Antilochus that “great longing for him has come upon the Danaans” at Patroclus' death (17.690), and when Antilochus leaves to carry the news, “great longing for him came upon the Pylians” (17.704).
grc: [πόθῳ]
---

The verse is the same for the dead man and the living messenger, μεγάλη δὲ ποθὴ Δαναοῖσι τέτυκται and μεγάλη δὲ ποθὴ Πυλίοισιν ἐτύχθη (17.690, 17.704). It is the longing that the army felt for Achilles (see [[longing-for-achilles]]).
