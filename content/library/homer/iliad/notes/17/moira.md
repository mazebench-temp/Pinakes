summary: “Now death and fate have overtaken him”, said twice of Patroclus (17.478, 17.672; see [[thanatos]]). The Trojan voice over the body accepts the same end: “even if it is our fate to be brought down beside this man, / all of us alike, let no one yet hang back from the war” (17.421–422; see [[tis-speech]]).
