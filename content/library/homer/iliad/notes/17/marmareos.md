---
summary: The aegis that Zeus takes up on Ida is “gleaming.”
---

“And then the son of Cronus took up the tasseled aegis, / gleaming, and covered Ida in clouds” (17.593–594; [[tasseled]], [[aegis]], [[ida]]). μαρμαρέην stands at the head of the verse, after the noun. In Book 14 the same adjective was the sea's, “the gleaming sea” by which Hera swore (14.273); here it is the aegis, which flashes with the lightning of the next verse. See [[zeus]].
