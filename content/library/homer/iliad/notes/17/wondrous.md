---
summary: Apollo has cast “a heaven-sent rout” among Ajax's companions.
---

“And very quickly he saw him on the left of all the battle, / encouraging his companions and urging them on to fight; / for Phoebus Apollo had cast a heaven-sent rout among them” (17.116–118; [[phoebus]], [[rout]], [[ajax]]). θεσπέσιος, “of divine sound,” is here a rout that comes from the god, as the panic of the Achaeans at the opening of Book 9 was “heaven-sent” (9.2). See [[apollo]].
