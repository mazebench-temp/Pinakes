**One of ten.** Hector, in Achilles' armor, urges on “Mesthles and Glaucus and Medon and Thersilochus” and six more leaders of the allies (17.216–218; see [[hector]] and [[trojan-allies]]). Walter Leaf notes that these names are mostly taken from the Catalogue (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 17.216); Thersilochus is not in it, and the poem does not say whom he led. The next name in the list is that of the Paeonian leader Asteropaeus (17.217; see [[asteropaeus]]), and in Book 21 a Thersilochus is among the Paeonians whom Achilles kills after Asteropaeus has fallen (21.209).
