---
summary: Apollo taunts Hector for giving way to Menelaus, “who before now at least / was a soft spearman”; the book adds μαλθακός.
grc: [μαλθακὸς]
---

“Hector, which other of the Achaeans would still fear you, / now that you have shrunk back before Menelaus, who before now at least / was a soft spearman?” (17.586–588; [[spearmen]], [[far-worker]], [[menelaus]]). μαλθακός is a by-form of μαλακός, found only here in Homer. Apollo, in the likeness of Phaenops, shames Hector with his enemy's softness, and Hector goes back into the fight (17.591–592; [[phaenops-son-of-asius]]). See [[hector]].
