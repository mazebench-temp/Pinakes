---
summary: The φρένες “dark on every side” are filled with grief and with courage: dread grief clouds Hector's heart at Euphorbus' death (17.83), and Automedon's and Menelaus' hearts are filled with courage and strength and with the daring of a fly (17.499, 17.573). Hector tells Glaucus that he thought his wisdom surpassed all the Lycians', and now scorns his mind (17.171–173; see [[what-a-word]]).
