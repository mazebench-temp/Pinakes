summary: Achilles does not know of Patroclus' death, for the fight is “far away from the swift ships”; Meriones bids Idomeneus drive “until you reach the swift ships”; and Menelaus has sent Antilochus “to the swift ships”; the swift whip of Automedon is excepted.
except: [17.430]
---

“for they were fighting far away from the swift ships, / under the wall of the Trojans” (17.403–404; [[brilliant]], [[achilles]]): the distance is why Achilles has not heard. Meriones gives Idomeneus the reins: “Whip them on now, until you reach the swift ships” (17.622; [[meriones]], [[idomeneus]]). And Menelaus tells the Ajaxes: “That man I have sent to the swift ships / to go to Achilles, swift of foot” (17.708–709; [[antilochus]], [[swift]]). At 17.430 θοῇ is the swift whip with which Automedon strikes the horses, and it belongs to [[swift]]. See [[ships]].
