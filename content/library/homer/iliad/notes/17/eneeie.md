---
summary: The book names Patroclus' gentleness twice. Zeus, seeing Hector in Achilles' armor, says he has killed the companion of the best of men, “gentle and strong” (17.204), and Menelaus, leaving the body to look for Antilochus, charges the Ajaxes and Meriones: “now let each remember the gentleness of poor Patroclus; / for he knew how to be kind to all / while he lived” (17.670–672).
