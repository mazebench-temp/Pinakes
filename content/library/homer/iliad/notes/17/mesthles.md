---
summary: Mesthles, the Maeonian, is named first among the ten leaders of the allies whom Hector urges on when he has put on the armor of Achilles (17.216). It is his only appearance after the Catalogue.
grc: [Μέσθλην]
---

“Mesthles and Glaucus and Medon and Thersilochus” (17.216): Hector's list of the allies begins with him (see [[hector]] and [[trojan-allies]]). Walter Leaf notes that these names are mostly taken from the Catalogue, where Mesthles leads the Maeonians with his brother Antiphus (2.864–866; *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 17.216; see [[maeonians]] and [[antiphus-of-maeonia]]). The poem gives him nothing to do.
