---
summary: Zeus swears that Hector will not ride behind the immortal horses, “nor in your intricately worked chariot.”
---

“But not behind you, nor in your intricately worked chariot, / will Hector, son of Priam, ride; I will not allow it” (17.448–449; [[son-of-priam]], [[chariot]], [[the-horses-of-achilles]]). See [[zeus]].
