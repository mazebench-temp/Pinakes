---
summary: Nestor sends his sons into the war “from the black ships,” and Ajax says the Achaeans fear that Hector's hands “will fall upon the black ships.”
---

“And the two, watchful against the death and the panic of their companions, / were fighting apart, since so Nestor had commanded / urging them on to war from the black ships” (17.381–383; [[thrasymedes]], [[antilochus]], [[nestor]]). πόλεμον δέ is πόλεμόνδε, “to war” (see the translation's note on 17.383). Ajax: the Achaeans “say that no longer / will the might and the irresistible hands of man-slaying Hector / be held back, but will fall upon the black ships” (17.637–639; [[irresistible-hands]]).
