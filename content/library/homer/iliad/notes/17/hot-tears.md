---
summary: The horses of Achilles weep for Patroclus, “and hot tears / flowed from their eyelids to the ground.”
refs: [17.437-438]
---

{{quote:202afbb7-20ab-5cae-b07a-09eb5f65f48a}}

Their tears for Patroclus are the “hot tears” of mourning (17.437; [[perikalles]], [[the-horses-of-achilles]]). The formula δάκρυα θερμά is split across the verses, δάκρυα δέ σφι / θερμὰ κατὰ βλεφάρων, and the lines are counted by reference. The tears of grief for the dead are given to the immortal horses. See [[tears]].
