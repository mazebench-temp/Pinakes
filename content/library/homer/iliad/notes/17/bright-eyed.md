---
summary: “And the Trojans first pushed back the bright-eyed Achaeans,” the verse of the fight over Sarpedon in Book 16.
---

“And the Trojans first pushed back the bright-eyed Achaeans; / they left the body and shrank back” (17.274–275; [[achaeans]]). The verse was used in Book 16 when the Trojans pushed back the Achaeans over the body of Sarpedon (16.569). Here it is Patroclus' body that the Achaeans leave, until Ajax rallies them (17.278–287; [[like-a-boar]], [[fighting-over-the-body]]).
