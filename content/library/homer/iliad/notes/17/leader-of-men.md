---
summary: Euphorbus calls Menelaus “leader of the people” in a whole verse of titles that the Odyssey uses in Menelaus' palace.
---

“Menelaus, son of Atreus, nurtured by Zeus, leader of the people, / draw back, and leave the body, and let the bloody spoils be” (17.12–13; [[zeus-nurtured]], [[son-of-atreus]], [[bloody-spoils]]). The verse of courtesy introduces a demand, and Euphorbus ends it with a threat to take Menelaus' life (17.17; [[honey-sweet]]). In the Odyssey the same verse is spoken to Menelaus by his guests in Sparta, Nestor's son Peisistratus and Telemachus (*Odyssey* 4.156, 4.291, 4.316, 15.64, 15.87). See [[menelaus]] and [[euphorbus]].
