---
summary: Athena comes to Menelaus “in the likeness of Phoenix in form and in tireless voice.”
---

“And first she spoke, urging on the son of Atreus, / mighty Menelaus; for he was close to her, / in the likeness of Phoenix in form and in tireless voice” (17.553–555; [[mighty]], [[phoenix]], [[athena]]). The half-verse δέμας καὶ ἀτειρέα φωνήν belongs to gods in disguise: Poseidon as Calchas in Book 13 (13.45), Athena as Deiphobus in Book 22 (22.227). See [[menelaus]].
