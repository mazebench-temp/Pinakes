---
summary: Athena comes down like a purple rainbow that Zeus stretches out as a portent “of war / or of chilling winter.”
---

“As when a purple rainbow is stretched out for mortals / by Zeus from heaven, to be a portent either of war / or of chilling winter, which from their work / makes men cease upon the earth, and troubles the flocks, / so she, wrapping herself in a purple cloud, / entered the crowd of the Achaeans” (17.547–552; [[mortal-men]], [[teras]], [[athena]]). The rainbow is a sign of storm, not of hope (see the translation's note on 17.547), and the winter it may bring stops all work on the land. See [[zeus]].
