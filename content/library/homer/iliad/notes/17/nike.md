---
summary: Victory belongs to Zeus and goes to the Trojans. Hector says that the mind of Zeus “takes away victory / easily” (17.177–178); Apollo tells Aeneas that Zeus wills victory for the Trojans (17.331–332); Zeus shakes the aegis and gives them victory (17.596), and Ajax and Menelaus see that he is giving them “the victory that turns the battle” (17.626–627). Menelaus' news to Antilochus is “victory is the Trojans'” (17.689).
