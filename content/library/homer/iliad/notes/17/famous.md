---
summary: Euphorbus and Hector speak of the Trojans' “famous allies,” the armor of Euphorbus and of Achilles is “the glorious armor” five times, Schedius ruled in “famous Panopeus,” and Ajax calls Menelaus “famous Menelaus”; the book adds κλειτῷ and ἀγακλεές.
grc: [κλειτῷ, ἀγακλεὲς]
---

**The allies.** Euphorbus claims to have hit Patroclus first, before any “of the Trojans or their famous allies” (17.14; [[trojan-allies]]), as at 3.451; and Hector, in Achilles' armor, “went among the famous allies, / with a great cry” (17.212–213).

**The glorious armor.** Apollo says that Menelaus would have carried off “the glorious armor of the son of Panthous” (17.70; [[euphorbus]]), and Hector sees him “taking away the glorious armor” (17.85). Hector has taken “the glorious armor from Patroclus” (17.125); the Trojans carry “the glorious armor of the son of Peleus” to the city (17.191); and Zeus foretells that Andromache will not receive “the glorious armor of the son of Peleus” from Hector (17.208; [[andromache]], [[exchange-of-armor]]).

**Panopeus and Menelaus.** Schedius, “by far the best of the Phocians, who in famous Panopeus / had his home” (17.307–308; [[panopeus]], [[schedius]]). And Ajax answers Menelaus: “All this you have said fairly, famous Menelaus” (17.716; [[menelaus]]). The vocative ἀγακλεές and the dative κλειτῷ are new to the entry; the vocative is Hera's word to Hephaestus in Book 21 (21.379). See [[kleos]].
