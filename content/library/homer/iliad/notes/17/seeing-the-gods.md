---
summary: Only Aeneas knows a god in this book: “Aeneas knew Apollo who shoots from afar / as he looked him in the face” (17.333–334), and tells Hector that a god has come and stood beside him (17.338). Hector does not know Apollo as Mentes or as Phaenops, nor Menelaus Athena as Phoenix, and Athena comes down hidden in a purple cloud (17.73–82, 17.551–555, 17.582–590; see [[divine-epiphany]]).
