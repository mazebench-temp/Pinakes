---
summary: Hippothous' “horsehair-crested helmet” splits around Ajax's spear; the book adds the nominative ἱπποδάσεια.
grc: [ἱπποδάσεια]
---

“struck him at close quarters through the bronze-cheeked helmet; / and the horsehair-crested helmet split around the point of the spear, / struck by the great spear and the strong hand” (17.294–296; [[bronze-cheeked]], [[strong-hand]], [[hippothous]]). The nominative ἱπποδάσεια κόρυς is new to the entry. See [[helmet]].
