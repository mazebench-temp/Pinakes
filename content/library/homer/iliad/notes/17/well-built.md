summary: Coeranus, Meriones' charioteer, “had followed him from well-built Lyctus”; the book adds the genitive ἐϋκτιμένης.
grc: [ἐϋκτιμένης]
---

“but Meriones' squire and charioteer, / Coeranus, who had followed him from well-built Lyctus” (17.610–611; [[coeranus-of-lyctus]], [[lyctus]], [[meriones]]). Lyctus was among the Cretan cities of the Catalogue (2.647). The genitive ἐϋκτιμένης is new to the entry.
