---
summary: “Brilliant” names Panthous' wife Phrontis in Euphorbus' threat, Achilles who does not yet know that Patroclus is dead, Thrasymedes sent to the Pylians, and twice Hector, in Menelaus' words and in Ajax's.
---

**Phrontis.** Euphorbus would lay Menelaus' head and armor “in the hands of Panthous and of brilliant Phrontis” (17.40; [[phrontis]], [[panthous]]), his father and mother, to end their lament for his brother.

**Achilles.** “Nor yet at all / did brilliant Achilles know that Patroclus was dead; / for they were fighting far away from the swift ships” (17.401–403; [[swift-ships]], [[achilles]]).

**Thrasymedes.** Menelaus “sent brilliant Thrasymedes to them” (17.705; [[thrasymedes]], [[pylians]]), to the Pylians whom Antilochus has left.

**Hector.** Menelaus does not think that Achilles will come now, “however angry he is with brilliant Hector” (17.710), and Ajax promises that he and the other Ajax will hold off “the Trojans and brilliant Hector” (17.719; [[the-two-ajaxes]], [[hector]]).
