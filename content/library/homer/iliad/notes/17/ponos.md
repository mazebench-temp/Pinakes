summary: The fight over the body is the book's toil. Euphorbus says that the toil will not be untried much longer (17.41); Apollo, as Mentes, goes back “into the toil of men” (17.82); Zeus stretches over Patroclus “an evil toil” of men and horses (17.400–401; see [[the-rope-of-war]]); and Ajax tells Menelaus and Meriones to lift the body and “carry it out of the toil” (17.718).
