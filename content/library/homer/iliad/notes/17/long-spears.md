---
summary: Hector's “long spear” sticks in the ground behind Automedon, and Idomeneus' snaps on Hector's corselet: “the long spear snapped at the socket.”
---

“for he bent forward, and the long spear behind him / stuck in the ground, and the butt quivered / of the spear” (17.527–529; [[obrimos]], [[automedon]], [[hector]]). Later Idomeneus hits Hector “on the corselet, on the chest beside the nipple; / but the long spear snapped at the socket, and they shouted, / the Trojans” (17.606–608; [[idomeneus]]). Both long spears of the book fail. See [[spear]].
