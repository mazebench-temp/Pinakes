---
summary: Not even Ares, “who rouses armies,” nor Athena, would have made light of the toil over the body.
---

“and around it the toil arose, / savage; not Ares, who rouses armies, nor Athena, / would have made light of it when they saw it, not even if great anger came upon them” (17.397–399; [[ares]], [[athena]], [[ponos]]). The two gods of war are named as judges of the fight, as in Book 13 they were named as judges of the battalions around the two Ajaxes, which not even Ares could have made light of, “nor Athena who rouses armies” (13.127–128). There the epithet was Athena's; here it is given to Ares. See [[fighting-over-the-body]].
