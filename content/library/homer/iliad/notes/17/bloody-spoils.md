---
summary: Euphorbus bids Menelaus leave “the bloody spoils” of Patroclus, and Automedon puts the “bloody spoils” of Aretus in his chariot.
---

“draw back, and leave the body, and let the bloody spoils be” (17.13; [[euphorbus]], [[menelaus]]): Euphorbus claims the spoils of the man whom none of the Trojans hit before him (17.14–15). Later Automedon kills Aretus and strips him: “So saying, he took the bloody spoils and put them in the chariot, / and mounted it himself, his feet and his hands above / bloody” (17.540–542; [[bloody]], [[automedon]], [[stripping-the-dead]]). See [[aretus]].
