{{quote:a52af6a8-5c41-5871-ae5d-1b000d6ab89a}}

Menelaus and Meriones carry the body like mules that drag a beam “down from the mountain along a rocky path” (17.743; [[mules]], [[krateros]]). The accusative παιπαλόεσσαν is new to the entry; in the Odyssey it is the epithet of a lookout and of Ithaca (*Odyssey* 10.97, 11.480). See [[menelaus]] and [[meriones]].
