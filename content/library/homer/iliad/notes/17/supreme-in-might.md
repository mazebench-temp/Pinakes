“and the earth was wet / with purple blood, and they fell heaped close together, / the dead of the Trojans and their mighty allies / and of the Danaans” (17.360–363; [[trojan-allies]], [[huge]]). The epithet of Zeus, given in the plural to kings (8.236), is given only here to the Trojans' allies. See [[androktasia]].
