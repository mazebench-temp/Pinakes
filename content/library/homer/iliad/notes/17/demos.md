---
summary: Apollo, as Periphas, tells Aeneas of men who saved their cities trusting in their numbers and “having a people above fear” (ὑπερδέα δῆμον, 17.330), a phrase whose sense was disputed in antiquity (see the translation's note on 17.330). Hector honored Podes most “of all the people” (17.576–577).
