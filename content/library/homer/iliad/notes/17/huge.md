---
summary: Hector answers Glaucus that he did stand up to “huge Ajax,” and “huge Ajax” commands the Achaeans to stand firm around the body.
---

“but now I utterly scorn your mind, from what you have said, / you who say that I did not stand up to huge Ajax” (17.173–174; [[glaucus]], [[hector]]). Then, after the Achaeans close their ranks: “So huge Ajax commanded; and the earth was wet / with purple blood” (17.360–361; [[fighting-over-the-body]]). He has bidden them let no one draw back and no one fight out in front, but stand firm and fight at close quarters (17.356–359). See [[ajax]].
