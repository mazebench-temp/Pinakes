---
summary: Menelaus bids the Ajaxes and Meriones remember the gentleness of Patroclus, who “knew how to be kind to all / while he lived.”
---

{{quote:1aa6b216-47c0-56eb-b781-f9e40fd0e52e}}

Patroclus “knew how to be kind to all / while he lived” (17.671–672; [[patroclus]], [[menelaus]]). Menelaus speaks as he leaves the body to find Antilochus. The last verse is the one with which Automedon ended his praise of Patroclus' skill with the horses (17.478; [[counselor-equal-to-the-gods]]). Zeus has called Patroclus “gentle and strong” (17.204; [[krateros]]), with ἐνηής, the adjective of the ἐνηείη, “gentleness,” that Menelaus now recalls.
