---
summary: Zeus himself takes up “the tasseled aegis,” covers Ida in clouds, and gives the victory to the Trojans.
---

{{quote:8c14ffd9-b19a-59fb-b868-2fbc8e162cf6}}

The tasseled aegis is in Zeus' own hands (17.593; [[son-of-cronus]], [[marmareos]], [[ida]], [[aegis]]), and with it the victory passes to the Trojans. Athena put on the tasseled aegis in Book 5 (5.738), and Zeus gave it to Apollo to shake at the Achaeans in Book 15 (15.229); here he holds it himself. See [[zeus]].
