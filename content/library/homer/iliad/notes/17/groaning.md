---
summary: Away from the mist the armies fight at their ease, “avoiding one another's groaning missiles.”
---

“and they fought with pauses between, / avoiding one another's groaning missiles, / standing far apart” (17.373–375; [[pitiless-bronze]]). The formula βέλεα στονόεντα, used in Books 8 and 15 of missiles poured out with a wondrous din (8.159, 15.590), here belongs to a fight at a distance, where men can still avoid them. Around the body in the mist the best men are worn down by the bronze (17.375–377). See [[arrows]].
