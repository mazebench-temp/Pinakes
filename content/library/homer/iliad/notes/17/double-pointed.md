---
summary: The Trojans follow the bearers of the body, “stabbing at them with swords and double-pointed spears,” in the verse of Books 13 and 15.
---

“so the Trojans for a while kept following in a mass, / stabbing at them with swords and double-pointed spears; / but whenever the two Ajaxes turned round / and stood against them, their color changed” (17.730–733; [[the-two-ajaxes]]). The verse is used in Book 13 of the Achaeans who drive Hector back (13.147), and in Book 15 of the Danaans who “for a while kept following in a mass” until they saw Hector and were afraid (15.277–280). Here the same pursuit is the Trojans', and the two Ajaxes turn on them like a boar on hounds (17.725–729; [[boar]]). See [[spear]].
