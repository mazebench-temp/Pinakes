---
summary: Outside the mist, “the other Trojans and the well-greaved Achaeans” fight at their ease under a clear sky.
---

“But the other Trojans and the well-greaved Achaeans / fought at their ease under the upper air, and the sharp light / of the sun was spread over them” (17.370–372; [[sharp]], [[helios]]). The verse-end pairing of the two armies here sets the rest of the field against the best men around the body, who fight in the mist (17.366–369, 17.375–377; [[pitiless-bronze]]). See [[achaeans]].
