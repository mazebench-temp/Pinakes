---
summary: Idomeneus had left “the curving ships” and come on foot.
---

“for at first Idomeneus had left the curving ships and come / on foot, and he would have put great power in the Trojans' hands, / had not Coeranus quickly driven up the swift-footed horses” (17.612–614; [[idomeneus]], [[coeranus-of-lyctus]], [[swift-footed]]). The parenthesis explains why Idomeneus is in Meriones' chariot (see the translation's note on 17.612–616). See [[ships]].
