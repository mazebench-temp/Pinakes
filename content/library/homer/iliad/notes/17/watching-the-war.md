summary: The fight over the body is seen and not seen. The mist hides it, and the narrator turns to the listener: “you would not have said / that the sun was still safe, or the moon” (17.366–367); not even Ares or Athena, seeing it, would have made light of it (17.398–399); Zeus watches Hector arm and the horses weep (17.198, 17.441); and when he scatters the mist at Ajax's prayer, “all the battle was seen” (17.650).
