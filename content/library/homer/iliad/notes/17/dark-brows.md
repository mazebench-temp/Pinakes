---
summary: Zeus nods to his promise for Hector “with his dark brows,” in the verse of his nod to Thetis.
---

“He spoke, and the son of Cronus nodded with his dark brows” (17.209; [[son-of-cronus]]). The verse is the one of Book 1 (1.528; [[neuo]]), and the nod again confirms a promise for Hector: here, great power in recompense for the death that is near him (17.206–208; [[hector]]). In the next verse Zeus fits Achilles' armor to Hector's body (17.210; see the translation's note on 17.210–211). See [[zeus]].
