---
summary: Athena, as Phoenix, speaks first to “mighty Menelaus,” and in the simile of the ridge that holds back water, “the harsh streams of mighty rivers.”
---

“And first she spoke, urging on the son of Atreus, / mighty Menelaus; for he was close to her” (17.553–554; [[athena]], [[phoenix]], [[menelaus]]). The epithet of strength is given to him as the goddess is about to put strength into his shoulders and knees (17.569). In the last simile of the carrying of the body the two Ajaxes hold back the Trojans as a wooded ridge holds back water, “which holds back even the harsh streams of mighty rivers” (17.749; [[grievous]], [[wooded]], [[the-two-ajaxes]]).
