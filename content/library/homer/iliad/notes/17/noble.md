---
summary: Ajax is “the son of noble Telamon,” and Athena warns Menelaus that “the trusted companion of noble Achilles” may be torn by dogs.
---

“so the son of noble Telamon, shining Ajax, / easily going in among them, scattered the battalions of the Trojans” (17.284–285; [[telamonian]], [[phaidimos]], [[telamon]]). Athena, in the likeness of Phoenix, says to Menelaus: “To you, Menelaus, it will be a shame and a reproach / if the trusted companion of noble Achilles / is torn by swift dogs under the wall of the Trojans” (17.556–558; [[trusted-companion]], [[swift-dogs]], [[oneidos]], [[phoenix]]). See [[achilles]].
