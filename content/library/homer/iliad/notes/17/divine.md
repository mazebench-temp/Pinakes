---
summary: Zeus sees Hector “arming himself in the armor of the divine son of Peleus.”
---

“And when Zeus the cloud-gatherer saw him from afar / arming himself in the armor of the divine son of Peleus, / he shook his head and spoke to his own heart” (17.198–200; [[cloud-gatherer]], [[son-of-peleus]], [[zeus]]). Hector has put on the armor that the gods gave Peleus (17.194–197; [[heavenly-gods]]), and Zeus, pitying him, foretells his death (17.201–208; [[wretch]], [[exchange-of-armor]]). See [[achilles]].
