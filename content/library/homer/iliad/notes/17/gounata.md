summary: Automedon leaves the outcome where it lies, “on the knees of the gods” (17.514; see [[on-the-knees-of-the-gods]]). The knees are the seat of strength: Zeus puts might into the knees of the weeping horses, and Athena strength into Menelaus' shoulders and knees (17.451, 17.569); Lycomedes' spear loosens the knees of Apisaon (17.349); and in the tug-of-war over the body the knees, shins and feet of every man are spattered with sweat (17.385–387).
