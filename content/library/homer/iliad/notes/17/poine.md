---
summary: Zeus gives Hector great power for the day “in recompense” (ποινήν), since he will not come home from the battle for Andromache to receive Achilles' armor from him (17.206–208). The penalty-word names what Zeus gives for what Hector will lose.
grc: [τείσεις]
en: [you will surely pay]
---

The power is granted as the price of a death, and Zeus nods to confirm it (17.209; see [[neuo]] and [[the-mind-of-zeus]]). The older payment is Euphorbus' threat at the start of the book: “now you will surely pay / for my brother, whom you killed” (τείσεις, 17.34–35), and he would end his parents' lament with Menelaus' head and armor (17.38–40; see [[lament]] and [[kasignetos]]).
