---
summary: Automedon speaks to the weeping horses of Achilles “with soothing words, and often with threats.”
---

“Truly Automedon, the valiant son of Diores, / often touched them, striking them with the swift whip, / and often spoke to them with soothing words, and often with threats” (17.429–431; [[strong-spear]], [[swift]], [[automedon]], [[the-horses-of-achilles]]). Gentleness and threats fail alike: the horses will go neither to the ships nor into the battle (17.432–433). See [[gentle-words]].
