---
summary: The flocks are named once in Book 17, in the simile of the rainbow: Zeus stretches it out as a portent of war or of chilling winter, which keeps men from their work and troubles the flocks (17.547–550).
grc: ["μῆλα δὲ κήδει"]
en: [flocks]
---

ἀνθρώπους ἀνέπαυσεν ἐπὶ χθονί, μῆλα δὲ κήδει, “makes men cease upon the earth, and troubles the flocks” (17.550; see [[like-rainbows]]). μῆλα are the small livestock, sheep and goats together, and the winter storm that the rainbow foretells harms the herdsman's beasts as it stops the farmer's work.
