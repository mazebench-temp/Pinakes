---
summary: Automedon throws his “long-shadowed spear” at Aretus, in the verse of the great duels.
---

“He spoke, and poising his long-shadowed spear, he let it fly, / and struck Aretus on his shield, balanced on every side” (17.516–517; [[balanced-on-every-side]], [[aretus]], [[automedon]]). The verse opens the casts of Menelaus against Paris (3.355), of Hector against Ajax (7.244) and of Achilles against Hector (22.273); here a charioteer uses it against a lesser Trojan, and the spear kills (17.518–524).
