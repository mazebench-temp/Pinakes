---
summary: The Achaeans stand around Patroclus “fenced with their bronze-fitted shields”; the book adds the dative plural χαλκήρεσιν.
grc: [χαλκήρεσιν]
---

“But the Achaeans / stood around the son of Menoetius with one heart, / fenced with their bronze-fitted shields” (17.266–268; [[son-of-menoetius]], [[shield]]). The wall of shields is the defense of the body, and over it Zeus pours a mist (17.268–273). The dative plural χαλκήρεσιν is new to the entry; in later books it is said of spears (18.534, 20.258).
