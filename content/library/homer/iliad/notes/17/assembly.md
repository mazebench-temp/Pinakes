summary: No assembly in Book 17. The two forms of ἀγορεύω are speaking: Euphorbus tells Menelaus that he boasts of his brother's death “as you speak” (ἐπευχόμενος δʼ ἀγορεύεις, 17.35), and Hector answers Glaucus' charge of cowardice, “as you say” (ὡς ἀγορεύεις, 17.180).
# ἀγορεύεις (17.35, 17.180): "speak, say", as at 16.101 and 16.627.
