# Πανοπῆϊ (17.307) occurs only here. Πανοπῆος at 23.665 is Panopeus the man, father of Epeius.
grc: [Πανοπῆϊ]
---

ὃς ἐν κλειτῷ Πανοπῆϊ / οἰκία ναιετάασκε πολέσσʼ ἄνδρεσσιν ἀνάσσων, “who in famous Panopeus / had his home and ruled over many men” (17.307–308; see [[schedius]]). The Catalogue lists Panopeus among the towns of the Phocians (2.520; see [[phocians]]); here it is the home of their best man as he dies, killed by the spear Hector threw at Ajax (17.304–311). κλειτός, “famous”, is its epithet only here (see [[famous]]); the Odyssey calls it καλλίχορος, “of the fair dancing places” (Od. 11.581).
