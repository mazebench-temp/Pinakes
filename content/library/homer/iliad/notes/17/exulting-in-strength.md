**The boar.** “nor the deadly wild boar's, whose spirit is greatest of all / in his breast, exulting surpassingly in his strength” (17.21–22; [[oloophron]], [[boar]]).

**The lion.** Ajax stands over the son of Menoetius “like a lion over his young, / when men meet him in the forest as he leads his little ones, / huntsmen; and he exults in his strength” (17.133–135; [[lion]], [[ajax]]).

Both uses are in similes of beasts, with the present indicative in place of the participle of the line-end formula (8.337, 12.42). The present σθένεϊ βλεμεαίνει is new to the entry.
