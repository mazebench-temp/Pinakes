---
summary: Automedon gives Alcimedon “the whip and the glossy reins.”
---

“But you, take the whip and the glossy reins, / and I will get down from the chariot to fight” (17.479–480; [[alcimedon]], [[automedon]]). The verse is Aeneas' to Pandarus in Book 5 (5.226), when he offers him the reins of his own horses; there Pandarus refuses and keeps to the fighting, here Alcimedon takes the reins (17.481–482). See [[chariot]].
