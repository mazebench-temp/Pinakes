---
summary: Lethus is named once, in the Catalogue's words, as the father of Hippothous: “the shining son of Lethus the Pelasgian” (17.288).
---

Λήθοιο Πελασγοῦ, “of Lethus the Pelasgian” (17.288), are the Catalogue's words for him (2.843), repeated when his son Hippothous drags the body of Patroclus by the foot and Ajax kills him (17.288–303; see [[hippothous]] and [[pelasgians]]).
