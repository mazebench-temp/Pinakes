summary: Two sons of Panthous die at Menelaus' hands, and the book remembers the parents. Hyperenor did not go home to gladden his wife and his devoted parents, and his brother Euphorbus blames Menelaus for their lament and hopes to lay Menelaus' head and armor in the hands of Panthous and Phrontis (17.24–40); Euphorbus dies in his place (17.43–60). Hippothous, killed over the body far from Larisa, “did not repay / his dear parents for his rearing” (17.301–302; see [[threptra]]).
