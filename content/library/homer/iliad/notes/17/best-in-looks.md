---
summary: Glaucus opens his rebuke of Hector with “Hector, best in looks,” the address with which Hector twice reproached Paris.
---

“Hector, best in looks, you fall far short in battle, it seems. / Truly it is for nothing that noble fame is yours, a runaway as you are” (17.142–143; [[glaucus]], [[kleos]]). The address is the one Hector uses to Paris, “Evil Paris, best in looks” (3.39, 13.769; [[paris]]), and Glaucus turns it on Hector himself. In what follows Glaucus reproaches him with leaving Sarpedon's body and with not standing up to Ajax (17.149–168; [[sarpedon]], [[ajax]]); Hector answers that the mind of Zeus is always stronger (17.176; [[the-mind-of-zeus]]), and puts on Achilles' armor. See [[hector]].
