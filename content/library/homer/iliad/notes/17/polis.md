---
summary: The city is where the Trojans want to take Patroclus' body and Achilles' armor: Hector sends the armor “to the city, to be great fame for him” (17.131, 17.191), and the Trojans hope “to drag him to their own city and win glory” (17.287, 17.419). Glaucus tells Hector to consider how he will save the city alone (17.144), and Achilles never expected Patroclus to sack the citadel, with him or without him (17.406–407).
---

The last simile of fire is a city's: the war blazes around the bearers of the body like a fire that rushes on “a city of men” and the houses dwindle in the great blaze (17.737–739; see [[the-burning-city]]).
