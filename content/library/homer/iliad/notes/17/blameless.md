---
summary: “Noble” names Patroclus as he falls and as Thrasymedes and Antilochus do not yet know he is dead, Achilles as Hector goes to put on his armor and as Ajax is ranked after him, and Laodocus, to whom Antilochus gives his armor.
---

**Patroclus.** Euphorbus did not neglect “noble Patroclus when he fell” (17.10; [[euphorbus]]), and Thrasymedes and Antilochus had not yet learned “that noble Patroclus was dead” (17.379; [[kydalimos]]).

**Achilles.** Hector bids the Trojans fight “while I put on the armor of noble Achilles” (17.186), and Ajax stood out “of all the Danaans, after the noble son of Peleus” (17.280; [[son-of-peleus]]).

**Laodocus.** Antilochus “gave his armor to his noble companion / Laodocus” (17.698–699; [[laodocus-companion-of-antilochus]], [[antilochus]]) before he runs to Achilles.
