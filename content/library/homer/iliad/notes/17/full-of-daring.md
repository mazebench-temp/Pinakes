---
summary: Glaucus wishes there were in the Trojans “a might full of daring,” such as comes into men who fight for their country.
---

“For if there were now in the Trojans a might full of daring, / undaunted, such as comes into men who for their country / have set toil and contest against hostile men, / we would quickly drag Patroclus into Ilios” (17.156–159; [[hostile-men]], [[glaucus]], [[menos]]). Glaucus wants the body to exchange for the armor and body of Sarpedon (17.160–163; [[sarpedon]]); he speaks to Hector, who has given way before Ajax (17.128–131, 17.166–168).
