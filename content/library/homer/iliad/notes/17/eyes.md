summary: Menelaus' “shining eyes / roved everywhere” to find Antilochus, as the eagle's do for the hare (17.674–681; see [[apostrophe]]); Ajax prays Zeus to “grant us to see with our eyes” (17.646); and Antilochus' eyes fill with tears at the news (17.695–696).
---

Glaucus accuses Hector of not daring to stand against Ajax “looking him in the eye” (17.167), and the lion over its young draws down the skin of his brow, “covering his eyes” (17.136; see [[the-lion-and-its-cubs]]).
