summary: Purple is the color of blood and of omen. Around the body “the earth was wet / with purple blood” (17.360–361); Athena comes down like a purple rainbow, “wrapping herself in a purple cloud” (17.547–551). Walter Leaf takes πορφύρεος to convey not a bright color but turbidity: the rainbow belongs to the storm-cloud, and “lurid as is the rainbow-cloud, so lurid was the cloud in which Athene wrapped herself” (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 17.547; see the translation's note on 17.547).
grc: [πορφυρέῃ, πορφυρέῳ]
