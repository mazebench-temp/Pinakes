The horses of Achilles stand like a pillar on a tomb, “holding the beautiful chariot” (17.436; [[the-horses-of-achilles]], [[tomb]], [[chariot]]). The beautiful chariot stands still, with the weeping horses in its yoke. See [[automedon]].
