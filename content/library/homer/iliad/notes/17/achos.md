---
summary: Grief strikes Hector twice: dread grief clouds his heart when Apollo tells him that Menelaus has killed Euphorbus (17.83), and “a black cloud of grief covered him” when Apollo tells him of Podes' death (17.591), the verse that will close the news of Patroclus' death to Achilles (18.22). Automedon fights on grieving for his companion and eases his heart of grief a little by killing Aretus (17.459, 17.538–539).
grc: [ἄχεος, ἀκηχέδατʼ]
---

Ajax imagines the Achaeans at the ships “grieving, I think, as they look this way” (ἀκηχέδατʼ, 17.637), and the grief of Achilles is the news that Antilochus runs to carry (17.640–701; see [[messenger-scene]] and [[penthos]]).
