summary: The body is fought for between the city and the ships: the Trojans hope to drag it to Ilios, “and the Achaeans' / to the hollow ships” (17.396–397), and at the end it is carried “toward the hollow ships” (17.736). Achilles is at the ships, far from the fighting, and does not know (17.403); the news is sent there (17.691, 17.708), and Ajax fears that Hector's might will fall on the black ships (17.638–639).
