---
summary: “Apollo, son of Zeus,” speaks to Aeneas in the likeness of the herald Periphas, in the verse with which he spoke to Hector in Book 16.
---

“in his likeness Apollo, son of Zeus, spoke to him” (17.326; [[periphas-son-of-epytus]], [[aeneas]]). The verse is the one of Book 16, when Apollo spoke to Hector in the likeness of Asius (16.720; [[asius-son-of-dymas]]), and it returns, with the epithet ἑκάεργος, when he speaks to Hector in the likeness of Phaenops (17.585; [[far-worker]]). See [[apollo]].
