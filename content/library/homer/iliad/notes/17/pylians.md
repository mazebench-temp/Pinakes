# Πυλίοισιν (17.704); Πυλίοισι (11.753) is claimed in the Book 11 note.
grc: [Πυλίοισιν]
refs: [17.703, 17.705]
---

μεγάλη δὲ ποθὴ Πυλίοισιν ἐτύχθη, “and great longing for him came upon the Pylians” (17.704; see [[antilochus]]). Antilochus has left his companions to take the news of Patroclus' death to Achilles (17.697–701), and the narrator gives his men's loss in the words Menelaus has just used of Patroclus and the whole army, “great longing for him has come upon the Danaans” (17.690; see [[danaans]]). Menelaus does not stay to help them himself, “but he sent brilliant Thrasymedes to them” (17.702–705; see [[thrasymedes]] and [[menelaus]]). Nestor had kept his two sons fighting apart from the struggle for the body (17.381–383; see [[nestor]]); now one of them leads the Pylians alone.
