---
summary: Apollo tells Aeneas that the Trojans “tremble beyond telling and do not fight.”
en: [beyond telling]
---

“but Zeus wills far rather for us than for the Danaans / victory; but you yourselves tremble beyond telling and do not fight” (17.331–332; [[periphas-son-of-epytus]], [[aeneas]], [[apollo]]). The neuter ἄσπετον is an adverb here, of fear that cannot be told. The god in the herald's likeness shames the Trojans with the will of Zeus ([[plan-of-zeus]]).
