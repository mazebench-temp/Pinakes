---
summary: Alcimedon asks Automedon which god has put “a profitless plan” in his breast, that he fights alone from the chariot.
---

“Automedon, which of the gods has put a profitless plan / in your breast, and taken away your good wits, / that you fight against the Trojans in the front of the throng / alone?” (17.469–472; [[alcimedon]], [[automedon]], [[boule]]). Alone in the chariot Automedon can neither drive nor fight (17.463–465; [[hieros]]), and his charges gain him nothing until Alcimedon takes the reins and he gets down to fight (17.479–483; [[glossy]]).
