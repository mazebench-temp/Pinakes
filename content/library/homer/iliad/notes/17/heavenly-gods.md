summary: Hector puts on the armor of Achilles, “which the heavenly gods / gave to his dear father.”
---

{{quote:35c4ed99-f1a4-528c-a5d5-b53cf90e47fe}}

Hector puts on the armor “which the heavenly gods / gave to his dear father” (17.195–196; [[son-of-peleus]], [[peleus]], [[exchange-of-armor]]). The gods' gift to Peleus is told only in a phrase. They also gave him the immortal horses (16.381, 16.867), and Hera recalls that all the gods came to his wedding with Thetis (24.59–63). See [[olympians]].
