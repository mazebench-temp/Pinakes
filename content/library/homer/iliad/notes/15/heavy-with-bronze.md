---
summary: When Zeus breaks Teucer's bowstring, “the arrow heavy with bronze” goes astray; the book adds the nominative χαλκοβαρής.
grc: [χαλκοβαρής]
---

“For he broke the well-twisted string on his flawless bow / as he was drawing it against him; and it went astray elsewhere, / the arrow heavy with bronze, and the bow fell from his hand” (15.463–465; [[teucer]], [[arrows]], [[bow]], [[blameless]]). The arrow is named by its weight, the bronze of its head, as it fails: run over into a new verse, ἰὸς χαλκοβαρής takes the place of the death it was meant to bring to Hector (15.458–460; [[armed-in-bronze]]). The Odyssey has the same words for the arrow of Odysseus that passes through all the axes (*Odyssey* 21.421–423). The nominative χαλκοβαρής is new to the entry.
