---
summary: Andraemon is named as the father of Thoas, who advises the chiefs to stand together against Hector (15.281).
---

τοῖσι δʼ ἔπειτʼ ἀγόρευε Θόας Ἀνδραίμονος υἱός, “Then Thoas, son of Andraemon, spoke among them” (15.281; see [[thoas]]): the father's name in the genitive with υἱός, as in the Catalogue and in Book 13 (2.638, 13.216).
