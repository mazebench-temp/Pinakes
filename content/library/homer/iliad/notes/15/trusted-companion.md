---
summary: Stichius dies, “a trusted companion of great-hearted Menestheus,” and Ajax mourns Lycophron, “our trusted companion,” killed by Hector.
---

**Stichius.** “Hector killed Arcesilaus and Stichius, / the one a leader of the bronze-armored Boeotians, / the other a trusted companion of great-hearted Menestheus” (15.329–331; [[stichius]], [[menestheus]], [[great-hearted]], [[hector]]).

**Lycophron.** Hector's spear misses Ajax and kills Lycophron, and Ajax calls to his brother: “Teucer, my friend, our trusted companion has been killed, / the son of Mastor, who came from Cythera and lived in our house, / and whom we honored in our halls like our own dear parents” (15.437–439; [[ajax]], [[teucer]], [[lycophron]], [[my-dear]], [[exile-and-refuge]]). The formula's first two uses are for men killed in this book; three of its later uses are for Patroclus (17.557, 18.235, 18.460), who, after a killing, was taken into the house of Peleus and brought up with Achilles, as Lycophron lived in Ajax's house (23.85–90; [[patroclus]]).
