---
summary: Of the many spears thrown as Apollo leads the Trojans, some are fixed “in the flesh of young men swift in war.”
---

“And many spears from bold hands / were fixed, some of them, in the flesh of young men swift in war, / and many, too, in between, before they could reach the white flesh, / stood fixed in the ground” (15.314–317; [[bold]], [[white]], [[spear]]). The verse-end ἀρηϊθόων αἰζηῶν is the one of Teucer's boast in Book 8, that all eight of his arrows were fixed “in the flesh of young men swift in war” (8.297–298; [[teucer]]). The young men are named by their speed in battle at the moment they are struck.
