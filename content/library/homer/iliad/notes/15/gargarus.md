ἀνὰ Γαργάρῳ ἄκρῳ, “on the peak of Gargarus”, are the words in which Zeus slept there with Hera (14.352; see [[the-deception-of-zeus]]). A θυόεν νέφος, “a fragrant cloud”, is wreathed around him like a crown (ἐστεφάνωτο); on the same peak a golden cloud hid him and Hera (14.350–351; see [[the-golden-cloud]]). θυόεις, “fragrant”, from θύος, “incense”, occurs only here in Homer; the summit's altar is θυήεις, “fragrant” with sacrifice (8.48; see [[fragrant]]). From the peak he sends Iris to Poseidon and Apollo to Hector (15.157–235; see [[zeus]], [[iris]] and [[apollo]]).
