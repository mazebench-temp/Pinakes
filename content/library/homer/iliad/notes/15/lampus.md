# Λάμπος and the patronymic Λαμπετίδης (15.526) occur only here.
grc: ["^Λάμπος", Λαμπετίδης]
# Λαομεδοντιάδης (15.527) is Lampus.
refs: [15.527]
---

Λαμπετίδης, ὃν Λάμπος ἐγείνατο φέρτατον υἱὸν / Λαομεδοντιάδης (15.526–527): the patronymic, found nowhere else, is explained at once, “the son of Lampus, the strongest son that Lampus, / son of Laomedon, fathered”. Λαομεδοντιάδης, “son of Laomedon”, goes with Lampus, as Aeneas' genealogy confirms (20.237–238; see [[laomedon]]). The strongest of his sons wounds no one: Meges' corselet stops his spear, Meges shears the plume from his helmet, and Menelaus spears him from behind (15.525–543; see [[dolops-son-of-lampus]], [[meges]] and [[menelaus]]). Hector, seeing the Achaeans busy about Dolops' armor, rebukes Melanippus, son of Hicetaon, for not being moved by his cousin's death (15.553–555; see [[melanippus-son-of-hicetaon]]).
