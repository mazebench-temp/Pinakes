grc: ["κλυτὸν ἐννοσίγαιον"]

“And standing near she spoke to the famous shaker of the earth” (15.173), and “Deeply troubled, the famous shaker of the earth said to her: / ‘Ah, truly, brave as he is, he has spoken arrogantly’” (15.184–185; [[iris]], [[poseidon]], [[shaker-of-the-earth]]). The title that marked the beginning and the end of Poseidon's intervention in Book 14 (14.135, 14.510) frames the exchange in which he is ordered to give it up. The accusative κλυτὸν ἐννοσίγαιον is new to the entry; the Odyssey has it in the Cyclops' mouth (*Odyssey* 9.518).
