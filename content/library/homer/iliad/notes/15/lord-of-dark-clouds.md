---
summary: Hera ends her oath with a promise to advise even Poseidon “to go wherever you lead, lord of the dark clouds.”
---

“But I would advise even him / to go wherever you lead, lord of the dark clouds” (15.45–46; [[hera]], [[poseidon]], [[zeus]], [[oath]]). The vocative κελαινεφές closes the speech in which Hera has sworn that Poseidon did not act at her will (15.36–46; [[blessed-gods]], [[earth-shaker]]). The address is a prayer's: Agamemnon opened his prayer before the first day of battle with it (2.412), and here it is the submission of a wife who has just been threatened. Zeus smiles at it (15.47; [[father-of-men-and-gods]]).
