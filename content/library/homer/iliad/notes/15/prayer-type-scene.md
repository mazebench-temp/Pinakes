summary: Nestor's prayer at the ships has the parts of the scene: the gesture, “stretching out his hands to the starry sky”; the invocation, “Father Zeus”; the claim, the sacrifices and the promise of homecoming at Argos; the request, to ward off the pitiless day; and the answer, the thunder of Zeus, which the Trojans take for their own sign (15.367–380; see [[prayer]] and [[nestor]]).
