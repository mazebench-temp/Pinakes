---
summary: Patroclus spreads healing drugs “on the grievous wound” of Eurypylus.
---

“And cheered him with talk, and on the grievous wound / he spread healing drugs against the black pains” (15.393–394; [[patroclus]], [[eurypylus]], [[black-pains]], [[healing]]). λυγρός is rendered “grievous” here, as in the entry's other senses of what brings grief. The phrase ἕλκεϊ λυγρῷ is found only here in Homer. The wound is the one that Paris' arrow made in Eurypylus' thigh (11.581–584; [[paris]]).
