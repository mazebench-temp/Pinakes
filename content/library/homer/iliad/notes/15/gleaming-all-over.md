---
summary: Ares orders Terror and Rout to yoke his horses and puts on “his gleaming armor” to avenge his son.
---

“So he spoke, and he told Terror and Rout to yoke / his horses, and he himself put on his gleaming armor” (15.119–120; [[ares]], [[terror]], [[rout]], [[ascalaphus]]). The verse-end ἔντεʼ ἐδύσετο παμφανόωντα is the one in which Meleager at last armed and went out to save his city (9.596; [[meleager]]). Here the god arms against the will of Zeus, and the arming is undone: Athena takes the helmet from his head, the shield from his shoulders and the spear from his hand before he can leave (15.121–142; [[athena]], [[furious]], [[arming-scene]]).
