“Then Hector killed Schedius, son of Perimedes, / leader of the Phocians, and Ajax killed Laodamas, / leader of the foot soldiers, the splendid son of Antenor” (15.515–517; [[laodamas]], [[antenor]], [[ajax]], [[splendid]], [[schedius-son-of-perimedes]]). The verses pair a leader killed on each side. Laodamas is another of the sons of Antenor to die in the battle for the ships and the wall, after Iphidamas and Coon at Agamemnon's hands (11.221–263) and Archelochus at Ajax's (14.463–464; [[archelochus]]). The phrase Ἀντήνορος ἀγλαὸν υἱόν, with the epithet between father and son, is new to the entry.
