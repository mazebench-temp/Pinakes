---
summary: Iris and Apollo come before “Zeus the cloud-gatherer,” who sits on Gargarus wreathed in a fragrant cloud, and Zeus the cloud-gatherer sends Apollo to Hector.
---

“They came to Ida of the many springs, mother of wild beasts, / and they found the far-seeing son of Cronus on the peak of Gargarus, / sitting, and a fragrant cloud was wreathed around him. / And the two came before Zeus the cloud-gatherer / and stood” (15.151–155; [[iris]], [[apollo]], [[gargarus]], [[fragrant]], [[far-seeing]]). The cloud around the god on the peak follows the golden cloud in which he lay with Hera (14.342–351; [[the-golden-cloud]]). When Iris has gone, “then Zeus the cloud-gatherer spoke to Apollo” (15.220), in the line-end νεφεληγερέτα Ζεύς, and sends him with the aegis to Hector (15.221–235; [[aegis]], [[hector]]).
