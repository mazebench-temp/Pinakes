---
summary: Before the war Melanippus “grazed shambling cattle / in Percote.”
---

“For a time he grazed shambling cattle / in Percote, while the enemy were still far away” (15.547–548; [[melanippus-son-of-hicetaon]], [[percote]], [[cattle]], [[destructive]]). The cattle that are a man's wealth and the sign of peace come into the story of a Trojan's life before the war; Strabo cites these verses for the extent of Priam's land along the coast (*Geography* 13.1.7). In Book 21 Poseidon reminds Apollo that he once herded the shambling cattle of Laomedon (21.448).
