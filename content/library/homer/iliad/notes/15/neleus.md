---
summary: Neleus is named in his son's patronymic when Zeus thunders, “hearing the prayers of the old son of Neleus” (15.378).
---

ἀράων ἀΐων Νηληϊάδαο γέροντος, “hearing the prayers of the old son of Neleus” (15.378; see [[nestor]], [[son-of-neleus]] and [[geron]]). Nestor has prayed to Zeus to remember the thigh pieces burned to him in Argos and to ward off the pitiless day, and Zeus answers with thunder, which the Trojans take for their own (15.370–380).
