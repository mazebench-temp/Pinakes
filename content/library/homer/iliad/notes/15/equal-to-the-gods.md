---
summary: Rebuked by Hector, Melanippus follows him into the fight, “a man equal to the gods.”
---

“So saying, he led the way, and the other followed, a man equal to the gods” (15.559; [[melanippus-son-of-hicetaon]], [[hector]]). The verse is used three times, and each time the man who follows has the epithet: Ajax following Menelaus to the rescue of Odysseus (11.472), Melanippus here, and Meriones following Patroclus (16.632). Hector has just reproached his cousin for holding back while the Achaeans strip Dolops (15.552–558; [[dolops-son-of-lampus]]); the praise goes to the man who answers the reproach by following. Antilochus kills him soon after (15.576–578; [[antilochus]]).
