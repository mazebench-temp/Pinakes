---
summary: Polites, Priam's son, kills the Achaean Echius “in the front line of battle” (15.339–340), in the verse in which Polydamas kills Mecisteus.
---

Μηκιστῆ δʼ ἕλε Πουλυδάμας, Ἐχίον δὲ Πολίτης / πρώτῃ ἐν ὑσμίνῃ, “And Polydamas killed Mecisteus, and Polites Echius, / in the front line of battle” (15.339–340). Mecisteus is the “son of Echius” who carried the wounded Teucer and Hypsenor from the field (8.333, 13.422); the poem does not say whether the Echius whom Polites kills is his father (see [[echius-killed-by-polites]], [[mecisteus-son-of-echius]] and [[polydamas]]). In Book 13 Polites led his wounded brother Deiphobus out of the battle (13.533–539; see [[deiphobus]]). Echius is the only man he kills in the poem.
