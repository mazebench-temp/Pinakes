---
summary: Iris and Apollo come “to Ida of the many springs, mother of wild beasts,” and find Zeus on Gargarus.
---

“They came to Ida of the many springs, mother of wild beasts, / and they found the far-seeing son of Cronus on the peak of Gargarus” (15.151–152; [[ida]], [[iris]], [[apollo]], [[of-the-many-springs]], [[far-seeing]], [[gargarus]]). It is the third time the verse brings gods to Ida: Zeus himself in Book 8 (8.47), Hera and Sleep in Book 14 (14.283), and here the two messengers whom Zeus has summoned to undo what Hera and Sleep did there. The dual τὼ … ἐλθόντε follows at once (15.154).
