summary: Hector calls to the Trojans, “Rush on the ships, and leave the bloody spoils!” and threatens death to any man he sees hanging back.
---

“And Hector called out to the Trojans, shouting aloud: / ‘Rush on the ships, and leave the bloody spoils! / Whomever I see away from the ships, on the other side, / there and then I will devise his death’” (15.346–349; [[hector]], [[stripping-the-dead]], [[exhortation]]). The Trojans have been stripping the men they killed while the Achaeans fled into the ditch (15.343–345). Nestor gave the same order to the Achaeans in their pursuit of Book 6, “let no one now fling himself on the spoils and stay behind,” but promised that they could strip the dead at their ease afterward (6.67–71); Hector promises no spoils, only death and dogs for the man who hangs back (15.349–351; [[dogs]]).
