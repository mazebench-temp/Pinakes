# Μυκηναῖον (15.638), "of Mycenae", and Μυκηναίων (15.643), "of the Mycenaeans", occur only here.
grc: [Μυκηναῖον, Μυκηναίων]
en: [Mycenaeans]
---

ὃ δʼ οἶον ἔπεφνε Μυκηναῖον Περιφήτην, “but he killed only Periphetes of Mycenae” (15.638), and of him: καὶ νόον ἐν πρώτοισι Μυκηναίων ἐτέτυκτο, “and in mind he was among the first of the Mycenaeans” (15.643; see [[periphetes-of-mycenae]] and [[hector]]). The adjective and the name of the people occur only here in Homer. Elsewhere the Iliad names the city as Agamemnon's, “rich in gold” (2.569; 7.180, 9.44, 11.46), and as one of the three cities Hera loves most (4.52). His father Copreus carried the messages of Eurystheus to Heracles (15.639–640; see [[copreus]] and [[eurystheus]]), and in later accounts Mycenae was Eurystheus' city, where Copreus found refuge (Apollodorus, *Library* 2.5.1).
