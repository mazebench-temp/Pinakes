---
summary: Iris is to go “among the army of the bronze-armored Achaeans,” and Hector kills Arcesilaus, “a leader of the bronze-armored Boeotians.”
---

Zeus sends Iris “so that she may go among the army of the bronze-armored Achaeans / and tell lord Poseidon / to cease from war” (15.56–58; [[iris]], [[poseidon]], [[achaeans]]), with the line-end Ἀχαιῶν χαλκοχιτώνων. In the first killings after Hector's return the epithet is given to the Boeotians: “Hector killed Arcesilaus and Stichius, / the one a leader of the bronze-armored Boeotians” (15.329–330; [[arcesilaus]], [[boeotians]], [[hector]]). It is the only time the Boeotians have it; it has been given before to the Epeians (4.537, 11.694), to the Cretans (13.255) and, in the title of Aeneas, to the Trojans (5.180). Arcesilaus was one of the five Boeotian leaders of the Catalogue (2.495).
