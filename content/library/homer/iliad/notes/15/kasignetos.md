summary: Book 15 is a book of brothers and cousins. Poseidon's claim rests on the three brothers born of Cronus; Teucer and Ajax fight together and speak to each other as brothers; and Hector, losing his cousins Caletor, Dolops and Melanippus one after another, calls on all his kinsmen.
grc: [γνωτοί, γνωταί]
en: [kinsmen, kinswomen]
passages:
  - 15.185-199 | The three brothers
  - 15.436-477 | Ajax and Teucer

**Divine brothers.** “For we are three brothers, born of Cronus, whom Rhea bore, / Zeus and I, and the third is Hades” (ἀδελφεοί, 15.187–188; see [[the-lots-of-the-three-brothers]], [[rhea]] and [[cronus]]). Zeus' order claims the right of the elder brother, and Iris settles the quarrel with the Erinyes who follow the elder (15.166, 15.204; see [[the-erinyes-follow-the-elder]] and [[genee]]).

**Brothers in arms.** Ajax and his half-brother Teucer fight side by side at the ships, and the verse that introduces each one's appeal is the same: “And Ajax shuddered, and spoke to his brother” (15.436), “And Teucer shuddered, and spoke to his brother” (15.466; see [[ajax]] and [[teucer]]). Medon, killed by Aeneas, was “the brother of Ajax”, the other Ajax, son of Oileus, and had gone into exile for killing “a kinsman of his stepmother” (γνωτόν, 15.334–336; see [[medon]], [[ajax-son-of-oileus]] and [[in-laws]]).

**Hector's kinsmen.** On the Trojan side kinship is the call to battle. Hector threatens that a man who hangs back will lie unburied: “his kinsmen / and kinswomen shall not give him his portion of fire” (γνωτοί τε γνωταί τε, 15.349–350; see [[cremation]]). His cousins die in turn. When Ajax kills Caletor, Hector sees “his cousin” fall (ἀνεψιόν, 15.422; see [[caletor]]); when Menelaus kills Dolops, “Hector called on his kinsmen, / all of them” (κασιγνήτοισι, 15.545–546; see [[dolops-son-of-lampus]]), and rebukes Melanippus for not being moved “now that your cousin is killed” (15.554); and Melanippus himself falls a few verses later (15.576–578; see [[melanippus-son-of-hicetaon]]). Caletor's father Clytius, Dolops' father Lampus and Melanippus' father Hicetaon are Priam's brothers, the elders at the Scaean gate of Book 3 (3.146–147; 20.237–238; see the translation's note on 15.526–527).
