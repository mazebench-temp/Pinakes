---
summary: Hera swears by “your own sacred head,” and Iris goes down from Ida “to sacred Ilios.”
---

**Zeus' head.** In her oath Hera calls to witness Earth, Heaven and the water of Styx, “and your own sacred head, and the wedded bed of us two, / by which I would never swear idly” (15.39–40; [[hera]], [[oath]], [[zeus]]). The head of Zeus is the head with which he nods his assent (15.75; 1.524–527), and it is sacred because it is a god's.

**Ilios.** Sent by Zeus to Poseidon, Iris “went down from the mountains of Ida to sacred Ilios” (15.169; [[iris]], [[troy]], [[ida]]), in the verse with which she went to Hector in Book 11 (11.196).
