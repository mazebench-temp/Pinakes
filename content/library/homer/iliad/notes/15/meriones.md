---
summary: Meriones is named among the leaders who, on Thoas' advice, gather the best men to stand against Hector (15.301–305).
---

“Teucer and Meriones and Meges, the equal of Ares” (15.302): Meriones stands with Idomeneus, whose companion he is, among the leaders who call the best men together against Hector while the multitude goes back to the ships (15.301–305; see [[idomeneus]], [[thoas]] and [[battle-order]]). He has no other part in the book. In Book 13 he fought beside Idomeneus on the left of the ships, where he killed Adamas and Harpalion (13.567–575, 13.650–655; see [[adamas]] and [[harpalion]]).
