---
summary: Meges, “the equal of Ares,” stands among the best of the Achaeans who meet Hector's return, and later kills Croesmus and shears the plume from Dolops' helmet.
---

“Those around Ajax and lord Idomeneus, / Teucer and Meriones and Meges, the equal of Ares, / called the best men and set the battle in order / against Hector and the Trojans” (15.301–304; [[meges]], [[ajax]], [[idomeneus]], [[teucer]], [[meriones]], [[battle-order]]). The epithet is the one with which Meges entered the Catalogue as leader of the men of Dulichium (2.627). It stands in a list of names, but the book gives it substance: Meges springs at Polydamas and kills Croesmus in his place, and when Dolops strikes his shield he strikes back at Dolops' helmet and breaks off its plume (15.520–538; [[croesmus]], [[dolops-son-of-lampus]], [[bronze-fitted]]).
