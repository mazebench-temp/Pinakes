---
summary: Hera swears by Earth “and the wide Heaven above,” and Poseidon says that in the division by lot “Zeus got the wide sky”; the book adds the nominative Οὐρανὸς εὐρύς.
grc: ["Οὐρανὸς εὐρὺς"]
en: [wide Heaven]
---

**The oath.** “Let Earth now be witness to this, and the wide Heaven above, / and the down-flowing water of Styx” (15.36–37; [[hera]], [[oath]], [[earth]], [[sky]], [[styx]], [[blessed-gods]]). The text capitalizes Earth and Heaven here as powers called to witness, and the translation renders them so; the same verses are Calypso's oath in the Odyssey, where the editors print them in lowercase (*Odyssey* 5.184; see the translation's note on 15.36–38). The nominative Οὐρανὸς εὐρύς is new to the entry.

**The lot.** “And Zeus got the wide sky, in the upper air and the clouds; / but the earth is still common to all, and high Olympus” (15.192–193; [[zeus]], [[poseidon]], [[the-lots-of-the-three-brothers]], [[high-olympus]]). The formula οὐρανὸν εὐρύν, elsewhere the sky to which men look when they pray or complain (3.364, 7.178), is here Zeus' share of the world.
