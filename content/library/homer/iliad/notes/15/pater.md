summary: Zeus is “the father of men and gods” as he pities Hector and as he smiles at Hera's oath (15.12, 15.47), “Father Zeus” in Nestor's prayer (15.372), and “father Zeus” before whom, with Hector, the Achaeans flee (15.637); Apollo “did not fail to heed his father” (15.236). Among men, Periphetes is the better son of “a far worse father” (15.641; see [[fathers-and-sons]]).
