summary: Hera asks the gods whether they are still eager to stop Zeus “by word or by force” (ἢ ἔπει ἠὲ βίῃ, 15.106), the pair with force in place of deed; and Zeus, sending Apollo to Hector, says that from the time the Achaeans reach the ships he himself “will devise both deed and word” to let them breathe again (ἔργον τε ἔπος τε, 15.234; see [[plan-of-zeus]]).
