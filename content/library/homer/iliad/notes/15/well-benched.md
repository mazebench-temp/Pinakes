---
summary: Ajax tells Teucer to take up a spear: “Let them not, even though they have overpowered us, take without a struggle / the well-benched ships.”
---

“But take a long spear in your hands, and a shield on your shoulder, / and fight the Trojans, and rouse the rest of the army. / Let them not, even though they have overpowered us, take without a struggle / the well-benched ships; let us remember the joy of battle” (15.474–477; [[ajax]], [[teucer]], [[long-spears]], [[charme]]). The accusative νῆας ἐϋσσέλμους opening the verse is the one of Odysseus' warning to Achilles in the embassy, that it was in doubt whether the Achaeans would save or lose the well-benched ships unless he put on his courage (9.230–231; [[embassy]]). Now the ships are about to be taken. See [[ships]].
