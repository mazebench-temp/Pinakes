---
summary: Hector rages at the ships “as when spear-brandishing Ares, or deadly fire, / rages in the mountains.”
---

“He raged as when spear-brandishing Ares, or deadly fire, / rages in the mountains, in the thickets of the deep forest” (15.605–606; [[hector]], [[ares]], [[deadly]], [[forest-fire]], [[blosyros]]). The epithet that Agamemnon gave to the Trojans' allies (2.131) and the narrator to Polydamas in Book 14 (14.449) is here the war god's own, in a simile that makes Hector his likeness. The verb μαίνομαι, “rage,” is repeated in the simile itself (μαίνετο … μαίνηται). See [[like-huge-ares]].
