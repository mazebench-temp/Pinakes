---
summary: Hector throws “his shining spear” at Ajax, Antilochus throws his “shining spear” at Melanippus, and the plume of Dolops' helmet falls in the dust “bright with fresh purple.”
---

**Two throws.** After Caletor's death Hector calls to the Trojans, “So saying, he threw his shining spear at Ajax. / He missed him, but then Lycophron” (15.429–430; [[hector]], [[ajax]], [[lycophron]]), and Antilochus, urged on by Menelaus, “leapt out from the champions and threw his shining spear, / glancing around him” (15.573–574; [[antilochus]], [[menelaus]]), and kills Melanippus. The line-end ἀκόντισε δουρὶ φαεινῷ is the formula of the throw, and each time the spear finds a man, though not always the man aimed at.

**The plume.** Meges breaks the horsehair plume from Dolops' helmet, “and it all fell / to the ground in the dust, bright with fresh purple” (15.537–538; [[meges]], [[dolops-son-of-lampus]], [[horsehair-crested]], [[purple]]). φοίνικι φαεινός is the phrase of the belt that Oeneus gave Bellerophon as a guest-gift, “a belt bright with purple” (6.219); here the brightness of the dye lies in the dust.
