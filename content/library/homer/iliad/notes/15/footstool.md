summary: Driven back by missiles, Ajax gives ground a little from the deck “to the seven-foot bench” of the ship, the θρῆνυς, which elsewhere in Homer is a footstool; here it is a bench in the ship, a thwart or the steersman's seat, and Ajax stands on it to keep the fire from the ships (15.727–731; see the translation's note on 15.729, [[ajax]] and [[ships]]).
grc: ["θρῆνυν ἐφʼ ἑπταπόδην"]
