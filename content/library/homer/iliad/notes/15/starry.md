summary: Nestor prays at the ships “stretching out his hands to the starry sky”; the book adds the accusative ἀστερόεντα.
grc: [ἀστερόεντα]
---

“And above all Nestor, the Gerenian, the guardian of the Achaeans, / prayed, stretching out his hands to the starry sky” (15.370–371; [[nestor]], [[prayer]], [[sky]], [[guardian-of-the-achaeans]]). The gesture of prayer is made toward the sky, where the god lives, and the epithet is the sky's standing one, though it is day. The whole verse is the one in which the Cyclops prays to his father Poseidon in the Odyssey (*Odyssey* 9.527). The accusative ἀστερόεντα is new to the entry. Zeus hears and thunders (15.377; [[counselor]]).
