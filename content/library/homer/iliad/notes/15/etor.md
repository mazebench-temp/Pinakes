summary: Poseidon's “heart does not shrink” from speaking to Zeus as an equal (15.166, 15.182); Hector, struck by Ajax's stone, thought he would die that day, “since I was gasping out my heart” (φίλον ἄϊον ἦτορ, 15.252; see the translation's note on 15.252); and Hector asks Melanippus whether his “dear heart” is not moved by his cousin's death (15.553–554).
