---
summary: Hera takes the cup from “fair-cheeked Themis,” who runs first to meet her when she comes back to Olympus; the book adds the dative καλλιπαρῄῳ.
grc: [καλλιπαρῄῳ]
---

“She let the others be, but from fair-cheeked Themis / she took the cup; for she came running first to meet her” (15.87–88; [[themis-the-goddess]], [[hera]], [[cup]]). The epithet of the captive women of Book 1, Chryseis and Briseis, and of Theano, priestess of Athena (1.143, 1.184, 6.298), is here given to a goddess for the first time; it is given to Leto in Book 24 (24.607). The dative καλλιπαρῄῳ is new to the entry.
