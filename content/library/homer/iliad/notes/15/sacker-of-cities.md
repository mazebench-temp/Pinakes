---
summary: Zeus recalls the day “when the goddess Thetis clasped my knees, / begging me to honor Achilles, sacker of cities.”
---

“As I promised him at first, and nodded my head to it, / on the day when the goddess Thetis clasped my knees, / begging me to honor Achilles, sacker of cities” (15.75–77; [[zeus]], [[thetis]], [[achilles]], [[thetis-supplicates-zeus]], [[plan-of-zeus]]). The last verse is the one in which Athena, in Book 8, complained that Zeus had fulfilled the plans of Thetis (8.370–372); there the goddess resented the promise, and here the god who made it declares that he will keep it until Achilles' wish is fulfilled (15.72–77).
