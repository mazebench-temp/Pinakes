---
summary: The son of Oileus is named in Book 15 only as Medon's brother, in the verses of Medon's exile repeated from Book 13 (15.333–334).
---

“One was the bastard son of divine Oileus, / Medon, the brother of Ajax” (15.333–334 = 13.694–695; see [[medon]] and [[oileus]]). Αἴαντος ἀδελφεός, “the brother of Ajax”, is the son of Oileus, and the entry's Book 13 phrase claims it (see [[ajax]] for Telamonian Ajax, who excepts the line). It is his only mention in the book. The pair of the two Ajaxes, named together so often in Books 12 and 13, is not named here, and “Ajax” among the leaders who stand against Hector is the son of Telamon (15.301; see [[the-two-ajaxes]] and the translation's note on 15.301).
