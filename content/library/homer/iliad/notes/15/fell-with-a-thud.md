---
summary: Three men fall “with a thud”: Caletor, struck by Ajax as he brings fire to the ship, “and the brand dropped from his hand” (15.421); Croesmus, killed by Meges (15.524); and Melanippus, killed by Antilochus, “and darkness covered his eyes” (15.578). Each time the second half of the verse tells what follows, not the clatter of the armor (see [[androktasia]]).
