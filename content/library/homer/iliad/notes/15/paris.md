---
summary: Paris strikes the Achaean Deiochus from behind, at the base of the shoulder, as he flees among the champions (15.341–342).
---

Δηΐοχον δὲ Πάρις βάλε νείατον ὦμον ὄπισθε, “And Paris struck Deiochus at the base of the shoulder from behind” (15.341; see [[deiochus]]), as he fled among the champions. He is the last of six Trojan leaders who kill eight Achaeans as the battle scatters behind Apollo and the aegis (15.328–342; see [[trojans]]). The end of the next verse, διὰ πρὸ δὲ χαλκὸν ἔλασσεν, “and drove the bronze straight through” (15.342), is the half-verse of Hector's spear in Patroclus' flank (16.821). Paris is not named again in the book.
