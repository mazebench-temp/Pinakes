---
summary: Thoas, seeing Hector risen again, tells the Achaeans that he does not stand forth as champion “without / loud-thundering Zeus”; the book adds the genitive ἐριγδούπου.
grc: [ἐριγδούπου]
---

“But once again one of the gods has rescued and saved / Hector, who has loosened the knees of many of the Danaans, / as I think he will now too; for not without / loud-thundering Zeus does he stand forth as champion, so eager” (15.290–293; [[thoas]], [[hector]], [[zeus]]). Thoas sees a god behind the man without naming him; it is Apollo who has revived him (15.243–262; [[apollo]]). The genitive ἐριγδούπου, with Ζηνός, is new to the entry. Zeus thunders in this book in answer to Nestor's prayer (15.377; [[counselor]]).
