summary: The quarrel of the brothers is a quarrel of honor: Zeus orders Poseidon out of the battle as the stronger and the elder, and Poseidon answers that he is Zeus' “equal in honor”, since each of the three sons of Cronus received his share by lot (15.185–199; see [[time]] and [[the-lots-of-the-three-brothers]]). The honor Zeus promised Thetis for Achilles drives the rest of the book (15.72–77, 15.596–602).
