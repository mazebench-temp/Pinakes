---
summary: Iris and Apollo come “to Ida of the many springs, mother of wild beasts,” where Zeus sits on Gargarus.
---

“They came to Ida of the many springs, mother of wild beasts, / and they found the far-seeing son of Cronus on the peak of Gargarus” (15.151–152; [[ida]], [[mother-of-wild-beasts]], [[iris]], [[apollo]], [[gargarus]]). The verse brings gods to Ida for the third time (8.47, 14.283). In Book 14 the epithet named the mountain where Hera found Zeus and where she claimed her horses stood (14.157, 14.307); here the mountain's peak is the seat from which Zeus, awake, gives his orders.
