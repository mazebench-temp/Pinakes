---
summary: Raging at the ships like Ares, Hector has foam at his mouth, and his eyes blaze “under his grim brows”; the book adds the dative βλοσυρῇσιν.
grc: [βλοσυρῇσιν]
---

“He raged as when spear-brandishing Ares, or deadly fire, / rages in the mountains, in the thickets of the deep forest; / and foam formed around his mouth, and his two eyes / blazed under his grim brows, and his helmet / shook terribly around his temples as he fought, / Hector” (15.605–610; [[hector]], [[spear-brandishing]], [[deadly]], [[brows]], [[helmet]]). The grim face that Ajax wore when he went out against Hector in Book 7, “smiling with grim face” (7.212), is now Hector's, and the simile that gives it to him names Ares, as Ajax was likened to Ares then (7.208; [[like-huge-ares]]). The dative βλοσυρῇσιν is new to the entry.
