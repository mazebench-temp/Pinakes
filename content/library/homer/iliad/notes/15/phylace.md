---
summary: Phylace is named again as the place of Medon's exile, in the verses repeated from Book 13 when Aeneas kills him (15.335).
---

“Medon, the brother of Ajax; but he lived / in Phylace, away from his native land, since he had killed a man” (15.334–335 = 13.695–696; see [[medon]] and [[exile-and-refuge]]). The verses are said over Medon's body when Aeneas kills him (15.332–336). Phylace was the town of Protesilaus, and at the end of the book Hector seizes the stern of the ship that brought Protesilaus to Troy (15.704–706; see [[protesilaus]]).
