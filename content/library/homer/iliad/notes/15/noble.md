---
summary: Teucer's arrow kills Cleitus, “the companion of Polydamas, the noble son of Panthous.”
---

“And he struck Cleitus, the splendid son of Peisenor, / the companion of Polydamas, the noble son of Panthous, / as he held the reins in his hands” (15.445–447; [[cleitus]], [[peisenor]], [[polydamas]], [[panthous]], [[splendid]], [[teucer]]). Two epithets of praise stand in two verses, ἀγλαόν for the charioteer and ἀγαυοῦ for his master, before the arrow “of many groans” strikes the charioteer's neck (15.451; [[of-the-many-groans]]). Polydamas, the noble son, catches the horses and gives them to Astynous (15.453–457; [[astynous-son-of-protiaon]]).
