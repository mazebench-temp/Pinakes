---
summary: Ajax will no longer stand back “where the other sons of the Achaeans had stood back,” and goes up on the decks of the ships.
---

“Nor did it please the heart of great-hearted Ajax any longer / to stand where the other sons of the Achaeans had stood back; / but he went up and down the decks of the ships with long strides” (15.674–676; [[ajax]], [[great-hearted]], [[ships]]). The army, driven from the first row of ships, has stood its ground among the huts (15.655–658); the formula υἷες Ἀχαιῶν names the many from whom the one man separates himself. See [[achaeans]].
