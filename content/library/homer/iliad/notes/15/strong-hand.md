---
summary: Athena takes the spear “from his strong hand” as she disarms Ares.
---

“Had not Athena, terribly afraid for all the gods, / rushed out through the doorway and left the throne where she sat, / and taken the helmet from his head and the shield from his shoulders, / and she took the spear from his strong hand and stood it aside, / the bronze spear” (15.123–127; [[athena]], [[ares]], [[spear]], [[gleaming-all-over]]). The phrase στιβαρῆς ἀπὸ χειρός usually tells of a spear thrown from a strong hand, often in vain (13.505, 14.455); here a goddess takes the spear out of the hand of the war god before he can throw it. See [[furious]].
