summary: The last verse of the six that tell the clash of two armies (4.451 = 8.65), “and the earth ran with blood”, closes the fight around Protesilaus' ship, lengthened by an epithet: “and the black earth ran with blood” (ῥέε δʼ αἵματι γαῖα μέλαινα, 15.715), as it will again when Achilles routs the Trojans (20.494; see [[black-earth]]).
