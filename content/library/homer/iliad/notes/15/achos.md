summary: Athena warns Ares that he will come back to Olympus “grieving though you are” if he defies Zeus; Poseidon feels “this dread grief” when Zeus rebukes him as an inferior; and the companions of Periphetes cannot help him, “though grieving for their companion” (15.133, 15.208, 15.651; see [[ares]], [[poseidon]] and [[periphetes-of-mycenae]]).
