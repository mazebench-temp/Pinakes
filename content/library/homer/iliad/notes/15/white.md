---
summary: Many spears stand in the ground “before they could reach the white flesh,” longing to glut themselves.
---

“And many, too, in between, before they could reach the white flesh, / stood fixed in the ground, longing to glut themselves on flesh” (15.316–317; [[bold]], [[spear]], [[homers-lifeless-things-alive]]). The two verses are those of the spears thrown at Ajax in Book 11 (11.573–574; [[ajax]]). χρόα λευκόν, “the white flesh,” is the body the spears desire and do not reach.
