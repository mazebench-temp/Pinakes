---
summary: The Lycians are named only in Hector's two calls to the “Trojans and Lycians and Dardanians who fight hand to hand”, to save the body of his cousin Caletor and to fight at the ships when Zeus has broken Teucer's bowstring (15.424–425, 15.485–486). Their king Sarpedon appears only in Zeus' forecast of his death (15.67).
