Κλονίον δʼ ἕλε δῖος Ἀγήνωρ, “and brilliant Agenor killed Clonius” (15.340; see [[agenor]] and [[brilliant]]). Half a verse is all his death is given, in the list of the Achaeans killed when Apollo has shaken the aegis (15.318–342; see [[aegis]] and [[androktasia]]). He is the third of the five Boeotian leaders of the Catalogue to die, after Prothoenor (14.449–452) and Arcesilaus, whom Hector killed eleven verses earlier (15.329–330; see [[boeotians]], [[prothoenor]] and [[arcesilaus]]). The other two, Peneleos and Leitus, are both wounded in the fight over Patroclus' body (17.597–604; see [[peneleos]] and [[leitus]]).
