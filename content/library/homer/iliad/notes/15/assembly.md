summary: No assembly meets in Book 15, but Thoas is praised for his speaking in it: “in the assembly few of the Achaeans / could beat him, when the young men contended in speeches” (15.283–284; see [[thoas]]); here he gives counsel standing in the battle. The other words of the family are speaking, not assembling.
# ἀγορεύεις "you are speaking" (15.53, Zeus to Hera), ἀγόρευε "spoke among them" (15.281) and ἀγορήσατο "addressed them" (15.285) are speech in the field, not an assembly.
except: [15.53, 15.281, 15.285]
