---
summary: Laomedon is named in the patronymic of his son Lampus, the father of Dolops (15.526–527). Three of his grandsons, the sons of Priam's brothers Clytius, Lampus and Hicetaon, die at the ships in this book.
---

Λαομεδοντιάδης, “son of Laomedon” (15.527), is said of Lampus, as the narrator gives the line of Dolops, “the son of Lampus, the strongest son that Lampus, / son of Laomedon, fathered” (15.526–527; see [[lampus]] and [[dolops-son-of-lampus]]). The herald Idaeus called Priam by the same patronymic (3.250). Lampus, Clytius and Hicetaon are Laomedon's sons with Priam (20.237–238), and their sons Caletor, Dolops and Melanippus are killed at the ships in this book, Caletor by Ajax, Dolops by Menelaus and Melanippus by Antilochus (15.419–421, 15.540–543, 15.575–578; see [[caletor]] and [[melanippus-son-of-hicetaon]]).
