---
summary: “Brilliant Agenor” kills Clonius, a leader of the Boeotians, in the rout behind Apollo and the aegis (15.340).
---

Κλονίον δʼ ἕλε δῖος Ἀγήνωρ, “and brilliant Agenor killed Clonius” (15.340; see [[clonius]] and [[brilliant]]). Clonius was one of the five leaders of the Boeotians in the Catalogue (2.494–495). In the same rout Hector kills another of the five, Arcesilaus (15.329–330; see [[arcesilaus]]), and in Book 14 Polydamas killed a third, Prothoenor (14.449–452; see [[prothoenor]] and [[boeotians]]).
