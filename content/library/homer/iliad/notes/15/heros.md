summary: In Book 15 the heroes are always the Achaeans: “the Achaean heroes” missed Poseidon when he left (15.219), and they are the men whom Apollo is sent to put to flight and promises to turn back (15.230, 15.261), whom the Trojans hope to kill at the ships (15.702), and whom Ajax addresses at the last, “Friends, Danaan heroes, attendants of Ares” (15.733; see [[attendants-of-ares]]).
