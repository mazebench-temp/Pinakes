# Πάνθου, the contracted genitive (15.522; 17.9, 17.23, 17.59), always names him as a father.
grc: [Πάνθου]
---

Πάνθου υἱὸν, “the son of Panthous” (15.522), is Polydamas, whom Apollo saves from Meges' spear (15.520–522; see [[polydamas]]). The patronymic Πανθοΐδαο names him a few lines earlier, when Teucer kills his charioteer (15.446; see [[cleitus]]). In Book 17 Πάνθου υἱός is his brother Euphorbus (17.9, 17.59).
