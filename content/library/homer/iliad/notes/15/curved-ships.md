---
summary: Zeus' heart wishes to give Hector glory, “so that on the curved ships he might cast wondrous fire.”
---

“For his heart wished to hold out glory to Hector, / son of Priam, so that on the curved ships he might cast wondrous fire, / tireless fire, and fulfill the excessive prayer of Thetis / in full” (15.596–599; [[hector]], [[wondrous]], [[tireless]], [[thetis-supplicates-zeus]], [[plan-of-zeus]]). The dative νηυσὶ κορωνίσι, usually the place where the army waits or where a man sits idle (2.297, 9.609), is here the target of the fire. Achilles asked Thetis to beg Zeus to “pen the Achaeans in among their ships' sterns and along the sea” (1.409); at the curved ships that prayer is now to be fulfilled. See [[ships]].
