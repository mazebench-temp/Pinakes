---
summary: Hera, back on Olympus with Zeus' threats, laughs at the feast of the gods, but only with her lips: “and she laughed / with her lips, but her forehead above her dark brows / was not warmed” (15.101–103). It is the opposite of the unquenchable laughter that ended the gods' quarrel in Book 1; her laugh opens the bad news that sends Ares to his armor (see [[hera]], [[feast]] and [[nemesis]]).
