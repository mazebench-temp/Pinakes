summary: Seeing with one's own eyes: Thoas sees Hector risen, “a great wonder that I see with my eyes” (15.286); Hector sees his cousin fall and Teucer's missiles foiled “with his eyes” (15.422, 15.488); and Zeus waits “to see with his eyes the blaze of a burning ship” (15.600). Hector's eyes blaze under his grim brows (15.607–608), and Athena thrusts the mist from the Achaeans' eyes (15.668; see [[achlys]]).
