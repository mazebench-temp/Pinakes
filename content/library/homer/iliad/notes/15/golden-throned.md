---
summary: Zeus wakes on Ida “beside Hera of the golden throne”; the book adds the genitive χρυσοθρόνου.
grc: [χρυσοθρόνου]
---

“And Zeus awoke / on the peaks of Ida, beside Hera of the golden throne, / and he sprang up and stood, and saw the Trojans and the Achaeans” (15.4–6; [[zeus]], [[hera]], [[ida]], [[the-deception-of-zeus]]). Book 1 ended with Zeus asleep and Hera of the golden throne beside him (1.611), and in Book 14 the same epithet named her as she looked out from Olympus and planned the deception (14.153). Here it marks the end of the deception: the god wakes, sees what has been done, and turns on the goddess beside him (15.12–33). The genitive χρυσοθρόνου is new to the entry.
