summary: No duel is fought in Book 15. Thoas says that Hector would not “stand forth as champion” without Zeus (πρόμος, 15.293); the word of the champion who meets another in single combat is used of a front-fighter in the open battle, as at 5.533.
# πρόμος here is a champion in the general fighting, not in a duel.
except: [15.293]
