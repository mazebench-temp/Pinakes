---
summary: Another of Antenor's sons dies at the ships: Ajax kills Laodamas, “leader of the foot soldiers, the splendid son of Antenor” (15.516–517).
---

Αἴας δʼ ἕλε Λαοδάμαντα / ἡγεμόνα πρυλέων Ἀντήνορος ἀγλαὸν υἱόν (15.516–517): Laodamas led the men who fought on foot, πρυλέες (see [[laodamas]] and [[ajax]]). In Book 14 Ajax's spear, thrown at Polydamas, killed another of Antenor's sons, Archelochus (14.463–464; see [[archelochus]]), and in Book 11 Agamemnon killed two more, Iphidamas and Coon (11.221–263; see [[iphidamas]] and [[coon]]).
