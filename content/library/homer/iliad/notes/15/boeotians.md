---
summary: Two more of the five Boeotian leaders of the Catalogue die in the rout that follows Apollo's shaking of the aegis: Arcesilaus, killed by Hector, and Clonius, killed by Agenor (15.329–330, 15.340).
---

“Hector killed Arcesilaus and Stichius, / the one a leader of the bronze-armored Boeotians” (15.329–330; see [[arcesilaus]], [[hector]] and [[bronze-armored]]), and eleven verses later “brilliant Agenor killed Clonius” (15.340; see [[clonius]] and [[agenor]]). The poem does not call Clonius a Boeotian here; the Catalogue named him last of their five leaders, with Peneleos, Leitus, Arcesilaus and Prothoenor (2.494–495). Prothoenor fell to Polydamas in Book 14 (14.449–452; see [[prothoenor]]), so that three of the five are dead in two books, all at the hands of Trojans in the battle by the ships and the wall. Peneleos and Leitus survive to be wounded over Patroclus' body (17.597–604; see [[peneleos]] and [[leitus]]).
