---
summary: Apollo finds “the son of wise-hearted Priam, brilliant Hector,” sitting up and gathering his spirit.
---

“He found the son of wise-hearted Priam, brilliant Hector, / sitting up, no longer lying down, and he was just gathering his spirit again” (15.239–240; [[apollo]], [[hector]], [[priam]], [[son-of-priam]], [[brilliant]]). The verse is the one in which Iris found Hector in Book 11 (11.197), and Achilles used it in Book 9 when he said he would not think of fighting until Hector came to the ships of the Myrmidons (9.651). Each time Priam has the epithet, and Hector is named by his father before his own name.
