---
summary: Zeus bids Poseidon go “among the tribes of the gods, or into the shining sea,” Iris repeats the order, and Zeus tells Apollo that Poseidon “has gone into the shining sea.”
---

Zeus' order to Poseidon, carried by Iris, ends in the formula: “Bid him cease from battle and war / and go among the tribes of the gods, or into the shining sea” (15.160–161; [[zeus]], [[iris]], [[poseidon]]), and Iris repeats it word for word, changing only the person (15.176–177; [[message-repeated]]). Poseidon chooses the sea, which his lot made his own (15.190; [[gray-sea]], [[the-lots-of-the-three-brothers]]), and Zeus tells Apollo that the earth-holder “has gone into the shining sea, avoiding the sheer anger / of mine” (15.223–224; [[earth-holder]], [[steep]]). The verse-end εἰς ἅλα δῖαν is the one in which Agamemnon proposed to drag the ships into the sea in Book 14 (14.76); here it is the god, not the ships, that goes. See [[sea]].
