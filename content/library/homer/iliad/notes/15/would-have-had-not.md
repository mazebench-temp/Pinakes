summary: Twice the narrator stops at the edge of another outcome. Ares' rush to avenge his son would have brought a greater wrath from Zeus on all the gods, “had not Athena, terribly afraid for all the gods”, run out and disarmed him (15.121–127); and Teucer would have stopped Hector's fighting at the ships if he had hit him, “But he did not escape the shrewd mind of Zeus” (15.458–465). Each time a god turns the story back (see [[ares]], [[teucer]] and [[the-help-of-zeus-is-easy-to-see]]).
