summary: Meges strikes the crown of Dolops' “bronze-fitted, horsehair-crested helmet,” and Hector's kinsmen hurry to strip “the bronze-fitted armor” from Dolops' body; the book adds the genitive χαλκήρεος.
grc: [χαλκήρεος]
---

“And Meges, on his bronze-fitted, horsehair-crested helmet, / stabbed the topmost peak with his sharp spear, / and broke off the horsehair plume from it” (15.535–537; [[meges]], [[dolops-son-of-lampus]], [[horsehair-crested]], [[helmet]], [[sharp]]). The pair χαλκήρης ἱπποδάσεια is the one the Locrians lacked, who had no bronze-fitted, horsehair-crested helmets (13.714); the Odyssey uses it of the helmets brought out of the storeroom for the fight with the suitors (*Odyssey* 22.111, 22.145). When Menelaus has killed Dolops from behind, “The two hurried to strip the bronze-fitted armor from his shoulders” (15.544; [[menelaus]], [[stripping-the-dead]]), and Hector calls on his kinsmen to stop them (15.545–558). The genitive χαλκήρεος is new to the entry.
