# Ἱκεταονίδην (15.546), found only here, and Ἱκετάονος (15.576).
grc: [Ἱκεταονίδην, Ἱκετάονος]
---

Hector rebukes first “the son of Hicetaon, / mighty Melanippus” (15.546–547), and Antilochus kills “the son of Hicetaon, high-hearted Melanippus” (15.576; see [[melanippus-son-of-hicetaon]], [[hector]] and [[antilochus]]). Before the rebuke the narrator gives the son's history: he grazed cattle at Percote until the Achaean ships came, then came back to Ilios and lived beside Priam, who honored him like his own children (15.547–551; see [[percote]] and [[priam]]). Hicetaon is Priam's brother (20.237–238), and Melanippus is Hector's cousin.
