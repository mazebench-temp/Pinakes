summary: Nestor's prayer at the ships recalls the thigh-pieces burned at Argos: “if ever anyone in Argos rich in wheat, / burning to you the fat thigh pieces of ox or sheep, / prayed that he might return, and you promised and nodded your assent, / remember these things, Olympian” (15.372–375). The sacrifices of the past are his claim on Zeus, as Chryses' were in Book 1 (1.39–41; see [[prayer]] and [[sacrifice]]).
