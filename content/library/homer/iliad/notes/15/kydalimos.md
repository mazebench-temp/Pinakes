---
summary: At the ships “Hector went straight at glorious Ajax,” and the two fight over one ship.
---

“And others were fighting their battle around other ships, / and Hector went straight at glorious Ajax. / The two were toiling over one ship” (15.414–416; [[hector]], [[ajax]], [[ships]]). Neither can drive the other off, “since a god had brought him there” (15.418). In the Iliad the epithet usually names Menelaus; this is the only time it is given to Ajax, at the moment the battle narrows to a single hull and its two champions.
