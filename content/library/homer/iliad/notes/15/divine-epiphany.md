summary: Apollo comes undisguised to Hector, who is sitting up again among his companions: he asks why Hector sits apart, Hector asks in turn “Who are you, good sir, among the gods, who question me face to face?”, and the god names himself, Phoebus Apollo of the golden sword, sent by Zeus to stand by him; then he breathes might into him and goes before him into the battle wrapped in cloud (15.239–262, 15.306–308; see [[apollo]] and [[hector]]).
