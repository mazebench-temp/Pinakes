---
summary: The Achaeans fight from the ships “with the long spears that lay ready for them on the ships,” Ajax tells Teucer to take “a long spear,” and at the end of the book Ajax stabs the fire-bearers “with his long spear.”
---

“The others from the black ships, having climbed up high, / with the long spears that lay ready for them on the ships, / for fighting at sea, jointed, clad in bronze at the tip” (15.387–389; [[black-ships]], [[spear]]). The ships carry spears made for sea fights, and the book will show one of them in Ajax's hands, “a great spear for fighting at sea, / joined with bands, twenty-two cubits long” (15.677–678; [[ajax]]). When Teucer's bowstring breaks, Ajax tells him: “but take a long spear in your hands, and a shield on your shoulder, / and fight the Trojans” (15.474–475; [[teucer]]). And in the last verses, “Ajax would wait for him and stab him with his long spear; / and twelve he stabbed hand to hand in front of the ships” (15.745–746; [[blazing-fire]]).
