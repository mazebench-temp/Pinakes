summary: Two exiles die in the battle at the ships: Medon, Oileus' bastard son, who lived in Phylace after killing a kinsman of his stepmother, killed by Aeneas (15.332–336), and Lycophron, who fled Cythera after a killing and lived in Ajax's house, honored “like our own dear parents”, killed by Hector's spear (15.430–439; see [[medon]], [[lycophron]] and the translation's note on 15.430–433).
