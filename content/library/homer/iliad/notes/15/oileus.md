---
summary: Oileus is named in the verses of Medon's birth and exile, repeated from Book 13 when Aeneas kills his bastard son (15.333–336).
---

“One was the bastard son of divine Oileus, / Medon, the brother of Ajax; but he lived / in Phylace, away from his native land, since he had killed a man, / a kinsman of his stepmother Eriopis, whom Oileus had as wife” (15.333–336 = 13.694–697; see [[medon]], [[eriopis]], [[phylace]], [[nothos]] and [[divine]]). The verses that introduced Medon at the ships in Book 13 are said again over his body, when Aeneas kills him with Iasus (15.332). The father's name comes twice, for the son and for the wife. His other son, the lesser Ajax, is named in the book only as Medon's brother (15.334; see [[ajax-son-of-oileus]]).
