---
summary: Zeus gives Hector glory, but already “Pallas Athena” is rousing his fated day against him, under the strength of the son of Peleus.
---

“Honored him and gave him glory. Short-lived he was to be; / for already she was rousing his fated day against him, / Pallas Athena, under the strength of the son of Peleus” (15.612–614; [[athena]], [[hector]], [[achilles]], [[short-lived]], [[son-of-peleus]]). The full name is held back to the start of the last verse, after the work she is doing has been told. At the height of Hector's success, the narrator names the goddess who will bring him to his death at Achilles' hands, as she does in Book 22 (22.214–247). See [[plan-of-zeus]].
