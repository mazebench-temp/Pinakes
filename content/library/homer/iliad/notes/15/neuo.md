summary: Two nods of Zeus are remembered: the nod he gave Thetis, “as I promised him at first, and nodded my head to it” (ἐπένευσα, 15.75), which binds him until Achilles' wish is fulfilled; and the nod of assent that Nestor says Zeus gave to any Achaean at Argos who prayed for his return, “and you promised and nodded your assent” (κατένευσας, 15.374; see [[thetis-supplicates-zeus]] and [[prayer]]). Teucer's plume nods terribly above his helmet (15.481; see [[arming-scene]]).
