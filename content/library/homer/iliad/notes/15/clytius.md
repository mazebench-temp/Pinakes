# Κλυτίοιο (15.419, 15.427). In the Odyssey the same form is the house of another Clytius, Peiraeus' father (Od. 16.327).
grc: [Κλυτίοιο]
---

ἔνθʼ υἷα Κλυτίοιο Καλήτορα φαίδιμος Αἴας, “Then shining Ajax struck Caletor, son of Clytius” (15.419). Caletor is carrying fire to a ship when Ajax's spear hits him in the chest, and Hector sees “his cousin” fall in front of the black ship (15.419–423; see [[caletor]] and [[ajax]]): Clytius, one of the elders on the wall, is Priam's brother (3.147, 20.237–238; see [[trojan-elders]]). Hector calls on the Trojans and Lycians, “but save the son of Clytius, so that the Achaeans do not / strip him of his armor” (15.427–428; see [[hector]] and [[stripping-the-dead]]), and throws at Ajax, but his spear kills Lycophron instead (15.429–435; see [[lycophron]]).
