summary: The Achaeans fight “from the black ships, having climbed up high,” and Caletor falls “in front of the black ship” as he brings fire; the black night of 15.324 is excepted, and the book adds the phrase νηῶν ὕψι μελαινάων.
grc: ["νηῶν ὕψι μελαινάων"]
except: [15.324]
---

When the Trojans come over the wall and drive their chariots in, the two armies fight hand to hand at the sterns, “the one side from their chariots, / the others from the black ships, having climbed up high, / with the long spears that lay ready for them on the ships” (15.386–388; [[ships]], [[long-spears]], [[double-pointed]]). The adjective stands apart from νηῶν, after ὕψι, and the phrase νηῶν ὕψι μελαινάων is new to the entry. When Ajax strikes Caletor as he brings fire to a ship, Hector sees his cousin “fallen in the dust in front of the black ship” (15.419–423; [[caletor]], [[ajax]], [[hector]]); the first man in the book to carry fire to the ships dies before the hull he meant to burn.

At 15.324, μελαίνης νυκτὸς ἀμολγῷ, “in the dead of black night,” belongs to [[black-night]], and the line is excepted.
