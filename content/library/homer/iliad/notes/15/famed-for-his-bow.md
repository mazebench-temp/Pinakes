---
summary: Zeus bids Hera call to Ida “Iris to come, and Apollo famed for his bow”; the book adds the accusative κλυτότοξον.
grc: [κλυτότοξον]
---

“But if you are speaking truly and exactly, / go now among the tribes of the gods, and call here / Iris to come, and Apollo famed for his bow” (15.53–55; [[zeus]], [[hera]], [[iris]], [[apollo]]). Iris is to send Poseidon out of the battle, and Apollo to put Hector back into it (15.56–62; [[plan-of-zeus]]). The archer god is named by his weapon, but in this book he carries the aegis in place of the bow (15.229–230, 15.307–311; [[aegis]]), and it is Teucer's bow, Apollo's gift (15.441), whose string Zeus breaks ([[blameless]]). The accusative κλυτότοξον is new to the entry.
