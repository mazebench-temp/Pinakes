summary: Hector threatens any Trojan he finds away from the ships: “there and then I will devise his death, and his kinsmen / and kinswomen shall not give him his portion of fire when he is dead, / but dogs shall tear him in front of our city” (15.349–351). The verse of his own challenge in Book 7, where he asked that his body be given back so that the Trojans might give him “my portion of fire” (7.79–80), becomes a threat against his own men (see [[honoring-the-dead]] and [[stripping-the-dead]]).
