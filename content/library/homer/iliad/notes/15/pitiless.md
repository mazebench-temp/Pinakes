---
summary: Nestor prays to Zeus, “remember these things, Olympian, and ward off the pitiless day.”
---

“Remember these things, Olympian, and ward off the pitiless day, / and do not let the Achaeans be so beaten down by the Trojans” (15.375–376; [[nestor]], [[prayer]], [[olympian]]). νηλεὲς ἦμαρ is the day of death, and ἀμύνω νηλεὲς ἦμαρ is said of men who defend themselves or others: Odysseus wounded among the Trojans (11.484), Eurypylus calling on the Achaeans to protect Ajax (11.588), Idomeneus in close fighting (13.514). Here the old man asks the god to do it for them. See [[father-zeus]].
