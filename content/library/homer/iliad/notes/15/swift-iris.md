---
summary: Zeus sends Iris to Poseidon with the formula of his commands, “Go, swift Iris.”
---

“Go, swift Iris, to lord Poseidon, / take him all this message, and do not be a false messenger” (15.158–159; [[zeus]], [[iris]], [[poseidon]], [[messenger-scene]]). It is the third of the four commands that open with βάσκʼ ἴθι, Ἶρι ταχεῖα: Iris has been sent to turn back Hera and Athena (8.399) and to warn Hector (11.186), and here she is to send a god out of the battle. Zeus adds a warning to the messenger herself, μὴ δὲ ψευδάγγελος εἶναι, “do not be a false messenger” (15.159). Iris carries the message in his words, changing only the person (15.176–183; [[message-repeated]]), and then asks Poseidon whether he will soften his answer (15.201–204; [[wind-footed]]).
