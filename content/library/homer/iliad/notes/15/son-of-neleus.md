---
summary: Zeus thunders, “hearing the prayers of the old son of Neleus.”
---

“So he spoke in prayer, and Zeus the counselor thundered loudly, / hearing the prayers of the old son of Neleus” (15.377–378; [[nestor]], [[counselor]], [[prayer]], [[neleus]]). The patronymic Νηληϊάδαο and γέροντος, “old,” name Nestor by his father and his age in the verse that tells that his prayer was heard. The Trojans hear the same thunder and spring on the Argives more fiercely (15.379–380; [[aegis-bearing]]).
