summary: Whoever of the Trojans rushes at the hollow ships “with blazing fire,” Ajax waits for him and stabs him; the book adds the form πυρὶ κηλείῳ.
grc: ["πυρὶ κηλείῳ"]
---

“And whoever of the Trojans rushed at the hollow ships / with blazing fire, to do a favor to Hector who urged them on, / Ajax would wait for him and stab him with his long spear; / and twelve he stabbed hand to hand in front of the ships” (15.743–746; [[ajax]], [[hector]], [[hollow-ships]], [[long-spears]]). The book ends on the formula of the fire that Hector means to bring to the ships. In Book 8 he “would have burned the balanced ships with blazing fire” had Hera not roused Agamemnon (8.217–219), and Agamemnon told the army that Hector “will soon burn the ships with blazing fire” (8.235); here his men come close enough to be killed with the fire in their hands. The text prints κηλείῳ, with the diphthong, and the form is new to the entry. The ship of Protesilaus burns at the beginning of Book 16 (16.122–124; [[protesilaus]]).
