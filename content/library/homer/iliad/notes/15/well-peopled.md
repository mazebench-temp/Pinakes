---
summary: Zeus reminds Hera that she carried Heracles off “to well-peopled Cos.”
---

“Whom you, winning over the storms with the help of the North Wind, / sent over the barren sea, devising evil, / and then you carried him off to well-peopled Cos” (15.26–28; [[hera]], [[heracles]], [[cos]], [[boreas]], [[barren-sea]], [[hera-drives-heracles-to-cos]]). The second half of the verse is the one in which Sleep told the same story in Book 14 (14.255); Κόων δʼ εὖ ναιομένην is Κόωνδε, “to Cos.” What Sleep told as his own fear of Zeus' anger, Zeus tells as the reason for his punishment of Hera (15.18–24; [[hera-hung-from-the-sky]]).
