summary: Poseidon, yielding to Iris, uses the first half of the formula and changes the second: “and I will tell you something else, and this I threaten from my heart” (15.212). In place of advice to be laid to heart comes a threat against Zeus: if he spares Troy, there will be incurable anger between them (15.213–217; see [[cholos]] and [[poseidon]]).
