---
summary: Poseidon names Hermes, with Athena, Hera and Hephaestus, among the gods against whose will Zeus would be sparing Ilios.
grc: [Ἑρμείω]
---

Yielding to Zeus, Poseidon warns that if Zeus spares steep Ilios “against my will and that of Athena, driver of spoil, / of Hera and Hermes and lord Hephaestus”, there will be anger between the brothers beyond healing (15.213–217; see [[poseidon]] and [[zeus]]). Ἑρμείω is a contracted genitive of Ἑρμείας, found only here; the Odyssey has the full form Ἑρμείαο (*Odyssey* 12.390, 15.319). Hermes has taken no part in the fighting, but he is counted with the gods who favor the Achaeans, and in Book 20 he goes with them to the ships (20.33–35; see [[athena]], [[hera]] and [[hephaestus]]).
