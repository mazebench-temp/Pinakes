---
summary: Teucer kills Cleitus, “the splendid son of Peisenor,” and Ajax kills Laodamas, “the splendid son of Antenor.”
---

The verse-end ἀγλαὸν υἱόν names two Trojans killed by the two sons of Telamon. Teucer “struck Cleitus, the splendid son of Peisenor, / the companion of Polydamas, the noble son of Panthous” (15.445–446; [[cleitus]], [[peisenor]], [[teucer]], [[noble]]), and Ajax “killed Laodamas, / leader of the foot soldiers, the splendid son of Antenor” (15.516–517; [[laodamas]], [[antenor]], [[ajax]], [[son-of-antenor]]). The phrase praises a son by his father; it was used of Periphas, “the splendid son of Ochesius,” whom Ares was stripping (5.842–843). Here, as there, it is said of a man killed in battle.
