---
summary: The river Selleis is named with Ephyra in the history of Meges' corselet, in the half-verse that brought Astyocheia from her home (15.531 = 2.659).
---

ἐξ Ἐφύρης, ποταμοῦ ἄπο Σελλήεντος, “from Ephyra, from the river Selleis” (15.531 = 2.659; see [[ephyra]], [[phyleus]], [[meges]] and [[euphetes]]). The Selleis of the Troad, by Arisbe, has the same name in the same case (2.839, 12.97), and its entry excepts this line (see [[selleis-arisbe]]). Strabo has the Selleis flowing from Pholoe into the sea between Cyllene and Cape Chelonatas, in Elis, with Ephyra on its banks (*Geography* 8.3.5; see [[cyllene-elis]] and [[strabo]]).
