grc: [ἀμφιγύοις]

The formula frames the fighting of the book. Before they see Hector risen, the Danaans follow the Trojans in a body, “stabbing at them with swords and double-pointed spears” (15.277–278; [[danaans]], [[antlered]]), the verse of 13.147. When the Trojans come over the wall, the armies fight “at the sterns / hand to hand with double-pointed spears” (15.385–386; [[stern]], [[black-ships]]), the only time the word stands inside the verse; the form ἀμφιγύοις is new to the entry. At the ship of Protesilaus no one waits any longer for arrows or javelins: “they fought with sharp axes and battle-axes / and with great swords and double-pointed spears” (15.711–712; [[axe]], [[sharp]], [[protesilaus]]), where μεγάλοισι takes the place of the particle in the formula.
