---
summary: Apollo bids Hector urge on his horsemen “to drive their swift horses against the hollow ships,” and promises to make the road smooth.
---

“But come now, urge on your many horsemen / to drive their swift horses against the hollow ships; / and I will go on ahead, and the road for the horses / I will make smooth all the way” (15.258–261; [[apollo]], [[hector]], [[horses]], [[hollow-ships]]). In Book 12 Polydamas warned against driving “our swift horses” through the ditch (12.62; [[polydamas]]); here the god promises to clear the way, and he does, kicking the banks of the trench into it and bridging a path for the chariots (15.355–359; [[the-achaean-wall]], [[chariot-drawing]]).
