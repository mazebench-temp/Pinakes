---
summary: Eriopis, the wife of Oileus, is named again when Aeneas kills her stepson Medon, in the verses of his exile repeated from Book 13 (15.336).
---

γνωτὸν μητρυιῆς Ἐριώπιδος, ἣν ἔχʼ Ὀϊλεύς, “a kinsman of his stepmother Eriopis, whom Oileus had as wife” (15.336 = 13.697; see [[medon]], [[oileus]] and [[exile-and-refuge]]). The verse comes back with the three before it when Aeneas kills Medon (15.332–336). Homer names her only in these two places, and only through the killing that sent her stepson into exile.
