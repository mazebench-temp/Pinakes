---
summary: Zeus foretells that “shining Hector” will kill Patroclus, Zeus bids Apollo take “shining Hector” as his care, and “shining Ajax” kills Caletor as he brings fire to a ship.
---

**Hector.** In Zeus' forecast, “and shining Hector will kill him with the spear / before Ilios” (15.65–66; [[plan-of-zeus]], [[patroclus]]), and in his orders to Apollo, “and let shining Hector be your own care, you who shoot from afar” (15.231; [[apollo]], [[hekatebolos]]). The formula φαίδιμος Ἕκτωρ, which closed three verses of Book 14 before his fall, is spoken now by the god who will raise him again and decides how far he will go. See [[hector]].

**Ajax.** “Then shining Ajax struck Caletor, son of Clytius, / as he brought fire to the ship, in the chest with his spear” (15.419–420; [[ajax]], [[caletor]], [[clytius]]). The formula φαίδιμος Αἴας is Ajax's own, as φαίδιμος Ἕκτωρ is Hector's (5.617, 11.496); here it names the man who keeps Hector from the ships.
