“Hector killed Arcesilaus and Stichius, / the one a leader of the bronze-armored Boeotians, / the other a trusted companion of great-hearted Menestheus” (15.329–331; see [[hector]], [[stichius]], [[boeotians]] and [[bronze-armored]]). The Greek names him second and describes him first, and the translation turns the names around so that “the one … the other” point to the right men (see the translation's note on 15.329–331). He dies among the Achaeans whom the Trojans kill when Apollo has shaken the aegis (15.318–342; see [[aegis]] and [[androktasia]]). He is the second of the five Boeotian leaders of the Catalogue to fall, after Prothoenor, whom Polydamas killed in Book 14 (2.494–495, 14.449–452; see [[prothoenor]]); Clonius, the last named of the five, dies eleven verses later (15.340; see [[clonius]]).
