summary: The exclamation opens three speeches of dismay: Poseidon's at Zeus' order, “Ah, truly, brave as he is, he has spoken arrogantly” (15.185); Thoas' at Hector's return, “Ah, truly this is a great wonder that I see with my eyes” (15.286; the verse of 13.99); and Teucer's at his broken bowstring, “Ah, truly the plans of our battle are being utterly cut short” (15.467).
