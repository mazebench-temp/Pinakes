---
summary: Menestheus is named when Hector kills his “trusted companion” Stichius; the Athenian leader Iasus dies at Aeneas' hand in the same rout (15.329–337).
---

τὸν δὲ Μενεσθῆος μεγαθύμου πιστὸν ἑταῖρον, “the other a trusted companion of great-hearted Menestheus” (15.331): Stichius, whom Hector kills when Apollo has shaken the aegis (see [[stichius]], [[great-hearted]] and [[trusted-companion]]). In Book 13 Stichius carried the body of Amphimachus back with him and followed him at the head of the Athenians where Hector broke in (13.195–196, 13.689–691). In the same list Aeneas kills Iasus, “a leader of the Athenians”, whom the poem names nowhere else (15.337; see [[iasus]] and [[athenians]]). Menestheus himself does nothing in the book.
