---
summary: Asius, the narrator says, was not going to go back home “to windy Ilios,” in the verse of Hector's hope at nightfall in Book 8.
---

“The fool! He was not going to escape the evil fates and, glorying in his horses and chariot, from the ships go back home to windy Ilios” (12.113–115; [[asius]], [[nepios]], [[troy]]). The verse ἂψ ἀπονοστήσειν προτὶ Ἴλιον ἠνεμόεσσαν is the one in which Hector told the Trojans, as night fell in Book 8, that he had thought to destroy the ships and go back home before the dark (8.498–499). The homecoming that Hector put off is denied to Asius, whose death by Idomeneus' spear the next verses foretell (12.116–117; [[of-evil-name]], [[noble]]).
