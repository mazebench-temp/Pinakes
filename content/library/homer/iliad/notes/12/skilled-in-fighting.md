---
summary: The sons of Antenor, Archelochus and Acamas, are “well skilled in every kind of fighting” as they go with Aeneas in the fourth column.
---

“The fourth were led by the brave son of Anchises, Aeneas, and with him the two sons of Antenor, Archelochus and Acamas, well skilled in every kind of fighting” (12.98–100; [[archelochus]], [[acamas]], [[son-of-antenor]]). The verse is the one that named the same brothers in the Trojan Catalogue (2.823), and the dual formula keeps its habit of praising a pair of brothers. As in Book 5, the pair will be broken: Archelochus is killed by a spear thrown at Polydamas (14.459–464). See [[the-fight-at-the-wall]].
