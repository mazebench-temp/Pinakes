---
summary: Asius, “leader of men,” will not leave his horses and chariot at the ditch, and the narrator calls him a fool.
---

“But the son of Hyrtacus, Asius, leader of men, would not leave his horses there, and his charioteer and attendant, but drove close to the swift ships with them, the fool!” (12.110–113; [[asius]], [[hyrtacus]], [[nepios]]). The title is the one the Trojan Catalogue gave him (2.837), and the book has just repeated the Catalogue's verses on his great tawny horses from the river Selleis (12.96–97 = 2.838–839; [[tawny]]). The leader is the one man who does not obey Polydamas' counsel (12.108–109), and the narrator's νήπιος follows his title within three verses. See [[chariot]].
