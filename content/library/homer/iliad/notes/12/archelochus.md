---
summary: Archelochus, son of Antenor, goes against the wall with his brother Acamas in Aeneas' company, in the Catalogue's verse (12.100 = 2.823).
---

Ἀρχέλοχός τʼ Ἀκάμας τε μάχης εὖ εἰδότε πάσης, “Archelochus and Acamas, well skilled in every kind of fighting” (12.100): the dual praises both brothers, as in the Catalogue (2.823; see [[acamas]], [[aeneas]], [[antenor]] and [[skilled-in-fighting]]). It is his only appearance between the Catalogue and his death in Book 14, when the spear that Telamonian Ajax throws at Polydamas kills him instead, “for the gods had planned his death” (14.459–464; see [[polydamas]]).
