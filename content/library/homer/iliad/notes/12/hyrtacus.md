summary: Hyrtacus is named only in his son's patronymic, three times: in the Catalogue's verse “Asius, son of Hyrtacus” (12.96, 12.163), and once in the order of the Catalogue's verse before it (12.110).
# The father's own name, Ὑρτάκου (13.759, 13.771), will need adding when Book 13 is indexed.
---

Ἄσιος Ὑρτακίδης, “Asius, son of Hyrtacus”, opens the verse that brings Asius into the third company (12.96 = 2.838) and the verse in which he turns on Zeus (12.163; see [[asius]]). At 12.110 the patronymic comes first, ἀλλʼ οὐχ Ὑρτακίδης ἔθελʼ Ἄσιος ὄρχαμος ἀνδρῶν, “but the son of Hyrtacus, Asius, leader of men, would not”, in the order of the Catalogue's first verse on Asius (2.837; see [[leader-of-men]]). Book 13 names the father himself, twice, in “Asius, son of Hyrtacus”: Ἄσιον Ὑρτάκου υἱόν (13.759) and Ἄσιος Ὑρτάκου υἱός (13.771).
