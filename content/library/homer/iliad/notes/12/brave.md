---
summary: Aeneas, “the brave son of Anchises,” leads the fourth column, in the words that introduced him in the Trojan Catalogue.
---

“The fourth were led by the brave son of Anchises, Aeneas, and with him the two sons of Antenor, Archelochus and Acamas, well skilled in every kind of fighting” (12.98–100; [[aeneas]], [[anchises]], [[son-of-antenor]], [[skilled-in-fighting]]). The verses take up the Dardanian entry of the Trojan Catalogue, where the same formula introduced Aeneas and the same verse named the sons of Antenor (2.819–823). The formula ἐῢς πάϊς Ἀγχίσαο occurs three times in the Iliad, always of Aeneas, the third time when he obeys Hector's call in the fight over Patroclus (17.491). See [[the-fight-at-the-wall]].
