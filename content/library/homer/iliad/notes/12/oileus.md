“At once he addressed the son of Oileus with winged words” (12.365; see [[ajax-son-of-oileus]]). Until now the poem has named the lesser Ajax by his father in the phrase Ὀϊλῆος ταχὺς Αἴας, “swift Ajax, son of Oileus” (2.527). The patronymic Ὀϊλιάδης is new here, and in Homer it always names him (13.203, 13.712, 14.446, 16.330, 23.759); like every patronymic, it counts as a mention of the father too.
