---
summary: The two Ajaxes join the title to Zeus' name in the formula “the Olympian lord of the lightning.”
---

The two Ajaxes end their call along the towers with a hope: “press forward, and urge one another on, in the hope that Zeus may grant it, the Olympian lord of the lightning, that we thrust back the quarrel and drive the enemy to the city” (12.274–276; [[lord-of-the-lightning]], [[the-two-ajaxes]]). Ὀλύμπιος does not stand alone for the god here, as when “the Olympian” roused the Trojans' might again in Book 8 (8.335), but goes with his name in the line-end formula of Book 1 (1.580, 1.609). It is the only time the book calls Zeus Olympian, and the Achaeans who invoke him are the side from which he is turning the day (12.173–174, 12.437; [[plan-of-zeus]]). See [[zeus]].
