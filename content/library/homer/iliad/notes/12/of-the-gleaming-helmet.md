---
summary: “Looking at him darkly, Hector of the gleaming helmet said”: the verse that introduces Hector's rebuke of Polydamas over the omen.
---

Polydamas has read the eagle as a warning, and Hector answers: “Looking at him darkly, Hector of the gleaming helmet said: ‘Polydamas, what you say is no longer pleasing to me’” (12.230–231; [[looking-darkly]], [[polydamas]]). The pair of verses comes back almost unchanged when Polydamas advises a retreat into the city after Achilles has shouted from the ditch, and Hector rejects him again (18.284–285); the first verse also introduces his rebuke of Glaucus (17.169). The rebuke here ends with the one omen Hector will accept and a threat of death for any man who holds back (12.243–250; [[one-omen-is-best]]). See [[hector]] and [[helmet]].
