---
summary: Menoetius is named in Book 12 only in its first verse, in his son's title, “the valiant son of Menoetius” (12.1).
relations:
  - child: patroclus (12.1)
---

Μενοιτίου ἄλκιμος υἱός, “the valiant son of Menoetius”, is the title that Book 11 gave Patroclus three times (11.605, 11.814, 11.837), and Book 12 begins with it (12.1; see [[patroclus]] and [[son-of-menoetius]]). The book names the father and never the son.
