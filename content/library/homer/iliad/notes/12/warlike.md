---
summary: Sarpedon chooses Glaucus and “warlike Asteropaeus” as the best men after himself, at Asteropaeus' first appearance in the poem.
---

“And Sarpedon led the famous allies, and took Glaucus with him and warlike Asteropaeus; for they seemed to him to be the best by far of the rest, after himself” (12.101–104; [[asteropaeus]], [[sarpedon]], [[glaucus]], [[famous]]). Asteropaeus is named here for the first time, and the epithet goes with him: when he pities a fallen comrade in the fight over Patroclus he is again ἀρήϊος Ἀστεροπαῖος (17.352), before Achilles kills him at the river (21.139–204). See [[aristos]].
