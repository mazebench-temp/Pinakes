---
summary: The bees of Book 12 are in Asius' simile. The two Lapiths will not give way from the gate, like wasps or bees that have made their homes beside a rocky road and fight off the hunting men for the sake of their young (12.167–172).
grc: [μέλισσαι]
---

σφῆκες μέσον αἰόλοι ἠὲ μέλισσαι, “wasps with nimble waists, or bees” (12.167): the speaker offers both creatures and does not choose between them (see [[wasps]] and [[wasps-at-the-roadside]]). The bees of Book 2 poured out of a hollow rock in endless swarms, an image of number (2.87–93; see [[swarming-bees]]). These stay in their “hollow house” (κοῖλον δόμον, 12.169) and defend it, an image of a stand. The honey that names the bee (μέλι) is in the epithet of the Lycian kings' wine in the same book, μελιηδής, “honey-sweet” (12.320; see [[honey-sweet]]).
