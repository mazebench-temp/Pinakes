---
summary: As Teucer goes with Ajax to Menestheus' tower, “Pandion carried Teucer's curved bow.”
---

“And with him went Teucer, his brother by the same father, and with them Pandion carried Teucer's curved bow” (12.371–372; [[teucer]], [[pandion]]). Teucer has just come back to the fighting after Hector's stone broke his bowstring and numbed his hand in Book 8 (8.324–329), and another man carries his bow as they go along inside the wall. At the tower he uses it: he hits Glaucus in the bared arm and Sarpedon's shield strap (12.387–391, 12.400–402; [[skilled-with-the-bow]]). Pandion is named only here. See [[bow]].
