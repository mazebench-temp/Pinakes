“And with him the two sons of Antenor, / Archelochus and Acamas, well skilled in every kind of fighting” (12.99–100): the Catalogue's verses for the Dardanians (2.822–823; see [[aeneas]], [[archelochus]], [[acamas]] and [[son-of-antenor]]). Agenor leads the second company with Paris and Alcathous (12.93; see [[agenor]]). Of the fifteen leaders of the five companies, three are sons of Antenor and five are sons of Priam (see [[sons-of-priam]] and [[trojans]]). In Book 7 Antenor urged the Trojans to give Helen back, and Hector rejects Polydamas' counsel here in the four verses with which Paris answered him then (12.231–234 = 7.357–360; see [[polydamas]] and [[paris]]). Archelochus will die in Book 14, by the spear that Ajax throws at Polydamas (14.459–464).
