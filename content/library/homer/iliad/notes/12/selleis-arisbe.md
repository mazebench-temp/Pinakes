---
summary: The river Selleis is named again in the Catalogue's verse on Asius' horses, “great and tawny horses, from the river Selleis” (12.97 = 2.839).
---

ποταμοῦ ἄπο Σελλήεντος, “from the river Selleis” (12.97): the verse is repeated from the Catalogue, where Asius leads the men of Percote, Practium, Sestus, Abydus and Arisbe (2.835–839; see [[asius]] and [[arisbe]]). The Selleis of Ephyra in the Achaean catalogue is another river (2.659; see [[selleis-ephyra]]).
