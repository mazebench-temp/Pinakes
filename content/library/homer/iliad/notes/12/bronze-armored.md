---
summary: Thootes runs “along the wall of the bronze-armored Achaeans” and greets the Ajaxes as “leaders of the bronze-armored Argives,” in Agamemnon's words from the review.
---

“And he went running along the wall of the bronze-armored Achaeans, and came and stood beside the Ajaxes, and at once he said: ‘Ajaxes, leaders of the bronze-armored Argives’” (12.352–354; [[thootes]], [[the-two-ajaxes]]). The first verse recalls the day the wall was built, when the gods gazed at “the great work of the bronze-armored Achaeans” (7.444; [[the-achaean-wall]]). The second repeats the verse with which Agamemnon greeted the two Ajaxes when he reviewed the army in Book 4 (4.285); a herald now uses the king's form of address to bring them a captain's request. See [[achaeans]] and [[heralds]].
