---
summary: Aeneas leads the fourth company against the wall with the two sons of Antenor, in the verses of the Trojan Catalogue (12.98–100).
refs: [12.98]
---

τῶν δὲ τετάρτων ἦρχεν ἐῢς πάϊς Ἀγχίσαο / Αἰνείας, ἅμα τῷ γε δύω Ἀντήνορος υἷε, “The fourth were led by the brave son of Anchises, / Aeneas, and with him the two sons of Antenor” (12.98–99). The second half of each verse comes from the Catalogue, where Aeneas leads the Dardanians, and the next verse, on Archelochus and Acamas, is repeated whole (2.819, 2.822–823; see [[archelochus]], [[acamas]], [[dardanians]] and [[trojan-catalogue]]). The fourth company has the leaders of the Catalogue's Dardanian contingent, and “the brave son of Anchises” is the Catalogue's title for him (see [[brave]] and [[anchises]]).

Anchises' son-in-law Alcathous leads the second company (12.93; see [[alcathous]] and [[the-fight-at-the-wall]]). Aeneas is not named again in the book. In Book 13 Deiphobus finds him standing at the back of the throng, angry with Priam, and brings him into the fight over Alcathous' body (13.455–505; see [[deiphobus]]).
