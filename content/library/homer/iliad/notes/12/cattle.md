---
summary: The only live cattle of Book 12 are a herd in a half-verse, the cattle with curving horns against which Zeus rouses Sarpedon like a lion (12.293). Elsewhere in the book cattle are hides, the oxhide shields of both armies.
---

λέονθʼ ὣς βουσὶν ἕλιξιν, “like a lion against cattle with curving horns” (12.293; see [[lion-among-cattle]]). The rest of the book's cattle are leather. The narrator's first picture of the dead at Troy is of oxhide shields and helmets fallen in the dust by the Simoeis (βοάγρια, 12.22). The Trojans lock their shields together (βόεσσι, 12.105) and hold their dry oxhides high as they come at the wall (βόας αὔας, 12.137); the Danaans fence the battlements with oxhides (ῥινοῖσι βοῶν, 12.263); Sarpedon's bronze shield is lined inside with oxhides stitched close (βοείας, 12.296); and over the battlements the two sides hack at “the oxhide shields around one another's chests” (12.425; see [[shield]]).
