---
summary: The “two sons of Antenor,” Archelochus and Acamas, go with Aeneas in the fourth column.
grc: ["Ἀντήνορος υἷε"]
---

“The fourth were led by the brave son of Anchises, Aeneas, and with him the two sons of Antenor, Archelochus and Acamas, well skilled in every kind of fighting” (12.98–100; [[archelochus]], [[acamas]], [[aeneas]]). The dual Ἀντήνορος υἷε and the whole of the last verse repeat the Dardanian entry of the Trojan Catalogue (2.822–823; [[skilled-in-fighting]], [[brave]]). Acamas was among the three sons of Antenor at the head of the Trojans in Book 11 (11.59–60). The pair will be parted by the spear that Ajax throws at Polydamas, which kills Archelochus (14.459–464), and Acamas kills a Boeotian standing over his brother (14.476–477). See [[antenor]].
