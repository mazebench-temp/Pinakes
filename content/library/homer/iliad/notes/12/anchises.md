---
summary: Anchises is named in his son's title, “the brave son of Anchises” (12.98), and his son-in-law Alcathous leads the second company against the wall (12.93).
---

ἐῢς πάϊς Ἀγχίσαο, “the brave son of Anchises” (12.98), is the Catalogue's title for Aeneas, who leads the fourth company (2.819; see [[aeneas]]). Book 12 does not say that Alcathous, who leads the second company with Paris and Agenor (12.93), is Anchises' son-in-law; Book 13 says so as it tells his death (13.428–433; see [[alcathous]]). The house of Anchises has a leader in two of the five companies (see [[the-fight-at-the-wall]]).
