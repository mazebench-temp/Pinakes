# Εὐρύπυλον at Od. 11.520 is Eurypylus, son of Telephus, killed by Neoptolemus: except it when the
# Odyssey is indexed.
grc: [Εὐρύπυλον]
---

“So in the hut the valiant son of Menoetius / was tending wounded Eurypylus” (12.1–2; see [[patroclus]]). βεβλημένον, “wounded”, literally “hit”, is the participle that has marked him since Paris' arrow struck his thigh (11.592, 11.809, 11.822; see [[healing]]). He has no other part in the book. When the narrator comes back to the hut in Book 15, Patroclus leaves him to the care of an attendant and runs to Achilles (15.390–404).
