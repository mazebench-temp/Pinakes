---
summary: Asius' “great and tawny horses” from the river Selleis, the horses he will not leave at the ditch, are named in the verses of the Trojan Catalogue.
---

“And a third with them was Asius, the hero, Asius, son of Hyrtacus, whom his horses carried from Arisbe, great and tawny horses, from the river Selleis” (12.95–97; [[asius]], [[arisbe]], [[selleis-arisbe]]). The two verses repeat the Catalogue (2.838–839). Here they introduce the horses that will be his undoing: he alone refuses to leave them and his chariot at the ditch, and drives them at the gate (12.110–120; [[leader-of-men]], [[nepios]]).
