---
summary: From the towers the Achaeans throw stones, “defending themselves and the huts and the swift-sailing ships.”
---

While the Lapiths fight before the gate, “these, with stones, from the well-built towers kept shooting, defending themselves and the huts and the swift-sailing ships; and the stones fell to the ground like snowflakes” (12.154–156; [[well-built]], [[a-snow-of-stones]], [[stone-throwing]]). The ships that could carry the army home are what the wall now has to save, with the huts beside them ([[hut]]). See [[ships]].
