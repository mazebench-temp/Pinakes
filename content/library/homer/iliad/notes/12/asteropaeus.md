**At the wall.** He is first named among the leaders of the five columns that attack the Achaean wall on foot. Book 12 gives him nothing more to do (see [[sarpedon]], [[glaucus]] and [[trojan-allies]]). The Catalogue does not name him: there Pyraechmes leads the Paeonians, “with their curved bows” (2.848; see [[paeonians]] and [[pyraechmes]]).
