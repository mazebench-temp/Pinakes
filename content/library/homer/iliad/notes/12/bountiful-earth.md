---
summary: The snowflakes of the stone simile pour down “upon the bountiful earth,” and Leonteus brings his victims down to it “one after another,” in the verse of Teucer's killings in Book 8.
---

**The snow.** The stones from the towers fall like snowflakes “that a blustering wind, driving the shadowy clouds, pours down thick upon the bountiful earth” (12.157–158; [[a-snow-of-stones]], [[blustering]], [[shadowy-mountains]]).

**The fallen.** Leonteus kills Antiphates with his sword, “and then Menon and Iamenus and Orestes, all of them, one after another, he brought down to the bountiful earth” (12.193–194; [[leonteus]], [[menon]], [[iamenus]], [[orestes-the-trojan]]). The second verse is the one that ended Teucer's run of eight killings in Book 8 (8.277), and it will end one of Patroclus' (16.418). See [[earth]].
