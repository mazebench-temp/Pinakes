The third company is led by two sons of Priam and by Asius, from the towns on the Hellespont (see [[deiphobus]], [[asius]] and [[sons-of-priam]]). Helenus, “by far the best of the bird-seers” among the Trojans (6.76; see [[best-of-bird-seers]]), goes against the wall as a fighter. When the eagle drops the serpent among Hector's men, he is in another company, and it is Polydamas who reads the sign, saying how a prophet would read it (12.210–229; see [[polydamas]] and [[divination]]). He is not named again in the book. In Book 13 Menelaus drives a spear through the hand that holds his bow (13.581–600), and Hector, looking for him and the other leaders of the third company, learns that he and Deiphobus have gone back wounded (13.758–783).
