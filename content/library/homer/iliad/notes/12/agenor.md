summary: Agenor, son of Antenor, leads the second company against the wall with Paris and Alcathous (12.93).
---

{{quote:26e930a9-ed3d-5e97-b70d-204d18da2cd3}}

In Book 11 Agenor stood with his brothers Polybus and Acamas among the Trojan leaders on the rise of the plain (11.59–60). At the wall three sons of Antenor lead: Agenor in the second company, and Archelochus and Acamas with Aeneas in the fourth (12.93, 12.99–100; see [[antenor]], [[archelochus]], [[acamas]] and [[the-fight-at-the-wall]]). He is not named again in the book. In Book 13 he is with Paris again when Aeneas calls on them over the body of Alcathous, who led the second company with them (13.489–490; see [[alcathous]] and [[paris]]).
