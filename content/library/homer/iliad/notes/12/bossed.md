---
summary: In the hail of stones from both sides, “the helmets rang dry all around, and the bossed shields.”
---

“So the missiles poured from their hands, from the Achaeans and from the Trojans too; and the helmets rang dry all around, and the bossed shields, as men were struck with stones like millstones” (12.159–161; [[a-snow-of-stones]], [[stone-throwing]], [[helmet]]). The phrase ἀσπίδες ὀμφαλόεσσαι ends the verse, as in the clash of the armies in Books 4 and 8 (4.448, 8.62). There the shields pressed against each other; here they are struck by stones like millstones (μυλάκεσσι, only here). See [[shield]].
