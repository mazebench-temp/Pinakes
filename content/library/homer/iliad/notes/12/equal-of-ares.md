---
summary: Leonteus is “the equal of Ares, ruin of mortals,” in the ἶσος form of the comparison.
grc: ["ἶσον Ἄρηϊ"]
---

“The one the son of Peirithous, mighty Polypoetes, the other Leonteus, the equal of Ares, ruin of mortals” (12.129–130; [[leonteus]], [[ruin-of-mortals]]). The comparison here uses ἶσος (“equal”), as in Hector's verse in Book 11 (βροτολοιγῷ ἶσος Ἄρηϊ, 11.295), not ἀτάλαντος (“of equal weight”), and the accusative ἶσον is added to the entry's forms. Leonteus is measured by the war god, and in the next verses both Lapiths by the oaks that stand against wind and rain (12.131–136; [[oaks-on-the-mountains]]). See [[ares]].
