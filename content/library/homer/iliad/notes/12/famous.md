---
summary: Three words of the family: the “glorious hecatombs” the Achaeans did not give for the wall, the “famous allies” Sarpedon leads, and the “far-famed allies” who obey Polydamas.
grc: [ἀγακλειτῶν]
---

**The hecatombs.** “And they did not give glorious hecatombs to the gods” (12.6; [[hecatomb]]): the narrator repeats a verse of Poseidon's complaint in Book 7 (7.450) to give the reason the wall will not last ([[the-achaean-wall]], [[immortals]]).

**The allies.** In the order of the five columns, “Sarpedon led the famous allies” (12.101; [[sarpedon]], [[trojan-allies]]), with ἀγακλειτός, “very famous,” the intensive form with ἀγα-; it is added to the entry's forms. When the columns move, “the other Trojans and their far-famed allies obeyed the counsel of blameless Polydamas” (12.108–109; [[blameless]]), with τηλεκλειτοί, the word of the formula Τρῶες ὑπέρθυμοι τηλεκλειτοί τʼ ἐπίκουροι (6.111, 9.233). See [[kleos]].
