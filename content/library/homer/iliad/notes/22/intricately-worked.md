καλὸς δαιδάλεος is the pair used of Achilles' new shield as he arms (19.380) and of the shield and helmet as Hephaestus makes them (18.390, 18.612; see the translation's table of formulas). See [[the-shield-of-achilles]] and [[the-making-of-the-arms]].
