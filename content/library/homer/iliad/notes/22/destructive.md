summary: Hecuba begs Hector to “ward off the enemy / from inside the wall” (22.84–85).
grc: ["δήϊον ἄνδρα"]
---

δήϊον ἄνδρα, “the enemy,” is new to the entry, and it also takes in Hector's prayer for his son in Book 6, that he may come home “when he has killed his enemy” (6.481; see the translation's table of key words, under δήϊος). See [[hecuba]] and [[hector]].
