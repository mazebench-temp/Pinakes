---
summary: Hector imagines laying down his arms before Achilles: “Or what if I set down my bossed shield” (22.111).
grc: [ὀμφαλόεσσαν]
---

The accusative ὀμφαλόεσσαν is new to the entry (see the translation's table of formulas, under ἀσπὶς ὀμφαλόεσσα). The shield that Hector thinks of setting down is the one he leaned against a jutting tower as he waited (22.97; [[phaeinos]], [[heavy-helmet]]). See [[hector]] and [[shield]].
