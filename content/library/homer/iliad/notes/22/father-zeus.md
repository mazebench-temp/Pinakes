---
summary: Athena tells Achilles that Hector cannot escape now, “not even if far-working Apollo should go to great lengths, / groveling before aegis-bearing father Zeus” (22.220–221).
---

πατρὸς Διὸς αἰγιόχοιο, “of aegis-bearing father Zeus,” fills the end of the verse as at 7.60 and 11.66. Before it stands προπροκυλινδόμενος, “groveling,” a word Homer uses once more, in Eumaeus' account of the stranger's journey from Crete (*Odyssey* 17.522–525).
