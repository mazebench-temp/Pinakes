---
summary: The pursuit is compared to a chariot race: “And as when prize-winning single-hoofed horses round the turning posts / run very swiftly” (22.162–163).
---

The prize is “a tripod or a woman, in honor of a man who has died” (22.164): the race of the simile is run at funeral games, like the chariot race for Patroclus in the next book (see [[prize-winning]], [[horses]] and [[contests]]).
