# περνάς "selling" (22.45), a participle of πέρνημι; περάαν alone is "cross" (2.613, 12.63).
grc: [περνὰς]
en: [selling them off]
---

On the wall Priam begs Hector not to face the man “who has made me bereft of many brave sons, / killing them, and selling them off to islands far away” (κτείνων καὶ περνὰς νήσων ἔπι τηλεδαπάων, 22.44–45; see [[sons-of-priam]] and [[priam]]). The half-line is Poseidon's from the book before, where Laomedon threatened to sell the two gods “off to islands far away” (21.454), and Priam names at once the two sons he cannot see among the Trojans, Lycaon, whom Achilles had sold into Lemnos, and Polydorus (22.46–48; see [[lycaon-son-of-priam]] and [[polydorus-son-of-priam]]). See [[achilles]].
