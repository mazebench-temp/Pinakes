---
summary: Achilles answers Hector's offer of an agreement with a challenge: “Remember all your prowess; now you must indeed / be a spearman and a bold warrior; / there is no escape for you any longer” (22.268–270).
---

The order is the leaders' call, “remember your furious courage” (μνήσασθε δὲ θούριδος ἀλκῆς, 6.112 and often), spoken by an enemy, with ἀρετή in the place of ἀλκή (see [[alke]]). Its second half is the charge of the dying Sarpedon to Glaucus (22.268–269; 16.492–493), and Diomedes once said that the Achaeans used to marvel at Hector “for being a spearman and a bold warrior” (5.601–602; see [[tharsos]] and [[no-oaths-between-lions-and-men]]). Achilles adds at once that the prowess will not help: Athena will bring Hector down by his spear (22.270–271).
