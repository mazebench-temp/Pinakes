---
summary: Priam foresees his own end at the hands of Zeus: “whom the father, the son of Cronus, on the threshold of old age / will destroy in a grievous fate” (22.60–61).
---

The patronymic opens Priam's picture of the sack of Troy, the sons killed, the daughters dragged away, the children dashed to the ground, and the old king himself torn by his own dogs at his door (22.59–76; [[old-age-and-memory]]). See [[priam]] and [[zeus]].
