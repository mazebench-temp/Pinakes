---
summary: Hector wears “the fine armor he had stripped from mighty Patroclus when he killed him” (22.323), and Achilles looks for the place where the bronze leaves his flesh bare.
---

The verse is Hector's own boast of Book 17 told in the third person (22.323, as 17.187). The armor of Achilles covers all of Hector but the throat, where Achilles' spear goes in (22.322–327). See [[patroclus]], [[hector]] and [[the-making-of-the-arms]].
