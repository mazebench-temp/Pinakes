---
summary: The Trojan women washed their clothes at the springs “before the sons of the Achaeans came” (22.156); “the other sons of the Achaeans ran up around” the body of Hector (22.369); and Achilles leads the “young men of the Achaeans, singing the paean” back to the ships (22.391).
---

The first verse-end is the one of Achilles' words about the wealth of Troy “in former days, in time of peace” (22.156 = 9.403; [[the-springs-of-the-scamander]]). The sons of the Achaeans who run up gaze at Hector's stature and looks and stab him (22.369–375; [[tis-speech]]), and the young men who sing the paean are the army going home with the body (22.391–394; [[paean]]).
