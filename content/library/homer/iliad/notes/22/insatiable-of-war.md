---
summary: Athena tells Achilles that the two of them will cut down Hector, “insatiable of battle though he is” (22.218).
grc: ["μάχης ἄατόν"]
en: [insatiable of battle]
---

μάχης ἄατόν περ ἐόντα has the uncontracted form of the adjective and the genitive μάχης, “of battle,” in place of πολέμοιο. Polydamas had said it of Achilles in the contracted form (13.746); here the goddess says it of the man she is about to deceive (see the translation's table of formulas, under πολέμου ἀκόρητος). See [[hector]] and [[athena]].
