---
summary: Hector imagines giving Helen and the treasures “to the sons of Atreus to take away” (22.117).
---

The words are Antenor's proposal in the Trojan assembly of Book 7, “let us give Argive Helen, and the possessions with her, / to the sons of Atreus to take away” (7.350–351); Paris would give back the possessions but not the woman (7.357–364). Hector thinks of making the offer himself, and dismisses it (22.111–122; [[pondering-two-ways]], [[helen]]). See [[agamemnon]] and [[menelaus]].
