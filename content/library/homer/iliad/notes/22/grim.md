summary: Athena, as Deiphobus, tells Hector that she came out against the will of their parents, for “my heart within me was worn down by grim grief” (22.242).

The grief is a goddess's invention, spoken to win Hector's trust (22.239–247; [[truth-and-deception]], [[dear-brother]]). See [[athena]] and [[deiphobus]].
