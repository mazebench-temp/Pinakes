---
summary: When Hector knows that the gods have called him to his death, he draws “the sharp sword / that hung beneath his flank, great and heavy”, and swoops on Achilles “brandishing his sharp sword” (22.306–311).
---

The sword is his last weapon. His spear has glanced from Achilles' shield, and Deiphobus, whom he calls on for another, is not there (22.289–299; see [[deiphobus]]). He never strikes with it: Achilles comes on behind his shield and drives his spear through the one gap the armor leaves, at the throat (22.312–329; see [[the-death-of-hector]] and [[the-eagle-flying-high]]).
