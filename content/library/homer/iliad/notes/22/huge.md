---
summary: Hector stands at the gates and waits “for huge Achilles as he came nearer” (22.92).
---

The epithet follows Book 21, where Priam on the tower “caught sight of huge Achilles” (21.527). Here it closes the scene of the parents' pleas: “but they did not persuade Hector's heart, / but he waited for huge Achilles as he came nearer” (22.91–92), and the simile of the serpent at its hole follows (22.93–97; [[the-serpent-at-its-hole]]).
