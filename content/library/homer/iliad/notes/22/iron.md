---
summary: Dying, Hector gives up hope of persuading Achilles: “for truly the heart in your breast is iron” (22.357).
grc: [σιδήρεος]
---

σιδήρεος, “of iron”, is here the image of a heart that will not yield to prayers (see [[not-stone-or-iron]]). The same image comes twice in the last book, when Hecuba and then Achilles say to Priam σιδήρειόν νύ τοι ἦτορ, of the heart that lets him go among the men who killed his sons (24.205, 24.521).
