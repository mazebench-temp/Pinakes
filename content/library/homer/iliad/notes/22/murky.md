summary: The eagle of Hector's last charge goes down to the plain “through the murky clouds” (22.309); and when Andromache sees Hector dragged behind the chariot, “murky night covered her eyes” (22.466).
grc: [ἐρεβεννῶν]
---

The genitive plural ἐρεβεννῶν is new to the entry. The night that covers Andromache's eyes is the night of the dying in battle (22.466, as 5.659, 13.580; [[darkness-covered-his-eyes]]). See [[andromache]] and [[high-flying]].
