---
summary: The formula πότνια μήτηρ comes three times: the false Deiphobus says that “our father and our lady mother” begged him to stay inside (22.239), Hector offers Achilles the ransom that “my father and my lady mother will give you” (22.341), and Achilles answers that “not even so shall your lady mother” lay Hector on a bier (22.352–353).
---

Each time the lady mother is Hecuba, named by her son, by the goddess in her son's likeness, and by his killer. The last use answers Hecuba's own fear at the beginning of the book, that she would never “weep for you on a bier, dear scion, whom I bore myself” (22.86–87, 22.352–353; [[honoring-the-dead]]).
