---

ἶσος Ἐνυαλίῳ, found only here, is new to the entry: the form with ἶσος of ἀτάλαντος Ἐνυαλίῳ, as ἶσος Ἄρηϊ is of ἀτάλαντος Ἄρηϊ (see the translation's table of formulas, under ἶσος Ἄρηϊ). The war god is named with an epithet found nowhere else in Homer ([[of-the-shaking-helmet]], [[enyalius]]), and the sight of Achilles brandishing the Pelian ash sets Hector to flight (22.131–137; [[the-pelian-ash]]). See [[achilles]].
