---
summary: Hecuba ends her plea with the fate of an unburied man: “beside the ships of the Argives, the swift dogs will devour you” (22.89).
grc: ["κύνες ταχέες"]
---

The order κύνες ταχέες is new to the entry (ταχέες κύνες, 17.558). The dogs return through the book in Achilles' threats and in Andromache's lament (22.335, 22.348–354, 22.509; [[dogs]]). See [[hecuba]] and [[honoring-the-dead]].
