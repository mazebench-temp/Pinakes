---
summary: The washing troughs by the springs are where the Trojan women washed “the glossy clothes” in time of peace (22.154); and Andromache flings away “the glossy bindings” of her hair (22.468).
---

The glossy reins of the earlier books give way to the things of women: the clothes washed before the Achaeans came (22.153–156; [[the-springs-of-the-scamander]]), and the headdress Andromache wore since her wedding day (22.468–472; [[veil]]). See [[trojan-women]] and [[andromache]].
