---
summary: Hecuba warns Hector that if Achilles kills him she will never weep for him on a bier, “nor will your richly dowered wife” (22.86–88).
---

This is the second of the epithet's two uses for Andromache, in a verse of mourning denied (see the entry). The wife whom Hecuba names is weaving at home, unaware, when the book turns to her (22.437–446; [[andromache]], [[lament]]). See [[hecuba]].
