summary: Priam begs the Trojans to let him go to the ships: “Let me beg this reckless man, this doer of violent deeds” (22.418).
grc: [ὀβριμοεργόν]
---

The accusative is new to the entry. Priam names Achilles with the word Dione used of Heracles (5.403), and in the same breath hopes that “he may respect my years and pity / my old age” (22.418–420; [[supplication]], [[old-age-and-memory]]). See [[priam]] and [[achilles]].
