summary: Hecuba begs Hector not to “stand forth as champion against this man” (22.85), and he stands. When the two meet, Hector proposes the terms of his duel in Book 7, the gods as witnesses and the body given back (22.254–259), and Achilles refuses them (22.261–272); Athena promises Achilles that she will “persuade him to fight you face to face” (22.223).
refs: [22.85, 22.223, 22.248-272]
---

πρόμος, “champion”, is the word of Hector's challenge in Book 7, where he called on the Achaeans to send out their best man as champion (7.75; see [[the-duel-of-hector-and-ajax]]). The meeting of Book 22 is not an agreed duel: no armies sit down to watch, no ground is measured, and the terms are refused (see [[no-oaths-between-lions-and-men]] and [[the-death-of-hector]]).
