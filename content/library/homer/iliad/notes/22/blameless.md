---
summary: Hector thinks of going unarmed “to meet noble Achilles” (22.113), and after Achilles' spear has missed him, “Hector spoke to the noble son of Peleus” (22.278).
---

Both are Hector's moments of hope. In the first he imagines setting down his shield and helmet, leaning his spear against the wall, and offering Achilles Helen and the treasures (22.111–121); in the second he has just avoided the spear and taunts Achilles, “You missed!” (22.279).
