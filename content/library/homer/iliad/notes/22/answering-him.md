---
summary: Athena answers Zeus' question “in turn” (22.177), and Zeus answers her (22.182); Hector answers Athena in Deiphobus' likeness, and she answers him again (22.232, 22.238).
---

The two exchanges are a god's concession and a god's deceit. Zeus gives way to Athena's protest, “Answering her, Zeus the cloud-gatherer said” (22.182; [[cloud-gatherer]]). Hector's reply to the false Deiphobus is introduced by the verse of his reply to Andromache in Book 6 (22.232 = 6.440; [[of-the-gleaming-helmet]]), and the goddess's second speech, introduced like her answer to Zeus, persuades him to stand (22.238–247; [[gray-eyed]], [[truth-and-deception]]).
