summary: Andromache fears for Astyanax, “For even if he escapes the tearful war of the Achaeans” (22.487), and pictures the orphan going “in tears” to his widowed mother (22.499).
grc: [δακρυόεις]
---

The nominative δακρυόεις, of the child, is new to the entry. In Book 6 Andromache took the child from Hector “laughing through her tears” (δακρυόεν γελάσασα, 6.484); here the tears are the child's, in her picture of his life without a father (22.490–499; [[astyanax]]). See [[andromache]] and [[polemos]].
