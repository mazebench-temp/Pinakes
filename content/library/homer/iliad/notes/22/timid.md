---
summary: Achilles pursues Hector as a falcon “swoops easily after a timid dove” (22.139–140).
grc: [τρήρωνα]
---

The accusative τρήρωνα is new to the entry: τρήρωνα πέλειαν, “a timid dove,” the line-end of the archery contest of the next book (23.853, 23.855, 23.874) and of an omen in the Odyssey (*Odyssey* 20.243). See [[the-falcon-and-the-dove]], [[doves]] and [[achilles]].
