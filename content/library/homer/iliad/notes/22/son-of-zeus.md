---
summary: Hector names Apollo “the far-shooting son of Zeus” in his recognition that the gods have called him to his death (22.302).
grc: ["Διὸς υἷι"]
---

The dative Διὸς υἷι is new to the entry. Hector joins the father and the son, Ζηνί τε καὶ Διὸς υἷι ἑκηβόλῳ, the two gods who once protected him (22.301–303; [[far-shooter]]). See [[apollo]] and [[zeus]].
