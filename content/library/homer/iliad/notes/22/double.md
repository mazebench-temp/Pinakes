δίπλακα πορφυρέην is the half-verse of Helen's web in Book 3 (3.126). Helen wove into hers the struggles of the Trojans and the Achaeans (3.126–128); Andromache, who does not yet know of Hector's death, works flowers into hers (22.441; [[inlaid]], [[weaving]]). See [[andromache]] and [[helen]].
