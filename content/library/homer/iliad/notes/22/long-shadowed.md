---
summary: The verse of the duel's spear casts is spoken twice, of Achilles and then of Hector: “He spoke, and poising his long-shadowed spear, he let it fly” (22.273, 22.289).
---

The whole verse is the one of Menelaus' cast at Paris, of Hector's at Ajax, and of others (3.355, 7.244). Achilles' cast misses, for Hector sinks down and the spear flies over him; Hector's strikes the middle of Achilles' shield and glances off (22.273–276, 22.289–291).
