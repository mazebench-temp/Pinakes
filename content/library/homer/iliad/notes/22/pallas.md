---
summary: Achilles tells Hector that “at once Pallas Athena / will bring you down by my spear” (22.270–271); and when his first cast misses, “Pallas Athena snatched it up” and gave it back to him (22.276).
---

Both uses fall in the duel, and both make the goddess the agent: Achilles names her as the power that will kill Hector, and the narrator shows her restoring the spear, unseen by Hector (22.276–277; [[shepherd-of-the-people]], [[truth-and-deception]]). See [[athena]].
