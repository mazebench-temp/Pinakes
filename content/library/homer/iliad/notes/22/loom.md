---
summary: Andromache is weaving “a web in the inner part of the high house” when Hector dies (22.440); at the wailing from the tower “the shuttle fell from her to the ground” (22.448).
en: [web]
---

Here ἱστός is the web on the loom, with the cloth itself in apposition in the next verse, as at Helen's web in Book 3 (3.125–126; see the translation's key-word row ἱστός and [[purple]]). The κερκίς, “the shuttle”, found only here in the Iliad, falls from her hand when she hears Hecuba's wail (22.447–448; see [[weaving]] and [[andromache]]).
