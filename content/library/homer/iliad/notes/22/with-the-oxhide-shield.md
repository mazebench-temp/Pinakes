---
summary: Achilles refuses Hector's compact: there will be no oaths between them “before one or the other has fallen / and glutted Ares with blood, the warrior with the oxhide shield” (22.266–267).
---

The verse is Diomedes' to Pandarus and Aeneas (5.289), and in Book 20 it told what Achilles' heart bade him do to Hector (20.77–78). Here he says it to Hector himself (22.261–267; [[oath-formulas]]). See [[ares]] and [[achilles]].
