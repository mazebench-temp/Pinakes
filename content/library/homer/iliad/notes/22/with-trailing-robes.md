---
summary: Hector cannot go back into the city: “I would feel shame before the Trojans and the Trojan women with trailing robes” (22.105).
---

The verse is the one with which he told Andromache in Book 6 why he could not hold back from the fighting (22.105 = 6.442; see the translation's table of formulas, under Τρῶας καὶ Τρῳάδας). Then it was shame at being called a coward; here it is shame at having destroyed the army by his own recklessness (22.104–107; [[aidos]], [[polydamas]]). See [[trojan-women]] and [[hector]].
