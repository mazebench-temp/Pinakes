# θεὸν ἄμβροτον (22.9) and θεὸς ἄμβροτος (20.358; again 24.460): ἄμβροτος, "immortal", beside θεός.
grc: ["θεὸν ἄμβροτον", "θεὸς ἄμβροτος"]
---

**Mortal and immortal.** αὐτὸς θνητὸς ἐὼν θεὸν ἄμβροτον sets the two words side by side, the man pursuing the god (22.8–9; [[mortal-men]]). ἄμβροτος, “immortal” (ἀ- and βροτός, “mortal”), is the synonym of ἀθάνατος; with θεός it is new to the entry, and it also takes in Achilles' words about Ares, “though he is an immortal god” (20.358).

**Achilles' answer.** “Lie dead; and I will accept my doom then, whenever / Zeus is willing to bring it to pass, and the other immortal gods” (22.365–366) repeats the words with which he accepted his death in Book 18, when he resolved to kill Hector (18.115–116; [[the-short-life-of-achilles]]).
