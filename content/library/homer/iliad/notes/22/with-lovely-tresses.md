---
summary: Andromache calls “to her handmaids with lovely tresses” to heat the bath for Hector (22.442), and then speaks “among her slave women with lovely tresses” when she hears the wailing (22.449).
grc: [ἐϋπλοκάμοις, ἐϋπλοκάμοισι]
---

The datives ἐϋπλοκάμοις and ἐϋπλοκάμοισι are new to the entry: the epithet of the Trojan women is given to the serving women of Hector's house, first ἀμφίπολοι and then δμῳαί (see the translation's table of key words, under ἀμφίπολος). See [[handmaids]], [[bathing]] and [[andromache]].
