# μηρίʼ ἔκηε (24.34) will need adding with Book 24.
grc: ["μηρίʼ ἔκηεν"]
---

The sacrifices are Hector's claim on the god (see [[sacrifice]], [[reciprocity-with-the-gods]] and [[zeus]]). Apollo makes the same claim for him in the last book, when he reproaches the gods with the thighs that Hector burned for them (μηρίʼ ἔκηε, 24.33–34).
