summary: Achilles goes against Hector and “nodded with his shining helmet / with its four ridges” (22.314–315).
grc: [τετραφάλῳ]
en: [four ridges]
---

The dative τετραφάλῳ is new to the entry. The plumes that wave around the helmet are told in the words of Achilles' arming, “the fair plumes / of gold, which Hephaestus had set thick about the crest” (22.315–316, as 19.382–383; see the translation's note on 22.315–316).
