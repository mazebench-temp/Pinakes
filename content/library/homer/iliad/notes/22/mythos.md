---
summary: Hector, missed by Achilles' spear, calls him “a glib talker and a cheat with words” (22.281). Zeus “began to speak” among the watching gods (22.167), and tells Athena that he does not speak “in full earnest” (22.183–184).
---

ἀρτιεπής, “glib talker”, and ἐπίκλοπος, “cheat”, are said of μῦθοι: Hector takes Achilles' claim that Athena will bring him down as a lie meant to frighten him (22.270–282; see [[words-and-violence]] and [[truth-and-deception]]). The lie in the book is Athena's, told in Deiphobus' voice (22.229–246).
