---
summary: Zeus tells his daughter, “Take heart, Tritogeneia, dear child” (22.183), and lets her go down to end Hector's life; Achilles tells Hector that now he must be “a spearman and a bold warrior” (22.269).
---

Zeus' words are those of Book 8, where he said them smiling to Athena (22.183–184 = 8.39–40; see [[tritogeneia]] and [[the-gods-mirror-the-mortals]]). The verse of the bold warrior is Sarpedon's to Glaucus and Diomedes' of Hector himself (16.493, 5.602; see [[arete]]); Achilles says it to a man who has just fled from him three times around the city (22.250–251; see [[retreat]]).
