---
summary: Achilles to Hector: “now you must indeed / be a spearman and a bold warrior” (22.268–269).
---

The verse αἰχμητήν τʼ ἔμεναι καὶ θαρσαλέον πολεμιστήν is used three times: of Hector, in Diomedes' words when he sees Ares at his side (5.601–604), in the dying Sarpedon's charge to Glaucus, with the same νῦν σε μάλα χρή (16.492–493), and here as Achilles' challenge to the man he is about to kill. See [[achilles]], [[hector]] and [[tharsos]].
