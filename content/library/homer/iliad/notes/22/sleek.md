λιπαρὴν καλύπτρην is new to the entry. The translation keeps Hecuba's καλύπτρη, “head covering,” apart from Andromache's κρήδεμνον, “veil,” which she flings away in the same gesture later in the book (22.468–470; see the translation's note on 22.468–470 and [[veil]]).
