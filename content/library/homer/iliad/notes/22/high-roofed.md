---
summary: Andromache “was weaving a web in the inner part of the high house” when the wailing reached her (22.440).
grc: ["δόμου ὑψηλοῖο"]
en: [high house]
---

μυχῷ δόμου ὑψηλοῖο is new to the entry, and found only here in the Iliad. The phrase places her in the innermost room of Hector's house, where she knows nothing of what has happened (22.437–446; [[weaving]]). See [[andromache]] and [[house]].
