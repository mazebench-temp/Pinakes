---
summary: Hector's spear has glanced off Achilles' shield, “and he stood downcast, for he had no other ash spear” (22.293).
---

He calls on Deiphobus for a long spear, and Deiphobus is not there (22.294–295; [[long-spears]], [[deiphobus]]). Achilles' own ash spear, the Pelian ash that Athena gave back to him, kills Hector (22.276–277, 22.326–328; [[the-pelian-ash]], [[heavy-with-bronze]]). See [[hector]] and [[spear]].
