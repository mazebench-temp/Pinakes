summary: The Achaeans run up and gaze at “the stature and admirable looks / of Hector” before they stab him (22.370–371).

en: [admirable looks]
---

εἶδος ἀγητόν is new to the entry. The phrase of praise was Hera's reproach of the Achaeans, “admirable in looks” (5.787); here it is the enemy's admiration for the body they are about to wound (22.369–375; [[tis-speech]]). See [[hector]].
