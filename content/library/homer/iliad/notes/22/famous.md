---
summary: Hector promises that if he wins he will strip Achilles “of your glorious armor” and give back the body (22.258–259); and Achilles, having killed him, lifts “the glorious armor” into his chariot (22.399).
---

The glorious armor that Achilles lifts into the chariot is his own former armor, which Hector stripped from Patroclus and was wearing (22.322–323, 22.368; [[might-of-patroclus]], [[stripping-the-dead]]). Zeus foretold that Hector would never come home for Andromache to receive “the glorious armor of the son of Peleus” from him (17.207–208). See [[achilles]] and [[hector]].
