---
summary: The whole-line formula brings Hector and Achilles face to face: “and when they had come close, advancing against each other, / great Hector of the gleaming helmet spoke to him first” (22.248–249).
---

The couplet is the one of Diomedes' meeting with Glaucus (6.121–122) and of Achilles' meetings with Aeneas and Asteropaeus, where Achilles spoke first (20.176–177, 21.148–149). Here Hector speaks first, and he asks not who the other is, as Diomedes and Achilles asked (6.123, 21.150), but for terms for the body of the loser (22.250–259; [[of-the-gleaming-helmet]], [[genealogy-in-battle]]).
