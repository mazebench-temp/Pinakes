---
summary: Athena addresses Zeus as “Father, lord of the gleaming thunderbolt and of the dark clouds” before she rebukes him (22.178).
en: [of the dark clouds]
---

The vocative κελαινεφές follows ἀργικέραυνε, and the two epithets of the storm god make one address (see the translation's table of formulas, under Ζεῦ πάτερ ἀργικέραυνε; [[of-the-gleaming-thunderbolt]], [[what-a-word]]). See [[zeus]] and [[athena]].
