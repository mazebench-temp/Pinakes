summary: Hector, knowing he is deceived, says that his death was long ago dearer “to Zeus and to the far-shooting son of Zeus, who before now / protected me with ready hearts” (22.302–303).
---

The god who left Hector when the scales sank (22.213) is named in Hector's recognition by his archer's epithet and his parentage (22.297–303; [[son-of-zeus]], [[truth-and-deception]]). See [[apollo]] and [[hector]].
