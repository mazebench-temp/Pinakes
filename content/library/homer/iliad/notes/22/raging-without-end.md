---
summary: Hector stands before the gates, “raging without end to fight with Achilles” (22.36).
---

ἄμοτον μεμαώς is the phrase of the Trojans who followed Hector against the ships, “raging without end” (13.40, 13.80); here Hector's fury holds him outside while his father begs him to come in (22.33–37; [[priam]]). See [[hector]].
