---
summary: Achilles' spear goes through Hector's neck, “but the ash spear heavy with bronze did not cut the windpipe, / so that he might still say something to him in answer” (22.328–329).
---

χαλκοβάρεια is the epithet of a helmet in Book 11, “the helmet, heavy with bronze,” that did not hold Agamemnon's spear (11.96); here it is the epithet of the Pelian ash (see the translation's table of key words). The wound leaves Hector his voice for the plea and the prophecy that follow (22.338–360; [[the-pelian-ash]], [[death-of-achilles-foretold]]). See [[achilles]] and [[hector]].
