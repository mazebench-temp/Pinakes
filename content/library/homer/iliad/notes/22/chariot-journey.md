---
summary: The verse of the chariot's flight is used for the dragging of Hector's body: “he whipped them to a run, and the pair flew on not unwilling” (22.400).
---

τὼ δʼ οὐκ ἀέκοντε πετέσθην is the half-verse of the gods' journeys (5.366, 5.768, 8.45) and of men's drives to the ships (10.530, 11.519). Here the horses fly toward the ships with Hector's head trailing in the dust (22.398–405; [[the-dragging-of-hector]]). See [[horses]] and [[chariot]].
