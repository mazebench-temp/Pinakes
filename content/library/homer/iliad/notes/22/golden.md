---
summary: Andromache flings from her head “the veil that golden Aphrodite had given her / on the day when Hector of the gleaming helmet took her” (22.470–471).
grc: ["χρυσῆ Ἀφροδίτη"]
---

The contracted nominative χρυσῆ Ἀφροδίτη is new to the entry. The goddess appears here only as the giver of the bride's veil, the gift of the wedding day now thrown away (22.468–472; [[veil]], [[aphrodite]]). See [[andromache]].
