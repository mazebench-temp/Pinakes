---
summary: Athena, in the likeness of Deiphobus, urges Hector on “so that we may see whether Achilles / will kill us both and carry off our bloody spoils / to the hollow ships” (22.244–246).
---

The goddess speaks as Hector's brother and leads him to his death “by craft” (22.247; [[truth-and-deception]], [[deiphobus]]). Before the book ends Achilles strips the bloody armor from Hector's own shoulders (22.368–369; [[bloody]]).
