---
summary: Priam tells Hector that to the rest of the people “the sorrow will be shorter-lived” for his lost sons, unless Hector too dies (22.54–55).
grc: [μινυνθαδιώτερον]
en: [shorter-lived]
---

The comparative is found only here, of grief, not of a life (see the translation's table of key words, under μινυνθάδιος). The grief of the people for Lycaon and Polydorus will pass, but Hector's death would be the city's (22.52–58; [[lycaon-son-of-priam]], [[polydorus-son-of-priam]]). See [[priam]].
