---
summary: Priam's picture of the sack of Troy includes “little children / dashed to the ground in grim combat” (22.63–64).
---

The formula of challenges to single combat, “to fight him face to face in grim combat” (3.20, 7.40), names here the slaughter of the children when the city falls (22.62–65; see the translation's table of formulas). See [[priam]] and [[old-age-and-memory]].
