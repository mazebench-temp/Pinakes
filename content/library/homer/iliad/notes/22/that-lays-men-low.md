---
summary: Zeus sets in the golden scales “two fates of death that lays men low, / one of Achilles, and one of Hector, tamer of horses” (22.210–211).
---

The verse is the one of the scales in Book 8 (22.210 = 8.70), where the fates of the two armies were weighed; here they are the fates of two men (22.209–213; [[the-scales-of-zeus]], [[ker-doom]], [[tamer-of-horses]]). See [[zeus]].
