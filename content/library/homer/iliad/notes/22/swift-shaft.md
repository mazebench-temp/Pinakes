---
summary: Hector's spear glances off Achilles' shield, and Hector is angry “that his swift missile had flown from his hand in vain” (22.292).
---

The verse is the one of Hector's anger when his spear failed against Ajax in Book 14, just before Ajax's stone struck him down (14.406–407); here the anger is followed by his recognition that he has been deceived (22.291–299). See [[hector]] and [[ash-spear]].
