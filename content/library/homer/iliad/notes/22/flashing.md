αἰόλαι εὐλαί, “writhing worms,” is new to the entry (see the translation's table of key words, under αἰόλος). Achilles feared that flies would breed worms in Patroclus' wounds (19.24–27); in Andromache's words the worms are the end of Hector's body. See [[andromache]] and [[curved-ships]].
