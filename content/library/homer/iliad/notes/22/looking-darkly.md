---
summary: Achilles answers both of Hector's requests with the verse of the glare: the proposal to give back the loser's body (22.260) and the dying plea for burial (22.344), “Looking at him darkly, swift-footed Achilles said.”
---

The verse is the one with which Achilles answered Agamemnon in the first quarrel (1.148), and it introduces his rebuke of Priam in the last book (24.559). Here it opens the two refusals: “Hector, accursed one, do not talk to me of agreements” (22.261) and “Do not implore me, you dog, by my knees or my parents” (22.345; [[no-oaths-between-lions-and-men]], [[supplication]]).
