---
summary: Andromache sees Hector dragged behind the chariot, “And murky night covered her eyes, / and she fell backward, and breathed out her soul” (22.466–467).
en: [murky night covered her eyes]
---

The verse, with τήν for τόν, is the one of Tlepolemus' death and of Deipyrus' (5.659, 13.580), and the next verse goes on as if she had died, ἀπὸ δὲ ψυχὴν ἐκάπυσσε, “and breathed out her soul” (22.467). She comes to herself only after her husband's sisters and his brothers' wives have held her up among them (22.473–475; [[murky]], [[in-laws]]). See [[andromache]].
