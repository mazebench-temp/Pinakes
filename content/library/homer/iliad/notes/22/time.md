---
summary: Hector, deceived, promises the false Deiphobus more honor than before: “but now I mean to honor you even more in my heart”, since he alone dared to come out to him (22.235–237).
grc: [τιμήσασθαι]
---

The honor Hector promises is a brother's, owed for a risk taken for his sake (22.233–237; see [[deiphobus]], [[kasignetos]] and [[truth-and-deception]]). Hecuba remembers that the Trojans greeted Hector himself “as a god” (22.434–435; see [[kydos]] and [[gods-and-mortals]]).
