---
summary: Hector waits at the gates “leaning his shining shield against a jutting tower” (22.97); and Achilles, charging, “nodded with his shining helmet” (22.314).
---

The shield is the one Hector thinks of setting down as he ponders (22.111; [[bossed]]); the helmet is the one Hephaestus made for Achilles (22.314–316; [[four-ridged]], [[helmet]]). See [[hector]] and [[achilles]].
