The young Achaeans sang the paean to Apollo at Chryse, to end the plague (1.472–474); here it is a song of victory over the man Apollo protected until the scales turned (22.203–213), as they bring the body to the ships (see [[kydos]] and [[apollo]]). Hector, dying, has just named Apollo with Paris as the killers of Achilles (22.359–360; see [[death-of-achilles-foretold]]).
