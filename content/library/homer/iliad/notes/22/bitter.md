---
summary: Achilles shakes his head at his men “and would not let them hurl their bitter missiles at Hector” (22.206).
grc: ["πικρὰ βέλεμνα"]
---

πικρὰ βέλεμνα is new to the entry and found only here. Achilles will not share the glory: “lest someone win the glory by hitting him, and he himself come second” (22.207; [[kydos]]). See [[achilles]].
