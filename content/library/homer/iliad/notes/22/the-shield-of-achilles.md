# Unnamed: the shield struck (22.290-291) and the shield before Achilles' chest (22.313-314).
refs: [22.290-291, 22.313-314]
---

**Hector's spear.**

{{quote:e23d18b8-491a-5ebc-b0f5-2559550a4871}}

The spears of Aeneas and Asteropaeus were held by the gold, “the gifts of the god” (20.267–268, 21.164–165); Hector's glances far off, and the poem does not say how (see [[shield]]). Hector is angry “that his swift missile had flown from his hand in vain”, the verse said when his spear struck Ajax where the two straps crossed his chest and did him no harm (22.292 = 14.407; see [[swift-shaft]] and [[the-death-of-hector]]).

**Before his chest.** In the charge Achilles “covered his chest in front with his shield, / fine and intricately worked” (22.313–314). καλὸν δαιδάλεον, “fine and intricately worked”, are the words of the arming, “so from the shield of Achilles the blaze reached the upper air, / the fine shield, intricately worked” (19.379–380), and of the helmet in the making, “fine and intricately worked” (18.612; see [[intricately-worked]], [[helmet]] and [[the-making-of-the-arms]]). Behind it he looks for the place where Hector's own armor, Achilles' first armor, leaves him bare (22.321–325; see [[the-armor-of-peleus]]).
