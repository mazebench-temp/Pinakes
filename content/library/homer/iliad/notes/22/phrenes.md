---
summary: Priam asks for pity “while I am still alive and aware” (ἔτι φρονέοντα, 22.59); Achilles brandishes his spear “with evil intent against brilliant Hector” (22.320); Hector “understood in his heart” that Athena had deceived him (22.296), and tells the man who kills him, “truly the heart in your breast is iron” (22.357). Andromache comes to herself when “her spirit was gathered back into her heart” (22.475).
---

φρονέω, the verb of the φρένες, gives Priam's wish to be pitied while he can still feel it (22.59) and Achilles' set purpose as he looks for the place to strike (22.320–321; see [[pity]] and [[death-in-battle]]). Hector's understanding comes when he calls for a spear and his brother is not there (22.294–299; see [[truth-and-deception]] and [[thymos]]).
