---
summary: As the gods watch the pursuit, “the father of men and gods began to speak” (22.167), asking them whether to save Hector.
---

τοῖσι δὲ μύθων ἦρχε πατὴρ ἀνδρῶν τε θεῶν τε is the verse that opens Zeus' speech to the gods in the Odyssey's first council (*Odyssey* 1.28), and it opens his speech to Thetis about Hector's body in the last book (24.103–104). See [[zeus]] and [[watching-the-war]].
