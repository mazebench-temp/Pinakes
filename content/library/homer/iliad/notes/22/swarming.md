summary: “and among the Trojan women Hecuba led the unceasing lamentation” (22.430).
---

ἁδινοῦ ἐξῆρχε γόοιο is the verse-end with which Achilles led the lament over Patroclus (18.316); it returns for Achilles at Patroclus' pyre (23.17) and for Hecuba at Hector's (24.747). See [[lament]] and [[hecuba]].
