---
summary: Athena comes to Achilles at the springs: “Now at last I hope that we two, shining Achilles, dear to Zeus, / will carry off great glory for the Achaeans to the ships” (22.216–217).
---

The vocative Διῒ φίλε is used three times in the Iliad: by Calchas to Achilles, as he asks for his protection (1.74), by Achilles to Patroclus, as he sends him to Nestor (11.611), and here by Athena, who comes to him as soon as the scales of Zeus have sunk on Hector's side (22.209–214; [[the-scales-of-zeus]]). The goddess joins herself to the hero, νῶϊ, “we two”. See [[achilles]], [[athena]] and [[phaidimos]].
