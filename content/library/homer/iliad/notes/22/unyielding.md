---
summary: Athena comes to Hector “in the likeness of Deiphobus in form and in tireless voice” (22.227).
---

δέμας καὶ ἀτειρέα φωνήν is the verse-end of a god in a man's likeness: Poseidon as Calchas (13.45), Athena as Phoenix (17.555), and Athena as Deiphobus here ([[truth-and-deception]], [[deiphobus]]). See [[athena]].
