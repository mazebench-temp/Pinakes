---
summary: Athena snatches up Achilles' spear and gives it back to him, “unseen by Hector, shepherd of the people” (22.277).
---

The title of the leader stands at the moment he is deceived: Hector does not see that Achilles has his spear again, and when his own spear glances off the shield he calls in vain to the brother who is not there (22.276–299; [[truth-and-deception]], [[deiphobus]]). Andromache will call him the city's protector in other words: “for you alone guarded their gates and long walls” (22.507). See [[hector]].
