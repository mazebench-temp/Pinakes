summary: Grief is claimed falsely and then truly. Athena, as Deiphobus, says that their parents begged him to stay inside, “but my heart within me was worn down by grim grief” (22.239–242); Andromache, lamenting, tells the dead Hector, “you go to the house of Hades … and leave me in hateful grief” (22.482–483).
grc: [πένθεϊ]
---

πένθεϊ λυγρῷ, “by grim grief”, ends a verse of Telemachus' in the Odyssey, “leave me alone with grim grief” (*Odyssey* 2.70), whose opening Priam uses when he asks his friends to let him go to Achilles (22.416; see [[truth-and-deception]], [[andromache]] and [[achos]]).
