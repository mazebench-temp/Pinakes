summary: Hector promises not to defile Achilles' body if he wins (22.256); Achilles promises that the dogs and birds will tear Hector's “shamefully” (22.336), devises “shameful deeds” against him (22.395), and Zeus gives Hector to his enemies “to defile in his own native land” (22.404).
grc: ["ἀεικέα μήδετο ἔργα"]
---

The verb opens and closes the scene. Hector's promise, “for I will not defile you terribly” (οὐ … ἔκπαγλον ἀεικιῶ, 22.256), is answered almost a hundred and fifty lines later by the narrator's “to defile in his own native land” (ἀεικίσσασθαι, 22.404). Between them stand Achilles' refusal of an agreement (22.261–272), his threat (ἀϊκῶς, 22.335–336) and the deed (ἀεικέα … ἔργα, 22.395, a verse that returns at 23.24).
