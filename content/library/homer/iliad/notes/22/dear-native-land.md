---
summary: Zeus gave Hector to his enemies “to defile in his own native land” (22.403–404).
---

ἑῇ ἐν πατρίδι γαίῃ is the formula of homecoming turned against the man who has never left home: Hector dies and is dragged in sight of his own city, his parents and his wife (22.401–409, 22.462–465). The verse makes Zeus the giver of the defilement (22.403–404; [[zeus]]). See [[hector]].
