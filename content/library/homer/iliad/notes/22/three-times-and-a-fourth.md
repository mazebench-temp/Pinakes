# τρὶς Πριάμοιο πόλιν (22.165), τὸ τέταρτον ἐπὶ κρουνοὺς (22.208), τρὶς περὶ ἄστυ (22.251): each only here.

The fourth time is again the turning point, but here no hero is stopped by a god's shout. At the fourth circuit the fates of both men are weighed, Hector's sinks, and “Phoebus Apollo left him” (22.208–213; [[the-scales-of-zeus]], [[phoebus]]). In Book 20 Apollo had hidden Hector in mist while Achilles sprang three times at him and shouted after him at the fourth (20.443–454); the god who saved him then now abandons him. Hector's own words begin with the count: “I will no longer flee from you, son of Peleus, as before / I fled three times around the great city of Priam” (22.250–251). See [[hector]] and [[achilles]].
