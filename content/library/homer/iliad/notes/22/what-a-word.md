summary: Athena answers Zeus' wish to save Hector: “Father, lord of the gleaming thunderbolt and of the dark clouds, what a thing you have said!” (22.178).
---

οἷον ἔειπες stands where Hera, protesting against the rescue of Sarpedon, had ποῖον τὸν μῦθον ἔειπες (16.440), and the three verses that follow are hers (22.179–181 = 16.441–443; [[not-all-the-gods-approve]]). See [[athena]], [[zeus]] and [[of-the-gleaming-thunderbolt]].
