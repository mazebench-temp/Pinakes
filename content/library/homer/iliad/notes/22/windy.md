---
summary: Hector and Achilles run “past the lookout and the windy wild fig tree” (22.145).
grc: [ἠνεμόεντα]
---

The fig tree near the wall is named twice before without an epithet (6.433, 11.167); here it has the epithet that is above all Troy's own (see [[the-fig-tree]] and [[the-lookout-at-troy]]). The accusative ἠνεμόεντα is new to the entry. See [[the-walls-of-troy]].
