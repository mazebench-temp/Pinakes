---
summary: In Andromache's picture of the orphan the boy whose parents are both alive drives him from the feast, “striking him with his hands and rebuking him with abusive words: / ‘Off with you! Your father does not feast among us’” (22.497–498).
grc: [ὀνειδείοισιν]
---

ὀνειδείοισιν, the fuller form of ὀνειδείοις, is found only here; elsewhere the abusive words are ὀνειδείοις ἐπέεσσιν, said of Hera provoking Zeus, of Thersites abusing the kings and of Hera rebuking Artemis (1.519, 2.277, 21.480). Here the abuser is a child, and his words come with blows (see [[words-and-violence]], [[childhood]] and [[the-day-of-orphanhood]]).
