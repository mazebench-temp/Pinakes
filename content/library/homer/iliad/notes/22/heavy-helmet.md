---
summary: Hector imagines setting down “my bossed shield / and my heavy helmet” and going unarmed to Achilles (22.111–112).
---

The formulas of Hector's own arms come in the one passage where he thinks of laying them down (22.111–121; [[bossed]]); he rejects the thought, fearing that Achilles would kill him “naked, / just like a woman, once I have taken off my armor” (22.124–125). See [[hector]] and [[helmet]].
