---
summary: Hecuba begs Hector, “And his mother on the other side mourned, shedding tears … and shedding tears she addressed him with winged words” (22.79–81).
grc: ["δάκρυ χέουσʼ"]
---

The phrase comes twice in three verses, the second time with the elided δάκρυ χέουσʼ, new to the entry, before the formula of address (22.81; [[winged-words]]). Between the two she loosens the fold of her robe and holds up her breast (22.80; [[hecuba]], [[mothers-and-sons]]). See [[tears]].
