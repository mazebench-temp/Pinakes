---
summary: When Hector dies, Andromache is weaving “a double-folded purple cloth”, working flowers of many colors into it (22.440–441).
---

δίπλακα πορφυρέην is the half-verse of Helen's web in Book 3, “a double-folded purple cloth”, into which she was working the many trials of the Trojans and Achaeans (3.125–128). Helen weaves the war; Andromache weaves flowers (θρόνα, found only here in Homer; see [[weaving]] and [[loom]]).
