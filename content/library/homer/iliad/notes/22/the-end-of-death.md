---
summary: “As he spoke, the end of death covered him” (22.361): Hector dies at the end of his prophecy of Achilles' death.
---

The verse is the one of Sarpedon's death and of Patroclus' (16.502, 16.855), and, as with Patroclus, the two verses of the soul's flight follow it (22.362–363; [[leaving-manhood-and-youth]]).

{{quote:06ecc1eb-d7ce-56b6-bd11-384c1400e4fc}}

Achilles' answer to the dead man is introduced by the verse with which Hector spoke to the dead Patroclus (22.364, as 16.858; [[brilliant]]). See [[hector]] and [[death-of-achilles-foretold]].
