---
summary: Hector ends his monologue: “let us see to which of us the Olympian will hold out triumph” (22.130).
---

The name stands alone for Zeus, as the god who grants the victory (see the translation's table of formulas, under εὖχος ὀρέξαι). Hector leaves the outcome to Zeus, and the narrator has Zeus weigh it in the scales (22.209–213; [[the-scales-of-zeus]]). See [[zeus]] and [[hector]].
