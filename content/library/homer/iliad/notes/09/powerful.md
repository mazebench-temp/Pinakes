---
summary: Nestor says that no one will dishonor his word, “not even powerful Agamemnon,” and Achilles uses the same title of the king who took back his prize.
---

Nestor claims the right to speak before the king: “no one will dishonor my word, not even powerful Agamemnon” (9.61–62; [[time]]). In Achilles' reply to Odysseus the formula has the opposite force. His prize, he says, “the very man who gave it has taken back again in his arrogance, powerful Agamemnon” (9.367–368; [[geras]]). The title of power is the name of a man who used it to insult.
