---
summary: Four times in the night Agamemnon is addressed as κύδιστε, “most glorious”, on the night of his worst defeat. Odysseus offers Achilles the glory of killing Hector, and is himself greeted by the king as “great glory of the Achaeans”.
passages:
  - 9.300-306 | Odysseus: pity the Achaeans
---

**Most glorious.** Nestor (9.96, 9.163), Odysseus (9.677) and Diomedes (9.697) all address the king with the same verse, “Most glorious son of Atreus, Agamemnon, lord of men” (Ἀτρεΐδη κύδιστε ἄναξ ἀνδρῶν Ἀγάμεμνον). κύδιστος is the superlative of the stem of κῦδος, the glory of success that Zeus grants and takes away. The formula is courtesy, and on this night it is also a reminder: Zeus has taken the glory from Agamemnon and given it to Hector, as the king complained (8.237; see [[ate]]), and three of the four speeches it opens tell him what he does not want to hear.

**Glory offered.** Odysseus ends his speech with the one inducement that is not a gift: if Achilles hates Agamemnon and his gifts, let him pity the rest, “like a god they will honor you; for you would win very great glory in their eyes” (μάλα μέγα κῦδος ἄροιο, 9.302–303), since Hector, in his frenzy, would come within reach (9.304–306). κῦδος is the glory of a victory, won in the moment and seen by others (see [[time]]). Achilles answers with κλέος instead, the fame that will be told after him, and with the life that fame would cost (9.410–416; see [[kleos]]). In Book 16 he will ask Patroclus to win him “great honor and glory” from the Danaans (τιμὴν μεγάλην καὶ κῦδος, 16.84).

**Great glory of the Achaeans.** At the end Agamemnon greets the returning Odysseus, “Come, tell me, much-praised Odysseus, great glory of the Achaeans” (μέγα κῦδος Ἀχαιῶν, 9.673). The title belongs elsewhere to Nestor and to Odysseus (10.87, 10.544), and the Sirens hail Odysseus with the same words, πολύαινʼ Ὀδυσεῦ, μέγα κῦδος Ἀχαιῶν (*Odyssey* 12.184; see [[great-glory-of-the-achaeans]] and [[much-praised]]). The man who brings back the refusal is called the glory of the army.
