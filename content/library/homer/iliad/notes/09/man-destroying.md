---
summary: Phoenix warns Achilles that if he enters “man-destroying war” without the gifts, he will not be held in the same honor.
---

“But if you enter man-destroying war without the gifts, you will no longer be held in the same honor, though you turn back the war” (9.604–605; [[time]], [[evil-day]]). The epithet of war comes at the end of Phoenix's argument from Meleager, who fought too late and lost the gifts (9.597–599). Achilles answers that he has no need of that honor (9.607–608). See [[polemos]].
