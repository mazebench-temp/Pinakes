---
summary: In the four verses known only from Plutarch, Phoenix says he planned to kill his father “with the sharp bronze.”
---

“Then I planned to kill him with the sharp bronze; but one of the immortals checked my anger” (9.458–459). These verses are in no manuscript of the Iliad; Plutarch quotes them and says that Aristarchus removed them out of fear (*How the Young Man Should Study Poetry* 26F; [[phoenix-patricide-lines]], [[the-flight-of-phoenix]]). The formula of the killing weapon, ὀξέϊ χαλκῷ, is in the line of a son who meant to kill his father ([[immortals]]). See [[phoenix]].
