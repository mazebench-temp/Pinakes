---
summary: Peleus told his son to “cease from strife that devises evil,” as Odysseus reminds Achilles.
en: [that devises evil]
---

Odysseus quotes Peleus' parting words: “My child, Athena and Hera will give you strength, if they will; but you, hold back your proud heart in your breast; for kindliness is better; and cease from strife that devises evil, so that all the more the Argives may honor you, both young and old” (9.254–258; [[eris]], [[great-hearted]]). The father's advice names the quarrel in which his son now persists. Phoenix too was sent by Peleus, and he gives his own account of what Peleus wanted for Achilles: “to be a speaker of words and a doer of deeds” (9.443; [[speaker-of-words-doer-of-deeds]], [[peleus-sends-achilles-to-troy]]).
