---
summary: Idas, Cleopatra's father, was “the strongest of men on earth in those days,” and took up his bow against Apollo.
---

“And of Idas, who was the strongest of men on earth in those days; and he even took up his bow against the lord Phoebus Apollo” (9.558–560; [[idas]], [[idas-and-apollo]]). ἐπιχθόνιοι are the men who live on the earth, set against the gods of heaven, and Idas was the strongest of them in the generation before the war, as Nestor said that none “of the mortals now living on earth” could fight the Lapiths of his youth (1.271–272). The man who was strongest on earth fought a god. See [[phoebus]].
