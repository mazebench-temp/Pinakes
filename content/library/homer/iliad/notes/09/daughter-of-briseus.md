---
summary: Agamemnon offers to return “the daughter of Briseus” with an oath that he never lay with her, and Odysseus repeats the offer.
grc: ["κούρη Βρισῆος"]
---

“These I will give him, and with them will be the one I took from him then, the daughter of Briseus; and I will swear a great oath besides that I never went up into her bed or lay with her” (9.131–133; Odysseus' repetition, 9.273–275; [[briseis]], [[briseus]], [[oath]]). Agamemnon does not name her: she is the woman he took, and her father's daughter. Achilles names her as his wife, without her name either: “he keeps the wife dear to my heart” (9.336; [[thymares]]). Briseis is given back in Book 19, and Agamemnon swears the oath (19.246, 19.258–265). See [[taking-of-briseis]].
