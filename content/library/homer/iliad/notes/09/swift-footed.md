summary: The formula introduces each of Achilles' four speeches in the embassy, his welcome and his three answers, as he refuses to move.

{{quote:d8847278-d6b8-53f3-b964-af25e6fc32b5}}

The verse “Answering him, swift-footed Achilles said” introduces his answers to Odysseus, to Phoenix and to Ajax (9.307, 9.606, 9.643), and “swift-footed Achilles said” his welcome to the envoys (9.196). Each of the three answers holds a different position: he will sail at dawn (9.356–363), he will decide at dawn whether to go or stay (9.618–619), he will not think of war until Hector reaches the Myrmidons' ships (9.650–655; [[the-embassy-to-achilles]]). As in Book 1, where the formula marks him sitting idle by his ships (1.488–492), the epithet of speed belongs to a man who is going nowhere. See [[achilles]].
