---
summary: Marpessa's mother wept because “the far-working Phoebus Apollo” had snatched her daughter away.
---

“Because her mother, having the lot of the halcyon bird of many sorrows, wept that the far-working Phoebus Apollo had snatched her away” (9.562–564; [[of-many-sorrows]], [[phoebus]], [[marpessa]]). The archer's title names the god who took a mortal bride from her husband, whom Idas had challenged with his own bow (9.558–560; [[idas-and-apollo]]). See [[apollo]].
