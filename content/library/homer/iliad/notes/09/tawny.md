---
summary: Agamemnon offers Achilles “twenty gleaming cauldrons,” αἴθωνας λέβητας.
grc: [αἴθωνας]
---

“And twenty gleaming cauldrons, and twelve horses” (9.123; Odysseus' repetition, 9.265; [[the-gifts-of-agamemnon]]). Of bronze vessels the word means the gleam of the metal, and the translation renders it “gleaming,” as it does of iron (4.485, 7.473). The same verse counts out the cauldrons when the gifts are carried from Agamemnon's hut in Book 19 (19.244). Compare [[untouched-by-fire]], the epithet of the tripods in the verse before.
