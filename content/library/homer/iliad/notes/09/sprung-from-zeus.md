---
summary: Nestor gives the title to Agamemnon as he reminds him how he took Briseis; Achilles and Ajax use the formal address of Odysseus; and Achilles calls Ajax “Telamonian Ajax, sprung from Zeus.”
---

Nestor addresses Agamemnon in the middle of a reproach: the thought he has held “ever since the time when you, sprung from Zeus, took the girl Briseis and went from the hut of Achilles in his anger” (9.106–107; [[taking-of-briseis]]). The honorific softens the charge. Achilles opens his great reply with the formal address of Odysseus, “Son of Laertes, sprung from Zeus, Odysseus of many devices” (9.308), and Ajax uses it to tell Odysseus that it is time to go (9.624; [[son-of-laertes]], [[of-many-devices]]). Achilles answers Ajax with the same honor: “Telamonian Ajax, sprung from Zeus, lord of the people” (9.644; [[telamonian]]).
