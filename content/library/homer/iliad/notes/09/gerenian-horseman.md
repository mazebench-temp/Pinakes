---
summary: Nestor is “the horseman Nestor” when he rises in the assembly, and “the Gerenian horseman” when he accepts the gifts, chooses the envoys and sends them off with his instructions.
grc: ["ἱππότα Νέστωρ"]
---

**The horseman.** “And the horseman Nestor stood up among them and spoke” (9.52): the old title, here without Γερήνιος, introduces his praise and correction of Diomedes and his first advice, to set the watch and feast the elders (9.53–78; [[son-of-tydeus]], [[night-watch]]).

**The Gerenian horseman.** In the council the full formula introduces his answer to Agamemnon's offer, “the gifts you offer lord Achilles are no longer to be scorned” (9.162–164), and his choice of envoys (9.165–172). As they leave, “the Gerenian horseman Nestor gave them many instructions, glancing at each of them, and at Odysseus most of all” (9.179–180). The old man who can no longer fight in the front line plans the night: the watch, the feast, the embassy. See [[nestor]] and [[the-embassy-to-achilles]].
