---
summary: The seven towns that Agamemnon offers all lie “near the sea, on the borders of sandy Pylos.”
---

“All lie near the sea, on the borders of sandy Pylos” (9.153 = 9.295; [[pylos]], [[the-seven-cities]]). The epithet of Nestor's city places the gift on the edge of Nestor's own kingdom, and how Agamemnon could give towns there is not explained. Nestor, who answers the offer, does not mention it (9.163–164).
