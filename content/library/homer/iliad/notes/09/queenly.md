---
summary: Two mothers in Phoenix's story are “lady mother”: Marpessa, who named her daughter Alcyone, and Althaea, who joins the appeal to her son after cursing him.
---

“In those days, in the halls, her father and her lady mother called her by the name of Alcyone” (9.561–562; [[marpessa]], [[cleopatra]], [[of-many-sorrows]]). And when the Curetes are at the gates, “his sisters and his lady mother” beg Meleager to fight (9.584–585; [[althaea]]). It is the same mother who had called on Hades and Persephone to give her son death (9.566–572; [[dread]]). The title of honor stands beside the curse. See [[the-wrath-of-meleager]].
