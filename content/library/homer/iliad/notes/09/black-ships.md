---
summary: The Trojans say they will “fall upon the black ships,” Odysseus tells Achilles, and Achilles says that Hector will be held back “around my hut and my black ship.”
grc: ["νηυσὶ μελαίνῃσιν"]
---

The black ships are the target and the last line of defense. Odysseus reports the Trojans' boast: they say they will no longer be held back, “but will fall upon the black ships” (9.234–235; ἐν νηυσὶ μελαίνῃσιν). The same verse is repeated by others in Books 11, 12 and 17 (11.824, 12.107, 12.126, 17.639). At the end of his answer to Ajax, Achilles names his own ship as the place where Hector will be stopped: “But around my hut and my black ship, I think, Hector will be held back, eager for battle though he is” (9.654–655). The other ships may burn. See [[ships]].
