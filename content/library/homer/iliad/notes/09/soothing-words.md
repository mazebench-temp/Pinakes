---
summary: Nestor proposes to win Achilles back “with kindly gifts and with soothing words.”
---

“Let us consider how we may make amends to him and win him over with kindly gifts and with soothing words” (9.112–113; [[gentle-words]]). Nestor's plan has two parts, and Agamemnon supplies only one. He lists the gifts at length and ends with words that do not soothe: Achilles is to be tamed, since Hades is implacable, and to submit to the man who is more kingly (9.158–161; [[implacable]], [[untamable]]). ἀμείλιχος, “implacable,” is the negative of the word that Nestor used. Odysseus keeps the gifts and leaves out the demand (9.260–306; [[message-repeated]]).
