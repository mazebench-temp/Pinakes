---
summary: Achilles asks whether the sons of Atreus are “the only ones of mortal men who love their wives.”
---

“Are they the only ones of mortal men who love their wives, the sons of Atreus? For any man who is good and sensible loves his own and cares for her” (9.340–342; [[son-of-atreus]], [[thymares]]). The old epithet of humankind widens the question: every man loves his wife, and Achilles is one of them. See [[achilles]].
