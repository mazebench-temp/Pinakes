---
summary: Odysseus names his witnesses: “Ajax and the two heralds, both wise men.”
---

“So he spoke; and these men too who came with me are here to tell it, Ajax and the two heralds, both wise men” (9.688–689; [[heralds]], [[eurybates]], [[odius-the-herald]]). The dual πεπνυμένω ἄμφω is the one used in Book 7 of the heralds Talthybius and Idaeus, who parted Hector and Ajax (7.276–278). Here it vouches for the report: the heralds heard everything. Odysseus does not mention Phoenix, who stayed behind (9.690–692). See [[the-embassy-to-achilles]].
