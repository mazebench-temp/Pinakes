---
summary: Three words of the family describe Agamemnon's “glorious gifts,” the Trojans' “far-famed allies” camped near the ships, and “the famous shaker of the earth” who could give Achilles a fair voyage home.
---

περικλυτά: “And before you all I will name the glorious gifts” (9.121; [[the-gifts-of-agamemnon]]). τηλεκλειτοί: Odysseus tells Achilles that “the high-hearted Trojans and their far-famed allies” have pitched camp near the ships and the wall (9.232–233; [[high-hearted]], [[trojan-allies]]). κλυτός: Achilles will be in Phthia on the third day “if the famous shaker of the earth grants me fair sailing” (9.362–363; [[shaker-of-the-earth]]). The fame of the gifts, the fame of the enemy and the fame of the god who rules the road home are all set before a man who is weighing his own fame against his life ([[kleos]], [[imperishable]]).
