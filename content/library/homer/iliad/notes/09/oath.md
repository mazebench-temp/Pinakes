summary: Agamemnon promises, with Briseis, “a great oath” that he never went to her bed, and Odysseus repeats the promise to Achilles (9.132–134, 9.274–276); the oath is sworn in Book 19 over a boar, by Zeus, Earth, Sun and the Erinyes.
grc: [ὀμεῖται]

**The promise.** Briseis is to go back with the other gifts, and with her an oath: “I will swear a great oath besides that I never went up into her bed or lay with her, as is the custom of mankind, of men and women” (9.132–134). The oath belongs to the gift: it promises that the prize comes back as it was taken (see [[briseis]] and [[the-gifts-of-agamemnon]]). Odysseus passes the promise on in the third person, “he will swear a great oath besides” (ὀμεῖται, 9.274), and calls Achilles “my lord” in the verse that names the custom (9.276; see [[message-repeated]] and [[themis]]).

**The answer.** Achilles does not mention the oath. He speaks of Briseis as Agamemnon's bedfellow: “he keeps the wife dear to my heart; let him lie beside her and take his pleasure” (9.336–337; see [[marriage]]).

**Sworn.** In Book 19 Odysseus proposes, in his verses of Book 9, that Agamemnon stand up among the Argives and swear to Achilles that he never went up into her bed (19.175–177 ≈ 9.274–276). Agamemnon swears it standing over a boar that Talthybius holds, cutting hair from its head and lifting his hands to Zeus: “Let Zeus now be my witness first, highest and best of the gods, and Earth and Sun and the Erinyes, who under the earth punish men, whoever swears a false oath, that I never laid a hand on the girl Briseis” (19.250–255, 19.258–261). He cuts the boar's throat, and Talthybius throws it into the sea for the fishes (19.266–268; see [[erinyes]]).

**The oath of Book 1.** The embassy itself answers an older oath. In the first assembly Achilles swore by the scepter that “a longing for Achilles will come upon the sons of the Achaeans, on all of them together”, when they fell at Hector's hands (1.233–244; see [[longing-for-achilles]] and [[scepter]]). In Book 9 the army sends to ask for him.
