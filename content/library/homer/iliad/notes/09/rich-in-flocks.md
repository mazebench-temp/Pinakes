---
summary: The men of the seven towns that Agamemnon offers are “rich in flocks and rich in cattle,” πολύρρηνες πολυβοῦται.
grc: [πολύρρηνες]
---

“And in them live men rich in flocks and rich in cattle, who will honor him with gifts like a god” (9.154–155; Odysseus' repetition, 9.296–297; [[rich-in-cattle]], [[the-seven-cities]]). πολύρρην = πολύς (“many”) + a form of the word for a lamb (ἀρήν), the same word as in πολύαρνι: “with many sheep.” It occurs only in this verse; the Odyssey has the related πολύρρηνος once, of Pelias, who lived in Iolcus “rich in flocks” (Od. 11.256–257). With πολύαρνι and πολύμηλος (2.106, 2.605, 2.705) it makes a third word of the entry, and the translation renders all three “rich in flocks.” See [[sleek]].
