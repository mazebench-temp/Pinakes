---
summary: Phoenix says that Achilles came to Troy too young to know the assemblies, “where men become distinguished.”
grc: [ἀριπρεπέες]
en: [distinguished]
---

“A child, who knew nothing yet of war, common to all, nor of assemblies, where men become distinguished” (9.440–441; [[common-to-all]], [[assembly]]). The word that Hector used in his prayer for his son, that he might be “preeminent among the Trojans” (6.477), is used here of the distinction won by speaking. The man who is distinguished in the assembly is the one Phoenix was sent to make of Achilles: “a speaker of words and a doer of deeds” (9.443; [[speaker-of-words-doer-of-deeds]]). The translation renders it here “distinguished.” See [[phoenix]].
