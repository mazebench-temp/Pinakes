---
summary: Odysseus warns Achilles that “the high-hearted Trojans and their far-famed allies” are camped by the ships, in the words of Hector's own rallying cry.
---

“For near the ships and the wall they have pitched their camp, the high-hearted Trojans and their far-famed allies” (9.232–233; [[famous]], [[trojan-allies]]). The verse is the one with which Hector rallied the Trojans and their allies in Book 6, before he went to the city (6.111). In Odysseus' mouth the enemy's pride in themselves becomes a reason for fear. See [[trojans]].
