---
summary: Artemis is “of the golden throne” in Phoenix's story, as she sends the boar against Oeneus for forgetting her.
---

“For Artemis of the golden throne had roused an evil against them, angry because no harvest offering on the slope of his orchard was made to her by Oeneus” (9.533–535; [[artemis]], [[oeneus]]). In Book 1 the epithet belonged to Hera (1.611); here it is given to the goddess whose anger begins the war of the Curetes and the Aetolians. Five verses later she is “the divine child, she of the showering arrows” (9.538; [[of-the-showering-arrows]], [[brilliant]]). See [[the-wrath-of-meleager]].
