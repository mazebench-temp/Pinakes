---
summary: Achilles will not marry Agamemnon's daughter even if she “equaled gray-eyed Athena in handiwork.”
grc: [γλαυκώπιδι]
---

“Not if she rivaled golden Aphrodite in beauty and equaled gray-eyed Athena in handiwork” (9.389–390; [[golden]]). Athena is the goddess of women's work at the loom as well as of war, and Achilles names her as the measure of skill as Aphrodite is of beauty. The dative γλαυκώπιδι is the only form of the epithet in the book. See [[athena]].
