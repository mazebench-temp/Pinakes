summary: The din of the quarrel rises “over the boar's head and its shaggy hide.”
grc: [λαχνήεντι]
---

“But she raised around it a great din and shouting over the boar's head and its shaggy hide, between the Curetes and the great-hearted Aetolians” (9.547–549; [[the-calydonian-boar]], [[great-hearted]]). The trophies of the hunt, head and bristly hide, become the cause of war, and Artemis, who sent the boar, stirs the quarrel over them. Homer does not say who claimed them ([[the-wrath-of-meleager]]).
