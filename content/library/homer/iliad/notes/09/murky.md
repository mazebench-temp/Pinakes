---
summary: Phoenix escapes from his father's house on “the tenth murky night.”
---

“But when the tenth murky night came upon me, then I broke the closely fitted doors of the chamber” (9.474–475; [[closely-fitted]], [[well-fenced]]). For nine nights his kinsmen had kept watch on him by turns with two fires burning (9.470–473). The adjective is the one used of the night that covers a dying man's eyes (5.659). Phoenix tells of that night on another night, sitting in Achilles' hut during the embassy. See [[the-flight-of-phoenix]].
