---
summary: Nestor tells the Achaeans to yield to black night, in the verse Hector spoke to the Trojans the evening before.
---

“But now let us yield to black night and make ready our supper” (9.65–66). The verse is the one with which Hector began his night orders to the Trojans on the plain (8.502–503). The two camps receive the same advice in the same words: eat, set a guard, and wait for the dawn. The Trojans keep their fires burning near the ships (9.76–77), and Nestor ends: “This night will either shatter the army or save it” (9.78; [[this-night-will-shatter-or-save]]). The Achaean sentries go out to sit between the ditch and the wall (9.80–88; [[night-watch]], [[yield-to-night]]). In this verse μελαίνῃ describes night, not the ships ([[black-ships]]).
