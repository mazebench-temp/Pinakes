---
summary: Achilles seats the envoys on chairs spread with purple rugs (9.200); purple coverings are a host's honor to his guests.
grc: [πορφυρέοισιν]
---

“And seated them on chairs with purple rugs” (τάπησί τε πορφυρέοισιν, 9.200). Purple coverings are the honor a house gives its guests. When Priam stays the night in Achilles' hut, Achilles has beds made for him and his herald with fine purple blankets and rugs spread over them (24.643–646), in the verses with which Helen has beds made for Telemachus and Peisistratus in Sparta (Od. 4.296–299), and Circe's handmaids spread purple coverings on the thrones (Od. 10.352–353).
