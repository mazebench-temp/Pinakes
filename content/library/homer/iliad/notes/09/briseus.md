---
summary: Briseus is named in Agamemnon's offer, which promises to give back “the daughter of Briseus” with an oath that he never lay with her, and again when Odysseus repeats the offer (9.132, 9.274).
---

Agamemnon names her as Achilles did in Book 1, κούρη Βρισῆος, “the daughter of Briseus” (9.132; 1.392), in the verse that leads into his oath (see [[briseis]] and [[the-gifts-of-agamemnon]]). Odysseus repeats the verse, changing only the person of the verb, “he will swear” (9.274). These three lines are all that Homer says of him.
