---
summary: Achilles tells the envoys to let the chiefs find a better plan to save the army “beside the hollow ships.”
grc: [γλαφυρῇς]
---

“So that they may think of some other, better plan in their minds, which may save their ships and the army of the Achaeans beside the hollow ships, since this one is not ready to their hand, the one they devised now, while I nurse my wrath apart” (9.423–426; [[menis]], [[boule]]). The form νηυσὶν ἔπι γλαφυρῇς, with the preposition after the noun, is a variant of the formula. See [[ships]].
