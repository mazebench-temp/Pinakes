---
summary: Amyntor curses his son Phoenix and calls on “the hateful Erinyes.”
grc: [στυγεράς]
---

“And my father, suspecting it at once, cursed me many times, and called on the hateful Erinyes that he would never set upon his knees a dear son born of me” (9.453–456; [[erinyes]], [[amyntor]], [[curses]]). The adjective from the root of Styx, the river of hate, describes the avengers of a father's curse. It is their standing epithet in the Odyssey too (Od. 2.135). See [[the-flight-of-phoenix]].
