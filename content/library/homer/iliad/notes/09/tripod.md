---
summary: Seven tripods “untouched by fire” head the list of Agamemnon's gifts, and Achilles names tripods among the things a man can win, as he cannot win back his life (9.122, 9.264, 9.407).
---

The list of gifts begins with them: “seven tripods untouched by fire, and ten talents of gold” (ἕπτʼ ἀπύρους τρίποδας, 9.122 = 9.264). Tripods that have never stood on the fire are treasure, not kitchen ware, and they come first, before the gold, the cauldrons and the horses (see [[the-gifts-of-agamemnon]] and [[possessions]]). Achilles answers with the same goods in a maxim: “cattle and fat sheep can be had by raiding, and tripods can be won, and the tawny heads of horses, but a man's soul cannot come back again” (9.406–408; see [[psyche]]). In Book 19 the tripods are the first things carried out of Agamemnon's hut, “seven tripods, which he had promised him” (19.243).
