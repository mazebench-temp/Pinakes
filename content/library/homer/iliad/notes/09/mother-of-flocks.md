---
summary: Phoenix came as a fugitive to “deep-soiled Phthia, mother of flocks,” where Peleus received him.
---

“Then I fled far away through Hellas with its wide dancing floors, and came to deep-soiled Phthia, mother of flocks, to lord Peleus; and he received me gladly” (9.478–480; [[deep-soiled]], [[with-wide-dancing-floors]], [[peleus]]). The two epithets of Phthia praise the land that gave the exile a home, wealth and people to rule (9.483–484; [[wealthy]], [[dolopians]]). See [[phthia]] and [[the-flight-of-phoenix]].
