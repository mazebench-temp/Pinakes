---
summary: Odysseus reports Achilles' threat to drag “his well-benched, curving ships to the sea” at dawn.
---

“And he himself threatened that when dawn appears he will drag his well-benched, curving ships to the sea” (9.682–683; [[well-benched]]). Odysseus' report turns Achilles' words of 9.357–361 into a threat. The two epithets describe the ships as fine and seaworthy, ready for the voyage that does not take place. See [[ships]].
