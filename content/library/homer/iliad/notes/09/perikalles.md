---
summary: The elders of the Aetolians offer Meleager “a beautiful domain” of fifty acres in the richest part of the plain.
grc: [περικαλλὲς]
---

“Where the plain of lovely Calydon was richest, there they bade him choose a beautiful domain of fifty acres, half of it vineyard, and half bare plowland, to be cut from the plain” (9.577–580; [[lovely]], [[calydon]]). A τέμενος is land cut out for a king or a god, and the offer is the Aetolians' gift for Meleager's return. He refuses it, fights in the end without it, and loses it (9.597–599). The beautiful estate is Phoenix's image of the gifts that Achilles is refusing ([[the-gifts-of-agamemnon]], [[the-wrath-of-meleager]]).
