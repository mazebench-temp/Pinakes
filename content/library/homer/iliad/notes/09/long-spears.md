---
summary: The hundred young men under each captain of the watch march out “holding long spears in their hands.”
---

“Seven were the leaders of the sentries, and with each a hundred young men marched, holding long spears in their hands” (9.85–86; [[night-watch]]). The verse-end formula, used of armed men on the move, here describes the guard that will sit by the ditch all night with its fires (9.87–88). See [[spear]].
