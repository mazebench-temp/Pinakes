---
summary: Ascalaphus and his brother Ialmenus, “sons of Ares”, are the second and third of the seven captains who lead the sentries out on the night of the embassy (9.82).
grc: [Ἀσκάλαφον]
---

“And around Ascalaphus and Ialmenus, sons of Ares” (9.82): the brothers are named together, with their father, as they were in the Catalogue (2.512; see [[ialmenus]] and [[ares]]), and this is the last time the poem names them together. Each leads a hundred young men to sit between the ditch and the wall (9.80–88; see [[night-watch]]). Ascalaphus returns in Book 13 beside two of his fellow captains, Aphareus and Deipyrus, when Idomeneus calls them to help him against Aeneas (13.477–479), and all three are killed in the fighting that follows (13.518–520, 13.541–544, 13.576–580; see [[aphareus]] and [[deipyrus]]).
