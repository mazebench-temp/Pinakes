---
summary: Achilles says that Agamemnon stayed “beside the swift ships” while he fought, and Phoenix asks whether he has no wish to ward off the fire “from the swift ships.”
grc: [θοῇσι]
---

Achilles' complaint: “all of them I would bring and give to Agamemnon, son of Atreus; and he, staying behind beside the swift ships, would take them, and share out a few, and keep many” (9.331–333; [[achilles-raids]]). Phoenix's appeal: “you have no wish at all to ward off from the swift ships the destroying fire” (9.435–436; [[aidelos]]). The same dative νηυσὶ θοῇσι places Agamemnon at ease in the camp and the ships in danger. See [[ships]].
