---
summary: If Troy is taken, Achilles may choose twenty Trojan women, “the most beautiful after Argive Helen.”
---

“And let him choose for himself twenty Trojan women, the most beautiful after Argive Helen” (9.139–140; Odysseus' repetition, 9.281–282; [[trojan-women]]). Helen is the measure of beauty in the list of spoil, and she is the cause of the war that Achilles questions in his answer: “Was it not for the sake of Helen of the lovely hair?” (9.339; [[lovely-haired]], [[helen]]). The text prints αἴ κε in Agamemnon's verse and αἵ κε in Odysseus'; the sense is the same.
