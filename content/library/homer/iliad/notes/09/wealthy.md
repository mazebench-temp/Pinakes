---
summary: Peleus received the fugitive Phoenix, loved him like a son, “and made me wealthy, and gave me many people.”
---

“And he made me wealthy, and gave me many people, and I lived at the far edge of Phthia, ruling over the Dolopians” (9.483–484; [[dolopians]], [[peleus]]). Phoenix came with nothing, having broken out of his father's house, and Peleus gave him what a father gives an heir: he loved him “as a father loves his son, his only son, his darling, heir to great possessions” (9.481–482; [[darling]]). The wealth and the people are the counterpart of the son his own father cursed him never to have (9.453–457). See [[phoenix]].
