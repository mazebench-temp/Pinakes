---
summary: “For we shall never now take wide-streeted Troy”: the last verse of Agamemnon's proposal to flee, repeated from his test of the army.
---

Agamemnon ends his speech with the verse with which he ended the test of the army in Book 2: “for we shall never now take wide-streeted Troy” (9.28 = 2.141). In Book 2 the words were meant to be refused, and the army ran to the ships; now they are meant, and the army sits silent until Diomedes speaks (9.29–31; [[the-test-of-the-army]], [[good-at-the-war-cry]]).
