summary: Telamon is named in Book 9 only in the titles of his son Ajax, “the godlike son of Telamon” (9.623) and “Telamonian Ajax, sprung from Zeus, lord of the people” (9.644).
grc: [Τελαμωνιάδης]
relations:
  - child: ajax (9.623, 9.644)
---

The patronymic in the nominative, ἀντίθεος Τελαμωνιάδης, “the godlike son of Telamon”, brings Ajax in to speak at the end of the embassy (9.622–623; see [[antitheos]]). Achilles answers him with Hector's verse of address, Αἶαν διογενὲς Τελαμώνιε κοίρανε λαῶν (9.644 = 7.234; see [[telamonian]] and [[ajax]]).
