---
summary: Odysseus fears for “the well-benched ships,” and Achilles threatens to drag his own “well-benched, curving ships” to the sea at dawn.
---

“It is in doubt whether we save or lose the well-benched ships, unless you put on your courage” (9.230–231). The ships of the Achaeans are what the embassy asks Achilles to save, and the same epithet describes the ships on which he threatens to leave: Odysseus reports that he “will drag his well-benched, curving ships to the sea” when dawn appears (9.682–683; [[curving]]).
