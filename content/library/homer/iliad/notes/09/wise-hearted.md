---
summary: Achilles will not think of war “until the son of wise-hearted Priam, brilliant Hector,” reaches the Myrmidons' ships.
---

“For I will not think of bloody war until the son of wise-hearted Priam, brilliant Hector, comes to the huts and ships of the Myrmidons, killing the Argives, and burns the ships with fire” (9.650–653; [[bloody]], [[brilliant]]). Achilles names Hector with his father's epithet at the moment he sets the condition of his return. It is the third of his answers, after sailing at dawn and deciding at dawn ([[the-embassy-to-achilles]]). See [[hector]] and [[priam]].
