---
summary: Achilles weighs his life against all the treasure of Apollo “in rocky Pytho.”
grc: [πετρηέσσῃ]
---

“Nor all that is shut within the stone threshold of the Archer, Phoebus Apollo, in rocky Pytho” (9.404–405; [[aphetor]], [[pytho]]). Pytho was rocky in the Catalogue too (2.519); here the epithet describes the place of the richest treasury in Greece, the offerings stored in Apollo's house at Delphi. A verse of the Homeric Hymn to Apollo ends in the same words, Πυθοῖ ἔνι πετρηέσσῃ (*Hymn* 3.390). See [[apollo]].
