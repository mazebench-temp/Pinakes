---
summary: Agamemnon measures his gift of twelve horses by “the prizes these single-hoofed horses have carried off for me.”
grc: [μώνυχες]
---

“No man would be landless who had as much, nor lacking in precious gold, as the prizes these single-hoofed horses have carried off for me” (9.125–127; [[precious]], [[prize-winning]]). The commonest epithet of horses here takes the nominative plural, μώνυχες ἵπποι. Odysseus, repeating the offer, changes the verse to “as the prizes Agamemnon's horses have won with their feet” (9.269), and the epithet drops out. See [[horses]] and [[the-gifts-of-agamemnon]].
