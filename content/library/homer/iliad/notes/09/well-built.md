---
summary: The seven women of Lesbos were taken when Achilles himself took “well-built Lesbos.”
---

Agamemnon's gift of seven women comes from Achilles' own conquest: “women of Lesbos, whom, when he himself took well-built Lesbos, I chose out for myself” (9.129–130; Odysseus' repetition, 9.271–272). The town that Achilles sacked supplies the gift with which Agamemnon would pay him. Achilles brought from Lesbos the woman who sleeps beside him at the end of the night, Diomede (9.664–665; [[lesbos]], [[diomede]], [[achilles-raids]]).
