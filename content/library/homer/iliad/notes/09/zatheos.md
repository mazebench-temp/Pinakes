summary: Pherae, one of the seven towns that Agamemnon offers Achilles, is “holy Pherae.”
grc: [ζαθέας]
---

“And holy Pherae and Antheia of the deep meadows” (9.151 = 9.293; [[pherae-in-messenia]], [[of-the-deep-meadows]], [[the-seven-cities]]). Pherae is the town of Diocles, whose sons Orsilochus and Crethon fell in Book 5 (5.541–549). The poem does not say what made it holy; the epithet usually marks a place sacred to a god, as Cilla and Crisa are in Books 1 and 2. Of the seven towns, Pherae alone appears again in Homer: Telemachus and Peisistratus spend the night there on the road from Pylos to Sparta (Od. 3.488). See [[hieros]].
