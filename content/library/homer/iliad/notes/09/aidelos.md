---
summary: Phoenix asks Achilles whether he has no wish at all to ward off “the destroying fire” from the swift ships.
---

“If it is homecoming, shining Achilles, that you are turning over in your mind, and you have no wish at all to ward off from the swift ships the destroying fire, since anger has fallen upon your heart” (9.434–436; [[phaidimos]], [[swift-ships]], [[nostos]]). The fire that leaves nothing behind is the fire Hector threatens (9.241–243; [[ravening]]). Phoenix ends his speech on the same danger: it would be worse to defend the ships when they are already burning (9.601–602). See [[phoenix]].
