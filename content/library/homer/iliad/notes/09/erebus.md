---
summary: Erebus is named once in Book 9, in Phoenix's story of Meleager, where the Erinys hears Althaea's curse on her son “out of Erebus” (9.572).
grc: [Ἐρέβεσφιν]
---

Ἐρέβεσφιν keeps the old ending -φι(ν), which serves here for the genitive after ἐξ: “out of Erebus”, as Heracles was sent to bring the hound of Hades “from Erebus” (ἐξ Ἐρέβευς, 8.368). The Erinys who hears the curse dwells in the darkness below, “she of the implacable heart” (9.571–572; see [[erinyes]], [[curses]] and [[the-death-of-meleager]]). Earlier in the same speech Phoenix breaks out of his father's house on the tenth night, a night ἐρεβεννή, “murky”, dark as Erebus (9.474; see [[murky]] and [[the-flight-of-phoenix]]).
