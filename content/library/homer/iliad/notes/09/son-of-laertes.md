---
summary: Achilles and Ajax both address Odysseus with the full formal verse, “Son of Laertes, sprung from Zeus, Odysseus of many devices”: Achilles to begin his great refusal, Ajax to tell Odysseus that it is time to go.
---

Achilles' answer to the embassy begins with the verse (9.308), and the warning that follows is against the man “who hides one thing in his mind and says another” (9.313). Some readers have heard πολυμήχανος, “of many devices”, answered in that warning (see [[of-many-devices]] and [[truth-and-deception]]). Ajax uses the same verse to turn from Achilles to Odysseus: “let us go” (9.624–625). Diomedes used it in Book 8 to call Odysseus back from flight (8.93). See [[odysseus]], [[laertes]] and [[sprung-from-zeus]].
