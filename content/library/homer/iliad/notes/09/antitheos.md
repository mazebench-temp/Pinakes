“And Ajax, the godlike son of Telamon, spoke among them” (9.622–623; [[telamonian]]). The honorific of heroes, “a match for the gods,” is given to Ajax as he begins the last speech of the embassy, the shortest and the plainest. Earlier books gave it to heroes of the past and to warriors of both sides, often as they fell (1.264, 5.705, 8.275); here, for the first time, it is given to Ajax ([[ajax]]).
