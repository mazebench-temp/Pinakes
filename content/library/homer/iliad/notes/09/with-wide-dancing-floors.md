summary: Phoenix fled from his father's house “through Hellas with its wide dancing floors” to Phthia.

---

“Then I fled far away through Hellas with its wide dancing floors, and came to deep-soiled Phthia, mother of flocks, to lord Peleus” (9.478–480; [[hellas]], [[deep-soiled]], [[mother-of-flocks]]). Hellas has its two epithets in Phoenix's speech: “Hellas of the lovely women” as the country he left (9.447; [[of-the-lovely-women]]) and “Hellas with its wide dancing floors” as the land he crossed. In the Iliad Hellas is a district beside Phthia, and Book 10 puts the house of Amyntor at Eleon in Boeotia (10.266–267); how Phoenix's flight is to be mapped was argued in antiquity ([[hellas-or-ormenium]], [[the-flight-of-phoenix]]).
