---
summary: The ships of the Achaeans bring wine to Agamemnon “day by day from Thrace over the wide sea.”
---

Nestor reminds Agamemnon that he can afford to feast the elders: “Your huts are full of wine, which the ships of the Achaeans bring you day by day from Thrace over the wide sea” (9.71–72; [[thrace]], [[wine]], [[feast]]). The phrase ἐπʼ εὐρέα πόντον is the variant with πόντος of the entry's formula. The sea that the army has crossed as a road home in Agamemnon's speech (9.27) is here a road of supply.
