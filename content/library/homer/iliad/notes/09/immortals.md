---
summary: Nestor calls Achilles “the best of men, whom the immortals themselves honored”; in the verses Aristarchus removed, “one of the immortals” stops Phoenix from killing his father.
---

Nestor reproaches Agamemnon for dishonoring “the best of men, whom the immortals themselves honored” (9.110–111; [[time]]). The honor the gods give and the dishonor a king inflicts stand in the same verse. In the four verses known only from Plutarch, Phoenix says that he planned to kill his father, “but one of the immortals checked my anger,” setting before him the talk of the people (9.458–461; [[phoenix-patricide-lines]], [[the-flight-of-phoenix]]). The god is not named.
