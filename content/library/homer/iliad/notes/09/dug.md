---
summary: Nestor sends the sentries to spend the night “beside the ditch they have dug, outside the wall.”
---

“And let the sentries, each of them, spend the night beside the ditch they have dug, outside the wall” (9.66–67; [[night-watch]]). The ditch that Hector boasted his horses would leap (8.179) is now the Achaeans' front line, and the seven captains with their hundred men each sit “midway between the ditch and the wall” (9.85–88). Achilles mocks the work: Agamemnon has built a wall and driven a ditch, “wide and great,” with stakes in it, and still cannot hold back Hector (9.348–352; [[the-achaean-wall]]).
