---
summary: Thrasymedes, son of Nestor, “shepherd of the people,” is the first named of the seven captains of the watch.
---

The sentries hurry out “around Thrasymedes, son of Nestor, shepherd of the people” (9.81; [[thrasymedes]], [[night-watch]]). The kings' title of care for their men goes to the young son of the old counselor, at the head of the list of seven captains who guard the camp through the night. See [[nestor]].
