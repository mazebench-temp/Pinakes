---
summary: Diomedes answers Agamemnon's proposal to flee: “the other long-haired Achaeans will stay until we sack Troy.”
---

“But the other long-haired Achaeans will stay until we sack Troy. And if they too wish, let them flee with their ships to their dear native land” (9.45–47; [[dear-native-land]]). Diomedes gives the army its formula in the act of speaking for it: the long-haired Achaeans will stay. Then he goes further, and says that he and Sthenelus alone will fight on (9.48–49).
