---
summary: Agamemnon recalls Zeus' promise that he would “sack well-walled Ilios and then sail home,” in the verse with which he tested the army in Book 2.
---

“Hard god, who once promised me and nodded his assent that I would sack well-walled Ilios and then sail home” (9.19–20). The verse repeats 2.113, which Agamemnon spoke to test the army; now he proposes flight in earnest ([[hard]], [[the-test-of-the-army]]). The walls that the epithet praises are the walls the army cannot take. See [[troy]].
