---
summary: Agamemnon sends the clear-voiced heralds to summon every man to the assembly by name, but not to shout.
---

The epithet of the heralds' carrying voice stands beside an order to keep it down. Agamemnon goes to and fro, “ordering the clear-voiced heralds to summon every man by name to the assembly, but not to shout” (9.10–12; [[heralds]], [[assembly-type-scene]]). The same phrase called the army with a shout in Book 2, first to the assembly and then to war (2.50–52, 2.442–444). Now the army is beaten and the Trojans are camped near the ships, and the assembly is called man by man. The poem does not say why.
