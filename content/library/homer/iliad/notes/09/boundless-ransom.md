---
summary: Agamemnon offers Achilles “a boundless ransom,” the formula of the ransom for a captive used of his amends.
---

“But since I was blinded, obeying my wretched mind, I am willing to make amends and give a boundless ransom” (9.119–120). The verse returns when Agamemnon pays in Book 19 (19.138). ἄποινα is the price paid to free a captive or recover a body; here it is the price of an insult. Achilles is not a captive and the gifts do not buy him. Achilles refuses it: “His gifts are hateful to me, and I honor him at a hair's worth” (9.378; [[apoina]], [[the-gifts-of-agamemnon]]).
