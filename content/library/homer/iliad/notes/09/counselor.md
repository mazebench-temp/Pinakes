---
summary: Achilles dismisses Agamemnon: “for Zeus the counselor has taken away his wits.”
---

“Let him go to his ruin at his ease; for Zeus the counselor has taken away his wits” (9.376–377; [[ate]], [[phrenes]]). The epithet of the god whose plans are fulfilled stands in the verse that explains Agamemnon's folly as the god's doing. Agamemnon himself said as much at the start of the night (9.18) and will say it again in Book 19 (19.86–90). Achilles uses the explanation as a dismissal, not an excuse. See [[zeus]].
