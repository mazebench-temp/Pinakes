---
summary: Moved at last by his wife's plea, Meleager goes out and puts on “his gleaming armor.”
---

“And his heart was stirred as he heard of these evil deeds, and he went, and put on his gleaming armor” (9.595–596; [[meleager]], [[cleopatra]]). The arming of Meleager is told in one verse, the verse in which Ares arms to avenge his son in Book 15 (15.120). The armor that Thetis promises Achilles is “gleaming” too (18.144). See [[the-wrath-of-meleager]].
