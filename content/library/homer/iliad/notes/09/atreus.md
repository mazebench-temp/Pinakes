---
summary: Atreus is named only in his sons' patronymic: twenty lines call Agamemnon “son of Atreus”, and one, in the plural, names both sons (9.341).
---

“Most glorious son of Atreus, Agamemnon, lord of men” is the address that opens four speeches to the king, two of Nestor's, Odysseus' report and Diomedes' last word (9.96, 9.163, 9.677, 9.697; see [[son-of-atreus]] and [[lord-of-men]]). In Achilles' mouth the patronymic is the name of the man who wronged him: “Agamemnon, son of Atreus” will not persuade him (9.315), the son of Atreus stayed by the ships and kept most of the spoil (9.331–333), and treated him “as if I were some dishonored vagrant” (9.647–648). Once Achilles uses the plural: “Are they the only ones of mortal men who love their wives, the sons of Atreus?” (9.340–341; see [[atreidae]]). See [[agamemnon]].
