---
summary: Artemis, angry at Oeneus, is “the divine child, she of the showering arrows,” as she sends the boar of Calydon.
---

“And in her anger the divine child, she of the showering arrows, roused against him a wild boar, a lurker in the grass, with white tusks” (9.538–539; [[brilliant]], [[lurker-in-the-grass]], [[white-tusked]]). The archer goddess does not shoot here; she sends a beast. Her epithet is the one that Andromache used when she told how Artemis struck down her mother in Eetion's halls (6.428).
