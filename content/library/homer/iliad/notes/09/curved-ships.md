---
summary: Achilles tells Phoenix that the portion of Zeus “will keep me beside the curved ships” as long as he lives.
---

“I think I am honored by the portion of Zeus, which will keep me beside the curved ships, as long as breath stays in my breast and my knees have strength to move” (9.608–610; [[aisa]], [[time]]). The ships beside which he sits are the ships on which, a few hundred verses earlier, he said he would sail home (9.356–363). The honor of Zeus keeps him there. See [[ships]] and [[achilles]].
