---
summary: Odysseus addresses Achilles as “My friend” as he turns from the danger of the army to the advice of Peleus.
en: [My friend]
---

“My friend, surely your father Peleus charged you on that day when he sent you from Phthia to Agamemnon” (9.252–253; [[peleus]]). Odysseus uses the endearment of comrades at the turn of his speech, from the danger to the army to the father's advice: hold back your proud heart, for kindliness is better (9.254–258; [[great-hearted]], [[evil-scheming]]). The translation renders πέπον here “My friend.” See [[odysseus]].
