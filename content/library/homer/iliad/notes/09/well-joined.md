---
summary: Oeneus, begging his son to fight, shakes “the jointed doors” of Meleager's chamber.
grc: [κολλητὰς]
en: [jointed]
---

“Standing on the threshold of the high-roofed chamber, shaking the jointed doors, imploring his son” (9.582–583; [[high-roofed]], [[oeneus]]). κολλητὰς σανίδας are door-planks glued and fitted together, a joiner's work like the bodies of chariots (4.366). The translation renders the word here “jointed.” The old king stands outside his son's closed door and shakes it, and the door does not open ([[closely-fitted]], [[the-wrath-of-meleager]]).
