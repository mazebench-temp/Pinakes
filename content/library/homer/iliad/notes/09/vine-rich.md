---
summary: Pedasus, the last of the seven towns that Agamemnon offers Achilles, is “vine-rich.”
---

“And fair Aepeia and vine-rich Pedasus” (9.152 = 9.294) ends the list of the seven towns ([[pedasus-in-messenia]], [[aepeia]], [[the-seven-cities]]). This Pedasus lies near the sea on the borders of Pylos (9.153), and is not the Trojan town of Elatus on the Satnioeis ([[pedasus-on-the-satnioeis]]). With [[grassy]] Hire and Antheia [[of-the-deep-meadows]], the vines complete a picture of good country.
