---
summary: Ialmenus is named with his brother Ascalaphus among the seven captains of the watch (9.82), the only time he appears in the Iliad after the Catalogue.
grc: [Ἰάλμενον]
---

“And around Ascalaphus and Ialmenus, sons of Ares” (9.82; see [[ascalaphus]], [[ares]] and [[night-watch]]). The brothers lead the men of Aspledon and Minyan Orchomenus in the Catalogue (2.511–516), and here they are two of the seven captains who take the sentries out between the ditch and the wall. Orchomenus, their city, is named again in this book, as a byword for wealth (9.381; see [[orchomenus]]).
