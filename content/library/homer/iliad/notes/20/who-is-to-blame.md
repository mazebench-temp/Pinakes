---
summary: Poseidon pleads for Aeneas as a man “who is not to blame”, who suffers “for nothing, for the sake of others' griefs” (20.297–298).
refs: [20.297-299]
---

The blame for the war lies elsewhere, and Poseidon does not say where; Walter Leaf reports a scholion that Priam's suspicion of Aeneas came from his having no stake of his own in the war (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 20.298; see [[aitios]] and [[achos]]).
