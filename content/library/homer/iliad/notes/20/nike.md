---
summary: Aeneas says that if a god were to stretch the outcome of war evenly, Achilles “would not very easily / win” (20.101–102). Polydorus “surpassed them all in speed of foot” (20.410).
---

The outcome of war is pictured as a rope pulled from both ends (see [[the-rope-of-war]] and [[the-bounds-of-victory]]). Polydorus excels in speed of foot, and his speed carries him to his death (20.410–412; see [[arete]]).
