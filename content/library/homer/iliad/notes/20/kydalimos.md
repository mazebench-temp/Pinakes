---
summary: Athena turns Hector's spear back “from glorious Achilles with a breath, / blowing very gently” (20.439–440).
---

Walter Leaf sets the gentle breath beside Apollo's rescue of Hector a few verses later, “very easily, as a god can” (20.444): a breath from a goddess is enough to turn a spear (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 20.439). See [[athena]] and [[divine-rescue]].
