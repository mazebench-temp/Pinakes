---
summary: Achilles taunts Aeneas with the hope “that you will rule over the horse-taming Trojans / with the honor of Priam” (20.180–181).
---

Aristarchus athetized 180–186 as mean and unworthy of Achilles (see the translation's note on 20.180–186). Poseidon later prophesies that Aeneas will indeed rule the Trojans (20.307–308; [[might-of-aeneas]]). See [[trojans]] and [[aeneas]].
