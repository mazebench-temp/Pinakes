---
summary: When the assembly ends, “the son of Cronus ... roused relentless war” (20.31).
---

“So spoke the son of Cronus, and roused relentless war” (20.31; [[son-of-cronus]]). Zeus has just let the gods help whichever side they wish, and the war he rouses is theirs as well as men's (20.23–25; [[the-battle-of-the-gods]]). See [[polemos]].
