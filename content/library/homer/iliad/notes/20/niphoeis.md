---
summary: Iphition was born “beneath snowy Tmolus, in the rich land of Hyde” (20.385), the only place in Homer where Tmolus is called snowy.
---

The Trojan Catalogue names the Maeonians “who were born beneath Tmolus” (2.866), and the translation's note on 20.381–395 reports that some added this verse there (Strabo, *Geography* 13.4.6; see [[hyle-or-hyde]]). See [[tmolus]], [[hyde]], [[rich-land]] and [[iphition]].
