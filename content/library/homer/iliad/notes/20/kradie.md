---
summary: In the simile of the lion struck by a young man's spear, “his valiant heart groans within him” (ἐν δέ τέ οἱ κραδίῃ στένει ἄλκιμον ἦτορ, 20.169).
---

The ἦτορ groans within the κραδίη: the heart as the seat of feeling sits within the heart that beats (see [[etor]] and [[wounded-lion]]).
