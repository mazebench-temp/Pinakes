---
summary: Achilles stabs Demoleon in the temple “through the bronze-cheeked helmet” (20.397), in verses that nearly repeat Polypoetes' killing of Damasus (20.397–400 ~ 12.183–186).
---

“he stabbed in the temple, through the bronze-cheeked helmet. / Nor did the bronze helmet hold it, but right through it / the point, pressing on, broke the bone, and the brain / inside was all spattered” (20.397–400). Walter Leaf notes the borrowing (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 20.397), and the next two verses come from Books 11 and 5 (20.401 ~ 11.423, 20.402 = 5.56; on 20.401). See [[demoleon]], [[helmet]] and [[androktasia]].
