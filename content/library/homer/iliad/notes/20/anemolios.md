---
summary: Hera would have Achilles learn “that the best of the immortals love him, / and that those are empty wind who before now / have warded off the war and the combat from the Trojans” (20.122–124).
grc: [ἀνεμώλιοι]
---

Elsewhere the word is said of words and weapons that come to nothing; here Hera says it of gods, the gods who have helped the Trojans. Walter Leaf, who takes Hera's audience to be Poseidon and Athena alone (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 20.114), notes that the lines would be intolerably rude and provocative if the gods of the other side were present (on 20.125; see [[hera]] and [[the-battle-of-the-gods]]).
