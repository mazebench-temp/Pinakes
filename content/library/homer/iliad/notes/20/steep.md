---
summary: Poseidon shakes the boundless earth “and the steep peaks of the mountains” (20.58).
---

ὀρέων αἰπεινὰ κάρηνα, “the steep peaks of the mountains,” is said of the mountains where the nymphs dwell in the Odyssey (*Odyssey* 6.123), and αἰπεινὰ κάρηνα of Mycale in the Trojan Catalogue (2.869). See [[earth-shaker]] and [[boundless-sea]].
