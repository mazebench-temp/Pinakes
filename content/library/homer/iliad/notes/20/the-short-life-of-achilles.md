---
summary: Poseidon tells Aeneas to keep away from Achilles until “Achilles has met his death and his fate” (20.337), and then to fight among the foremost, since no other Achaean will kill him (20.338–339).
refs: [20.336-339]
---

The god speaks of Achilles' death as certain, as Thetis did when he chose to kill Hector, “for straight after Hector your own fate is ready” (18.96; see [[prophecy]] and [[moira]]).
