---
summary: Achilles taunts Aeneas with the honors he might hope for: to rule the Trojans “with the honor of Priam” (20.181), or a domain marked out by the Trojans, “a fair one, of orchard and plowland, for you to hold” (20.185). Achilles calls Patroclus “my honored companion” (20.426).
refs: [20.180-186, 20.426]
---

The domain is the honor the Lycians gave Bellerophon, in the same words (6.194–195), and the one Sarpedon says he and Glaucus hold by the Xanthus (12.313–314; see [[time]] and [[kingship-and-authority]]).
