---
summary: “Brilliant” is Achilles as he meets Aeneas (20.160, 20.177), as he kills Iphition and Polydorus (20.386, 20.388, 20.413), and as he springs at the mist (20.445); Hector in Aeneas' genealogy and when Athena turns his spear back to him (20.240, 20.428, 20.440); and the Achaeans in Achilles' call (20.354).
---

Three of Achilles' verses have the longer formula ποδάρκης δῖος Ἀχιλλεύς (20.177, 20.413, 20.445; [[podarkes]]). Aeneas ends his account of the house of Dardanus with the two cousins side by side: “and Anchises fathered me, and Priam brilliant Hector” (20.240; [[genealogy-in-battle]]). See [[achilles]] and [[hector]].
