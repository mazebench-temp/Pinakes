Aeneas' genealogy measures his ancestors against all men: the richest, the most beautiful, the one the gods took for his beauty (20.219–235; [[erichthonius]], [[ganymede]], [[genealogy-in-battle]]). Hades' dwellings would be laid bare “to mortals and immortals” (20.64; [[immortals]]). See [[meropes]] and [[glorious]].
