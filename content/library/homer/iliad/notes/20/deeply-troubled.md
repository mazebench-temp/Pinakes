---
summary: Achilles, seeing his spear on the ground and no man, speaks “deeply troubled” to his own heart (20.343).
---

The verse is the one with which Odysseus, left alone among the Trojans, spoke to his heart (11.403; Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 20.343). What troubles Achilles is a wonder, not a danger: Aeneas was dear to the gods after all (20.344–348; [[o-popoi]]). See [[achilles]].
