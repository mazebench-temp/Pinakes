---
summary: Achilles tells Aeneas that killing him will not make him king: “Priam will not put the prize in your hand for that; / for he has sons” (20.182–183).
---

The prize is the rule itself, which Priam would not hand on while he has sons of his own (see [[kingship-and-authority]] and [[time]]). Aristarchus athetized 20.180–186 as mean in style and thought and unworthy of Achilles (Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 20.180; see the translation's note on 20.180–186).
