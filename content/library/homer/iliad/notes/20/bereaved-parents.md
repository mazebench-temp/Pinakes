summary: Aeneas tells Achilles that one pair of parents “will weep for their dear son / today” (20.210–211). Priam would not let his youngest son fight, “because he was the youngest born among his children, / and the dearest to him” (20.409–410), and Achilles kills Polydorus as he shows off his speed (20.407–418).
refs: [20.210-211, 20.407-418]
---

Priam's care for his youngest son fails: the boy disobeys, and dies in front of his brother Hector (20.419–423; see [[polydorus-son-of-priam]], [[priam]] and [[fathers-and-sons]]).
