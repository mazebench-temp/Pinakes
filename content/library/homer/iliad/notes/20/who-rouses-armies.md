---
summary: Strife, “who rouses armies,” rises when the gods come down (20.48), and Apollo, “who rouses armies,” rouses Aeneas against Achilles (20.79).
---

“But Apollo, who rouses armies, roused Aeneas straight / to face the son of Peleus” (20.79–80): λαοσσόος and ὦρσεν stand side by side, and the god's epithet is what he does. Earlier Strife rose up with the same epithet, ὦρτο δʼ Ἔρις κρατερὴ λαοσσόος (20.48; [[strife]], [[krateros]]). See [[apollo]] and [[aeneas]].
