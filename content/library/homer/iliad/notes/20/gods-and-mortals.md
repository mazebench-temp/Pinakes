---
summary: Zeus says of the mortals, “I care for them, even as they perish” (20.21); the Achaeans exult “as long as the gods were apart from mortal men” (20.41); Aidoneus fears that his dwellings will “be laid bare to mortals and immortals” (20.64). Achilles fears a spear, not knowing that the gods' gifts “are not easy / for mortal men to master” (20.265–266), and he says that not even Ares or Athena could deal with so great a battle alone (20.358–359).
refs: [20.21, 20.41, 20.64-65, 20.265-266, 20.356-359]
---

Hector puts the difference in one line: he could fight the immortals in words, “but with the spear it is hard, since they are far stronger than we are” (20.368; see [[gods-are-hard-to-face]], [[immortals]] and [[mortal-men]]).
