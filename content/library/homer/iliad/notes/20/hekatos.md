---
summary: Artemis is “sister of the Far-Shooter” as she stands against Hera (20.71), and Poseidon says that Aeneas obeyed “the words of Apollo the Far-Shooter” (20.295).
---

These are the last two of the four uses in the Iliad, each in the genitive at the end of the verse (1.385, 7.83). Poseidon's verse blames Apollo for sending Aeneas against Achilles and then leaving him to die (20.293–296; [[grim]]). See [[apollo]].
