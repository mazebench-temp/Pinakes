---
summary: Achilles reminds Aeneas that he sacked Lyrnessus “and took away the day of freedom from the women, and led them off / as captives” (20.193–194).
refs: [20.191-194]
---

The verse is Hector's of what Patroclus meant to do at Troy, with “captive” for “Trojan” (16.831; see [[eleutheros]], [[captive-women]] and [[the-sack-of-lyrnessus]]).
