---
summary: Poseidon saves Aeneas “so that the lineage may not perish without seed and without a trace, / the lineage of Dardanus” (ἄσπερμος … καὶ ἄφαντος, 20.303–304).
grc: ["ἄσπερμος γενεὴ καὶ ἄφαντος"]
en: [without seed and without a trace]
---

ἄσπερμος, “without seed,” is found only here in Homer (see the translation's table of key words). The two privatives stand on either side of γενεή, the lineage that Poseidon means to save (see [[genee]], [[dardanus]] and [[might-of-aeneas]]).
