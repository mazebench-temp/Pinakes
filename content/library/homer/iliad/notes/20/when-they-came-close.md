---
summary: The verse brings Achilles and Aeneas face to face, and Achilles speaks first (20.176–177).
---

“And when they had come close, advancing against each other, / brilliant swift-footed Achilles spoke to him first” (20.176–177). The same two verses bring Achilles and Asteropaeus together in Book 21, and there too a genealogy follows (21.148–149). In Book 6 the first speaker was Diomedes, and his question led to Glaucus' genealogy and a friendship (6.121–122; see the Book 6 note). Here the speeches lead to the duel (20.178–258; [[genealogy-in-battle]], [[achilles-and-aeneas]]).
