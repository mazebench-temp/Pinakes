---
summary: When Aeneas is gone, Achilles says that “He will have no heart to try me again” (20.349), and turns to the rest: “I will go against the other Trojans and make trial of them” (20.352).
grc: [πειρηθῆναι]
en: [try, make trial]
---

To try a man is to fight him; Achilles' trial of the Trojans is the killing that follows (20.381–503).
