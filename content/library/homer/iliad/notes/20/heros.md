---
summary: Apollo addresses Aeneas as “hero” when he bids him pray (20.104), and Poseidon sends him flying “over many ranks of heroes, and many ranks of horses” (20.326).
---

As a form of address ἥρως is a courtesy among fighting men, as Patroclus speaks to the wounded Eurypylus (11.819, 11.838); here a god uses it to a mortal.
