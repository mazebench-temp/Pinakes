---
summary: Aeneas compares a quarrel of words to women “who in their anger over heart-devouring strife / go out into the middle of the street and quarrel with each other” (20.253–254).
---

Walter Leaf finds the phrase “almost like a travesty” of the place where Hector used it of his duel with Ajax (7.301), and reports Aristarchus' athetesis of 251–255 (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 20.251; see the translation's note on 20.251–255). See [[eris]].
