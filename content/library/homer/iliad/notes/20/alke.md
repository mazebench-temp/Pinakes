---
summary: Aeneas ends his speech to Achilles, “You will not turn me from courage with words, eager as I am” (20.256), and Achilles leaps among the Trojans “clothed in courage in his heart” (20.381).
---

φρεσὶν εἱμένος ἀλκήν (20.381) is the garment of courage that the two Ajaxes wore when nine men rose to face Hector, “clothed in furious courage” (θοῦριν ἐπιειμένοι ἀλκήν, 7.164). In it Achilles begins the killing that fills the rest of the book (see [[the-aristeia]]).
