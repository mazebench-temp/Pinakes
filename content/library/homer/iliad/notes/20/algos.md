---
summary: Poseidon asks the gods “why does this man, who is not to blame, suffer sorrows now / for nothing, for the sake of others' griefs” (20.297–298), when Aeneas always gives them pleasing gifts.
---

The sorrows are the war's, which Aeneas did not cause; the god's argument rests on the gifts (20.298–299; see [[reciprocity-with-the-gods]], [[achos]] and [[who-is-to-blame]]).
