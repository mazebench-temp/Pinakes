---
summary: Clytius is named among the sons of Laomedon in Aeneas' genealogy, in the verse that named him among the elders on the wall (20.237–238 = 3.147).
---

“and Laomedon fathered Tithonus and Priam, / Lampus and Clytius and Hicetaon, offshoot of Ares” (20.237–238; see [[laomedon]], [[lampus]] and [[hicetaon]]). The second verse repeats the one that named the three brothers among the elders on the wall (3.147; see [[trojan-elders]]); here it makes them Priam's brothers. Walter Leaf notes that Book 15 carries the genealogy further, giving each of the three a son (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 20.238). Clytius' son was Caletor, Hector's cousin, whom Ajax killed as he carried fire to a ship (15.419–422; see [[caletor]]).
