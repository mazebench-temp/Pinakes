---
summary: The gods sit down in Zeus' house “in the polished colonnades, which for father Zeus / Hephaestus had made with his cunning skill” (20.11–12).
grc: [ξεστῇς]
---

The colonnades are αἴθουσαι, not benches (Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 20.11). Priam's palace has the same polished colonnades (ξεστῇς αἰθούσῃσι, 6.243; see the Book 6 note), and the verse that follows echoes the first book, where Hephaestus built each god's house “with his cunning skill” (1.607–608; [[with-cunning-skill]]). The Odyssey has ξεστῇς of polished oars (*Odyssey* 12.172). See [[hephaestus]] and [[father-zeus]].
