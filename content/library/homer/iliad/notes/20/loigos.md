---
summary: Aeneas says that no man can fight Achilles face to face, “for always at his side is one of the gods, who keeps ruin away” (20.98).
---

The line is Diomedes' of Hector in Book 5, when Ares stood beside him (5.603); Aeneas says it of Achilles, and later in the book Athena turns Hector's spear back from Achilles with a breath (20.438–440; see [[epitarrhothos]] and [[divine-intervention]]).
