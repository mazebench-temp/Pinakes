summary: Poseidon says that Aeneas will die “because he obeyed the words of Apollo the Far-Shooter” (20.295), and Tros, son of Alastor, begs Achilles for his life, “the fool, and he did not know that the other would not be persuaded” (20.466).
grc: [πειθόμενος]
---

20.466 is the verse in which Nestor tells how Agamemnon stayed to win Athena over with sacrifices, not knowing that she would not be persuaded, “for the mind of the gods who are forever is not quickly turned” (*Odyssey* 3.146–147; see [[even-the-gods-can-be-turned]]). Here a man begs another man, and the man will not be turned (see [[supplication]] and [[obedience-and-persuasion]]).
