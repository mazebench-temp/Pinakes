---
summary: Achilles fears that Aeneas' long-shadowed spear will pass through his shield (20.262–263), and then throws his own: “Then second in his turn Achilles let fly his long-shadowed spear” (20.273).
---

The second verse is the one in which Ajax answers Hector's cast in their duel, “Then second in his turn / Ajax, sprung from Zeus, let fly his long-shadowed spear” (7.248–249; see the translation's table of formulas). See [[spear]] and [[achilles-and-aeneas]].
