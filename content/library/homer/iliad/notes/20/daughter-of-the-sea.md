---
summary: Aeneas names Achilles' mother “Thetis of the lovely tresses, daughter of the sea” (20.207), the word's only use in the Iliad.
---

Apollo has just given Thetis her older title, the daughter “of the Old Man of the Sea” (20.107; [[daughter-of-zeus]]). Aristarchus athetized 205–209 as needless (see the translation's note on 20.205–209). See [[thetis]].
