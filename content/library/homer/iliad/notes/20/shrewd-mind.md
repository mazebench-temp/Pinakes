---
summary: Hermes is the god “who excels in his shrewd mind” (20.35).
---

“and the great helper / Hermes, who excels in his shrewd mind” (20.34–35; [[great-helper]]). ἐπὶ … κέκασται is a verb in tmesis, “excels,” and ἐπί does not go with φρεσί (Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 20.35; see the translation's note on 20.35). See [[hermes]] and [[phrenes]].
