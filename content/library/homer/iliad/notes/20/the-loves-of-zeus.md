---
summary: Aeneas begins his lineage with Zeus: “first Zeus the cloud-gatherer fathered Dardanus” (20.215). Poseidon calls Dardanus the son “whom the son of Cronus loved above all the children / who were born to him from mortal women” (20.304–305).
refs: [20.215, 20.304-305]
---

The poem does not name Dardanus' mother. The love of Zeus for his son is Poseidon's reason for saving Aeneas, so that the lineage of Dardanus may not perish, though Zeus has come to hate the lineage of Priam (20.302–306; see [[dardanus]], [[genee]] and [[philos]]).
