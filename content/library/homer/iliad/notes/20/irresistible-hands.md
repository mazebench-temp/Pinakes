---
summary: The book ends: “And he was eager to win glory, / the son of Peleus, and his irresistible hands were spattered with gore” (20.502–503).
---

The last verse is the one that marked Agamemnon's rampage before his wounding, with Πηλεΐδης for Ἀτρεΐδης (11.169; Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 20.495). See [[achilles]] and [[the-aristeia]].
