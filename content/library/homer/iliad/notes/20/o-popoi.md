---
summary: Poseidon pities Aeneas, “Ah, truly I have grief for great-hearted Aeneas” (20.293), and Achilles wonders at his disappearance, “Ah, truly this is a great wonder that I see with my eyes” (20.344).
---

The second verse is the one with which Poseidon rebuked the young Achaeans when the Trojans had reached the ships (13.99; Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 20.343); Achilles will speak it again when Lycaon comes back from Lemnos (21.54). See [[poseidon]], [[achos]] and [[achilles]].
