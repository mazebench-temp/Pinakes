summary: Apollo, “son of Zeus,” speaks to Aeneas in Lycaon's likeness (20.82) and answers his reluctance (20.103).

“in his likeness Apollo, son of Zeus, spoke to him” (20.82). The verse introduces Apollo's speeches in disguise: as Asius to Hector (16.720) and as the herald Periphas to Aeneas himself (17.326). See [[apollo]] and [[lycaon-son-of-priam]].
