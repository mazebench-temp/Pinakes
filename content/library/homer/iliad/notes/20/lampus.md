---
summary: Lampus is named among the sons of Laomedon in Aeneas' genealogy, in the verse that named him among the elders on the wall (20.237–238 = 3.147).
---

Λάμπόν τε Κλυτίον θʼ Ἱκετάονά τʼ ὄζον Ἄρηος, “Lampus and Clytius and Hicetaon, offshoot of Ares” (20.238): the verse that named the three brothers among the elders at the Scaean gates (3.146–147) names them here as the sons of Laomedon, with Tithonus and Priam (20.237; see [[laomedon]], [[clytius]], [[hicetaon]] and [[trojan-elders]]). Walter Leaf thinks the verse may have been borrowed to systematize the genealogy of the house of Priam, as Book 15 gives each of the three a son (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 20.238). Lampus' son is Dolops, whom Menelaus killed at the ships, “the strongest son that Lampus, / son of Laomedon, fathered” (15.526–527; see [[dolops-son-of-lampus]]).
