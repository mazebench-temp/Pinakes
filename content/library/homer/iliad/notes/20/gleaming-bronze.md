---
summary: Aeneas goes through the champions “armored in gleaming bronze” (20.111), and Hera points him out to Poseidon and Athena in the same words (20.117).
---

“and he went through the champions, armored in gleaming bronze” (20.111) is a whole formulaic verse, used before of Hector, Aeneas, Menelaus and others going out to fight (4.495, 5.562, 5.681, 17.3, 17.87, 17.592). Hera takes up its second half at once, “Here is Aeneas, gone forth armored in gleaming bronze” (20.117; [[hera]]). See [[aeneas]].
