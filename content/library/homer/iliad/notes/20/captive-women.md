---
summary: Achilles reminds Aeneas of the sack of Lyrnessus: he “took away the day of freedom from the women, and led them off / as captives” (20.193–194).
refs: [20.191-194]
---

Briseis was taken in that sack, after much toil, when Achilles “sacked Lyrnessus and the walls of Thebe” (2.690–691); Achilles names the city to Aeneas, who escaped it, and not the woman (see [[briseis]], [[eleutheros]] and [[slavery-and-freedom]]).
