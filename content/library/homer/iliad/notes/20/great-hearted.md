---
summary: μεγαλήτωρ is Aeneas' epithet in the book (20.175, 20.263, 20.293, 20.323) and his father Anchises' (20.208); Achilles speaks to his own μεγαλήτορα θυμόν when Aeneas vanishes (20.343); and Achilles is μεγάθυμος as his horses trample the dead (20.498).
---

The epithet follows Aeneas through the duel: Achilles goes to meet him (20.175), fears his spear (20.263), Poseidon grieves for him (20.293) and pulls Achilles' spear out of his shield (20.323). See [[aeneas]], [[anchises]] and [[achilles]].
