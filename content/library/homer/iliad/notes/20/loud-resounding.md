---
summary: Athena shouts “on the loud-resounding shores” (20.50), the epithet's only use of the shore in Homer.
---

“standing now beside the ditch they had dug, outside the wall, / and now on the loud-resounding shores she shouted aloud” (20.49–50). Athena's shout goes from the ditch to the sea, and Ares answers it from the height of the city and from Callicolone (20.51–53; [[callicolone]], [[sharp]]). See [[athena]] and [[the-battle-of-the-gods]].
