summary: Poseidon would “lead him out from death” (20.300), and Aeneas “was glad to escape from death” (20.350); Hector, saved by Apollo, has escaped death again (20.449). Achilles tells Iphition, “here is your death” (20.390), Echeclus is seized “by purple death” (20.477), and Deucalion waits for Achilles “seeing death before him” (20.481). The book also measures mortals: Erichthonius was “the richest of mortal men”, Ganymede “the most beautiful of mortal men” (20.220, 20.233), and Zeus loved Dardanus above the children born to him “from mortal women” (20.305).
grc: [θανάτου, θνητάων]
