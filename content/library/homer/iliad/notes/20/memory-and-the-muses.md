---
summary: Aeneas tells Achilles that each knows the other's lineage by report: “We know each other's lineage, and we know each other's parents, / hearing the tales mortal men have told of old; / but by sight you have never yet seen mine, nor I yours” (20.203–205).
refs: [20.203-205, 20.213-214]
---

The contrast of hearing and seeing is the poet's before the Catalogue, “we hear only the report, and know nothing” (2.486; see [[we-hear-only-the-report]]). Aeneas adds that “many men know” his lineage (20.214), and tells it (see [[genee]] and [[epos]]).
