---
summary: “and quickly it is husked beneath the feet of the loud-bellowing bulls” (20.497), the simile for Achilles' horses trampling the dead.
---

λέπτʼ ἐγένοντο, “is husked,” is from λέπω, “peel” (Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 20.497; see the translation's note on 20.497). See [[bulls-on-the-threshing-floor]] and [[broad-browed]].
