---
summary: Achilles, “looking at him darkly,” speaks to Hector, who has come against him at the sight of Polydorus: “Come nearer, so that you may the sooner reach the bounds of destruction” (20.428–429).
---

The verse of threat is the one Diomedes spoke to Glaucus before they found that they were guest-friends (20.429 = 6.143; Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 20.429; see [[the-bounds-of-victory]]). Hector answers in the three verses with which Aeneas answered Achilles' taunt (20.431–433 = 20.200–202). See [[achilles]] and [[hector]].
