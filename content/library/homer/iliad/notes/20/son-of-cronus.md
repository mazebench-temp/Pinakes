---
summary: The son of Cronus rouses the war when the assembly ends (20.31); in Poseidon's speech he may be angered if Achilles kills Aeneas, he loved Dardanus above all his sons by mortal women, and he has come to hate the lineage of Priam (20.301–306).
---

“So spoke the son of Cronus, and roused relentless war” (20.31; [[relentless]]). In Poseidon's speech the patronymic comes three times in six verses, and it carries the argument: Aeneas must be saved lest the son of Cronus be angry, because the son of Cronus loved Dardanus, and because the son of Cronus has turned against the house of Priam (20.300–308; [[poseidon]], [[dardanus]], [[genee]], [[might-of-aeneas]]).
