---
summary: Where Polydorus' belt was clasped “the corselet met it in a double layer” (20.415), the verse of Pandarus' arrow (4.133); Achilles' javelin goes through to the navel (20.416).
---

What was double is discussed in the note on Book 4. The two verses return unchanged (20.414–415 = 4.132–133), but the wound does not: the arrow that went through belt, corselet and band in Book 4 only grazed Menelaus' skin (4.135–139), and the spear here goes straight through beside the navel (20.416; see [[belt]] and [[polydorus-son-of-priam]]).
