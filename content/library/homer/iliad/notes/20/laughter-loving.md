---
summary: Laughter-loving Aphrodite is the last of the gods who go over to the Trojans (20.40).
---

“and Leto and Xanthus and laughter-loving Aphrodite” (20.40). She is not paired with any god in the verses that follow (20.67–74); in Book 21 Athena strikes her down as she leads the wounded Ares from the battle (21.423–426). Apollo reminds Aeneas in this book that she is his mother and Zeus' daughter (20.105–107; [[daughter-of-zeus]]). See [[aphrodite]].
