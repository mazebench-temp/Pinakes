---
summary: Artemis of the showering arrows goes to the Trojans with her brother (20.39) and stands against Hera, “sister of the Far-Shooter” (20.70–71).
---

“against Hera stood she of the golden distaff, the clamorous / Artemis of the showering arrows, sister of the Far-Shooter” (20.70–71; [[of-the-golden-distaff]], [[clamorous]], [[hekatos]]). Two verses give her three epithets and name her brother. The meeting is fought out in Book 21, where Hera takes her bow from her shoulders and beats her about the ears with it (21.489–492). See [[artemis]].
