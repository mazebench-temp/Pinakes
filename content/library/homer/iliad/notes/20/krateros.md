---
summary: When the gods come down among men, “mighty Strife rose up, who rouses armies” (20.48).
---

“But when the Olympians had come into the throng of men, / mighty Strife rose up, who rouses armies, and Athena shouted” (20.47–48; [[strife]], [[who-rouses-armies]]). Aristarchus began the main clause at “and Athena shouted” (Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 20.48; see the translation's note on 20.48).
