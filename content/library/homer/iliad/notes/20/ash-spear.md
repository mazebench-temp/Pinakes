---
summary: Aeneas' spear goes through two of the shield's layers and is stopped in the gold: “and in that the ash spear was stopped” (20.272).
---

Aristotle quotes the half-verse as τῇ ῥʼ ἔσχετο χάλκεον ἔγχος, with “bronze” for the Iliad's “ash,” as a word that seems to signify a contradiction (*Poetics* 25, 1461a31–35; see the translation's note on 20.272 and [[the-bronze-spear-stopped]]). The verse is in the passage that Aristarchus athetized (20.269–272; [[crook-footed]]). See [[spear]] and [[the-shield-of-achilles]].
