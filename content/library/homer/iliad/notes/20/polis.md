---
summary: Ares shouts to the Trojans “from the topmost height of the city” (20.52), and Poseidon's earthquake shakes Ida, “and the city of the Trojans and the ships of the Achaeans” (20.60). In Aeneas' genealogy Dardanus founded Dardania, “since sacred Ilios was not yet / built in the plain, a city of mortal men” (20.216–217).
---

The city of the plain is the last stage of a history of settlement: first Dardania on the slopes of Ida, then Ilios below (20.216–218; see [[dardania]], [[plato-the-slopes-of-ida]] and [[troy]]).
