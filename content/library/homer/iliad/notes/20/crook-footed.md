---
summary: In verses that Aristarchus athetized, the shield's five layers were beaten out by “the crook-footed god” (20.269–272).
---

“since the crook-footed god had beaten out five layers, / two of bronze, and two of tin inside, / and one of gold, and in that the ash spear was stopped” (20.270–272). Aristarchus rejected 269–272 because the layers contradict the shield of Book 18 (18.481), and Walter Leaf calls the lines spurious (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 20.269; see the translation's note on 20.269–272). This is the second of the three places where the name stands for Hephaestus; the third is Hera's call to him against the river (21.331).
