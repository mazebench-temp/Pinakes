---
summary: Achilles' heart bids him “glut Ares, the warrior with the oxhide shield,” with Hector's blood (20.77–78).
---

“for it was with his blood above all that his heart bade him / glut Ares, the warrior with the oxhide shield” (20.77–78). The verse is Diomedes' threat to Pandarus and Aeneas in Book 5 (5.289; Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 20.78), and Achilles speaks it to Hector himself in Book 22 (22.267). See [[ares]], [[achilles]] and [[hector]].
