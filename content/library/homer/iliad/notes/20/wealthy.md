---
summary: Erichthonius “was the richest of mortal men” (20.220), with three thousand mares in the marshland.
grc: [ἀφνειότατος]
en: [richest]
---

The superlative stands only here in Homer. The next verses give the wealth in horses (20.221–222; [[the-mares-of-erichthonius]]), and Ganymede, two generations later, is “the most beautiful of mortal men” (20.233; [[mortal-men]]). See [[erichthonius]].
