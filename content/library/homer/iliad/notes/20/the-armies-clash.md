summary: The last half-verse of the armies' clash, “and the earth ran with blood” (4.451 = 8.65), ends the simile of the forest fire, said here of one man's killing: “chasing the men he was killing; and the black earth ran with blood” (20.494).
en: [ran with blood]
---

In Books 4 and 8 the half-verse ends the clash of two armies, with the cries of killers and killed; in Book 15 it ends the fight at the ships (15.715). Here there is one killer, and the blood is that of the men he chases (20.490–494; [[forest-fire]], [[black-earth]], [[like-a-god]]). See [[achilles]].
