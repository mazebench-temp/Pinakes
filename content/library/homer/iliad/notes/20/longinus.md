summary: On the Sublime calls the episode “the Theomachy”. It quotes Hades' fear at Poseidon's earthquake as an image of the whole world overturned, calls such passages impious unless they are read as allegory, and prefers Poseidon's journey in Book 13, in a version that takes in a verse of the same earthquake.
