---
summary: The narrator calls Achilles a fool for fearing Aeneas' spear: he did not consider “that the glorious gifts of the gods are not easy / for mortal men to master, nor do they give way” (20.265–266).
---

θεῶν ἐρικυδέα δῶρα is Paris' phrase in Book 3: “The glorious gifts of the gods are not to be cast aside” (3.65; [[gifts-of-the-gods]]). Here the gift is Achilles' shield, and the gold, “the gifts of the god,” holds the spear back (20.268; [[the-shield-of-achilles]], [[nepios]]).
