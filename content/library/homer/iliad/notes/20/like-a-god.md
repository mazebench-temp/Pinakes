---
summary: Achilles rushes on “like a god” at his fourth charge against the mist that hides Hector (20.447), and in the simile of the forest fire, “so he rushed everywhere with his spear, like a god” (20.493).
---

At 20.447 the verse is the fourth assault of [[three-times-and-a-fourth]], but no god turns him back. At 20.493 the formula leaves the pattern altogether and closes a simile: “As a wondrous fire rages through the deep valleys / of a dry mountain, ... so he rushed everywhere with his spear, like a god, / chasing the men he was killing” (20.490–494; [[forest-fire]], [[wondrous]]). See [[achilles]].
