---
summary: The book opens on the Achaeans arming “beside the curved ships” around Achilles (20.1–2), the arming with which Book 19 ended.
---

“So they were arming themselves beside the curved ships, / around you, son of Peleus, insatiable of battle, the Achaeans” (20.1–2). The verb takes up the end of Book 19, where the Achaeans arm throughout the army and Achilles arms in their midst (19.352, 19.364; see [[arming-scene]]). See [[ships]], [[apostrophe]] and [[insatiable]].
