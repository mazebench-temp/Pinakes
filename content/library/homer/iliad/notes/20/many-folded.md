---
summary: Zeus bids Themis call the gods to assembly “from the summit of many-folded Olympus” (20.4–5), and he himself will stay “in a fold of Olympus” to watch the war (20.22).
---

“And Zeus bade Themis call the gods to assembly / from the summit of many-folded Olympus” (20.4–5; [[themis-the-goddess]], [[olympus]]). κρατός, “head,” is used of a mountaintop only here (Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 20.5; see the translation's note on 20.5). The epithet's folds come back in Zeus' own words: “But I for my part will stay here in a fold of Olympus” (πτυχὶ Οὐλύμποιο, 20.22; [[watching-the-war]]). The other use of the epithet for Olympus is at its gates, where Iris turns back Hera and Athena (8.411).
