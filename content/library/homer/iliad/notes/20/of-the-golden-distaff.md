---
summary: Artemis, “she of the golden distaff, the clamorous,” stands against Hera (20.70–71), the epithet's second and last use in the Iliad.
grc: [χρυσηλάκατος]
---

The two epithets stand together here as at their first use, the dance of Artemis in which Hermes saw Polymele (16.183; Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 20.70). See [[clamorous]], [[of-the-showering-arrows]] and [[artemis]].
