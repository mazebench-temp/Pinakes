“and Athena shouted, / standing now beside the ditch they had dug, outside the wall, / and now on the loud-resounding shores she shouted aloud” (20.48–50; [[loud-resounding]]). The verse-end τάφρον ὀρυκτὴν τείχεος ἐκτός is the one with which Nestor posted the guards beside the ditch in Book 9 (9.67; Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 20.49). See [[the-achaean-wall]] and [[athena]].
