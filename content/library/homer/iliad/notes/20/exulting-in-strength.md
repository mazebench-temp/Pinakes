---
summary: Hephaestus goes to the battle of the gods “exulting in his strength,” though he limps on slender shins (20.36–37).
---

“and Hephaestus went with them, exulting in his strength, / limping; and his slender shins moved nimbly beneath him” (20.36–37). The half-verse is the one used of Hector going among the foremost (8.337), and the second verse is the one that described Hephaestus coming from his forge to meet Thetis (18.411; Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 20.36).
