summary: Apollo urges Aeneas on with the rank of his mother: “for they say that you too were born of Zeus' daughter Aphrodite, / and he is from a lesser goddess” (20.105–106). Aeneas sets the two mothers side by side before the fight: “your mother is Thetis of the lovely tresses, daughter of the sea”, “and my mother is Aphrodite” (20.207–209).
refs: [20.104-107, 20.206-211]
---

In Book 5 Aphrodite carried her son out of the battle, and was wounded for it (5.311–343); here it is Poseidon who saves him (20.318–340; see [[aphrodite]], [[thetis]], [[meter]] and [[divine-rescue]]).
