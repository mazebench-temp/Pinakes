---
summary: In the pairs of gods Athena is set against Ares, who is named here only as Enyalius.
---

“and against Enyalius the gray-eyed goddess Athena” (20.69; see [[ares]], [[athena]] and [[the-battle-of-the-gods]]). In the list of pairs Ares is named only by this name, as when Zeus fits Achilles' armor to Hector's body and Ares enters into him, “dread Enyalius” (17.210–211), and when Hector says, “Enyalius is impartial, and he kills the man who would kill” (18.309). The genitive Ἐνυαλίοιο is found in the Iliad only here and at 13.519, where the son of Enyalius is Ascalaphus, whose death Ares does not yet know (see [[ascalaphus]]). In Book 21 the pair fight: Ares strikes Athena's aegis, and she fells him with a stone (21.391–408).
