---
summary: Iphition, the first man Achilles kills when he leaps among the Trojans, is “the brave son of Otrynteus, leader of many people” (20.383).
---

The title gives the man his standing, and the book takes it away at once: Achilles' boast over him names his birthplace and his father's domain (20.389–392; see [[iphition]] and [[laos]]).
