---
summary: The verse introduces the two answers in the gods' council over Aeneas: Poseidon answers Hera's call to turn Aeneas back (20.132), and Hera answers Poseidon's resolve to save him (20.309).
---

The two speakers change places. In the first exchange Hera wants the gods to act and Poseidon counsels them to sit apart (20.115–143); in the second Poseidon wants to save Aeneas and Hera leaves it to him, reminding him of her oath never to help the Trojans (20.293–317; [[evil-day]]).
