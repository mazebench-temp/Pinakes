---
summary: Poseidon asks Zeus, with the epithet alone, why he has called the gods together: “Why, lord of the gleaming thunderbolt, have you called the gods to assembly again?” (20.16).
---

The vocative stands here without “Father Zeus,” and the translation renders it “lord of the gleaming thunderbolt” (see its table of epithets). Poseidon, who came up from the sea to the assembly, asks whether Zeus is pondering something about the Trojans and the Achaeans (20.13–18), and Zeus answers him as [[shaker-of-the-earth]] (20.20). See [[zeus]] and [[poseidon]].
