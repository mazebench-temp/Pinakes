---
summary: Artemis stands against Hera, “sister of the Far-Shooter” (20.71), and Hector, when he sees “his brother Polydorus” holding his bowels in his hands, can no longer keep away from Achilles (20.419–423).
---

Apollo had told Hector to wait for Achilles in the crowd, and Hector obeyed (20.375–380); his brother's death undoes the god's warning, “and he could no longer bear / to range far off for long” (20.421–422). Polydorus was the youngest and dearest of Priam's sons (20.407–410; see [[polydorus-son-of-priam]], [[hector]] and [[bereaved-parents]]).
