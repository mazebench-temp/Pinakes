---
summary: Hephaestus built the colonnades of Zeus' house “with his cunning skill” (20.12), in the verse that closed the first book.
---

“which for father Zeus / Hephaestus had made with his cunning skill” (20.11–12). The verse is the one with which Book 1 ends the gods' feast, where Hephaestus has built a house for each of them on Olympus (Ἥφαιστος ποίησεν ἰδυίῃσι πραπίδεσσι, 1.607–608). The gods now gather in that house, but not to feast. See [[hephaestus]] and [[polished]].
