---
summary: Hicetaon, “offshoot of Ares”, is named among the sons of Laomedon in Aeneas' genealogy, in the verse that named him among the elders on the wall (20.237–238 = 3.147).
---

Ἱκετάονά τʼ ὄζον Ἄρηος, “and Hicetaon, offshoot of Ares” (20.238): the warrior's epithet comes with the verse, which first named him with Lampus and Clytius among the elders at the Scaean gates (3.147; see [[offshoot-of-ares]], [[trojan-elders]], [[lampus]] and [[clytius]]). In Aeneas' genealogy the three are the sons of Laomedon with Tithonus and Priam (20.237; see [[laomedon]]). Walter Leaf notes that Book 15 gives each of the three brothers a son (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 20.238): Hicetaon's was Melanippus, whom Priam honored like his own children and whom Antilochus killed (15.546–578; see [[melanippus-son-of-hicetaon]]).
