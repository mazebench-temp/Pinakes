---
summary: Apollo goes to the Trojans as “Phoebus of the unshorn hair” (20.39), the epithet's only use in Homer.
---

“Phoebus of the unshorn hair, and Artemis of the showering arrows” (20.39; [[phoebus]], [[of-the-showering-arrows]]). The list of the gods who take sides gives several of them rare titles: Hermes is “the great helper” (20.34), Ares “of the gleaming helmet” (20.38), and in the pairing Hermes is σῶκος, “strong” (20.72; [[great-helper]], [[of-the-gleaming-helmet]], [[sokos]]). See [[apollo]].
