---
summary: The gods sit apart on either side, and neither party will begin “harsh war,” though Zeus bids them (20.153–155).
---

The translation's note on 20.155 explains Ζεὺς δʼ ἥμενος ὕψι κέλευε in the usual way, “though Zeus, sitting on high, bade them,” and reports that Walter Leaf, with Hentze, took κέλευε absolutely, “was supreme over them.” See [[the-battle-of-the-gods]] and [[polemos]].
