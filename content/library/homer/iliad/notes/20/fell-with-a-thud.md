---
summary: Iphition, his head split in two by Achilles' spear, “fell with a thud; and brilliant Achilles boasted over him” (20.387–388).
---

Only the first half of the whole verse is used, and the killer's boast takes the place of the clatter of armor, as when Idomeneus killed Othryoneus (13.373) and Odysseus killed Socus, in the same verse with δῖος Ὀδυσσεύς for δῖος Ἀχιλλεύς (11.449). The boast follows at once (20.389–392; [[most-terrible-of-men]], [[euchomai]]). See [[iphition]] and [[achilles]].
