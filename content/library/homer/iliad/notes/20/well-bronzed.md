---
summary: Poseidon draws “the ash spear with its good bronze” out of Aeneas' shield and sets it before Achilles' feet (20.322–324).
---

Aristarchus athetized 322–324: Achilles' spear went through the shield and stood in the ground, so Poseidon could not draw it from the shield (see the translation's note on 20.322–324). See [[ash-spear]] and [[divine-rescue]].
