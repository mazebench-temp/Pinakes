---
summary: Achilles stabs Demoleon, “a brave defender against battle, the son of Antenor”, in the temple through the bronze-cheeked helmet (20.395–400).
---

“and after him Demoleon, / a brave defender against battle, the son of Antenor, / he stabbed in the temple” (20.395–397; [[demoleon]], [[antenor]], [[achilles]]). Ἀντήνορος υἱὸν ends the verse as it did for Pedaeus, the first of Antenor's sons to die (5.69; [[pedaeus]]); the phrase names Archelochus in the nominative (14.463; [[archelochus]]), and Book 21 gives it to Agenor (21.546, 21.579; [[agenor]]). Demoleon is the last of the sons of Antenor killed in the poem, after Pedaeus, Iphidamas and Coon, Archelochus, Laodamas and Acamas (5.69–75, 11.221–263, 14.459–464, 15.516–517, 16.342–344).
