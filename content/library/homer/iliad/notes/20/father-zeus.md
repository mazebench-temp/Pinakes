---
summary: “Father Zeus” in Book 20 is not an address: Hephaestus built the colonnades “for father Zeus” (20.11), and Achilles boasts that he sacked Lyrnessus “with Athena and father Zeus” (20.192).
---

In Achilles' taunt Zeus is on both sides within three verses: Achilles sacked Lyrnessus “with Athena and father Zeus,” but “Zeus and the other gods rescued you” (20.192–194), as Aeneas had himself said that Zeus rescued him (20.92–93). Walter Leaf reports Heyne's remark on the lightness with which the names of gods are used here as forms of speech (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 20.194).
