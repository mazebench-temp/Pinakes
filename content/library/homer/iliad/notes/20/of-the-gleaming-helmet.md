---
summary: Ares goes to the Trojans as “Ares of the gleaming helmet” (20.38), the only time the epithet is given to anyone but Hector; Hector has it when, unafraid, he answers Achilles (20.430).
---

“And to the Trojans went Ares of the gleaming helmet, and with him / Phoebus of the unshorn hair, and Artemis of the showering arrows” (20.38–39; [[ares]], [[of-the-unshorn-hair]]). Walter Leaf notes that κορυθαίολος is only here applied to any but Hector (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 20.38; see the translation's note on 20.38–39). Hector's own formula introduces his answer to Achilles over Polydorus' body: “Unafraid, Hector of the gleaming helmet answered him” (20.430; [[answering-him]]). See [[hector]].
