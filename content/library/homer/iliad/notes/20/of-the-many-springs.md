---
summary: All the feet of “Ida of the many springs” shake with Poseidon's earthquake (20.59), and in Aeneas' genealogy the Dardanians dwelt on its slopes before Ilios was built in the plain (20.218).
---

“And all the feet of Ida of the many springs were shaken, / and its peaks, and the city of the Trojans and the ships of the Achaeans” (20.59–60); “but they still dwelt on the slopes of Ida of the many springs” (20.218; [[dardania]]). Plato quotes the second passage in the *Laws* (681e; see the translation's note on 20.218). See [[ida]].
