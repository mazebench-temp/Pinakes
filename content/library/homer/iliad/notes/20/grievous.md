---
summary: The Achaeans exult because Achilles has appeared, “who had long ceased from grievous battle” (20.42–43).
---

“so long the Achaeans exulted greatly, because Achilles / had appeared, who had long ceased from grievous battle” (20.42–43). The words are those with which Book 18 explained the trembling in the Trojans' assembly after Achilles had appeared at the ditch (18.247–248), and the next verse here repeats the trembling that came over the Trojans when Ajax strode out against Hector (20.44 = 7.215; Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 20.42).
