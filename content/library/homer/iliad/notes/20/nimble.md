---
summary: Aeneas remembers that when Achilles drove him from Ida, Zeus rescued him, “who roused my might and my nimble knees” (20.92–93).
---

The knees are Aeneas' own, in flight: Achilles came upon his cattle on Ida and sacked Lyrnessus and Pedasus, “but Zeus / rescued me, who roused my might and my nimble knees” (20.90–93; [[the-sack-of-lyrnessus]]). Achilles tells the same story in his taunt, with Aeneas running down the mountains “on swift feet” (20.188–190; [[swift]]). See [[aeneas]].
