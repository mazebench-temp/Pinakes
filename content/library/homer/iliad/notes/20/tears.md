---
summary: Aeneas tells Achilles that one of their two pairs of parents will mourn today: “Of these, one pair or the other will weep for their dear son / today” (20.210–211).
---

ἕτεροι, “one pair or the other” (Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 20.210), are Peleus and Thetis or Anchises and Aphrodite, whom Aeneas has just named (20.206–209; see [[bereaved-parents]] and [[mothers-and-sons]]).
