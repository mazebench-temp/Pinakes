summary: Ares calls “shrilly” to the Trojans from the city (20.52); Achilles draws his sharp sword against Aeneas (20.284); Hector comes on “brandishing his sharp spear” (20.423) and says that his weapon too is sharp (20.437); Achilles stabs Areithous with his sharp spear (20.488).
---

ὀξὺ … κελεύων is the shrill call of the war cry, as in Book 18 (see the Book 18 note); the translation renders it “shrilly” (20.52). Hector's claim answers Achilles' threat: “since my weapon too is sharp at the point” (20.437), and the spear he throws is turned back by Athena's breath (20.438–441).
