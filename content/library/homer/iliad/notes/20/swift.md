---
summary: Achilles reminds Aeneas how he drove him down from Ida “on swift feet” (20.188–190).
grc: ["ταχέεσσι πόδεσσι"]
---

“Do you not remember when you were alone, and I drove you / away from the cattle, down the mountains of Ida on swift feet, / swiftly?” (20.188–190). Aeneas had told Apollo the same story (20.90–93; [[nimble]]). The phrase also catches 21.564, where Agenor fears that Achilles will catch him with his swift feet. See [[the-sack-of-lyrnessus]].
