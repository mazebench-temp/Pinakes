summary: Apollo sets Aeneas' mother above Achilles': Aeneas was born “of Zeus' daughter Aphrodite,” and Achilles of a lesser goddess, “for one is the daughter of Zeus, and the other of the Old Man of the Sea” (20.105–107).
grc: ["Διὸς κούρης"]
refs: [20.107]
---

The argument turns on the fathers of the two goddesses: Aphrodite is Zeus' daughter, Thetis the daughter of the Old Man of the Sea ([[daughter-of-the-old-man]], [[old-man-of-the-sea]]). Aeneas himself names his mother without her father when he answers Achilles: “and my mother is Aphrodite” (20.209). See [[aphrodite]], [[thetis]] and [[aeneas]].
