---
summary: Achilles rushes at Aeneas “like a lion, / a ravager, whom men are eager to kill” (20.164–165).
grc: [σίντης]
---

The simile begins on the word: the lion is a σίντης, and the men gather to kill it for that reason (Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 20.165, comparing 11.481). Leaf, agreeing with others, calls the simile that follows the most finished in Homer (on 20.164). See [[like-a-lion]], [[wounded-lion]] and [[achilles]].
