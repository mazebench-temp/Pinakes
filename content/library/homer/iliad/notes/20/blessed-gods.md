“So the blessed gods urged both sides on / and brought them together, and made heavy strife break out among them” (20.54–55). The phrase ἔριδα ῥήγνυντο, “made strife break out,” is unique (Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 20.55; see the translation's note on 20.55). See [[olympians]], [[eris]] and [[the-battle-of-the-gods]].
