---
summary: The foals of the North Wind run “on the very crests of the breakers of the gray sea” (20.229).
grc: ["ἁλὸς πολιοῖο"]
---

The masculine πολιοῖο with ἅλς stands here for the commoner feminine πολιῆς ἁλός; the Odyssey has the same phrase twice (*Odyssey* 5.410, 9.132). See [[the-mares-of-erichthonius]] and [[sea]].
