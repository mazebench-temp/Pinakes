---
summary: Iphition, the first man Achilles kills when he returns to the battle, was born “beneath snowy Tmolus, in the rich land of Hyde” (20.385).
---

{{quote:d6c72224-c614-5666-85fa-d71dda2f0435}}

Τμώλῳ ὕπο νιφόεντι, “beneath snowy Tmolus” (20.385; see [[iphition]] and [[otrynteus]]). The Catalogue said of the Maeonians that they were “born beneath Tmolus” (ὑπὸ Τμώλῳ γεγαῶτας, 2.866; see [[maeonians]]), and the verse on Iphition gives the mountain an epithet that Homer uses only of mountains, νιφόεις, “snowy”, as of the mountains of the Thracians and of Olympus (14.227, 18.616; see [[niphoeis]]). Strabo reports that some added the whole verse “beneath snowy Tmolus, in the rich land of Hyde” after the Catalogue's lines on the Maeonians (*Geography* 13.4.6; see [[hyde]]), and Walter Leaf counts the borrowing from the Catalogue among the signs that the Iphition episode is late (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 20.381; see the translation's note on 20.381–395). In Achilles' boast the birthplace is placed by the Gygaean lake, between the Hyllus and the Hermus (20.390–392; see [[gygaean-lake]], [[hyllus]] and [[hermus]]).
