---
summary: “Poseidon the earth-holder” goes to the Achaean ships with Hera, Athena and Hermes (20.33–35).
---

“and Poseidon the earth-holder, and the great helper / Hermes” (20.34–35). The pair Ποσειδάων γαιήοχος … ἐριούνης comes again in the Odyssey, as the gods gather to see Ares and Aphrodite caught in Hephaestus' net (*Odyssey* 8.322–323; see [[great-helper]]). See [[poseidon]].
