---
summary: Hera's oath: never to help the Trojans, “not even when all Troy blazes with ravening fire” (20.316).
grc: [μαλερῷ]
---

Hector threatened the Achaean ships with this fire in Book 9 (9.242); here it is the fire that will burn Troy, and the verse comes again in Book 21 (21.375). See [[evil-day]], [[troy]] and [[sack-of-troy-foretold]].
