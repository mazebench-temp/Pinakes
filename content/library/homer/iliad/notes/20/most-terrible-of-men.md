---
summary: Achilles boasts over Iphition: “You lie there, son of Otrynteus, most terrible of all men” (20.389).
---

The words that Agamemnon and Iris spoke to Achilles himself (1.146, 18.170) are now his own, said over the first man he kills on his return. Walter Leaf finds the phrase meaningless of an unknown warrior and counts it among the signs that the Iphition episode is later (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 20.381; see the translation's note on 20.381–395). The boast goes on to the dead man's birthplace by the Gygaean lake, “by the fish-filled Hyllus and the eddying Hermus” (20.390–392; [[fish-filled]], [[eddying]]). See [[iphition]] and [[euchomai]].
