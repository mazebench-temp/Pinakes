---
summary: λυγρὸν ὄλεθρον twice in eight verses: Achilles' shield “had kept grim destruction from him” (20.289), and Poseidon says of Aeneas that Apollo “will do nothing to ward off grim destruction from him” (20.296).
---

The god's gift saves Achilles; the god who sent Aeneas will not save him. See [[the-shield-of-achilles]], [[hekatos]] and [[would-have-had-not]].
