---
summary: Zeus addresses Poseidon as “shaker of the earth” when he grants that Poseidon knows his plan (20.20), and Hera addresses him so when she leaves Aeneas' fate to him (20.310).
---

“You know, shaker of the earth, the plan in my breast, / for which I have gathered you” (20.20–21; [[plan-of-zeus]]); “Shaker of the earth, consider for yourself in your own mind / whether you will rescue Aeneas” (20.310–311). Between the two addresses Poseidon does what his epithet says, shaking the earth beneath the gods' battle (20.57–58; [[earth-shaker]]). See [[poseidon]].
