---
summary: As the gods come together, “terribly the father of men and gods thundered / from on high” while Poseidon shook the earth below (20.56–58).
---

The thunder of Zeus above and the earthquake of Poseidon below shake the whole world, and Hades beneath it leaps from his throne in fear (20.56–65; [[earth-shaker]], [[dank]]). See [[zeus]] and [[the-battle-of-the-gods]].
