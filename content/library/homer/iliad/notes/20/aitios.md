---
summary: Poseidon pleads for Aeneas as a man “who is not to blame” (ἀναίτιος, 20.297), who suffers for the sake of others' griefs.
grc: [ἀναίτιος]
---

Paris used the word of himself when Hector blamed him, “since your heart is set on blaming one who is not to blame” (13.775). Here a god uses it of a mortal who suffers for others' griefs (see [[who-is-to-blame]] and [[algos]]).
