---
summary: Hector: “I will go against him, even if his hands are like fire, / if his hands are like fire, and his might like gleaming iron” (20.371–372).
---

αἴθωνι σιδήρῳ is the iron of the chariot-maker's axe in a simile and the iron with which the Achaeans buy wine (4.485, 7.473); here it is the hardness of Achilles' might. See [[hands-like-fire]], [[epanalepsis]], [[iron]] and [[hector]].
