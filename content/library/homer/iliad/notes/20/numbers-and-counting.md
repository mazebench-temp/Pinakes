---
summary: Erichthonius had “three thousand horses” in his marsh pastures, and the North Wind sired twelve foals on them (20.221–225; see [[the-mares-of-erichthonius]]); the insults Aeneas and Achilles could speak would overload “a ship of a hundred benches” (20.247); the layers of Achilles' shield are five, “two of bronze, and two of tin inside, / and one of gold” (20.270–272); and Achilles charges Hector's mist three times and a fourth (20.445–447).
refs: [20.221-225, 20.247, 20.269-272, 20.445-447]
---

The ship of a hundred benches is a ship larger than any ever seen (Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 20.247), and the five layers were athetized by Aristarchus as contradicting the shield of Book 18 (see the translation's note on 20.269–272 and [[three-times-and-a-fourth]]).
