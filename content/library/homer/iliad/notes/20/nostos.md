---
summary: Aeneas tells Achilles that the two of them will not, with childish words, “part like this and come back from the battle” (20.211–212).
grc: [ἐξαπονέεσθαι]
en: [come back]
---

ἐξαπονέεσθαι is the return from the day's fighting that Zeus refused Patroclus, “that he should come back safe from the battle” (16.252), and διακρινθέντε, “part”, is the regular word for the end of a fight that has been decided (Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 20.141; 3.98, 3.102). One of the two, Aeneas means, will not come back.
