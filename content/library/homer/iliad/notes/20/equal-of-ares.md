---
summary: The Trojans tremble at the sight of Achilles “flashing in his armor, the equal of Ares, ruin of mortals” (20.46).
---

“but a dread trembling came over the limbs of every Trojan, / in their fear, when they saw the swift-footed son of Peleus / flashing in his armor, the equal of Ares, ruin of mortals” (20.44–46; [[ruin-of-mortals]]). The comparison comes a moment before Ares himself goes among the Trojans and shouts from the city (20.47–53). The trembling is the verse that came over the Trojans when Ajax strode out against Hector like huge Ares (20.44 = 7.215; see [[like-huge-ares]]). See [[achilles]] and [[ares]].
