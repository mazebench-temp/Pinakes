---
summary: Achilles taunts Aeneas that Priam “is steady, and not light of mind” (20.183); Achilles himself promises that “no man / of the Trojans will rejoice, whoever comes near my spear” (20.362–363); and the narrator says of him that “he was no sweet-tempered man, nor gentle-hearted” (20.467).
refs: [20.183, 20.362-363, 20.467]
---

The last is the narrator's own judgment, given as Tros clasps Achilles' knees: two compounds found nowhere else in Homer are denied to say how savage he is (see [[sweet-tempered]] and [[gentle-hearted]]). ἀεσίφρων is discussed in the translation's note on 20.183.
