---
summary: Not even Ares or Athena, Achilles says, “could deal with the mouth of so great a battle and toil through it” (20.359).
---

Walter Leaf takes καὶ πονέοιτο as a hendiadys, “by dint of labour”, with the word's frequent sense of the toil of battle (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 20.359). The “mouth” of battle is the phrase of 10.8 and 19.313 (see the translation's row on ὑσμίνης στόμα and [[polemos]]).
