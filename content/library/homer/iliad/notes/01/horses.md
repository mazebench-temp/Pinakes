---
summary: Horses appear in Book 1 only as the plunder of raids: the Trojans, says Achilles, never drove off his cattle or his horses.
---

Book 1 has no chariots and no battle, and horses appear once, as the kind of property whose theft starts a war. Achilles has no such grievance against the Trojans: they never drove off his [[cattle|cattle]] or his horses, or ravaged his harvest in Phthia ({{ref:Iliad 1.154-156}}; see [[raiding|raiding]]).
