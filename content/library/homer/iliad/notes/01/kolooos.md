summary: Hephaestus uses the noun of the scene Zeus and Hera have just made among the gods, whom they "set brawling".
---

Hephaestus uses it in his protest against the quarrel of his parents:

{{quote:5a2706ca-b8f2-53c2-8513-42e204015bf9}}

The phrase κολῳὸν ἐλαύνετον means "you two drive on a brawl". Hephaestus gives the quarrel of the king and queen of the gods a word for noise, and he sets it against the pleasure of the feast. See [[quarrel-on-olympus]] and [[worse-things-prevail]].
