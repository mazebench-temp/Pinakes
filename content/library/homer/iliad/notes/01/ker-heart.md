Apollo comes down from Olympus χωόμενος κῆρ, “angry at heart”: the accusative says where the anger is felt.

{{quote:8abdce84-18c6-5c4b-8248-b5684ab52038}}

Achilles, withdrawn from assembly and war, “wasted away his own heart” (φθινύθεσκε φίλον κῆρ). The verb is iterative: it happened day after day.

{{quote:3b337c12-b3ad-521a-b960-c2c9a3ecb0e7}}

The last two uses frame the second half of the book. Achilles' withdrawal consumes his heart; Hera's submission bends hers. Both have been silenced by a stronger will.
