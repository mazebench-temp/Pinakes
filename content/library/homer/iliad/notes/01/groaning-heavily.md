Achilles groans before telling his mother what, as he says, she already knows, and then he tells her anyway ([[retelling]]). The swift-footed hero ([[swift-footed]]) is here described by his grief. The formula links this scene to the two later scenes of mourning in which the same words recur, when Patroclus weeps for the army and when Achilles grieves for Patroclus ([[speak-out]]).
