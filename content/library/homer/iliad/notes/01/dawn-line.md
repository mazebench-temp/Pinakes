The formula divides the night at Chryse from the voyage home, and it pairs with the sunset line two verses before ([[sunset-lines]]). The book keeps careful count of its days: nine days of plague ({{ref:Iliad 1.53}}), the tenth for the assembly ({{ref:Iliad 1.54}}), and twelve days until the gods return from the Ethiopians ({{ref:Iliad 1.425}}). The twelfth-dawn line closes that count, and Thetis goes up to Olympus at once:
