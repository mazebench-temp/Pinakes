Calchas: the far-shooter will not thrust away the shameful ruin until the girl is returned.

{{quote:3e012a68-d687-584b-9194-ab6d66ad184e}}

Achilles uses it of himself. If the Achaeans ever need him "to ward off shameful ruin" from the others, they will not have him.

{{quote:85191c44-1917-5e3c-8f76-8238d12e77df}}

Thetis once warded off shameful ruin from Zeus.

{{quote:9b2da0ea-742c-5f24-9b54-136f28797584}}

Chryses' second prayer: "now at last ward off this shameful ruin from the Danaans". The plague is over.

The four uses trace the book's movement. The ruin that Apollo brings and ends, Achilles will now withhold. Achilles' protection of the Achaeans becomes the thing he refuses. See [[loigos]].
