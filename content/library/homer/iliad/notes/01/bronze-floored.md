---
summary: “The bronze-floored house” of Zeus on Olympus, where Thetis promises to go (1.426).
---

Thetis promises Achilles that when Zeus comes back from the Ethiopians, “then I will go for you to the bronze-floored house of Zeus” (1.426) and clasp his knees. She finds him instead apart from the other gods, on the highest peak of Olympus (1.498–499); the house is where the gods gather at the end of the book (1.533–535, 1.570).
