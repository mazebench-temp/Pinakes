---
summary: The dogs of Book 1 feed on the unburied dead, die first in Apollo's plague, and supply Achilles' insults for Agamemnon: “dog-face” and “with the eyes of a dog”.
---

**Carrion.** In the first lines of the poem the wrath of Achilles made the dead “the spoil of dogs and of every bird” ({{ref:Iliad 1.4-5}}).

{{quote:0f38ffeb-58e9-5305-966c-031eba176b01}}

**Plague.** Apollo's arrows strike first the [[mules|mules]] and “the swift dogs” of the camp, then the men ({{ref:Iliad 1.50-52}}).

**Insult.** Achilles calls Agamemnon κυνῶπα, “dog-face” ({{ref:Iliad 1.159}}; see [[dog-face]]), and a man “with the eyes of a dog and the heart of a deer” ({{ref:Iliad 1.225}}; see [[dog-eyes-deer-heart]], [[eyes|eyes]] and [[deer|deer]]).
