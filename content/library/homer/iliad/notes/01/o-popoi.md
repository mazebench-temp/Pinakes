Nestor opens with dismay at the sight of the two best Achaeans quarreling, and his first argument is the pleasure it would give Priam and the Trojans. The whole verse recurs in Book 7, where Nestor rebukes the Achaeans for their reluctance to face Hector in single combat (Il. 7.124).

Book 1 has a second exclamation, ὤ μοι (“ah me”), which opens Achilles' first outburst against Agamemnon ({{ref:Iliad 1.149}}) and Thetis' lament for her son ({{ref:Iliad 1.414}}).
