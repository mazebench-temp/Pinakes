Achilles uses it to turn from refusal to threat. He will not fight with his hands for the girl, since those who gave her are taking her back. But let Agamemnon try to take anything else from beside his ship, and his dark blood will flow around Achilles' spear ({{ref:Iliad 1.301-303}}). The formula marks the line Achilles draws: the girl he gives up, nothing more.
