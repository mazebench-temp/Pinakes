---
summary: The throne on which [[Zeus]] sits after the gods rise to meet him, and the golden throne of [[Hera]] in her standing epithet.
---

When Zeus returns to his house after granting Thetis' request, the gods rise from their seats (ἕδεα) to meet him, and he sits down on his throne ({{ref:Iliad 1.536}}; see [[rising-for-zeus|rising for Zeus]]). Hephaestus warns that Zeus could blast them all “from our seats” if he chose ({{ref:Iliad 1.580-581}}).

{{quote:f0f1e66b-40a2-523f-af46-661f5aa21cdd}}

The book's last line names Hera χρυσόθρονος, “of the golden throne” ({{ref:Iliad 1.611}}; see [[gold-and-silver]]).
