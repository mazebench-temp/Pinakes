τράφεν ἠδ’ ἐγένοντο says that the men of Nestor's generation were raised and born with him. The emphasis falls on the upbringing they shared, and birth comes second. Unless ἐγένοντο is taken in a weaker sense (“lived, came of age”), the natural order is reversed. The translation restores it: “who had been born and raised with him.”
