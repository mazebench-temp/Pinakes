---
summary: The drink of the gods, which Hephaestus draws from the mixing bowl and pours for them at their feast on Olympus.
---

Book 1 serves it once. After the quarrel of Zeus and Hera, [[Hephaestus]] goes round from left to right and pours for the other gods, drawing sweet nectar from the [[mixing-bowl|mixing bowl]] ({{ref:Iliad 1.597-598}}). The verb, οἰνοχόει, is literally “poured wine” (see [[cupbearing|cupbearing]]).
