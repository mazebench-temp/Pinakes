---
summary: The funeral pyres of the plague dead, which burn without stop in the Achaean camp.
---

In Book 1 the pyres are the visible toll of Apollo's [[arrows|arrows]]: “the pyres of the dead burned on, thick and constant” ({{ref:Iliad 1.52}}). The line gives no names and no funerals, only fires burning always (αἰεί) and crowded together (θαμειαί), day after day for nine days (see [[cremation|cremation]] and [[wrath-of-apollo]]).
