---
summary: The mast of the ship that takes Chryseis home is lowered into its crutch on arrival at Chryse and set up again for the voyage back (1.433–435, 1.480–481).
---

At Chryse the crew lower the mast by the [[forestays|forestays]] into the [[mast-crutch|mast-crutch]] and row in; leaving, they set it up again and spread the [[sails|sails]].
