---
summary: In Book 1 he is never named on his own, only as the father in the patronymics Κρονίδης and Κρονίων, “son of Cronus”.
---

The patronymics name Zeus through his father seven times in Book 1 (1.397, 1.405, 1.498, 1.502, 1.528, 1.539, 1.552), among them the line of the great nod: “he spoke, and the son of Cronus nodded with his dark brows” (1.528).
