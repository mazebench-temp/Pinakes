---
summary: She rises from the sea to comfort her son and supplicates [[Zeus]] to honor him, reminding him how she once saved him from bondage.
---

Thetis is a mother first. Achilles weeps by the sea and prays to her, and she comes up at once, strokes him, and asks what is wrong:

{{quote:f195ffe7-3958-5081-8d08-59467f833b33}}

When he has told his story, her answer is a lament. She knows his life will be short:

{{quote:8177126f-a070-5891-bee9-8d80c2c18630}}

She promises to go to Zeus when he returns from the Ethiopians (1.419–427). On the twelfth day she goes up to Olympus, kneels, holds his knees and chin, and asks:

{{quote:80aa964c-2080-5ec2-ba6d-4a6a7c65e425}}

Zeus is silent. She holds on and asks again (1.512–516), and he nods. See [[thetis-supplicates-zeus]].
