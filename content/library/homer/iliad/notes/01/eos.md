ῥοδοδάκτυλος = ῥόδον (rose) + δάκτυλος (finger): rosy-fingered, the first light spreading like fingers. ἠριγένεια = ἦρι (early) + γίγνομαι (be born): early-born. The whole line recurs as a formula, twenty times in the Odyssey alone.
