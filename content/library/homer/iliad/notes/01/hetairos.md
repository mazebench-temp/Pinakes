---
summary: [[Patroclus]] obeys his “dear companion” [[Achilles]], and Achilles leaves his companions to weep alone by the sea.
---

Both kings have their companions. Agamemnon tells Achilles to go home “with your ships and your companions” (1.179), and says he will send Chryseis home with his own (1.183). After the assembly Achilles goes back to his huts with the son of Menoetius and his companions (1.307).

{{quote:47d59219-9c47-5774-b474-92186d70ff95}}

Patroclus obeys “his dear companion” (φίλῳ ἑταίρῳ) and hands over Briseis. Then Achilles withdraws from his companions and sits alone by the sea. The withdrawal from the army begins with a withdrawal from his own men.
