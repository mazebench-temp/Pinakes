---
summary: The whole barley grains taken up before the prayer at Chryse and thrown forward before the victims are killed.
---

At Chryse, when the victims stand around the [[altar|altar]], the Achaeans wash their hands and take up the barley ({{ref:Iliad 1.449}}). Chryses prays, and when the prayer is over they throw the grains forward (προβάλοντο) and the killing begins ({{ref:Iliad 1.458-459}}; see [[sacrifice|sacrifice]]).
