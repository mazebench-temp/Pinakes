The two occurrences frame the speech of Calchas: Achilles sits and Calchas rises; Calchas sits and Agamemnon rises. Each new speaker is named at the start of the next verse with his full titles ([[son-of-thestor]], [[best-of-bird-seers]]; [[son-of-atreus]], [[wide-ruling]]).

After that the orderly alternation breaks down. Achilles and Agamemnon answer one another directly; Nestor “springs up” (ἀνόρουσε, {{ref:Iliad 1.248}}) after Achilles has sat down in anger ({{ref:Iliad 1.246}}); and at the end both men rise together and dissolve the assembly (ἀνστήτην, {{ref:Iliad 1.305}}).

In Book 1 the scepter appears only in Achilles' hand, when he swears by it and flings it down ({{ref:Iliad 1.234-246}}).
