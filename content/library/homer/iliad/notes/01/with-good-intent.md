Both men are introduced with credentials: Calchas with his seercraft ([[best-of-bird-seers]], [[past-present-future]]), Nestor with his age and eloquence ([[sweet-of-speech]], [[clear-voiced-orator]]). Both are vouched for with the same line, and both try to serve the common good. Both are rebuffed. Agamemnon insults Calchas ([[prophet-of-evils]]) and brushes Nestor's counsel aside ({{ref:Iliad 1.286-291}}). The formula sets the adviser's good intent against the passions of the two kings.
