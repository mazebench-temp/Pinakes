At Chryse the line follows the feast after the sacrifice and leads into the drinking and the paean. The young men fill the mixing bowls, serve everyone, and first pour drops for the gods ([[feast-type-scene]], [[sacrifice-type-scene]]). It comes right after [[equal-feast]], and the two lines form the standard close of a sacrificial meal; the same pair returns at Agamemnon's sacrifice in Book 2.
