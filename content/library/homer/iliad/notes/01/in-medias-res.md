The proem shows the method. It names the wrath, its cost and its starting point, "from the time when first the two of them stood apart in strife", and the story begins nine years into the war. Everything earlier (the Judgment of Paris, Helen, Aulis, the raids) comes into the poem only in allusions and memories, which the Myths gather by period.
