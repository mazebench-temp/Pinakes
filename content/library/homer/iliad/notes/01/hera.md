---
summary: She cares for the Achaeans: she prompts Achilles to call the assembly, sends [[Athena]] to stop his sword, and quarrels with Zeus when she guesses what he has promised [[Thetis]].
---

Hera acts first on earth and then in heaven. On the tenth day of the plague it is she who puts the assembly into Achilles' mind:

{{quote:d4414450-e9d5-5349-b7e3-778ccddc0b1b}}

When Achilles draws his sword on Agamemnon, she sends Athena, "loving both men alike in her heart and caring for them both" (1.196, 1.209). She wants neither leader of the Achaeans dead.

On Olympus she sees through Zeus at once. She has noticed Thetis at his knees and guesses the price:

{{quote:fb131b1f-c22d-5f03-9bf4-60667e2f4a9f}}

Zeus answers with a threat, and "ox-eyed queen Hera was afraid" (1.568). Her son Hephaestus reconciles them with a cup and a story, and she smiles (1.595–596).

Achilles names her among the three gods who once tried to bind Zeus (1.399–400). See [[binding-of-zeus]].
