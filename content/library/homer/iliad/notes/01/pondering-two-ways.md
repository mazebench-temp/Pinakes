---
summary: The formulas frame Achilles' choice whether to kill Agamemnon (1.189, 1.193).
---

{{quote:5eb3e8ba-c93a-56bf-b6cc-c025f3361962}}

The formulas give the most dangerous moment of the quarrel its shape. Achilles' heart is divided within his shaggy chest ([[shaggy-chest]]): to draw his sword and kill Agamemnon, or to check his anger. The second formula keeps the question open while the hand is already moving — “while he was pondering this … and drawing his great sword from its sheath.” At that moment Athena comes ([[divine-epiphany]]). The deliberation is interrupted, and the choice is then given back to Achilles as a matter of obedience.
