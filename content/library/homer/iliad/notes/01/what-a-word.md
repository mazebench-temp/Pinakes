Zeus has told Hera not to pry into his counsels ({{ref:Iliad 1.545-550}}). She answers with the formula of protest, claims that she has never pried, and then pries at once, naming Thetis and guessing the promise to honor Achilles ({{ref:Iliad 1.555-559}}). The formula opens her attack.
