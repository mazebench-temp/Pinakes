Zeus says it when he foresees Hera's anger at his promise to Thetis; Hephaestus says it when that anger has broken out, and his words repeat his father's.

The noun behind the adjective is the book's word for the plague. λοιγός is the ruin that Achilles wants Apollo to keep away ({{ref:Iliad 1.67}}), that Apollo will not thrust off until Chryseis is returned ({{ref:Iliad 1.97}}), and that Chryses finally asks him to end ({{ref:Iliad 1.456}}; see [[shameful-ruin]]). On Olympus the same word names the danger of a quarrel among the gods. Hephaestus ends it with laughter instead of a sacrifice ([[unquenchable-laughter]]).
