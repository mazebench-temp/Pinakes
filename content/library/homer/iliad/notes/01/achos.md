Agamemnon rises ἀχνύμενος after Calchas has named the cause of the plague.

At Agamemnon's threat to take Briseis, ἄχος comes upon Achilles, and his heart is divided whether to kill.

{{quote:3150434f-2166-5dc6-9da0-2e1c819220ac}}

{{quote:92c6af88-8b29-5cbe-b912-41a16c29207c}}

The same phrase, ἀχνύμενός περ with χραισμεῖν, comes in two very different speeches. Achilles tells Agamemnon that when Hector kills the Achaeans he will be powerless to help them, “for all your grief”. Hephaestus tells his mother that if Zeus strikes her he will be powerless to help her, “for all my grief”. The mortal threat and the divine one are cast in the same words.
