---
summary: Both halves of Book 1 end in sleep. The embassy lies down by the stern cables at [[Chryse]], and on Olympus the gods go home to bed and Zeus lies down beside Hera, "where he always lay down when sweet sleep came upon him."
---

Each half of the book closes with a night. At Chryse, after the sacrifice and the paean, the men sleep beside their ship until dawn:

{{quote:f418e217-79f5-5845-a919-a3c756e7828e}}

On Olympus the quarrel between Zeus and Hera ends in laughter and song, and then everyone goes to bed:

{{quote:b5cd3c08-d42b-5906-b53c-dd9bedcd4774}}

The calm does not last. Book 2 begins with the rest of the gods and men asleep all night, but "sweet sleep did not hold Zeus" (2.1–2). He is working out how to honor Achilles, and he sends the dream to Agamemnon. Readers have noticed the tension with 1.611, where Zeus "slept"; there the verb may mean no more than that he lay down to rest. See [[plan-of-zeus]].
