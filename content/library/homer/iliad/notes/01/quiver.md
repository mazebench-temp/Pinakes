---
summary: The lidded quiver that [[Apollo]] carries on his shoulders when he comes down from Olympus to shoot the plague.
---

Apollo comes down from Olympus “with his bow on his shoulders and his close-covered quiver”, and the arrows clatter on his shoulders as he moves (1.44–47). ἀμφηρεφής, “covered all round”, describes a quiver with a lid. See [[bow]], [[arrows]] and [[wrath-of-apollo]].
