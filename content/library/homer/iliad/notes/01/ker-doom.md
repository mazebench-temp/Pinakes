---
summary: κήρ appears once in Book 1, when [[Achilles]] says that battle “looks like death” to [[Agamemnon]].
---

{{quote:fdf96563-8a53-5bef-8efa-7a2027967008}}

Achilles taunts Agamemnon with cowardice. The king has never dared to arm for battle with the people, or to go out on ambush with the best of the Achaeans; to him that “looks like κήρ”. Ambush was the hardest test of a fighter's nerve (see [[aristos|ἄριστος]]).
