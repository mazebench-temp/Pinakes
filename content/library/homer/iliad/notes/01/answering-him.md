{{quote:4f796f14-68ce-5a0e-8dd2-6fab9f660729}}

The same line brings in Achilles in the assembly and Zeus on Olympus; only the name at the end changes. The quarrel among men and the quarrel among gods are told in the same language. In the assembly the formula alternates between the two antagonists, Achilles (84, 215) and Agamemnon (130, 285).
