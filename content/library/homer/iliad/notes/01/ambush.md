---
summary: Achilles taunts Agamemnon that he has never dared to go on ambush with the best of the Achaeans.
---

The ambush is the test of courage, and that is the point of Achilles' taunt. Agamemnon, with “the eyes of a dog and the heart of a [[deer|deer]]”, has never dared to arm for battle with the army or to go on ambush with the best of the Achaeans ({{ref:Iliad 1.225-228}}).
