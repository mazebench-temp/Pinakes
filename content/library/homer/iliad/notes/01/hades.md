summary: The proem says Achilles' wrath hurled many souls "to Hades".
---

{{quote:5fbdb8ad-4245-5590-ad70-17444ee00287}}

Ἄϊδι προΐαψεν: "hurled down to Hades". In Homer "Hades" is almost always the god (23.244 may mean the place); the realm is his "house" (δόμος Ἀΐδαο). The dative here means "to Hades", into his keeping. The proem contrasts the ψυχαί (souls) that go below with αὐτούς ("the men themselves"), the bodies left for dogs and birds. For Homer the self is the body. See [[psyche]].
