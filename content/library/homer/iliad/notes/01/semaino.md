---
summary: [[Agamemnon]] complains that [[Achilles]] wants to give orders to all; Achilles answers that Agamemnon may give orders to others, but not to him.
---

{{quote:601d94ba-4ab2-52c0-97c5-87800278e02c}}

Agamemnon's complaint about Achilles builds to it. Achilles wants to be above all others, to have power over all, to be lord over all, and to give orders to all, “but there is one, I think, who will not obey him”.

{{quote:1f377cda-8ffc-5e2e-8daa-439797aea0fb}}

Achilles turns the words back. He would be called a coward and a nobody if he gave way to Agamemnon in everything. Let him give orders to others, but not to him: “I, I think, will obey you no longer.” The verb of command is answered in both speeches by the verb of obedience, πείθομαι.
