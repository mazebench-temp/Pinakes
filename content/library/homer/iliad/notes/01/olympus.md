---
summary: Half of Book 1's scenes among the gods take place on its peaks.
---

Olympus is where the gods have their homes (1.18) and from whose peaks Apollo strides down in anger (1.44). Athena returns there from the quarrel (1.221), and Zeus returns there from the Ethiopians (1.494). The book's last hundred lines are set there: Thetis' supplication, the quarrel of Zeus and Hera, the feast, and the gods going to bed in the houses Hephaestus built (1.606–608).
