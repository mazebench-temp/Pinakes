---
summary: The chief sacrificial victims of Book 1. Chryses has burned the thighs of bulls and goats for Apollo, and the army offers him hecatombs of bulls and goats on the shore.
---

In Book 1 the bull is twice paired with the [[goats|goat]]. Chryses reminds Apollo of the thighs “of bulls and goats” he has burned ({{ref:Iliad 1.40-41}}), and after the purification the army offers perfect [[hecatomb|hecatombs]] “of bulls and goats” on the shore ({{ref:Iliad 1.315-316}}; see [[sacrifice|sacrifice]]).
