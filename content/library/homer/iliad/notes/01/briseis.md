ἀέκουσα, "unwilling". It is a single word, but Homer rarely gives captives any feelings at all. Achilles calls her "the girl whom the sons of the Achaeans gave me" (1.392), and he tells Agamemnon he will not fight for her with his hands, "since you who gave her are taking her away" (1.299).

Thetis calls her "the fair-belted woman" (1.429) as she leaves Achilles angry over her.
