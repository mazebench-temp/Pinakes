τιμὴν ἀρνύμενοι Μενελάῳ: "to win satisfaction for Menelaus". Achilles reduces the war to a private debt: the Trojans owe Menelaus τιμή, compensation, and Achilles fights to collect it, though he has no quarrel of his own. Helen is not named in Book 1. See [[war-for-menelaus]].

He is also one of the two sons of Atreus whom Chryses begs "most of all" (1.16, 1.375).
