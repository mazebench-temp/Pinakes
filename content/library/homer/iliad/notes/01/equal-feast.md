The line appears first at the sacrificial feast at Chryse, where men and god share the victim, and then at the feast of the gods on Olympus, after the quarrel of Zeus and Hera ([[feast-type-scene]]). The repetition gains force from the rest of the book. The quarrel in the assembly was about unequal shares: Achilles never receives a prize equal to Agamemnon's, though he does most of the fighting ({{ref:Iliad 1.163-168}}). The two feasts that close the book are its only scenes of fair shares, and Achilles is at neither.
