ἠγάθεος = ἀγα- (very) + θεῖος (divine): sacred

{{quote:b31ec9d9-8c86-58ba-a7ca-97fb0f9eb0f4}}

τηλόθεν ἐξ ἀπίης γαίης: "far away, from a distant land". The Lapiths summoned Nestor all the way from Pylos, which is the measure of his reputation.
