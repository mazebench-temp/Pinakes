πατὴρ ἀνδρῶν τε θεῶν τε: Zeus is "father of men and gods". On Olympus the gods rise "to meet their father" (1.534), and Hephaestus calls him "our dear father Zeus" (1.578).

Achilles' father appears only through his halls (1.396). The father of Briareus is surpassed by his son (1.404), and Thetis' father, the Old Man of the Sea, is the one she sits beside in the depths (1.358). See [[fathers-and-daughters]] and [[zeus]].
