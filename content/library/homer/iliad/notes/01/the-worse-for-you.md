Agamemnon sends his heralds with the warning that if Achilles resists, he will come himself with more men. Zeus warns Hera that her suspicion will only estrange her from him. The same words in the mouths of the king of men and the king of gods show how closely the quarrel on Olympus is modeled on the quarrel in the camp ([[ring-composition]]).
