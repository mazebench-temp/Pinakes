The heart deliberates. When Agamemnon has spoken, grief comes upon Achilles, and his ἦτορ within his shaggy chest is torn two ways (διάνδιχα μερμήριξεν): draw the sword and kill, or check his anger. The shaggy chest is a mark of strength; elsewhere a warrior's heart can itself be called “shaggy” (λάσιον κῆρ, 2.851).
