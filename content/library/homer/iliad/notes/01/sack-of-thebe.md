---
summary: Achilles tells Thetis how the Achaeans sacked Thebe, the sacred city of Eetion, and chose Chryseis from its spoils for Agamemnon.
---

Achilles begins his account to Thetis at the source: the raid that produced Chryseis. The four lines connect the plague back to Achilles' own victory. The prize the Achaeans chose for Agamemnon from Achilles' conquest is the one whose return costs Achilles Briseis (see [[chryseis]] and [[briseis]]).
