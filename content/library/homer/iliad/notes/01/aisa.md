{{quote:376f719c-c3b5-5071-8c14-d3a2262bc62c}}

Thetis uses the word twice in her first reply to her son. His αἶσα is short, “not long at all”, and she bore him to an evil αἶσα. She wishes that he could at least sit by the ships free of tears and harm.

The word joins Achilles' short life to the logic of honor. A small portion of life, he has just told her, ought to be made up with a large portion of honor (1.352–354).
