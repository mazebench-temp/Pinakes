---
summary: In Book 1 it appears only in the epithet αἰγίοχος, “aegis-bearing”, twice of Zeus.
---

The aegis itself does not appear in Book 1. It is present only in Zeus's epithet αἰγίοχος (αἰγίς + ἔχω, “holding the aegis”): Achilles addresses Athena as “child of aegis-bearing Zeus”, and she returns “to the house of aegis-bearing Zeus”.
