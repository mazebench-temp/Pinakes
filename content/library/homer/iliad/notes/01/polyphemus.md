summary: Nestor names “godlike Polyphemus” among the Lapiths of his youth, the strongest men he ever saw (1.262–264).
# The Lapith's forms are claimed here, in the Iliad only: the Cyclops of the Odyssey (polyphemus-the-cyclops) has the same name.
grc: [Πολύφημον]
en: [Polyphemus]
---

Nestor's list of the men of his youth, “Caeneus and Exadius and godlike Polyphemus” (1.264), is the only place the Iliad names him (see [[nestor-and-the-centaurs]] and [[antitheos]]).
