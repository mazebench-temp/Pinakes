---
summary: Thetis' words to her weeping son (1.363): “speak out, do not hide it in your mind, so that we both may know.”
---

{{quote:19791026-55d9-51c8-8a09-d60adef79847}}

The first line returns whole in Book 18, and the second in part (“speak out, do not hide it”), when Thetis comes to Achilles again, after the death of Patroclus (Il. 18.73–74). In Book 16 Achilles himself speaks the second line to Patroclus, who stands weeping before him for the Achaeans (Il. 16.19). The formula joins three scenes of grief: the loss of Briseis, the ruin of the army, and the loss of Patroclus. See also [[called-him-by-name]] and [[groaning-heavily]].
