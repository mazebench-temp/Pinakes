αἰχμηταί: "spearmen". Achilles' point is that the war is not his own. The Trojans never raided his cattle or his crops; mountains and the sea lie between.

{{quote:c6a8eeb9-d014-5817-9b7c-01c6c21cb99b}}

Nestor imagines their joy at the quarrel.

{{quote:f51cc86c-36ec-5dd4-aff7-a7ebd7d0d966}}

Achilles asks Zeus to help them "pen the Achaeans in among their ships' sterns". His request turns the Trojans into the instrument of his revenge.

{{quote:4401c1aa-123e-553e-8470-b966895dd040}}

Thetis repeats the request, and Zeus grants it.
