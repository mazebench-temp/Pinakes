The line gives Calchas his authority before he speaks ([[best-of-bird-seers]], [[with-good-intent]]). Its subject is time, and the scene depends on it: Calchas explains the past (the dishonoring of Chryses) to account for the present (the plague) and to prescribe the future (the return of Chryseis and a hecatomb).

The seer's knowledge is set against the king's blindness. Achilles says that Agamemnon “does not know how to look both before and after” ([[before-and-after]], {{ref:Iliad 1.343}}).
