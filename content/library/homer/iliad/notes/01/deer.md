---
summary: The emblem of cowardice in Achilles' insult: Agamemnon has “the eyes of a dog and the heart of a deer”.
---

Achilles joins two animals in one line of abuse. Agamemnon has “the eyes of a dog and the heart of a deer”: shameless and cowardly at once. He has never dared to arm for battle or go on [[ambush|ambush]] with the best of the Achaeans ({{ref:Iliad 1.225-228}}; see [[dog-eyes-deer-heart]] and [[dogs|dogs]]).

{{quote:f6f786dc-f769-511a-993b-bf95a3c926cc}}

Agamemnon uses the same image on his own troops: “Why do you stand like this, dazed, like fawns” (Iliad 4.243).
