---
summary: Thetis comes at dawn with the armor, finds her son lying over Patroclus, takes his hand and calls him “My child” (19.4–8). She promises to keep his friend's body sound, tells him to renounce his wrath and arm, and puts might into him (19.29–39). Achilles knows that he will die “far from my dear father and mother” (19.422).
refs: [19.1-39, 19.422]
---

Achilles answers her, “My mother” (19.21), and asks for help for the body (see [[thetis]] and [[called-him-by-name]]). Her care extends to his friend: she keeps the flies from Patroclus and drips ambrosia and nectar into his nostrils (19.29–39; see [[honoring-the-dead]]). Zeus speaks to Athena as a father to a daughter, “My child” (19.342).
