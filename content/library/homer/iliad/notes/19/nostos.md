---
summary: Achilles had hoped that he alone would die at Troy, “and that you would go back to Phthia”, bringing his son from Scyros to see his house (19.328–333).
---

The homecoming Achilles gave up for himself he had kept for Patroclus, and Patroclus is dead (see [[the-two-fates-of-achilles]], [[phthia]] and [[neoptolemus]]). Achilles now expects to die “far from my dear father and mother” (19.422).
