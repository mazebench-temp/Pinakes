---
summary: Achilles answers Agamemnon's offer of gifts: “But now let us remember the joy of battle / at once” (19.148–149).
---

μνησώμεθα χάρμης, “let us remember the joy of battle”, is the narrator's formula for an army that turns to fight again (μνήσαντο δὲ χάρμης, 4.222, 8.252, 14.441, 15.380), spoken here as an order in the first person. Achilles wants battle, not gifts (see [[doron]]).
