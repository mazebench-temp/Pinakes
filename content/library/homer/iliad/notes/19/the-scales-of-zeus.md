---
summary: Odysseus tells Achilles that men soon have their fill of the din of battle, whose harvest is smallest "when he tips the scales, / Zeus, who is the dispenser of war for men" (19.223–224).
grc: ["κλίνῃσι τάλαντα"]
---

κλίνῃσι τάλαντα, only here in Homer, means that Zeus has decided the battle, as when he holds out his golden scales at 8.69 (Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 19.223). See [[satiety-of-battle]], [[dispenser-of-war]] and [[zeus]].
