---
summary: The seven women among Agamemnon's gifts are led out, “women skilled in flawless handiwork,” as he promised them in Book 9.
---

“and quickly they led out women skilled in flawless handiwork, / seven, and the eighth was fair-cheeked Briseis” (19.245–246; [[fair-cheeked]], [[briseis]]). The verse is the one in which Agamemnon promised “seven women skilled in flawless handiwork,” women of Lesbos, and Odysseus repeated the promise to Achilles (9.128, 9.270; [[lesbos]]). What was offered in Book 9 is now delivered, with the tripods, the cauldrons and the horses of the same list (19.243–244; [[the-gifts-of-agamemnon]], [[captive-women]]). Here, as there, ἀμύμων praises the work of the women's hands.
