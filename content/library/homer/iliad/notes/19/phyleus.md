# Φυλεΐδην (19.239), only here, is Meges' patronymic and counts for the father too.
grc: [Φυλεΐδην]
---

Φυλεΐδην τε Μέγητα, “and Meges, son of Phyleus” (19.239; see [[meges]]), is one of the young men whom Odysseus takes to Agamemnon's hut to bring out the gifts for Achilles (19.238–248; see [[the-gifts-of-agamemnon]]). The poem often names Meges only as “the son of Phyleus” (5.72, 10.110, 15.519, 15.528, 16.313); here, as at 13.692, the patronymic stands beside his name.
