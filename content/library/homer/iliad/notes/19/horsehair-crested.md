---
summary: Achilles' new helmet shines like a star, “the helmet with its horsehair crest”; the book adds the nominative ἵππουρις.
grc: [ἵππουρις]
en: [with its horsehair crest]
---

“and lifting the helmet, / he set it, heavy, on his head; and like a star it shone, / the helmet with its horsehair crest, and around it waved the plumes / of gold, which Hephaestus had set thick about the crest” (19.380–383; [[helmet]], [[heavy-helmet]], [[the-making-of-the-arms]]). Walter Leaf thinks 19.382 brought in from 22.315, where Achilles is running and the waving plumes have more point, and notes the awkward repetition of τρυφάλεια (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 19.382; see the translation's note on 19.382–383). The nominative ἵππουρις is new to the entry.
