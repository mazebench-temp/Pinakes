---
summary: Achilles' heart goes without drink and food “for longing for you” (σῇ ποθῇ, 19.321).
---

σῇ ποθῇ is “longing for you”, the possessive standing for the object, as ἐμὴν … ἀγγελίην, “the grievous news of me” (19.336–337; Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 19.336). In Book 1 Achilles swore that the Achaeans would long for him (1.240); now he longs for Patroclus (see [[fasting]] and [[lament]]).
