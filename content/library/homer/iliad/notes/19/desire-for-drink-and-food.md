---
summary: The words of the formula that ends a feast are turned to fasting. Odysseus says that those who survive the war “must remember drink and food” (19.231), and Achilles says that his heart “goes without drink and food” for longing for Patroclus (19.320).
refs: [19.231, 19.320]
---

πόσιος καὶ ἐδητύος, “drink and food”, fills the same place in the verse as in the formula, but with other verbs: μεμνῆσθαι, “remember”, and ἄκμηνον, “going without” (see [[fasting]] and [[feast]]).
