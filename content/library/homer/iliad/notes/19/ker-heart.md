---
summary: Achilles and Agamemnon raged in strife “grieving at heart” (19.57), and in his lament for Patroclus Achilles says that “my heart / goes without drink and food, though they are here within” (19.319–320).
---

ἀχνυμένω κῆρ, “grieving at heart”, is the dual of a phrase used of single men (see [[achos]]). The heart that goes without food is the heart that fasts in mourning (see [[fasting]] and [[pothe]]).
