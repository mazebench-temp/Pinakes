---
summary: The book opens as “saffron-robed Dawn” rises from the streams of Oceanus, and Thetis comes to the ships with the armor.
---

“Now saffron-robed Dawn rose from the streams of Oceanus / to bring light to the immortals and to mortals; / and she came to the ships, bringing the gifts from the god” (19.1–3; [[eos]], [[oceanus]], [[immortals]], [[thetis]]). The day it opens is the day on which Achilles returns to the war and kills Hector (Books 19–22). The book of the armor begins as Book 8 began, with the epithet that the Iliad keeps for decisive dawns (8.1). See [[dawn-line]].
