---
summary: Agamemnon names Ate “the eldest daughter of Zeus, who blinds all,” the daughter Zeus flings from Olympus when she has blinded him.
---

“Blind Folly is the eldest daughter of Zeus, who blinds all, / ruinous; her feet are tender, for she does not step / upon the ground, but walks over the heads of men” (19.91–93; [[ate]], [[tender]]). πρέσβα, the word of Hera's title “august goddess,” is rendered here “eldest” ([[august-goddess]]). The title is the one Phoenix gave the Prayers, who follow Ate to heal what she has harmed: “For the Prayers too are the daughters of great Zeus” (9.502; [[litai]]). In Agamemnon's story the daughter blinds her own father, and Zeus seizes her by the hair and hurls her from the starry sky (19.95–131; [[of-shining-tresses]], [[starry]], [[zeus]]).
