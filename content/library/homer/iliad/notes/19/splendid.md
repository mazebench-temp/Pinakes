---
summary: Achilles delights in holding “the splendid gifts of the god,” and tries the new armor to see whether “his splendid limbs moved freely in it.”
---

**The gifts of the god.** “and he delighted in holding in his hands the splendid gifts of the god” (19.18; [[the-making-of-the-arms]], [[doron]]). In Book 18 Achilles told his mother that the armor Hector took had been “the splendid gifts that the gods gave to Peleus” (18.84); the new armor, ἀγλαὰ δῶρα again, is the gift of one god to the son.

**The limbs.** “And brilliant Achilles tried himself in his armor, / whether it fitted him and his splendid limbs moved freely in it” (19.384–385; [[brilliant]], [[arming-scene]]).
