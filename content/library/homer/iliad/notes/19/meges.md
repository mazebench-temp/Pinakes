# Μέγητα and Φυλεΐδην (19.239) occur only here; the patronymic counts for Phyleus too.
grc: [Μέγητα, Φυλεΐδην]
---

{{quote:1c4067cf-0c8e-52a0-b6f1-1106e66eb419}}

Φυλεΐδην τε Μέγητα, “and Meges, son of Phyleus” (19.239; see [[phyleus]]). Agamemnon told Odysseus to choose “young men, the best of all the Achaeans” to bring the gifts from his ship (19.193), and with Odysseus “the other young men of the Achaeans carried the gifts” (19.248; see [[odysseus]] and [[the-gifts-of-agamemnon]]). So the leader of the forty ships of Dulichium and the Echinae is counted among the young men (2.625–630; see [[dulichium]]). Of those named with him, Thoas and Meriones were among the young men whom Poseidon rallied at the ships, “Shame, Argives, young men!” (13.92–95; see [[thoas]] and [[meriones]]), and in Book 15 Meges stood with Teucer and Meriones among the best men against Hector (15.301–302).
