---
summary: Agamemnon promises gifts that “will satisfy your heart”; the translation renders μενοεικέα so here.
en: [satisfy your heart]
---

“But if you wish, wait, eager though you are for Ares, / and attendants will take the gifts from my ship / and bring them, so that you may see that what I give will satisfy your heart” (19.142–144; [[the-gifts-of-agamemnon]], [[agamemnon]]). The word that described the feasts of the embassy night in Book 9 (9.90, 9.227) is given to the gifts that the embassy offered. Achilles answers that the gifts are Agamemnon's to give or keep, and asks for the war (19.146–153; [[most-glorious]]).
