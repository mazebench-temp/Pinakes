---
summary: In Agamemnon's story “Zeus did not perceive her treachery at all” (19.112), and Odysseus claims to surpass Achilles “in understanding” (νοήματι, 19.218).
grc: [νοήματι]
en: [understanding]
---

οὐ … ἐνόησεν, “did not perceive”, is the failure of the god's mind on which Hera's trick depends (see [[ate]] and [[truth-and-deception]]). νόημα is the thought that νοέω produces, here a man's wit, the advantage of the older man (19.218–219; see [[metis]]).
