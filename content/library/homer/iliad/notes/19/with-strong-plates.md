---
summary: Corselets “with strong plates” pour out of the ships with the helmets, shields and spears as the Achaeans arm.
---

“so then, thick and fast, the helmets, shining brightly, / were carried out from the ships, and bossed shields / and corselets with strong plates and ash spears” (19.359–361; [[corselet]], [[bossed]], [[ash-spear]], [[born-of-the-clear-sky]]). Walter Leaf suspects the verse as a late addition, betrayed by its awkward position (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 19.361; see the translation's note on 19.361).
