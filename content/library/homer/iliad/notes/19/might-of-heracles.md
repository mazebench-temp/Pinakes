---
summary: Agamemnon's story of Ate begins “on the day when mighty Heracles / was to be born to Alcmene in well-crowned Thebes.”
---

“Hera, female though she was, deceived with her treachery / on the day when mighty Heracles / was to be born to Alcmene in well-crowned Thebes” (19.97–99; [[heracles]], [[alcmene]], [[well-crowned]], [[thebes]]). The periphrasis names the hero by his strength before he is born, in the story of how Hera's trick put him under the labors of Eurystheus (19.103–133; [[eurystheus]], [[hera]], [[ate]]).
