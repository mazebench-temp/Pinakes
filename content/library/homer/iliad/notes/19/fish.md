# ἰχθύσιν also catches 21.122, where Achilles tells the dead Lycaon to lie among the fish.
grc: [ἰχθύσιν]
en: [fishes]
---

ῥῖψʼ ἐπιδινήσας βόσιν ἰχθύσιν, “whirling it around, food for the fishes” (19.268). The boar on which Agamemnon swore is thrown away, not eaten (see [[boar]] and [[sea]]). βόσις, “food”, is found only here in Homer. In the books that follow the fish feed on the dead (21.120–127; see the entry). In the simile of the shield the sea is ἰχθυόεις, “fish-filled” (19.378; see [[fish-filled]] and [[the-fire-seen-by-sailors]]).
