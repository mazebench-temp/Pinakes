---
summary: Briseis calls herself “wretched” as she begins her lament over Patroclus; the book adds the dative δειλῇ.
grc: ["=δειλῇ"]
---

“Patroclus, dearest to my heart, wretched that I am” (19.287; [[briseis]], [[dear-to-my-heart]]). The word of pity that speakers address to others is turned on herself, as Thetis turned it in Book 18, “Ah me, wretched that I am” (18.54). The dative δειλῇ is new to the entry.
