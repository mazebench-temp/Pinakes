---
summary: Odysseus persuades Agamemnon, who rejoices “to hear your word”, and the gifts are brought at once: “No sooner was the word spoken than the work was done” (19.185, 19.242). He does not persuade Achilles to eat, and Achilles asks the elders, “if any of my dear companions will obey me”, not to press him (19.305).
refs: [19.185-187, 19.242, 19.305]
---

ἐπιπείθεθʼ, “will obey”, is to Walter Leaf “if any of you will yield to my wishes” (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 19.305; see [[peitho]] and [[supplication]]).
