---
summary: Achilles begins his lament: “Truly once, you too, most ill-fated one, dearest of my companions.”
---

“Truly once, you too, most ill-fated one, dearest of my companions, / would yourself set a pleasant meal before me in the hut” (19.315–316; [[patroclus]], [[laros]], [[lament]]). It is the first use of the word in Homer; the others are spoken over Hector, by Priam and twice by Andromache (22.428, 22.485, 24.727).
