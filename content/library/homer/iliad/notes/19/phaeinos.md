---
summary: Automedon takes up “the shining whip” and mounts behind Achilles' horses.
---

“And he, taking the shining whip / that fitted his hand, sprang up behind the horses, / Automedon” (19.395–397; [[automedon]], [[well-joined]], [[the-horses-of-achilles]]). μάστιγα φαεινήν is the whip that Odysseus forgot to take from Rhesus' chariot (10.500), and the one Apollo will strike from Diomedes' hands in the chariot race (23.384). In Book 17 the same Automedon tried in vain to drive the horses on with the whip (17.429–430); now they are yoked for Achilles.
