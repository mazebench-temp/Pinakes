---
summary: The two sons of Atreus are named together once, among the six who stay with Achilles when he refuses food and sends the other kings away (19.310).
---

δοιὼ δʼ Ἀτρεΐδα μενέτην, “but the two sons of Atreus stayed” (19.310): the name, the numeral and the verb are all dual (see [[agamemnon]] and [[menelaus]]). With Odysseus, Nestor, Idomeneus and Phoenix they stay to comfort Achilles in his grief (19.310–312; see [[achilles]] and [[elders]]). The dual Ἀτρεΐδα is found elsewhere in the Iliad only in Book 1, where Chryses begs “most of all the two sons of Atreus, marshals of the people” (1.16 = 1.375). Chryses appealed to the pair as the army's commanders, and Agamemnon's refusal began the troubles that end here (1.22–32).
