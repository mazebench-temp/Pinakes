---
summary: The blaze of Achilles' shield reaches the upper air, “the fine shield, intricately worked.”
---

“so from the shield of Achilles the blaze reached the upper air, / the fine shield, intricately worked; and lifting the helmet, / he set it, heavy, on his head” (19.379–381; [[the-shield-of-achilles]], [[helmet]]). The adjective δαιδάλεος names the shield by the work Hephaestus put into it, “many intricate designs” (18.482). In the same book the Myrmidons cannot look at the new armor when “all its intricate designs rang,” and Achilles takes his fill of delight “gazing on the intricate designs” (19.13, 19.19); the noun δαίδαλα of those verses is not claimed by this entry.
