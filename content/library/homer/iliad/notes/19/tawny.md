---
summary: Among Agamemnon's gifts the young men bring out “twenty gleaming cauldrons,” in the verse of his promise in Book 9.
---

“seven tripods they brought from the hut, which he had promised him, / and twenty gleaming cauldrons, and twelve horses” (19.243–244; [[the-gifts-of-agamemnon]], [[tripod]]). The second verse is the one of Agamemnon's list in Book 9 (9.123), and Walter Leaf notes the repetition (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 19.244). αἴθων here is the gleam of bronze, rendered “gleaming.”
