---
summary: Agamemnon ends his oath and cuts the boar's throat “with the pitiless bronze.”
---

“He spoke, and cut the boar's throat with the pitiless bronze” (19.266; [[boar]], [[oath]], [[sacrifice]]). The verse of the oath victim recalls Book 3, where Agamemnon cut the throats of the lambs at the oath of the duel with the same words for the bronze (3.292). The knife is the one that “always hung beside the great sheath of his sword” (19.252–253; [[knife]]).
