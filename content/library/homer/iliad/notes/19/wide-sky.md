---
summary: Agamemnon, cutting the boar's hairs, prays “looking up into the wide sky.”
---

“And praying he spoke, looking up into the wide sky: / ‘Now let Zeus be witness first, highest and best of the gods’” (19.257–258; [[prayer]], [[oath]], [[most-high]]). The half-line ἰδὼν εἰς οὐρανὸν εὐρύν ends verses of complaint, as when Menelaus groans that his sword has broken on Paris' helmet (3.364), and of prayer, as when the army prays that the lot fall to Ajax and that he win the duel with Hector (7.178, 7.201). Here it opens the oath that ends the quarrel.
