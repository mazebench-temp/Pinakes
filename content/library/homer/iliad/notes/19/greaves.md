---
summary: Achilles puts on the greaves first, “fine ones, fitted with silver ankle-pieces”, in the two verses of every arming scene (19.369–370).
---

κνημῖδας μὲν πρῶτα περὶ κνήμῃσιν ἔθηκε / καλὰς ἀργυρέοισιν ἐπισφυρίοις ἀραρυίας: “First he put the greaves around his shins, / fine ones, fitted with silver ankle-pieces” (19.369–370 = 3.330–331, 11.17–18, 16.131–132; see [[arming-scene]]). Thetis had asked Hephaestus for “fine greaves fitted with ankle-pieces”, and he made them “of soft tin” (18.459, 18.613; see [[tin]], [[heanos]] and the translation's note on 18.613). The formula gives them silver ankle-pieces as it gave them to Paris' greaves (see [[gold-and-silver]]); the making of the arms says nothing of the ankle-pieces.
