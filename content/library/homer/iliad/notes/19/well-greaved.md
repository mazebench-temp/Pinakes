---
summary: “The well-greaved Achaeans rejoiced” when Achilles renounced his wrath.
---

“So he spoke, and the well-greaved Achaeans rejoiced / that the great-hearted son of Peleus had renounced his wrath” (19.74–75; [[achaeans]], [[great-hearted]], [[menis]]). The army's standing epithet marks the whole host's joy at the end of the quarrel that had cost it so many lives (19.61–62; [[untold]]).
