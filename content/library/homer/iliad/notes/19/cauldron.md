---
summary: Twenty gleaming cauldrons are carried out of Agamemnon's hut among the gifts for Achilles, in the verse of the promise of Book 9 (19.244 = 9.123).
---

αἴθωνας δὲ λέβητας ἐείκοσι, δώδεκα δʼ ἵππους, “and twenty gleaming cauldrons, and twelve horses” (19.244 = 9.123 = 9.265). The cauldrons come after the seven tripods (19.243; see [[tripod]]), and with the rest of the gifts the Myrmidons carry them to Achilles' ship and set them in his huts (19.278–280; see [[the-gifts-of-agamemnon]]). αἴθων, “gleaming” here, is “tawny” of horses, lions and eagles, and is said of iron as well as of bronze vessels (see [[tawny]]).
