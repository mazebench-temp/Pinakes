summary: The gathering of Achilles' second assembly is told in a variant of the verse, “And when all the Achaeans were gathered together” (19.54), and the next verse is the one that followed the formula in Book 1: “swift-footed Achilles stood up among them and spoke” (19.55 = 1.58).
grc: ["πάντες ἀολλίσθησαν", "τοῖσι δʼ ἀνιστάμενος μετέφη"]
en: [were gathered together, stood up among them and spoke]
---

ἀολλίζω, “gather together”, from ἀολλής, “all together”, replaces ἤγερθεν ὁμηγερέες. In Book 1 Achilles called the assembly to ask why Apollo was angry (1.54–58); here he calls it to end his own wrath (see [[assembly-type-scene]] and [[menis]]).
