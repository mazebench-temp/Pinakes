---
summary: Athena leaps down from the sky “like a long-winged, shrill-voiced bird of prey.”
---

“So saying he urged on Athena, who was eager already; / and she, like a long-winged, shrill-voiced bird of prey, / leapt down from the sky through the upper air” (19.349–351; [[athena]], [[like-a-bird-of-prey]], [[harpe]], [[long-winged]]). The bird's cry belongs to the likeness only: Athena drips the nectar and ambrosia into Achilles' breast and goes back to her father's house (19.352–356; [[lovely]], [[eristhenes]]).
