---
summary: Agamemnon offers his gifts again in the verse of Book 9: “I am willing to make amends and give a boundless ransom” (19.138 = 9.120).
---

ἄποινα is the word for the price of a captive or a body (see [[boundless-ransom]]). Agamemnon uses it for the price of an injury. Walter Leaf notes that 19.137–138 are copied from 9.119–120, and that they fall among the verses marked with obeli in one manuscript (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 19.136, 19.137; see the translation's note on 19.136–141). This time the ransom is paid (19.238–281; see [[the-gifts-of-agamemnon]] and [[propitiation]]).
