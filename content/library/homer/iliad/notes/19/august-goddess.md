“Blind Folly is the eldest daughter of Zeus, who blinds all, / ruinous” (19.91–92; [[ate]], [[daughter-of-zeus]], [[deadly]]). πρέσβα, which in Hera's whole-verse title is rendered “august” (5.721, 14.194), is rendered here by its first sense, “eldest.” The phrase gives Ate the rank of the first-born among the children of Zeus, and in Agamemnon's story she blinds the father himself (19.95–113; [[zeus]]). The phrase πρέσβα Διὸς θυγάτηρ is new to the entry.
