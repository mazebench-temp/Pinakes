---
summary: Thetis, bringing the armor at dawn, stands among the weeping Myrmidons, “brilliant among goddesses.”
---

“She found her dear son lying with his arms around Patroclus, / weeping loudly; and many of his companions around him / were weeping; and she stood among them, brilliant among goddesses” (19.4–6; [[thetis]], [[the-making-of-the-arms]]). In Book 18 the formula was given to Charis as she led Thetis into the house of Hephaestus, where the armor was made (18.388); now it is Thetis' own as she brings it. It is hers once more, when she takes up her dark veil to go to Zeus at Iris' call (24.93). See [[brilliant]].
