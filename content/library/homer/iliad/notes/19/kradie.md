---
summary: Odysseus asks Achilles to bear with his counsel: “So let your heart be patient with my words” (19.220).
---

ἐπιτλήτω, “let it be patient, acquiesce”, with the dative occurs only here (Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 19.220). The heart that must endure is the κραδίη, and in the Odyssey Odysseus says the same to his own: “Endure, my heart; you endured a worse thing once” (*Odyssey* 20.18). See [[satiety-of-battle]].
