---
summary: The book ends as Achilles drives his “single-hoofed horses” among the foremost.
---

“He spoke, and with a cry drove his single-hoofed horses among the foremost” (19.424; [[the-horses-of-achilles]], [[achilles]]). After Xanthus' prophecy and his answer, “I will not stop until I have given the Trojans their fill of war” (19.423), the plainest epithet of horses closes the book.
