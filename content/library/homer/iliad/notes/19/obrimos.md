---
summary: Xanthus begins his prophecy with “mighty Achilles,” the only time the epithet of Ares and Hector is given to Achilles; the book adds the elided ὄβριμʼ.
grc: ["ὄβριμʼ"]
---

“Yes, we will save you still, this time at least, mighty Achilles; / but the day of your destruction is near” (19.408–409; [[xanthus-the-horse]], [[achilles]], [[day-of-destruction]]). ὄβριμος is the epithet of Ares and of Hector (8.473, 10.200); here the horse gives it to Achilles in the verse that foretells his death. The elided vocative ὄβριμʼ is new to the entry.
