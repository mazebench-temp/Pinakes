---
summary: Achilles wants the army to see him again “among the foremost / destroying the battalions of the Trojans with his bronze spear” (19.151–152), and the book ends with him driving “among the foremost” (19.424). Odysseus warns that the din of battle will last long “once the battalions / of men first engage” (19.158–159), and Xanthus tells how Apollo killed Patroclus “among the champions” (19.414 = 18.456).
---

The arming of the whole army comes before Achilles' own: helmets, shields, corselets and spears pour out of the ships, and he arms in their midst (19.357–364; see [[arming-scene]]). The battle order itself is not told until the armies meet in the next book (see [[the-fourth-day-of-battle]]).
