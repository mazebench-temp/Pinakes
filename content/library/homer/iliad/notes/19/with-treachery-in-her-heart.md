---
summary: In Agamemnon's story, “queen Hera, with treachery in her heart,” asks Zeus to swear his oath.
---

“And queen Hera, with treachery in her heart, addressed him: / ‘You will prove false, and hereafter will not put fulfillment to your word’” (19.106–107; [[hera]], [[queenly]], [[olympian]]). The whole verse introduced three of Hera's speeches in the deception of Zeus in Book 14 (14.197, 14.300, 14.329). In Agamemnon's apology it introduces the deception that blinded Zeus: “and Zeus did not perceive her treachery at all” (19.112; [[ate]]).
