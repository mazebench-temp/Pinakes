# Κρειοντιάδην (19.240), found only here, is Lycomedes' patronymic and counts for the father too.
# Creon of Thebes (creon-of-thebes) excepts the line.
grc: [Κρειοντιάδην]
---

καὶ Κρειοντιάδην Λυκομήδεα, “and Lycomedes, son of Creon” (19.240; see [[lycomedes]] and [[the-gifts-of-agamemnon]]). The patronymic in -ιάδης occurs only here; in Book 9 the son was Κρείοντος υἱόν, “the son of Creon” (9.84). The poem tells nothing more of the father. He is not Creon of Thebes, the father of Megara (*Odyssey* 11.269; see [[creon-of-thebes]]).
