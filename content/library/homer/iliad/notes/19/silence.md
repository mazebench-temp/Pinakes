---
summary: While Agamemnon prays over the boar, the Argives “all sat there in silence in their places, / … listening to the king, as was right” (19.255–256).
---

The silence of the army is the silence due to a prayer and an oath, not the silence of awe or of refusal (see [[prayer]] and [[oath]]). ἐπʼ αὐτόφιν, “in their places”, is strange to Walter Leaf, who reports Bekker's conjecture αὐτόθι (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 19.255; see the translation's note on 19.252–256). Earlier in the same assembly Agamemnon complained that in the din of men no one could listen or speak (19.81–82; see [[assembly]]).
