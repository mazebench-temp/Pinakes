---
summary: In Agamemnon's story Hera brings Zeus the news of Eurystheus' birth: “Father Zeus of the gleaming thunderbolt, I will put a word in your mind.”
---

“And she herself, bringing the news, addressed Zeus, son of Cronus: / ‘Father Zeus of the gleaming thunderbolt, I will put a word in your mind: / now a brave man is born who will be lord of the Argives’” (19.120–122; [[hera]], [[father-zeus]], [[son-of-cronus]], [[eurystheus]]). The first use of the epithet in the poem comes from the goddess who has just outwitted the god of the thunderbolt: Ate has blinded him, and his oath holds (19.95–113; [[ate]], [[brave]]).
