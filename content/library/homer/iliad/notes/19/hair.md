---
summary: At the oath Agamemnon begins by "cutting the first hairs from the boar" and lifts his hands to Zeus (19.254), as at the oath of Book 3, where hair was cut from the heads of the lambs (3.273). Leto is "of the lovely hair" (19.413).
---

Walter Leaf takes ἀπὸ τρίχας ἀρξάμενος as the cutting of the hairs as first-fruits of the victim, the ritual word standing for the act (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 19.254). See [[oath]], [[sacrifice]] and [[boar]].
