---
summary: The heart of a fed man is bold: “the heart in his breast is bold, and his limbs / do not grow weary” (19.169–170). Achilles will not let food “sate my dear heart” (19.307), and as he arms “into his heart / entered unbearable grief” (19.366–367).
---

Odysseus' argument places courage in the ἦτορ and makes it depend on food (see [[tharsos]] and [[food-and-wine-are-might-and-courage]]); Achilles keeps his ἦτορ empty, and grief fills it (see [[achos]] and [[fasting]]).
