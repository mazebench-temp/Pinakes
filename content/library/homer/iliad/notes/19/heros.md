summary: Thetis tells Achilles to call “the Achaean heroes” to assembly, and he rouses them (19.34, 19.41); Agamemnon addresses them as “Friends, Danaan heroes, attendants of Ares” (19.78); the ash spear is “death for heroes” (19.391).
---

The verse of the spear is that of Book 16, where Patroclus left it behind (19.388–391 = 16.141–144; see [[the-pelian-ash]]).
