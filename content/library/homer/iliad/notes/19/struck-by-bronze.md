---
summary: Achilles fears that flies will enter Patroclus' body “through the wounds the bronze has struck.”
---

“I fear that in the meantime into the valiant son of Menoetius / flies will make their way, through the wounds the bronze has struck, / and breed worms, and defile the body” (19.24–26; [[flies]], [[son-of-menoetius]], [[strong-spear]]). The wounds are those of Euphorbus' spear between the shoulders and of Hector's in the flank (16.806–808, 16.820–821; [[the-death-of-patroclus]]). Thetis answers that she will keep the flies away, and drips ambrosia and nectar into the body (19.29–39; [[silver-footed]], [[slain-in-battle]], [[red]]).
