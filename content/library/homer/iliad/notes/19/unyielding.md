---
summary: Odysseus would have the fed army fight on “without rest, forever, / our bodies clothed in unyielding bronze.”
---

“and all who survive the hateful war / must remember drink and food, so that still more / we may fight against hostile men without rest, forever, / our bodies clothed in unyielding bronze” (19.230–233; [[odysseus]], [[hostile-men]], [[bronze]]). The bronze that does not wear down is set beside the men who do, unless they eat.
