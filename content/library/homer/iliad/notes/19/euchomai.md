---
summary: The verb has both its senses in the book. In Agamemnon's story Zeus speaks “boasting among all the gods” (εὐχόμενος, 19.100); at the oath Agamemnon “prayed”, and “praying he spoke” (19.255, 19.257).
---

The boast of Zeus and the prayer of Agamemnon are both solemn declarations before an audience, the gods in one, the army in the other, and both bind the speaker (see [[prayer]], [[oath]] and [[hear-me]]).
