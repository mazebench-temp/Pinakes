# Κρειοντιάδην (19.240), found only here, is his patronymic and counts for the father (creon) too.
grc: [Κρειοντιάδην]

καὶ Κρειοντιάδην Λυκομήδεα, “and Lycomedes, son of Creon” (19.240; see [[creon]], [[odysseus]] and [[the-gifts-of-agamemnon]]). Κρειοντιάδης occurs only here; in Book 9 he was “the son of Creon, brilliant Lycomedes”, Κρείοντος υἱόν (9.84). He was then the last of the seven captains of the watch on the night of the embassy. Meriones, who kept the watch with him, is chosen with him now, and so are “the sons of glorious Nestor”, one of whom, Thrasymedes, was another of the captains (19.238; 9.80–87; see [[night-watch]], [[meriones]] and [[thrasymedes]]). He is counted among the κούρητες, the “young men” who carry the gifts (19.193, 19.248; see [[kouretes]] and [[achaeans]]).
