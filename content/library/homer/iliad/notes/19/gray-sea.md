---
summary: After Agamemnon's oath Talthybius flings the boar “into the great gulf of the gray sea.”
---

“And Talthybius flung it into the great gulf of the gray sea, / whirling it around, food for the fishes” (19.267–268; [[talthybius]], [[boar]], [[oath]]). The victim of an oath is neither burnt nor eaten but cast away (see the translation's note on 19.267). By the shore of the gray sea Achilles sat weeping apart when Briseis was taken from him (1.348–350); the same sea now receives the victim of the oath that she was untouched (19.258–265).
