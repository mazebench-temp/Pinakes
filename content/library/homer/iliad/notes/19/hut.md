---
summary: The huts frame the reconciliation. The gifts that Odysseus promised "in your huts" (19.141) are brought from Agamemnon's hut (19.191, 19.241–243), where Briseis "remained untouched" (19.263), and set in Achilles' huts (19.280); Agamemnon is to give Achilles "a rich feast in his huts" (19.179). Patroclus lies in Achilles' hut (19.211), where Briseis left him alive (19.288) and where he used to set a meal before his friend (19.316).
---

One man's quarters are κλισίαι, "huts", in the plural (19.141, 19.179, 19.263, 19.280), and κλισίη, "hut", in the singular (19.191, 19.211, 19.241, 19.243, 19.316; see the translation's row for κλισίη, κλισίαι). See [[the-gifts-of-agamemnon]], [[briseis]] and [[patroclus]].
