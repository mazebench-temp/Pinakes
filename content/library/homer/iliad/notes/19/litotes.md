summary: Odysseus' counsel is full of understatement: the battle will last “no short time” (19.157); Achilles is better than he “by no small amount” with the spear (19.217); and “it is no cause for blame that a king / should make amends” (19.182–183).
refs: [19.157, 19.182, 19.217]
---

οὐκ ὀλίγον, “no short”, “no small”, is said twice in the same speeches (19.157, 19.217; see [[no-blame-for-a-king-to-make-amends]]).
