---
summary: Achilles answers his horse's prophecy with the verb of the seer: “Xanthus, why do you prophesy death for me? You have no need to.” (19.420). Hector answered the dying Patroclus with the same question (16.859).
---

μαντεύομαι is what a μάντις does, to speak of what is hidden by a god's gift. Xanthus speaks because Hera has given him a voice (19.407; see [[xanthus-the-horse]] and [[prophecy]]). The two answers differ. Hector asked who knew whether Achilles might not die first by his spear (16.859–861); Achilles answers that he knows his fate himself (19.421; see [[death-of-achilles-foretold]]).
