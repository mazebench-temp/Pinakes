---
summary: Thetis promises to ward off “the flies, which feed on men slain in battle,” from Patroclus' body.
---

“From him I will try to ward off the savage tribes, / the flies, which feed on men slain in battle; / for even if he lies here for a full year's round, / his flesh will always be sound, or even better” (19.30–33; [[flies]], [[thetis]], [[patroclus]], [[silver-footed]]). The verse returns in Book 24, when Hermes tells Priam that the worms which feed on men slain in battle have not touched Hector's body (24.414–415).
