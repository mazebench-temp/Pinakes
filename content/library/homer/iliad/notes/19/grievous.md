“even they came to the assembly then, because Achilles / had appeared, who had long ceased from grievous battle” (19.45–46; [[assembly]], [[achilles]]). The verse was spoken of the Trojans in Book 18, who held their assembly standing, “for trembling held them all, because Achilles / had appeared” (18.247–248). The same appearance brings to the assembly the helmsmen and stewards who had always stayed by the ships (19.42–44), and fills the Trojans with fear. The verse is used a third time in Book 20, of the Achaeans' exultation and the Trojans' trembling while the gods still keep apart from the battle (20.41–44).
