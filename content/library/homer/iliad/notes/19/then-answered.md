---
summary: Thetis answers her son's fear for Patroclus' body, “Then the goddess silver-footed Thetis answered him” (19.28), in the verse with which she answered him in Book 18 (18.127).
---

In Book 18 the verse introduced her promise to come back at dawn with armor from Hephaestus (18.127–137). Now she has come, and it introduces her promise to keep the flies from the body (19.29–33; see [[thetis]], [[flies]] and [[honoring-the-dead]]).
