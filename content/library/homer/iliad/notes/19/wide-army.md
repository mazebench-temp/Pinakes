---
summary: Agamemnon sends Talthybius “through the wide army of the Achaeans” for the boar of the oath.
---

“And let Talthybius quickly, through the wide army of the Achaeans, / make ready a boar for me, to sacrifice to Zeus and the Sun” (19.196–197; [[talthybius]], [[boar]], [[oath]]). In Book 1 Talthybius and Eurybates went along the shore to take Briseis from Achilles' hut (1.320–327); here the herald goes through the army for the victim of the oath that she was untouched (19.258–265; [[taking-of-briseis]]).
