---
summary: Odysseus gives daring another source: the man who has had his fill of wine and food fights all day, and “the heart in his breast is bold” (θαρσαλέον, 19.169).
---

In Homer θάρσος is nearly always a god's gift, and in this book the gods give it too: Thetis puts into Achilles “a might full of daring” (μένος πολυθαρσές, 19.37; see [[full-of-daring]]). Odysseus' boldness comes from a meal (see [[food-and-wine-are-might-and-courage]] and [[etor]]).
