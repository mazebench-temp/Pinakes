# εὐχόμενος at 19.100: Zeus "boasting" among the gods, not praying (euchomai keeps it).
except: [19.100]
---

The prayer is an oath, and it asks for nothing but witnesses (see [[prayer-type-scene]] and [[oath]]). εὐχόμενος in Agamemnon's story is the other sense of the verb: Zeus, “boasting among all the gods”, announces the birth of a ruler of his blood (19.100; see [[euchomai]] and [[hear-me]]). Achilles' “Father Zeus, truly great are the blind follies you give to men” (19.270) is addressed to a god but asks nothing; it is a verdict on the oath, not a prayer (see [[ate]]).
