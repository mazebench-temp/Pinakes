---
summary: Thetis bids Achilles renounce his wrath “against Agamemnon, shepherd of the people”; Talthybius stands with the boar beside “the shepherd of the people” at the oath; and the new armor lifts Achilles, “the shepherd of the people,” like wings.
---

**Agamemnon.** Thetis: “But you, call the Achaean heroes to an assembly, / renouncing your wrath against Agamemnon, shepherd of the people” (19.34–35; [[menis]], [[agamemnon]]). At the oath, “Talthybius, like a god in his voice, / holding the boar in his hands, stood beside the shepherd of the people” (19.250–251; [[talthybius]], [[boar]], [[like-a-god-in-his-voice]]).

**Achilles.** The title passes to Achilles as he tries on the armor: “and it was like wings to him, and it lifted the shepherd of the people” (19.386; [[the-making-of-the-arms]]). It was his once before, when Patroclus came weeping to “Achilles, shepherd of the people” (16.2). The title of kings is given to him as he arms to lead the army again.
