---
summary: Odysseus tells Achilles that “the Achaeans cannot mourn a corpse with the belly” (πενθῆσαι, 19.225).
grc: [πενθῆσαι]
en: [mourn]
---

The verb names mourning by fasting, which Odysseus ridicules for an army in the field (Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 19.225; see [[weep-for-a-day-and-bury]] and [[fasting]]).
