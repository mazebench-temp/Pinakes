---
summary: The “bossed shields” are carried out of the ships with the helmets, corselets and spears as the Achaeans arm.
---

“so then, thick and fast, the helmets, shining brightly, / were carried out from the ships, and bossed shields / and corselets with strong plates and ash spears” (19.359–361; [[born-of-the-clear-sky]], [[with-strong-plates]], [[ash-spear]]). The army's round shields are named by their bosses as the arms stream out like snowflakes; Achilles' own shield, a few verses on, is named by its light, which shines far off “as of the moon” (19.373–374; [[the-shield-of-achilles]]).
