---
summary: Odysseus takes “the sons of glorious Nestor” with the young men who fetch Agamemnon's gifts.
---

“He spoke, and took with him the sons of glorious Nestor, / and Meges, son of Phyleus, and Thoas and Meriones, / and Lycomedes, son of Creon, and Melanippus” (19.238–240; [[nestor]], [[meges]], [[thoas]], [[meriones]], [[lycomedes]], [[the-gifts-of-agamemnon]]). The genitive κυδαλίμοιο, which closes the verse for Menelaus in the formula Μενελάου κυδαλίμοιο (4.100, 13.601, 17.69), is given to Nestor only here, at the end of the verse that begins with his name.
