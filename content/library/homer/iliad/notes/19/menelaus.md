---
summary: Menelaus is not named in Book 19. He is one of “the two sons of Atreus” who stay with Achilles after the assembly, when the other kings are sent away (19.310).
---

δοιὼ δʼ Ἀτρεΐδα μενέτην, “but the two sons of Atreus stayed” (19.310; see [[atreidae]] and [[agamemnon]]). With his brother, Odysseus, Nestor, Idomeneus and Phoenix he stays to comfort Achilles, who will not eat while Patroclus lies unburied (19.309–313; see [[achilles]] and [[elders]]). The war is still fought for his sake: in his lament Achilles says that he is fighting the Trojans in a foreign land “for the sake of Helen, at whom men shudder” (19.324–325; see [[helen]] and [[war-for-menelaus]]).
