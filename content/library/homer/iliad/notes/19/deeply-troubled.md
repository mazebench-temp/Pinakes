---
summary: Achilles answers his horse's prophecy “Deeply troubled” (19.419), in the verse that introduced his answer to Patroclus' plea for his armor (16.48).
---

The prophecy tells him nothing new, and he says so: “I know well myself that it is my fate to perish here” (19.421; see [[death-of-achilles-foretold]] and [[xanthus-the-horse]]). What troubles him is the telling. The same verse introduces his answer to Apollo, who has drawn him away from the city, in Book 22 (22.14).
