---
summary: Achilles had hoped that he alone would perish at Troy, “far from horse-pasturing Argos,” and that Patroclus would go home.
---

“For before this the heart in my breast hoped / that I alone would perish far from horse-pasturing Argos, / here in Troy, and that you would go back to Phthia” (19.328–330; [[argos]], [[phthia]], [[patroclus]]). The half-line is the one in which Odysseus feared that it might be the fate of all the Achaeans “to perish in Troy, far from horse-pasturing Argos” (9.246). Achilles had wished that fate on himself alone, with Patroclus spared to carry his son home (19.331–333; [[neoptolemus]]).
