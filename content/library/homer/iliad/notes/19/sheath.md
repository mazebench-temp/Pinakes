---
summary: Agamemnon's knife hangs “beside the great sheath of his sword” (19.253), in the verse of the oath sacrifice of Book 3 (3.272).
---

ἥ οἱ πὰρ ξίφεος μέγα κουλεὸν αἰὲν ἄωρτο, “that always hung beside the great sheath of his sword” (19.253 = 3.272), with κουλεόν, the form with the long first syllable (see the entry, [[knife]] and [[oath]]).
