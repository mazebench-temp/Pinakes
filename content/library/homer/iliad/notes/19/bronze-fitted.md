---
summary: Agamemnon comes to the assembly with the wound that Coon, son of Antenor, gave him “with his bronze-fitted spear.”
---

“And last of all came Agamemnon, lord of men, / with his wound; for him too in the fierce battle / Coon, son of Antenor, had stabbed with his bronze-fitted spear” (19.51–53; [[coon]], [[son-of-antenor]], [[lord-of-men]], [[krateros]]). When the blow was struck in Book 11 the spear was “the shining spear” (11.253), and χαλκήρης went to Agamemnon's own spear, with which he killed Coon over his brother's body (11.260). Here the epithet passes to the spear that wounded the king. See [[agamemnon]].
