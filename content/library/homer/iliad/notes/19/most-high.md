---
summary: Agamemnon begins his oath, “Now let Zeus be witness first, highest and best of the gods”; the book adds ὕπατος.
grc: [ὕπατος]
---

“Now let Zeus be witness first, highest and best of the gods, / and Earth and the Sun and the Erinyes, who beneath the earth / punish men, whoever has sworn a false oath” (19.258–260; [[zeus]], [[oath]], [[wide-sky]]). θεῶν ὕπατος καὶ ἄριστος is used again by Achilles when he swears by Zeus not to wash before Patroclus is burned (23.43), and the whole verse by Odysseus in the Odyssey (*Odyssey* 19.303). The nominative ὕπατος is new to the entry.
