---
summary: Odysseus ends his terms for the reconciliation with a maxim: “For it is no cause for blame that a king / should make amends to a man, when he was the first to grow angry” (19.182–183).
---

νεμεσσητόν is what would rouse the indignation of others. The maxim answers the fear that keeps a king from yielding (see [[no-blame-for-a-king-to-make-amends]], [[basileus]] and [[aidos]]).
