---
summary: Agamemnon's story is set “on the day when mighty Heracles / was to be born to Alcmene in well-crowned Thebes.”
---

“Hera, female though she was, deceived with her treachery / on the day when mighty Heracles / was to be born to Alcmene in well-crowned Thebes” (19.97–99; [[thebes]], [[alcmene]], [[might-of-heracles]]). It is the only place in Homer where the epithet is given to a city (Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 19.99). The Thebes is Boeotian Thebes, where Zeus in Book 14 remembers loving Alcmene (“nor Alcmene in Thebes,” 14.323).
