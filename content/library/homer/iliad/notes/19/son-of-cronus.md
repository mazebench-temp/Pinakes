---
summary: In Agamemnon's story Hera brings the news to “Zeus, son of Cronus,” and at the end of the day “the son of Cronus” pities the mourners and sends Athena to Achilles.
---

**Hera's news.** “And she herself, bringing the news, addressed Zeus, son of Cronus” (19.120; [[hera]], [[father-zeus]]): the birth of Eurystheus, which binds Zeus to his oath (19.121–124).

**The mourners.** “and the son of Cronus saw them weeping and pitied them, / and at once he addressed Athena with winged words” (19.340–341; [[zeus]], [[athena]]). The first verse is the one in which Zeus pitied the weeping horses of Achilles in Book 17 (17.441). He sends Athena to drip nectar and ambrosia into the fasting Achilles (19.342–348; [[lovely]]).
