---
summary: Agamemnon welcomes Odysseus' counsel: “I rejoice, son of Laertes, to hear your word.”
---

“I rejoice, son of Laertes, to hear your word; / for you have gone through all of it rightly and told it in full” (19.185–186; [[odysseus]], [[agamemnon]]). Everywhere else in the Iliad the vocative Λαερτιάδη stands in the whole verse of formal address, “Son of Laertes, sprung from Zeus, Odysseus of many devices” (2.173, 9.308, 10.144); here Agamemnon uses the patronymic alone, in the warmth of agreement.
