---
summary: The mountain is named in the verses of Achilles' spear, the Pelian ash, cut “from the peak of Pelion” (19.391 = 16.144).
---

When Achilles arms, he takes up the spear that Chiron gave to Peleus (see [[the-pelian-ash]], [[chiron]] and [[peleus]]). Its name, Πηλιάδα μελίην, “the Pelian ash” (19.390), is the mountain's, and the next verse says why: it came Πηλίου ἐκ κορυφῆς, “from the peak of Pelion, to be death for heroes” (19.391 = 16.144). The Iliad names the mountain four times, and this is the last (2.744, 2.757, 16.144, 19.391); the spear keeps its name, “Pelian”, in the later books (20.277, 21.162, 22.133).
