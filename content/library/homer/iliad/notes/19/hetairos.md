---
summary: The companion is Patroclus, mourned by the many companions. Thetis finds Achilles with “many of his companions around him” weeping (19.5–6); Achilles will not eat “while my companion is dead”, with his companions weeping around the body (19.210–213); his lament calls Patroclus “dearest of my companions” (19.315), and Zeus sees him “mourning his dear companion” (19.345).
---

Achilles also calls the kings who stay with him “my dear companions” (19.305), with the word of a leader's own men. See [[philos]], [[patroclus]] and [[lament]].
