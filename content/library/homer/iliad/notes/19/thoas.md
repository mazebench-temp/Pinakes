---
summary: Thoas, the leader of the Aetolians, is one of the young men whom Odysseus takes to Agamemnon's hut to bring out the gifts for Achilles (19.238–248).
---

{{quote:1c4067cf-0c8e-52a0-b6f1-1106e66eb419}}

Θόαντά τε, “and Thoas” (19.239; see [[odysseus]] and [[the-gifts-of-agamemnon]]). He is counted among the κούρητες, the “young men” who carry the gifts (19.193, 19.248; see [[kouretes]]). In Book 15 he was the man whom few of the Achaeans could beat in the assembly, “when the young men contended in speeches” (15.283–284), and in Book 13 Poseidon rallied him with Meriones and Antilochus as “young men” (13.92–95; see [[meriones]] and [[antilochus]]). He leads forty ships (2.638–644; see [[aetolians]]), and he is not named again in the book.
