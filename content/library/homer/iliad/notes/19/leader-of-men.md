---
summary: Briseis, finding Patroclus dead, addresses him as “leader of the people.”
---

“I left you alive when I went from the hut, / and now I find you dead, leader of the people, / as I come back; so evil follows evil for me always” (19.288–290; [[patroclus]], [[briseis]]). The vocative ὄρχαμε λαῶν, a title of address to kings, Agamemnon in Odysseus' rebuke (14.102) and Menelaus in Euphorbus' challenge (17.12), is given here by a captive woman to the man who was kind to her (19.300; [[meilichos]]).
