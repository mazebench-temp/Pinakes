---
summary: Achilles will take no comfort “before he entered the mouth of bloody war.”
---

The elders try to comfort Achilles as he refuses food, “but not at all in his heart / would he be comforted, before he entered the mouth of bloody war” (19.312–313; [[achilles]], [[polemos]]). πόλεμος αἱματόεις is the war he named when he refused Ajax in Book 9, “bloody war,” which he would not think of until Hector came to his ships (9.650). Now he will take no comfort until he is inside it. For the mouth of war Walter Leaf refers to “the great mouth of bitter war” in the simile that opens Book 10 (10.8; *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 19.313).
