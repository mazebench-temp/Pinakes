---
summary: Agamemnon comes to the assembly with the wound that “Coon, son of Antenor,” gave him.
---

“for him too in the fierce battle / Coon, son of Antenor, had stabbed with his bronze-fitted spear” (19.52–53; [[coon]], [[agamemnon]], [[bronze-fitted]]). Coon, “the eldest-born son of Antenor,” stabbed Agamemnon in the arm when the king had killed his brother Iphidamas, and as he dragged the body away Agamemnon killed him over it (11.248–261; [[antenor]], [[iphidamas]]). The patronymic names him once more as the cause of the king's wound.
