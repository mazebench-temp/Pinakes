summary: Achilles' second assembly keeps the order of his first and reverses its outcome. He summons the army himself, shouting along the shore, and even the helmsmen and the stewards come (19.40–46); the wounded kings come last and sit in front (19.47–53); “when all the Achaeans were gathered together, / swift-footed Achilles stood up among them and spoke”, the second verse that of Book 1 (19.54–55; 1.58); Agamemnon answers without standing (19.76–77); and Achilles alone dissolves the assembly (19.276–277).

**Summons and gathering.** Thetis tells her son to call the Achaean heroes to an assembly (19.34), and he does it without heralds, going along the shore “shouting terribly” (19.40–41). Even the men who used to stay by the ships come, the helmsmen and the stewards who deal out the food (19.42–46). Walter Leaf would suspect those two verses of being a gloss, were it not for the interest in feeding that runs through the whole book (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 19.43; see [[fasting]]). The wounded come limping on their spears, Diomedes and Odysseus, and Agamemnon “last of all” (19.47–53; see [[the-wounded-chiefs]]).

**Speakers.** The first speaker rises in the verse of Book 1, where Achilles also called the assembly and spoke first (19.55 = 1.58; see [[when-they-had-gathered]]). Agamemnon speaks “from where he sat, and not standing up in their midst” (19.77), and opens with a maxim about the man who stands to speak (19.79–80; see [[listen-to-the-man-who-stands]] and [[sat-down-stood-up]]). Later he stands, as Odysseus asked, to swear the oath over the gifts set out “in the middle of the assembly” (19.175, 19.249–250; see [[oath]]), and Achilles stands to answer it (19.269).

**Dissolution.** The assembly of Book 1 broke up when the two men “stood up, and dissolved the assembly” after their quarrel (1.304–305). This one ends with the quarrel renounced, the gifts paid and the oath sworn: Achilles sends the men to their meal, “and quickly dissolved the assembly”, and they scatter each to his own ship (19.275–277; see [[assembly]] and [[the-renunciation-of-wrath]]). αἰψηρήν, “quickly”, Leaf takes as “quick to disperse at his word” (on 19.276).
