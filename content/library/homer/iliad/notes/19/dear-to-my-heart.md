---
summary: Briseis begins her lament over the dead Patroclus: “Patroclus, dearest to my heart”; the book adds the half-verse without ἐμῷ.
grc: ["κεχαρισμένε θυμῷ"]
en: [dearest to my heart]
---

“Patroclus, dearest to my heart, wretched that I am, / I left you alive when I went from the hut” (19.287–288; [[patroclus]], [[briseis]], [[wretch]]). πλεῖστον κεχαρισμένε θυμῷ is the address Achilles used to Patroclus in Book 11 (11.608) with πλεῖστον, “most,” in place of “my”: the captive speaks to the dead man in the words of his dearest friend.
