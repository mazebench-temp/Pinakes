“And they set them in the middle of the assembly, and Agamemnon / stood up; and Talthybius, like a god in his voice, / holding the boar in his hands, stood beside the shepherd of the people” (19.249–251; [[talthybius]], [[boar]], [[shepherd-of-the-people]]). The herald's voice is praised at the moment the army falls silent to hear the oath (19.255–265; [[oath]], [[wide-sky]]).
