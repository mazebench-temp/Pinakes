---
summary: Achilles had hoped that Patroclus would show his son “my wealth and my slaves and my great high-roofed house.”
---

“so that you might take my son in a swift black ship / from Scyros, and show him everything, / my wealth and my slaves and my great high-roofed house” (19.331–333; [[neoptolemus]], [[black-ships]], [[house]]). The adjective is ὑψερεφής, as in Pandarus' oath (5.213). The whole verse is the Odyssey's verse of home: Odysseus longs to see again his wealth, his slaves and his great high-roofed house (*Odyssey* 7.225), and Penelope speaks of hers in the same words (*Odyssey* 19.526). Achilles gives it to the home in Phthia that he will not see again.
