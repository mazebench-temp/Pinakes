---
summary: Glory is given by gods. Achilles names the dead whom Hector brought down “when Zeus gave him glory” (19.204), and Xanthus says that Apollo killed Patroclus “and gave glory to Hector” (19.414). Achilles addresses Agamemnon twice as κύδιστε, “Most glorious” (19.146, 19.199).
---

The two verses are old ones. 19.204 is the narrator's verse for Hector at the height of his success, at the ships in Book 8 and in the question of whom he killed first and last in Book 11 (8.216, 11.300; see [[whom-first-whom-last]]), and 19.414 is Thetis' account of Patroclus' death to Hephaestus (18.456; see [[the-death-of-patroclus]]). The glory is Hector's and its giver is a god; the book ends with Achilles setting out to take it back (see [[the-fourth-day-of-battle]]).
