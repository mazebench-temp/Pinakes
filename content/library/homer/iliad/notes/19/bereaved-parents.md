---
summary: Achilles imagines his own father bereaved: Peleus in Phthia “letting his tender tears fall / for lack of such a son”, or already dead, or barely alive in hateful old age, “always waiting for / the grievous news of me, when he hears that I have perished” (19.322–324, 19.334–337).
refs: [19.322-324, 19.334-337]
---

The bereaved fathers of the poem are usually the fathers of the men Achilles and the others kill; here he speaks of his own, before his death (see [[peleus]], [[fathers-and-sons]] and [[the-short-life-of-achilles]]).
