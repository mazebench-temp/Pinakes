---
summary: When Agamemnon's gifts reach Achilles' huts, “the noble attendants drove the horses to the herd.”
---

“And they set them in the huts, and seated the women, / and the noble attendants drove the horses to the herd” (19.280–281; [[the-gifts-of-agamemnon]], [[horses]]). The twelve horses of the gifts, the prize-winners of Agamemnon's promise (9.123–124, 19.244), are taken over by Achilles' own attendants. The phrase θεράποντες ἀγαυοί occurs only here.
