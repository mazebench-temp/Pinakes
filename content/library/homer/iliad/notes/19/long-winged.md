---
summary: Athena leaps down from the sky “like a long-winged, shrill-voiced bird of prey”; the book adds τανυπτέρυγι.
grc: [τανυπτέρυγι]
---

“So saying he urged on Athena, who was eager already; / and she, like a long-winged, shrill-voiced bird of prey, / leapt down from the sky through the upper air” (19.349–351; [[athena]], [[harpe]], [[like-a-bird-of-prey]], [[shrill-voiced]]). The bird of the simile has two epithets, one for its wings and one for its cry. The dative τανυπτέρυγι is new to the entry.
