---
summary: When Achilles arms, he takes up the Pelian ash “which Chiron gave to his dear father” (19.390), in the verses of Book 16; it is the last time the Iliad names the Centaur.
---

The last of Achilles' arms is his father's spear, “the Pelian ash, which Chiron gave to his dear father / from the peak of Pelion, to be death for heroes” (19.390–391 = 16.143–144; see [[the-pelian-ash]], [[peleus]] and [[pelion]]). The armor he puts on is a god's new work (see [[hephaestus]]); the spear is the Centaur's old gift, which came to him from his father (see [[achilles]]). Chiron is named in four lines of the Iliad, and this is the last of them (4.219, 11.832, 16.143, 19.390). Each time he has given something: drugs to Asclepius, his knowledge of medicine to Achilles, and this spear to Peleus.
