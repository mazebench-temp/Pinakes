summary: The injury to Achilles' honor is repaid in public: the gifts are set out where all the Achaeans can see them, the prize is returned untouched, and the king swears to it (19.172–177, 19.243–266). Achilles hardly looks at the payment (19.147–148); the insult he means to avenge now is Hector's (19.208).
refs: [19.89, 19.172-177, 19.243-266]

What was taken in the assembly is restored in the assembly (19.88–89, 19.249; see [[geras]] and [[assembly]]). The payment comes after the honor has ceased to matter to the man who is paid (see [[doron]], [[lobe]] and [[time]]).
