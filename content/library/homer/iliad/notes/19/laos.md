---
summary: Agamemnon is twice “shepherd of the people” (19.35, 19.251), and the armor lifts Achilles, “the shepherd of the people”, like wings (19.386). Briseis calls the dead Patroclus “leader of the people” (19.289). The army is roused to war and dismissed to its meal (19.139, 19.171), and Odysseus' summons leaves none of it behind (19.234).
---

ὄρχαμε λαῶν, “leader of the people” (19.289), is a title of kings and leaders, and Briseis gives it to Patroclus (see [[leader-of-men]] and [[shepherd-of-the-people]]).
