summary: Thetis, bringing the armor, takes her son's hand and speaks to him: “and she clung to his hand, and spoke to him, and called him by name” (19.7), the verse with which Hecuba and Andromache met Hector in Book 6 (6.253, 6.406) and Charis met Thetis herself at Hephaestus' house (18.384).
---

Thetis finds Achilles lying over Patroclus' body, weeping, with his companions around him (19.4–6), and the gesture of greeting between kin takes him by the hand away from the dead. Her first words are “My child” (19.8; see [[mothers-and-sons]] and [[thetis]]).
