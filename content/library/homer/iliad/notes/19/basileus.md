---
summary: Odysseus' maxim is about kings: “it is no cause for blame that a king / should make amends to a man” (19.182–183). At the oath the Argives sit silent, “listening to the king, as was right” (19.256), and Achilles, refusing food, sends “the other kings” away (19.309).
---

The kings who stay are the sons of Atreus, Odysseus, Nestor, Idomeneus and Phoenix (19.309–311; see [[elders]] and [[geron]]). See [[no-blame-for-a-king-to-make-amends]] and [[kingship-and-authority]].
