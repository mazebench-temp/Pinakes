---
summary: Achilles had hoped that Patroclus would bring his son from Scyros “in a swift black ship.”
---

“so that you might take my son in a swift black ship / from Scyros, and show him everything” (19.331–332; [[swift-ships]], [[neoptolemus]], [[scyros]]). The voyage Achilles imagined for Patroclus, home to Phthia and over to Scyros, would have been made in the ship of both standing epithets, θοῇ … νηῒ μελαίνῃ, the words in which he spoke of his own ship in Book 1 (“beside my swift black ship,” 1.300). See [[patroclus]].
