---
summary: Twice Achilles' eyes blaze. At the sight of the armor "his eyes / shone out terribly beneath his lids, like a blaze" (19.16–17), and as he arms "his two eyes / shone like the blaze of fire" (19.365–366). The gifts are brought into the assembly so that all the Achaeans "may see them with their eyes" (19.174).
