summary: Achilles will go against the Trojans “once more and make trial of them” (19.70). Thetis “will try to ward off” the flies from Patroclus' body (πειρήσω, 19.30), and Achilles, armed, “tried himself in his armor”, whether it fitted him (πειρήθη, 19.384).
grc: [πειρήσω, πειρήθη]
en: [will try, tried himself]
---

The trial of the armor is the last step of the arming: Walter Leaf takes ἐφαρμόσσειε as “whether he had fitted them on well”, and the translation has “whether it fitted him” (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 19.385; see the translation's note on 19.385). The armor lifts him like wings (19.386; see [[arming-scene]]).
