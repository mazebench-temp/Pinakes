---
summary: Zeus tells Athena that Achilles sits mourning “before the straight-horned ships.”
---

“Is Achilles then no longer at all in your mind? / There he sits before the straight-horned ships, / mourning his dear companion” (19.343–345; [[zeus]], [[athena]], [[achilles]]). The verse-end is the one of Book 18, where Antilochus “found him in front of the straight-horned ships” to bring the news of Patroclus' death (18.3; Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 19.344). Achilles is still where the news found him.
