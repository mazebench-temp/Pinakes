summary: Achilles would have the army eat only at sunset, “when we have avenged the insult” (τεισαίμεθα λώβην, 19.208). The outrage to be paid for is no longer Agamemnon's but Hector's.
---

In Book 9 Achilles refused Agamemnon's gifts, saying that the king would not win him over “until he has paid me back for all his heart-grieving insult” (9.386–387; see [[honor-and-dishonor]]). Agamemnon's debt is paid in this book with gifts and an oath. The new outrage is the killing of Patroclus, and it can be paid only in blood (see [[poine]] and [[fasting]]).
