---
summary: Among the kings who stay to comfort Achilles is “the old horse-driving Phoenix.”
---

“but the two sons of Atreus stayed, and brilliant Odysseus, / Nestor and Idomeneus and the old horse-driving Phoenix, / comforting him in his deep grief” (19.310–312; [[phoenix]], [[nestor]], [[idomeneus]], [[brilliant]]). γέρων ἱππηλάτα Φοῖνιξ is the formula in which Phoenix spoke at last in the embassy (9.432) and led the fourth rank of the Myrmidons (16.196). In Book 18 the same title was Peleus' (18.331), and Achilles' lament turns to Peleus a few verses later (19.322–337; [[peleus]]).
