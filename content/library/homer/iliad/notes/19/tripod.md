---
summary: The seven tripods that Agamemnon promised are the first of his gifts to be carried out of his hut for Achilles (19.243).
---

ἑπτὰ μὲν ἐκ κλισίης τρίποδας φέρον, οὕς οἱ ὑπέστη, “seven tripods they brought from the hut, which he had promised him” (19.243). In the promise they were “untouched by fire” (ἀπύρους, 9.122); the adjective is dropped here, and the next verse, with the twenty cauldrons and the twelve horses, is the verse of the promise (19.244 = 9.123; see [[cauldron]], [[horses]] and [[the-gifts-of-agamemnon]]). The Myrmidons carry the gifts to Achilles' ship and set them in his huts (19.278–280; see [[myrmidons]]).
