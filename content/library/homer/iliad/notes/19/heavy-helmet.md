---
summary: Achilles sets the helmet Hephaestus made on his head, “heavy”; the book adds the bare accusative βριαρήν.
grc: [βριαρήν]
en: [heavy]
---

“and lifting the helmet, / he set it, heavy, on his head; and like a star it shone” (19.380–381; [[helmet]], [[horsehair-crested]]). The helmet is the one Hephaestus made, “a heavy helmet fitted to his temples” (18.611); here it is called τρυφάλεια, and βριαρήν, standing apart at the head of the next verse, keeps the epithet of the formula κόρυς βριαρή. See [[the-making-of-the-arms]].
