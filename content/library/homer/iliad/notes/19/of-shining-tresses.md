---
summary: Zeus seizes Ate “by her head of shining tresses” and swears that she will never come back to Olympus.
---

“So she spoke, and sharp grief struck him deep in his heart; / and at once he seized Blind Folly by her head of shining tresses, / angered in his heart, and swore a mighty oath” (19.125–127; [[ate]], [[zeus]], [[sharp]], [[krateros]]). The epithet of a goddess's beauty is given to Ate as she is seized by the hair and hurled from the starry sky to the works of men (19.128–131; [[starry]], [[daughter-of-zeus]]).
