---
summary: After the oath Achilles “stood up and spoke among the war-loving Argives.”
---

“whirling it around, food for the fishes; but Achilles / stood up and spoke among the war-loving Argives” (19.268–269; [[argives]], [[achilles]]). It is the only place where the epithet is given to the Argives; Achilles speaks to the army he will lead into battle, and dismisses it to its meal so that they may join in the clash of Ares (19.270–275).
