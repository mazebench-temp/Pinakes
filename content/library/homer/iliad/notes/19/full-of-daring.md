---
summary: Thetis puts “a might full of daring” into Achilles before he calls the assembly, and ambrosia and nectar into the dead Patroclus.
---

“So she spoke, and put in him a might full of daring, / and into Patroclus in turn ambrosia and red nectar / she dripped through his nostrils, so that his flesh might be sound” (19.37–39; [[thetis]], [[menos]], [[red]], [[ambrosia]]). The two verses join the living son and the dead friend: the goddess gives the one the might for war and keeps the other's body from decay. What Glaucus wished the Trojans had in Book 17 (17.156), Achilles receives from his mother.
