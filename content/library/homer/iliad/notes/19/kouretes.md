---
summary: Agamemnon tells Odysseus to “choose young men, the best of all the Achaeans” to bring the gifts from his ship (19.193), and Odysseus takes with him the sons of Nestor, Meges, Thoas, Meriones, Lycomedes and Melanippus; they carry the gifts to the assembly, “the other young men of the Achaeans” (19.238–240, 19.248).
---

The chosen young men are leaders, not servants: the sons of Nestor are Antilochus and Thrasymedes, and Meges, Thoas and Meriones lead contingents of their own (see [[antilochus]], [[thrasymedes]], [[meges]], [[thoas]], [[meriones]], [[lycomedes]] and [[melanippus-the-achaean]]). The gifts are carried with honor by men of rank, before the whole army (see [[the-gifts-of-agamemnon]]).
