summary: The book remembers the raid in which Briseis was taken. Achilles wishes Artemis had killed her “on the day when I took her, when I destroyed Lyrnessus” (19.60), and Briseis remembers how Achilles killed her husband “and sacked the city of divine Mynes” (19.295–296).
refs: [19.60]
---

The raid is told from both sides, by the man who took the prize and by the woman taken (see [[the-sack-of-lyrnessus]], [[briseis]] and [[captive-women]]). Among Agamemnon's gifts are seven women “skilled in flawless handiwork” (19.245–246), women he promised in Book 9 from those Achilles himself took when he sacked Lesbos (9.128–130; see [[the-gifts-of-agamemnon]]).
