---
summary: Automedon and Alcimus yoke Achilles' horses and pull the reins back “to the well-joined chariot.”
---

“and the bits / they set in their jaws, and pulled the reins back / to the well-joined chariot” (19.393–395; [[chariot]], [[automedon]], [[the-horses-of-achilles]]). The chariot is the one from which Achilles will speak to his horses and hear Xanthus answer (19.397–424; [[xanthus-the-horse]]).
