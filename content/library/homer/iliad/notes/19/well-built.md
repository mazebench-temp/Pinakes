---
summary: Athena goes back “to the well-built house of her mighty father”; the book adds πυκινὸν δῶ.
grc: ["πυκινὸν δῶ"]
---

“and she herself went to the well-built house of her mighty father” (19.355; [[athena]], [[eristhenes]], [[zeus]]). The Greek is πυκινὸν δῶ, “close-built,” the adjective of Amyntor's house in Book 10 (πυκινὸν δόμον, 10.267), here of the house of Zeus on Olympus. The phrase πυκινὸν δῶ is new to the entry.
