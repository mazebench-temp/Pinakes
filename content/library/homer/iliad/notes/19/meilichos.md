“Therefore I weep for you without end, dead, who were always kind” (19.300; [[briseis]], [[patroclus]]). The last word of her lament is the one Menelaus used of Patroclus in Book 17, that he “knew how to be kind to all” (17.671). Patroclus had kept her from weeping when Achilles killed her husband, and promised that she would be Achilles' wedded wife (19.295–299; [[divine]], [[marriage]]). The accusative μείλιχον is new to the entry.
