summary: Antenor is named only in his son's patronymic: Agamemnon comes to the assembly with the wound that “Coon, son of Antenor” gave him (19.53).
---

Κόων Ἀντηνορίδης, “Coon, son of Antenor” (19.53; see [[coon]] and [[son-of-antenor]]). Coon was the eldest of Antenor's sons; he wounded Agamemnon through the arm as the king stripped his brother Iphidamas, and Agamemnon killed him over his brother's body (11.221–263; see [[iphidamas]] and [[agamemnon]]). The king comes last to the assembly of reconciliation, still wounded (19.51–53).
