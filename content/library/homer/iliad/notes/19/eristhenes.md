---
summary: Having dripped nectar and ambrosia into Achilles' breast, Athena goes back “to the well-built house of her mighty father.”
---

“and she herself went to the well-built house of her mighty father, / and they poured out away from the swift ships” (19.355–356; [[athena]], [[well-built]], [[swift-ships]]). Zeus had sent her with the words “My child” (19.342), and the epithet ends her errand where it began, with the father who pitied the mourners and sent her (19.340–348; [[zeus]], [[ambrosia]], [[nectar]]).
