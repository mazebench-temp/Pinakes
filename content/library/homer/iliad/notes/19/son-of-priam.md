---
summary: Achilles will not eat while the men lie torn whom “Hector, son of Priam,” brought down when Zeus gave him glory.
---

“But now they lie torn, those whom he brought down, / Hector, son of Priam, when Zeus gave him glory, / and you two urge us to food!” (19.203–205; [[hector]], [[kydos]]). The patronymic names the enemy at the head of the verse, as Achilles sets the dead against the meal (19.205–214).
