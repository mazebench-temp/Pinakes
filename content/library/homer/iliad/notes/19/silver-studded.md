---
summary: Achilles arms: “And around his shoulders he slung his silver-studded sword.”
---

“And around his shoulders he slung his silver-studded sword / of bronze, and then his shield, great and heavy, / he took up” (19.372–374; [[sword]], [[arming-scene]]). The verse is the third step of the arming of Paris (3.334) and of Patroclus (16.135), and Achilles' arming follows the same order: greaves, corselet, sword, shield, helmet, spear (19.369–391; see the translation's note on 19.369–373). The poem does not say where this sword came from: Hephaestus made the shield, the corselet, the helmet and the greaves (18.609–613), and the spear is his father's (19.387–391).
