Μηριόνην τε, “and Meriones” (19.239; see [[odysseus]] and [[the-gifts-of-agamemnon]]). He is counted among the κούρητες, the “young men” who carry the gifts (19.193, 19.248; see [[kouretes]]), as Poseidon called him and the others he rallied at the ships, “Shame, Argives, young men!” (13.93–95; see [[thoas]]). On the night of the embassy he was one of the seven captains of the watch who sat with their young men between the ditch and the wall, with Lycomedes, who is chosen here too, and Thrasymedes, a son of Nestor (9.81–87; see [[night-watch]], [[lycomedes]] and [[thrasymedes]]). The gifts offered that night are carried out by men who kept watch while they were offered. His commander Idomeneus is one of the six who stay with Achilles after the assembly (19.311; see [[idomeneus]]).
