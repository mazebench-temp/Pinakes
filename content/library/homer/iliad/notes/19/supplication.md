---
summary: The elders gather around Achilles “begging him to take his meal”, and he begs them in turn: “I beg you, if any of my dear companions will obey me, / do not bid me, before that, with food or drink / to sate my dear heart” (19.304–307).
---

Neither side makes the gestures of a suppliant; λίσσομαι is here the urgent request of friends (see [[gounata]]). In Book 9 Agamemnon sent the best men of the Achaeans “to entreat” him to return to the war, and he refused them (9.520). Now he has returned, and he refuses them the meal (see [[fasting]] and [[obedience-and-persuasion]]).
