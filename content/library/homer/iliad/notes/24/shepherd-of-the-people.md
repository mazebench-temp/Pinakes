---
summary: Achilles warns Priam that a counselor who saw him “would at once tell Agamemnon, shepherd of the people” (24.654).
---

Ἀγαμέμνονι ποιμένι λαῶν ends the verse as it did when Achilles lay idle in his anger “against Agamemnon, shepherd of the people” (2.772, 7.230) and when he unsaid his wrath (19.35). Agamemnon does not appear in the last book; he is the danger that Achilles and Hermes both name (24.654–655, 24.687–688; [[son-of-atreus]]). See [[agamemnon]].
