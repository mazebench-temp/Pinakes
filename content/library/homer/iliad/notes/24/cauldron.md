---
summary: Four cauldrons go into Priam's ransom for Hector, after two gleaming tripods (24.233).
---

“and brought out two gleaming tripods and four cauldrons” (24.233). The pair of tripod and cauldron is the pair of the gift lists, Agamemnon's twenty cauldrons beside seven tripods (9.122–123, 19.243–244; see [[tripod]] and [[the-gifts-of-agamemnon]]) and the prizes Achilles brings out for the games, “cauldrons and tripods” (23.259; see the note on Book 23). Priam's ransom has four (see [[apoina]]). The washing before the libation a little later is done with a χέρνιβον, “basin”, and the ancient critics who athetized that verse objected that Homer calls the basin λέβης (24.304; Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 24.304; see [[basin-and-pitcher]]).
