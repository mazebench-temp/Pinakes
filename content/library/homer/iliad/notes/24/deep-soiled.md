---
summary: Thetis weeps for the fate of her son, “who was to / perish in deep-soiled Troy, far from his native land” (24.85–86).
---

The second half of the verse, ἐν Τροίῃ ἐριβώλακι τηλόθι πάτρης, was Zeus' word for Sarpedon, whom he would not save: he was to die “in deep-soiled Troy, far from his native land” (16.461). Achilles had set the fields of Troy against “deep-soiled Phthia,” which the Trojans never harmed (1.154–156); now his mother mourns that he will die in Troy and not in Phthia.
