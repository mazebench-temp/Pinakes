---
summary: Zeus tells Thetis that he will send Iris “to great-hearted Priam” (24.117), and gives Iris the message, “take a message to great-hearted Priam within Ilios” (24.145).
---

Πριάμῳ μεγαλήτορι is the phrase of Hector's bitter wish in Book 6, that Paris had died, for Zeus reared him to be a bane to the Trojans “and to great-hearted Priam and his sons” (6.282–283). In the last book it names the man Zeus has chosen to ransom his son alone (24.117–119, 24.145–148; [[message-repeated]]). See [[priam]].
