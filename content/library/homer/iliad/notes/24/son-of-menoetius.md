---
summary: Achilles drags Hector three times “around the mound of the dead son of Menoetius” (24.16).
---

Μενοιτιάδαο θανόντος, “of the dead son of Menoetius,” is the patronymic's last use in the poem, at the mound heaped in Book 23 (23.255–257; [[tomb]]). Achilles rests in his hut between the draggings (24.14–18; [[the-dragging-of-hector]]). See [[patroclus]].
