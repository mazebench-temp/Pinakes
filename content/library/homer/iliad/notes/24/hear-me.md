---
summary: “So he spoke in prayer, and Zeus the counselor heard him” (24.314): Zeus answers Priam's prayer for a sign.
---

The closing verse of the answered prayer, ὣς ἔφατʼ εὐχόμενος, τοῦ δʼ ἔκλυε, follows Priam's prayer as it followed Chryses' twice and Achilles' for Patroclus (1.43, 1.457; 16.249, with μητίετα Ζεύς as here). The god's hearing is shown at once: “and at once he sent an eagle” (24.314–321; [[counselor]], [[surest-of-birds]], [[prayer-type-scene]]). See [[prayer]] and [[zeus]].
