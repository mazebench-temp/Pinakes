---
summary: “Then brilliant swift-footed Achilles spoke to him in turn” (24.668): Achilles promises Priam the truce he asks for.
---

The verse-end ποδάρκης δῖος Ἀχιλλεύς is used here for the last time in the poem, to bring in Achilles' last words to Priam, “This too shall be as you ask, old Priam; / for I will hold back the war for as long a time as you bid” (24.669–670; [[brilliant]], [[swift-footed]]).
