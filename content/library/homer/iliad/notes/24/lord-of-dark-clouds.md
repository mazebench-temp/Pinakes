---
summary: Hecuba tells Priam, “But pray then to the son of Cronus, lord of the dark clouds” (24.290), for a bird of omen.
---

κελαινεφέϊ Κρονίωνι ends the verse of Hector's refusal in Book 6: he would not pour wine to Zeus with unwashed hands, for it is not right to pray to the son of Cronus, lord of the dark clouds, spattered with blood (6.266–268). Priam has his hands washed before he pours and prays (24.302–307; [[libation]], [[prayer]]). See [[zeus]] and [[hecuba]].
