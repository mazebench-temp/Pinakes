# λαχνήεντʼ (24.451), only here in Homer.
grc: [λαχνήεντʼ]
---

λαχνήεις, the epithet of the Centaurs and the boar's hide, is given to the thatch of the high hut that the Myrmidons built for their lord, described as Priam reaches it (24.448–456; [[hut]], [[myrmidons]]). The translation's table of key words gives λαχνήεις ὄροφος as “a shaggy thatch.” See [[achilles]].
