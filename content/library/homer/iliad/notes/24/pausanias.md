---
summary: Pausanias went up Sipylus and saw Niobe: near at hand a crag with no shape of a woman, from farther off a woman in tears with her head bowed. He writes of Tantalus and Pelops as having lived among his own people, and counts the story that she weeps in summer among the marvels that the credulous add to the truth.
