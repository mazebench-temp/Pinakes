# κρυεροῖο γόοιο (24.524): the phrase, so that the chilling rout of 13.48 is not taken in by a bare form.
grc: ["κρυεροῖο γόοιο"]
---

κρυερός, the companion of κρυόεις, is said here of lament: the cold of fear becomes the cold of grief that does no good, and Achilles bids Priam sit down and let their sorrows lie, since the gods have spun a life of grief for wretched mortals (24.522–526; [[the-two-jars-of-zeus]], [[lament]]). See [[achilles]].
