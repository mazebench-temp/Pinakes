# πολυδαιδάλῳ (24.597), only here in Homer.
grc: [πολυδαιδάλῳ]
---

The chair is the one from which Achilles rose to raise Priam, and to which he returns after laying Hector on the wagon, to tell Priam that his son is released (24.515, 24.596–599; [[throne]]). The epithet of fine craftsmanship is given here to furniture, as elsewhere to corselets, shields and bowls. See [[achilles]].
