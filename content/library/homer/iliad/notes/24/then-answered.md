τὸν δʼ ἠμείβετʼ ἔπειτα γέρων Πρίαμος θεοειδής is a verse of this book alone: Priam's answers to the god on the plain and to Achilles in the hut, asking for his son and then for the days of the funeral (24.372–405, 24.552–558, 24.659–667; [[godlike]], [[answering-him]]). Thetis' verse is the verse of her answers to Achilles in Books 18 and 19 (18.127, 19.28; [[silver-footed]]). See [[priam]].
