---
summary: Hecuba laments that when Achilles “had taken your life with the long-edged bronze,” he dragged Hector many times around the mound of Patroclus (24.754–756).
---

Hector used the verse-end ταναήκεϊ χαλκῷ in his challenge in Book 7, of his own death if he lost: “if that man takes my life with the long-edged bronze,” let him take my armor but give my body back (7.77–80). His mother uses it for that death (24.754). The entry tells the whole story of the formula. See [[hecuba]] and [[the-dragging-of-hector]].
