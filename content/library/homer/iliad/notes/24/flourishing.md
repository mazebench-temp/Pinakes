---
summary: Achilles, remembering Patroclus, “let big tears fall” (24.9), and the brothers and companions who gather Hector's bones weep, “and the big tears ran down their cheeks” (24.794).
---

The first tears of the book and the last are θαλερὸν δάκρυ: Achilles' as he lies sleepless (θαλερὸν κατὰ δάκρυον εἶβεν, 24.9), and the Trojans' over the white bones (θαλερὸν δὲ κατείβετο δάκρυ παρειῶν, 24.794). See [[tears]], [[achilles]] and [[the-funeral-of-hector]].
