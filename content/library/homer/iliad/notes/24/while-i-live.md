# ὁρᾶν φάος ἠελίοιο (24.558), only here in the Iliad.
grc: ["ὁρᾶν φάος ἠελίοιο"]
en: [see the light of the sun]
---

To live and to see the light are one. Priam's thanks for his life, from the man who might have killed him, are the last words of his request for the body, and Achilles answers with a warning not to provoke him (24.553–570; [[looking-darkly]]). See [[priam]].
