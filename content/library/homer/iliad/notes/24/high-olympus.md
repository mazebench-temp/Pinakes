---
summary: Hermes goes back “to high Olympus” twice: after he has brought Priam to Achilles' hut (24.468), and after he has brought him back to the ford of the Xanthus (24.694).
---

The two departures close the god's two errands with the same half-verse, ἀπέβη πρὸς μακρὸν Ὄλυμπον. The first time he leaves because it would be a thing for blame for an immortal god to welcome mortals openly (24.462–464); the second time his escort is done, and Dawn spreads over the earth (24.692–695).
