# ἀρὴν καὶ λοιγὸν ἀμῦναι (24.489), only here in Homer.
grc: ["ἀρὴν καὶ λοιγὸν ἀμῦναι"]
en: [ward off disaster and ruin]
---

The phrase of protection is used of the defender who is absent: Achilles, who might ward off ruin from his father, is at Troy (24.486–492; [[peleus]], [[old-age-and-memory]]). ἀρή is the word for harm or bane, not the ἀρή “prayer” or “curse” of other books (see [[curses]] and [[prayer]]). See [[supplication]].
