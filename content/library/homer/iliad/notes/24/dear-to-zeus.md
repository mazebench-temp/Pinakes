---
summary: Priam goes straight to the house “where Achilles, dear to Zeus, used to sit” (24.472).
---

The phrase is used of Achilles before, when he rises to show himself at the ditch (18.203). Here it names the man whom Zeus has just commanded, through Thetis, to give up the body, and whose hut Zeus' son Hermes has opened for Priam (24.457–472; [[hut]]). See [[achilles]].
