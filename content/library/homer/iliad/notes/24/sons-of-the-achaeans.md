---
summary: Priam tells Achilles, “I had fifty when the sons of the Achaeans came” (24.495).
---

υἷες Ἀχαιῶν closes the verse in which Priam counts his sons, before he counts what is left of them (24.493–501). The fathers who came to Troy are also on Priam's mind: Achilles' father hopes every day to see his son come home (24.486–492). See [[sons-of-priam]] and [[achaeans]].
