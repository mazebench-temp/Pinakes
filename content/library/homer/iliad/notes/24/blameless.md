---
summary: Iris finds Thetis among the sea goddesses, weeping “for the fate of her noble son, who was to / perish in deep-soiled Troy, far from his native land” (24.85–86).
---

In her lament among the Nereids Thetis called him “a son, noble and strong” (18.55); in the last book Iris finds her weeping in her cave for the fate of that son, who is still alive (24.83–86; [[deep-soiled]], [[thetis]]). See [[achilles]].
