---
summary: Andromache laments that their son, “whom you and I begot, both most ill-fated,” will not reach his youth (24.727–728).
---

The verse is the one she spoke when she first saw Hector dead, ὃν τέκομεν σύ τʼ ἐγώ τε δυσάμμοροι (22.485 = 24.727): the word joins the two parents in one fate. Helen calls herself ἄμμορος, “ill-fated,” in her own lament (24.773). See [[andromache]], [[astyanax]] and [[lament]].
