---
summary: Priam's prayer opens with the address “Father Zeus, who rule from Ida, most glorious, greatest” (24.308).
---

The whole verse is the address of the oath and the prayers of Books 3 and 7 (3.276, 3.320, 7.202; [[father-zeus]], [[who-rules-from-ida]]). It is the last time the superlative is said in the poem. See [[zeus]] and [[prayer]].
