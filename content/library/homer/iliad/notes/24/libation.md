summary: Before Priam sets out, Hecuba brings wine in a golden cup so that he may pour to Zeus and pray for a safe return and a bird of omen; he washes his hands, takes the cup, and pours, looking up to the sky, in the verses of Achilles' libation for Patroclus, and Zeus sends his eagle. Zeus himself counts Hector's libations among the gifts that made him dear to the gods (24.69–70, 24.283–321).
# λείψαντε (24.285) and σπεῖσον (24.287) are found in the Iliad only here.

**Hector's gifts.** Zeus answers Hera that Hector too was dear to the gods: “For my altar never lacked an equal share of the feast, / libation and the savor of the burnt fat” (24.69–70), the verses in which he spoke of Troy in Book 4 (4.48–49; see [[equal-feast]] and [[zeus]]).

**Hecuba's cup.** As the wagon is yoked, Hecuba comes “holding wine sweet to the heart in her right hand / in a golden cup, so that they might pour a libation before they went” (24.284–285; see [[hecuba]] and [[cup]]). The pouring before a journey is the parting cup of the Odyssey, where Menelaus brings it to his guests in the same verses (*Odyssey* 15.148–150; Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 24.284). Her words join the libation to the prayer: “Here, pour a libation to father Zeus, and pray that you come home / again from hostile men” (24.287–288), and ask for his bird of omen on the right (24.290–298; see [[prayer]]). In Book 6 she offered Hector wine to pour to Zeus, and he would not pour it with unwashed hands (6.258–268; see [[hand-washing]]).

**The pouring.** Priam has water poured over his hands, takes the cup from his wife, and “then he prayed, standing in the middle of the enclosure, and poured out the wine, / looking up to the sky” (24.302–307). The two verses are those of Achilles' libation to Zeus of Dodona when he sent Patroclus into battle (24.306–307 ≈ 16.231–232; Leaf on 24.306; see [[achilles]]). Zeus granted Achilles half his prayer, and Patroclus did not come back (16.249–252); Priam's he grants, sending the eagle on the right across the city (24.314–321; see [[eagle]] and [[priams-palace]]).
