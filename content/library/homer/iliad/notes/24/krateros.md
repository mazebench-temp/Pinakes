---
summary: Hecuba says that Hector was fated to glut the dogs far from his parents, “at the side of a mighty man” (24.211–212).
---

ἀνδρὶ πάρα κρατερῷ, “beside a mighty man,” is Hecuba's word for Achilles, whose liver she would eat; it closes the verse of the thread Fate spun for Hector at his birth (24.209–213; [[mighty-fate]], [[hecuba]]). See [[achilles]].
