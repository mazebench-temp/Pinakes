---
summary: Hecuba comes to Priam “holding wine sweet to the heart in her right hand / in a golden cup” for a libation before he goes (24.284–285).
en: [sweet to the heart]
---

οἶνον μελίφρονα is the wine Hecuba offered Hector in Book 6, to pour to Zeus and to drink, and he refused it, “Do not bring me wine sweet to the heart” (6.258–264). Priam accepts the cup: he washes his hands, pours the wine and prays for a sign (24.302–313; [[libation]], [[prayer]]). See [[hecuba]].
