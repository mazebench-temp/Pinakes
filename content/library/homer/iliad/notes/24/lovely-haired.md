---
summary: Hermes tells Priam to beg Achilles by his father “and his mother of the lovely hair” (24.466), and Achilles tells Priam that “even Niobe of the lovely hair remembered food” (24.602).
---

μητέρος ἠϋκόμοιο is found only here in Homer. Priam does not follow Hermes' advice to the letter: he speaks of Achilles' father and of his own sons, and not of Thetis or of Achilles' child (24.486–506; [[supplication]]). Niobe's epithet begins Achilles' story of the mother who lost all her children and still ate (24.601–620; [[niobe]], [[mythological-paradigm]]).
