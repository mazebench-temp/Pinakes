---
summary: Zeus' message to Achilles, given to Thetis and delivered by her, is that the gods are angry because he “keeps Hector beside the curved ships” and has not released him (24.115, 24.136).
---

The verse is said twice, the second time by Thetis with the person changed, “you keep Hector beside the curved ships and have not released him” (24.115–116, 24.135–136; [[message-repeated]]). The ships where the body lies are the ships Hector fought to burn. See [[thetis]] and [[the-dragging-of-hector]].
