---
summary: After the mound is heaped, the Trojans “gathered together and feasted well on a glorious feast / in the house of Priam” (24.802–803).
---

ἐρικυδέα δαῖτα ends the verse of the funeral feast, the last meal of the poem; the Odyssey has the same verse-end for the feast of Nestor's people at Pylos (*Odyssey* 3.66). Zeus' own altar never lacked “an equal share of the feast” from Hector (24.69; [[equal-feast]]). See [[the-funeral-of-hector]] and [[feast]].
