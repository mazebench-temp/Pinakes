---
summary: Priam tells Hecuba that his might and heart bid him go to the ships, “into the wide army of the Achaeans” (24.198–199).
---

στρατὸν εὐρὺν Ἀχαιῶν is the place where Hecuba fears he will die: alone before the eyes of the man who killed his sons (24.199–205). Priam goes into it at night, unseen, with Hermes (24.443–447). See [[priam]] and [[achaeans]].
