---
summary: In Achilles' story Apollo killed the sons of Niobe, “and the daughters Artemis of the showering arrows” (24.605–606).
---

The archer goddess whose arrows are the sudden death of women kills Niobe's six daughters, as she killed Andromache's mother in Eetion's halls (6.428; [[the-children-of-niobe]]). Her brother kills the sons with the silver bow (24.605; [[god-of-the-silver-bow]]). Hecuba's lament turns the image to Hector, who lies fresh in her halls, “like one whom Apollo, god of the silver bow, / has come upon and killed with his gentle arrows” (24.758–759).
