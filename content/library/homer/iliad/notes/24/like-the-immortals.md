---
summary: Priam's first words to Achilles are “Remember your father, Achilles, like the gods” (24.486).
---

θεοῖς ἐπιείκελʼ Ἀχιλλεῦ, the vocative of praise, has been used to Achilles by Phoenix, who reared him (9.485, 9.494), by the dying Hector (22.279), and by the soul of Patroclus (23.80). Priam's supplication opens with it, and with the name of Peleus, “who is of my age, on the deadly threshold of old age” (24.486–487; [[supplication]], [[peleus]]). See [[achilles]].
