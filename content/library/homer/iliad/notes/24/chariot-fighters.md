---
summary: “Now the other gods and the men who fight from chariots / slept all night long” (24.677–678), all but Hermes, who keeps awake to bring Priam out of the camp.
---

The verse that opened Book 2 opens the last night of the poem, and its second line is the line of Book 10 (24.677 = 2.1, 24.678 = 10.2; [[soft]], [[hypnos]]). In Book 2 Zeus lay awake devising the slaughter he had promised Thetis; here Hermes lies awake devising how to send King Priam away from the ships unseen (24.679–681; [[when-other-mortals-are-asleep]]). See [[hermes]].
