---
summary: Zeus will not let the gods steal “bold Hector” from Achilles (24.71–72), and at the end the Trojans carry “bold Hector” out to his pyre, shedding tears (24.786).
---

The epithet that Polydamas and Andromache gave him in life (12.60, 22.454–456) frames the story of his body in the last book. The theft the gods proposed is refused, the body is ransomed instead, and it comes home to the pyre with the same word (24.71–76, 24.786–787; [[the-dragging-of-hector]], [[the-funeral-of-hector]]).
