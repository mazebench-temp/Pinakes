summary: The gods' plan to steal Hector's body pleases all but Hera, Poseidon and “the gray-eyed maiden” (24.25–26).
---

γλαυκώπιδι κούρῃ is found only here in the Iliad. Athena is one of the three gods who hold to their hatred of Ilios, and the next verses give the reason: the insult of Alexander, who praised the goddess who offered him lust (24.25–30; [[the-judgment-of-paris]]). Walter Leaf reports that Aristarchus athetized these verses (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 24.23). See [[athena]].
