---
summary: Thetis and Iris find Zeus on Olympus, and around him “the blessed gods who live forever, sat assembled” (24.98–99).
---

εἵαθʼ ὁμηγερέες, “sat assembled,” opens two verses fifteen lines apart: the sea goddesses gathered around Thetis in her cave, weeping for her son (24.83–84), and the gods gathered around Zeus, who receive her with a golden cup (24.98–102; [[blessed-gods]]). Thetis has said that she feels shame to mingle with the immortals (24.90–91). See [[olympians]] and [[thetis]].
