---
summary: Zeus sends Iris to Priam: “Go, swift Iris, leave the seat of Olympus” (24.144).
---

This is the last of the four commands that open with βάσκʼ ἴθι, Ἶρι ταχεῖα (8.399, 11.186, 15.158). Here the order is to “take a message to great-hearted Priam,” and Iris delivers it in Zeus' words (24.144–158, 24.171–187; [[message-repeated]], [[messenger-scene]]). See [[iris]].
