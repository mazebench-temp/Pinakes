---
summary: “Answering her, Zeus the cloud-gatherer said” (24.64): Zeus answers Hera's protest that Hector and Achilles cannot have equal honor.
---

The verse introduces Zeus' judgment in the quarrel of the gods over the body: the honor of the two will not be the same, but Hector too was dearest to the gods of all the mortals in Ilios, and the body will be ransomed, not stolen (24.64–76; [[answering-him]], [[time]]). It is the epithet's last use in the poem. See [[zeus]] and [[hera]].
