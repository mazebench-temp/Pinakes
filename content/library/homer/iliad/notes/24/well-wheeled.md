summary: The mule wagon of the ransom is “well-wheeled” in Zeus' order and Iris' message (24.150, 24.179), as Priam bids his sons make it ready and as they bring it out (24.189, 24.266), and when Andromache and Hecuba rush to it at the gates of Troy (24.711).
---

The entry tells the whole story. In the book the epithet follows the wagon from Zeus' instructions to Priam's return, and its last use is the women's rush to touch Hector's head (24.710–712; [[wagon]], [[lament]]). See [[the-ransom-of-hector]].
