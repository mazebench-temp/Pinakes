---
summary: Priam tells Achilles that of his fifty sons, “Of most of them furious Ares has loosened the knees” (24.498).
---

θοῦρος Ἄρης stands for the war that has taken Priam's sons, and the verse ends in the formula of death in battle ([[loosened-his-limbs]]). See [[ares]] and [[sons-of-priam]].
