# περικαλλέας (24.229), only here in the Iliad.
grc: [περικαλλέας]
---

The epithet begins and ends the list of the ransom: the first gift, the robes, and the most precious, the cup that the Thracians gave Priam when he came to them on an embassy, which “not even this / did the old man spare in his halls” (24.228–237; [[the-ransom-of-hector]], [[cup]]). Nestor's cup was δέπας περικαλλές in the same words (11.632; [[the-cup-of-nestor]]). See [[robe]] and [[priam]].
