# δυσμενέων ἀνδρῶν (24.288), the formula in the genitive, and δυσμενέες (24.365; also 10.100, already claimed).
grc: ["δυσμενέων ἀνδρῶν", δυσμενέες]
en: [hostile]
---

The two warnings are the fear of Priam's wife and of his disguised guide: the enemy camp, where a man who goes alone at night may not come back (24.287–289, 24.362–369; [[breathing-fury]]). The word is the word of Book 10, where the enemy are near at hand in the dark (10.100). See [[priam]].
