---
summary: Priam's sons yoke “the strong-hoofed mules that work in harness, / which the Mysians had once given to Priam” (24.277–278).
---

The epithet of horses is given here to mules, as in the Odyssey, where Nausicaa yokes her strong-hoofed mules to drive home from the river (*Odyssey* 6.252–253). The second epithet of the verse, ἐντεσιεργούς, is found only here ([[that-works-in-harness]]). See [[mules]] and [[wagon]].
