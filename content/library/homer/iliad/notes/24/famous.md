# κλυτὸν Ἄργος (24.437), κλυτὰ δῶρα (24.458), κλυτοῦ Ἕκτορος (24.789): each only here in the Iliad.
grc: ["κλυτὸν Ἄργος", "κλυτὰ δῶρα", "κλυτοῦ Ἕκτορος"]
en: [glorious]
---

κλυτός, the commonest of the family, is said in this book of a place, a gift, a house and a man. Argos is the farthest place Hermes can think of to show his zeal as an escort (24.437; [[argos]]). The gifts are the ransom, which Hermes drives through the gate he has opened (24.457–458). κλυτὰ δώματα is the formula of the famous house, here Priam's, where the singers are set beside the corded bed (24.719–720; [[priams-palace]]). And the phrase “famous Hector” is found only at his pyre (24.789; [[kleos]]). See [[hector]].
