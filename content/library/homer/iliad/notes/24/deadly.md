# ὀλοῷ (24.39, 24.487): only in this book in the Iliad.
grc: [ὀλοῷ]
---

The same dative serves two very different things. In Apollo's protest Achilles is ὀλοός, ruinous, the man who has no mind that can be bent and no pity (24.39–45; [[supple]], [[pity]]). In Priam's supplication old age has a deadly threshold, the phrase of Priam's own picture of his death in Book 22 (ἐπὶ γήραος οὐδῷ, 22.60), now said of Peleus, who is of Priam's age (24.486–487; [[old-age-and-memory]]). See [[achilles]] and [[peleus]].
