# ἐΰννητόν (24.580), only here in the Iliad.
grc: [ἐΰννητόν]
---

The tunic, from Priam's own ransom, is used to clothe the body: the slave women wash and anoint Hector and put a fine mantle and a tunic around him before Achilles lays him on the bier (24.580–590; [[tunic]], [[the-ransom-of-hector]]). See [[hector]].
