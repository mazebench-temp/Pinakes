---
summary: “Looking at him darkly, swift-footed Achilles said” (24.559), when Priam refuses to sit and asks for the body at once.
---

The glare answers Priam's haste. Priam has refused the chair, told Achilles to release Hector “quickly,” and wished him a safe return home (24.553–558), and Achilles replies that he means to release Hector without being told, since his mother brought him Zeus' word, and warns Priam not to stir his heart further, “lest I do not spare even you, old man” (24.560–570). The old man is afraid and obeys (24.571; [[so-he-spoke]]). See [[achilles]] and [[priam]].
