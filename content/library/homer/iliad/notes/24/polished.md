# ἐϋξέστῃ (24.280), ἐϋξέστου (24.578), ἐϋξέστην (24.590): each only here in the Iliad.
grc: [ἐϋξέστῃ, ἐϋξέστου, ἐϋξέστην]
---

ἐΰξεστος, “well-planed,” is the craftsman's word for the wagon of the ransom, ἐϋξέστη ἀπήνη, which carries the gifts out to the camp and the body back to Troy (24.275–276, 24.578–579, 24.589–590; [[wagon]]). The polished manger is Priam's own, at which he reared the horses that draw his chariot (24.279–280; [[horses]]). See [[the-ransom-of-hector]].
