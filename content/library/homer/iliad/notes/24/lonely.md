# οἰοπόλοισιν (24.614), only here in the Iliad.
grc: [οἰοπόλοισιν]
---

The lonely place of the similes of Books 13, 17 and 19 is here the place of a mourning mother turned to stone, where the nymphs dance (24.614–617; [[niobe]], [[sipylus]]). See [[the-children-of-niobe]].
