# στονόεσσαν (24.721), only here in the Iliad.
grc: [στονόεσσαν]
---

στονόεσσα ἀοιδή, “the song of groaning,” is the only use of the epithet in the Iliad that is not of missiles. The professional singers sing the dirge and the women answer them with groans, before the three laments of Andromache, Hecuba and Helen (24.719–723; see the translation's note on 24.720–722; [[lament]]).
