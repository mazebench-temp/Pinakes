summary: Twenty-nine speeches close with the formula. Among the gods they close in obedience, “So he spoke, and storm-footed Iris rose to carry the message” (24.77, 24.159); in Priam's house in grief and haste (24.200, 24.228, 24.247, 24.265); on the journey and in the hut in fear and its easing (24.358, 24.424, 24.571, 24.689); and in Troy in the three laments, “So she spoke, weeping” (24.746, 24.760, 24.776).
# ἦ ῥʼ, Ἀχιλεὺς δʼ (24.643): the phrase, only here.
grc: ["ἦ ῥʼ Ἀχιλεὺς"]
---

**The gods obey.** Zeus' orders are followed at once: Iris rises (24.77, 24.159), Thetis “did not disobey” (24.120), and so does Hermes (24.339; [[silver-footed]], [[guide]]); the prayer verse, “So he spoke in prayer, and Zeus the counselor heard him” (24.314), turns the formula to the god's answer ([[hear-me]]).

**Priam's house.** Hecuba answers Priam's plan with a wail (24.200); ἦ καί carries Priam from speech to action as he opens his chests and drives the Trojans off with his staff (24.228, 24.247); and his sons obey “fearing their father's rebuke,” the verse of the Lycians and of Antilochus' horses with πατρός for ἄνακτος (24.265; 12.413, 23.417, 23.446).

**Fear.** Priam's terror at the sight of Hermes, “the old man's mind was confounded” (24.358), turns to joy at his news (24.424). In the hut the verse of Chryses' fear in Book 1, ὣς ἔφατʼ, ἔδεισεν δʼ ὃ γέρων καὶ ἐπείθετο μύθῳ, “So he spoke, and the old man was afraid, and obeyed his word,” closes Achilles' warning to Priam (1.33 = 24.571; [[book-1-and-book-24]]), and its first half returns when Hermes wakes him (24.689). Priam's supplication ends in tears, “So he spoke, and in him he roused the longing for lament for his father” (24.507), the verse that roused the Myrmidons' lament in Book 23 (23.108; [[the-longing-for-lament]]).

**The laments.** ὣς ἔφατο κλαίουσʼ, “So she spoke, weeping,” closes each of the three laments, Andromache's with the verse of Briseis' lament and of Andromache's own in Book 22, “and the women groaned with her” (24.746 = 19.301, 22.515), Hecuba's with relentless lamentation (24.760; [[relentless]]), and Helen's with the countless people groaning (24.776; [[boundless-sea]]). See [[lament]].
