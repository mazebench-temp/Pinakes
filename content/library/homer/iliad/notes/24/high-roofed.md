# ὑψορόφοιο (24.317), only here in the Iliad.
grc: [ὑψορόφοιο]
---

The epithet of the inner room joins Priam's treasure chamber, from which the ransom comes, to the rich man's door of the simile of the eagle, “well fitted with bolts” (24.191–192, 24.317–320; [[fragrant]], [[wealthy]]). See [[priams-palace]] and [[eagle]].
