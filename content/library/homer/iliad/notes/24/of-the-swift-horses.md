summary: Hecuba bids Priam ask for a sign so that, trusting in it, he may go “to the ships of the Danaans of the swift horses” (24.295), and Priam repeats the words in his prayer (24.313).
---

The last two of the formula's nine uses in the Iliad are in the same verse, the second with the person changed, for Priam repeats Hecuba's words in his prayer (24.292–295 = 24.310–313; [[prayer]], [[eagle]]). See [[danaans]].
