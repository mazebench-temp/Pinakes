summary: Hermes wakes Priam in Achilles' porch and asks how he can still sleep “among enemy men” (24.683–684).

The plural as a noun, “the enemy,” is the use the entry describes, here in Hermes' warning. Achilles has spared him, but if Agamemnon and the Achaeans learn of him, his sons will have to pay three times the ransom for him alive (24.683–688; [[dream-scene]], [[hermes]]).
