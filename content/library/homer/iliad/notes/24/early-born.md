---
summary: “And when early-born Dawn appeared, the rosy-fingered, / then around the pyre of famous Hector the people were astir” (24.788–789).
---

The dawn line of 1.477 is used whole in the Iliad only there and here, on the morning when the Trojans quench Hector's pyre and gather his bones ([[dawn-line]], [[rosy-fingered]]). The day before, the tenth, had come with an epithet found nowhere else in the Iliad, φαεσίμβροτος ἠώς, the dawn “bringing light to mortals” (24.785; [[bringing-light-to-mortals]]). See [[eos]] and [[the-funeral-of-hector]].
