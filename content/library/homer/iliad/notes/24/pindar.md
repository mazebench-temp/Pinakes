---
summary: Pindar consoles Hieron with a saying of the men of old, one good for two woes, and with Peleus, who feasted with the gods at his wedding and whose only son by Thetis died by the bow, in the order of Achilles' consolation of Priam.
---

- *Pythian* 3.80–103, on 24.527–540: [[pindar-the-jars-of-zeus]] and [[the-two-jars-of-zeus]].
- “And the gods feasted with both”, at the weddings of Cadmus and of Peleus (*Pythian* 3.93), as Hera reminds Apollo that all the gods came to the wedding of Thetis (24.62–63; Leaf on 24.63): [[peleus]] and [[apollo]].
