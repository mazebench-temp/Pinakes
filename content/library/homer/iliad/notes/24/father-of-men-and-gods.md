---
summary: “And among them the father of men and gods began to speak” (24.103): Zeus tells Thetis why he has called her.
---

The verse opened Zeus' question to the gods in Book 22, whether to save Hector from Achilles or let him die (22.167). In the last book it opens his answer to the gods' quarrel over Hector's body: Achilles shall have the glory, and Thetis is to tell him to give the body back (24.103–119).
