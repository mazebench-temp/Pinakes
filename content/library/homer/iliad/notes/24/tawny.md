---
summary: Among the gifts of the ransom Priam brings out “two gleaming tripods and four cauldrons” (24.233).
---

αἴθων, “fiery, tawny,” is said of iron and of bronze vessels as well as of beasts: the translation renders it “gleaming” of the tripods, as of the cauldrons in Book 9 (24.233; [[tripod]], [[the-ransom-of-hector]]). See [[priam]].
