# γναμπτὸν (24.41), only here in Homer.
grc: [γναμπτὸν]
en: [that can be bent]
---

The book has both senses of the word that the entry describes: the mind that cannot be bent, which Apollo sets beside the lion that gives way to its strength (24.39–45; [[like-a-lion]]), and the limbs in which fear shows itself (24.358–360). See [[achilles]] and [[priam]].
