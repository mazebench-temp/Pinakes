---
summary: Thetis sits down “beside father Zeus” (24.100); Hecuba tells Priam, “Here, pour a libation to father Zeus” (24.287); and Priam's prayer opens with the whole-verse address, “Father Zeus, who rule from Ida, most glorious, greatest” (24.308).
---

The address is the verse of the oaths and prayers of the war: Agamemnon's oath before the duel and the armies' prayers before the duels of Book 3 and Book 7 (3.276, 3.320, 7.202). Priam uses it to ask for a welcome and a sign, and Zeus sends the eagle (24.306–321; [[who-rules-from-ida]], [[most-glorious]], [[prayer]]). See [[zeus]].
