---
summary: Thetis goes to Olympus, “and before her swift Iris, wind-footed, / led the way” (24.95–96).
---

ποδήνεμος ὠκέα Ἶρις is the last of its ten uses in Homer. Iris leads Thetis up through the parted sea to the sky and to Zeus (24.95–98; [[storm-footed]], [[swift-footed]]). See [[iris]] and [[thetis]].
