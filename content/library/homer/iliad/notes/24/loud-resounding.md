---
summary: Priam drives his chariot “out from the doorway and the loud-resounding colonnade” of his palace (24.323).
---

ἐκ δʼ ἔλασε προθύροιο καὶ αἰθούσης ἐριδούπου is the verse with which Telemachus and Peisistratus drive out from the houses where they have been guests, Diocles' at Pherae and Menelaus' at Sparta (*Odyssey* 3.493, 15.146, 15.191; [[chariot-journey]]). Here it sends Priam out from the colonnade where he had driven off the Trojans with his staff (24.237–247). See [[priams-palace]].
