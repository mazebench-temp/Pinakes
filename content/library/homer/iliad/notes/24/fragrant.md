---
summary: Priam goes down “into the fragrant chamber, / high-roofed, of cedar” to choose the ransom (24.191–192).
---

The verse is the one in which Hecuba went down to choose the robe for Athena in Book 6, with αὐτός for αὐτή (6.288; [[high-roofed]]). From its chests Priam takes the robes, cloaks, talents, tripods and the Thracian cup (24.228–237; [[the-ransom-of-hector]]). See [[priams-palace]].
