# ἀγήνορι θυμῷ (24.42), only here in Homer.
grc: ["ἀγήνορι θυμῷ"]
---

The dative of the formula θυμὸς ἀγήνωρ, “the proud heart,” is set in Apollo's simile of the lion that goes among the flocks to take his feast: Achilles has lost all pity (24.41–44; [[like-a-lion]], [[thymos]]). See [[achilles]] and [[apollo]].
