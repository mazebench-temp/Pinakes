---
summary: “Then the goddess silver-footed Thetis answered her” (24.89), when Iris calls her to Zeus, and “So he spoke, and the goddess silver-footed Thetis did not disobey” (24.120).
---

θεὰ Θέτις ἀργυρόπεζα closes the verse as in the scenes of Book 18 and 19, where Thetis answered her son and went to Olympus for the arms (18.127, 18.146, 19.28). In the last book it frames her two answers to Zeus' summons: she will go, though ashamed to mingle with the immortals, and she obeys his order to go to her son (24.89–92, 24.120–122).
