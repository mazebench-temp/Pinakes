# πολυδάκρυτος (24.620), only here in the Iliad.
grc: [πολυδάκρυτος]
en: [much wept for]
---

πολυδάκρυτος, “much wept for,” turns the epithet of war to the one who is mourned. Achilles' promise is kept in Troy, where the whole city weeps for Hector at the gates and in his house (24.707–776; [[lament]], [[tears]]). See [[hector]].
