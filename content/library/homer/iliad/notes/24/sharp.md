---
summary: Hermes, in his false tale, has seen Hector drive the Achaeans to their ships and keep “cutting them down with the sharp bronze” (24.391–393).
---

ὀξέϊ χαλκῷ, the formula of the weapon, belongs here to the stranger's memory of Hector at his greatest, while Achilles stayed out of the fight “being angry with the son of Atreus” (24.391–395; [[truth-and-deception]]). See [[hector]] and [[bronze]].
