---
summary: Lamenting Hector, Helen says that when anyone else in the house rebuked her, Hector held them back, and that Priam, her father-in-law, “was always kind, like a father” (24.768–772).
---

The half-verse spares one man among all those who might rebuke her, in a parenthesis: ἑκυρὸς δὲ πατὴρ ὣς ἤπιος αἰεί (24.770). See the article above, [[helen]] and [[priam]].
