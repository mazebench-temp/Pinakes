summary: Priam finds Achilles just finished eating, “and the table still stood beside him” (24.476); at the meal Achilles offers him, Automedon “took bread and set it out on the table / in beautiful baskets” (24.625–626).
---

The table frames Priam's night in the hut. When he enters unseen, Achilles has just ceased “eating and drinking; and the table still stood beside him” (24.475–476; see [[achilles]] and [[hut]]). After Niobe's example the two eat together: Achilles slaughters a sheep, and “Automedon took bread and set it out on the table / in beautiful baskets, while Achilles served the meat” (24.621–626; see [[feast]] and [[automedon]]).
