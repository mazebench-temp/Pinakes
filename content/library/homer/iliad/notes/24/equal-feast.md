---
summary: Zeus tells Hera that Hector never failed him: “For my altar never lacked an equal share of the feast, / libation and the savor of the burnt fat” (24.69–70).
---

The verse is Zeus' praise of Troy in Book 4, where he would not willingly destroy the city that honored him so (4.48–49 = 24.69–70). In the last book it is said of Hector alone, the mortal dearest to the gods in Ilios, as the reason why Zeus will see the body returned (24.66–76; [[sacrifice]], [[hector]]). See [[zeus]].
