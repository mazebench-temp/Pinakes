---
summary: Hermes tells Priam that the worms that “feed on men slain in battle” have not eaten Hector (24.414–415).
---

The verse is the verse of Achilles' fear for the body of Patroclus, which Thetis promised to keep from the flies (19.30–31); now it is said of Hector's body, kept whole by the gods for twelve days (24.411–423; [[maggots]]). See [[the-dragging-of-hector]] and [[hermes]].
