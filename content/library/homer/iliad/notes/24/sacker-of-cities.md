---
summary: Zeus tells Thetis that a quarrel has arisen among the gods “about Hector's corpse and Achilles, sacker of cities” (24.108).
---

Zeus gives him the epithet of the destroyer in the verse that sets him against the body he is to give back (24.107–111). It is its last use in the poem. Mourning Hector, Andromache foresees that the city “will be sacked,” πέρσεται, the verb from which the epithet is made (24.728–729). See [[achilles]].
