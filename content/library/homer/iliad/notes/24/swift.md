---
summary: Hecuba bids Priam ask Zeus for “a bird of omen, his swift messenger” (24.292), and Priam asks for it in the same words (24.310).
---

ταχύς, the epithet of the Locrian Ajax, is said here of the bird that Zeus sends as his messenger, “dearest of birds” to him and the strongest (24.292–294 = 24.310–312, with the person changed; [[prayer]]). The answer is the eagle (24.315; [[surest-of-birds]], [[eagle]]).
