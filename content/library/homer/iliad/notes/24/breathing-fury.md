# μένεα πνείοντας (24.364), the accusative of the entry's formula: only here.
grc: ["μένεα πνείοντας"]
---

The formula that described the Abantes in the Catalogue and the Achaeans marching or holding their ground (2.536, 3.8, 11.508) is used here by a god in disguise, to show Priam what he is driving into: an army breathing fury, at night (24.362–369; [[hostile-men]]). The Achaeans Priam meets are asleep (24.443–447). See [[achaeans]] and [[hermes]].
