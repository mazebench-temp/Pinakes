---
summary: Achilles tells Priam that a messenger came to him from Zeus, “my mother, who bore me, the daughter of the Old Man of the Sea” (24.561–562).
---

In Book 1 the title closed the verse that named Thetis as the goddess who had plotted with Zeus, ἀργυρόπεζα Θέτις θυγάτηρ ἁλίοιο γέροντος (1.538, 1.556). Achilles fills the first half of the verse with his own words, μήτηρ, ἥ μʼ ἔτεκεν, “my mother, who bore me,” and makes her the messenger of Zeus whose word he is obeying (24.560–562; [[messengers-of-zeus]]). He tells Priam so to warn him not to provoke him further (24.560–570). See [[thetis]] and [[old-man-of-the-sea]].
