# εὐεργής (24.396), the nominative: only here in the Iliad.
grc: [εὐεργής]
---

νηῦς εὐεργής is the Odyssey's phrase for a ship; the Iliad gives the word to chariots (5.585, 13.399, 16.743). Hermes uses it in the story he tells Priam of himself, one of the Myrmidons, who came in a single ship with Achilles (24.396–400; [[truth-and-deception]], [[myrmidons]]). See [[hermes]].
