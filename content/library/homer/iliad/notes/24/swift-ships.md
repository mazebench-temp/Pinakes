---
summary: The book begins as the people scatter “to go to the swift ships” (24.1–2); Priam wishes his sons had been killed “beside the swift ships in Hector's place” (24.254); Hermes would escort Priam “in a swift ship” (24.438); and Achilles knows that a god led Priam “to the swift ships of the Achaeans” (24.564).
---

The first verse of the book, λῦτο δʼ ἀγών, λαοὶ δὲ θοὰς ἐπὶ νῆας ἕκαστοι, closes the games and sends the army back to the ships, leaving Achilles alone with his grief (24.1–3). The two θοήν of the night verse are excepted (24.366, 24.653; [[swift-night]]). See [[ships]].
