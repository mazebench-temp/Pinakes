# ἀμειλίχου (24.734), only here in Homer.
grc: [ἀμειλίχου]
---

The entry describes the three uses, of Hades, of the Erinys and of this master. Here the word is one of a pair in Andromache's lament: the master will be “implacable,” ἀμείλιχος, because Hector “was not kind,” μείλιχος, in battle (24.734, 24.739; [[meilichos]], [[captive-women]]). See [[astyanax]] and [[andromache]].
