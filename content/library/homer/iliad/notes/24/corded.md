# τρητοῖς (24.720), the short dative of the entry's form: only here in the Iliad.
grc: [τρητοῖς]
---

τρητοῖς ἐν λεχέεσσι is the bed of the Odyssey's hosts and guests; in the Iliad the epithet was given before only to the bed of Paris and Helen (3.448). Here the bed is a bier, the body's resting place for the nine days of mourning (24.719–722, 24.664; [[bed]], [[lament]]). See [[hector]].
