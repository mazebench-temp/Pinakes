---
summary: Thetis, “the brilliant goddess,” takes up a dark veil before she goes to Olympus (24.93).
---

“So she spoke, and the brilliant goddess took a veil, / dark blue, and no garment was ever blacker than this” (24.93–94). The formula of a goddess's rank is set against her mourning: Walter Leaf notes that the use of black as a sign of mourning is found only here in Homer (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 24.93). Its last use in the Iliad is also its second for Thetis, who had it when she brought Achilles his armor (19.6). See [[thetis]] and [[veil]].
