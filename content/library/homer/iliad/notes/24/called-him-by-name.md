---
summary: Thetis sits beside her son, “and stroked him with her hand, and spoke to him, and called him by name” (24.127); and Hecuba “stood before the horses, and spoke to him, and called him by name” (24.286).
---

The first is the whole verse of Book 1, where Thetis came up from the sea to her weeping son (1.361 = 24.127): it is her last visit in the poem, and its message is that he must give up the body (24.128–137; [[thetis]]). The second keeps the half-line and changes the gesture: Hecuba does not touch Priam, but stands in front of his horses with the cup of wine and bids him pray (24.283–298; [[hecuba]], [[libation]]).
