summary: In his parable of the jars Achilles tells Priam of “the man to whom Zeus who delights in thunder gives a mixture” of evils and blessings (24.529).
---

The epithet of the thunderbolt names the god who keeps the jars on his floor and deals out from them to mortals (24.527–533; [[the-two-jars-of-zeus]]). See [[zeus]].
