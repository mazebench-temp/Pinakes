# ἁδινὰ, the adverb (24.123, 24.510; also 23.225, already claimed in the phrase ἁδινὰ στεναχίζων).
grc: [ἁδινὰ]
en: [without ceasing, unceasingly]
---

ἁδινά, the adverb, is the word of unceasing grief: Achilles' in his hut before his mother speaks to him (24.123), and Priam's, crouched before the feet of the man who killed his son, while Achilles weeps for his father and for Patroclus (24.509–512). The verse of 24.747 is Hecuba's verse of Book 22, where she led the lament of the Trojan women for the first time (22.430; [[lament]]). See [[hecuba]] and [[priam]].
