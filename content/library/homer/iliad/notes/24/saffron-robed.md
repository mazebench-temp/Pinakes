---
summary: As Hermes leaves Priam at the ford, “saffron-robed Dawn was spreading over all the earth” (24.695).
---

The verse is the first verse of Book 8, with δέ for μέν (8.1 = 24.695): it opened the day of the Trojan victory, and here it opens the day on which Priam brings Hector's body home (24.695–697; [[dawn-line]]). It is the epithet's last use. See [[eos]].
