# καλλιζώνων (24.698), the genitive of the entry's form: only here in the Iliad.
grc: [καλλιζώνων]
---

The phrase of the whole people names Troy at dawn, before Cassandra sees her father from Pergamus and calls the city out to meet the body (24.697–706; [[cassandra]]). Then every man and every woman comes to the gates (24.707–709). See [[trojan-women]].
