# ἄργυφον (24.621), the short form: only here in the Iliad.
grc: [ἄργυφον]
---

ὄϊν ἄργυφον, the short form of the adjective, is the sheep of the last meal of the poem, killed by Achilles with his own hand (24.621–627; [[feast-type-scene]], [[sheep]]). The long form named the cave of Thetis in Book 18 (18.50). See [[achilles]].
