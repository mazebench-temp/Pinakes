---
summary: “and at once he sent an eagle, surest in omen of winged birds” (24.315): Zeus answers Priam's prayer.
---

The verse answered Agamemnon's prayer in Book 8 (8.247). Here Priam has asked, in the words Hecuba gave him, for “a bird of omen, your swift messenger,” on the right (24.308–313), and the eagle comes, with wings as wide as a rich man's door (24.315–321; [[eagle]], [[high-roofed]]). See [[prayer]] and [[zeus]].
