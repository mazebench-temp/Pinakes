---
summary: Iris leaps into the sea “between Samos and rocky Imbros” to bring Thetis to Olympus (24.78–79).
---

μεσσηγὺς … καὶ Ἴμβρου παιπαλοέσσης is the half-verse of Poseidon's cave in Book 13, “between Tenedos and rocky Imbros” (13.33). In the sea between the islands, where Poseidon stabled his horses, Iris dives to the cave of Thetis (24.78–84; [[imbros]], [[samothrace]]). παιπαλόεις, “rugged,” is another word than πετρήεις; the translation renders both “rocky.” See [[iris]].
