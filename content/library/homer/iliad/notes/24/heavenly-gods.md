# Οὐρανίωνες alone (24.547); 1.570, 17.195 and 24.612 have θεοὶ Οὐρανίωνες, already claimed.
grc: [Οὐρανίωνες]
---

The two uses come within seventy verses of each other, and both make the gods the authors of sorrow and of its end. The heavenly ones brought Priam his war (24.543–548), and the same heavenly ones buried the children of Niobe when no one else could (24.610–612; [[the-children-of-niobe]]). See [[olympians]].
