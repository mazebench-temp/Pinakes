---
summary: Achilles tells Priam to sleep outside, “lest one of the Achaeans / come in here, a counselor, one of those who always / sit beside me and take counsel with me” (24.650–652).
---

βουληφόρος is the word for the leaders who sit in council with a king, and Achilles' fear is that one of them will come to him at night and see Priam, and tell Agamemnon (24.650–655; [[black-night]]). See [[elders]] and [[achilles]].
