---
summary: Zeus bids Hermes “lead Priam to the hollow ships of the Achaeans” (24.336), and Andromache foresees the women of Troy “carried off in the hollow ships” (24.731).
---

The first verse uses κοῖλος, the second its synonym γλαφυρός. Priam comes to the hollow ships to ransom his son and goes home; the wives and children of Troy will go to the same ships as captives, “and I among them” (24.729–732; [[andromache]], [[captive-women]]).
