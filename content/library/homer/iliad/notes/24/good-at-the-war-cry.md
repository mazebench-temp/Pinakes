---
summary: Priam, rebuking his nine surviving sons, names among them “Polites, good at the war cry” (24.250).
---

The formula of Menelaus and Diomedes is given here, once in the Iliad, to a son of Priam, and in a list of sons whom their father calls “liars and dancers” (24.249–262). Polites was the son whose likeness Iris took when she came to warn the Trojans (2.791–794; [[polites]], [[sons-of-priam]]).
