---
summary: Priam finds Achilles with only two companions busy at his side, “the hero Automedon and Alcimus, offshoot of Ares” (24.474).
---

The epithet of the leaders of the Catalogues is given here to a companion of Achilles. Alcimus is named with Automedon again as the two companions Achilles honored most after Patroclus, who unyoke Priam's horses and mules (24.573–576; [[alcimedon]], [[automedon]]). See [[achilles]].
