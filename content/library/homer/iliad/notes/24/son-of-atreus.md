# Ἀτρεΐωνι (24.395), only here in the Iliad.
grc: [Ἀτρεΐωνι]
---

Both uses are Hermes'. The first is part of his false tale, in which the quarrel of Book 1 explains why a Myrmidon watched Hector from the side (24.394–395; [[truth-and-deception]]); the second is the warning that wakes Priam before dawn (24.683–689). See [[agamemnon]] and [[shepherd-of-the-people]].
