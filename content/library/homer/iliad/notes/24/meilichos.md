---
summary: Andromache tells her son that the Achaeans will take revenge on him, “For your father was not kind in grim battle” (24.739).
---

οὐ γὰρ μείλιχος ἔσκε πατὴρ τεὸς ἐν δαῒ λυγρῇ: the word of Patroclus' kindness (17.671, 19.300) is denied of Hector in battle. Andromache gives it as the reason why some Achaean, whose brother, father or son Hector killed, will throw the boy from the tower (24.734–739; [[astyanax]], [[grim]]); and it is also why the people mourn him (24.740). Helen's lament ends with Hector's kindness to her (24.771–775). See [[andromache]] and [[implacable]].
