---
summary: Priam tells Achilles of his sons, “Of most of them furious Ares has loosened the knees” (24.498).
---

ὑπὸ γούνατʼ ἔλυσεν, “loosened the knees,” is the variant of the formula with the knees, used here of a war's worth of deaths at once, as Priam counts the fifty sons he had and the few left (24.495–501; [[furious]], [[sons-of-priam]]). See [[ares]].
