---
summary: Hermes, as a young Myrmidon, tells Priam that he has seen Hector many times “in the battle where men win glory” (24.391).
---

μάχῃ ἔνι κυδιανείρῃ is the battle in which Hector won his glory, as the god in disguise remembers it for his father (24.390–394; [[kydos]]). See [[hector]] and [[hermes]].
