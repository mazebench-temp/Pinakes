---
summary: “but Achilles slept in the inner part of the well-built hut, / and beside him lay fair-cheeked Briseis” (24.675–676).
---

The verse that put Achilles to sleep beside Diomede at the end of the embassy puts him to sleep beside Briseis at the end of Priam's visit (9.663–665; [[fair-cheeked]]). Priam and the herald sleep in the porch (24.673–674); Achilles sleeps within, beside the woman whose seizure began his quarrel with Agamemnon (1.345–348). See [[hut]] and [[briseis]].
