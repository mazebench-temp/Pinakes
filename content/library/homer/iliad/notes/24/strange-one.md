---
summary: Priam tells his wife, “Strange one, an Olympian messenger has come to me from Zeus” (24.194).
---

δαιμονίη is the husband's address to his wife: Zeus' to Hera and Hector's to Andromache in the farewell of Book 6 (1.561, 6.486). Priam uses it to tell Hecuba of Iris' message and to ask her counsel, and she answers with a wail (24.193–205). See [[hecuba]] and [[priam]].
