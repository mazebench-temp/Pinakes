summary: Herodotus shows the things of this book in later use: the chest filled with treasure, the wagon that two sons drew to the temple for their mother, the herald's staff found on the shore at Mycale, and the stranger who comes, a killer, to be purified in a king's house.
---

- The eight chests filled with stones and topped with gold to deceive Polycrates (*Histories* 3.123.2): [[larnax]].
- Cleobis and Biton, who drew their mother's wagon to the temple of Hera (*Histories* 1.31.2–5): [[wagon]].
- The herald's staff, κηρυκήιον, seen lying at the edge of the surf at Mycale (*Histories* 9.100.1): [[wand]] and [[heralds]].
- Adrastus, “not clean of hands”, purified by Croesus at Sardis, where purification is much as among the Greeks (*Histories* 1.35.1–2), against the exile of the simile, who comes to a rich man's house (24.480–482): [[the-killer-in-exile]].
