---
summary: Hermes leaves Priam at the ford of “the eddying Xanthus, whom immortal Zeus begot” (24.692–694).
---

The two verses that bring Priam and the herald to the ford brought Hector there in Book 14, when his companions carried him from the fight, struck down by Ajax's stone, and poured water on him to revive him (14.429–436); in Book 21 they bring the Trojans fleeing from Achilles to the river (24.692–693 = 14.433–434 = 21.1–2; [[fair-flowing]]). Now Hector's body crosses it. At the ford Hermes' escort ends: he goes back to Olympus, and Dawn spreads over the earth (24.694–695). See [[scamander]] and [[hermes]].
