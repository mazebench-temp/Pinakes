# φάρεα λευκά (24.231), only here in Homer.
grc: ["φάρεα λευκά"]
---

The white mantles are among the cloths of the ransom, two of which are left to wrap the body (24.229–231, 24.580; [[cloak]]). ὀστέα λευκά, the white bones, is the phrase of the bones of Patroclus (23.252): at both funerals the bones are gathered from the quenched pyre and put in a golden vessel (24.791–795; [[the-funeral-of-hector]]). See [[the-funeral-of-patroclus]].
