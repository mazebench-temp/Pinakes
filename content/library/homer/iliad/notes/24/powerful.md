# γένετο κρειόντων (24.539): the phrase, so that ὕπατε κρειόντων (8.31, most-high) is not taken in.
grc: ["γένετο κρειόντων"]
en: [princely]
---

παίδων … κρειόντων, “princely sons,” are the sons who would rule after their father. Peleus had only one, doomed to die young, far from home (24.538–542), while Priam had many, as Achilles says next (24.543–548; [[sons-of-priam]], [[peleus]]). See [[the-two-jars-of-zeus]].
