---
summary: “and wise-hearted Idaeus drove them” (24.325): the herald drives the mule wagon as Priam leaves the city.
---

The herald is given an epithet of heroes. Zeus had told Priam to take with him a herald, an older man, to guide the mules and bring back the body (24.149–151), and Idaeus does both (24.324–325, 24.696–702; [[idaeus]], [[heralds]]).
