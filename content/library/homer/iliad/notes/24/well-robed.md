---
summary: Helen recalls the in-laws who rebuked her, “one of my brothers-in-law or my husband's sisters, or his brothers' well-robed wives” (24.769).
---

γαλόων ἢ εἰνατέρων εὐπέπλων is the phrase of Hector's question in Book 6, whether Andromache had gone to his sisters or his brothers' well-robed wives (6.378, 6.383). In Helen's lament the same women are among those from whose words Hector protected her (24.767–772; [[in-laws]], [[helen]]).
