---
summary: “So he spoke, and storm-footed Iris rose to carry the message” (24.77, 24.159): twice in Book 24 Iris sets out with Zeus' orders, first to Thetis and then to Priam.
---

The book has two of the epithet's three uses in Homer, both in the same verse. The first time Iris leaps into the sea between Samos and Imbros to find Thetis (24.77–86), the second she goes to Priam's house and finds the cries of lament (24.159–160; [[messenger-scene]]). See [[iris]].
