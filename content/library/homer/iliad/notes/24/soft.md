# καλύψαντες μαλακοῖσιν (24.796): the phrase, so that the soft words of 1.582 are not taken in.
grc: ["καλύψαντες μαλακοῖσιν"]
---

The entry tells the pattern of the sleepers and the one who wakes, here for the third time (24.677–681; [[chariot-fighters]], [[when-other-mortals-are-asleep]]). The second use is the last touch of care for the body: the bones gathered from the pyre are put into a golden chest and covered with soft purple robes before they go into the trench (24.795–797; [[purple]], [[the-funeral-of-hector]]).
