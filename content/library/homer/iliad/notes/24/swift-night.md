---
summary: Hermes and Achilles warn Priam in the same verse, “if one of them should see you through the swift black night” (24.366, 24.653).
---

The verse-end θοὴν διὰ νύκτα μέλαιναν, of journeys through enemy ground at night, is used twice in the book, and both times the whole verse is the same (see [[black-night]]). Priam's journey to the hut and back is made through that night (24.351–694). See [[hermes]] and [[achilles]].
