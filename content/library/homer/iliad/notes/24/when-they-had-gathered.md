# ἤγερθεν ὁμηγερέες τʼ ἐγένοντο (24.790), the variant of 1.57: only here in the Iliad.
grc: ["ἤγερθεν ὁμηγερέες τʼ ἐγένοντο"]
---

The verse that gathered the first assembly of the poem (1.57) gathers the last company of the Iliad, the Trojans at Hector's pyre (24.788–791), and what follows is not a speech but the gathering of the bones (24.791–796; [[the-funeral-of-hector]]). See [[assembly-type-scene]].
