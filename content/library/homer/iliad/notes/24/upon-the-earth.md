---
summary: Priam tells Hecuba that if any other “of the men on earth” had bidden him go, he would call it a lie (24.220–222); and he tells Achilles that he has endured “what no other mortal on earth has yet endured” (24.505).
---

The first use sets the men on earth, seers and priests, against the goddess Priam heard himself (24.220–224); the second makes Priam the one man on earth who has kissed the hand of his sons' killer (24.505–506; [[supplication]]). See [[priam]] and [[gods-and-mortals]].
