# πέπνυσαί (24.377), the perfect itself: only here in the Iliad.
grc: [πέπνυσαί]
---

Priam gives the god in disguise the praise of a sensible speaker, in the verb from which the epithet is formed: πέπνυσαί τε νόῳ, “you are wise in mind” (24.376–377). The stranger's wisdom is real; the parents he will name are invented (24.397; [[truth-and-deception]], [[polyctor]]). See [[hermes]].
