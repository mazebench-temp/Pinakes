---
summary: Priam prays, “Father Zeus, who rule from Ida, most glorious, greatest” (24.308).
---

The verse of the prayers at Troy is Priam's own prayer before he goes to Achilles. Hecuba had told him to pray to the god “of Ida, who looks down on all of Troy” (24.290–291), and Zeus answers him with the eagle (24.314–321; [[father-zeus]], [[most-glorious]], [[ida]]).
