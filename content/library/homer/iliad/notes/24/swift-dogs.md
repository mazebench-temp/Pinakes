# ἀργίποδας κύνας (24.211), the compound of ἀργός and πούς: only here in Homer.
grc: ["ἀργίποδας κύνας"]
---

ἀργίπους, “swift-footed” or “white-footed,” is made from the same ἀργός as the formula κύνες ἀργοί, and the translation renders it “swift,” as in the formula (see the translation's table of key words). Hecuba's fear is the fear of the whole book: Hermes tells Priam that the dogs have not eaten his son (24.411), and the gods have kept the body whole (24.18–21; [[dogs]], [[the-dragging-of-hector]]). See [[hecuba]].
