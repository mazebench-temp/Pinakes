---
summary: The bulls of Book 7 are hides. Tychius made Ajax's shield of seven oxhides “of well-fed bulls”, with an eighth layer of bronze (7.222–223).
---

ὅς οἱ ἐποίησεν σάκος αἰόλον ἑπταβόειον / ταύρων ζατρεφέων: Tychius “made him the flashing shield of seven oxhides of well-fed bulls” (7.222–223; see [[tychius]] and [[the-shield-of-ajax]]). ζατρεφής, “well-fed”, is ζα-, “very”, and τρέφω, “rear, feed”: the Odyssey uses it of seals, fat hogs and goats (*Odyssey* 4.451, 14.19, 14.106). A well-fed bull gives a thick hide, and the thickness is tested at once: Hector's spear cuts through six layers and stops in the seventh (7.247–248). The bull, the grandest of victims, is here the stuff of the shield that saves Ajax's life. See [[cattle]].
