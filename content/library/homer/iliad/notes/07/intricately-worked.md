---
summary: Ajax's spear forces its way through Hector's “intricately worked corselet,” in the verse used of Paris' borrowed corselet in Book 3.
---

“And it forced its way through the intricately worked corselet” (7.252). The verse is repeated from Book 3, where the corselet was the one that Paris had borrowed from his brother Lycaon (3.332–333, 3.358). Here it is Hector's, and like Paris' it holds only so far: the spear goes on to cut the tunic beside the flank, and the man escapes by swerving (7.253–254; [[black-doom]]).
