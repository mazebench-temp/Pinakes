The Arcadians of the Catalogue sail to Troy in ships lent by Agamemnon, since they cared nothing for the sea (2.603–614). In Nestor's story they are the enemies of his youth, fighting the Pylians by the Celadon at the walls of Pheia (7.133–135; see [[pylians]], [[celadon]] and [[pheia]]). They are ἐγχεσίμωροι, “fighters with the spear” (7.134; see [[fighters-with-the-spear]]), but their champion wears the armor of a man famous for not fighting with the spear, Areithous the mace-man, which came to him from his master Lycurgus (7.137–149; see [[ereuthalion]], [[areithous]] and [[lycurgus-of-arcadia]]). Homer does not say where the war was fought, and ancient readers who wanted the battle nearer to Arcadia moved it to the Acidon near Lepreum ([[strabo|Strabo]] 8.3.21; see [[iardanus]]). In later genealogies Lycurgus was a king of Arcadia and the grandfather of Agapenor, the Arcadian leader at Troy (Pausanias 8.4.10, 8.5.2; see [[agapenor]]).
