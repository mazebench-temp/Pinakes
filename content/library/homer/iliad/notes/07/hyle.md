---
summary: Hyle is the home of Tychius, “by far the best of leather-workers”, who made Ajax's shield of seven oxhides (7.220–221).
---

σκυτοτόμων ὄχʼ ἄριστος Ὕλῃ ἔνι οἰκία ναίων, “by far the best of leather-workers, who lived in Hyle” (7.221). The Boeotian town of the Catalogue (2.500), where the rich Oresbius lived (5.708–709), is in Book 7 the home of a craftsman, and the shield of Ajax of Salamis was made in Boeotia (see [[tychius]] and [[the-shield-of-ajax]]). The same book has the common noun twice, when both armies send men “to go for wood” (μεθʼ ὕλην, 7.418, 7.420); only the capital tells the town from the forest.

Here too some ancient texts read Ὕδῃ, “in Hyde”, the Lydian town of 20.385. Strabo rejects the reading with a joke, “for Ajax did not send to Lydia for his shield” (9.2.20), and reports elsewhere that some did make Tychius a Lydian of Hyde (13.4.6; see [[hyle-or-hyde]] and [[strabo]]).
