{{quote:f3ca9825-24c9-53a3-9191-11743a501c8f}}

Phylomedusa is named only here, as the mother of the first man to die in Book 7 ([[phylomedusa]], [[menesthius]]). The epithet of Hera's beauty is given to her as it was to Helen's attendant Clymene (3.144) and will be to the Nereid Halie (18.40). The killing is told in the style of the battle lists, with the victim's home and both his parents, and the mother's epithet gives her a line of honor in her son's obituary ([[androktasia]], [[the-war-in-small]], [[areithous]]). See [[paris]].
