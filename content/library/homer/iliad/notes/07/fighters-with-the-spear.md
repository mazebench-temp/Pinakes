The epithet stands beside the Arcadians, Nestor's enemies in his youth, and may take in the Pylians too ([[arcadians]], [[pylians]]). In the Catalogue the Arcadians are the inland people who cared nothing for the sea and came to Troy in Agamemnon's ships (2.603–614). Here, in the past, they are spearmen, and their champion wears the armor of Areithous, who was famous for not fighting with the spear at all (7.140–141; [[areithous]], [[long-spears]]). The meaning of the second part of the compound is uncertain (see the translation's note on 7.134). See [[nestor-and-ereuthalion]], [[celadon]], [[pheia]] and [[iardanus]].
