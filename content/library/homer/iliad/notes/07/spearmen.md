---
summary: The herald Idaeus parts Hector and Ajax with praise for both: “both of you are spearmen; this indeed we all know.”
---

{{quote:a6051940-4457-517d-b6c3-a037f449a54e}}

Here αἰχμητά is a dual, “both of you spearmen,” and it makes the two men a pair; the same form is the old nominative when Pandarus speaks of “the old spearman Lycaon,” his father (5.197). The herald speaks to both champions as one, “dear sons,” and gives each the warrior's name in the same breath (7.279–281). The praise lets both stop without shame: neither has lost, and everyone knows what each can do. Hector repeats the point when he agrees, calling Ajax “the mightiest of the Achaeans” with the spear (7.288–289). See [[heralds]], [[idaeus]] and [[the-duel-of-hector-and-ajax]].
