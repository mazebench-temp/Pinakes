---
summary: Thucydides takes the Achaean wall as evidence that the Greeks won a battle when they landed, and so places its building at the start of the war, not in its tenth year. His account of the Athenian funeral for the war dead describes the custom that Nestor's words in Book 7 recall, the bones of the fallen brought home.
