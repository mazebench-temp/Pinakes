---
summary: Apollo, watching the battle from Pergamus, sees Athena come down from Olympus and goes out to meet her at the oak.
---

When Athena comes darting down to help the Argives, “Apollo rose up to meet her, / looking down from Pergamus, for he wanted victory for the Trojans” (7.20–21; see [[apollo]] and [[athena]]). Περγάμου ἐκκατιδών, “looking down from Pergamus”, is the half-line with which he called to the Trojans in Book 4 (4.508). The citadel is still his watchtower, as it was when he sat “on the height of Pergamus” after rescuing Aeneas (5.460). The two gods meet halfway, at the oak before the city (7.22; see [[the-oak-of-zeus]]).

His temple stands on the same height (5.445–448). Hector's challenge promises it the armor of the man he kills: he will carry it “to sacred Ilios / and hang it on the temple of Apollo the Far-Shooter” (7.82–83; see [[temple]] and [[hector]]).
