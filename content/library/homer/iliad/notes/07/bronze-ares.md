---
summary: In Nestor's story the armor of Areithous was a gift of “bronze Ares,” which passed by conquest from the mace-man to Lycurgus and from Lycurgus to Ereuthalion.
---

{{quote:5fa35ae5-ddef-5f14-974e-e4c35ef9d967}}

The god of war gave the armor, and war passed it on. Lycurgus killed its first owner by a trick in a narrow road, where the iron mace was no use, stripped him, and wore the armor himself “into the toil of Ares”; when he grew old he gave it to Ereuthalion, who wore it to challenge the Pylians and was killed by the young Nestor (7.143–156; [[areithous]], [[lycurgus-of-arcadia]], [[ereuthalion]]). The formula of the metal suits a story told through its armor, and the history of the armor is a history of its owners' deaths. Nestor does not say what became of it after Ereuthalion fell. See [[nestor-and-ereuthalion]] and [[stripping-the-dead]].
