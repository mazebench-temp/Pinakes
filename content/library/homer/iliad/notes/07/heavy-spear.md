---
summary: Ajax's “heavy spear” goes through Hector's shining shield and his corselet, in the verses of Menelaus' throw in Book 3.
---

“And through the shining shield went the heavy spear, and it forced its way through the intricately worked corselet” (7.251–252). The two verses are those of Menelaus' throw at Paris (3.357–358), and they return once more for the spear of Socus that pierces Odysseus' shield (11.435–436). In both duels the spear that goes through belongs to the Achaean and misses the flesh: Paris and Hector both swerve and escape (3.360, 7.254; [[black-doom]]). Ajax's spear grazes Hector's neck a few lines later, when he stabs at close quarters (7.260–262; [[dark-blood]]). See [[balanced-on-every-side]], [[phaeinos]] and [[intricately-worked]].
