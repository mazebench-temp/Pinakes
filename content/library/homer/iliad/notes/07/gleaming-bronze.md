---
summary: “Ajax armed himself in gleaming bronze”: the arming of the champion before the duel is told in a single verse.
grc: [νώροπι χαλκῷ]
---

“So they spoke, and Ajax armed himself in gleaming bronze” (7.206). While the army prays, Ajax arms, and the narrator gives the arming one line; the next line says only that he put all his armor about his body (7.207). The same half-verse arms Patroclus in Book 16 (16.130), where the arming is then told piece by piece. Here the full account is saved for the shield, which is described when Ajax comes near Hector (7.219–223; [[seven-oxhide]], [[arming-scene]]). The epithet of the old armor formula comes back from Book 2, where Agamemnon put on the gleaming bronze to stand out among the heroes (2.578). See [[ajax]].
