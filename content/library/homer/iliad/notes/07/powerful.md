“And then powerful Agamemnon spoke to Idaeus” (7.405). The formula κρείων Ἀγαμέμνων introduces the king's decision after the army has shouted its approval of Diomedes' refusal (7.403–404; [[good-at-the-war-cry]]). He makes the army's answer his own, “my pleasure is the same,” and grants the burning of the dead in his own name (7.406–411; [[loud-thundering]], [[the-truce-for-the-dead]]). The two verses with the fuller title εὐρὺ κρείων are listed under [[wide-ruling]] (7.107, 7.322). See [[agamemnon]].
