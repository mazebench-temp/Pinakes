passages:
  - 7.476-482 | The night of thunder
---

“And they poured the wine from their cups on the ground, and no one dared drink before he had poured a libation to the son of Cronus, supreme in might” (οἶνον δʼ ἐκ δεπάων χαμάδις χέον, 7.480–481). The δέπας is the cup men drink from and the cup they pour from for the gods. At Chryse the young men served the wine in cups, first pouring drops for the libation (1.471), and Hecuba offered Hector wine to pour to Zeus before he drank (6.258–260; see [[libation]] and [[wine]]). Here the cups are filled from the wine the Achaeans have just bought from Lemnos, and the first of it goes on the ground because Zeus is thundering (7.467–479; see [[teras]]).
