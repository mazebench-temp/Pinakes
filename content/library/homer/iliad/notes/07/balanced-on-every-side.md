{{quote:ec27f4f8-59f2-55b7-abfc-bb9d82669040}}

The five verses after the throw are the verses of Book 3, where Menelaus' spear went through Paris' shield (3.356–360). The round shield is Hector's ἀσπίς, set against the great body shield of Ajax, which the narrator calls a σάκος ([[seven-oxhide]], [[shield]]). Ajax's throw is the one that goes through; Hector's own spear stopped in the seventh hide (7.245–248). See [[heavy-spear]], [[phaeinos]], [[intricately-worked]] and [[black-doom]].
