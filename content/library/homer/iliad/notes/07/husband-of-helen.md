{{quote:8cee439f-605a-56ae-83e8-9b89a391ffe2}}

Antenor has proposed to give back Argive Helen and her possessions (7.350–351; [[argive-helen]]). The formula that names Paris by his wife stands in the verse that brings him to his feet to answer, and his answer keeps her: “I will not give back the woman,” though he will give the possessions and add more of his own (7.362–364). In Book 3 the same verse armed him to fight for her against Menelaus (3.329). See [[paris]], [[lovely-haired]] and [[helen]].
