---
summary: The line-end “the son of Cronus, supreme in might” closes the sacrifice for Ajax and the libation poured in fear of Zeus' thunder at the end of the book.
---

When the duel is over, Agamemnon sacrifices an ox “to the son of Cronus, supreme in might” (7.315), in verses taken almost whole from the sacrifice before the first battle (2.402–403; [[five-years-old]]). There Zeus accepted the offering and did not grant the prayer (2.419–420). At the end of the book the same half-verse closes the night's libation:

{{quote:f233a103-34a1-5742-8956-6efeb72d8ce7}}

The god of surpassing strength shows it in his thunder, and the feasters pour out the wine to him before anyone dares to drink ([[libation]], [[pale-fear]]). See [[son-of-cronus]] and [[zeus]].
