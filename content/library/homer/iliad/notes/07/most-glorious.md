---
summary: The army's prayer before the duel of Hector and Ajax calls Zeus “most glorious, greatest,” in the verse that opened the oaths and prayers of Book 3.
---

{{quote:0ce2ea4e-b83e-5c42-a26b-d3d31c0c40ed}}

The address Ζεῦ πάτερ Ἴδηθεν μεδέων κύδιστε μέγιστε opens Agamemnon's oath in Book 3 (3.276) and the people's prayer as the lots were shaken for the duel of Paris and Menelaus (3.320); it returns here and in Priam's prayer before he sets out for Achilles (24.308). The superlative of κῦδος addresses the god who gives glory, and the prayer asks for it: “grant victory to Ajax, and that he win a splendid triumph,” or, if Zeus loves Hector too, “equal strength and glory to them both” (7.203–205; [[kydos]], [[splendid]]).
