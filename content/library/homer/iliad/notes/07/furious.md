{{quote:5153eedb-7af4-524d-ad81-372a289f58f6}}

θοῦρις ἀλκή, the “furious courage” that men are told to remember in the exhortations (6.112), is here something the two Ajaxes wear. The line-end θοῦριν ἐπιειμένοι ἀλκήν is theirs alone in the Iliad (7.164, 8.262, 18.157; [[clothed-in-courage]]). Among the nine volunteers the pair are named together and described by one formula; the lot will choose the greater of them ([[ajax]], [[ajax-son-of-oileus]]). See [[alke]].
