summary: Glaucus, “leader of the Lycian men”, kills the Achaean Iphinous as Hector and Paris return to the battle (7.13–16).
en: [Lycian men]
---

The Lycians are named once in Book 7, in the title of Glaucus as he makes the third of the Trojans' three kills at the start of the book (7.13; see [[glaucus]] and [[iphinous]]). The sons of Priam make the other two, Paris and Hector (7.8–12; see [[menesthius]] and [[eioneus]]), and the Lycian leader completes the list. Sarpedon, their king, is not named in the book. The Lycians are among the allies whom Antenor and Priam address in the Trojan assembly, “Trojans and Dardanians and allies” (7.348, 7.368; see [[trojan-allies]]).
