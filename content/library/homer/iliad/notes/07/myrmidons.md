---
summary: Nestor calls Peleus “the brave counselor and speaker of the Myrmidons” (7.126).
---

The Myrmidons are named once in Book 7, as Peleus' people: at home in Phthia the old king is ἐσθλὸς Μυρμιδόνων βουληφόρος ἠδʼ ἀγορητής, “the brave counselor and speaker of the Myrmidons” (7.126; see [[peleus]] and [[man-of-counsel]]). Their leader at Troy is Achilles, who, as Ajax tells Hector, “lies among his curved, seafaring ships” (7.229; see [[achilles]]), and they have no part in the fighting of the book. They go into battle under Patroclus in Book 16.
