---
summary: Helen is “of the lovely hair” in the formula that names Paris as her husband, as he rises to refuse to give her back.
---

“Brilliant Alexander, the husband of Helen of the lovely hair” (7.355): the genitive ἠϋκόμοιο completes the line-end formula of Paris ([[husband-of-helen]]). The woman whom Antenor has just called Argive Helen, to be given back with her possessions (7.350; [[argive-helen]]), is named by her beauty in the verse of the man who will not give her up. See [[helen]] and [[paris]].
