summary: “The Gerenian horseman Nestor” orders the casting of lots and shakes the helmet from which the lot of Ajax leaps.
---

When the nine volunteers have stood up, “again the Gerenian horseman Nestor spoke among them” (7.170) and told them to cast lots. When the army has prayed, “the Gerenian horseman Nestor shook the lots” (7.181), and the lot of Ajax leaps out:

{{quote:320c95e4-0f83-5653-bb62-3484f546aeb7}}

The old man who shamed the army into volunteering also manages the choice among them ([[lots]], [[nestor]]). His two other speeches in the book, the rebuke that shames the army and the proposal of the truce and the wall, are introduced without it (7.123, 7.324–326).
