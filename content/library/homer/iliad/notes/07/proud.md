The line-end Τρώων ἀγερώχων, “of the proud Trojans,” closes Nestor's plan with the reason for it. It names the Trojans five times in the Iliad (3.36, 5.623, 7.343, 16.708, 21.584), and in Book 2 the same epithet described the Rhodians (2.654). So far the war has been fought on the plain; the old counselor foresees that the Trojans may drive the Achaeans back on their ships, and Book 8 bears him out ([[the-achaean-wall]], [[nestor]], [[trojans]]).
