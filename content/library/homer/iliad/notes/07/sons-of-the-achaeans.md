---
summary: “All the sons of the Achaeans shouted aloud” in approval of Diomedes' refusal of the Trojan offer.
---

{{quote:aea09b7d-48bb-5279-a0f3-eeb8cdc40ef7}}

The line-end υἷες Ἀχαιῶν names the army as a body at the moment it speaks with one voice. It first fell silent at the herald's message, as it fell silent at Hector's challenge (7.398, 7.92; [[silence]]), and Diomedes' short speech turns the silence into a shout. Agamemnon takes the shout as the army's answer: “you yourself hear the word of the Achaeans, how they answer you” (7.406–407; [[powerful]], [[good-at-the-war-cry]]). See [[achaeans]].
