summary: The Achaeans buy the Lemnian wine with bronze and “gleaming iron,” in the line-end αἴθωνι σιδήρῳ.
---

{{quote:81427023-1f12-5ac5-beff-c7992af3f2b0}}

αἴθωνι σιδήρῳ, which Book 4 used of the chariot-maker's axe (4.485), here names iron as a thing of value, listed with bronze, hides, cattle and slaves as the price of the wine (7.473–475; [[iron]], [[trade]], [[bronze]]). The epithet that makes horses and lions tawny makes the metal gleam. See [[wine]] and [[euneus]].
