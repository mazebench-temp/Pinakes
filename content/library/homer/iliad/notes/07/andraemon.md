---
summary: Andraemon is named in his son's patronymic, “Thoas, son of Andraemon”, among the nine leaders who volunteer to meet Hector (7.168).
grc: [Ἀνδραιμονίδης]
---

Ἀνδραιμονίδης, “son of Andraemon” (7.168), occurs only here in Homer. Elsewhere the poems name Thoas with his father's name in full: Ἀνδραίμονος υἱός (2.638, 15.281; Od. 14.499), and Ἀνδραίμονος υἷι (13.216). See [[thoas]].
