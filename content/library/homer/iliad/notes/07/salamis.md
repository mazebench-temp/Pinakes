---
summary: Ajax names Salamis as the island where he was born and raised, in the boast with which he arms to fight Hector (7.198–199).
grc: [Σαλαμῖνι]
---

οὐδέ τι ἰδρείῃ, ἐπεὶ οὐδʼ ἐμὲ νήϊδά γʼ οὕτως / ἔλπομαι ἐν Σαλαμῖνι γενέσθαι τε τραφέμεν τε, “nor by skill; for I do not think that I was born and bred in Salamis so unskilled as that” (7.198–199). The lot has fallen to Ajax, and he tells the Achaeans that no one will drive him back, by force or by skill (see [[ajax]]). ἰδρείη is knowledge, skill; νῆϊς, “unknowing, unskilled”, occurs elsewhere in Homer only when Odysseus tells a Phaeacian that he is not unskilled in contests (Od. 8.179). Ajax credits his island's upbringing with his skill, not with his strength.

Homer names Salamis twice. In the Catalogue it is the island from which Ajax brought his twelve ships (2.557); here it is where he was born and raised. These lines stand in the five verses (7.195–199) that were reported to have been athetized in antiquity, so the one Homeric line that makes Salamis Ajax's birthplace is one that some ancient critics rejected.
