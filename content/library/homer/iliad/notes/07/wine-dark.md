---
summary: The sailor who passes the tomb in Hector's challenge is “sailing in his many-benched ship over the wine-dark sea.”
---

{{quote:49b62789-a319-5614-b469-0ea22b08bbda}}

The traveler's phrase ἐπὶ οἴνοπα πόντον puts the imagined speaker at sea, in the future, looking at the shore ([[many-benched]], [[broad-hellespont]]). It is the only wine-dark sea of Book 7. The formula belongs to voyages, and the Odyssey is full of them; here it carries a stranger past the Hellespont, where the Achaean camp stands, long after the war. See [[sea]] and [[born-hereafter]].
