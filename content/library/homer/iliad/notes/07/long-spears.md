---
summary: In Nestor's story Areithous did not fight “with bows or the long spear,” and in the duel Hector and Ajax pull their long spears free and fall on each other.
---

Nestor explains the nickname of Areithous: men and women called him the mace-man “because he did not fight with bows or the long spear, but broke the battalions with an iron mace” (7.140–141; [[areithous]], [[mace]]). The long spear is the ordinary weapon, and the man who fought without it is remembered for that.

In the duel, after the first throws, “the two, drawing out the long spears with their hands at the same moment, fell upon each other like lions that eat raw flesh or wild boars” (7.255–257; [[eating-raw-flesh]], [[lions-and-boars]]). The spears that were thrown are now used to stab at close quarters: Hector's point bends on Ajax's shield, and Ajax's goes through Hector's and grazes his neck (7.258–262; [[dark-blood]]).
