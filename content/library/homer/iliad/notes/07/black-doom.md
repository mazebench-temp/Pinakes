---
summary: Hector swerves aside from Ajax's spear “and avoided black doom,” in the verse that ended Menelaus' throw at Paris in Book 3.
---

“The spear; but he swerved aside and avoided black doom” (7.254). The whole verse is repeated from the duel of Book 3 (3.360), and it gives Hector the escape that Paris had. There Paris escaped the spear, and Aphrodite snatched him away soon after (3.380–382). Hector escapes by his own quickness, and when Ajax's stone knocks him down a few lines later Apollo sets him on his feet (7.272). The formula of the fate a man avoids marks the moment at which the duel could have ended. See [[ker-doom]], [[balanced-on-every-side]] and [[the-duel-of-hector-and-ajax]].
