---
summary: Euaemon is named as the father of Eurypylus, “the splendid son of Euaemon”, among the nine leaders who volunteer to meet Hector (7.167).
---

Εὐαίμονος ἀγλαὸς υἱός, “the splendid son of Euaemon” (7.167), is the half-verse that names his son wherever he acts: in the Catalogue, in the first battle, in Book 8 and in Book 11 (2.736, 5.79, 8.265, 11.575). Homer knows Euaemon only as a father (see [[eurypylus]]).
