---
summary: Hector's “unyielding bronze” cuts through six layers of Ajax's shield and stops in the seventh.
---

{{quote:9b3a3b72-26a2-54ee-9d8d-ed778bb25c2f}}

The formula of bronze that is never worn down is set against the one thing that stops it. The spear-point goes through the outer bronze and six hides of the shield Tychius made, “cutting its way” (δαΐζων), and the seventh hide holds (7.220–223; [[seven-oxhide]], [[tychius]]). The count is exact because the layers decide the throw, as they do again when Aeneas' spear passes two of the five layers of Achilles' shield and stops in the gold (20.267–272). See [[bronze]] and [[the-duel-of-hector-and-ajax]].
