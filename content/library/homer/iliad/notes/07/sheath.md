summary: Hector gives Ajax his silver-studded sword “with its sheath and its well-cut strap” (σὺν κολεῷ, 7.304).

passages:
  - 7.303-305 | Hector and Ajax exchange gifts
---

Hector gives the sword whole, “bringing it with its sheath and its well-cut strap” (σὺν κολεῷ τε φέρων καὶ ἐϋτμήτῳ τελαμῶνι, 7.304; see [[sword]] and [[guest-gifts]]). κολεῷ is the dative of κολεός; Book 1 has the word in both its spellings, κολεοῖο and κουλεόν (1.194, 1.220), and Book 3 has κουλεόν (3.272). A sword given with its sheath and strap is ready to wear. The same verse gives a sword with its sheath and strap to Diomedes at the funeral games of Patroclus (23.825), after his fight in armor with Ajax.
