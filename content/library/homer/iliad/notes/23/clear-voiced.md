---
summary: In Agamemnon's hut the kings “at once … ordered the clear-voiced heralds / to set a great tripod over the fire” for Achilles' bath (23.39–40).
---

αὐτίκα κηρύκεσσι λιγυφθόγγοισι κέλευσε is the verse of Agamemnon summoning the army in Book 2 (2.50, 2.442), here in the plural, κέλευσαν. The heralds' voices are not needed: Achilles refuses the bath (23.42–47; [[clotted-blood]], [[bathing]]). See [[heralds]].
