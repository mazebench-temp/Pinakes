# ἱπποδάμου (23.472, only here).
grc: [ἱπποδάμου]
---

The epithet is Tydeus', as in Agamemnon's reproach of his son (4.370), and comes in a race of horses: Diomedes drives the horses of Tros that he took from Aeneas (23.290–292). ἱπποδάμου is new to the entry. See [[tydeus]], [[diomedes]] and [[son-of-tydeus]].
