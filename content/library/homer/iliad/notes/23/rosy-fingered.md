---
summary: After the night of the ghost, “rosy-fingered Dawn appeared to them as they wept / around the pitiable corpse” (23.109–110).
---

The verse-end φάνη ῥοδοδάκτυλος Ἠώς is that of the whole dawn line (1.477), with the mourners in place of ἦμος δʼ ἠριγένεια: the day of the pyre begins in lament (see [[dawn-line]] and [[lament]]). In the Odyssey the same turn is given to a dawn that Athena holds back from Odysseus and Penelope as they weep (*Odyssey* 23.241). See [[eos]].
