# ἠεροειδέα (23.744), of the sea, as often in the Odyssey (Odyssey 2.263, 3.105, 4.482, 5.164).
grc: [ἠεροειδέα]
---

ζόφον ἠερόεντα, “the misty darkness,” is Hades' lot in Poseidon's account of the three brothers (15.191), and in the Odyssey the darkness to which Elpenor and Odysseus come (*Odyssey* 11.57, 11.155). ἐπʼ ἠεροειδέα πόντον is the Odyssey's phrase for the sea seen from afar, used of the sea in the Iliad only here (23.744); ἠεροειδέα is new to the entry.
