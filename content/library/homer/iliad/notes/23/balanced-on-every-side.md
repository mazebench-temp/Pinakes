summary: In the armed duel “Ajax stabbed at his shield, balanced on every side, / but did not reach the flesh; for the corselet within protected him” (23.818–819).
---

The line-end formula is said of Diomedes' shield: Ajax strikes it, while Diomedes keeps reaching over Ajax's “great shield” to his neck (23.818–821; [[phaeinos]]). See [[shield]], [[ajax]] and [[diomedes]].
