---
summary: Ajax addresses Odysseus in the wrestling with the formal verse, “Son of Laertes, sprung from Zeus, Odysseus of many devices, / either lift me, or I you” (23.723–724).
---

The verse is spoken seven times in the Iliad, and Ajax has used it before: in the embassy, to tell Odysseus that it was time to go (9.624). Here it proposes a way out of the deadlock, “and all the rest will be the business of Zeus” (23.724), and Odysseus answers it with a trick, a kick at the hollow of the knee (23.725–728; [[kerdos]], [[of-many-devices]], [[sprung-from-zeus]]).
