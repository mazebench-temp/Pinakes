# χυτὴν ἐπὶ γαῖαν (23.256; again Odyssey 3.258).
grc: ["χυτὴν ἐπὶ γαῖαν"]
en: [loose earth]
---

The mound is raised over the place of the pyre, while the bones stay in the hut (23.252–257; see the translation's notes on 23.254 and 23.255, and [[tomb]]). The phrase χυτὴν ἐπὶ γαῖαν ἔχευαν, rendered here “heaped up the loose earth,” is new to the entry. See [[cremation]] and [[patroclus]].
