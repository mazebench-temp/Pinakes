summary: Hesiod won a tripod with handles for a song at the funeral games of Amphidamas at Chalcis, where the dead man's sons set out the prizes, as the sons of Amarynceus do in Nestor's memory; and the Shield gives Heracles the horse Arion.

- **The games of Amphidamas.** “There I crossed over to Chalcis for the games of wise Amphidamas; and the sons of the great-hearted man set out many prizes announced beforehand; and there, I say, I won with a song and carried off a tripod with handles” (*Works and Days* 654–657). Nestor remembers the games of Amarynceus at Buprasium, “and his sons set out prizes in honor of the king” (23.631), and Achilles' first prize for the chariot race is “a tripod with handles” (23.264): [[title-of-book-23]] and [[the-funeral-games-of-amarynceus]].
- Heracles' “great horse, dark-maned Arion” (*Shield* 120): [[arion-the-horse]].
