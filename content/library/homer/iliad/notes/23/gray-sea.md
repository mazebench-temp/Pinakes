---
summary: When the chariots are on the last stretch, “back toward the gray sea, then the prowess of each / showed itself” (23.374–375).
---

The race is run out from the shore and back toward it (see the translation's note on 23.373). θῖνʼ ἔφʼ ἁλὸς πολιῆς is a verse-start of the shore (1.350, 13.682, 14.31); here ἂψ ἐφʼ ἁλὸς πολιῆς turns it into the direction of the race. The gray iron of the prizes (23.261) is excepted. See [[arete]], [[turning-post]] and [[chariot]].
