---
summary: The winds that Iris summons come “to deep-soiled Troy” and fall on the pyre (23.215–216).
---

Τροίην ἐρίβωλον ἱκέσθην echoes the arrival of Thetis and the Nereids at Troy in Book 18, Τροίην ἐρίβωλον ἵκοντο (18.67): both times divine powers come over the sea to the Achaean camp because of Patroclus' death (see [[zephyrus]] and [[boreas]]). See [[troy]].
