---
summary: Idomeneus, watching the chariot race, makes out the horse in the lead, “bay in all the rest, but on its forehead / there was a white mark, round like the moon” (23.454–455).
grc: [μήνη]
---

λευκὸν σῆμα … περίτροχον ἠΰτε μήνη, “a white mark, round like the moon” (23.455): μήνη is the word for the moon of the later books, as in the blaze of Achilles' shield, “as of the moon” (ἠΰτε μήνης, 19.374). There it is the moon's light, here its roundness (see [[like-the-moon]]). The horse is φοῖνιξ, “bay”, the color, not the name (23.454; see [[horses]]).
