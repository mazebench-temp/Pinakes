# ἁδινὰ στεναχίζων (23.225; again Odyssey 24.317).
grc: ["ἁδινὰ στεναχίζων"]
---

τοῖσι δὲ Πηλεΐδης ἁδινοῦ ἐξῆρχε γόοιο is the verse with which Achilles began the lament in Book 18 (18.316; [[man-slaying]]); Hecuba leads the Trojan women with the same words (22.430). ἁδινὰ στεναχίζων ends the simile of the father who burns the bones of his son (23.222–225; [[the-father-burning-his-sons-bones]], [[lament]]), and is new to the entry. See [[achilles]].
