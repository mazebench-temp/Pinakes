---
summary: The companions lay the bones of Patroclus in the huts “and covered them with a soft linen cloth” (23.254).
---

The verse is the one with which the washed body was laid on the bier and covered in Book 18, ἐν λεχέεσσι δὲ θέντες ἑανῷ λιτὶ κάλυψαν (18.352), with the huts in place of the bier; now the cloth covers the bones in their golden bowl, which will stay in the hut until Achilles' own death (23.243–254; see the translation's note on 23.254 and [[cremation]]). See [[patroclus]].
