---
summary: The woodcutters reach “the slopes of Ida of the many springs” (23.117).
---

The mountain where the gods arrive and Zeus sits watching (8.47, 14.283, 15.151) is here a forest worked by men, who climb it “uphill and downhill, sideways and aslant” with their mules (23.116–122; [[high-leafed]]). See [[ida]].
