# κύκλου ποιητοῖο (23.340) and τρίποδος πέρι ποιητοῖο (23.718): each only here.
grc: ["κύκλου ποιητοῖο", "τρίποδος πέρι ποιητοῖο"]
en: [well-made]
---

ποιητός, “made,” is the epithet's word for what is built with skill, from houses to the shield of Achilles, “strongly made” (18.608; see the entry); here it is said of a wheel and of a prize tripod. The two phrases and the English “well-made” are new to the entry. See [[chariot]], [[tripod]] and [[turning-post]].
