---
summary: Achilles opens the chariot race and the boxing with the same address, “Son of Atreus, and you other well-greaved Achaeans” (23.272, 23.658); and the wrestlers' deadlock begins “to vex the well-greaved Achaeans” (23.721).
---

The address of the games is built on the verse of Nestor's proposal to burn the dead in Book 7, with ἐϋκνήμιδες Ἀχαιοί in place of ἀριστῆες Παναχαιῶν (7.327; 23.236; [[son-of-atreus]], [[contest-scene]]). In the wrestling the crowd tires of a match in which neither can throw the other, and Ajax proposes that each try to lift the other (23.719–724). See [[achaeans]].
