---
summary: Achilles asks for a modest tomb for Patroclus, to be made wide and high later by “you who after me / are left behind among the many-benched ships” (23.247–248).
---

The ships are named as the place of the survivors, those who will outlive Achilles: the mound is to be finished after his own death, for both of them (23.243–248; [[tomb]], [[fame-and-the-tomb]]). See [[achilles]] and [[ships]].
