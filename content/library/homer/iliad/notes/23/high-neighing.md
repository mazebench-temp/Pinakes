# ὑψηχέας (23.27, only here).
grc: [ὑψηχέας]
---

This is the epithet's second and last use in Homer, after the horses of Hera in Book 5 (5.772). The horses have been kept under the yoke for the mourning, as Achilles ordered: “let us not yet loose the single-hoofed horses from under the chariots” (23.7–11; [[single-hoofed]]). ὑψηχέας is new to the entry. See [[horses]] and [[myrmidons]].
