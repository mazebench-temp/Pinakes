---
summary: For the archery Achilles sets up “the mast of a dark-prowed ship” and ties the dove to it (23.852), and the stricken dove settles “on the mast of the dark-prowed ship” before it falls (23.878).
---

These are two of the epithet's three uses in the Iliad, the other in the simile of Hector's charge at the ships (15.693); the Odyssey has it often. The translation keeps the rendering “dark-prowed” (see the translation's table of key words). See [[mast]], [[doves]] and [[ships]].
