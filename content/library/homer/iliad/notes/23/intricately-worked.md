# πολυδαίδαλοι (23.743, only here), of craftsmen.
grc: [πολυδαίδαλοι]
en: [skilled in intricate work]
---

Here alone the epithet of richly crafted things is given to the craftsmen: the Sidonians made the bowl, and Phoenician traders brought it over the sea (23.741–745). Walter Leaf notes that Homer always keeps the Sidonians as craftsmen apart from the Phoenicians as traders (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 23.743; see the translation's note on 23.743–745). πολυδαίδαλοι is new to the entry.
