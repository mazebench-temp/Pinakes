# κρείοντʼ (23.630, only here). At 23.887 κρείων belongs to εὐρὺ κρείων, "wide-ruling".
grc: ["κρείοντʼ"]
except: [23.887]
---

κρείων Εὔμηλος is found only here (23.354): the title that goes with Agamemnon's name is given to the leader of the men of Pherae (2.711–715), as is “lord of men” (23.288; [[lord-of-men]]). Amarynceus is the king of the Epeians at whose funeral games Nestor won every contest but the chariot race (23.629–642; [[the-funeral-games-of-amarynceus]]). At the javelin Agamemnon has the fuller title, εὐρὺ κρείων (23.887; [[wide-ruling]]), and the line is excepted here. See [[agamemnon]] and [[eumelus]].
