# οἴνοπι (23.316; the dative, as in the Odyssey's ἐνὶ οἴνοπι πόντῳ).
grc: [οἴνοπι]
---

Achilles' look over the sea recalls his first scene of grief, when he sat “on the shore of the gray sea, looking out over the boundless deep” (1.350; [[gray-sea]]); here he prays to the river of his homeland, which he will never see again (23.144–151). The helmsman is one of Nestor's three examples of cunning, between the woodcutter and the charioteer (23.315–318; [[metis]]). οἴνοπι is new to the entry. See [[achilles]] and [[sea]].
