# δίπλακι (23.243, only here).
grc: [δίπλακι]
---

δίπλαξ is a cloth folded double elsewhere (3.126, 22.441); here it is a double layer of fat, which Walter Leaf takes to keep out air and moisture (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 23.243; see the translation's note on 23.243). The bones are kept in the hut until Achilles' own death (23.244, 23.254; [[phiale]], [[cremation]]). δίπλακι is new to the entry. See [[patroclus]].
