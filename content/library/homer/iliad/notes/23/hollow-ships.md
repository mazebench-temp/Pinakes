---
summary: Three prizes are carried “to the hollow ships”: the lump of iron, by the companions of Polypoetes (23.849), the single axes, by Teucer (23.883), and the cauldron of the javelin, which Achilles bids Agamemnon take there (23.892).
---

γλαφυρός, the synonym of κοῖλος, ends the weight throw (23.849), and κοίλας ἐπὶ νῆας the archery and the javelin (23.883, 23.892). At 23.419 κοίλη is the hollow road where Antilochus passes Menelaus, and is excepted. See [[ships]] and [[contest-scene]].
