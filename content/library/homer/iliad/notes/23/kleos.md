---
summary: Achilles will not race, for his horses “have lost the noble fame of such a charioteer” (23.280), Patroclus, who used to wash their manes and pour oil on them.
---

κλέος ἐσθλόν, “noble fame”, is the fame of the charioteer that the horses have lost with him (23.280; see [[the-horses-of-achilles]]). The horses stand and mourn him with their manes on the ground (23.283–284). In the archery Teucer does not promise Apollo, and Meriones does, “that he would offer a glorious hecatomb of firstborn lambs” (κλειτὴν ἑκατόμβην, 23.863–864, 23.872–873; see [[hecatomb]]).
