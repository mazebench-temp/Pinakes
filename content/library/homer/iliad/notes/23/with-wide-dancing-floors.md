# εὐρυχόρῳ (23.299; also Odyssey 6.4, 11.256).
grc: [εὐρυχόρῳ]
---

Zeus had given him great wealth, and he preferred to stay at home and take his pleasure (23.296–299). The epithet of Hellas and of Mycalessus (2.498, 9.478) is given to Sicyon only here, and εὐρυχόρῳ is new to the entry. See [[echepolus-son-of-anchises]], [[sicyon]] and [[windy]].
