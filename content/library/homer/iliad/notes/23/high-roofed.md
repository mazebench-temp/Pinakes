# δώματος ὑψηλοῖο (23.713; it also takes in 16.213, the same verse).
grc: ["δώματος ὑψηλοῖο"]
---

The second verse of the simile is the one of the Myrmidons' ranks in Book 16, packed like the stones of the wall of a high house (16.212–213); here the high house gives the image of two men leaning together like the rafters of a gabled roof (see the translation's note on 23.712–713 and [[the-rafters-of-a-high-house]]). The phrase is new to the entry and takes in Book 16's verse as well. See [[famous]] and [[contest-scene]].
