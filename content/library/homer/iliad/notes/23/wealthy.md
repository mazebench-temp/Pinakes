# εὐηφενέων (23.81, only here), as εὐηφενέος of Socus (11.427).
grc: [εὐηφενέων]
---

Walter Leaf notes that the reading with γ, εὐηγενέων, “well-born,” is as old as Aeschines, who quotes the passage (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 23.81; see the translation's note on 23.77–91). The prophecy of Achilles' death is told in the address “Achilles, like the gods” (23.80; [[like-the-immortals]], [[death-of-achilles-foretold]]). εὐηφενέων is new to the entry. See [[trojans]].
