---
summary: Diomedes gives Euryalus “the well-cut straps of an ox of the fields” for the boxing (23.684); and the son of Oileus, his mouth full of dung, stands “holding in his hands the horn of the ox of the fields,” his prize (23.780).
---

βοὸς ἀγραύλοιο ends both verses, as it ended the verse of Diomedes sleeping on the hide of an ox of the fields (10.155). The boxing straps are the simple thongs wound around the knuckles (see the translation's note on 23.684 and [[well-cut]]). See [[cattle]].
