---
summary: Achilles leads the lament “laying his man-slaying hands on the breast of his companion” (23.18).
---

The couplet 23.17–18 is the one with which he began the lament in Book 18 (18.316–317; [[son-of-peleus]]). In the next book Priam will kiss the same hands, χεῖρας δεινὰς ἀνδροφόνους, which have killed many of his sons (24.478–479). See [[achilles]] and [[lament]].
