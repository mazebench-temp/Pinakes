---
summary: Achilles throws “four horses with high-arched necks” on the pyre of Patroclus, groaning loudly (23.171–172).
---

The horses die with the sheep, the cattle, two of the nine table dogs and the twelve Trojans (23.166–176; [[cremation]], [[human-sacrifice]]). Their bones are burned at the edge of the pyre, “horses and men all mingled together,” apart from those of Patroclus (23.241–242). See [[the-funeral-of-patroclus]], [[horses]] and [[pyres]].
