---
summary: Ajax addresses Odysseus in the deadlock of the wrestling, “Son of Laertes, sprung from Zeus, Odysseus of many devices” (23.723).
---

The formal verse is spoken seven times in the Iliad, twice by Ajax (9.624, 23.723; [[son-of-laertes]]). Its epithet of contrivance is answered at once by Odysseus' trick (23.725–728; [[of-many-wiles]]). See [[odysseus]] and [[ajax]].
