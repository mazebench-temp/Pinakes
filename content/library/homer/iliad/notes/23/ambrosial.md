---
summary: Aphrodite anoints Hector's body “with rose-sweet oil, / ambrosial,” so that his skin should not be torn as Achilles drags him (23.186–187).
---

Divine oil keeps the body whole, as the ambrosia that Thetis dropped into Patroclus' nostrils kept his flesh from corruption (19.38–39); here the goddess who protects the body is Aphrodite (23.184–187; [[daughter-of-zeus]], [[rose-sweet]]). See [[hector]] and [[aphrodite]].
