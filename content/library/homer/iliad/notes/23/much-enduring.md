---
summary: “Much-enduring brilliant Odysseus” tries in his turn to lift Ajax in the wrestling (23.729) and takes up the mixing bowl, the first prize of the footrace (23.778).
---

These are the last two of the formula's five uses in the Iliad (8.97, 9.676, 10.248). Both come in the games and in the same verse-shape, a verb of lifting and the formula: δεύτερος αὖτʼ ἀνάειρε (23.729) and κρητῆρʼ αὖτʼ ἀνάειρε (23.778). In the first he “moved him a little from the ground, but could not lift him” (23.730); in the second he lifts the prize, “since he came in first” (23.779).
