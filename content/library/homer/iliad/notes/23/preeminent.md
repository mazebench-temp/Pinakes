---
summary: From his lookout Idomeneus “made out a horse, conspicuous, in the lead,” bay with a white mark on its forehead (23.453–455).
---

ἀριπρεπέα, rendered “conspicuous,” marks the horse he sees from afar, as of the aegis in Book 15 (15.309; see the entry). He guesses that the driver is Diomedes, and he is right (23.470–472, 23.499–501). See [[idomeneus]] and [[horses]].
