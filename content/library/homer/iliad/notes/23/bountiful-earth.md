# πίλνατο πουλυβοτείρῃ (23.368, only here), with πίλνατο between χθονί and its epithet.
grc: ["πίλνατο πουλυβοτείρῃ"]
---

χθονὶ πουλυβοτείρῃ is the earth on which men lay down their arms and fall (see the entry); here a verb stands between the noun and the epithet, and the earth is the course of the race (23.362–372; [[like-a-cloud-or-a-storm]]). The phrase is new to the entry. See [[chariot]] and [[earth]].
