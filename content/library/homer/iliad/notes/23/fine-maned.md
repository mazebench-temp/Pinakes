# καλλίτριχος (23.525, only here), of a single mare.
grc: [καλλίτριχος]
---

The translation keeps καλλίθριξ, “fine-maned,” apart from ἐΰθριξ, “well-maned,” the epithet of the Myrmidons' horses and of the teams of Antilochus and Meriones as they make ready (23.13, 23.301, 23.351; see the translation's table of key words and [[well-maned]]). καλλίτριχος is new to the entry. See [[aethe-and-podargus]], [[horses]] and [[meriones]].
