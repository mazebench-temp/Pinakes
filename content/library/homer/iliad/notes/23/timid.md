---
summary: Achilles ties “a timid dove” to the mast for the archery (23.853), promises the double axes to whoever hits it (23.855), and Meriones sees it “high up under the clouds” and shoots it (23.874).
---

τρήρωνα πέλειαν ends all three verses. Teucer cuts the cord, the dove flies up, and Meriones strikes it under the wing as it circles; it settles on the mast and falls dead (23.862–881; [[doves]], [[mast]]). See [[meriones]] and [[teucer]].
