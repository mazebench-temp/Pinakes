# ἄπυρον (23.267, 23.885) and ἀπύρωτον (23.270), the related form: each only here.
grc: [ἄπυρον, ἀπύρωτον]
---

The cauldron of the chariot race is “a fine one, holding four measures, still white as it was” (23.268; [[white]]), and Menelaus takes it (23.613; [[gleaming-all-over]]). The bowl, ἀμφίθετον φιάλην ἀπύρωτον, is left over and given to Nestor without a contest (23.615–623; [[phiale]]). The cauldron of the javelin is “worth an ox, flowered,” and goes to Agamemnon, who gives it to his herald (23.885, 23.890–897; [[flowering]]). The two forms are new to the entry. See [[cauldron]] and [[contest-scene]].
