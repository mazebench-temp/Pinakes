---
summary: Teucer misses the dove, but “the bitter arrow cut the cord clean through” (23.867).
---

πικρὸς ὀϊστός ends the verse, as it does of the arrows of Pandarus and Helenus (4.134, 5.99, 13.587, 13.592); here the arrow does no harm but cuts a cord, and Teucer, who made no vow to Apollo, wins only the single axes (23.862–869, 23.883; [[far-shooter]], [[firstborn]]). See [[teucer]] and [[arrows]].
