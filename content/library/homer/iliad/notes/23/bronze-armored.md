---
summary: Menelaus asks for a fair judgment “lest someday one of the bronze-armored Achaeans say” that he won by lies and rank (23.575).
---

The verse-end τις … Ἀχαιῶν χαλκοχιτώνων is the frame of the anonymous Achaean who speaks over the body of Patroclus in Book 17, “and this is what a man would say, of the bronze-armored Achaeans” (17.414; [[tis-speech]]); here Menelaus fears such a voice. See [[achaeans]] and [[menelaus]].
