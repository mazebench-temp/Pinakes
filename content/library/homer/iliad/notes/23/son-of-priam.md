# υἷος δὲ Πριάμοιο (23.746, only here).
grc: ["υἷος δὲ Πριάμοιο"]
---

The first is Achilles' last threat over the pyre, and the gods answer it at once: Aphrodite keeps the dogs from the body and anoints it with oil (23.184–187; [[aphrodite]]). The second recalls Lycaon's first capture, when Achilles sold him to Lemnos and “the son of Jason paid the price” (21.40–41), before he killed him by the river (21.34–127; [[lycaon-son-of-priam]]). υἷος δὲ Πριάμοιο is new to the entry. See [[hector]] and [[priam]].
