---
summary: Eight contests follow the frame: the chariot race, the boxing, the wrestling, the footrace, the armed duel, the weight throw, the archery and the javelin (23.257–897).
---

The verses of the frame are found only in this book. Each contest has a moment that the frame does not foresee: in the chariot race the quarrels over the race and its prizes and the reconciliation of Menelaus and Antilochus (23.448–613), in the boxing the boast of Epeius (23.667–675), in the wrestling and the duel the draws that end with equal prizes (23.735–737, 23.822–823), in the footrace the fall of the son of Oileus in the dung (23.773–784), and in the javelin a prize given without a contest (23.884–897).
