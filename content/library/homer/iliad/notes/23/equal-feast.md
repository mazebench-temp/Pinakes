---
summary: The supper to which Achilles yields after refusing to wash is told as an equal feast: “and no one's heart lacked an equal share of the feast” (23.56).
---

Achilles himself calls the meal “the hateful feast” (23.48), but the narrator gives it the formula of every feast (see [[desire-for-drink-and-food]] and [[hateful]]). Earlier that evening he had given the Myrmidons “a funeral feast to satisfy the heart” beside his ship (23.29; [[satisfying]]). See [[feast]] and [[achilles]].
