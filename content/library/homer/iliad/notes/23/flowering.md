---
summary: For the javelin Achilles sets out “a cauldron untouched by fire, worth an ox, flowered” (23.885).
en: [flowered]
---

Walter Leaf takes ἀνθεμόεντα of a vessel adorned with flowers (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 23.885; see the translation's note on 23.884–886), and the translation renders it “flowered”; the English is linked here. Agamemnon receives the cauldron without a throw and gives it to Talthybius (23.890–897; [[perikalles]], [[untouched-by-fire]]). See [[cauldron]].
