---
summary: Achilles' terms for the armed duel: whoever first reaches the other's flesh “and touch his inward parts through the armor and the black blood” wins the sword (23.805–807).
---

The phrase διά τʼ ἔντεα καὶ μέλαν αἷμα is the one of Diomedes and Odysseus walking over the battlefield on the night raid (10.298). An ancient note quoted by Walter Leaf reports that 23.806 was athetized as taken from there; Leaf thinks the repetition loose but does not reject the line (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 23.806; see the translation's note on 23.798–825).
