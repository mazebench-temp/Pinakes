---
summary: Apollo strikes “the shining whip” from Diomedes' hands (23.384); the corselet of Asteropaeus has “a casting of shining tin” around it (23.561); and in the duel Diomedes keeps reaching Ajax's neck “with the point of his shining spear” (23.821).
---

μάστιγα φαεινήν is the whip that Odysseus forgot to take from Rhesus' chariot and that Automedon takes up for Achilles' horses (10.500, 19.395); Athena gives it back to Diomedes (23.389–390). The tin of the corselet is the metal of the Shield of Achilles and of his greaves (18.574, 18.613; [[tin]]). See [[diomedes]] and [[corselet]].
