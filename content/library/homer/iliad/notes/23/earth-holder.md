# γαιήοχον (23.584, only here).
grc: [γαιήοχον]
---

The accusative is the oath-form of the double title γαιήοχος ἐννοσίγαιος (9.183, 13.43); see [[shaker-of-the-earth]] and [[oath-formulas]]. γαιήοχον is new to the entry. See [[poseidon]].
