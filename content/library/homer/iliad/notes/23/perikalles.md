---
summary: The last verse of the book: Agamemnon “gave the beautiful prize to the herald Talthybius” (23.897).
---

The prize is the cauldron of the javelin contest, “untouched by fire, worth an ox, flowered,” which Achilles gives Agamemnon without a throw (23.885–895; [[flowering]], [[untouched-by-fire]]). The king hands it to his herald, Talthybius, who had led Briseis away from Achilles' hut in Book 1 (1.320–348). See [[agamemnon]] and [[talthybius]].
