ποδώκεος Αἰακίδαο is a verse-end of the Iliad and the Odyssey (2.860, 16.134, 17.486; *Odyssey* 11.471, 11.538). Here it places Achilles' ship as the gathering point of the mourning Myrmidons, before the kings lead him away to Agamemnon (23.28–36; [[swift-footed]]). See [[achilles]] and [[aeacus]].
