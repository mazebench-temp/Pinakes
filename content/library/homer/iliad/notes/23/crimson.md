---
summary: In the wrestling, “thick welts along their ribs and shoulders / rose up, crimson with blood” (23.716–717).
---

This is the epithet's only use in the Iliad for anything but a cloak (see the entry): the color of the dye is the color of the welts raised by the wrestlers' grip (23.710–718; [[contest-scene]]). See [[ajax]] and [[odysseus]].
