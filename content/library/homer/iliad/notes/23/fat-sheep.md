---
summary: Before the pyre the Myrmidons flay “many fat sheep and shambling cattle with curving horns” (23.166).
---

The whole verse returns from Phoenix's story in Book 9, where his kinsmen slaughtered the beasts to keep him at home (9.466); here they are victims for the dead, whose fat covers the body of Patroclus (23.167–169; [[shambling]], [[cremation]]). See [[sheep]].
