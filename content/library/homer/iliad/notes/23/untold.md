---
summary: The woodcutters throw down “the vast load of wood” for the pyre on the shore (23.127).
---

ἄσπετον ὕλην is the boundless forest that fire burns in the simile of the Achaean muster (2.455), and the vast wood that the Trojans gather for nine days for Hector's pyre in the last book (24.784). See [[pyres]] and [[ida]].
