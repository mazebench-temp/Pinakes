# κλυτὸς ἤραρε τέκτων (23.712, only here).
grc: ["κλυτὸς ἤραρε τέκτων"]
---

ἀγακλῆος Μενελάου is the genitive of ἀγακλεής, kept apart from the name of Agacles (16.571; see the entry and [[famed-for-his-spear]]). κλειτὴν ἑκατόμβην is the hecatomb of the archer's vow (4.102; [[firstborn]], [[far-shooter]]). The famous craftsman builds the roof of a high house in the simile of the wrestlers (23.711–713; [[the-rafters-of-a-high-house]]); the phrase is new to the entry. See [[menelaus]] and [[contest-scene]].
