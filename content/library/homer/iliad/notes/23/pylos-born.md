# Πυλοιγενέες (23.303, only here).
grc: [Πυλοιγενέες]
---

This is the epithet's second and last use in Homer, after Nestor himself, “the king born in Pylos” (2.54; see the entry). The verse names the horses after their master's house: Antilochus is “the son of Nestor, … the son of Neleus,” and the team is his father's, “the horses of his father” (23.302–303, 23.402; [[son-of-neleus]], [[swift-footed]]). See [[pylos]] and [[antilochus]].
