---
summary: Odysseus “prayed in his heart to gray-eyed Athena” for help in the footrace (23.769).
---

The prayer is silent, ὃν κατὰ θυμόν, and a single verse long: “Hear me, goddess, come as a good helper to my feet” (23.770; [[hear-me]]). Athena answers by lightening his limbs and by tripping the son of Oileus (23.771–777; [[divine-intervention]]). See [[athena]] and [[odysseus]].
