---
summary: Menelaus, scepter in hand, speaks among the Argives as “the man equal to the gods” (23.569); and Euryalus alone stands up against Epeius, “a man equal to the gods” (23.677).
---

Euryalus has the formula as he had it in the Catalogue, where he came “third” after Diomedes and Sthenelus (2.565); in the boxing he stands up alone against Epeius and is knocked out (23.677–699; [[euryalus]]). Menelaus' verse opens his complaint against Antilochus (23.569–585; [[tis-speech]]). See [[menelaus]].
