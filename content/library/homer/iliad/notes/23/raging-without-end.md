# ἄμοτον κεχολωμένος (23.567, only here): the adverb of the phrase with the participle of anger.
grc: ["ἄμοτον κεχολωμένος"]
en: [angry without end]
---

The adverb that names unceasing fury in battle is given here to the anger of a dispute at the games, and the anger ends in the same scene: when Antilochus yields the mare, Menelaus' heart is warmed and he gives way in turn (23.596–611; see the translation's formula rows and [[amends-and-reconciliation]]). The phrase is new to the entry. See [[menelaus]] and [[antilochus]].
