# Νέστωρ Νηλήϊος (23.349) and Νηλεΐδαο (23.652): each only here. At 23.514 Νηλήϊος is Antilochus, "of the line
# of Neleus", which the phrase leaves out.
grc: ["Νέστωρ Νηλήϊος", Νηλεΐδαο]
---

Three forms of the patronymic are used of Nestor in this book: Νηληϊάδης (23.303), the adjective Νηλήϊος, as at 10.18 (23.349), and Νηλεΐδης, found only here (23.652). The adjective is also given to Antilochus, “Antilochus, of the line of Neleus” (23.514), named after his grandfather as Achilles is after Aeacus (see the translation's note on 23.514 and [[grandson-of-aeacus]]); the entry takes only Nestor's verse.
