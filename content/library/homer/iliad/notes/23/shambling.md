---
summary: Before the pyre the Myrmidons flay “many fat sheep and shambling cattle with curving horns” (23.166).
---

The verse is the one of the feast with which Phoenix's kinsmen tried to keep him at home (9.466; [[fat-sheep]], [[with-curving-horns]]). From the fat of all the victims Achilles covers the body (23.167–169; [[cremation]]). See [[cattle]].
