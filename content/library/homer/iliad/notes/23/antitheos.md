---
summary: Achilles sets “godlike Phoenix, his father's squire,” beside the turning post as a lookout (23.360); and “the strong might of godlike Leonteus” rises for the weight throw (23.837).
---

Phoenix is named here for the only time in the games, and he does nothing in the sequel; Walter Leaf reports that critics have rejected 23.360, or 359–361, for that reason (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 23.360; see the translation's note on 23.359–361). Leonteus' epithet stands in the periphrasis with μένος (23.837; [[krateros]], [[offshoot-of-ares]]). See [[phoenix]] and [[leonteus]].
