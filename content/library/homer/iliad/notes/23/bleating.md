---
summary: At the funeral feast “many sheep and bleating goats” have their throats cut beside Achilles' ship (23.31).
---

The epithet's other use in the Iliad is in Paris' boast over the wounded Diomedes, of the Trojans who shudder at him “as bleating goats shudder at a lion” (11.383). Here the goats are slaughtered with the oxen and the white-tusked hogs (23.30–34; [[white-tusked]]). See [[goats]] and [[sacrifice]].
