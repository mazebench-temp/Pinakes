---
summary: Achilles speaks “among his war-loving companions” as he keeps the Myrmidons in their chariots to mourn (23.5), and orders “the war-loving Myrmidons” to arm and drive in the procession to the pyre (23.129).
---

Μυρμιδόνεσσι φιλοπτολέμοισι is the phrase of Achilles' charge to Patroclus to lead them into battle (16.65); in this book the war-loving people march only for the dead, in armor, in the chariots and on foot behind the body (23.129–134; [[myrmidons]], [[cremation]]). See [[achilles]].
