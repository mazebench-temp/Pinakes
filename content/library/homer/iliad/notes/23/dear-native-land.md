---
summary: Achilles tells the Spercheius that Peleus prayed for his homecoming “to my dear native land,” and adds, “But now, since I am not going home to my dear native land, / I would grant my hair to the hero Patroclus” (23.145, 23.150–151).
---

“But now, since I am not going home to my dear native land” is the verse with which Achilles accepted his death in Book 18 (18.101); here it turns the vow made for his return into a gift to the dead (23.144–151; [[hair]], [[nostos]]). See [[achilles]], [[peleus]] and [[spercheius]].
