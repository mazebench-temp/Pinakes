# Πατροκλῆος δειλοῖο (23.65, 23.105, 23.221; it also takes in 17.670) and δειλοὺς (23.223, only here).
grc: ["Πατροκλῆος δειλοῖο", δειλοὺς]
en: [poor]
---

Πατροκλῆος δειλοῖο is the verse-end with which Menelaus asked the Achaeans to remember “the gentleness of poor Patroclus” (17.670); in this book it is said of his soul each time (23.65, 23.105, 23.221; [[psyche]]). The parents of the simile are the δειλοί of the entry, the mourners (23.222–224; [[the-father-burning-his-sons-bones]]). The new forms take in Book 17's verse as well. See [[patroclus]].
