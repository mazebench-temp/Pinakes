# ὑπερθύμοισι (23.512; also Odyssey 7.59).
grc: [ὑπερθύμοισι]
---

Both uses are in the formal lines of the race: the verse of Antilochus' entry, which goes on to his grandfather, “the son of Neleus” (23.302–303; [[splendid]], [[son-of-neleus]]), and the taking of the first prize (23.511–513; [[mighty]]). ὑπερθύμοισι is new to the entry. See [[nestor]] and [[sthenelus]].
