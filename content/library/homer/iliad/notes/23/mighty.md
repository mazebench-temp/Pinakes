# ἴφθιμα κάρηνα (23.260, only here): the heads of oxen, as ἰφθίμους κεφαλάς of men.
grc: ["ἴφθιμα κάρηνα"]
---

Sthenelus, Diomedes' charioteer, does not dally: he takes the woman and the tripod and gives them to his companions (23.510–513; [[high-hearted]]). The prize list of 23.259–261 names the oxen by their heads, the periphrasis that Homer uses of men, as the “mighty heads” of heroes sent to Hades (11.55). See [[sthenelus]], [[cattle]] and [[contest-scene]].
