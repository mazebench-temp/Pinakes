---
summary: Actor is named in the patronymic of his twin sons, “the two sons of Actor”, who beat the young Nestor in the chariot race at the funeral games of Amarynceus (23.638).
---

οἴοισίν μʼ ἵπποισι παρήλασαν Ἀκτορίωνε, “Only with horses did the two sons of Actor drive past me” (23.638; see [[the-moliones]] and [[the-funeral-games-of-amarynceus]]). In Nestor's tale of the Epeian war they were “the two sons of Actor, the Moliones”, and in the next verse he called Poseidon their father (11.750–751; see [[poseidon]]); here they are only the sons of Actor, and twins (23.641). In the Catalogue the same dual names their sons, Amphimachus and Thalpius, the Epeian leaders (2.620–621; see [[amphimachus]] and [[thalpius]]).
