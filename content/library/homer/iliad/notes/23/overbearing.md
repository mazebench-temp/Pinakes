---
summary: Menelaus gives the mare back to Antilochus “so that these men too may know / that my heart is never overbearing and harsh” (23.610–611).
---

The word of blame is denied of himself by the man who has just accused Antilochus of cheating (23.570–585). His forgiveness is a show of character before the army, as his complaint feared the verdict of the army (23.575–578; [[tis-speech]], [[amends-and-reconciliation]]). See [[menelaus]].
