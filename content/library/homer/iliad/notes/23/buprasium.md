---
summary: Buprasium is where the Epeians buried their king Amarynceus and held his funeral games, in which the young Nestor won every contest but the chariot race (23.630–642).
# Βουπρασίῳ (23.631), a dative of place, occurs only here.
grc: [Βουπρασίῳ]
---

ὡς ὁπότε κρείοντʼ Ἀμαρυγκέα θάπτον Ἐπειοὶ / Βουπρασίῳ, “as when the Epeians were burying powerful Amarynceus / at Buprasium” (23.630–631): the bare dative, without a preposition, is the place where the funeral and its games were held (see [[amarynceus]], [[epeians]] and [[the-funeral-games-of-amarynceus]]). In the Catalogue Buprasium is the first-named town of the Epeians, before “shining Elis” (2.615; see [[elis]]). In Nestor's other tale of his youth it is the end of the pursuit, where the Pylians drove the routed Epeians back into their own land before turning their horses home to Pylos (11.756–760; see [[nestor-and-the-epeians]]). Here the Pylians come to Buprasium as competitors, and Nestor has no match there among the Epeians, the Pylians or the Aetolians (23.632–633).
