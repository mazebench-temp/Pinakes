---
summary: After the supper Achilles lies down alone among the Myrmidons “on the shore of the loud-roaring sea” (23.59).
---

The verse-end is that of Chryses going in silence along the shore in Book 1 and of the envoys walking to Achilles' hut in Book 9 (1.34, 9.182). Achilles lies there groaning, and the soul of Patroclus comes to him in his sleep (23.59–65; [[groaning-heavily]], [[dream-scene]]). See [[achilles]] and [[sea]].
