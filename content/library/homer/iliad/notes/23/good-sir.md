# φέριστοι (23.409, only here).
grc: [φέριστοι]
en: [good sirs]
---

The address of a stranger who may be a god (6.123, 15.247) is turned to horses, as Antilochus pleads with them not to be shamed by a mare: “so that she does not pour disgrace on you two, / Aethe, female though she is” (23.408–409). His next verses threaten them with death at Nestor's hands (23.410–413; [[it-will-be-fulfilled]]). φέριστοι is new to the entry. See [[antilochus]] and [[aethe-and-podargus]].
