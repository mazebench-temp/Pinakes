---
summary: In the wrestling “their backs creaked under bold hands, / tugged hard” (23.714–715).
---

θρασειάων ἀπὸ χειρῶν ends the verse, as it ends the verses of spears and javelins thrown “from bold hands” in battle (11.571, 13.134, 15.314); here the hands grip and pull. See [[ajax]], [[odysseus]] and [[contest-scene]].
