# ἀργιόδοντες (23.32; also Odyssey 11.413, 14.532).
grc: [ἀργιόδοντες]
---

The swine are slaughtered with the oxen, sheep and goats of Achilles' feast for the Myrmidons (23.29–34; [[bleating]], [[flourishing]]). The translation renders the word “white-tusked” here, as of the boar whose tusks covered Odysseus' helmet in Book 10 (10.264; see the translation's table of key words). ἀργιόδοντες is new to the entry. See [[sacrifice]] and [[feast]].
