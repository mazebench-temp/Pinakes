---
summary: Achilles' horses mourn their charioteer: “Him the two stand and mourn, and their manes / rest on the ground” (23.283–284).
grc: [πενθείετον]
---

πενθείετον is a dual of the verb πενθέω, “mourn”, said of the two horses (23.283; see [[the-horses-of-achilles]]). The verb occurs in the Iliad only here and in Odysseus' advice that the Achaeans eat, for they “cannot mourn a corpse with the belly” (19.225).
