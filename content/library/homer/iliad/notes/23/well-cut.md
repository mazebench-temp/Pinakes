# ἐϋτμήτους (23.684, only here).
grc: [ἐϋτμήτους]
---

The two uses cover both kinds of leather of the entry: the boxer's thongs, simple straps wound around the knuckles (see the translation's note on 23.684 and [[of-the-fields]]), and the baldric of a sword (23.824–825; [[silver-studded]]). ἐϋτμήτους is new to the entry. See [[diomedes]] and [[euryalus]].
