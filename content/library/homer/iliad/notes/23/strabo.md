---
summary: Strabo takes from this book that Patroclus came from Opus after a killing, that the Spercheius was Achilles' own river, and that Panopeus was the home of Epeius, and records the memorials of Achilles, Patroclus and Antilochus near Sigeum.
---

- Patroclus of Opus, who fled to Peleus after an unintended killing; the boy he killed was called Aeanes, and a precinct and a spring named after him were shown (*Geography* 9.4.2, on 23.85–90): [[amphidamas-of-opus]], [[opus]] and [[menoetius]].
- The Spercheius, Achilles' local river, since he says that he grew his hair for it (*Geography* 9.5.9, on 23.142): [[the-lock-for-the-spercheius]] and [[spercheius]].
- Panopeus, “the native land of Epeius” (*Geography* 9.3.14, on 23.665): [[epeius]] and [[panopeus]].
- The shrine and memorial of Achilles near Sigeum, and the memorials of Patroclus and Antilochus, to whom the people of Ilium make offerings (*Geography* 13.1.32): [[the-funeral-of-patroclus]] and [[tomb]].
