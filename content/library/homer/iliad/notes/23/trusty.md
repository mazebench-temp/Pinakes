---
summary: Achilles addresses his men, “Myrmidons of the swift horses, my trusty companions” (23.6).
---

ἐρίηρες ἑταῖροι are the companions who carry a fallen man from the field (8.332, 13.421); here they are the companions who will drive three times around the body of Patroclus (23.6–13; [[of-the-swift-horses]], [[hetairos]]). See [[myrmidons]].
