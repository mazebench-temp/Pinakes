# Πλευρώνιον (23.635), the adjective "of Pleuron", occurs only here.
grc: [Πλευρώνιον]
---

Ἀγκαῖον δὲ πάλῃ Πλευρώνιον, ὅς μοι ἀνέστη, “and in wrestling Ancaeus of Pleuron, who stood up against me” (23.635; see [[ancaeus-of-pleuron]] and [[the-funeral-games-of-amarynceus]]). Πλευρώνιος, “of Pleuron”, makes him an Aetolian: Pleuron is the first of the Aetolian towns in the Catalogue (2.639), and Nestor has just named “the great-hearted Aetolians” among his rivals (23.633; see [[aetolians]]). Pleuron and steep Calydon were the home of Oeneus and his brothers (14.115–117; see [[oeneus]]).
