---
summary: Achilles addresses his men, “Myrmidons of the swift horses, my trusty companions” (23.6).
---

The epithet is elsewhere the Danaans', in the line-end formula Δαναῶν ταχυπώλων; only here is it given to the Myrmidons, as Achilles keeps them in their chariots to drive three times around the body (23.6–13; [[trusty]], [[myrmidons]]). See [[horses]].
