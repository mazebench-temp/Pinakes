summary: The “shining limbs” of Achilles are weary from chasing Hector when sleep comes over him (23.63), and those of Euryalus give way under Epeius' blow (23.691); and the son of Oileus, after his fall in the dung, is “shining Ajax” as he takes the ox (23.779).
---

φαίδιμος Αἴας is elsewhere Telamonian Ajax (5.617, 7.187, 11.496, 15.419, 17.284); only here is it said of the son of Oileus, at the moment of his humiliation (23.779–784; [[ajax-son-of-oileus]]). φαίδιμα γυῖα is said of the sleeper and of the beaten boxer: Achilles' limbs “were very weary / from chasing Hector toward windy Ilios” (23.63–64; [[sweet-sleep]], [[windy]]), and Euryalus' “gave way beneath him” (23.691; [[the-leaping-fish]]).
