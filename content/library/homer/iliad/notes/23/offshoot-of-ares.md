---
summary: “Then second in his turn Leonteus, offshoot of Ares, let it fly” (23.841): the Lapith throws the lump of iron after Epeius.
---

Leonteus, Polypoetes' fellow leader of the Lapiths, rises in the periphrasis “the strong might of godlike Leonteus” (23.837; [[krateros]], [[antitheos]]) and throws in the Catalogue's title; neither he nor Epeius nor Ajax matches Polypoetes (23.842–847). See [[leonteus]] and [[polypoetes]].
