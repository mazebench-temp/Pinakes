# ἠθείη κεφαλὴ (23.94, only here).
grc: ["ἠθείη κεφαλὴ"]
---

ἠθείη κεφαλή is found only here; the translation renders it “dear heart,” as φίλη κεφαλή (8.281), and keeps it apart from ἠθεῖε, “Dear brother” (see the translation's note on 23.94 and [[dear-brother]]). Walter Leaf reports that the scholia take the word as an address of a younger man to an elder, and Patroclus was the elder (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 23.94; 11.787). See [[achilles]] and [[patroclus]].
