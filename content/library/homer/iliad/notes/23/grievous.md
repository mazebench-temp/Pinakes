---
summary: Achilles sets out prizes “for the painful boxing” (23.653) and “for the painful wrestling” (23.701).
---

πυγμαχίης ἀλεγεινῆς and παλαισμοσύνης ἀλεγεινῆς stand at the same place in the verse, at the head of two contests of body against body (see [[contest-scene]] and [[contests]]). The boxing ends with Euryalus spitting blood, the wrestling with welts “crimson with blood” and a draw (23.697, 23.716–717, 23.735–737; [[crimson]]). See [[epeius]], [[ajax]] and [[odysseus]].
