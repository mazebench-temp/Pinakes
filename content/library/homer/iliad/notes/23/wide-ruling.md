---
summary: At the javelin “up rose the son of Atreus, wide-ruling Agamemnon,” and Achilles gives him the prize without a contest (23.887–897).
---

The line-end formula Ἀτρεΐδης εὐρὺ κρείων Ἀγαμέμνων is the one with which Agamemnon rose to answer Calchas in Book 1 (1.102), and he is given the same title at the end of the games: Achilles praises him as “the best in strength and in throwing” and gives him the cauldron, and the spear goes to Meriones (23.890–896).
