# δουρὶ κλειτὸς (23.355, only here): the uncompounded form of δουρικλειτός, as δουρὶ κλυτός beside δουρικλυτός.
grc: ["δουρὶ κλειτὸς"]
---

δουρὶ κλειτός stands beside δουρὶ κλυτός as δουρικλειτός beside δουρικλυτός (see the entry), and the translation renders both “famed for his spear” (see the translation's formula rows). In the games the title of the warrior is given to men who are competing, not fighting: Menelaus in the chariot race, and Diomedes as he serves as second to his kinsman Euryalus, “encouraging him with words, and he greatly wished him victory” (23.681–682; [[son-of-tydeus]]). See [[menelaus]] and [[diomedes]].
