---
summary: Achilles tells the Spercheius that Peleus vowed him “a holy hecatomb” and the shearing of his hair if he came home (23.146).
---

The vow of Peleus is told in full: the lock of hair, a holy hecatomb, and fifty uncut rams “into your waters, where you have a precinct and a fragrant altar” (23.144–148; [[fragrant]], [[hecatomb]]). Since Achilles will not go home, he gives the hair to Patroclus instead (23.150–151; [[hair]], [[spercheius]]). See [[achilles]] and [[peleus]].
