---
summary: Achilles cuts off “his fair hair” at the pyre (23.141), and Menelaus is “fair-haired Menelaus” three times in the chariot race: as he rises, as he drives behind Diomedes, and as he rebukes Antilochus (23.293, 23.401, 23.438).
---

The hair Achilles cuts is the hair Athena caught in Book 1, when she held him back from Agamemnon “by his fair hair” (1.197); he had grown it for the river Spercheius and now gives it to the dead (23.141–153; [[hair]], [[spercheius]]). For Menelaus it is the standing epithet. See [[achilles]] and [[menelaus]].
