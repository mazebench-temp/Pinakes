---
summary: Odysseus prays on the last stretch of the footrace, “So he spoke in prayer, and Pallas Athena heard him” (23.771).
---

The verse is the one of Diomedes' prayer in Book 5, with the same answer: she made his limbs light (5.121–122 = 23.771–772; [[hear-me]]). See [[athena]] and [[odysseus]].
