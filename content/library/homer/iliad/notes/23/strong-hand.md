---
summary: The boxers raise “their strong hands against each other” (23.686), the wrestlers take hold of each other “with strong hands” (23.711), and Ajax hurls the lump of iron “from his strong hand” beyond the marks of all (23.842–843).
---

The strong hands of battle, Sarpedon's on the battlement and the hand that throws a javelin (12.397, 14.455), are here the hands of the contests: χερσὶ στιβαρῇσιν ends the verse in the boxing and the wrestling, and χειρὸς ἄπο στιβαρῆς opens the verse of Ajax's throw (see [[contest-scene]]). See [[ajax]], [[epeius]] and [[odysseus]].
