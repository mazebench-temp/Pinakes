---
summary: Achilles promises the winner of the armed duel “this silver-studded sword, / a fine Thracian one, which I took from Asteropaeus” (23.807–808).
---

The sword was among the armor Achilles stripped from Asteropaeus after killing him by the river (21.179–183); he gives it to Diomedes “with its sheath and its well-cut strap” (23.824–825; [[well-cut]]). See [[sword]] and [[asteropaeus]].
