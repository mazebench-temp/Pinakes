---
summary: In the archery Teucer does not promise Apollo “that he would offer a glorious hecatomb of firstborn lambs” and misses the dove (23.863–865), while Meriones promises it in the same verse and hits it (23.872–876).
---

The verse is Athena's advice to Pandarus and his vow before he shot at Menelaus (4.102, 4.120), and these are its last two uses. Walter Leaf takes the hecatomb of lambs as “Apollo's fixed price for a successful shot” (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 23.864; see [[far-shooter]] and [[famous]]).
