---
summary: Achilles gives the Myrmidons “a funeral feast to satisfy the heart” (23.29); the mourners heap up “ample wood” for the pyre (23.139); and Nestor prays that the gods give Achilles “thanks in return, to satisfy your heart” (23.650).
en: [ample]
---

The translation renders μενοεικής “satisfying,” “to satisfy the heart” and, of wood, “ample” (see the translation's table of key words); the English “ample” is linked here. τάφος, the funeral, is never the tomb in Homer (see the translation's note on 23.29). See [[feast]], [[pyres]] and [[charis]].
