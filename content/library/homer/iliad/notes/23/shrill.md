# φυσῶντες λιγέως (23.218, only here).
grc: ["φυσῶντες λιγέως"]
---

πνοιῇ ὕπο λιγυρῇ is the half-verse of the winnowing wind in the simile of Book 13 (13.590). λιγέως, “shrilly,” is an adverb of the same root, like λίγʼ in Briseis' wailing over Patroclus (19.284). See [[boreas]], [[zephyrus]] and [[pyres]].
