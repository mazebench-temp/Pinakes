---
summary: Achilles asks Agamemnon to send for wood at dawn, “so that the tireless fire may burn him away / the sooner from our eyes” (23.52–53).
---

The formula ἀκάματον πῦρ is elsewhere the fire of battle, at the ships and around Achilles' head (15.731, 16.122, 18.225); here it is the fire of the funeral that Achilles wants for Patroclus, so that “the army may turn to its tasks” (23.53; [[pyres]], [[cremation]]). See [[achilles]].
