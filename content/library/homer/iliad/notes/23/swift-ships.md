---
summary: In Nestor's lesson “by cunning again a helmsman on the wine-dark sea / steers a swift ship buffeted by the winds” (23.316–317).
---

The swift ship is the second of Nestor's three examples of cunning, between the woodcutter and the charioteer (23.315–318; [[metis]], [[wine-dark]]). See [[nestor]] and [[ships]].
