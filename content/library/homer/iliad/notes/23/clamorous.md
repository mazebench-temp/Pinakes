# κελαδεινὸν (23.208, only here).
grc: [κελαδεινὸν]
---

The epithet of Artemis, goddess of the noisy hunt (16.183, 20.70, 21.511), is given here in the masculine to the West Wind, the only such use in Homer; the winds come “with a wondrous din, driving the clouds in confusion before them” (23.213; [[wondrous]]). The narrator's own word for the West Wind a few verses before is “stormy” (23.200; [[stormy]]). κελαδεινόν is new to the entry. See [[zephyrus]] and [[iris]].
