---
summary: Meges kills Pedaeus, “son of Antenor,” a bastard whom Antenor's wife Theano reared as her own.
grc: [Ἀντήνορος υἱὸν]
---

“And Meges killed Pedaeus, son of Antenor, who was a bastard, but brilliant Theano had reared him with care like her own dear children, to please her husband” (5.69–71; [[pedaeus]], [[theano]], [[nothos]]). The periphrasis Ἀντήνορος υἱός stands where the patronymic Ἀντηνορίδης stood in Books 3 and 4. The son of the elder who received the Achaean envoys (3.205–207) is killed in the first round of Book 5, and the note on his stepmother's care is his only obituary ([[antenor]], [[the-war-in-small]]).
