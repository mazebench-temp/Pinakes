---
summary: Pandarus swears by the high-roofed house he hopes to see again; the adjective here is ὑψερεφής, the translation's other “high-roofed.”
grc: [ὑψερεφὲς]
---

Pandarus ends his complaint with an oath: “But if I come home and see with my eyes my native land and my wife and my great high-roofed house, then may a stranger cut my head from my shoulders on the spot, if I do not put this bow in the blazing fire” (5.212–215; [[bow]], [[phaeinos]]). The word is ὑψερεφής (“with a high roof,” from ὕψι and ἐρέφω, “roof over”), a synonym of ὑψόροφος, and the translation gives both the same English. The house is the goal of the return he will not make: a few lines later he is dead (5.290–296; [[nostos]], [[pandarus]]).
