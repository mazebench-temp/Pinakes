---
summary: Zeus smiles and calls “golden Aphrodite” to tell her that the works of war are not hers.
grc: [χρυσῆν Ἀφροδίτην]
---

Athena has mocked the wounded Aphrodite, saying she must have scratched her hand on a golden brooch while caressing some Achaean woman (5.421–425; [[well-robed]]). Zeus smiles:

{{quote:a1050ae1-7a2c-5e7d-a764-e9674819233c}}

He names her by the epithet of her beauty as he gives her back her proper sphere, “the lovely works of marriage” ([[smiling-at-him]], [[aphrodite]]). The gold of her epithet answers the gold of Athena's brooch.
