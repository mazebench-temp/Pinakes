---
summary: The title of goddesses is given to Enyo, the war goddess who comes into battle with Ares.
grc: [πότνιʼ]
---

As Hector charges, “Ares led them, and lady Enyo, she bringing with her Tumult, shameless in combat” (5.592–593; [[enyo]], [[shameless-in-combat]]). In Book 1 the title honored Hera and Thetis; here it is given to the goddess of the slaughter of battle. She is one of the goddesses “who rule over the war of men,” as Diomedes knows that Aphrodite is not (5.330–333; [[sacker-of-cities]]). πότνια is a title of authority, and Enyo's authority is over war.
