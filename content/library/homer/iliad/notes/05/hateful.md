---
summary: Phaestus, killed by Idomeneus, falls from his chariot, “and hateful darkness seized him.”
grc: [στυγερὸς]
---

“And he fell from the chariot, and hateful darkness seized him” (5.47; [[phaestus-son-of-borus]], [[darkness-covered-his-eyes]]). The epithet that Books 2 and 4 gave to war, and Helen to herself in Book 3, here describes the darkness of death, one of the formulas of a warrior's end ([[androktasia]]). Zeus later uses the superlative of Ares: “you are the most hateful to me” (ἔχθιστος, 5.890), a different word ([[aidelos]]).
