---
summary: Zeus gives Hera leave to punish Ares with Athena's warlike title: “rouse Athena, driver of spoil, against him.”
grc: [ἀγελείην]
---

Hera asks Zeus whether he will be angry if she strikes Ares and chases him from the battle. He answers:

{{quote:ff20775f-4c93-5fe4-9062-fbff86eb3b16}}

The father names his daughter by her title as a goddess of war, the one who drives off booty, as he sends her against his son. His reason is her record: she is used to bringing Ares “evil pains” ([[ares]], [[athena]]). She does so at once, driving Diomedes' chariot at Ares and pushing the spear into his flank (5.835–859; [[diomedes-wounds-ares]]).
