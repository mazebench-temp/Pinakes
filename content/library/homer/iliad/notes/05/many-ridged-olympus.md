---
summary: Hera finds Zeus sitting apart on the topmost peak of many-ridged Olympus, in the verse that found him there for Thetis in Book 1.
---

“And they found the son of Cronus sitting apart from the other gods on the topmost peak of many-ridged Olympus” (5.753–754; [[most-high]], [[son-of-cronus]]). In Book 1 nearly the same two verses found Zeus alone for Thetis' supplication (1.498–499). Here Hera and Athena drive up to him in their chariot, and Hera asks leave to strike Ares. As in Book 1 a goddess comes to the solitary god with a request, and he grants it.
