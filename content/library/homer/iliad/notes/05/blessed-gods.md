---
summary: The blessed gods bleed ichor in Book 5, and Diomedes remembers that Athena forbade him to fight them face to face.
grc: [μακάρεσσι θεοῖς]
---

The phrase for the gods' carefree state comes at the moment when one of them is hurt. Diomedes' spear pierces Aphrodite's hand, “and the immortal blood of the goddess flowed, ichor, such as flows in the blessed gods” (5.339–340; [[ichor]], [[gods-wounded-by-mortals]]). The blessed are not beyond pain.

Later Diomedes tells Athena why he is giving ground before Ares: “You would not let me fight face to face with the blessed gods, with any of the others,” except Aphrodite (5.819–821). He repeats her own command of 5.130–132, with the blessed gods in place of “the immortal gods” ([[immortals]]).
