---
summary: The Paphlagonians are “great-hearted shield-bearing” as their leader Pylaemenes is killed.
---

“There they killed Pylaemenes, the equal of Ares, leader of the great-hearted shield-bearing Paphlagonians” (5.576–577; [[pylaemenes]], [[paphlagonians]], [[equal-of-ares]]). The people's epithet frames the death of their leader, as the shield-bearing ranks framed Pandarus and Machaon in Book 4 (4.90–91, 4.201–202).
