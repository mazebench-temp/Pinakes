---
summary: In Book 5 the epithet of Hector goes to Telamonian Ajax, “shining Ajax,” as he runs up to strip Amphius.
---

“And he fell with a thud; and shining Ajax ran up to strip his armor; but the Trojans showered spears on him” (5.617–618; [[telamonian]], [[fell-with-a-thud]]). The formula φαίδιμος Ἕκτωρ gives way to φαίδιμος Αἴας. Hector is in the same scene, killing Menesthes and Anchialus two verses before Ajax's throw (5.608–609), but he is not named by the epithet in Book 5. The shining is given to the Achaean, and it comes just before Ajax is pushed back from the body.
