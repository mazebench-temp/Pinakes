In Book 3 the formula belonged to the challenge to single combat. In Book 5 Dione uses it in her warning about Diomedes:

{{quote:eb6af802-d7ab-5073-997a-ba8d195879e8}}

The man who fights with the immortals does not live long, and his children do not call him papa at his knees when he comes home “from war and grim combat” ([[who-fights-the-gods]], [[dione]]). The phrase that usually names the fighting names here the return from it, which the fighter of gods will not have. Dione then pictures his wife, Aegialeia, waking her household with her wailing (5.410–415; [[periphron]]).
