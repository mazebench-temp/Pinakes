---
summary: Phereclus built for Alexander “the balanced ships that began the evil.”
---

“It was he who built for Alexander the balanced ships that began the evil, which became an evil for all the Trojans and for himself” (5.62–64; [[phereclus]], [[that-began-the-evil]]). The ordinary epithet of well-made ships stands beside the unique one. The builder's skill, for which Athena loved him (5.60–61), made them trim and seaworthy; what they carried made them the beginning of the war ([[ships]], [[paris]]).
