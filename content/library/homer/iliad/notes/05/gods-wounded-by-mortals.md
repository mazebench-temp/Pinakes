---
summary: Only in this book does a mortal wound a god: Diomedes wounds Aphrodite and then Ares, and Dione tells of three gods whom men hurt before, Ares, Hera and Hades. The gods bleed ichor, cry out in pain and are healed at once; they cannot die, and their wounds show how far the book lets a mortal go.
---
