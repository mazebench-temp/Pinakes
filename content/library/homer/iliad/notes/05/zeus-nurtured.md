---
summary: Ares, in the likeness of Acamas, calls to the sons of Priam and to Priam himself with the royal epithet.
grc: [διοτρεφέεσσι]
---

The epithet of kings is spoken twice in two lines. The narrator says that Ares “called to Priam's sons, whom Zeus had nurtured,” and his first words are “Sons of Priam, the king whom Zeus has nurtured” (5.463–464; [[sons-of-priam]], [[priam]]). The war god, disguised as the Thracian Acamas, flatters the royal house with its title as he shames it: how long will they let their people be killed, and wait until the fighting reaches the gates (5.465–466; [[baneful]], [[exhortation]])?
