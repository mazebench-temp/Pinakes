---
summary: The rims of the wheels of Hera's chariot are of imperishable gold.
grc: [ἄφθιτος]
---

Hebe fits the wheels to Hera's chariot, and the narrator stops to describe them: “Their rims are of imperishable gold, and over them are fitted tires of bronze, a wonder to see” (5.724–725; [[chariot]], [[hebe]], [[flaming]]). The gold of a god's chariot does not decay. In Book 2 the epithet belonged to Agamemnon's scepter, the work of Hephaestus; here it marks the chariot on which two goddesses go out to stop the god of war.
