---
summary: Hera's horses fly between earth and the starry sky.
grc: [οὐρανοῦ ἀστερόεντος]
---

Hera whips her horses, “and the pair flew on not unwilling between earth and the starry sky” (5.768–769; [[with-golden-headbands]], [[high-neighing]]). The epithet of the sky marks the space the goddesses cross in a few bounds, which the next simile measures by the view from a lookout over the sea (5.770–772; [[the-lookout]]).
