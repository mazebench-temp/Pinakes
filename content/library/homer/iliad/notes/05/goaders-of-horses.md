---
summary: Pandarus calls on the “great-hearted Trojans, goaders of horses” as he boasts that Diomedes is hit.
---

Having shot Diomedes in the shoulder, Pandarus shouts to his side: “Rise up, great-hearted Trojans, goaders of horses; the best of the Achaeans is hit” (5.102–103; [[great-hearted]], [[best-of-the-achaeans]]). The horsemen's title that Agamemnon gave to the Cadmeians in the story of Tydeus (4.391) is given to the Trojans by the archer who came on foot, having left his own horses at home (5.192–205). His boast is premature: the arrow “had not overcome him” (5.106; [[swift-shaft]]).
