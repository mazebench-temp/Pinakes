---
summary: Athena arms herself “for tearful war”; the adjective here is δακρυόεις, from the same δάκρυ as πολύδακρυς.
grc: [δακρυόεντα]
---

“And putting on the tunic of Zeus the cloud-gatherer she armed herself in armor for tearful war” (5.736–737; [[arming-scene]]). The word is δακρυόεις, “full of tears,” formed like πολύδακρυς from δάκρυ (“tear”; [[tears]]), and the translation gives both the same English. The whole verse is used again when Athena arms in Book 8 (Il. 8.388). The tears of war are men's; the gods wounded in this book are healed at once (5.416–417, 5.899–906).
