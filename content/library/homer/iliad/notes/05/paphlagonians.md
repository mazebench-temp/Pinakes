---
summary: The Paphlagonians lose their leader Pylaemenes, “leader of the great-hearted shield-bearing Paphlagonians”, to Menelaus, and his charioteer Mydon to Antilochus (5.576–589).
---

{{quote:d128ce05-c59b-57fb-81f9-4458117fb533}}

Book 5 gives the people two epithets in one verse: μεγάθυμοι, “great-hearted” (see [[great-hearted]]), and ἀσπισταί, “shield-men”, rendered “shield-bearing” (5.577; see [[shield-bearing]]). Their leader falls to Menelaus' spear, and his charioteer Mydon to Antilochus' sword (5.576–589; see [[pylaemenes]] and [[mydon]]).

Their leader returns alive in Book 13, following his son's body back to Troy (13.656–659). On the contradiction, see [[pylaemenes]].
