---
summary: Wind-footed Iris leads the wounded Aphrodite out of the battle and drives her to Olympus in Ares' chariot.
grc: [ποδήνεμος]
---

Iris comes to Aphrodite unbidden, as a helper rather than a messenger: “and wind-footed Iris took her and led her out of the throng, weighed down with pain” (5.353–354). She takes the reins of the chariot Ares lends his sister and whips the horses to Olympus, where “swift Iris, wind-footed, halted the horses,” unyoked them, and set ambrosial fodder before them (5.365–369; [[with-golden-headbands]], [[ambrosial]], [[iris]]). The epithet of speed names her twice as she carries a goddess out of the war, and in the second verse it has its usual formula.
