---
summary: The Thracians are named once, in the title of their leader Acamas, whose likeness Ares takes to rouse the sons of Priam (5.462).
---

εἰδόμενος Ἀκάμαντι θοῷ ἡγήτορι Θρῃκῶν, “in the likeness of Acamas, the swift leader of the Thracians” (5.462). The god borrows the authority of an ally's commander to shame the sons of Priam (see [[acamas-of-thrace]] and [[sons-of-priam]]). The Thracians lost their other leader, Peirous, in the first battle (4.517–538; see [[peirous]]), and Acamas falls to Ajax at the start of Book 6 (6.5–11).
