---
summary: The title is given to Hypeiron as Diomedes kills him, to Aeneas as Apollo sends him back to battle with renewed might, and twice to Menelaus as Antilochus comes to his side.
---

Diomedes “killed Astynous and Hypeiron, shepherd of the people” (5.144; [[hypeiron]]). The title of a leader is given to a man of whom nothing else is told. It marks Aeneas' return: Apollo sends him from the inner shrine “and put might into the breast of the shepherd of the people” (5.512–513; [[menos]], [[aeneas]]). And it names Menelaus when he advances on Aeneas and Antilochus fears for him: “he feared greatly for the shepherd of the people, that he might come to harm,” and “Antilochus took his stand close beside the shepherd of the people” (5.565–570; [[antilochus]], [[menelaus]]). The title of the leader who protects his people is given to the leader whom a younger man protects.
