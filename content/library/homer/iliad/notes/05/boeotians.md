πὰρ δέ οἱ ἄλλοι / ναῖον Βοιωτοὶ μάλα πίονα δῆμον ἔχοντες, “and near him lived other Boeotians, holding a very rich land” (5.709–710). Hyle is one of the Boeotian towns of the Catalogue (2.500), and the rich land is the country around “the Cephisian lake” (see [[oresbius]], [[hyle]] and [[cephisian-lake]]). Their leaders fall later in the poem: Prothoenor, Arcesilaus and Clonius are killed in Books 14 and 15.
