---
summary: In Dione's story Hades, shot by Heracles, goes up to the house of Zeus and high Olympus to be healed.
---

“And he went to the house of Zeus and high Olympus, grieving at heart, pierced through with pains; for the arrow had been driven into his massive shoulder” (5.398–400; [[heracles-wounds-hera-and-hades]], [[hades]]). The god of the dead leaves his own realm to be healed where the gods of the sky live, and Paeëon cures him there (5.401–402; [[pain-killing]]). In Book 1 the Hundred-Hander came to high Olympus to protect Zeus; here a wounded god comes to it for help. At the end of the book Ares does the same (5.868–869; [[steep]]).
