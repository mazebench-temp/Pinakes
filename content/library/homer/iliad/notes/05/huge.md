---
summary: In Book 5 the word of uncanny size belongs to the powers of death and war: huge Hades, the huge spear of Ares, and huge Periphas, whom Ares kills and strips.
---

Dione's story of the gods wounded by Heracles includes the god of the dead: “And among them huge Hades endured a swift arrow” (5.395; [[heracles-wounds-hera-and-hades]], [[swift-shaft]]). As Ares leads the Trojans with Hector, “Ares wielded a huge spear in his hands” (5.594). And the one man whom Ares kills alone is “huge Periphas,” “by far the best of the Aetolians,” whom he is stripping when Diomedes comes at him, and leaves lying where he fell (5.842–848; [[periphas]], [[blood-stained]]). In Book 3 πελώριος measured Agamemnon and Ajax; here it is the size of the god of death and the god of war, and of the man who fell to one of them.
