---
summary: Powerful Agamemnon kills Deicoon, companion of Aeneas, just after his exhortation to the army.
---

Agamemnon's exhortation (“Friends, be men,” 5.529–532) is followed at once by a killing: he throws and strikes Deicoon, whom the Trojans honored as they did the sons of Priam, and “powerful Agamemnon struck him on the shield with his spear” (5.533–537; [[deicoon]], [[exhortation]]). The spear goes through shield and belt into the belly, and Deicoon falls with a thud (5.538–540; [[fell-with-a-thud]]). The king's title stands at the moment he makes his words good.
