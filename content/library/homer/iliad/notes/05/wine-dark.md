---
summary: In the simile of the lookout, a man gazes over the wine-dark sea into the haze, and his view measures the leap of the gods' horses.
---

“As far as a man sees with his eyes into the haze as he sits on a lookout, gazing over the wine-dark sea, so far do the high-neighing horses of the gods leap at a bound” (5.770–772; [[the-lookout]], [[high-neighing]]). The sea's epithet belongs to a scene of watching, far from the battle. The distance a man can see is the stride of a god's horse.
