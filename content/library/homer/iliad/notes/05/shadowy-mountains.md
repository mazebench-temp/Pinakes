---
summary: The epithet describes clouds, not mountains, in the simile of the Danaans standing like motionless clouds on the mountain tops.
---

{{quote:4dc3a21a-abd9-5eca-b526-496738183bb2}}

The Danaans hold their ground like clouds that Zeus has set on the mountain tops in windless weather, while the North Wind and the other winds sleep, which scatter “the shadowy clouds” when they blow ([[clouds-on-the-mountains]]). In Book 1 σκιόεις described the mountains between Phthia and Troy; here the shadow is the clouds' own. Their stillness depends on the winds' sleep, and the adjective belongs to the clouds that the winds scatter.
