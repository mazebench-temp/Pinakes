---
summary: Sarpedon warns Hector that the enemy will soon sack his well-peopled city.
grc: [εὖ ναιομένην πόλιν]
---

Sarpedon's rebuke ends with a threat to the city itself: if Hector and his people are caught like prey in a net, the enemy “will soon sack your well-peopled city” (5.487–489; [[the-net]], [[sarpedon]]). In Book 1 Achilles used the formula of the towns of the Troad that the army had sacked (1.163–164); here it is Troy's turn in prospect. The praise of the city's people comes in the verse that foresees its fall.
