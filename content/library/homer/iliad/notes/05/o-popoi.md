---
summary: Hera cries ὢ πόποι to Athena as Hector and Ares cut down the Argives (5.714), in the verse she used in Book 2 when the army was running for the ships (2.157).
---

Hera sees Hector and Ares killing the Argives and at once calls to Athena: “Ah, child of aegis-bearing Zeus, Atrytone, surely it was an empty word we promised Menelaus, that he would sack well-walled Ilios and then sail home, if we let baneful Ares rage like this” (5.714–717). The first verse is the one with which she sent Athena to stop the flight of the army in Book 2 (5.714 = 2.157), and in both books her cry sets Athena in motion. It is heard once more in the battle of the gods, when Hera sees Aphrodite leading the fallen Ares out of the fighting, and it again sends Athena after them (21.418–422). See [[hera]] and [[athena]].
