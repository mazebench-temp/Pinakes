---
summary: Sarpedon, wounded, addresses Hector as “Son of Priam” as he begs not to be left as prey for the Danaans.
---

Sarpedon's appeal opens with the patronymic: “Son of Priam, do not leave me as prey for the Danaans to lie here, but defend me” (5.684–685; [[dear-native-land]], [[sarpedon]]). Earlier he had rebuked Hector for leaving the fighting to the allies, while Hector's brothers and brothers-in-law cowered (5.472–477). The ally who shamed the son of Priam now depends on him, and Hector passes by without a word (5.689–691; [[of-the-gleaming-helmet]]). Two other sons of Priam, Echemmon and Chromius, are killed by Diomedes in their chariot (5.159–165; [[echemmon]], [[chromius-son-of-priam]]), named in the periphrasis υἷας Πριάμοιο ([[sons-of-priam]]).
