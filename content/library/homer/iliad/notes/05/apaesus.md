{{quote:c55e674a-2e5b-590e-af43-041c53d6d052}}

ὅς ῥʼ ἐνὶ Παισῷ ναῖε, “who in Paesus lived” (5.612–613). The Catalogue calls the town Ἀπαισός (2.828), and Book 5 Παισός, the name it had in historical times; the two forms are usually taken to name one place. In the Catalogue it was one of the towns of the sons of Merops, Adrestus and Amphius (2.828–830; see [[the-sons-of-merops]] and [[amphius]]). The man of Paesus killed in Book 5 is another Amphius, the son of Selagus, led to Troy by his fate (see [[amphius-son-of-selagus]]).
