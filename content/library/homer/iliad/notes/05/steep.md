---
summary: Twice in Book 5 a wounded god reaches “the seat of the gods, steep Olympus”: Aphrodite in Ares' chariot, and Ares himself.
grc: [αἰπὺν]
---

The same half-verse, θεῶν ἕδος αἰπὺν Ὄλυμπον, marks the two retreats of wounded gods. Iris drives Aphrodite, “and quickly they came to the seat of the gods, steep Olympus” (5.367), where Dione comforts her. Ares, wounded by Diomedes, rises with the clouds, and “swiftly he came to the seat of the gods, steep Olympus” (5.868), where he complains to Zeus ([[olympus]], [[gods-wounded-by-mortals]]). The steep mountain is the refuge that mortals cannot reach.
