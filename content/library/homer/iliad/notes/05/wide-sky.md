---
summary: The wounded Ares rises into the wide sky like a dark mist from the clouds.
---

“And as a murky mist appears out of the clouds when after heat a stormy wind arises, so to Diomedes, son of Tydeus, bronze Ares appeared as he went up with the clouds into the wide sky” (5.864–867; [[dark-mist-from-the-clouds]], [[bronze-ares]]). The formula for heaven marks the god's retreat from the field. Earlier the dust of battle rose into “the brazen sky” (5.504; [[brazen]]); now the god of war himself goes up into the sky, and Diomedes watches him go.
