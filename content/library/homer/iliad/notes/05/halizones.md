---
summary: The Halizones are named for the last time in the poem when Agamemnon kills their leader Odius (5.39).
---

Book 5 names them once, in the title of the first man killed when the Achaean leaders turn the Trojans back: “great Odius, leader of the Halizones” (5.39; see [[odius]]). Their other leader, Epistrophus, is never mentioned after the Catalogue (2.856), and the people themselves do not appear again in the Iliad: their one appearance in the fighting is the death of their leader. See [[trojan-allies]].
