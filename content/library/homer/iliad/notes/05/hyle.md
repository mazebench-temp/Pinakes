The town of the Catalogue (2.500) is now the home of one of the dead (see [[oresbius]]). The lake beside it is named for the river Cephisus; Strabo held that it was not Lake Copais but the small lake by Hyle, which took the town's name (see [[cephisian-lake]]). Some ancient texts read Ὕδῃ, “in Hyde”, a town in Lydia (20.385), here and in the line on Tychius, the leatherworker of Hyle who made Ajax's shield (7.221); Strabo rejects the reading (see [[hyle-or-hyde]]). The next words settle it: “and near him lived other Boeotians” (5.709–710; see [[boeotians]]).
