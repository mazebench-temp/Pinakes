---
summary: Aphrodite is “laughter-loving” as she complains to Dione of the wound Diomedes has given her.
grc: [φιλομμειδὴς]
---

Dione asks which of the gods has hurt her daughter, and “laughter-loving Aphrodite answered her: The son of Tydeus wounded me, high-hearted Diomedes” (5.375–376; [[dione]], [[high-hearted]]). The formula keeps its epithet whatever the goddess's mood, and here the goddess of smiles comes “grieved at heart,” in terrible pain (5.352, 5.364). Book 5 spells the word with a double μ, φιλομμειδής, the usual spelling; Book 3 printed it with one. A few lines later Zeus himself smiles at her, and sends her back to the works of marriage (5.426–430; [[smiling-at-him]], [[golden]]).
