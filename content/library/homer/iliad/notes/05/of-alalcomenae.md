---
summary: Athena of Alalcomenae returns to the house of Zeus at the end of Book 5, having stopped Ares.
---

The epithet closes the book with Argive Hera's, in the verse Zeus used against them in Book 4 (5.907–909; [[argive]]). If the ancient gloss “the Protectress” is right, the title fits the book it ends: Athena has protected Diomedes from Ares, catching the god's spear in her hand and pushing it away from the chariot (5.853–854), and she has turned the war god back from the Achaeans ([[athena]]).
