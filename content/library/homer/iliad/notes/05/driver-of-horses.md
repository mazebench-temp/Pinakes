---
summary: Orestes, “driver of horses,” is one of the six Achaeans killed by Hector and Ares; he is not Agamemnon's son.
---

“Godlike Teuthras, and after him Orestes, driver of horses” (5.705; [[orestes-the-achaean]], [[antitheos]]). The epithet that Book 2 gave to Pelops names a man known only from this list of the dead. He shares his name with Agamemnon's son, who is at home in Mycenae (Il. 9.142–143), and is another man. See [[bronze-ares]] and [[armed-in-bronze]] for the list.
