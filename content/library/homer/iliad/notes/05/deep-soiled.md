---
summary: The epithet names Tarne, the home of Phaestus, son of the Maeonian Borus.
grc: [ἐριβώλακος]
---

“And Idomeneus slew Phaestus, son of the Maeonian Borus, who had come from deep-soiled Tarne” (5.43–44; [[phaestus-son-of-borus]], [[borus]], [[tarne]]). The only thing said of the dead man's home is that it was rich farmland. Like the obituaries of Amphius, “rich in grainland” (5.612–613), and of Oresbius, whose Boeotian neighbors held “a very rich land” (5.707–710), the verse measures a death by the good land left behind ([[the-war-in-small]], [[of-the-flashing-band]]).
