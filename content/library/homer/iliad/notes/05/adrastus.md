Ἀδρηστίνη is a feminine patronymic, “daughter of Adrastus”. Dione uses it in her warning to Aphrodite: the man who fights the gods does not come home, and Aegialeia, the wise daughter of Adrastus, will wake the household with her wailing for him (5.410–415; see [[aegialeia]]). Diomedes is tied to Adrastus twice over. His father Tydeus married one of Adrastus' daughters (14.121), and he himself married Aegialeia. See [[diomedes]] and [[tydeus]].
