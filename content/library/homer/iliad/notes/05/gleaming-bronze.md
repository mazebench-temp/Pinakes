---
summary: The formula κεκορυθμένος αἴθοπι χαλκῷ, “armored in gleaming bronze,” names Menelaus and Hector as each goes through the champions to meet the enemy.
---

Book 5 has the formula with αἶθοψ in place of νῶροψ. Menelaus, pitying the sons of Diocles, “went through the champions, armored in gleaming bronze” (5.562; [[dear-to-ares]]), and Hector, seeing Odysseus kill the Lycians, “went through the champions, armored in gleaming bronze, bringing terror to the Danaans” (5.681). The same verse brings a man from each side into the front rank. αἶθοψ, “fiery-looking,” is the word that describes wine as “sparkling” (5.341; [[wine]]).
