---
summary: The lesser Ajax is one of “the two Ajaxes” who, with Odysseus and Diomedes, urge on the Danaans as they hold their ground against the Trojan rally (5.519–520).
refs: [5.519-520]
---

Αἴαντε δύω, “the two Ajaxes” (5.519), are Telamonian Ajax and the son of Oileus, as in Books 2 and 4 (see [[ajax]]). With Odysseus and Diomedes they urge on the Danaans, who stand firm “like clouds that the son of Cronus has set in windless weather on the tops of the mountains” (5.522–523; see [[clouds-on-the-mountains]]). He is not named alone in Book 5.
