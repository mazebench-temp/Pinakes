summary: Diomedes prays to Athena with the formula, Hera calls her by it before they arm, and Diomedes answers her rebuke with “daughter of aegis-bearing Zeus.”
grc: [θύγατερ Διὸς αἰγιόχοιο]
---

The formula frames Athena's part in Book 5. Wounded by Pandarus, Diomedes begins his prayer with it: “Hear me, child of aegis-bearing Zeus, Atrytone” (5.115; [[hear-me]], [[atrytone]], [[prayer-type-scene]]). Hera, seeing the Argives killed, begins her proposal to Athena with the same half-verse after ὢ πόποι (5.714; [[o-popoi]]), as she did in Book 2. The narrator names Athena as “daughter of aegis-bearing Zeus” as she arms (5.733; [[arming-scene]]). And when Athena has rebuked him with his father, Diomedes answers: “I know you, goddess, daughter of aegis-bearing Zeus” (5.815). The man to whom Athena gave the power to tell god from man recognizes her (5.127–128; [[achlys]]).
