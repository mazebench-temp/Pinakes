---
summary: Ajax, stripping Amphius, fears “the strong ring of the proud Trojans” around the body.
---

Telamonian Ajax kills Amphius and runs up to strip him, but the Trojans shower spears on him. “And he feared the strong ring of the proud Trojans around the body, who stood over it, many and brave, holding their spears” (5.623–624; [[fighting-over-the-body]], [[krateros]], [[brave]]). The Trojans' standing epithet, “lordly, proud,” comes as they win a point: the greater Ajax gives ground, reeling (5.626; [[telamonian]]).
