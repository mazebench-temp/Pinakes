---
summary: Book 5 names one Maeonian, Borus, whose son Phaestus, from Tarne, Idomeneus kills (5.43–44).
---

Idomeneus kills “Phaestus, son of the Maeonian Borus, who had come from deep-soiled Tarne” (5.43–44). Μῄων, “a Maeonian”, is the singular of Μῄονες. Tarne is not in the Catalogue, which places the Maeonians beneath Mount Tmolus (2.864–866). See [[phaestus-son-of-borus]], [[borus]] and [[tarne]].
