summary: Pylaemenes, leader of the Paphlagonians, is “the equal of Ares” as Menelaus and Antilochus kill him, on the day Ares himself fights for the Trojans.
grc: [ἀτάλαντον Ἄρηϊ]
---

“There they killed Pylaemenes, the equal of Ares, leader of the great-hearted shield-bearing Paphlagonians” (5.576–577; [[pylaemenes]], [[paphlagonians]]). The Catalogue's formula is given to a Trojan ally at his death, on the day when the war god is on the field in person, leading the Trojans with Hector (5.590–595). Pylaemenes is killed here and appears alive in Book 13, following the body of his son Harpalion in tears (Il. 13.643–644, 13.656–659), one of the best-known inconsistencies of the poem.
