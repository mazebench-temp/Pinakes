---
summary: The shining things of Book 5 are a fire, a robe, a shield and a spear: the fire in which Pandarus would burn his bow, Aphrodite's robe, Diomedes' shield under Apollo's blows, and the spear of Telamonian Ajax.
grc: [φαεινοῦ, φαεινὴν]
en: [blazing]
---

Pandarus, cursing his bow, swears that if he gets home he will put it “in the blazing fire,” broken in pieces (φαεινῷ ἐν πυρί, 5.215; [[bow]]). Aphrodite spreads before Aeneas “a fold of her shining robe” as a shelter from missiles (5.315; [[robe]], [[of-the-swift-horses]]). When Diomedes attacks Aeneas under Apollo's protection, “three times Apollo battered back his shining shield” (5.437; [[three-times-and-a-fourth]]). Ajax, pitying Menesthes and Anchialus as they fall, “threw his shining spear” and killed Amphius (5.610–612; [[amphius-son-of-selagus]]).
