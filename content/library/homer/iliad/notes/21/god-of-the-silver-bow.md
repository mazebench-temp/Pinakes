---
summary: The river addresses Apollo: “Ah, god of the silver bow, child of Zeus” (21.229).
grc: [ἀργυρότοξε]
---

The full vocative ἀργυρότοξε is new to the entry. Walter Leaf defends the river's reproach, condemned because Apollo is not there to hear it, as an angry partisan's outburst (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.229; see the translation's note on 21.229–232 and [[son-of-zeus]]). See [[apollo]] and [[scamander]].
