---
summary: Priam orders the gatekeepers to hold the gates open, and when the army is inside, to “put the closely fitted doors back in their place again” (21.535).
---

The doors are σανίδες, the leaves of the gate, as in the Odyssey's palace (*Odyssey* 2.344). ἐπανθέμεναι, “put back,” is Aristarchus' reading; some city texts had ἐπʼ ἂψ θέμεναι (Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.535; see the translation's note on 21.535).
