---
summary: Agenor's heart is “valiant” as he waits for Achilles (21.571–572), and he tells Achilles that there are many “valiant men” within the city (21.586).
grc: ["ἄλκιμον ὁρμᾶτο"]
---

The first use stands across the verse-end, ἐν δέ οἱ ἦτορ / ἄλκιμον, “and his valiant heart / within him was eager to make war and to fight” (21.571–572); the phrase claimed is the one on the second verse. The second is Agenor's challenge: “For there are many of us within it, valiant men, / who in front of our dear parents and wives and sons / guard Ilios” (21.586–588). See [[agenor]].
