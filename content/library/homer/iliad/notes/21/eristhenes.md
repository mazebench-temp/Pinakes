---
summary: Achilles over the dead Asteropaeus: “It is hard for you to strive with the children of the mighty son of Cronus, / even though you are sprung from a river” (21.184–185).
---

The epithet comes, as always, with Zeus as father: here of the children of Zeus, against whom a river's son cannot stand (21.184–191; [[son-of-cronus]], [[zeus-is-mightier-than-rivers]]). παισίν, “children,” is plural because the statement is general (Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.185; see the translation's note on 21.184–191). See [[zeus]] and [[asteropaeus]].
