---
summary: Achilles tells the dead Lycaon that the Trojans will perish “until we reach the city of sacred Ilios” (21.128); and Apollo goes “into sacred Ilios,” anxious for its wall (21.515).
---

In the first verse Walter Leaf notes that Ἰλίου neglects the old digamma of the name, a ground for suspecting the verse (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.128). In the second Apollo goes to guard the city “lest the Danaans sack it against fate that day” (21.515–517; [[the-walls-of-troy]], [[apollo]]). See [[troy]].
