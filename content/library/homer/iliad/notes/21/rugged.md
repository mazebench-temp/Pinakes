---
summary: Athena takes up a stone “that lay on the plain, black and jagged and great” (21.404), and fells Ares with it.
---

The two verses are those of the stone Hector threw at Ajax (21.403–404 = 7.264–265; [[strong-hand]]). The stone was “the boundary stone of a field,” set there by men of former times (21.405; [[boundary-stones]], [[stone-throwing]]). See [[athena]] and [[ares]].
