summary: Altes “rules over the war-loving Leleges” (21.86), Peleus “is lord over many Myrmidons” (21.188), and Scamander fears for “the great city of lord Priam” (21.309); the god is “far-working lord Apollo” (21.461).
---

ἀνάσσει is present, as the manuscripts have it; the variant ἄνασσε may be a correction to agree with the sack of Pedasus told in Book 20 (20.92; Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.86; see the translation's note on 21.86–87 and [[altes]]). Laomedon, the gods' master for a year, is called by another word (21.445; see [[semaino]]).
