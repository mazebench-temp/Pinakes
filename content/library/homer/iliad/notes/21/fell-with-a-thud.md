---
summary: Ares falls under Athena's stone, covers seven plethra, grimes his hair with dust, “and his armor clattered about him” (21.407–408).
grc: ["τεύχεά τʼ ἀμφαράβησε"]
en: [his armor clattered about him]
---

The second half of the formula, ἀράβησε δὲ τεύχεʼ ἐπʼ αὐτῷ, is given to a god, with the compound ἀμφαράβησε, found only here. The god's fall is measured as no man's is: “He covered seven plethra as he fell” (21.407; see the translation's note on 21.407 and [[loosened-his-limbs]]). See [[ares]] and [[athena]].
