---
summary: The gods' quarrels open with abuse three times: Ares “spoke abusive words” to Athena (21.393), Artemis to Apollo (21.471), and Hera rebuked Artemis “with abusive words” (21.480).
grc: [ὀνείδειον]
---

The formulas are those of men's quarrels, and 21.480 is Thersites' line adapted (2.277); Walter Leaf notes that it was unknown to Eustathius and interpolated after Aristonicus, who understood the verb of speaking from 21.478 (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.480; see the translation's note on 21.480). Each abusive speech ends in blows except Artemis', which Apollo answers with silence (21.478; see [[words-and-violence]] and [[neikos]]).
