summary: Lycaon tells Achilles that his mother bore two sons to Priam, “and you will cut the throats of both” (21.89), and Achilles tells the dead Lycaon that his mother will not lament him (21.123–124). Agenor fights for the city “in front of our dear parents and wives and sons” (21.587).
refs: [21.84-91, 21.123-124, 21.587]
---

The brother, Polydorus, died in Book 20 (20.407–418). Priam will speak of both sons from the wall, not knowing that they are dead: if they are alive he will ransom them, and if they are in the house of Hades it is grief for him and their mother (22.46–53; see [[laothoe]], [[polydorus-son-of-priam]] and [[lycaon-son-of-priam]]).
