summary: Scamander calls the Simoeis to his aid, “Dear brother, let us both together check the strength of this man” (21.308). Apollo will not fight Poseidon, “his father's brother” (21.469), and “his sister” Artemis rebukes him for it (21.470).
grc: [πατροκασιγνήτοιο]
---

The rivers are brothers as children of Oceanus, “from whom all rivers and all the sea / and all springs and deep wells flow” (21.196–197; see [[oceanus]] and [[rivers]]). πατροκασίγνητος, “father's brother”, is found only here in the Iliad; the Odyssey uses it of Poseidon as the uncle of Athena, whom she will not oppose (*Odyssey* 6.329–330, 13.341–343; see [[aidos]]).
