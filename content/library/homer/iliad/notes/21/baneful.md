---
summary: Priam fears “that this baneful man may leap inside the wall” (21.536).
grc: ["οὖλος ἀνὴρ"]
---

οὖλος is Ares' epithet in Book 5 (οὖλος Ἄρης, 5.461, 5.717), and Priam gives it to the man whom the Trojans flee as they would the god of war. The phrase οὖλος ἀνήρ is found only here; the translation renders it “this baneful man” (see its table of key words). See [[achilles]] and [[priam]].
