---
summary: “on it blood-stained Ares stabbed her with his long spear” (21.402), the last of the epithet's four uses in Homer.
---

The other three are in Book 5, where Athena set Diomedes on to wound Ares (5.31, 5.455, 5.844). Ares recalls that wound as he attacks her (21.396–398), and the same goddess now fells him with a stone (21.403–406; [[diomedes-wounds-ares]], [[long-spears]], [[furious]]). See [[ares]].
