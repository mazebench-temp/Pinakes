summary: Lycaon: “and to be short-lived my mother / bore me, Laothoe, daughter of the old man Altes” (21.84–85).
---

The word with which Achilles described himself to his mother in Book 1, “Mother, since you bore me to be short-lived” (1.352), is Lycaon's for himself. Achilles answers that he too must die (21.108–113; [[mighty-fate]], [[the-short-life-of-achilles]]). See [[lycaon-son-of-priam]].
