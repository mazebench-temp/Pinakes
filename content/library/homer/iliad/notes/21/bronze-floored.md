---
summary: Poseidon would not go back “to Olympus, to the bronze-floored house of Zeus” without a fight (21.438); and Artemis goes there in tears (21.505).
---

The two verses end alike, Διὸς ποτὶ χαλκοβατὲς δῶ, as Thetis' promise to go to Zeus in Book 1 (1.426). The god who would not go back unfought does not fight, and the goddess who fought goes back beaten (21.436–440, 21.505–513). See [[olympus]] and [[zeus]].
