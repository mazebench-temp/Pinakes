---
summary: Athena, over the fallen Ares and Aphrodite: “Would that all who help the Trojans were now such as these / … so bold and hardy, as Aphrodite was” (21.428–430).
grc: [τλήμονες]
en: [hardy]
---

τλήμονες, “hardy,” is said in mockery of the goddess who has just been struck down (see the translation's table of key words and its note on 10.231, where τλήμων is “enduring” and also “daring, hardy”). See [[athena]], [[aphrodite]] and [[trojan-allies]].
