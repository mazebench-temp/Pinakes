---
summary: Apollo will not fight Poseidon “for the sake of mortals, / wretched creatures” (21.463–464).
grc: [δειλῶν]
---

The word of pity with which mortals address a man near death is Apollo's word for all mortals, who blaze with life and waste away like leaves (21.463–466; [[who-eat-the-fruit-of-the-field]], [[the-generations-of-leaves]]). See [[apollo]] and [[mortal-men]].
