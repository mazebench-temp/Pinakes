---
summary: Achilles drives the Trojans to the city, “and a mighty frenzy / held his heart unceasingly” (21.542–543).
---

The mighty frenzy is the one that Odysseus saw in Hector in Book 9, κρατερὴ δέ ἑ λύσσα δέδυκεν, “a mighty frenzy has entered into him” (9.239); now it holds Achilles (see [[krateros]] and [[the-aristeia]]).
