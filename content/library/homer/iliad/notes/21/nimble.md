---
summary: The river's wave overtakes Achilles, “nimble though he was” (21.264); and Achilles remembers his mother's word that he would perish “by the swift arrows of Apollo” (21.278).
en: [swift]
---

The man of the swift feet is outrun by the water: “so the wave of the stream kept overtaking Achilles, / nimble though he was; for the gods are stronger than men” (21.263–264; [[the-channel-and-the-gardener]]). The second use gives the epithet to Apollo's arrows, which will kill Achilles “under the wall of the armored Trojans” (21.277–278; [[death-of-achilles-foretold]], [[close-armored]]). See [[achilles]].
