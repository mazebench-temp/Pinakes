---
summary: The river ends his plea to Achilles: “But come, leave off; I am filled with amazement, leader of the people” (21.221).
---

ὄρχαμε λαῶν is a title of address for leaders: Agamemnon (14.102), Menelaus (17.12), and Patroclus in Briseis' lament (19.289). The river gives it to Achilles as he asks him to stop killing in his streams (21.214–221). See [[scamander]] and [[achilles]].
