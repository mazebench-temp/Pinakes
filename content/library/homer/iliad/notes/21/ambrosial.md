---
summary: Artemis sits weeping on her father's knees, “and the ambrosial robe trembled around her” (21.507).
---

The robe is ἑανός, the goddess's garment (see the entry [[robe]]). The scene follows the wounding of Aphrodite in Book 5, where Diomedes' spear went through the ambrosial robe that the Graces made (5.338; [[heavenly-gods]]). See [[artemis]] and [[zeus]].
