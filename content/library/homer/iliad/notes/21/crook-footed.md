---
summary: Hera calls her son to fight the river: “Rise, crook-footed one, my child” (21.331).
grc: [κυλλοπόδιον]
---

The vocative κυλλοπόδιον is found only here. The A scholia record that the verse was athetized, since Hera should not name her son's lameness when she asks his help; Walter Leaf calls the verse indispensable and gives Cobet's explanation of the scholion (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.331; see the translation's note on 21.331). See [[hephaestus]] and [[hera]].
