---
summary: Artemis taunts Apollo for carrying “a bow as empty as the wind” (21.474); Hera takes the bow from Artemis' shoulders and beats her about the ears with it (21.489–492), and Leto gathers up “the curved bow” and the arrows (21.502–504).
---

καμπύλα τόξα, “the curved bow”: Walter Leaf notes that the epithet belongs to the bow, though τόξα here plainly means the arrows, a standing epithet used mechanically (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.502; see the translation's note on 21.502–503). The archer goddess is beaten with her own weapon (see [[artemis]], [[hera]], [[leto]] and [[the-battle-of-the-gods]]).
