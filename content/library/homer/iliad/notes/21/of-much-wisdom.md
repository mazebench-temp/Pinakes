---
summary: The river is worn down by “the blast of the strength / of Hephaestus, of much wisdom” (21.366–367).
grc: [πολύφρονος]
---

The genitive πολύφρονος is the form of the Odyssey's two uses for Hephaestus (*Odyssey* 8.297, 8.327). Walter Leaf takes βίηφι as a genitive, “the breath of Hephaestus' might” (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.367). See [[hephaestus]] and [[of-many-wiles]].
