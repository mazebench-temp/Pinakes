---
summary: Hera to Artemis: “How do you now mean, you shameless dog, to stand / against me?” (21.481–482).
---

The insult is the one Iris spoke to Athena in Book 8, when she had given her Zeus' message: “But you are most dread, you shameless dog” (8.423; Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.481). Hera's speech goes on to the bow and the lion (21.482–488; [[bow-bearer]], [[a-lion-to-women]]). See [[hera]] and [[artemis]].
