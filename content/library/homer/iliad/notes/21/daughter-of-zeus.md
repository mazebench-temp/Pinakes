---
summary: “and Aphrodite, daughter of Zeus, took him by the hand and led him away” (21.416), when Athena has felled Ares.
---

The scholia report that the Separatists, who gave the Iliad and the Odyssey to different poets, argued from this verse: the Iliad makes Aphrodite the companion of Ares openly, the Odyssey the wife of Hephaestus, whose bed Ares shames in secret (Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.416; see the translation's note on 21.416).
