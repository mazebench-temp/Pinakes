---
summary: Lycaon: “and now again into your hands / deadly fate has put me” (21.82–83).
---

μοῖρʼ ὀλοή is the fate that the dying Patroclus names with Apollo as his killer, “But deadly fate and the son of Leto have killed me” (16.849). Lycaon's fate brings him back to Achilles on the twelfth day after his return from Lemnos (21.80–83; [[lycaon-son-of-priam]], [[moira]]). See [[father-zeus]].
