---
summary: Athena, having boasted over Ares, “turned her shining eyes away” (21.415).
---

The half-verse πάλιν τρέπεν ὄσσε φαεινώ is the one with which Zeus turned his eyes away from the battle at the beginning of Book 13 (13.3). Aphrodite comes at once to lead Ares away (21.416). See [[athena]].
