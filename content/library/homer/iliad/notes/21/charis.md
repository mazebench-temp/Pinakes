---
summary: Poseidon reproaches Apollo for helping the Trojans after Laomedon cheated them both of their wage: “It is to his people that you now do a favor” (21.458).
---

A favor answers a favor, and Laomedon gave none: he “robbed us by force of our whole wage” and threatened the two gods (21.451–455). Poseidon's argument turns the exchange against the Trojans (see [[reciprocity-with-the-gods]] and [[poseidon-and-apollo-serve-laomedon]]).
