---
summary: Over the dead Lycaon Achilles boasts that no mother will mourn him: “nor will your mother / lay you on a bier and lament for you, but Scamander / will carry you, eddying, into the broad bosom of the sea” (21.123–125).
grc: [γοήσεται]
---

To lie unlamented is part of the threat, and Achilles will make it again over Hector, in the same words: his mother will not lay him on a bier and lament him (22.352–353). See [[lycaon-son-of-priam]], [[laothoe]] and [[the-death-of-lycaon]].
