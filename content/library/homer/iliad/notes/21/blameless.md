---
summary: Apollo sets on Agenor, “a man, the son of Antenor, noble and strong” (21.546).
---

ἀμύμονά τε κρατερόν τε is the half-verse with which Athena found Pandarus (4.89) and with which Thetis spoke of her son (18.55). Walter Leaf compares the shape of the verse with 4.194, φῶτʼ Ἀσκληπιοῦ υἱόν (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.544). See [[agenor]], [[son-of-antenor]] and [[krateros]].
