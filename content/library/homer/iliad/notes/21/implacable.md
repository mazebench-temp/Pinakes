---
summary: Lycaon ends his plea, “but the voice he heard would not be soothed” (21.98).
---

The half-verse is the end of the sons of Antimachus' plea to Agamemnon, “but the voice they heard would not be soothed” (11.137). There the suppliants spoke “with soothing words” (11.137); here Lycaon begs “with words” (21.98), and the answer is the same refusal (21.99–113; [[supplication]], [[soothing-words]]). See [[lycaon-son-of-priam]] and [[achilles]].
