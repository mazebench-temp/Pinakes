---
summary: “Meanwhile the son of Peleus, holding his long-shadowed spear, / leapt at Asteropaeus, eager to kill him” (21.139–140).
---

The spear is the Pelian ash that Achilles raises a few verses later (21.161–162; [[the-pelian-ash]]). When he throws it, the verse begins as the one in which he threw his long-shadowed spear at Aeneas, δεύτερος αὖτʼ Ἀχιλεύς (21.169, as 20.273), with another epithet, “his straight-flying ash” ([[straight-flying]]). See [[asteropaeus]] and [[spear]].
