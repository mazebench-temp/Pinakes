---
summary: Achilles tells the Trojans that the river they have honored, to whom they have “thrown single-hoofed horses alive into its eddies,” will not save them (21.132); and he kills “the Trojans themselves and their single-hoofed horses alike” (21.521).
---

The sacrifice of live horses to a river has no parallel in Homer (Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.131; see the translation's note on 21.130–135 and [[rivers]]). See [[horses]] and [[sacrifice]].
