---
summary: The Paeonians flee “when they saw their best man in the fierce battle” fall (21.207). Achilles in the river wishes that Hector had killed him, “the best man bred here” (21.279), and Athena, standing over Ares, tells him he has not yet realized “how much stronger / I claim to be than you” (21.410–411).
---

Achilles wants a death that matches him, at the hands of the best of the enemy: “then a brave man would have been the killer, and he would have slain a brave man” (21.280; see [[the-short-life-of-achilles]]). The best of the Paeonians is Asteropaeus, and the best of the Trojans is Hector (see [[asteropaeus]] and [[hector]]).
