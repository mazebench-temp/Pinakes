---
summary: Achilles drives his sword into Lycaon beside the neck, “and all the way in / the two-edged sword went” (21.117–118).
---

The sword is drawn as Lycaon sits with his hands stretched out (21.115–116), and the two-edged blade goes in to the hilt (see the entry, [[sword]] and [[the-death-of-lycaon]]).
