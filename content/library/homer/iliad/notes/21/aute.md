---
summary: Scamander “called to Simoeis with a shout” (21.307), Hera “gave a great shout, terribly afraid for Achilles” (21.328), and Agenor “gave a great shout” before his throw (21.582).
---

Hera's shout sends Hephaestus against the river, and Agenor's challenge is the last speech of a Trojan to Achilles before Apollo takes his place (21.583–589; see [[hera]] and [[agenor]]).
