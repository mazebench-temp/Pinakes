---
summary: After Asteropaeus' death Achilles goes “after the Paeonians who fight from chariots,” still fleeing along the river (21.205).
---

The phrase is the one with which Pyraechmes' Paeonians were named in Book 16 (16.287). The Paeonians have three characters in the poem: archers “with their curved bows” in the Catalogue (2.848), spearmen “with their long spears” in Asteropaeus' mouth (21.155; [[long-spears]]), and chariot fighters here. Seven of them die by name in the next two verses (21.209–210; [[androktasia]]). See [[paeonians]].
