summary: Artemis is “Artemis of the wild” as she rebukes Apollo (21.471); and Hera tells her that it is better “to slay wild beasts on the mountains / and wild deer than to fight in force with those stronger than you” (21.485–486).
grc: [ἀγροτέρη, ἀγροτέρας]
---

The title of Artemis and the epithet of hunted beasts meet in the same scene: the goddess of the wild is sent back to the wild deer (21.470–471, 21.485–486; [[queenly]], [[a-lion-to-women]]). See [[artemis]] and [[hera]].
