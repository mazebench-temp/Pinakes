---
summary: Achilles takes twelve young men alive out of the river “as the penalty for Patroclus, son of Menoetius, who had died” (21.28).
---

The twelve were promised at the end of Book 18, “twelve / splendid children of the Trojans” whom Achilles vowed to kill at Patroclus' pyre (18.336–337), and they die there in Book 23 (23.175–176; see the translation's note on 21.28). ποινή, “the penalty,” is the word's fixed rendering ([[poine]]). See [[patroclus]], [[human-sacrifice]] and [[well-cut]].
