summary: Achilles meets Lycaon, “a son of Priam, descendant of Dardanus,” fleeing out of the river (21.34); and Lycaon's plea is closed as “the shining son of Priam” spoke (21.97).
grc: ["υἷι Πριάμοιο"]
refs: [21.97]
---

Lycaon is Priam's son by Laothoe, daughter of Altes, and the full brother of Polydorus, whom Achilles killed in Book 20 (21.84–91; [[laothoe]], [[altes]], [[polydorus-son-of-priam]]). Achilles answers him that no Trojan shall escape death, “and above all of the sons of Priam” (21.105; [[sons-of-priam]]). The phrase υἷι Πριάμοιο also catches Polites, in whose likeness Iris speaks in Book 2 (2.791). See [[lycaon-son-of-priam]], [[phaidimos]] and [[priam]].
