---
summary: “But he, sprung from Zeus, left his spear there on the bank” (21.17), as Achilles leaps into the river with his sword.
---

The epithet stands with the article, αὐτὰρ ὃ διογενής, a use Walter Leaf calls very rare in Homer (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.17). The spear left “leaning against the tamarisks” (21.18) is in Achilles' hand again when Lycaon comes (21.67; see the translation's note on 21.67–70). See [[achilles]].
