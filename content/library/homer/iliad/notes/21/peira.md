---
summary: Achilles will not stop until he has “made trial of Hector / face to face” (21.225–226); Agenor “would not flee before he had made trial of Achilles” (21.580); and Poseidon reproaches Apollo, “you do not try with us / to bring it about that the overbearing Trojans perish” (21.458–459).
grc: [πειρᾷ, πειρήσαιτʼ]
---

πειρηθῆναι ἀντιβίην, “make trial face to face”, is the phrase of 5.220 (Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.225), and Leaf notes that πειρήσαιτο is the only optative after πρίν in Homer, apart from 9.489 (on 21.580; see [[agenor]]).
