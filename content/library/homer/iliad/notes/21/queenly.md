summary: Artemis rebukes Apollo: “But his sister rebuked him harshly, the mistress of wild beasts, / Artemis of the wild” (21.470–471).
en: [mistress]
---

πότνια θηρῶν, “mistress of wild beasts,” is found only here in Homer (see the translation's table of formulas). The second verse was athetized as superfluous after it: who is the hunting goddess if not Artemis? Walter Leaf thinks it may be a gloss, but harmless (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.471; see the translation's note on 21.471). Ida is μήτηρ θηρῶν, “mother of wild beasts” ([[mother-of-wild-beasts]]); Artemis is their mistress. See [[artemis]] and [[wild]].
