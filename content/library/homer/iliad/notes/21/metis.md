---
summary: The fish in the burning river are “distressed by the breath of Hephaestus of many wiles” (21.355): the epithet of Odysseus is given to the smith.
grc: [πολυμήτιος]
---

Twelve lines later the same blast is “the strength / of Hephaestus, of much wisdom” (21.366–367), and the translation keeps the two epithets apart (see the translation's row for πολύφρων, [[of-many-wiles]], [[of-much-wisdom]] and [[hephaestus]]).
