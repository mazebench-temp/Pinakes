---
summary: Achilles lifts his long spear over Lycaon (21.67); Asteropaeus leads “the Paeonian men with their long spears” (21.155); and Ares stabs at Athena's aegis “with his long spear” (21.402).
grc: [δολιχεγχέας]
---

δολιχεγχέας, “with their long spears,” is found only here. In the Catalogue the Paeonians are archers, “the Paeonians with their curved bows” (2.848), and Asteropaeus is not among their leaders (see the translation's note on 21.154–158); Walter Leaf adds that the verse-end δολίχʼ ἔγχεα χερσὶν ἔχοντες is used of the Thracians (4.533; *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.154). See [[paeonians]], [[asteropaeus]] and [[spear]].
