---
summary: The river swears, as Hera and Athena had sworn, “never to ward off the evil day from the Trojans” (21.374).
---

The three verses of the oath, 21.374–376, are the three in which Hera told Poseidon of her oath and Athena's in Book 20 (20.315–317; Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.374). The river swears the two goddesses' oath against Troy as the price of his release (21.369–376; [[ravening]], [[warlike]], [[oath]]). See [[scamander]] and [[hera]].
