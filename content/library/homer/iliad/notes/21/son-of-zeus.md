---
summary: The river reproaches Apollo: “Ah, god of the silver bow, child of Zeus, you have not kept / the plans of the son of Cronus” (21.229–230).
grc: ["ἀργυρότοξε Διὸς τέκος"]
en: [child of Zeus]
---

Διὸς τέκος is everywhere else in Homer Athena's title, usually in the address “child of aegis-bearing Zeus” (1.202, 5.714, 21.420; [[child-of-aegis-bearing-zeus]]). The river gives it to Apollo, with his own epithet of the silver bow (21.229; [[god-of-the-silver-bow]], [[o-popoi]]). See [[apollo]] and [[scamander]].
