---
summary: Hera “rebuked her of the showering arrows with abusive words” (21.480) before beating Artemis with her own bow.
grc: [ἰοχέαιραν]
---

The verse is unknown to Eustathius, and Walter Leaf takes it as a later addition, adapted from Thersites' verse “to rail at the kings with abusive words” (2.277); Aristonicus had understood προσέφη from 21.478 (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.480; see the translation's note on 21.480). The archer goddess's arrows fall out as she is beaten (21.492). See [[artemis]], [[hera]] and [[bow-bearer]].
