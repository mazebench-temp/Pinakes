---
summary: “Then the well-crowned, the clamorous goddess spoke to him in turn” (21.511): Artemis, unnamed, answers Zeus.
---

κελαδεινή stands for Artemis without her name, as at 16.183 (Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.511), and with [[well-crowned]] it fills the verse. See [[artemis]] and [[answering-him]].
