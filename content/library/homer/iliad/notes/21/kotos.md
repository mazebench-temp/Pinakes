---
summary: Poseidon and Apollo left Laomedon's service unpaid: “And we two went back with resentful hearts” (21.456).
grc: [κεκοτηότι]
---

The grudge is still held. Poseidon wants Laomedon's people to perish “utterly and wretchedly, with their children and their modest wives” (21.460; see [[cholos]] and [[poseidon-and-apollo-serve-laomedon]]).
