summary: Artemis “rebuked him harshly” when Apollo would not fight (21.470), Hera “rebuked her” in turn (21.480), and Artemis tells her father that Hera is the one “from whom strife and quarrel have fastened upon the immortals” (21.513).
---

21.480 adapts Thersites' line, “to rail at the kings with abusive words” (2.277; see [[oneidos]], [[eris]] and [[words-and-violence]]).
