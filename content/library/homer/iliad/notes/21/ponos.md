---
summary: Twice the river ponders how “he might stop from his toil / brilliant Achilles” (21.137–138, 21.249–250), and in the simile of the smoke “Achilles brought toil and sorrows on the Trojans” (21.525).
---

Walter Leaf takes πόνοιο of martial exploits, as often, but thinks φόνοιο, “slaughter”, rather more suitable (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.137; see the translation's note on 21.137 and [[smoke-from-a-burning-city]]).
