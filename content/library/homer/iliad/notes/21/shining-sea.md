---
summary: The river cannot “pour my stream into the shining sea,” so choked is he with the dead (21.219).
---

The shining sea of the river's complaint stands at the end of the course that the corpses block (21.218–220; [[lovely]]). δῖαν at 21.43 is “shining Arisbe,” a town, and is excepted. See [[scamander]] and [[sea]].
