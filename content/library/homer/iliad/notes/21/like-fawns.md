---
summary: Achilles leads the twelve young men out of the river “dazed, like fawns” (21.29), the words of Agamemnon's rebuke to the Argives (4.243).
grc: ["τεθηπότας ἠΰτε νεβρούς"]
en: ["dazed, like fawns"]
---

τεθηπότας ἠΰτε νεβρούς is the accusative of Agamemnon's τεθηπότες ἠΰτε νεβροί (4.243). There the fawns stand still because they have run until they can run no more; here they are captives, taken alive “as the penalty for Patroclus” and led out to be killed at his pyre (21.27–32; see [[human-sacrifice]] and [[poine]]).
