summary: “The white-armed goddess Hera” hears the river's oath and stops Hephaestus (21.377), sees Aphrodite leading Ares away and sends Athena after her (21.418), and smiles at Athena's boast (21.434); Artemis names “white-armed Hera” to Zeus as the goddess who beat her (21.512).
---

“So she spoke, and the white-armed goddess Hera smiled” (21.434) is the verse with which Hera smiled at Hephaestus' cup in Book 1 (1.595); Walter Leaf thinks it interpolated from there (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.434; see the translation's note on 21.434).
