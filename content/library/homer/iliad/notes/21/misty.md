---
summary: Achilles, seeing Lycaon alive, imagines that the Trojans he has killed “will rise up again from under the misty darkness” (21.56).
grc: [ἠερόεντος]
---

ζόφος ἠερόεις is the misty darkness of the dead, the share that fell to Hades when the world was divided by lot (15.191; Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.54). See [[hades]] and [[lycaon-son-of-priam]].
