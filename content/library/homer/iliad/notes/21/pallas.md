---
summary: Poseidon tells Achilles that he and “Pallas Athena” have come to help him (21.290); and “Pallas Athena laughed” over the fallen Ares (21.408).
---

The first verse, “with the consent of Zeus, I and Pallas Athena” (21.290), was athetized in antiquity, since a god in a man's likeness should not name himself; Walter Leaf answers that Achilles must know that the help is divine, and that the disguise only spares mortal eyes (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.290; see the translation's note on 21.290).
