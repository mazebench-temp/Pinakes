---
summary: Lemnos did not hold Lycaon, “the deep of the gray sea, which holds back many against their will” (21.59).
---

πόντος ἁλὸς πολιῆς, “the deep of the gray sea” (see the translation's note on 21.59). The sea “holds back” (ἐρύκει) as the earth does in the verse that answers it: Achilles will see whether Lycaon comes back from below as well, or whether “the life-giving earth will hold him back, which holds back even the strong” (21.62–63; [[life-giving-earth]]). See [[egatheos]], [[lemnos]] and [[sea]].
