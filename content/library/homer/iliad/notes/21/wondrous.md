---
summary: “So she spoke, and Hephaestus made ready a wondrous fire” (21.342); and “So she spoke, and Hephaestus quenched the wondrous fire” (21.381).
---

The two verses, alike but for the verb, open and close the fire against the river (21.342–382). In Book 20 Achilles was likened to a wondrous fire raging through the valleys (20.490); here the fire is Hephaestus' own (see the Book 20 note and [[tireless]]). See [[hephaestus]] and [[scamander]].
