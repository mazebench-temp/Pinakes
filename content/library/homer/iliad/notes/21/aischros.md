---
summary: Poseidon urges Apollo to fight: “it would be the more shameful if without a fight / we went back to Olympus, to the bronze-floored house of Zeus” (21.437–438).
---

It is the warrior's argument, as Odysseus put it to the army in Book 2, “it is shameful to stay so long and go home empty-handed” (2.298). Apollo answers with another shame, the shame of fighting his father's brother (21.468–469; see [[aidos]] and [[the-gods-mirror-the-mortals]]).
