---
summary: Lycaon meets Achilles “naked, without helmet or shield, and he had no spear either”: he had flung them all to the ground as he fled out of the river (21.50–52).
---

γυμνός, “naked”, is unarmed, as the rest of the verse explains (see [[lycaon-son-of-priam]] and [[the-death-of-lycaon]]).
