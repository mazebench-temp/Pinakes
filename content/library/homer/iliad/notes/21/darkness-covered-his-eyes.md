---
summary: Achilles' sword strikes Asteropaeus beside the navel, “and all / his bowels poured out on the ground, and darkness covered his eyes / as he gasped” (21.180–182).
---

The verses are those of the death of Diores in the first battle, “and all / his bowels poured out on the ground, and darkness covered his eyes” (4.525–526; Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.180). Achilles springs on his chest, strips him, and speaks his boast (21.182–199; [[euchomai]], [[zeus-is-mightier-than-rivers]]). See [[asteropaeus]] and [[androktasia]].
