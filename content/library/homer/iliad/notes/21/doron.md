---
summary: Asteropaeus' spear cannot pierce Achilles' shield, “for the gold held it back, the gifts of the god” (21.165), and Agenor's javelin rings off his greave, “for the gifts of the god held it back” (21.594).
---

21.165 is the verse of 20.268, and Walter Leaf thinks 21.594 added to recall the god's making of the arms, since A omits it (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.594; see the translation's notes on 21.165 and 21.594). The god is Hephaestus (see [[the-shield-of-achilles]] and [[gifts-of-the-gods]]).
