summary: Achilles remembers his mother's word that he would die “under the wall of the armored Trojans” (21.277), and Athena wishes that all the Trojans' helpers were as weak as Aphrodite “whenever they fight with the armored Argives” (21.429).
grc: [θωρηκτάων, θωρηκτῇσιν]
en: [armored]
---

The book has the epithet without πύκα, “closely,” and gives it to both armies: the Trojans under whose wall Achilles must fall (21.277–278; [[death-of-achilles-foretold]]), and the Argives against whom the Trojans' allies fight (21.428–429). See [[trojans]] and [[argives]].
