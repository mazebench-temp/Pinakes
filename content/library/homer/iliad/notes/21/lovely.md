---
summary: The river complains to Achilles: “for my lovely streams are full of corpses” (21.218).
grc: [ἐρατεινὰ]
---

The epithet that names pleasant places and things is set beside the bodies that choke the river: “for my lovely streams are full of corpses, / and I can find no way to pour my stream into the shining sea, / choked with corpses” (21.218–220; [[shining-sea]]). See [[scamander]].
