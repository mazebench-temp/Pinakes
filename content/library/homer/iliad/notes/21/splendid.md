---
summary: Hephaestus' fire dries the plain, “and the bright water was checked” (21.345).
---

ἀγλαὸν ὕδωρ, “bright water,” is the spring at Aulis, “where bright water flowed” (2.307). Here the water is the flood that the river had spread over the plain, and fire stops it (21.300, 21.343–345). See [[hephaestus]] and [[scamander]].
