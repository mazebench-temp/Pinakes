summary: Hera rouses Athena against Aphrodite with the verse of Book 5: “Ah, child of aegis-bearing Zeus, Atrytone” (21.420).

The verse is 5.714, where Hera sent Athena against Ares (see [[o-popoi]] and [[child-of-aegis-bearing-zeus]]). Athena has just taken Ares' spear on the aegis itself (21.400–402; [[tasseled]]). See [[athena]] and [[hera]].
