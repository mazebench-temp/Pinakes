---
summary: Achilles springs away from the rising river “as far as the cast of a spear” (21.251), the half-verse that measured Apollo's causeway over the Achaean ditch (15.358).
grc: ["ὅσον τʼ ἐπὶ δουρὸς ἐρωή"]
en: [as far as the cast of a spear]
---

In Book 15 the measure was a path, “as far as the cast of a spear / goes, when a man throws it to test his strength” (15.358–359). Here it is a leap, and the next verse turns the leap into a simile: Achilles darts away “with the swoop of an eagle, the black hunter” (21.252; see [[like-an-eagle]] and [[achilles-and-the-river]]).
