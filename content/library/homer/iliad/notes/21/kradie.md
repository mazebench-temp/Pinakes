---
summary: Poseidon mocks Apollo, “Little fool, how senseless is the heart you have!” (21.441); Apollo “cast daring into” Agenor's heart (21.547), and Agenor's “heart heaved with many thoughts as he waited” (21.551).
---

πόρφυρε, “heaved” (21.551), is the verb of the heaving sea at 14.16 (see the translation's row for πορφύρω). Agenor's heart then speaks to him as Odysseus' did and Hector's will (21.562 = 11.407 = 22.122; see [[pondering-two-ways]], [[tharsos]] and [[noos]]).
