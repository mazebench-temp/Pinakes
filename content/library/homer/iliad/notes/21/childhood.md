---
summary: Achilles fears to die “like a swineherd boy / whom a torrent sweeps away as he crosses it in winter” (21.282–283). Artemis, beaten, runs to her father like a child: “the maiden sat in tears on her father's knees”, and he asks her, “Who of the heavenly gods has done such things to you, dear child” (21.506–509).
refs: [21.282-283, 21.505-513]
---

The gods' insult in the book is the child's word, νηπύτιε, “Little fool” (21.410, 21.441, 21.474; see [[nepios]], [[the-swineherd-boy]] and [[fathers-and-daughters]]).
