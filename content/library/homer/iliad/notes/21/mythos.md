summary: Poseidon “began to speak” to Achilles in the river (21.287); Ares and Artemis open their quarrels with “abusive words” (21.393, 21.471); and Apollo answers Poseidon, “you would not call me sound of mind” if he fought him for the sake of mortals (21.462).
grc: [μυθήσαιο]
en: [call]
---

τοῖσι, “among them”, comes from the formula (7.445), though Poseidon speaks to Achilles alone (Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.287; see the translation's note on 21.287 and [[oneidos]]).
