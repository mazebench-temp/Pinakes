---
summary: Achilles tells the Trojans that the river will not protect them, though they have long sacrificed many bulls to it and thrown single-hoofed horses alive into its eddies (21.130–132).
grc: [ἱερεύετε]
---

Bulls are a river's victims elsewhere too: Nestor's Pylians offered “a bull to Alpheus” (11.728). The living horses thrown into the water have no parallel in Homer (Leaf on 21.131; see the translation's note on 21.130–135, [[rivers]], [[scamander]] and [[horses-for-the-rivers]]).
