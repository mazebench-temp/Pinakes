---
summary: Lycaon was taken “from his father's orchard” (21.36) and came home “to his father's house” (21.44), and Achilles is “the son of a brave father” (21.109). Among the gods Zeus is the father: Achilles cries “Father Zeus” in the river (21.273), Artemis sits “on her father's knees” and calls him “father” (21.506, 21.512), and the gods sit down “beside the father, lord of the dark clouds” (21.520).
