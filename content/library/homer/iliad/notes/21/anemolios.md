---
summary: Artemis taunts Apollo for refusing to fight: “Little fool, why do you carry a bow as empty as the wind?” (21.474).
grc: [ἀνεμώλιον]
en: [as empty as the wind]
---

Walter Leaf takes ἀνεμώλιον αὔτως together, like μὰψ αὔτως, and compares Pandarus' complaint of his bow, “for it goes with me as empty wind” (5.216; *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.473). A few lines later Hera takes Artemis' own bow from her shoulders and beats her with it (21.489–492; see [[nepios]]).
