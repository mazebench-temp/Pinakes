---
summary: Hera tells Hephaestus not to let the river “turn you back with soothing words and threats” (21.339).
---

The couplet is Apollo's to Aeneas in Book 20, “and do not let him at all / turn you back with wretched words and threats” (20.108–109), with soothing words for wretched ones (Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.338). The river begs Hephaestus first and then Hera, and it is Hera who stops the fire (21.356–382). See [[hephaestus]] and [[hera]].
