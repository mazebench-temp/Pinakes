---
summary: Not even “lord Achelous” can match himself with Zeus (21.194); and “the powerful earth-shaker” challenges Apollo (21.435).
en: [lord]
---

κρείων is the title of a lord: Achelous, named only here in Homer, is the greatest of the rivers that Zeus outranks (21.194; see the translation's note on 21.194–197 and [[zeus-is-mightier-than-rivers]]), and κρείων ἐνοσίχθων is Poseidon's formula, here as he turns to Apollo to begin their fight (21.435–440; [[earth-shaker]], [[the-battle-of-the-gods]]). See [[poseidon]].
