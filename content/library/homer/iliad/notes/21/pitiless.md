---
summary: Achilles marvels that Lycaon “has come back, escaping the pitiless day” (21.57).
---

The pitiless day is the day of death, as in Book 11 and Book 17 (11.484, 17.511). Lycaon escaped it once, when Achilles sold him instead of killing him; he will not escape it twice (21.99–113; [[lycaon-son-of-priam]], [[supplication]]). See [[achilles]].
