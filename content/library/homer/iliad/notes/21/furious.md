summary: Athena strikes “furious Ares on the neck, and loosened his limbs” (21.406).

θοῦρος is Ares' epithet of Book 5, where he was wounded by Diomedes (5.30, 5.35, 5.355, 5.454, 5.830). He falls covering seven plethra and grimes his hair with dust (21.407; [[loosened-his-limbs]], [[stone-throwing]]). See [[ares]] and [[athena]].
