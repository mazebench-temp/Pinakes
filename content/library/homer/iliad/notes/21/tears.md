---
summary: Artemis, beaten by Hera with her own bow, flees “in tears, like a dove” (21.493), leaves her bow behind, and “the maiden sat in tears on her father's knees” (21.506).
---

Tears in the book are the goddess's alone. She is comforted as Aphrodite was by Dione in Book 5 (5.370–374), and Zeus laughs as he asks her who has hurt her (21.508–509; see [[laughter-of-the-gods]], [[fathers-and-daughters]] and [[the-dove-and-the-hawk]]).
