---
summary: “And the old man Priam stood on the divine tower” (21.526) and saw Achilles coming.
---

Walter Leaf takes θείου of a tower built by a god, Poseidon, and notes that θεῖος is not a mere synonym of ἱερός, the common epithet of citadels; compare the towers “built by the gods” (θεοδμήτων, 8.519) (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.526; see the translation's note on 21.526). Poseidon has just told Apollo that he built the wall (21.446–447). See [[the-tower-of-ilios]], [[priam]] and [[the-walls-of-troy]].
