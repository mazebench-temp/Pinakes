---
summary: Lycaon was ransomed from Lemnos by a guest-friend, “Eetion of Imbros” (21.42–43), and he claims a guest's standing with Achilles himself: “for with you first I tasted the grain of Demeter” (21.76).
---

Walter Leaf takes πρώτῳ, “first”, as first among the Greeks, which makes the bond closer, as when Odysseus tells Nausicaa that he came to her first (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.76; *Odyssey* 6.175–176). The claim does not hold: Achilles' “friend” is said to a man he kills (21.106; see [[eetion-of-imbros]], [[aidos]] and [[supplication]]).
