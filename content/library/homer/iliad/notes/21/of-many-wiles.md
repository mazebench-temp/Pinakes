---
summary: The eels and fishes suffer, “distressed by the breath of Hephaestus of many wiles” (21.355).
grc: [πολυμήτιος]
---

The genitive πολυμήτιος is the one place in Homer where πολύμητις is not Odysseus'. Twelve verses later Hephaestus is “of much wisdom” (21.367; [[of-much-wisdom]]); the translation keeps the two epithets apart (see its table of key words). See [[hephaestus]] and [[fish]].
