---
summary: Achilles' spear misses Asteropaeus and is driven “halfway into the bank” (21.172), and Asteropaeus tries to break “the ash spear of the grandson of Aeacus” (21.178).
---

μεσσοπαγές, “fixed halfway,” is the reading of the manuscripts; Aristarchus read μεσσοπαλές, which Walter Leaf finds unsatisfactory in either of its senses (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.172; see the translation's note on 21.172). The spear is the Pelian ash that only Achilles can wield (16.140–144; [[the-pelian-ash]]): Asteropaeus shakes it three times and cannot draw it out (21.174–177; [[three-times-and-a-fourth]]), and Achilles draws it out of the bank when Asteropaeus is dead (21.200). See [[grandson-of-aeacus]] and [[spear]].
