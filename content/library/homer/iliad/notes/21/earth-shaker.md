---
summary: Poseidon is the earth-shaker as he comes with Athena to the struggling Achilles and speaks to him (21.287), and as he challenges Apollo to fight (21.435).
---

“And among them Poseidon the earth-shaker began to speak” (21.287) is a verse of Book 7 (7.445). τοῖσι, “among them,” is taken over mechanically from that verse, Walter Leaf notes, since Poseidon speaks to Achilles alone (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.287; see the translation's note on 21.287). The second use pairs the epithet with κρείων: “And the powerful earth-shaker spoke to Apollo” (21.435; [[powerful]]). Leaf finds this bellicose Poseidon hardly the god of Book 20, who wished the gods to sit apart (on 21.436). See [[poseidon]], [[shaker-of-the-earth]] and [[the-battle-of-the-gods]].
