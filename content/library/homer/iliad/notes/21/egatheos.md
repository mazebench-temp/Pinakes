---
summary: Lemnos, where Achilles sold Lycaon, is “sacred Lemnos” twice, in Achilles' wonder at his return and in Lycaon's plea (21.58, 21.79).
grc: [ἠγαθέην]
---

Achilles marvels that Lycaon has come back, “though he was sold into sacred Lemnos; nor did it hold him, / the deep of the gray sea” (21.58–59; [[gray-sea]]); Lycaon reminds him that he was sold “into sacred Lemnos,” where he fetched him “the worth of a hundred oxen” (21.79). The form ἠγαθέην is new to the entry; the Odyssey gives it to Pylos (*Odyssey* 2.308). See [[lemnos]] and [[lycaon-son-of-priam]].
