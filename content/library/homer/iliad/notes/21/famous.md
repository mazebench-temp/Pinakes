summary: Poseidon bids Achilles pen the Trojans “within the glorious walls of Ilios” (21.295); Hera calls Hephaestus “my famous child” (21.379); and Priam urges on “the famous gatekeepers” (21.530).
grc: ["κλυτὰ τείχεα", ἀγακλειτοὺς]
---

κλυτὰ τείχεα (21.295) and ἀγακλειτούς (21.530) are new to the entry. The glorious walls are the walls that Priam's gatekeepers hold open for the fleeing army and close again behind it (21.530–537; [[the-walls-of-troy]]). See [[hephaestus]] and [[priam]].
