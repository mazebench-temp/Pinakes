---
summary: Poseidon reminds Apollo that they served Laomedon for a year “for a fixed wage; and he was our master and gave us our orders” (21.445).
grc: [σημαίνων]
---

Walter Leaf renders σημαίνων “giving orders”, as in Agamemnon's complaint that Achilles wants “to give orders to all” (1.289; *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.445). Here the gods take orders from a mortal king (see [[poseidon-and-apollo-serve-laomedon]] and [[slavery-and-freedom]]).
