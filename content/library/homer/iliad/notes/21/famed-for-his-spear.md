---
summary: Asteropaeus' father Pelegon was “famed for his spear” (21.159); and “Achilles, famed for his spear, leapt into the midst” of the river (21.233).
grc: ["κλυτὸν ἔγχεϊ"]
---

κλυτὸν ἔγχεϊ, of Pelegon, son of the Axius, is a form of the epithet found only here (21.159; [[pelegon]], [[genealogy-in-battle]]). Achilles is δουρικλυτός as he leaps from the bank into the river and the river rushes on him (21.233–234). See [[achilles]].
