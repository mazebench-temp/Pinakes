---
summary: Ares stabs “at the tasseled aegis, / the terrible one, which not even the thunderbolt of Zeus overpowers” (21.400–401).
---

Most manuscripts have ἀσπίδα, “shield,” for αἰγίδα; but θυσσανόεσσα is said only of the aegis, and Aristonicus remarked on the description of the shield of Zeus himself as the thing his own thunderbolt cannot overpower (Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.401; see the translation's note on 21.401). Ares' spear does nothing, and Athena answers with a stone (21.402–406). See [[aegis]], [[thunderbolt]] and [[athena]].
