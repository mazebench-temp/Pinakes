---
summary: Artemis, beaten with her own bow, flees “in tears, like a dove” (21.493), “so she fled in tears” (21.496), and “the maiden sat in tears on her father's knees” (21.506).
grc: [δακρυόεσσα]
---

The nominative δακρυόεσσα comes three times in fourteen verses, in the simile of the dove, in its close, and on Olympus (21.493–506; [[the-dove-and-the-hawk]], [[doves]]). See [[artemis]] and [[zeus]].
