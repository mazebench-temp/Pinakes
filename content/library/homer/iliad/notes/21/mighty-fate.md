---
summary: Achilles tells Lycaon that he too must die: “yet over me too hang death and mighty fate” (21.110).
---

θάνατος καὶ μοῖρα κραταιή is the formula with which the dying Patroclus foretold Hector's death (16.853). Achilles now says it of himself, and goes on: “there will come a morning or an evening or a midday / when someone will take the life from me too in the work of Ares” (21.111–112; see the translation's note on 21.111).
