summary: The book moves toward the city. Achilles will kill until “we reach the city of sacred Ilios” (21.128); Scamander fears that Achilles “will sack the great city of lord Priam” (21.309); Apollo goes into Ilios, “for the wall of the well-built city was on his mind” (21.516); and at the end “the city was filled with them as they crowded in” (21.607).
grc: [πόληος, "=πόλεος"]
---

πόλεος at 21.567 is printed as the digitized text has it, and must be scanned as two syllables; Walter Leaf prints πόλιος (see the translation's note on 21.567). The smoke of a burning city is the simile for Achilles' advance (21.522–525; see [[smoke-from-a-burning-city]], [[troy]] and [[the-walls-of-troy]]).
