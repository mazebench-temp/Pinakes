---
summary: Achilles, in the river, says that “No other of the heavenly gods is so much to blame for this / as my dear mother” (21.275–276); and Zeus asks the weeping Artemis, “Who of the heavenly gods has done such things to you, dear child” (21.509).
---

Zeus' question repeats the words with which Dione asked the wounded Aphrodite in Book 5 (21.509–510 = 5.373–374). Walter Leaf notes that the whole scene is built on that one, and that the second verse is left out here by almost all the manuscripts (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.509; see the translation's note on 21.509–510).
