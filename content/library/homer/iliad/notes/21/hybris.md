---
summary: Three speakers call the Trojans ὑπερφίαλοι, “overbearing”: Achilles, who will not stop “slaying the overbearing Trojans” (21.224); Athena, who reproaches Ares because he helps “the overbearing Trojans” (21.414); and Poseidon, who wants “the overbearing Trojans” to perish (21.459).
grc: [ὑπερφιάλους, ὑπερφιάλοισιν]
---

Poseidon's charge rests on his story of Laomedon, who robbed him and Apollo of their wage and threatened to bind them, sell them to islands far away and lop off their ears (21.451–455; see [[laomedon]], [[poseidon-and-apollo-serve-laomedon]] and [[overbearing]]).
