---
summary: Achilles tells the dead Lycaon that Scamander will carry him “into the broad bosom of the sea” (21.125).
grc: ["εὐρέα κόλπον"]
en: [broad bosom]
---

εὐρέα κόλπον is the sea's “broad bosom,” into which Thetis sent her sisters back from the shore (18.140). Here the river will carry Lycaon's body there, unburied (21.122–127; [[eddying]], [[white]]). See [[sea]] and [[lycaon-son-of-priam]].
