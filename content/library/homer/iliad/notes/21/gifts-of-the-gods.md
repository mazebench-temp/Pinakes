---
summary: The gifts of the god guard Achilles twice: Asteropaeus' spear cannot break his shield, “for the gold held it back, the gifts of the god” (21.165 = 20.268), and Agenor's bronze springs back from his greave, “for the gifts of the god held it back” (21.594).
refs: [21.165, 21.594]
---

Both times the gift is the armor that Hephaestus made at Thetis' request (18.369–617; see [[the-shield-of-achilles]], [[greaves]] and [[the-making-of-the-arms]]). Walter Leaf thinks 21.594 an addition meant to bring in an allusion to the making of the arms, and notes that the manuscript A omits it (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.594; see the translation's note on 21.594).
