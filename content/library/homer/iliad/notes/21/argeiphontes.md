---
summary: “And the guide, the slayer of Argus, spoke to Leto” (21.497), refusing to fight her.
---

διάκτορος ἀργεϊφόντης is the verse-end of Hermes' titles, here and in Book 2 (2.103; [[guide]]). He will not trade blows “with the wives of Zeus the cloud-gatherer” (21.498–501; [[cloud-gatherer]]). See [[hermes]] and [[leto]].
