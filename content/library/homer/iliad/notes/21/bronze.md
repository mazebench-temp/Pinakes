---
summary: Agenor reasons that even Achilles' flesh “can be pierced by the sharp bronze” (21.568), but the bronze of his spear springs back from the greave of newly wrought tin (21.591–594).
---

Bronze is the metal of blade and spear: Lycaon cut the fig shoots “with the sharp bronze” (21.37), Achilles draws “the bronze spear” out of the bank (21.200), and Laomedon threatened to lop off the gods' ears “with the bronze” (21.455). The house of Zeus on Olympus is “bronze-floored” (21.438, 21.505). See [[greaves]], [[tin]] and [[gifts-of-the-gods]].
