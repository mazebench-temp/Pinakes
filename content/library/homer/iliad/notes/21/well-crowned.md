---
summary: “Then the well-crowned, the clamorous goddess spoke to him in turn” (21.511).
grc: [ἐϋστέφανος]
---

The epithet of Thebes in Book 19 (19.99) is here a title of Artemis, the second of its two uses in the Iliad. With [[clamorous]] it names the goddess without her name, as she answers her father in tears (21.509–513). See [[artemis]].
