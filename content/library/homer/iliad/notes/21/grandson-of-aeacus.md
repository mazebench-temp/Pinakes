---
summary: Asteropaeus, unable to draw Achilles' spear from the bank, wishes “to bend and break the ash spear of the grandson of Aeacus” (21.178).
---

The patronymic prepares Achilles' boast over the dead man, where he traces his line through Peleus and Aeacus to Zeus: “Peleus, son of Aeacus; and Aeacus was the son of Zeus” (21.189). There Αἰακίδης is Peleus, and the line is claimed by [[aeacus]] and [[peleus]], not by this entry. See [[ash-spear]], [[the-pelian-ash]] and [[asteropaeus]].
