---
summary: Hera will rouse “the West Wind and the brightening South Wind” to carry Hephaestus' fire against the Trojans (21.334).
---

The verse-end ἀργεστᾶο Νότοιο is the one of the simile in Book 11 (11.306; Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.334). The winds are never sent (see the translation's note on 21.335). See [[notus]], [[zephyrus]] and [[hera]].
