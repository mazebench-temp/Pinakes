summary: Poseidon tells Apollo, “Little fool, how senseless is the heart you have!” (21.441). Achilles “caught sight of” Lycaon (21.49), Hera saw Aphrodite lead Ares away (21.418), Agenor caught sight of Achilles (21.550), and fears that Achilles “may notice me” if he runs for the plain (21.563).
grc: [ἄνοον]
en: [senseless]
---

ἄνοος, “senseless”, is found only here in Homer. Apollo's want of sense, in Poseidon's eyes, is that he does not remember what Laomedon did to them both (21.441–457; see [[nepios]] and [[kradie]]).
