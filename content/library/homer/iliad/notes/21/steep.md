---
summary: The Trojans fall into the river, “and the sheer streams roared” (21.9); and Lycaon's grandfather Altes holds “steep Pedasus on the Satnioeis” (21.87).
grc: [αἰπήεσσαν]
---

αἰπὰ ῥέεθρα, “the sheer streams,” are the streams of the Styx in Book 8 (8.369); here they are the Xanthus, as half the routed army falls into it “with a great crash” (21.9; [[scamander]]). αἰπήεσσαν, of Pedasus, is found only here; Walter Leaf derives it from αἰπός, as μεσήεις from μέσος (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.87; see the translation's note on 21.86–87). Elsewhere the town is “steep Pedasus” with αἰπεινήν (6.35). See [[pedasus-on-the-satnioeis]] and [[satnioeis]].
