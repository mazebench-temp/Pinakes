summary: The river grants Achilles the upper hand and resents it: “Achilles, beyond all men you are strong, and beyond men you do evil” (21.214), and of him to the Simoeis, “who now prevails, and is bent on being the equal of the gods” (21.315).
grc: [κρατέεις, κρατέει]
en: [you are strong, prevails, prevailing]
---

κρατέει with ὅς τις ὅδε, “whoever he is who is prevailing here”, is Aeneas' question about Diomedes and Sarpedon's about Patroclus (5.175, 16.424), and Calchas' word for Agamemnon's power (1.78–79). Achilles had already told the grandson of the Axius that the race of Zeus is mightier, κρείσσων, than a river's (21.190–191; see [[zeus-is-mightier-than-rivers]] and [[krateros]]).
