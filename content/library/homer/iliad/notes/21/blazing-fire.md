---
summary: In the simile of Achilles' slaughter, smoke goes up to the sky “from a burning city” (21.523).
en: [burning]
---

The simile is adapted from Book 18, where the smoke of a besieged city rises from an island (18.207; Walter Leaf, *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.522; see the translation's note on 21.522–525 and [[wide-sky]]). See [[smoke-from-a-burning-city]] and [[achilles]].
