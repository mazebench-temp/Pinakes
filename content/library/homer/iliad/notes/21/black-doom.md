summary: Lycaon runs to clasp Achilles' knees, wishing above all “to escape evil death and black doom” (21.66).

The half-verse θάνατόν τε κακὸν καὶ κῆρα μέλαιναν comes again in the Odyssey, of Antinous, who does not imagine that one man among many will bring it on him (*Odyssey* 22.14). See [[ker-doom]], [[supplication]] and [[lycaon-son-of-priam]].
