---
summary: Agenor catches sight of “Achilles, sacker of cities” (21.550) and stops to think.
---

Aristonicus noted that the epithet is frequent of Odysseus and here used of Achilles, against the Separatists, who gave it to Odysseus in the Odyssey and Achilles in the Iliad; some texts, he says, read Ἀχιλλέα Πηλείωνα instead. Walter Leaf points out that Achilles is πτολίπορθος three other times in the Iliad (8.372, 15.77, 24.108) and Odysseus twice (2.278, 10.363), so the argument comes to little (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.550).
