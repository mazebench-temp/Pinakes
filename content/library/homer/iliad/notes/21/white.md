---
summary: Achilles tells the dead Lycaon that a fish will come up “to eat the white fat of Lycaon” (21.127).
grc: [ἀργέτα]
---

ἀργέτα δημόν is the white fat of Patroclus' lament in Book 11, that the leaders of the Danaans were destined “to glut the swift dogs in Troy with your white fat” (ἀργέτι δημῷ, 11.816–818). The fat that was to feed the dogs of Troy feeds the fishes of its river. δημός, “fat,” is kept out of [[demos]], “people,” which excepts the line. See [[fish]] and [[lycaon-son-of-priam]].
