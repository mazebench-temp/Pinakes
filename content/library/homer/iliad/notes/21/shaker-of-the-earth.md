---
summary: Apollo answers Poseidon's challenge: “Shaker of the earth, you would not call me sound of mind / if I should fight with you for the sake of mortals” (21.462–463).
---

The address, used by Zeus and Hera in Book 20 (20.20, 20.310), opens Apollo's refusal and his image of mortals like leaves (21.462–467; [[the-generations-of-leaves]], [[gods-and-men-not-alike]]). See [[poseidon]], [[apollo]] and [[earth-shaker]].
