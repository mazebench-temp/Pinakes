---
summary: Hermes will not fight Leto: “it is a hard thing / to trade blows with the wives of Zeus the cloud-gatherer” (21.498–499).
---

ἀλόχοισι, “the wives,” is plural because the sentiment is general, and Walter Leaf hears in it an allusion to Hera's victory over Artemis (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 21.499). See [[zeus]], [[leto]] and [[hermes]].
