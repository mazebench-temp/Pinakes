---
summary: Hector, revived at the ford, vomits dark blood “kneeling”; Archelochus' head strikes the ground before his knees.
en: [kneeling]
---

“and kneeling he vomited dark blood” (ἑζόμενος δʼ ἐπὶ γοῦνα κελαινεφὲς αἷμʼ ἀπέμεσσεν, 14.437; see [[hector]] and [[dark-blood]]): carried to the ford of the Xanthus, Hector gets his breath back when they pour water over him, gets onto his knees, and falls back into the dark (14.433–439; see [[healing]]). ἐπὶ γοῦνα, “onto the knees”, is found only here in Homer. Archelochus, struck where the head and neck join, falls so that “his head and mouth and nostrils struck the ground / far sooner than his shins and knees” (14.465–468; see [[archelochus]]).
