---
summary: Hector draws back “avoiding doom”, but Ajax's stone strikes him as he goes; Polydamas “avoided black doom” by darting aside, and the spear kills Archelochus, “for the gods had planned his destruction”.
---

Hector, his spear wasted, “drew back into the crowd of his companions, avoiding doom” (κῆρʼ ἀλεείνων, 14.408; see [[hetairos]]), and Ajax's stone finds him as he goes (14.409–413). Then Ajax throws at Polydamas: “Polydamas himself avoided black doom, / darting aside, but the son of Antenor caught it, / Archelochus; for the gods had planned his destruction” (ἀλεύατο κῆρα μέλαιναν, 14.462–464; see [[black-doom]], [[archelochus]] and [[boule]]). The half-verse ἀλεύατο κῆρα μέλαιναν is used of Paris and of Hector dodging a spear in their duels (3.360, 7.254) and of Hector, stunned by Diomedes' spear, driving back into the crowd (11.360); here the doom one man avoids falls on another.
