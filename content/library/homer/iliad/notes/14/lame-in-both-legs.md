---
summary: Hera promises Sleep a throne that Hephaestus, “my son, the god lame in both legs,” will make; the book adds ἀμφιγυήεις without περικλυτός.
grc: [ἀμφιγυήεις]
---

“A fine throne, imperishable forever, / golden; and Hephaestus, my son, the god lame in both legs, / will make it with skill, and set a footstool beneath it for your feet” (14.238–240; [[hephaestus]], [[throne]], [[imperishable]], [[sleep]]). Here the epithet stands after πάϊς, “son,” and not after περικλυτός, and it is spoken by Hera of her own son. Hephaestus is also the builder of her chamber with its secret bolt (14.166–168, 14.338–339; [[closely-fitted]]). The word ἀμφιγυήεις alone is new to the entry; it stands after κλυτός when Hephaestus has finished Achilles' armor (18.614).
