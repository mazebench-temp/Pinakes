---
summary: Nestor tells Agamemnon that the Trojans “keep up a relentless battle at the swift ships”; the book adds the accusative ἀλίαστον.
grc: [ἀλίαστον]
---

“And they keep up a relentless battle at the swift ships / without rest; nor could you tell any longer, however closely you looked, / on which side the Achaeans are driven in turmoil and confusion” (14.57–59; [[nestor]], [[swift-ships]], [[agamemnon]]). Nestor's account of the war is the reason for his advice: the wall is down, the fighting has reached the ships, and the wounded kings must not go into it (14.55–63; [[the-achaean-wall]]). The accusative ἀλίαστον is new to the entry.
