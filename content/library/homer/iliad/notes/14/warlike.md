---
summary: The wounded kings go among the men and exchange “the armor of war,” giving the good to the brave and the worse to the worse man.
---

“And the kings themselves, stabbed though they were, marshaled them, / the son of Tydeus, and Odysseus, and the son of Atreus, Agamemnon; / going among them all, they exchanged the armor of war: / the brave man put on the good, and gave the worse to the worse man” (14.379–382; [[the-wounded-chiefs]], [[shield]]). The phrase ἀρήϊα τεύχεα is the armor of war as it is redistributed at Poseidon's advice, the best shields and helmets to the best men (14.370–377; [[poseidon]], [[steadfast-in-battle]], [[gleaming-bronze]]).
