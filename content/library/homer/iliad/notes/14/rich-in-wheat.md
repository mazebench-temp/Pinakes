---
summary: Tydeus in Argos had “fields enough / bearing wheat,” in Diomedes' account of his father's wealth; the book adds πυροφόροι.
grc: [πυροφόροι]
en: [bearing wheat]
---

“He married one of the daughters of Adrastus, and lived in a house / wealthy in substance, and he had fields enough / bearing wheat, and many rows of orchard trees all around, / and he had many flocks” (14.121–124; [[tydeus]], [[adrastus]], [[wealthy]], [[argos]]). πυροφόρος, “wheat-bearing,” which Book 12 gave to the land that the Lycians granted their kings (12.314), here describes the estate that Tydeus won in Argos, the land that Nestor will call πολύπυρος in his prayer at the ships (15.372). The form πυροφόροι is new to the entry.
