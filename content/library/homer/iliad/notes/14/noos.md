summary: Hera plans to beguile “the mind of aegis-bearing Zeus”, the mind that no man can hold back, and Sleep has lulled it once before; Aphrodite's strap holds the persuasion that “steals the mind even of men who think shrewdly”; and Nestor, at a loss, asks the kings to consider “if the mind can do any good”.

  - 14.249-262 | Sleep lulled the mind of Zeus
---

**The mind of Zeus.** Hera ponders “how she might beguile the mind of aegis-bearing Zeus” (ἐξαπάφοιτο Διὸς νόον αἰγιόχοιο, 14.160; see [[apate]]), and Sleep recalls the day when “I then lulled the mind of aegis-bearing Zeus” (ἔλεξα Διὸς νόον αἰγιόχοιο, 14.252; see [[sleep]] and [[hypnos]]). The mind of Zeus is his will, the plan that rules the war, and elsewhere it cannot be resisted. Nestor told Diomedes that “No man can hold back the mind of Zeus, / however mighty he is” (8.143–144; see [[the-mind-of-zeus]]), the narrator will say that the mind of Zeus is always stronger than men's (Διὸς κρείσσων νόος, 16.688), and in the Odyssey Hermes tells Calypso that no other god can get around the mind of aegis-bearing Zeus or bring it to nothing (*Odyssey* 5.103–104). Book 14 does not resist the mind of Zeus; it puts it to sleep (see [[plan-of-zeus]] and [[the-deception-of-zeus]]). When he wakes, it is the mind of Zeus that rouses Hector again (15.242).

**The stolen mind.** The last power in Aphrodite's strap is “the persuasion that steals the mind even of men who think shrewdly” (ἔκλεψε νόον, 14.217; see [[aphrodites-band]] and [[the-persuasion-that-steals-the-mind]]). κλέπτω is to take by stealth; the mind is robbed without knowing it. Zeus, whose “shrewd mind” the narrator names twice in the book (14.165, 14.294), is its victim (see [[phrenes]]).

**The mind at a loss.** Nestor, meeting the wounded kings with the wall down, says, “So let us consider how these things are to be, / if the mind can do any good” (εἴ τι νόος ῥέξει, 14.61–62; see [[nestor]]). The old counselor admits that thinking may not be enough, and the plan comes from the youngest (see [[metis]]).
