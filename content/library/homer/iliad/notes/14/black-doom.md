---
summary: Polydamas, at whom Ajax throws, “avoided black doom,” and the spear kills Archelochus instead.
---

Ajax answers Polydamas' boast with a spear: “Polydamas himself avoided black doom, / darting aside, but the son of Antenor caught it, / Archelochus; for the gods had planned his destruction” (14.462–464; [[polydamas]], [[ajax]], [[archelochus]]). The verse-end ἀλεύατο κῆρα μέλαιναν is the one that saved Paris and Hector from a spear in their duels (3.360, 7.254) and Hector again when Diomedes' spear struck his helmet (11.360). Each time the man aimed at escapes; here another dies in his place, and the narrator gives the reason in the gods' plan. See [[ker-doom]] and [[antenor]].
