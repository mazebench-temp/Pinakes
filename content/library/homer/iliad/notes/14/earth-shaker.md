---
summary: Poseidon shouts like ten thousand men as “the powerful earth-shaker,” and later leads the Achaeans openly as “Poseidon the earth-shaker.”
---

**The shout.** In the likeness of an old man, Poseidon goes across the plain and shouts: “so loud was the voice that the powerful earth-shaker / sent out of his breast; and he put great strength into each of the Achaeans” (14.150–151; [[ten-thousand-men]], [[powerful]], [[poseidon]]). κρείων ἐνοσίχθων is the formula of 13.10, where Poseidon “kept no blind watch” over the battle from Samothrace; at 14.135 the same verse is spoken of “the famous shaker of the earth” ([[shaker-of-the-earth]], [[famous]]).

**The lead.** When Sleep has told him that Zeus is asleep, he leads the army himself: “they went; and Poseidon the earth-shaker led them, / holding a dread long-edged sword in his strong hand, / like lightning” (14.384–386; [[long-edged]], [[strong-hand]], [[like-lightning]], [[poseidon-rallies-the-achaeans]]). The god who in Book 13 helped in secret, in the likeness of Calchas and of Thoas (13.43–45, 13.215–216), now goes in front under his own name.
