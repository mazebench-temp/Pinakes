---
summary: In the invocation before the rout, the poet asks the Muses which of the Achaeans was the first “to win the bloody spoils of men,” the one use of βροτόεις outside the formula ἔναρα βροτόεντα.
grc: ["βροτόεντʼ ἀνδράγριʼ"]
---

“Tell me now, Muses, you who have your homes on Olympus, / who was the first of the Achaeans to win the bloody spoils of men, / when the famous shaker of the earth had turned the battle” (14.508–510; [[invocation-of-the-muses]], [[muses]], [[shaker-of-the-earth]]). ἀνδράγρια, “spoils taken from men,” is found only here in Homer, and the translation renders the phrase “the bloody spoils of men,” keeping it apart from the formula ἔναρα βροτόεντα. The answer follows at once: Telamonian Ajax, who stabbed Hyrtius, and after him Antilochus, Meriones, Teucer and Menelaus (14.511–519; [[ajax]], [[hyrtius]], [[stripping-the-dead]]). The phrase βροτόεντʼ ἀνδράγριʼ is new to the entry.
