---
summary: Hera and Sleep come to “Ida of the many springs, mother of wild beasts,” in the verse that brought Zeus himself to the mountain in Book 8.
---

“And they came to Ida of the many springs, mother of wild beasts, / to Lectum, where they first left the sea” (14.283–284; [[ida]], [[lectum]], [[of-the-many-springs]], [[hera]], [[sleep]]). The verse that brought Zeus to his seat on Ida in Book 8 (8.47) here, with the dual ἱκέσθην, brings the two gods who will put him to sleep. Sleep climbs into a tall fir to wait, and Hera goes up to Gargarus (14.285–293; [[gargarus]]).
