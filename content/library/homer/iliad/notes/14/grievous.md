---
summary: The first of the three sounds in the simile of the shout is the wave “roused from the deep by the grievous blast of the North Wind.”
---

“Not so loud does the wave of the sea roar against the dry land, / roused from the deep by the grievous blast of the North Wind” (14.394–395; [[boreas]], [[waves-on-the-shore]]). The epithet of pain is given to the wind that drives the wave on the shore; the wave, the fire and the wind are all less loud than the shout of the armies (14.396–401; [[blazing-fire]], [[high-leafed]]). Book 24 joins war and the sea under the same adjective, πτολέμους ἀλεγεινά τε κύματα (24.8).
