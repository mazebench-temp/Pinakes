---
summary: Hector, struck down by Ajax's stone, is carried toward the city “groaning heavily”, in the verse that carried the wounded Deiphobus there in Book 13.
---

“and they carried him toward the city, groaning heavily” (οἳ τόν γε προτὶ ἄστυ φέρον βαρέα στενάχοντα, 14.432; see [[hector]] and [[chariot]]). The verse is the last of three that come whole from Book 13, where Polites led his wounded brother Deiphobus out of the battle to his horses and chariot and they bore him to the city (14.430–432 = 13.536–538; see the note on Book 13). The plural βαρέα, “heavily”, belongs to the wounded who are carried away: Teucer and Hypsenor are borne to the ships in the same half-verse (8.334, 13.423). Hector's groaning ends at the ford of the Xanthus, where his companions pour water on him and he vomits dark blood and falls back into darkness (14.433–439; see [[darkness-covered-his-eyes]]).
