summary: The poet calls on the Muses, “you who have your homes on Olympus,” in the verse that opened the Catalogue, before the list of the Achaeans' kills in the rout.
---

“Tell me now, Muses, you who have your homes on Olympus” (14.508; [[muses]], [[invocation-of-the-muses]]) is the verse that opened the Catalogue of Ships (2.484) and the list of Agamemnon's opponents in Book 11 (11.218); it returns once more when the poet asks how fire first fell on the ships (16.112–113). Here it introduces a short list of who killed whom when Poseidon had turned the battle (14.508–522; [[bloody-spoils]], [[famous]]).
