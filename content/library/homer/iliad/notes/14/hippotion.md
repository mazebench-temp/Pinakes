# Ἱπποτίωνα (14.514): the only other form of the name in Homer is the father's genitive (13.792).
grc: [Ἱπποτίωνα]
relations:
  - slayer: meriones (14.514)
---

Μηριόνης δὲ Μόρυν τε καὶ Ἱπποτίωνα κατέκτα, “and Meriones killed Morys and Hippotion” (14.514; see [[meriones]] and [[morys]]). A book earlier Morys was named as υἷʼ Ἱπποτίωνος, “son of Hippotion” (13.792). If the Hippotion who dies here is that father, father and son die together, by the same hand. The entry takes the two as one man, since the poem gives no ground for telling them apart, but the identification is not certain.
