---
summary: Satnius, whose name echoes the river's, was born on its banks to the herdsman Enops and a Naiad, and the son of Oileus kills him (14.442–447).
---

“The son of Enops, whom a noble Naiad nymph bore / to Enops as he tended his cattle by the banks of the Satnioeis” (14.444–445; see [[satnius]], [[enops]] and [[nymphs]]). παρʼ ὄχθας, “by the banks”, is the phrase in which Elatus lived by the Satnioeis (6.34), and Simoeisius was born “beside the banks of the Simoeis” (4.475; see [[simoeisius]]).
