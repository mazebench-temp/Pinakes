---
summary: Panthous is named in his son's patronymic when Polydamas comes to defend Satnius' body (14.450), and in Polydamas' boast, where he calls himself “the great-hearted son of Panthous” (14.454). Hyperenor, whom Menelaus kills at the end of the book (14.516–519), is another of his sons, as Book 17 shows (17.23–28, 17.34–35).
