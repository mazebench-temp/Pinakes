---
summary: Nestor takes a strong spear “tipped with sharp bronze,” the son of Oileus stabs Satnius “with his sharp spear,” and Peneleos draws “his sharp sword” to strike off Ilioneus' head.
---

Nestor arms with his son's shield and “a strong spear, tipped with sharp bronze” (14.11–12; [[nestor]], [[thrasymedes]], [[strong-spear]]). The son of Oileus “leapt upon Satnius and stabbed him with his sharp spear” (14.443; [[ajax-son-of-oileus]], [[satnius]]), with the longer adjective ὀξυόεις. Peneleos, “drawing his sharp sword, / struck him full in the middle of the neck” (14.496–497; [[peneleos]], [[ilioneus]], [[sword]]). The superlative is used of the Sun, “whose light is the sharpest of all for seeing” (14.345; [[helios]]): not even he, Zeus says, could see through the golden cloud (14.342–345).
