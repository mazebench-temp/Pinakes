---
summary: There is no embassy in Book 14. The ἀγγελίη of 14.355 is the message that Sleep runs to carry to Poseidon, that Zeus is asleep.
except: [14.355]
---

When Zeus is asleep on Gargarus, “sweet Sleep went running to the ships of the Achaeans / to carry the message to the earth-holder, the shaker of the earth” (ἀγγελίην ἐρέων, 14.354–355; see [[sleep]] and [[poseidon]]). ἀγγελίη here is a message, as at 13.252 and 15.640, not the embassy of men sent with words to another people or another man (see the article above). Sleep is sent by no one: the poem does not say that Hera told him to go, and his message is his own news, that Poseidon may help the Danaans “while he still sleeps” (14.357–360; see [[poseidon-rallies-the-achaeans]]).
