---
summary: The Locrian Ajax is “swift Ajax, the son of Oileus,” first to leap on Satnius, and “the swift son of Oileus,” who kills the most in the rout.
---

“Then by far the first, swift Ajax, the son of Oileus, / leapt upon Satnius and stabbed him with his sharp spear” (14.442–443; [[ajax-son-of-oileus]], [[satnius]], [[sharp]]). At the end of the book the formula returns with υἱός for Αἴας: “But most were killed by Ajax, the swift son of Oileus; / for there was no one like him to pursue on foot / men who fled in fear, when Zeus rouses rout among them” (14.520–522). The last verses say what his epithet means in battle, speed in pursuit. Zeus, whom the general verse names as the god who rouses rout, lies asleep on Gargarus while the Trojans flee (14.352–353; [[the-deception-of-zeus]]).
