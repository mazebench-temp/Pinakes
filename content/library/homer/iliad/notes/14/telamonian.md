---
summary: “Great Telamonian Ajax” fells Hector with a stone, Polydamas' boast stirs “the son of Telamon,” and Telamonian Ajax is the first to win spoils in the rout.
---

“Then, as he went away, great Telamonian Ajax / with a stone” struck Hector in the chest (14.409–412; [[ajax]], [[hector]], [[stone-throwing]]). Polydamas' boast stirs “the heart of wise-hearted Ajax, / the son of Telamon; for the man fell very close to him” (14.459–460; [[wise-hearted]], [[polydamas]]), with the patronymic Τελαμωνιάδῃ run over into the next verse. And in the answer to the invocation, “Telamonian Ajax was the first; he stabbed Hyrtius” (14.511; [[hyrtius]], [[bloody-spoils]]). The epithet keeps him apart from the son of Oileus, who fights in the same scenes (14.442, 14.520; [[ajax-son-of-oileus]], [[swift]]).
