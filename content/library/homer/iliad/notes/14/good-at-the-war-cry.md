---
summary: Diomedes, “good at the war cry,” speaks last among the wounded kings, the youngest, and his advice is followed.
---

“And among them spoke also Diomedes, good at the war cry: / ‘The man is near; we shall not search long’” (14.109–110; [[diomedes]]). In Book 9, when Agamemnon first proposed flight in earnest, Diomedes answered him in the same formula (9.31); here Odysseus has already rebuked the proposal (14.82–102; [[odysseus]]), and Diomedes offers the better plan that Agamemnon asked for. He is the youngest by birth, and he defends his right to speak by his father's line (14.110–127; [[tydeus]], [[genealogy-in-battle]]). His advice is to go to the war, wounded as they are, and to urge on the others without fighting (14.128–132), and the kings obey (14.133–134).
