---
summary: Hera sees Zeus sitting on Ida, “and he was hateful to her heart.”
---

“But Zeus, on the topmost peak of Ida of the many springs, / she saw sitting, and he was hateful to her heart” (14.157–158; [[hera]], [[zeus]], [[ida]]). The adjective is the predicate: Zeus himself is στυγερός to Hera, as the sight of Poseidon in the battle had made her rejoice (14.155–156; [[poseidon]]). The plan that follows, to beguile his mind, grows from that hatred (14.159–165; [[the-deception-of-zeus]]).
