---
summary: Oileus is named only in his son's titles: “swift Ajax, the son of Oileus”, who kills Satnius, and “Ajax, the swift son of Oileus”, who kills the most in the rout (14.442, 14.446, 14.520).
---

The lesser Ajax is named by his father each time he acts in the book: Ὀϊλῆος ταχὺς Αἴας, “swift Ajax, the son of Oileus”, as he leaps on Satnius (14.442); Ὀϊλιάδης, “the son of Oileus”, as he stabs him (14.446); and Αἴας … Ὀϊλῆος ταχὺς υἱός, “Ajax, the swift son of Oileus”, who kills the most when the Trojans are routed (14.520; see [[ajax-son-of-oileus]] and [[swift]]). The father's name tells him apart from Telamonian Ajax, who fights in the same scenes (14.409, 14.459–475, 14.511; see [[ajax]] and [[telamon]]).
