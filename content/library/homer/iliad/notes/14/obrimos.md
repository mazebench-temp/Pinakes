---
summary: Agamemnon fears that “mighty Hector” will make good his threat to burn the ships and kill the men.
---

“I fear that mighty Hector may make good his word to me, / as he once threatened, speaking among the Trojans, / that he would not go back from the ships to Ilios / before he had set fire to the ships and killed the men themselves” (14.44–47; [[hector]], [[agamemnon]]). The last words repeat those of Hector's own threat in Book 8, “so that I may set fire to the ships and kill the men themselves” (8.182). The epithet of Ares, given to Hector since Book 8 (8.473), stands in the mouth of the king who fears him.
