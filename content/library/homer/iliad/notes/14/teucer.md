---
summary: In the rout at the end of the book, “Teucer slew Prothoon and Periphetes” (14.515).
---

Teucer has one verse in the book, in the list of the Achaeans who win bloody spoils after Poseidon has turned the battle: “and Teucer slew Prothoon and Periphetes” (14.515; see [[invocation-of-the-muses]] and [[bloody-spoils]]). ἐνήρατο, “slew”, is from ἐναίρω, the verb of ἔναρα, the spoils taken from the dead; the poem does not say whether he killed them with the bow or with the spear, which he used in Book 13 against Imbrius (13.170–181; see [[imbrius]] and [[bow]]). Neither Trojan is named elsewhere (see [[prothoon]] and [[periphetes]]); the Periphetes whom Hector kills in Book 15 is an Achaean of Mycenae (15.638). His brother, Telamonian Ajax, heads the same list (14.511; see [[ajax]]).
