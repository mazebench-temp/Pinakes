---
summary: πότνια Ἥρη stands six times in the book, more than in any other: three times with “ox-eyed,” and three times in the verse that introduces her treacherous speeches.
---

With βοῶπις the title marks Hera's plan, her smile and her answer to Sleep (14.159, 14.222, 14.263; [[ox-eyed]]). With δολοφρονέουσα it introduces her lies: to Aphrodite, “And queen Hera, with treachery in her heart, addressed her” (14.197), and twice to Zeus on Gargarus (14.300, 14.329; [[with-treachery-in-her-heart]]). The title of the queen of the gods is kept through her scheme, in the book in which she prevails over the king of the gods. See [[hera]] and [[the-deception-of-zeus]].
