---
summary: Zeus names Perseus, son of Danae, “preeminent among all men”; the book adds the accusative ἀριδείκετον ἀνδρῶν.
grc: ["ἀριδείκετον ἀνδρῶν"]
---

“Nor when I loved Danae of the lovely ankles, daughter of Acrisius, / who bore Perseus, preeminent among all men” (14.319–320; [[perseus]], [[danae]], [[of-the-lovely-ankles]], [[the-loves-of-zeus]]). The phrase that named Coon, Antenor's eldest son, “preeminent among men” (11.248; [[coon]]), is here strengthened with πάντων, “among all,” for a son of Zeus. The accusative ἀριδείκετον ἀνδρῶν is new to the entry.
