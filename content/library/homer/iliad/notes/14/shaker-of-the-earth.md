---
summary: Poseidon is “the famous shaker of the earth” as he enters the battle and in the invocation that marks his turning of it, and “the earth-holder, the shaker of the earth” when Sleep brings him the message.
---

The title frames Poseidon's part in the book. It opens his intervention: “Nor did the famous shaker of the earth keep a blind watch, / but he went along with them in the likeness of an old man” (14.135–136; [[famous]], [[poseidon]], [[divine-epiphany]]), the verse with which Book 13 began his help, there with κρείων ἐνοσίχθων (13.10; [[earth-shaker]]). It names him as Sleep's message comes, “to carry the message to the earth-holder, the shaker of the earth” (14.355; [[earth-holder]], [[sleep]]). And it closes his work in the invocation before the rout, “when the famous shaker of the earth had turned the battle” (14.510; [[invocation-of-the-muses]], [[bloody-spoils]]).
