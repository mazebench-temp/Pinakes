summary: Hector is angry “that his swift missile had flown from his hand in vain,” when his spear is stopped by the straps on Ajax's chest; the book adds the translation's “swift missile.”
en: [swift missile]
---

“And these two protected his tender flesh. And Hector was enraged / that his swift missile had flown from his hand in vain, / and he drew back into the crowd of his companions, avoiding doom” (14.406–408; [[hector]], [[ajax]], [[silver-studded]], [[tender]]). Here βέλος ὠκύ is a thrown spear, and the translation renders it “swift missile.” The same two verses return when Hector's spear rebounds from Achilles' shield (22.291–292). As Hector withdraws, Ajax's stone strikes him, and the word βέλος comes back for the stone, which “still overpowered his spirit” as he lay at the Xanthus (14.409–413, 14.439; [[stone-throwing]]).
