---
summary: Odysseus rejects the proposal “to drag the well-benched ships to the sea,” and Agamemnon answers in the same words.
---

Odysseus: “you who bid us, while war and the war cry are joined, / to drag the well-benched ships to the sea” (14.96–97; [[odysseus]]). Agamemnon, yielding: “but I am not ordering them against their will, / the sons of the Achaeans, to drag the well-benched ships to the sea” (14.105–106; [[agamemnon]], [[sons-of-the-achaeans]]). The verse passes from the rebuke to the reply, and the ships stay where they are. See [[ships]].
