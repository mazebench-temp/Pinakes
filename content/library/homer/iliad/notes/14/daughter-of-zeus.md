---
summary: Aphrodite is “daughter of Zeus” as she answers Hera and as she goes home, having given her the strap that will overcome her father.
---

“Then Aphrodite, daughter of Zeus, answered her” (14.193), and when she has given Hera the embroidered strap, “Aphrodite, daughter of Zeus, went to her house” (14.224; [[aphrodite]], [[aphrodites-band]]). Hera asks her favor with a reminder of the war that divides them, “because I help the Danaans and you the Trojans” (14.192), and Aphrodite grants it because Hera sleeps “in the arms of Zeus, the greatest of all” (14.213; [[laughter-loving]]). The daughter of Zeus gives the means of deceiving Zeus. See [[hera]] and [[the-deception-of-zeus]].
