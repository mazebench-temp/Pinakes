---
summary: Hera comes over the sea from Athos to Lemnos, “the city of divine Thoas,” where she meets Sleep.
---

“And from Athos she stepped out onto the billowing sea, / and came to Lemnos, the city of divine Thoas. / There she met Sleep, the brother of Death” (14.229–231; [[lemnos]], [[thoas-of-lemnos]], [[sleep]], [[billowing]]). The genitive θείοιο usually closes the verse with a hero's name, above all Odysseus' (2.335, 9.218, 11.806), and Oileus' (13.694); here it stands before the name of the Lemnian king, at the end of a verse that names his city. Lemnos is the island where Hephaestus fell from heaven (1.593) and from which Euneus, son of Jason, sent wine to the Achaeans (7.467–469; [[euneus]]). See [[hera]].
