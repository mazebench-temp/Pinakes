---
summary: Nestor ponders whether to go “into the throng of the Danaans of the swift horses” or to Agamemnon.
---

“So the old man pondered, torn in his heart / two ways: whether to go into the throng of the Danaans of the swift horses, / or to the son of Atreus, Agamemnon, shepherd of the people” (14.20–22; [[nestor]], [[pondering-two-ways]], [[the-heaving-sea]]). The two choices fill one verse each, and each ends in a formula, Δαναῶν ταχυπώλων and ποιμένα λαῶν ([[shepherd-of-the-people]], [[danaans]]). Nestor chooses the king (14.23–24).
