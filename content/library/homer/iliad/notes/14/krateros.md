---
summary: Around the fallen Satnius, “Trojans and Danaans joined in fierce battle.”
---

The son of Oileus stabs Satnius in the flank, “and he fell back; and around him / Trojans and Danaans joined in fierce battle” (14.447–448; [[satnius]], [[ajax-son-of-oileus]], [[trojans]], [[danaans]]). κρατερὴ ὑσμίνη is the battle as a contest of strength, as in the verse of men who “toiled on through the fierce battle” (5.84, 5.627). Here it gathers around a fallen man, and out of it Polydamas comes to defend the body (14.449–450; [[polydamas]]).
