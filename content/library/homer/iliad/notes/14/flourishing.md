---
summary: Nestor, at his wine, hears that the cry “of the young men in their prime” is louder now beside the ships.
---

“Consider, brilliant Machaon, how these things are to be; / the cry of the young men in their prime is louder now beside the ships” (14.3–4; [[nestor]], [[machaon]]). The genitive θαλερῶν αἰζηῶν closes the verse as it did in Book 10, of the helmet that “guards the heads of young men in their prime” (10.259; [[helmet]]). The shout of the fighting men reaches the old man over his wine, and he goes out to look (14.1–15).
