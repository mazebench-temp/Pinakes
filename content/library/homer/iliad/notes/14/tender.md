---
summary: The two straps across Ajax's chest “protected his tender flesh” from Hector's spear.
---

“Where the two straps were stretched across his chest, / the one of his shield, the other of his silver-studded sword; / and these two protected his tender flesh” (14.404–406; [[ajax]], [[silver-studded]], [[shield]], [[hector]]). τέρενα χρόα is the soft skin of the body, which the straps of the warrior's gear save, and Hector's spear falls away (14.406–408; [[swift-shaft]]).
