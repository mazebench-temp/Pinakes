---
summary: Agamemnon asks Nestor why he has left “man-destroying war.”
---

“Nestor, son of Neleus, great glory of the Achaeans, / why have you left man-destroying war and come here?” (14.42–43; [[agamemnon]], [[nestor]], [[great-glory-of-the-achaeans]], [[polemos]]). The question comes from a king who has himself been driven out of the fighting by a wound, to the old man who has come out to look at it (14.27–40; [[the-wounded-chiefs]]). The formula πόλεμον φθισήνορα names the war that Agamemnon will propose to escape by night (14.65–81).
