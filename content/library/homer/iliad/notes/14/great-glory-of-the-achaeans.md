---
summary: Agamemnon greets Nestor as “Nestor, son of Neleus, great glory of the Achaeans,” and asks why he has left the war.
---

“Nestor, son of Neleus, great glory of the Achaeans, / why have you left man-destroying war and come here?” (14.42–43; [[agamemnon]], [[nestor]], [[son-of-neleus]], [[man-destroying]]). Agamemnon used the same verse on the night of Book 10, when he went to Nestor's hut for counsel (10.87). Here the two meet by day among the ships, the king wounded and the old man come to look at the battle, and Agamemnon goes on to fear that Hector will make good his threat to burn the ships (14.44–51; [[hector]]).
