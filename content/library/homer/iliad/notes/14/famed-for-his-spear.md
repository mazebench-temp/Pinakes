---
summary: The son of Oileus, “famed for his spear,” kills Satnius, in the half-verse with which Meges killed Pedaeus in Book 5.
---

“Him the son of Oileus, famed for his spear, coming close, / stabbed in the flank, and he fell back” (14.446–447; [[ajax-son-of-oileus]], [[satnius]]). The half-verse τὸν μὲν Ὀϊλιάδης δουρὶ κλυτὸς ἐγγύθεν ἐλθὼν is the one of Meges' kill in Book 5, τὸν μὲν Φυλεΐδης δουρὶ κλυτὸς ἐγγύθεν ἐλθὼν (5.72; [[meges]]), with another patronymic in the same place. It is the first time the epithet goes to the Locrian Ajax, who has just been named “swift Ajax, the son of Oileus” in his own formula (14.442; [[swift]]), and who kills the most men in the rout at the end of the book (14.520–522).
