summary: In How the Young Man Should Study Poetry Plutarch rejects the allegorists who made Hera's adornment for Zeus and the enchantments of Aphrodite's strap a cleansing of the air, and reads the scene as a lesson of the poet's own: favor won from a husband by drugs, witchcraft and guile is short-lived and turns to anger, as Zeus' threat on waking shows.
