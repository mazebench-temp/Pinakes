summary: Telamonian Ajax's first kill in the rout is Hyrtius, “leader of the stout-hearted Mysians”; the book adds the genitive plural καρτεροθύμων.
grc: [καρτεροθύμων]
---

“Telamonian Ajax was the first; he stabbed Hyrtius, / son of Gyrtius, leader of the stout-hearted Mysians” (14.511–512; [[ajax]], [[hyrtius]], [[gyrtius]], [[mysians]]). The epithet that Pandarus gave Diomedes and the narrator gave Achilles (5.277, 13.350) is here given to a people: the Mysians, whom the opening of Book 13 named as those “who fight hand to hand” (13.5; [[hand-to-hand-fighters]]). The answer to the poet's question, who was the first to win the bloody spoils (14.508–510; [[bloody-spoils]]), is the death of their leader. The genitive plural καρτεροθύμων is new to the entry.
