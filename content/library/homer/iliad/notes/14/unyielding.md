---
summary: As Nestor ponders, “the unyielding bronze rang about their bodies.”
---

“And they kept killing one another, / fighting; and the unyielding bronze rang about their bodies / as they stabbed at one another with swords and double-pointed spears” (14.24–26; [[double-pointed]], [[bronze]]). The bronze that does not wear down sounds over the men who are being worn down, while Nestor goes to the wounded kings (14.27–29; [[nestor]], [[the-wounded-chiefs]]).
