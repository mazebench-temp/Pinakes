---
summary: Hera's plan is to pour sleep on Zeus' eyelids “and his shrewd mind”; the book adds the formula without movable ν.
grc: [φρεσὶ πευκαλίμῃσι]
---

“To see if he might long to lie in love beside / her body, and she might pour sleep, harmless and warm, / upon his eyelids and his shrewd mind” (14.163–165; [[hera]], [[zeus]], [[hypnos]], [[the-deception-of-zeus]]). The mind that is to be overcome is praised as shrewd. When Zeus sees her on Gargarus, desire wraps “his shrewd mind” (14.294), in another phrase that the translation renders the same way (πυκινὰς φρένας; [[shrewd-word]], [[eros]]). The form φρεσὶ πευκαλίμῃσι, without movable ν, is new to the entry; it is also the form of the formula at 15.81 and 20.35.
