---
summary: Hera swears her oath to Sleep naming “all the gods / beneath Tartarus, who are called Titans”, the only place where Homer names the Titans and the only other book in which he names Tartarus.
grc: [ὑποταρταρίους]
---

**The gods beneath Tartarus.** Sleep asks for witnesses among “the gods below who are around Cronus” (14.274), and Hera “named all the gods / beneath Tartarus, who are called Titans” (τοὺς ὑποταρταρίους οἳ Τιτῆνες καλέονται, 14.278–279; see [[titans]], [[cronus]], [[oath]] and [[hera]]). ὑποταρταρίους, “beneath Tartarus”, is found only here. In Book 8 Zeus speaks of Iapetus and Cronus sitting at the lowest limits of earth and sea, “and deep Tartarus is all around them” (8.478–481; see [[iapetus]]); here the Titans are beneath it. The oath is sworn by the Styx, the river of the world below, and the powers of that world are its witnesses (see [[styx]]).
