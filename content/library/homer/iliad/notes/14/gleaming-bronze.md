---
summary: When the kings have exchanged the armor and the army has put “the gleaming bronze” about their bodies, Poseidon leads them out.
---

Poseidon has told the Argives to put on the best and biggest shields and helmets and to give the small shields to worse men (14.370–377); the wounded kings go among them and exchange the armor, “the brave man put on the good, and gave the worse to the worse man” (14.379–382; [[the-wounded-chiefs]], [[shield]]). Then: “But when they had put the gleaming bronze about their bodies, / they went; and Poseidon the earth-shaker led them” (14.383–384; [[earth-shaker]], [[poseidon-rallies-the-achaeans]]). The phrase νώροπα χαλκόν is the bronze in which Agamemnon stood out in the Catalogue and armed for his day of fighting (2.578, 11.16); here it is the bronze of a whole army, rearmed by a god's advice.
