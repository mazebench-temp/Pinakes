---
summary: The wounded kings come up from the ships, which were drawn up far from the battle “on the shore of the gray sea,” the first of them hauled up toward the plain.
---

“For the ships were drawn up far away from the battle, / on the shore of the gray sea; for the first of them they had hauled up toward the plain, / and built the wall along their sterns” (14.30–32; [[ships]], [[the-achaean-wall]], [[the-wounded-chiefs]]). The half-verse θῖνʼ ἔφʼ ἁλὸς πολιῆς is the one of Achilles sitting apart by the sea (1.350) and of the ships of Ajax and Protesilaus where Hector broke in (13.682). The narrator explains how the kings came to be far from the fighting: the beach was too narrow for all the ships, so they were drawn up in rows, filling the shore between the headlands (14.33–36).
