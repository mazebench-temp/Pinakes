---
summary: “The kings whom Zeus has nurtured” are the three wounded kings, Diomedes, Odysseus and Agamemnon, who meet Nestor and later, “stabbed though they were”, marshal the army themselves.
---

“And the kings whom Zeus has nurtured met Nestor / as they came up from beside the ships, all who had been hit by the bronze” (διοτρεφέες βασιλῆες, 14.27–28; see [[zeus-nurtured]] and [[kings]]). In Book 14 the word names only these three, and only as a group. After Poseidon's order to exchange the arms, “the kings themselves, stabbed though they were, marshaled them” (τοὺς δʼ αὐτοὶ βασιλῆες ἐκόσμεον οὐτάμενοί περ, 14.379; see [[the-wounded-chiefs]] and [[exchange-of-armor]]). Before the Catalogue the kings whom Zeus has nurtured marshaled the army around Agamemnon, with Athena among them (2.445–446); here they do it wounded, and Poseidon leads the army out (14.384; see [[poseidon]]).
