---
summary: “Shining Hector” marshals the Trojans, leads them against Poseidon, and throws first at Ajax, three times in fifteen verses before his fall.
---

The formula φαίδιμος Ἕκτωρ closes three verses within fifteen: “And on the other side shining Hector marshaled the Trojans” (14.388), “dark-haired Poseidon and shining Hector” (14.390; [[dark-haired]], [[poseidon]]), and “At Ajax first shining Hector threw / his spear” (14.402–403; [[ajax]], [[silver-studded]]). The next time the narrative gives him an epithet, he is “the shepherd of the people” lying in the dust under Ajax's stone (14.409–424; [[stone-throwing]], [[shepherd-of-the-people]]).
