---
summary: Agamemnon fears that the other “well-greaved Achaeans” are laying up anger against him, just as Achilles is.
---

“Ah, truly the other well-greaved Achaeans too / are laying up anger against me in their hearts, just as Achilles is, / and are not willing to fight at the sterns of the ships” (14.49–51; [[agamemnon]], [[achilles]], [[o-popoi]]). The king's fear names the army by its formula. Poseidon answers it later in the book, telling the Argives that they will not miss Achilles if they rouse one another (14.366–369; [[hollow-ships]]). See [[achaeans]].
