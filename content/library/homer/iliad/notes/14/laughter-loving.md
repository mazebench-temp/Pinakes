---
summary: Aphrodite, “laughter-loving,” grants Hera's request, for Hera sleeps “in the arms of Zeus, the greatest of all,” and Hera smiles as she takes the strap.
---

“Then laughter-loving Aphrodite spoke to her in turn: / ‘It is not possible, nor would it be fitting, to refuse your word; / for you sleep in the arms of Zeus, the greatest of all’” (14.211–213; [[aphrodite]], [[aphrodites-band]]). The goddess of smiles does not see through Hera's story, and the smile passes to Hera: when Aphrodite has given her the strap, “ox-eyed queen Hera smiled, / and smiling, she put it in her bosom” (μείδησεν … μειδήσασα, 14.222–223; [[ox-eyed]]), with the verb from which the epithet is formed. The text prints φιλομειδής with a single μ, as at 3.424 (see the translation's note on 14.211).
