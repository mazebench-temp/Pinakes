---
summary: Hera recalls how “far-seeing Zeus” put Cronus beneath the earth, and asks Sleep whether “far-seeing Zeus” will help the Trojans as much as he raged for Heracles.
---

In Hera's lie to Aphrodite, Oceanus and Tethys took her from Rhea “when far-seeing Zeus put Cronus / down beneath the earth and the barren sea” (14.203–204; [[cronus]], [[rhea]], [[barren-sea]]). To Sleep, who fears Zeus' anger, she answers: “Do you think that far-seeing Zeus will help the Trojans as much / as he was enraged for Heracles, his own son?” (14.265–266; [[sleep]], [[heracles]], [[hera-drives-heracles-to-cos]]). The god whose epithet is “far-seeing” sees nothing of what is done in this book: the deception is planned out of his sight, and on Gargarus he promises Hera a cloud that not even the Sun could see through (14.342–345; [[cloud-gatherer]], [[helios]], [[the-deception-of-zeus]]).
