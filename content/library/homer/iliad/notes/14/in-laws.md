---
summary: Hera sees Poseidon in the battle, “her own brother and her brother-in-law”. As Zeus' brother he is her δαήρ, the word Helen uses of Hector.
grc: [δαέρα]
---

“Her own brother and her brother-in-law, and she rejoiced in her heart” (αὐτοκασίγνητον καὶ δαέρα, 14.156; see [[kasignetos]], [[poseidon]] and [[hera]]). δαήρ is a husband's brother. Helen calls Hector so (6.344, 6.355), and at the end of the poem she laments him as the dearest to her heart of all her husband's brothers (δαέρων πολὺ φίλτατε πάντων, 24.762). Among the gods the two kinds of kin are one: Poseidon is Hera's brother by birth and her brother-in-law by her marriage to Zeus.
