---
summary: Sleep makes Hera swear with one hand on “the bountiful earth” and the other on the gleaming sea, so that the gods below may be witnesses.
---

“Come now, swear to me by the inviolable water of Styx, / and with one hand take hold of the bountiful earth, / and with the other the gleaming sea, so that all of them may be / witnesses between us, the gods below who are around Cronus” (14.271–274; [[oath]], [[sleep]], [[styx]], [[inviolable]], [[marmareos]], [[cronus]]). Elsewhere the formula is the ground on which men lay down their arms and fall; here the earth is touched in an oath, so that the powers beneath it may hear. Althaea, too, beat the earth with her hands as she called on Hades and Persephone (9.568–569; [[all-nourishing]]). See [[earth]] and [[the-deception-of-zeus]].
