---
summary: Hera and Sleep, crossing from Lemnos to Ida, leave “the city of Lemnos and Imbros” behind them.
---

**On the way to Ida.** When Hera has sworn her oath to Sleep, “the two set off, leaving the city of Lemnos and Imbros, / clothed in mist, swiftly making their way” (14.281–282; see [[hera]], [[sleep]] and [[lemnos]]), and they first touch land at Lectum, the headland of Ida (14.283–284; see [[lectum]]). Imbros lies between Lemnos and the Troad, near the cave where Poseidon left his horses in Book 13, “between Tenedos and rocky Imbros” (13.32–33; see [[poseidon]]). Both gods who help the Achaeans in these books cross the same waters.
