---
summary: “The kings whom Zeus has nurtured,” Diomedes, Odysseus and Agamemnon, all wounded, meet Nestor among the ships.
---

“And the kings whom Zeus has nurtured met Nestor / as they came up from beside the ships, all who had been hit by the bronze: / the son of Tydeus, and Odysseus, and the son of Atreus, Agamemnon” (14.27–29; [[the-wounded-chiefs]], [[kings]], [[nestor]]). The formula of royal dignity, διοτρεφέες βασιλῆες, names three kings who were wounded in Book 11, Agamemnon by Coon, Diomedes by Paris' arrow, and Odysseus by Socus (11.248–253, 11.369–378, 11.434–438). They will go back to the battle wounded, and marshal the army without fighting (14.128–134, 14.379–382).
