---
summary: Before the fallen Hector the Trojans hold up “their well-rounded shields.”
---

“For before that the best stood over him, / Polydamas and Aeneas and brilliant Agenor, / and Sarpedon, leader of the Lycians, and noble Glaucus. / And of the others not one neglected him, but before him / they held their well-rounded shields” (14.424–428; [[hector]], [[shield]], [[shepherd-of-the-people]]). The round shields make a wall around a man who cannot fight, and his companions lift him and carry him out of the toil (14.428–432; [[swift-footed]]).
