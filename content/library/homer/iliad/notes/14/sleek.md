---
summary: Hera binds her fine sandals under her “shining feet,” and promises Sleep a footstool on which to rest his “shining feet” at the banquet; the book adds λιπαρούς.
grc: [λιπαρούς]
---

The dressing verse, “and under her shining feet she bound her fine sandals” (14.186; [[sandals]], [[dressing-scene]], [[hera]]), ends Hera's adornment as it ended Agamemnon's dressing on the morning of the Dream (2.44). Fifty lines later the shining feet are Sleep's: Hephaestus will set a footstool under the golden throne, “on which you may rest your shining feet at the banquet” (14.240–241; [[throne]], [[sleep]], [[imperishable]]). The accusative λιπαρούς is new to the entry.
