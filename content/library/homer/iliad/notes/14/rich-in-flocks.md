---
summary: Ilioneus is the son of “Phorbas, rich in flocks,” whom Hermes loved most of the Trojans and granted wealth.
---

“Who stabbed Ilioneus, / son of Phorbas, rich in flocks, whom most / of the Trojans Hermes loved, and granted him wealth; / and to him the mother bore Ilioneus, an only child” (14.489–492; [[ilioneus]], [[phorbas-father-of-ilioneus]], [[hermes]], [[peneleos]]). The epithet of wealth in flocks, the favor of a god and the gift of possessions (κτῆσις) all describe the father whose only son is about to die. In the Catalogue the epithet went to Iphiclus, the father of Podarces (2.705); here it goes to a Trojan. See [[noble]].
