---
summary: The title names Agamemnon as he speaks to Nestor among the wounded kings, and Poseidon as he shouts like ten thousand men.
---

“And powerful Agamemnon spoke and said to him” (14.41; [[agamemnon]], [[nestor]]): the king, wounded, fears Hector's threat and the anger of his own army (14.42–51). A hundred lines later the same title names a god: “so loud was the voice that the powerful earth-shaker / sent out of his breast” (14.150–151; [[earth-shaker]], [[ten-thousand-men]], [[poseidon]]), as Poseidon put strength into every Achaean. The king who will propose flight and the god who restores the army's courage share the old title of rule.
