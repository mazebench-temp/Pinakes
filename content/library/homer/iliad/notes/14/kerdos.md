---
summary: Nestor's indecision ends in the verse of resolution, “And as he pondered, this seemed to him the better way”.
---

“And as he pondered, this seemed to him the better way: / to go to the son of Atreus” (ὧδε δέ οἱ φρονέοντι δοάσσατο κέρδιον εἶναι, 14.23–24; see [[nestor]], [[pondering-two-ways]] and [[decision-scene]]). The verse settled Deiphobus' choice in the book before, when the better way was to fetch Aeneas (13.458; see the note on Book 13). Here it is to go to the king rather than “into the throng of the Danaans of the swift horses” (14.21–22; see [[agamemnon]]), and the king is already coming up from the ships (14.27–29).
