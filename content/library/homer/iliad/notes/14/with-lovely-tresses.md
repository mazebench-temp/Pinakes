---
summary: Nestor tells Machaon to drink until “Hecamede of the lovely tresses heats a warm bath.”
---

“But you, for now, sit and drink the sparkling wine, / until Hecamede of the lovely tresses heats a warm bath / for you and washes away the clotted blood” (14.5–7; [[hecamede]], [[machaon]], [[clotted-blood]], [[bathing]]). The captive woman who mixed the drink for Nestor and Machaon in Book 11 keeps her epithet (11.624). Later in the book Zeus names Demeter with another word for braided hair, καλλιπλόκαμος, which the translation renders the same way, “of the lovely tresses” (14.326; [[of-the-lovely-tresses]]).
