---
summary: Poseidon names Hector twice by his father in his speech to the Argives: they must not yield the victory to “Hector, son of Priam,” who will not stand his ground.
---

“Argives, are we once again to yield the victory to Hector, / son of Priam, so that he may take the ships and win glory?” (14.364–365) and, at the end of the plan, “I do not think / that Hector, son of Priam, will stand his ground any longer, however eager he is” (14.374–375; [[hector]], [[poseidon]], [[poseidon-rallies-the-achaeans]]). The god names the enemy leader in full, with his patronymic, as a man names the enemy he means to face. See [[priam]].
