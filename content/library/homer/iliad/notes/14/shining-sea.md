---
summary: Agamemnon proposes to haul the nearest ships down and “drag all of them into the shining sea,” to anchor them until night.
---

“The ships that are drawn up first, nearest the sea, / let us haul down, and drag all of them into the shining sea, / and anchor them out on their mooring-stones, until there comes / the immortal night” (14.75–78; [[agamemnon]], [[ships]], [[immortal-night]]). The verse-end εἰς ἅλα δῖαν is the one of Agamemnon's order to launch a ship for Chryse (1.141) and of the army's rush to drag the ships down when he tested them in Book 2 (2.152). What was a test then is here his counsel in earnest, and Odysseus rejects it (14.82–102; [[of-many-wiles]]). See [[sea]].
