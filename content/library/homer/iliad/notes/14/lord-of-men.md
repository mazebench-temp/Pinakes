---
summary: Agamemnon, “lord of men,” proposes to launch the ships and escape by night, yields to Odysseus' rebuke, and leads the wounded kings back to the battle.
---

The title marks the three turns of the scene among the wounded kings. Agamemnon proposes flight: “Then Agamemnon, lord of men, spoke to him in turn” (14.64), and his plan follows, to drag the ships down to the sea and anchor them until night (14.65–81; [[agamemnon]], [[immortal-night]]). He yields to Odysseus: “Then Agamemnon, lord of men, answered him: / ‘Odysseus, you have touched me deeply to the heart with a grievous / rebuke’” (14.103–105; [[odysseus]]). And when Diomedes has given his advice, he leads: “They went, and Agamemnon, lord of men, led them” (14.134; [[the-wounded-chiefs]]). Poseidon meets them at once and takes his right hand (14.135–137; [[poseidon]]).
