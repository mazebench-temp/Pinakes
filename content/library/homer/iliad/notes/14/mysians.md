---
summary: Hyrtius, son of Gyrtius, “leader of the stout-hearted Mysians”, is the first man killed when Poseidon has turned the battle (14.511–512).
---

Μυσῶν ἡγήτορα καρτεροθύμων, “leader of the stout-hearted Mysians” (14.512): Telamonian Ajax kills him, the first name in the list of the slain for which the narrator calls on the Muses (14.508–512; see [[hyrtius]], [[ajax]] and [[invocation-of-the-muses]]). καρτερόθυμος, “stout-hearted”, is elsewhere given to single heroes, Diomedes and Achilles (5.277, 13.350), and in the Odyssey Heracles (*Odyssey* 21.25; see [[karterothymos]]). Hyrtius is not one of the Mysian leaders of the Catalogue, Chromis and the augur Ennomus (2.858; see [[chromis]] and [[ennomus]]).
