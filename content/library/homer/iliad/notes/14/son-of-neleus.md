---
summary: Agamemnon greets Nestor as “Nestor, son of Neleus, great glory of the Achaeans.”
---

“Nestor, son of Neleus, great glory of the Achaeans, / why have you left man-destroying war and come here?” (14.42–43; [[nestor]], [[agamemnon]], [[great-glory-of-the-achaeans]], [[neleus]]). The vocative Νηληϊάδη opens the whole verse of address that Agamemnon used when he came to Nestor's hut at night (10.87) and Idomeneus when he sent Nestor to carry the wounded Machaon from the battle (11.511). Machaon is still with Nestor at the start of this book (14.1–8; [[machaon]]).
