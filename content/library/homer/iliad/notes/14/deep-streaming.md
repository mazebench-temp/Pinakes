---
summary: Hera tells Zeus that she has come to him first, lest he be angry if she went off in silence “to the house of deep-streaming Oceanus.”
---

“For fear you might be angry with me afterward, if in silence / I went off to the house of deep-streaming Oceanus” (14.310–311; [[oceanus]], [[hera]], [[the-deception-of-zeus]]). The verse-end βαθυρρόου Ὠκεανοῖο closes the lie that began with “the limits of the all-nourishing earth” (14.301; [[all-nourishing]]). Earlier in the book Sleep spoke of “the streams of the river / Oceanus, who is the origin of all,” as something he could easily lull to sleep (14.245–246; [[sleep]]).
