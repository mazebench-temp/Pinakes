---
summary: When Zeus is asleep, Sleep runs to the ships “to carry the message to the earth-holder, the shaker of the earth.”
---

“And sweet Sleep went running to the ships of the Achaeans / to carry the message to the earth-holder, the shaker of the earth” (14.354–355; [[sleep]], [[sweet-sleep]], [[shaker-of-the-earth]], [[poseidon]]). The line-end formula γαιήοχος ἐννοσίγαιος stands in the dative, naming the god to whom the message is sent: “Now, Poseidon, help the Danaans with a ready heart, / and grant them glory, if only for a little while, while he still sleeps” (14.357–358; [[poseidon-rallies-the-achaeans]]). The two titles of Poseidon's power over the earth come at the moment when Zeus' power is suspended. See [[the-deception-of-zeus]].
