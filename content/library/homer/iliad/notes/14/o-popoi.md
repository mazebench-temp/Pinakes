---
summary: Agamemnon's cry when he meets Nestor with the wall down and the battle at the ships: “Ah, truly the other well-greaved Achaeans too / are laying up anger against me in their hearts, just as Achilles is”.
---

The cry comes in the middle of Agamemnon's first speech of the book, when he has told Nestor his fear that Hector's threat to burn the ships is being fulfilled: “Ah, truly the other well-greaved Achaeans too / are laying up anger against me in their hearts, just as Achilles is, / and are not willing to fight at the sterns of the ships” (ὢ πόποι ἦ ῥα καὶ ἄλλοι, 14.49–51; see [[agamemnon]] and [[cholos]]). ὢ πόποι is a cry of dismay, followed as usual by ἦ, “truly” (see the article above), and Agamemnon's dismay is that the anger of Book 1 may have spread from Achilles to the whole army. Nestor does not take it up; he answers only that the wall is down (14.53–63).
