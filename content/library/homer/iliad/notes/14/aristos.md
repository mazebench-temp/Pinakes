summary: Hera's plan comes in the verse of the best plan; Aphrodite calls Zeus “the greatest of all”; Poseidon orders the best and biggest shields for the best men; and the best of the Trojans stand over the fallen Hector.
grc: ["τοῦ ἀρίστου"]
en: [the greatest of all]
---

**The best plan.** “And this plan seemed to her in her heart the best” (ἥδε δέ οἱ κατὰ θυμὸν ἀρίστη φαίνετο βουλή, 14.161) is the verse of Zeus' plan to send the Dream and of Agamemnon's to go to Nestor by night (2.5, 10.17; see [[boule]] and [[decision-scene]]).

**The greatest of all.** Aphrodite cannot refuse Hera, “for you sleep in the arms of Zeus, the greatest of all” (Ζηνὸς γὰρ τοῦ ἀρίστου, 14.213; see [[aphrodite]] and [[zeus]]). The phrase τοῦ ἀρίστου occurs only here in Homer.

**The best arms and the best men.** Poseidon bids the Argives put on “the shields that are best in the army and biggest” (ἀσπίδες ὅσσαι ἄρισται, 14.371), and the kings share out the armor so that “the brave man put on the good, and gave the worse to the worse man” (14.382; see [[exchange-of-armor]]). On the other side, when Hector falls, “the best stood over him” (περίβησαν ἄριστοι, 14.424): Polydamas, Aeneas, Agenor, Sarpedon and Glaucus (14.425–426; see [[hector]]).
