---
summary: Diomedes is “the son of Tydeus” in the list of the wounded kings, and in the same book he tells his father's story.
---

“The son of Tydeus, and Odysseus, and the son of Atreus, Agamemnon” (14.29, 14.380; [[diomedes]], [[the-wounded-chiefs]], [[son-of-atreus]]). The patronymic that names Diomedes in the list becomes his argument when he speaks: “but I too claim to be of the line of a brave father, / Tydeus, whom the heaped earth covers at Thebes” (14.113–114; [[heaped-earth]]). He goes on to tell how Tydeus settled in Argos, married a daughter of Adrastus, grew rich, and surpassed all the Achaeans with the spear (14.119–125; [[tydeus]], [[adrastus]], [[wealthy]]).
