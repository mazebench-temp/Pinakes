---
summary: When Hera's perfumed oil was stirred “in the bronze-floored house of Zeus,” its fragrance reached to earth and heaven.
---

Hera anoints herself in her own chamber, which Hephaestus built with a secret bolt (14.166–169; [[closely-fitted]]), with an oil so fragrant that “when it was stirred in the bronze-floored house of Zeus, / even so, its fragrance reached to earth and heaven” (14.173–174; [[ambrosial]], [[hera]]). The house of Zeus, which in Book 1 was the goal of Thetis' appeal (1.426), is here the measure of a scent: stirred there, it filled the world. See [[zeus]] and [[dressing-scene]].
