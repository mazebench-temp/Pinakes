---
summary: Odysseus asks Agamemnon whether he is so eager “to leave the wide-streeted city of the Trojans / behind.”
---

“Are you so eager, then, to leave the wide-streeted city of the Trojans / behind, for whose sake we have suffered so many evils?” (14.88–89; [[odysseus]], [[agamemnon]], [[troy]]). In Book 2 the wide-streeted city was always the object of the verb “take,” in the Dream's false promise, in Agamemnon's pretended despair and in Calchas' prophecy (2.12, 2.141, 2.329); here it is the city the Achaeans would leave behind.
