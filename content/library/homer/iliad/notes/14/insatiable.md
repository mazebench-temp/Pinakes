---
summary: Acamas calls the Argives “insatiable of threats,” the one use of the epithet for anything but battle.
---

“Argives, you arrow-fighters, insatiable of threats” (14.479; [[acamas]], [[arrow-fighters]]). Every other use in Homer names men who never have enough of battle or war (7.117, 12.335, 13.621, 13.639, 20.2). In Book 13 Menelaus had complained that the Trojans are insatiable of battle (13.639; [[satiety-of-all-things]]); here a Trojan answers that the Argives are insatiable only of threats.
