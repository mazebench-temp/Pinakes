summary: Polydamas, “spear-brandishing,” comes to the defense of the fallen Satnius and kills Prothoenor; the book adds the nominative ἐγχέσπαλος.
grc: [ἐγχέσπαλος]
---

“And to his defense came spear-brandishing Polydamas, / the son of Panthous, and he hit Prothoenor on the right shoulder” (14.449–450; [[polydamas]], [[panthous]], [[prothoenor]], [[satnius]]). The epithet that Agamemnon gave the Trojan allies in Book 2 (2.131; [[trojan-allies]]) is here the epithet of one man, as he comes to stand over a fallen comrade; in Book 15 it is Ares' in a simile of Hector's rage (15.605). The nominative ἐγχέσπαλος is new to the entry.
