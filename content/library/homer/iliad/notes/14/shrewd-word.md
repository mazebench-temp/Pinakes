---
summary: As Zeus sees Hera on Gargarus, desire wraps “his shrewd mind,” πυκινὰς φρένας; the book adds the phrase.
grc: ["πυκινὰς φρένας"]
---

“And as he saw her, so desire wrapped his shrewd mind, / as when they first lay together in love, / going to bed together without the knowledge of their dear parents” (14.294–296; [[zeus]], [[eros]], [[philotes]], [[hera]]). The adjective of the shrewd word and the shrewd plan (2.55, 10.302) here describes the mind itself, at the moment it is overcome. Hera's plan had named the same mind with another formula, “his shrewd mind” (φρεσὶ πευκαλίμῃσι, 14.165; [[shrewd-mind]]), and the strap she carries holds “the persuasion that steals the mind even of men who think shrewdly” (πύκα περ φρονεόντων, 14.217; [[aphrodites-band]], [[peitho]]). The phrase πυκινὰς φρένας is new to the entry.
