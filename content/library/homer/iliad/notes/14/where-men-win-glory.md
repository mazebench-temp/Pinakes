---
summary: From Olympus Hera sees Poseidon “busying himself in the battle where men win glory.”
---

“And at once she knew / the one busying himself in the battle where men win glory, / her own brother and her brother-in-law, and she rejoiced in her heart” (14.154–156; [[poseidon]], [[hera]], [[golden-throned]]). The formula μάχην ἀνὰ κυδιάνειραν is used of a god who is helping one side in disguise, in the likeness of an old man (14.135–136). ποιπνύοντα, “busying himself,” is the word of Hephaestus bustling through the hall with the wine in Book 1 (1.600).
