---
summary: Acamas, whom Ajax kills in the first lines of Book 6, is “the best among the Thracians”.
grc: [Θρῄκεσσι]
---

Ajax's first kill of the book is the Thracians' leader: “the man who was the best among the Thracians, the son of Eussorus, Acamas, tall and brave” (6.7–8; see [[acamas-of-thrace]] and [[eussorus]]). In the Trojan catalogue Acamas and Peirous led the Thracians “whom the strong-flowing Hellespont encloses” (2.844–845). Peirous fell in the first battle (4.517–538; see [[peirous]]), and Ares took the likeness of Acamas to rouse the sons of Priam (5.461–462). With Acamas dead, neither leader of the Hellespont Thracians is left, and the next Thracians of the poem are newcomers, Rhesus and his men, camped “apart, newly come, the farthest out of all” (10.434).
