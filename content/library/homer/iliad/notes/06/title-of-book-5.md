Diomedes still dominates the first half of Book 6. He kills Axylus and his charioteer (6.12–19), Helenus calls him the strongest of the Achaeans and sends Hector to have the women pray that Athena may keep him from the city (6.96–101), he meets Glaucus between the armies (6.119–236), and the robe from Sidon is laid on Athena's knees with a prayer for his fall (6.297–311). The later title of Book 6 names only its end (see [[title-of-book-6]]).
