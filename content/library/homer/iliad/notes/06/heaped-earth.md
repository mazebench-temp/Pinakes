---
summary: “The heaped earth,” the earth piled over a grave, in Hector's wish to be dead before Andromache is dragged into slavery.
---

“But may I be dead, and the heaped earth cover me, / before I hear your cries as they drag you away” (6.464–465; [[hector]], [[andromache]], [[captive-women]]). It is the phrase's first use in the poem.
