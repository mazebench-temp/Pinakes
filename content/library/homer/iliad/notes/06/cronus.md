---
summary: Cronus is named in Book 6 only through his son. “The son of Cronus” blinds Lycurgus and takes away Glaucus' wits, and Hector will not pray to “the son of Cronus, lord of the dark clouds” with blood on his hands.
---

Zeus is named through his father three times in Book 6, and each time as a power to be feared. As Κρόνου πάϊς, “the son of Cronus”, he blinds Lycurgus for attacking Dionysus (6.139; see [[lycurgus]]). Odysseus used the same phrase with Cronus' epithet, “the son of crooked-counseling Cronus” (2.205, 2.319; see [[crooked-counseling]]); here it stands alone. As Κρονίδης he takes away Glaucus' wits, so that Glaucus gives gold armor for bronze (6.234; see [[glaucus]]). And Hector will not pour wine or pray with unwashed hands to Κρονίων, the son of Cronus, “lord of the dark clouds” (κελαινεφέϊ Κρονίωνι, 6.267; see [[lord-of-dark-clouds]]). See [[son-of-cronus]] and [[zeus]].
