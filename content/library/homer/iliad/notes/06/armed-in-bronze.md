---
summary: The epithet names Sarpedon at his birth, the only time it is not Hector's, and Hector in the verse that makes Andromache his wife.
grc: [χαλκοκορυστήν]
---

In Glaucus' genealogy Laodameia “bore godlike Sarpedon, armed in bronze” (6.199; [[sarpedon]], [[antitheos]]). It is the only use of the word in the Iliad that does not name Hector. The other use in Book 6 is his line-end formula: Andromache is the daughter of Eetion, “it was his daughter who was the wife of Hector armed in bronze” (ἔχεθʼ Ἕκτορι χαλκοκορυστῇ, 6.398; [[andromache]], [[eetion]]). The bronze of the formula is what frightens his son seventy lines later: the child shrinks back “afraid of the bronze and the plume of horsehair” (6.469; [[horsehair-crested]], [[hector]]).
