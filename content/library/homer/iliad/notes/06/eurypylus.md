---
summary: Eurypylus kills and strips Melanthius, the last of the fourteen men killed in the list that opens Book 6 (6.36).
passages:
  - 6.35-36 | Eurypylus kills Melanthius
---

{{quote:1c68aa02-4f99-5a1b-987d-cdaa11a69c17}}

Eurypylus closes the list as he closed the six leaders' kills at the start of Book 5 (5.76–83). There he ran down the priest's son Hypsenor and cut off his arm; here he gets half a line and one verb, ἐξενάριξεν, “slew and stripped” (6.36; see [[melanthius-the-trojan]] and [[stripping-the-dead]]). The next Trojan in the rout is not killed at once but taken alive (6.37–38; see [[adrastus-the-trojan]]).
