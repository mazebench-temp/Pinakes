---
summary: Hector hopes to set up the mixing bowl of freedom “for the everlasting gods of heaven” when the Achaeans have been driven from Troy.
grc: [θεοῖς αἰειγενέτῃσι]
---

“If ever Zeus grants us, for the everlasting gods of heaven, to set up the mixing bowl of freedom in our halls” (ἐπουρανίοισι θεοῖς αἰειγενέτῃσι, 6.527–528; [[heavenly-gods]], [[mixing-bowl]], [[eleutheros]]). In Book 3 the everlasting gods received the wine of the oath poured out by both armies (3.295–296). Here they are to receive a bowl of thanksgiving that will never be set up: the verse joins two epithets of the gods' permanence, “of heaven” and “everlasting,” in a hope for the city that will fall ([[a-day-will-come]], [[hector]]).
