---
summary: Arisbe is the home of Axylus, son of Teuthras, the rich man who kept open house beside the road and whom Diomedes kills (6.12–19).
grc: [Ἀρίσβῃ]
---

ἐϋκτιμένῃ ἐν Ἀρίσβῃ, “in well-built Arisbe” (6.13; see [[well-built]]). The Catalogue called the town “shining” and made it the home of Asius and his horses (2.836–839; see [[asius]]). Its one citizen in Book 6 is a host, “a friend to all men”, who lived beside the road and welcomed everyone who came by (6.14–15; see [[axylus]] and [[xenia]]). The lines that describe him echo those on Diocles, who “lived in well-built Pherae” (5.543).
