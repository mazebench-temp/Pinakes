---
summary: Telamonian Ajax is the first of the Achaean leaders to kill his man when the battle is left to the mortals, breaking a Trojan battalion and striking down the Thracian Acamas.
---

The list of killings that opens Book 6 begins with the greater Ajax, and with a full verse of his titles: “Telamonian Ajax, the bulwark of the Achaeans, was the first to break a battalion of the Trojans” (6.5–6; [[bulwark-of-the-achaeans]]). He brings “light” (φόως) to his companions, relief in the fighting, by killing the best of the Thracians, Acamas son of Eussorus, with a spear through the helmet into the forehead (6.6–11; [[acamas-of-thrace]], [[horsehair-crested]], [[darkness-covered-his-eyes]]). In Book 5 Ajax was driven back from the body of Amphius (5.615–626). Here, first of the leaders, he breaks the Trojan line ([[ajax]], [[androktasia]]).
