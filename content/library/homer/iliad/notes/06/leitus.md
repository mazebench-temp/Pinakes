The Boeotian leader's only kill in the Iliad takes half a line. Λήϊτος ἥρως, “the hero Leitus” (6.35; see [[heros]]), catches a man in flight (φεύγοντʼ, 6.36; see [[phylacus-the-trojan]]). The verb is ἕλε, literally “took”, the verb of the first kill of the poem, Antilochus' (ἕλεν, 4.457). Leitus returns only twice: Poseidon rouses him with Teucer and others at the ships (13.91), and in the fight over Patroclus Hector wounds him in the wrist and he leaves the battle (17.601–604).
