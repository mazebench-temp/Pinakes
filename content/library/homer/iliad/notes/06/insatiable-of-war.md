---
summary: Ares, “insatiable of war,” kills Bellerophon's son Isander as he fights the Solymi.
---

“And Ares, insatiable of war, killed his son Isander as he fought the glorious Solymi; and Artemis of the golden reins killed his daughter in her anger” (6.203–205; [[isander]], [[kydalimos]], [[of-the-golden-reins]]). When Bellerophon was hated by all the gods, the gods' hatred fell on his children: the war god takes the son in battle, and the goddess of women's deaths takes the daughter (6.200–205; [[bellerophon]]). In Book 5 the formula named Ares at his two humiliations, bound in a jar and wounded by Diomedes (5.388, 5.863). In Glaucus' story he has his full power ([[ares]]).
