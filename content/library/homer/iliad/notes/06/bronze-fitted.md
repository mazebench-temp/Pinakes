---
summary: The battle left to the mortals surges across the plain as the two armies aim their “bronze-fitted spears” at one another.
grc: [χαλκήρεα]
---

“And again and again the battle surged this way and that across the plain as they aimed their bronze-fitted spears at one another between the streams of the Simoeis and the Xanthus” (χαλκήρεα δοῦρα, 6.2–4; [[spear]], [[simoeis]], [[scamander]]). In Book 3 the epithet described the helmet in which the lots were shaken for the duel (3.316). Here it opens the fighting of Book 6, before any man is named: the spears are fitted with bronze points, and the next verses tell whose they were ([[androktasia]], [[dread-din-of-battle]]).
