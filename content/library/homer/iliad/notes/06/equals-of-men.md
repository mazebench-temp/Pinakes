---
summary: Bellerophon's third labor is to kill the Amazons, “the equals of men.”
grc: [ἀντιανείρας]
---

“Third, he killed the Amazons, the equals of men” (6.186; [[amazons]], [[bellerophon]]). This is the second and last time the Iliad names the Amazons. In Book 3 Priam remembered them as the enemy he fought as a young man, beside the Phrygians on the Sangarius (3.184–189; [[priam-and-the-amazons]]). In Glaucus' story they are one of the tasks set to kill a hero, after the Chimaera and the Solymi. Both times the epithet praises them as a match for men in war.
