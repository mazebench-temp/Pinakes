---
summary: The robes in Hecuba's storeroom came from Sidon with Paris, “sailing over the wide sea,” εὐρέα πόντον, a related formula of the sea's breadth.
grc: [εὐρέα πόντον]
en: [wide sea]
---

Paris brought the Sidonian women and their robes to Troy, “sailing over the wide sea on that voyage on which he brought back Helen” (ἐπιπλὼς εὐρέα πόντον, 6.291–292; [[sidon]], [[the-abduction-of-helen]]). εὐρύς (“wide, broad”) is the adjective of the “broad back of the sea,” and εὐρέα πόντον ends the verse twice more in Homer (Il. 9.72; Od. 24.118). The sea's breadth is here the distance Helen was carried ([[sea]], [[daughter-of-a-noble-father]]).
