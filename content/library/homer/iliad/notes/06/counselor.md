---
summary: “Zeus the counselor” lay with Bellerophon's daughter Laodameia, who bore him Sarpedon.
---

“With Laodameia Zeus the counselor lay, and she bore godlike Sarpedon, armed in bronze” (6.198–199; [[laodameia]], [[sarpedon]], [[son-of-zeus]]). The god of counsel enters Glaucus' genealogy as the father of his cousin and fellow commander. The same god's plan will cost that son his life: in Book 16 Zeus considers saving Sarpedon, and Hera persuades him to let him die (Il. 16.431–461; [[plan-of-zeus]]). His mother Laodameia is killed by Artemis in the next verses (6.205; [[of-the-golden-reins]]).
