---
summary: Diomedes ends the story of Lycurgus with his conclusion: “Nor would I wish to fight with the blessed gods.”
---

“Nor would I wish to fight with the blessed gods” (6.141). In Book 5 Diomedes told Athena that she would not let him fight “face to face with the blessed gods” (5.819), and on that day he wounded two of them with her leave (5.330–351, 5.855–859; [[gods-wounded-by-mortals]]). In Book 6 he gives his own reason, the fate of Lycurgus, and his aristeia is over ([[the-aristeia]], [[who-fights-the-gods]]). Three verses earlier the gods are “the gods who live at ease” (6.138; [[who-live-at-ease]]); the ease of the blessed is the other side of their power to punish.
