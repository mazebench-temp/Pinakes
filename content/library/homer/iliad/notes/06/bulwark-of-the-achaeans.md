---
summary: The narrator gives Ajax his title as he opens the fighting of Book 6, the first to break a Trojan battalion.
---

“Telamonian Ajax, the bulwark of the Achaeans, was the first to break a battalion of the Trojans” (6.5–6). In Book 3 Helen named him with the same formula from the wall (3.229); here the narrator uses it in action. The wall of the Achaeans is the man who breaks the enemy's line, and his first victim is the best of the Thracians ([[acamas-of-thrace]], [[telamonian]]). The book of the Iliad that turns from the battlefield to the city and its own walls begins with the Achaean who is himself called a wall ([[ajax]], [[the-tower-of-ilios]]).
