---
summary: The epithet names the Lapith Polypoetes as he kills Astyalus in the list of killings that opens Book 6.
---

“And Polypoetes, steadfast in battle, killed Astyalus” (6.29; [[polypoetes]], [[astyalus]]). In the Catalogue the epithet introduced him as a leader of the Lapiths (2.740), and in the list of Book 6 it is all that is said of him: one verse, one killing, and nothing about the victim but his name. The list gives each Achaean leader a man, and several of them a formula ([[androktasia]], [[the-war-in-small]]).
