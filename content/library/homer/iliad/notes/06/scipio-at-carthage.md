---
summary: The two lines Scipio spoke over burning Carthage are also Hector's, who says them to Andromache of his own city, and grieves more for her captivity than for Troy.
passages:
  - 6.447-465 | Hector foretells the fall of Troy
---

Strabo quotes the first of the two lines as Homer's plain statement that Ilios was destroyed, against the claim of the Ilians of his day that their city had never been wiped out (13.1.41; see [[strabo]] and [[a-day-will-come]]).
