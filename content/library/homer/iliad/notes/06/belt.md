---
summary: Oeneus gave Bellerophon a belt “bright with purple” as his guest-gift (6.219). In Book 7 Ajax gives Hector a belt in the same words, in exchange for a sword.
passages:
  - 6.215-221 | Oeneus and Bellerophon
---

“Oeneus gave a belt bright with purple” (Οἰνεὺς μὲν ζωστῆρα δίδου φοίνικι φαεινόν, 6.219). The ζωστήρ is a warrior's belt, and it is the host's gift to a warrior guest; the guest gave back a golden cup (6.220; see [[cup]] and [[guest-gifts]]). φοῖνιξ is the purple dye, and the belt was colored with it (see [[purple]]). The same verse, with Ajax in the place of Oeneus, ends the duel of Ajax and Hector in Book 7. The two agree to part as friends and exchange gifts, and Hector gives a silver-studded sword with its scabbard and strap, “and Ajax gave a belt bright with purple” (Αἴας δὲ ζωστῆρα δίδου φοίνικι φαεινόν, 7.303–305). The gift of a guest-friendship two generations old becomes the gift between enemies who stop fighting for a night (see [[belt]] and [[xenia]]).
