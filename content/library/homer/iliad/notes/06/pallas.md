---
summary: “Pallas Athena shook her head”: the goddess refuses the Trojan women's prayer in a single verse.
---

“So she spoke in prayer, but Pallas Athena shook her head” (ἀνένευε δὲ Παλλὰς Ἀθήνη, 6.311). The verse has the shape of the formula that ends a heard prayer, “So he spoke in prayer, and Pallas Athena heard him” (5.121, and in the plural Il. 10.295; [[prayer]]), with the verb of refusal in place of the verb of hearing. ἀνανεύω is the upward nod of “no,” the opposite of κατανεύω, the nod of assent that Thetis asked of Zeus and received (1.514, 1.524–528). The robe, the vow of twelve cows and Theano's prayer are all refused ([[the-robe-for-athena]], [[never-touched-by-the-goad]], [[athena]]).
