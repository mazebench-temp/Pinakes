---
summary: The gods gave Bellerophon “beauty and lovely manhood,” and the wife of Proetus desires him.
---

“To him the gods gave beauty and lovely manhood; but Proetus devised evil against him in his heart” (6.156–157; [[bellerophon]], [[proetus]]). ἠνορέη is “manhood, manly strength,” and the epithet of desire, from the root of ἔραμαι (“love”), makes it desirable. The next verses show who desired it: “The wife of Proetus, brilliant Anteia, was mad with longing to lie with him in secret love” (6.160–161; [[anteia]], [[eros]]). The gods' gift is the beginning of Bellerophon's troubles ([[beauty-and-its-price]]).
