---
summary: Nestor calls the army “Friends, Danaan heroes, attendants of Ares,” and tells them to kill now and strip the dead later.
---

The whole verse of address that Agamemnon used to begin his test of the army in Book 2 (2.110) opens Nestor's exhortation here: “Friends, Danaan heroes, attendants of Ares, let no one now fling himself on the spoils and stay behind so as to come back to the ships carrying the most; but let us kill men” (6.67–70; [[exhortation]], [[nestor]]). The servants of the war god are told to serve him first and take their own profit afterward, “at your ease” (6.70–71; [[stripping-the-dead]]). The speech follows at once on Agamemnon's killing of Adrastus: after ransom has been refused, spoil is postponed ([[adrastus-the-trojan]]).
