---
summary: Menelaus is on the point of sending the captured Adrastus “to the swift ships of the Achaeans” when Agamemnon comes running.
---

The formula θοὰς ἐπὶ νῆας Ἀχαιῶν names the camp as the place where a captive is kept for ransom. Menelaus was persuaded, “and now he was on the point of sending him to the swift ships of the Achaeans, giving him to his attendant to lead down” (6.52–53). Adrastus himself had spoken of the ships: his father would pay if he heard that his son was alive “at the ships of the Achaeans” (6.50). He never reaches them ([[adrastus-the-trojan]], [[boundless-ransom]], [[ships]]).
