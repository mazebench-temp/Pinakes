---
summary: Diomedes speaks to Glaucus, “the shepherd of the people,” with soothing words, and proposes that they be guest-friends.
---

“And spoke to the shepherd of the people with soothing words” (6.214; [[soothing-words]]). The title of kings, which Books 2 and 4 gave to Agamemnon, is given to the Lycian, who shares the command of his people with Sarpedon (2.876; [[glaucus]], [[lycians]]). It comes at the moment the enemy becomes a friend: the next words are Diomedes' discovery that they are guest-friends from their fathers' time (6.215; [[xenia]], [[glaucus-and-diomedes]]).
