---
summary: The serpent of Book 6 is the hind part of the Chimaera, “a lion in front, a serpent behind, and in the middle a she-goat” (6.181).
---

In Book 2 the serpent was a portent sent by Zeus at Aulis, and in Book 3 a danger met in a mountain glen (2.308–320, 3.33–37). In Book 6 it is part of a monster. The Chimaera that Bellerophon killed in Lycia was πρόσθε λέων, ὄπιθεν δὲ δράκων, μέσση δὲ χίμαιρα, “a lion in front, a serpent behind, and in the middle a she-goat”, and she breathed out fire (6.179–183; see [[chimaera]], [[lion]], [[goats]] and [[bellerophon-in-lycia]]). Homer does not say what the serpent's part was. The *Theogony* gives the Chimaera three heads, one of them “of a mighty serpent” (*Theogony* 321–322), and [[apollodorus|Apollodorus]] gives her a serpent's tail (*Library* 2.3.1). Bellerophon killed her “obeying the portents of the gods” (θεῶν τεράεσσι πιθήσας, 6.183; see [[teras]]).
