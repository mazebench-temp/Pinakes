---
summary: Sarpedon is “godlike” in Glaucus' genealogy, in the verse of his birth as the son of Zeus and Laodameia.
---

“And she bore godlike Sarpedon, armed in bronze” (6.199; [[sarpedon]], [[laodameia]], [[armed-in-bronze]]). In Book 5 the honorific named Sarpedon three times as he fought and was carried from the field (5.629, 5.663, 5.692). Here it stands in the verse of his birth, and for once the praise is literal: the man called a match for the gods is the son of Zeus (6.198; [[counselor]], [[son-of-zeus]]).
