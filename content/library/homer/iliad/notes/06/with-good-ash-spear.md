---
summary: Hector foresees the fall of “Priam, and the people of Priam of the good ash spear,” in the verses that Agamemnon spoke in Book 4.
---

“For I know this well in my mind and in my heart: a day will come when sacred Ilios will perish, and Priam, and the people of Priam of the good ash spear” (6.447–449). The three verses are Agamemnon's in Book 4, where he foretold the punishment of the oath-breakers (4.163–165), and the verse with the epithet is also the one in which Zeus spoke of his love for the city (4.47). Hector speaks them of his own city and father, and goes on to say that it is not their sorrow that troubles him most, but his wife's (6.450–455; [[a-day-will-come]], [[hieros]], [[priam]], [[foresight]]).
