---
summary: Hector, rallying the Trojans, goes through the army “brandishing his sharp spears,” in the verses that described his rally in Book 5.
---

“And at once he leapt from his chariot to the ground in his armor, and brandishing his sharp spears he went everywhere through the army” (6.103–104). The three verses of Hector's rally are the same as in Book 5, where the plural ὀξέα first described his spears (5.494–496; [[dread-din-of-battle]], [[exhortation]]). A warrior who carries two spears can throw one and keep one, as Paris did when he stepped out in Book 3 (3.18; [[spear]], [[hector]]).
