---
summary: Hector will not pray to “the son of Cronus, lord of the dark clouds,” while he is spattered with blood.
---

“And with unwashed hands I shrink from pouring sparkling wine to Zeus; nor may a man in any way offer the son of Cronus, lord of the dark clouds, a prayer when he is spattered with blood and gore” (6.266–268; [[hand-washing]], [[libation]], [[son-of-cronus]]). The storm god's title stands against the blood on a warrior's hands. Before a prayer or a libation the hands are washed, as before the prayer at Chryse (1.449) and before Achilles' prayer for Patroclus (Il. 16.228–230; [[prayer]], [[purification]]). Hector keeps the rule even in haste, and sends his mother to pray in his place (6.269–278).
