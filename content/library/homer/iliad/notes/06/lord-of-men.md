---
summary: Agamemnon, lord of men, kills Elatus of Pedasus in the list of killings that opens Book 6, just before he refuses the ransom of Adrastus.
---

“And Agamemnon, lord of men, slew Elatus; he lived beside the banks of the fair-flowing Satnioeis, in steep Pedasus” (6.33–35; [[elatus]], [[satnioeis]], [[pedasus-on-the-satnioeis]], [[fair-flowing]], [[steep]]). As in Book 5 (5.38), the commander's title marks his first kill of the day. A few lines later the same king runs up to stop his brother from sparing a suppliant, and there the narrator calls him “the hero” and “powerful Agamemnon” (6.53–65; [[powerful]], [[adrastus-the-trojan]]).
