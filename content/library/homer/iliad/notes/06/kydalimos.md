---
summary: Glaucus twice calls the Solymi “glorious”: the people whom Bellerophon fought in his second labor, and against whom his son Isander died.
grc: [κυδαλίμοισι]
---

The same verse-end names the enemy in two generations. Bellerophon's second labor: “Second, he fought with the glorious Solymi; that, he said, was the fiercest battle of men he ever entered” (6.184–185). And his son's death: “Ares, insatiable of war, killed his son Isander as he fought the glorious Solymi” (6.203–204; [[solymi]], [[isander]], [[insatiable-of-war]]). The father's hardest battle is the son's last. The epithet of glory, which Book 4 gave to Menelaus (4.100, 4.177), praises the enemy whose defeat was an achievement ([[kydos]]).
