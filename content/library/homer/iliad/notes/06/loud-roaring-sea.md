---
summary: Helen wishes that on the day she was born a storm had swept her away into the wave of the loud-roaring sea.
---

“How I wish that on the day when my mother first bore me an evil storm of wind had carried me away to a mountain or into the wave of the loud-roaring sea, where the wave would have swept me away before these things came to pass” (6.345–348; [[helen]]). In Book 1 the sea roared beside a priest who could not answer a king (1.34). Here it is the grave Helen wishes she had found on the day of her birth, before the war was fought for her ([[who-is-to-blame]], [[sea]]).
