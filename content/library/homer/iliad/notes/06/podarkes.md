---
summary: Andromache gives Achilles his full formula, “brilliant swift-footed Achilles,” in the verse that tells how he killed her seven brothers in one day.
---

“And the seven brothers I had in our halls all went down on a single day into the house of Hades; for brilliant swift-footed Achilles killed them all among their shambling cattle and white sheep” (6.421–424; [[sack-of-thebe]], [[shambling]], [[white]]). The whole-line formula ποδάρκης δῖος Ἀχιλλεύς, which in Books 1 and 2 named Achilles as he answered Agamemnon and as he lay idle by his ships (1.121, 2.688), here names him in a victim's account of his work. His speed is what caught seven brothers among their herds ([[brilliant]], [[achilles]], [[andromache]]).
