---
summary: Dawn opens the first day of battle in an unusual way. Instead of rising from her bed, she climbs high Olympus to announce the light to Zeus and the other gods.
---

{{quote:10963bc5-a466-55e2-b6a6-770a3e43f573}}

The day she announces is the first day of fighting in the poem, and it does not end until Book 7. Here Dawn does not rise “rosy-fingered” from the sea or from Tithonus' bed, as in the usual formulas (1.477, 11.1–2). She goes up to Olympus as a messenger to the gods, while below Agamemnon has the heralds summon the army (2.50–52). See [[dawn-line]] and [[high-olympus]].
