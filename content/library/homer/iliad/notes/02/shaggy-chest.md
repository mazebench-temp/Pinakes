Πυλαιμένεος λάσιον κῆρ, “the shaggy heart of Pylaemenes,” names the man by the seat of his courage, as βίη Ἡρακληείη names Heracles by his strength ([[might-of-heracles]]). The heart (κῆρ) sits in the chest, and the chest of a strong man is hairy; the phrase joins the two. The same expression describes Patroclus' heart in Book 16 (Il. 16.554).
