summary: The word occurs once in Book 2, in Agamemnon's prayer that Hector's companions may fall around him in the dust.
grc: [ἑταῖροι]
---

ἑταῖρος (“companion”) occurs only once in Book 2, and of the enemy. Before the battle Agamemnon sacrifices an ox to Zeus and prays that before sunset he may burn Priam's hall, tear Hector's tunic with the bronze, and see “many of his companions around him” fall headlong in the dust and bite the earth (2.412–418). A leader's companions stand and fall around him. Zeus accepts the offering but does not grant the prayer (2.419–420). See [[hector]] and [[prayer]].
