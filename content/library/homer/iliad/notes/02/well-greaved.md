summary: Odysseus uses the formula once, in his appeal to the army to stay at Troy.
---

{{quote:03c00703-208c-55c1-aa7f-9cc5fe7297b3}}

Odysseus ends his account of the portent at Aulis with a call to the whole army by its formal name. The well-greaved Achaeans are the army ready for battle, and he asks them to stay until Priam's city falls. The formula is the counterpart of Chryses' address in Book 1 ({{ref:Iliad 1.17}}). There a priest wished the army the sack of Troy; here Odysseus holds it to that goal.
