---
summary: The Catalogues give the word to two peoples, the Abantes of Euboea and the Cicones of Thrace.
grc: [αἰχμηταὶ]
---

{{quote:a4e4786f-95d8-5b2e-af90-29bd60829ecd}}

The Abantes are “spearmen, eager with outstretched ash spears to tear the corselets around the chests of their enemies.” The Catalogue describes few peoples by their way of fighting; the Abantes are described in detail ([[breathing-fury]], [[swift]], [[long-haired]]). On the Trojan side Euphemus leads “the Cicones, spearmen” ({{ref:Iliad 2.846}}), in the same genitive αἰχμητάων that Achilles used of the Trojans in Book 1.
