---
summary: The Catalogue names Ephyra on the river Selleis as the city from which Heracles carried off Astyocheia, the mother of Tlepolemus (2.657–660).
---

Tlepolemus' mother, Astyocheia, was brought by Heracles “from Ephyra, from the river Selleis”, when he had sacked many cities (2.658–660; see [[astyocheia]], [[tlepolemus]], [[heracles]] and [[selleis-ephyra]]). The half-verse returns of the corselet that Phyleus brought from the same place (15.531; see the note on Book 15).
