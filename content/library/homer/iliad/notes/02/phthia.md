The Catalogue gives most leaders a list of towns. Achilles is given regions. [[pelasgian-argos|Pelasgian Argos]], Phthia and [[Hellas]] are districts, and only three names, [[Alus]], [[Alope]] and [[Trachis]], are clearly towns. The borders are vague, and scholars have long disagreed about how Achilles' realm fits with the kingdoms of [[Protesilaus]] and [[Eumelus]] that follow it in the list. Phthia itself was probably the valley of the Spercheius and the country around it: Peleus vowed his son's hair to the river Spercheius if he came home (23.141–151).

The people of these lands have three names, "Myrmidons and Hellenes and Achaeans" (see [[myrmidons]] and [[hellenes]]), and they fill fifty ships. The number is respectable but not the largest: Agamemnon brings a hundred and Nestor ninety. The Catalogue is more interested in why Achilles' men are not on the field at all (2.686–694; see [[achilles-withdraws]]).

In Book 1 Achilles threatened to sail home to Phthia (1.169–171). In Book 2 the whole army nearly does what he threatened, rushing to the ships to sail home (2.142–154), until Odysseus turns it back.
