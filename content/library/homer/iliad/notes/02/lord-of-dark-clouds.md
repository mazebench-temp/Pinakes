---
summary: Agamemnon invokes Zeus as lord of the dark clouds in his prayer before battle.
grc: [κελαινεφὲς]
---

{{quote:e7ee5ec4-5e3e-5deb-8295-b58b728f5a85}}

The vocative κελαινεφές stands in a row of titles: most glorious, greatest, lord of the dark clouds, dwelling in the upper air ([[most-glorious]], [[dwelling-in-the-upper-air]]). Agamemnon asks that the sun not set before he has burned Priam's hall. The god of the storm clouds accepts the offering and refuses the prayer.
