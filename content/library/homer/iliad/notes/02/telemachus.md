---
summary: Threatening Thersites, Odysseus stakes his name as “the father of Telemachus” on his word (2.259–260), the first of the Iliad's two mentions of his son.
---

{{quote:9f242ea9-2026-5f64-b415-010ee8baaf42}}

Odysseus swears by his head and by his title as father that he will strip Thersites and send him howling back to the ships (2.258–264; see [[thersites]] and [[odysseus-and-thersites]]). The other mention comes in Book 4, when he answers Agamemnon's rebuke (4.354).
