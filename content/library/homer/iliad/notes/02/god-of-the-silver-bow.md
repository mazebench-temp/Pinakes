---
summary: The Catalogue names Apollo, god of the silver bow, as the god who reared the mares of Eumelus.
grc: [ἀργυρότοξος]
---

{{quote:3d3eb9d9-432c-5e1a-8b5b-b5977726e84c}}

In Book 1 the epithet belonged to Apollo as the archer who sent the plague. In the Catalogue the same god appears as a breeder of horses: he reared the finest mares in the army, those of Eumelus of Pherae, “both of them mares, carrying the panic of Ares” ([[the-mares-of-eumelus]]). Homer does not say why. Later poets told that Apollo once served Eumelus' father Admetus as his herdsman, as Apollo himself says in the prologue of Euripides' *Alcestis*.
