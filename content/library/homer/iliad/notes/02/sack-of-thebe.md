---

{{quote:63dc3fe5-137d-5006-8724-9fe60eea5aeb}}

In Book 1 the sack of Thebe is Achilles' own story, told to Thetis. In Book 2 the narrator confirms it in his own voice, in a single phrase: Achilles sacked "Lyrnessus and the walls of Thebe". The two cities stand for the two captive women of the quarrel, Chryseis from Thebe and Briseis from Lyrnessus (see [[captive-women]] and [[the-sack-of-lyrnessus]]).
