---
summary: The tombs of Book 2 are old landmarks, not new graves (2.604, 2.793, 2.814).
passages:
  - 2.811-815 | Batieia, the tomb of Myrine
---

Book 2 has three tombs, and all three are old landmarks rather than new graves: the tomb of Aepytus in Arcadia, which marks a whole district (2.604; [[tomb-of-aepytus]]); the tomb of old [[Aesyetes]], from which [[Polites]] watches the Achaean ships (2.793); and the mound before Troy that men call [[Batieia]] and the gods call the tomb of [[Myrine]] (2.814).
