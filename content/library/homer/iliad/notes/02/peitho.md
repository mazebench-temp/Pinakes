**The false order.** After the council the scepter-bearing kings rise and obey “the shepherd of the people” (πείθοντο, 2.85), and the army streams to the assembly. There Agamemnon ends his test with the formula of command: “Come then, let us all do as I say (πειθώμεθα πάντες): let us flee with our ships to our dear native land” (2.139–140). It is the one order in the book that the army obeys at once, and he does not mean it. In Book 9 he will speak the same three lines in earnest (9.26–28). See [[the-test-of-the-army]].

**Obedience restored.** Athena “did not disobey” Hera and goes down to Odysseus (οὐδʼ ἀπίθησε, 2.166), and Odysseus turns the men back with words and the scepter (see [[obedience-and-persuasion]]). Nestor then recalls the pledges the army gave when it set out: “into the fire, then, with the counsels and plans of men, the libations of unmixed wine and the right hands in which we trusted” (ἐπέπιθμεν, from πέποιθα, “trust”, 2.340–341; see [[oath]]). He tells Agamemnon to “take good counsel yourself, and listen to another” (πείθεό τʼ ἄλλῳ, 2.360), and to separate the army by tribes and clans: “if you do this, and the Achaeans obey you (πείθωνται), then you will know which of the leaders is a coward” (2.364–365; see [[phylon]]). In Book 1 Nestor asked both kings to obey him, and neither did (1.259, 1.274). Now Agamemnon praises him (2.370–374), and after the sacrifice, when Nestor tells him to summon the army, he “did not disobey” (2.441).

**Trusting in.** The perfect participle πεποιθώς, “trusting in”, describes two men by what they rely on. Menelaus goes among his men “trusting in his own eagerness” (2.588), and Polites, the Trojans' lookout, sits on the tomb of Aesyetes “trusting in his speed of foot” (2.792).

**Disobedience.** The Trojan Catalogue ends with a refusal. Merops of Percote knew the art of prophecy above all men and would not let his sons go to war, “but they would not obey him at all, for the fates of black death were leading them on” (οὔ τι πειθέσθην, 2.833–834; see [[the-sons-of-merops]]).
