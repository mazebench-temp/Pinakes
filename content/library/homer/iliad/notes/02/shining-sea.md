# δῖαν at 2.615 (Elis) and 2.836 (Arisbe) is not the sea: see brilliant.
except: [2.615, 2.836]
---

{{quote:5b5cf69b-f2b3-5737-a0d6-1a1445f8fa3c}}

The phrase εἰς ἅλα δῖαν (“to the shining sea”) closes the verse as it did in Book 1, when Agamemnon ordered a ship launched for Chryse ({{ref:Iliad 1.141}}). Then it began a voyage of reparation. Here it is the goal of a stampede home, stopped only when Hera and Athena intervene. The same adjective is given later in the book to two places, Elis and Arisbe, where the translation also renders it “shining” ([[brilliant]]).
