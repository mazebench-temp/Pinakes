summary: Hephaestus made the scepter that Agamemnon carries, and at the sacrifice his name stands for the fire.
---

{{quote:778cced3-e225-5437-8786-4ee584223e25}}

The scepter that Agamemnon holds is one that “Hephaestus had toiled to make” (2.101). Its pedigree begins with the craftsman god: he gave it to Zeus, Zeus to Hermes, and Hermes to the first mortal king in the line. See [[the-scepter-of-agamemnon]] and [[scepter]].

At Agamemnon's sacrifice the entrails are held “over the flame of Hephaestus” (ὑπείρεχον Ἡφαίστοιο, 2.426). The god's name stands for fire itself, as it does again in Phoenix's story of his youth, where swine are stretched “over the flame of Hephaestus” (9.467–468). See [[sacrifice-type-scene]].
