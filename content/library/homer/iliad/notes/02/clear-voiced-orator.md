{{quote:20db32a1-d07d-537d-b4d1-6c0df404e7bd}}

The concessive phrase λιγύς περ ἐὼν ἀγορητής admits that Thersites has the gift that Book 1 praised in Nestor ({{ref:Iliad 1.248}}). The admission is part of the rebuke. Odysseus sets it against his first word for Thersites, ἀκριτόμυθε ([[reckless-in-speech]]): the voice is clear, and the judgment is missing. The contrast between the two speakers is drawn again later in the book, where Nestor's counsel restores the army's order ({{ref:Iliad 2.336-368}}).
