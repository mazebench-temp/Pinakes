---
summary: At Agamemnon's sacrifice to Zeus before battle, the kings take up the barley grains around the ox and scatter them when the prayer is over.
passages:
  - 2.410-418 | Agamemnon's prayer
  - 2.421-432 | The sacrifice to Zeus
---

Barley is taken up and thrown at Agamemnon's sacrifice to Zeus on the morning of battle. The kings he has summoned “stood around the ox and took up the barley grains” (βοῦν δὲ περιστήσαντο καὶ οὐλοχύτας ἀνέλοντο, 2.410), and Agamemnon prays that Troy may fall before sunset. “When they had prayed and scattered the barley grains” (αὐτὰρ ἐπεί ῥʼ εὔξαντο καὶ οὐλοχύτας προβάλοντο, 2.421), the ox is killed. The second verse is the one used at Chryse (1.458), and the first ends in the same half-verse as the taking up of the barley there (1.449); for the two sacrifices compared, see [[sacrifice-type-scene]].
