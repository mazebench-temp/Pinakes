---
summary: The Trojan Catalogue gives the epithet to Larisa, home of the Pelasgians who fight for Troy.
grc: [ἐριβώλακα]
---

{{quote:936006d4-a5e1-5c59-a029-78b0b053377f}}

The Pelasgians who come to Troy's aid live in “deep-soiled Larisa.” In the Iliad the epithet belongs to fertile lands on both sides: Achilles' Phthia in Book 1, and Troy itself (Τροίην ἐριβώλακα, as at Il. 3.74). Here it is the only description the Catalogue gives of an allied town.
