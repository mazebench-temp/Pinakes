---
summary: Dogs appear in Book 2 only in Agamemnon's threat: a man who hangs back from the battle will not escape the dogs and the birds.
---

Agamemnon ends his orders for the battle with a threat.

{{quote:fa08eb5f-4760-5801-882c-7c556ba84d40}}

“The dogs and the birds” (κύνας ἠδʼ οἰωνούς) is the pair of the proem, where the wrath made the dead “the spoil of dogs and of every bird” (1.4–5). There the unburied dead were the cost of the quarrel; here the king promises the same end to his own shirkers (see [[birds]] and [[cremation]]).
