---
summary: Book 2 has the related formula θεῶν αἰειγενετάων, “the everlasting gods,” as each man sacrifices before battle and prays to escape death.
grc: [θεῶν αἰειγενετάων]
en: [everlasting gods]
---

αἰειγενέτης = αἰεί (“always”) + γεν- (“be, come to be,” as in γίγνομαι): “who always are,” the same idea as θεοὶ αἰὲν ἐόντες. The genitive plural θεῶν αἰειγενετάων closes the verse.

{{quote:a976b3b0-4bea-57a2-b9cf-3c8573056ef3}}

Before the battle each soldier sacrifices to one of the everlasting gods, “praying to escape death and the toil of Ares.” The formula names the gods by the one thing the men who pray to them cannot have.
