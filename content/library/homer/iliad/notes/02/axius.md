{{quote:06984e1b-ed6f-5268-b011-74490c5b9573}}

εὐρὺ ῥέων, “broad-flowing”. The second verse takes up the river's name again to praise it; the Trojan Catalogue repeats a name at the head of the next verse in the same way with Asius (2.837–838) and with Nastes (2.870–871; see [[asius]] and [[nastes]]). The Paeonians' town Amydon lay on the river (see [[amydon]] and [[paeonians]]). The verse returns when Patroclus kills Pyraechmes, who led the Paeonians from Amydon (16.288; see [[pyraechmes]]), and in Book 21 the Paeonian Asteropaeus, a grandson of the river, repeats the praise of its water as he tells Achilles his lineage (21.157–158; see [[asteropaeus]]).
