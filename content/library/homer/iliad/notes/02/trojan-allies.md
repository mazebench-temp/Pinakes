---
summary: Book 2 lists the allies in the Trojan catalogue, after Hector's own Trojans, and notices their many languages.
passages:
  - 2.815-877 | The Trojan catalogue
---

At the end of Book 2 Iris names the allies' difficulty. They are many, and "the tongues of men scattered far and wide are many and various" (2.804), so each leader must command his own people. The Trojans and their allies then form up at [[Batieia]] (2.815), and the Trojan catalogue lists them ([[trojan-catalogue]]). After [[Hector]]'s Trojans and [[Aeneas]]' Dardanians come the other towns of the Troad and the Hellespont, down to the [[Pelasgians]] of Larisa. Then the list runs out from Troy along four lines: west into Europe ([[Thracians]], [[Cicones]], [[Paeonians]]), east along the Black Sea coast ([[Paphlagonians]], [[Halizones]]), inland ([[Mysians]], [[Phrygians]]), and south ([[Maeonians]], [[Carians]], [[Lycians]]). Each line ends with a people who come "from far away" (τηλόθεν or τῆλε: 2.849, 2.857, 2.863, 2.877). Unlike the Achaean catalogue, the Trojan catalogue gives no numbers of ships or men.
