---
summary: The Catalogue uses the word in its praise of Menestheus: no man on earth was his equal at marshaling horses and men, except Nestor.
grc: [ἐπιχθόνιος]
---

{{quote:6ffb7ac3-3690-5022-88e6-7d776d5d098b}}

The Athenian leader is given a superlative of skill, measured against all men on earth. The one exception is Nestor, “for he was older.” In Book 1 Nestor used the same word to measure the men of his own time against the heroes of old ({{ref:Iliad 1.266}}, {{ref:Iliad 1.272}}). Here the narrator uses it to set Nestor at the top of the scale.
