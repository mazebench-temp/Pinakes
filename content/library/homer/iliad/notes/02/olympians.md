Zeus' message to Agamemnon, repeated three times, says that the gods who live on Olympus are no longer divided, because Hera has won them all over (2.13–15, 2.30–32, 2.67–69). Their agreement is the Dream's reason why Troy can fall now, and it is false. In Book 1 the gods were divided, and Zeus has just promised Thetis to help the Trojans. See [[dream]] and [[homes-on-olympus]].

Before the first battle each Achaean sacrifices “to one or another of the everlasting gods”, praying to escape death (2.400–401). The same formula of the homes on Olympus is used of the Muses at the start of the Catalogue (2.484).
