{{quote:2120afa7-03c3-5a09-b697-ecb38d557968}}

The formula is the one the entry quotes from the Odyssey, where it is Odysseus' standing address. Here it is used for the first time in the poem. It joins three titles, divine descent, father and resourcefulness ([[son-of-laertes]], [[of-many-devices]]), and Athena uses all of them as she asks him to stop the army's flight. The line recurs six more times in the Iliad.
