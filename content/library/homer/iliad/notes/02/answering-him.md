---
summary: Agamemnon's reply to Nestor (2.369) is the only reply in Book 2 that a reply formula introduces. The line is the one that brought in his answers in the quarrel of Book 1, but what follows it has changed.
---

Book 2 is a book of commands, messages and speeches to crowds, and its speeches are answered by deeds. Zeus speaks and the Dream goes; Hera speaks and Athena goes; Athena speaks and Odysseus runs; Odysseus threatens and Thersites weeps (see [[so-he-spoke]]). Only once does one speaker answer another in the usual form, and [[then-answered]], used five times in Book 1, does not occur at all.

The line is 2.369, the same as 1.130 and 1.285: “Answering him, powerful Agamemnon said.” In Book 1 it brought in Agamemnon's answers to Achilles and then to Nestor. That second answer granted the old man that “all that you have said is fair and right”, and turned at once to attack Achilles (1.286–291). In Book 2 he begins with praise again, “Once again, old man, you surpass the sons of the Achaeans in the assembly”, and wishes he had ten such counselors (2.370–374). Then he says what he would not say in Book 1.
