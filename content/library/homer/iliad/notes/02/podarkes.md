# Ποδάρκης at 2.704 is Podarces, brother of Protesilaus.
except: [2.704]
---

{{quote:8dabd452-362d-5346-b996-1374b97efce9}}

The formula ποδάρκης δῖος Ἀχιλλεύς ([[brilliant]]) names Achilles in the Catalogue's entry for his own people, and he is lying still. The epithet of speed stands beside κεῖτο (“lay”), as in Book 1 the swift-footed hero sat by his ships ([[swift-footed]], [[achilles-withdraws]]).

The same word is a man's name. Podarces, the younger brother of Protesilaus, leads his dead brother's men ({{ref:Iliad 2.704}}; [[podarces]]).
