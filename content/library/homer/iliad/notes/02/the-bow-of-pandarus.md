---
summary: Apollo himself gave Pandarus his bow. The Catalogue's one line about him points ahead to the arrow that breaks the truce in Book 4.
---

The Catalogue gives most Trojan leaders a name and a father. Pandarus gets one thing more, his bow: ᾧ καὶ τόξον Ἀπόλλων αὐτὸς ἔδωκεν, “to whom Apollo himself had given his bow” (2.827). It picks him out for the shot that will break the truce (4.93–147; see [[pandarus]] and [[pandarus-breaks-the-truce]]).
