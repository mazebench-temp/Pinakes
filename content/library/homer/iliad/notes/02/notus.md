---
summary: In Book 2 it drives the waves in two similes for the army's roar and rush.
---

With the East Wind it heaps up the long waves of the Icarian Sea, as the assembly heaves at Agamemnon's word (2.144–146; see [[waves-of-the-icarian-sea]]), and when his speech is done the Argives shout like a wave that it drives against a headland (2.394–397; see [[wave-on-the-headland]]).
