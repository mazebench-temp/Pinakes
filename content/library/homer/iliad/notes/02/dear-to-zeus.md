---

{{quote:bf92e6b6-e0b8-5d95-88ff-c1ae38a348a6}}

Phyleus is “the horseman, dear to Zeus” ([[gerenian-horseman]]). The next line gives his story: he “went off to live in Dulichium, angry with his father” ([[phyleus-and-augeas]]). The phrase that honored Achilles and Apollo in Book 1 goes here to a man remembered for a quarrel with a king, like Achilles himself.
