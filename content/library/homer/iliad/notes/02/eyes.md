---
summary: The only eyes in Book 2 are Agamemnon's, which Zeus makes like his own as the king moves among his troops: “in eyes and head like Zeus who delights in thunder”.
grc: [ὄμματα]
passages:
  - 2.477-479 | Like Zeus, Ares and Poseidon
---

As the leaders marshal the army, Agamemnon moves among them, “in eyes and head like Zeus who delights in thunder, in waist like Ares, and in chest like Poseidon” (2.478–479), and Zeus makes him stand out like a bull above the herd (2.480–483; see [[like-zeus-ares-and-poseidon]] and [[bull-above-the-herd]]). In Book 1 Agamemnon's eyes were “like blazing fire” with anger, and Achilles called them the eyes of a dog (1.104, 1.225). Now, “on that day” (ἤματι κείνῳ, 2.482), the day on which he thought he would take Priam's city (2.37), they are the eyes of Zeus.
