---
summary: Poseidon lends Agamemnon his chest in the simile of the marshaling, and his grove at Onchestus is named in the Catalogue.
grc: [Ποσειδάωνι, Ποσιδήϊον]
---

As the army is marshaled, Agamemnon is like Zeus in eyes and head, like Ares in his waist, “and in chest like Poseidon” (2.479). The brother of Zeus lends the king his breadth. See [[like-zeus-ares-and-poseidon]].

In the Boeotian part of the Catalogue, Onchestus is “the splendid grove of Poseidon” (Ποσιδήϊον ἀγλαὸν ἄλσος, 2.506). Ποσιδήϊος is the adjective of his name: the grove belongs to him. See [[onchestus]].
