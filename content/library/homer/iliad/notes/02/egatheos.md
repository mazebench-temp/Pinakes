---
summary: Book 2 gives the epithet to Lemnos, the island where Philoctetes lies wounded.
---

{{quote:5e7fcbc1-de56-57b6-8339-0df9381c2bed}}

Philoctetes lies “in sacred Lemnos,” where the army left him with a festering snakebite. The epithet is conventional, but it sits oddly with the scene of abandonment. Lemnos is Hephaestus' island in Book 1, where he fell when Zeus threw him from heaven and the Sintians cared for him ({{ref:Iliad 1.593-594}}; [[lemnos]], [[philoctetes-on-lemnos]]).
