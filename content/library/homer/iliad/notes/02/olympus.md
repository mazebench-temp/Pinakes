---
summary: Dawn climbs high Olympus to announce the day of battle, and Athena darts down from its peaks to stop the flight to the ships. The gods and the Muses “have their homes on Olympus”.
---

Olympus frames the first day of battle. Dawn “went up to high Olympus to announce the light to Zeus and the other immortals” (2.48–49), and Athena comes “darting down from the peaks of Olympus” at Hera's word (2.167). The gods “who have their homes on Olympus” are the subject of the Dream's lie (2.13, 2.30, 2.67), and the poet calls on the Muses in the same words (2.484). The Muses are “Olympian” (Ὀλυμπιάδες, 2.491). See [[high-olympus]] and [[homes-on-olympus]].
