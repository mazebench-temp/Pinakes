# ἐΐσας at 2.765 describes the level backs of Eumelus' mares.
except: [2.765]
---

{{quote:6e50452b-d36f-5b47-87f0-80316249e217}}

Nireus is the most handsome man at Troy after Achilles and a weakling, and his three ships are the smallest force in the Catalogue. The epithet “balanced” is the only praise his fleet receives.

The same adjective has another use nearby that is not about ships. The mares of Eumelus are “alike in coat and in age, their backs as level as a builder's line” (σταφύλῃ ἐπὶ νῶτον ἐΐσας, {{ref:Iliad 2.765}}). There ἐΐσας keeps its basic sense, “even, equal.” The equal feast of the sacrifice is another (δαιτὸς ἐΐσης, {{ref:Iliad 2.431}}; [[equal-feast]]).
