**Nestor's advice.** At the end of his speech to the assembly Nestor turns from rebuke to a practical counsel for Agamemnon.

{{quote:f16ae393-9e49-5b21-a212-5efe1cc9e567}}

κρῖνε, “separate, sort out”, is the verb of choosing and judging. The next line enacts the order it recommends: ὡς φρήτρη φρήτρηφιν ἀρήγῃ, φῦλα δὲ φύλοις, “so that clan may help clan, and tribe tribe”. Each noun is repeated in a different case (see [[polyptoton]]), and the order of 2.362, tribes then clans, is reversed in 2.363.

Nestor gives two reasons. Men fight better beside their kin, who “help” them (ἀρήγῃ). And the order will let Agamemnon see: he will know which of the leaders and which of the people is a coward and which is brave, “for they will fight each among their own” (κατὰ σφέας, 2.366), and whether it is “by the will of heaven” that he does not sack the city, “or by the cowardice of men and their ignorance of war” (2.367–368). The division is a way of knowing. Agamemnon, who has just been deceived about the will of the gods, is to learn it from the conduct of his men. See [[the-army-and-its-leaders]].

The advice is followed in a general way. After the meal the kings “rushed about marshaling them”, with Nestor's verb (θῦνον κρίνοντες, 2.446), and the leaders sort the men as goatherds sort their flocks (2.474–477; see [[goatherds-sorting-their-flocks]]). But the Catalogue that follows orders the army by region and leader, not by tribe and clan.

**The Rhodians.** Tlepolemus' Rhodians “lived in Rhodes, arranged in three divisions (διὰ τρίχα κοσμηθέντες), Lindus and Ialysus and chalk-white Cameirus” (2.655–656). When Tlepolemus came to the island, “they settled there in three groups, by tribes” (τριχθὰ … καταφυλαδόν, 2.668). The three cities of Rhodes are three tribal settlements. See [[rhodes]], [[rhodians]], [[tlepolemus]] and [[the-flight-of-tlepolemus]].

**The Pelasgians.** In the Trojan catalogue Hippothous “led the tribes of the Pelasgians” (φῦλα Πελασγῶν, 2.840). Here φῦλα means little more than “peoples”: a people counted as a group of tribes. See [[pelasgians]] and [[hippothous]].
