In Book 1 the swift-sailing ships stood idle beside Achilles. In Book 2 Nestor recalls them in motion, on the day the fleet set out for Troy with Zeus' sign of good omen ([[the-departure-from-aulis]]). He uses the memory against the men who want to sail home now: the same ships carried the army to Troy under a promise that has not yet been fulfilled.
