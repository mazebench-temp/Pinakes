Καινεΐδης, "son of Caeneus", is the patronymic of [[Coronus]]: Leonteus is "son of high-hearted Coronus, son of Caeneus". So the Catalogue sets the grandson of one of Nestor's heroes (1.264) beside the son of another, Peirithous. It says nothing of Caeneus' own strange end, which later poets describe.
