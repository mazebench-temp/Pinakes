τείχεα Θήβης, "the walls of Thebe": the city is remembered by its walls, as Andromache later calls it "Thebe of the high gates" (6.416). The line joins the two raids that gave the Achaeans the captive women of the quarrel. [[Chryseis]] was taken at Thebe (1.366–369), and [[Briseis]] at [[Lyrnessus]]. Homer does not say whether the two towns fell in the same campaign. See [[sack-of-thebe]] and [[the-sack-of-lyrnessus]].
