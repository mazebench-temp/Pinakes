---
summary: Two of Priam's sons appear: Polites, the lookout whose voice Iris borrows, and Hector, "son of Priam", who leads the Trojans out.
---

Two sons of Priam appear in Book 2, at the beginning and the end of the Trojan scene. Iris takes the voice of [[Polites]], "Priam's son", who watches for the Achaeans from the tomb of Aesyetes, trusting in his speed of foot (2.791–794). The Trojan catalogue begins with [[Hector]], Πριαμίδης, "son of Priam", who leads the largest and best contingent (2.816–818). In Book 1 Nestor imagined all of Priam's sons rejoicing at the Achaeans' quarrel (1.255). In Book 2 they are the city's lookout and its commander.
