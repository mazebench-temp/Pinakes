---
summary: κλυτός, “famous”, is used of a woman in the Catalogue: Hippodameia, wife of Peirithous and mother of Polypoetes, keeping the masculine ending (2.742).
grc: ["κλυτὸς Ἱπποδάμεια"]
en: [famous]
---

“Famous Hippodameia bore him to Peirithous” (2.742; see [[hippodameia]], [[peirithous]] and [[polypoetes]]). κλυτός has no separate feminine form here, as in the Odyssey's κλυτὸς Ἀμφιτρίτη (Od. 5.422). In Book 2 the adjective is otherwise the δουρὶ κλυτός, “famed for his spear”, of Idomeneus and Tlepolemus (2.645, 2.650, 2.657; see [[famed-for-his-spear]]).
