{{quote:6829645b-0fe0-5f11-82d9-60928be5bbb3}}

The second formula mentioned in the entry, μερόπεσσι βροτοῖσι(ν), appears here for the first time in the poem. Odysseus opens his speech by telling Agamemnon what the flight to the ships would do to him: it would make him “the most despised of all mortal men.” The word for humankind widens the shame beyond the army to everyone who will hear of it.

The Trojan Catalogue has a man called Merops, the seer of Percote ({{ref:Iliad 2.831}}; [[merops]]). His name is the same word.
