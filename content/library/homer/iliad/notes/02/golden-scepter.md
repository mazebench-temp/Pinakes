The scepter is the one Hephaestus made for Zeus, which Odysseus has borrowed from Agamemnon ([[the-scepter-of-agamemnon]]). Its gold marks its divine origin, and in Odysseus' hand it becomes a weapon. In Book 1 a golden scepter gave the priest Chryses no protection against a king; in Book 2 a golden scepter is used on a commoner who has abused one.
