In Book 1 Achilles speaks of the raids in general: the Achaeans have plundered "the cities", and he has done the greater part of the fighting (1.125, 1.163–168). The narrator now gives names. Achilles sacked [[Lyrnessus]] and [[Thebe]], and killed [[Mynes]] and [[epistrophus-of-lyrnessus|Epistrophus]], the sons of lord [[Euenus]]. διαπορθήσας, "having sacked utterly", and πολλὰ μογήσας, "after much toil", confirm what Achilles claimed: the prize taken from him was one he had won by his own labor. See [[the-sack-of-lyrnessus]].
