---
summary: In Book 2 it joins the South Wind to raise the long waves of the Icarian Sea, in the simile for the assembly's panic.
---

With the South Wind it rushes down “from the clouds of father Zeus” and raises the long waves of the Icarian deep, as the assembly is stirred when Agamemnon tells the army to go home (2.144–146; see [[waves-of-the-icarian-sea]] and [[notus]]).
