The Catalogue ends its account of the best men and horses with Achilles, who is not fighting. He lies “among his curved, seafaring ships” ([[curved-ships]]). In Book 1 the seafaring ship brought Chryseis home; in Book 2 the ships made for crossing the sea stand on the beach with their owner, as idle as his horses.
