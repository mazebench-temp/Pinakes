---
summary: Odysseus tells Thersites that he speaks only to taunt (κερτομέων).
grc: [κερτομέων]
en: [taunt]
passages:
  - 2.246-256 | Odysseus rebukes Thersites
---

Odysseus sums up Thersites' speech in a word: “you sit hurling reproaches … and you speak only to taunt” (σὺ δὲ κερτομέων ἀγορεύεις, 2.255–256). In Book 1 κερτόμια were a queen's words to a king, Hera needling Zeus about his secret plans; here the taunting is aimed at the king in open assembly, and it ends in a beating.
