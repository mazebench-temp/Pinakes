summary: Dawn goes up to high Olympus at the start of the day on which the army is summoned.

{{quote:10963bc5-a466-55e2-b6a6-770a3e43f573}}

The epithet opens the long day that runs from Book 2 to Book 7. Dawn climbs to “high Olympus” to announce the light to Zeus and the other gods, and on earth Agamemnon, already dressed, has the heralds call the army ([[dawn-line]]). Book 2 also names the mountain's “peaks” (Οὐλύμποιο καρήνων, {{ref:Iliad 2.167}}), from which Athena darts down to stop the flight.
