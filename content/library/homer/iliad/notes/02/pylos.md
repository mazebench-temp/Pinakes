## Nestor's home

{{quote:3a8197f9-c1b1-520f-bb75-3ebe58e4fccd}}

Agamemnon holds his council of elders beside the ship of the "Pylos-born king" (Πυλοιγενής; [[pylos-born]]).

{{quote:f8b2b44c-271a-5d52-b238-2ed56a862b5d}}

Nestor is "king of sandy Pylos" (Πύλοιο ... ἠμαθόεντος; [[sandy]]). Both phrases define him by his city before he speaks.

## The Catalogue

{{quote:7343bfd8-c264-5769-9476-26fc9778c001}}

Nestor's kingdom comprises Pylos itself, [[Arene]], [[Thryum]], "the ford of the [[Alpheus]]", [[Aepy]], [[Cyparisseis]], [[Amphigeneia]], [[Pteleus]], [[helos-pylos|Helos]] and [[Dorium]]. At Dorium the list stops for the story of Thamyris, whom the Muses maimed there (2.594–600; see [[thamyris-and-the-muses]]). Then comes the leader, "the Gerenian horseman Nestor", with ninety ships (2.601–602), second only to Agamemnon's hundred.

The Alpheus fixes the northern edge of the kingdom. Elsewhere it "flows broad through the land of the Pylians" (5.545), and in Nestor's stories of his youth the Epeians besiege Thryoessa, "far off on the Alpheus, the last town of sandy Pylos" (11.711–712). Of the other towns only Cyparisseis can be placed with some confidence, at Kyparissia on the west coast of Messenia; most were already hard to find in antiquity. That is why the location of Nestor's Pylos was argued from Strabo's day to Blegen's (see [[strabo-and-the-catalogue]]). The Linear B tablets from the palace at Ano Englianos name many places in the kingdom, but few can be matched with the Catalogue's list (see [[catalogue-and-mycenae]]).
