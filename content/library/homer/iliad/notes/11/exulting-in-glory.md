---
summary: Zeus sits apart from the other gods, “exulting in his glory,” and watches the battle.
---

The other gods blame Zeus for meaning to give the Trojans glory, but he pays them no heed: “withdrawing apart, away from the others he sat, exulting in his glory, looking out on the city of the Trojans and the ships of the Achaeans” (11.80–82; [[watching-the-war]], [[kydos]]). The verses repeat the scene of Book 8, where Zeus sits on the peaks of Ida, exulting in his glory (8.51–52). See [[zeus]].
