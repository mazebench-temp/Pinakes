---
summary: “The ranks of the shield-bearing Trojans” surround Odysseus while he ponders.
---

“While he was pondering this in his mind and in his heart, the ranks of the shield-bearing Trojans came on, and hemmed him in among them” (11.411–413; [[pondering-two-ways]]). The same verse brought the Trojan ranks on while Machaon was tending Menelaus' wound in Book 4 (4.220–221). See [[odysseus]] and [[trojans]].
