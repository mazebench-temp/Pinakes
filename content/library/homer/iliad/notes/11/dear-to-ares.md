---
summary: Menelaus, “dear to Ares,” hears Odysseus' three shouts.
---

Odysseus, wounded and surrounded, shouts three times “as loud as a man's head could hold, and three times Menelaus, dear to Ares, heard him shouting” (11.462–463). Menelaus calls Ajax, and the two bring Odysseus out of the throng (11.464–488; [[telamonian]], [[warlike]]). See [[menelaus]] and [[odysseus]].
