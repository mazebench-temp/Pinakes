---
summary: Hector, obeying Zeus' message, goes through the army and awakes “the dread din of battle.”
---

Hector leaps from his chariot in his armor, brandishing his spears, and goes everywhere through the army, “urging them to fight, and he awoke the dread din of battle” (11.211–213). The Trojans rally and stand facing the Achaeans (11.214). The same verses describe Hector's rallies in Book 5 and Book 6 (5.494–497, 6.103–106). See [[hector]].
