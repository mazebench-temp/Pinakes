---
summary: Neleus' four “prize-winning” horses, kept by Augeas, are the debt behind Nestor's raid.
grc: [ἀθλοφόροι]
en: [prize-winning]
---

“For to him too a great debt was owed in shining Elis, four prize-winning horses with their chariot, which had gone to the games; for they were going to race for a tripod; but Augeas, lord of men, kept them there, and sent the driver away, grieving for his horses” (11.698–702; [[brilliant]], [[lord-of-men]], [[augeas]]). Neleus takes his share of the spoil in payment (11.703–705; [[division-of-spoils]], [[contests]]). See [[neleus]] and [[nestor-and-the-epeians]].
