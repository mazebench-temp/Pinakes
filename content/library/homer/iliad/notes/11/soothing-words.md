---
summary: The sons of Antimachus beg Agamemnon “with soothing words.”
grc: [μειλιχίοις]
---

“So the two, weeping, addressed the king with soothing words; but the voice they heard would not be soothed” (11.136–137; [[implacable]]). The verse sets the suppliants' gentleness against the king's refusal, which follows: they will pay for their father's shameful insult (11.138–142; [[sons-of-antimachus]], [[supplication]]). See [[agamemnon]].
