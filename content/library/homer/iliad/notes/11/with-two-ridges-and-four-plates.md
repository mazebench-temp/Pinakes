---
summary: Agamemnon arms with a helmet “with two ridges and four plates,” in the verse of Athena's arming.
---

“And on his head he set a helmet with two ridges and four plates, with a horsehair crest, and the plume nodded terribly above it” (11.41–42; [[horsehair-crested]]). The first verse is the one used of Athena's helmet when she arms for battle in Book 5 (5.743). See [[helmet]] and [[arming-scene]].
