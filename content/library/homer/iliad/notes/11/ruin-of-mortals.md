---
summary: Hector, setting the Trojans on the Achaeans, is “the equal of Ares, ruin of mortals.”
grc: [βροτολοιγῷ]
---

“So against the Achaeans he set on the great-hearted Trojans, Hector, son of Priam, the equal of Ares, ruin of mortals” (11.294–295; [[equal-of-ares]], [[the-hunter-and-his-hounds]]). The general article lists the other men compared in the same words. See [[hector]] and [[ares]].
