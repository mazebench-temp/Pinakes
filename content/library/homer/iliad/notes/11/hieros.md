---
summary: The “sacred” day and darkness that frame the battle, “sacred Ilios,” the “sacred stream” of the Alpheus, and the “holy barley” of Nestor's drink.
grc: [ἱεροῦ]
---

The day and the night are sacred. The battle is level “as long as it was morning and the sacred day was growing” (11.84 = 8.66), and Zeus promises Hector power to kill until he reaches the ships, “and the sun goes down and sacred darkness comes on” (11.194, repeated by Iris at 11.209; [[message-repeated]], [[the-third-day-of-battle]]). Iris goes down from Ida “to sacred Ilios” (11.196; [[troy]]). In Nestor's story the Pylians come at midday “to the sacred stream of Alpheus” (11.726; [[alpheus]]). And Hecamede sets beside Nestor's cup “the meal of holy barley” (11.631; [[barley-meal]], [[kykeon]]).
