---
summary: Ares takes no part in Book 11, but his name measures men and stands for war. Hector and Patroclus are each “the equal of Ares”, Menelaus is “dear to Ares”, the battle of Nestor's youth is “the great work of Ares”, and Podalirius “awaits the sharp Ares of the Trojans”.
---

**The equal of Ares.** Hector sets the Trojans on the Achaeans as “the equal of Ares, ruin of mortals” (βροτολοιγῷ ἶσος Ἄρηϊ, 11.295), and he leads them so again in Book 13 (13.802; see [[ruin-of-mortals]] and [[hector]]). Three hundred verses later Patroclus comes out of Achilles' hut at his friend's call, “the equal of Ares; and this was the beginning of evil for him” (11.604; see [[patroclus]] and [[the-beginning-of-evils]]). The two men whom the likeness joins in this book are the killer and the killed of Book 16. There Patroclus charges three times “the equal of swift Ares” (θοῷ ἀτάλαντος Ἄρηϊ), and the fourth time, “like a god”, he meets Apollo and his death (16.784–789).

**Dear to Ares.** When Odysseus shouts three times, “three times Menelaus, dear to Ares, heard him shouting” (11.463; see [[menelaus]]). The text prints ἄρηι φίλος as two words, as it does for Meleager (9.550), and the index counts the line to Ares; elsewhere the epithet is one word, ἀρηΐφιλος (see [[dear-to-ares]]).

**War itself.** In Nestor's story the Epeians besieging Thryoessa find “the great work of Ares” in front of them (μέγα ἔργον Ἄρηος, 11.734), a phrase found only here (see [[nestor-and-the-epeians]]). Eurypylus tells Patroclus that one of the two healers lies wounded and the other “on the plain awaits the sharp Ares of the Trojans” (11.836; see [[podalirius]]): the god's name stands for the Trojans' attack, as when the leaders speak of going out to “rouse sharp Ares” (2.440, 4.352, 8.531). Ares' companions Terror and Rout surround the Gorgon on Agamemnon's shield (11.37; see [[terror]] and [[rout]]), and his sister Strife is the one god in the battle (11.3–14, 11.73–74; see [[strife]]).
