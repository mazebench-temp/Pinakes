---
summary: Coon, eldest son of Antenor, is “preeminent among men.”
grc: ["ἀριδείκετος ἀνδρῶν"]
en: [preeminent among men]
---

“But when Coon, preeminent among men, caught sight of him, the eldest-born son of Antenor, strong grief veiled his eyes for his fallen brother” (11.248–250; [[eldest-born]]). ἀριδείκετος, “conspicuous, much pointed out,” is from the same intensive ἀρι- as ἀριπρεπής and from the root of δείκνυμι, “show”; the translation gives the two the same English. The same phrase praises Perseus in Zeus' list of his loves (14.319–320), and in the Odyssey πάντων ἀριδείκετε λαῶν is a verse of address to Alcinous (Od. 8.382, and five times more). See [[coon]].
