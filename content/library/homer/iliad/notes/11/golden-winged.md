---
summary: Zeus on Ida sends “golden-winged Iris” to Hector.
---

“And he urged on golden-winged Iris to carry a message” (11.185), the verse with which Zeus sent her from Ida to Hera and Athena in Book 8 (8.398; [[storm-footed]]). Here the message goes to Hector, and Iris delivers it word for word (11.186–209; [[message-repeated]], [[swift-iris]], [[wind-footed]]). See [[iris]].
