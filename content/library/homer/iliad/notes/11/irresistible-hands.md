---
summary: Agamemnon pursues the Trojans to the city, “and his irresistible hands were spattered with gore.”
---

“He kept following, shouting, the son of Atreus, and his irresistible hands were spattered with gore” (11.168–169). The half-verse is used again of Achilles at the end of his rout of the Trojans (20.503). See [[agamemnon]] and [[the-aristeia-of-agamemnon]].
