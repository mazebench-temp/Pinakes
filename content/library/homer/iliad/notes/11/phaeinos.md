---
summary: φαεινός describes the spears of Coon and Eurypylus, Odysseus' shield, and Nestor's chair.
---

Coon stabs Agamemnon through the forearm, “and the point of the shining spear went straight through” (11.253; [[eldest-born]]). Socus' spear goes “through the shining shield” of Odysseus (11.435; [[heavy-spear]]). Eurypylus comes to Ajax's side and throws “his shining spear,” killing Apisaon (11.577–579; [[apisaon]]). And Nestor rises to greet Patroclus “from his shining chair” (11.645; [[throne]]). See [[spear]].
