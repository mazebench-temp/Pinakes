---
summary: “Shining Ajax” sweeps over the plain like a river in flood.
---

“And as when a river in flood comes down to the plain, … so then shining Ajax swept over the plain, driving them in confusion, cutting down horses and men” (11.492–497; [[river-in-flood]]). It is Ajax's one advance in the book before Zeus puts him to flight (11.544). See [[ajax]].
