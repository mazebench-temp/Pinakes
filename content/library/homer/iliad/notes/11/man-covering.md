---
summary: Agamemnon takes up his “man-covering” shield as he arms.
grc: [ἀμφιβρότην]
---

“And he took up the furious shield, man-covering, intricately worked” (11.32; [[furious]], [[intricately-worked]]). The great body shield of the epithet is here the shield of circles and bosses with the Gorgon at its center, which the poem goes on to describe (11.33–40; [[grim-faced]]). See [[shield]] and [[agamemnon]].
