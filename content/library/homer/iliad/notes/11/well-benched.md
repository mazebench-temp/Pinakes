---
summary: Zeus promises Hector power to kill “until he comes to the well-benched ships.”
---

“Then I will put power in his hands to kill, until he comes to the well-benched ships, and the sun goes down and sacred darkness comes on” (11.192–194), and Iris repeats it to Hector (11.207–209; [[message-repeated]], [[hieros]]). The promise sets the term of the long day of battle, which ends only at 18.239–242 ([[the-third-day-of-battle]], [[plan-of-zeus]]). See [[ships]].
