---
summary: Five sons of Antenor are named in the book: Polybus, Agenor and Acamas among the Trojan leaders, and Iphidamas and Coon, who come against Agamemnon and die.
grc: ["Ἀντήνορος υἷες"]
en: [sons of Antenor]
---

The Trojans are drawn up around Hector, Polydamas and Aeneas, “and the three sons of Antenor, Polybus and brilliant Agenor, and young Acamas, like the immortals” (11.59–60; [[polybus]], [[agenor]], [[acamas]]). Two more come forward against Agamemnon. Iphidamas, “son of Antenor, tall and brave,” is the first to face him, and is killed (11.221–247; [[iphidamas]], [[brave]]); Coon, “the eldest-born son of Antenor,” wounds Agamemnon over his brother's body and is killed in turn (11.248–261; [[coon]], [[eldest-born]]). The narrator closes the episode: “There the sons of Antenor, at the hands of the king, the son of Atreus, fulfilled their fate and went down into the house of Hades” (11.262–263). See [[antenor]].
