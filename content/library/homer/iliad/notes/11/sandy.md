---
summary: Thryoessa lies “on the border of sandy Pylos.”
---

“There is a city, Thryoessa, a steep hill, far off on the Alpheus, on the border of sandy Pylos” (11.711–712; [[steep]], [[thryum]]). The Epeians besiege it, and the Pylians march out to relieve it (11.713–736; [[nestor-and-the-epeians]]). See [[pylos]].
