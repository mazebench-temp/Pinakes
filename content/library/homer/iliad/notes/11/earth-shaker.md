---
summary: Poseidon, “the wide-ruling earth-shaker,” saves the Moliones in a mist in Nestor's story.
---

“And I would have slain the two sons of Actor, the Moliones, had not their father, the wide-ruling earth-shaker, saved them from the war, hiding them in thick mist” (11.750–752; [[would-have-had-not]]). The Moliones are sons of Actor by name and of Poseidon by birth ([[the-moliones]]). Agamemnon's title εὐρὺ κρείων is joined to Poseidon's epithet only here ([[wide-ruling]]). See [[poseidon]].
