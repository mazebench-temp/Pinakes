---
summary: In the battle of the reapers neither side thinks of “deadly rout.”
---

The Trojans and Achaeans leap at each other like reapers cutting their swaths through a rich man's field, “and neither side thought of deadly rout” (11.67–71; [[the-reapers]]). φόβος here is flight in panic ([[rout]]). The two sides stay level until the hour when the woodcutter makes his meal, when the Danaans break the Trojan battalions (11.84–91). See [[the-third-day-of-battle]].
