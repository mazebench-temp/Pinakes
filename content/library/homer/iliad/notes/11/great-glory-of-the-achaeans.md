---
summary: Idomeneus addresses Nestor as “great glory of the Achaeans” when he asks him to carry Machaon off.
---

“Nestor, son of Neleus, great glory of the Achaeans, come, mount your chariot, and let Machaon go up beside you” (11.511–513; [[son-of-neleus]]). Agamemnon and Odysseus used the same verse of address to Nestor during the night (10.87, 10.555). Idomeneus' reason is the saying that “a healer is a man worth many others” (11.514; [[a-healer-is-worth-many]]). See [[nestor]] and [[idomeneus]].
