---
summary: Iphidamas' spear cannot pierce Agamemnon's “gleaming belt,” and Diomedes strips Agastrophus' “gleaming corselet.”
---

Iphidamas stabs Agamemnon at the waist, below the corselet, “but he did not pierce the gleaming belt”; the point meets the silver and bends like lead (11.234–237; [[like-lead]], [[belt]]). Diomedes, when he has killed Agastrophus, is taking “the gleaming corselet of mighty Agastrophus from his chest” as Paris shoots him (11.373–374; [[mighty]], [[heavy-helmet]]). See [[corselet]].
