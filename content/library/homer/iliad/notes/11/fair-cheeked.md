---
summary: Theano, the mother of Iphidamas, is “fair-cheeked” in the account of his upbringing.
---

Iphidamas was reared in Thrace by Cisseus, “his mother's father, who fathered fair-cheeked Theano” (11.223–224; [[cisseus]]). Theano is the wife of Antenor and Athena's priestess at Troy, who laid the Trojan women's robe on the goddess' knees with the same epithet (6.297–303; [[theano]]). Her son dies at Agamemnon's hands a few verses later, and his brother Coon after him (11.241–263; [[iphidamas]], [[coon]]).
