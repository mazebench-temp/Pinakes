---
summary: Zeus is “aegis-bearing” once in Book 11, in the comparison of Hector's flashing bronze to his lightning.
---

As Hector moves between the foremost and the hindmost of the Trojans, “all of him in bronze shone like the lightning of aegis-bearing father Zeus” (11.65–66; [[father-zeus]], [[baleful]], [[the-baleful-star]]). The god whose lightning is named is the god who will give Hector his day (11.192–194; [[plan-of-zeus]]). See [[zeus]] and [[aegis]].
