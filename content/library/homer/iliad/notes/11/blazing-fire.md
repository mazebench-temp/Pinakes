---
summary: “So they fought on like blazing fire”: the battle around Ajax and Eurypylus.
---

The verse closes the rally around Ajax: Eurypylus has called the Achaeans to stand by him, and they come with their shields slanted and their spears raised (11.586–595); “So they fought on like blazing fire” (11.596). The narrative turns from it to Nestor's mares carrying Machaon out of the war (11.597–598). The same verse, ὣς οἳ μὲν μάρναντο δέμας πυρὸς αἰθομένοιο, closes episodes of the battle at 13.673 and 18.1. See [[ajax]] and [[eurypylus]].
