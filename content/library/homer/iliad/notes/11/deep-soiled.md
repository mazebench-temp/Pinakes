---
summary: Iphidamas was reared in “deep-soiled Thrace, mother of flocks.”
---

“Iphidamas, son of Antenor, tall and brave, who was reared in deep-soiled Thrace, mother of flocks” (11.221–222; [[mother-of-flocks]], [[brave]]). The same pair of epithets describes Phthia in Phoenix's story (9.479). Cisseus, his mother's father, raised him there and gave him his own daughter in marriage (11.223–226; [[cisseus]], [[thrace]]).
