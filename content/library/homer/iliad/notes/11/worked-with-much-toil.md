---
summary: The sons of Antimachus offer Agamemnon the bronze, gold and “iron worked with much toil” in their father's house.
---

“There are many treasures lying in the house of Antimachus, bronze and gold and iron worked with much toil” (11.132–133; [[boundless-ransom]]). The verse is the one in which Adrastus begged Menelaus for his life (6.48) and Dolon begged Odysseus and Diomedes (10.379); all three suppliants are killed ([[supplication]]). See [[sons-of-antimachus]] and [[iron]].
