---
summary: Patroclus laments that the Achaean leaders are destined “to glut the swift dogs in Troy.”
grc: ["ταχέας κύνας"]
---

“Ah, wretched leaders and rulers of the Danaans, so you were destined, far from your friends and your native land, to glut the swift dogs in Troy with your white fat” (11.816–818; [[wretch]], [[dear-native-land]], [[white]]). The dogs are ταχέες, the ordinary adjective of speed, here as of the dogs of Book 3 (3.26). See [[dogs]].
