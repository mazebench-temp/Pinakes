---
summary: Patroclus asks Eurypylus whether the Achaeans can still hold back “huge Hector.”
---

“But come, tell me this, Eurypylus, hero nurtured by Zeus: will the Achaeans still somehow hold back huge Hector, or will they now perish, beaten down by his spear?” (11.819–821; [[zeus-nurtured]]). Eurypylus answers that there is no defense left, and that they will fall among the black ships (11.822–824; [[black-ships]]). See [[hector]].
