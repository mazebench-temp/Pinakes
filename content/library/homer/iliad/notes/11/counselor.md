---
summary: Agamemnon, wounded, says that “Zeus the counselor” did not allow him to fight all day.
---

Leaving the battle, Agamemnon calls on the leaders of the Argives to ward off the din of battle from the ships, “since Zeus the counselor did not allow me to fight the Trojans all day long” (11.276–279). He sees the hand of Zeus in his wound, and rightly: Zeus has made the wound the signal for Hector's charge (11.191–194; [[message-repeated]], [[plan-of-zeus]]). See [[zeus]] and [[agamemnon]].
