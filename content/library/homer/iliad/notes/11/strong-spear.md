summary: Agamemnon arms with “two strong spears”; the Trojans around Odysseus are “many and valiant”; Patroclus is “the valiant son of Menoetius.”
grc: ["ἄλκιμα δοῦρε", ἄλκιμοι, ἄλκιμος]
en: [valiant]
---

The arming verse has two spears, as at Patroclus' arming in Book 16: “and he took two strong spears tipped with bronze, sharp” (11.43–44; [[tipped-with-bronze]], [[sharp]], [[arming-scene]]). Of men ἄλκιμος is “valiant”: the Trojans close around the wounded Odysseus, “many and valiant” (11.483; [[of-subtle-wiles]]), and Patroclus is three times “the valiant son of Menoetius” (11.605, 11.814, 11.837; [[son-of-menoetius]]).
