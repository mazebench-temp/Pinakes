---
summary: Agamemnon is “wide-ruling” as he kills Isus and Antiphus and as he wrests Iphidamas' spear from him; in Nestor's story Poseidon is “the wide-ruling earth-shaker.”
---

“This time the son of Atreus, wide-ruling Agamemnon,” kills Isus and Antiphus, whom Achilles had once captured on Ida (11.107; [[isus]], [[antiphus-son-of-priam]]). When Iphidamas' spear bends on his belt, “wide-ruling Agamemnon caught the spear in his hand and dragged it toward him, eager as a lion” (11.238–239; [[panaiolos]]). In Nestor's story the title is given to Poseidon, who saves the Moliones: “had not their father, the wide-ruling earth-shaker” (11.751; [[earth-shaker]], [[the-moliones]]).
