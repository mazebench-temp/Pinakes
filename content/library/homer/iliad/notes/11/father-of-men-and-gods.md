---
summary: “The father of men and gods” comes down to Ida to send his message to Hector.
---

Agamemnon has nearly reached the city wall, “then the father of men and gods sat down on the peaks of Ida rich in springs, coming down from heaven, with the lightning in his hands” (11.181–184; [[rich-in-springs]]). The formula names him at the moment he acts to turn the day (11.185–194; [[plan-of-zeus]], [[message-repeated]]). See [[zeus]].
