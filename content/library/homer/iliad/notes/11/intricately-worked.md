---
summary: Agamemnon's shield and Odysseus' corselet are “intricately worked.”
grc: [πολυδαίδαλον]
---

Agamemnon takes up “the furious shield, man-covering, intricately worked,” whose circles, bosses, Gorgon and serpent the poem then describes (11.32–40; [[furious]], [[man-covering]], [[grim-faced]]). The work does not always protect: Socus' spear goes through Odysseus' shield “and it forced its way through the intricately worked corselet” (11.436; [[heavy-spear]]). See [[shield]] and [[corselet]].
