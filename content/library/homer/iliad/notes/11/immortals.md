---
summary: Dawn rises “to bring light to the immortals and to mortals,” and young Acamas is “like the immortals.”
---

The book opens with Dawn rising from the bed of Tithonus, “to bring light to the immortals and to mortals” (11.1–2 = Od. 5.1–2; [[dawn-line]], [[tithonus]]). Among the Trojan leaders is Antenor's son, “young Acamas, like the immortals” (11.60; [[like-the-immortals]], [[acamas]]). See [[theos]].
