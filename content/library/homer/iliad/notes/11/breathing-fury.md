---
summary: The Achaeans, “breathing fury,” fear for the wounded Machaon.
---

When Paris' arrow strikes Machaon, “the Achaeans breathing fury were terribly afraid, that if the war turned the enemy might somehow take him” (11.508–509; [[three-barbed]], [[machaon]]). The formula of fighting spirit names the army at a moment of fear. Idomeneus sends Nestor to carry the healer off (11.510–515; [[a-healer-is-worth-many]]). See [[menos]].
