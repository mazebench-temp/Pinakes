---
summary: Socus, brother of Charops, is “wealthy Socus.”
grc: [εὐηφενέος]
---

Odysseus wounds Charops, “the own brother of wealthy Socus” (11.426–427), and Socus comes to defend him (11.428; [[equal-to-the-gods]]). εὐηφενής = εὖ (“well”) + ἄφενος (“wealth”), the noun from which ἀφνειός is formed: “well-to-do, wealthy.” Homer uses it once more, when the ghost of Patroclus tells Achilles that he too is fated to die beneath the wall of the wealthy Trojans (23.80–81). See [[socus]].
