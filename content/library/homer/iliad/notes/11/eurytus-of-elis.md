---
summary: Eurytus is one of the two Moliones of Nestor's story: a boy among the Epeians who besieged Thryoessa, saved by his father Poseidon from Nestor's spear (11.709–710, 11.750–752).
refs: [11.709-710, 11.750-752]
---

Book 11 does not name him. “The two Moliones”, “the two sons of Actor”, arm among the Epeians who besiege Thryoessa, “still boys”, and Poseidon hides them in mist when Nestor would have killed them (11.709–710, 11.750–752; see [[the-moliones]]). The Catalogue names Actor's sons as Cteatus and Eurytus, fathers of the Epeian leaders Amphimachus and Thalpius (2.620–621; see [[cteatus]] and [[thalpius]]). This Eurytus is not the archer king of Oechalia (see [[eurytus]]).
