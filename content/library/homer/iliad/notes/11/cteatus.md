---
summary: Cteatus is one of the two Moliones of Nestor's story: a boy among the Epeians who besieged Thryoessa, saved by his father Poseidon from Nestor's spear (11.709–710, 11.750–752).
refs: [11.709-710, 11.750-752]
---

Book 11 does not name him. Nestor speaks of “the two Moliones”, “the two sons of Actor”, who armed among the Epeians “still boys, not yet well skilled in furious courage”, and whom “their father, the wide-ruling earth-shaker” hid in mist when Nestor would have killed them (11.709–710, 11.750–752; see [[the-moliones]]). That they are Cteatus and Eurytus follows from the patronymic: the sons of Actor in the Catalogue are Cteatus and Eurytus (2.621), and when Cteatus' son Amphimachus falls, Poseidon is angry “for his grandson” (13.185, 13.206–207; see [[amphimachus]] and [[eurytus-of-elis]]).
