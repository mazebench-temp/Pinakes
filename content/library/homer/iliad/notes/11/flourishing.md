---
summary: The “young men in their prime” who hunt the boar in the simile of Odysseus at bay.
---

“And as when around a boar the dogs and young men in their prime rush” (11.414–415): the boar comes out of a deep thicket, whetting its white tusk, and the men stand their ground, terrible though it is; so the Trojans rush around Odysseus (11.414–420; [[the-boar-at-bay]], [[supple]], [[white]]). θαλεροὶ αἰζηοί is the formula for young men at the height of their strength.
