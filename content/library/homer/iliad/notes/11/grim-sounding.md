---
summary: Cebriones speaks of “the edge of grim-sounding war,” and Eurypylus fears that Ajax will not escape from it.
---

Cebriones, beside Hector in the chariot, says that the two of them are fighting “at the edge of grim-sounding war” while Ajax routs the other Trojans (11.523–526; [[cebriones]]). Eurypylus, wounded, calls the Achaeans to stand around Ajax, “and I do not think he will escape from grim-sounding war” (11.589–590). See [[polemos]].
