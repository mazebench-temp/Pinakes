---
summary: The “hollow ships” are home in Strife's verses and the place to which the wounded are driven.
---

Strife's shout makes war sweeter to the Achaeans “than to go home in the hollow ships to their dear native land” (11.13–14 = 2.453–454; [[dear-native-land]]). After that the hollow ships are where the wounded go. Agamemnon and Diomedes each order a charioteer “to drive to the hollow ships; for his heart was distressed” (11.273–274, 11.399–400), Agamemnon's horses fly “toward the hollow ships” (11.281), and Nestor's horses carry Machaon there, “for there their hearts were glad to go” (11.519–520; [[chariot-journey]], [[the-wounded-chiefs]]).
