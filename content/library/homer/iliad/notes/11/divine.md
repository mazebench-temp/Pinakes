---
summary: Patroclus runs back past “the ships of divine Odysseus,” where the assembly place and the altars were.
---

“But when he came running to the ships of divine Odysseus, Patroclus, where their place of assembly and of justice was, where too the altars of the gods had been built for them” (11.806–808; [[themis]], [[assembly]]): there, in the middle of the camp, he meets the wounded Eurypylus. Odysseus' ship is where Strife stood at dawn to shout to both ends of the line (11.5–9). See [[odysseus]].
