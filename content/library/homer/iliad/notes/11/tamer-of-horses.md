---
summary: Hippasus, the father of Socus, is “tamer of horses” in Odysseus' boast, and Ajax holds off “the horse-taming Trojans.”
---

Odysseus boasts over Socus: “Socus, son of wise-hearted Hippasus, tamer of horses, the end of death was too quick for you” (11.450–451; [[wise-hearted]], [[hippasus-father-of-socus]]). Ajax, giving ground, “would remember his furious courage and turn back again, and hold off the battalions of the horse-taming Trojans” (11.566–568; [[furious]]). The name Hippodamus at 11.335 is a Trojan killed by Odysseus ([[hippodamus]]). See [[trojans]].
