---
summary: The baleful star of the simile sinks again “into the shadowy clouds.”
---

“And as a baleful star shows itself from out of the clouds, shining bright, and then again sinks into the shadowy clouds, so Hector now would appear among the foremost, and now among the hindmost” (11.62–65; [[baleful]], [[the-baleful-star]]). Here σκιόεις is said of clouds, as the general article notes. See [[hector]].
