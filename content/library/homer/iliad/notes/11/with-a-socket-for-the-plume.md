---
summary: Hector's helmet, Apollo's gift, is “three-layered, with its socket for the plume.”
grc: [αὐλῶπις]
---

Diomedes' spear glances off Hector's helmet, “three-layered, with its socket for the plume, which Phoebus Apollo had given him” (11.352–353; [[three-layered]], [[phoebus]]). The helmet is a τρυφάλεια, as the epithet always requires. See [[helmet]] and [[hector]].
