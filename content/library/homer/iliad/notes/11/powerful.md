summary: “Powerful Agamemnon” takes the sons of Antimachus and pursues the Trojans to the city; in the fuller εὐρὺ κρείων (11.107, 11.238, 11.751) the word belongs to wide-ruling.
except: [11.107, 11.238, 11.751]
---

The title marks Agamemnon three times in his aristeia: when he catches the sons of Antimachus in their chariot (11.126; [[sons-of-antimachus]]), when he follows the routed Trojans, “always killing, calling on the Argives” (11.153–154), and in the simile of the lion and the cattle, “so powerful Agamemnon, son of Atreus, pursued them, always killing the hindmost” (11.177–178; [[lion]]). Three times the fuller title εὐρὺ κρείων is used, twice of Agamemnon and once of Poseidon, and those verses belong to [[wide-ruling]]. See [[agamemnon]].
