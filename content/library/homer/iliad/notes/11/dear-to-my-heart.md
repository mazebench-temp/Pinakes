---
summary: Achilles calls Patroclus “dear to my heart” as he sends him to Nestor.
---

“Brilliant son of Menoetius, dear to my heart, now I think the Achaeans will stand around my knees beseeching me” (11.608–610; [[brilliant]], [[son-of-menoetius]]). The address opens the errand that the narrator has just called “the beginning of evil” for Patroclus (11.604; [[the-death-of-patroclus]]). See [[achilles]] and [[patroclus]].
