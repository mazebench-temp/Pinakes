---
summary: Nestor and Odysseus came to “the well-appointed house of Peleus” to gather the army.
---

“We had come to the well-appointed house of Peleus, gathering the army throughout the bountiful land of Achaea” (11.769–770; [[bountiful-earth]], [[peleus-sends-achilles-to-troy]]). Of a house the translation renders the phrase “well-appointed,” as in Book 6. See [[peleus]].
