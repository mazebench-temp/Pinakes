---
summary: Diomedes warns Odysseus of “mighty Hector” rolling down on them like a bane.
---

“Here is this bane rolling down on us, mighty Hector; but come, let us stand firm and hold our ground and beat him off” (11.347–348). Diomedes then hits Hector's helmet with his spear and drives him back into the throng (11.349–356; [[long-shadowed]], [[three-layered]]). See [[hector]] and [[diomedes]].
