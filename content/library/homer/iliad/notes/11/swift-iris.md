---
summary: Zeus sends Iris to Hector: “Go, swift Iris, and tell this word to Hector.”
---

“Go, swift Iris, and tell this word to Hector: as long as he sees Agamemnon, shepherd of the people, rushing among the champions, … so long let him draw back” (11.186–189; [[shepherd-of-the-people]]). Iris repeats the message to Hector word for word (11.200–209; [[message-repeated]], [[golden-winged]]). See [[iris]].
