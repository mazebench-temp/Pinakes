---
summary: Nestor's cup is “studded with golden nails,” in the phrase used of Achilles' scepter in Book 1.
---

“And beside them a beautiful cup, which the old man had brought from home, studded with golden nails” (11.632–633; [[perikalles]]). The phrase opens the verse here as it did at 1.246, where it describes the scepter that Achilles throws to the ground. The cup has four handles, each with two golden doves, and two bases; another man could barely lift it full, but Nestor lifts it without toil (11.633–637; [[the-cup-of-nestor]]). See [[nestor]].
