summary: Nestor and Machaon step down onto “the bountiful earth” at Nestor's hut, and Nestor recalls gathering the army “throughout the bountiful land of Achaea.”
grc: ["Ἀχαιΐδα πουλυβότειραν"]
en: [bountiful land]
---

“They themselves stepped down onto the bountiful earth” (11.619) as they reach Nestor's hut and Eurymedon unyokes the horses ([[eurymedon-attendant-of-nestor]]). In his speech to Patroclus Nestor gives the epithet to a land: he and Odysseus came to Peleus' house “gathering the army throughout the bountiful land of Achaea” (11.769–770; [[achaea]], [[peleus-sends-achilles-to-troy]]). The phrase Ἀχαιΐδα πουλυβότειραν occurs only here. See [[nestor]].
