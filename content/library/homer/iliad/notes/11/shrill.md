---
summary: Cebriones lashes Hector's horses “with the shrill whip.”
grc: [λιγυρῇ]
---

“So he spoke, and lashed the fine-maned horses with the shrill whip; and they, feeling the stroke, lightly bore the swift chariot among the Trojans and the Achaeans” (11.531–533; [[fine-maned]], [[swift]]). It is the only whip in Homer called λιγυρός; the word is otherwise used of the wind, of a bird and of the Sirens' song. See [[cebriones]].
