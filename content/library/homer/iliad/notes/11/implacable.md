---
summary: The sons of Antimachus beg Agamemnon with soothing words, but hear a voice that will not be soothed.
grc: [ἀμείλικτον]
en: [would not be soothed]
---

“So the two, weeping, addressed the king with soothing words; but the voice they heard would not be soothed” (11.136–137; [[soothing-words]], [[sons-of-antimachus]]). ἀμείλικτος, “not to be soothed,” is the verbal adjective of μειλίσσω, of the same family as ἀμείλιχος and μειλίχιος. The half-verse returns when Lycaon begs Achilles for his life (21.97–98). See [[agamemnon]] and [[supplication]].
