---
summary: Terror and Rout surround the Gorgon on Agamemnon's shield (11.37), the second of the three times the Iliad names Terror, always beside Rout.
---

On the shield that Agamemnon takes up for his day of battle, “the grim-faced Gorgon was set as a crown, / glaring terribly, and around her were Terror and Rout” (11.36–37; see [[gorgon]], [[rout]] and [[shield]]). In Book 4 the two drive the armies on with Ares and Athena (4.439–440), and in Book 15 Ares bids them yoke his horses (15.119–120; see [[ares]]). On the shield they do nothing. They stand around the Gorgon as Strife, Courage and Onslaught stand within the crown of Rout on the aegis (5.739–741; see [[aegis]]), and the poem does not say whether they are figures worked in the metal or the powers themselves.
