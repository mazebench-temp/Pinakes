---
summary: Patroclus runs along the ships “to Achilles, grandson of Aeacus.”
---

Nestor's speech has stirred Patroclus' heart, “and he went running along the ships to Achilles, grandson of Aeacus” (11.804–805). He does not arrive in this book: at the ships of Odysseus he meets the wounded Eurypylus and stays to tend him (11.806–848; [[healing]]). He stands by Achilles at the beginning of Book 16 (16.2). See [[achilles]] and [[patroclus]].
