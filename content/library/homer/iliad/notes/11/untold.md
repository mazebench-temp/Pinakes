---
summary: Iphidamas promised flocks “pastured for him in untold numbers,” and Neleus took “a vast amount” of the spoil.
grc: [ἄσπετα]
---

Iphidamas gave a hundred cattle as his bride-price and promised a thousand goats and sheep, “which were pastured for him in untold numbers” (11.244–245; [[marriage]], [[mother-of-flocks]]). In Nestor's story Neleus, angered by Augeas' words and deeds, “took out for himself a vast amount” of the spoil and gave the rest to the people to divide (11.703–705; [[division-of-spoils]], [[prize-winning]]). See [[neleus]].
