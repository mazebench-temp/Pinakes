---
summary: Merops would not let his sons go into “man-destroying war.”
---

The sons of Merops of Percote, “who above all men knew the art of prophecy, and would not let his sons go into man-destroying war” (11.329–331 = 2.831–833), are killed by Diomedes ([[the-sons-of-merops]], [[merops]], [[black-death]]). See [[polemos]].
