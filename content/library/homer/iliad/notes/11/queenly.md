---
summary: Odysseus tells Socus that his “lady mother” will not close his eyes, and Nestor guesses that Achilles' “lady mother” has told him something from Zeus.
---

“Ah, wretch, your father and your lady mother will not close your eyes in death, but the birds that eat raw flesh will tear at you” (11.452–454; [[wretch]], [[eating-raw-flesh]]). In Nestor's plan the phrase names Thetis: “if in his mind he is shunning some oracle, and his lady mother has told him something from Zeus” (11.794–795; [[thetis]]). See [[meter]].
