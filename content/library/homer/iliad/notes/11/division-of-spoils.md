**Nestor's prize.** Hecamede, daughter of Arsinous, came to Nestor from the sack of Tenedos: “the Achaeans picked out (ἔξελον) for him, because he was the best of all in counsel” (11.626–627; see [[hecamede]] and [[arsinous]]). The army chooses a prize of honor for a leader out of the common spoil, as the sons of the Achaeans chose Briseis for Achilles, in the same verb (16.56, 18.444; see [[geras]]). Nestor's is given for counsel, not for fighting: ἀριστεύεσκεν, “he was the best”, is said of his advice (see [[aristos]]).

**The Pylian division.** After the raid on Elis the heralds call at dawn “for all to come to whom a debt was owed in shining Elis”, and the leading men of the Pylians gather and divide the spoil, “for the Epeians owed a debt to many”, since Heracles had left Pylos weak and the Epeians had treated the Pylians with outrage (11.685–695; see [[heralds]], [[heracles-sacks-pylos]] and [[nestor-and-the-epeians]]). The spoil pays the debts, each creditor in turn. Neleus takes a herd of cattle and a great flock of sheep, three hundred head with their herdsmen, because a great debt was owed to him too: four prize-winning horses that Augeas kept from the games (11.696–702; see [[contests]] and [[augeas]]). **The words of fair shares.** δαιτρεύω (11.688, 11.705), “divide, carve”, is the verb of the δαιτρός, the carver who serves out portions at a feast, and of δαίς, the feast in which each has his share (see [[feast]]). ἴσης, “of his fair share” (11.705), is the equal portion of the “equal feast” (δαὶς ἐΐση; see [[equal-feast]]), and the word of Achilles' bitter complaint in Book 9: “The same share (ἴση μοῖρα) for the man who stays behind and for one who fights his hardest” (9.318). The whole half-verse returns in the Odyssey, when Odysseus divides the spoil of the Cicones and the Cyclops' sheep among his men “so that no one might go away cheated of his fair share” (Odyssey 9.42, 9.549). Achilles' complaint was that Agamemnon kept most for himself (9.330–333); here the king also takes “a vast amount” for himself, but for a debt owed to him, and the people divide the rest.
