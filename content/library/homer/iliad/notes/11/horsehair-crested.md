---
summary: Agamemnon's helmet has “a horsehair crest,” and the plume nods terribly above it.
---

“And on his head he set a helmet with two ridges and four plates, with a horsehair crest, and the plume nodded terribly above it” (11.41–42; [[with-two-ridges-and-four-plates]]). The second verse is the one of Paris' arming (3.337), and the first is the one of Athena's (5.743; [[arming-scene]]). See [[helmet]].
