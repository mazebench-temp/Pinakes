---
summary: Socus' “heavy spear” goes through Odysseus' shield and corselet, and Odysseus draws it out of his own flesh.
---

“And through the shining shield went the heavy spear, and it forced its way through the intricately worked corselet, and tore all the skin from his ribs” (11.435–437; [[phaeinos]], [[intricately-worked]]); Athena keeps it from his entrails (11.437–438; [[pallas]]). When Socus is dead, Odysseus draws “wise-hearted Socus' heavy spear out of his flesh and out of the bossed shield” (11.456–457; [[bossed]], [[wise-hearted]]). See [[socus]] and [[odysseus]].
