---
summary: The book opens with Dawn rising from the bed of “noble Tithonus.”
---

“Now Dawn rose from her bed beside noble Tithonus, to bring light to the immortals and to mortals” (11.1–2; [[dawn-line]], [[immortals]]). The two verses open the fifth book of the Odyssey as well (Od. 5.1–2). Tithonus, a son of Laomedon, is named in the Iliad only here and in Aeneas' genealogy (20.237; [[tithonus]]).
