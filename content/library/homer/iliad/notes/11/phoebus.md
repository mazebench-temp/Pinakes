---
summary: Hector's helmet was the gift of “Phoebus Apollo,” and Diomedes says that “Phoebus Apollo” has saved him again.
---

Diomedes' spear glances off Hector's helmet, “three-layered, with its socket for the plume, which Phoebus Apollo had given him” (11.352–353; [[three-layered]], [[with-a-socket-for-the-plume]]). When Hector escapes in his chariot, Diomedes taunts him: “now again Phoebus Apollo has saved you, to whom you must pray when you go into the thud of javelins” (11.363–364). Apollo has no other part in the book. See [[apollo]].
