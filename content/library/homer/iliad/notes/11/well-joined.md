---
summary: Iris finds Hector “standing among his horses and his well-joined chariot.”
grc: [κολλητοῖσιν]
---

“She found the son of wise-hearted Priam, brilliant Hector, standing among his horses and his well-joined chariot” (11.197–198; [[wise-hearted]], [[brilliant]]). He is in his chariot, out of the fighting, as Zeus drew him from it (11.163–164). See [[chariot]] and [[hector]].
