---
summary: Hector, on the left of the battle, is “doing grim deeds” with spear and horsemanship.
---

Hector is fighting by the Scamander, “in the throng with them, doing grim deeds with his spear and his horsemanship, and he wrecked the battalions of young men” (11.502–503). He has not yet heard that Ajax is routing the Trojans elsewhere; then Cebriones turns his chariot toward Ajax (11.497–499, 11.521–530; [[cebriones]], [[unquenchable-cry]]). See [[hector]].
