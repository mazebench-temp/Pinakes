---
summary: Helen “of the lovely hair” is named in the verse that introduces Paris as he shoots Diomedes and Machaon.
---

“But Alexander, the husband of Helen of the lovely hair” (11.369, 11.505): the epithet of Helen belongs to the verse that names Paris as the archer who wounds Diomedes and Machaon ([[husband-of-helen]]). See [[helen]].
