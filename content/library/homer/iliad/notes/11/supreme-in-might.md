summary: In Nestor's story the Pylians sacrifice to “Zeus, supreme in might,” by the Alpheus.
---

“There we offered fine sacrifices to Zeus, supreme in might, and a bull to Alpheus, and a bull to Poseidon, and to gray-eyed Athena a cow from the herd” (11.727–729; [[gray-eyed]], [[sacrifice]]). See [[zeus]] and [[nestor-and-the-epeians]].
