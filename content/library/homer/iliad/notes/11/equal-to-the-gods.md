---
summary: Socus, Ajax and Patroclus are each “a man equal to the gods” as they step into a scene.
---

The verse-end ἰσόθεος φώς is given three times in Book 11, each time to a man as he comes forward: Socus, who comes to defend his brother Charops (11.427–428; [[socus]], [[wealthy]]); Ajax, who follows Menelaus to rescue Odysseus (11.472); and Patroclus, who appears at the door of Nestor's hut (11.644; [[parching]]). Two of the three are about to die: Socus at once (11.446–449), Patroclus in Book 16.
