---
summary: Diomedes, “good at the war cry,” shudders when he sees Hector coming at him.
---

“Seeing him, Diomedes, good at the war cry, shuddered” (11.345): Hector has seen Diomedes and Odysseus killing his men and rushes at them, shouting (11.343–344). The verse is the one of Book 5, where Diomedes sees Ares beside Hector (5.594–596). This time he stands, calls Odysseus to him and hits Hector's helmet with his spear (11.346–353; [[three-layered]]).
