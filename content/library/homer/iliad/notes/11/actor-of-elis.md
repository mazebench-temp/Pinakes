Ἀκτορίωνε, “the two sons of Actor” (11.750), is the only mention of Actor of Elis in the book. In the next verse Nestor calls Poseidon their father (11.751), so the twins are Actor's in name and Poseidon's by birth (see [[the-moliones]] and [[poseidon]]).

He is not the Actor whom Nestor names a few verses later as the father of Menoetius (Ἄκτορος υἱός, 11.785; see [[actor-father-of-menoetius]]).

## Outside Homer

A scholium on 11.750 reports that Hesiod made Actor and Molione their parents in name and Poseidon their father in fact (Hesiod fr. 9 Evelyn-White), and Apollodorus makes Actor a brother of Augeas (*Library* 2.7.2).
