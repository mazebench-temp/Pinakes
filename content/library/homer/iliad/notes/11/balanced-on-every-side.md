---
summary: Hector carries his round shield among the foremost, and Socus' spear goes through Odysseus' shield, “balanced on every side.”
---

“And Hector among the foremost carried his shield, balanced on every side” (11.61), as he moves through the Trojan ranks like a baleful star ([[baleful]], [[the-baleful-star]]). Socus' spear strikes Odysseus' shield of the same kind: “he stabbed at his shield, balanced on every side,” and the spear goes through it and through his corselet (11.434–436; [[phaeinos]], [[intricately-worked]]).
