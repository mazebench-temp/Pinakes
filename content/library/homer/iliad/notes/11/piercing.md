---
summary: Patroclus cuts the “sharp, piercing arrow” from Eurypylus' thigh.
grc: [περιπευκές]
---

“There he laid him out, and with a knife cut from his thigh the sharp, piercing arrow” (11.844–845; [[sharp]], [[knife]]). περιπευκής, “very sharp,” has the stem πευκ- of ἐχεπευκής and of the “bitter” war of Book 10 ([[bitter-war]]); it occurs only here. See [[arrows]] and [[healing]].
