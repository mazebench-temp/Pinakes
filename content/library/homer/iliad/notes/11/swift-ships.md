---
summary: Zeus sends Strife to “the swift ships of the Achaeans”; the ships are where Agamemnon saw Isus and Antiphus, where Ajax keeps the Trojans from going, and where Nestor fears the fire.
grc: [θοῇσιν]
---

“And Zeus sent Strife forth to the swift ships of the Achaeans” (11.3; [[strife]]) opens the day. Agamemnon recognizes Isus and Antiphus, “for he had seen them before by the swift ships” when Achilles brought them as captives (11.111–112). Ajax, giving ground, “kept them all from making their way to the swift ships” (11.569; [[retreat]]), and Nestor asks whether Achilles will wait “until the swift ships near the sea burn with consuming fire” (11.666–667; [[destructive]]).
