---
summary: Patroclus addresses Nestor twice as “old sir, nurtured by Zeus,” and Eurypylus as “hero nurtured by Zeus.”
---

Patroclus refuses Nestor's chair: “There is no time to sit, old sir, nurtured by Zeus, and you will not persuade me” (11.648), and he ends with the same address: “You know well, old sir, nurtured by Zeus, what kind of man he is” (11.653–654; [[geron]]). To the wounded Eurypylus he says: “But come, tell me this, Eurypylus, hero nurtured by Zeus” (11.819; [[huge]]). See [[patroclus]].
