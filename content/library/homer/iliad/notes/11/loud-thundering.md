---
summary: The dust of the rout rises under “the thundering hooves of horses.”
grc: [ἐρίγδουποι]
en: [thundering]
---

“Foot soldiers killed foot soldiers fleeing under compulsion, and horsemen horsemen; and under them the dust rose from the plain, raised by the thundering hooves of horses” (11.150–152). Zeus' epithet, the word for thunder, is given to the noise of hooves, its only use in Homer of anything but Zeus. See [[horses]].
