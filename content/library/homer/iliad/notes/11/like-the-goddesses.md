---
summary: Hecamede, who mixes the drink for Nestor and Machaon, is “the woman, like the goddesses.”
grc: ["ἐϊκυῖα θεῇσιν"]
---

“In it the woman, like the goddesses, made a mixture for them with Pramnian wine” (11.638–639; [[pramnian]], [[kykeon]]). The captive from Tenedos, chosen for Nestor from the spoil (11.625–627), is named with the same praise as Castianeira, the mother of Gorgythion (8.305). See [[hecamede]].
