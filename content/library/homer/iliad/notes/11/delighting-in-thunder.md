---
summary: Peleus burns the thigh pieces “to Zeus who delights in thunder” when Nestor and Odysseus come to his house.
---

Nestor and Odysseus find Peleus sacrificing in his courtyard: “the old horse-driving Peleus was burning the fat thigh pieces of an ox to Zeus who delights in thunder in the enclosure of the courtyard” (11.772–774; [[horse-driving]], [[sacrifice]]). He pours wine over the burning offerings from a golden goblet as the guests arrive (11.774–775; [[peleus-sends-achilles-to-troy]]). See [[zeus]] and [[peleus]].
