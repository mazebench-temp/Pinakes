---
summary: Hector calls on the “Trojans and Lycians and Dardanians who fight hand to hand” when Agamemnon leaves the field.
---

Hector sees Agamemnon going away and shouts: “Trojans and Lycians and Dardanians who fight hand to hand, be men, my friends, and remember your furious courage” (11.286–287 = 8.173–174; [[furious]], [[exhortation]]). Their best man is gone, he says, and Zeus has given him a great triumph (11.288–290). See [[dardanians]], [[lycians]] and [[hector]].
