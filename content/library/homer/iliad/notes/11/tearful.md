---
summary: Achilles watches “the sheer toil and the tearful onslaught” from his ship.
---

“For he was standing on the stern of his ship, huge in the hull, watching the sheer toil and the tearful onslaught” (11.600–601; [[huge-hulled]], [[steep]], [[watching-the-war]]). ἰῶκα is a form of ἰωκή, “onslaught” ([[onslaught]]). See [[achilles]].
