---
summary: In Nestor's story the Pylians sacrifice a cow “to gray-eyed Athena” by the Alpheus.
---

“There we offered fine sacrifices to Zeus, supreme in might, and a bull to Alpheus, and a bull to Poseidon, and to gray-eyed Athena a cow from the herd” (11.727–729; [[supreme-in-might]], [[sacrifice]]). Athena has come by night to bring the Pylians word and gather the army (11.714–717), and she turns it back at the end of the pursuit (11.758). See [[athena]] and [[nestor-and-the-epeians]].
