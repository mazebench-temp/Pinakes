---
summary: “Swift Iris, wind-footed” obeys Zeus and goes down from Ida to Hector.
---

“So he spoke, and swift Iris, wind-footed, did not disobey, and she went down from the mountains of Ida to sacred Ilios” (11.195–196; [[hieros]]). She delivers Zeus' message to Hector word for word (11.199–209; [[message-repeated]], [[golden-winged]], [[swift-footed]]). See [[iris]].
