---
summary: Ajax swings “his shield of seven oxhides” behind his back as Zeus puts him to flight.
---

“And he stood amazed, and swung his shield of seven oxhides behind his back” (11.545): Ajax gives ground with the great shield covering him, and the Trojans' spears stick in it as they follow (11.565, 11.572; [[bold]], [[the-donkey-in-the-field]]). He has carried it “like a tower” to Odysseus' rescue (11.485; [[like-a-tower]]), and Cebriones knows him by it: “for he has the broad shield around his shoulders” (11.526–527). See [[the-shield-of-ajax]] and [[ajax]].
