---
summary: Menoetius charged Patroclus to give Achilles “a shrewd word” and good counsel.
---

Nestor repeats Menoetius' parting words to his son: “But speak a shrewd word to him well, and give him counsel, and direct him; and he will obey you, to his good” (11.788–789; [[menoetius]], [[obedience-and-persuasion]]). Nestor's own shrewd word follows, the plan that Patroclus go out in Achilles' armor (11.790–803; [[the-persuasion-of-a-friend]]). See [[patroclus]].
