summary: Strife stands by Odysseus' black ship, and Eurypylus says that the Achaeans will fall among the black ships and asks to be led to his own; κῆρα μέλαιναν (11.360, 11.443) belongs to black doom.
except: [11.360, 11.443]
---

Strife takes her stand by “Odysseus' black ship, huge in the hull” (11.5; [[huge-hulled]]), which lies in the middle of the camp. At the end of the book the wounded Eurypylus tells Patroclus that there will be no defense for the Achaeans any longer, “but they will fall among the black ships” (11.823–824), and asks him to lead him “to my black ship” (11.828). In Book 9 the same words were said of the Trojans, who “will fall upon the black ships” (9.235). The two verses in which μέλαινα describes doom belong to [[black-doom]] (11.360, 11.443).
