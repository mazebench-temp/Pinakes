---
summary: The title is given to Agamemnon in Zeus' message, to Diomedes, to Machaon three times, and to the Trojans Bianor and Apisaon as they die.
---

**Agamemnon.** Zeus' message to Hector names him twice in the same words: as long as Hector sees “Agamemnon, shepherd of the people,” rushing among the champions, he is to hold back (11.187, 11.202; [[message-repeated]]).

**The wounded.** Paris bends his bow at “the son of Tydeus, shepherd of the people” (11.370); he stops “Machaon, shepherd of the people,” as he fights (11.506), and the formula follows Machaon as Nestor's mares carry him from the field (11.598) and as Patroclus recognizes him in Nestor's hut (11.651; [[the-wounded-chiefs]]). Patroclus leads Eurypylus, “the shepherd of the people,” to his hut to treat him (11.842; [[healing]]).

**The dead.** Agamemnon's first victim is “Bianor, shepherd of the people” (11.92; [[bianor]]), and Eurypylus kills “Apisaon, son of Phausius, shepherd of the people” (11.578; [[apisaon]]). See [[agamemnon]], [[machaon]] and [[eurypylus]].
