---
summary: Menelaus hears “the cry of stout-hearted Odysseus” and calls Ajax to his rescue.
grc: [ταλασίφρονος]
---

“The cry of stout-hearted Odysseus has come around me, as though, all alone, he were being overpowered by the Trojans” (11.466–468). The epithet of endurance is given to Odysseus at the moment when he is wounded and alone, holding out until help comes (11.473–488; [[the-jackals-and-the-stag]]). It is the only time the Iliad gives the word to Odysseus, whose standing epithet it becomes in the Odyssey. See [[odysseus]] and [[menelaus]].
