Hector “called out to the Trojans and Lycians” (Τρωσί τε καὶ Λυκίοισιν, 11.285) in the verse that introduces the cry twice more, at the ships (15.424, 15.485), and the cry itself is the one he raised in Book 8 (11.286–287 = 8.173–174; see [[hector]], [[trojans]] and [[dardanians]]). Of all the allies, only the Lycians have a place in it. Their leaders, Sarpedon and Glaucus, are not named in Book 11; they come forward at the Achaean wall in the next book (12.101–104, 12.310–328; see [[sarpedon]] and [[glaucus]]).
