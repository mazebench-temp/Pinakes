---
summary: Agamemnon stabs Coon and Odysseus stabs Chersidamas “beneath the bossed shield,” and Odysseus draws Socus' spear out of his own.
---

Agamemnon stabs Coon “beneath the bossed shield” as Coon drags his brother's body into the throng (11.259–260; [[bronze-fitted]], [[coon]]). Odysseus, surrounded, stabs Chersidamas “in the navel, beneath the bossed shield” as he leaps down from his chariot (11.423–425; [[chersidamas]]). After killing Socus he draws Socus' spear “out of his flesh and out of the bossed shield” (11.456–457; [[heavy-spear]]), and the blood that spurts from the wound brings the Trojans down on him (11.458–460).
