---
summary: Aeneas, “honored by the Trojan people like a god,” is among the leaders drawn up around Hector.
---

The Trojans are drawn up around Hector, Polydamas, “and Aeneas, who was honored by the Trojan people like a god,” and the three sons of Antenor (11.56–60; [[blameless]], [[son-of-antenor]]). Aeneas has no other part in the book. The formula was last used of Agamemnon at the beginning of Book 10 (10.33). See [[aeneas]].
