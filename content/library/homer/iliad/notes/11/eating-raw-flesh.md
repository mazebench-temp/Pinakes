---
summary: The jackals of the simile “eat raw flesh,” and Odysseus promises Socus to the birds “that eat raw flesh.”
grc: [ὠμοφάγοι, ὠμησταὶ]
---

In the simile of the wounded stag, “the jackals that eat raw flesh devour it in the mountains” until a lion comes (11.479; [[the-jackals-and-the-stag]], [[blood-red]]). The synonym ὠμηστής, “eating raw,” is the word of Odysseus' boast over Socus: his father and mother will not close his eyes, “but the birds that eat raw flesh will tear at you, beating their thick wings around you” (11.453–454; [[wretch]], [[birds]]). The translation renders the two words alike. See [[jackal]].
