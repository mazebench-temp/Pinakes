---
summary: Iphidamas dies “far from his wife, whom he had wooed,” his wedded wife, of whom he had seen no joy.
grc: [μνηστῆς]
en: [whom he had wooed]
---

“So he fell there and slept the sleep of bronze, pitiable, far from his wife, whom he had wooed, fighting for his townsmen, his wedded wife, from whom he had seen no joy, though he had given much” (11.241–243). Two epithets of the lawful wife stand in successive verses: μνηστή, the wife won by courtship and gifts, and κουριδίη, the wedded wife ([[marriage]]). The gifts follow: a hundred cattle given, and a thousand goats and sheep promised (11.244–245; [[untold]]). See [[iphidamas]] and [[husbands-and-wives]].
