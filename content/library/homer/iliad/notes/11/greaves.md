---
summary: Agamemnon puts on his greaves first, in the two verses of every full arming (11.17–18 = 3.330–331).
---

“First he put the greaves around his shins, fine ones, fitted with silver ankle-pieces” (11.17–18). The pair of verses is the same in all four full armings, Paris', Agamemnon's, Patroclus' and Achilles' (3.330–331, 11.17–18, 16.131–132, 19.369–370; see [[arming-scene]]). In Agamemnon's arming it is plain formula, and the description begins with the next piece, the corselet (see [[corselet]]). The men who follow him into the Trojan line are, in their standing epithet, the “well-greaved” Achaeans (11.149; see [[well-greaved]]).
