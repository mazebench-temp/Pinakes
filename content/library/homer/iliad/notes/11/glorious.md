---
summary: Iphidamas reached “the measure of glorious youth” in Thrace.
grc: [ἐρικυδέος]
---

Cisseus raised Iphidamas in his house, “and when he reached the measure of glorious youth, he kept him there, and gave him his daughter” (11.225–226; [[cisseus]], [[iphidamas]]). A few verses later he dies, “far from his wife, whom he had wooed” (11.241–243; [[wedded]]).
