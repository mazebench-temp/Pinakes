---
summary: Menelaus addresses Ajax as “sprung from Zeus”; Eurypylus is so named as he meets Patroclus, and so addresses him.
---

Menelaus calls Ajax to Odysseus' rescue: “Telamonian Ajax, sprung from Zeus, lord of the people” (11.465; [[telamonian]], [[koiranos]]). At the end of the book Patroclus meets Eurypylus, “the son of Euaemon, sprung from Zeus, shot in the thigh with an arrow” (11.810), and Eurypylus answers him with the same word: “Patroclus, sprung from Zeus, no defense of the Achaeans will there be any longer” (11.823–824; [[black-ships]]).
