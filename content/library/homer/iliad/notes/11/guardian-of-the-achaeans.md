---
summary: Patroclus calls Nestor “the Gerenian, the guardian of the Achaeans” when he tells Eurypylus of his message.
---

“I am going to tell wise-hearted Achilles the word with which Nestor charged me, the Gerenian, the guardian of the Achaeans; but even so I will not leave you in your distress” (11.839–841; [[gerenian-horseman]], [[wise-hearted]]). The word Nestor gave him is the plan that Patroclus go out in Achilles' armor (11.796–803); Patroclus puts it off to care for Eurypylus (11.842–848). See [[nestor]] and [[patroclus]].
