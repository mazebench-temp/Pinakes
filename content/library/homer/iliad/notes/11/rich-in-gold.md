---
summary: Athena and Hera thunder at Agamemnon's arming, “honoring the king of Mycenae rich in gold.”
---

“And Athena and Hera thundered over him, honoring the king of Mycenae rich in gold” (11.45–46). The thunder of the two goddesses closes the arming scene (11.15–46; [[arming-scene]]). See [[mycenae]] and [[agamemnon]].
