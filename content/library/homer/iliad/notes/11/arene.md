---
summary: Arene marks the place where the river Minyeios falls into the sea; there the Pylian horsemen waited for dawn on their march to relieve Thryoessa (11.722–724).
grc: [Ἀρήνης]
---

“There is a river, Minyeios, that falls into the sea / near Arene, where we waited for brilliant Dawn, / we horsemen of the Pylians” (11.722–724; see [[minyeios]] and [[pylians]]). From there the army marched at dawn and reached the Alpheus at midday (11.725–726; see [[alpheus]] and [[nestor-and-the-epeians]]). In the Catalogue Arene is the second of Nestor's towns, after Pylos (2.591).

## Outside Homer

Strabo reports that Arene could be found nowhere for certain, and that it was placed near Samicum in Triphylia, by the river Anigrus, “formerly called Minyeios” (*Geography* 8.3.19).
