---
summary: Hector sets the Trojans on the Achaeans as a hunter sets his “white-toothed hounds” on a boar or a lion.
grc: [ἀργιόδοντας]
en: [white-toothed]
---

“And as a hunter sets his white-toothed hounds on a wild boar or a lion, so against the Achaeans he set on the great-hearted Trojans” (11.292–294; [[wild]], [[the-hunter-and-his-hounds]]). This is the epithet's only use of dogs; elsewhere it describes swine, and the translation renders it “white-toothed” here. See [[dogs]] and [[hector]].
