---
summary: The gods blame “the son of Cronus, lord of the dark clouds,” for meaning to give glory to the Trojans.
grc: [κελαινεφέα]
---

“And they all blamed the son of Cronus, lord of the dark clouds, because he meant to hold out glory to the Trojans” (11.78–79; [[son-of-cronus]], [[kydos]]). Zeus pays them no heed and sits apart, exulting in his glory (11.80–83; [[exulting-in-glory]]). See [[zeus]].
