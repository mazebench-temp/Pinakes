---
summary: Zeus' message tells Hector to send the army “to fight the enemy,” and Nestor fears “consuming fire” at the ships.
grc: ["μάρνασθαι δηΐοισι"]
---

In the message that Zeus gives to Iris and Iris repeats to Hector, Hector is to order the rest of the army μάρνασθαι δηΐοισι, “to fight the enemy in the fierce battle” (11.190, 11.205; [[message-repeated]], [[krateros]]). The adjective of fire is in Nestor's question: will Achilles wait “until the swift ships near the sea burn with consuming fire in spite of the Argives”? (11.666–667; [[swift-ships]]). See [[achilles]].
