---
summary: Agamemnon leaps in where the battalions are most confused, “and with him the other well-greaved Achaeans.”
---

“These he left; and where the most battalions were thrown into confusion, there he leapt in, and with him the other well-greaved Achaeans” (11.148–149): the rout that follows the death of the sons of Antimachus, foot soldiers killing foot soldiers and horsemen horsemen (11.150–152; [[loud-thundering]]).
