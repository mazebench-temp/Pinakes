---
summary: In the simile of the jackals the Trojans close around Odysseus as jackals around a wounded “horned stag.”
en: [horned]
---

“The Trojans pressed, as blood-red jackals in the mountains press around a horned stag that is wounded” (11.474–475): a hunter's arrow has struck the stag, which runs while its blood is warm; when it falls, the jackals tear it, until a god brings a lion and they scatter ([[blood-red]], [[eating-raw-flesh]], [[ravager]], [[the-jackals-and-the-stag]]). The wounded Odysseus is the stag, and Ajax the lion (11.473–486). The translation renders κεραός “horned” here. See [[deer]].
