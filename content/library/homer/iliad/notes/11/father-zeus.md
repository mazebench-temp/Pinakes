---
summary: Hector shines like the lightning of “aegis-bearing father Zeus,” and “father Zeus, throned on high” puts Ajax to flight.
---

In his bronze Hector “shone like the lightning of aegis-bearing father Zeus” (11.66; [[aegis-bearing]]). Later, when Hector has avoided Ajax, “father Zeus, throned on high, roused Ajax to flight” (11.544; [[high-throned]]); Ajax swings his shield behind him and gives ground (11.545–547; [[seven-oxhide]]). See [[zeus]].
