---
summary: The ships of Achilles and Ajax, drawn up at the two ends of the line, and the twelve ships that Iphidamas left at Percote.
---

Strife stands on Odysseus' ship in the middle of the line, so that her shout carries to Ajax and to Achilles, “who had hauled up their balanced ships at the farthest ends” (11.5–9 = 8.222–226; [[huge-hulled]], [[strife]]). Iphidamas, coming from Thrace, left “these balanced ships” at Percote and came to Ilios on foot (11.228–230; [[curved-ships]], [[percote]], [[iphidamas]]). See [[ships]].
