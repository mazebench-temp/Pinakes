---
summary: Agamemnon “put on the gleaming bronze” at the start of his arming.
---

“And the son of Atreus shouted and bade them gird themselves, the Argives; and he himself put on the gleaming bronze” (11.15–16). The half-verse sums up the arming that follows: greaves, corselet, sword, shield, helmet and spears (11.17–46; [[arming-scene]]). See [[agamemnon]] and [[bronze]].
