---
summary: “Brilliant swift-footed Achilles” sees Nestor's chariot carrying a wounded man from the battle.
---

“And brilliant swift-footed Achilles saw him and took note; for he was standing on the stern of his ship” (11.599–600; [[brilliant]], [[huge-hulled]]). Achilles watches the rout of the Achaeans from his ship and sends Patroclus to find out who the wounded man is (11.599–615; [[watching-the-war]]). See [[achilles]].
