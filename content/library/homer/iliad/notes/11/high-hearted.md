---
summary: “The high-hearted Trojans and their allies” follow Ajax as he gives ground.
---

“So then great Ajax, the son of Telamon, the high-hearted Trojans and their allies gathered from many lands kept following, stabbing with their spears at the middle of his shield” (11.563–565; [[gathered-from-many-lands]], [[the-donkey-in-the-field]]). The epithet of spirit belongs to the pursuers of a man who retreats slowly and turns on them again and again (11.566–574; [[retreat]]). See [[trojans]] and [[ajax]].
