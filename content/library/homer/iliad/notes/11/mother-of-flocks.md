summary: Iphidamas was reared in “deep-soiled Thrace, mother of flocks.”
grc: ["μητέρι μήλων"]
---

“Iphidamas, son of Antenor, tall and brave, who was reared in deep-soiled Thrace, mother of flocks” (11.221–222; [[deep-soiled]]). The epithet of a land rich in sheep fits the story that follows: Iphidamas paid a hundred cattle as his bride-price and promised a thousand goats and sheep (11.244–245; [[untold]]). See [[thrace]] and [[iphidamas]].
