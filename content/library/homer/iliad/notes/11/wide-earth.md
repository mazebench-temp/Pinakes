---
summary: Agamede “knew all the drugs that the wide earth nourishes.”
---

Mulius, Nestor's first kill, “had his eldest daughter, fair-haired Agamede, who knew all the drugs that the wide earth nourishes” (11.740–741; [[agamede]], [[fair-haired]]). The phrase εὐρεῖα χθών, which elsewhere ends the wish “then may the wide earth gape for me” (4.182, 8.150), names here the earth that grows the healer's herbs ([[healing]]). See [[nestor-and-the-epeians]].
