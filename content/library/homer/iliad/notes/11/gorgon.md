summary: The Gorgon is the device of Agamemnon's shield, set as a crown on it, “grim-faced” and “glaring terribly”, with Terror and Rout around her (11.36–37).
grc: [Γοργὼ]
refs: [11.37]
passages:
  - 11.32-40 | The shield of Agamemnon
---

Agamemnon's shield has ten circles of bronze and twenty bosses of tin around a boss of dark blue enamel (11.33–35). “And on it the grim-faced Gorgon was set as a crown, / glaring terribly, and around her were Terror and Rout” (11.36–37; see [[shield]], [[terror]] and [[rout]]). Γοργώ, the nominative of her name, occurs only here; the genitive gives Hector “the eyes of the Gorgon” (Γοργοῦς, 8.349; see [[the-eyes-of-the-gorgon]]). On Athena's aegis she is only a head among the powers, and Rout is the crown around them (5.738–742; see [[aegis]]). On Agamemnon's shield she is herself the crown, ἐστεφάνωτο, and Terror and Rout stand around her (see the translation's note on 11.36–37).

βλοσυρῶπις, “grim-faced”, occurs only here (see [[grim-faced]] and [[blosyros]]). δεινὸν δερκομένη, “glaring terribly”, is said elsewhere of men who face each other in single combat: Menelaus and Paris (3.342) and, at the funeral games, Ajax and Diomedes (23.815). Her power, as in the likeness of Hector's eyes, is in her look.
