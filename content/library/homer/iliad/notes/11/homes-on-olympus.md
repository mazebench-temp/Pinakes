---
summary: The Muses, “who have their homes on Olympus,” are asked who first came against Agamemnon.
---

“Tell me now, Muses, you who have your homes on Olympus, who was the first to come against Agamemnon, whether of the Trojans themselves or of their famous allies” (11.218–220). The first verse is the one that opens the Catalogue of Ships (2.484), and the question introduces Iphidamas (11.221; [[invocation-of-the-muses]], [[famous]]). See [[muses]].
