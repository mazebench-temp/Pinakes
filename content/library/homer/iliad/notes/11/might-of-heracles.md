---
summary: Nestor tells how “mighty Heracles” came and ill-used Pylos, killing his eleven brothers.
---

“For mighty Heracles had come and ill-used us in the years before, and all our best were killed; for we were twelve sons of noble Neleus; of these I alone was left” (11.690–693; [[heracles-sacks-pylos]], [[blameless]]). The attack left Pylos weak, and the Epeians took advantage of it (11.688–695; [[nestor-and-the-epeians]]). See [[heracles]].
