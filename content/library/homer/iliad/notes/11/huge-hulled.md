---
summary: Odysseus' ship in the middle of the line, where Strife stands, and Achilles' ship, from whose stern he watches the battle, are “huge in the hull.”
---

Strife “stood by Odysseus' black ship, huge in the hull, which lay in the middle” (11.5–6 = 8.222–223; [[black-ships]], [[strife]]). Later Achilles watches the rout of the Achaeans: “for he was standing on the stern of his ship, huge in the hull, watching the sheer toil and the tearful onslaught” (11.600–601; [[steep]], [[tearful]], [[watching-the-war]]). The book opens on one great ship and turns to the plot of its second half on another. See [[ships]].
