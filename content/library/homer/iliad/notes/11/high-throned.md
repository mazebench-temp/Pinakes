---
summary: “Father Zeus, throned on high” puts Ajax to flight.
---

Hector avoids Ajax (11.540–542), and “father Zeus, throned on high, roused Ajax to flight” (11.544; [[father-zeus]]). Ajax stands amazed, swings his shield of seven oxhides behind his back and gives ground, glancing around like a wild beast (11.545–547; [[seven-oxhide]], [[retreat]]). See [[zeus]] and [[ajax]].
