---
summary: Odysseus ends his threat to Socus with the formula of the boast: “your soul to Hades of the famous horses.”
---

Wounded by Socus, Odysseus answers that Socus will die that day, “and brought down by my spear you will give me triumph, and your soul to Hades of the famous horses” (11.444–445; [[black-doom]]). The verse is the one Sarpedon spoke to Tlepolemus (5.654), and Meriones will say it to Aeneas (16.625). Odysseus makes it good at once: Socus turns to run and is speared in the back (11.446–449).
