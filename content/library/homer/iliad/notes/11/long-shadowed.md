---
summary: Diomedes casts his “long-shadowed spear” at Hector and hits his helmet.
---

“He spoke, and poising his long-shadowed spear, he let it fly, and hit him, and did not miss, aiming at his head” (11.349–350). The bronze of the spear glances off the bronze of Hector's helmet, Apollo's gift (11.351–353; [[three-layered]]). See [[diomedes]] and [[spear]].
