summary: Socus addresses Odysseus as “much-praised Odysseus” in his challenge.
grc: [πολύαινε]
---

“Much-praised Odysseus, insatiable of tricks and toil” (11.430; [[insatiable-of-war]]): the praise that Agamemnon and Nestor gave Odysseus in the camp (9.673, 10.544) is spoken by an enemy, as the opening of a taunt. See [[socus]] and [[odysseus]].
