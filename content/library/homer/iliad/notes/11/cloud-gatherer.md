---
summary: Diomedes says that “Zeus the cloud-gatherer” wishes to give power to the Trojans.
---

Odysseus has called Diomedes to stand with him against Hector. Diomedes agrees, “but short will be our profit of it, since Zeus the cloud-gatherer wishes to give power to the Trojans rather than to us” (11.317–319; [[plan-of-zeus]]). He is right: Zeus has promised Hector power to kill until he reaches the ships (11.192–194), and before long both men are wounded (11.376–378, 11.434–438; [[the-wounded-chiefs]]).
