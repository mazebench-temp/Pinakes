---
summary: Nestor's cup, which he brought from home, is “beautiful.”
---

“And beside them a beautiful cup, which the old man had brought from home, studded with golden nails” (11.632–633; [[studded-with-golden-nails]]): four handles with two golden doves at each, and two bases below; another man could barely lift it full (11.633–637; [[the-cup-of-nestor]]). In Book 24 Priam takes as part of Hector's ransom a δέπας περικαλλές that the Thracians had given him (24.234–235). See [[nestor]] and [[cup]].
