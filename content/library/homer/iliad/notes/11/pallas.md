---
summary: “Pallas Athena” does not let Socus' spear reach Odysseus' entrails.
---

Socus' spear goes through Odysseus' shield and corselet and tears the skin from his ribs, “but it was not allowed by Pallas Athena to mingle with the man's entrails” (11.437–438; [[heavy-spear]]). Apart from the thunder with Hera at Agamemnon's arming (11.45), it is Athena's only act in the fighting of this book; in Nestor's story she rouses and leads the Pylians (11.714–721, 11.758). See [[athena]] and [[odysseus]].
