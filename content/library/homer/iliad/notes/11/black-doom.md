---
summary: Hector escapes “black doom” after Diomedes' spear strikes his helmet, and Odysseus promises it to Socus.
---

Hector comes to himself after the blow on his helmet, leaps back into his chariot, drives into the multitude, “and avoided black doom” (11.359–360; [[three-layered]]). Odysseus, wounded by Socus, turns on him: “but for you, I say, slaughter and black doom will come here this very day” (11.443–444; [[wretch]], [[of-the-famous-horses]]). See [[ker-doom]] and [[black-death]].
