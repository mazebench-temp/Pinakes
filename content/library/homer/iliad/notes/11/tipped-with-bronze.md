---
summary: Agamemnon arms with “two strong spears tipped with bronze.”
---

“And he took two strong spears tipped with bronze, sharp; and far off the bronze shone from them up into the sky” (11.43–44; [[strong-spear]], [[sharp]]). Paris carried two spears “tipped with bronze” when he came out before the Trojan ranks in Book 3 (3.18). See [[arming-scene]] and [[spear]].
