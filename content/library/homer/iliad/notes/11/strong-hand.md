---
summary: Hector, stunned by Diomedes' spear, sinks to his knees and leans “with his strong hand” on the earth.
---

“And Hector quickly ran back a measureless way and mingled with the throng, and he sank to his knees and stayed there, and leaned with his strong hand on the earth; and black night covered his eyes” (11.354–356; [[darkness-covered-his-eyes]]). The same verse describes Aeneas when Diomedes' stone strikes him (5.309–310). See [[hector]].
