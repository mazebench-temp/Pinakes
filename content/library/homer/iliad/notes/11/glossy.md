---
summary: The “glossy reins” slip from the hands of the sons of Antimachus.
---

Peisander and Hippolochus are driving their horses together when “the glossy reins had slipped from their hands, and the two were thrown into confusion” (11.128–129), and Agamemnon rushes at them like a lion (11.129–130; [[sons-of-antimachus]]). See [[chariot]].
