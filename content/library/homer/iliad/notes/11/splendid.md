---
summary: Paris' gold for Antimachus is “splendid gifts,” and Eurypylus is “the splendid son of Euaemon.”
---

Antimachus had taken “Alexander's gold, splendid gifts,” and would not let Helen be given back (11.124–125; [[antimachus]], [[fair-haired]]). When Ajax is hard pressed, “the splendid son of Euaemon” sees him, and Eurypylus comes to stand beside him (11.575–577; [[eurypylus]]). See [[euaemon]].
