---
summary: “Warlike Menelaus” leads Odysseus out of the throng, “warlike Idomeneus” fights beside Nestor, and Nestor hopes that “the warlike sons of the Achaeans” may draw breath.
---

When Ajax comes up with his shield, “warlike Menelaus led him out of the throng, holding him by the hand, until his attendant drove the horses near” (11.487–488; [[menelaus]], [[odysseus]]). On the left of the battle an unquenchable cry goes up “around great Nestor and warlike Idomeneus” (11.500–501; [[unquenchable-cry]], [[idomeneus]]). In Nestor's plan, if the Trojans take Patroclus for Achilles, “the warlike sons of the Achaeans may draw breath” (11.800; [[sons-of-the-achaeans]]).
