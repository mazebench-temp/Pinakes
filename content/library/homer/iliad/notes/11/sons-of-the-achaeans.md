---
summary: Nestor asks why Achilles grieves for “the sons of the Achaeans,” and hopes that “the warlike sons of the Achaeans” may draw breath.
---

“Why does Achilles grieve like this for the sons of the Achaeans, as many as have been struck by missiles?” (11.656–657): Nestor's answer to Achilles' question opens his long speech. At its end he proposes that Patroclus go out in Achilles' armor, so that the Trojans hold off, “and the warlike sons of the Achaeans may draw breath, worn out as they are” (11.799–801; [[warlike]], [[brief-is-the-breathing-space]]).
