---
summary: Agamemnon kills Coon with a “bronze-fitted” spear, and Nestor kills Mulius with his.
---

“He stabbed him with his bronze-fitted spear, and loosened his limbs” (11.260): Agamemnon kills Coon, the eldest son of Antenor, as he drags his brother's body away ([[eldest-born]], [[loosened-his-limbs]]). In Nestor's story the same weapon kills the first man of the battle with the Epeians: “As he came on I struck him with my bronze-fitted spear” (11.742; [[mulius]]). The young Nestor, who came to the battle on foot because his father had hidden his horses (11.717–721), leaps into the dead man's chariot (11.743–744). See [[spear]].
