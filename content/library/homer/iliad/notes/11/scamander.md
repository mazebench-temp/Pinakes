ὄχθας πὰρ ποταμοῖο Σκαμάνδρου, “beside the banks of the river Scamander” (11.499). While Ajax routs the Trojans elsewhere, Hector does not know of it, “since he was fighting on the left of all the battle, / beside the banks of the river Scamander, where most of all / the heads of men were falling, and an unquenchable cry went up / around great Nestor and warlike Idomeneus” (11.498–501; see [[hector]], [[nestor]] and [[idomeneus]]). By the river Paris wounds Machaon with an arrow, and Nestor carries the healer to the ships (11.504–520; see [[paris]] and [[machaon]]). The fighting of the first day was on the same ground: Nestor spoke then of the Achaeans “whose dark blood about the fair-flowing Scamander / sharp Ares has now scattered” (7.329–330; see [[fair-flowing]]).
