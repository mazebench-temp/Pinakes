---
summary: The wounded Agamemnon calls on the leaders to ward off the din of battle from “the seafaring ships.”
---

“Friends, leaders and rulers of the Argives, it is for you now to ward off from the seafaring ships the grievous din of battle, since Zeus the counselor did not allow me to fight the Trojans all day long” (11.276–279; [[counselor]]). The ships are now the thing to be defended. See [[ships]].
