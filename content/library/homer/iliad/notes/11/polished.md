---
summary: Hecamede sets before Nestor and Machaon a fine table, “well polished.”
---

“She first pushed up before the two of them a table, a fine one, well polished, with feet of dark blue enamel” (11.628–629; [[with-feet-of-dark-enamel]], [[table]]). ἐΰξοος is the word of the woodworker's finish, used of chariots and bows ([[well-joined]]). See [[hecamede]].
