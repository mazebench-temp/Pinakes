---
summary: Iphidamas comes to Troy with “twelve curved ships.”
---

Iphidamas, newly married in Thrace, came “from the bridal chamber at the report of the Achaeans, with twelve curved ships that followed him” (11.227–228), and left them at Percote to come to Ilios on foot (11.229–230; [[balanced-ships]], [[percote]]). See [[iphidamas]] and [[ships]].
