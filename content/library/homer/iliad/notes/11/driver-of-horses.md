---
summary: Oileus, Bianor's companion, is “driver of horses” as Agamemnon kills him.
---

“Him first, and then his companion, Oileus, driver of horses” (11.93): Oileus leaps down from the chariot to face Agamemnon and is stabbed through his helmet (11.94–98; [[heavy-with-bronze]], [[bianor]]). He is a Trojan, not the father of the Locrian Ajax ([[oileus-the-trojan]]). See [[agamemnon]].
