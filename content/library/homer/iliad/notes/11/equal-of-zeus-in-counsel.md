---
summary: Iris addresses Hector as “the equal of Zeus in counsel” when she brings him Zeus' message.
---

“Hector, son of Priam, the equal of Zeus in counsel, father Zeus has sent me to tell you this” (11.200–201; [[son-of-priam]], [[message-repeated]]). Helenus addressed Hector with the same verse in Book 7 (7.47). The praise comes with an order to hold back from the fighting as long as Agamemnon is in it (11.202–205). See [[hector]] and [[iris]].
