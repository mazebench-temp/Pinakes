---
summary: The serpents on Agamemnon's corselet are like the rainbows that Zeus sets in the clouds, “a portent for mortal men.”
---

The dark blue serpents of the corselet stretch toward its neck, “three on each side, like the rainbows that the son of Cronus sets in the clouds, a portent for mortal men” (11.26–28; [[like-rainbows]], [[teras]]). See [[corselet]].
