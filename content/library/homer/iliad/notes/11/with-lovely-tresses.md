---
summary: Hecamede, Nestor's captive from Tenedos, is “of the lovely tresses” as she makes the mixed drink.
grc: [ἐϋπλόκαμος]
en: [of the lovely tresses]
---

“And Hecamede of the lovely tresses made them a mixed drink, whom the old man had won from Tenedos, when Achilles sacked it” (11.624–625; [[kykeon]], [[tenedos]]). She has the epithet again when Nestor tells Machaon to sit and drink until she heats water for his bath (14.5–7). See [[hecamede]] and [[like-the-goddesses]].
