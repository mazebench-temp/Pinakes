The book opens with two armies set against each other. The Trojans come on with noise, like cranes that bring slaughter to the Pygmies ([[cranes-and-pygmies]]); the Achaeans come on in silence, “breathing fury, eager in their hearts to stand by one another.” The fury is silent and shared. See [[silence]].
