---
summary: The variant κοῦροι Ἀχαιῶν, “young men of the Achaeans,” occurs twice: in Agamemnon's order to hold fire and in Priam's blessing of Agamemnon.
---

Agamemnon stops his men from shooting at Hector: “Hold, Argives! Do not shoot, young men of the Achaeans!” (3.82). On the wall Priam, blessing Agamemnon, measures his fortune by the same young men: “truly many young men of the Achaeans have been made subject to you” (3.183; [[child-of-fortune]]). The army that obeys its king in the first passage is the king's wealth in the second.
