---
summary: Book 3 gives κρείων to Agamemnon as he sends for the lambs, and to Helicaon, son of Antenor.
except: [3.178]
---

When the duel is agreed, Hector sends two heralds to Troy for the lambs and for Priam, “and powerful Agamemnon sent Talthybius to go to the hollow ships” for a lamb (3.116–120; [[hollow-ships]]). The formula κρείων Ἀγαμέμνων closes the verse as it does in Books 1 and 2.

A few lines later the title is given to a Trojan. Iris takes the likeness of Laodice, “whom the son of Antenor, powerful Helicaon, had as his wife” (3.123; [[helicaon]], [[laodice]]). At 3.178 κρείων is part of the fuller title [[wide-ruling]], and that line is listed there.
