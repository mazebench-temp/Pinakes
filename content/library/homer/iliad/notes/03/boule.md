---
summary: Hector says that he would want (βουλοίμην) Paris never to have been born, rather than live as a disgrace.
grc: [βουλοίμην]
en: [would want]
---

Hector's rebuke begins with a wish: “I wish you had never been born, or had died unmarried. That is what I would want (καί κε τὸ βουλοίμην), and it would be far better than for you to be a disgrace like this” (3.40–42). βούλομαι is the verb of preference, and Hector states his preference between a dead brother and a disgraced one.
