---
summary: In Book 3 the verb is used not of a god but of a helmet: as Paris arms, the horsehair plume “nodded terribly” above it.
grc: [ἔνευεν]
passages:
  - 3.336-337 | Paris' helmet
---

Paris sets on his head a well-made helmet with a horsehair crest, “and the plume nodded terribly above it” (δεινὸν δὲ λόφος καθύπερθεν ἔνευεν, 3.337). The verb is the one used of the nod of Zeus in Book 1 (1.528). Here it describes the menace of a crest, the last touch of the arming before Paris takes up his spear. The same line comes back when Agamemnon and Patroclus arm (11.42, 16.138). See [[arming-scene]] and [[helmet]].
