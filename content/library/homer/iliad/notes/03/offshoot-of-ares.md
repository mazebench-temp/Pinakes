summary: The Trojan elder Hicetaon, a brother of Priam, is “offshoot of Ares” in the list of elders at the Scaean gates.
---

{{quote:17640301-838e-5af0-9040-051cafc21222}}

The whole verse Λάμπόν τε Κλυτίον θʼ Ἱκετάονά τʼ ὄζον Ἄρηος returns in Aeneas' account of the house of Dardanus, where Lampus, Clytius and Hicetaon are sons of Laomedon, brothers of Priam and Tithonus (Il. 20.237–238). In Book 3 the warrior's epithet is given to an old man who is done with war because of his age (3.150). See [[hicetaon]], [[trojan-elders]].
