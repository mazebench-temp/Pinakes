{{quote:3c697f55-1b5c-581a-9dc0-a79ce19074de}}

In Book 2 the phrase named Helen as the prize that the Achaeans would leave in Troy if they fled. Here it names what the oath promised them: if Menelaus won, the Trojans would give back Helen and the possessions and pay satisfaction (3.284–287). Paris has vanished, and Agamemnon claims the victory. The Achaeans applaud (3.461). The Trojans do not answer, and in Book 4 the truce is broken before Helen is returned.
