---
summary: Helen asks Aphrodite whether she will lead her on to one of the “well-peopled cities” of Phrygia or Maeonia.
grc: [εὖ ναιομενάων]
---

“Will you lead me still farther on, to one of the well-peopled cities of Phrygia or of lovely Maeonia?” (3.400–401). The formula of prosperous towns names, in Helen's mouth, the places where Aphrodite might find her yet another man ([[meropes]], [[lovely]]). Both lands send allies to Troy in the Trojan Catalogue, and both lie inland in Asia Minor ([[phrygia]], [[maeonia]]).
