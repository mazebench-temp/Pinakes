---
summary: Argos is the home the Achaeans will go back to if the duel ends the war, “horse-pasturing Argos” (3.75, 3.258).
---

In the terms of the duel, if the war ends the Achaeans are to go home “to horse-pasturing Argos and Achaea of the lovely women” (3.75, 3.258; see [[achaea]] and [[horse-pasturing]]). Argos is again the homeland of the whole army, as in Book 2 (2.115, 2.287). When Paris has vanished, Agamemnon demands “Argive Helen” back (3.458; see [[argive-helen]]).
