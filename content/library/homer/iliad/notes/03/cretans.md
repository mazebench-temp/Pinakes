---
summary: From the wall Helen points out Idomeneus standing among the Cretans like a god, with the leaders of the Cretans gathered around him (3.230–231).
grc: [Κρήτεσσι]
---

The Cretans are seen as a group around their king: Idomeneus “stands among the Cretans like a god, and around him the leaders of the Cretans are gathered” (3.230–231). ἀγοί (“leaders”) is from ἄγω (“lead”). See [[idomeneus]].
