{{quote:27d7d2a3-08b2-5bb6-8afe-b2a33ff1cf75}}

The verse Ἄργος ἐς ἱππόβοτον καὶ Ἀχαιΐδα καλλιγύναικα closes Paris' proposal and Idaeus' report of it (3.75, 3.258). It answers the line before it: the Trojans stay in deep-soiled Troy, and the Achaeans go home to the land of horses and lovely women ([[deep-soiled]], [[of-the-lovely-women]]). Here Argos has its wide sense, the homeland of the Achaeans ([[argos]]).
