{{quote:23b23e3f-dc2a-58df-b128-e067ee9d4b04}}

The singular κούρη Διὸς αἰγιόχοιο is elsewhere a formula of Athena (for example Il. 5.733), and in the plural it names the Muses ({{ref:Iliad 2.491-492}}). Book 3 gives it to Helen, at the moment she obeys Aphrodite and sits down before Paris, turning her eyes away. Her descent from Zeus is repeated through the second half of the book ([[daughter-of-zeus]], 3.199, 3.418). Here the grand formula comes between her fear of the goddess and her rebuke of her husband (3.428–436).
