{{quote:e12d7b62-92de-51f2-86ac-41170cbc3c37}}

Priam asks about the man who towers above the Argives by his head and broad shoulders, and Helen answers with a formula that joins size and use: he is “huge” ([[huge]]) and he is the army's wall. The same formula describes Ajax when he goes out to fight Hector in Book 7 (Il. 7.211). In Book 1 Nestor gave the title to Achilles (1.283–284). In Book 3 Achilles is absent, and the title belongs to Ajax. See [[ajax]].
