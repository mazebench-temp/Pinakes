---
summary: Agamemnon's knife “always hung beside the great sheath of his sword” (3.272).
---

At the oath Agamemnon draws “the knife that always hung beside the great sheath of his sword” (ἥ οἱ πὰρ ξίφεος μέγα κουλεόν αἰὲν ἄωρτο, 3.272). The knife was worn at the sword's side, always ready. The form is κουλεόν, as when Achilles thrust his sword back into its sheath (1.220), and the whole verse returns when Agamemnon swears again in Book 19 (19.253). See [[knife]] and [[oath]].
