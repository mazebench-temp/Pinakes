---
summary: Priam calls his city “windy Ilios” as he leaves the oath.
---

“I will go back to windy Ilios, since I could never bear to see with my own eyes my dear son fighting with Menelaus, dear to Ares” (3.305–307). Ἴλιον ἠνεμόεσσαν is the city's formula in the Iliad, and here it is the home to which the old king withdraws from the sight of the duel. See [[troy]], [[equal-to-the-gods]].
