---
summary: Helen remembers Idomeneus coming “from Crete” to be Menelaus' guest (3.233).
grc: [Κρήτηθεν]
---

Κρήτηθεν (“from Crete”) is the island's only mention in Book 3. Helen recalls that Menelaus often entertained Idomeneus in their house “whenever he came from Crete” (3.232–233): Crete and Sparta were joined by guest-friendship before the war. See [[idomeneus]] and [[xenia]].
