---
summary: The epithet occurs three times in Book 3, always in the whole verse that pairs the bronze-armored Achaeans with the horse-taming Trojans.
---

The verse Τρώων θʼ ἱπποδάμων καὶ Ἀχαιῶν χαλκοχιτώνων follows the war from Helen's loom to the oath. She is weaving into her web the many trials “of the horse-taming Trojans and the bronze-armored Achaeans” that they suffer for her sake (3.126–128); Iris calls her to see their “wondrous doings” (3.130–131; [[wondrous]]); and the herald Idaeus tells Priam that their best men summon him to the plain (3.250–252).
