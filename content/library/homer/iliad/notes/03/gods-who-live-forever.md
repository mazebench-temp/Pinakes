---
summary: The dative θεοῖς αἰειγενέτῃσιν names the gods to whom both armies pour the wine of the oath.
grc: [θεοῖς αἰειγενέτῃσιν]
---

When Agamemnon has cut the lambs' throats, the men draw wine from the mixing bowl, pour it out, “and prayed to the everlasting gods” (3.295–296; [[libation]]). The prayer that follows is a curse on the oath-breakers and their children (3.298–301). The gods who live forever are called to witness a curse on the generations of mortal men. See [[oath]].
