---
summary: Three of Euripides' plays answer Book 3. The Helen sends only a phantom to Troy, the Trojan Women puts Helen on trial for the war, and the Phoenician Women repeats the view from the wall at Thebes.
---

- *Helen* (412 BCE), with the real Helen in Egypt and a phantom at Troy; the phantom first appears at the end of *Electra*: [[helen-in-egypt]].
- *Trojan Women* (415 BCE): Helen blames Aphrodite, and Hecuba answers that her own mind "became Cypris": [[euripides-trojan-women]].
- *Phoenician Women* (about 410 BCE): Antigone looks down from the palace roof and an old servant names the Argive leaders: [[euripides-teichoskopia]].
