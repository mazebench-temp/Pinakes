---
summary: Paris slings on a silver-studded sword in his arming, and Menelaus' silver-studded sword shatters on Paris' helmet.
---

The arming verse ἀμφὶ δʼ ἄρʼ ὤμοισιν βάλετο ξίφος ἀργυρόηλον gives Paris his sword (3.334; [[arming-scene]]). He never uses it. It is Menelaus who draws his, after his spear has failed to kill:

{{quote:404eb1ad-3c5f-5c3a-91ed-9136ed8220df}}

The fine sword breaks on the ridge of Paris' helmet ([[horsehair-crested]]), and Menelaus complains to Zeus that his sword has broken in his hands and his spear was thrown in vain (3.365–368). See [[sword]].
