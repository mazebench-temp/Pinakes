The Phrygians on the Sangarius were “the people of Otreus and godlike Mygdon” ([[mygdon]], [[otreus]], [[priam-and-the-amazons]]). In Book 1 Nestor gave the same epithet to the Lapith Polyphemus, one of the heroes of his youth ({{ref:Iliad 1.264}}). Two old men, one on each side of the war, remember the leaders they fought beside when they were young, and both call them godlike.
