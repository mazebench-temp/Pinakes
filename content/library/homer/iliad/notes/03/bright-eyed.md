---
summary: Priam and Helen, looking down from the wall, both call the Achaean host “bright-eyed.”
grc: [ἑλίκωπας]
---

The formula ἑλίκωπες Ἀχαιοί comes twice in the view from the wall ([[the-view-from-the-wall]]). Priam ends his memory of the Phrygian host and the Amazons with it: “but even they were not as many as the bright-eyed Achaeans” (3.190). Helen uses it as she looks over the army for faces she knows: “And now I see all the other bright-eyed Achaeans, whom I would know well and could tell you their names” (3.234–235). The king of Troy and the Greek woman beside him name the host below with the same formula of praise.
