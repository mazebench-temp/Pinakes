---
summary: Idaeus brings golden cups (κύπελλα) from Troy to the oath, and in cups (δέπα) the armies draw the wine they pour out.
grc: [κύπελλα]
---

The herald Idaeus carries the mixing bowl from Troy “and golden cups” (χρύσεια κύπελλα, 3.248). At the oath the wine is drawn from the bowl “in cups” (δεπάεσσιν) and poured on the ground (3.295–296). Both words for the cup stand in one scene, as both named the one cup of Hephaestus in Book 1 (1.584, 1.596). No one drinks from them. See [[libation]] and [[mixing-bowl]].
