---
summary: Menelaus is “warlike” as he arms for the duel, in one line set against the long arming of Paris.
---

Paris' arming takes nine lines, from the greaves to the spear (3.330–338; [[arming-scene]]). Menelaus' takes one: “And in the same way warlike Menelaus put on his armor” (3.339). ἀρήϊος is his standing epithet, and here it does the work of the whole scene: he needs no description to be ready for war. See [[dear-to-ares]].
