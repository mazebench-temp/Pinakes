---
summary: When Hector comes forward between the armies to announce the duel, the Achaeans shoot arrows and throw stones at him until Agamemnon stops them (3.79–83).
grc: [ἰοῖσιν]
---

Hector goes into the middle and holds back the Trojan ranks, “but the long-haired Achaeans kept shooting at him, taking aim with arrows (ἰοῖσιν) and pelting him with stones” (3.79–80). Agamemnon shouts: “Hold, Argives! Do not shoot, young men of the Achaeans! Hector of the gleaming helmet means to say something” (3.82–83). These are the only arrows of Book 3. The truce they interrupt will be broken by another, Pandarus' arrow in Book 4 (see [[bow]] and [[the-bow-of-pandarus]]).
