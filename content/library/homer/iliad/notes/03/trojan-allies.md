# ἐπίκουρος at 3.188 is Priam, once an ally of the Phrygians against the Amazons.
except: [3.188]
---

The allies appear at the end of the book, as part of the Trojan side. When Aphrodite has taken Paris away, "none of the Trojans or their famous allies" (Τρώων κλειτῶν τʼ ἐπικούρων; see [[famous]]) could show him to Menelaus, for he was hateful to them all (3.451–454). Then Agamemnon calls on "Trojans and Dardanians and allies" to give back Helen and the possessions (3.456–460; see [[dardanians]]). The terms of the oath are demanded of them as well.

Earlier, on the wall, Priam gives the word a history. In his youth he went to Phrygia and fought there "as an ally" (ἐπίκουρος), counted among the Phrygians of Otreus and Mygdon on the Sangarius, on the day the Amazons came (3.184–189; see [[priam-and-the-amazons]] and [[phrygians]]). The king who once went to fight in other men's wars now depends on allies to defend his city.
