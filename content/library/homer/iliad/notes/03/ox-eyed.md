---
summary: The epithet of Clymene, one of the two handmaids who go with Helen to the wall.
---

Helen does not go out alone. “Two handmaids went along with her, Aethra, daughter of Pittheus, and ox-eyed Clymene” (3.143–144). Hera's epithet is given to a servant, which shows that it is a term of beauty and not a sign of divinity (see the Meaning above). See [[clymene]] and [[aethra]].
