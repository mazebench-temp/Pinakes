---
summary: Helen names Odysseus by his father: “the son of Laertes, Odysseus of many wiles” (3.200).
relations:
  - child: odysseus (3.200)
---

Λαερτιάδης is heard here for the first time in the nominative; in Book 2 Athena used the vocative (2.173). Helen names each of the first two men by father, epithet and name: “the son of Atreus, wide-ruling Agamemnon” (3.178), “the son of Laertes, Odysseus of many wiles” (3.200). See [[son-of-laertes]] and [[odysseus]].
