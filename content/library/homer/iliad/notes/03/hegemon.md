---
summary: Book 3 begins with the armies marshaled “each company with its leaders”, and the old men on the tower are “the leaders of the Trojans”.
grc: [ἡγεμόνεσσιν]
---

The book's first line joins it to the end of Book 2: “Now when they had been marshaled, each company with its leaders” (ἅμʼ ἡγεμόνεσσιν ἕκαστοι, 3.1), the leaders whom the Catalogues have just named. The old men sitting on the tower, done with war, are still “the leaders of the Trojans” (Τρώων ἡγήτορες, 3.153). Leadership in counsel outlasts leadership in war.
