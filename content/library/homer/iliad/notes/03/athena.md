summary: Athena takes no part in Book 3. Her name is heard once, from Paris, who says that Menelaus won “with Athena's help”.
---

{{quote:547b685e-ecba-5024-aa0e-46931b724c5a}}

Nothing in the story supports him. The poem shows Aphrodite helping Paris (3.374–382), but no god standing by Menelaus, whose spear and sword fail him on their own (3.355–368). Paris explains his defeat as he explains his beauty, by the gods (compare 3.64–66). At the start of Book 4 Zeus says that Menelaus has two helpers among the goddesses, Hera and Athena, but that they sit apart and watch, while Aphrodite always stands by Paris (4.7–12).
