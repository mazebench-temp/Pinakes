---
summary: Helen names Odysseus by his father as she identifies him for Priam.
grc: [Λαερτιάδης]
---

“This is the son of Laertes, Odysseus of many wiles” (3.200). The patronymic and the personal epithet fill the verse after οὗτος δʼ αὖ, “this one, again.” Helen names Odysseus in full: by his father, his quality and his home ([[of-many-wiles]], [[craggy]]). In Book 2 the patronymic was part of Athena's formal address, διογενὲς Λαερτιάδη ({{ref:Iliad 2.173}}).
