---
summary: There is no supplication in Book 3. The chin, which a suppliant touches, appears only as the place where Paris' helmet strap chokes him (3.372).
except: [3.372]
---

When Thetis supplicated Zeus, she took hold of his knees with one hand and of his chin with the other (ὑπʼ ἀνθερεῶνος, 1.500–501). In Book 3 the word comes only in the duel. Menelaus drags Paris by the helmet, “and the richly worked strap choked him under his soft throat, the strap drawn tight under his chin (ὑπʼ ἀνθερεῶνος) to hold the helmet” (3.371–372; see [[helmet]]). No one in the book kneels to another or clasps his knees.
