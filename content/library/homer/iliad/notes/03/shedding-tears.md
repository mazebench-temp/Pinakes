---
summary: Helen weeps as she leaves her chamber for the wall, “letting her tender tears fall” (3.142), and tells Priam that she wastes away in tears (3.176).
en: [tears fall]
---

When Iris has put longing in her heart for her former husband, her city and her parents, Helen veils herself and hurries from her chamber, τέρεν κατὰ δάκρυ χέουσα, “letting her tender tears fall” (3.142). The formula has an epithet, τέρεν, “tender, soft” ([[tender]]), and it stands not in the frame of a speech, as with Thetis (1.413), but in the movement of a woman going out, silent. Helen speaks of her tears herself when she answers Priam: “that did not happen; and so I waste away in tears” (κλαίουσα τέτηκα, 3.176). See [[helen]] and [[tears]].
