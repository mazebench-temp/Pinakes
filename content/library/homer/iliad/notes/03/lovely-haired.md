---
summary: The epithet names Helen in the formula for Paris, “the husband of Helen of the lovely hair.”
---

At the start of his arming Paris is “brilliant Alexander, the husband of Helen of the lovely hair” (3.329; [[husband-of-helen]]). In Book 2 the epithet belonged to Briseis, the woman over whom Achilles is angry ({{ref:Iliad 2.689}}). In Book 3 it belongs to the woman for whom the duel is fought. Helen's hair is not otherwise described. It is Paris' hair that Hector counts among “the gifts of Aphrodite” (3.54–55).
