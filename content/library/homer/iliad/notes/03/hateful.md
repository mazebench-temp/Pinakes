---
summary: Helen calls herself “hateful” as she refuses Aphrodite's summons.
grc: [στυγερὴν]
---

“Is it because Menelaus has now beaten brilliant Alexander and wants to take me, hateful as I am, home with him, that you have come and stood here now, with treachery in your heart?” (3.403–405). The word of war and death is Helen's word for herself, as “dog-faced” was ([[dog-face]], 3.180). στυγερήν can mean hated by others or loathsome in her own eyes; the Greek does not decide.

At the end of the book it is Paris who is hated: none of the Trojans would have hidden him, “for he was hateful to them all, like black doom” (ἀπήχθετο, 3.454; [[black-doom]]).
