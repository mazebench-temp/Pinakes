---
summary: Priam asks Helen to name two Achaeans with the formula “so tall and brave”: Agamemnon and Ajax.
---

Priam uses the formula ἠΰς τε μέγας τε in two questions from the wall. The first is about Agamemnon, “who this Achaean man is, so tall and brave” (3.167). Priam at once qualifies the size: others are taller by a head, but none is so handsome or so like a king (3.168–170). The second is about Ajax (3.226), who does tower above the Argives by his head and broad shoulders (3.227; [[huge]], [[bulwark-of-the-achaeans]]). The formula of praise is also a question of identity: the old king sees the quality before he knows the name. See [[the-view-from-the-wall]].
