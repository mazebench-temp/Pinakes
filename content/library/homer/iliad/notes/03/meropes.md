---
summary: Helen asks Aphrodite whether there is some other “mortal man” dear to her in Phrygia or Maeonia.
en: [mortal man]
---

“Will you lead me still farther on, to one of the well-peopled cities of Phrygia or of lovely Maeonia, if there too there is some mortal man who is dear to you?” (3.400–402). Helen accuses the goddess of handing her on from one favorite to another ([[well-peopled]], [[lovely]]). The formula for mortal men sets them apart from the goddess who chooses among them. See [[helen-and-aphrodite]].
