Hector sends two heralds to the city, unnamed, for the Trojan lambs and for Priam (3.116–117). Agamemnon sends his own herald by name for the lamb the Achaeans are to give to Zeus (3.104). οὐκ ἀπίθησε (“he did not disobey”) is the formula of ready obedience, used of Athena obeying Hera in Book 2 (2.166). In Book 1 Talthybius went “unwilling” to take Briseis (1.327); here the errand is a sacrifice, and the heralds of both sides serve at the oath together (3.268–274). See [[heralds]] and [[oath]].
