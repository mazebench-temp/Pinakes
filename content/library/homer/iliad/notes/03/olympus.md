Olympus is named once in Book 3, in Helen's rebuke of Aphrodite:

{{quote:a597c362-d6e6-5694-adf8-d958a691e854}}

Helen's sarcasm makes Olympus what the goddess would have to give up. "The paths of the gods" (θεῶν κελεύθου) lead back there, and a goddess who cares so much for a mortal man should stay on earth with him, to suffer for him and guard him. See [[aphrodite]] and [[helen-and-aphrodite]].
