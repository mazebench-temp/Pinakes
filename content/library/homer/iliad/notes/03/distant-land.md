---
summary: Hector reproaches Paris for bringing Helen home “from a distant land.”
---

“Was it as such a man that in your seafaring ships you sailed over the sea, gathering your trusty companions, and, mingling with foreigners, brought back a lovely woman from a distant land, the bride of a people of spearmen?” (3.46–49). Hector's question is about distance and consequence: Paris went far to fetch a woman who would be a great grief to his father, his city and all the people (3.50–51). As in Nestor's words (1.270), ἀπίη γαῖα here means a far-off land (see the Meaning above). See [[seafaring-ship]], [[trusty]], [[spearmen]], [[the-abduction-of-helen]].
