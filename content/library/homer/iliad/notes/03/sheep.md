Priam, on the wall, likens Odysseus to a thick-fleeced ram going through “a great flock of white sheep” (3.196–198; see [[ram-among-the-sheep]]). Aphrodite comes to Helen in the likeness of an old wool-comber, who used to work fine wool for her in Lacedaemon (3.386–388; see [[helen-and-aphrodite]]).
