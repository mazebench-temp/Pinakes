The title ἄναξ ἀνδρῶν, “lord of men”, marks Agamemnon's three acts of authority in Book 3: he shouts to stop the Achaeans shooting at Hector (3.81), he rises to lead the oath (3.267), and he claims the victory for his brother (3.455; see [[lord-of-men]]).

Menelaus addresses the god with the short vocative ἄνα: “Lord Zeus, grant me vengeance on the man who first wronged me” (Ζεῦ ἄνα, 3.351). The lord of the gods does not grant it (3.361–368). See [[prayer]] and [[poine]].
