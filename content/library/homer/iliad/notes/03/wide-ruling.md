In Book 1 the whole formula Ἀτρεΐδης εὐρὺ κρείων Ἀγαμέμνων introduced Agamemnon rising in anger, and Achilles used it with bitterness. On the wall it is Helen's formal answer to Priam's question, and the next verse explains the title: a good king and a strong spearman ([[spearmen]]). The third verse turns to Helen herself ([[dog-face]]). Priam answers by calling Agamemnon blessed ([[child-of-fortune]]). At 3.178 κρείων is part of the fuller title, and the line is excluded from [[powerful]].
