---
summary: The Argives are the army Paris challenges and Agamemnon holds back, and the side to which the Trojans are to pay satisfaction under the oath.
grc: [Ἀργείους, Ἀργείοις]
---

Paris challenges “all the best of the Argives” (3.19), and Agamemnon stops the archers with “Hold, Argives!” (3.82). Menelaus thinks that “the Argives and the Trojans” should now be parted (3.98–99). In Agamemnon's oath the Trojans are to “pay the Argives such satisfaction as is fitting” (3.286). From the wall Ajax is seen “towering above the Argives” (3.227). As in Books 1 and 2, the name stands for the army as a whole. See [[achaeans]] and [[danaans]].
