---
summary: Agamemnon names them once, when he calls on "Trojans and Dardanians and allies" to give back Helen.
grc: [Δάρδανοι]
---

Agamemnon's last speech in the book is addressed to the whole Trojan side: κέκλυτέ μευ Τρῶες καὶ Δάρδανοι ἠδʼ ἐπίκουροι, "Hear me, Trojans and Dardanians and allies" (3.456). In later books Trojan speakers open their own assemblies with the same line (7.348, 7.368, 8.497). The Achaean king uses it across the lines to demand Helen and the possessions and the satisfaction sworn in the oath (3.457–460). See [[trojans]] and [[trojan-allies]].

Priam himself is Δαρδανίδης, "descendant of Dardanus" (3.303), after the founder of the line ([[dardanus]]).
