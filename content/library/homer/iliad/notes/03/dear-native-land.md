---
summary: The narrator says that Helen's brothers lie buried “in their own dear native land.”
grc: [φίλῃ ἐν πατρίδι γαίῃ]
---

{{quote:6e2a25e6-f610-5278-8aee-a6803789e5b9}}

The formula of homecoming, the goal of every return, names the land where Castor and Polydeuces lie dead. They did not come to Troy. The dear native land that the Achaeans long to see again holds them, while their sister looks for them on the plain ([[life-giving-earth]], [[the-death-of-the-dioscuri]]).
