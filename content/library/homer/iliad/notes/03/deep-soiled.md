---
summary: Paris' proposal, repeated by the herald Idaeus, promises the Trojans that they will live on in “deep-soiled Troy.”
---

The terms of the duel end with the future of both peoples: the Trojans “live in deep-soiled Troy,” and the Achaeans go back to Argos and Achaea (3.73–75). Paris proposes them, and the herald Idaeus repeats them to Priam with the verbs changed to the first person, “and the rest of us … would live in deep-soiled Troy” (3.256–258). The rich land of Troy is what the Trojans stand to keep. See [[horse-pasturing]], [[of-the-lovely-women]].
