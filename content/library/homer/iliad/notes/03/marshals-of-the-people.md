summary: Helen gives the dual title to her brothers Castor and Polydeuces, whom she looks for in vain among the Achaean leaders.
---

{{quote:70e03702-ee22-558f-a91f-bbf93bc38989}}

In Book 1 the dual κοσμήτορε λαῶν named the two sons of Atreus, Agamemnon and Menelaus (1.16, 1.375). Helen gives it to another pair of brothers, her own. She expects them to be among the leaders of the army that has come for her, as the sons of Atreus are. She cannot see them, and she guesses at the reason: they stayed at home, or they came and will not fight for shame of her. The narrator tells what she does not know: they are dead and buried in Lacedaemon ([[life-giving-earth]], [[the-death-of-the-dioscuri]]). See [[castor]], [[polydeuces]].
