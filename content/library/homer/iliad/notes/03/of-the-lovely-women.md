---
summary: Achaea is “of the lovely women” in the verse that sends the Achaeans home if Paris wins.
---

The verse “to horse-pasturing Argos and Achaea of the lovely women” comes twice, in Paris' proposal and in Idaeus' report of it (3.75, 3.258; [[horse-pasturing]]). The duel is fought for one woman, and the epithet promises the other side a land of lovely women if they go home without her. See [[achaea]].
