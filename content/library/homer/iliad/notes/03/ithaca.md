---
summary: Helen names Odysseus as the man “raised in the land of Ithaca, craggy though it is” (3.201).
grc: [Ἰθάκης]
---

{{quote:28db2924-e00a-5a24-a5c9-5c8ad4c29c07}}

κραναός (“craggy, rugged”) is Ithaca's epithet in Homer (see [[craggy]]). The concessive περ (“though it is”) makes the rough island the more remarkable for the man it raised. Odysseus says the same of his home in the Odyssey: “rugged, but a good nurse of young men” (Od. 9.27). See [[odysseus]].
