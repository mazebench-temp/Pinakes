---
summary: Paris slips back “into the crowd of the proud Trojans.”
---

At the sight of Menelaus Paris draws back like a man who has met a serpent, “so back into the crowd of the proud Trojans he slipped” (3.33–36; [[man-who-meets-a-serpent]]). The formula Τρώων ἀγερώχων honors the people into whom their champion retreats. Hector soon calls the same people “great cowards” (δειδήμονες, 3.56), for not having stoned Paris long ago.
