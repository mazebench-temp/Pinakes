---
summary: Before the oath the heralds pour water over the hands of the kings (3.270), the first act of the rite.
grc: [ὕδωρ ἐπὶ χεῖρας ἔχευαν]
en: [poured water over the hands]
---

The heralds bring the offerings together, mix wine in the bowl, “and poured water over the hands of the kings” (βασιλεῦσιν ὕδωρ ἐπὶ χεῖρας ἔχευαν, 3.270). At Chryse the men washed their own hands (χερνίψαντο, 1.449). Here servants of the rite pour the water, and only the kings are washed, the men who will touch the victims, pray and swear. The half-line is the one used when heralds serve a libation or a meal (9.174). At Agamemnon's sacrifice in Book 2 there was no washing at all (2.410).
