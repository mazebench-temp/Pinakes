Helen enters the Iliad with Hera's commonest epithet. The white arms are those of a woman who lives indoors, and Iris finds her in the hall, weaving the trials of the war into a great web (3.125–128; [[weaving]]). The narrator praises her with other epithets as the book goes on: [[brilliant]] in the formula δῖα γυναικῶν (3.171, 3.228, 3.423), [[long-robed]] (3.228), and child of Zeus ([[daughter-of-zeus]], 3.199, 3.418).
