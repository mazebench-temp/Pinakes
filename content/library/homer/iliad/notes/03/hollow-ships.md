---
summary: Agamemnon sends Talthybius “to the hollow ships” for a lamb for the oath.
grc: [γλαφυρὰς]
---

“And powerful Agamemnon sent Talthybius to go to the hollow ships, and told him to bring a lamb” (3.118–120; [[talthybius]]). The synonym γλαφυρός, “hollowed,” stands here with the preposition between noun and adjective, νῆας ἔπι γλαφυράς. The Achaeans' lamb comes from the camp, the Trojans' two lambs from the city (3.116–117).
