---
summary: The superlative opens three prayers to Zeus at the oath and the lots, in the address Ζεῦ … κύδιστε μέγιστε, “most glorious, greatest.”
---

Agamemnon's oath begins with the whole line Ζεῦ πάτερ Ἴδηθεν μεδέων κύδιστε μέγιστε, “Father Zeus, who rule from Ida, most glorious, greatest” (3.276), and the people of both armies pray with the same line while the lots are shaken (3.320). Between them the curse spoken over the wine begins with the shorter address, Ζεῦ κύδιστε μέγιστε (3.298; [[who-rules-from-ida]], [[father-zeus]]).

The same opening began Agamemnon's prayer before battle in Book 2 ({{ref:Iliad 2.412}}), which Zeus refused. The prayers of Book 3 fare no better: after the curse, “the son of Cronus was not yet going to fulfill their prayer” (3.302; [[son-of-cronus]]).
