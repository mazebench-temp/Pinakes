---
summary: Atreus is named only in his son's patronymic, three times, always of Agamemnon and always in words about the quarrel: twice by Achilles, once by Patroclus, in the verses of Achilles' prayer to Thetis (16.59, 16.76, 16.273).
---

Ἀτρεΐδης, “the son of Atreus”, is Agamemnon each time (see [[agamemnon]] and [[son-of-atreus]]). Achilles uses it twice. Agamemnon took the girl back “as if I were some dishonored vagrant” (16.58–59), the verse with which he answered Ajax in the embassy (9.648; see [[like-a-dishonored-vagrant]]); and the voice he has not heard is “the voice of the son of Atreus shouting / from his hated head” (16.76–77; see [[achilles]]). Patroclus uses it in the words Achilles spoke to his mother in Book 1, “and so that the son of Atreus, wide-ruling Agamemnon, may learn / his own blind folly” (16.273–274 = 1.411–412; see [[patroclus]], [[wide-ruling]] and [[ate]]).
