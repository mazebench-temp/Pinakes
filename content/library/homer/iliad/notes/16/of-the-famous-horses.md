---
summary: Meriones answers Aeneas that, struck by his spear, he too would give “your soul to Hades of the famous horses.”
---

“If I too could strike you full in the middle with the sharp bronze, / at once, strong though you are and trusting in your hands, / you would give me triumph, and your soul to Hades of the famous horses” (16.623–625; [[meriones]], [[aeneas]], [[sharp]], [[krateros]]). The verse-end is the one in which Sarpedon and Odysseus threatened their enemies (5.654, 11.445); here it is a reply, and Patroclus rebukes Meriones for spending words when he should fight (16.626–631; [[son-of-menoetius]]). See [[hades]].
