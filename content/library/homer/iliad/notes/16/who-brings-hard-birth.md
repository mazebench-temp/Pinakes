“But when Eileithyia, who brings hard birth, had brought him / out into the light, and he saw the rays of the sun” (16.187–188; [[eileithyia]], [[eudorus]], [[polymele]]). In Book 11 the Eileithyiai were many, the daughters of Hera who bring the sharp pangs of labor (11.269–271); here the goddess is one, as in Zeus' boast in Book 19 (19.103). The singular μογοστόκος is new to the entry. See [[hermes]].
