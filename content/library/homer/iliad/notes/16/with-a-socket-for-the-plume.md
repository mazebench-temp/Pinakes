---
summary: Apollo strikes Achilles' helmet from Patroclus' head, and it rolls under the horses' feet, “the helmet with its socket for the plume.”
---

“And Phoebus Apollo struck the helmet from his head, / and it rolled with a clatter under the horses' feet, / the helmet with its socket for the plume, and the horsehair was stained / with blood and dust” (16.793–796; [[phoebus]], [[horsehair-crested]], [[helmet]]). The epithet of the helmet, τρυφάλεια αὐλῶπις, is given at the moment it is lost. Zeus will give it to Hector to wear, “and his destruction was near him” (16.799–800; [[divine]]).
