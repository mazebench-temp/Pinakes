---
summary: Apollo breaks “the long-shadowed spear” in Patroclus' hands.
---

“And the long-shadowed spear was all broken in his hands, / heavy, huge, and massive, tipped with bronze; and from his shoulders / the full-length shield fell to the ground with its strap” (16.801–803; [[heavy-spear]], [[full-length]], [[apollo]]). The epithet of the spear of a duel is given to a spear that is never thrown; it breaks with the rest of the armor under the god's hand. See [[spear]] and [[patroclus]].
