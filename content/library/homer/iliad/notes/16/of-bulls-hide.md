---
summary: Hector covers his broad shoulders with “his shield of bull's hide” as Ajax waits to throw.
---

“And great Ajax was ever eager to throw his spear at Hector armed in bronze; / but he, in his skill at war, / covering his broad shoulders with his shield of bull's hide, / watched for the whizzing of arrows and the thudding of spears” (16.358–361; [[armed-in-bronze]], [[hector]], [[ajax]], [[shield]]). The shield of bull's hide was Deiphobus' when Meriones' spear struck it in Book 13 (13.160–163); here it is Hector's. Hector knows that the battle has turned, “but even so he stood his ground” (16.362–363; [[that-turns-the-battle]]).
