---
summary: Zeus honors Sarpedon, whom Patroclus is to kill “in deep-soiled Troy, far from his native land.”
---

“honoring his dear son, whom Patroclus was to / kill in deep-soiled Troy, far from his native land” (16.460–461; [[bloody]], [[sarpedon]]). The rich soil of Troy is the land where the stranger will die, and the verse sets it against his own country, to which his body will be carried (16.454–457; [[wide-lycia]]). See [[troy]].
