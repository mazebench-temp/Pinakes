---
summary: Patroclus bids the Myrmidons fight “so that the son of Atreus, wide-ruling Agamemnon, may learn / his own blind folly.”
---

“so that the son of Atreus, wide-ruling Agamemnon, may learn / his own blind folly, in that he paid no honor to the best of the Achaeans” (16.273–274; [[son-of-atreus]], [[powerful]], [[ate]]). The two verses are the ones in which Achilles said the same of Agamemnon in Book 1, when he asked Thetis to beg Zeus that the Achaeans be driven back to the ships (1.411–412). Patroclus puts Achilles' wish into his own exhortation. See [[agamemnon]].
