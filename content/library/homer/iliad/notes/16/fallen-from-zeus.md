---
summary: Menesthius, the first leader of the Myrmidons, is the son of the Spercheius, “the river fallen from Zeus.”
---

{{quote:0410b1b9-b02d-54d2-84d5-100927d57bd6}}

The first leader of the Myrmidons is the son of a river god: Polydora, the daughter of Peleus, lay with the Spercheius, the river of Achilles' own land, to which Peleus vowed his son's hair (23.141–144), and bore him Menesthius, though by name he was the son of Borus ([[menesthius-son-of-spercheius]], [[spercheius]], [[polydora]], [[borus-son-of-perieres]]). The epithet marks the river as the god it is; in the next verses he is “tireless Spercheius” (16.176; [[tireless]]). See [[myrmidons]].
