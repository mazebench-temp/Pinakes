---
summary: “Zeus the counselor” hears Achilles' prayer for Patroclus and grants him one thing and refuses the other.
---

{{quote:94f9322a-7ea1-5f54-9685-301dbba8cdca}}

The verse that answers the prayer is the old one of answered prayers, ὣς ἔφατʼ εὐχόμενος, τοῦ δʼ ἔκλυε: Phoebus Apollo heard Chryses so (1.43, 1.457), and Pallas Athena heard Diomedes (5.121). Here the god of counsel hears, and divides: Patroclus will drive the battle from the ships, but he will not come back ([[plan-of-zeus]], [[prayer]], [[achilles]], [[patroclus]]). Later in the book the same verse closes Glaucus' prayer, and Apollo grants it whole at once (16.527; [[phoebus]]). See [[zeus]].
