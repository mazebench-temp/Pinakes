---
summary: Hector bids “wise-hearted Cebriones” lash the horses into the war.
---

“and shining Hector bade wise-hearted Cebriones / lash the horses into the war” (16.727–728; [[cebriones]], [[phaidimos]], [[hector]]). Apollo has just sent Hector against Patroclus (16.721–725), and Cebriones' drive is his last: within a few verses Patroclus' stone strikes him from the chariot (16.733–743). See [[the-death-of-patroclus]].
