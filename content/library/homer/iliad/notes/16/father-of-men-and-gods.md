---
summary: The father of men and gods gives way to Hera and lets Sarpedon die, shedding bloody raindrops in his honor.
---

“So she spoke, and the father of men and gods did not disobey; / and he shed bloody raindrops on the earth, / honoring his dear son, whom Patroclus was to / kill in deep-soiled Troy, far from his native land” (16.458–461; [[hera]], [[bloody]], [[deep-soiled]], [[sarpedon]]). The title of the father of all stands beside the grief of a father for one son. He does what Hera has advised, and the only honor he gives Sarpedon before his death is the rain of blood ([[the-death-of-sarpedon]]). See [[zeus]].
