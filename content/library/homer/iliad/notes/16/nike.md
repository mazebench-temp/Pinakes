---
summary: Victory is given and taken by the gods. Zeus wishes victory for the Trojans when he breaks Ajax's spear (16.121); Hector knows “the victory that turns the battle” (16.362); the mind of Zeus “takes away victory easily” (16.689); and the dying Patroclus tells Hector that Zeus and Apollo have given him the victory (16.845).
---

Hector's knowledge and Patroclus' last words make a pair: Hector knows when victory has turned against him and saves his companions (16.362–363; see [[that-turns-the-battle]]), and Patroclus, in his last speech, denies Hector the victory he boasts of: “to you they have given / victory, Zeus, son of Cronus, and Apollo, who brought me down / easily” (16.844–846; see [[the-mind-of-zeus]] and [[victory-shifts]]).
