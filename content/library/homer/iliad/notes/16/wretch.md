---
summary: Hector taunts the dying Patroclus: “Ah, wretch, not even Achilles, brave as he is, has helped you.”
---

“Ah, wretch, not even Achilles, brave as he is, has helped you, / who, I suppose, staying behind, gave you many commands as you went” (16.837–838; [[brave]], [[hector]]). The address of pity or scorn to a man who does not see his end is spoken here by the man who does not see his own: Patroclus answers that death stands close beside Hector (16.852–854). See [[patroclus]].
