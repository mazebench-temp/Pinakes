“And many sharp spears were fixed around Cebriones, / and feathered arrows leaping from the bowstrings, / and many great stones battered the shields” (16.772–774; [[sharp]], [[cebriones]], [[arrows]]). The adjective of winged words is here the arrows', as in Book 5 (5.171). The phrase ἰοί τε πτερόεντες is new to the entry.
