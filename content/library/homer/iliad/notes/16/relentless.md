---
summary: When the fire is out and the Danaans pour after the Trojans, “a relentless din arose.”
---

“and the Danaans poured after them / among the hollow ships, and a relentless din arose” (16.295–296; [[wondrous]], [[hollow-ships]]). The verse ended Book 12, when Hector broke the gate and the Trojans poured in among the ships (12.470–471); now the Danaans pour after them in the same words. See [[the-achaean-wall]].
