---
summary: Patroclus claims the title for Achilles before the Myrmidons, “the son of Peleus, who is by far the best / of the Argives beside the ships”, and repeats Achilles' charge that Agamemnon “paid no honor to the best of the Achaeans” (16.271–274 ~ 1.411–412). Apollo tells Patroclus that Achilles is “far better than you” (16.709).
