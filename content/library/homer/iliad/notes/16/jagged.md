---
summary: Patroclus grasps “a stone, / jagged and gleaming,” and kills Cebriones with it; the book adds ὀκριόεντα.
grc: [ὀκριόεντα]
---

“and with the other he grasped a stone, / jagged and gleaming, that his hand closed around, / and he threw it, setting himself” (16.734–736; [[cebriones]], [[sharp]], [[stone-throwing]]). The stone strikes Hector's charioteer on the forehead and crushes his brows (16.737–741). The accusative ὀκριόεντα is new to the entry. See [[patroclus]].
