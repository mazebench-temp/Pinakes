---
summary: “The mind of Zeus and the noble Trojans” overpower Ajax at the ship.
---

“and Ajax no longer stood his ground; for he was hard pressed by missiles; / the mind of Zeus and the noble Trojans were overpowering him / as they struck him” (16.102–104; [[ajax]], [[trojans]], [[plan-of-zeus]]). The god and the men are joined in one subject; the epithet of praise is given to the enemy at the moment they defeat the best defender of the ships. See [[thunders-on-high]].
