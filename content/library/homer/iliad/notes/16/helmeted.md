---
summary: Meriones kills “a helmeted man of the Trojans,” Laogonus.
---

“Then in turn Meriones killed a helmeted man of the Trojans, / Laogonus, the bold son of Onetor” (16.603–604; [[bold]], [[laogonus]], [[meriones]]). The formula Τρώων ἕλεν ἄνδρα κορυστήν was the verse of the first kill of a battle (4.457, 8.256); here it begins a new round of killing after Glaucus' kill of Bathycles (16.593–600). See [[helmet]].
