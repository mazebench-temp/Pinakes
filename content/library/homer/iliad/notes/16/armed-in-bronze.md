---
summary: Ajax aims at “Hector armed in bronze,” Glaucus goes to “Hector armed in bronze” to rouse him for Sarpedon, and Zeus plans that Patroclus drive back “Hector armed in bronze.”
---

“And great Ajax was ever eager to throw his spear at Hector armed in bronze” (16.358; [[ajax]]); Glaucus “went after Aeneas and Hector armed in bronze” (16.536; [[glaucus]], [[aeneas]]); and Zeus decides that Patroclus “should once again drive the Trojans and Hector armed in bronze / toward the city” (16.654–655; [[plan-of-zeus]]). Each time the formula ends the verse and Hector is the object of another man's aim, appeal or plan. See [[hector]].
