---
summary: Tydeus is named only in his son's title, Τυδεΐδης, “son of Tydeus”: in Patroclus' list of the wounded and in Achilles' answer, that the spear no longer rages in the hands of Diomedes (16.25, 16.74).
---

βέβληται μὲν ὃ Τυδεΐδης κρατερὸς Διομήδης, “The son of Tydeus, mighty Diomedes, has been shot” (16.25), the verse in which Nestor named the wounded to Patroclus (11.660; see [[the-wounded-chiefs]] and [[patroclus]]). Achilles answers with the father's name too: “For not in the hands of Diomedes, son of Tydeus, / does the spear rage to ward off ruin from the Danaans” (16.74–75; see [[diomedes]], [[son-of-tydeus]] and [[achilles]]).
