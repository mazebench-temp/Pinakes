---
summary: Rout is the motion of the battle in Book 16. Patroclus sends rout among the Paeonians, and the Trojans flee the ships “in headlong rout” (16.291–304); they remember “the ill-sounding rout” and forget their courage (16.356–357); the Lycians flee when Hector does (16.657–659); and in the fight over Cebriones “neither side thought of deadly rout” (16.771).
