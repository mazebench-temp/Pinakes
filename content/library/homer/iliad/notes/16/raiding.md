summary: Achilles won Briseis with his spear when he sacked a well-walled city (16.57), and the trace horse Pedasus he brought back when he took the city of Eetion (16.153). The great sack is the one denied: Apollo tells Patroclus that it is not his portion that the city of the Trojans be sacked by his spear (16.708), and Hector mocks him for having said he would ravage it (16.830).
