---
summary: Thestor sits crouching, out of his wits, “in the well-polished chariot.”
---

“and he rushed at Thestor, son of Enops, / the second; he sat crouching in the well-polished chariot; / for he was struck out of his wits, and out of his hands / the reins had slipped” (16.401–404; [[thestor-son-of-enops]], [[chariot]]). Patroclus drags him from it on his spear like a fish on a line (16.406–409; [[gleaming-bronze]], [[the-fisherman]]).
