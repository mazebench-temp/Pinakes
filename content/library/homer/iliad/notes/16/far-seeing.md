---
summary: Achilles prays, “send glory with him, far-seeing Zeus,” as he sends Patroclus out.
---

“but I am sending my companion with many Myrmidons / to fight; send glory with him, far-seeing Zeus, / and embolden the heart in his breast” (16.240–242; [[prayer]], [[kydos]], [[myrmidons]]). The epithet comes in the vocative, in the middle of the prayer to the Zeus of Dodona; Achilles asks that Hector may learn whether Patroclus can fight alone, and that he come back unharmed (16.242–248). Zeus grants the first and refuses the second (16.250–252; [[counselor]]). See [[zeus]] and [[achilles]].
