---
summary: Achilles opens the chest that Thetis filled “with tunics / and cloaks to keep off the wind and woolly rugs,” and takes out the cup for his libation.
---

{{quote:2494f196-6ce3-5081-990d-0bbadd76bcdc}}

Before he prays for Patroclus, Achilles opens the chest that his mother packed for him when he sailed, “filling it well with tunics / and cloaks to keep off the wind and woolly rugs” (16.223–224; [[thick-and-woolly]], [[intricately-worked]], [[chest]], [[thetis]]). From it he takes the cup from which no other man drinks and from which he pours libations to no god but Zeus ([[libation]], [[father-zeus]]). See [[achilles]].
