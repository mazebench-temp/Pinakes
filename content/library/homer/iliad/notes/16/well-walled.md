---
summary: Achilles won Briseis with his spear “when I sacked a well-walled city”; the book adds εὐτείχεα.
grc: [εὐτείχεα]
---

“The girl whom the sons of the Achaeans picked out for me as my prize, / and I won her with my spear, when I sacked a well-walled city” (16.56–57; [[briseis]], [[sons-of-the-achaeans]]). The city is Lyrnessus (2.690–691), and the epithet, elsewhere Troy's, is given to one of the cities that Achilles took. The form εὐτείχεα is new to the entry. See [[achilles]].
