---
summary: The sons of the Achaeans would have taken “high-gated Troy” at the hands of Patroclus, had not Apollo stood on the tower.
---

“There the sons of the Achaeans would have taken high-gated Troy / at the hands of Patroclus; for he raged ahead with his spear, / had not Phoebus Apollo stood on the well-built tower” (16.698–700; [[would-have-had-not]], [[sons-of-the-achaeans]], [[well-built]]). In Book 6 the epithet was Thebe's, Eetion's city, which Achilles sacked (6.415–416); here it is Troy's in the verse that says how near it came to being taken ([[sack-of-troy-foretold]]). See [[troy]].
