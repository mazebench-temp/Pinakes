---
summary: The trace horse Pedasus keeps pace with “the immortal horses,” Hera warns that other “sons of immortals” fight at Troy, and Apollo drives Patroclus back from the wall “with his immortal hands.”
---

**The horses.** Pedasus, the trace horse taken from the city of Eetion, “mortal though he was, kept pace with the immortal horses” (16.154; [[the-horses-of-achilles]], [[pedasus-the-horse]]). Sarpedon's spear will kill him and miss the immortal pair (16.466–469).

**The sons of gods.** Hera warns Zeus against saving his son: “for many are fighting around the great city of Priam, / sons of immortals, and you will stir a dread grudge in them” (16.448–449; [[hera]], [[sarpedon]]).

**Apollo's hands.** At the wall, “three times Apollo drove him back, / thrusting at the shining shield with his immortal hands” (16.703–704; [[apollo]], [[phaeinos]], [[three-times-and-a-fourth]]). See [[olympians]].
