---
summary: After Automedon cuts the dead trace horse loose, Sarpedon and Patroclus “came together again in heart-devouring strife.”
---

“and the two were set straight, and pulled taut in the reins. / And the two men came together again in heart-devouring strife” (16.475–476; [[sarpedon]], [[patroclus]], [[eris]]). The phrase ἔριδος πέρι θυμοβόροιο is the one in which Hector, ending his duel with Ajax, imagined men saying that they “fought in heart-devouring strife” and parted in friendship (7.301); this strife ends in Sarpedon's death (16.477–486).
