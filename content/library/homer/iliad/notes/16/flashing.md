---
summary: Ajax's left shoulder grows weary “from always holding his flashing shield steady.”
---

“and his left shoulder grew weary / from always holding his flashing shield steady; nor could they / shake him where he stood, pressing on him with their missiles” (16.106–108; [[ajax]], [[shield]]). The great shield of Ajax is αἰόλος, flashing or shifting, as it was in Book 7 (7.222; [[the-shield-of-ajax]]). The epithet of quick light is given to the shield that a tired man holds without moving.
