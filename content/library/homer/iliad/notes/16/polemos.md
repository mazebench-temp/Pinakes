summary: War is what Achilles holds back from and Patroclus enters. Achilles had said he would not end his wrath until “the war cry and the war” reached his own ships (16.63); he forbids Patroclus to fight on without him (16.89); and Zeus grants that he will drive “the war and the battle” from the ships, but not that he will come back from it (16.251–252).
