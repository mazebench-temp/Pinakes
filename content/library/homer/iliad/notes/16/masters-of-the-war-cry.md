---
summary: Over the body of Cebriones, “two masters of the war cry, / Patroclus, son of Menoetius, and shining Hector,” fight like lions.
---

“The two fought over Cebriones like lions, / which on the peaks of a mountain, over a slain deer, / both of them hungry, fight in high spirits: / so over Cebriones two masters of the war cry, / Patroclus, son of Menoetius, and shining Hector, / were eager to cut each other's flesh with the pitiless bronze” (16.756–761; [[two-lions]], [[son-of-menoetius]], [[phaidimos]], [[pitiless-bronze]]). The title of the Athenians in Book 4 (4.328) and of Meriones and Antilochus in Book 13 (13.93, 13.479) is given here to the two men who will face each other at the end of the book. See [[cebriones]].
