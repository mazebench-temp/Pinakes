---
summary: Apollo strikes Patroclus, and “the full-length shield” of Achilles falls from his shoulders with its strap.
---

The shield is the last piece of the borrowed armor to fall before Apollo loosens the corselet: “And the long-shadowed spear was all broken in his hands, / heavy, huge, and massive, tipped with bronze; and from his shoulders / the full-length shield fell to the ground with its strap” (16.801–803; [[long-shadowed]], [[heavy-spear]], [[apollo]]). Patroclus stands stripped of his armor, and Euphorbus strikes him from behind (16.805–815; [[euphorbus]]). See [[shield]] and [[patroclus]].
