---
summary: Idomeneus kills Erymas “with the pitiless bronze,” Patroclus wants to bring down Sarpedon's defenders “with the pitiless bronze,” and Patroclus and Hector, over Cebriones, are eager to cut each other's flesh “with the pitiless bronze.”
---

“And Idomeneus stabbed Erymas in the mouth with the pitiless bronze” (16.345; [[idomeneus]], [[erymas-killed-by-idomeneus]]); Patroclus to the Ajaxes, over Sarpedon: “and some of his companions, / who defend him, we might bring down with the pitiless bronze” (16.560–561; [[ajax]]); and over Cebriones, the two masters of the war cry “were eager to cut each other's flesh with the pitiless bronze” (16.761; [[masters-of-the-war-cry]]). The verse-end νηλέϊ χαλκῷ comes once in the killing among the leaders, once in the fight for Sarpedon's body and once in the fight for Cebriones'. See [[bronze]].
