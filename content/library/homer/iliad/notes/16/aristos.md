---
summary: “The best” are wounded at the ships: “all those who were the best before / lie among the ships, shot or stabbed” (16.23–24). Patroclus calls Achilles “by far the best of the Argives” and wants Agamemnon to learn that he paid no honor to “the best of the Achaeans” (16.271–274); Glaucus mourns Sarpedon, “the best of men” (16.521).
---

ἀριστεύεσκε, “was always the best in the fighting”, is said of two leaders on the Trojan side when they fall: Pyraechmes, the Paeonians' leader, whose death routs his men (16.292), and Sarpedon (16.551). For the title, see [[best-of-the-achaeans]] and [[the-wounded-chiefs]].
