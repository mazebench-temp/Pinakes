---
summary: When the fire is out, the Trojans are not yet fleeing in rout “away from the black ships”; the book adds the phrase μελαινάων ἀπὸ νηῶν.
grc: ["μελαινάων ἀπὸ νηῶν"]
---

“for the Trojans were not yet fleeing before the Achaeans, dear to Ares, / in headlong rout away from the black ships, / but they still stood against them, and gave way from the ships under compulsion” (16.303–305; [[dear-to-ares]]). With ἀπό the adjective comes before the noun, as it did with ἐπί in Books 5 and 8 (μελαινάων ἐπὶ νηῶν); the phrase μελαινάων ἀπὸ νηῶν is new to the entry. See [[ships]].
