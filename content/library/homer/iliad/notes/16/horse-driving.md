---
summary: The fourth rank of the Myrmidons is led by “the old horse-driving Phoenix.”
---

“The fourth was led by the old horse-driving Phoenix” (16.196; [[phoenix]], [[myrmidons]]). The title of the old horsemen, Peleus, Oeneus and Tydeus, was Phoenix's when he rose to speak in the embassy (9.432), where he told Achilles how Peleus took him in (9.480–484). Of the five leaders he is the only one whose story the poem has already told. See [[old-age-and-memory]].
