---
summary: Achilles still measures the war by his honor: Agamemnon treated him “as if I were some dishonored vagrant” (16.59), and Patroclus is to win him “great honor and glory” but not to fight on without him, or “you will make me more dishonored” (16.84–90). Patroclus rouses the Myrmidons to honor Achilles, so that Agamemnon may learn that he paid no honor to the best of the Achaeans (16.271–274; see [[time]]).
