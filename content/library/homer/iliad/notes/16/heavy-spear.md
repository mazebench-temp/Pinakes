---
summary: Patroclus leaves behind Achilles' spear, “heavy, huge, and massive,” which no other Achaean could wield; at the end the spear in his own hands, described in the same words, is broken by Apollo.
---

**Achilles' spear.** “Only the spear of the noble grandson of Aeacus he did not take, / heavy, huge, and massive; no other of the Achaeans / could wield it, but Achilles alone knew how to wield it, / the Pelian ash” (16.140–143; [[the-pelian-ash]], [[blameless]]). The verse is used of Athena's spear, “with which she overpowers the ranks of men” (5.746, 8.390).

**Patroclus' spear.** When Apollo strikes him, “the long-shadowed spear was all broken in his hands, / heavy, huge, and massive, tipped with bronze” (16.801–802; [[long-shadowed]], [[full-length]]). The words of Achilles' spear are given to the spear that Patroclus carried instead of it.
