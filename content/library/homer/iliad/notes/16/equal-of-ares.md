---
summary: “Three times then he sprang at them, the equal of swift Ares”: Patroclus' last charge.
---

“Three times then he sprang at them, the equal of swift Ares, / shouting terribly, and three times he killed nine men. / But when for the fourth time he rushed on like a god” (16.784–786; [[swift]], [[like-a-god]], [[three-times-and-a-fourth]]). The half-verse θοῷ ἀτάλαντος Ἄρηϊ is Meriones' in Book 13 (13.295, 13.328, 13.528) and Hector's in Book 8 (8.215); the verse begins as Diomedes' attack on Apollo (5.436). The equal of Ares meets Apollo, and the next verse tells the end of his life (16.787–789; [[apostrophe]]). See [[patroclus]].
