summary: Brothers fight brothers at the ships: the two sons of Nestor kill the two sons of Amisodarus, “So these two, brought down by two brothers” (16.326), after Maris has sprung at Antilochus “angered for his brother” (16.320). Sarpedon's brothers and kinsmen will bury him (16.456, 16.674); Hera is Zeus' “sister and wife” (16.432); and Asius, whose likeness Apollo takes, is Hecuba's own brother (16.718).
