---
summary: The θυμός is the life that leaves the dying, “his spirit left him” (16.410, 16.606–607, 16.743), and the heart in which men feel and want: Achilles' dread grief, the spirit Zeus let loose in Patroclus' breast (16.691), the craven heart he put in Hector (16.656), and the heart that still made Achilles wish to watch the battle (16.255–256).
