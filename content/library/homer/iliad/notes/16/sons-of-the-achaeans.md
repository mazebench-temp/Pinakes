summary: Patroclus asks for Achilles' armor so that “the warlike sons of the Achaeans may draw breath,” Achilles recalls the girl whom “the sons of the Achaeans picked out” for him, and the sons of the Achaeans would have taken Troy at Patroclus' hands.
---

“and the warlike sons of the Achaeans may draw breath” (16.42; [[warlike]]); “The girl whom the sons of the Achaeans picked out for me as my prize” (16.56; [[briseis]], [[geras]]); and “There the sons of the Achaeans would have taken high-gated Troy / at the hands of Patroclus” (16.698–699; [[high-gated]], [[would-have-had-not]]). The formula ends the verse each time. The army that gave Achilles his prize nearly takes the city under the man he sends in his place. See [[achaeans]].
