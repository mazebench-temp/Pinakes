---
summary: Aeneas' spear goes quivering into the earth, “since it had sped in vain from his strong hand.”
---

“And Aeneas' spear went quivering into the earth, / since it had sped in vain from his strong hand” (16.614–615; [[aeneas]], [[meriones]]). The two verses are the ones in which Aeneas' spear missed Idomeneus in Book 13 (13.504–505); Aeneas misses again in the same words. See [[obrimos]] and [[spear]].
