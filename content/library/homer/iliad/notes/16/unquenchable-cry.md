---
summary: The Myrmidons pour out from the ships like wasps, “and an unquenchable cry went up.”
---

“With the heart and spirit of these the Myrmidons then / poured out from the ships; and an unquenchable cry went up” (16.266–267; [[wasps]], [[myrmidons]]). The verse-end βοὴ δʼ ἄσβεστος ὀρώρει is the one of the fighting in Books 11 and 13 (11.500, 13.169, 13.540); here it is the shout of men going in fresh. The same adjective has been said of the flame on Protesilaus' ship, “an unquenchable flame” (16.123).
