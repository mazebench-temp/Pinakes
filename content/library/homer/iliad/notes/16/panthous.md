---
summary: Panthous is named in the patronymic of two of his sons: Glaucus goes to “Polydamas, son of Panthous” (16.535), and “Euphorbus, son of Panthous”, is the first to wound Patroclus (16.808).
---

Πανθοΐδης names both sons in this book: Polydamas, among the Trojan leaders whom Glaucus calls to fight for Sarpedon's body (16.535; see [[polydamas]]), and Euphorbus, who comes into the poem as he strikes Patroclus in the back (16.807–815; see [[euphorbus]]). In Book 17 Menelaus kills Euphorbus, as he had killed Hyperenor, another son of Panthous (17.9–60, 14.516–519; see [[hyperenor]]).
