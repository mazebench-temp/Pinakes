summary: Zeus answers Achilles' prayer with a nod and its opposite: “the father granted him one thing, and refused him the other” (ἀνένευσε, “nodded no”, 16.250–252). The plume of Patroclus' helmet nods above him (16.138), and the Myrmidons stand so close that their crests touch when they nod (16.217).
grc: [ἀνένευσε]
en: [refused]
---

ἀνανεύω, “nod upward”, is the refusal, as κατανεύω, “nod down”, is the grant (see the article above). The verse names both acts and leaves the second unsaid to Achilles: he prays, pours, and goes back to his hut to watch (16.253–256; see [[prayer]]).
