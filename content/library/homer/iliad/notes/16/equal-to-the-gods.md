---
summary: Patroclus leads the way, and Meriones follows him, “a man equal to the gods.”
---

“So saying, he led the way, and the other followed, a man equal to the gods” (16.632; [[meriones]], [[patroclus]]). Patroclus has rebuked Meriones for trading taunts with Aeneas when he should fight (16.626–631; [[my-dear]]), and the formula of following closes the scene. The verse is the one with which Menelaus led Ajax to Odysseus (11.472) and Hector led Melanippus (15.559). Patroclus himself is ἰσόθεος φώς in Books 9 and 11 (9.211, 11.644).
