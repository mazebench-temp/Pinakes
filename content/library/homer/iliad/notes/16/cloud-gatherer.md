---
summary: “Zeus the cloud-gatherer” sends Apollo to cleanse Sarpedon's body and have Sleep and Death carry it to Lycia.
---

“And then Zeus the cloud-gatherer spoke to Apollo: / ‘Come now, dear Phoebus, go and cleanse the dark blood / from Sarpedon, out of range of the missiles’” (16.666–668; [[apollo]], [[phoebus]], [[dark-blood]]). The god of the clouds gives orders for the care of his son's body: Apollo is to wash it in the river, anoint it with ambrosia, clothe it in immortal clothing, and send it “with Sleep and Death, the twins” to Lycia (16.669–675; [[sleep]], [[death]], [[ambrosia]], [[the-prize-of-the-dead]]). In the next verse the epithet of the storm god, κελαινεφής, is given to the blood ([[lord-of-dark-clouds]]). See [[zeus]], [[sarpedon]] and [[the-death-of-sarpedon]].
