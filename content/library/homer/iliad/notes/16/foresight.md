---
summary: The narrator sees Patroclus' end from his first request, “great fool that he was” (16.46–47), and tells it again and again: Zeus refuses his safe return (16.250–252), Patroclus forgets Achilles' word (16.684–687), the end of his life appears (16.787). Patroclus sees Hector's end as he dies, and Hector refuses to see it (16.851–861).
---

The characters do not see what the narrator sees. Achilles prays for Patroclus' return without knowing that half his prayer is refused, and stands at his hut to watch (16.249–256; see [[prayer]]). Patroclus, “the fool”, pursues the Trojans to the city (16.686; see [[nepios]]). Hector, told that death and fate stand beside him, answers “Who knows” (16.853, 16.860; see [[prophecy]]).
