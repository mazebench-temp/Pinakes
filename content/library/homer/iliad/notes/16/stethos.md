summary: The chest is the place of the corselet and of the wound: Patroclus puts on Achilles' corselet around his chest (16.133), sets his heel on Sarpedon's chest to draw out the spear (16.503), and is himself the lion struck in the chest (16.753); Hector imagines Achilles ordering him to tear the tunic around Hector's chest (16.841).
