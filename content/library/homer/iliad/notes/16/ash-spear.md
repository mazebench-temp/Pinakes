---
summary: Hector lops the point off Ajax's “ash spear,” and Euphorbus snatches his “ash spear” out of Patroclus' back.
---

**Ajax.** “Hector came up close and struck Ajax's ash spear / with his great sword, behind the point, at the socket, / and lopped it clean off” (16.114–116; [[ajax]], [[hector]], [[telamonian]]); Ajax is left brandishing the docked spear.

**Euphorbus.** After his throw, Euphorbus “ran back again and mingled with the throng, / snatching the ash spear out of the flesh, and he did not stand his ground / against Patroclus, naked though he was, in the combat” (16.813–815; [[euphorbus]], [[patroclus]]). The phrase δόρυ μείλινον names the shaft that breaks in the first fight of the book and the one that wounds Patroclus in the last. See [[spear]].
