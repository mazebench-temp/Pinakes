---
summary: Laogonus' father Onetor was the priest of Zeus of Ida, “and was honored by the people like a god.”
---

“Laogonus, the bold son of Onetor, who was the priest of Zeus / of Ida, and was honored by the people like a god” (16.604–605; [[onetor]], [[laogonus]], [[ida]], [[priesthood]]). The relative clause follows Onetor, and the honor is the father's: the priest is revered by his people, and his son dies by Meriones' spear (see the translation's note on 16.604–605). The verse-end was said of Dolopion, the priest of Scamander whose son Hypsenor Eurypylus killed (5.76–78; [[bold]]). See [[zeus]].
