---
summary: Achilles' prayer to Zeus of Dodona takes its request from Chryses' second prayer to Apollo: “once before you listened to my word when I prayed, / and you honored me, and struck the army of the Achaeans hard; / so now once more fulfill for me this wish” (16.236–238 ~ 1.453–455). Zeus fulfills half of it.
---

Chryses' second prayer asked Apollo to undo what his first had done, and ward off the plague from the Danaans (1.451–456). Achilles' claim is the same: the god honored him once and struck the Achaeans hard, as Zeus did when he granted Thetis' request (see [[thetis-supplicates-zeus]] and [[time]]). His wish is that Patroclus drive the battle from the ships and come back unharmed (16.241–248). Zeus grants the first and refuses the second (16.250–252; see [[prayer]] and [[neuo]]).
