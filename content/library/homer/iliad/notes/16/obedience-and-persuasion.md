summary: Achilles' instruction to Patroclus begins “But obey” and sets the limit, to come back from the ships (16.83–96); the narrator says that if Patroclus had kept the word of the son of Peleus he would have escaped death (16.686–687); and Hector imagines Achilles' word as an order to kill him, which “persuaded your mind, fool that you were” (16.838–842; see [[peitho]]).
