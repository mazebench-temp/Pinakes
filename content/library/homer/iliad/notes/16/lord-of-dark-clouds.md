---
summary: Zeus is not called “lord of the dark clouds” in Book 16; the only κελαινεφές of the book is the dark blood that Zeus bids Apollo cleanse from Sarpedon, and the line is excepted.
except: [16.667]
---

The adjective of the storm god comes once in the book, of blood: “Come now, dear Phoebus, go and cleanse the dark blood / from Sarpedon” (16.667–668; [[dark-blood]], [[apollo]], [[sarpedon]]). κελαινεφὲς αἷμα is “dark blood,” as at 4.140, 5.798 and 14.437, and the line belongs to [[dark-blood]]. The verse before names the speaker as Zeus [[cloud-gatherer]].
