---
summary: The spring of the simile of Patroclus' tears “pours its murky water down a sheer rock.”
---

“that pours its murky water down a sheer rock” (16.4; [[sheer]], [[of-dark-water]], [[hot-tears]]), the verse of Agamemnon's tears in Book 9 (9.15). The water is dark twice in the two verses, κρήνη μελάνυδρος and δνοφερὸν … ὕδωρ. See [[patroclus]].
