---
summary: Zeus wonders whether to snatch Sarpedon alive “out of the tearful battle.”
---

“whether, while he is still alive, out of the tearful battle / I should snatch him up and set him down in the rich land of Lycia, / or bring him down now at the hands of the son of Menoetius” (16.436–438; [[divine-rescue]], [[decision-scene]], [[sarpedon]]). The battle is tearful for those who weep the dead; Zeus thinks of sparing his son from it, and in the end he honors him with a rain of blood (16.459–460; [[bloody]]). See [[zeus]].
