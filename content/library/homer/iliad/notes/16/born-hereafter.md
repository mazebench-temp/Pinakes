---
summary: Patroclus asks Achilles what good anyone, “even one born hereafter,” will have of him if he does not save the Argives; the book adds the singular ὀψίγονος.
grc: [ὀψίγονός]
---

“you whose prowess is a curse! What good will anyone have of you, even one born hereafter, / if you do not ward off shameful ruin from the Argives?” (16.31–32; [[whose-prowess-is-a-curse]], [[shameful-ruin]]). Elsewhere the men born hereafter are those who will remember a man's deeds and judge them (3.353, 7.87); here they are the ones who would profit from Achilles' excellence, and Patroclus says none will. The singular ὀψίγονος is new to the entry. See [[achilles]] and [[kleos]].
