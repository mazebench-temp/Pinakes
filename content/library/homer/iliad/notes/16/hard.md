---
summary: Achilles quotes the Myrmidons' reproach, “Hard son of Peleus, your mother reared you on anger.”
---

“‘Hard son of Peleus, your mother reared you on anger, / pitiless, who keep your companions by the ships against their will; / let us at least go back home again with our seafaring ships, / since such evil anger has fallen on your heart’” (16.203–206; [[son-of-peleus]], [[pitiless]], [[seafaring-ship]], [[cholos]]). Achilles repeats his men's complaint in order to turn it: the battle they wanted is here (16.207–209). The pair σχέτλιος and νηλής is the one in which Ajax summed up Achilles at the end of the embassy, “hard man … pitiless one” (9.630–632; [[embassy]]). See [[achilles]] and [[myrmidons]].
