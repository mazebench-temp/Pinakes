---
summary: Patroclus tells Achilles that “the horseman Peleus was not your father after all”; the book adds “ἱππότα Πηλεύς.”
grc: ["ἱππότα Πηλεύς"]
---

“Pitiless one, the horseman Peleus was not your father after all, / nor Thetis your mother” (16.33–34; [[pitiless]], [[gray-green-sea]], [[peleus]]). The horseman's title ἱππότα, given to Nestor and to other men of the older generation, is Peleus' here for the first time, and it stands for the father whom Patroclus denies to Achilles. Thirteen verses earlier the narrator has called Patroclus himself “horseman” with another word ([[hippeus]]). The phrase ἱππότα Πηλεύς is new to the entry.
