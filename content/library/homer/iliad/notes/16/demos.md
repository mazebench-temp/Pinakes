---
summary: The δῆμος is Lycia, the land to which Sarpedon belongs: Zeus would set him down alive “in the rich land of Lycia”, and Sleep and Death set his body down “in the rich land of wide Lycia” (16.437, 16.455, 16.673, 16.683; see [[divine-rescue]]); Glaucus calls on Apollo “who are somewhere in the rich land of Lycia” (16.514). Onetor, priest of Zeus, was honored by his people like a god (16.605).
