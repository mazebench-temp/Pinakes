---
summary: The Myrmidons' helmets and “bossed shields” fit together in their ranks like the stones of a wall.
---

“And as when a man fits the wall together with close-set stones / of a high house, keeping out the force of the winds, / so closely were the helmets and bossed shields fitted together” (16.212–214; [[the-stones-of-a-wall]], [[myrmidons]], [[shield]]). The next two verses are those of the Achaean line in Book 13, “Shield pressed on shield, helmet on helmet, man on man” (16.215 = 13.131; [[horsehair-crested]]).
