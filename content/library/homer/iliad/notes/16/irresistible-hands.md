---
summary: Achilles prays that Hector may learn whether Patroclus can fight alone, “or whether his hands rage irresistible / only when I go into the toil of Ares.”
---

“so that Hector too / may know whether our attendant knows how to fight / even alone, or whether his hands rage irresistible / only when I go into the toil of Ares” (16.242–245; [[prayer]], [[patroclus]], [[ares]]). The hands that are irresistible are the formula of might in Ajax's and Zeus' mouths (13.77, 8.450); here they are Patroclus', and Achilles' prayer asks whether they are his own. See [[achilles]].
