---
summary: Patroclus gives ground from the wall, “avoiding the wrath of Apollo who shoots from afar.”
---

“So he spoke, and Patroclus gave ground a long way back, / avoiding the wrath of Apollo who shoots from afar” (16.710–711; [[menis]], [[apollo]], [[patroclus]]). The god's wrath, μῆνις, is named as the thing Patroclus avoids; it is the word of Apollo's wrath in Book 1 (1.75) and of Achilles' own. He will not avoid the god for long (16.788–806). See [[the-death-of-patroclus]].
