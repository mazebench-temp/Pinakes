---
summary: Achilles pours his libation looking up to the sky, and “he did not escape the notice of Zeus who delights in thunder”; the book adds the accusative τερπικέραυνον.
grc: [τερπικέραυνον]
---

“Then he prayed, standing in the middle of the enclosure, and poured out the wine, / looking up to the sky; and he did not escape the notice of Zeus who delights in thunder” (16.231–232; [[libation]], [[prayer]], [[achilles]]). The accusative τερπικέραυνον is new to the entry. The prayer that follows calls him by his titles of Dodona (16.233–235; [[wintry]], [[with-unwashed-feet]]). See [[zeus]].
