---
summary: In the rout from the ships, “many swift chariot-drawing horses” break their masters' chariots in the ditch; the book adds ἐρυσάρματες.
grc: [ἐρυσάρματες]
---

“And many swift chariot-drawing horses in the ditch / broke their lords' chariots at the end of the pole and left them” (16.370–371; [[swift-footed]], [[the-achaean-wall]], [[chariot]]). The horses that in Book 15 drew the chariots over the ditch on the road Apollo made (15.354) now break them in it as the Trojans flee. The nominative ἐρυσάρματες is new to the entry.
