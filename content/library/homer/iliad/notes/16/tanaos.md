---
summary: The Trojans give ground “as far as the rush of a long javelin carries,” thrown in a contest or in war.
---

When Patroclus has killed Sthenelaus with a stone, the champions and Hector fall back: “and as far as the rush of a long javelin carries, / which a man throws, trying his strength, either in a contest / or in war, under the attack of enemies who destroy the spirit, / so far the Trojans gave ground, and the Achaeans pushed them back” (16.589–592; [[that-destroys-the-spirit]], [[destructive]], [[sthenelaus]]). The measure is taken from the games and from war at once. See [[spear]].
