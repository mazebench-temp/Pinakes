---
summary: “The mighty slayer of Argus” falls in love with Polymele when he sees her in the dance of Artemis; the book adds the nominative ἀργεϊφόντης.
grc: [ἀργεϊφόντης]
---

“the daughter of Phylas; the mighty slayer of Argus / fell in love with her, when he saw her with his eyes among the girls singing and dancing / in the dance of Artemis of the golden distaff, the clamorous” (16.181–183; [[polymele]], [[phylas]], [[of-the-golden-distaff]], [[clamorous]]). The half-verse κρατὺς ἀργεϊφόντης names Hermes again as he flies to Priam (24.345) and to Calypso (*Odyssey* 5.49). Four verses later he is “Hermes the helper” ([[akaketa]]). The nominative ἀργεϊφόντης is new to the entry. See [[hermes]] and [[eudorus]].
