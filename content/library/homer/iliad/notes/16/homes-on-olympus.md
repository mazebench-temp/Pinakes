“Tell me now, Muses, you who have your homes on Olympus, / how fire first fell upon the ships of the Achaeans” (16.112–113; [[invocation-of-the-muses]], [[muses]], [[olympus]]). The verse opened the Catalogue of Ships (2.484) and the lists of those who first faced Agamemnon and first won spoils from the Trojans (11.218, 14.508), and it marks the moment toward which Zeus' plan has moved since Book 1, the fire at the ships ([[plan-of-zeus]], [[tireless]]).
