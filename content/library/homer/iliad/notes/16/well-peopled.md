grc: ["εὖ ναιομένῳ"]
---

“the son of great-hearted Agacles, brilliant Epeigeus, / who had been lord in well-peopled Budeum / before” (16.571–573; [[epeigeus]], [[budeum]], [[exile-and-refuge]]). The prosperous town is named at the moment its lord's exile is told: he left it for killing a cousin (16.573–574). The dative εὖ ναιομένῳ is new to the entry.
