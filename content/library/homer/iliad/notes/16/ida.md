# Ἰδαίου (16.605), "of Ida", in Διὸς ἱρεὺς / Ἰδαίου, "the priest of Zeus / of Ida"; zeus claims it too.
grc: [Ἰδαίου]
---

**From Ida.** When Zeus sends Apollo for Sarpedon's body, the god “went down from the mountains of Ida into the dread din of battle” (16.677; see [[apollo]] and [[zeus]]), the half-verse that took Iris and Apollo from Zeus on Gargarus to the war in Book 15 (15.152–153, 15.169, 15.237; see [[gargarus]]).

**Zeus of Ida.** Meriones kills “Laogonus, the bold son of Onetor, who was the priest of Zeus / of Ida, and was honored by the people like a god” (16.604–605; see [[laogonus]], [[onetor]] and [[priesthood]]). Ἰδαῖος, “of Ida”, is the god's title once more, when Hecuba bids Priam pray to the Zeus of Ida who looks down on all Troy (24.290–291). Both armies pray to “Father Zeus, who rule from Ida” (3.276, 3.320, 7.202), and he has a precinct and a fragrant altar on Gargarus (8.47–48).
