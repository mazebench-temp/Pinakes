---
summary: Patroclus bids the Myrmidons “remember your furious courage,” and the routed Trojans “forgot their furious courage.”
---

“Myrmidons, companions of Achilles, son of Peleus, / be men, my friends, and remember your furious courage” (16.269–270; [[exhortation]], [[myrmidons]]); and in the rout, “and they remembered / the ill-sounding rout, and forgot their furious courage” (16.356–357; [[ill-sounding]], [[trojans]]). The formula of the exhortation is turned against the Trojans within ninety verses. See [[alke]].
