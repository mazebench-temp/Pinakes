summary: The assembly is named once, in the simile of the autumn storm, where men “by force in the assembly judge crooked ordinances” and Zeus sends the flood against them (16.387; see [[themis]]). The other forms of ἀγορεύω in the book are speaking, not assembly.
# ἀγόρευον (16.101) and ἀγορεύεις (16.627) are "spoke, say", as at 13.81.
except: [16.101, 16.627]
