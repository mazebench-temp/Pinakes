---
summary: Hector answers the dead Patroclus' prophecy: “Who knows if Achilles, son of Thetis of the lovely hair, / may not first be struck by my spear.”
---

“And to him, though he was dead, shining Hector spoke: / ‘Patroclus, why do you prophesy sheer destruction for me? / Who knows if Achilles, son of Thetis of the lovely hair, / may not first be struck by my spear and lose his life?’” (16.858–861; [[hector]], [[thetis]], [[steep]], [[phaidimos]]). Hector names Achilles by his divine mother as he boasts that he may kill him ([[death-of-achilles-foretold]]). See [[achilles]].
