---
summary: Patroclus slings Achilles' “silver-studded sword / of bronze” around his shoulders.
---

“And around his shoulders he slung his silver-studded sword / of bronze, and then his shield, great and heavy” (16.135–136; [[sword]], [[shield]], [[arming-scene]]). The verse is the sword verse of the arming, used for Agamemnon (2.45), for Paris (3.334) and, later, for Achilles himself (19.372). See [[patroclus]].
