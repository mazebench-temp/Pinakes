---
summary: When Hector lops off his spear point, “Telamonian Ajax” brandishes the docked spear to no purpose.
---

“Hector came up close and struck Ajax's ash spear / with his great sword, behind the point, at the socket, / and lopped it clean off; and Telamonian Ajax / brandished the docked spear in his hand to no purpose” (16.114–117; [[ash-spear]], [[hector]], [[ajax]]). The book opens with Ajax giving way at last at the ship of Protesilaus (16.101–111), and his formula marks the end of his long defense. See [[thunders-on-high]].
