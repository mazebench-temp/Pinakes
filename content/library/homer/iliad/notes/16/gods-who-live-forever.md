---
summary: Achilles warns Patroclus not to lead on toward Ilios, “lest one of the everlasting gods from Olympus / come in against you.”
---

“And do not, exulting in war and combat, / and killing the Trojans, lead the way on toward Ilios, / lest one of the everlasting gods from Olympus / come in against you; far-working Apollo loves them dearly” (16.91–94; [[far-worker]], [[apollo]], [[olympus]]). The warning names the god who will do it: Apollo drives Patroclus back from the wall and strikes him (16.698–711, 16.788–804). See [[achilles]] and [[patroclus]].
