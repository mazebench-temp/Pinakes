---
summary: Patroclus brings down nine Trojans and Lycians, “all of them, one after another, … to the bountiful earth.”
---

“Then Erymas and Amphoterus and Epaltes, / Tlepolemus, son of Damastor, and Echius and Pyris, / Ipheus and Euippus and Polymelus, son of Argeas, / all of them, one after another, he brought down to the bountiful earth” (16.415–418; [[erymas-killed-by-patroclus]], [[the-aristeia]]). The earth that feeds many receives the nine at the end of a list without a single wound. See [[death-in-battle]].
