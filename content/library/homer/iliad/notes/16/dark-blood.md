---
summary: Apollo dries “the black blood” of Glaucus' wound, and Zeus sends him to cleanse “the dark blood” from Sarpedon's body; the book adds κελαινεφὲς αἷμα.
grc: ["κελαινεφὲς αἷμα"]
---

**Glaucus.** In answer to his prayer, “At once he stopped the pains, and from the grievous wound / he dried the black blood, and put might into his heart” (16.528–529; [[far-shooter]], [[glaucus]], [[healing]]).

**Sarpedon.** “Come now, dear Phoebus, go and cleanse the dark blood / from Sarpedon, out of range of the missiles” (16.667–668; [[cloud-gatherer]], [[phoebus]], [[sarpedon]]). The phrase is κελαινεφὲς αἷμα, with the adjective of the storm cloud that is Zeus' own epithet, and the line is excepted from [[lord-of-dark-clouds]]. Both the living and the dead are cleansed of blood by Apollo in the book. The phrase κελαινεφὲς αἷμα, with the adjective first, is new to the entry.
