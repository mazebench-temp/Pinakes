---
summary: Thetis is the mother in Achilles' life as Patroclus and the Myrmidons see it: the source of any oracle he may be shunning (16.36–37, 16.50–51), the one whom Patroclus disowns for him, “nor Thetis your mother” (16.34), and the mother who, the Myrmidons said, “reared you on anger” (16.203). Achilles' first image is of another mother, the one a little girl runs beside, weeping to be picked up (16.7–10; see [[like-a-girl]]).
