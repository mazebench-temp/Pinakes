---
summary: Patroclus asks whether Achilles is shunning an oracle his mother told him from Zeus, and Achilles says he heeds none (16.36–37, 16.50–51; see [[theopropion]]). At the end of the book the dying Patroclus prophesies Hector's death, and Hector answers, “why do you prophesy sheer destruction for me?” (16.859; see [[prophecy]]).
