---
summary: The catalogue of the Myrmidons begins with the fifty ships in which Achilles, “dear to Zeus,” led his men to Troy.
---

“Fifty were the swift ships in which Achilles, / dear to Zeus, led his men to Troy; and in each / were fifty men, companions, at the rowing benches” (16.168–170; [[swift-ships]], [[myrmidons]], [[achilles]]). Διῒ φίλος opens the verse after the enjambed name. Achilles is dear to Zeus in the book in which Zeus grants one of his prayers and refuses the other (16.249–252; [[counselor]]). See [[zeus]].
