summary: Achilles names Menoetius by his father when he asks the weeping Patroclus for news from Phthia: “Yet they say that Menoetius, son of Actor, still lives” (16.14).
# The accusative of the phrase; actor-son-of-azeus, which claims the bare Ἄκτορος, excepts 16.14.
grc: ["Ἄκτορος υἱόν"]
---

ζώειν μὰν ἔτι φασὶ Μενοίτιον Ἄκτορος υἱόν, “Yet they say that Menoetius, son of Actor, still lives” (16.14; see [[menoetius]], [[achilles]] and [[patroclus]]). It is the phrase in which Nestor named Menoetius when he recalled the charge he gave his son (Μενοίτιος Ἄκτορος υἱός, 11.785). Achilles names both fathers by their own fathers, “Menoetius, son of Actor” and “Peleus, son of Aeacus” (16.14–15; see [[aeacus]] and [[peleus]]). The Actor whose son Echecles marries Polymele later in the book is another man (Ἀκτορίδαο, 16.189; see [[actor-father-of-echecles]] and [[echecles]]).
