summary: The Myrmidons arm “with great-hearted Patroclus,” Patroclus fights at the ship of “great-hearted Protesilaus,” Sarpedon dies like a great-hearted bull, Epeigeus is the son of “great-hearted Agacles,” Glaucus kills “great-hearted Bathycles,” and Hector sees “great-hearted Patroclus” giving ground.
---

**Patroclus.** “And those who had armed with great-hearted Patroclus / marched on” (16.257–258), with the synonym μεγαλήτωρ; and at the end, “And when Hector saw great-hearted Patroclus / giving ground, struck by the sharp bronze” (16.818–819; [[sharp]], [[hector]]).

**Others.** Patroclus' first cast falls “beside the stern of the ship of great-hearted Protesilaus” (16.286; [[protesilaus]]); Sarpedon dies like a bull killed by a lion, “tawny and great-hearted, among the shambling cattle” (16.488; [[tawny]], [[shambling]]); among the Myrmidons fall “the son of great-hearted Agacles, brilliant Epeigeus” (16.571; [[agacles]], [[epeigeus]]) and Bathycles, whom Glaucus kills, “great-hearted Bathycles, / the dear son of Chalcon” (16.594–595; [[bathycles]], [[chalcon]]).
