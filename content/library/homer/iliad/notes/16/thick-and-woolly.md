summary: Thetis filled her son's chest with tunics, cloaks “and woolly rugs”; the book adds οὔλων … ταπήτων.
grc: ["οὔλων τε ταπήτων"]
en: [woolly]
---

“filling it well with tunics / and cloaks to keep off the wind and woolly rugs” (16.223–224; [[to-keep-off-the-wind]], [[chest]], [[thetis]]). The thick wool of Nestor's cloak in Book 10 (10.134) is the wool of rugs here. The phrase οὔλων τε ταπήτων is new to the entry.
