---
summary: Sarpedon dies under Patroclus like a bull, “tawny and great-hearted,” killed by a lion.
---

“As a lion comes upon a herd and kills a bull, / tawny and great-hearted, among the shambling cattle, / and it dies groaning under the lion's jaws, / so under Patroclus the leader of the shield-bearing Lycians / raged as he was killed” (16.487–491; [[great-hearted]], [[shambling]], [[lion]], [[cattle]], [[sarpedon]]). αἴθων, the color of fire, marks the bull that stands out from the herd, as Sarpedon among the Lycians. See [[the-death-of-sarpedon]].
