summary: Hermes sees Polymele among the girls “in the dance of Artemis of the golden distaff, the clamorous.”

{{quote:69b15dc0-ec58-5048-8c15-2d353e7193a9}}

Polymele, the mother of Eudorus, was seen by Hermes among the girls singing and dancing in the chorus of Artemis; he went up to her room at once and lay with her in secret (16.184; [[polymele]], [[eudorus]], [[akaketa]], [[argeiphontes]], [[hermes]]). The two epithets of the goddess fill the verse after her name, as they will again in the battle of the gods (20.70; [[clamorous]]). See [[artemis]].
