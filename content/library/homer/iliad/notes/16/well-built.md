---
summary: Apollo stands “on the well-built tower” of Troy to drive Patroclus back; the book adds ἐϋδμήτου.
grc: [ἐϋδμήτου]
---

“had not Phoebus Apollo stood on the well-built tower, / intending his destruction, and helping the Trojans” (16.700–701; [[phoebus]], [[deadly]], [[high-gated]]). Poseidon says that he and Apollo built the wall of Troy for Laomedon (7.452–453), and the god stands on his own work. The genitive ἐϋδμήτου is new to the entry. See [[the-walls-of-troy]].
