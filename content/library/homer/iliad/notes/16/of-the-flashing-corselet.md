---
summary: The first rank of the Myrmidons is led by “Menesthius of the flashing corselet, / son of Spercheius.”
---

“One rank was led by Menesthius of the flashing corselet, / son of Spercheius, the river fallen from Zeus” (16.173–174; [[menesthius-son-of-spercheius]], [[spercheius]], [[fallen-from-zeus]]). The epithet comes at the end of the verse, after the name, as it did of Antiphus, son of Priam, in Book 4 (4.489). See [[myrmidons]] and [[corselet]].
