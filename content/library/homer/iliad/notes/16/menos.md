---
summary: Might is given and taken. Achilles and Patroclus urge on “the might and spirit of every man” (16.210, 16.275); Apollo puts might into Glaucus' heart (16.529); Meriones tells Aeneas that he cannot quench the might of all men (16.621); and Ajax, son of Oileus, “loosed his strength”, the might, of Cleobulus with a sword stroke (16.332; see [[loosened-his-limbs]]).
