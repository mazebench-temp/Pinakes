---
summary: Achilles pours wine to Zeus from a cup kept for no other god before he prays for Patroclus, “standing in the middle of the enclosure, and poured out the wine, / looking up to the sky” (16.231–232).
grc: [σπένδεσκε, σπείσας]
en: [pour libations, poured the libation]
---

The cup is reserved: no other man drank from it, “nor did he pour libations from it to any god, except father Zeus” (16.226–227; see [[cup]]). Before the libation it is cleansed with sulfur and washed, and Achilles washes his own hands, as Hector would not pour to Zeus with unwashed hands (16.228–230; 6.266–268; see [[hand-washing]] and [[purification]]). The libation goes with the prayer, in one sentence: “Now when he had poured the libation and prayed to father Zeus” (σπείσας τε καὶ εὐξάμενος, 16.253; see [[prayer]]).
