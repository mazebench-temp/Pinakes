---
summary: Sarpedon dies like a bull killed by a lion “among the shambling cattle.”
---

“As a lion comes upon a herd and kills a bull, / tawny and great-hearted, among the shambling cattle” (16.487–488; [[tawny]], [[great-hearted]], [[cattle]], [[lion]]). The cattle of the herd keep their standing epithet in the simile of Sarpedon's death; he is the bull that stands out from them. See [[sarpedon]].
