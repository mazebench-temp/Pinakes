---
summary: Apollo warns Patroclus that it is not his portion that “the city of the proud Trojans be sacked by your spear.”
---

“Draw back, Patroclus, sprung from Zeus! It is not your portion / that the city of the proud Trojans be sacked by your spear, / nor by Achilles, who is far better than you” (16.707–709; [[sprung-from-zeus]], [[aisa]], [[sack-of-troy-foretold]]). The verse-end Τρώων ἀγερώχων, said of the Trojans by their enemies and by the narrator (3.36, 5.623, 7.343), is spoken here by the god who protects the city. See [[apollo]] and [[trojans]].
