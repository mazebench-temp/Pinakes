---
summary: Aeneas' “long spear” misses Meriones and sticks in the ground behind him.
---

“But he, looking straight at it, avoided the bronze spear; / for he bent forward, and the long spear behind him / stuck in the ground, and the butt quivered / of the spear” (16.610–613; [[meriones]], [[aeneas]], [[obrimos]]). The adjective here is μακρός, as with Aeneas' spear in Book 5 (5.297), not the δολιχός of the plural formula; the translation renders both “long.” See [[spear]].
