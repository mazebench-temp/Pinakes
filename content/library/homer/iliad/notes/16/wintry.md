---
summary: Achilles prays to Zeus, “ruling over wintry Dodona.”
---

“Lord Zeus, of Dodona, Pelasgian, who dwell far away, / ruling over wintry Dodona, and around you the Selli / dwell, your interpreters, with unwashed feet, sleeping on the ground” (16.233–235; [[dodona]], [[pelasgians]], [[selli]], [[with-unwashed-feet]]). The epithet of the place is the one the Catalogue gave it (2.750). Achilles calls on the god of a far, cold sanctuary, “who dwell far away” (16.233), from the camp at Troy. See [[zeus]].
