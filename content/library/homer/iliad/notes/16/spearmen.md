---
summary: Sarpedon bids Glaucus be “a spearman and a bold warrior,” and the sons of Amisodarus are “the spearmen sons of Amisodarus”; the book adds ἀκοντισταί.
grc: ["υἷες ἀκοντισταὶ"]
---

“now you must indeed / be a spearman and a bold warrior” (16.492–493; [[my-dear]], [[glaucus]]), Sarpedon's charge to Glaucus, who cannot hold his spear for his wound (16.517–520). And Atymnius and Maris, killed by Antilochus and Thrasymedes, were “the spearmen sons of Amisodarus, who reared the Chimaera” (16.328; [[amisodarus]], [[atymnius-son-of-amisodarus]], [[maris]], [[chimaera]]). ἀκοντισταί, from ἄκων, “javelin,” are javelin-men, a different word from αἰχμηταί; the translation renders both “spearmen,” and the phrase υἷες ἀκοντισταί is new to the entry.
