{{quote:1e45a55e-35ad-52ce-b783-766c1a0c1c45}}

Patroclus puts on everything else of Achilles' armor, the greaves, the starry corselet, the sword, the shield and the helmet, and takes two strong spears that fitted his grip (16.131–139; see [[arming-scene]] and [[patroclus]]). The spear stays behind because no other Achaean could wield it (see [[the-pelian-ash]], [[ash-spear]] and [[pelion]]). Chiron gave it to Peleus, and Achilles inherited it, as he inherited his immortal horses and the armor that the gods gave to Peleus at his wedding (16.381, 16.867; 17.194–197, 18.84–85; see [[peleus]] and [[achilles]]). The same verses describe the spear when Achilles at last takes it up himself (19.388–391), and with it he kills Hector (22.133, 22.326; see [[hector]]).
