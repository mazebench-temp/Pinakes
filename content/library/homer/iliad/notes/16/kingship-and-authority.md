summary: Achilles rules the Myrmidons through five leaders, “and he himself, with great power, was lord over all” (16.171–172). Glaucus praises Sarpedon as a king who “guarded Lycia with his judgments and his strength” (16.542), and the simile of the storm sets Zeus' anger against men who judge crookedly in the assembly (16.386–388; see [[themis]]).
