---
summary: After the fire is put out, the Trojans are not yet fleeing “before the Achaeans, dear to Ares.”
---

“for the Trojans were not yet fleeing before the Achaeans, dear to Ares, / in headlong rout away from the black ships, / but they still stood against them” (16.303–305; [[black-ships]], [[achaeans]]). The phrase ἀρηϊφίλων ὑπʼ Ἀχαιῶν belongs to the verse in which the Trojans would have fled into Ilios before the Achaeans, “dear to Ares” (6.73); here the rout is still to come. See [[ares]].
