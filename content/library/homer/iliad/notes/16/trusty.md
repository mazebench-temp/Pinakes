---
summary: Hector, knowing the battle has turned, “stood his ground, and saved his trusty companions.”
---

“but even so he stood his ground, and saved his trusty companions” (16.363; [[that-turns-the-battle]], [[hector]], [[hetairos]]). The accusative ἐρίηρας ἑταίρους is said here of the Trojans whom Hector protects in the rout, before his own horses carry him out of the battle (16.367–368).
