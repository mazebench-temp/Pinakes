---
summary: Achilles hopes the Danaans will “send back to me the beautiful girl / and give splendid gifts besides.”
---

“so that you may win me great honor and glory / from all the Danaans, and they send back to me the beautiful girl / and give splendid gifts besides” (16.84–86; [[briseis]], [[splendid]], [[time]], [[kydos]]). The girl and the gifts had been offered to Achilles in Book 9, and he refused them (9.121–157, 9.378–387). See [[achilles]].
