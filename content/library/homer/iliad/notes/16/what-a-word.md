summary: Two protests. Achilles answers Patroclus' request with “Ah, Patroclus, sprung from Zeus, what a thing you have said!” (16.49), and Hera answers Zeus' wish to save Sarpedon with the line of protest she speaks to him six times in the Iliad, “Most dread son of Cronus, what a thing you have said!” (16.440; 1.552, 4.25, 8.462, 14.330, 18.361).
