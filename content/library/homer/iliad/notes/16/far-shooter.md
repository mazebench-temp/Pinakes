---
summary: The wounded Glaucus prays “to far-shooting Apollo,” and the god heals him at once.
---

“And he prayed and spoke to far-shooting Apollo: / ‘Hear me, lord, who are somewhere in the rich land of Lycia / or in Troy’” (16.513–515; [[glaucus]], [[prayer]], [[hear-me]], [[lycia]]). The archer god is asked to heal the wound that Teucer's arrow made (16.510–512; [[teucer]]), and “Phoebus Apollo heard him”: he stopped the pains, dried the black blood and put might into his heart (16.527–529; [[phoebus]], [[dark-blood]], [[healing]]). See [[apollo]].
