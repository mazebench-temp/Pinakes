---
summary: Achilles twice names “powerful Agamemnon”: the king who took the girl, and the king who might have been kind to him.
---

“powerful Agamemnon took her back out of my hands” (16.58; [[son-of-atreus]], [[briseis]]), and “soon they would flee and fill the gullies / with their dead, if powerful Agamemnon / were kind to me” (16.71–73). Patroclus adds the fuller title, “wide-ruling Agamemnon,” when he wants the king to learn his folly (16.273; [[wide-ruling]]). See [[agamemnon]] and [[achilles]].
