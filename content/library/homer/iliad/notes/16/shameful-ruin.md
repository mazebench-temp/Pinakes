summary: Patroclus asks what good Achilles' prowess will be to anyone “if you do not ward off shameful ruin from the Argives” (16.32), and Achilles answers that Diomedes' spear no longer rages “to ward off ruin from the Danaans” (16.75); he sends Patroclus instead, “to ward off ruin from the ships” (16.80; see [[loigos]]).
