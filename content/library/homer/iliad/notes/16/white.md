---
summary: Idomeneus' spear passes through Erymas' head “and split the white bones”; the book adds ὀστέα λευκά.
grc: ["ὀστέα λευκά"]
---

“And Idomeneus stabbed Erymas in the mouth with the pitiless bronze; / and the bronze spear passed straight through / below the brain, and split the white bones” (16.345–347; [[pitiless-bronze]], [[idomeneus]], [[erymas-killed-by-idomeneus]]). The white bones are the living skull here; later the phrase will name the bones gathered from the pyres of Patroclus and Hector (23.252, 24.793). The phrase ὀστέα λευκά is new to the entry.
