---
summary: Patroclus' first spear kills Pyraechmes, leader of the Paeonians, and his men flee: “for Patroclus sent rout among them all / when he killed their leader, who was always the best in the fighting” (16.291–292).
refs: [16.290-291]
---

In the Catalogue the Paeonians are ἀγκυλότοξοι, “with their curved bows” (2.848); here they are Παίονας ἱπποκορυστάς, “the Paeonians who fight from chariots” (16.287), as again when Achilles turns on them after killing Asteropaeus (21.205; see [[pyraechmes]]). When their leader falls on his back in the dust, “his companions around him fled in rout, / the Paeonians” (16.290–291), and Patroclus drives the Trojans from the ships and puts out the fire (16.293). They are the first of the Trojan army to break before him.
