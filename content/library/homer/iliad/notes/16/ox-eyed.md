---
summary: “Ox-eyed queen Hera” answers Zeus when he thinks of saving Sarpedon.
---

“Then ox-eyed queen Hera answered him: / ‘Most dread son of Cronus, what a thing you have said!’” (16.439–440; [[queenly]], [[most-dread]], [[hera]]). The verse introduced her answers to Zeus in the quarrels of Book 1 and Book 4 (1.551, 4.50), and here too it opens a protest. See [[zeus]] and [[sarpedon]].
