---
summary: Patroclus' first spear kills Pyraechmes, who had led “the Paeonians who fight from chariots”; the book adds the accusative ἱπποκορυστάς.
grc: [ἱπποκορυστὰς]
---

“and he hit Pyraechmes, who had led the Paeonians who fight from chariots / from Amydon, from the broad-flowing Axius” (16.287–288; [[pyraechmes]], [[paeonians]], [[amydon]], [[axius]]). Patroclus' first cast, thrown into the throng at Protesilaus' ship, kills their leader, and the Paeonians flee (16.284–292). The epithet will return for them in Book 21, when Achilles goes among them (21.205). The accusative ἱπποκορυστάς is new to the entry.
