---
summary: Cleobulus' eyes are seized by “purple death and mighty fate,” and the dying Patroclus tells Hector that “death and mighty fate stand close beside you.”
---

“and his eyes / were seized by purple death and mighty fate” (16.333–334; [[purple-death]], [[cleobulus]], [[ajax-son-of-oileus]]), the verse of Hypsenor's death in Book 5 (5.83). And Patroclus to Hector: “you yourself will not live long, but already / death and mighty fate stand close beside you, / to be brought down at the hands of Achilles, noble grandson of Aeacus” (16.852–854; [[death-of-achilles-foretold]], [[moira]], [[hector]]). The second use is a prophecy, and the verse will be Thetis' to Achilles (24.132).
