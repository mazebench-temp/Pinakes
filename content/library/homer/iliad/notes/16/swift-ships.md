---
summary: The Trojans throw fire “on the swift ship”; Achilles led fifty “swift ships” to Troy; the Myrmidons threatened the Trojans “beside the swift ships,” Achilles prays that Patroclus come back to them, and Glaucus recalls the Danaans killed beside them.
---

**The fire.** “And they threw tireless fire / on the swift ship, and at once an unquenchable flame poured over it” (16.122–123; [[tireless]], [[protesilaus]]).

**Achilles' ships.** “Fifty were the swift ships in which Achilles, / dear to Zeus, led his men to Troy” (16.168–169; [[dear-to-zeus]], [[myrmidons]]); the Myrmidons used to threaten the Trojans “beside the swift ships” while Achilles kept them idle (16.201); and Achilles prays that Patroclus “come back to me unharmed to the swift ships” (16.247; [[hand-to-hand-fighters]]).

**The Trojan side.** Glaucus warns Hector that the Myrmidons will defile Sarpedon's body, angry for all the Danaans “whom we killed with our spears beside the swift ships” (16.546–547; [[glaucus]]). See [[ships]].
