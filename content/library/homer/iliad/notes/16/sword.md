---
summary: Swords cut where spears fail. Hector shears off Ajax's spear-point with his great sword (16.114–117); Ajax son of Oileus kills Cleobulus with his hilted sword, “and the whole sword grew warm with blood” (16.332–333); Lycon's sword shatters on Peneleos' helmet while Peneleos' sinks into Lycon's neck (16.337–341); and Automedon cuts the dead trace horse loose with his sword (16.473–474).
---

Patroclus himself slings on Achilles' “silver-studded sword / of bronze” (16.135–136; see [[arming-scene]]), but the killings the book describes are done with his spear and with stones (see [[androktasia]]).
