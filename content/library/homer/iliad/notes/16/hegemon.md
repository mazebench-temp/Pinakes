summary: Achilles orders the Myrmidons under five leaders, whom he trusted to give the orders (16.171–172), and sets them all in order “with their leaders” (16.198). At the ships the Achaean leaders kill each his man (16.307, 16.351; see [[androktasia]]); Pyraechmes, the Paeonians' leader, falls first (16.292); and Sarpedon and Glaucus are “leader of the shield-bearing Lycians” (16.490, 16.541, 16.593), the dying Sarpedon charging Glaucus to rouse the Lycian leaders (16.495, 16.532).
