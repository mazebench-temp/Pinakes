---
summary: Laogonus dies under Meriones' spear: “quickly his spirit / went from his limbs, and hateful darkness seized him.”
---

“Him he struck under the jaw and the ear; and quickly his spirit / went from his limbs, and hateful darkness seized him” (16.606–607; [[laogonus]], [[meriones]], [[darkness-covered-his-eyes]]). The two verses are those of Euchenor's death at Paris' hands (13.671–672). See [[death-in-battle]].
