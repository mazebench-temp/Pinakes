---
summary: Over Sarpedon's body the men stab at one another “with swords and double-pointed spears.”
---

“so the thudding rose from them, up from the wide-wayed earth, / of bronze and hide and well-made shields of oxhide, / as they stabbed at one another with swords and double-pointed spears” (16.635–637; [[wide-wayed-earth]], [[well-made]]). The verse-end is the one of the close fighting in Books 13 and 15 (13.147, 15.278). See [[spear]] and [[fighting-over-the-body]].
