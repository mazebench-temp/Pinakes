---
summary: Patroclus tells Achilles that the sea and “the lofty rocks” must have borne him; the book adds the plural ἠλίβατοι.
grc: [ἠλίβατοι]
---

“but the gray-green sea bore you, / and the lofty rocks, since your mind is harsh” (16.34–35; [[gray-green-sea]], [[pitiless]]). The rocks of the similes of Book 15 (15.273, 15.619) are here the parents of a hard man. The plural ἠλίβατοι is new to the entry. See [[achilles]] and [[patroclus]].
