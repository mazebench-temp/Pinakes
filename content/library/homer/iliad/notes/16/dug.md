---
summary: The Trojans in flight are held back against their will by “the ditch that had been dug.”
---

“and he left the army / of the Trojans, whom the ditch that had been dug held back against their will” (16.368–369; [[the-achaean-wall]], [[hector]]). Here the subject is the ditch itself, in the nominative, and the translation renders it “the ditch that had been dug” (see its row on τάφρος ὀρυκτή). The Achaeans' work traps the Trojans, as it trapped the Achaeans in Book 15 (15.344), and many chariots break in it (16.370–371; [[chariot-drawing]]).
