---
summary: Achilles opens the chest that Thetis put on his ship, “fine and intricately worked”; the book adds δαιδαλέης.
grc: [δαιδαλέης]
---

“But Achilles / went into his hut, and opened the lid of a chest, / fine and intricately worked, which silver-footed Thetis / had put on his ship for him to take” (16.220–223; [[chest]], [[silver-footed]], [[to-keep-off-the-wind]]). The genitive δαιδαλέης is new to the entry. In the chest is the cup from which only Zeus receives libations (16.225–227; [[father-zeus]]).
