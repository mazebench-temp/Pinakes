summary: Hera answers Zeus' wish to save Sarpedon with the verse of Book 4, “Do it; but not all of us other gods approve” (16.443 = 4.29), and adds the reason: every god with a son at Troy will want to save him too. Zeus yields in the verse with which he yielded to her in Book 4, “the father of men and gods did not disobey” (16.458 = 4.68).
passages:
  - 16.439-458 | Hera's answer
---

In Book 4 the gods' approval was asked for a peace, here for a man's life. Hera's speech goes on from the warning to a counsel: let Sarpedon die, and send him home for burial (16.444–457; see [[lay-it-to-heart]] and [[the-prize-of-the-dead]]). Athena will use the verse once more, of Hector (22.181).
