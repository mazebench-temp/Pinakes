---
summary: The Myrmidons used to tell Achilles, “let us at least go back home again with our seafaring ships.”
---

“‘let us at least go back home again with our seafaring ships, / since such evil anger has fallen on your heart’” (16.205–206; [[hard]], [[pitiless]], [[cholos]], [[nostos]]). The seafaring ships are the ships of return, and the Myrmidons' threat to sail home is the one Achilles made in Book 9 (9.356–363). See [[myrmidons]] and [[ships]].
