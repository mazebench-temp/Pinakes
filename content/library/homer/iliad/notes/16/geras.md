---
summary: Achilles names the wrong once more, the prize the Achaeans gave him and Agamemnon took back (16.53–59), and Hera names the honors of burial “the prize of the dead” (16.457 = 16.675).
---

The girl was a γέρας, given by the army (ἔξελον υἷες Ἀχαιῶν, 16.56; see [[division-of-spoils]]) and won by Achilles' spear when he sacked a city (16.57; see [[raiding]]); to take it back was to rob an equal “because he is greater in power” (16.53–54; see [[kratos]]). The book ends its use of the word with the share of the dead, a tomb and a pillar for Sarpedon (see [[the-prize-of-the-dead]]).
