---
summary: When Hector lops off the point of his spear, “Telamonian Ajax / brandished the docked spear in his hand to no purpose.”
---

“and lopped it clean off; and Telamonian Ajax / brandished the docked spear in his hand to no purpose, and far away from him / the bronze point fell to the ground with a clang” (16.116–118; [[telamonian]], [[ash-spear]], [[ajax]], [[hector]]). The headless shaft is the sign by which Ajax knows that Zeus has turned against him (16.119–121; [[thunders-on-high]]), and he gives way, and the fire falls on the ship. See [[spear]].
