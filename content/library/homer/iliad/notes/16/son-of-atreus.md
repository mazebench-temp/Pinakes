---
summary: Achilles names Agamemnon, “the son of Atreus,” as the man who took his prize and whose voice he no longer hears, and Patroclus wants “the son of Atreus, wide-ruling Agamemnon,” to learn his folly.
---

Achilles' grievance: “powerful Agamemnon took her back out of my hands, / the son of Atreus, as if I were some dishonored vagrant” (16.58–59; [[powerful]], [[briseis]]). The patronymic opens the verse after the name, as a second naming. He hears no longer “the voice of the son of Atreus shouting / from his hated head” (16.76–77). And Patroclus to the Myrmidons: “so that the son of Atreus, wide-ruling Agamemnon, may learn / his own blind folly, in that he paid no honor to the best of the Achaeans” (16.273–274; [[wide-ruling]], [[ate]], [[best-of-the-achaeans]]).
