---
summary: The Trojans flee from the ships “with a wondrous din,” and in the simile of the winds the trees crash together “with a wondrous din.”
---

“and they fled in rout, / the Trojans, with a wondrous din; and the Danaans poured after them / among the hollow ships” (16.294–296; [[relentless]], [[hollow-ships]]); and in the forest that the East and South Winds shake, the branches dash together “with a wondrous din, and there is a crashing as they break” (16.769; [[smooth-barked]], [[long-edged]]). θεσπέσιος is the noise beyond human measure, of a rout and of a storm. See [[phobos]].
