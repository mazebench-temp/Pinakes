---
summary: The horsehair-crested helmet had never been stained with dust while it guarded “the head and the graceful brow of a divine man, / Achilles.”
---

“before that it had not been lawful / for the horsehair-crested helmet to be stained with dust, / but it guarded the head and the graceful brow of a divine man, / Achilles; but then Zeus gave it to Hector / to wear on his head, and his destruction was near him” (16.796–800; [[horsehair-crested]], [[themis]], [[helmet]]). ἀνδρὸς θείοιο is found only here. The divine man is absent, and his helmet rolls under the horses' feet (16.793–795; [[with-a-socket-for-the-plume]]). See [[achilles]] and [[hector]].
