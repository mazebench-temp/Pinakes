---
summary: Patroclus comes and stands beside “Achilles, shepherd of the people,” in tears; the pastoral title of kings is given to Achilles for the first time.
---

“So they fought around the well-benched ship; / and Patroclus came and stood beside Achilles, shepherd of the people, / shedding hot tears like a spring of dark water” (16.1–3; [[well-benched]], [[hot-tears]], [[of-dark-water]]). The title, most often Agamemnon's, is here Achilles'; the verse ends as when Antilochus came to stand beside Menelaus (5.570). See [[achilles]] and [[patroclus]].
