---
summary: Peleus and Thetis sent the exile Epeigeus with Achilles “to Ilios of the fine horses.”
---

“and they sent him to follow Achilles, breaker of men, / to Ilios of the fine horses, to fight the Trojans” (16.575–576; [[breaker-of-men]], [[epeigeus]]). The verse begins as in Book 5, where the sons of Diocles followed the Argives “to Ilios of the fine horses” and died there (5.550–551). Epeigeus too dies at Troy, struck by Hector's stone (16.577–580). See [[troy]].
