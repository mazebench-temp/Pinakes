summary: Missiles press Ajax at the ship and fly around the bodies of Sarpedon and Cebriones, from which the dead are carried “out of range of the missiles” (16.102–122, 16.639, 16.668, 16.678, 16.781). The one arrow named is Teucer's, still troubling Glaucus' arm (16.511).
# βέλος of a thrown spear or stone, not an arrow: Patroclus' spear (16.480, as 5.18), his stone (16.737), Euphorbus' spear (16.812).
except: [16.480, 16.737, 16.812]
---

Over Cebriones “feathered arrows leaping from the bowstrings” join the spears and stones (16.773; see [[bow]]), and Eurypylus, among the wounded chiefs, “has been shot in the thigh with an arrow” (16.27; see [[the-wounded-chiefs]]).
