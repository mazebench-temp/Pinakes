---
summary: When Hector lops off the point of Ajax's spear, Ajax knows the work of the gods: “Zeus who thunders on high” is cutting short his plans and wishes victory for the Trojans.
---

“And Ajax knew in his noble heart, and shuddered, / the work of the gods, how he was utterly cutting short the plans of his battle, / Zeus who thunders on high, and wished victory for the Trojans; / and he drew back out of range of the missiles” (16.119–122; [[ajax]], [[blameless]], [[hector]], [[ash-spear]]). The epithet stands at the head of the verse, as always. The spear point falls clanging to the ground, and the fire falls on the ship that Ajax has defended through Book 15 (16.114–124; [[tireless]], [[protesilaus]]). See [[zeus]].
