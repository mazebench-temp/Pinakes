summary: The μῦθος is the commanding word: Achilles lays “a stern command” on the Myrmidons (16.199) and tells Patroclus to obey “the end of my word” (16.83); Hera protests, “what a thing you have said!” (16.440); and Patroclus tells Meriones there is “no need to heap up words, but to fight” (16.631; see [[the-outcome-of-war]]).
grc: ["μύθου τέλος"]
en: [the end of my word]
