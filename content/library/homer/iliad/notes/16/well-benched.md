---
summary: The book opens on the fight “around the well-benched ship” of Protesilaus.
---

“So they fought around the well-benched ship; / and Patroclus came and stood beside Achilles, shepherd of the people” (16.1–2; [[shepherd-of-the-people]], [[protesilaus]], [[ships]]). The first verse of Book 16 joins the last scene of Book 15, where Hector held the stern of Protesilaus' ship and called for fire (15.704–725); the epithet names the one ship over which both books are fought. See [[ships]].
