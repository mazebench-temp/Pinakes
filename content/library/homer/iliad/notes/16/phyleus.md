---
summary: Phyleus is named only in his son's patronymic, when Meges, “the son of Phyleus”, kills Amphiclus (16.313).
---

Φυλεΐδης, “the son of Phyleus” (16.313), is the only name the book gives Meges as he kills Amphiclus (see [[meges]] and [[amphiclus]]). The patronymic stands for the son, as it did when Dolops struck the shield of “the son of Phyleus” in Book 15 and the corselet Phyleus had brought from Ephyra saved him (15.528–534; see [[euphetes]]).
