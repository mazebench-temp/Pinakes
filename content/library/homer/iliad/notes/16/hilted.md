---
summary: Ajax, son of Oileus, takes Cleobulus alive and kills him with a blow of his “hilted sword”; the book adds κωπήεντι.
grc: [κωπήεντι]
en: [hilted]
---

“And Ajax, son of Oileus, sprang at Cleobulus / and took him alive, caught in the turmoil; but there and then / he loosed his strength, striking him on the neck with his hilted sword, / and the whole sword grew warm with blood” (16.330–333; [[ajax-son-of-oileus]], [[cleobulus]], [[sword]], [[purple-death]]). The verse-end ξίφει κωπήεντι is new to the entry, and it will return for Achilles (20.475). The man taken alive is killed at once.
