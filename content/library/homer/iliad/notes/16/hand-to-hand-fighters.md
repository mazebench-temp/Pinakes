---
summary: Achilles prays that Patroclus come back with “his companions who fight hand to hand,” and Patroclus calls Achilles and “his attendants who fight hand to hand” the best of the Argives; the book adds ἀγχέμαχος.
grc: [ἀγχεμάχοις, ἀγχέμαχοι]
---

“then let him come back to me unharmed to the swift ships, / with all his armor and his companions who fight hand to hand” (16.247–248; [[prayer]], [[swift-ships]]), Achilles prays, and Zeus refuses the return (16.252). Patroclus tells the Myrmidons to honor “the son of Peleus, who is by far the best / of the Argives beside the ships, he and his attendants who fight hand to hand” (16.271–272; [[son-of-peleus]], [[best-of-the-achaeans]]). The adjective ἀγχέμαχος belongs to the same family as ἀγχιμαχηταί, and the forms ἀγχεμάχοις and ἀγχέμαχοι are new to the entry. See [[myrmidons]].
