---
summary: “Hector, son of Priam,” kills Patroclus.
---

“so, though the valiant son of Menoetius had killed many, / Hector, son of Priam, robbed him of his life with the spear at close range” (16.827–828; [[son-of-menoetius]], [[hector]]). The patronymic stands beside Patroclus' own at the moment of the kill, as the lion of the simile beside the boar (16.823–826). See [[priam]].
