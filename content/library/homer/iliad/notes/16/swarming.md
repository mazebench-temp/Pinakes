---
summary: Patroclus' spear strikes Sarpedon “where the midriff closes around the throbbing heart”; the book adds ἁδινὸν κῆρ.
grc: ["ἁδινὸν κῆρ"]
en: [throbbing]
---

“and the shaft did not fly from his hand in vain, / but hit him where the midriff closes around the throbbing heart” (16.480–481; [[sarpedon]], [[phrenes]]). ἁδινός, “thick, close,” of swarming bees and of loud groaning, is said here of the heart that beats fast; the translation renders it “throbbing.” The phrase ἁδινὸν κῆρ is new to the entry. See [[the-death-of-sarpedon]].
