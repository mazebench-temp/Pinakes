---
summary: Patroclus' tears are like a spring “that pours its murky water down a sheer rock.”
---

“shedding hot tears like a spring of dark water / that pours its murky water down a sheer rock” (16.3–4; [[hot-tears]], [[of-dark-water]], [[dnopheros]]). The verse is the second half of the simile of Agamemnon's tears in Book 9 (9.15). The rock is sheer, and the water runs straight down it, as the tears down Patroclus' face. See [[patroclus]].
