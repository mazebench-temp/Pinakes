---
summary: Patroclus, the narrator says, would have escaped “the evil doom of black death” if he had kept Achilles' word.
---

“and was greatly blinded, / the fool; if he had kept the word of the son of Peleus, / he would have escaped the evil doom of black death” (16.685–687; [[ate]], [[nepios]], [[ker-doom]]). The genitive μέλανος θανάτοιο closes the verse, as in the Catalogue and in Book 11, where “the fates of black death were leading” the sons of Merops (2.834, 11.332); here κῆρ is singular, Patroclus' own doom. See [[patroclus]].
