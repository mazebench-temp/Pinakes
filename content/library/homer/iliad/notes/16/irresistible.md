---
summary: The sons of Amisodarus were the sons of the man who reared “the Chimaera, / the irresistible, an evil to many men.”
---

“the spearmen sons of Amisodarus, who reared the Chimaera, / the irresistible, an evil to many men” (16.328–329; [[amisodarus]], [[chimaera]], [[spearmen]]). The Chimaera of Glaucus' story of Bellerophon (6.179–182; [[bellerophon-in-lycia]]) is named here a second time, as the monster that a Lycian lord reared. The epithet is hers in both places.
