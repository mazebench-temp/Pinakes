**The armor.** “So put my glorious armor on your shoulders” (16.64; [[exchange-of-armor]], [[achilles]]). κλυτὰ τεύχεα is the armor that Patroclus will lose; its helmet Zeus gives to Hector (16.799–800).

**Two Trojans.** “there Patroclus struck famous Thrasymelus, / who was the good attendant of lord Sarpedon” (16.463–464; [[thrasymelus]], [[brave]]), and his stone struck “Cebriones, the bastard son of famous Priam” (16.738; [[cebriones]], [[priam]]). ἀγακλειτός and ἀγακλεής, “very famous,” are new to the entry. At 16.571 Ἀγακλῆος, with a capital, is the name of Epeigeus' father, and the pattern leaves it to [[agacles]].
