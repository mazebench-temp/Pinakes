---
summary: Patroclus tells Achilles that he is no son of Peleus and Thetis: “the gray-green sea bore you, / and the lofty rocks, since your mind is harsh.”
---

{{quote:0bc02e2b-35b0-5c0b-ab9a-0ff57500fcf1}}

Patroclus denies Achilles his parents: he is too harsh to be the son of “the horseman Peleus” and of Thetis, and must have been born of the sea and the rocks ([[pitiless]], [[lofty]], [[gerenian-horseman]], [[peleus]]). The mother he is denied is herself a goddess of the sea, who sits “in the depths of the sea beside her aged father” (1.358; [[thetis]]). See [[achilles]], [[patroclus]] and [[sea]].
