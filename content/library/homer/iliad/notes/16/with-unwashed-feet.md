---
summary: Achilles prays to the Zeus of Dodona, around whom dwell the Selli, “your interpreters, with unwashed feet, sleeping on the ground.”
---

{{quote:21d392f3-75db-5c4b-87a5-77b034892276}}

The two epithets close the address to the god, after his interpreters, ὑποφῆται, have been named ([[selli]], [[dodona]], [[wintry]]). Achilles has just cleansed his cup and washed his hands for the libation (16.228–230), and he calls on a god whose servants go unwashed and sleep on the earth. The prayer asks that Patroclus win glory and come back unharmed, and Zeus grants the first and refuses the second (16.236–252; [[counselor]], [[plan-of-zeus]]). See [[zeus]].
