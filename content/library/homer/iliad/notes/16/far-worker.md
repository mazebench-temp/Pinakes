---
summary: Achilles warns Patroclus not to lead on toward Ilios: “far-working Apollo loves them dearly.”
---

“lest one of the everlasting gods from Olympus / come in against you; far-working Apollo loves them dearly” (16.93–94; [[gods-who-live-forever]], [[apollo]], [[trojans]]). Achilles names the god who will stop Patroclus: Apollo, the friend of the Trojans, drives him back three times from the wall (16.702–711) and strikes him at the end (16.788–806). In Book 5 ἑκάεργος was the god who shouted at Diomedes in the same assault on him (5.439; [[three-times-and-a-fourth]]). See [[achilles]] and [[patroclus]].
