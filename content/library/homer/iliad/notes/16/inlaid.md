---
summary: Patroclus puts on Achilles' corselet, “the inlaid corselet, starry.”
---

“second, he put on the corselet around his chest, / the inlaid corselet, starry, of the swift-footed grandson of Aeacus” (16.133–134; [[starry]], [[corselet]], [[arming-scene]]). The two epithets stand together at the head of the verse, and the corselet is named by its owner, not its wearer ([[grandson-of-aeacus]]). Apollo will loosen it from Patroclus at the end (16.804; [[son-of-zeus]]). See [[exchange-of-armor]].
