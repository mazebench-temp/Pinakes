---
summary: Achilles' word to Patroclus is the one word of the book that is not kept: “if he had kept the word of the son of Peleus, / he would have escaped the evil doom of black death” (16.686–687). Zeus listened to Achilles' word before (16.236), and Patroclus tells Meriones that abusive words will not move the Trojans (16.628).
