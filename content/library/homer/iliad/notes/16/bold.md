---
summary: Meriones kills Laogonus, “the bold son of Onetor,” the priest of Zeus of Ida.
---

“Then in turn Meriones killed a helmeted man of the Trojans, / Laogonus, the bold son of Onetor, who was the priest of Zeus / of Ida, and was honored by the people like a god” (16.603–605; [[helmeted]], [[honored-like-a-god]], [[laogonus]], [[onetor]], [[meriones]]). A priest's son dies, as Phegeus, son of Dares, priest of Hephaestus, and Hypsenor, son of Dolopion, priest of Scamander, died in Book 5 (5.9–19, 5.76–83; [[priesthood]]). Sarpedon calls Glaucus to be “a bold warrior” (16.493) with another word, θαρσαλέος.
