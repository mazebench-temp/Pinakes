{{quote:7e9121f3-9f79-51e5-8c0e-18cdb3521b3c}}

ἴθυσεν δὲ διὰ προμάχων ἴρηκι ἐοικὼς / ὠκέϊ, ὅς τʼ ἐφόβησε κολοιούς τε ψῆράς τε: “and he made straight through the champions like a hawk, / a swift one, which puts jackdaws and starlings to flight” (16.582–583). The words are those that brought Apollo down from Ida, ἴρηκι ἐοικὼς / ὠκέϊ (15.237–238), but the epithet that followed there, “dove-killer”, gives way to the hawk's quarry, κολοιοί, jackdaws, and ψῆρες, starlings, small birds of the flock, named here and once more in the Iliad (17.755; see [[jackdaws]] and [[starlings]]). ἐφόβησε, “puts to flight”, is an aorist of what a hawk always does. The tenor keeps the line of the flight: ἴθυσεν, “he made straight”, and then ὣς ἰθὺς Λυκίων Πατρόκλεες ἱπποκέλευθε / ἔσσυο καὶ Τρώων, κεχόλωσο δὲ κῆρ ἑτάροιο, “so straight at the Lycians, Patroclus, urger of horses, / you rushed, and at the Trojans, and your heart was angered for your companion” (16.584–585). The narrator turns to speak to Patroclus himself, as he does again and again in this part of the book (see [[apostrophe]] and [[urger-of-horses]]).

The point of likeness is speed and terror: one fast bird against many smaller ones, which scatter. The cause is grief. Hector has killed the Myrmidon Epeigeus with a stone as he took hold of Sarpedon's body, “and grief came upon Patroclus for his fallen companion” (16.577–581; see [[epeigeus]]). He answers in kind, with a stone, striking Sthenelaus, son of Ithaemenes, on the neck and breaking its tendons; the champions and Hector himself give ground the length of a javelin's cast (16.586–592; see [[sthenelaus]] and [[the-cast-of-a-spear]]).

The jackdaws and starlings come back in Book 17, where they are the Achaeans: they flee before Aeneas and Hector like a cloud of starlings or jackdaws that scream when they see a hawk coming, the bringer of death to small birds (17.755–759). The first half-verse of this simile returns there too, for Ajax, who makes straight through the champions like a wild boar (17.281–283). See [[hawk]].
