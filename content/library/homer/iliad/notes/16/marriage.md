summary: The catalogue of the Myrmidons tells two marriages that follow a god's love: Borus son of Perieres married Polydora “openly, after giving a boundless bride-price”, though her son was the river Spercheius' (16.175–178), and Echecles took Polymele to his house with a countless bride-price after she had borne Hermes' son (16.189–190; see [[bride-price]]). Hera is Zeus' “sister and wife” (16.432).
