# Ἑλλάδι (16.595) occurs only here in Homer.
grc: [Ἑλλάδι]
---

Χάλκωνος φίλον υἱόν, ὃς Ἑλλάδι οἰκία ναίων, “the dear son of Chalcon, who had his home in Hellas” (16.595; see [[bathycles]] and [[chalcon]]). Glaucus kills him in the fight over Sarpedon's body (16.593–599; see [[glaucus]]). He fights among the Myrmidons, as the Catalogue joins Hellas to Phthia in Achilles' kingdom: “who held Phthia and Hellas of the lovely women, / and were called Myrmidons and Hellenes and Achaeans” (2.683–684; see [[phthia]], [[myrmidons]] and [[hellenes]]). Phoenix, the leader of the fourth rank of the Myrmidons in this book (16.196), had also come from Hellas, leaving it when he fled his father (9.447, 9.478; see [[phoenix]]).
