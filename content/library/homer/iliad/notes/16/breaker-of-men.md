---
summary: Automedon is the man whom Patroclus honored most “after Achilles, breaker of men,” and Peleus and Thetis sent the exile Epeigeus “to follow Achilles, breaker of men.”
---

“And he bade Automedon yoke the horses quickly, / whom he honored most after Achilles, breaker of men, / and who was the most to be trusted to await his shout in battle” (16.145–147; [[automedon]]). And of Epeigeus: “he came as a suppliant to Peleus and to silver-footed Thetis; / and they sent him to follow Achilles, breaker of men, / to Ilios of the fine horses” (16.574–576; [[epeigeus]], [[silver-footed]], [[of-the-fine-horses]]). Both times the epithet names Achilles as the leader others follow, in a book in which he does not fight. See [[achilles]].
