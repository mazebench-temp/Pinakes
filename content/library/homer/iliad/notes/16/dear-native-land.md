---
summary: Glaucus reproaches Hector with the allies who fight “far from their friends and their native land,” and Hector mocks Patroclus for wanting to carry the Trojan women to “your dear native land.”
---

Glaucus to Hector: “Hector, now you have utterly forgotten your allies, / who for your sake, far from their friends and their native land, / are wasting away their lives” (16.538–540; [[trojan-allies]], [[glaucus]]). Hector to Patroclus: “Patroclus, I suppose you said that you would ravage our city, / and take away the day of freedom from the Trojan women, / and bring them in your ships to your dear native land, / you fool!” (16.830–833; [[trojan-women]], [[eleutheros]], [[nepios]]). One speech blames Hector for the strangers who die far from home; the other mocks the stranger who will never go home. See [[hector]] and [[patroclus]].
