summary: The heart that grieves and holds firm: Hera tells Zeus, “if he is dear to you, and your heart grieves for him” (16.450); Glaucus' heart is stirred when he cannot help Sarpedon (16.509); the Myrmidons and the wasps have “valiant hearts” (16.209, 16.264); and the Lycians flee when they see their king “harmed in the heart”, lying dead (16.660).
