---
summary: Glaucus tells Hector that “bronze Ares” has brought Sarpedon down by the spear of Patroclus.
---

“Sarpedon lies dead, the leader of the shield-bearing Lycians, / who guarded Lycia with his judgments and his strength; / bronze Ares has brought him down by the spear of Patroclus” (16.541–543; [[glaucus]], [[shield-bearing]], [[sarpedon]]). War itself is the killer, and Patroclus' spear its instrument; the formula χάλκεος Ἄρης last ended a verse in Nestor's story of Areithous, whom Lycurgus killed: “and he stripped him of the armor that bronze Ares had given him” (7.146).
