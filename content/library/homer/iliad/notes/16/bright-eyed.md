---
summary: As the fight over Sarpedon begins, “the Trojans first pushed back the bright-eyed Achaeans.”
---

“And the Trojans first pushed back the bright-eyed Achaeans; / for a man was hit who was by no means the worst among the Myrmidons” (16.569–570; [[epeigeus]], [[achaeans]]). The verse will return in the fight over Patroclus' body (17.274). Before Book 16 the plural formula was used of the Achaeans who sent Chryseis home (1.389) and as Priam and Helen spoke of them from the wall (3.190, 3.234).
