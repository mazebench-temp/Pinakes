**Book III.** To blame, take a metaphor from the worse things of the same genus: so “Iphicrates called Callias a begging priest of the Mother and not a torchbearer, and Callias said he must be uninitiated, or he would not have called him a begging priest but a torchbearer” (1405a19–21). Both have to do with a god, but one is honored and the other not (see [[iphicrates]] and [[metaphora]]). The Callias of Book I's examples is another matter (see [[callias]]).
