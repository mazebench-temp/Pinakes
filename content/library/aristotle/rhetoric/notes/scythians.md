---
summary: Praise must speak of what is honored by each audience, among Scythians, Laconians or philosophers.
heading: In the Rhetoric
---

“Consider also before whom the praise is given; for as Socrates used to say, it is not hard to praise Athenians among Athenians. One must speak of what is honored among each audience as present, as among Scythians, or Laconians, or philosophers” (1367b7–10; see [[praising-athenians-among-athenians]] and [[laconians]]).
