---
summary: With Theagenes of Megara, Aristotle's precedent for the argument from example, that a man who asks for a bodyguard is aiming at tyranny.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: [Πεισίστρατος]
---

The example argues from like to like under a common class: “For instance, that in asking for the guard Dionysius is plotting a tyranny; for Pisistratus before him asked for a guard when he was plotting, and when he got it made himself tyrant, and so did Theagenes in Megara; and all the others they know of become examples for Dionysius” (1357b30–34; see [[paradeigma]], [[dionysius-of-syracuse]] and [[theagenes-of-megara]]). The story is the one Herodotus tells (1.59).
