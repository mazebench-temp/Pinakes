**Book III.** The strung-on style is the ancient one: “[This is the setting forth of the inquiry of Herodotus of Thurii]” (1409a28). Ross brackets the words as an addition to Aristotle's text; they are the first words of the *Histories*, with “of Thurii”, the city in southern Italy where Herodotus settled, in place of the “of Halicarnassus” of our manuscripts (see [[periodos]]). The narration should also tell what bears on one's own virtue or the other's vice, as in Herodotus' story of the Egyptian deserters (1417a5–7; *Histories* 2.30): when the king urged them not to abandon their children and wives, they answered that they would get others (see [[diegesis]]).
