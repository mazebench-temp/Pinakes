**Book II.** “Before Greece”, in Antiphon's *Meleager* (1399b28; see [[witnesses-of-meleagers-valor]]); “[of Greece]”, bracketed by Ross, in the lines on Oeneus (1397b22; see [[is-not-oeneus-pitiable]]).

**Book III.** Three metaphors of chapter 10 make Greece a person: Leptines would not look on while Greece was made one-eyed (1411a5–6; see [[leptines]]); “so that Greece cried out” (1411a26–27); and at the tomb of those who died at Salamis Greece should cut her hair (1411a31–33; see [[salamis]] and [[before-the-eyes]]).
