---
summary: Leodamas, accusing Chabrias, argued that the doer is more guilty than the planner, the opposite of what he had argued against Callistratus.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: [Χαβρίου]
---

Leodamas argued, “accusing Chabrias, that the doer did more wrong than the planner, for it would not have happened had there been no one to do it” (1364a21–23; see [[leodamas]]). The quarrel may be the one Demosthenes recalls, when Leodamas challenged the grant of honors voted to Chabrias and lost (*Against Leptines* 146). Book III has his defender Lycoleon point to his bronze statue as if to a suppliant (1411b6–10).

**Book III.** See [[lycoleon]] (1411b6–10): “not even ashamed before his suppliant, the bronze statue”.
