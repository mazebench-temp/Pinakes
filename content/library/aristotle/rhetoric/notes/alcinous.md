**Book III.** The long story Odysseus tells Alcinous (*Odyssey* 9–12) “is retold to Penelope in sixty lines” (1417a14–15); in our text the retelling runs to thirty-four (23.310–343). It is usually taken as Aristotle's example of a narration that can be shortened (see [[phayllus]] and [[diegesis]]).
