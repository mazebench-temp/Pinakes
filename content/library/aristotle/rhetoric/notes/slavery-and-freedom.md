summary: Alcidamas: no one is a slave by nature; Gelon enslaved a city; the marks of a free man.
heading: In the Rhetoric
refs: [1367a29-33, 1373a23, 1373b18]
---

Three passages touch the theme. Alcidamas says that God has left all men free and nature has made no one a slave (1373b18; see [[god-has-left-all-men-free]]). Gelon enslaved a city before Aenesidemus could (1373a22–24; see [[gelon]]). And the free man is known by what he does not do: long hair in Lacedaemon shows that he does no laborer's work, and “it is the mark of a free man not to live at another's beck” (1367a32–33; see [[eleutheros]]).
