---
summary: The third proof lies in the speech itself, when it shows the truth or what seems true.
heading: In the Rhetoric
refs: [1356a3-4, 1356a19-20]
---

The third kind of artistic proof lies “in the speech itself, by showing or seeming to show something” (1356a3–4), and “through the speech people are persuaded when we show the truth or what seems true from what is persuasive in each case” (1356a19–20; see [[three-kinds-of-proof]], [[enthymema]] and [[paradeigma]]). In the *Rhetoric* λόγος is also simply a speech, and the faculty of speech that is more proper to a human being than the use of the body (1355b1–2).
