**Book III.** Rare words, double words and coined words should be used seldom and in few places (1404b28–30). Lycophron's and Alcidamas' rare words are the second source of frigidity (1406a7–10; see [[psychron]]); rare words suit the epic poets, “for they are dignified and self-assured” (1406b2–3; see [[epic]]); and they give no learning, for they are unknown to us (1410b12; see [[asteion]]).
