**Book II.** One blames from what belongs or seems to belong to the subject, “for instance, that they enslaved the Greeks, and that they reduced to slavery those who had fought with them against the barbarian and distinguished themselves, the Aeginetans and Potidaeans” (1396a17–20; see [[aegina]] and [[athenians]]).
