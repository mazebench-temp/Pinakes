**Book II.** “The Lampsacenes buried Anaxagoras, though he was a foreigner, and honor him still to this day” (1398b16–17), one of Alcidamas' examples of the honor all men pay the wise (see [[all-men-honor-the-wise]] and [[lampsacenes]]).
