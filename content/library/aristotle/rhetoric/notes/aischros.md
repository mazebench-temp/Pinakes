---
summary: The shameful is the target of blame, as the noble is of praise; αἰσχρός is also “ugly”.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: ["!αἰσχυν*"]
---

“After this, let us speak about virtue and vice, the noble and the shameful; for these are the targets of one who praises and blames” (1366a23–24; see [[kalon]] and [[three-kinds-of-rhetoric]]). People are ashamed of shameful things when they say them, do them or are about to (1367a7–8; see [[shame-prevents-me]]). The word keeps its sense of ugliness: “if the other brothers are ugly and one is handsome” (1362a8), “the poor and ugly man in cases of adultery” (1372a23).

**Book II.** Shame, αἰσχύνη, the emotion, has its own entry (see [[aischyne]]).
