**Book II.** “At Thebes, when Ismenias and Stilbon disputed, the woman of Dodona showed that he was Ismenias' son, and for this reason they held Thettaliscus to be Ismenias' son” (1398b3–5; see [[the-peparethian-woman]], [[stilbon]] and [[thettaliscus]]). Who the woman of Dodona was Aristotle does not say.
