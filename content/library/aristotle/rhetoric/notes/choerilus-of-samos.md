**Book III.** Epideictic proems may come from appeals to the hearer, “if the speech is about something paradoxical or difficult or much talked of by many, so as to win pardon, as Choerilus: ‘But now, when all has been parceled out’” (1415a2–4; see [[prooimion]]).
