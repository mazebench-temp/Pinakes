---
summary: Aristotle's model of a court where speakers are forbidden to speak outside the subject.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: ["Ἀρείῳ πάγῳ"]
---

“For everyone either thinks that the laws ought to prescribe this, or actually applies it and forbids speaking outside the subject, as on the Areopagus too, and in this they are right” (1354a21–24). The court is the example of a well-governed procedure that leaves the handbooks' appeals to anger, pity and envy no room (see [[the-handbooks]] and [[pathos]]).
