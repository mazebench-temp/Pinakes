**Book II.** Good families degenerate: the gifted into madder characters, “like the descendants of Alcibiades and of Dionysius the elder; steady ones into stupidity and sluggishness, like the descendants of Cimon, Pericles and Socrates” (1390b27–31; see [[the-gifts-of-fortune]], [[alcibiades]], [[dionysius-of-syracuse]], [[pericles]] and [[socrates]]).
