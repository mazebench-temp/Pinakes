**Book III.** In 3.6 conciseness speaks without a connective, “though not disconnected” (1407b37–1408a1; see [[onkos]]). In 3.12 asyndeta are rejected in writing but used in debate, for they must be acted (1413b19–31), and they amplify: “I came, I talked with him, I entreated” (1413b34–1414a1), as Homer amplifies Nireus (1414a2–7; see [[aristotle-nireus]]). The epilogue ends with them: “I have spoken; you have heard; you have the facts; judge” (1420a7–8; see [[epilogos]]).
