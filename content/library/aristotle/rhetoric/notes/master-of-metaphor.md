---
summary: Metaphor, unlike the other kinds of word, cannot be learned from another.
heading: In the Rhetoric
---

**Book III.** As in the *Poetics*, where a gift for metaphor “cannot be learned from another, and it is a sign of natural gifts” (*Poetics* 1459a7), the *Rhetoric* says that metaphor “cannot be borrowed from anyone else” (1405a9–10; see [[metaphora]]). Yet it adds that the method can show what makes urbane sayings, though only the gifted or the trained can make them (1410b7–9; see [[asteion]]).
