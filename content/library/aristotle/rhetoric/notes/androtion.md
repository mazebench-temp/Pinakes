**Book III.** The first of Aristotle's examples of the simile: “the one Androtion made of Idrieus, that he was like puppies let off the chain: they fly at people and bite, and Idrieus, freed from his chains, was savage” (1406b27–29; see [[idrieus]] and [[eikon]]).
