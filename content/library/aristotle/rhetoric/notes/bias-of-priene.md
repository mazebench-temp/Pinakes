**Book II.** The old “neither love nor hate strongly, but in keeping with Bias' precept they both love as though they will hate and hate as though they will love” (1389b23–25; see [[love-as-though-you-will-hate]] and [[the-characters-of-the-ages]]). In the chapter on maxims the same words, without his name, are a saying that one may correct to show a better character (1395a26–33).
