**Book III.** Style is proportionate when weighty matters are not spoken of offhandedly nor cheap ones with dignity; “otherwise it looks like comedy, as in what Cleophon does; for some things he said were as if one were to say ‘queenly fig tree’” (1408a14–16). This is the tragic poet of the *Poetics*, whose characters are like ourselves, not the demagogue (see [[cleophon-the-demagogue]] and [[prepon]]).
