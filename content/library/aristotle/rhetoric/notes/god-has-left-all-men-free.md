summary: Alcidamas' Messenian Speech, the third witness to the law of nature after Sophocles and Empedocles.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: ["ἐλευθέρους ἀφῆκε πάντας θεός"]
en: [God has left all men free]
---

The common law is “something that all divine, a justice and injustice common by nature, even where there is no association or covenant between people” (1373b7–9), and Aristotle's witnesses are Sophocles' Antigone, Empedocles and Alcidamas (1373b9–18; see [[nomos]] and [[eleutheros]]).
