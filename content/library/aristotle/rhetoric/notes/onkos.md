**Book III.** “The following contribute to the bulk of style: using a description instead of a name” (1407b26–27); and for conciseness the opposite (1407b28). The poets' plural for one harbor, “to Achaean harbors”, and “Here are the many-leaved folds of the tablet” make one into many (1407b33–35; see [[achaeans]] and [[iphigenia-among-the-taurians]]); and Antimachus' “There is a small windy hill” describes by negation, which can be amplified without limit (1408a2–4; see [[antimachus-of-colophon]]).
