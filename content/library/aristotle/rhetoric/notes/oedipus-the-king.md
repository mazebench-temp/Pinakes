**Book III.** The prologues of plays, like the proems of speeches, give a sample of the subject: tragic poets make clear what the play is about, if not at once, then somewhere, “as Sophocles does: ‘My father was Polybus’” (1415a20–21; *Oedipus the King* 774; see [[polybus-of-corinth]] and [[prooimion]]).
