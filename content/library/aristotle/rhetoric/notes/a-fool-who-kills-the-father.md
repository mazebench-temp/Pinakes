---
summary: A proverb as testimony, for a speaker who urges killing the sons of those whose fathers one has killed.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: ["νήπιος ὃς πατέρα"]
en: [A fool, who kills the father]
---

“And on killing the sons of those whose fathers one has killed: ‘A fool, who kills the father and leaves the sons’” (1376a5–7; see [[non-artistic-proofs]]).

**Book II.** In the chapter on maxims it is a hackneyed maxim for urging the destruction of an enemy's children, “though they have done no wrong” (1395a16–18; see [[gnome]]).
