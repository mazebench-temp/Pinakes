**Book II.** The topic from the more and the less, where neither is more nor less: “if not even Theseus did wrong, neither did Alexander; and if not the sons of Tyndareus, neither did Alexander” (1397b22–24). The sons of Tyndareus carried off the daughters of Leucippus, as Theseus carried off Helen (see [[polydeuces]], [[theseus]] and [[paris]]).
