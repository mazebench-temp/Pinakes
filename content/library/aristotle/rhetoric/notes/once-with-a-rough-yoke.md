---
summary: Quoted for the greatness that occasion and circumstance give a deed, and again for praise of a rise beyond what was fitting.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: ["πρόσθε μὲν ἀμφʼ ὤμοισιν"]
en: [Once, with a rough yoke on my shoulders]
---

In chapter 7 occasions, ages, places, times and powers make things great: “Hence too the epigram on the Olympic victor: ‘Once, with a rough yoke on my shoulders, I carried fish from Argos to Tegea’” (1365a24–27; see [[megethos]]). In chapter 9 the same verse is praise, beside Iphicrates' “From what to what”, for a man who has risen above what was fitting (1367b17–19; see [[from-what-to-what]]).
