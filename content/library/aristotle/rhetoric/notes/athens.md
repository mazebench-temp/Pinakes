**Book II.** At Athens Mantias disputed with his son, and the mother declared the truth (1398b1–2; see [[mantias]]).

**Book III.** Speaking to the assembly allows few digressions, “so when at a loss one should do what the orators at Athens do, and Isocrates: for even when giving advice he accuses” (1418a29–31; see [[isocrates]]).
