---
summary: People do wrong when they expect the gain to exceed the penalty.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: [κέρδη, κέρδους]
en: [gain, gains]
---

People do wrong when they think they will go unnoticed or unpunished, or that the penalty “will be less than the gain to themselves or to those they care for” (1372a8–9), and when the gain is clear, great or near and the penalty small, unclear or far off (1372a36–37; see [[adikia]]).
