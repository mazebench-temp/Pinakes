**Book II.** People are calmed if they think the other will not perceive that he is punished because of them; “for anger is against the individual, as is clear from the definition. That is why the poet has rightly written ‘say that Odysseus, sacker of cities,’ as though he would not have been avenged unless the other perceived both by whom and for what” (1380b20–25; see [[praotes]] and [[orge]]).
