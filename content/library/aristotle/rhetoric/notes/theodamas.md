**Book III.** “Theodamas likened Archidamus to Euxenus without his geometry; it is by analogy, for Euxenus will be Archidamus with geometry” (1406b30–32). The simile works by proportion, like the metaphor by analogy (see [[archidamus]], [[euxenus]] and [[eikon]]).
