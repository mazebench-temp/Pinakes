---
summary: Quoted with Hera's “a thing to boast of” for the topic that what has cost much toil is a good.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: ["αἰσχρόν τοι δηρόν τε μένειν"]
en: [It is shameful to stay so long]
---

Aristotle quotes only the first half of the line, “It is shameful to stay so long” (1363a6), for the topic that what has cost much toil or expense is good (see [[a-thing-to-boast-of-for-priam]]). The half he leaves out, going home empty-handed, is what would turn the nine years into loss.
