**Book II.** Theodectes' *Ajax*: Diomedes chose Odysseus not to honor him but so that his follower might be inferior (1399b28–31), and Odysseus tells Ajax why, though he is braver, he does not seem so (1400a28–29; see [[theodectes]] and [[topics-of-enthymemes]]).
