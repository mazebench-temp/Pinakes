**Book III.** Three things must be treated: the sources of proof, style, and “how the parts of the speech should be arranged” (1403b6–8). After style, “it remains to speak of arrangement” (1414a29–30). Chapter 13 gives the two necessary parts, statement and proof (1414a31–37; see [[pistis]]), mocks the handbooks' divisions (1414a37–b18; see [[the-handbooks]], [[theodorus-of-byzantium]] and [[licymnius-of-chios]]), and allows at most four: “proem, statement, proof and epilogue” (1414b8–9; see [[prooimion]], [[diegesis]] and [[epilogos]]).
