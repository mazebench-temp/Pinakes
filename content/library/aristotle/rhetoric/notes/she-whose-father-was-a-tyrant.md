---
summary: Praise for moderation beyond what was fitting: a tyrant's daughter who was not arrogant.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: ["ἣ πατρός τε καὶ ἀνδρὸς"]
en: [She whose father, husband]
---

The last of three examples of praise for a person who “goes toward the better and the nobler” beyond what was fitting (1367b14–21; see [[from-what-to-what]]). Aristotle quotes only the line that names the tyrants; the praise, her modesty, is in the line he leaves out.
