“Wish is longing for the good (for no one wishes for anything unless he thinks it good); irrational longings are anger and appetite” (1369a2–4; see [[seven-causes-of-action]]). “For appetite is longing for the pleasant” (1370a17–18). Some appetites are irrational, natural ones through the body, like hunger and thirst; others are rational, “those that come from being persuaded” (1370a18–27; see [[hedone]]). Lack of self-control “concerns everything people long for” (1372b13).
