summary: Few premises of enthymemes are necessary; the necessary sign is a tekmērion; the necessary is painful.
heading: In the Rhetoric
---

“Few of the premises from which rhetorical syllogisms are drawn are necessary”, for most actions can be otherwise (1357a22–25; see [[enthymema]] and [[eikos]]). “Of these, the necessary sign is a tekmērion” (1357b4; see [[tekmerion]]). And since force is contrary to nature, “the necessary is painful” (1370a9–10; see [[every-necessary-thing-is-grievous]]).
