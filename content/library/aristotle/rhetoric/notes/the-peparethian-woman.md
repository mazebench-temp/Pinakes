**Book II.** The topic from induction: “from the Peparethian woman, that about their children women everywhere determine the truth; for at Athens, when Mantias the orator disputed with his son, the mother declared the truth, and at Thebes, when Ismenias and Stilbon disputed, the woman of Dodona showed that he was Ismenias' son, and for this reason they held Thettaliscus to be Ismenias' son” (1398a33–1398b5; see [[mantias]], [[ismenias]] and [[topics-of-enthymemes]]).
