---
summary: In the Rhetoric γένος is chiefly Aristotle's “class” or “genus”, and once birth.
heading: In the Rhetoric
---

Aristotle uses γένος of a logical class. Rhetoric's art “is not concerned with any special, defined class of things” (1355b34; see [[rhetorike]]); an example works when two cases “fall under the same class” (1357b29; see [[paradeigma]]); and the special premises are “the premises special to each genus” (1358a31; see [[topos]]). Once it keeps the older sense of birth: people choose what is fitting to their birth and power (1363a29).
