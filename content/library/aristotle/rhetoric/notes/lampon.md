**Book III.** Interrogation is opportune when one more question brings out an absurdity: “as Pericles questioned Lampon about the initiation into the rites of the Savior goddess, and when Lampon said that no one uninitiated could hear of them, asked whether he knew them himself, and when he said he did, ‘And how, when you are uninitiated?’” (1419a2–5; see [[pericles]] and [[erotesis]]).
