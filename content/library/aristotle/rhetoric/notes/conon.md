**Book II.** Isocrates' praise of Evagoras rests on his judgment: “Conon at any rate, in his misfortune, passing over all the others, came to Evagoras” (1399a4–6; see [[conon-came-to-evagoras]]). Among the topics from the name: “Conon called Thrasybulus the bold in counsel” (1400b20; see [[thrasybulus]]).
