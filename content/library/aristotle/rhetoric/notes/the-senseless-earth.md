**Book II.** People are not angry with the dead, “since they have suffered the utmost and will feel no pain or perception, which is what the angry aim at. That is why the poet spoke well about Hector, when he wished to stop Achilles' anger against the dead man: ‘for in his rage he defiles the senseless earth’” (1380b26–30; see [[praotes]]).
