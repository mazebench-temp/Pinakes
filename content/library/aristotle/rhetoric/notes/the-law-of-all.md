---
summary: Empedocles on not killing living things, a law that is not just for some and unjust for others.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: ["πάντων νόμιμον"]
en: [law of all]
---

“And as Empedocles says about not killing what has life; for this is not just for some and unjust for others, ‘but the law of all stretches without a break through the wide-ruling air and through the boundless light’” (1373b13–17; see [[nomos]]).
