summary: What a worse man can do, a better man can do more: Isocrates' remark about him.
heading: In the Rhetoric
---

**Book II.** Among the topics of the possible: “if it is possible for those who are worse, weaker and less intelligent, it is more so for their opposites; as Isocrates said it would be strange if Euthynus had learned something and he himself could not discover it” (1392b10–13; see [[isocrates]] and [[koina]]).
