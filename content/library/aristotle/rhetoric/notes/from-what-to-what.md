In chapter 7 Iphicrates' self-praise shows how circumstances make a deed great: he “praised himself by saying from what beginnings he had come to this” (1365a27–28). In chapter 9 the phrase, quoted now in its proverbial form, is praise of a man who “goes toward the better and the nobler” beyond what was fitting (1367b14–18; see [[once-with-a-rough-yoke]]).
