**Book III.** “And as Homer often does, making lifeless things alive through metaphor. In all of them it is by making activity that he is well liked” (1411b31–33): six lines follow, and Aristotle explains them by the analogy of Sisyphus and his stone (1412a5–7; see [[before-the-eyes]] and [[sisyphus]]).
