**Book III.** Accused of impiety for a line of the *Hippolytus*, Euripides answered “that Hygiaenon was doing wrong in bringing the judgments of the Dionysiac contest into the courts; for he had given an account of them there” (1416a32–34): the play had been judged at the festival already (see [[hygiaenon]] and [[euripides]]).
