summary: Epimenides divined not the future but the past.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: [Ἐπιμενίδης]
---

**Book III.** Speaking to the assembly is harder than pleading in court, because it concerns the future, while the past is knowable “even to seers, as Epimenides the Cretan said (for he did not divine about what was to be, but about what had happened and was obscure)” (1418a23–26; see [[three-kinds-of-rhetoric]]).
