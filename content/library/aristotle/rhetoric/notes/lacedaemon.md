---
summary: In Lacedaemon long hair is noble, the sign of a free man who does no laborer's work.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: [Λακεδαίμονι]
---

“And what is peculiar to each people is noble, and whatever signs there are of what each people praises: in Lacedaemon, for instance, wearing long hair is noble, for it is the sign of a free man, since it is not easy for a man with long hair to do any hired laborer's work” (1367a28–32; see [[kalon]] and [[eleutheros]]).
