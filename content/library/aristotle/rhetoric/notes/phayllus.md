**Book III.** Aristotle's examples in the account of narration, “the tale to Alcinous, which is retold to Penelope in sixty lines, and the way Phayllus treated the Cycle, and the prologue in the Oeneus” (1417a14–16), are usually taken as long stories told briefly (see [[diegesis]]). What Phayllus wrote is not known.
