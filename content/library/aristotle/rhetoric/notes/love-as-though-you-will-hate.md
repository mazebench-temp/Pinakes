**Book II.** The old “both love as though they will hate and hate as though they will love” (1389b24–25; see [[the-characters-of-the-ages]]). Character appears better if one says “that one should not, as they say, love as though one will hate, but rather hate as though one will love” (1395a26–27), or adds the reason: “One should love, not as they say, but as though one will love always; for the other belongs to a schemer” (1395a29–30; see [[gnome]]).
