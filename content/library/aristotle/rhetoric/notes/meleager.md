**Book II.** In Antiphon's play Plexippus fails to notice his needs (1379b14–16; see [[plexippus]]); the heroes came to the hunt “to be witnesses to the valor of Meleager before Greece” (1399b26–28; see [[witnesses-of-meleagers-valor]] and [[meleager-antiphon]]); and in an unnamed tragedy Oeneus is pitiable for the loss of his glorious son (1397b20–22; see [[is-not-oeneus-pitiable]]).
