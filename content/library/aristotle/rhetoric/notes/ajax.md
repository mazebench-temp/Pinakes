**Book II.** Hector “avoided battle with Ajax, son of Telamon”, for Zeus was indignant whenever he fought a better man (1387a33–35; see [[aristotle-nemesis]]); in Theodectes' *Ajax* Odysseus tells him why, though braver, he does not seem so (1400a28–29; see [[ajax-plays]]).
