---
summary: Health is a part of happiness only if it lets a man use his body: many are healthy as Herodicus is said to have been, and no one would call them happy for it.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: [Ἡρόδικος]
---

“The excellence of the body is health, and health such that people are free of disease while using their bodies; for many are healthy as Herodicus is said to have been, and no one would call them happy for their health, because they abstain from all, or most, of what human beings do” (1361b3–6; see [[eudaimonia]]).
