---
summary: What is not forced is pleasant, for force is contrary to nature, and the necessary is painful.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: ["ἀναγκαῖον πρᾶγμʼ ἀνιαρὸν"]
en: [necessary thing is by nature grievous]
---

Pleasure is a settling into one's natural state, so what is not forced is pleasant, “for force is contrary to nature, which is why the necessary is painful, and it was rightly said, ‘For every necessary thing is by nature grievous’” (1370a9–11; see [[hedone]]).
