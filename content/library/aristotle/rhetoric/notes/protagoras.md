**Book II.** People were justly offended by his professed teaching, making the weaker argument the stronger, “for it is a lie, and not true but an apparent probability, and found in no art except rhetoric and eristic” (1402a23–28; see [[the-art-of-corax]]).

**Book III.** The fourth part of speaking good Greek: “as Protagoras divided the genders of nouns, male, female and things; for these too must be given correctly” (1407b6–8; see [[gender-of-nouns]] and [[hellenizein]]).
