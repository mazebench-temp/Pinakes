**Book II.** The topic from induction: “from the Peparethian woman, that about their children women everywhere determine the truth; for at Athens, when Mantias the orator disputed with his son, the mother declared the truth” (1398a33–1398b2; see [[the-peparethian-woman]] and [[topics-of-enthymemes]]).
