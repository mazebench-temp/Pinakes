**Book II.** The refutative topic from inconsistency: “He says he loves you, yet he conspired with the Thirty” (1400a17–18). Leodamas, accused by Thrasybulus of having had his name erased from a stele under the Thirty, answered that the Thirty would have trusted him more had his enmity to the people stayed inscribed (1400a32–36; see [[leodamas]] and [[thrasybulus]]). Polycrates said that Thrasybulus “overthrew thirty tyrants”, combining them into a greater number of deeds (1401a34–36; see [[polycrates]]).
