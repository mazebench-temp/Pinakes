---
summary: The exception to the topic that the opposite of what the enemy wants is good.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: ["τὰ κακὰ συνάγει"]
en: [misfortunes bring people together]
---

After “Surely Priam would rejoice”, Aristotle qualifies the rule: “This is not always so, but for the most part; for nothing prevents the same thing from sometimes being advantageous to opponents; hence it is said that misfortunes bring people together, when the same thing is harmful to both” (1362b36–38; see [[surely-priam-would-rejoice]]). He does not say whose saying it is.
