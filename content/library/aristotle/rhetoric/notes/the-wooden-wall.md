---
summary: An interpreter of oracles as a witness about the future.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: ["ξύλινον τεῖχος"]
en: [the wooden wall]
---

“Such witnesses, then, concern the past; about the future the interpreters of oracles are witnesses too, as Themistocles said that they must fight at sea, speaking of the wooden wall” (1375b35–1376a2; see [[non-artistic-proofs]]).
