---
summary: Euctemon's suicide after an outrage makes the wrong greater: the victim punished himself so severely that the doer deserved more.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: [Εὐκτήμονος]
---

A wrong is greater “if the one who suffered and was wronged punished himself severely; for the doer deserves a still greater punishment, as Sophocles, speaking for Euctemon, who had cut his own throat after suffering outrage, said he would not assess the penalty at less than the victim had assessed it for himself” (1374b34–1375a1; see [[sophocles-the-orator]] and [[hybris]]).
