---
summary: Aenesidemus congratulated Gelon with a game prize for enslaving a city first: people wrong those who would otherwise be wronged by others.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: [Αἰνεσίδημος]
---

People wrong “those who are going to be wronged by others if not by themselves, there being no longer time to deliberate, as Aenesidemus is said to have sent the cottabus prize to Gelon, who had enslaved a city, because he had got there first, since he himself had meant to” (1373a21–24; see [[gelon]]). The prize is a joke: Gelon has won the game that Aenesidemus was playing.
