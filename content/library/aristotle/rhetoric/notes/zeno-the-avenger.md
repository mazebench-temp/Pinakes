---
summary: Aristotle's example of a man for whom a wrong brought praise, because by it he avenged a father or mother.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: [Ζήνωνι]
---

People do wrong when the wrong brings them praise and the penalty falls only on money or exile, “as when it happened that one avenged at the same time a father or mother, as with Zeno” (1372b3–6). Freese notes that who Zeno was, and what the story was, is unknown.
