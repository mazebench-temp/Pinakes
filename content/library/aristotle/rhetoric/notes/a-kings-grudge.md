**Book II.** People expect regard from their inferiors, “that is why it has been said, ‘Great is the spirit of kings whom Zeus has nurtured,’ and ‘still he keeps a grudge thereafter’; for they are vexed because of their superiority” (1379a4–7; see [[aristotle-anger]] and [[orge]]).
