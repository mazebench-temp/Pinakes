**Book III.** Metaphors should be taken from things akin, so that the kinship is clear at once, “as in the well-liked riddle, ‘I saw a man who with fire had welded bronze upon a man’; for the process has no name, but both are a kind of application, and so he called the applying of the cupping glass a welding” (1405b1–3); “for metaphors are riddles” (1405b4–5; see [[metaphora]]).
