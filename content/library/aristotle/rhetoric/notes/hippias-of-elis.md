---
summary: Rhetoric studies what seems so not to Socrates or to Hippias but to kinds of people.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: [Ἱππίᾳ]
en: [Hippias]
---

“neither will rhetoric study what is reputable to the individual, Socrates or Hippias, but what is reputable to such and such people, just as dialectic does” (1356b33–35). The pairing of the two names recalls the Socratic dialogues, where they meet (see [[socrates]] and [[callias]]).
