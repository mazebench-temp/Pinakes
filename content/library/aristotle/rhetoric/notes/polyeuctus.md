**Book III.** “What Polyeuctus said of a man with a stroke, Speusippus: that he could not keep still, though fortune had bound him in a five-holed disease” (1411a21–23). The five-holed stocks held neck, hands and feet (see [[speusippus]] and [[metaphora]]).
