summary: An example from past events: Darius and Xerxes took Egypt before they crossed to Greece.

**Book II.** Telling of past events is the first kind of example: one must prepare against the king and not allow him to subdue Egypt, “for in the past Darius did not cross over before he had taken Egypt, but having taken it he crossed; and again Xerxes did not set about it before he had taken it” (1393a32–1393b3; see [[paradeigma]] and [[xerxes]]). Herodotus' history does not quite fit the argument: Egypt was conquered by Cambyses (3.1–16), and the revolt that Darius meant to put down broke out only after Marathon (7.1). It was Xerxes who reconquered Egypt before invading Greece (7.7).
