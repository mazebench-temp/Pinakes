---
summary: Temper is one of the seven causes of action, an irrational longing that seeks revenge; Homer's words on it show that anger is pleasant.
heading: In the Rhetoric
en: [temper]
---

“So all that people do they must do from seven causes: chance, nature, force, habit, reasoning, temper, appetite” (1369a5–7; see [[seven-causes-of-action]]). “By temper and anger, acts of revenge” (1369b11–12), and even anger is pleasant for its hope of revenge, as Homer wrote “of temper” (1370b10–12; see [[sweeter-than-dripping-honey]]). Some wrongs are done “through emotion”, and “temper will be discussed under the emotions” (1373b36).
