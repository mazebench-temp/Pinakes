**Book II.** His fable for the people of Himera (1393b8–22; see [[the-horse-and-the-stag]]) and his warning to the Locrians (1395a1–2; see [[the-cicadas-on-the-ground]]).

**Book III.** Urbane apophthegms “come from not meaning what is said, as Stesichorus' saying, that the cicadas will sing to themselves from the ground” (1412a22–24): a threat that the land will be laid waste and its trees cut down (see [[asteion]]).
