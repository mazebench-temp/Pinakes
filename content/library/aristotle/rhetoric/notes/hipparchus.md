**Book II.** The fallacy from a sign: “Lovers are useful to cities; for the love of Harmodius and Aristogeiton overthrew the tyrant Hipparchus” (1401b10–12; see [[apparent-enthymemes]], [[harmodius]] and [[aristogeiton]]). Aristotle calls him the tyrant, as the Athenians did; in the *Constitution of the Athenians* the ruler is Hippias (18).
