---
summary: Imitations are pleasant even when what they imitate is not, because one learns that this is that.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: ["μιμ*"]
en: [imitations, imitated]
---

“Imitations, such as painting and sculpture and poetry, and everything that is well imitated, even if the thing imitated is not itself pleasant; for one does not delight in that, but there is a syllogism, that this is that, so that one comes to learn something” (1371b6–10; see [[pleasure-of-imitation]] and [[hedone]]).
