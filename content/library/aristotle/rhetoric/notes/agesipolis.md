**Book II.** The topic from a previous judgment, against which one cannot well judge: “Hegesipolis at Delphi, after first consulting the oracle at Olympia, asked the god whether he thought the same as his father, since it would be shameful to say the opposite” (1398b33–1399a1; see [[olympia]] and [[pytho]]). Aristotle's text spells the name Ἡγησίπολις.
