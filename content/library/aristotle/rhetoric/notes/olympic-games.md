en: [Olympia, Olympic victor]
---

To show that Dorieus has won a crown game it is enough to say that he has won at Olympia, for everyone knows that the prize is a crown (1357a19–21; see [[dorieus]] and [[enthymema]]). An epigram on an Olympic victor, once a fish-carrier, shows how occasion makes a deed great (1365a25–27), and the same verse returns in chapter 9 as praise for success beyond expectation (1367b19; see [[once-with-a-rough-yoke]]).
