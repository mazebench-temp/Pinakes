---
summary: People wrong those with whom they have already quarreled, as Callippus did in the affair of Dion, for such things seem almost no wrong.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: [Κάλλιππος]
---

Among those whom people are ready to wrong are “those against whom they have made complaints and with whom they have already broken, as Callippus did in the affair of Dion; for such things too seem almost no wrong” (1373a18–20; see [[dion-of-syracuse]]). The wrongdoer tells himself that the friendship was over before the deed.
