---
summary: Change is pleasant, for it brings a return to nature.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: ["μεταβολὴ πάντων γλυκύ"]
en: [Change in all things is sweet]
---

“And change is pleasant; for change brings a return to nature, since sameness always produces an excess of the settled state; hence it was said, ‘Change in all things is sweet’” (1371a25–28; see [[hedone]]).
