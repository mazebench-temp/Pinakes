**Book II.** The topic from definition: “why Socrates said he would not go to Archelaus: for he said it was outrage to be unable to repay benefits alike as well as injuries” (1398a24–26; see [[socrates]] and [[hybris]]). On this definition, to receive what one cannot return is to be put beneath the giver.
