**Book II.** People are more prone to shame when they will go about among those who know: “whence Antiphon the poet, when he was about to be put to death on the plank by Dionysius, seeing those who were to die with him veiling their faces as they went through the gates, said, ‘Why do you veil your faces? Is it so that none of these may see you tomorrow?’” (1385a9–13; see [[aischyne]]).
