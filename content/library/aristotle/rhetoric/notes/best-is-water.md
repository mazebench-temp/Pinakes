---
summary: The plentiful is greater than the rare in use: “hence it is said, ‘Best is water.’”
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: ["ἄριστον μὲν ὕδωρ"]
en: [Best is water]
---

The rare is greater than the plentiful, “as gold than iron, though it is less useful” (1364a24–25), and yet the plentiful is greater in another way, “because its use exceeds; for often exceeds seldom; hence it is said, ‘Best is water’” (1364a26–28). The same topic argues both ways (see [[megethos]]). Aristotle does not name Pindar; the line was known to everyone.
