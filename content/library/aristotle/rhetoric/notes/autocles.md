**Book II.** The topic from a judgment, where it is not noble to judge against the judges: “as Autocles said against Mixidemides, that it was right for the Dread Goddesses to submit to justice on the Areopagus, but not for Mixidemides” (1398b26–29; see [[mixidemides]], [[areopagus]] and [[erinyes]]). The Dread Goddesses, the Erinyes, stood trial on the Areopagus against Orestes, as in Aeschylus' *Eumenides*.
