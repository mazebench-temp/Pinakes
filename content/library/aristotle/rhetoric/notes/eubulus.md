---
summary: Eubulus used against Chares in court what Plato had said to Archibius, that admitting one's wickedness had become common in the city: a recent witness.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: [Εὔβουλος]
---

Recent witnesses are well-known men who have judged some question, for their judgments are useful to those disputing the same questions, “as Eubulus in the courts used against Chares what Plato said to Archibius, that admitting to being wicked has grown in the city” (1376a7–11; see [[chares]], [[plato-the-comic-poet]] and [[archibius]]).
