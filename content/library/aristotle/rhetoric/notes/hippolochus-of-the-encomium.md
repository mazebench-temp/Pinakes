Among the means of amplification: “if the first encomium was composed for him, as for Hippolochus, and the setting up of a statue in the marketplace for Harmodius and Aristogeiton” (1368a16–18; see [[auxesis]] and [[harmodius]]). Nothing more is known of Hippolochus or of the encomium.
