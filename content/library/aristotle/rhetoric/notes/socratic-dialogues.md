summary: The Socratic dialogues show character, because they speak of choice.
heading: In the Rhetoric
---

**Book III.** The narration should show character, which comes from making the choice plain: “That is why mathematical discourses have no characters, because they have no choice either (for they do not have a purpose), but the Socratic dialogues do; for they speak of such things” (1417a19–22; see [[ethos]] and [[prohairesis]]).
