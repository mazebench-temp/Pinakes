---
summary: People wrong those whom they can repay afterward with many just acts.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: ["ἀδικεῖν ἔνια"]
en: [one must do some wrongs]
---

“And those whom they can wrong and then do many just things, as if easily making amends; as Jason the Thessalian said one must do some wrongs in order to be able to do many just things too” (1373a24–27; see [[adikia]]).
