**Book II.** People love those like themselves, “if they do not get in our way and our living does not come from the same source; for then it becomes ‘potter against potter’” (1381b15–17; see [[philos]]). In the chapter on envy: “since people compete for honor with their rivals … they must envy these most of all; that is why it has been said, ‘potter against potter too’” (1388a14–17; see [[phthonos]]).
