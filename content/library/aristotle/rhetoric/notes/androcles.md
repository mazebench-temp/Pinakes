**Book II.** The topic from things that are thought to happen but are incredible: they would not be believed unless they were true. Its example is Androcles' retort when he was accusing a law and the assembly shouted him down (1400a9–14; see [[the-laws-need-a-law]] and [[topics-of-enthymemes]]).
