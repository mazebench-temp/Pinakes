grc: ["πιθαν*"]
en: [persuasive, persuasion, means of persuasion, persuasiveness]
---

The function of rhetoric is “to see the means of persuasion available in each case” (1355b10–11), and it belongs to the same art “to see both the persuasive and the apparently persuasive” (1355b15–16; see [[rhetorike]]). The definition makes it a capacity “to see, in each case, the possible means of persuasion” (1355b25–26). “The persuasive is persuasive to someone” (1356b28), which is why rhetoric studies what convinces kinds of people, not individuals (see [[callias]]).
