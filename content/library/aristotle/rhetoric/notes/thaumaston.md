“Learning and wondering are pleasant for the most part; for in wondering there is the desire to learn, so that the wonderful is desired” (1371a31–33). Imitations, reversals and narrow escapes are pleasant “for all these are wonderful” (1371b10–12; see [[peripeteia]] and [[hedone]]). Being admired, θαυμάζεσθαι, is pleasant for the same reason as being honored (1371a22).
