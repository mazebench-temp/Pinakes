# Plato at 1406b32 is the philosopher, author of the Republic.
except: [1398b31, 1406b32]
---

See [[eubulus]] (1376a9–11). A comic poet's jibe at the city's morals is just the kind of judgment a speaker could quote as testimony; Freese's note takes the speaker to be probably the comic poet rather than the philosopher, and the identification cannot be proved.
