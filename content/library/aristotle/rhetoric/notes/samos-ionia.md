**Book II.** Cydias, speaking to the Athenian assembly “about the Samian cleruchy”, the Athenian settlement on the island, asked his hearers to imagine the Greeks standing around them in a circle, watching what they would vote (1384b32–35; see [[cydias]] and [[aischyne]]). Aesop told his fable of the fox and the hedgehog “speaking to the assembly at Samos” (1393b23; see [[samians]]).
