**Book III.** The diminutive makes both the bad and the good less, “as Aristophanes jokes in the Babylonians, ‘goldlet’ for ‘gold’, ‘cloaklet’ for ‘cloak’, ‘abuselet’ for ‘abuse’, and ‘diseaselet’ for ‘disease’” (1405b30–33). The *Babylonians*, now lost, was produced in 426 BCE (see [[epitheton]]).
