---
summary: Honor is a sign of a reputation for doing good; its parts are listed, and honor is among the most pleasant things.
heading: In the Rhetoric
---

“Honor is a sign of a reputation for doing good” (1361a28), and “the parts of honor are sacrifices, memorials in verse and without verse, privileges, grants of land, front seats, tombs, statues, maintenance at public expense, barbarian honors such as prostrations and making way, and the gifts that each people prizes” (1361a34–37). A gift pleases both lovers of money and lovers of honor (1361a37–b2; see [[doron]]). Honor and good reputation “are among the most pleasant things” (1371a8; see [[hedone]]), deeds whose prize is honor are noble (1366b34–35), and the greater honor marks the greater good (1365a7–8).
