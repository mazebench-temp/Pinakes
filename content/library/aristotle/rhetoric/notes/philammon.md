**Book III.** The comic poets make similes that answer each other: “He carries his legs as curly as parsley. Like Philammon sparring with the punching bag” (1413a13–14). Turned into a hyperbole, the simile becomes “You would have thought him Philammon fighting the punching bag” (1413a26–27; see [[eikon]] and [[hyperbole]]).
