**Book III.** Among the ways to fail at clear speech is ambiguity, used by those who have nothing to say: the hearers “feel what the many feel at the seers': when the seers speak ambiguously, they nod along: ‘Croesus, crossing the Halys, will destroy a great empire.’” (1407a36–38). Seers speak in general terms, because error is less likely so (1407b1–2; see [[hellenizein]]).
