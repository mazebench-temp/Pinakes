summary: What is natural is greater than what is acquired, “hence the poet says, ‘I am self-taught.’”
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: [αὐτοδίδακτος]
en: [I am self-taught]
---

“And what is natural is greater than what is acquired, for it is harder. Hence the poet says, ‘I am self-taught’” (1365a29–30; see [[megethos]]). Aristotle reads the word as a boast of natural gifts against learned skill.
