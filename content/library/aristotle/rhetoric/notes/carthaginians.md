---
summary: Those who plunder the Carthaginians wrong people far away, from whom revenge is slow.
heading: In the Rhetoric
---

People wrong “both those far off and those near: from the near, the taking is quick; from the far, revenge is slow, as with those who plunder the Carthaginians” (1372b26–28). The raiders are presumably the Greeks of Sicily and Italy (see [[adikia]]).
