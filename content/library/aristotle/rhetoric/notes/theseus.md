---
summary: Theseus chose Helen: what the wise and good have preferred is good.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: [Θησεὺς]
---

Among the topics of the good: “what one of the wise or of the good, men or women, has preferred, as Athena chose Odysseus, Theseus Helen, and Alexander was chosen by the goddesses, and Homer chose Achilles” (1363a16–19). Theseus' choice is his abduction of Helen as a girl (see [[helen-and-theseus]]).

**Book II.** If Theseus did no wrong, neither did Alexander (1397b22–23); Isocrates argued that Helen was good because Theseus chose her (1399a2; see [[helen]]).
