**Book III.** The paean that begins with a long syllable and ends with three short ones suits the beginning, as in “Delos-born, or Lycia” and “Golden-haired Far-Shooter, son of Zeus” (1409a14–15), the openings of paeans to Apollo (see [[paean]] and [[rhythmos]]).
