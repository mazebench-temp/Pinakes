except: [1408a28]

People wrong “those whom they can wrong and then do many just things, as if easily making amends; as Jason the Thessalian said one must do some wrongs in order to be able to do many just things too” (1373a24–27; see [[some-wrongs-for-many-just-deeds]]). The ruler's maxim becomes a topic for the prosecutor, who shows that the defendant counted on doing good afterward.
