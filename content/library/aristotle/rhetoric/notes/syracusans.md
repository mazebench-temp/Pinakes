---
summary: Euripides' answer to the Syracusans, praised as an example of shame before new friends.
heading: In the Rhetoric
---

**Book II.** People feel shame before those who have lately wished to become their friends, “for they have seen only the best; that is why Euripides' answer to the Syracusans was a good one” (1384b14–16; see [[euripides]] and [[aischyne]]). What the answer was Aristotle does not say.
