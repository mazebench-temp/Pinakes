**Book III.** What is written should be easy to read and to speak, and the writings of Heraclitus are not: “To punctuate Heraclitus is a task, because it is unclear which word goes with which, the later or the earlier, as at the very beginning of his book; for he says, ‘Though this account holds always men prove uncomprehending’; it is unclear with which word ‘always’ should be punctuated” (1407b14–18). Ἀεί can go with “holds” or with “prove uncomprehending” (see [[hellenizein]]).
