---
summary: Victory is noble and pleasant, for it gives an image of superiority.
heading: In the Rhetoric
---

“And winning is pleasant, not only to lovers of victory but to all; for there arises an image of superiority, which all have an appetite for” (1370b32–34; see [[hedone]]). Victory and honor are among noble things (1366b34–1367a1), and the contentious are moved to wrong by the love of victory (1368b21).
