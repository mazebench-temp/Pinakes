**Book III.** Frigidity comes first from double words, “as in Lycophron's ‘the many-faced sky of the great-peaked earth’ and ‘narrow-passaged shore’” (1405b36–37), and second from rare words, “as Lycophron called Xerxes ‘a monstrous man’ and Sciron ‘a ravager of a man’” (1406a7–8). In the last phrase the word for “ravager”, σίνις, is also the name of another brigand killed by Theseus (see [[psychron]], [[xerxes]] and [[sciron]]).
