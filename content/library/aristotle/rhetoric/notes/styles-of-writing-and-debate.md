**Book III.** “It must not escape our notice that a different style suits each kind” (1413b3). The written style is the most precise; the style of debate the most suited to delivery, either that which shows character or the emotional (1413b8–10). Chaeremon and Licymnius are good to read (1413b12–14; see [[chaeremon]] and [[licymnius-of-chios]]). Repetition and asyndeta belong to debate and must be acted (1413b17–1414a7; see [[delivery]] and [[asyndeton]]). The style of speaking to the assembly is “exactly like shadow-painting” (1414a8–9), and the epideictic “is the most suited to writing, for its function is to be read” (1414a18–19; see [[three-kinds-of-rhetoric]]).
