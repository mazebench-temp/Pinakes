At the end of chapter 8, after a brief account of four constitutions and their ends, Aristotle refers the reader to the *Politics*: “for these matters are worked out precisely in the Politics” (1366a21–22; see [[politeia]]). The *Rhetoric*'s scheme of four constitutions, democracy, oligarchy, aristocracy and monarchy, is simpler than the *Politics*' six.
