---
summary: The Tenedians cited Periander of Corinth as an ancient witness in their dispute with the Sigeans.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: [Περιάνδρῳ]
---

“And the Tenedians recently used Periander of Corinth against the Sigeans” (1375b30–31). Periander's judgment is cited as testimony, perhaps his award in the war for Sigeum (Herodotus 5.95); the Tenedians' own dispute is otherwise unknown (see [[tenedos]] and [[sigeum]]).
