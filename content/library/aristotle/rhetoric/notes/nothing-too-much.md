**Book II.** The young err in everything “on the side of excess and vehemence, contrary to Chilon's maxim (for they do everything too much)” (1389b2–4; see [[chilon]]). In the chapter on maxims it is one of the sayings that have become public property, which a speaker may contradict when his character will appear better for it (1395a20–33; see [[gnome]]).
