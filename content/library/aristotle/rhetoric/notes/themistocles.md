summary: Interpreters of oracles are witnesses about the future, as Themistocles was when he said that they must fight at sea, speaking of the wooden wall.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: [Θεμιστοκλῆς]
---

“About the future the interpreters of oracles are witnesses too, as Themistocles said that they must fight at sea, speaking of the wooden wall” (1376a1–2; see [[the-wooden-wall]]). Aristotle classes him with the ancient witnesses, whose authority comes from the past and who cannot be bribed (see [[non-artistic-proofs]]).
