---
summary: The greatest part of a great thing is great: the city's youth is its spring.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: [ἔαρ]
en: [the spring]
---

“And the greatest part of something great: as Pericles, delivering the funeral oration, said that the youth had been taken from the city as if the spring had been taken out of the year” (1365a31–33; see [[megethos]]). The young are the greatest part of the city, as spring is of the year.
