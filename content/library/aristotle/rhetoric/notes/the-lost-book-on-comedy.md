See [[poetics]] (1372a1–2).

**Book III.** The use of jest in debate: “it has been said in the books on poetics how many kinds of the laughable there are, of which some suit a free man and others do not” (1419b6–7). The surviving *Poetics* has no such list, which was probably in its lost book on comedy (see [[geloion]] and [[poetics]]).
