**Book III.** When a swallow let fall her droppings on Gorgias, he said, “Shameful, Philomela!” (1406b17). Aristotle calls it “the best of the tragic manner”: what is not shameful in a bird would be in a maiden, and he rebuked her “by calling her what she was, not what she is” (1406b16–19; see [[gorgias]]).
