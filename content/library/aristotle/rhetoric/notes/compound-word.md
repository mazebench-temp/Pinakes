**Book III.** Double words are to be used seldom in prose (1404b28–30), and they are the first source of frigidity, as in Lycophron, Gorgias and Alcidamas (1405b35–1406a6; see [[psychron]]). People use them when a thing has no name and the compound is easily formed, but if overdone the result is wholly poetic, “So double words are most useful to the writers of dithyrambs (for they are noisy)” (1406b1–2; see [[dithyramb]]). They suit a speaker carried away by emotion (1408b11–12).
