“Every syllogism alike is for dialectic to consider”, so whoever is best able to see how a syllogism comes about will also be most skilled in enthymemes (1355a8–11; see [[enthymema]]). “If in general anything at all must be shown either by syllogism or by induction (and this is clear to us from the Analytics), each of the two must be the same as each of these” (1356b8–11; see [[paradeigma]]). Some signs yield a syllogism and are conclusive, others do not (1357b4–21; see [[tekmerion]]).

**Book II.** The enthymeme is a syllogism (1395b22–24), and as there are apparent syllogisms there are apparent enthymemes (1400b34–37; see [[apparent-enthymemes]]).
