---
summary: The companion who chose Odysseus. Diomedes' choice of Odysseus can be put in a better or a worse light.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: ["^Διομήδην"]
---

**Book II.** Praise of Achilles for what he shares with others praises him no more than Diomedes (1396b11–14); in Theodectes' *Ajax*, Diomedes chose Odysseus not to honor him but to have a lesser follower (1399b29–31; see [[ajax-plays]]).

**Book III.** The same act can be done for more than one reason, and the slanderer takes it in the worse sense: “when Diomedes chose Odysseus, the one says that it was because he thought Odysseus the best, the other that it was not, but because he alone would not rival him, being worthless” (1416b12–15; *Iliad* 10.242–247; see [[diabole]]).
