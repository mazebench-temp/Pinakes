**Book III.** Someone said in court against Peitholaus and Lycophron: “These men sold you when they were at home, and now that they have come to you they have bought you” (1410a17–19), an antithesis (see [[antithesis]]). Peitholaus himself “called the Paralus ‘the people's club’, and Sestus ‘the meal-tray of the Piraeus’” (1411a13–15): the state trireme, and the town on the Hellespont through which Athens' grain passed (see [[metaphora]]).
