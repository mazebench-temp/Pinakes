**Book III.** The period must be complete and not broken off “like the iambics of Sophocles, ‘This land is Calydon, of Pelops' country’” (1409b9–10): read to the end of the verse, the line puts Calydon in the Peloponnese. The line is the opening of Euripides' *Meleager* (fragment 515 Nauck), which goes on “across the strait”; Aristotle's “Sophocles” is a slip of memory or of the text (see [[euripides]] and [[periodos]]).
