“The end of democracy is freedom” (1366a4; see [[politeia]]). Possessions are a part of wealth when they are “fit for a free man” (1361a16–17), and those that bring no profit are nobler, “for they are more fitting for a free man” (1367a27–28); in Lacedaemon long hair “is the sign of a free man”, who does no hired laborer's work (1367a29–31; see [[lacedaemon]]). Alcidamas' sentence makes freedom a law of nature (1373b18; see [[god-has-left-all-men-free]]).
