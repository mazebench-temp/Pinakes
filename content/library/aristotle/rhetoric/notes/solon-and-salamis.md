en: [used Homer as a witness about Salamis]
---

“By ancient I mean the poets and all other well-known men whose judgments are on record; as the Athenians used Homer as a witness about Salamis” (1375b28–30; see [[non-artistic-proofs]] and [[homer]]). The other examples follow at once: Periander for the Tenedians, Solon's elegies against Critias (see [[periander]] and [[tell-red-haired-critias]]).
