**Book III.** Among the means of bulk: “Antimachus' practice is useful, describing a thing by what it does not have, as he does with Teumessus: ‘There is a small windy hill’; for in this way the description is amplified without limit” (1408a2–4). The verse is from his *Thebaid*; Teumessus is a hill in Boeotia (see [[onkos]]).
