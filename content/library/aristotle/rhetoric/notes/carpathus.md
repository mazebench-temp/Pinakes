**Book III.** Proverbs are metaphors from species to species: “if someone brings something in himself, expecting good from it, and then is harmed, ‘Like the Carpathian with the hare,’ people say” (1413a17–19). The islanders of Carpathus were said to have brought in hares, which then overran the island (see [[metaphora]]).
