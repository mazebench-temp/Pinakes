---
summary: In the Rhetoric εἶδος is Aristotle's “kind”, “species” or “form”.
heading: In the Rhetoric
---

Each kind of rhetorical speaking, by example or by enthymeme, has its merits (1356b19; see [[paradeigma]]); the special premises are the “species” of each genus (1358a17, 1358a31; see [[topos]]); praise and counsel share a common form (1367b37; see [[praise-and-blame]]); and there is an appetite for each kind of nourishment (1370a22–23; see [[epithymia]]).
