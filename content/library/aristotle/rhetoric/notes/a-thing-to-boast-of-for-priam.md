---
summary: Quoted, with Odysseus' “It is shameful to stay so long”, for the topic that what has cost much toil or expense is good.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: ["εὐχωλὴν Πριάμῳ"]
en: [to Priam a thing to boast of]
---

What has cost much toil is good, “for this already seems a good, and such a thing is taken to be an end, the end of many efforts, and the end is a good” (1363a3–5). Hera's words show the topic at work: Helen, for whom many have died, has become the end of the war, and to give her up would turn the toil into the enemy's gain (see [[shameful-to-stay-long]] and [[the-water-jar-at-the-door]]).
