---
summary: The Corinthians took it as an insult when Simonides wrote that Ilium does not blame them; Periander was “the Corinthian”.
heading: In the Rhetoric
---

What even enemies do not blame is a good, “that is why the Corinthians thought themselves insulted by Simonides when he wrote, ‘The Corinthians Ilium does not blame’” (1363a14–16; see [[ilium-does-not-blame-the-corinthians]]). In chapter 15 the tyrant is “Periander of Corinth” (1375b31; see [[periander]]).
