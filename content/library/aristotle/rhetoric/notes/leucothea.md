---
summary: To sacrifice and to lament together is a contradiction: Xenophanes to the Eleates.
heading: In the Rhetoric
---

**Book II.** Among the topics of enthymemes, when something is about to be done that is opposite to what has been done, one looks at both together, “as Xenophanes advised the Eleates, when they asked whether they should sacrifice to Leucothea and lament her or not, that if they suppose her a goddess they should not lament her, and if a woman, they should not sacrifice to her” (1400b5–8; see [[xenophanes]], [[eleates]] and [[topics-of-enthymemes]]).
