---
summary: People wrong those against whom they have a pretext.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: ["προφάσεως δεῖται"]
en: [wickedness needs only a pretext]
---

“And those against whom they have a pretext, because their ancestors or they themselves or their friends have done harm, or meant to, to the wrongdoers themselves, or their ancestors, or those they care for; for as the proverb says, wickedness needs only a pretext” (1373a1–3; see [[poneria]]).
