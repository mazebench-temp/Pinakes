**Book II.** The topic from definition: “as in the Alexander, that all would agree that the disorderly are not content with the enjoyment of one body” (1398a22–23). The fallacy from the consequent: “in the Alexander, that he was great-souled; for he despised the company of the many and spent his time on Ida by himself; since the great-souled are of this kind, he too would seem great-souled” (1401b20–23; see [[apparent-enthymemes]] and [[ida]]).
