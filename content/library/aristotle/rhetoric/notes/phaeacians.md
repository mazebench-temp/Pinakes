**Book III.** The proem should make the hearer well disposed, “And since it is well said, ‘Grant that I come to the Phaeacians as one welcomed and pitied,’ these are the two things to aim at” (1415b26–28; *Odyssey* 6.327; see [[welcomed-and-pitied]], [[prooimion]] and [[odysseus]]).
