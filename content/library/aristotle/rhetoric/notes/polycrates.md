**Book II.** Two of the apparent enthymemes come from him: “what Polycrates said of Thrasybulus, that he overthrew thirty tyrants; for he combines them” (1401a34–36; see [[thrasybulus]] and [[the-thirty]]), and “what Polycrates says of the mice, that they came to the rescue by gnawing through the bowstrings” (1401b15–16), where an accident is taken for a ground of praise. The mice are those that saved Egypt from the army of Sennacherib by eating the bowstrings and shield-straps of his soldiers in the night (Herodotus 2.141). See [[apparent-enthymemes]].
