**Book III.** Repetition must be varied in delivery, “so the actor Philemon did in Anaxandrides' Old Men's Madness whenever he said ‘Rhadamanthus and Palamedes’, and with the ‘I’ in the prologue of the Pious” (1413b25–27; see [[anaxandrides]] and [[delivery]]).
