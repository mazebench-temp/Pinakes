**Book II.** “One should also use hackneyed and common maxims, if they are useful; for because they are common, as if everyone agreed, they seem right; for instance, for one exhorting men to face danger without having sacrificed, ‘One omen is best: to fight in defense of our native land’” (1395a10–14; see [[aristotle-one-omen]] and [[gnome]]).
