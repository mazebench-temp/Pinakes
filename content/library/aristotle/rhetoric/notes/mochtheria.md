---
summary: Depravity lies in the choice; misfortunes do not come from depravity.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: ["μοχθηρ*"]
en: [depravity]
---

People choose to do wrong from vice and lack of self-control, “for if people have some depravity” (1368b14), each does wrong in his own field. “For depravity and wrongdoing lie in the choice” (1374a11; see [[prohairesis]]), and “misfortunes are acts that are unexpected and do not come from depravity” (1374b6–7; see [[epieikes]]).
