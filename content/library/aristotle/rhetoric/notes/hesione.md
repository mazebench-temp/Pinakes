**Book III.** Tokens can be used by either side: “in the Teucer Odysseus says that Teucer is a kinsman of Priam, for Hesione was Priam's sister; and Teucer replies that his father, Telamon, was Priam's enemy” (1416b1–3; see [[teucer]], [[telamon]] and [[diabole]]).
