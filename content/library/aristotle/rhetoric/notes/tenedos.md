summary: The Tenedians cited Periander of Corinth as a witness against the Sigeans, and Achilles' wrath at Tenedos is an example of a fallacy.
heading: In the Rhetoric
---

See [[periander]] and [[sigeum]] (1375b30–31). Achilles, who “conceived his wrath against the Achaeans at Tenedos” because he was not invited to dinner, is the instance of the fallacy of the accidental (1401b17–19; see [[achilles]] and [[the-quarrel-of-odysseus-and-achilles]]).
