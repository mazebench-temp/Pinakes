summary: Cited for the claim that every demonstration is by syllogism or induction, for necessary and non-necessary premises, and for the kinds of signs.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: ["^Ἀναλυτικ*"]
en: [Analytics]
---

The Analytics shows that anything proved must be proved by syllogism or by induction (1356b8–10), what follows from premises that hold only for the most part (1357a29–30), and why some signs are conclusive and others not (1357b21–25; see [[semeion]] and [[tekmerion]]).

**Book II.** “That every sign is invalid is clear to us from the Analytics” (1403a4–5), and so is the validity of the tekmērion (1403a10–12; see [[enstasis]]).
