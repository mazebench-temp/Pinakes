“And Cleophon used Solon's elegies against Critias, saying that the family had long been licentious; for otherwise Solon would never have written, ‘Tell red-haired Critias, for me, to listen to his father’” (1375b31–34; see [[cleophon-the-demagogue]] and [[non-artistic-proofs]]).
