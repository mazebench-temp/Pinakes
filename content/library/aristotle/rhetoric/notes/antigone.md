# Ἀντιγόνης at 1417a30 is the play (antigone-play).

“As Sophocles' Antigone clearly says that it is just, though forbidden, to bury Polynices, as being just by nature” (1373b9–11; see [[not-of-today-or-yesterday]] and [[nomos]]).

**Book III.** Sophocles makes Haemon “speak for Antigone to his father as if others were saying it” (1418b31–32; see [[haemon-son-of-creon]]). Her own reason for burying her brother is quoted from the play (1417a29–33; see [[antigone-play]]).
