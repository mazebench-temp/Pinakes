**Book II.** “That is why Philocrates answered well when someone, the people being angry, said, ‘Why don't you defend yourself?’ ‘Not yet,’ he said. ‘But when?’ ‘When I see someone else slandered.’ For people become calm when they have spent their anger on another” (1380b8–11; see [[praotes]]).
