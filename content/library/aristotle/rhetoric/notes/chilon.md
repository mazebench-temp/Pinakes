**Book II.** The young “err in everything on the side of excess and vehemence, contrary to Chilon's maxim (for they do everything too much)” (1389b2–4): the maxim is “Nothing too much” (see [[nothing-too-much]] and [[the-characters-of-the-ages]]). Alcidamas' induction that all men honor the wise includes him: “the Lacedaemonians made Chilon one of their elders, though they are the least fond of letters” (1398b14–15; see [[all-men-honor-the-wise]]).
