---
summary: A man may refuse to accept an oath by quoting Xenophanes; and the saying can be turned around.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: ["ἴση πρόκλησις"]
en: [is not a fair challenge, from the impious to the pious]
---

“And Xenophanes' saying fits: that ‘this is not a fair challenge, from the impious to the pious,’ but it is as if a strong man were to challenge a weak one to strike or be struck” (1377a19–21). Then the reverse: “one should turn Xenophanes' saying around and say that it is fair this way, if the impious man tenders and the pious man swears” (1377a22–24; see [[oath]] and [[non-artistic-proofs]]).
