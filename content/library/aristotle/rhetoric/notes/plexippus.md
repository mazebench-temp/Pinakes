**Book II.** People are angry “if they do not perceive their needs, as Plexippus in Antiphon's play was with Meleager; for not perceiving is a sign of slight” (1379b14–16; see [[antiphon-the-tragic-poet]] and [[oligoria]]). What the scene was is lost.
