**Book III.** “The Dog called the Attic taverns ‘Spartan messes’” (1411a24–25): the Spartans ate together in public messes, and so, in effect, did the Athenians in their taverns (see [[metaphora]]). The Dog is Diogenes, though Aristotle names him only by his nickname.
