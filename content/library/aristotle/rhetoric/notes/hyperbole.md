**Book III.** The account closes chapter 11 (1413a21–b2): see the article. The examples are the black eye as a basket of mulberries, Philammon fighting the punching bag, the curly legs, and Achilles' “not if he gave me as much as the sand and the dust” (1413a32; see [[philammon]] and [[the-sand-and-the-dust]]); and the Attic orators use hyperboles most (1413b2; see [[attica]]).
