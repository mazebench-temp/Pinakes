**Book III.** When one premise is obvious, ask only the other and state the conclusion: “as Socrates, when Meletus denied that he believed in gods, but had said that he spoke of some daimonion, asked whether daimones were not either children of gods or something divine, and when Meletus said they were, said, ‘Is there anyone, then, who thinks that there are children of gods, but not gods?’” (1419a8–12; Plato, *Apology* 27b–d; see [[socrates]] and [[erotesis]]).
