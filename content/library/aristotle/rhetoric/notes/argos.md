---
summary: The fish-carrier of the epigram walked from Argos to Tegea; in Argos the man on whose account a law was made is penalized.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: [Ἄργους]
---

The Olympic victor once carried fish “from Argos to Tegea” (1365a26–27; see [[once-with-a-rough-yoke]]). In chapter 14 a wrong is greater if it called for new penalties, “as in Argos a man is penalized on whose account a law is made, and those on whose account the prison was built” (1375a4–6; see [[adikia]]). Nothing more is known of the Argive custom.
