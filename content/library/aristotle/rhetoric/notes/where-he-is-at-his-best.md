---
summary: It is pleasant to spend one's time on that in which one seems to be at one's best.
heading: In the Rhetoric
grc: ["βέλτιστος ὤν"]
en: [to where he happens to be at his best]
---

Since most people are fond of honor, it is pleasant “to spend one's time on that in which one seems to be at one's best, as the poet says” (1371b30–32; see [[hedone]] and [[time]]).
