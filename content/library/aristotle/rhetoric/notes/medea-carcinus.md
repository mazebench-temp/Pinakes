**Book II.** The topic of accusing or defending from the mistakes made: “as in the Medea of Carcinus some accuse her of having killed her children, since at any rate they are not to be seen (for Medea made a mistake in sending the children away), and she defends herself by saying that she would have killed not the children but Jason; for it would have been a mistake not to do this, if she had really done the other” (1400b9–15; see [[topics-of-enthymemes]] and [[theodorus-of-byzantium]]).
