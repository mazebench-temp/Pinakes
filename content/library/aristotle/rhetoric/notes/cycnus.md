summary: Killing Cycnus is one of the things that belong to Achilles alone, and so a particular ground for praising him.
heading: In the Rhetoric
---

**Book II.** Praise must come from what is particular to its subject, “for instance, killing Hector, the best of the Trojans, and Cycnus, who prevented all from landing, being invulnerable” (1396b14–17; see [[achilles]] and [[topos]]).
