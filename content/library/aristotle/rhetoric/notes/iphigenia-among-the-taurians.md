---

**Book III.** Making one into many gives bulk, as the poets do: “Here are the many-leaved folds of the tablet” (1407b35; line 727, where Iphigenia hands over her letter; see [[onkos]]). And proems may slander or dispel fears: “‘My lord, I will not say that I come in haste,’ ‘Why this preamble?’” (1415b20–21), the first from Sophocles' *Antigone* (223), the second Thoas' question to Iphigenia (1162; see [[antigone-play]] and [[prooimion]]).
