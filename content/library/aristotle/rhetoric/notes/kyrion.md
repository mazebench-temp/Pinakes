**Book III.** “Of nouns and verbs, the standard ones make the style clear” (1404b5–6), and “the standard and the proper word and metaphor are the only ones useful for the style of bare prose” (1404b31–33), since everyone uses them in conversation. Standard words that mean the same are synonyms, such as “proceed” and “walk” (1405a1–3); one word is more standard than another and more like the thing, against Bryson (1405b9–12; see [[bryson]]); and standard words teach us nothing new, for we know them already (1410b13; see [[asteion]]).
