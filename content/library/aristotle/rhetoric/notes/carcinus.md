**Book II.** His *Medea* (1400b9–15; see [[medea-carcinus]]).

**Book III.** If something is incredible, one must promise to give the reason, “as Jocasta in Carcinus' Oedipus keeps promising when the man who is searching for her son questions her” (1417b18–20). The play is lost; Aristotle names Carcinus' *Medea* in Book II (see [[jocasta]] and [[diegesis]]).
