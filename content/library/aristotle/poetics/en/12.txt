1452b14	The parts of tragedy that must be used as its constituent kinds
1452b15	we have spoken of before; by quantity, the separate sections
1452b16	into which it is divided are these: prologue, episode, exodos,
1452b17	choral part, and of the choral part, the parodos and the stasimon; these are common
1452b18	to all plays, while songs from the stage and kommoi are peculiar to some.
1452b19	The prologue is the whole part of a tragedy before the entry of the
1452b20	chorus; an episode is a whole part of a tragedy between
1452b21	whole choral songs; the exodos is the whole part of a tragedy after
1452b22	which there is no song of the chorus. Of the choral part, the parodos is the first
1452b23	whole utterance of the chorus; a stasimon is a song of the chorus without anapests
1452b24	or trochees; and a kommos is a lament shared by the chorus and those on
1452b25	the stage. The parts of tragedy that must be used as constituent kinds
1452b26	we have spoken of before; by quantity
1452b27	these are the separate sections into which it is divided.
