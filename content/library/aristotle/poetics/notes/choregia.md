“To produce this effect through spectacle is less a matter of art and needs a sponsor's money” (1453b7–8; see [[opsis]]). The beginnings of comedy are obscure because it was not taken seriously: “a chorus of comedians was granted by the archon only late; before that they were volunteers” (1449b1–2; see [[the-origins-of-comedy]]).
