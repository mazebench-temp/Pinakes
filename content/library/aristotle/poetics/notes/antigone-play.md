---
grc: [Ἀντιγόνῃ]
en: [the Antigone]
---

To be about to act knowingly and not act is the worst of the four ways a tragic deed can go, and “no one composes in this way, except rarely, as in the Antigone, where Creon is threatened by Haemon” (1453b37–1454a2; see [[haemon-threatens-creon]] and [[the-best-tragic-plot]]).
