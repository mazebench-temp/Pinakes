---
summary: Aristotle's rewording of a Homeric line in standard words.
grc: [νῦν δέ μʼ ἐὼν, ἀσθενικὸς καὶ ἀειδής]
en: [and now one who is small and of no account, and now one who is little and, weakly and ugly]
refs: [1458b26]
---

Aristotle keeps the meter and the sense and changes the words: μικρός, “little”, for ὀλίγος, ἀσθενικός, “weakly”, for οὐτιδανός, and ἀειδής, “ugly”, for ἀεικής (1458b25–28). The line loses its distinction (see [[kyrion]]).
