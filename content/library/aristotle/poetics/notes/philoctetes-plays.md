---
grc: [Φιλοκτήτῃ, Φιλοκτήτης]
en: [the Philoctetes, Philoctetes]
---

Aeschylus “in his Philoctetes” wrote “the ulcer that eats the flesh of my foot”, and Euripides, changing one word, made it beautiful (1458b22–24; see [[the-ulcer-that-eats-my-foot]]). A *Philoctetes* is among the tragedies made from the *Little Iliad* (1459b5; see [[little-iliad]]).
