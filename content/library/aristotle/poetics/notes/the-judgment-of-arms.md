The tragedies of suffering are illustrated by “the Ajaxes and the Ixions” (1455b34–1456a1; see [[ajax-plays]]), and the Judgment of Arms heads the list of tragedies drawn from the Little Iliad (1459b4–5; see [[little-iliad]]).
