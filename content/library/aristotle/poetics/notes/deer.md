---
summary: Not knowing that a female deer has no horns is a lesser fault than painting one unrecognizably.
grc: [ἔλαφος]
en: [a female deer]
---

An error of fact is less serious than an error of art: “it is a lesser fault not to know that a female deer has no horns than to paint one unrecognizably” (1460b31–32; see [[problems-and-solutions]] and [[painting-and-poetry]]). Pindar gives the hind that Heracles chased golden horns, “a golden-horned female deer” (*Olympian* 3.29).
