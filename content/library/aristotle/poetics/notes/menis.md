---
summary: The first word of the Iliad, quoted in Protagoras' criticism of its opening.
---

“Sing, goddess, the wrath” is quoted for Protagoras' criticism that Homer gave an order where he meant a prayer (1456b15–19; see [[protagoras-on-the-first-line]]). Protagoras also held that μῆνις ought to be masculine (Aristotle, *Sophistical Refutations* 14, 173b17–22; see [[gender-of-nouns]]).
