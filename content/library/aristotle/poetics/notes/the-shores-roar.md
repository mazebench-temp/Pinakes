---
summary: The last of Aristotle's examples of a poetic word replaced by a standard one.
grc: [ἠιόνες βοόωσιν, ἠιόνες κράζουσιν]
en: [the shores roar, the shores shriek]
---

Aristotle's κράζουσιν, “shriek”, is an everyday word for harsh cries, of birds as of people; Homer's βοόωσιν, “roar”, is epic (1458b31).
