---
summary: A problem solved by metaphor, "alone" said for "best known".
grc: [ἄμμορος]
en: [she alone has, no share]
refs: [1461a20]
---

Critics objected that other stars, too, never set. Aristotle answers that “alone” is said by metaphor, “for the best known is alone” (1461a20–21): the Bear is the one constellation that everyone knows never to set. Strabo makes the same defense in other terms, taking the Bear to stand for all the stars of the arctic circle (*Geography* 1.1.6). See [[problems-and-solutions]].
