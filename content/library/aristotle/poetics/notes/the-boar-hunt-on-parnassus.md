---
summary: One of the things that happened to Odysseus which Homer did not make part of the Odyssey's single action.
refs: [1451a24-29]
---

In composing the Odyssey Homer “did not put in everything that happened to him, for example his being wounded on Parnassus and his feigning madness at the mustering, of which neither, when the one had happened, made it necessary or probable that the other should happen” (1451a24–28; see [[unity-of-action]] and [[odysseus-feigns-madness]]). The wound does have a place in the poem, as the origin of the scar by which Odysseus is recognized, but as a story told in passing, not as part of the plot.
