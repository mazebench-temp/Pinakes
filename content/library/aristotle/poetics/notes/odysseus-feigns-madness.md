---
summary: The second of Aristotle's examples of an event Homer left out of the Odyssey.
refs: [1451a26-28]
---

Homer left out Odysseus' “feigning madness at the mustering”, which has no necessary or probable connection with his wounding on Parnassus (1451a24–28; see [[the-boar-hunt-on-parnassus]] and [[unity-of-action]]).
