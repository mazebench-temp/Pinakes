---
summary: Lyre music is one of the arts of imitation; and the lyre player's song brings tears to Odysseus' eyes in the tale told to Alcinous.
grc: [κιθαρ*]
en: [lyre, lyre player]
---

The music of the κιθάρα, the concert lyre, is named with that of the pipe among the imitative arts (1447a15, 1447a24, 1448a10; see [[aulos]]). In the recognition through memory, Odysseus, “hearing the lyre player”, remembered and wept (1455a2–3; see [[demodocus]] and [[the-tale-to-alcinous]]).
