---
summary: The tragedies about Ajax are Aristotle's example of the tragedy of suffering.
---

The tragedy of suffering is illustrated by “the Ajaxes and the Ixions” (1455b34–1456a1), plays like Sophocles' *Ajax* on his madness and suicide after the award of Achilles' arms (see [[ajax-plays]], [[the-judgment-of-arms]] and [[four-kinds-of-tragedy]]).
