---
summary: Alluded to in the spearhead birthmark of the Earth-born, Aristotle's first example of a sign born with the body.
refs: [1454b22]
---

“The spearhead which the Earth-born bear” is a congenital sign, the first kind of token by which characters are recognized (1454b21–22; see [[the-spearhead-of-the-earth-born]] and [[semeion]]).
