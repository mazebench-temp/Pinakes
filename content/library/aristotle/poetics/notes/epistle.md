---
summary: The letter in the Iphigenia, Aristotle's example of the best kind of recognition.
grc: [ἐπιστολῆς, γράμματα]
en: [the letter, a letter]
---

Iphigenia is recognized by Orestes “from the sending of the letter” (1452b6–7), a recognition that comes from the incidents themselves, “for it is probable that she should wish to send a letter” (1455a18–19), while Orestes has to declare himself (1454b31–35; see [[anagnorisis]]).
