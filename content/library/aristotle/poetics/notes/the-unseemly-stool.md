---
summary: A second Homeric line rewritten in standard words.
grc: [ἀεικέλιον καταθεὶς, δίφρον μοχθηρὸν]
en: [an unseemly stool and a small table, setting down a wretched stool and a little table]
refs: [1458b28]
---

Aristotle puts μοχθηρόν, “wretched”, for ἀεικέλιον, “unseemly”, and μικράν, “little”, for ὀλίγην (1458b28–30; see [[kyrion]]).
