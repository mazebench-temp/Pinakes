---
summary: Spears planted upright, a bronze spear stopped in a shield, a spearhead birthmark and a Cypriot word for the spear.
grc: [ἔγχεα]
en: [spears]
---

The spears “upright on their butt-spikes” were the custom of the time, as they still are among the Illyrians (1461a1–4; see [[aristotle-upright-spears]]); Aeneas' spear was stopped by the gold of Achilles' shield (1461a33–34; see [[the-bronze-spear-stopped]]). The descendants of the Sown Men bore a spearhead birthmark (1454b22; see [[the-spearhead-of-the-earth-born]]), and σίγυνον, “spear”, is a standard word in Cyprus and a rare word at Athens (1457b6; see [[sigynon]]).
