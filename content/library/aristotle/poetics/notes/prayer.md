---
summary: The forms of utterance include prayer, which Protagoras said Homer confused with command in the Iliad's first line.
except: [1453a35]
---

“What a command is, and a prayer” belongs to the art of delivery (1456b11), and Protagoras faulted Homer because, “thinking to pray”, he gave the Muse an order (1456b15–18; see [[forms-of-utterance]] and [[protagoras-on-the-first-line]]).
