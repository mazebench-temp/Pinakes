---
summary: A problem in Homer's description of Dolon, solved by the meaning of a word.
grc: [Δόλωνα]
---

Homer says that Dolon “was ill-favored to look at” (10.316), yet fast on his feet. Aristotle takes εἶδος here to mean not the body but the face, “for the Cretans call a fair face εὐειδής, ‘well-favored’” (1461a12–14; see [[aristotle-dolons-face]] and [[eidos]]).
