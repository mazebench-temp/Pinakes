---
summary: Early tragedy used the tetrameter because it was satyric and danced; the iambic replaced it; and it remains a lively meter, suited to dancing.
grc: [τετραμετρ*]
en: [tetrameter]
---

Tragedy's “meter changed from tetrameter to iambic. At first they used the tetrameter because the poetry was satyric and more suited to dancing” (1449a21–23; see [[satyr-play]] and [[iambic-trimeter]]). “The iambic and the tetrameter are lively, the one suited to dancing, the other to action” (1459b37–1460a1).
