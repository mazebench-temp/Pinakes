---
summary: In the Poetics ἔλεγχος is a refutation, the dialectician's test of a contradiction.
en: [refutations]
refs: [1461b16]
---

Things said in contradiction “should be examined as refutations are in arguments: whether it is the same thing, in relation to the same thing, and in the same way” (1461b15–18; see [[problems-and-solutions]]). The Homeric word means disgrace; Aristotle's is the technical term of dialectic, the refutation that Socrates made famous (see [[socrates]]).
