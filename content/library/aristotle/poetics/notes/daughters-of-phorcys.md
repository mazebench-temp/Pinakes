---
grc: [Φορκίδες]
en: [the Daughters of Phorcys]
---

The fourth kind of tragedy is named by a corrupt word, “such as the Daughters of Phorcys and the Prometheus and all plays set in Hades” (1456a2–3; see [[four-kinds-of-tragedy]]); the examples, with their monsters and remote places, suggest a tragedy of spectacle.
