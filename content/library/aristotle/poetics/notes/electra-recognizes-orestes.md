---
summary: Aristotle's example of the fourth kind of recognition, by reasoning.
refs: [1455a4-6]
---

“The fourth comes from reasoning, as in the Libation Bearers: that someone like her has come; no one is like her but Orestes; so he has come” (1455a4–6; see [[anagnorisis]]). Recognitions by reasoning rank second, after those that arise from the incidents themselves (1455a16–21).
