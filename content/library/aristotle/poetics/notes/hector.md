---
summary: The pursuit of Hector, Aristotle's example of the irrational that epic can carry and of an impossibility justified by its effect.
---

The pursuit of Hector would seem ridiculous on the stage, with the men standing still and Achilles waving them back, but in epic it goes unnoticed (1460a14–17), and it is the example of an impossibility that is right because it makes the scene more astonishing (1460b23–26; see [[the-pursuit-of-hector]]).
