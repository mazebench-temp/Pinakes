---
summary: Aristotle's example of metaphor from species to genus.
refs: [1457b12]
---

“From species to genus: ‘Truly Odysseus has done countless good deeds’; for countless is many, and he has used it here instead of many” (1457b11–13). The particular number, ten thousand, which is what μυρίοι means as a number, stands for the general “many” (see [[metaphora]]).
