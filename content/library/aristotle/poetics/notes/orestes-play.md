“An example of baseness of character that is not necessary is Menelaus in the Orestes” (1454a28–29), and criticism of baseness is right when it is used without necessity, “as in the Orestes that of Menelaus” (1461b21; see [[poneria]] and [[four-aims-of-character]]).
