en: [the False Messenger]
---

“There is also a composite kind, made from a false inference by the audience, as in Odysseus the False Messenger: that he strings the bow and no one else does is contrived by the poet, a premise, and so is his saying he would recognize the bow, which he had not seen; but to make it seem that he would be recognized by that means, through this, is a false inference” (1455a12–16). Kassel's lines 14a and 14b come from the Riccardianus.
