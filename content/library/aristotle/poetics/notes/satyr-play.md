---
grc: [σατυρικ*]
en: [satyric]
---

Tragedy grew “from slight plots and comic diction, since it had changed from the satyric”, and at first used the tetrameter “because the poetry was satyric and more suited to dancing” (1449a19–23; see [[the-origins-of-tragedy]]). Whether Aristotle means that tragedy grew out of the satyr play, or only that early tragedy had a satyric character, is disputed.
