---
summary: Aristotle's first example of the best kind of tragic deed, about to be done in ignorance and prevented by a recognition.
refs: [1454a4-7]
---

“Best of all is the last, as in the Cresphontes, where Merope is about to kill her son, and does not kill him, but recognizes him” (1454a4–7; see [[the-best-tragic-plot]] and [[cresphontes-play]]). The terror comes from the deed about to be done, and the repugnance of a deed done knowingly is avoided.
