---
summary: One of the heads of the few houses on which the finest tragedies are composed.
grc: [Μελέαγρον]
---

Meleager is named among Alcmaeon, Oedipus, Orestes, Thyestes and Telephus as one of those about whom the finest tragedies are composed, all of whom happened to suffer or to do terrible things (1453a18–22; see [[the-few-houses]]). The tragic version was the story of the brand on which his life depended, which his mother Althaea burned after he killed her brothers; Phrynichus was said to be the first to bring it on stage (Pausanias 10.31.4), and Euripides wrote a *Meleager*. The Iliad tells a different story, his wrath (see [[the-wrath-of-meleager]] and [[the-death-of-meleager]]).
