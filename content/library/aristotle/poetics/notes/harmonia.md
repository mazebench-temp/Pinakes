---
summary: Melody is a medium of imitation; the pipe and lyre use it with rhythm, the dance without it; the love of it is natural; and “embellished language” has it.
grc: [ἁρμονι*]
en: [melody, tone]
---

The arts imitate “in rhythm and speech and melody” (1447a22); melody and rhythm alone are used by the music of the pipe and lyre and the panpipes, rhythm without melody by dancers (1447a23–28; see [[aulos]], [[lyre]] and [[syrinx]]). Melody and rhythm are natural to us like imitation itself (1448b20–21). We seldom speak hexameters, and only “when we depart from the tone of speech” (λεκτικῆς ἁρμονίας, 1449a27–28). Embellished language has “rhythm and melody” (1449b29).
