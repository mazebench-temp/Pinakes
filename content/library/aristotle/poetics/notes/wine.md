---
summary: Mixed wine is still called wine, and the gods, who drink no wine, have it poured for them: problems solved by the custom of the language.
grc: [οἶνον]
en: [Mixed wine, wine]
---

Some problems are solved by the custom of the language: “mixed wine is still called wine”, and so Ganymede “pours wine for Zeus”, though the gods do not drink wine (1461a27–31; see [[aristotle-wine-of-the-gods]]). The order to mix the wine stronger, ζωρότερον, raises another problem (1461a14–16; see [[stronger-or-quicker]]).
