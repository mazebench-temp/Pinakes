---
summary: Plays set in Hades are among Aristotle's examples of the fourth kind of tragedy.
grc: [ᾅδου]
---

The fourth kind of tragedy, whose name is lost in the manuscripts, is illustrated by the *Daughters of Phorcys*, the *Prometheus* and “all plays set in Hades” (1456a2–3; see [[four-kinds-of-tragedy]]); scenes in the underworld called for spectacle.
