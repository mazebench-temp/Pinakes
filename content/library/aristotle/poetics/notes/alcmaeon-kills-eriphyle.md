---
summary: A traditional story that the poet must keep, though he may find his own way of handling it; Astydamas made Alcmaeon kill his mother in ignorance.
refs: [1453b22-26, 1453b33]
---

“The stories that have been handed down cannot be undone, I mean such as the death of Clytemnestra at the hands of Orestes, and of Eriphyle at the hands of Alcmaeon; but the poet must find out for himself how to use the traditional material well” (1453b22–26; see [[traditional-stories]]). Astydamas found a way within the tradition: his Alcmaeon kills his mother in ignorance and recognizes her afterward, inside the tragedy itself (1453b33; see [[alcmaeon-play]] and [[astydamas]]).
