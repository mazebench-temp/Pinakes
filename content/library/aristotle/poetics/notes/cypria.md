---
grc: [Κύπρια, Κυπρίων]
en: [Cypria]
---

Unlike Homer, the others compose about one man, one period, and “one action of many parts, like the poet of the Cypria and of the Little Iliad. So from the Iliad and the Odyssey one tragedy is made from each, or two at most, but many from the Cypria” (1459b1–4; see [[unity-of-action]] and [[little-iliad]]).
