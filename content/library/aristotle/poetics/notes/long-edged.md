---
summary: The Homeric formula ends a phrase that Aristotle gives as a metaphor from species to species.
---

“Cutting with the long-edged bronze” is one of Aristotle's two examples of metaphor from species to species, “cutting” said for “drawing off” (1457b13–16; see [[drawing-off-life-with-bronze]]). The phrase ends in the Homeric line-end formula ταναήκεϊ χαλκῷ.
