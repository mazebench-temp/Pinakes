---
summary: Aristotle's second example of a recognition contrived by the poet.
grc: [κερκίδος φωνή]
en: [the voice of the shuttle]
---

The second kind of recognition is the one contrived by the poet, and therefore inartistic, like Orestes declaring himself in the *Iphigenia*, “so too in Sophocles' Tereus the voice of the shuttle” (1454b30–37; see [[anagnorisis]]). The woven message proves the crime, but the poet, not the plot, provides it.
