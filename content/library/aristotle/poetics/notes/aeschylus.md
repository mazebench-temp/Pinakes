grc: [Αἰσχύλ*]
en: [Aeschylus]
---

“The number of actors was first raised from one to two by Aeschylus, who also reduced the part of the chorus and made speech take the leading role” (1449a15–18; see [[the-origins-of-tragedy]] and [[hypokrites]]). The recognition in the *Libation Bearers*, “someone like her has come; no one is like her but Orestes; so he has come”, is Aristotle's example of recognition by reasoning (1455a4–6; see [[libation-bearers]] and [[electra-recognizes-orestes]]). Those who have made “a Niobe, and not as Aeschylus did” fail in the competition: Aeschylus took one part of the story, not the whole (1456a16–19; see [[niobe-play]]). Aeschylus and Euripides wrote the same iambic line about Philoctetes' ulcer, and Euripides, by changing one word, made it beautiful (1458b19–24; see [[the-ulcer-that-eats-my-foot]]). Aeschylus' *Daughters of Phorcys* and *Prometheus* may be among the examples of the fourth kind of tragedy (1456a2–3; see [[daughters-of-phorcys]] and [[prometheus-play]]).
