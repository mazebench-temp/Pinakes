---
summary: Ganymede pours wine for Zeus, though the gods drink no wine.
grc: [οἰνοχοεύειν]
en: [pours wine for Zeus]
---

The verb for pouring wine is used of serving the gods their nectar, as smiths who work iron are still called bronze-smiths (1461a29–31; see [[aristotle-wine-of-the-gods]] and [[ganymede]]).
