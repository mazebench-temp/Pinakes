---
summary: The first of Aristotle's examples of metaphor, from genus to species.
grc: [νηῦς δέ μοι ἥδʼ ἕστηκεν]
en: [Here my ship stands]
refs: [1457b11]
---

Aristotle defines metaphor as the transfer of a word that belongs to something else, “either from genus to species, or from species to genus, or from species to species, or by analogy” (1457b7–9; see [[metaphora]]). This half-line is his example of the first kind, “for lying at anchor is a kind of standing” (1457b10–11): the general word “stand” replaces the particular ὁρμεῖν, “lie at anchor”.
