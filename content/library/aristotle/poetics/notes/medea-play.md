en: [the Medea]

“The resolutions of plots too must come about from the plot itself, and not, as in the Medea, from the machine” (1454a37–b1; see [[the-machine]]). The unmotivated arrival of Aegeus in the play is probably the irrationality Aristotle criticizes at 1461b20–21 (see [[aegeus]]).
