---
summary: An irrational element that Sophocles put inside the drama instead of outside it.
refs: [1460a31]
---

The irrational should be outside the plot, “as with Oedipus' not knowing how Laius died, and not in the drama, like those who report the Pythian games in the Electra” (1460a28–31; see [[alogon]]). Aristotle does not say what is irrational in the report; the usual explanation is the anachronism, since the Pythian games were founded long after the heroic age.
