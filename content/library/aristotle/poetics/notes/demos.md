---
summary: The Athenian word for the villages of Attica, set against the Dorian κῶμαι, and the democracy of the Megarians.
grc: [δήμους, δημοκρατίας]
en: [dēmoi, democracy]
---

The Megarians claim that comedy arose under their democracy, and the Dorians derive the name from their word for villages, κῶμαι, where the Athenians say δῆμοι (1448a31–37; see [[komos]] and [[the-origins-of-comedy]]).
