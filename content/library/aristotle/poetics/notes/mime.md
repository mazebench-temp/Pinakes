“We would have no common name to give the mimes of Sophron and Xenarchus and the Socratic dialogues” (1447b9–11): both are imitations in prose, which shows that the art of imitation in words is not defined by meter (see [[sophron]], [[xenarchus]] and [[socratic-dialogues]]).
