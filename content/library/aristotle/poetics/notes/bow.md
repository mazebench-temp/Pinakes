---
summary: The bow that only Odysseus could string, in a tragedy about him.
grc: [τόξον]
en: [the bow]
---

In *Odysseus the False Messenger*, that Odysseus alone strings the bow is a premise contrived by the poet, and so is his saying that he would recognize the bow, which he had not seen; the play builds a false inference on it (1455a13–16; see [[odysseus-the-false-messenger]] and [[paralogismos]]).
