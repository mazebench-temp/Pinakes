en: [interludes]
---

“In the other poets the songs belong no more to the plot than to any other tragedy; that is why they sing interludes, a practice first begun by Agathon. Yet what difference is there between singing interludes and fitting a speech from one play into another, or a whole episode?” (1456a27–32; see [[chorus]] and [[agathon]]).
