---
summary: An irrational element placed inside the drama instead of outside it.
refs: [1460a32]
---

The irrational should be outside the plot, “and not in the drama, like those who report the Pythian games in the Electra, or the man in the Mysians who comes without a word from Tegea to Mysia” (1460a28–32; see [[alogon]] and [[the-false-report-of-orestes-death]]). Aristotle does not say what is irrational in it, presumably that a man could make so long a journey without speaking to anyone.
