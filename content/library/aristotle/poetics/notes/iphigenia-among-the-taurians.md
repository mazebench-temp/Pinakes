---
summary: The Iphigenia is the second model play of the Poetics: mutual recognition, the deed about to be done and recognized in time, the probable letter, and a plot outlined in general terms.
grc: [Ἰφιγενείᾳ, οἷον τῆς Ἰφιγενείας]
en: [the Iphigenia]
---

The play is cited for a mutual recognition, in which Iphigenia is known by the letter and Orestes needs another proof (1452b5–8); for the best kind of tragic deed, “in the Iphigenia, the sister her brother”, recognized just before the sacrifice (1454a7; see [[the-best-tragic-plot]]); for the inartistic part of the recognition, where Orestes “himself says what the poet wants” (1454b30–35); and for the best kind, arising from the incidents, “for it is probable that she should wish to send a letter” (1455a16–19; see [[epistle]]). Chapter 17 sets out its general outline before names and episodes (1455b2–15; see [[the-recognition-of-orestes-and-iphigenia]] and [[katharsis]]).
