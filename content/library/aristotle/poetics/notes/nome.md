grc: [νόμων, νόμους]
en: [nomes]
---

Nomes, like dithyrambs, use rhythm, song and meter all at once (1447b24–28), and like them may portray their subjects better or worse (1448a14–16). The corrupt word before “Cyclopes” at 1448a15 may have named a nome, perhaps Timotheus' *Persians* (see [[cyclops-dithyrambs]]).
