“A verb is a composite significant sound with time, no part of which has meaning by itself, as with nouns: ‘man’ or ‘white’ does not signify when, but ‘walks’ or ‘has walked’ signifies in addition, the one the present time, the other the past” (1457a14–18). A statement can lack verbs, like the definition of man (1457a24–26; see [[logos]]).
