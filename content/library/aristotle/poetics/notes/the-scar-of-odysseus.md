---
summary: Aristotle's example of a sign that can be used better or worse.
grc: [οὐλῆς, οὐλαί]
en: [scars, scar]
---

Scars are the first of the acquired signs, those on the body (1454b24). Odysseus was recognized through his scar in one way by the nurse and in another by the swineherds: the recognition that comes out of a reversal, as in the Bath, is better than one made for proof (1454b25–30; see [[semeion]] and [[anagnorisis]]).
