---
summary: The rite of purification by which Orestes and Iphigenia escape in the Iphigenia among the Taurians.
refs: [1455b15]
---

The episodes of the *Iphigenia* must be proper to it, as are the madness through which Orestes was caught “and the rescue through the purification” (1455b14–15): Iphigenia pretends that the stranger and the image must be cleansed in the sea, and so they reach the ship (see [[the-recognition-of-orestes-and-iphigenia]] and [[katharsis]]).
