---
summary: The comic mask illustrates the ridiculous, an ugliness without pain; who introduced masks into comedy is not known.
grc: [πρόσωπα, γελοῖον πρόσωπον]
en: [mask, masks, the comic mask]
---

The ridiculous is a part of the ugly, “an error or ugliness that causes no pain and does no harm; to take the obvious example, the comic mask is something ugly and distorted without pain” (1449a34–37; see [[geloion]]). The history of comedy is obscure: “who introduced masks or prologues or the number of actors and all such things is not known” (1449b4–5; see [[the-origins-of-comedy]]).
