---
summary: Aristotle's example of an episode by which Homer varied the Iliad without making the whole war his plot.
grc: [νεῶν καταλόγῳ]
en: [the catalogue of ships]
---

Homer did not try to make the whole war into a poem: “as it is, he has taken one part and used many of the others as episodes, such as the catalogue of ships and other episodes, with which he divides up his poem” (1459a35–37; see [[epeisodion]] and [[unity-of-action]]).
