---
summary: A subject too large for one tragedy, and the title of plays made from it.
refs: [1456a16-18, 1459b7]
---

All who made “the whole Sack of Troy, and not a part of it as Euripides did”, either failed or fared badly in the competition, and even Agathon failed in this alone (1456a15–19; see [[agathon]] and [[epic-and-tragedy]]). The Sack of Troy and the Trojan Women are also titles in the list of tragedies drawn from the Little Iliad (1459b7; see [[sack-of-troy]] and [[trojan-women-play]]).
