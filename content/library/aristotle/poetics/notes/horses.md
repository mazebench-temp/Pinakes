---
summary: A painted horse with both right legs thrown forward is Aristotle's example of an error that does not belong to the art itself.
grc: [ἵππον]
en: [a horse]
---

If the poet chose wrongly, “such as a horse with both right legs thrown forward at once”, or erred in some particular art such as medicine, the error does not belong to the art of poetry itself (1460b15–21; see [[problems-and-solutions]]). The pace, in which a horse moves both legs on one side together, is in fact a natural gait of some horses.
