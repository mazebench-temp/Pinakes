---
summary: Protagoras faulted the Iliad's invocation for giving the goddess an order.
---

The first words of the Iliad, “Sing, goddess, the wrath”, are quoted for Protagoras' complaint that Homer, thinking to pray, gives an order; Aristotle sets the question aside as belonging to the art of delivery (1456b15–19; see [[protagoras-on-the-first-line]] and [[forms-of-utterance]]).
