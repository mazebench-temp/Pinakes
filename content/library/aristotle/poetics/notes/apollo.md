---
summary: The god who sends Orestes to the Taurians, whose reason Aristotle puts outside the plot.
refs: [1455b7]
---

In the outline of the *Iphigenia*, “that the god ordered him … to go there, and for what purpose, lies outside the plot” (1455b7–8; see [[the-recognition-of-orestes-and-iphigenia]]). The god is Apollo, who sends Orestes to bring back the image of Artemis.
