---
summary: "Signify", the verb of Aristotle's theory of language.
---

A noun or verb is a sound that signifies, and a statement is one either by signifying one thing or by being bound together (1457a10–30; see [[onoma]], [[rhema]] and [[logos]]). When a word seems to signify a contradiction, one must consider in how many ways it might signify in the passage (1461a31–33; see [[the-bronze-spear-stopped]]).
