---
summary: Πηληιάδεω, the patronymic of the first line of the Iliad, is Aristotle's example of a lengthened word.
---

A word is lengthened if it has a longer vowel than its own or an inserted syllable: “as poleōs becomes polēos, and Pēleidou Pēlēiadeō” (1458a1–4; see [[epektetamenon]]). The form is Homer's, from the first line of the Iliad: Πηληϊάδεω Ἀχιλῆος (1.1).
