---
summary: The cup of Dionysus in Aristotle's metaphor of analogy.
grc: [φιάλ*]
en: [cup]
---

“The cup stands to Dionysus as the shield to Ares; so the poet will call the cup ‘the shield of Dionysus’ and the shield ‘the cup of Ares’” (1457b20–22), or call the shield a cup that is “not of Ares but wineless” (1457b32; see [[the-cup-of-ares]] and [[metaphora]]).
