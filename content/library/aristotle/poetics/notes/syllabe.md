“A syllable is a sound without meaning, composed of a mute and a letter that has sound; for GR without A is †a syllable, and† so it is with A, as GRA” (1456b34–37). The text is corrupt, and the point disputed: whether GR without a vowel counts as a syllable. A lengthened word has a longer vowel than its own “or has a syllable inserted” (1458a1–2; see [[epektetamenon]]).
