“Arētēr (‘one who prays’) for priest” is a coined word (1457b35; see [[pepoiemenon]]); Homer calls Chryses an ἀρητήρ at the opening of the Iliad (1.11). In the outline of the *Iphigenia*, the girl “came to hold this priesthood”, and later the brother of the priestess happened to come (1455b5–7; see [[the-recognition-of-orestes-and-iphigenia]]).
