Chapter 16 ranks the kinds of recognition. The first and “least artistic”, the one most used “for want of resource”, is recognition through signs (1454b19–21; see [[semeion]] and [[anagnorisis]]). Signs are either congenital or acquired. The congenital are illustrated by this line and by the stars that Carcinus used in his *Thyestes* (1454b22–23; see [[thyestes-play]]); the acquired by scars, necklaces, and the little boat in the *Tyro* (1454b24–25; see [[the-scar-of-odysseus]] and [[the-boat-of-tyro]]).
