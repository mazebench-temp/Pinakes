---
summary: A line from Aeschylus' Philoctetes, rewritten by Euripides, and a Philoctetes among the tragedies made from the Little Iliad.
---

Aeschylus and Euripides wrote the same iambic line about Philoctetes' wound with one word changed, and Euripides' rare word made it beautiful (1458b22–24; see [[the-ulcer-that-eats-my-foot]]). The *Philoctetes* is the second of the tragedies Aristotle lists from the Little Iliad (1459b5; see [[philoctetes-brought-from-lemnos]] and [[philoctetes-plays]]).
