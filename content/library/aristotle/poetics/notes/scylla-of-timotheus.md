---
grc: [Σκύλλῃ, Σκύλλαν]
en: [the Scylla]
---

“The lament of Odysseus in the Scylla” is unseemly and inappropriate (1454a30–31; see [[four-aims-of-character]] and [[lament]]), and bad pipers drag the chorus leader about “if they are piping the Scylla”, miming the monster's grip (1461b31–32; see [[aulos]]).
