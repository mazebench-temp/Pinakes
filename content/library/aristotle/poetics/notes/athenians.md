---
summary: The Dorians argued for their claim to drama from Athenian words: the Athenians call villages dēmoi, not kōmai, and say prattein, not dran.
grc: [Ἀθηναίους]
---

The Dorians “say that they call the outlying villages kōmai, while the Athenians call them dēmoi … and that for ‘doing’ they themselves say dran, while the Athenians say prattein” (1448a35–b2; see [[dorians]], [[komos]] and [[drama]]).
