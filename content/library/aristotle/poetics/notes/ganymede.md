---
summary: Ganymede pours wine for Zeus, though the gods drink no wine: a problem solved by the custom of the language.
---

“Those who work iron are called bronze-smiths, and so it is said that Ganymede ‘pours wine for Zeus’, though the gods do not drink wine” (1461a29–31; see [[aristotle-wine-of-the-gods]] and [[cupbearing]]).
