---
summary: The goddess of the Taurians, to whom strangers were sacrificed.
refs: [1455b5]
---

In the outline of the *Iphigenia*, the girl was settled in a land “where it was the custom to sacrifice strangers to the goddess”, and became her priestess (1455b4–6; see [[human-sacrifice]] and [[the-recognition-of-orestes-and-iphigenia]]). The goddess is Artemis, whom the Greeks identified with the maiden goddess of the Taurians.
