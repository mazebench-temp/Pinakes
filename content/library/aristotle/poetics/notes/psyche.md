---
summary: The plot is the soul of tragedy; and the mind is misled by false inference.
grc: [ψυχὴ]
en: [mind]
---

The plot is “the first principle and, as it were, the soul of tragedy” (1450a38–39; see [[the-soul-of-tragedy]]). When a true consequence is added to a false premise, “our mind falsely infers that the first is true as well” (1460a24–25; see [[paralogismos]]). In a quoted phrase, bronze “draws off the life” (1457b14; see [[drawing-off-life-with-bronze]]), and the power of tragedy to sway the soul is its ψυχαγωγία (see [[psychagogia]]).
