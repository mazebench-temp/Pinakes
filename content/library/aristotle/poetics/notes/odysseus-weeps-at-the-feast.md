---
summary: Aristotle's example of recognition through memory.
refs: [1455a2-3]
---

The third kind of recognition is through memory, when someone feels something on seeing or hearing it, as “in the tale told to Alcinous, where, hearing the lyre player, he remembered and shed tears, and so they were recognized” (1454b37–1455a3; see [[anagnorisis]] and [[the-tale-to-alcinous]]).
