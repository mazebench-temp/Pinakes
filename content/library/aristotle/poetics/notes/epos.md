---
summary: In the Poetics the plural ἔπη means epic poetry or epic verse, as in “tragedies and epics”.
grc: [ἐπῶν]
en: [epics, epic verse, epic]
---

The plural of ἔπος names hexameter poetry: poets became producers of tragedies “instead of epics” (1449a5), the early tragedians did the same with time “as in epics” (1449b15–16), and whoever knows about tragedy “knows about epics too” (1449b18). The fitting use of words shows best “in epic verse” (1458b16), Hector's pursuit goes unnoticed “in epic” (1460a17), and Sophocles' *Oedipus* would be diluted if put “into as many verses as the Iliad has” (1462b3; see [[epic]]).
