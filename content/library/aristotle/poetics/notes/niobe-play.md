---
grc: [Νιόβην]
en: [Niobe]
---

An epic structure should not be made into a tragedy: those who have made “the whole Sack of Troy … or Niobe, and not as Aeschylus did, either fail or fare badly in the competition” (1456a15–18). Aeschylus, like Euripides with Troy, took a part of the story (see [[sack-of-troy]]).
