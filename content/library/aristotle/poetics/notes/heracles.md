---
summary: The poets of a Heracleid thought that because Heracles was one man, his story must be one plot.
---

Some poets have gone wrong in composing a *Heracleid*, a *Theseid* and such poems, “for they think that since Heracles was one, the plot too must be one” (1451a19–22). Many things happen to one man that make no unity; Homer saw this, and built the Odyssey around one action (see [[heracleid]] and [[unity-of-action]]).
