---
summary: A tragedy named after Peleus is Aristotle's example of the tragedy of character, and his patronymic of a lengthened word.
---

The tragedy of character is illustrated by “the Women of Phthia and the Peleus” (1456a1–2; see [[peleus-play]] and [[women-of-phthia]]), and the patronymic Πηλείδου, “son of Peleus”, lengthened to Πηληιάδεω, is the example of a lengthened word (1458a4; see [[son-of-peleus]]).
