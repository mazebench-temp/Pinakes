---
summary: The sea battle at Salamis, Aristotle's example of an event that shares a time with another without sharing its end.
grc: [Σαλαμῖνι]
---

The sea battle at Salamis and the battle with the Carthaginians in Sicily were fought at the same time but tended to no one end, as the events in a history do (1459a24–29; see [[salamis-and-himera]] and [[poetry-and-history]]).
