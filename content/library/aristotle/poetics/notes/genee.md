---
summary: In the Poetics γένος is the genus of the theory of metaphor, a class of people, or a family.
grc: [γένους, γενῶν]
en: [genus, families, class]
---

Metaphor may go from genus to species, from species to genus, or from species to species (1457b7–16; see [[metaphora]] and [[eidos]]). Goodness of character is possible “in every class”, even in women and slaves (1454a20), and the tragic heroes come from distinguished families like those of Oedipus and Thyestes (1453a11; see [[the-few-houses]]).
