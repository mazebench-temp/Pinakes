In a compound noun “we do not use a part as having a meaning of its own: in Theodorus, for example, the -dōros means nothing” (1457a12–14; see [[onoma]]). Chapter 21:

{{quote:607bd1f8-21eb-5980-b934-93cf99497767}}

The many-part names of the Massaliots are lost but for one, which is itself a river list (see [[hermocaicoxanthus]] and [[massaliots]]). “Double words suit dithyrambs best” (1459a9; see [[dithyramb]]), and it is a great thing to use them fittingly (1459a4–5).
