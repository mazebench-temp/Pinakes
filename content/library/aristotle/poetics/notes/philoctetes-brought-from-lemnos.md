The Philoctetes is the second play in the list of tragedies drawn from the Little Iliad (1459b5; see [[philoctetes-plays]] and [[little-iliad]]). Aristotle quotes a line of Aeschylus' version, and Euripides' rewriting of it, in the chapter on diction (1458b22–24; see [[the-ulcer-that-eats-my-foot]]).
