---
summary: Her death at the hands of Orestes is Aristotle's example of a traditional story that the poet cannot undo.
grc: [Κλυταιμήστραν]
---

“The stories that have been handed down cannot be undone, I mean such as the death of Clytemnestra at the hands of Orestes” (1453b22–24; see [[orestes-kills-clytemnestra]] and [[traditional-stories]]). Kassel prints the spelling Κλυταιμήστρα, without the ν of our manuscripts of Homer, the form found on Attic vases and probably the older one.
