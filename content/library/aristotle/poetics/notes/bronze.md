Those who work iron are still called bronze-smiths, χαλκέας (1461a29; see [[aristotle-wine-of-the-gods]] and [[iron]]). Bronze appears in a riddle, the cupping glass welded on with fire (1458a29–30; see [[the-riddle-of-the-cupping-glass]]), in a phrase of Empedocles, “with the bronze drawing off his life” (1457b13–14; see [[drawing-off-life-with-bronze]]), and in Aristotle's version of a Homeric line, “there the bronze spear was stopped” (1461a33–34; see [[the-bronze-spear-stopped]]).
