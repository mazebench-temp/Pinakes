A composite recognition can be made “from a false inference by the audience”, as in *Odysseus the False Messenger* (1455a12–16; see [[odysseus-the-false-messenger]]). Chapter 24 explains the mechanism:

{{quote:7a6deac2-9fc7-5069-86b7-f5d2fd422ebc}}

The passage from the Bath (Odyssey 19) is the example: the disguised Odysseus describes clothes and a brooch that Odysseus wore, and Penelope, knowing the description to be true, infers that the stranger really entertained her husband (see [[the-bath]] and [[homer-taught-lies]]).
