grc: [Ἰλίου πέρσις, πέρσιν Ἰλίου]
en: [Sack of Troy]
---

An epic structure should not be made into a tragedy: “all who have made the whole Sack of Troy, and not a part of it as Euripides did, or Niobe, and not as Aeschylus did, either fail or fare badly in the competition; even Agathon failed in this alone” (1456a15–19; see [[agathon]]). The “Sack of Troy” is also one of the tragedies made from the *Little Iliad* (1459b7; see [[little-iliad]]).
