---
summary: An external token of recognition, a contrived sign.
grc: [περιδέραι*]
en: [necklaces]
---

Necklaces are the example of the external signs, “others external, such as necklaces, and as in the Tyro, through the little boat” (1454b24–25), and the best recognitions are the ones that do without contrived signs and necklaces (1455a16–21; see [[semeion]] and [[anagnorisis]]).
