---
summary: A greave of newly wrought tin, a problem solved by the custom of the language.
grc: [κνημὶς]
en: [a greave]
---

Mixed wine is still called wine, and so the poet composed “a greave of newly wrought tin” (1461a27–28; see [[aristotle-wine-of-the-gods]] and [[tin]]): the greave was of bronze with tin in it, and still called tin.
