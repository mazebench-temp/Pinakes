The first part of tragedy named in chapter 6 is “the adornment of spectacle” (ὁ τῆς ὄψεως κόσμος, 1449b32–33; see [[opsis]]). As a kind of noun the ornament stands fourth in the list of chapter 21 (1457b1–3), but the text passes from metaphor to the coined word without defining it (1457b33); a lacuna is generally assumed. In chapter 22 ornament keeps diction from being commonplace (1458a32–34), and with the standard word and metaphor it is one of the kinds fit for iambics, since one would use them in prose too (1459a13–14).
