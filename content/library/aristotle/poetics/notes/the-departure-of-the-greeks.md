summary: The probable subject of the Sailing Away, one of the tragedies made from the Little Iliad.
refs: [1459b7]
---

The Sailing Away closes the main list of tragedies drawn from the Little Iliad (1459b7; see [[the-sailing-away]] and [[little-iliad]]). A sailing away of another kind, the Achaeans' rush to the ships in Iliad 2, which Athena stops, is Aristotle's example of a resolution brought about by a god rather than by the plot (1454b2; see [[the-test-of-the-army]]).
