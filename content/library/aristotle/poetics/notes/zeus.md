---
summary: Zeus appears in the Poetics only in a Homeric problem about the wine that Ganymede pours for him.
---

Ganymede is said to “pour wine for Zeus”, though the gods do not drink wine (1461a29–31; see [[aristotle-wine-of-the-gods]] and [[ganymede]]). The opening of Iliad 2, where all others sleep but Zeus lies awake, is quoted in a problem about the word “all” (1461a16–20; see [[all-the-gods-slept]]).
