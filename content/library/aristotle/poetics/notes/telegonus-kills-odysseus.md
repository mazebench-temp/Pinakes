---
summary: The terrible deed done in ignorance inside the tragedy itself.
refs: [1453b29-34]
---

When the deed is done in ignorance and the relationship recognized later, it may lie outside the drama, as in Sophocles' *Oedipus*, or within the tragedy itself, “there is Astydamas' Alcmaeon, or Telegonus in Odysseus Wounded” (1453b29–34; see [[odysseus-wounded]] and [[the-best-tragic-plot]]).
