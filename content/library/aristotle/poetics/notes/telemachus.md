---
summary: The son plotted against in Aristotle's outline of the Odyssey, and the grandson who did not meet Icarius in Sparta.
refs: [1455b20-21]
---

In the outline of the Odyssey, the hero's property is being wasted by suitors and “his son plotted against” (1455b19–21; see [[the-return-of-odysseus]]). Critics thought it absurd that Telemachus did not meet his grandfather Icarius when he went to Lacedaemon (1461b4–6; see [[the-icarius-problem]]).
