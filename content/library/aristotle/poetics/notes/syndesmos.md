---
grc: [συνδεσμ*]
en: [connective, bound together, by being bound together]
---

“A connective is a sound without meaning that neither prevents nor produces a single significant sound out of several sounds … and that it is not fitting to put by itself at the beginning of a statement, such as men, ētoi, de” (1456b38–1457a4). The text of the definitions of connective and joint is confused, and the definitions seem to have been interchanged in transmission. A statement can be one “by being bound together out of several: the Iliad is one by being bound together” (1457a28–30; see [[logos]] and [[iliad]]).
