summary: The shield of the metaphor of analogy, "the cup of Ares", and the shield of Achilles that stopped Aeneas' spear.

The shield stands to Ares as the cup to Dionysus, so the poet may call the shield “the cup of Ares” (1457b20–23, 1457b32; see [[the-cup-of-ares]] and [[phiale]]). The five layers of Achilles' shield raise a problem in chapter 25 (1461a33–34; see [[the-bronze-spear-stopped]]).
