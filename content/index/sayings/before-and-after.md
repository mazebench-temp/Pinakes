πρόσσω καὶ ὀπίσσω: literally "forward and backward". In Homer the phrase describes the judgment of the wise. Menelaus asks that old Priam swear the oath, since an old man "looks before and after" (3.109). Polydamas "alone saw before and after" (18.250). Achilles denies Agamemnon exactly this faculty, which Calchas has in full (1.70). See [[foresight]].
