## The saying

In the third book Aristotle quotes the image again, among metaphors by analogy: Pericles said that “the youth who perished in the war had vanished from the city as if someone had taken the spring out of the year” (*Rhetoric* 3.10, 1411a2–4). Which funeral speech is meant is not said, and the image is not in the speech Thucydides gives Pericles for the dead of the war's first year (2.35–46; see [[pericles]]).

Herodotus gives the same image to Gelon of Syracuse. Refused the command of the Greek forces against Xerxes, Gelon sent the envoys home to tell Greece that the spring had been taken out of its year; Herodotus explains that his army was the best part of the Greek forces, as spring is the best part of the year (7.162; see [[gelon]]).
