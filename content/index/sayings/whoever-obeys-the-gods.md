{{quote:12990fc4-72d0-57c1-8324-f833dfc03b11}}

The maxim is a rule of reciprocity: obedience to the gods earns their attention in return. Achilles will soon test it, asking his mother to have Zeus listen to him. See [[reciprocity-with-the-gods]] and [[obedience-and-persuasion]].
