{{quote:d18d93c6-1657-5e66-bf69-7fa8d7aaedee}}

A general statement dropped into a practical proposal. Achilles lists three ways of learning a god's mind: the seer (μάντις), who reads birds and signs; the priest (ἱερεύς), who serves the god; and the interpreter of dreams (ὀνειροπόλος). The maxim explains why the last belongs on the list.

The next book tests it. Zeus sends Agamemnon a lying Dream (Iliad 2.1–40), and it does come from Zeus, though not to tell the truth. See [[onar]] and [[divination]].
