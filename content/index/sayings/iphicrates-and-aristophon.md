{{quote:72e597af-914a-5b49-9f56-601242954337}}

The retort belongs, it seems, to the trial of the generals after the defeat at Embata in 356 BCE, in which Iphicrates was acquitted (see [[aristophon]]). Aristotle notes the condition of the argument: it works only if the accuser is the likelier wrongdoer; against an Aristides it would look absurd (*Rhetoric* 2.23, 1398a8–10; see [[aristides]]). See [[iphicrates]] and [[topics-of-enthymemes]].
