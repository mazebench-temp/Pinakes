{{quote:5a2706ca-b8f2-53c2-8513-42e204015bf9}}

τὰ χερείονα, "the worse things", means both the quarrel and the mortals it concerns. From Olympus, human suffering is a spoiler of good company. The line sums up the gods' distance from the war they direct. See [[gods-and-mortals]] and [[quarrel-on-olympus]].
