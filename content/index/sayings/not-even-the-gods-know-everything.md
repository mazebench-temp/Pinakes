{{quote:128d3752-e4af-5134-a53b-2fe798a170ae}}

The topic from the more and the less: if something does not belong where it would be more likely to belong, it does not belong where it is less likely; and if it belongs where it is less likely, it belongs where it is more likely, as the man who beats even his father beats his neighbors (*Rhetoric* 2.23, 1397b11–19). See [[topics-of-enthymemes]].
