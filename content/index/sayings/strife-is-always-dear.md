{{quote:dd7d76f9-2011-5e4e-b482-bebbf82c121b}}

In Book 5 Zeus rebukes the wounded Ares with the same words: "Most hateful to me are you of all the gods who hold Olympus, for strife is always dear to you, and wars and battles" (5.890–891). The repetition puts Achilles in Ares' place, the one his leader cannot abide. In Book 1 the line is meant as an insult; set beside Book 5 it becomes a description of the war god himself.
