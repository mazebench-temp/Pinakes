The topic from the name collects puns: θρασύμαχος, “bold in fight”, is Thrasymachus' name used as an adjective; πῶλος is a colt, a jibe at a young and frisky speaker; δράκων is a serpent, and Draco's laws were known for their harshness. Conon's pun on Thrasybulus, “bold in counsel”, stands beside them (*Rhetoric* 2.23, 1400b17–23).
