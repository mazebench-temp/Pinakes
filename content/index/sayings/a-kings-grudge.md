A small treatise on royal anger in four lines. It distinguishes χόλος, the anger that can be "digested" (καταπέψῃ) for the day, from κότος, the grudge that is stored "in his chest" until fulfilled. Calchas is right about Agamemnon, and Achilles' wrath will prove him right about anger in general. See [[cholos]] and [[kotos]].

Aristotle drew on the Iliad's anger for his account of the emotion in the *Rhetoric*; see [[aristotle-anger]].
