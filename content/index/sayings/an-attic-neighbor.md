{{quote:bd585b9c-51a0-5e04-87d3-bb06b1d89388}}

In Thucydides the Corinthians describe the Athenians to the Spartans as men born neither to have rest themselves nor to allow it to others (1.70.9), and the proverb says the same in two words. Aristotle cites it as one of the proverbs that are maxims too (*Rhetoric* 2.21, 1395a18–20). See [[athenians]], [[attica]] and [[gnome]].
