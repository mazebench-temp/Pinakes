{{quote:9e16fe2c-8f83-554e-b99f-2667599f74a6}}

Caunus, founder of the city of that name in Caria, was loved by his sister Byblis and fled from her (Ovid, *Metamorphoses* 9.450–665; Parthenius, *Love Stories* 11). The proverb serves as an objection: there would be no such phrase if there were not wicked loves (*Rhetoric* 2.25, 1402a37–1402b3). See [[enstasis]].
