{{quote:bb42f5e9-44bd-5fd1-9d20-b82717c545e8}}

The topic from correlatives: if acting nobly or justly belongs to the one, being so treated belongs to the other, and if giving the order was right, so was carrying it out. Diomedon's retort works because selling and buying are correlatives; what is not shameful in the seller is not shameful in the buyer (*Rhetoric* 2.23, 1397a23–28). Aristotle adds the warning that the inference can mislead: a man may have suffered justly and yet not justly at your hands (1397a28–1397b1). See [[diomedon]] and [[topics-of-enthymemes]].
