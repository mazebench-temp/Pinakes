{{quote:7b07b6b8-d353-5137-a7cd-bae353fe421f}}

The joke rests on Aristotle's observation that “a revenge taken earlier on someone else also stops a greater anger against another”: let the people spend their anger on a new victim, and they will hear the defense calmly (*Rhetoric* 2.3, 1380b7–11). The case of Ergophilus, which follows, shows the same thing in the courts (see [[ergophilus]]). See [[philocrates]].
