{{quote:131ec8ea-3dfa-5445-82e2-91e3ce1fbff7}}

The retort sets Socrates' judgment, which neither could gainsay, against Plato's claim, and Aristotle gives it under the topic from a previous judgment, where it is not noble to judge against the judge (*Rhetoric* 2.23, 1398b20–33). What Plato had promised is not told. See [[aristippus]], [[plato]] and [[topics-of-enthymemes]].
