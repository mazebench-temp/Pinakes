**After the embassy.** Odysseus has brought back the refusal, and the kings sit in silence (9.693–695). Diomedes breaks it as he broke the silence after Agamemnon's proposal at the start of the night (9.29–31), with the same verse (9.31 = 9.696). He wishes the king had never begged Achilles, and leaves Achilles to fight when his heart and a god move him (9.697–703; see [[thymos]]). Then he turns from the man who refused to the army that remains, with the plainest advice of the book: eat, drink, sleep, and at dawn draw up the army before the ships and fight in the front yourself (9.704–709). μένος, “might”, and ἀλκή, “courage”, are the words for a fighter's strength and his will to stand (see [[menos]] and [[alke]]). Diomedes, who began the night defending his courage (9.34–39), ends it by saying that courage comes from bread and wine. The kings approve, pour libations and go to sleep (9.710–713).

**Book 19.** The verse returns when the quarrel ends. Achilles wants the army to fight at once, unfed; Odysseus answers that the battle will be long, and that a man who has not eaten cannot fight all day, while a man filled with wine and food fights with a bold heart until the end: “bid the Achaeans eat by the swift ships of food and wine; for in that is might and courage” (19.160–161). The second verse is Diomedes' (19.161 = 9.706), and the speech around it argues what Diomedes only stated (19.155–172). Achilles refuses to eat until Patroclus is avenged (19.205–214), and Athena, sent by Zeus, drops nectar and ambrosia into his breast so that hunger will not weaken his knees (19.347–354). The man who will not eat is fed by the gods.

**Wine and strength.** Hecuba offers Hector wine with the same thought in Book 6: “For in a weary man wine greatly increases might” (6.261). Hector refuses it in the same two words, μένος and ἀλκή: “for fear you unnerve me and rob me of my might, and I forget my courage” (6.264–265; see [[wine]]). Diomedes gives the kings wine at night to fight at dawn; Hector, in the middle of the day's battle, will not drink.
