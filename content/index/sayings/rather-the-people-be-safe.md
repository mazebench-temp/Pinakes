The line is the language of a good king, and Homer lets it stand. But the next line begins αὐτὰρ ἐμοί, "but for me", and the prize he demands turns his concession into the quarrel. It reads as Agamemnon's attempt to sound like a shepherd of his people. See [[kingship-and-authority]].
