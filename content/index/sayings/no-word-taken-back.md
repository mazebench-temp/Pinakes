παλινάγρετος = πάλιν (back) + ἀγρέω (take): "taken back". Three negatives, not revocable, not deceptive, not unfulfilled, define the nod as the strongest pledge among the gods. The Greeks' own word for such a sign is τέκμωρ (1.526). See [[tekmor]], [[neuo]] and [[thetis-supplicates-zeus]].
