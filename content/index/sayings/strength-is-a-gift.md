{{quote:1b40b17a-1ef0-5382-9398-f2b0a34d6773}}

Homer's heroes regularly credit the gods with their gifts, but here the idea is turned into a put-down. If the gods gave Achilles his strength, it earns him no special honor. Agamemnon's own claim rests on something he thinks the gods gave him too: kingship, the scepter, and "the glory Zeus has given" (1.279).
