{{quote:c2926c27-066e-5a0b-9bca-7eaae260bbb7}}

Robbing the dead was a byword for meanness. Aristotle lists among the shameful deeds “making a profit from petty or shameful things, or from the helpless, for instance the poor or the dead”, and the proverb sums up the type (*Rhetoric* 2.6, 1383b22–25). In the *Nicomachean Ethics* the dicer and the footpad are Aristotle's examples of sordid gain (4.1, 1122a7–11). See [[aischyne]] and [[kerdos]].
