**The terms.** Odysseus' speech to Achilles and Agamemnon sets out how the quarrel is to end: the gifts brought into the middle of the assembly for all to see, Agamemnon's oath that he never lay with Briseis, a rich feast in his huts, “so that you may have no lack of justice” (19.172–180; see [[the-gifts-of-agamemnon]], [[oath]], [[feast]] and [[themis]]). Then he turns to the king, and the maxim closes the speech. νεμεσσητόν is what others would blame (see [[nemesis]] and [[no-blame]]). ἀπαρέσσασθαι, “make amends”, is the verb of Nestor's counsel in Book 9 and of Agamemnon's offer, “I am willing to make amends” (9.112, 9.120 = 19.138), and Odysseus has just used it of the feast (ἀρεσάσθω, 19.179; see [[propitiation]] and [[amends-and-reconciliation]]). The maxim meets the fear that keeps a king from yielding: it takes nothing from a king's honor to pay what he owes (see [[basileus]] and [[kingship-and-authority]]). Calchas described the other kind of king in Book 1, who swallows his anger for the day but keeps a grudge until he has fulfilled it (1.80–83; see [[a-kings-grudge]]).

**Who was first.** ὅτε τις πρότερος χαλεπήνῃ, “when he was the first to grow angry”, ends three other verses, where the verb is ἀπαμύνασθαι, “ward off”: Hermes tells Priam that neither he nor the old man with him is young enough to ward off a man when someone is the first to grow angry (24.368–369), and Telemachus twice says the same of himself (*Odyssey* 16.71–72, 21.132–133). There the one who is first to grow angry is the man to be warded off. Here that reading does not fit, since Agamemnon began the quarrel, and Walter Leaf understands a king who has been the first to quarrel: he need not be ashamed to make amends. He reports Ameis and Hentze's reading, which joins βασιλῆα ἄνδρα, “to make amends to a man of royal rank”, that is, to Achilles, and calls the couplet “a not very skilful development of a conventional line” (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 19.182). The translation keeps the ambiguity of the Greek (see the translation's note on 19.182–183).
