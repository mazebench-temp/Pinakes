The text leaves unclear whether Empedocles is cited for “the evening of life”, for “the sunset of life”, or for both; neither phrase is found in his surviving fragments (see [[empedocles]]). Homer has the same thought without the figure: “on the threshold of old age” (ἐπὶ γήραος οὐδῷ, 22.60, 24.487).
