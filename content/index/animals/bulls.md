The bull is the grandest victim a Greek could offer, and bulls are especially Poseidon's. The Pylians sacrifice black bulls to him on the shore (Odyssey 3.5–8), and Nestor remembers offering one bull to the river Alpheus and another to Poseidon (Iliad 11.727–728).
