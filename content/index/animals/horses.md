Horses are the most prized animals of the Homeric world. They draw the heroes' chariots and are among the richest spoils and prizes.

{{notes}}

## Elsewhere in Homer

Achilles' own horses, Xanthus and Balius, are immortal, foaled by the Harpy Podarge to the West Wind (Iliad 16.148–151).
