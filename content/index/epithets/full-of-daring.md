## Meaning

πολυθαρσής = πολυ- (“much”) + θάρσος (“daring, boldness”; [[tharsos]]): “very bold.” Homer has it only in the phrase μένος πολυθαρσές, “a might full of daring”: Glaucus wishes the Trojans had it (17.156); Thetis puts it into Achilles before he calls the Achaeans to assembly (19.37); and Odysseus asks Athena to stand by him and put it into him against the suitors (*Odyssey* 13.387). See [[menos]].
