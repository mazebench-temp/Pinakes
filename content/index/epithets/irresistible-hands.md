## Meaning

ἄαπτος is usually analyzed as ἀ- (“not”) + ἅπτω (“touch, lay hold of”): “not to be touched, not to be withstood.” The phrase χεῖρες ἄαπτοι is a formula for the hands of great warriors in the Iliad.

## Use

{{quote:12ae56a3-8ec3-5809-a85b-5f2e39e57c55}}

Zeus' last word to Hera is a threat of physical force, and it works: she sits in silence, bending her heart to obedience ({{ref:Iliad 1.568-569}}). The threat of hands on Olympus answers an oath in the assembly, where Achilles swore that no one would lay “heavy hands” on Calchas (βαρείας χεῖρας, {{ref:Iliad 1.89}}).
