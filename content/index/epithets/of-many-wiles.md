## Meaning

πολύμητις = πολύς (“much, many”) + μῆτις (“counsel, cunning intelligence”): “of many counsels.” μῆτις is practical intelligence, the power to devise a way through a difficulty. The same noun gives Zeus his epithet μητίετα ([[counselor]]). In Homer πολύμητις belongs almost entirely to Odysseus; the Iliad gives it once to Hephaestus (Il. 21.355).
