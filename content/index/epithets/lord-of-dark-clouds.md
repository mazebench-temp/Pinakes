## Meaning

κελαινεφής = κελαινός (“dark, black”) + νέφος (“cloud”): “wrapped in dark clouds,” the storm god. The word is also used of blood, αἷμα κελαινεφές, “dark blood” (for example Il. 4.140), where the sense has faded to “dark.” Compare αἷμα κελαινόν in Achilles' threat to Agamemnon ({{ref:Iliad 1.303}}).

## Use

{{quote:291064b7-aa7c-5a11-82d3-6889243fc5d1}}

Achilles recalls Thetis' boast that she alone of the immortals warded off ruin from “the son of Cronus, lord of the dark clouds,” when the other Olympians wished to bind him ([[binding-of-zeus]]). The storm god's epithet sets up the paradox of the story: the master of the sky once needed a sea goddess to save him.
