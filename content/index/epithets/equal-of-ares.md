## Meaning

ἀτάλαντος (“of equal weight,” from τάλαντον, “scale, balance”; see [[equal-of-zeus-in-counsel]]) + Ἄρηϊ, dative of Ἄρης: “equal in the balance to Ares.” The comparison is with the war god in his own sphere. The formula closes the verse. It is given to Meges, Meriones, Hector, Patroclus and others, often in the fuller form θοῷ ἀτάλαντος Ἄρηϊ, “equal to swift Ares.” For Meriones there is a variant with the war god's other name: ἀτάλαντος Ἐνυαλίῳ ἀνδρειφόντῃ, “the equal of Enyalius, slayer of men” ([[enyalius]]).
