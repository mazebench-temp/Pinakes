## Meaning

ἠχήεις is formed from ἠχή (“sound, noise, echo”) with the adjective suffix -ήεις: “full of sound, resounding.”

## Use

{{quote:9cad1330-71d6-5e71-bf40-75b03d97b291}}

The echoing sea and the [[shadowy-mountains]] are the barriers that separate Achilles' home from Troy. The two epithets turn his argument into a landscape: so much land and water lie between Phthia and Troy that the Trojans could never have wronged him, so he has no quarrel of his own with them. He fights only for Agamemnon, and Agamemnon repays him with dishonor.
