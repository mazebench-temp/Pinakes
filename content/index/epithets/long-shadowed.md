## Meaning

δολιχόσκιος = δολιχός (“long”) + σκιά (“shadow”): “casting a long shadow,” an image of the long ash spear raised against the light. It is used only of the spear (ἔγχος), and always at the end of the verse.

The same verse, ἦ ῥα καὶ ἀμπεπαλὼν προΐει δολιχόσκιον ἔγχος, introduces the first throw in the duel of Hector and Ajax (Il. 7.244) and in the duel of Achilles and Hector (Il. 22.273). The first formal duel of the Iliad already has the language of the later ones ([[single-combat]], [[the-duel-of-paris-and-menelaus]]).
