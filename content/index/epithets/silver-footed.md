## Meaning

ἀργυρόπεζα = ἄργυρος (“silver”) + πέζα (“foot,” related to πούς): “with silver feet.” The image may be the gleam of a sea goddess' feet in the water, or divine radiance in general. Like [[god-of-the-silver-bow]], it gives a god a precious metal. In Homer it belongs to Thetis.
