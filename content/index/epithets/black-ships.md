## Meaning

μέλας (feminine μέλαινα) means “black, dark.” The hulls of Greek ships were coated with pitch to keep them watertight, which made them black.

## Use

Occurrences: {{ref:Iliad 1.141}}, {{ref:Iliad 1.300}}, {{ref:Iliad 1.329}}, {{ref:Iliad 1.433}}, {{ref:Iliad 1.485}}.

{{quote:2158de20-15a8-5d8a-af25-c0c9717bc7ed}}

The black ship frames the voyage to Chryse. Agamemnon orders it drawn down to the sea (141), the sails are stowed in it on arrival (433), and it is drawn up on the land on its return (485) ([[sailing-type-scene]]). At 329 the heralds find Achilles sitting beside his hut and his black ship, where he will stay for the rest of the book. At 300 he claims what lies beside “my swift black ship” as his own, which no one may take ([[swift-ships]]).
