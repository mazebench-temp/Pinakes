## Meaning

βωτιάνειρα = βωτ- (from βόσκω, “feed, pasture”; compare βώτωρ, “herdsman”) + ἀνήρ (“man”): “feeding men.” The land that feeds men also rears heroes.

## Use

{{quote:119775e7-7007-5efd-ae95-23e7475265d4}}

Two epithets fill the verse around the name: [[deep-soiled]] and “nurse of men.” The rich naming lets Achilles dwell for a moment on home in the middle of his tirade. Phthia returns fifteen lines later as the destination of his threat to leave ({{ref:Iliad 1.169}}).
