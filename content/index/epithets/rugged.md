## Meaning

τρηχύς is the Ionic form of τραχύς (“rough, rugged, jagged”). Homer uses it of rocky ground and stony coasts, and of a stone thrown in battle.

Odysseus' famous praise of Ithaca uses it: the island is τρηχεῖ’, ἀλλ’ ἀγαθὴ κουροτρόφος, “rugged, but a good nurse of young men” (Od. 9.27).

## Use

{{quote:9ffc8cb3-07d1-5bdc-9e58-ed1ffeaf54b1}}

Aegilips, somewhere in Odysseus' kingdom, is rugged; its exact place is unknown. In the Thessalian part of the Catalogue Olizon, in Philoctetes' realm, has the same epithet in the same form and place in the verse ({{ref:Iliad 2.717}}).
