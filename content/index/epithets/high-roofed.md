## Meaning

ὑψόροφος = ὕψι (“high”) + ὀροφή (“roof”): “high-roofed.” It describes the θάλαμος, the inner room of a house where the family keeps its treasures or where husband and wife sleep. Priam's storeroom is both “fragrant” and “high-roofed” (Il. 24.191–192; [[fragrant]]).

## Use

{{quote:d5c749fa-1901-54b4-a1f4-8697210ebe9f}}

Aphrodite leads Helen to Paris' house, and Helen goes into the high-roofed chamber where the goddess set Paris down (3.382). There she sits facing him, reproaches him, and yields (3.424–447). See [[house]], [[helen-and-aphrodite]].
