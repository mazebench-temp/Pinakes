## Meaning

ὑψόροφος = ὕψι (“high”) + ὀροφή (“roof”): “high-roofed.” It describes the θάλαμος, the inner room of a house where the family keeps its treasures or where husband and wife sleep. Priam's storeroom is both “fragrant” and “high-roofed” (Il. 24.191–192; [[fragrant]]). Its twin ὑψερεφής is said of the whole house, ὑψερεφὲς μέγα δῶμα, “great high-roofed house” (5.213, 19.333), and in the Odyssey ὑψόροφος too names the house of a homecoming, οἶκον ἐς ὑψόροφον (*Odyssey* 5.42, 7.77).
