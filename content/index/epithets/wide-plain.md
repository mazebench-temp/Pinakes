## Meaning

σπιδής occurs only here, in διὰ σπιδέος πεδίοιο, and is explained as “wide, extensive”; its derivation is not known. The translation follows the usual explanation.

## Use

{{quote:86f19102-62fb-51d4-9776-1b4d482743ff}}

The plain is the country of the Epeians between the Alpheus, by which the Pylians fought them at sunrise, and Buprasium, where the pursuit ended (11.733–760; [[rich-in-wheat]], [[alpheus]]). See [[nestor-and-the-epeians]].
