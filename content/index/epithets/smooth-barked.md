## Meaning

τανύφλοιος = τανυ- (from τανύω, “stretch”) + φλοιός (“bark”): with bark stretched tight, smooth. The first part is the one in τανύηκες, “long-edged,” and τανυήκεας, “long-pointed” ([[long-edged]]). The cornel, κράνεια, is named only here in the Iliad; in the Odyssey Circe throws acorns and cornel fruit to Odysseus' companions turned into swine (*Odyssey* 10.242).
