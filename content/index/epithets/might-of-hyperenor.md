## Meaning

βίη (“force, might”) + Ὑπερήνορος (“of Hyperenor”): Hyperenor himself in his strength, as in [[might-of-diomedes]], [[might-of-heracles]], [[might-of-eteocles]] and [[might-of-priam]]. For Hyperenor the phrase occurs only once (17.24). The translation renders it “mighty Hyperenor.”
