## Meaning

δῖος (feminine δῖα) goes back to the root of Ζεύς, the god of the bright sky. Of heroes it has come to mean “brilliant, noble” ([[brilliant]]). Of the sea it keeps closer to the old sense, “bright, gleaming.”

## Use

{{quote:7206c10c-f85e-59b2-bef4-3927a18f4773}}

The black ship and the shining sea stand in the same verse, one epithet for each ([[black-ships]]). The phrase εἰς ἅλα δῖαν, “into the shining sea,” is formulaic at the end of the line. Agamemnon's order begins the sailing sequence that runs through the book ([[sailing-type-scene]]).
