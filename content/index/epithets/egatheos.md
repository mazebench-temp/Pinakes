## Meaning

ἠγάθεος = ἠ-, ἀγα- (an intensive prefix, “very,” as in ἀγάννιφος, [[snowy-olympus]]) + θεῖος (“divine”): “very holy.” It is used of places: in the Iliad of Pylos (1.252), Lemnos (2.722, 21.58, 21.79) and the Nysean mount (6.133), and in the Odyssey of Pylos (Od. 2.308, 4.599, 4.702, 5.20, 14.180) and of Pytho, the sanctuary at Delphi (Od. 8.80). The translation renders it “sacred,” keeping “holy” for ζάθεος.

## Use

{{quote:0b90c7af-6e9e-569b-9d5c-62d62a310ebb}}

Nestor was born and raised in sacred Pylos with a generation now gone, and he rules among the third. The epithet adds dignity to his credentials before he speaks. Pylos is the place of his past, and he returns to it in his story: he joined the Lapiths “from Pylos, far away, from a distant land” ({{ref:Iliad 1.269-270}}; [[distant-land]]).
