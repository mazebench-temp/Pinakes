## Meaning

ἐΰθριξ = εὖ (“well”) + θρίξ, stem τριχ- (“hair”): “with good hair,” of a horse's mane. The translation keeps it apart from καλλίθριξ, “fine-maned,” which stands in the same place before ἵππους (see the translation's table of key words and [[fine-maned]]).

## Use

The Myrmidons drive “their well-maned horses” three times around the body of Patroclus before the funeral feast (23.13), and two of the charioteers make ready their teams for the race in the same verse, with a new name and a new number: Ἀντίλοχος δὲ τέταρτος ἐΰτριχας ὁπλίσαθʼ ἵππους, Μηριόνης δʼ ἄρα πέμπτος ἐΰτριχας ὁπλίσαθʼ ἵππους (23.301, 23.351). Compare [[single-hoofed]] and [[high-neighing]].
