## Meaning

ἀνεμοσκεπής = ἄνεμος (“wind”) + σκέπας (“shelter, cover”): giving shelter from the wind. The χλαῖνα, the heavy woolen cloak, was a garment against the cold and a blanket at night ([[cloak]]).
