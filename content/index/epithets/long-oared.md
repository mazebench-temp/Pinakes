## Meaning

δολιχήρετμος = δολιχός (“long”) + ἐρετμόν (“oar”): “with long oars”.

## Use

The Odyssey uses it six times: of Ajax's ships (*Odyssey* 4.499), of the ship on which, Penelope says, Odysseus left Ithaca (*Odyssey* 23.176), and of the one on which the beggar says he left Crete (*Odyssey* 19.339); and three times of the Phaeacians, in the whole verse Φαίηκες δολιχήρετμοι, ναυσίκλυτοι ἄνδρες, the Phaeacians of the long oars, men famed for their ships (*Odyssey* 8.191, 8.369, 13.166).
