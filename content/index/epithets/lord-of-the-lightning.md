## Meaning

ἀστεροπητής is formed from ἀστεροπή (“lightning,” related to ἀστράπτω, “flash”) with the agent suffix -της: “the lightener, he who hurls the lightning.” It is joined with [[olympian]] (Ὀλύμπιος) in a formula that closes the verse.

## Use

{{quote:f2497230-c6c9-511f-adb5-c4bdbda0887c}}

In Hephaestus' mouth the formula is a warning: the lord of the lightning could blast them all from their seats. The sentence breaks off before it says what would follow ([[aposiopesis]]).

At the end of the book the same phrase closes the day:

{{quote:95b0933f-f50b-5a86-8ad9-f21867ef468d}}

The terrible title is given to a god going peacefully to sleep beside Hera. The formula that Hephaestus used as a threat now marks the order that has been restored.
