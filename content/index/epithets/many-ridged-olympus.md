## Meaning

πολυδειράς = πολύς (“many”) + δειράς (“ridge, saddle,” related to δειρή, “neck”): “with many ridges.” It describes the massif of Olympus with its ranges and summits.

## Use

{{quote:c0955636-a76c-5439-8ca5-151557bd273f}}

The whole verse, “on the topmost peak of many-ridged Olympus,” recurs elsewhere in the Iliad (for example Il. 5.754 and 8.3). Here it sets the scene for Thetis' supplication. Zeus sits apart from the other gods on the summit, and she must climb to him ([[supplication-type-scene]], [[far-seeing]]).
