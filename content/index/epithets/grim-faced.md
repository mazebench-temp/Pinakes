## Meaning

βλοσυρῶπις = βλοσυρός (“grim, fearsome”; [[blosyros]]) + ὤψ (“eye, face”): “grim-eyed, grim-faced.” The ending -ῶπις is that of βοῶπις (“ox-eyed,” [[ox-eyed]]) and γλαυκῶπις (“gray-eyed,” [[gray-eyed]]). The compound occurs only here. Its first part describes Ajax “smiling with grim face” as he goes out to fight Hector (7.212), and Hector's eyes, blazing under grim brows as he rages at the ships (15.607–608).

The Gorgon's power is in her look. On the aegis is “the Gorgon's head, the head of the dread monster, dreadful and terrible” (5.741–742; [[aegis]]); Hector in the rout of Book 8 has “the eyes of the Gorgon or of Ares, ruin of mortals” (8.349; [[the-eyes-of-the-gorgon]]); and in the Odyssey Odysseus leaves the land of the dead in fear that Persephone will send up the Gorgon's head from Hades (Od. 11.633–635). See [[gorgon]].

## Use

{{quote:546f2f8f-ee4e-5113-8a6d-31d346daf248}}

The Gorgon is the center of the device on Agamemnon's shield, “set as a crown” on it, with Terror and Rout around her (11.36–37; [[terror]], [[rout]]). Terror and Rout are the companions of Ares (4.440, 15.119), and Rout crowns the aegis itself (5.739). See [[shield]], [[arming-scene]] and [[agamemnon]].
