## Meaning

ἐΰξοος = εὖ (“well”) + ξέω (“scrape, plane, smooth”): “well-planed, polished.” It praises the craftsman's finish on wooden things: the chariot, the yoke, spear shafts, and above all the bow. In the Odyssey it is the standing epithet of Odysseus' great bow, τόξον ἐΰξοον, which the suitors cannot string (Od. 21).

## Use

{{quote:c7b73d87-8a9e-50d6-a223-f7b0d99d5f5b}}

Agamemnon's order to prepare for battle pictures the day's labor: the warrior's shield strap soaked with sweat ([[man-covering]]), and the horse sweating “as it strains at the polished chariot.” The fine finish of the chariot sets off the hard work that is coming.
