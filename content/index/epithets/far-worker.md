## Meaning

ἑκάεργος is usually analyzed as ἑκάς (“far off”) + ἔργον (“work”): “who works from afar,” another way of describing the archer god. As with [[far-shooter]], the first element may instead be connected with ἑκών (“willing”), and the analysis of the whole word is disputed.

## Use

The three occurrences trace the resolution of the plague. Agamemnon names the god who must be appeased:

{{quote:1d5af7e4-0464-5ee1-8bae-af78add57f62}}

At Chryse the young Achaeans sing the paean to him all day, and he is pleased:

{{quote:af53eecd-4810-5d86-821a-1add0b12c5f5}}

And on the next morning he sends them home with a following wind:

{{quote:99eb5acb-f1be-537c-b3fa-594a8e1cbf35}}

The god whose arrows worked destruction from afar now works a safe voyage. The same title covers both.
