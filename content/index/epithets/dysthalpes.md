## Meaning

δυσθαλπής = δυσ- (“hard, ill”) + θάλπω (“warm”): “hard to warm,” of a winter in which no warmth is to be had. It is found only here in Homer (17.549). θάλπω is the verb of the suitors warming the bow at the fire (*Odyssey* 21.179, 21.184), and θαλπωρή, “comfort,” is what Andromache says she will lose with Hector (6.411–412). The compound is formed like [[wintry]] (δυσχείμερος) and [[ill-sounding]] (δυσκέλαδος). The adjective of icy cold, κρυόεις, has its own entry ([[chilling]]).
