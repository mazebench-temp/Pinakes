## Meaning

δουρί, dative of δόρυ (“spear”) + κλυτός (“famous,” literally “heard of,” from κλύω, “hear”): “famous with the spear.” κλυτός is from the same root as κλέος, fame as what is heard and told of a man ([[kleos]]). The spear is the chief weapon of the Iliad's heroes, and a man's reputation rests on it. Homer uses the phrase ten times, of Idomeneus, Tlepolemus, Meges, Diomedes, Odysseus and others.
