## Meaning

ποντοπόρος = πόντος (“the open sea”) + πόρος, πείρω (“crossing, pass through”): “crossing the sea.”

## Use

{{quote:09df390d-d212-5586-8090-6c4bff359a43}}

The epithet closes the four-line disembarkation, in which each verse begins with ἐκ, “out”: out go the mooring-stones, out go the men, out goes the hecatomb, and out of the seafaring ship steps Chryseis ([[anaphora]], [[sailing-type-scene]]). The ship has crossed the sea, and the girl is home.
