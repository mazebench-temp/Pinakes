## Meaning

ἀμβρόσιος = ἀ- (“not”) + βροτός (“mortal”), from the same root as ἄμβροτος (“immortal”) and ἀμβροσίη, the food of the gods: “immortal, divine.” Whatever belongs to the gods can be ambrosial: their food, their sandals, their hair, and the night that comes from them (νύκτα δι’ ἀμβροσίην, “through the ambrosial night”).

## Use

{{quote:640348fc-7fe1-56c7-a683-ce6bf661080e}}

The adjective opens the second line of the nod. The hair flows forward as the head bends, and the next words say it again in another form: “from his immortal head” (κρατὸς ἀπ’ ἀθανάτοιο; see [[immortals]]). Two words for deathlessness stand in two lines, framing a gesture that decides the deaths of many ([[dark-brows]]).
