## Meaning

ἀμφιστρεφής = ἀμφί (“around, on both sides”) + στρέφω (“turn”): “turning about, turned every way.” It occurs only here: the dark blue serpent coiled on the silver strap of Agamemnon's shield has three heads “turned every way, growing from a single neck” (11.39–40). Compare [[grim-faced]], the Gorgon on the same shield.

## Use

{{quote:546f2f8f-ee4e-5113-8a6d-31d346daf248}}

The serpent ends the description of the shield, as the serpents of dark blue enamel end the description of the corselet; there they stretch up toward the neck, three on each side, like the rainbows that Zeus sets in the clouds as a portent for mortals (11.26–28; [[corselet]], [[teras]]).
