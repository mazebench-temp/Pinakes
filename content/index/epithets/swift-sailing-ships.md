## Meaning

ὠκύπορος = ὠκύς (“swift”) + πόρος (“passage, crossing,” from πείρω, “pass through”): “going swiftly.” It shares its first element with [[swift-footed]] and [[swift-fated]].

## Use

{{quote:5d8d1425-21a2-571f-97a2-2870ee92caa3}}

{{quote:f3e30585-702e-5835-9c17-38647c20a88c}}

The phrase νηυσὶ παρήμενος ὠκυπόροισι, “sitting beside the swift-sailing ships,” occurs twice: first as Thetis' instruction, then as its fulfillment. Both times it describes stillness. The swift hero sits by the swift ships, which could carry him home ({{ref:Iliad 1.169-170}}) but do not move.
