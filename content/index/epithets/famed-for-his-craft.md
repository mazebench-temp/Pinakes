## Meaning

κλυτοτέχνης = κλυτός (“famous,” from κλύω, “hear”: “much heard of”) + τέχνη (“craft, skill”): “renowned for his skill.” It is one of a group of epithets that name Hephaestus' art; compare [[with-cunning-skill]] and [[lame-in-both-legs]] (περικλυτὸς ἀμφιγυήεις).

## Use

{{quote:4042e236-3350-5fbf-a485-befae7356dd0}}

The craftsman takes the floor when the gods are dismayed. His craft is not in question here; what he makes is peace, with a speech, a cup, and his own ungainly bustling as he pours the nectar ([[unquenchable-laughter]]). The epithet looks ahead to the end of the book, where the houses the gods sleep in turn out to be his work ({{ref:Iliad 1.606-608}}).
