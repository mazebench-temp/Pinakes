## Meaning

εὖ (“well”) + ναιόμενον (“inhabited,” from ναίω, “dwell”) + πτολίεθρον (“citadel, town,” an extended form of πτόλις, the epic form of πόλις): “a well-inhabited town.”

## Use

{{quote:a751e124-801c-5049-9c75-42d0d98d38da}}

Achilles speaks of the towns of the Troad that the army has sacked in nine years of raiding. The formula praises a town at the moment of its destruction, and it is in the division of such towns' spoils that Agamemnon always takes the larger share. The sack of one such town, Thebe, is how Chryseis came to the camp ({{ref:Iliad 1.366-369}}; [[hieros]]).
