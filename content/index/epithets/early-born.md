## Meaning

ἠριγένεια = ἦρι (“early, at dawn”) + γεν- (“be born”): “the early-born, child of the morning.” It belongs to Dawn alone and can stand by itself as her name.

## Use

{{quote:5f17e46d-dabc-5c20-8806-a61055da12dc}}

The epithet opens the dawn formula ([[dawn-line]]), and [[rosy-fingered]] closes it. Dawn is here a figure in the sky, born new each day. The day she brings is the day of return: the ship puts out from Chryse, and far-working Apollo sends a following wind ([[far-worker]]).
