## Meaning

νηκερδής = νη- (“not”) + κέρδος (“gain, profit”; [[kerdos]]): “without gain.” In the Iliad it is said only of the plan that Alcimedon thinks a god has put in Automedon's breast (17.469). In the Odyssey Eumaeus praises the beggar's story because he has not spoken a word without profit, ἔπος νηκερδές (*Odyssey* 14.509). The negative prefix νη- is the one of νηλής, “pitiless” ([[pitiless]]).
