## Meaning

θοός (“quick, nimble”) is the adjective that describes the Achaeans' ships as [[swift-ships]]. Of men it praises speed in attack: Ares is “swift” in the formula θοῷ ἀτάλαντος Ἄρηϊ ([[equal-of-ares]]), and a man is θοὸς πολεμιστής, a quick fighter. ταχύς (“swift, fast”) is the ordinary word for speed. In the Iliad it is fixed to the Locrian Ajax in the line-end formula Ὀϊλῆος ταχὺς Αἴας, “swift Ajax, son of Oileus” (seven times), which tells him apart from his great namesake. Speed is his gift: in the funeral games of Book 23 he runs the footrace and would have won, had Athena not tripped him (Il. 23.754–777).

## Use

{{quote:178e4953-dad3-587e-b806-70070e4cf292}}

The Locrian Ajax enters the Catalogue with his formula. The Abantes are θοοί, in a verse that also gives them their hair “long at the back” ([[long-haired]], {{ref:Iliad 2.542}}). And the leader of the Magnetes is θοὸς too, in a phrase that plays on his name: Πρόθοος θοός, “swift Prothous” ({{ref:Iliad 2.758}}). The name Prothoos means something like “running out in front.”
