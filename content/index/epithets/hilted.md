## Meaning

κωπήεις = κώπη (“handle, hilt”) + -ήεις (“furnished with”): “with a hilt.” κώπη is the hilt of a sword, as of Achilles' sword in *Iliad* 1, when he holds “his heavy hand on the silver hilt” and thrusts the sword back into its sheath (1.219–220). In the Iliad the adjective occurs three times: of the swords at the ship of Protesilaus, where it stands beside μελάνδετος, “black-bound,” found only there (15.713), and in the verse-end ξίφει κωπήεντι of two killings, by the son of Oileus and by Achilles (16.330–332, 20.474–475). The translation joins the two words of 15.713, “with black-bound hilts.” See [[sword]].
