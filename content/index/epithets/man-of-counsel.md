## Meaning

βουληφόρος = βουλή (“counsel, plan; the council”) + -φόρος (“bearing,” from φέρω): “one who gives counsel.” It is an epithet of the leaders who sit in council with the king. In Book 2 the Dream reminds Agamemnon that “a man of counsel” should not sleep all night (Il. 2.24).

## Use

{{quote:684f8632-dc33-5178-8115-f5f38e754f8d}}

Agamemnon names four men who qualify: Ajax, Idomeneus, Odysseus, and Achilles, the last with a hostile superlative ([[most-terrible-of-men]]). The command goes to Odysseus ({{ref:Iliad 1.311}}), the counselor par excellence, [[of-many-wiles]]. The mission needs a man who can speak well to an injured priest.
