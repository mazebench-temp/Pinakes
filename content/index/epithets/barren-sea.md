## Meaning

The ancient explanation analyzed ἀτρύγετος as ἀ- (“not”) + τρυγάω (“gather in the harvest”): “where no harvest is gathered,” the barren sea as opposed to the fruitful land. Others connected it with τρύω (“wear out”), making it “unwearied, restless.” The word also describes the αἰθήρ, the bright upper air (Il. 17.425), where neither sense is obviously right. The meaning remains uncertain; the translation follows the ancient view.

The shared formula joins the reconciliation with the god to the renewed quarrel among men. Compare the sea along which Chryses walked away, [[loud-roaring-sea]].
