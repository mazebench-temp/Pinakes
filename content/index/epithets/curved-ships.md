## Meaning

κορωνίς is related to κορώνη (“crow,” and anything hooked or curved like a crow's beak). It describes the upswept stern and bow of the ships.

## Use

{{quote:cdba331b-989e-545e-966c-6f0b0945ba6c}}

Achilles threatens to go home to Phthia “with my curved ships.” The ships of the camp are the means of the threat that runs through the book: departure. He does not carry it out; he sits beside the ships instead ([[swift-sailing-ships]]). Agamemnon answers the threat with contempt, “Run away, then” ({{ref:Iliad 1.173}}).
