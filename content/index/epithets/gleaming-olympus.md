## Meaning

αἰγλήεις is formed from αἴγλη (“radiance, gleam,” the brightness of the clear sky) with the adjective suffix -ήεις: “full of light.” It describes the bright upper air where the gods live, above the clouds.

## Use

{{quote:8d6d9997-d49c-526e-a498-bff487423ca4}}

The epithet sets two worlds against each other in a single verse. Thetis leaps from gleaming Olympus into the deep sea (ἅλα βαθεῖαν, [[deep-sea]]), and Zeus goes to his house. The bright height and the dark depth are the homes of the two parties to the bargain just sealed with a nod.
