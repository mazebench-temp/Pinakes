## Meaning

ποικίλος means “many-colored, dappled,” and so “elaborately wrought”: armor worked in different metals, chariots adorned with bronze, robes woven with patterns. A leopard's spotted skin is ποικίλη (Il. 10.30). The word can describe a mind as well: Odysseus is ποικιλομήτης, “of subtle wiles” (Il. 11.482).

## Use

When the lot has fallen, the two armies sit down in their ranks, “where each man's high-stepping horses stood and inlaid armor lay” (3.327; [[high-stepping]]). The armor was laid on the ground for the truce (3.113–115). Paris' own armor is ποικίλα when he arms to go back to the fighting in Book 6 (κλυτὰ τεύχεα ποικίλα χαλκῷ, Il. 6.504).
