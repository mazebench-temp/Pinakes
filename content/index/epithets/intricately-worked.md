## Meaning

πολυδαίδαλος = πολύς (“much”) + δαιδάλλω (“work with art, embellish”), from the family of δαίδαλον (“a work of art”) and of the name Daedalus, the craftsman who made a dancing floor for Ariadne at Cnossus (Il. 18.590–592): “richly wrought.”

## Use

Menelaus' spear “forced its way through the intricately worked corselet” of Paris (3.358; [[heavy-spear]]). The corselet is not Paris' own: he borrowed it from his brother Lycaon and fitted it to himself (3.332–333; [[lycaon-son-of-priam]]). The spear cuts his tunic and misses his flesh (3.359–360; [[black-doom]]). See [[corselet]].
