## Meaning

πάντοσε (“in every direction”) + ἴση, ἐΐση (“equal”): a shield “equal in every direction,” that is, round and evenly balanced. The lengthened form ἐΐση is the same adjective that describes the ships in [[balanced-ships]] (νῆες ἐΐσαι). The adjective once began with a digamma (ϝίσος), which is why the final vowel of πάντοσε is not elided before ἴσην in the text of Book 3. The formula ἀσπίδα πάντοσε ἴσην closes the verse.
