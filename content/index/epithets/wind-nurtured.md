## Meaning

ἀνεμοτρεφής = ἄνεμος (“wind”) + τρέφω (“nourish, rear”): “reared by the wind.” Homer uses it twice. Of Agamemnon's spear (11.256) it is usually explained of the wood, an ash grown tough in the winds of a mountain; the ash spear of Achilles was given to Peleus by Chiron, cut from the peak of Pelion (Πηλίου ἐκ κορυφῆς, 16.143–144; [[ash-spear]]). Of a wave it is the swell that the wind raises: Hector falls on the Achaeans as a wave swollen by the wind under the clouds falls on a swift ship (15.624–625).
