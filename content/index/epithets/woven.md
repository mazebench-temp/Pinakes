## Meaning

πλεκτός is the verbal adjective of πλέκω (“plait, twist”): “plaited, woven.” At the vintage on the shield of Achilles the girls and young men carry “the honey-sweet fruit in woven baskets” (πλεκτοῖς ἐν ταλάροισι, 18.568; [[the-vintage]]), and the Cyclops sets his curdled milk in the same baskets (πλεκτοῖς ἐν ταλάροισιν, *Odyssey* 9.246–247). Andromache throws from her head the plaited band of her headdress (πλεκτὴν ἀναδέσμην) when she sees Hector dragged behind the chariot (22.468–470), and in the Odyssey πλεκτή is a twisted rope (*Odyssey* 22.175, 22.192).

## Outside Homer

In the Hesiodic *Shield of Heracles* the dust is struck up beneath πλεκτοῖσιν … ἅρμασι, chariots of plaited work, and the hooves of horses (*Shield of Heracles* 61–63).
