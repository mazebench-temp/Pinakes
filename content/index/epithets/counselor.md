## Meaning

μητίετα is formed from μῆτις (“counsel, cunning intelligence”) with the archaic nominative ending in short -τα (see [[cloud-gatherer]]): “the one of counsel, the deviser.” μῆτις is the same quality that makes Odysseus πολύμητις ([[of-many-wiles]]). In Hesiod's *Theogony*, Metis is a goddess, Zeus' first wife, whom he swallows so that her wisdom becomes his own (886–900).

## Use

{{quote:5b1bd100-0b94-517c-abaf-e43ebcd07b90}}

Agamemnon claims that above all Zeus the counselor will honor him. The listener knows better. The proem has already said that the plan of Zeus (Διὸς βουλή, {{ref:Iliad 1.5}}) was being fulfilled, and the plan will honor Achilles at Agamemnon's cost ([[plan-of-zeus]]).

{{quote:50d5966d-7c53-5bf9-9de4-b5df333955ac}}

Thetis turns the epithet into a request, addressing Zeus as Ὀλύμπιε μητίετα Ζεῦ ([[olympian]]); the form μητίετα serves unchanged as a vocative. The plan she asks for is the one Zeus then devises. Hera will call him δολομῆτα for it, “schemer” ([[schemer]]), turning his μῆτις into δόλος (“trickery”).
