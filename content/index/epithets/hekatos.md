## Meaning

ἕκατος is probably a shortened form of the compound epithets [[far-shooter]] (ἑκηβόλος) and [[hekatebolos]] (ἑκατηβόλος), used as a name. The goddess Hecate (Ἑκάτη), who does not appear in Homer, has a name of the same shape. The connection is often suggested but not certain.

## Use

{{quote:54333287-465c-54b0-9acb-05a70ff66b19}}

Achilles uses the short form at the end of the verse, θεοπροπίας ἑκάτοιο, “the oracles of the Far-Shooter.” The phrase joins the archer and the prophet: the god who shoots the plague is also the god whose seer explains it ([[best-of-bird-seers]]).

## Outside Homer

Hesiod's *Theogony* praises Hecate at length (411–452).
