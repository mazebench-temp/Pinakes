## Meaning

πολυηγερής = πολύς (“many”) + the root of ἀγείρω (“gather, assemble”): “gathered many together.” It occurs only here. The allies of the Trojans came from many lands and spoke many tongues (2.803–804, 4.437–438), and their epithets say so: πολύκλητοι, “called from many lands” ([[called-from-many-lands]]), and the commoner κλειτοί, “famous” ([[famous]]). The translation gives πολυηγερέες a rendering close to that of πολύκλητοι. See [[trojan-allies]].

## Use

{{quote:881351d1-7c4e-5fbc-88b0-7ea9fc20ca92}}

Zeus has put Ajax to flight, and he gives ground slowly, like a donkey that the boys cannot drive from the grain ([[sluggish]], [[the-donkey-in-the-field]]). His pursuers are the whole Trojan army, “the high-hearted Trojans and their allies” ([[high-hearted]]), stabbing at the middle of his shield. He turns on them again and again and keeps them from the ships (11.566–574). See [[ajax]] and [[telamonian]].
