## Meaning

ὑψιβρεμέτης = ὕψι (“on high”) + βρέμω (“roar, resound”) + the agent suffix -της: “who roars on high,” the god of thunder.

## Use

{{quote:dacdba9f-2c2f-5703-aa63-cc413fdac444}}

Achilles names Zeus with two titles, Ὀλύμπιος ([[olympian]]) at the end of one verse and ὑψιβρεμέτης at the start of the next, where the run-over gives it weight. The grand titles make the complaint sharper: the god who thunders from heaven has not granted even a little honor to a short-lived man ([[short-lived]]). It is the first time Achilles speaks of Zeus as the source of his honor, and it leads directly to the request that Thetis carries to Olympus.
