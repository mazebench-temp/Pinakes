## Meaning

χαλκότυπος = χαλκός (“bronze”; [[bronze]]) + the root of τύπτω (“strike”): “struck by bronze,” of wounds dealt by bronze weapons. Homer has it only once: Achilles fears that flies will enter the body of Patroclus “through the wounds the bronze has struck” (κατὰ χαλκοτύπους ὠτειλάς, 19.25). The bronze that strikes is that of the formulas of killing, “the sharp bronze” and “the pitiless bronze” ([[sharp]], [[pitiless-bronze]]). See [[patroclus]] and [[flies]].
