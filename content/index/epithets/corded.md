## Meaning

τρητός is the verbal adjective of τετραίνω (“bore, pierce”): “bored through.” The wooden frame of a bed was drilled so that cords or leather straps could be stretched across it. Odysseus tells how he made his own bed: he bored it with a drill and stretched across it a strap of ox-hide dyed red (Od. 23.198–201). The phrase τρητοῖς ἐν λεχέεσσι recurs in the Odyssey for the beds of hosts and guests (for example Od. 3.399).
