## Meaning

πολιός means “gray, grizzled,” the color of old men's hair, of wolves, and of iron. Of the sea it describes the whitish gray of water whipped into foam near the shore.

Achilles sits apart by the gray sea and looks out over the boundless deep ([[boundless-sea]]). His mother rises from the same gray sea like a mist ([[like-a-mist]]). The color word, repeated from one scene to the next, joins the son on the shore to the mother in the water.
