## Meaning

δυσχείμερος = δυσ- (“hard, ill”) + χεῖμα (“winter, storm”): a place of hard winters. Dodona, the oracle of Zeus, lies in a high valley of Epirus. Homer gives the word only to Dodona, both times at the end of a phrase that names the place: the Enienes and Perrhaebians of the Catalogue “who had made their homes around wintry Dodona” (2.750), and Achilles' prayer to Zeus “ruling over wintry Dodona” (16.234). See [[dodona]].
