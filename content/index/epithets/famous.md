## Meaning

κλειτός is a verbal adjective from the family of κλέος (“fame, report”; [[kleos]]): “famous, renowned.” It is the standing epithet of the Trojans' allies, κλειτοὶ ἐπίκουροι (for example Il. 6.227), with fuller forms such as τηλεκλειτοί, “far-famed” (Il. 6.111).

## Use

{{quote:f33d8b09-db86-5ab5-9ca6-26eb273ec9dc}}

After Aphrodite has carried Paris off, Menelaus ranges the field looking for him. No one can show him where Paris is, and no one would have hidden him out of love: “he was hateful to them all, like black doom” ([[black-doom]], [[like-black-doom]]). The famous allies came to fight for the man they now will not shelter. See [[trojan-allies]].
