## Meaning

πολυστάφυλος = πολύς (“many”) + σταφυλή (“bunch of grapes”): “rich in grapes,” a wine country. Homer uses it only in the Catalogue. A related epithet of the same list is ἀμπελόεις (“full of vines”), given to Epidaurus ({{ref:Iliad 2.561}}).

## Use

{{quote:47736121-c8c1-5759-aa0a-6413c90e4751}}

Histiaea, in the north of Euboea, is “rich in grapes” in the list of the Abantes' towns, and Arne is so in Boeotia ({{ref:Iliad 2.507}}). The epithet is one of the few in the Catalogue that describe the land's produce, beside flowering Pyrasus, “the precinct of Demeter” ([[flowering]]), and the flocks of Arcadian Orchomenus ([[rich-in-flocks]]).
