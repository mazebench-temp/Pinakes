## Meaning

κρείων is an old word for “ruler, lord,” used in Homer as a title. It is the same word as the name Creon (Κρέων), “ruler.” Its etymology is uncertain. Besides Agamemnon, Homer gives the title to Poseidon and to other rulers.

With the adverb εὐρύ (“widely”) it forms the fuller title [[wide-ruling]] (εὐρὺ κρείων). The lines with the fuller title are listed under that entry.
