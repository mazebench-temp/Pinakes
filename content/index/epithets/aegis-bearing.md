## Meaning

αἰγίοχος = αἰγίς (“aegis”) + -οχος (“holding,” from ἔχω). The aegis is a divine object of terror, which Zeus lends to Athena and Apollo; shaken in battle, it routs armies. What it was and where its name comes from are disputed. It is usually connected with αἴξ (“goat”), as a goatskin. Ancient scholars also linked it with καταιγίς (“squall”), making it the storm cloud of the weather god.

Achilles greets her as the child of aegis-bearing Zeus ([[child-of-aegis-bearing-zeus]]), and when she has done her work she returns “to the house of aegis-bearing Zeus, among the other gods.” The epithet stands for the authority behind her.
