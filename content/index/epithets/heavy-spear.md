## Meaning

ὄβριμος means “heavy, mighty,” from the family of βρίθω (“be heavy”) and βριθύς (“heavy”). Homer gives it to spears and stones, and to warriors: Hector (Il. 8.473) and Achilles (ὄβριμʼ Ἀχιλλεῦ, Il. 19.408).

## Use

{{quote:8d137ebe-05e9-57c3-9a29-1644fa2267f4}}

Menelaus' throw: the heavy spear passes through Paris' shining shield ([[phaeinos]]) and his intricately worked corselet ([[intricately-worked]]), and cuts his tunic beside the flank (3.359–360). The two verses are used again for Ajax's throw at Hector (Il. 7.251–252) and for the spear of Socus that pierces Odysseus' shield (Il. 11.435–436). Compare Paris' throw, whose point bends back on Menelaus' shield (3.346–349; [[balanced-on-every-side]]).
