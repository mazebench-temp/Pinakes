## Meaning

Θεστορίδης = Θέστωρ + the patronymic suffix -ίδης: “son of Thestor.” The father's name may contain θεσ-, the element meaning “god” in θέσφατος (“spoken by a god, decreed”); if so, it is a fitting name for a seer's father. The etymology is uncertain.

## Use

{{quote:f4ba9e53-7f0a-588b-ad00-a9685f1dc979}}

The patronymic comes between the name and the professional title ([[best-of-bird-seers]]), the standard order of a formal introduction. Thestor is named nowhere else in Book 1. The Iliad also has another son of a Thestor, a warrior named Alcmaon (Il. 12.394).
