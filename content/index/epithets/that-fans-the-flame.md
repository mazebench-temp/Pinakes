## Meaning

εὔπρηστος = εὖ (“well”) + πρήθω, a verb both of blowing and of burning. Walter Leaf renders it “well-puffed” and compares the wind that blew into the sail (πρῆσεν, 1.481; *Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 18.471); the same verb is used of burning doors “with consuming fire” (πρῆσαι, 2.415). In the forge of Hephaestus the bellows, twenty in all, blew on the crucibles, “sending out every kind of blast that fans the flame” (18.470–471; [[forge]]).
