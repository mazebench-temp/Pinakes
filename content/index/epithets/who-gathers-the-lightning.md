## Meaning

στεροπηγερέτα = στεροπή (“flash of lightning”) + ἀγείρω (“gather”) + the old agent ending in short -τα: “he who gathers the lightning.” It is the twin of [[cloud-gatherer]] (νεφεληγερέτα), with the same ending and the same place at the end of the verse before Ζεύς. The lightning itself is Zeus' weapon, and his title as its wielder is ἀστεροπητής ([[lord-of-the-lightning]]); compare [[thunders-on-high]] and [[delighting-in-thunder]].
