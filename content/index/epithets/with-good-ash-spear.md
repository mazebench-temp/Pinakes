## Meaning

ἐϋμμελίης = ἐΰ (“good, well”) + μελίη (“ash tree,” and so a spear with an ashwood shaft; [[spear]]): “with a good ash spear.” The ash spear is the heroic weapon. Achilles' own is an ash from Mount Pelion, which Chiron gave to his father (Il. 16.140–144; [[chiron]]). Homer gives the epithet to Priam, in the verse καὶ Πρίαμος καὶ λαὸς ἐϋμμελίω Πριάμοιο; to the sons of Panthous, Euphorbus among them (Il. 17.9, 17.23, 17.59); and in the Odyssey to Nestor's son Peisistratus (Od. 3.400).

The epithet praises the old king as a spearman when the poem foresees his fall ([[priam]]).
