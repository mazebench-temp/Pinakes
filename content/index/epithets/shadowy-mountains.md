## Meaning

σκιόεις is formed from σκιά (“shadow”) with the adjective suffix -όεις: “shadowy, full of shade.” Of mountains it suggests wooded slopes, or ranges darkened by distance. The phrase οὔρεά τε σκιόεντα, “and shadowy mountains,” recurs in the Odyssey.

## Use

{{quote:5e411674-e4e2-5bbf-ba6e-bd08b1660620}}

Paired with the [[echoing-sea]], the shadowy mountains measure the distance between Achilles' home and Troy. Achilles is making a legal point, that the Trojans never wronged him, and he makes it with a landscape. The epithets bring home to the listener how far from home he is fighting, and for whom.
