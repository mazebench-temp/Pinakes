## Meaning

βίη (“force, might”) + Πατρόκλοιο (“of Patroclus”): Patroclus himself in his strength, as in [[might-of-diomedes]], [[might-of-heracles]] and [[might-of-priam]]. Both uses end a verse about the armor of Achilles: Hector speaks of “the fine armor I stripped from mighty Patroclus when I killed him” (17.187), and when Achilles faces Hector the narrator says that Hector's body was covered by the armor he had stripped from mighty Patroclus (22.322–323). The translation renders it “mighty Patroclus.”
