## Meaning

πολύκλυστος = πολύς (“much”) + κλύζω (“wash, dash against”): the sea that dashes in many waves.

## Use

The verse-end always names the open sea that sets a place or a people apart: the island of Pharos off Egypt (*Odyssey* 4.354), the Phaeacians, who in Nausicaa's words live far off in the sea at the edge of the world (*Odyssey* 6.204), and the sea in which, Odysseus says in disguise, all his companions perished (*Odyssey* 19.277).
