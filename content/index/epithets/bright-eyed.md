## Meaning

The second element is ὤψ (“eye, face”). The first is disputed. It may be ἕλιξ (“twisting, spiral”), giving “with rolling, lively eyes,” or a word for a color or a brightness. Ancient explanations varied, and modern ones still do. The translation renders it “bright-eyed.” The feminine ἑλικῶπις is rare; the masculine plural is part of a formula for the army, ἑλίκωπες Ἀχαιοί.
