## Meaning

ἀμφίκομος = ἀμφί (“around”) + κόμη (“hair”): with hair all around, of a bush thick with leaves. κόμη is also the leafage of a tree: Odysseus cut off the foliage of the long-leaved olive to make his bed (*Odyssey* 23.195). The word is found only here in Homer (17.677).
