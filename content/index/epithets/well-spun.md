## Meaning

ἐΰννητος = εὖ (“well”) + νέω (“spin”): “well-spun.” The young men of the dance on the shield of Achilles wear tunics “well-spun, shining softly with oil” (18.595–596). When Achilles gives back the body of Hector, his companions leave two cloaks and a well-spun tunic in the wagon to wrap it in (24.580), and in the palace of Alcinous fine, well-spun robes, the work of women, are spread over the seats along the walls (*Odyssey* 7.95–97). See [[tunic]] and [[weaving]].
