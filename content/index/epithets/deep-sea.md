## Meaning

βαθύς means “deep.” The noun βένθος (“depth”) comes from the same root, and *Iliad* 1 uses it for Thetis' home: she sits ἐν βένθεσσιν ἁλός, “in the depths of the sea” ({{ref:Iliad 1.358}}).

## Use

{{quote:fc279c64-9b3b-5248-a20a-2a3aed34096f}}

The adjective is separated from its noun by the verb, εἰς ἅλα ἆλτο βαθεῖαν, “into the sea she leapt, the deep one,” so that the leap happens between the two words. In the same verse stands the height she leaves, gleaming Olympus ([[gleaming-olympus]]).
