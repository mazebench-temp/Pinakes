## The pattern

μά is a particle of swearing, followed by the accusative of the god or thing sworn by: ναὶ μά (“yes, by …”) affirms, and οὐ μά (“no, by …”) denies. An oath is a “great oath” (μέγας ὅρκος) when it is sworn formally, with its witness named. The oath binds the one who swears to what he says, with the god or the object as guarantor, and breaking it brings the anger of the gods.
