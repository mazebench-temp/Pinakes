## The pattern

1. **Shock.** A speech or an event provokes a crisis.
2. **Division.** The character “ponders two ways” (διάνδιχα μερμήριξεν) in heart, mind or spirit. The formula has its own entry, [[pondering-two-ways]].
3. **Alternatives.** Two courses are set out as a double question, ἤ … ἦε, “whether … or”.
4. **Resolution.** One course seems better, and the character acts on it. Sometimes a god decides.

{{notes}}

## Elsewhere in Homer

Diomedes, torn two ways whether to turn and fight Hector, ponders three times, and three times Zeus thunders from Ida (Iliad 8.167–171).
