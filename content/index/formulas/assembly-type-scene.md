## The pattern

1. **Summons.** A leader calls the people to assembly (ἀγορὴν δὲ καλέσσατο λαόν), often through heralds.
2. **Gathering.** “When they had gathered and were all assembled” ([[when-they-had-gathered]]).
3. **Speeches.** Each speaker stands up (ἀνιστάμενος), speaks, and sits down, and the next one stands ([[sat-down-stood-up]]). The speaker holds the [[scepter|scepter]].
4. **Dissolution.** The assembly is “loosed” (λῦσαν δʼ ἀγορήν), and the people scatter.

{{notes}}

## Elsewhere in Homer

A herald puts the scepter into the speaker's hand (Iliad 23.567–568; Odyssey 2.37–38). Achilles calls an assembly again in Book 19, to renounce his wrath (19.40–41), and this time he dissolves it himself (19.276).
