## The pattern

ὅ σφιν (“he, to them”) + ἐὺ φρονέων (“thinking well,” with good intent toward them) + ἀγορήσατο (“spoke in the assembly,” from ἀγοράομαι) + καὶ μετέειπεν (“and spoke among them”). The line vouches for the speaker's good will. It comes after a description of the speaker's credentials and just before his words. The Iliad uses it again for Nestor and for other counselors, such as Polydamas, Hector's cautious adviser, in Book 18.
