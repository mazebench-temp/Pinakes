## The pattern

ὧδε γὰρ ἐξερέω (“for I will speak out thus”) + τὸ δὲ καὶ τετελεσμένον ἔσται (“and it will surely be accomplished”), from τελέω (“bring to its end, fulfill”). The perfect participle makes the future certain: “it will be a thing done.” A weaker variant substitutes the speaker's opinion: τὸ δὲ καὶ τελέεσθαι ὀΐω, “and I think it will be fulfilled.”
