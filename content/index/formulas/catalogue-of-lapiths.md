## The pattern

A catalogue is a list of names, each with a brief attribute: a patronymic, an epithet, a home. Epic uses catalogues to measure and to honor; the great example is the Catalogue of Ships in Book 2. Short catalogues appear inside speeches, where a list of names carries the authority of the past.

## Outside Homer

The battle of Lapiths and Centaurs became one of the favorite subjects of Greek art. It fills the west pediment of the temple of Zeus at Olympia and the south metopes of the Parthenon. Ovid tells the story at length in Book 12 of the *Metamorphoses*, and he gives it to the aged Nestor to tell.
