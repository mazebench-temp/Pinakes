## The pattern

ποῖον (“what sort of”) + τὸν μῦθον (“the word”) + ἔειπες (“you have said”): an exclamation, “what a thing you have said!” It completes the whole line αἰνότατε Κρονίδη, ποῖον τὸν μῦθον ἔειπες, which in the Iliad is Hera's protest to Zeus ([[most-dread]]). A related exclamation, ποῖόν σε ἔπος φύγεν ἕρκος ὀδόντων, “what a word has escaped the fence of your teeth,” rebukes a speaker for something said rashly.
