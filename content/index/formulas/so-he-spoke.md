## The pattern

ὣς (“thus”) + ἔφατο or φάτο (“he spoke,” from φημί): “thus he spoke.” The formula marks the end of a speech, and the rest of the verse usually turns at once to its effect. It has several variants:

- ὣς ἔφατ’ and ὣς φάτο, the commonest;
- ὣς ἄρ’ ἔφη, with the particle ἄρα;
- ὣς εἰπών, “so saying,” which leads straight into the next action;
- ὣς ἄρα φωνήσασα, “so she spoke” (1.428);
- ἦ καί, “he spoke, and …,” where ἦ is the old imperfect of ἠμί (“say”) (1.219, 1.528).
