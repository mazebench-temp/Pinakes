## The pattern

μερμηρίζω (“ponder, be anxious”) + διάνδιχα (“in two ways”): the mind divided between two courses. The alternatives follow as a double question, ἤ … ἦε (“whether … or”). ὁρμαίνω (“turn over, stir up”) + κατὰ φρένα καὶ κατὰ θυμόν (“in mind and in heart”) describes the same state as a process still going on. Diomedes is “torn two ways” in the same words in Book 8 (Il. 8.167). The whole pattern, with its resolution, is the [[decision-scene]].
