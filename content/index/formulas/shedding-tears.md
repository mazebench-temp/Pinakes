## The pattern

δάκρυ (“tear”) + χέω (“pour, shed”): “pouring a tear,” with the participle in whatever case the sentence needs. κατὰ δάκρυ χέουσα, with κατά (“down”) in tmesis, is “letting the tears run down.” The phrase often stands in a speech frame: ὣς φάτο δάκρυ χέων, “so he spoke, shedding tears.”
