## The pattern

ἐμεῦ ζῶντος (“while I live”) + καὶ ἐπὶ χθονὶ δερκομένοιο (“and look upon the earth”), a genitive absolute. To see is to be alive; the dead do not see the light. The phrase makes a promise last as long as the speaker's life. In the Odyssey the suitor Eurymachus uses the same words in a false promise to Penelope (Od. 16.439).
