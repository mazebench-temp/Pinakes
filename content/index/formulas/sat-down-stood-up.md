## The pattern

ἤτοι ὅ γ’ ὣς εἰπών (“he, having spoken thus”) + κατ’ ἄρ’ ἕζετο (“sat down”) + τοῖσι δ’ ἀνέστη (“and among them stood up …”). The next verse names the new speaker. The line enacts the procedure of the Homeric assembly: a man stands to speak and sits when he has done ([[assembly-type-scene]]). Elsewhere a herald puts the scepter into the speaker's hand (Il. 23.567–568; Od. 2.37–38).
