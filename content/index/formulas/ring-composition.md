## The pattern

In ring composition a passage comes back to its starting point, and the elements in between are often arranged in mirror order: A B C B′ A′. The return may be a repeated word, a repeated line, or a return to the opening theme. In an oral poem the pattern serves singer and audience alike: it marks where a unit begins and ends, and it gives a digression a way home. Cedric Whitman (*Homer and the Heroic Tradition*, 1958) traced ring patterns across the whole Iliad, and Dieter Lohmann (*Die Komposition der Reden in der Ilias*, 1970) showed how pervasive they are in its speeches.
