## The pattern

κρατερόν (“strong, harsh”) + μῦθον (“word, command”) + ἐπιτέλλω (“enjoin, lay upon,” here split in tmesis as ἐπὶ … ἔτελλε): “and he laid a harsh word upon him.” μῦθος in Homer is often an authoritative utterance: an order, a threat, a formal speech. The half-line follows a verb of sending away and says how it was done.
