The Greek dialect of Cyprus, written in a syllabic script into the fourth century BCE, preserved words lost elsewhere. Aristotle's example: “sigynon is standard for the Cypriots, but a rare word for us” (1457b6; see [[sigynon]], [[glotta]] and [[cyprus]]). The *Cyprians* of Dicaeogenes is a different matter (see [[cyprians-play]]).
