The relative clause that follows puts the two peoples of Gouneus, or at least the Perrhaebians, "around wintry Dodona", in Epirus, far to the west of the later home of the Aenianes under Mount Oeta. Ancient writers explained the difference by migrations. The Aenianes were among the northern peoples who gave earth and water to Xerxes in 480 BC (Herodotus 7.132).
