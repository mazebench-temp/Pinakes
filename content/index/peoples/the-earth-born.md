## The story

Cadmus, founding Thebes, killed the serpent of Ares and on Athena's advice sowed its teeth; armed men sprang up and fought until five were left, the ancestors of the Theban nobility (Euripides, *Phoenician Women* 657–675; Apollodorus 3.4.1). Their families were said to carry a birthmark shaped like a spearhead (see [[cadmus-and-the-sown-men]]).

## In the Poetics

The first example of recognition by congenital signs is “the spearhead which the Earth-born bear” (1454b22; see [[the-spearhead-of-the-earth-born]] and [[semeion]]). Plutarch says the mark was a spear (*On the Delays of Divine Vengeance* 563a).
