---
title: The suitors
greek: μνηστῆρες
kind: people
tags: [house-of-odysseus]
summary: The young nobles of Ithaca and the islands who courted Penelope and wasted Odysseus' property for three years, until he killed them all.
aliases: [mnesteres, suitors, the wooers]
---

Telemachus counts them: fifty-two from Dulichium, twenty-four from Same, twenty from Zacynthus and twelve from Ithaca (*Odyssey* 16.245–253). Their leaders are Antinous and Eurymachus. In Aristotle's outline of the Odyssey, the hero's “property is being wasted by suitors and his son plotted against” (1455b19–21; see [[the-return-of-odysseus]]).
