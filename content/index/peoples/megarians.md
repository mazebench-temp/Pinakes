The Megarians are named among the Dorians who claim tragedy and comedy: “comedy by the Megarians, both those here, who say it began when they had their democracy, and those from Sicily” (1448a30–33). The Megarian democracy followed the fall of the tyrant Theagenes in the sixth century; the Sicilian Megarians are those of Megara Hyblaea, Epicharmus' city by one tradition (see [[megara]], [[dorians]], [[epicharmus]] and [[the-origins-of-comedy]]).
