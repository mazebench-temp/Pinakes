μενεπτόλεμοι, "steadfast in battle" (see [[steadfast-in-battle]]). In historical times Perrhaebia was the hill country north of the Thessalian plain, around the western foot of Mount Olympus, and the Perrhaebians were subjects of the Thessalians. Homer puts them "around wintry Dodona" and by the Titaressus (2.750–751). They too gave earth and water to Xerxes (Herodotus 7.132).
