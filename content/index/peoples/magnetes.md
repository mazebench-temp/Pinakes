The Catalogue ends with them. Magnesia was the mountainous coast of eastern Thessaly, running from the mouth of the Peneius past Ossa and Pelion to the peninsula that closes the Pagasetic Gulf. In historical times the Magnesians were subjects of the Thessalians. Two cities of Asia Minor, Magnesia on the Maeander and Magnesia under Mount Sipylus, bore their name. The lodestone was ἡ Μαγνῆτις λίθος, "the Magnesian stone", the source of the word "magnet", though which Magnesia gave it the name is uncertain.
