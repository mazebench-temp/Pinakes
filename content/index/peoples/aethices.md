καὶ Αἰθίκεσσι πέλασσεν: "and pushed them to the Aethices". The Centaurs were driven out of Pelion, in the east, toward the mountains of the far west. Later geographers placed the Aethices on Mount Pindus, between Thessaly and Epirus. Homer names them only here.
