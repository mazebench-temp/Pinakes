summary: The people of Paros in the Cyclades, the home of Archilochus, whom they honored though he was a slanderer.
aliases: [Paros]
---

Paros, famous for its marble, sent colonists to Thasos in the seventh century BCE, Archilochus among them. The Parians honored the poet with a shrine, the Archilocheion. See [[archilochus]].
