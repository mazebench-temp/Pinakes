---
title: Eleates
greek: Ἐλεᾶται
kind: people
tags: [greek-west]
summary: The people of Elea (Velia) on the west coast of southern Italy, founded by Phocaeans about 540 BCE; the city of Parmenides and Zeno. Xenophanes advised them about the worship of Leucothea.
aliases: [Elea, Velia]
grc: ["^Ἐλεάτ*"]
---

The Phocaeans who fled the Persians settled at Elea after the battle of Alalia (Herodotus 1.163–167). Diogenes Laertius says that Xenophanes wrote a poem on the founding of Elea (9.20). See [[xenophanes]].
