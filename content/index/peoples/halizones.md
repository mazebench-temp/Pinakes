The Halizones and their town are known only from these lines and from the death of their leader Odius in Book 5 (5.38–42). The name might mean "girdled by the sea" (ἅλς, "sea" + ζώνη, "belt"), or be connected with the river Halys, but neither is certain. They come after the Paphlagonians in the Catalogue, which suggests the Black Sea coast farther east, and the silver of [[Alybe]] has been connected with the mining country of Pontus, where the Chalybes, famous in later times as ironworkers, lived. The question occupies a long discussion in Strabo, who reviews the views of earlier scholars (*Geography*, Book 12). No answer has won general agreement.
