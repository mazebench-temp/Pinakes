## Etymology

Δαναοί (Danaoi). Greek tradition derived the name from Danaus, a king of Argos who came from Egypt with his fifty daughters. Egyptian texts of the 14th century BCE mention a land *Tanaja*, which some have connected with the Danaans. Both explanations are uncertain.
