summary: The board of thirty men who ruled Athens after its surrender to Sparta in 404 BCE, the “Thirty Tyrants”; Thrasybulus and the exiles overthrew them the next year.
aliases: [Thirty Tyrants, the Thirty Tyrants]
---

Appointed with Spartan support to draw up new laws, the Thirty, led by Critias, put to death some fifteen hundred citizens and metics and disarmed the rest; Thrasybulus seized Phyle and then the Piraeus, Critias fell in battle, and in 403 the democracy was restored with an amnesty (Xenophon, *Hellenica* 2.3–4; *Constitution of the Athenians* 34.2–40). See [[critias]], [[thrasybulus]] and [[piraeus]].
