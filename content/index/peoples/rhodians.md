ἀγέρωχος, "proud, lordly": Homer also gives the epithet to the Trojans and to the Mysians, and its etymology is disputed. The Rhodians lived in three divisions, one for each of their cities, [[Lindus]], [[Ialysus]] and [[Cameirus]] (2.655–656), settled "by tribes" when Tlepolemus came to the island (2.668). See [[rhodes]] and [[the-flight-of-tlepolemus]].
