## Outside Homer

Achilles brought fifty ships of Myrmidons, Hellenes and Achaeans from Phthia (Iliad 2.681–685). Later legend explained the name from μύρμηκες, "ants": Zeus turned the ants of Aegina into men for his son Aeacus, Achilles' grandfather (Ovid, *Metamorphoses* 7.614–660).
