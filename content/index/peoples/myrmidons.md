## In Book 1

{{quote:e51df9ae-ed22-5b10-aacc-b2ce6dc44d18}}

Agamemnon tells Achilles to go home and "lord it over the Myrmidons", making his kingdom sound small.

{{quote:83e02d8c-2b24-5869-a17f-29ea690c076d}}

The heralds come "to the huts and ships of the Myrmidons", at the edge of the camp.

## Outside Homer

Achilles brought fifty ships of Myrmidons, Hellenes and Achaeans from Phthia (Iliad 2.681–685). Later legend explained the name from μύρμηκες, "ants": Zeus turned the ants of Aegina into men for his son Aeacus, Achilles' grandfather (Ovid, *Metamorphoses* 7.614–660).
