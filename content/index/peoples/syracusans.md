Syracuse was founded from Corinth by Archias, the year after the Chalcidians founded Naxos (Thucydides 6.3.1–2). It defeated the Athenian expedition of 415–413 and, under Dionysius I (tyrant 405–367), was the capital of an empire over much of Sicily and southern Italy (see [[dionysius-of-syracuse]]).
