The Laconians are the people of Lacedaemon, ruled from Sparta (see [[lacedaemon]] and [[sparta]]). In the *Poetics* critics “think he was a Laconian”, Icarius, so that it seems absurd that Telemachus did not meet him in Lacedaemon (1461b4–6; see [[the-icarius-problem]] and [[icarius]]). Laconian women give their name to a tragedy (see [[laconian-women]]).
