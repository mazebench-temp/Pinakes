summary: The people of Thebes in Boeotia in the historical period: the allies of Persia in 480 BCE, the enemies of Athens in the Peloponnesian War, the masters of Greece after Leuctra (371), and the allies of Athens against Philip at Chaeronea (338).
---

Under Epaminondas and Pelopidas the Thebans broke Spartan power at Leuctra in 371 BCE. In the Sacred War they fought the Phocians, who had seized Delphi, and called in Philip of Macedon, who ended the war in 346. In 339 they joined Athens against him; after Philip's death the city revolted, and Alexander destroyed it in 335. See [[thebes]], [[cadmeians]] and [[philip-of-macedon]].
