## The people

Greek tradition told that the Dorians came into the Peloponnese from the north with the descendants of Heracles, two generations after the Trojan War (see [[the-return-of-the-heraclidae]]). Homer names them once, in the Odyssey, among the peoples of Crete (*Odyssey* 19.177).

## In the Poetics

“This is also why both tragedy and comedy are claimed by the Dorians”: comedy by the Megarians and the Sicilians, tragedy by some in the Peloponnese, “taking the names as their evidence”. They call outlying villages κῶμαι, the Athenians δῆμοι, and they say δρᾶν for “do”, the Athenians πράττειν (1448a29–b2; see [[komos]], [[drama]] and [[the-origins-of-comedy]]).
