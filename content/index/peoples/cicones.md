αἰχμηταί, "spearmen" ([[spearmen]]). In Book 17 Apollo rouses Hector in the likeness of Mentes, "leader of the Cicones" (17.73). In the Odyssey, Odysseus sacks their city Ismarus on his way home from Troy, and the Cicones, rallying their neighbors, kill six men from each of his ships (Odyssey 9.39–61). From Maron, priest of Apollo at Ismarus, he had the strong wine that later made the Cyclops drunk (9.196–211). The Cicones thus belong both to Troy's alliance and to the first adventure of the homecoming. Herodotus still names them among the Thracian peoples through whose land Xerxes marched (7.110).
