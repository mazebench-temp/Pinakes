Their towns are [[Cynus]], [[Opus]], [[Calliarus]], [[Bessa]], [[Scarphe]], [[Augeiae]], [[Tarphe]] and [[Thronium]] on the river [[Boagrius]] (2.531–533). They are the Locrians "who live across from holy [[Euboea]]": the eastern Locrians, on the Euboean Gulf and the Malian coast. The western Locrians, on the Gulf of Corinth, are not mentioned.

{{quote:1050ba00-e67c-5238-a3bb-f04596e556bb}}

## Elsewhere in Homer

Their way of fighting is unexpected after the Catalogue's praise of their leader's spear. In Book 13 the Locrians do not stand in close combat, for "they had no bronze helmets with horsehair crests, no round shields and no ash spears". They trust in bows and slings of twisted wool, and shoot from behind the heavy-armed men (13.712–722).

## Outside Homer

Later Locrians paid for Ajax's violence to Cassandra at the fall of Troy. According to later sources they sent two girls every year to serve Athena at Ilion, the "Locrian Maidens"; Lycophron's *Alexandra* tells of the custom (1141–1173).
