Ἀτρεΐδα δύω, κοσμήτορε λαῶν: "the two sons of Atreus, marshals of the people". Every word is dual: Greek has a grammatical number for pairs. Chryses addresses them first because they command the host. The same two lines are repeated in Achilles' retelling (1.374–375).
