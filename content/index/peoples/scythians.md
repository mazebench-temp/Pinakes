---
title: Scythians
greek: Σκύθαι
kind: people
tags: [non-greek, edge-of-world]
summary: The nomad peoples of the steppe north of the Black Sea, to the Greeks the type of the northern barbarian; Aristotle's example of an audience whose idea of what is praiseworthy differs from that of Athenians.
grc: ["^Σκύθ*"]
---

Herodotus devotes most of his fourth book to them, their origins, their customs of war and burial, and Darius' failed campaign against them (4.1–144). Homer does not name them; later geographers identified with them the Mare-milkers and the Abii at the edge of the world in Iliad 13.5–6 (see [[strabo-on-the-mare-milkers]] and [[abii]]).
