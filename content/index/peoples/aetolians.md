Their towns are [[Pleuron]], [[Olenus]], [[Pylene]], [[chalcis-aetolia|Chalcis]] by the sea and rocky [[Calydon]] (2.639–640). Their leader is [[Thoas]], son of Andraemon, and the Catalogue explains why: "the sons of great-hearted [[Oeneus]] were no more, nor was he himself still living, and fair-haired [[Meleager]] was dead" (2.641–642). The old royal house of Calydon had come to an end (see [[the-death-of-meleager]]).

## Elsewhere in Homer

In Phoenix's story of Meleager, the Aetolians defend Calydon against the Curetes (9.529–599; see [[the-wrath-of-meleager]]). Diomedes' father Tydeus was a son of Oeneus who went to Argos and settled there (14.113–125), so the Argive leader Diomedes is an Aetolian by descent: "an Aetolian by birth", Idomeneus calls him (23.471).

## Outside Homer

In the third century BC the Aetolian League became one of the leading powers of Greece.
