“There could also be triple and quadruple nouns and multiple ones, like most of those of the Massaliots, Hermocaicoxanthus…” (1457a34–b1; see [[massalia]], [[hermocaicoxanthus]] and [[compound-word]]). Whether Aristotle means a habit of the Massaliot dialect or the style of a Massaliot poet is unknown, since the text breaks off.
