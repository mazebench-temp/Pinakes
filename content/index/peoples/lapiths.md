Book 1 names [[Peirithous]], [[Dryas]], [[Caeneus]], [[Exadius]], [[Polyphemus]] and [[Theseus]], but not the people as a whole. Homer uses the name Λαπίθαι in Iliad 12.128 and 12.181, for Polypoetes and Leonteus, two Lapith chiefs at Troy. See [[nestor-and-the-centaurs]].
