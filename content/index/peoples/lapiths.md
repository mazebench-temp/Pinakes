The Iliad uses the name Λαπίθαι only in Book 12 (12.128, 12.181), for Polypoetes and Leonteus, the two Lapith chiefs at Troy; the Odyssey uses it once, in the story of the Centaur Eurytion (21.297). See [[nestor-and-the-centaurs]].
