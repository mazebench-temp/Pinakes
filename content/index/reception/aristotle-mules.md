Chapter 25 of the *Poetics* is a handbook for defending Homer against critics' "problems". One solution is the rare word (γλῶττα). "First he went after the οὐρῆας" may not mean the mules but the guards (φύλακες). Homer uses the related word οὖρος, "watcher", of Nestor as "guardian of the Achaeans". The objection it answered was presumably that the god acted absurdly in shooting animals first.

Most modern readers keep the mules. A plague that strikes animals before men is realistic, and the order builds toward the human dead.
