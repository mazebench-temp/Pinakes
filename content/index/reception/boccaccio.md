---

*Il Filostrato* (1330s), a poem in eight-line stanzas, tells the love of Troiolo, a son of Priam, for Criseida, the daughter of the seer Calchas, and her betrayal of him with Diomede. Chaucer made it the source of his *Troilus and Criseyde*.

Boccaccio brought the Calabrian scholar Leontius Pilatus to Florence, lodged him in his house and learned some Greek from him. There Leontius made a Latin prose version of the Iliad and the Odyssey (about 1360–1362) for Boccaccio and his friend Petrarch, the first complete Latin translation of the two poems.
