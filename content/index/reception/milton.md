*Paradise Lost* appeared in ten books in 1667 and in twelve in 1674. Milton knew Homer in Greek, and the poem takes from him its similes, its catalogues, its councils of the powers of heaven and hell, and, in the war in heaven of the sixth book, its battles.

- Satan wounded by Michael, and Moloch put to flight bellowing (*Paradise Lost* 6): [[milton-wounded-angels]].
- Michael clears Adam's sight to show him the future (*Paradise Lost* 11.411–416): [[mist-from-the-eyes]].
