Cicero's Cato, arguing that old age is not burdensome, points to Nestor. Homer says his speech flowed sweeter than honey, Cato observes, and Nestor needed no bodily strength for that. He adds that Agamemnon never wished for ten men like Ajax, but for ten like Nestor (Iliad 2.370–374).
