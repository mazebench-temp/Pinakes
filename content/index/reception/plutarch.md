summary: Greek biographer and philosopher from Chaeronea in Boeotia (c. 46–c. 120 CE), author of the Parallel Lives and the essays of the Moralia, who quotes Homer constantly.
---

Plutarch was a Platonist and for many years a priest at Delphi. His *Lives* preserve stories of Homer's text being used, or altered, for political ends. In the *Life of Solon* he tells how Athens was said to have won Salamis with a line added to the Catalogue of Ships ([[solon-and-salamis]]). In the *Life of Theseus* he reports the similar charge that Peisistratus inserted a line about Theseus into the Odyssey ([[theseus-line]]). Among the *Moralia*, the essay *How the Young Man Should Study Poetry* is a guide to reading Homer with a critical eye.
