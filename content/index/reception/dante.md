summary: Dante Alighieri (1265–1321), the Florentine poet of the Divine Comedy. He read no Greek and knew Homer only by name, through the Latin poets; his guide through Hell is Virgil, from whom he takes the image of the dead falling like autumn leaves.
---

The *Comedy* tells the poet's journey through Hell, Purgatory and Paradise; Virgil guides him through the first two. Dante knew the Trojan War from Virgil, Ovid and Statius, and from the Latin histories of the Middle Ages. In Limbo he meets the great poets of antiquity, led by one who carries a sword in his hand: “that is Homer, sovereign poet” (*Inferno* 4.86–88).

- The souls at the river of Hell, falling like leaves in autumn (*Inferno* 3.112–117): [[leaves-of-the-dead]].
