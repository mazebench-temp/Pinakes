Marcus Fabius Quintilianus came from Calagurris in Spain and taught rhetoric at Rome, where he was the first teacher of Latin rhetoric to be paid a salary from the public treasury. In the tenth book of the *Institutio* he reviews the authors an orator should read, and begins with Homer, who "gave the model and the origin of every part of eloquence", as [[oceanus|Ocean]], in Homer's own words, is the source of all rivers and springs (10.1.46).
