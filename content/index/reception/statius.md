summary: Publius Papinius Statius (c. 45–c. 96 CE), Roman poet of Naples, author of the Thebaid, an epic in twelve books on the war of the Seven against Thebes, and of an unfinished Achilleid.
---

The *Thebaid* was published about 92 CE, after twelve years' work (12.811–812). It ends by telling the poem not to rival the divine *Aeneid*, but to follow it at a distance and always worship its footsteps (12.816–817). The *Achilleid*, left unfinished at his death, tells of Achilles' boyhood and his stay on Scyros among the daughters of Lycomedes. He also wrote the *Silvae*, occasional poems.

Statius was widely read in the Middle Ages. Dante makes him a secret Christian, who joins Virgil and Dante on the mountain of Purgatory and stays with Dante to the end of the *Purgatorio* (cantos 21–33). Chaucer names “Stace” among the great poets.
