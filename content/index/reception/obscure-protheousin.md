Taken from προθέω, "run forward", the line asks: "do his insults therefore rush forth to be spoken?" The Pinakes translation follows this reading. An ancient alternative derives the verb from προτίθημι, "set before, grant", making the gods the subject: "have the gods therefore granted him to speak insults?" That sense is clearer but the form is harder. Either way Agamemnon's point is plain: strength in war does not give a man the right to abuse his king.
