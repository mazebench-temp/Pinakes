summary: Greek satirist from Samosata on the Euphrates (born c. 120 CE), whose comic dialogues and fantasies are full of Homer's heroes and gods.
---

Lucian was a Syrian who wrote in classical Attic Greek. His *Dialogues of the Gods* and *Dialogues of the Dead* take the myths at their word and make them absurd, and his *True Histories* parodies travelers' tales, with a visit to Homer on the Isle of the Blessed.
