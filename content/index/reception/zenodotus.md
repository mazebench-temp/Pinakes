title: Zenodotus
greek: Ζηνόδοτος
kind: author
tags: [scholarship]
summary: The first head of the Library of Alexandria (c. 280 BCE) and the first critical editor of Homer. His reading "a feast for all the birds" at Iliad 1.5 is the most famous textual variant in Book 1.
---

Zenodotus of Ephesus compared manuscripts of Homer and marked lines he doubted, founding the tradition of Alexandrian scholarship that runs through Aristophanes of Byzantium to Aristarchus. Callimachus, whose *Pinakes* catalogued the Library's holdings, worked in the same Library under Ptolemy II; this project is named after that catalogue. See [[zenodotus-feast-for-birds]].
