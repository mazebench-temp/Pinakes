The twelve books of the *Meditations*, addressed to himself, were written in Greek, some of them in camp on the Danube frontier. They are notes for his own use, and they often recall a line of the poets as a brief reminder of a Stoic truth: that everything passes, and that the fear of death is unreasonable.

- “The wind scatters the leaves on the ground”: your children too are little leaves (*Meditations* 10.34): [[the-man-of-chios]].
