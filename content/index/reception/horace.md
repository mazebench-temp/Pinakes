See [[in-medias-res]]. In the *Epistles* (1.2) Horace also reads the Iliad as a moral lesson on the follies of kings: "whatever madness the kings commit, the Achaeans pay for it" (quidquid delirant reges plectuntur Achivi, 1.2.14). That line is a fair summary of Book 1.
