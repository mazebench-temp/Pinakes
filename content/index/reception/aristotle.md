summary: The philosopher (384–322 BCE) who made Homer the model of epic in the Poetics and used Achilles' anger to define anger in the Rhetoric.
---

Aristotle wrote a lost work, *Homeric Problems*, solving difficulties in the poems. Chapter 25 of the *Poetics* preserves a sample, including the mules of Iliad 1.50: [[aristotle-mules]]. In the *Rhetoric* he defines anger through Achilles' complaint at 1.356: [[aristotle-anger]]. The *Poetics* praises Homer for giving the Iliad a single unified action and not telling the whole war (chapter 23).
