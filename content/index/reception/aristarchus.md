summary: Aristarchus of Samothrace (c. 216–144 BCE), the greatest of the Alexandrian editors of Homer. His judgments survive in the scholia, the marginal notes of medieval manuscripts.
---

Aristarchus judged Homer by Homer's own usage ("explaining Homer from Homer"). He marked lines as spurious (athetesis) and argued with Zenodotus' readings. On Iliad 1 his views survive in the scholia to the Venetus A manuscript. For examples see [[zenodotus-feast-for-birds]] and [[aristarchus-heeos]].
