---
title: Strabo
greek: Στράβων
kind: author
tags: [scholarship]
summary: Greek geographer (c. 64 BCE–c. 24 CE) who treated Homer as the first geographer. His Geography preserves the anecdote of Phidias and the nod of Zeus, and describes the temple of Apollo Smintheus at Chryse.
---

Strabo's *Geography* opens by claiming Homer as the founder of geography, and it discusses Homeric places at length, above all the Troad (Book 13). For Iliad 1 he supplies [[phidias-zeus]] (8.3.30) and [[smintheus-and-the-mice]] (13.1.48), and he locates [[chryse]] and [[cilla]].
