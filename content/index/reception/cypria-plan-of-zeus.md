The scholion tells the story. Earth was weighed down by the numberless race of men, who were impious besides, and she asked Zeus to lighten her load. He caused first the Theban war and then the Trojan. The scholion quotes seven lines of the *Cypria*: "There was a time when the countless tribes of men… and Zeus took pity…" The quotation ends with the same words as Iliad 1.5, "and the plan of Zeus was fulfilled".

Whether the Iliad poet knew this story, or the *Cypria* poet took the phrase from the Iliad and built a cosmic plan on it, cannot be decided. The *Cypria* is later in its final form, but the myth may be older. See [[plan-of-zeus]].
