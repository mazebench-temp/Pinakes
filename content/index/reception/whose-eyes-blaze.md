δεινὼ δέ οἱ ὄσσε φάανθεν: "and terrible to him (οἱ) the two eyes shone". Grammatically οἱ can be "to him" (the eyes appeared terrible to Achilles) or "his" (Achilles' eyes blazed). Ancient and modern readers are divided:

- **Athena's eyes.** Recognition by the eyes suits a goddess whose epithet is γλαυκῶπις, "gray-eyed" or "gleaming-eyed". The Pinakes translation follows this reading.
- **Achilles' eyes.** Blazing eyes are a sign of rage, as Agamemnon's eyes were "like blazing fire" at 1.104, and Achilles is still angry.

The dual ὄσσε, "the two eyes", is used of both elsewhere.
