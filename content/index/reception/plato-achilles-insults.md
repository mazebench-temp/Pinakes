Discussing the self-control (σωφροσύνη) that the guardians need, Socrates approves Homer where subjects obey their rulers in silence. He asks whether Achilles' words to Agamemnon are fine, "and the rest, and all the insolent things that private persons have said in prose or verse to their rulers". His answer is that they are not fit for the young to hear, though they may give some other pleasure.
