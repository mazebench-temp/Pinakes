---
title: Apollodorus' Library
greek: Ἀπολλοδώρου Βιβλιοθήκη
kind: author
tags: [scholarship]
summary: A handbook of Greek mythology, probably of the first or second century CE, attributed to Apollodorus. It is a comprehensive summary of the myths from the creation to the Returns.
---

Its epitome covers the Trojan War and the Returns. Pinakes cites it under "Outside Homer" for stories Homer only alludes to, like Thetis' attempt to make the infant Achilles immortal ([[thetis-in-the-house-of-peleus]]).
