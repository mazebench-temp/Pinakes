---
title: Stesichorus
greek: Στησίχορος
kind: author
tags: [art]
summary: Greek lyric poet of Himera in Sicily (first half of the sixth century BCE), who told the stories of epic in long narrative songs. He was said to have been struck blind for slandering Helen, and to have recovered his sight by recanting.
---

Stesichorus' poems survive only in quotations and in fragments recovered from papyri. Their subjects came from epic and myth: a *Sack of Troy*, *Returns*, an *Oresteia*, and the *Geryoneis*, on Heracles' fight with the three-bodied Geryon. Quintilian praised him for "bearing the weight of epic song on the lyre" (10.1.62).

His *Helen* and the *Palinode* that retracted it are the source of the story that only a phantom of Helen went to Troy: [[stesichorus-palinode]].
