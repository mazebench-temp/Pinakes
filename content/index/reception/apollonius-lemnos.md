Homer knows the parents of the king of Lemnos: Euneus is “son of Jason, … whom Hypsipyle bore to Jason, shepherd of the people” (7.468–469; see [[euneus]] and [[hypsipyle]]). It is Jason's first appearance in Homer. The Odyssey adds only the Argo, which alone sailed through the Wandering Rocks on its way home from Aeetes, because Hera loved Jason (*Odyssey* 12.69–72; see [[jason]]).

Apollonius tells how the child came to be. On the outward voyage the Argo puts in at Lemnos. A year before, the women of the island had killed all its men. Aphrodite, whom they had long neglected, had turned their husbands from them to captive women from Thrace, and in their anger the women killed the men and the captives together. Only Hypsipyle spared her father, the king Thoas, and set him adrift in a chest. Fearing the Thracians, and now the strangers, the women arm themselves; then, on the advice of Hypsipyle's old nurse Polyxo, who asks who will plow the fields and defend the island when they are old, they decide to take the heroes in. Hypsipyle receives Jason in her father's house, tells him that the men were driven out, and offers him the rule of the island. The Argonauts stay, until Heracles, who has kept to the ship, shames them into sailing. At parting Hypsipyle asks Jason what she should do if she bears a child. If it is a son, he tells her, she should send him when he is grown to Iolcus, to comfort his parents (*Argonautica* 1.609–909).

Apollonius does not name the child. The son that Hypsipyle may bear is the Euneus whom Homer makes king of Lemnos after Jason, and whose ships sell wine to the Achaeans at Troy. The episode also gave Virgil one of the models for Dido, the queen who welcomes the hero, offers him her kingdom, and is left behind (see [[virgil]]).

Ovid gave Hypsipyle a letter of her own, written when she hears that Jason has come home to Thessaly with Medea (*Heroides* 6; see [[ovid]]). For the rest of her story, see [[euripides-hypsipyle]]; for the older poets, [[pindar-lemnian-women]] and [[the-lemnian-women]].
