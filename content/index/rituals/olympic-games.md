## The custom

The list of victors began, by ancient reckoning, in 776 BCE; Hippias of Elis was the first to compile it (see [[hippias-of-elis]]). The prize was a crown of wild olive, and a victor's city honored him with statues, dinners and poems; Pindar's *Olympian Odes* were written for such victors. Games with a crown for a prize, the Olympian, Pythian, Isthmian and Nemean, were called στεφανῖται, “crown games” (see [[pythian-games]]).
