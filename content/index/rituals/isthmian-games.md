---
title: The Isthmian Games
greek: Ἴσθμια
kind: ritual
tags: [festival, corinthia]
summary: The games held every second year at the Isthmus of Corinth in honor of Poseidon, one of the four Panhellenic festivals.
aliases: [Isthmia]
---

Pindar's *Isthmian* odes celebrate victors in them. The prize was a crown of pine or of celery.
