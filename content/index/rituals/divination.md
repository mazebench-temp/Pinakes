The μάντις (“seer”) reads the will of the gods from signs. Achilles names three kinds of expert: “some seer, or a priest, or even an interpreter of dreams (ὀνειροπόλος), for a dream, too, comes from Zeus” ({{ref:Iliad 1.62-63}}; see [[onar]] and [[a-dream-comes-from-zeus]]). [[Calchas]] is an οἰωνοπόλος, a “bird-seer” who reads the flight and cries of [[birds|birds]]. A seer's pronouncement is a θεοπρόπιον, “what the god makes known” (see [[theopropion]]), and his art is μαντοσύνη. For prophecy as a theme, see [[prophecy]].

## Outside Homer

[[Plato]] derives the name of the seer's art, μαντική, from μανία, “madness”, the inspired frenzy of the prophet (*Phaedrus* 244b–c).
