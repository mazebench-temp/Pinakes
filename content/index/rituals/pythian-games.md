## The custom

The Pythian Games were reorganized in 582 BCE; the musical contests, in singing to the lyre and playing the pipe, were their oldest part, and Pindar's *Pythian Odes* celebrate victors in the chariot race and other events. Aristotle and his nephew Callisthenes compiled a list of Pythian victors, honored by the Delphians about 330 BCE (see [[pytho]]).

## In the Poetics

Among irrational elements that should not be inside the drama: “like those who report the Pythian games in the Electra” (1460a30–31). In Sophocles' play the old tutor tells of Orestes' death in a chariot race at Delphi, but the games had not been founded in the heroic age (see [[electra-play]] and [[the-false-report-of-orestes-death]]).
