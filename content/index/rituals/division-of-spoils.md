The goods and captives taken in a raid were brought back to the camp and divided among the army. The leaders received, besides their share, a prize of honor ([[geras]]) chosen out and given by the army.

{{notes}}

## Outside Homer

After Plataea the Greeks set aside a tenth of the spoils for Apollo at Delphi and gifts for Olympia and the Isthmus, then divided the rest (Herodotus 9.81).
