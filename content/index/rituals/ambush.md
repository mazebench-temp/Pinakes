λόχος is a place of ambush, or the band lying in it. Only picked men went, and they had to wait in silence and fear. Idomeneus says that an ambush shows courage most clearly: the coward's color changes and his heart pounds, the brave man's does not (Iliad 13.276–286).

The war itself ends with an ambush: the Wooden Horse is a “hollow ambush” (κοῖλος λόχος, Odyssey 8.515).
