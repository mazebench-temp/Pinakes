## The custom

At the City Dionysia three tragic poets each presented three tragedies and a satyr play, and from 486 BCE comic poets competed as well; the Lenaea had its own contests. Ten judges, one from each tribe, ranked the poets, and the archon's list of victors (the *didaskaliai*) was recorded; Aristotle himself compiled *Victories at the Dionysia and Lenaea* and *Didaskaliai*, now lost.
