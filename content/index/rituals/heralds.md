The κῆρυξ carried a leader's messages, summoned the army to assembly and kept order in it, handed the [[scepter|scepter]] to each speaker, and served at sacrifices and oaths. Heralds were under the protection of Zeus, and their persons were not to be harmed.

{{notes}}

## Outside Homer

At Sparta a clan of hereditary heralds, the Talthybiadae, claimed descent from Talthybius. Herodotus tells how, after the Spartans had killed the heralds of Darius, the wrath of Talthybius fell on them, until two Spartans offered their lives to the Persian king in atonement (7.134–137).
