## The custom

At Athens the archon assigned each poet competing at the Dionysia a chorus, and a rich citizen, the choregus, bore its cost as a public service (λειτουργία). The victorious choregus could dedicate a monument; the Monument of Lysicrates (335/4 BCE) still stands in Athens.
