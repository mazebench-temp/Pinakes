ἀγορή (from ἀγείρω, “gather”) is both the gathering and its place. ἀγορεύω is “speak before the gathering”, and an ἀγορητής is a speaker.

{{notes}}

## Outside Homer

[[Hesiod]] describes the ideal speaker in the assembly: a king favored by the Muses, whose gentle words settle great quarrels while the people look to him (*Theogony* 80–92).
