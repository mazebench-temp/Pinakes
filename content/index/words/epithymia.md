## Meaning

ἐπιθυμέω is to set one's heart (θυμός) on something. Aristotle names three kinds of longing, ὄρεξις: appetite, temper and wish (*On the Soul* 2.3, 414b2); appetite is for the pleasant, wish for the good (*Nicomachean Ethics* 3.2, 1111b10–30). The *Rhetoric* gives the same division in brief (see [[thymos]]).
