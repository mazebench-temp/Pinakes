## Meaning

βάρβαρος is one whose speech sounds like “bar-bar”, a non-Greek, like the Carians whom Homer calls “of barbarous speech” (see [[barbarophonos]]); βαρβαρίζω is to speak like one. Later grammarians distinguished barbarism, a fault in a single word, from solecism, a fault of syntax.
