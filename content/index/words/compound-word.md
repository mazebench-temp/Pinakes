## Meaning

Greek forms compounds freely, and poetry most freely: Homer's epithets are mostly compounds, ῥοδοδάκτυλος, “rosy-fingered”, πόδας ὠκύς is a phrase but ποδάρκης a compound (see [[swift-footed]]). The dithyrambic poets of the fifth and fourth centuries were notorious for long new compounds, which Aristophanes parodies.
