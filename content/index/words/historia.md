---
title: ἱστορία
greek: ἱστορία
kind: term
tags: [literary-history]
summary: "Inquiry", and so the written account of what has happened: history, which Aristotle sets against poetry as the particular against the universal.
aliases: [historia, history, historian, inquiry]
---

The word comes from ἵστωρ, the one who knows; in the Iliad the ἵστωρ is the arbiter of a dispute, on the shield of Achilles and in the chariot race (18.501, 23.486). Herodotus opens his work as “the presentation of the inquiry (ἱστορίη) of Herodotus of Halicarnassus” in its first words, and Aristotle's own *History of Animals* is in Greek an “inquiry” into animals. In the *Poetics* history is the record of particulars, what Alcibiades did or suffered, and poetry is more philosophical (1451a36–b11; see [[poetry-and-history]], [[herodotus]] and [[katholou]]).
