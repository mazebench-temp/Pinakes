## Meaning

προαιρέομαι is to take one thing before another, to prefer. In the *Nicomachean Ethics* choice is “deliberate desire of things in our power” (3.3, 1113a10–11), and character is shown in what a man chooses; the *Poetics* says that character is what makes plain what sort of choice a person makes (1450b8–10; see [[ethos]]).
