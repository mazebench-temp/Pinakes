## Meaning

ὀλιγωρεῖν is “to make little of, to neglect”, from ὀλίγος, “little”, and ὤρα, “care”. In the *Rhetoric* it is the cause of anger, and its kinds are καταφρόνησις, “contempt”, the despising of what one thinks worth nothing; ἐπηρεασμός, “spite”, the thwarting of another's wishes for no gain of one's own; and ὕβρις, “outrage”, the shaming of another for the pleasure of feeling superior (see [[hybris]] and [[orge]]). Contempt returns in the chapter on emulation as emulation's opposite (see [[zelos]]).
