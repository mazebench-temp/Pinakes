## Meaning

διάνοια is the activity of the mind (διά + νόος), thinking, intention, meaning. It is not a Homeric word. In Plato it is discursive thought, reasoning that moves from premises to conclusions, as distinct from direct understanding (νόησις; *Republic* 6.511d–e).
