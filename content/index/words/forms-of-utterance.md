## Meaning

σχῆμα is shape or form. The σχήματα τῆς λέξεως are the kinds of speech act, distinguished by the speaker's intention. Protagoras is said to have been the first to divide utterances into kinds, prayer, question, answer and command (Diogenes Laertius 9.53–54).
