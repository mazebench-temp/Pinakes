## Meaning

θεοπρόπιον and θεοπροπίη are a god's pronouncement: an oracle or prophecy, as a seer reveals it. The verb θεοπροπέω means “prophesy, declare the will of the gods”, and a θεοπρόπος is a prophet. The first part of the word is θεός, “god”; the second is disputed.

## In Book 1

{{quote:1792af08-0d38-5a0b-af9b-6175c8f1ef57}}

Achilles guarantees Calchas' safety and tells him to reveal the oracle. The oracles are Apollo's: Calchas prays to Apollo when he reveals them.

{{quote:01467f7c-800c-5ec7-9926-92993c624ef9}}

Agamemnon's attack on Calchas uses the verb with contempt: “now among the Danaans you prophesy and declare” that the god is angry because of him. See [[calchas-evil-prophecies]].

In Achilles' retelling to Thetis the whole episode is reduced to one line: “a seer with sure knowledge declared to us the oracles of the Far-Shooter” (1.385).
