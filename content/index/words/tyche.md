## Meaning

τυγχάνω is to hit upon, to happen; Homer has the verb but not the noun. Tyche is one of the daughters of Ocean in Hesiod (*Theogony* 360). Aristotle's *Physics* analyzes chance and spontaneity as causes of what happens rarely and for the sake of something, but not by purpose (2.4–6). See [[moira]] and [[aisa]] for the Homeric words of fate and lot.
