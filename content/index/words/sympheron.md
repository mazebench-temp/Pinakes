## Meaning

συμφέρω is to bring together, and intransitively to come together, to agree; so τὸ συμφέρον is what goes along with one's purpose, what helps. Homer has the verb in its first sense, of fighters brought together in battle, and the adjective συμφερτή, “joined”, in Poseidon's maxim on the combined prowess of men (13.237; see [[joined-prowess]]). In the debates of Thucydides the advantageous is set against the just again and again; Aristotle makes it the end of one kind of speech and subordinates it to the good.
