## Meaning

μοχθηρός is “toilsome, wretched” (μόχθος, “toil”), and so “in a bad state”, and of character “bad, wicked”. Aristotle uses μοχθηρία in the *Ethics* for settled badness of character, which the wicked man does not even recognize in himself (*Nicomachean Ethics* 7.8, 1150b36).
