## Meaning

γλῶττα (Ionic γλῶσσα) is the tongue, and so a language, and then a word that needs explaining, a “gloss”. Collections of Homeric glosses were made from the fifth century on, and the Alexandrian scholars wrote books of them; English “gloss” and “glossary” descend from the word. Many of the obscure words of the Iliad are glosses in this sense (see [[obscure-gephyrai]] and [[obscure-protheousin]]).
