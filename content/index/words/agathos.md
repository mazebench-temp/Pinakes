## Meaning

In Homer ἀγαθός is good at something: good at the war cry, good with the fists, brave in battle, or of good family; its superlative ἄριστος names the best of the Achaeans (see [[aristos]] and [[good-at-the-war-cry]]). Philosophy turned it into the good itself. The *Nicomachean Ethics* opens: “every art and every inquiry, and likewise every action and choice, is thought to aim at some good; and so the good has rightly been declared to be that at which all things aim” (1.1, 1094a1–3).
