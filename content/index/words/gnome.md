## Meaning

γνώμη is a judgment or opinion, from γιγνώσκω, “know”; in the jurors' oath to judge “to the best of my judgment” it keeps its plain sense (see [[to-the-best-of-my-judgment]]). As a term of rhetoric and poetics it is the general saying, the sententia of the Latin rhetoricians: Theognis and the gnomic poets were made of it, and the tragedians filled their speeches with it. Aristotle's chapter on the maxim (*Rhetoric* 2.21) is the first theory of it; the *Rhetoric to Alexander* treats it too (11). See [[enthymema]] and [[praise-and-blame]].
