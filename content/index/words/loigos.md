## Meaning

λοιγός is ruin: the destruction brought by plague or war, the death of many. The adjective λοίγιος means “ruinous, disastrous”. The word most often stands in the formula ἀεικέα λοιγὸν ἀμῦναι, “to ward off shameful ruin”. The etymology is uncertain.
