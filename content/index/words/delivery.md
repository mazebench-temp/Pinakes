## Meaning

ὑποκριτική is the art of the ὑποκριτής, the actor (see [[hypokrites]]). Aristotle's *Rhetoric* says that delivery, how to use the voice for each feeling, loud or soft or middle, high or low or middle pitch, had only lately been studied, first in tragedy and rhapsody, and that Glaucon of Teos wrote on it (3.1, 1403b18–27).
