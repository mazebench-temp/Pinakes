## Meaning

διαλέγομαι is “converse, discuss”, and διαλεκτική the art of discussion by question and answer. In Plato's *Republic* dialectic is the highest study, the ascent by argument to the form of the good (7, 532a–534e). Aristotle makes it a method: the *Topics* teaches how to reason about any problem put forward from ἔνδοξα, reputable opinions, “those accepted by everyone, or by most, or by the wise” (1.1, 100b21–23), and to argue on either side without contradicting oneself (see [[topics-of-aristotle]]). He named Zeno of Elea its inventor (Diogenes Laertius 8.57).
