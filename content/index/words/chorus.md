## Meaning

χορός in Homer is a dance and the dancing floor (see [[dance]]). In the Athenian theater it was the group of citizens, twelve or fifteen in tragedy and twenty-four in comedy, who sang and danced the choral odes, led by the κορυφαῖος. Tragedy, Aristotle says, began with “those who led off the dithyramb”, a choral song (1449a10–11; see [[dithyramb]]).
