## Meaning

κολῳός means a brawl, a noisy wrangle. The noun appears only here in Homer. The related verb κολῳάω is used in Book 2 of Thersites, who "alone kept brawling" when the rest of the army had sat down (2.212). The word has been connected with κολοιός, the jackdaw, a noisy, chattering bird, but the etymology is uncertain.
