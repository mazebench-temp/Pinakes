## Meaning

ἀνάγκη is compulsion or necessity, and ἀναγκαῖος “necessary, compulsory”; in Homer ἀναγκαίη is the constraint of war or of need, and ἦμαρ ἀναγκαῖον “the day of compulsion”, slavery (16.836). In Aristotle's logic the necessary is what cannot be otherwise.
