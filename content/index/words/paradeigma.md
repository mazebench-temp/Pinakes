## Meaning

παραδείκνυμι is to show beside, and a παράδειγμα a pattern set beside a thing: an architect's model, a precedent, an example to follow or avoid. Aristotle makes it the rhetorical counterpart of induction, the argument from particular cases. The *Rhetoric*'s second book divides examples into past events and invented ones, comparisons such as Socrates used and fables such as Aesop's and Stesichorus' (2.20).
