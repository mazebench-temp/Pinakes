## Meaning

κήρ (feminine) is doom: the fate of death that waits for each man, and sometimes a death-spirit in person (plural Κῆρες). Achilles says that his mother told him of two κῆρες, two ways his life could end (9.411). When Achilles and Hector meet, Zeus lays two κῆρες of death in his scales, one for each man (22.210).

The word has the same letters as κῆρ, “heart”. The two differ in gender and accent: “heart” has the circumflex, “doom” the acute (grave before another word, as at 1.228). See [[ker-heart|κῆρ]].
