## Meaning

εὐδαίμων is one who has a good δαίμων, a good divine lot (see [[daimon]]); Homer calls the fortunate ὄλβιος or μάκαρ, and εὐδαίμων appears first in Hesiod (*Works and Days* 826). Herodotus' Solon tells Croesus that no one should be called happy before his end (1.30–33; see [[solon]]). In the *Nicomachean Ethics* happiness is the highest good, “an activity of the soul in accordance with virtue” over a complete life (1.7, 1098a16–18).
