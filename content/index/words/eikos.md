## Meaning

εἰκός is the neuter participle of ἔοικα, “be like, seem”: what is likely, reasonable, fitting. Argument from probability was the first weapon of Greek rhetoric, credited to Corax and Tisias in Sicily; Plato's *Phaedrus* mocks it (273a–c), and Aristotle's *Rhetoric* defines the probable as “what happens for the most part” (1.2, 1357a34–35).
