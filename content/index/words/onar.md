## Meaning

ὄναρ is a dream, and ὄνειρος the more common word for it. Dreams in Homer come from outside the sleeper and can carry a god's message. An ὀνειροπόλος (from ὄνειρος and πολέω, “busy oneself with”) is a man who deals with dreams and interprets them.

## Related words

- μάντις (“seer”), ἱερεύς (“priest”).

## Outside Homer

In the *Odyssey* Penelope distinguishes dreams that come through the gate of horn, which are true, from those that come through the gate of ivory, which deceive (*Odyssey* 19.562–567).
