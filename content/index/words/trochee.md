## Meaning

τροχαῖος is “running” (τρέχω). The trochee is a long syllable followed by a short; the trochaic tetrameter, four pairs of trochees, was the meter of early tragedy, and remained in use for lively scenes (see [[trochaic-tetrameter]]).
