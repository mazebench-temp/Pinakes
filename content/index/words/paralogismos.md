## Meaning

παραλογισμός is reasoning that goes astray (παρά, “beside”, λογισμός, “reckoning”). In Aristotle's logic it is a fallacious syllogism; the *Sophistical Refutations* classifies such fallacies, among them the fallacy of the consequent, inferring the antecedent from the consequent (5, 167b1–8).
