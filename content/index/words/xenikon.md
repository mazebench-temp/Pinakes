## Meaning

ξενικός is “of a stranger, foreign” (ξένος). The *Rhetoric* says that people are affected by style as by strangers and fellow citizens, so that one should make one's language a little foreign, “for people are admirers of what is far off” (*Rhetoric* 3.2, 1404b11).
