## Meaning

πρᾶος is “gentle, mild”, used of tame animals, of soft words, and of people slow to anger. In the *Nicomachean Ethics* πραότης is the virtue in the matter of anger, the mean between irascibility and a slavish want of spirit; the gentle man is angry at the right things, with the right people, as he should and as long as he should (4.5, 1125b26–1126a3).
