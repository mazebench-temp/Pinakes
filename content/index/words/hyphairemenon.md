## Meaning

The examples are all old poetic words: κρῖ, “barley”, is Homeric (κρῖ λευκόν, 5.196; see [[barley-meal]]), δῶ “house” Homeric and Hesiodic (see [[house]]), and ὄψ, “sight”, is Empedocles' (see [[one-sight-from-both]]).
