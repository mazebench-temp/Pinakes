## Meaning

ἀναιδείη (Attic ἀναίδεια) is the absence of [[aidos|αἰδώς]]: the refusal to be checked by anyone's claim, human or divine. The adjective ἀναιδής means “shameless”. It can be said even of a thing that will not yield. The stone of Sisyphus is λᾶας ἀναιδής, the “shameless stone” that keeps rolling back (*Odyssey* 11.598).
