## Meaning

κομμός is from κόπτω, “strike”, of mourners beating their heads and breasts. The great kommos of the *Libation Bearers* (306–478), sung by Orestes, Electra and the chorus over Agamemnon's tomb, is the most famous example (see [[lament]]).
