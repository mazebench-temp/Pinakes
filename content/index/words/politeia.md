## Meaning

πολίτης is a citizen, and πολιτεία the citizen's condition and the arrangement of offices in a city. The *Politics* classifies constitutions by who rules and for whose good: kingship, aristocracy and polity are correct, tyranny, oligarchy and democracy their deviations (3.7, 1279a22–b10; see [[politics-of-aristotle]]).
