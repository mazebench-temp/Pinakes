## Meaning

τόπος is a place. The technical sense may come from the art of memory, which stored things in imagined places; the *Topics* compares the arguer who has his topics at hand to the man trained in memory, for whom the places alone bring back the things (8.14, 163b28–32). Aristotle also calls a topic a στοιχεῖον, an “element” (*Rhetoric* 2.26, 1403a17–18). The second book of the *Rhetoric* lists twenty-eight topics of enthymemes and the topics of apparent ones (2.23–24); the *Topics* is a handbook of them for dialectic (see [[topics-of-aristotle]]).
