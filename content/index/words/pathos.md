## Meaning

πάθος belongs to πάσχω, “suffer, undergo, experience”, the opposite of acting (ποιέω, πράττω). It can be a misfortune, an experience, or a feeling of the soul. Herodotus has Croesus say that his sufferings have been his lessons (τὰ δέ μοι παθήματα ἐόντα ἀχάριτα μαθήματα γέγονε, 1.207), the pun that Aeschylus' “learning through suffering” (πάθει μάθος, *Agamemnon* 177) also makes.
