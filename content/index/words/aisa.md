## Meaning

αἶσα is a share or portion, and so a person's lot: what is allotted to him, above all the span of his life. Like [[moira|μοῖρα]], it begins from the idea of a fair division. κατʼ αἶσαν means “fittingly”, and ὑπὲρ αἶσαν “beyond what is due”. Paris uses both when he admits that Hector has rebuked him “fairly and not beyond what is fair” (3.59). The word is often compared with Oscan *aeteis*, “of a part”, but its origin is not certain.
