Herodotus says that among the Ligurians above Massalia σιγύνναι means traders, and among the Cypriots spears (5.9). Aristotle: “the same word can be both rare and standard, but not for the same people: sigynon is standard for the Cypriots, but a rare word for us” (1457b4–6; see [[glotta]], [[kyrion]] and [[cypriots]]).
