## Meaning

ἑταῖρος (Epic also ἕταρος) is a companion: a man who follows a leader by choice and loyalty rather than by birth, and shares his ship, his table and his fighting. The word is probably related to ἔτης, “kinsman”, and to the reflexive ἑ, “oneself”, so that a companion is “one of one's own”, but this is not certain.
