## Meaning

μετάβασις is a “passing over”, from μεταβαίνω, “pass from one place to another”. In the *Poetics* it is the movement of the plot between εὐτυχία, good fortune, and δυστυχία, misfortune. Reversal and recognition are kinds of μεταβολή, “change”, within it (1452a22–31; see [[peripeteia]] and [[anagnorisis]]).
