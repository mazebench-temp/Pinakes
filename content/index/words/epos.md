## Meaning

ἔπος is a word, an utterance, something said. It began with a digamma (ϝέπος) and is cognate with Sanskrit *vácas*, “word”, and Latin *vox*, “voice”; the aorist εἶπον, “I said”, comes from the same root. In the plural it is often simply “words, speech”, as in the formula ἔπεα πτερόεντα προσηύδα, “addressing her with winged words” (1.201). It is paired with ἔργον, “deed”: ἢ ἔπει ἢ ἔργῳ, “by word or deed” (1.395, 1.504).

Where [[mythos|μῦθος]] tends to be authoritative speech, a command or a threat, ἔπος is the neutral word for what is said.
