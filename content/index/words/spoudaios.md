## Meaning

σπουδαῖος comes from σπουδή, “haste, effort, earnestness”: a man worth taking seriously, a person of worth, and so “good” in a moral and social sense; of a thing, “serious, important”. φαῦλος is “slight, cheap, ordinary”, and of people “mean, inferior, bad”. The pair is common in fourth-century prose, where it can describe social standing, moral quality, and the quality of a work.
