## Meaning

In Homer ἥρως is a respectful word for a warrior. It carries no hint of divinity. The Iliad calls the men who fought at Troy “demigods” (ἡμίθεοι) only once, looking back from a later age (12.23; see [[hemitheos]]). In later Greek the heroes are the powerful dead, honored with cult at their tombs; Hesiod's race of heroes, “who are called demigods”, already points that way (*Works and Days* 159–160). The etymology is uncertain.
