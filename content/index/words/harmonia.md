## Meaning

ἁρμονία is a joining, from ἁρμόζω, “fit together”: in Homer the joints and fastenings of Odysseus' raft (*Odyssey* 5.248, 5.361), and an agreement between enemies (22.255). In music it is the tuning of the strings, and so a scale or mode, the Dorian, the Phrygian, the Lydian, each held to have its own effect on character (Plato, *Republic* 3.398c–399c; Aristotle, *Politics* 8.5, 1340a40–b5).
