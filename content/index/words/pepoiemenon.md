## Meaning

πεποιημένον is “made”, the perfect participle of ποιέω, used here in its literal sense: a word the poet makes. ἀρητήρ is Homer's word for the priest Chryses (1.11, 1.94; see [[priesthood]]); whether it was really coined by a poet is doubtful.
