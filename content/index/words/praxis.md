## Meaning

πρᾶξις is the noun of πράττω, “do, act, fare”. οἱ πράττοντες are “the people acting”, the characters of a drama. Homer has the Ionic form πρῆξις, “business, result”, and Aristotle reports that the Athenians say πράττειν where the Dorians say δρᾶν, both meaning “do” (1448b1–2; see [[drama]]).
