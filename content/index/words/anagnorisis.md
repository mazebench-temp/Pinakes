## Meaning

ἀναγνωρίζω is “know again, recognize”, ἀναγνώρισις the noun. Recognition scenes are as old as Greek poetry: the Odyssey is built from them, Odysseus known by his dog, his nurse, his son, his wife and his father, and tragedy inherited them (see [[odysseus-recognized-by-his-nurse]]). Aristotle made the recognition a part of the plot and the subject of a whole chapter.

## After Aristotle

“Discovery” is the older English rendering. Terence Cave's *Recognitions* (1988) traces the device and Aristotle's theory of it through European literature.
