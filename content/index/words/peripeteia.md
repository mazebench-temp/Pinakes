## Meaning

περιπέτεια is a “falling around”, from περιπίπτω, “fall in with, fall into”: a sudden turn, a change of fortune. Aristotle makes it technical. A reversal is not any change of fortune (that is μετάβασις; see [[metabasis]]) but an action that produces the opposite of what it was meant to do.

{{notes}}

## After Aristotle

“Peripety” entered English criticism in the seventeenth century. Modern readers have often taken the word as a synonym for the change of fortune; Aristotle's examples show that he means irony of action, the deed that recoils on its doer.
