## Meaning

Aristotle's examples are epic forms, which to an Athenian sounded lengthened versions of his own: πόληος is the Homeric genitive of πόλις, Πηληιάδεω the epic patronymic of Achilles beside the Attic Πηλείδου (see [[son-of-peleus]]).
