## Meaning

ἦθος is a person's settled disposition, the kind of person he is; in the plural, characters or manners. In Homer the word occurs only in the plural ἤθεα, the haunts or accustomed places of animals, “the haunts and pasture of the horses” (6.511). From this the sense of “custom, habit” and then “character” developed. Aristotle derives ἠθική ἀρετή, “virtue of character”, from ἔθος, “habit” (*Nicomachean Ethics* 2.1, 1103a17–18).
