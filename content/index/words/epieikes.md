## Meaning

ἐπιεικής is “fitting, suitable” (εἰκός, “likely, fitting”), and of persons “reasonable, fair, decent”, the man who does not insist on the letter of his rights. In Aristotle's *Ethics* ἐπιείκεια is equity, the correction of law where it fails through being general (*Nicomachean Ethics* 5.10, 1137b11–13).
