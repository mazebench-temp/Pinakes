## Meaning

ἄχος is grief or distress: the pain of loss, of humiliation, of seeing one's own suffer. It often comes suddenly and is close to anger. The verb ἄχνυμαι, “be distressed, grieve”, is most often met as the participle ἀχνύμενος.

L. R. Palmer proposed, and Gregory Nagy developed (*The Best of the Achaeans*, 1979), a derivation of Achilles' name from ἄχος and [[laos|λαός]]: “he whose people have grief”. The proposal is disputed.
