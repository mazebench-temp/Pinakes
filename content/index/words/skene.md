## Meaning

σκηνή is a tent or booth; in the theater it was the wooden building at the back of the orchestra, which served as palace or temple front and as the actors' dressing room, and from it comes “scene”. σκηνογραφία was the painting of its front with scenery; Vitruvius credits the first such painting at Athens to Agatharchus, for a tragedy of Aeschylus (7, preface 11).
