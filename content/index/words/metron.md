## Meaning

μέτρον is a measure, then due measure, moderation (“nothing too much”), and in poetry the meter, the measured pattern of long and short syllables. Homer has the word only in the sense of measure, length or size. Verse is ἔμμετρος, “in meter”; prose ἄμετρος or ψιλὸς λόγος, “bare speech”. Aristotle's own works on meter, to which the *Poetics* refers (1456b34, 1456b38), are lost.
