## Meaning

Protagoras is said to have been the first to distinguish the genders of nouns, “male, female and things” (Aristotle, *Rhetoric* 3.5, 1407b6–8). Aristotle calls the neuter τὰ μεταξύ, “those in between”; the grammatical term οὐδέτερον, “neither”, is later.
