## Meaning

ἐκπλήσσω is “strike out, drive out of one's senses”, and ἔκπληξις the shock of amazement or terror. Gorgias makes it the effect of speech; for the critics of later antiquity it became the special power of the sublime style (Longinus, *On the Sublime* 15.2).
