## Meaning

σῆμα and σημεῖον are a sign, mark or token: in Homer a grave-marker, a portent, or the “grim signs” Proetus sent with Bellerophon (6.168; see [[bellerophons-letter]]). In the recognition scenes of epic and tragedy a token proves identity: Odysseus' scar, the lock of hair and the footprints at Agamemnon's tomb in the *Libation Bearers*, the woven cloth that Orestes carries.
