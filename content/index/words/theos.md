## Meaning

θεός (god) is used of male and female deities alike, and θεά is the special feminine form. A god in Homer is defined by one thing above all: gods do not die. They are ἀθάνατοι, "deathless", and θεοὶ αἰὲν ἐόντες, "who live forever" (1.290, 1.494). Mortals are βροτοί. The etymology of θεός is disputed.
