## Meaning

ἄλγος is pain, of body or of mind. In the plural, ἄλγεα, it means the sufferings that fall on people, especially in war: “woes, sorrows”. The Odyssey's proem uses it as the Iliad's does, of the many sorrows Odysseus suffered at sea (*Odyssey* 1.4). The etymology is uncertain.
