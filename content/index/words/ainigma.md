## Meaning

αἴνιγμα comes from αἶνος, a tale with a hidden meaning, and αἰνίσσομαι, “speak darkly”. Riddles were a game of the Greek symposium; the riddle of the Sphinx was the most famous, and the riddle Homer could not solve, of the fishermen and their lice, was told in the *Contest of Homer and Hesiod*.
