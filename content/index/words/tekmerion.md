## Meaning

τεκμήριον is the diminutive of τέκμαρ, τέκμωρ, a fixed sign or limit (see [[tekmor]]). Historians and orators use it of evidence that settles a question: Thucydides judges the remote past “from evidences” (ἐκ τεκμηρίων, 1.1.3). Aristotle limits it to the conclusive sign, and the *Prior Analytics* gives the same doctrine as the *Rhetoric* (2.27, 70b1–6; see [[analytics]] and [[semeion]]).
