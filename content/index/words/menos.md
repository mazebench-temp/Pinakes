## Meaning

μένος (“might, fury, ardor”) is a surge of energy: the force in a warrior's limbs, the power of a god, the strength of fire, wind or a river. A god can put it into a man, and it can fill him (1.103). The word is cognate with Sanskrit *mánas*, “mind, spirit”, from a root meaning “have in mind, be intent on”. The same root gives μέμονα, “be eager, strive”.
