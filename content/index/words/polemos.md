## Meaning

πόλεμος (also πτόλεμος, a doublet like πόλις and πτόλις) is war, and also the fighting of a single day: “the battle”. μάχη is a battle or fight, and μάχομαι “fight”. Among the epithets of war are πολυάϊξ, “furious, rushing” (1.165), and κακός, “evil” (1.284). The etymology of πόλεμος is unknown.

## Outside Homer

Heraclitus called war “father of all and king of all” (fragment B53 DK).
