## Meaning

στοιχεῖον is one of a row (στοῖχος), and so an element; the letters of the alphabet are the elements of speech, and the four elements of Empedocles the elements of nature. Plato's *Theaetetus* (201e–203c) and *Cratylus* (424c–425b) already analyze speech into letters and syllables.
