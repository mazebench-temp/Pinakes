## Meaning

μέλος in Homer is a limb of the body; the sense “song, melody” is later, first in the *Homeric Hymn to Hermes* and the lyric poets. μελοποιία is the making of song, the composition of words for music, and μελοποιός a composer of songs, a lyric poet.
