## Meaning

ἐπίηρα φέρειν means "to do someone a favor, to please them". The word is written as one word or as two, ἐπὶ ἦρα, and ancient scholars already argued over which was right. Written as two words, the phrase contains ἦρα φέρειν, which is used elsewhere in Homer (*Iliad* 14.132; *Odyssey* 3.164) and means the same. In Homer the noun ἦρα appears only in this phrase.
