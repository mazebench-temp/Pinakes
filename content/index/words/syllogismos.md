## Meaning

συλλογίζομαι is to reckon up, compute, conclude. Aristotle defines the συλλογισμός as “an argument in which, certain things being laid down, something other than these follows of necessity through them” (*Topics* 1.1, 100a25–27; *Prior Analytics* 1.1, 24b18–20), and the *Prior Analytics* sets out its forms (see [[analytics]]). Its counterpart is ἐπαγωγή, induction, the argument from particulars to the universal. The false syllogism, the παραλογισμός, is the subject of the *Sophistical Refutations* (see [[paralogismos]]).
