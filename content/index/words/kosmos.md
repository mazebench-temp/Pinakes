## Meaning

κόσμος is order, good arrangement, and adornment: in Homer κατὰ κόσμον, “in good order”, of horses and troops and of speech, and the κόσμος of a woman is her finery; κοσμήτορες λαῶν, “marshals of the people”, are those who set men in order. Pythagoras is said to have been the first to call the universe κόσμος, “the order”.
