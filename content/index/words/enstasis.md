## Meaning

ἔνστασις is literally a standing against, an obstacle; in Aristotle's logic it is an objection to a premise, often a counter-instance to a universal claim. The *Topics* treats it in its last book (8.10, 161a1–15), and the *Prior Analytics* defines it as a premise contrary to a premise (2.26, 69a37). Solving, λύσις, is the general name for answering an argument (see [[dialektike]] and [[topics-of-aristotle]]).
