## Meaning

The iamb is a short syllable followed by a long (⏑ —). The iambic trimeter has six iambs grouped in three “measures”, with substitutions allowed; it was the meter of Archilochus' invective and became the meter of spoken dialogue in tragedy and comedy. Aristotle derives its name from ἰαμβίζειν, “lampoon” (see [[iambus]]).
