## Meaning

ἐνθυμέομαι is to take to heart, to consider (θυμός, “spirit”), and an ἐνθύμημα is what one considers: a thought, an argument. Isocrates uses the word for the thoughts with which an orator adorns his whole speech (*Against the Sophists* 16). Aristotle makes it the rhetorical form of deduction: “an enthymeme is a syllogism from probabilities or signs” (*Prior Analytics* 2.27, 70a10). Later handbooks defined it as a syllogism with one premise left unstated, which describes one of its features rather than its nature.
