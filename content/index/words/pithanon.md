## Meaning

πιθανός is “persuasive, plausible”, from πείθω, “persuade” (see [[peitho]]). Aristotle defines rhetoric as the power of seeing in each case “the possible means of persuasion” (τὸ ἐνδεχόμενον πιθανόν, *Rhetoric* 1.2, 1355b25–26).
