## Meaning

ὕπνος is sleep. "Sweet sleep" (γλυκὺς ὕπνος) is formulaic. Like Death, Sleep is also a god. In Book 14, Hera finds him on Lemnos and bribes him to put Zeus to sleep (14.230–291). In Book 16 he and his twin brother Death carry Sarpedon home (16.671–683).
