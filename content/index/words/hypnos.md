## Meaning

ὕπνος is sleep. “Sweet sleep” (γλυκὺς ὕπνος, νήδυμος ὕπνος) is formulaic (see [[sweet-sleep]]). Sleep comes upon a man and holds him, and it is poured around him like a liquid: Agamemnon's Dream finds him “sleeping in his hut, and ambrosial sleep was poured around him” (2.19). The verbs of sleeping are εὕδω and καθεύδω; κοιμάομαι is to lie down to sleep, and κοιμάω and κατευνάω are to lull another to sleep.

Sleep is also a god, Ὕπνος, the brother of Death, and with Death he carries Sarpedon's body home to Lycia (16.671–683). See [[sleep]] and [[death]].
