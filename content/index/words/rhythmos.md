## Meaning

ῥυθμός is measured movement, form, shape; the root is disputed (ῥέω, “flow”, or ἐρύω, “draw”). Ionic philosophers used it of the shape of atoms; in music and dance it is the ordered pattern of time, of which meter, in Aristotle's phrase, is a section.
