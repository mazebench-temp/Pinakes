## Meaning

σύστασις is a “standing together”, the noun of συνίστημι, “put together, construct”; σύνθεσις is a “putting together”. τὰ πράγματα are “the things done”, the incidents or events. Aristotle uses the phrases for the plot as a made thing: “by plot I mean here the arrangement of the incidents” (1450a4–5; see [[mythos]]).
