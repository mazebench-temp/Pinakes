## Meaning

πένθος is grief, and above all the grief of mourning. It belongs with πάσχω, “suffer”, and πάθος, “suffering”: all three come from one root. Where [[algos|ἄλγος]] is pain and [[achos|ἄχος]] a sharp distress, πένθος is the sorrow that settles in and lasts.
