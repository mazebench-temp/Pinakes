## Meaning

θαῦμα is a wonder, a thing to marvel at, and θαυμάζω “wonder, marvel”; in Homer θαῦμα ἰδέσθαι, “a wonder to behold”, is said of divine armor and craftsmanship. Plato and Aristotle make wonder the beginning of philosophy (*Theaetetus* 155d; *Metaphysics* 1.2, 982b12–13).
