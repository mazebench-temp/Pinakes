## Meaning

ζῆλος is eager rivalry, the wish to equal another, and also the admiration felt for someone thought enviable; ζηλωτός is “to be envied, admired”, as in the formula for calling someone happy. Hesiod's good Strife, that sets neighbor against neighbor in work, is a kind of ζῆλος (*Works and Days* 23, ζηλοῖ δέ τε γείτονα γείτων; see [[potter-against-potter]]). Aristotle separates it from envy (see [[phthonos]]).
