summary: The ἐπίθετον, a word added to a noun, such as “white” in “white milk”: in poetry an ornament, in prose a seasoning to be used with measure.
aliases: [epitheton, epithets]
---

In the *Rhetoric* epithets, like metaphors, can praise or blame: one may call a man “the matricide” or “the avenger of his father” (*Rhetoric* 3.2, 1405b20–23). In poetry it is appropriate to say “white milk”, but in prose epithets that are long, untimely or crowded are frigid (*Rhetoric* 3.3, 1406a10–32). “White milk” is Homer's: γάλα λευκόν (*Iliad* 4.434, 5.902).
