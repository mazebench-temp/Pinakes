## Revel and village

The κῶμος was the merry procession of revelers, with song, dance and the pipe, that followed a feast or honored a god or a victor; Pindar calls the celebration of an athlete's victory a κῶμος, and his odes were sung by such bands. Comedy, κωμῳδία, was taken to be the song of the κῶμος. The Dorians of the Peloponnese offered another derivation: they call the outlying villages κῶμαι, where the Athenians say δῆμοι, and they claimed that the comedians were named not from revelling (κωμάζειν) but from wandering from village to village when they were kept out of the city (1448a35–38; see [[demos]], [[dorians]] and [[the-origins-of-comedy]]).
