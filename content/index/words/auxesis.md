## Meaning

αὔξω, Homeric ἀέξω, is to increase, to make grow: anger “swells in the breasts of men like smoke” (ἀέξεται, 18.110; see [[sweeter-than-dripping-honey]]). In rhetoric αὔξησις and its opposite, minimizing, are the arts of making things great or small. The *Rhetoric*'s second book says that amplifying and minimizing are not themselves topics of enthymemes, but enthymemes that show a thing to be great or small (2.26, 1403a17–20).
