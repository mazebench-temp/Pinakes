## Meaning

ὑποκρίνομαι in Homer is “interpret, answer”: Polydamas says that a seer would interpret an omen as he does (12.228), and Penelope asks the disguised Odysseus to interpret her dream (*Odyssey* 19.535). The actor was originally the one who answered the chorus. In the fifth century the state assigned actors to the poets, and from 449 BCE there was a prize for the best tragic actor at the Dionysia.
