## Meaning

ἄδικος is without justice (δίκη), and ἀδικέω to act unjustly, to wrong someone. The *Nicomachean Ethics* distinguishes the same three kinds of harm as the *Rhetoric*: the misfortune, which is unexpected; the error, which is not unexpected but not vicious; and the wrong, done knowingly but from passion or from choice (5.8, 1135b11–25). Homer's words for what is right are θέμις and δίκη (see [[themis]]).
