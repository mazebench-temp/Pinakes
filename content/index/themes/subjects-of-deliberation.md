## The five subjects

People deliberate only about what can happen or not and lies within their power, and the matters that come before an assembly reduce to five: revenue, war and peace, the defense of the country, imports and exports, and legislation. For each the adviser needs knowledge of his own city and of others, which belongs to politics more than to rhetoric (see [[politeia]] and [[sympheron]]).
