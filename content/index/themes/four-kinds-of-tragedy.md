## The four kinds

The four answer, Aristotle says, to the parts of tragedy he has named, though which parts he means is disputed (see [[simple-and-complex-plots]] and [[pathos]]). The name of the fourth kind is corrupt in the manuscripts. Its examples, the *Daughters of Phorcys*, the *Prometheus* and plays set in Hades, suggest “spectacle” (ὄψις), and that is the usual restoration; but in chapter 24 the kinds of epic are listed as simple, complex, of character and of suffering, which suggests “simple” instead (1459b7–9; see [[opsis]], [[ajax-plays]], [[ixion-plays]], [[women-of-phthia]], [[peleus-play]], [[daughters-of-phorcys]] and [[prometheus-play]]).
