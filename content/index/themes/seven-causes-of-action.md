## The division

All that people do is done either not through themselves, by chance, nature or force, or through themselves, by habit or by longing, rational or irrational; rational longing is wish, which aims at the good, and the irrational longings are anger and appetite. Age, wealth and other conditions are not causes, though they go with some of them. The division serves the accuser and the defendant, who must show which causes were or were not present (see [[tyche]], [[epithymia]], [[thymos]] and [[prohairesis]]).
