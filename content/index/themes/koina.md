## The three common topics

Each kind of speech has its own subject, but all must sometimes argue that a thing is possible or impossible, that it has or has not happened, or will or will not happen, and that it is great or small. These are the common topics, κοινά, of the second book (2.18–19). Amplification suits epideictic, past fact the courts, the possible and the future the assembly (2.18, 1392a4–7). The first book had already named them as needed by all speakers (1.3, 1359a11–26). See [[topos]], [[auxesis]], [[megethos]] and [[three-kinds-of-rhetoric]].
