στείρη is the front of the keel, the stem that cuts the water. On the voyage back from Chryse, with Apollo's wind in the [[sails|sail]], the wave roars around the cutwater as the ship runs on ({{ref:Iliad 1.481-483}}). The Iliad seldom shows a ship under sail. The same lines describe Telemachus' voyage (*Odyssey* 2.427–429).
