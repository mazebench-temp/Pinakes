## The object

The masks of the Athenian theater covered the whole head, were made of linen, cork or wood, and were painted; they let three actors play every part, women included. Latin *persona* was the actor's mask before it was the person. Aristotle says that the art of the σκευοποιός, the maker of masks and costumes, matters more for spectacle than the poet's (1450b19–20; see [[opsis]]).
