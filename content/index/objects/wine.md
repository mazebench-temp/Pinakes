Wine was the ordinary drink of the Homeric table, mixed with water in the [[mixing-bowl|mixing bowl]] and poured out for the gods. Its standing epithet αἴθοψ is usually rendered “sparkling” or “fiery” (from αἴθω, “burn”, and ὤψ, “look”).

{{notes}}

## Outside Homer

[[Hesiod]] warns never to pour sparkling wine to Zeus at dawn with unwashed hands (*Works and Days* 724–725).
