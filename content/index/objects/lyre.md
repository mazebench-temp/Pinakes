The φόρμιγξ is Homer's word for the lyre of the singer. Demodocus and Phemius play it in the Odyssey, and Achilles sings to one in his hut, a lyre he took from the spoils of Eetion's city (Iliad 9.186–189).

{{notes}}

## Outside Homer

The Homeric Hymn to Hermes tells how the newborn Hermes made the first lyre from a tortoise shell (lines 24–51) and later gave it to Apollo.
