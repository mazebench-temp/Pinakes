## The object

The μηχανή was a crane at the side of the stage building, used to show gods appearing in the air and heroes in flight. A god “from the machine” (ἀπὸ μηχανῆς θεός) appeared at the end of many plays of Euripides to settle what the action could not; the Latin *deus ex machina* translates the Greek phrase. In the *Medea* the heroine escapes in the chariot of the Sun, drawn by serpents.
