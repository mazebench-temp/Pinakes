The σκάφη is a tub or trough, and so a small boat. The child set adrift in a vessel on the water is one of the commonest of legends: Danae and the infant Perseus in a chest, Moses in his basket of bulrushes, Romulus and Remus in their cradle on the Tiber. In Sophocles' *Tyro* the twins were recognized through the boat in which their mother had set them out (see [[tyro-and-her-sons]], [[tyro]] and [[tyro-play]]).
