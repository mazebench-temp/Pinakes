The πρότονοι (πρό + τείνω, “stretched forward”) are the two stays that hold the [[mast|mast]] from the front. On arrival at Chryse the crew lower the mast “by the forestays” and bring it to the [[mast-crutch|mast-crutch]]: paying out the stays lets the mast come down backward ({{ref:Iliad 1.434}}). When a ship puts to sea, the mast is raised and made fast with the forestays (*Odyssey* 2.425). In the storm after Thrinacia the wind snaps both of them and the mast falls (*Odyssey* 12.409–410).
