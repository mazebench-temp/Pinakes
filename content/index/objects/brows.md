Zeus seals his promise to [[Thetis]] with a nod of the head, “the greatest sign” he can give among the immortals ({{ref:Iliad 1.524-527}}; see [[neuo]] and [[tekmor]]). He nods “with his dark brows” (κυανέῃσιν ἐπ᾽ ὀφρύσι), his ambrosial [[hair|hair]] flows down, and Olympus shakes. κυάνεος, “dark”, comes from κύανος, a dark blue enamel or glass paste.

## Outside Homer

[[Strabo]] (8.3.30) reports that when Phidias was asked what model he would follow for his statue of Zeus at Olympia, he answered with these lines (see [[phidias-zeus]]).
