In Homer nectar is the gods' drink, as [[ambrosia|ambrosia]] is their food. It is red (19.38; *Odyssey* 5.93), and it is mixed in a bowl and served in cups like wine (1.597–598, 4.2–3). The name is often explained as “overcoming death” (νεκ-, “death”, and a root meaning “pass beyond”), but the derivation is uncertain.
