χαλκός is copper and its alloy bronze. The weapons, armor and cutting tools of Homer's heroes are bronze, and the word alone can mean a blade. For the precious metals, see [[gold-and-silver]].

Iron (σίδηρος; see [[iron]]) is known to the poems, but mostly as a precious metal and in similes. A lump of iron is a prize at Patroclus' games (Iliad 23.826–835), and a smith quenching a hot axe in cold water gives the sound of the Cyclops' eye (Odyssey 9.391–393). The heroes fight with the bronze of an earlier age, while the poet's own world already worked iron.
