πρυμνήσια (from πρύμνη, the [[stern|stern]]) are the stern ropes. A Homeric ship moored with its stern to the land, the bow held off by [[mooring-stones|mooring-stones]]. At Chryse the crew make the stern-cables fast when they land ({{ref:Iliad 1.436}}). After the sacrifice, the feast and the paean, they lie down to sleep beside them, and at dawn they put to sea ({{ref:Iliad 1.476}}). In the Odyssey, loosing the stern-cables (πρυμνήσια λῦσαι) is a regular step in setting sail.
