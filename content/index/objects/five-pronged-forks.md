A πεμπώβολον is a fork with five prongs (πέμπε, the Aeolic form of πέντε, “five”, + ὀβολός, “spit”). At Chryse, while the old priest burns the [[thigh-pieces|thigh-pieces]] and pours wine over them, young men stand beside him holding five-pronged forks ({{ref:Iliad 1.463}}). They were probably used to hold the entrails in the fire, since the next step is tasting them ({{ref:Iliad 1.464}}; see [[sacrifice|sacrifice]]). The same line describes Nestor's sacrifice at Pylos (Odyssey 3.460).
