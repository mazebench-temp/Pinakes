The Achaean ships were drawn up on the beach stern first, with their bows to the sea, so their sterns faced the plain and the enemy. Achilles asks [[Thetis]] to persuade Zeus to help the Trojans and pen the Achaeans in “among their ships' sterns and along the sea” as they are killed, so that they may all “have joy of their king”.

{{quote:4dd5afa9-8033-5b4c-9ce6-7a90e33c200d}}

The wish comes true in Book 15, when Hector reaches the ships, seizes the stern of Protesilaus' ship and calls for fire (Iliad 15.716–718). The [[stern-cables|stern-cables]] (πρυμνήσια) take their name from the stern.
