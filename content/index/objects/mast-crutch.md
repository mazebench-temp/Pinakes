ἱστοδόκη means “mast-receiver” (ἱστός, “mast”, + δέχομαι, “receive”). When the ship reaches the harbor at Chryse, the crew slacken the [[forestays|forestays]] and bring the [[mast|mast]] down into it ({{ref:Iliad 1.434}}). A Homeric ship lowered its mast in harbor and under oars, and the crutch held it where it lay. The word occurs only here in Homer.
