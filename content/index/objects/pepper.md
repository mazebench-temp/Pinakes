title: Pepper
greek: πέπερι

summary: Pepper, brought from India; the Greek word came from the East with the spice. For Aristotle one of the only three Greek nouns that end in iota.
aliases: [peperi]
---

Pepper reached the Greeks from India by way of Persia, and the Greek πέπερι goes back to the Sanskrit name of long pepper, *pippalī*. The Hippocratic doctors prescribed “the Indian remedy, which the Persians call pepper”, and Theophrastus describes a round kind and a long one (*Enquiry into Plants* 9.20.1). Aristotle lists πέπερι with μέλι and κόμμι as the only nouns ending in iota (1458a15–16; see [[honey]], [[gum]] and [[gender-of-nouns]]).
