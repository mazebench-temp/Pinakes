## The epigram

The victor, who had carried fish on a yoke across the mountains from Argos to Tegea, celebrated his victory by recalling the trade he had left (see [[argos]] and [[tegea]]). Aristotle names neither the victor nor the poet; editors print the verses among the fragments of Simonides (see [[simonides]]).
