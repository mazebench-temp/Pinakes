## The sentence

The manuscripts of the *Rhetoric* give only the reference, “as Alcidamas says in his Messenian Speech”; the words themselves come from an ancient commentator on the passage, and editors print them in the text (see [[messenian-speech]] and [[alcidamas]]). Spoken for the Messenians freed from Sparta, the sentence goes further than its occasion: no one is a slave by nature. In the *Politics* Aristotle reports that some thought slavery contrary to nature, a matter of law and force (1.3, 1253b20–23), and argues that some men are slaves by nature (1.5; see [[slavery-and-freedom]]).
