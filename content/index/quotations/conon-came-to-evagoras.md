Isocrates' encomium of Evagoras, king of Salamis in Cyprus, argues from the choice of the best judge: Conon, the first of the Greeks, chose him above all others as his refuge (*Evagoras* 51–52). Aristotle puts it under the topic from a previous judgment, beside Isocrates' proofs of Helen's worth from Theseus' choice and of Alexander's from the goddesses' (*Rhetoric* 2.23, 1399a1–6). See [[evagoras]], [[conon]], [[isocrates]] and [[topics-of-enthymemes]].
