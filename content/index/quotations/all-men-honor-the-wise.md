{{quote:064443fd-6980-5e48-9e91-8278f1ef635b}}

Each case is chosen to make the honor more striking: Archilochus was a slanderer, Homer not a citizen of Chios, Sappho a woman, the Spartans the least literary of the Greeks, Anaxagoras a foreigner at Lampsacus. Aristotle gives it as another example of induction (*Rhetoric* 2.23, 1398b10–20). From which of Alcidamas' works it comes is not known.
