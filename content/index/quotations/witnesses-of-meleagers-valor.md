The speaker is perhaps someone who wishes to explain why so many heroes joined the hunt. Aristotle sets beside it the argument from Theodectes' *Ajax*, that Diomedes chose Odysseus as his companion not to honor him but so that his follower might be the lesser man (*Rhetoric* 2.23, 1399b28–31; see [[ajax-plays]] and, for the choice, Iliad 10.242–247). See [[meleager-antiphon]], [[the-calydonian-boar]] and [[topics-of-enthymemes]].
