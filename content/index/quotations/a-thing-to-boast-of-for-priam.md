## In the Iliad

Agamemnon tests the army by proposing flight, and the men rush for the ships. Hera sends Athena down, and Athena repeats the same words to Odysseus, who goes through the camp and stops the flight (2.155–210; see [[the-test-of-the-army]]). The boast, εὐχωλή, is Helen herself: after so many deaths for her sake, the prize would be left in Troy (see [[argive-helen]] and [[euchomai]]).
