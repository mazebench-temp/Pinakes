Pindar's lines are from a lost poem to Pan, “dog” of the Great Mother, Rhea or Cybele, whose follower he was. The fallacy of homonymy uses the same word for different things: a speaker praising the dog brings in the dog in the heavens, the Dog Star, and Pan, because Pindar called him a dog (*Rhetoric* 2.24, 1401a15–21).
