{{quote:5294312a-949a-57ef-9bed-d0dd8de7d7bd}}

The topic from analogy pairs like cases with like treatment. Iphicrates' answer to those who made his tall son, still under age, perform a public service is the first example: if tall boys are men, short men will be voted boys. Theodectes applies the same proportion to the mercenaries (*Rhetoric* 2.23, 1399a35–1399b4). See [[the-law-theodectes]], [[strabax]], [[charidemus]], [[iphicrates]] and [[topics-of-enthymemes]].
