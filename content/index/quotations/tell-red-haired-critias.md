## The line

The Critias of Solon's verse is an ancestor of the oligarch, of the family that Plato's *Charmides* says was praised by Anacreon, Solon and many other poets (157e; see [[critias]] and [[solon]]). Advice to a young man to obey his father becomes, in Cleophon's hands, proof that the family needed such advice.
