## In Euripides

In the *Orestes* Electra tends her brother, sick and maddened after the murder of their mother; he asks her to lift him up and set him on his feet, and then to lay him down again, for “change in all things is sweet” (*Orestes* 218–234; see [[orestes-play]]). In the *Nicomachean Ethics* Aristotle quotes the line, “according to the poet”, and adds that change is sweet because of a certain badness in our nature, which is not simple (7.14, 1154b28–31; see [[euripides]]).
