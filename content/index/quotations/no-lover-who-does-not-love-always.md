Menelaus has decided that Helen will be taken home to die, and Hecuba warns him not to let her board his ship: once a lover, always a lover (*Trojan Women* 1049–1051). Euripides' line begins οὐκ ἔστʼ ἐραστής; Aristotle quotes it as οὐδεὶς ἐραστής. For Aristotle it is a maxim whose truth is plain to anyone who looks at it, and so needs no reason added (*Rhetoric* 2.21, 1394b14–16). See [[gnome]] and [[helen]].
