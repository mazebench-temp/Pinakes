## In the Iliad

Dione comforts the wounded Aphrodite with the pains that gods have suffered from men: Ares was bound by the sons of Aloeus, Hera was struck by Heracles “in the right breast with a three-barbed arrow”, and Hades was shot “in Pylos among the dead” (5.382–404; see [[dione]] and [[heracles-wounds-hera-and-hades]]). Homer has both forms of the adjective, δεξιός and δεξιτερός.
