The best maxims, Aristotle says, are enthymematic without being part of an enthymeme: the cause shows in the words themselves. Here “one should not keep it” is the maxim and “being mortal” the reason (*Rhetoric* 2.21, 1394b21–25). The line is probably from a tragedy; its author is unknown. See [[gnome]], [[orge]] and [[mortal-thoughts]].
