Isocrates' *Antidosis*, his defense of his life and teaching in the form of a court speech, argues at length that it is his philosophy, the education he gives, that is on trial; whether Aristotle quotes that speech or another is not certain. The topic: if the consequence is the same, the things from which it follows are the same, as Xenophanes said that those who say the gods are born are as impious as those who say they die (*Rhetoric* 2.23, 1399b5–11; see [[xenophanes]]). See [[isocrates]] and [[topics-of-enthymemes]].
