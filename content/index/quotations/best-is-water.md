## In Pindar

The ode for Hieron of Syracuse, victor in the horse race at Olympia in 476 BCE, opens with a list of the best of each kind: water is best, and gold, like fire blazing in the night, shines out above all proud wealth; but if you would sing of games, look for no star in the empty sky brighter than the sun, and no contest greater than Olympia (*Olympian* 1.1–7). Water stands first among the elements, gold among possessions, the sun among the stars, and Olympia among the games (see [[pindar]] and [[olympic-games]]).
