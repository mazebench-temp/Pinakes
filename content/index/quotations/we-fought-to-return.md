The topic: people do not always choose the same things before and after, but the reverse. At one time the exiles chose to fight in order to stay at home; now they would choose not to stay at home in order not to fight (*Rhetoric* 2.23, 1399b15–19). The same argument stands in the speech of Lysias against a proposal, after the return of the democrats under Thrasybulus, to limit the citizenship to those who owned land (34.11). See [[thrasybulus]], [[the-thirty]] and [[topics-of-enthymemes]].
