{{quote:423764cc-7a06-56bc-9397-011105ebb5e7}}

The line is an iambic trimeter, probably from a tragedy, but no ancient source names its author. Aristotle quotes it after saying that people envy those near them in time, place, age and reputation (*Rhetoric* 2.10, 1388a5–8): envy is felt toward one's like, and no one is more like than kin. See [[phthonos]].
