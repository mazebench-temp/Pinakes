{{quote:5a39b6eb-44b8-540c-9873-25b0d4725798}}

The glorious son is Meleager, the son of Oeneus king of Calydon, whose death came about through his mother Althaea (see [[meleager]] and [[oeneus]]). Who speaks, and to whom, is lost; perhaps someone comparing Oeneus' grief with that of another bereaved father. The words “of Greece”, τὸν Ἑλλάδος, are bracketed in Ross's text. See [[topics-of-enthymemes]].
