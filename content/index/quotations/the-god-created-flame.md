## The phrase

The poet is unknown. The Doric form θεοκτίσταν (Attic θεοκτίστην, “god-created”) is the dialect of choral lyric and of the choruses of tragedy, so the phrase may come from either. The one who sows is the sun, which casts its light abroad as a sower casts seed.
