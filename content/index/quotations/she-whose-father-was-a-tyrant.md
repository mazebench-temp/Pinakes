## The epitaph

Thucydides quotes the whole epitaph to show that Hippias, the son of Pisistratus, married his daughter Archedice to Aeantides, son of the tyrant of Lampsacus on the Hellespont, and that her tomb stood there: “This dust covers Archedice, daughter of Hippias, the foremost man in Greece of his day; though her father, husband, brothers and sons were tyrants, her mind was not lifted up to arrogance” (6.59.3). Aristotle names Simonides as the poet (see [[simonides]] and [[pisistratus]]).
