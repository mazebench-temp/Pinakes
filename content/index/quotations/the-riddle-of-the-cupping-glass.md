## The riddle

In full it is an elegiac couplet: “I saw a man who with fire had welded bronze upon a man, so closely that he made them one blood” (Athenaeus 10.452b, who gives it as Cleobulina's; see [[cleobulina]]). The answer is the cupping glass (σικύα), a bronze cup that the physician heated and pressed to the skin, so that as it cooled it drew the flesh and the blood into itself. Each word is true of the operation, and together they describe an impossibility, which, says Aristotle, is the form of a riddle (see [[ainigma]]).
