## In the Iliad

Nestor rises between Achilles and Agamemnon when their quarrel has gone as far as words can go. He begins not with the wrong done or the honor at stake but with the enemy: the quarrel of the two best men of the army is just what Priam and his sons would wish to hear of. Only then does he claim the authority of his age and give his advice (1.259–284; see [[nestor]] and [[quarrel-of-achilles-and-agamemnon]]).
