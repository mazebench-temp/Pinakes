## In the Odyssey

At the suitors' feast Telemachus seats his father, still disguised as a beggar, inside the hall by the stone threshold, “setting down an unseemly stool and a small table”, and gives him his share of the meat and wine (*Odyssey* 20.257–260; see [[telemachus]], [[stool]] and [[table]]). The poor seat belongs to the beggar's disguise, in the house that is his own.
