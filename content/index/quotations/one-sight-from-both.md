## The line

Empedocles explained sight by a fire inside the eye, which shines out through its membranes as the light of a lantern through its panes (fr. 84 DK; see [[empedocles]]). The half-line speaks of the two eyes, whose two sights become one. In Homer ὄψ is a different word, “voice”; Empedocles uses it, shortened from ὄψις, for “sight”.
