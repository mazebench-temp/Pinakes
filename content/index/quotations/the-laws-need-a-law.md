The topic: what is thought to happen although it is incredible must be true, for no one would believe it unless it were so. Androcles turned the assembly's protest into an argument: things that seem absurd may be true (*Rhetoric* 2.23, 1400a5–14). στέμφυλα are the cakes of pressed olives left when the oil has been made.
