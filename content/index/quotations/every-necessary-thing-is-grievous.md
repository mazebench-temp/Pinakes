## The line

In the *Metaphysics* Aristotle quotes the line under its author's name, among the senses of “necessary”: what is necessary is compulsion and force, “that is why Evenus says ‘every necessary thing is grievous’” (Δ 5, 1015a28–29; see [[evenus-of-paros]] and [[anankaion]]).
