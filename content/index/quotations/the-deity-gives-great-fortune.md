The topic: to say that the purpose for which a thing might be done is the purpose for which it was done, for instance that someone gave a gift in order to hurt by taking it away (*Rhetoric* 2.23, 1399b20–25). The thought that the gods raise men high to make their fall the greater is old; Herodotus' Artabanus says that the god strikes down what stands out (7.10ε). See [[tyche]] and [[topics-of-enthymemes]].
