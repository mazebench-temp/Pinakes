## The verses

The first, “I saw Epichares walking to Marathon”, is a sentence of plain prose that scans as verse only if short syllables are counted long, as the epic poets sometimes counted them (see [[epektetamenon]] and [[hexameter]]); Epichares is otherwise unknown. The second is corrupt: the manuscripts have an unintelligible word before “his hellebore”, the drug that was used as a purge and against madness, and editors have proposed “not having mixed” and “not desiring”.
