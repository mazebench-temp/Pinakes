The lines are from a lost play of Agathon. In the *Nicomachean Ethics* Aristotle quotes another verse of his on the same pair: “art loves chance, and chance art” (*Nicomachean Ethics* 6.4, 1140a19–20). Here the lines support a topic of the possible: if a thing can come about without art and preparation, it can all the more come about through art and care (*Rhetoric* 2.19, 1392b5–9). See [[agathon]] and [[koina]].
