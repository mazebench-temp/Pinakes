## In the Iliad

Before the chariot race at the funeral games for Patroclus, Nestor tells his son Antilochus how to drive round the turning post: “a dry stump stands about a fathom above the ground, of oak or of pine, which does not rot in the rain, and two white stones are propped against it on either side” (*Iliad* 23.326–329; see [[nestor]] and [[antilochus]]). Our manuscripts read οὐ, “not”.
