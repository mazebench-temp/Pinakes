The form ἐμίν, “to me”, is Doric. The thought is that of the drinking song that ranks health first, beauty second and honest wealth third, which Plato's Socrates quotes (*Gorgias* 451e) and the ancient commentators on the *Gorgias* assign to Simonides or to Epicharmus. Aristotle's line is not the drinking song, and its author is not known. See [[gnome]].
