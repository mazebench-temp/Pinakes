## The poem

The lines survive only in the *Rhetoric*. Aristotle gives the first words to Alcaeus and the answer to Sappho, and editors print the whole exchange among Sappho's fragments; some have thought that both parts are hers, the first a speaker she imagines. The meter is the Alcaic stanza. The answer turns on shame, αἰδώς (see [[aidos]]): it is the shame of the eyes that betrays a wish one should not speak, and a wish for what is good or noble would be spoken openly.
