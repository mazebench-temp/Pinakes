{{quote:f00f27e3-dc85-509c-8b4d-a2642b5f03fa}}

In Plato's *Apology* Socrates questions Meletus, who has accused him of not believing in the gods and of introducing new divine things: one who believes in divine things must believe in divinities, and divinities are gods or children of gods; so Meletus accuses him of believing in gods and not believing in them (27b–e). Aristotle reduces the argument to a definition: what the δαιμόνιον is (*Rhetoric* 2.23, 1398a15–17). See [[socrates]] and [[topics-of-enthymemes]].
