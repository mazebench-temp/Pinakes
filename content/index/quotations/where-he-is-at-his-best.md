## In Euripides

The *Antiope* staged a debate between the twin sons of Antiope, Zethus, who praised the active life of the farmer and the soldier, and Amphion, who defended music and the life of thought. Plato's Callicles quotes the same lines in the *Gorgias*, and borrows Zethus' reproaches for his attack on philosophy (484e–486c; see [[euripides]]).
