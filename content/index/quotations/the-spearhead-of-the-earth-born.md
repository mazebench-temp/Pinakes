## The line and the legend

The words are the end of an iambic trimeter, the meter of tragic dialogue; the play and its author are unknown. The Earth-born (Γηγενεῖς) are the Sown Men of Thebes, who sprang up armed from the teeth of the serpent of Ares when Cadmus sowed them in the ground (see [[the-earth-born]] and [[cadmus-and-the-sown-men]]). The few who survived their battle with one another became the ancestors of the old Theban nobility, and their descendants were said to carry the mark of that birth on their bodies. In one version of the Antigone story Creon recognizes the grown son of Haemon and Antigone by the mark that all of the serpent's race bore on their bodies (Hyginus, *Fabulae* 72). The line may come from a tragedy on that plot, but nothing shows it.
