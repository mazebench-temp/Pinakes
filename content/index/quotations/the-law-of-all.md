## In Empedocles

Empedocles taught that souls pass through the bodies of animals and plants, and so forbade the killing of any living thing and the eating of meat; in his *Purifications* the man who sacrifices his son in the form of a beast is blind to the kinship of all life (fr. 136–137 DK). The lines Aristotle quotes call the ban a law for all, not just for some and unjust for others (see [[empedocles]]).
