## In Homer

On the shield that Hephaestus makes for Achilles are the earth, the sky and the sea, the sun, the moon and the constellations, the Pleiades, the Hyades, Orion, “and the Bear, which they also call the Wagon, which turns in its place and watches Orion, and she alone has no share in the baths of Ocean” (*Iliad* 18.483–489). The same lines name the stars by which Odysseus steers from Calypso's island (*Odyssey* 5.272–275). In Greek latitudes the Great Bear never sets: it never bathes in the river Ocean that circles the earth (see [[oceanus]]).
