In Sophocles' *Tyro* the heroine was ill-treated by her stepmother Sidero until her sons rescued her (see [[tyro-play]] and [[tyro]]). The name Σιδηρώ is made from σίδηρος, “iron”, and the line says that she lives up to it. Ross prints σιδήρῳ, “to iron”; some editors print the name itself, Σιδηρώ. See [[topics-of-enthymemes]].
