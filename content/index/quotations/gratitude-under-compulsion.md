The lines are iambic, and are probably from a tragedy; their author is unknown. The argument fits Aristotle's own account of favor, which is a service done not in return for anything and not under compulsion (*Rhetoric* 2.7, 1385a17–19, 1385b1–3; see [[charis]]). See [[topics-of-enthymemes]].
