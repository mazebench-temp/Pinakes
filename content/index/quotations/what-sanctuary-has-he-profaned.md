The charge against Socrates was that he did not recognize the gods the city recognized and introduced new divine things (Plato, *Apology* 24b; Xenophon, *Memorabilia* 1.1.1). Theodectes' defense divides impiety into its parts and denies each. Whether his *Socrates* was a speech or a play is not known. See [[socrates]], [[theodectes]] and [[topics-of-enthymemes]].
