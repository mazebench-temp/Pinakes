Several poets wrote epics on Heracles: Pisander of Camirus and Panyassis of Halicarnassus, Herodotus' kinsman, are the best known. Aristotle's criticism is general: “all those poets seem to have gone wrong who have composed a Heracleid, a Theseid and such poems; for they think that since Heracles was one, the plot too must be one” (1451a19–22; see [[unity-of-action]] and [[heracles]]).
