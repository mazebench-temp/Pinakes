All three tragedians wrote a *Philoctetes*, and Dio Chrysostom compared the three (*Orations* 52). In Sophocles' version Odysseus and the young Neoptolemus come to Lemnos to take Philoctetes' bow by deceit (see [[philoctetes]] and [[philoctetes-on-lemnos]]).
