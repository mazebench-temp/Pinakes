aliases: [Aristotle's Politics]
---

The *Politics* begins with the household and the city as natural, reviews the best constitutions proposed and practiced, defines the citizen, and classifies constitutions into correct and deviant forms (kingship, aristocracy and polity; tyranny, oligarchy and democracy); its later books treat how constitutions are preserved and destroyed, and the education of the best city.
