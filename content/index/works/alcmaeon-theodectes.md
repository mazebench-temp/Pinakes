Alcmaeon's matricide was a common subject of tragedy; Aristotle names him among the few families on which the best tragedies are built (*Poetics* 13, 1453a20) and counts Astydamas' version among plays where the deed is done in ignorance (*Poetics* 14, 1453b33). In Theodectes' play Alphesiboea, Alcmaeon's wife, questions him about the murder (see [[alphesiboea]]). See [[theodectes]] and [[alcmaeon]].
