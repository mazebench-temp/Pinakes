## The poem

The Odyssey tells how Odysseus, ten years after the fall of Troy, reaches home from Calypso's island by way of the Phaeacians, to whom he tells his wanderings, and how with his son Telemachus and two loyal servants he kills the suitors who have been wasting his house and wooing his wife Penelope (see [[the-return-of-odysseus]]).
