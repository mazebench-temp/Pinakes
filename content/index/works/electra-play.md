title: Electra (Sophocles)
greek: Ἠλέκτρα
kind: work
tags: [play, tragedy, atreidae]
summary: Sophocles' tragedy of Electra, who waits for her brother Orestes to avenge their father; Orestes' old tutor reports his death in a chariot race at the Pythian Games, a report Aristotle calls irrational, perhaps for its anachronism.
aliases: [Electra, the Electra, Elektra]
---

Orestes returns to Mycenae with his old tutor and Pylades, and sends the tutor to announce that he has been killed in the chariot race at Delphi (680–763); Electra mourns over the urn that supposedly holds his ashes, until her brother reveals himself and kills Clytemnestra and Aegisthus. Euripides also wrote an *Electra* (see [[electra]] and [[the-false-report-of-orestes-death]]).
