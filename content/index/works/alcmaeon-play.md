In the traditional story Alcmaeon killed his mother knowingly, to avenge his father Amphiaraus (see [[alcmaeon-kills-eriphyle]]). Astydamas changed it, as Aristotle recommends, so that the deed was done in ignorance and recognized afterwards (see [[astydamas]] and [[alcmaeon]]).
