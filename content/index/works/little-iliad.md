## The poem

The *Little Iliad*, ascribed to Lesches of Pyrrha or Mitylene, told of the Judgment of Arms and Ajax's madness, the fetching of Philoctetes and Neoptolemus, the death of Eurypylus, Odysseus' spying in Troy as a beggar, the theft of the Palladium, and the Wooden Horse (see [[the-judgment-of-arms]], [[philoctetes-on-lemnos]], [[the-beggary]] and [[the-theft-of-the-palladium]]). Only fragments survive.
