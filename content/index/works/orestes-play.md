summary: Euripides' tragedy (408 BCE): after killing his mother, Orestes, sick with madness, is condemned to death by the Argives, and his uncle Menelaus refuses to help him. Aristotle's example of baseness of character beyond what the plot needs.
aliases: [Orestes, the Orestes]
---

Menelaus, newly returned with Helen, hesitates and then abandons his nephew to the Argive assembly, out of fear and self-interest. Orestes, Electra and Pylades seize Helen and Hermione, and Apollo descends at the end to settle everything (see [[orestes]] and [[menelaus]]).
