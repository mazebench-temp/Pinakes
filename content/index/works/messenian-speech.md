title: Messenian Speech

tags: [lost-work, rhetoric]
summary: A lost speech of Alcidamas, written for the freedom of Messene from Sparta in the 360s BCE, of which only one sentence survives: “God has left all men free; nature has made no one a slave.”
aliases: [Messeniacus]
---

After the Theban victory at Leuctra (371 BCE) Epaminondas refounded Messene, and the Messenians, Sparta's helots for generations, were free; the Spartans refused to recognize the new city. Alcidamas' speech took the Messenians' side. Its one surviving sentence comes from an ancient commentator on the *Rhetoric* (see [[god-has-left-all-men-free]] and [[alcidamas]]).
