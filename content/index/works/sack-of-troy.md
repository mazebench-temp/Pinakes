## The poem

The *Iliupersis* of Arctinus of Miletus told of the Wooden Horse, Laocoon, Sinon, the slaughter of Priam at the altar, the death of Astyanax, the rape of Cassandra by Ajax son of Oileus, and the sacrifice of Polyxena (Proclus, *Chrestomathy*). Polygnotus painted the same scene at Delphi (see [[polygnotus]]). The prophecy of the sack in the Iliad is indexed as [[sack-of-troy-foretold]].
