aliases: [Medea, the Medea]

Jason, for whom Medea betrayed her father and killed her brother, takes the Corinthian king's daughter as his wife. Medea sends the bride a poisoned robe, kills her two sons to punish Jason, and is carried off to Athens in a winged chariot sent by her grandfather the Sun, where Aegeus has promised her refuge (see [[medea]], [[medea-kills-her-children]] and [[aegeus]]).
