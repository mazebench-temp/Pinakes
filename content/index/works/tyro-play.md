Tyro, daughter of Salmoneus, bore twins to Poseidon and exposed them in a small boat or trough; they were found and raised by herdsmen, and later rescued their mother from her cruel stepmother Sidero (see [[tyro]] and [[tyro-and-her-sons]]).
