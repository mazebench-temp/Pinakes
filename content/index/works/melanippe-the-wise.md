Melanippe bore twins to Poseidon and hid them; when they were found suckled by a cow and her father proposed to burn them as a portent, she argued, as she had learned from her mother, that there are no portents, and set out a cosmology (fr. 484 Kannicht). Euripides also wrote a *Melanippe Captive* (see [[melanippe]]).
