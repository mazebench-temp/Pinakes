Philoxenus' *Cyclops* (or *Galatea*) made Polyphemus a lovesick shepherd courting the sea nymph Galatea, a theme Theocritus took up (Idyll 11); Timotheus' *Cyclops* is known from fragments (see [[philoxenus]], [[timotheus]] and [[cyclopes]]).
