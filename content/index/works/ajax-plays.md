title: Ajax (plays)
greek: Αἴας
kind: work
tags: [play, tragedy]
summary: Tragedies of Ajax son of Telamon, who went mad after losing the arms of Achilles and killed himself; Sophocles' Ajax survives. Aristotle names “the Ajaxes” as tragedies of suffering.
aliases: [Ajax, Aias, the Ajaxes, Ajax plays]
---

Sophocles' *Ajax* shows the hero, maddened by Athena, slaughtering cattle he takes for the Greek leaders, and his suicide by his own sword; Aeschylus' trilogy included the *Judgment of Arms*, and later poets, Astydamas and Theodectes among them, wrote plays on Ajax (see [[ajax]], [[the-judgment-of-arms]] and [[sophocles-tecmessa]]).
