After the brothers Eteocles and Polynices have killed each other, Creon, the new king of Thebes, forbids the burial of Polynices, who attacked the city (see [[the-seven-against-thebes]]). Antigone buries him and is condemned to be shut in a tomb. Creon relents too late: Antigone has hanged herself, and his son Haemon, finding her, spits in his father's face, draws his sword on him, misses as Creon flees, and kills himself; Creon's wife Eurydice kills herself too (see [[antigone]], [[creon-of-thebes]] and [[haemon-son-of-creon]]).
