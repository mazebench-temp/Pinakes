summary: Aristotle's treatise on dialectic, the art of arguing from reputable opinions on any question put, with its catalogue of topics; the Rhetoric refers to it for the theory of syllogism and induction in argument.
aliases: [Topica, Aristotle's Topics]
---

The *Topics*, in eight books, teaches how to argue for and against any thesis from ἔνδοξα, the opinions accepted by all, by most, or by the wise; it sets out the topics, the “places” from which arguments are drawn, and ends with the practice of question and answer. The *Sophistical Refutations* is its appendix, on fallacies. The *Rhetoric* opens by calling rhetoric the counterpart of dialectic, and its account of enthymeme and example, and of the topics, presupposes this work (see [[dialektike]] and [[topos]]).
