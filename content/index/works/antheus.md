The title may be *Antheus*, a man's name, or *Anthos*, “The Flower”; nothing of the play survives. It is the earliest known Greek tragedy with an invented plot: “in some none at all, as in Agathon's Antheus; for in this play the incidents and the names alike are invented, and it gives no less pleasure” (1451b21–23; see [[agathon]] and [[traditional-stories]]).
