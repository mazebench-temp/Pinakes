In *Prometheus Bound* Hephaestus chains Prometheus to a cliff in Scythia; the Ocean nymphs, Oceanus, and Io, driven over the earth by Hera, visit him, and he defies Zeus with a secret about his fall. Its authorship has been doubted on grounds of style and meter (see [[prometheus]] and [[the-punishment-of-prometheus]]).
