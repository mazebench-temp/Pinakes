The passage describing it is the most damaged in the *Poetics* (see [[anagnorisis]] and [[paralogismos]]). Odysseus, disguised as a messenger with false news of himself, seems to have been recognized when he alone could string the bow, as in the Odyssey (see [[bow]]).
