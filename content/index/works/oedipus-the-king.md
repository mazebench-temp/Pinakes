## The play

Thebes is stricken by plague; Apollo's oracle demands that the killer of King Laius be driven out. Oedipus, the city's savior from the Sphinx, curses the unknown killer and pursues the truth, against the warnings of the seer Teiresias and of his wife Jocasta, until a messenger from Corinth and an old shepherd reveal that he was the son of Laius and Jocasta, exposed as a baby, and that he killed his father on the road. Jocasta hangs herself and Oedipus blinds himself. The date is unknown; the plague has suggested the years after 430 BCE (see [[oedipus]] and [[the-discovery-of-oedipus]]).
