In the *Little Iliad* Odysseus, disfigured and dressed in rags, entered Troy as a spy; Helen recognized him and plotted with him, and he killed some Trojans and returned (Proclus; *Odyssey* 4.240–264; see [[odysseus-enters-troy-as-a-beggar]]).
