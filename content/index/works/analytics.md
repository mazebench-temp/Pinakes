tags: [philosophy]
summary: Aristotle's treatises on the syllogism (Prior Analytics) and on demonstration and scientific knowledge (Posterior Analytics), to which the Rhetoric refers for proof and for signs.
aliases: [Prior Analytics, Posterior Analytics]
---

The *Prior Analytics* sets out the forms of the syllogism; the *Posterior Analytics* treats demonstration and the first principles of the sciences. The last chapter of the *Prior Analytics* treats probabilities, signs and the enthymeme, and distinguishes the conclusive sign, the τεκμήριον, from the others (2.27, 70a3–b38; see [[tekmerion]]).
