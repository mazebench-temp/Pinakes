tags: [epic, comedy, poem, lost-work]
summary: The “Coward-iad”, a mock epic by Nicochares, named by Aristotle among works that imitate people worse than we are.

The title plays on Ἰλιάς: δειλός is “coward”. Nothing survives (1448a12–14; see [[nicochares]] and [[parody]]).
