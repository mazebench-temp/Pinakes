In Aristophanes' *Frogs* Euripides mocks Aeschylus for keeping Niobe seated and silent, her face covered, for a long part of the play (911–920). A papyrus preserves a speech from the play (fr. 154a Radt). See [[niobe]] and [[the-children-of-niobe]].
