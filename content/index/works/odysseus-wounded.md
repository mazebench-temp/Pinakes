Sophocles' *Odysseus Akanthoplēx* told how Telegonus, seeking his father, landed on Ithaca, fought with Odysseus without knowing him, and killed him with a spear tipped with the spine of a stingray, fulfilling the prophecy of a death from the sea; Chaeremon also wrote a *Traumatias* (see [[telegonus]] and [[telegonus-kills-odysseus]]).
