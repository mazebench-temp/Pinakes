---
title: Mysians (tragedy)
greek: Μυσοί
kind: work
tags: [play, tragedy, lost-work]
summary: A lost tragedy, by Aeschylus or Sophocles, both of whom wrote one, in which Telephus comes from Tegea to Mysia without speaking; Aristotle calls the silent journey irrational.
aliases: [Mysoi, Mysians, the Mysians]
---

Telephus, having killed his mother's brothers in Tegea, was polluted and might not speak until purified, and so traveled to Mysia in silence (see [[the-silence-of-telephus]] and [[telephus]]).
