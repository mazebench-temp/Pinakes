Fragments and the later mythographers tell the plot: Tereus, a Thracian king married to Procne, daughter of Pandion of Athens, raped her sister Philomela and cut out her tongue; Philomela wove the story into a robe; the sisters killed Tereus' son Itys and served him to his father, and all three were turned into birds (see [[procne-and-philomela]] and [[tereus]]). Aristophanes' *Birds* (414 BCE) presupposes the play.
