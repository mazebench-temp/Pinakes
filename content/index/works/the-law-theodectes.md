summary: A speech of Theodectes called The Law, from which Aristotle takes two arguments: no one hands his horses or ships to those who have ruined other people's, and a city that makes good mercenaries citizens should exile bad ones.
aliases: [Theodectes' Law, The Law]

Theodectes of Phaselis wrote speeches as well as tragedies. Nothing else of *The Law* is known. See [[theodectes]].
