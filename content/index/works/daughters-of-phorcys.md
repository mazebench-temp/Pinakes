kind: work
tags: [play, tragedy, lost-work]
summary: Aeschylus' lost play about Perseus and the Graeae, the daughters of Phorcys who shared one eye and one tooth; an example of the fourth kind of tragedy.
aliases: [Phorkides, Phorcides, Daughters of Phorcys]
---

Perseus stole the single eye of the Graeae to force them to show him the way to the Gorgons (see [[perseus-and-the-graeae]] and [[phorcys-the-sea-god]]). Aeschylus' *Phorkides* is lost.
