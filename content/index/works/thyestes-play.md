The story of Thyestes, who unknowingly ate his own children at his brother Atreus' table, was a favorite of the tragedians (see [[thyestes]] and [[carcinus]]); how the birthmarks figured in Carcinus' version is unknown, perhaps in identifying the remains of the children.
