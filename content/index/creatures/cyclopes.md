In Odyssey 9 the Cyclopes live without assemblies or laws, each ruling his own family; Polyphemus, son of Poseidon, traps Odysseus and his men in his cave, eats six of them, and is blinded with a sharpened stake after being made drunk; Odysseus escapes under a ram. Hesiod's Cyclopes, Brontes, Steropes and Arges, are sons of Earth and Sky (*Theogony* 139–146). Polyphemus the Cyclops is not the Lapith Polyphemus of the Iliad (see [[polyphemus]], [[cyclops-dithyrambs]] and [[small-and-of-no-account]]).
