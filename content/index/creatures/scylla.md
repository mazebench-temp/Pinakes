---
title: Scylla
greek: Σκύλλα
kind: creature
tags: [sea]
summary: The monster in a cave above the strait opposite Charybdis, with six heads on long necks, who snatched six of Odysseus' men from his ship.
aliases: [Skylla]
refs: [Poetics 1454a31, Poetics 1461b32]
---

Circe describes her as yelping like a puppy, with twelve feet and six necks, each with a head and three rows of teeth (*Odyssey* 12.85–100); Odysseus calls the sight of his men lifted screaming the most pitiable thing he saw in all his wanderings (*Odyssey* 12.245–259). Timotheus' dithyramb *Scylla* gave Odysseus a lament for them (see [[scylla-of-timotheus]]).
