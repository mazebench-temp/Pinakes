## In the poem

This is the first appearance of a god to a mortal in the poem. It stages a debate within Achilles as an encounter. Homer shows him deliberating "two ways" (διάνδιχα, 1.189), and the goddess arrives at the moment of decision. Her advice does not forbid anger, only killing: "reproach him with words". The Iliad shows the divine and the human as acting together. Athena intervenes and Achilles chooses; both are true.
