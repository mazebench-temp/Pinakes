Half a line gives a whole childhood: the goddess in the mortal household, telling stories of Olympus, and the boy listening. By the time of the Iliad she lives in the sea with her father (1.358).

## Outside Homer

Thetis says the gods gave her against her will to Peleus (Iliad 18.84–87, 18.432–434), and the gods came to the wedding (24.59–63). A later story tells why she left: she tried to make the infant Achilles immortal by fire, Peleus cried out when he saw it, and she went back to the sea (Apollodorus, *Library* 3.13.6).
