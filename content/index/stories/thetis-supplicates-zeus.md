## In the poem

The supplication follows the full ritual. Thetis kneels, takes his knees with her left hand and his chin with her right, and names a past service. When Zeus is silent she "holds on, clinging close" (ἐμπεφυυῖα, 1.513), the suppliant who will not let go. Her second plea is barer: say yes or no, "so that I may know well how far I am the most dishonored goddess of all" (1.516).

Zeus' nod is the poem's point of no return. From it follow the Trojan victories, the embassy, the death of Patroclus, and Achilles' return. See [[supplication]], [[neuo]] and [[plan-of-zeus]].
