## The story

In Hesiod, Prometheus cheats Zeus at Mecone in the division of the sacrifice, giving the gods the bones wrapped in shining fat. Zeus withholds fire, and Prometheus steals it back in a hollow fennel stalk; Zeus answers with Pandora, the first woman, and binds Prometheus with unbreakable chains to a pillar, sending an eagle to eat his immortal liver, which grows back each night, until Heracles kills the bird with Zeus' consent (Hesiod, *Theogony* 521–616; see [[prometheus]]). In Aeschylus' *Prometheus Bound*, Might and Violence and a reluctant Hephaestus nail him to a crag in Scythia; he defies Zeus, refusing to reveal the secret of the marriage that would overthrow him, and at the end is hurled into the depths amid thunder and earthquake (see [[prometheus-play]]). The lost *Prometheus Unbound* told of his release by Heracles.
