## In the poem

This is the fullest ritual scene in the Iliad, and it answers the opening scene point by point:

| Beginning | End |
|---|---|
| A father's ransom refused | The daughter given back "unbought, unransomed" |
| A prayer for vengeance | The same prayer turned to mercy |
| Arrows of plague | A song of healing |

It is also a model of how things should work between gods and men. The action moves slowly and in full detail through arrival, handing over, prayer, slaughter, feast and song, and a following wind home. See [[sacrifice-type-scene]], [[sailing-type-scene]] and [[reciprocity-with-the-gods]].
