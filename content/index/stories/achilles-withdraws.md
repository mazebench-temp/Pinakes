The verbs are iterative, πωλέσκετο and φθινύθεσκε ("he would go", "he kept wasting"): these are days of repeated absence. The wrath hurts him as well as the Achaeans. He "wasted away his own heart" and "longed for the war cry and for battle". He is kept from what defines him. See [[menis]].
