## In the poem

The scene is remarkable for its restraint. Agamemnon threatened to come himself (1.185) and sends heralds instead. Achilles threatened blood (1.303) and gives her up with courtesy, blaming only Agamemnon. The violence is all kept for his speech, which calls the heralds to witness before gods, men and "that harsh king" (1.338–344). Then he weeps. See [[captive-women]] and [[heralds]].
