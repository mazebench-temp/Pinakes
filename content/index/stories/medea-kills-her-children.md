## The story

After the voyage of the Argo, Jason and Medea lived in Corinth with their two sons. Jason agreed to marry the daughter of Creon, king of Corinth, and Creon ordered Medea into exile. She won a day's delay, secured a refuge at Athens from King Aegeus, who happened to pass through Corinth on his way from Delphi, and sent the bride a robe and a golden crown that burned her and her father to death. Then she killed her sons, so that her enemies should not, and so that Jason would be left with nothing, and fled with their bodies in a chariot sent by her grandfather the Sun (Euripides, *Medea*, 431 BCE; see [[medea]], [[medea-play]] and [[aegeus]]). In older versions the children died at the hands of the Corinthians, in revenge for the death of the princess (Pausanias 2.3.6).
