## The story

Plutarch tells it among the cases in which justice seems to be done by providence: Mitys of Argos was killed in a civil conflict, and later, at a festival, his bronze statue fell on the man who had killed him and killed him (*On the Delays of Divine Vengeance* 553d; see [[mitys]] and [[argos]]). Nothing else is known of Mitys, or of when this happened.
