Athena offers Achilles compensation instead of revenge. He takes it. In Book 9 Agamemnon sends the embassy with a vast list of gifts, and Briseis too, and Achilles refuses them. In Book 19, after Patroclus' death, the gifts are laid before the assembly (19.243–281), and Achilles hardly looks at them. The promise is kept, but it arrives too late to mean what it would have meant in Book 1. See [[doron]] and [[honor-and-dishonor]].
