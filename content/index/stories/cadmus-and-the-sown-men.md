## The story

When Cadmus, following the cow, reached the site of Thebes, he sent his companions to draw water from the spring of Ares, and the serpent that guarded it killed most of them. Cadmus killed the serpent, and on Athena's advice sowed its teeth. Armed men sprang up from the ground, whom the Thebans called the Sown Men (Σπαρτοί); they fought among themselves until only five were left, Echion, Udaeus, Chthonius, Hyperenor and Pelorus. For killing the serpent Cadmus served Ares for an “eternal year”, which was then eight years (Apollodorus 3.4.1–2; Euripides, *Phoenician Women* 638–675; see [[cadmus]]). Echion married Cadmus' daughter Agave and was the father of Pentheus, and the noble families of Thebes traced their descent from the five. Athena gave half the teeth to Aeetes of Colchis, and Jason had to sow them in his turn (Apollonius, *Argonautica* 3).

The Sown Men are the Earth-born (Γηγενεῖς) of tragedy, whose descendants carried the mark of their birth, a birthmark in the shape of a spearhead (see [[the-earth-born]] and [[the-spearhead-of-the-earth-born]]).
