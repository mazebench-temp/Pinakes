## The story

The Cypria told how the Greeks, gathering for the war, caught Odysseus feigning madness because he did not want to join the expedition: on the advice of Palamedes they seized his son Telemachus to punish him (Proclus, summary of the *Cypria*). Later writers add the details. Odysseus yoked a horse and an ox together and sowed salt, until Palamedes laid the baby in front of the plow (Hyginus, *Fabulae* 95), or drew his sword over the child (Apollodorus, *Epitome* 3.7). Odysseus never forgave him, and at Troy he brought about Palamedes' death (see [[palamedes]] and [[odysseus]]).
