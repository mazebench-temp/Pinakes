## The story

The Odyssey tells it as the old nurse's hands find the scar. Autolycus, the father of Odysseus' mother, “who excelled all men in thievery and in oaths”, had named the child when he was born and promised him gifts if he came to Parnassus. When Odysseus was grown he went, and hunting on the wooded slopes with the sons of Autolycus he was the first to charge a great boar, which rushed at him and tore his thigh above the knee with its tusk before he killed it with his spear. The sons of Autolycus bound the wound skillfully and stopped the dark blood with a charm (*Odyssey* 19.392–466; see [[autolycus]], [[parnassus]] and [[the-scar-of-odysseus]]).
