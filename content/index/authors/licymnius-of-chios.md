summary: Licymnius of Chios, dithyrambic poet and teacher of rhetoric of the late fifth century BCE, who wrote a handbook with many new names for the parts of a speech.
---

Plato's *Phaedrus* mocks “the Licymnian treasury of words” that he gave to his pupil Polus for fine speaking (267c). Aristotle names him three times in the *Rhetoric*. He is not the Licymnius of the Iliad, Heracles' uncle (see [[licymnius]]).
