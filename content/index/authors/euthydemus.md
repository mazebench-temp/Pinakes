title: Euthydemus
greek: Εὐθύδημος

tags: [sophist]
summary: A sophist from Chios who, with his brother Dionysodorus, taught fighting in armor and then eristic argument; Plato's Euthydemus shows them refuting all comers with verbal tricks.
grc: ["^Εὐθυδήμ*"]
---

In Plato's dialogue the brothers, now old, claim to teach virtue, and prove whatever they please by playing on the senses of words (*Euthydemus* 271c–272b). Aristotle cites “Euthydemus' argument” among the fallacies of combination: does one who is in Sicily know that there are triremes in the Piraeus (*Sophistical Refutations* 20, 177b12–13)?
