Magnes was the most victorious of the early comic poets, with eleven victories at the Dionysia. Aristophanes names his choruses, the *Lyre-players*, *Birds*, *Lydians*, *Gall-flies* and *Frogs*, and says the Athenians threw him out in his old age because he had lost his gift for mockery (*Knights* 520–525). Aristotle names him with Chionides (1448a34; see [[chionides]] and [[epicharmus]]).
