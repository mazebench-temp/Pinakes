Anaxagoras came from Clazomenae in Ionia and spent many years in Athens. His book taught that all things were together until Mind (νοῦς) set them moving and in order, and that the sun is a fiery stone. Charged with impiety, he left Athens for Lampsacus on the Hellespont. Diogenes Laertius reports that the Lampsacenes buried him with honor, and that at his request their children had a holiday every year in the month of his death (2.14–15). See [[pericles]].
