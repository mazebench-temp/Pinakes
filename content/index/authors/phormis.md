The Suda says Phormis (or Phormos) was a Syracusan, a friend of the tyrant Gelon and tutor of his children, and wrote comedies on myths. In the *Poetics* the words “Epicharmus and Phormis” stand beside the claim that “the making of plots came originally from Sicily” (1449b5–7), but most editors, Kassel among them, take them for a reader's note (see [[epicharmus]] and [[the-origins-of-comedy]]).
