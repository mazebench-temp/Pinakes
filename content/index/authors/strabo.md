title: Strabo
greek: Στράβων

tags: [scholarship]
summary: Greek geographer (c. 64 BCE–c. 24 CE) who treated Homer as the first geographer. His Geography follows the Catalogue of Ships region by region, and preserves anecdotes such as Phidias and the nod of Zeus.
---

Strabo's *Geography* opens by claiming Homer as the founder of geography, and it discusses Homeric places at length, above all the Troad (Book 13). For Iliad 1 he supplies [[phidias-zeus]] (8.3.30) and [[smintheus-and-the-mice]] (13.1.48), and he locates [[chryse]] and [[cilla]]. He argues for Nestor's Pylos in Triphylia ([[strabo-on-nestors-pylos]]). On the far peoples at the start of Iliad 13 he weighs Posidonius, Apollodorus and Eratosthenes ([[strabo-on-the-mare-milkers]], 7.3.2–10), and he finds that Homer's Cassandra is still a maiden, untouched by the Locrian Ajax ([[strabo-on-cassandra]], 13.1.40).
