title: Melanippides

tags: [lyric-poet, poet]
summary: Melanippides of Melos, dithyrambic poet of the later fifth century BCE, one of the innovators of the new music, who replaced the antistrophic structure of the dithyramb with free preludes.
---

In Xenophon's *Memorabilia* Aristodemus names him as the poet he most admires in the dithyramb (1.4.3).
