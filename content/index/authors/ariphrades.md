Aristophanes attacks an Ariphrades several times for obscene habits (*Knights* 1280–1289, *Wasps* 1280–1283, *Peace* 883–885), and calls him clever; whether he is the critic of the *Poetics* is unknown. Aristotle says that Ariphrades “made fun of the tragedians because they use expressions no one would use in conversation”, like δωμάτων ἄπο for ἀπὸ δωμάτων and Ἀχιλλέως πέρι for περὶ Ἀχιλλέως, not seeing that such unusual turns are what make diction uncommon (1458b31–1459a4; see [[ariphrades-and-the-tragedians]] and [[excellence-of-diction]]).
