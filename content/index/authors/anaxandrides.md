summary: Anaxandrides, a poet of Middle Comedy who won at Athens from the 370s to the 340s BCE, quoted by Aristotle for three of his lines and named for two of his plays.
---

Aristotle also quotes him in the *Nicomachean Ethics* for the city that passed decrees but cared nothing for its laws (7.10, 1152a22–23). Only fragments of his plays survive.
