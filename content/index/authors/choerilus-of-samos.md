tags: [epic-poet, poet]
summary: Choerilus of Samos, epic poet of the late fifth century BCE, who wrote of the Persian Wars and complained that the poets before him had taken every subject.
---

In the opening of his poem he calls happy the servant of the Muses who sang “when the meadow was still untouched”, for now all has been parceled out and there is no new chariot to drive.
