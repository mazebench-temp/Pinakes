summary: Tragic and dithyrambic poet of the late fifth or early fourth century BCE, whose Cyprians has a recognition through memory: a man weeps at a painting.

Dicaeogenes is known from a few fragments and from this passage: the third kind of recognition is through memory, “as in the Cyprians of Dicaeogenes, where he saw the painting and wept” (1454b37–1455a2; see [[cyprians-play]] and [[anagnorisis]]).
