Archilochus, the son of Telesicles, took part in the Parian settlement of Thasos, and his poems speak of war, of his own lost shield, and of the abuse he heaped on Lycambes, who had broken off his betrothal to his daughter Neobule. The ancients set him beside Homer as a master of his kind. In the third century BCE the Parians kept a sanctuary of him, and an inscription found on the island tells his life.
