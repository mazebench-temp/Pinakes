Hegemon, nicknamed “Lentil”, recited mock-epic parodies at Athens and also wrote a comedy. Athenaeus says that his *Battle of the Giants* was being performed when the news came of the disaster in Sicily in 413 BCE, and the Athenians stayed laughing in the theater (9.406e–407c). Aristotle names him with Nicochares among the poets who imitate people worse than we are: “Hegemon of Thasos, who first composed parodies” (1448a12–13; see [[parody]], [[nicochares]] and [[thasos]]).
