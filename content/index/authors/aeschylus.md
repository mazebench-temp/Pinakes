---

Aeschylus fought at Marathon. Seven of his plays survive, among them the *Oresteia* (458 BCE), whose first play, *Agamemnon*, begins as news of Troy's fall reaches Argos. Athenaeus reports that he called his tragedies "slices (τεμάχη) from the great banquets of Homer" (*Deipnosophistae* 8.347e). His lost *Myrmidons* staged Achilles' withdrawal from the fighting and the death of Patroclus.
