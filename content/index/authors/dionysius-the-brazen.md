summary: Dionysius Chalcus, “the Brazen”, an Athenian elegiac poet and orator of the fifth century BCE, said to have got his name by advising the Athenians to use bronze coinage.

---

Athenaeus explains the nickname by his proposal of a bronze coinage (15.669d). A few lines of his elegies survive.
