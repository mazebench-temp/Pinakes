summary: English playwright and poet (1564–1616). His Troilus and Cressida (c. 1602) sets a medieval love story in the Greek camp at Troy and gives a large part to Homer's Thersites.
---

Shakespeare knew the Trojan War mainly through medieval tradition: Chaucer's *Troilus and Criseyde*, and the Troy books of Lydgate and Caxton. He could also read Homer in George Chapman's *Seven Books of the Iliads* (1598), which contained Books 1, 2 and 7–11. In *Troilus and Cressida* Agamemnon, Nestor, Ulysses, Ajax and Achilles debate and quarrel in the camp, and Thersites rails at them all.
