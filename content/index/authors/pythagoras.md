tags: [philosopher]
summary: Pythagoras of Samos (sixth century BCE), who settled at Croton in southern Italy and founded a brotherhood with its own way of life; he left no writings.
grc: ["^Πυθαγόρ*"]
---

Herodotus calls him “not the weakest wise man of the Greeks” (Herodotus 4.95). Aristotle discusses the doctrines of “the so-called Pythagoreans”, who took numbers to be the principles of things (*Metaphysics* 1.5, 985b23–986a3). See [[italiots]].
