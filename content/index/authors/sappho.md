title: Sappho

summary: Lyric poet of Lesbos (active around 600 BCE), the most admired woman poet of antiquity. Her poem on "whatever one loves" takes Helen as its example.
---

Sappho sang in the Aeolic dialect of Lesbos, and the Alexandrian scholars collected her songs in several books. Only the *Hymn to Aphrodite* (fr. 1) survives complete in an ancient quotation; the rest are fragments, many of them recovered from papyri. Her language is full of epic words, and one fragment tells of the wedding of Hector and Andromache at Troy (fr. 44).
