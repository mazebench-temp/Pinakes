In the *Gorgias* Polus defends the orator's power and argues that the tyrant Archelaus is happy, until Socrates forces him to grant that doing wrong is worse than suffering it (461b–481b; see [[archelaus]]). The *Phaedrus* mentions his book of the arts of speech, with its “doubling of words” and “speaking in maxims” (267b–c). See [[gorgias]].
