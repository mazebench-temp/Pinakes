## Life and work

Epicharmus worked at Syracuse under the tyrants Gelon and Hieron. His comedies, in Doric, included mythological burlesques like *Hebe's Wedding* and the *Cyclops*, and comedies of character; many maxims were later attributed to him, and Plato's Socrates calls him the leader of comedy, as Homer is of tragedy (*Theaetetus* 152e).

## In the Poetics

The Sicilians claim comedy, “for the poet Epicharmus came from there, who lived long before Chionides and Magnes” (1448a31–34; see [[sicily]] and [[the-origins-of-comedy]]). “The making of plots [Epicharmus and Phormis] came originally from Sicily” (1449b5–7); Kassel brackets the two names as a gloss, but the claim is the same (see [[phormis]]).
