summary: Christopher Marlowe (1564–1593), English playwright and poet, author of Tamburlaine and Doctor Faustus. His Faustus greets the spirit of Helen with the most famous lines ever spoken to her in English.
---

Marlowe was a Cambridge scholar and an exact contemporary of Shakespeare; he was killed in a quarrel at Deptford at the age of twenty-nine. He translated Ovid's *Amores* and the first book of Lucan's *Pharsalia*, and wrote, with Thomas Nashe, *Dido, Queen of Carthage*, a play drawn from the *Aeneid*.
