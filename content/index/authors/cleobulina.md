Cleobulina appears in Plutarch's *Banquet of the Seven Sages* as a clever girl, also called Eumetis, who riddles with the guests (148c–e). Athenaeus quotes as hers the riddle “I saw a man who with fire had welded bronze upon a man” (10.452b), which Aristotle cites without naming its author (1458a29–30; see [[the-riddle-of-the-cupping-glass]] and [[ainigma]]).
