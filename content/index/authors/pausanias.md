The *Description of Greece* has ten books, beginning with Attica and ending with Phocis. Pausanias reports what he saw and what the local people told him, and he often sets beside it what Homer or Hesiod says; Pinakes cites him under "Outside Homer" for many places and cults. He is not the Spartan regent Pausanias who commanded at Plataea, nor the Pausanias who speaks in praise of Love in Plato's *Symposium* (180c–185c; see [[plato-two-aphrodites]]).
