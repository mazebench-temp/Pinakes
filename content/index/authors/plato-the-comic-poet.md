title: Plato, the comic poet
greek: Πλάτων
kind: author
tags: [comic-poet, poet, athenian]
summary: Athenian poet of Old Comedy (active c. 425–385 BCE), who attacked the politicians of his day in plays named after them; probably the Plato whose remark Eubulus quoted against Chares.
aliases: [Plato Comicus]
---

Some thirty titles of his comedies are known, among them *Hyperbolus* and *Cleophon*; the *Cleophon* took third place at the Lenaea of 405 BCE, when Aristophanes' *Frogs* won (*Frogs*, hypothesis). Only fragments survive. He is a different man from the philosopher (see [[plato]]).
