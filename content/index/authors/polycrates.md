Isocrates' *Busiris* is addressed to him and corrects his defense of the Egyptian king who killed strangers (11). Diogenes Laertius reports that the speech of accusation against Socrates was written by Polycrates, though Favorinus said that it could not have been delivered at the trial, since it mentions the rebuilding of the walls by Conon (2.38–39). See [[socrates]] and [[conon]].
