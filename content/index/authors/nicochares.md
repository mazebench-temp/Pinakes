Nothing is known of Nicochares beyond this passage; he may be the comic poet Nicochares who competed with Aristophanes in 388 BCE, but the name is not rare. His *Deiliad*, by its title a parody of epic about a coward (δειλός), is lost (1448a12–14; see [[deiliad]] and [[parody]]).
