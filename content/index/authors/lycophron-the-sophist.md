---
title: Lycophron the sophist
greek: Λυκόφρων
kind: author
tags: [sophist]
summary: Lycophron, a sophist of the early fourth century BCE, known from Aristotle for his view that law is a mere guarantee of rights and for a style crowded with poetic compounds.
---

Aristotle cites him several times: law, Lycophron said, is a compact, “a guarantor of men's just claims on one another” (*Politics* 3.9, 1280b10–12); he avoided the word “is” to escape the problem of the one and the many (*Physics* 1.2, 185b27–28); he called knowledge a communion of knowing and soul (*Metaphysics* Η 6, 1045b10). He is not Lycophron of Pherae (see [[lycophron-of-pherae]]).
