Aelian says that Dionysius of Colophon imitated the art of Polygnotus in everything except its grandeur, its exactness in expressing feeling, character and posture (*Varia Historia* 4.3). In the *Poetics* he stands between Polygnotus and Pauson: Polygnotus portrayed people better, Pauson worse, “and Dionysius like us” (1448a5–6; see [[better-worse-or-like-us]]).
