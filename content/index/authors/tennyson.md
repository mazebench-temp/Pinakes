Tennyson's translations from Homer are short. The first, a “Specimen of a Translation of the Iliad in Blank Verse”, renders the close of Book 8, the Trojans' night on the plain and their watch-fires; it was printed in 1863 and collected in 1864 among the “Experiments” of the *Enoch Arden* volume, beside poems in classical metres. The second, “Achilles over the Trench”, is from Book 18 (1877).
