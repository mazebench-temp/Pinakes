Sthenelus is mocked in Aristophanes' *Wasps* (1313), where a man is compared to him “shorn of his props”. In the *Poetics* his poetry is the example, with Cleophon's, of diction made of standard words, “the clearest … but it is mean” (1458a18–21; see [[cleophon]] and [[excellence-of-diction]]). He is not the Sthenelus of the Iliad, Diomedes' charioteer (see [[sthenelus]]).
