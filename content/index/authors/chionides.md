The Suda calls Chionides “the first actor (protagonist) of Old Comedy” and dates his first production eight years before the Persian Wars, that is, 487/6 BCE, the year after comedy was admitted to the Dionysia. A few titles survive. Aristotle names him with Magnes to date Epicharmus, who “lived long before Chionides and Magnes” (1448a33–34; see [[epicharmus]] and [[magnes]]).
