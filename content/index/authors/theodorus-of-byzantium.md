title: Theodorus of Byzantium
greek: Θεόδωρος

tags: [rhetoric, sophist]
summary: A teacher of rhetoric of the late fifth century BCE, whose handbook added new parts to the speech; Plato's Socrates calls him “the excellent word-wright of Byzantium”.
---

In the *Phaedrus* Socrates runs through the handbooks of rhetoric and their names for the parts of a speech, and Phaedrus recognizes “the excellent word-wright of Byzantium”, who taught refutation and further refutation (266e–267a). Aristotle puts him third in the succession of those who developed the art after its first inventors: Tisias, then Thrasymachus, then Theodorus (*Sophistical Refutations* 34, 183b31–32; see [[thrasymachus]]). Nothing of his handbook survives.
