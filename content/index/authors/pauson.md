A Pauson appears in Aristophanes as a byword for poverty (*Acharnians* 854, *Wealth* 602), perhaps the same man. Aristotle says that the young should look at Polygnotus rather than Pauson (*Politics* 8.5, 1340a35–36), and in the *Poetics* Pauson portrays people “worse” (1448a6; see [[polygnotus]] and [[better-worse-or-like-us]]).
