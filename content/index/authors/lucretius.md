Lucretius writes to free his readers from the fear of the gods and of death. Epicurus is his hero, the man “who surpassed the human race in genius” and put out the others as the risen sun puts out the stars (*On the Nature of Things* 3.1042–1044). Homer is for him the greatest of the poets, who alone among the companions of the Muses held the scepter, and who sleeps the same sleep as the rest (3.1037–1038).
