summary: Greek historian from Alexandria (second century CE), author of a Roman History arranged by peoples and wars. His account of the Third Punic War preserves Polybius' report of what Scipio said as Carthage burned.
---

Appian was born in Alexandria, practiced as an advocate in Rome, and under Antoninus Pius was given the rank of procurator. His *Roman History* told the wars of Rome one people or region at a time, in twenty-four books. Among those that survive are the books on the wars in Spain, with Hannibal, in Africa (the *Punica*, also called *Libyca*), in Illyria and Syria, against Mithridates, and five books on the Roman civil wars.

For Scipio's words at the fall of Carthage in 146 BCE he names his source, Polybius, who was there and heard them.
