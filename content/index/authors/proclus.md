---
title: Proclus' Chrestomathy
greek: Πρόκλου Χρηστομάθεια
kind: author
tags: [scholarship]
summary: A handbook of literature whose summaries of the lost poems of the Epic Cycle (the Cypria, Aethiopis, Little Iliad, Iliupersis, Nostoi and Telegony) are our main source for them.
---

The summaries survive in some manuscripts of the Iliad and in the ninth-century *Bibliotheca* of Photius. The author's identity is disputed: perhaps a grammarian of the second century CE, perhaps the fifth-century Neoplatonist Proclus (see [[proclus-the-neoplatonist]]). The periods of the Myths timeline from [[plan-of-zeus|the Cypria]] to the Telegony follow its order.
