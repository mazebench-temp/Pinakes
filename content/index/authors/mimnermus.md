summary: Greek elegiac poet of Colophon or Smyrna, active in the later seventh century BCE, who sang of youth, love and the misery of old age. His best-known poem turns Glaucus' leaves into an image of the brief flower of youth.
---

The Suda calls him an elegiac poet of Colophon, Smyrna or Astypalaea, and dates him to the 37th Olympiad (632–629 BCE; μ 1077). His poems survive in quotations, most of them in the anthology of Stobaeus. Pausanias knew an elegy of his on the battle of the Smyrnaeans against the Lydians under Gyges (9.29.4). He prayed to die at sixty, free of sickness and cares (fr. 6 West), and Solon answered him that he should make it eighty (Solon fr. 20 West).
