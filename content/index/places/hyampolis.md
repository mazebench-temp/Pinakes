Hyampolis lay in eastern Phocis, where the road from the Cephisus valley passes toward Opuntian Locris, near Abae and its oracle of Apollo.

## Outside Homer

Before the Persian invasion the Phocians defeated the Thessalian cavalry near Hyampolis by burying empty jars in a trench, where the horses broke their legs (Herodotus 8.28). The sanctuary excavated at Kalapodi nearby, in use from the Mycenaean period to Roman times, belonged either to Artemis Elaphebolos of Hyampolis or to the oracle of Abae; the question is debated.
