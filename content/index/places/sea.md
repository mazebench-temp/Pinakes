## Words for the sea

Homer has several words:

- θάλασσα, the sea in general (1.34, 1.157, 1.437, 1.496);
- ἅλς, the salt sea, especially near the shore (1.141, 1.316, 1.350, 1.359);
- πόντος, the open sea (1.350).

## Descriptions

{{quote:3cd9586f-518e-5811-8de0-c7e66c35ec1a}}

πολύφλοισβος = πολύς + φλοῖσβος (roar, din): loud-roaring. Chryses walks along it in silence, against its noise.

{{quote:804d7eba-d187-5d59-b3d3-f31be01751a7}}

ἀτρύγετος: "barren, unharvested" (ἀ- + τρυγάω, to harvest), a sea that yields no crop; ancient readers also explained it as "unwearied".

{{quote:4cdebb41-7bff-5fbc-b5ff-7d09f1185f6b}}

πολιή: gray, the color of foam and of old men's hair. ἀπείρων: boundless.

{{quote:a2b8d071-a4e8-5e97-b537-22ac2abe663d}}

ἠχήεσσα: echoing
