Asine stood on a rocky headland of the Argolic Gulf, east of Nauplion. With Hermione it "lies on the deep gulf" (2.560). It belongs to [[Diomedes]]' contingent.

## Outside Homer

Swedish excavations in 1922–1930 uncovered a prehistoric town with a Mycenaean settlement on the acropolis of Kastraki. Around 700 BC the Argives destroyed Asine, and its people, said to be Dryopes, were resettled by the Spartans at a new Asine in Messenia (Pausanias 2.36, 4.34). George Seferis took the Catalogue's bare "and Asine" as the epigraph of his poem "The King of Asine" (1940), a search on the acropolis for a king known, the poem says, only from a single uncertain word in the Iliad.
