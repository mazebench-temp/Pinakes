The lake took its name from the town. Homer names Boebe only here. It lay somewhere on the shore of the lake, but its exact site is uncertain. With [[Pherae]], [[Glaphyrae]] and [[Iolcus]] it made up the realm of Eumelus, who brought eleven ships to Troy (2.711–713).
