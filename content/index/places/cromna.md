Like Sesamus and Cytorus, Cromna was joined into the new city of Amastris around 300 BC (Strabo, *Geography*, Book 12). It lay on the coast a little east of Amasra. Of the four towns joined into Amastris, Sesamus, Cytorus, Cromna and Tieium, Tieium soon broke away again; the other three are all in Homer's list.
