# Πήδαιον at 5.69 is the man Pedaeus, a bastard son of Antenor (pedaeus).
except: [5.69]
---

ναῖε δὲ Πήδαιον πρὶν ἐλθεῖν υἷας Ἀχαιῶν, “he had lived in Pedaeum before the sons of the Achaeans came” (13.172). Imbrius, son of Mentor, returned from it to Ilios when the Achaean ships came, and lived beside Priam, whose bastard daughter Medesicaste he had married (13.171–176; see [[imbrius]] and [[medesicaste]]).

The town is named nowhere else, and the poem gives no clue to where it was. Its name has the same form as the accusative of Πήδαιος, Pedaeus, the bastard son of Antenor whom Meges kills (5.69; see [[pedaeus]]).
