ἔφαλος "by the sea" ([[by-the-sea]]) = ἐπί (on) + ἅλς (sea); the same epithet is given to Helos in Lacedaemon (2.584). Cerinthus lay on the east coast of [[Euboea]], near the modern village of Mantoudi, looking out on the open Aegean.
