νήσους τε Καλύδνας: "and the Calydnian islands". The group is usually identified with Kalymnos and the smaller islands near it, north of Cos. Homer names them only here. They sent their men with those of Cos, Nisyrus, Carpathus and Casus, thirty ships in all, under the grandsons of Heracles (2.678–680).
