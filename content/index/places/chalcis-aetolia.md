ἀγχίαλος "by the sea" ([[by-the-sea]]) = ἀγχι- (near) + ἅλς (sea). This Chalcis lay on the coast of Aetolia at the mouth of the river Evenus, below the mountain later called Varassova, near Calydon. It belongs to the [[Aetolians]]. The great Chalcis of Euboea is named a hundred lines earlier (2.537; [[chalcis]]).

## Outside Homer

Thucydides mentions it as a Corinthian settlement taken by the Athenians in 455 BC (1.108.5) and as a landmark near the Evenus (2.83.3).
