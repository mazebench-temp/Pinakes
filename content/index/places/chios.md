summary: The large Ionian island off the coast of Asia Minor, one of the cities that claimed Homer; the Homeridae, a guild of rhapsodes, were based there.
aliases: [Chians]
---

Simonides, or a poet quoted under his name, called Homer “the man of Chios” (see [[the-man-of-chios]]), and the *Hymn to Apollo* ends with the singer's description of himself as a blind man who lives in rocky Chios (172). Pindar speaks of the Homeridae, “the sons of Homer” (*Nemean* 2.1–2), and later writers place them on Chios. See [[homer]].
