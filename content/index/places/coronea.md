Coronea lay west of Haliartus, on a hill above the plain at the southwestern edge of Lake Copais.

## Outside Homer

Its sanctuary of Athena Itonia was the meeting place of the festival shared by all the Boeotians. Two battles were fought near it: in 447 BC the Boeotians defeated an Athenian army returning from Chaeronea (Thucydides 1.113), and in 394 BC Agesilaus fought the Thebans and their allies there (Xenophon, *Hellenica* 4.3).
