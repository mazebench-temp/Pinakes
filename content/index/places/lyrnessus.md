Book 2 names Lyrnessus with Thebe, the city of Eetion, as the towns of the Troad that Achilles sacked (see [[thebe]]). It was "the city of divine Mynes", and Achilles killed [[Mynes]] and his brother [[epistrophus-of-lyrnessus|Epistrophus]] there (2.692; 19.296).

## Elsewhere in Homer

Briseis, lamenting over Patroclus, tells how Achilles killed her husband and her three brothers when he sacked the city (19.291–300). Achilles wishes that Artemis had killed her "on the day I took Lyrnessus" (19.59–60). Aeneas, chased by Achilles from Mount Ida, fled to Lyrnessus, and escaped when Achilles sacked it with the help of Athena and Zeus (20.89–96, 20.187–194).

## Outside Homer

In Proclus' summary of the *Cypria*, Achilles drives off the cattle of Aeneas and sacks Lyrnessus, Pedasus and many of the towns around. The site has not been found; ancient geographers placed it near Thebe, in the plain behind Adramyttium. See [[the-sack-of-lyrnessus]].
