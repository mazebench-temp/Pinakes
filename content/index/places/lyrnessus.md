*Iliad* 2 names Lyrnessus with Thebe, the city of Eetion, as the towns of the Troad that Achilles sacked (see [[thebe]]). It was "the city of divine Mynes", and Achilles killed [[Mynes]] and his brother [[epistrophus-of-lyrnessus|Epistrophus]] there (2.692; 19.296).

## Elsewhere in Homer

Achilles and Briseis remember the sack when she is given back to him (19.59–60, 19.291–300). Aeneas, chased by Achilles from Mount Ida, fled to Lyrnessus, and escaped when Achilles sacked it with the help of Athena and Zeus (20.89–96, 20.187–194).

## Outside Homer

In Proclus' summary of the *Cypria*, Achilles drives off the cattle of Aeneas and sacks Lyrnessus, Pedasus and many of the towns around. The site has not been found. Strabo places it with Thebe in what was later called the plain of Thebe, behind Adramyttium: a deserted stronghold in his day, eighty-eight stades from that city (*Geography* 13.1.61; see [[strabo-on-the-city-of-mynes]]).
