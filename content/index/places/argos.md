ἐν Ἄργεϊ τηλόθι πάτρης: "in Argos, far from her native land". In the Catalogue of Ships Agamemnon's own city is Mycenae (2.569), while Diomedes rules the city of Argos (2.559). "Argos" here is the wider realm or homeland. It is the source of the name [[Argives]].
