πόλιν τʼ Ὀλοοσσόνα λευκήν: "the white city of Oloosson", perhaps for the pale earth or rock of its hill. The town lies below Mount Olympus on its southwest side, and its name survives in modern Elassona. Its men came to Troy with the other Lapiths, under Polypoetes and Leonteus.
