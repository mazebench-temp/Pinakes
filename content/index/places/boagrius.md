"Around the streams of the Boagrius" (Βοαγρίου ἀμφὶ ῥέεθρα) places [[Thronium]]. The name recalls βοάγρια, the oxhide shields of 12.22, a compound of βοῦς, "ox", and ἄγρη, "spoil, catch". It is a small river of the Locrian coast, and the only river named in the Locrian entry.
