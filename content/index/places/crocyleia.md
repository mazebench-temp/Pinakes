Crocyleia is named with rugged Aegilips among the places of the [[Cephallenians]]. Ancient scholars could not agree whether it lay on Ithaca, on one of the neighboring islands, or on the mainland opposite, and its site is unknown. It is one of six places of the Cephallenians that the Catalogue names, besides "the mainland" and "the coast across from them" (2.632–635).
