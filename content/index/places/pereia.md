{{quote:3d3eb9d9-432c-5e1a-8b5b-b5977726e84c}}

Homer names Pereia only here. Some manuscripts read ἐν Πιερίῃ, "in Pieria", the country north of Olympus, which Hera crosses on her way down from the mountain in Book 14 (14.226). Either way the pasture belongs to a god, not to Eumelus' family. See [[the-mares-of-eumelus]].
