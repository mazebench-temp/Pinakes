Hyperesia lay on a height above the southern shore of the Gulf of Corinth, in the coastal land later called Achaea. It belongs to [[Agamemnon]]'s realm.

## Outside Homer

Pausanias identifies it with Aegeira, one of the twelve cities of Achaea, which he says Homer called Hyperesia (7.26.1–2).
