ἐϋκτιμένην, "well-built" (see [[well-built]]). Ἰαωλκός is Homer's form; later Greek has Ἰωλκός. In the Odyssey, Pelias "lived in broad Iolcus, rich in flocks" (11.256–257). Later legend made it the home of Jason, whom Pelias sent for the Golden Fleece, and the harbor from which the Argo sailed (Pindar, *Pythian* 4; Apollonius, *Argonautica* 1). Mycenaean remains at Volos and at nearby Dimini are often connected with it.
