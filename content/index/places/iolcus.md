ἐϋκτιμένην, "well-built" (see [[well-built]]). Ἰαωλκός is Homer's form; later Greek has Ἰωλκός. In the Odyssey it is the city of Pelias, the son of Tyro and Poseidon (*Odyssey* 11.254–257; see [[tyro]]). Later legend made it the home of Jason, whom Pelias sent for the Golden Fleece, and the harbor from which the Argo sailed (Pindar, *Pythian* 4; Apollonius, *Argonautica* 1). Mycenaean remains at Volos and at nearby Dimini are often connected with it.
