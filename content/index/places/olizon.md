τρηχεῖαν, "rugged, rough" (see [[rugged]]): the same epithet marks rocky [[aegilips|Aegilips]] in Odysseus' kingdom (2.633). Olizon lay somewhere on the mountainous peninsula of Magnesia. Homer names it only here. Its men, like the rest of Philoctetes', rowed and fought as archers (2.719–720).
