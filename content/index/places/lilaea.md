Lilaea lay on the northern side of Parnassus, where the [[Cephisus]] breaks out of the mountain in strong springs (πηγῇς ἔπι Κηφισοῖο, "at the springs of the Cephisus"). The Homeric Hymn to Apollo also has the river pouring its water "from Lilaea" (241). Later tradition named the town after a Naiad, a daughter of the river.
