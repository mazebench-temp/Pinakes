{{quote:b4bb3e35-809b-5e70-b370-91a75fcc7170}}

The first night of the love that caused the war is remembered on the day the war was to be settled by a duel, and Paris says that his desire now is greater than it was then (3.442–446). See [[paris]] and [[helen]].

## Etymology

κραναός means “rocky, rugged”. Earlier in the book Helen uses the word of Ithaca, “craggy though it is” (κραναῆς, 3.201; see [[craggy]]). Κραναή may be a name, or simply “the rocky island”; the Oxford text prints it as a name.

## Outside Homer

Pausanias saw an island called Cranae lying off Gytheum, and reports that Alexander first lay with Helen there after carrying her off. On the mainland opposite was a sanctuary of Aphrodite Migonitis, said to have been founded by Alexander, and the whole place was called Migonium (Pausanias 3.22.1). Both names recall ἐμίγην (“I lay with”) in Paris' line. The islet of Marathonisi in the harbor of Gytheio is identified with it today.
