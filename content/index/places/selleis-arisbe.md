ποταμοῦ ἄπο Σελλήεντος, "from the river Selleis". A river of the same name flows by Ephyra in the Achaean catalogue, where Heracles took Astyocheia ([[selleis-ephyra]], 2.659). The lines about Asius and his horses return at 12.96–97. The Trojan Selleis was a small river near Arisbe, flowing into the Hellespont.
