Megara stood between Attica and the Corinthia, Athens' neighbor and often its enemy; its colonies included Byzantium and Megara Hyblaea in Sicily, the mother city of Selinus. “Megarian” jokes were a byword in Athenian comedy for coarse humor. Aristotle reports that comedy was claimed “by the Megarians, both those here, who say it began when they had their democracy, and those from Sicily” (1448a31–33; see [[megarians]] and [[the-origins-of-comedy]]).
