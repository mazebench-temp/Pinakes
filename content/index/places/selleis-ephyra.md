{{quote:8fb7d558-f13e-5edb-b2df-9f44174a7405}}

The half-line ἐξ Ἐφύρης ποταμοῦ ἄπο Σελλήεντος, "from Ephyra, from the river Selleis", is a formula: it recurs of the corselet of Meges (15.531). Where the river ran depends on where Ephyra was, and that was disputed in antiquity (see [[ephyra]]). A second Selleis, in the Troad, flows by Arisbe (2.839; see [[selleis-arisbe]]).
