A town of the same name lay in Opuntian Locris, where the Athenians defeated the Locrians in 431 BC (Thucydides 2.26.2). The Catalogue's Alope belongs to Achilles' realm, but where exactly it stood is not known. The Catalogue lists it between Alus and Trachis, and it probably lay in the same country, between Mount Othrys and Mount Oeta.
