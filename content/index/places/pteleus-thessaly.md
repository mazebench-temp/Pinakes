λεχεποίην = λέχος ("bed") + ποίη ("grass"): "bedded in grass", with deep meadows. Homer gives the same epithet to the river Asopus (4.383). The name comes from πτελέα, "elm". It is listed with [[Antron]] among the coastal towns of Protesilaus' realm. A second Pteleus belongs to Nestor's kingdom (2.594; see [[pteleus]]).
