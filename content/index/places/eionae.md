The name is the plural of ἠϊών, "shore, beach". Eionae is named with Troezen and vine-rich Epidaurus, towns of the eastern Argolid. It was a coastal place in [[Diomedes]]' contingent; later writers could no longer locate it, and its site is unknown. It is not Eion in Thrace.
