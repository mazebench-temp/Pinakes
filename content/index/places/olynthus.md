---
title: Olynthus
greek: Ὄλυνθος
kind: place
tags: [city]
summary: The chief city of the Chalcidice in northern Greece, which Athens tried to defend against Philip of Macedon in the Olynthian War (349–348 BCE); Philip took and destroyed it.
---

Demosthenes' three *Olynthiacs* urged the Athenians to send help.
