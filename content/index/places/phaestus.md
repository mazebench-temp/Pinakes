πόλεις εὖ ναιετοώσας: "well-peopled cities", literally "well inhabited" (see [[well-peopled]]). In the Odyssey, part of Menelaus' fleet is wrecked on the south coast of Crete, where the south wind drives a great wave "toward Phaestus" (3.293–296).

From 1900 Italian excavators uncovered a Minoan palace on the ridge at Phaistos, second in size only to Knossos. There in 1908 Luigi Pernier found the Phaistos Disc, a clay disc stamped with signs in a script that has never been read. The Linear B tablets from Knossos name the place pa-i-to.
