πέτρη Ὠλενίη: "the Olenian Rock". The adjective is usually connected with Olenus, a town of Achaea near the border of Elis. It is one of the four points that "enclose" the land of the Epeians (2.617). Nestor names it with Buprasium and Alesium as the limit of the Pylians' pursuit of the Epeians (11.756–758). It is usually identified with Mount Scollis, the modern Santameri, on the border of Elis and Achaea. It is not the Aetolian Olenus of 2.639.
