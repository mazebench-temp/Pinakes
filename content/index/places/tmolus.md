The Maeonians were "born beneath Tmolus" (ὑπὸ Τμώλῳ γεγαῶτας). In Book 20 Achilles kills Iphition, born "beneath snowy Tmolus, in the rich land of Hyde", by the Gygaean lake (20.382–392). The range, the modern Boz Dağ, rises south of Sardis. The Pactolus, which carried the gold dust of the Lydian kings, flows down from it through the city (Herodotus 5.101). Tmolus was also a mountain of Dionysus: the chorus of Euripides' *Bacchae* are Lydian women who have followed the god from there.
