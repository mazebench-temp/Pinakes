ἐσχατόωσα "at the border, at the edge", the participle of ἐσχατάω; the same word places Anthedon "at the land's end" of Boeotia (2.508). Myrsinus lay on the coast of northern Elis. It is the second of the four places that "enclose" the land of the [[Epeians]] (2.616–617). The name recalls μυρσίνη, "myrtle".
