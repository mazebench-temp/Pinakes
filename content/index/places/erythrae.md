The name means "the red (towns)", from ἐρυθρός, "red". Erythrae lay in southern Boeotia on the lower slopes of Cithaeron, near [[Plataea]]. It is not the Ionian city of the same name.

## Outside Homer

In 479 BC the Greek army, coming over Cithaeron, first took position at Erythrae, facing the Persians across the Asopus, before the battle of Plataea (Herodotus 9.19).
