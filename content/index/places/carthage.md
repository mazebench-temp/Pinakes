Carthage was founded from Tyre, by tradition in 814 BCE, and controlled western Sicily, Sardinia and the African coast. Aristotle praises its constitution in the *Politics* (2.11, 1272b24–1273b26). For the battle see [[salamis-and-himera]] and [[carthaginians]].
