ἀγχίαλον, "by the sea", literally "near the salt water" (see [[by-the-sea]]). The name looks like ἄντρον ("cave"). Antron lay near the mouth of the Malian Gulf, across the water from the northern tip of Euboea. Homer names it only here, among the towns of [[Protesilaus]].
