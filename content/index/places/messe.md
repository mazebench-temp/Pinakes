πολυτρήρων "of the many doves" ([[of-many-doves]]), an epithet shared with Thisbe in Boeotia (2.502). Messe belongs to Menelaus' [[Lacedaemon]]. Its site is unknown; ancient scholars identified it with Messa, a harbor on the Mani peninsula, or took the name as a shortened form of Messene.
