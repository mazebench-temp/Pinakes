ἱερός "holy" ([[hieros]]). The Echinae are the islands later called the Echinades, off the coast of Acarnania at the mouth of the river Achelous, "across the sea, opposite Elis". The name recalls ἐχῖνος, "hedgehog, sea urchin". They belong with [[Dulichium]] to [[Meges]].

## Outside Homer

Herodotus and Thucydides both observed that the silt of the Achelous was joining the islands to the mainland (Herodotus 2.10; Thucydides 2.102).
