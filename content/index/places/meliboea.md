Sailing along the south of Italy, Virgil's Aeneas passes "little Petelia, relying on the wall of Philoctetes, the leader from Meliboea", a town Philoctetes founded after the war (*Aeneid* 3.401–402). Meliboea is the one town of his realm that later tradition remembered as his home.
