## Outside Homer

Strabo discusses Chryse at length (*Geography* 13.1.48, 13.1.63). He locates the temple of Apollo Smintheus near Hamaxitus, with a statue by the sculptor Scopas that had a mouse under its foot. The ruins of the Hellenistic Smintheion survive at Gülpınar on the Biga peninsula. See [[smintheus-and-the-mice]].
