## In Book 1

{{quote:8056fbf6-35ca-5016-a86a-90087e870edc}}

ἀμφιβέβηκας: "you who stand guard over", literally "bestride", the pose of a guardian standing over what he protects.

{{quote:bd6293fe-865f-56f6-904c-ed63ce8e91ed}}

λιμὴν πολυβενθής: "the deep harbor". The Achaeans' landing is described step by step. See [[sailing-type-scene]].

## Outside Homer

Strabo discusses Chryse at length (*Geography* 13.1.48, 13.1.63). He locates the temple of Apollo Smintheus near Hamaxitus, with a statue by the sculptor Scopas that had a mouse under its foot. The ruins of the Hellenistic Smintheion survive at Gülpınar on the Biga peninsula. See [[smintheus-and-the-mice]].
