summary: Locri Epizephyrii, the Greek city on the toe of Italy founded by Locrians from Greece, famous for the early lawgiver Zaleucus. Stesichorus warned its people against outrage.
aliases: [Locri Epizephyrii, the Italian Locrians]
---

The city took its name from the Locrians of mainland Greece who founded it (see [[locrians]]). Aristotle names its lawgiver Zaleucus among the earliest makers of laws (*Politics* 2.12, 1274a22–23).
