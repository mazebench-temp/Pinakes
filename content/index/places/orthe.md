Its site is unknown. The name is the adjective ὀρθή, "upright, steep", perhaps for a steep citadel. The accent tells the place name Ὄρθην from the adjective ὀρθήν. It is named with Argissa, Gyrtone, Elone and Oloosson among the towns of the Lapiths.
