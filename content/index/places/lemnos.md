## Outside Homer

Lemnos was sacred to Hephaestus. The Odyssey calls it "dearest to him of all lands" (8.283–284), and its main city was later called Hephaestia. In the Iliad, Jason's son Euneus sends wine from Lemnos to the Achaean camp (7.467–469), and Philoctetes is left there wounded (2.721–723). The Argonauts' visit to the island's husbandless women was a famous episode (Apollonius, *Argonautica* 1.609–909).
