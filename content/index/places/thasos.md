Thasos was colonized from Paros in the seventh century, with the poet Archilochus among the settlers. In the *Poetics* the adjective “Thasian” identifies Hegemon, the first parodist (1448a12), and Hippias, who solved problems in Homer by accent (1461a22; see [[hegemon-of-thasos]] and [[hippias-of-thasos]]). Polygnotus, whom Aristotle also names, was a Thasian (see [[polygnotus]]).
