ἐΰκτιτος "well-built" ([[well-built]]). The name is the neuter of the adjective αἰπύς, "steep", which the Catalogue uses as an epithet of other places (2.538, 2.603). Aepy belongs to Nestor's kingdom; later writers looked for it in Triphylia, but its site is unknown.
