πολυστάφυλος "rich in grapes" ([[rich-in-grapes]]), an epithet shared with [[Arne]] in Boeotia (2.507). Histiaea lay in the north of [[Euboea]], facing the mainland across the channel.

## Outside Homer

It controlled the northern tip of the island and the strait of Artemisium, where the Greek fleet fought the Persians in 480 BC. When Athens put down the revolt of Euboea in 446 BC, Pericles drove out the Histiaeans and settled Athenians on their land (Thucydides 1.114.3); the town was later called Oreus.
