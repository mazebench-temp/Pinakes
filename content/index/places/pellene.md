Pellene lay on a hill inland from the southern shore of the Gulf of Corinth, near Sicyon, and belongs to [[Agamemnon]]'s realm.

## Outside Homer

It was the easternmost of the twelve cities of Achaea. Its games gave a warm woolen cloak as the prize; Pindar mentions the cloak among an athlete's victories (*Olympian* 9.97–98). It is not Pallene in Chalcidice.
