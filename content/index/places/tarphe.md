The name recalls τάρφος, "thicket", and the Homeric adjective ταρφύς, "thick, close-set". Tarphe is named with [[Thronium]] and the river [[Boagrius]] in the last line of the Locrian towns (2.533), which places it in the east of Locris, toward the Malian Gulf. Its exact site is uncertain.
