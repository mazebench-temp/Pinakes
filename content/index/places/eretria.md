Homer's form is Εἰρέτρια; the later city was Ἐρέτρια. It lay on the coast of [[Euboea]] facing the mainland, southeast of [[Chalcis]].

## Outside Homer

Eretria shared with Chalcis the lead in early colonization, and the two fought the Lelantine War over the plain between them (Thucydides 1.15.3). Eretria sent five ships to help the Ionian revolt (Herodotus 5.99), and in 490 BC the Persians took it by treachery, burned its temples and carried off its people (Herodotus 6.101, 6.119). The early Iron Age site at Lefkandi, on the coast between the two cities, may be the older settlement of one of them.
