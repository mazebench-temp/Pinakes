{{quote:fe00f2c5-6924-53ce-a99d-29fae1d8cfe5}}

κλωμακόεσσαν, "rocky, stepped with ledges", from κλῶμαξ, "a heap of stones". Homer uses the adjective only here. Mount Ithome in Messenia, the Messenians' fortress in their wars with Sparta, is a different place. The Thessalian Ithome is named with [[Tricca]] and Oechalia as the land of the sons of Asclepius.
