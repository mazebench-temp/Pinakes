Dorium is the last town of Nestor's kingdom, and the Catalogue pauses on it for a story: "where the Muses met Thamyris the Thracian and put an end to his singing" (see [[thamyris-and-the-muses]] and [[Thamyris]]).

## Outside Homer

Pausanias saw its ruins in northern Messenia and recalled that Homer set Thamyris' misfortune there (4.33.7). Nearby, he says, the river Balyra took its name from Thamyris, who threw away his lyre there after he was maimed (4.33.3). In the 1930s Natan Valmin excavated a prehistoric settlement at Malthi in northern Messenia and identified it with Dorium; the identification is possible but not proved.
