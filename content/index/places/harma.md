The name is the common noun ἅρμα, "chariot". Harma lay in eastern Boeotia near [[Mycalessus]], on the road from Thebes to Chalcis.

## Outside Homer

Later Greeks explained the name by the war of the Seven against Thebes: here the earth opened and swallowed the seer Amphiaraus with his chariot, or here the chariot of Adrastus broke as he fled. Another Harma lay in Attica, near Phyle.
