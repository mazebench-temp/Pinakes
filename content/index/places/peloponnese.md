The name, “island of Pelops” (see [[pelops]]), first appears in the *Homeric Hymn to Apollo* (250, 290); Homer calls the region Argos or Achaean Argos. In the *Poetics* tragedy is claimed “by some of those in the Peloponnese” (1448a34–35), perhaps the Sicyonians, whose “tragic choruses” in honor of Adrastus Herodotus describes (5.67; see [[dorians]] and [[the-origins-of-tragedy]]).
