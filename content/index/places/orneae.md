Orneae lay in the hills northwest of the plain of Argos, on the borders of the territory of Phlius, and belongs to [[Agamemnon]]'s realm.

## Outside Homer

The Argives besieged and destroyed Orneae in the winter of 416/415 BC (Thucydides 6.7). Pausanias derives its name from Orneus, son of Erechtheus (2.25.6), whom Plutarch makes the grandfather of the Athenian leader [[Menestheus]] (*Theseus* 32).
