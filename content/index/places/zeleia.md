The people of Zeleia are called Τρῶες, "Trojans", in the Catalogue, and ἀφνειοί, "wealthy" ([[wealthy]]). Homer calls it ἱερή, “sacred” (4.103, 4.121; see [[hieros]]). The town lay in the northeast of the Troad, on the Aesepus inland from the Propontis. In 334 BC the Persian commanders gathered their army at Zeleia before the battle of the Granicus (Arrian, *Anabasis* 1.12).
