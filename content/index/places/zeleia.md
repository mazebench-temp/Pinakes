The people of Zeleia are called Τρῶες, "Trojans", in the Catalogue, and ἀφνειοί, "wealthy" ([[wealthy]]). Pandarus vows a hecatomb to Apollo when he comes home "to the city of holy Zeleia" (4.101–103, 4.119–121), which suggests a cult of Apollo there. The town lay in the northeast of the Troad, on the Aesepus inland from the Propontis. In 334 BC the Persian commanders gathered their army at Zeleia before the battle of the Granicus (Arrian, *Anabasis* 1.12).
