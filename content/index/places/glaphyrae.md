The name is the adjective γλαφυρός ("hollow"), the epithet of the ships (see [[hollow-ships]]), used as a place name: "the Hollows". Only the accent tells Γλαφύρας apart from the adjective γλαφυράς. The site has not been identified. It is named between Boebe and Iolcus, among the towns that sent eleven ships under Eumelus.
