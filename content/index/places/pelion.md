εἰνοσίφυλλον, "with quivering leaves" (see [[with-quivering-leaves]]). The Catalogue names Pelion twice: the [[Magnetes]] live around it (2.757), and Peirithous drove the Centaurs from it to the Aethices (2.744; see [[lapiths-drive-out-the-centaurs]]).

Pelion was the home of [[chiron|Chiron]], the wise Centaur. Achilles' great spear is "the Pelian ash", which Chiron cut on the mountain's peak and gave to Peleus (16.143–144, 19.390–391). In the Odyssey the giant sons of Aloeus tried to pile Ossa on Olympus and Pelion on Ossa to reach heaven (11.315–316). Later poets set the wedding of Peleus and Thetis on Pelion, and the pine for the Argo was cut in its glens (Euripides, *Medea* 3–4).
