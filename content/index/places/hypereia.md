κρήνην Ὑπέρειαν: "the spring Hypereia". The name returns in Hector's farewell to Andromache. When Troy falls, he fears, she will weave at another woman's loom in Argos and carry water from the springs "Messeis or Hypereia" (6.456–457). Pindar puts a spring called Hypereia at Pherae (*Pythian* 4.125). The Odyssey's Hypereia, the Phaeacians' first home, is a different place (6.4).
