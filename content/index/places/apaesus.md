δῆμος Ἀπαισοῦ, "the land of Apaesus" ([[demos]]). In Book 5 Telamonian Ajax kills Amphius son of Selagus, "who lived in Paesus, rich in possessions" (5.612–613), which suggests that Apaesus and Paesus are the same place. Paesus was taken by the Persians during the Ionian Revolt (Herodotus 5.117), and it was later abandoned, its people moving to Lampsacus (Strabo, *Geography*, Book 13).
