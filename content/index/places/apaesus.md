δῆμος Ἀπαισοῦ, "the land of Apaesus" ([[demos]]). Homer also calls the town Παισός (5.612), which suggests that Apaesus and Paesus are the same place. Paesus was taken by the Persians during the Ionian Revolt (Herodotus 5.117), and it was later abandoned, its people moving to Lampsacus (Strabo, *Geography*, Book 13).
