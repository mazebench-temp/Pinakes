Ἀλφειοῖο πόρος "the ford of the [[Alpheus]]": Thryum stood where the river could be crossed. The name comes from θρύον, "rush". In Nestor's tale of his youth the same place is Thryoessa, "a steep hill far off on the Alpheus, the last town of sandy Pylos", which the Epeians besieged until the Pylians came to relieve it (11.711–713). It marks the northern edge of Nestor's kingdom.
