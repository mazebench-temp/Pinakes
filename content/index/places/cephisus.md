δῖος, "shining, brilliant" ([[brilliant]]), is used of rivers as it is of heroes. The [[Phocians]] lived along its banks and at its springs at [[Lilaea]]. The river flowed into Lake Copais, which is usually taken to be "the Cephisian lake" (λίμνη Κηφισίς, 5.709), though Strabo argued that Homer meant the smaller Lake Hylice beside Hyle (see [[cephisian-lake]]).

## Outside Homer

The Homeric Hymn to Apollo speaks of "the fair-flowing Cephisus, which pours its fair-flowing water from Lilaea" (240–241). Athens had a river of the same name.
