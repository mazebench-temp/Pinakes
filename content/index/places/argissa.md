Homer names Argissa only here. A prehistoric settlement mound on the Peneius west of Larisa, excavated as Argissa Magoula, is identified with it. Its lowest levels belong to some of the earliest farming villages in Greece. The men of Argissa came to Troy with the other Lapiths, in forty ships under Polypoetes and Leonteus (2.740–747).
