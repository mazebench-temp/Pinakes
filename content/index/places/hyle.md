Hyle lay on the lake north of Thebes that took its name, Hylice (modern Yliki). Homer names it twice more. The Trojans kill Oresbius, "who lived in Hyle, much intent on his wealth, on the shore of the Cephisian lake" (5.708–709), and Ajax's great shield was made by Tychius, "far the best of leatherworkers, who lived in Hyle" (7.220–221).

The accusative Ὕλην has the same letters as ὕλην, "forest" (2.455); only the capital tells them apart.
