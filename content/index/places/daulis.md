Daulis stood on a hill east of Delphi, above the road to Chaeronea; the modern village of Davleia keeps its name.

## Outside Homer

It was the scene of the story of Tereus, Procne and Philomela. Thucydides notes that Tereus lived in Daulis, in what was then Thracian country, and that poets call the nightingale "the Daulian bird" (2.29.3).
