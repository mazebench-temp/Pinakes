The name comes from πτελέα, "elm". This Pteleus is one of the nine places of Nestor's kingdom, named with Helos and Dorium in the line that leads into the story of Thamyris. Its site is unknown. A Pteleus "with its grassy meadows" in Thessaly belongs to Protesilaus' men (2.697; [[pteleus-thessaly]]).
