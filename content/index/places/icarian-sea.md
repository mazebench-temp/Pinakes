The assembly heaves like the long waves that the East Wind and the South Wind raise on this sea, rushing down from the clouds of Zeus. See [[eurus]], [[notus]] and [[waves-of-the-icarian-sea]].

The Icarian sea lies off the coast of Ionia, between Samos, Icaria and the islands to the west. Like the [[Cayster]] and the [[asian-meadow|Asian meadow]] in another simile of the same book (2.461), it belongs to the eastern Aegean, and many scholars take such details as signs of the poem's Ionian background.

## Outside Homer

Later tradition named the sea after Icarus, the son of Daedalus, who flew too near the sun on wings of wax and fell into it (Ovid, *Metamorphoses* 8.183–235).
