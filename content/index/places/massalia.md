Massalia was founded from Phocaea in Ionia about 600 BCE and became the chief Greek city of the western Mediterranean, trading up the Rhône. Aristotle wrote a constitution of the Massaliots, now lost. In the *Poetics* the multiple compound names of the Massaliots, like Hermocaicoxanthus, illustrate words of three or four parts (1457a34–b1; see [[massaliots]] and [[hermocaicoxanthus]]).
