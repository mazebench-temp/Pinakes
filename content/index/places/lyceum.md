The Lyceum lay east of the city, by the Ilissus. Socrates spent his days there, as Plato's dialogues tell (*Euthyphro* 2a, *Symposium* 223d), and in 335 BCE Aristotle opened his school there; his followers were later called Peripatetics, from the walk (περίπατος) where he taught. See [[aristotle]].
