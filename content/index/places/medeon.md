ἐϋκτίμενον πτολίεθρον "the well-built citadel" ([[well-built]]) is a formula of the Catalogue, used also of Hypothebae (2.505), Athens (2.546) and Mycenae (2.569). Medeon lay near Onchestus, by Lake Copais; its exact site is uncertain. It is not the Phocian Medeon on the Gulf of Corinth.
