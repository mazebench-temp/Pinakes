Alus lay in Achaea Phthiotis, on the coastal plain below Mount Othrys. When Xerxes marched through it in 480 BC, his guides told him the local story of Athamas and the sanctuary of Laphystian Zeus there (Herodotus 7.197). Homer names it only here. A Hellenistic city of Halos, built on the plain near modern Almyros, has been excavated.
