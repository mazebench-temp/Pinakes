{{quote:e5f1b7bf-a8ab-5872-bcc9-dc0cfd46b78f}}

Homer names it only here, and its site is unknown. It is listed with [[Methone]], [[Meliboea]] and [[Olizon]], towns of the Magnesian coast, and probably lay near them. Its men, like all of Philoctetes', were archers who also rowed his seven ships (2.718–720).
