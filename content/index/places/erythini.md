ὑψηλούς, "lofty". The name means "the Red Ones" (ἐρυθρός, "red"), and later geographers explained it by the color of the rock (Strabo, *Geography*, Book 12). In the Catalogue only the Erythini are called ὑψηλοί. They close the list of the Paphlagonian places, and, as often in the Catalogue, the last name in the line carries its epithet: "and lofty Erythini".
