Aegium lay on the southern shore of the Gulf of Corinth, on a terrace above a harbor; the modern town of Aigio stands on the site. It belongs to [[Agamemnon]]'s realm, "all along [[Aegialus]]".

## Outside Homer

In Hellenistic times the Achaean League held its regular meetings at Aegium, in the sanctuary of Zeus Homarios.
