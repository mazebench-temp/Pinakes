ἐρατεινός "lovely" ([[lovely]]). The same name with the same epithet recurs fifty lines later, for a town in Menelaus' Lacedaemon (2.583; [[augeiae-laconia]]). It is one of the eight towns of the Locrians (2.531–533). The Locrian Augeiae had disappeared by historical times, and its site is unknown.
