The name means "the place of cypresses" (κυπάρισσος, "cypress"). Cyparisseis is usually placed at the later Cyparissia, the modern Kyparissia, on the west coast of Messenia, one of the few towns of Nestor's kingdom that can be located with some confidence. It is not [[Cyparissus]] in Phocis (2.519).
