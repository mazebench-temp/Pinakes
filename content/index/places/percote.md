Percote lay on the strait between Abydos and Lampsacus. Merops is "of Percote" (Περκώσιος, 2.831), though his sons lead the men of Adrasteia and its neighbors, and Percote itself is listed under Asius. Hector's cousin Melanippus grazed his cattle at Percote before the Achaeans came (15.546–548), and Iphidamas, a son of Antenor raised in Thrace, left his ships there when he came to the war (11.229–230). In the Ionian Revolt the Persian general Daurises took Dardanus, Abydos, Percote, Lampsacus and Paesus, one a day (Herodotus 5.117).
