τρηχύς "rugged" ([[rugged]]). The name is also an adjective: Homer uses αἰγίλιψ of a sheer rock, down which a dark spring pours (9.15, 16.4). Ancient readers explained it as a cliff "abandoned even by goats" (αἴξ, "goat", + λείπω, "leave"). Aegilips belongs to the [[Cephallenians]]; its site is unknown.
