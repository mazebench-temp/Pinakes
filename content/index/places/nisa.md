ζαθέη "holy" ([[zatheos]]; ζα-, "very", + θεῖος, "divine"). Nisa is named with Arne, Midea and Anthedon at the end of the Boeotian list. No Boeotian town of this name was known in later times, and ancient critics proposed other readings and other identifications. It is not Nysa, the mountain of Dionysus (6.133).
