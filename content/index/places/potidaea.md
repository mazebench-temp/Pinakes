The revolt of Potidaea was one of the causes of the Peloponnesian War (Thucydides 1.56–66). The city surrendered in the winter of 430/29 BCE; its people were allowed to leave with a little money and clothing, and Athens sent settlers of its own (Thucydides 2.70). See [[aegina]].
