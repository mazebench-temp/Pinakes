The name is the common noun αἰγιαλός, "beach, shore", as in the surf simile of this book (2.210). Agamemnon's men live "all along Aegialus" (Αἰγιαλόν τ' ἀνὰ πάντα), the coastal land later called Achaea. A stretch of the Black Sea coast in Paphlagonia has the same name (2.855; [[aegialus-of-paphlagonia]]).

## Outside Homer

Herodotus says that the Ionians, while they lived in the Peloponnese in what is now called Achaea, were called "Pelasgian Aegialees", the people of the coast (7.94).
