Messeis is named only here. Hector pairs it with Hypereia, the Thessalian spring of the Catalogue (2.734; see [[hypereia]]), and places both “in Argos” (6.456), which may mean Greece at large or the Argolid (see [[argos]]). The two springs are not in one region, and they do not say where Andromache will be taken. They stand for the springs of a foreign land, where a captive fetches water “much against your will” (6.458; see [[doulios]] and [[captive-women]]). Pausanias was shown a spring called Messeis at Therapne, near Sparta (3.20.1).
