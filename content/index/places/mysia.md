The Mysians fight for Troy in the Iliad (see [[mysians]]). In the later myth Telephus, son of Heracles and Auge of Tegea, came to Mysia and became king there; when the Achaeans landed in Mysia on their way to Troy, Achilles wounded him (see [[telephus]]). In the *Poetics* it is irrational in the *Mysians* that a man comes “without a word from Tegea to Mysia” (1460a31–32; see [[mysians-play]] and [[the-silence-of-telephus]]).
