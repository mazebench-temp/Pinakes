Homer uses the singular Θέσπεια; the classical city was Θεσπιαί, Thespiae, in southwestern Boeotia below Helicon.

## Outside Homer

Hesiod's village of Ascra lay in its territory, and so did the Valley of the Muses on Helicon, where the Thespians held a festival of the Muses. The city was also known for its cult of Eros. Seven hundred Thespians came to Thermopylae with Leonidas, stayed when the others were sent away, and died there (Herodotus 7.202, 7.222).
