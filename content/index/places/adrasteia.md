Adrasteia lay on the coast of the Propontis, between Parium and Priapus. Its name is also a title of Nemesis, "she whom none escapes" (Aeschylus, *Prometheus Bound* 936), and ancient writers connected the town with a cult of the goddess (Strabo, *Geography*, Book 13). Its leader Adrestus bears a matching name ([[adrestus]]).
