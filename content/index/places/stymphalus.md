Stymphalus lay beside a lake in a closed basin of northeastern Arcadia, east of Pheneus. It is one of the towns of the [[Arcadians]].

## Outside Homer

In the sixth of his labors Heracles drove away the birds that infested the woods around the lake, the Stymphalian birds (Apollodorus 2.5.6).
