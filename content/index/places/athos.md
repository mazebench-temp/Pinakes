Athos is “a great and famous mountain” that runs out into the sea, inhabited, and joined to the mainland by an isthmus about twelve stadia wide (Herodotus 7.22.2). In 492 BCE the fleet of Mardonius was wrecked by a north wind as it rounded the mountain (Herodotus 6.44.2–3), and before the second invasion Xerxes had a canal dug through the isthmus; Herodotus describes the work (7.22–24; see [[herodotus]]).
