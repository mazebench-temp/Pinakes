Hyria opens the list of Boeotian towns, paired with [[Aulis]], where the fleet had gathered (2.303). It lay on the Euripus, the narrow strait between Boeotia and [[Euboea]], in the district later held by Tanagra.

## Outside Homer

The town is usually placed at the Mycenaean settlement near Dramesi, on the shore north of Aulis, though no inscription confirms it. In later legend its eponym Hyrieus was the father of the hunter Orion.
