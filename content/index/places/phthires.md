Φθιρῶν ὄρος, "the mountain of the Phthires", is a genitive plural, as if the mountain belonged to a people or a thing called Phthires; what the word meant is unknown. ἀκριτόφυλλος = ἄκριτος ("undistinguished, past counting") + φύλλον ("leaf"): "with leaves past counting", a thickly wooded height. The compound occurs only here. The same ἄκριτος describes Priam's "endless words" in Iris' speech (2.796) and Thersites, ἀκριτόμυθος (2.246). Ancient geographers identified the mountain with Latmus, the range east of Miletus, or with its neighbor Grion (Strabo, *Geography* 14.1).
