αἰπὺ πτολίεθρον "the steep citadel" ([[steep]]). Dium lay in the northwest of [[Euboea]], near Cape Cenaeum, looking across the water to the Locrian coast; its exact site is not known. It is not Dium in Macedonia, at the foot of Olympus.
