κοίλη "hollow": the Eurotas valley lies deep between the ranges of Taygetus and Parnon. κητώεσσα is uncertain in meaning; it is usually taken as "full of ravines" or "of the many clefts", and the translation renders it "with its many ravines". An ancient variant, καιετάεσσα, was explained from καιετοί, a Laconian word for clefts opened in the ground by earthquakes. The whole line recurs at the opening of Odyssey 4, when Telemachus arrives at Menelaus' palace (Od. 4.1).

The towns of the land are [[Pharis]], [[Sparta]], [[Messe]], [[Bryseae]], [[augeiae-laconia|Augeiae]], [[Amyclae]], [[Helos]], [[Laas]] and [[Oetylus]] (2.582–585). [[Menelaus]] leads them with sixty ships. His men arm apart from his brother's, and he goes among them "trusting in his own eagerness", longing "to pay them back for the struggles and groans for Helen's sake" (2.586–590).

## Elsewhere in Homer

Lacedaemon is Helen's homeland. In the Odyssey Telemachus visits Menelaus and Helen there (Od. 4).
