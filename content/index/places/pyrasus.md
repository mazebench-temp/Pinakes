ἀνθεμόεντα, "flowering" (see [[flowering]]). Δήμητρος τέμενος, "the precinct of [[Demeter]]", stands in apposition: the town is the goddess's land. A τέμενος (from τέμνω, "cut") is a piece of land cut off and set apart for a god or a king. Pyrasus lay near the later city of Thebes of Phthiotis, at modern Nea Anchialos, where a prehistoric settlement has been excavated.
