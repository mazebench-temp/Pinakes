Ἰηλυσός is the Ionic form of the name; later Greeks said Ialysos. Its acropolis stood on the hill now called Filerimos. Below it lay a large Mycenaean cemetery, in use from the fourteenth to the twelfth century BC, whose chamber tombs show that Greeks lived on Rhodes in the Late Bronze Age.
