{{quote:609f2d75-7254-5d5c-9e15-add5a31ddc7d}}

The name means "rush" (σχοῖνος), the marsh plant, and suits a low, wet place. The town lay somewhere in eastern Boeotia, perhaps near Thebes, but nothing has been found that can be attached to it with confidence. Homer names it only here.
