Tricca lay on the upper Peneius, in the district later called Hestiaeotis. It was proud of its connection with the healer's family: Strabo calls its sanctuary of Asclepius the oldest and most famous of all (*Geography* 9.5.17). Its men, with those of [[Ithome]] and Oechalia, came to Troy in thirty ships (2.729–733).
