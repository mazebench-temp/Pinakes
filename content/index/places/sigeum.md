title: Sigeum

tags: [troad, city]
summary: A town on the promontory at the mouth of the Hellespont in the Troad, near the tomb shown as Achilles', fought over by Athens and Mytilene in the sixth century BCE.
aliases: [Sigeion]
---

Homer does not name Sigeum. The Athenians took it from Mytilene, and in the war that followed the poet Alcaeus fled from the battle; Periander of Corinth, chosen as arbitrator, left each side what it held, and Pisistratus later installed his son Hegesistratus there as ruler (Herodotus 5.94–95; see [[periander]] and [[alcaeus]]). The headland was pointed out as the place of Achilles' tomb.
