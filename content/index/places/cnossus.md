Later in the Iliad, the shield of Achilles has a dancing floor like the one Daedalus made "in broad Cnossus" for Ariadne (18.590–592). In the Odyssey it is "a great city, where Minos ruled" (19.178–179).

Arthur Evans began to excavate the Bronze Age palace at Knossos in 1900. It is the largest of the Minoan palaces, and its archive of Linear B tablets, written in Greek, names the place ko-no-so. The tablets are usually dated to the fourteenth century BC, though some scholars put them later.
