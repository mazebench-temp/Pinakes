ἀργινόεντα, "chalk-white" (see [[chalk-white]]), from the root of ἀργός ("white, gleaming"): the same epithet marks [[Cameirus]] on Rhodes (2.656). It probably describes white limestone cliffs or soil. Nothing else is known of Lycastus, and its site has not been found.
