## The name

The verse that names the river ends with the same half-verse as Nestor's battle by the Iardanus of Elis in the Iliad, Ἰαρδάνου ἀμφὶ ῥέεθρα, “about the streams of the Iardanus” (*Odyssey* 3.292; *Iliad* 7.135; see [[iardanus]]). Homer names the Cretan river only here. Merry places it, with the city of the Cydonians, on the north coast of the island, though the Cydonians held the breadth of Crete as far as its south coast (Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on *Odyssey* 3.292; see [[cydonians]] and [[crete]]).
