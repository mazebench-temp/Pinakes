ποιήεις "grassy", from ποίη, "grass": Haliartus lay among the meadows on the southern shore of Lake Copais. The Homeric Hymn to Apollo also calls it "grassy Haliartus" (243), and Homer gives the same epithet to Hire in Messenia (9.150).

## Outside Homer

The Spartan commander Lysander was killed under its walls in 395 BC (Xenophon, *Hellenica* 3.5). The Romans destroyed the city in 171 BC for siding with Perseus of Macedon (Livy 42.63).
