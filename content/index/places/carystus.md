Carystus lay at the southern end of [[Euboea]], below Mount Ochi.

## Outside Homer

In 490 BC the Persians besieged Carystus until it surrendered (Herodotus 6.99); after the Persian Wars Athens forced it into its alliance (Thucydides 1.98.3). Its green-and-white marble, the Romans' *marmor Carystium*, now called cipollino, was used in Roman buildings.
