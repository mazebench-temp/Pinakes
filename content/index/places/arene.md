ἐρατεινός "lovely" ([[lovely]]). Arene is named second, after [[Pylos]], in Nestor's kingdom. In his story of the war with the Epeians, Nestor tells how the river [[minyeios|Minyeios]] "falls into the sea near Arene", where the Pylian horsemen waited for dawn before marching to the Alpheus (11.722–725).

## Outside Homer

Later writers placed Arene in Triphylia, near Samicum, north of the river Neda; the identification is not certain.
