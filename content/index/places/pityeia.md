The name comes from πίτυς, "pine". Later geographers placed the town in the territory of Parium, on the Propontis (Strabo, *Geography*, Book 13). It is named nowhere else in Homer. With [[Adrasteia]], [[Apaesus]] and the mountain of [[Tereia]], it makes up the realm of the sons of Merops, on the coast west of [[Zeleia]].
