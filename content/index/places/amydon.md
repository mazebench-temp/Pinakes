τηλόθεν, "from far away": Amydon marks the western end of the Trojan alliance. It lay on the lower Axius, in what was later Macedonia, but its site is not certainly known. The Catalogue's line returns when Patroclus kills Pyraechmes (16.288; see [[pyraechmes]]).

## Outside Homer

[[Strabo]] knew Amydon as a stronghold on the Axius, called Abydon in his own day, and says that it was razed by the Argeadae (*Geography* 7 fr. 20; again 7 fr. 23a). On the reading of the Catalogue's next verse, which speaks of the Axius' water, see [[axius]].
