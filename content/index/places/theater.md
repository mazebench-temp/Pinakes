## The place

θέατρον, from θεάομαι, “watch, behold”, was first the seating, then the whole building. At Athens the plays were performed in the Theater of Dionysus on the south slope of the Acropolis, at the festivals of the god; it was rebuilt in stone in Aristotle's lifetime under Lycurgus. θεαταί are the spectators.
