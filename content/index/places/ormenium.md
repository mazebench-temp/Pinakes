Homer names it only here, as the first of Eurypylus' places, and its site is unknown. The same realm includes the spring [[Hypereia]], [[Asterium]] and Mount [[Titanus]], and its men came to Troy under [[Eurypylus]], son of Euaemon, in forty ships (2.734–737).
