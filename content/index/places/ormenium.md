Homer names it only here, as the first of Eurypylus' places. The same realm includes the spring [[Hypereia]], [[Asterium]] and Mount [[Titanus]], and its men came to Troy under [[Eurypylus]], son of Euaemon, in forty ships (2.734–737).

## Outside Homer

Strabo knew it as Orminium, a village under Pelion on the Gulf of Pagasae, one of the towns joined into Demetrias, twenty-seven stades from that city by land (9.5.18; see [[strabo]]). Demetrius of Scepsis made it the home of Phoenix, reading Ὀρμένιον πολύμηλον at Iliad 9.447 (see [[hellas-or-ormenium]]).
