κλυτὰ δώματα, "famous homes". The river's name means "maidenly" (παρθένος, "maiden"). It is the modern Bartın Çayı, which reaches the sea a short way west of Amasra. In later geography Paphlagonia reached from about this river eastward to the Halys.
