Troezen lay on the coast of the Argolic peninsula facing the Saronic Gulf, opposite the island of Poros. It belongs to [[Diomedes]]' contingent.

## Outside Homer

In legend it was the city of Pittheus, grandfather of [[Theseus]], and the place of Theseus' birth; Euripides' *Hippolytus* is set there. In 480 BC the Athenians sent their women and children to Troezen when they abandoned their city (Herodotus 8.41). A copy of a decree ordering the evacuation, the "Themistocles Decree", was found at Troezen in 1959; it was inscribed in the third century BC, and its authenticity is debated.
