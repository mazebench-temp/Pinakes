ὄρος αἰπύ, "steep mountain" ([[steep]]). Tereia is the only mountain in the list, set among the towns of the Propontis coast. Ancient geographers disagreed about which height it was (Strabo, *Geography*, Book 13), and it has not been identified. The realm it belongs to, that of the sons of Merops, lay between Zeleia and the Hellespont towns of Asius.
