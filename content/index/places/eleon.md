Eleon is named first in a line of three Boeotian towns, with [[Hyle]] and [[Peteon]]. It lay in eastern Boeotia, on a hill at the edge of the plain between Thebes and Tanagra.

## Outside Homer

Excavations since 2011 on the hill at the village of Arma, identified with Eleon, have uncovered a Mycenaean settlement and an early Mycenaean funerary monument.
