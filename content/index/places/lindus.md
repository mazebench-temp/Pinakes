Lindus is the first of the three Rhodian cities in the Catalogue. Its temple of Athena Lindia stood high on the acropolis above the sea. An inscription of 99 BC, the Lindian Chronicle, lists the offerings that heroes of legend were said to have dedicated there. Cleobulus, counted among the Seven Sages, ruled Lindus in the sixth century BC.
