Zacynthus is the southernmost of the islands of the [[Cephallenians]].

## Elsewhere in Homer

In the Odyssey it is "wooded Zacynthus" (ὑλήεσσα), named with Dulichium and Same as the islands around Ithaca (Od. 1.246, 9.24), and it sends twenty of Penelope's suitors (Od. 16.250).

## Outside Homer

It is the modern Zakynthos, or Zante. Livy reports that Saguntum in Spain was said to have been founded by settlers from Zacynthus (21.7.2).
