Zacynthus is the southernmost of the islands of the [[Cephallenians]].

{{notes}}

## Elsewhere in Homer

In the Odyssey it is "wooded Zacynthus" (ὑλήεσσα, *Odyssey* 9.24; ὑλήεντι, *Odyssey* 1.246; see [[wooded]]), named with Dulichium and Same as the islands around Ithaca (*Odyssey* 1.246, 9.24), and it sends twenty of Penelope's suitors (*Odyssey* 16.250).

## Outside Homer

It is the modern Zakynthos, or Zante. Strabo describes it as lying a little farther west than Cephallenia, closer to the Peloponnese, a hundred and sixty stades round and sixty from Cephallenia, wooded but fruitful, with a notable city of the same name (*Geography* 10.2.18). Livy reports that Saguntum in Spain was said to have been founded by settlers from Zacynthus (Livy 21.7.2).
