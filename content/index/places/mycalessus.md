εὐρύχορος "with wide dancing floors" ([[with-wide-dancing-floors]]) = εὐρύς (wide) + χορός (dancing place). Mycalessus lay in eastern Boeotia, near Tanagra, on the road from Thebes to Chalcis. In the Homeric Hymn to Apollo the god passes through it in search of a place for his oracle (224).

## Outside Homer

In 413 BC Thracian mercenaries whom Athens was sending home fell on Mycalessus at dawn and killed everyone they found, even the boys in the town's largest school. Thucydides judged it a disaster as great as any that befell a whole city (7.29–30).
