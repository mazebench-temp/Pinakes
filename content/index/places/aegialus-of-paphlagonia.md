αἰγιαλός is the ordinary Greek word for a beach. Strabo describes a beach of this name on the Paphlagonian coast, more than a hundred stades long, with a village called after it (*Geography*, Book 12). The Achaean catalogue has another Aegialus, the coast of the northern Peloponnese in Agamemnon's realm ([[aegialus]], 2.575).
