This is a Boeotian town, not the Argive Midea whose Mycenaean citadel stands near Dendra in the Argolid. It is named with Arne, Nisa and Anthedon in the last lines of the Boeotian list. Its site is unknown; some ancient writers believed that, like [[Arne]], it had been swallowed by Lake Copais.
