Around 300 BC Amastris, a niece of the Persian king Darius III and wife of Dionysius, tyrant of Heraclea, joined Sesamus with Cytorus, Cromna and Tieium to found the city named after her; Sesamus became its citadel (Strabo, *Geography*, Book 12). The site is modern Amasra, on the Turkish Black Sea coast.
