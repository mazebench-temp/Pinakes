The name is the common noun ἕλος, "marsh". This Helos belongs to Nestor's kingdom; its site is unknown, and ancient scholars placed it near the Alpheus. The Helos of Lacedaemon, "the citadel by the sea", is ten lines earlier (2.584; [[helos]]).
