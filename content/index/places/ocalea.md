Ocalea lay between Haliartus and Alalcomenae, near the southern shore of Lake Copais. The Homeric Hymn to Apollo calls it "Ocalea of the many towers" and names it just before "grassy Haliartus" (242–243), as the god travels through Boeotia. The Catalogue pairs it with [[Medeon]]; its exact site has not been found.
