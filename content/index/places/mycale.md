αἰπεινὰ κάρηνα, "steep peaks" ([[steep]]). Mycale is the promontory opposite Samos, across a strait less than a mile and a half wide. The Ionians later held their common festival, the Panionia, at the Panionion on its northern slope (Herodotus 1.148). There in 479 BC the Greek fleet landed and destroyed the Persian army and the beached Persian ships, on the same day, it was said, as the battle of Plataea (Herodotus 9.90–106).
