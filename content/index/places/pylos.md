{{quote:b31ec9d9-8c86-58ba-a7ca-97fb0f9eb0f4}}

τηλόθεν ἐξ ἀπίης γαίης: "far away, from a distant land". The Lapiths summoned Nestor all the way from Pylos, which is the measure of his reputation.

## Outside Homer

A Mycenaean palace at Ano Englianos, excavated by Carl Blegen from 1939, is known as the Palace of Nestor. Its archive of Linear B tablets was among the first used to decipher the script in 1952.
