ὄρος αἰπύ "the steep mountain" ([[steep]]). Cyllene, the modern Kyllini, is the second-highest mountain of the Peloponnese, rising to about 2,370 meters over northeastern Arcadia. The Catalogue uses it to mark where the [[Arcadians]] live.

## Elsewhere in Homer

Hermes is "Cyllenian" (Κυλλήνιος) when he leads the souls of the suitors to the underworld (Od. 24.1). Otus "of Cyllene", a leader of the Epeians killed by Polydamas (15.518–519), probably came from Cyllene, the harbor town of Elis.

## Outside Homer

The Homeric Hymn to Hermes begins with his birth to Maia in a cave on Cyllene (1–7).
