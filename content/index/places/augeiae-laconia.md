ἐρατεινός "lovely" ([[lovely]]). The same name, with the same epithet, has already been given to a town of the Locrians (2.532; [[augeiae]]). This one belongs to Menelaus' [[Lacedaemon]]. It probably lay near Gytheum, on the Laconian Gulf, where a later town called Aegiae stood; the identification is uncertain.
