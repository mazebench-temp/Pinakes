Μινυήϊος means “Minyan”, like Minyan Orchomenus in Boeotia (Ὀρχομενὸν Μινύειον, 2.511), and the river is named only here. Nestor brings it in, as he brought in Thryoessa, with ἔστι δέ τις, “there is a …” (11.711, 11.722; see [[thryum]]). By it, near Arene, the Pylian horsemen waited for dawn while the foot soldiers streamed in, and from there they reached the Alpheus at midday (see [[arene]], [[alpheus]] and [[nestor-and-the-epeians]]).

## Outside Homer

Strabo reports that Arene, which could be found nowhere for certain, was placed most often near Samicum in Triphylia, “where the nearby river Anigrus, formerly called Minyeios, gives no small sign” (*Geography* 8.3.19; see [[strabo]]). He derives the name from Minyans, either those who came from Minyan Orchomenus with Chloris, Nestor's mother (compare *Odyssey* 11.281–286), or Minyans who were driven from Lemnos by way of Lacedaemon and settled around Arene (8.3.19).
