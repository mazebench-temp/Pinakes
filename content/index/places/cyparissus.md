The name means "cypress" (κυπάρισσος). It is the first of the towns of the [[Phocians]], named before rocky Pytho. Cyparissus lay near [[Pytho]] on the slopes of Parnassus, but later writers could not agree where, and the site has not been found. It is not [[Cyparisseis]] in Nestor's kingdom (2.593).
