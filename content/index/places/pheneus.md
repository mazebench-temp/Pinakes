Pheneus lay below Cyllene, in a high basin with no outlet to the sea; its water drains through sinkholes in the limestone, and when these are blocked the plain becomes a lake. It is the first of the Arcadian towns in the Catalogue.

## Outside Homer

Catullus recalls the story that Heracles drained the plain of Pheneus by cutting through the mountain (68.109–112), and Pausanias saw the channels said to be his work (8.14.1–3).
