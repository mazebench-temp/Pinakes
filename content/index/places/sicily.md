## The place

Greek colonies were founded in Sicily from the 730s BCE: Naxos, Syracuse, Megara Hyblaea, Gela, Acragas. Carthage held the west of the island. Under the tyrants Gelon and Hieron Syracuse became the greatest Greek city of the West, a patron of poets: Aeschylus, Pindar, Simonides and Bacchylides all went there. Homer mentions Sicily only in the Odyssey, as Σικανίη and as the land of the Σικελοί (*Odyssey* 24.307, 20.383).

## In the Poetics

The Dorians of Sicily claim comedy, “for the poet Epicharmus came from there” (1448a31–34; see [[epicharmus]] and [[dorians]]), and “the making of plots came originally from Sicily” (1449b5–7; see [[the-origins-of-comedy]]). The battle against the Carthaginians in Sicily, fought at the same time as Salamis, illustrates events joined by time but not by purpose (1459a24–27; see [[salamis-and-himera]] and [[carthaginians]]).
