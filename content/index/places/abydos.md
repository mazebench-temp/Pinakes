In the Iliad Abydos is a place of horses and of friends of Priam's house: Priam's bastard son Democoon came to the war from his mares there (4.499–500), and Phaenops, son of Asius, the dearest of Hector's guest-friends, had his home there (17.584). In historical times it was a colony of Miletus. At Abydos Xerxes sat on a throne of white stone built for him on a hill, viewed his army and his ships, and watched them race (Herodotus 7.44), and in the story of Hero and Leander it is Leander's home (see [[sestus]]).
