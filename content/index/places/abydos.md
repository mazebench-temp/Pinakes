In the Iliad Abydos is a place of horses and of friends of Priam's house. Apollo takes the form of Phaenops son of Asius, "who lived at Abydos", the dearest of Hector's guest-friends (17.582–584). In historical times it was a colony of Miletus. Xerxes watched his army cross the strait from a throne of white stone set up at Abydos (Herodotus 7.44), and in the story of Hero and Leander it is Leander's home (see [[sestus]]).
