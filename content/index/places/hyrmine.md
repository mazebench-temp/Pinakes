Hyrmine is the first of the four places that "enclose" the land of the Epeians (2.616–617). It lay on the coast of Elis, near the promontory later called Chelonatas.

## Outside Homer

Pausanias says that it was founded by Actor, father of the twin Epeian heroes, and named after his mother Hyrmine (5.1.11); in his time it had long been deserted.
