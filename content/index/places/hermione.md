"Hermione and Asine, which lie on the deep gulf" (βαθὺν κατὰ κόλπον ἐχούσας): the Catalogue gives the two towns a single description. Hermione lay at the southern end of the Argolic peninsula, facing the islands of Hydra and Dokos. It belongs to [[Diomedes]]' contingent.

## Outside Homer

Hermione had a famous sanctuary of Demeter Chthonia, and a chasm nearby was said to lead to the underworld (Pausanias 2.35). The town shares its name with Hermione, daughter of Menelaus and Helen (Od. 4.14).
