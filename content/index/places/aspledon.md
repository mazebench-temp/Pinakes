Aspledon is named with Minyan [[Orchomenus]], and the two towns are listed apart from the [[Boeotians]], with their own leaders, the sons of Ares, and thirty ships (2.511–516). It lay near Orchomenus at the edge of the Copaic basin; its site is uncertain, and it was abandoned in later times.
