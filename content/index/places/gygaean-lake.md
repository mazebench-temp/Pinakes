The lake "bore" Mesthles and Antiphus to Talaemenes: their mother was the nymph of the lake. Rivers and their nymphs are often parents and ancestors in the Iliad. The Paeonian Asteropaeus, for one, is the grandson of the river Axius (21.141–143). The lake returns in Book 20 as the ancestral domain of Iphition, whom Achilles kills (20.390–392). Herodotus places "the lake called Gygaean" beside the tomb of Alyattes near Sardis (1.93). The name recalls Gyges, the first king of the Mermnad dynasty of Lydia (Herodotus 1.8–14); whether the lake was named after the king or the king's family after the lake is not known. It is the modern Marmara Gölü.
