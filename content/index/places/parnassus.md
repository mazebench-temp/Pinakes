## The place

Parnassus rises above the sanctuary of Apollo at Delphi (see [[pytho]]). In the Odyssey Odysseus, visiting his grandfather Autolycus, hunts on Parnassus with Autolycus' sons and is gashed above the knee by a boar (*Odyssey* 19.428–466; see [[the-boar-hunt-on-parnassus]] and [[the-scar-of-odysseus]]). Later poets made it the mountain of Apollo and the Muses.

## In the Poetics

Homer did not put into the Odyssey everything that happened to Odysseus, “for example his being wounded on Parnassus and his feigning madness at the mustering” (1451a24–27; see [[unity-of-action]]). The wound is narrated in the Odyssey, but as a digression at the moment of recognition, not as part of the action.
