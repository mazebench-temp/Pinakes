παραὶ Βοιβηΐδα λίμνην: "beside the lake of Boebeis". The name is an adjective, "of Boebe", from the town on its shore. The lake lay in the plain below Mount Pelion and Mount Ossa. Pindar places Coronis, the mother of Asclepius, "by the banks of Boebias" (*Pythian* 3.34). The lake, later called Karla, was drained in the 1960s, and part of it has since been restored.
