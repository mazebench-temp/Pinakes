ζάθεος = ζα- (very) + θεῖος (divine): holy. Cilla is named only in Chryses' prayer formula, at 1.38 and again at 1.452. Strabo places it near Thebe and Adramyttium, with a temple of Apollo Cillaeus (*Geography* 13.1.62–63).
