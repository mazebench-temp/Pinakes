ἐρατεινός "lovely" ([[lovely]]). Mantinea lay in the plain of eastern Arcadia, north of Tegea, its neighbor and rival. It is one of the towns of the [[Arcadians]].

## Outside Homer

In 418 BC the Spartans defeated the Argives, the Athenians and their allies near Mantinea (Thucydides 5.64–74). In 362 BC the Thebans won a second battle there, but their leader Epaminondas was killed, and Xenophon ends his *Hellenica* with it (7.5). In Plato's *Symposium* Socrates learns the nature of love from Diotima, a woman of Mantinea (201d).
