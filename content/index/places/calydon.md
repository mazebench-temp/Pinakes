## Elsewhere in Homer

Calydon was the home of Oeneus and his sons. One of them, Tydeus, left it and after wandering settled in Argos, where he married a daughter of Adrastus; Diomedes is their son (14.119–125). It is also the scene of Phoenix's story of [[Meleager]], besieged by the Curetes while he keeps out of the fighting (9.529–599; see [[the-wrath-of-meleager]]).

## Outside Homer

The hunt of the Calydonian boar, with its gathering of heroes, became one of the favorite myths of poets and artists (Apollodorus 1.8.2–3; Ovid, *Metamorphoses* 8.260–444). The city lay on a ridge above the plain of the Evenus. Its sanctuary of Artemis Laphria has been excavated, by Danish archaeologists from 1926 and by a Greek and Danish team in this century. Augustus moved the cult image of Artemis Laphria from Calydon to his new foundation at Patrae (Pausanias 7.18.8–9).
