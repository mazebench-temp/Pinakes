Eutresis lay in southern Boeotia, on the road between Thespiae and Plataea, below Mount Helicon.

## Outside Homer

Hetty Goldman excavated the site in 1924–1927 and found a settlement occupied from the Neolithic period to the end of the Mycenaean age, with large houses of the Early Bronze Age.
