τῆλ᾽ ἐξ Ἀσκανίης, "from far-off Ascania". The name survived in Lake Ascania, the modern İznik Gölü, on whose shore Nicaea was later built. Ancient geographers also knew an Ascanian lake in Mysia and disputed which Ascania Homer meant. In Book 13 Ascania is "deep-soiled" (ἐριβῶλαξ), the home of allies who came late to the war (13.792–794).
