Later called Gyrton. It lay on the Peneius north of Larisa, on the way to the gorge of Tempe. Apollonius makes it the home of [[Coronus]], the father of Leonteus, when he lists him among the Argonauts (*Argonautica* 1.57–58). Its men came to Troy with the other Lapiths, under Polypoetes and Leonteus.
