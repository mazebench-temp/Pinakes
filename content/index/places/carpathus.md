Κράπαθος is Homer's form, with the ρ moved (metathesis); later Greek has Κάρπαθος. The sea between Crete and Rhodes took its name from the island, the Carpathian Sea. With [[Nisyrus]], [[Casus]], [[Cos]] and the [[Calydnae]] it sent thirty ships under the grandsons of Heracles (2.676–680).
