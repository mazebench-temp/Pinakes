Homer names Cretan Miletus only here. It is not the Carian [[miletus|Miletus]] of the Trojan Catalogue (2.868), but later writers linked the two cities. Ephorus, as Strabo reports him, said that Sarpedon, the brother of Minos, brought settlers from Cretan Miletus to found the Carian city (*Geography* 14.1.6). In Apollodorus the founder is Miletus, a son of Apollo, who fled from Minos to Caria (3.1.2). The Cretan town is usually placed near the village of Milatos, on the north coast east of Malia.
