Cytorus was one of the towns joined around 300 BC to form the city of Amastris (Strabo, *Geography*, Book 12). The mountain behind it was covered with box trees. Catullus calls it *Cytore buxifer*, "box-bearing Cytorus" (4.13), and Virgil speaks of "Cytorus waving with box" (*Georgics* 2.437).
