εἰνοσίφυλλος "with quivering leaves" ([[with-quivering-leaves]]). In the Catalogue the Cephallenians "held Ithaca and Neritum", as if the mountain were a town. The Odyssey names it twice as Ithaca's landmark: Odysseus' island has "a mountain in it, Neritum with its quivering leaves, conspicuous" (Od. 9.21–22), and when Athena reveals Ithaca to him, she points to "Neritum, the mountain clothed in forest" (Od. 13.351). It is usually identified with Mount Niritos in the north of Ithaki.
