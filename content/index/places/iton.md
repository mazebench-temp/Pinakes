μητέρα μήλων, "mother of flocks": good sheep country. In the same way Homer calls Mount Ida "mother of wild beasts" (8.47). The goddess of Iton, Athena Itonia, became a chief goddess of the Thessalians, and her cult spread to Boeotia, where she had a sanctuary near Coronea (Pausanias 9.34.1).
