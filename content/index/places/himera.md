Himera was founded from Zancle in the seventh century BCE (Thucydides 6.5.1). There Gelon and Theron defeated the Carthaginian army of Hamilcar, on the same day, the Sicilians said, as the battle of Salamis (Herodotus 7.165–167; see [[salamis-and-himera]] and [[gelon]]).
