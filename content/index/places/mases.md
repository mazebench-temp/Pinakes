Mases lay on the coast of the southern Argolid, near Hermione. It was never more than a small place, and its exact site is uncertain. The line gives Mases and Aegina to "the young men of the Achaeans" (κοῦροι Ἀχαιῶν). It is the last of the nine towns of [[Diomedes]]' contingent.
