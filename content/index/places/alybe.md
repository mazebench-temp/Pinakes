ὅθεν ἀργύρου ἐστὶ γενέθλη, "where there is the birth of silver": a place of silver mines. Alybe is one of the few places in the Catalogue named for what it produces, like the land of the Eneti with its wild mules (2.852). Some ancient scholars read the name as Chalybe, making it the land of the Chalybes of Pontus, the ironworkers of later legend. The site remains unknown. See [[gold-and-silver]].
