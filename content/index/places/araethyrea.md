ἐρατεινός "lovely" ([[lovely]]). Araethyrea belongs to [[Agamemnon]]'s realm.

## Outside Homer

Pausanias says that Araethyrea was the old name of the land later called Phliasia, whose city was Phlius, in the hills between Sicyon and Argos; it was named, he says, after a daughter of its first king Aras, and quotes the Catalogue's line (2.12.4–5).
