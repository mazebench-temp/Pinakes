πολύκνημος "of the many ridges" = πολύς (many) + κνημός (a spur or shoulder of a mountain, as in "the spurs of Ida", Ἴδης ἐν κνημοῖσι, 2.821). The epithet occurs only here in Homer. Eteonus lay in the hills of southern Boeotia; where exactly is not known.
