Ancient writers placed Graea near Tanagra or on the coast at Oropus, facing Euboea; neither placing is certain.

## Outside Homer

The Romans called the Greeks *Graeci*. Aristotle says that the people around Dodona now called Hellenes were once called Graikoi (*Meteorology* 1.14, 352b1–2). Others have looked to the Graioi of eastern Boeotia, some of whom may have joined the Euboean settlers of Cumae in Italy. Neither explanation can be proved.
