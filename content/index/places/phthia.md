{{quote:5fcc8156-0532-5730-a631-02ea2eabc6cd}}

ἐριβῶλαξ = ἐρι- (very) + βῶλαξ (clod of earth): deep-soiled. βωτιάνειρα = βόσκω (feed) + ἀνήρ (man): nurse of men. Achilles' point is the distance: Phthia is far from Troy, beyond "shadowy mountains and the echoing sea".

{{quote:fc70876d-1cf9-5130-a06e-ea30786fc9ad}}

Phthia is where he will go. He threatens it here, repeats it in Book 9 (9.356–363), and never does it.
