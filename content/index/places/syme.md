Σύμηθεν: "from Syme", with the suffix -θεν ("from"). Syme lies between Rhodes and the Cnidian peninsula of Asia Minor. Its three ships are the smallest contingent in the Catalogue, and its leader, [[Nireus]], is the most handsome of the Achaeans after Achilles but has the fewest men. Homer names Syme only here.
