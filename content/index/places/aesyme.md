title: Aesyme
greek: Αἰσύμη

tags: [city]
summary: The home of Castianeira, whom Priam brought to Troy as a bride and who bore him Gorgythion (8.304). Homer says nothing more of it; ancient commentators placed it in Thrace.
aliases: [Aisyme]

τόν ῥʼ ἐξ Αἰσύμηθεν ὀπυιομένη τέκε μήτηρ, “whom his mother, a bride brought from Aesyme, had borne” (8.304; see [[castianeira]] and [[gorgythion]]). Αἰσύμηθεν is the name with the ending -θεν, “from”, strengthened by ἐξ, as in ἐξ οὐρανόθεν, “from the sky” (8.19, 8.21). The word occurs only here in Homer, and the poem gives no hint of where the place lay.

Ancient commentators placed Aesyme in Thrace. Laothoe, another of Priam's wives and the mother of Lycaon, also came to him from another city: her father Altes ruled the Leleges at Pedasus on the Satnioeis (21.85–88; see [[pedasus-on-the-satnioeis]]).
