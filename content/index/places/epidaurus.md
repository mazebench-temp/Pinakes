ἀμπελόεις "vine-rich" = ἄμπελος (vine) + -όεις (full of). Homer gives the same epithet to Phrygia (3.184) and to Pedasus (9.152). Epidaurus lay on the Saronic Gulf, on the eastern coast of the Argolic peninsula, and belongs to [[Diomedes]]' contingent.

## Outside Homer

Its sanctuary of Asclepius, some miles inland, became the most famous healing sanctuary in Greece in the fifth and fourth centuries BC; its theater of the fourth century BC is still in use. An older sanctuary of Apollo Maleatas on the hill above it goes back to the Mycenaean period.
