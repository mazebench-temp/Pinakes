In the Odyssey, Eumelus lives at Pherae with his wife Iphthime, the sister of Penelope (*Odyssey* 4.797–798; see [[eumelus]] and [[iphthime]]). Diocles' Pherae, where Telemachus spends a night on his way to Sparta and again on his way home, is another town, in Messenia (Φηραί, *Odyssey* 3.488, 15.186; see [[pherae-in-messenia]]). Later legend made it the home of Admetus and Alcestis, and Euripides' *Alcestis* is set there. In the fourth century BC its rulers, above all Jason of Pherae, briefly made it the strongest power in Thessaly.
