ἐϋκτίμεναι "well-built" ([[well-built]]). Cleonae lay in a valley on the road between Corinth and Argos, and belongs to [[Agamemnon]]'s realm.

## Outside Homer

Nemea, with its games, lay in the territory of Cleonae. Pindar tells how Heracles lay in ambush "below Cleonae" and killed the Moliones, the twin sons of Actor, [[Cteatus]] and [[eurytus-of-elis|Eurytus]] (*Olympian* 10.26–34).
