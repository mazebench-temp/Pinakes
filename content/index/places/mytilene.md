Mytilene revolted from Athens in 428 BCE, and the Athenians, having voted to kill all its men, sent a second ship the next day to cancel the order (Thucydides 3.36–49). See [[lesbos]], [[sappho]], [[alcaeus]] and [[pittacus]].
