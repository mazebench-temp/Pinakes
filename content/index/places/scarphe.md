Scarphe lay near the coast of the Malian Gulf, not far east of Thermopylae; the later town was called Scarpheia. It stood close to the pass, on the road from central Greece into Thessaly. In 146 BC the Roman commander Metellus defeated the army of the Achaean League near it.
