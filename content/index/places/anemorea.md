The name suggests a windy height (ἄνεμος, "wind"). Anemorea lay somewhere on the slopes of Parnassus, near Delphi; no remains have been securely identified with it. It is one of the towns of the [[Phocians]], paired in its line with [[Hyampolis]], which lay far to the east; the Catalogue's pairs of names do not always go together on the map.
