αἰπεινός "steep" ([[steep]]). Gonoessa lay on a height near the coast of the Gulf of Corinth, between Aegeira and Pellene, and belongs to [[Agamemnon]]'s realm.

## Outside Homer

Pausanias identifies it with Donussa, a town destroyed by the Sicyonians. He reports the view that Homer had called it Donussa, and that when Peisistratus collected the scattered poems of Homer, he or one of his associates changed the name to Gonoessa through ignorance (7.26.13). It is one of the ancient testimonies to a Peisistratean edition of Homer (see [[homer]]).
