Τιτάνοιό τε λευκὰ κάρηνα: "the white peaks of Titanus", white perhaps with limestone rather than with snow. The name recalls the Titans. Homer names the mountain only here, and which height it was is uncertain. The people around it came to Troy under [[Eurypylus]] (2.736).
