Pleuron is the first town of the [[Aetolians]]. It lay in the coastal plain of Aetolia, west of Calydon. Homer names it with Calydon as the seat of Oeneus' family: Portheus' sons "lived in Pleuron and steep Calydon" (14.116), and Thoas ruled "in all Pleuron and steep Calydon" (13.217). Nestor once wrestled and beat Ancaeus "of Pleuron" (23.635).

## Outside Homer

Phrynichus' tragedy *The Women of Pleuron* told the death of Meleager (Pausanias 10.31.4). In the Hellenistic period the city moved to a new site on the slopes of Mount Arakynthos, where its walls still stand.
