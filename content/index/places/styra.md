Styra lay in southern [[Euboea]], on the coast facing Attica; the modern village of Styra keeps its name.

## Outside Homer

The Styrians fought with the Greek fleet at Artemisium and Salamis (Herodotus 8.1, 8.46) and with the army at Plataea (Herodotus 9.28).
