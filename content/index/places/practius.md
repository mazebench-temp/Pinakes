The line does not say what the Practius is. Later geographers knew it as a small river between Abydos and Lampsacus (Strabo, *Geography*, Book 13). It is named nowhere else in Homer. With Percote, Sestus, Abydos and Arisbe it makes up the realm of [[Asius]], the towns on both shores of the Hellespont's narrows.
