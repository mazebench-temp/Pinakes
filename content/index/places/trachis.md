Τρηχίς is the Ionic form of Τραχίς, and the name is the adjective τρηχύς ("rough, rugged"): Trachis lay among the cliffs where Mount Oeta comes down to the Malian Gulf. Sophocles' *Women of Trachis* is set there. Heracles lives at Trachis in exile, and from there he is carried up Oeta to be burned. In 426 BC the Spartans founded a colony nearby, Heraclea in Trachis (Thucydides 3.92).
