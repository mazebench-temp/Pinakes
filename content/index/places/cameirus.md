ἀργινόεντα, "chalk-white" (see [[chalk-white]]), is also the epithet of [[Lycastus]] on Crete (2.647). Cameirus stood on a hillside above the sea. Its Archaic cemeteries have produced a wealth of painted pottery, and the town itself, rebuilt in the Hellenistic period, has been excavated.
