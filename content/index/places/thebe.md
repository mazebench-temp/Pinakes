{{quote:aa442036-5e51-54e0-8580-f2c655049ad7}}

Not the Boeotian Thebes of Oedipus. This Thebe lay near Adramyttium in the southern Troad, "under wooded Placus" (Iliad 6.396–397). It was the home of Andromache, Hector's wife, whose father [[Eetion]] and seven brothers Achilles killed there (6.414–424). See [[sack-of-thebe]].
