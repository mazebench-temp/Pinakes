Olenus is named between Pleuron and Pylene among the towns of the [[Aetolians]]. It lay near Pleuron, but it was abandoned early, and its site is uncertain. It is not Olenus in Achaea, nor the "Olenian Rock" that marks the border of Elis (2.617; [[olenian-rock]]).
