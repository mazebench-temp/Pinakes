πολύμηλος "rich in flocks" ([[rich-in-flocks]]) = πολύς (many) + μῆλα (sheep and goats), apt for a town of upland pastures. Arcadian Orchomenus stood on a hill between two plains in eastern Arcadia, north of Mantinea; its ruins lie near the modern village of Orchomenos. It is not the Minyan [[Orchomenus]] of Boeotia (2.511).
