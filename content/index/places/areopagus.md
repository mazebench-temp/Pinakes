The council of the Areopagus was the oldest court of Athens. Myth told that the hill was named for the trial of Ares, accused of killing Halirrhothius, son of Poseidon (Pausanias 1.28.5), and in Aeschylus' *Eumenides* Athena founds the court there to try Orestes. After the reforms of Ephialtes (462/1 BCE) it kept its jurisdiction in cases of homicide. Its strict procedure was proverbial: speakers before it were held to the point.
