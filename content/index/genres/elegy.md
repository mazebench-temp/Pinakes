## The genre

The elegiac couplet joins a dactylic hexameter to a shorter line of the same rhythm (the “pentameter”). Archilochus, Tyrtaeus, Mimnermus, Solon and Theognis composed in it (see [[mimnermus]] and [[solon-and-salamis]]), and it became the meter of the epigram.
