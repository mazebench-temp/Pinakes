## The genre

Archaic ἴαμβος was a poetry of blame, often obscene, sung or recited at festivals; Archilochus of Paros (seventh century), Semonides of Amorgos and Hipponax of Ephesus are its poets. The meter takes its name from the genre, not the reverse.
