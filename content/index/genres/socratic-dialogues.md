## The genre

After Socrates' death in 399 BCE many of his followers wrote dialogues with him as speaker: Plato, Xenophon, Aeschines of Sphettus, Antisthenes, Phaedo and others. Aristotle's own lost dialogues, like *On Poets*, belonged to the same tradition (see [[on-poets]]).
