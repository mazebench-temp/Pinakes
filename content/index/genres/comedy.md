## The genre

κωμῳδία is “song of the κῶμος”, the band of revelers (see [[komos]]). At Athens comedy was admitted to the City Dionysia in 486 BCE and to the Lenaea about 440. Old Comedy, the comedy of [[Aristophanes]], Cratinus and Eupolis, mocked living men by name and mixed fantasy with politics; by Aristotle's day comedy had turned toward the plots of everyday life that Menander would perfect. Eleven plays of Aristophanes survive, and none of the earlier comic poets whom Aristotle names.
