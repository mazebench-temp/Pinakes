## The genre

μῖμος is an actor and the sketch he performs. Sophron's mimes, in rhythmic Doric prose, were divided into men's and women's mimes; fragments survive, and Theocritus' *Women at the Adonis Festival* (Idyll 15) is said to imitate one. Plato is reported to have kept Sophron's mimes under his pillow (Diogenes Laertius 3.18).
