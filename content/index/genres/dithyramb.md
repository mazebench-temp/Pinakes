## The genre

The dithyramb was sung in honor of Dionysus. Archilochus says he knows how to lead it, “thunderstruck in mind with wine” (fr. 120 West), and Arion of Methymna was said to have first composed and named it at Corinth (Herodotus 1.23). At Athens ten choruses of fifty men or boys, one from each tribe, competed at the City Dionysia. In the late fifth and fourth centuries the “New Dithyramb” of Melanippides, Philoxenus and Timotheus became a vehicle for virtuoso music, mimetic solos and extravagant compound words, which the comic poets ridiculed.
