## The genre

Mock epic is as old as the *Margites* and the *Battle of Frogs and Mice* (see [[margites]]). Hegemon of Thasos was famous for parodies recited at Athens in the late fifth century; Athenaeus tells that the audience laughed so hard at his *Battle of the Giants* that it did not notice the news of the disaster in Sicily (9.406e–407c).
