## The genre

νόμος means “law, custom” and also a melody, a set tune; the nome was a traditional solo composition, sung to the lyre (citharodic) or played on the pipe (auletic), and credited to Terpander. Timotheus' *Persians*, a citharodic nome on the battle of Salamis, survives in a papyrus of the fourth century BCE, the oldest Greek literary papyrus.
