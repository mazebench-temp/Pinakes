## The genre

ἐποποιία is “the making of ἔπη”, of verses, and ἔπη in the plural became the name of hexameter poetry. Greek epic was oral before it was written: the Iliad and the Odyssey (see [[homer]]) stand at the end of a tradition of singers, and around them were the poems of the Epic Cycle, which told the rest of the Trojan war and its sequel (see [[cypria]] and [[little-iliad]]), the Theban epics, and the genealogical and didactic poetry of Hesiod.
