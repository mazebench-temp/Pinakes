## The genre

Praise and blame are the two poles of archaic poetry, and ψόγος is blame: Archilochus' poems against Lycambes and his daughters, Hipponax's against the sculptor Bupalus (see [[iambus]]). In the Iliad Thersites is a speaker of blame, and is beaten for it (see [[thersites]]).
