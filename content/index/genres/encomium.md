## The genre

ἐγκώμιον is a song sung “in the κῶμος”, at the revel that celebrated a victor (see [[komos]]). Simonides and Pindar wrote encomia; in prose the encomium became a rhetorical form, of which Gorgias' *Helen* is a famous example (see [[gorgias-helen]]).
