## The genre

Each tragic poet at the Dionysia ended his set of plays with a satyr play, in which the satyrs, half men and half horses, followers of Dionysus, were set in a heroic story. Euripides' *Cyclops* survives whole, and much of Sophocles' *Trackers* on papyrus.
