Callistratus was among the leading politicians of the Second Athenian League. Plutarch tells how Demosthenes, as a boy, got himself taken to hear Callistratus speak in the trial over the loss of Oropus (366 BCE), and, seeing the crowd escort the victorious speaker home, resolved to master the art (*Demosthenes* 5). Condemned to death in his absence, he went into exile; when he came back to Athens trusting in an oracle, he was executed (Lycurgus, *Against Leocrates* 93).
