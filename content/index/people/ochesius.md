Ὀχησίου ἀγλαὸν υἱόν, “the splendid son of Ochesius” (5.843): the father is named to identify the son, “by far the best of the Aetolians”, whom Ares kills and strips before he turns on Diomedes (5.842–848; see [[periphas]] and [[aetolians]]). Nothing else is said of him.
