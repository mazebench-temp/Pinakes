Pelagon is the first of the five captains named when Agamemnon, on his review of the army, finds Nestor setting his companions in order: Pelagon, [[Alastor]], [[Chromius]], [[Haemon]] and [[Bias]] (4.295–296). The five appear only here, and the Catalogue gives the Pylians no leader but Nestor (2.591–602). μέγας (“great”) may describe his size or his standing. The name recalls πέλαγος (“the open sea”), but its sense cannot be pressed.

Another Pelagon, a Lycian, is the “dear companion” of Sarpedon who pushes the ash spear out of his thigh (5.694–695).
