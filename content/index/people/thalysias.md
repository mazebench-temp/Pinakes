Θαλυσιάδην Ἐχέπωλον, “Echepolus, son of Thalysias” (4.458): Θαλυσιάδης = Θαλυσίας + -άδης (“son of”). The poem says nothing more about him. The name recalls θαλύσια, the offering of the first fruits of the harvest, which Oeneus of Calydon failed to give to Artemis, and so brought the Calydonian boar on his land (9.533–542). See [[echepolus]] and [[antilochus]].
