Ναυβολίδης: "son of Naubolus". The name seems to join ναῦς, "ship", and βάλλω, "throw, launch". Through him the Catalogue traces the Phocian leaders back three generations: Naubolus, Iphitus, and his sons Schedius and Epistrophus. The same patronymic belongs to a young Phaeacian in the Odyssey (Od. 8.116), a different family.
