Νομίονος ἀγλαὰ τέκνα, "the splendid children of Nomion" ([[splendid]]). Nomion is known only as their father. The name looks like a formation from νομός, "pasture", as if "herdsman", but that is only a guess. His sons are named together twice, first as "Amphimachus and Nastes" and then, turned around, as "Nastes and Amphimachus" (2.870–871).
