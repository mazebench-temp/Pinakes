ἴφθιμος Πελάγων, ὅς οἱ φίλος ἦεν ἑταῖρος, “mighty Pelagon, who was his dear companion” (5.695). He is one of the companions (ἑταῖροι) who carry the wounded Sarpedon out of the battle with the spear still dragging in his thigh, for in their haste no one thought to draw it out (5.663–667; see [[hetairos]]). Under the oak of Zeus, Pelagon does it, and Sarpedon faints as the spear comes out, and revives in the North Wind (5.692–698; see [[the-oak-of-zeus]] and [[sarpedon]]). He is named only here.

The first of Nestor's five captains bears the same name (4.295; see [[pelagon]]). It recalls πέλαγος (“the open sea”), but its sense cannot be pressed.
