---
title: Eubulus
greek: Εὔβουλος
kind: person
tags: [statesman, athenian]
summary: Athenian statesman (c. 405–335 BCE), who restored the city's finances in the 350s and 340s through the festival fund and favored peace with Philip; the leading opponent of Demosthenes.
---

After the Social War Eubulus directed Athenian finance; Aeschines says that because of the people's trust in him those elected over the theoric fund, from which payments for the festivals were made to the citizens, came to control most of the city's revenues (3.25). He supported the peace with Philip in 346 BCE, and Demosthenes' speeches against the use of the fund are aimed at his policy.
