After killing Doryclus, Ajax wounds four Trojans in a verse and a half. The verb is repeated across the line-end, ἔπειτα δὲ Πάνδοκον οὖτα, / οὖτα δὲ Λύσανδρον, “and then wounded Pandocus, / and wounded Lysander” (11.490–491), and the names follow one another to the end of the verse (see [[doryclus]], [[lysander]], [[pyrasus-the-trojan]], [[pylartes]] and [[ajax]]). οὖτα, “wounded”, does not say whether he died, and the poem names him nowhere else.
