Alcmaeon came to Phegeus at Psophis in Arcadia to be purified of his mother's blood and married his daughter, Alphesiboea in Pausanias, Arsinoe in Apollodorus; when he left her for Callirhoe, her brothers killed him (Pausanias 8.24.8–10; Apollodorus 3.7.5). See [[alcmaeon]] and [[alcmaeon-theodectes]].
