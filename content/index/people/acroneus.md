## The name

Almost all the Phaeacian names, W. W. Merry notes, “are taken from circumstances of seafaring life” (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on *Odyssey* 8.111). Ἀκρόνεως seems to join ἄκρος, “topmost”, with the word for “ship”, νηῦς, which also ends the names of Anabesineus and Polyneus in the same list (*Odyssey* 8.113–114; see [[anabesineus]] and [[polyneus]]).
