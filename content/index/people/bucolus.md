Βουκολίδαο, “son of Bucolus” (15.338), is the patronymic of Sphelus, the father of Iasus, a leader of the Athenians whom Aeneas kills (15.332, 15.337–338; see [[sphelus]], [[iasus]] and [[athenians]]). Bucolus is known only from this word. His name is the common noun βουκόλος, “herdsman”, which the same book uses later in a simile, of the herdsman a wild beast kills beside his cattle (15.587; see [[the-beast-that-has-done-harm]]). Among the Trojans, Bucolion, the father of Aesepus and Pedasus, was the eldest son of Laomedon (6.21–24; see [[bucolion]]).
