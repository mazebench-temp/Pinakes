The *Telegony*, last poem of the Epic Cycle, told how Telegonus, sailing in search of his father, landed on Ithaca and killed Odysseus without knowing him, then took his body, with Penelope and Telemachus, to Circe; Telegonus married Penelope, and Telemachus Circe (Proclus, *Chrestomathy*; see [[telegonus-kills-odysseus]]). Aristotle cites “Telegonus in Odysseus Wounded” as a terrible deed done in ignorance within the play (1453b33–34; see [[odysseus-wounded]]).
