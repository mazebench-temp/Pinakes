She has a single line, in the verses that give Menesthius his parents as he dies: “whom the mace-man / Areithous fathered, and ox-eyed Phylomedusa bore” (7.9–10; see [[menesthius]] and [[areithous]]). βοῶπις, “ox-eyed”, is the standing epithet of Hera; among mortal women the Iliad gives it only to Helen's handmaid Clymene (3.144) and to Phylomedusa, and among the Nereids to Halie (18.40; see [[ox-eyed]]). The name joins φῦλον (“tribe, people”) and μέδουσα (“ruling”, as in the name Medusa). Nothing else is known of her.
