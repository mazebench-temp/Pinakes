ἰητῆρʼ ἀγαθώ, "good healers" (a dual): the two brothers are the army's doctors. Podalirius is named only once more in the Iliad. When the wounded Eurypylus asks Patroclus for help, he explains that of the two healers Machaon lies wounded in the huts, and Podalirius is out on the plain fighting the Trojans (11.833–836).

An early epic fragment, attributed to Arctinus' *Sack of Ilion*, divides the brothers' gifts: Machaon's hands were skilled at cutting out missiles and healing wounds, while Podalirius could recognize what was hidden and cure what seemed past cure. He was the first to see the signs of Ajax's madness.
