“Then Erymas and Amphoterus and Epaltes” (16.415): Amphoterus is the second of the nine men whom Patroclus kills one after another, with no blow told, after Pronous, Thestor and Erylaus (16.399–418; see [[erymas-killed-by-patroclus]] and [[patroclus]]).

The name is the adjective ἀμφότερος, “both”, with the accent moved to the last syllable; so the name Θόας differs from θοάς, “swift”, only in its accent (see [[thoas]]). Amphoterus is named only here.
