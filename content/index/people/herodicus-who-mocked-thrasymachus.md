---
title: Herodicus, who mocked Thrasymachus
greek: Ἡρόδικος
kind: person
tags: [sophist]
summary: A Herodicus who made jokes of the names of Thrasymachus, Polus and Draco. Who he was is not known; he is not Herodicus of Selymbria, the trainer, whom Aristotle names in Book I.
---

Plato's Gorgias had a brother Herodicus, a physician, with whom he visited the sick (*Gorgias* 448b, 456b), and a man of Gorgias' circle might well mock Gorgias' pupil Polus and the rival Thrasymachus. But nothing beyond the name connects him with Aristotle's Herodicus. See [[herodicus]], [[thrasymachus]], [[polus]] and [[draco]].
