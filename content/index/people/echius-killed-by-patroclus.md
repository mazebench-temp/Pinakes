Ἐχίον τε, “and Echius” (16.416), is the fifth name in the list of nine men whom Patroclus kills one after another, with no blow told (16.415–418; see [[erymas-killed-by-patroclus]] and [[patroclus]]).

The Iliad has three men of the name. Echius is the father of Mecisteus, who carries the wounded Teucer and Hypsenor to the ships (8.333, 13.422; see [[echius]] and [[mecisteus-son-of-echius]]); Polites, son of Priam, kills an Achaean Echius in the verse in which Polydamas kills Mecisteus (15.339; see [[echius-killed-by-polites]]); and this Echius dies on the Trojan side.
