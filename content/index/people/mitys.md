Nothing more is known of Mitys than the story of his statue, which Plutarch also tells: Mitys was killed in a civil conflict, and later, at a festival, his bronze statue fell on his killer and killed him (*On the Delays of Divine Vengeance* 553d; see [[the-statue-of-mitys]] and [[argos]]).
