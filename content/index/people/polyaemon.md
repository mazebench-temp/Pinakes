καὶ Πολυαιμονίδην Ἀμοπάονα, “and Amopaon, son of Polyaemon” (8.276), is the seventh of the eight men whom Teucer shoots from under the shield of Ajax (see [[amopaon]] and [[teucer]]). The father is known only from this patronymic.
