Leitus is named second among the Boeotian commanders. The Catalogue gives only his name; his father appears in Book 17, where he is "the son of great-hearted Alectryon" (17.602).

## Elsewhere in Homer

He kills Phylacus in the first day's fighting (6.35). In the fight over Patroclus' body Hector strikes him on the wrist, and he leaves the battle, no longer hoping to hold a spear against the Trojans (17.601–604).

## Outside Homer

Apollodorus lists him among the Argonauts and the suitors of Helen (1.9.16, 3.10.8).
