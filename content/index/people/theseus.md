Αἰγεΐδης: "son of Aegeus". ἐπιείκελος ἀθανάτοισιν: "like the immortals".

## The line

1.265 is missing from most of the medieval manuscripts, and it is identical to line 182 of the Hesiodic *Shield of Heracles*. Many editors think it was added to give Athens a hero in the Iliad; the Monro–Allen text prints it. See [[theseus-line]].

## Outside Homer

Theseus killed the Minotaur with Ariadne's help (Odyssey 11.321–325 names Ariadne "whom Theseus once was carrying from Crete to Athens"). In later tradition he was the friend of Peirithous and fought beside the Lapiths against the Centaurs.
