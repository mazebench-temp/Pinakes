Phalces is the first of six names in the list of men around Cebriones and Polydamas, where Hector and Paris go to the thickest of the fighting (13.789–792; see [[hector]], [[cebriones]] and [[polydamas]]). The relative clause that follows says that men of the list came from Ascania “as relief” the morning before; whether it takes in Phalces, Orthaeus and Polyphetes, or only Palmys, Ascanius and Morys, the poem does not make clear (see [[ascanius-the-mysian]] and [[ascania]]). In the rout of Book 14, when Poseidon has turned the battle, Antilochus kills a Phalces and Mermerus, and Meriones kills Morys and Hippotion (14.513–514; see [[antilochus]] and [[morys]]). No other Phalces appears in Homer.
