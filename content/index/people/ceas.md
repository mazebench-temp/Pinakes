Κεάδαο = Κεάδης, "son of Ceas", in the old genitive in -αο. Three generations, Ceas, Troezenus and Euphemus, make one of the two longest pedigrees in the Trojan catalogue; the other is that of the Pelasgian leaders, sons of Lethus son of Teutamus (2.843).
