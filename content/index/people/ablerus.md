The verb is ἐνήρατο, “slew”, from ἐναίρω, a verb formed on ἔναρα (“spoils”) that in Homer means simply “kill” (see [[stripping-the-dead]]). Ablerus is given nothing but his name, which occurs only here. Antilochus' first victim, Echepolus, had a father and a helmet described (4.457–462; see [[echepolus]]); in Book 6 the list moves too fast for either.
