τοὺς μὲν ὅ γʼ Ἀστυνόῳ Προτιάονος υἱέϊ δῶκε, “He gave them to Astynous, son of Protiaon” (15.455). When Teucer's arrow throws Cleitus from Polydamas' chariot and the horses bolt, Polydamas reaches them first, gives them to Astynous, and “urged him strongly to keep them close, with his eyes on him” (15.456), before going back among the champions (15.453–457; see [[polydamas]], [[cleitus]] and [[protiaon]]). He is named only here. The name joins ἄστυ, “town”, and νόος, “mind”, and is also that of the Trojan whom Diomedes kills with Hypeiron (5.144; see [[astynous]]).
