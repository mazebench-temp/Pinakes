When the Heraclidae conquered the Peloponnese, Temenus, Cresphontes and the sons of Aristodemus drew lots for Argos, Lacedaemon and Messene; Cresphontes, wanting Messene, threw a clod of earth into the water pitcher, so that it dissolved and his brothers' lots came out first (Apollodorus 2.8.4). He was killed in a revolt (see [[the-return-of-the-heraclidae]], [[merope]] and [[cresphontes-play]]).
