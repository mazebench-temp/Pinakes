## Outside Homer

Homer names him only here and in 2.746. Later writers tell that no weapon could wound him, so the Centaurs beat him down into the earth with pine trunks and he vanished alive (Apollonius, *Argonautica* 1.57–64). Ovid adds that he was born a girl, Caenis, and changed into an invulnerable man by Poseidon (*Metamorphoses* 12.189–209, 12.459–535).
