---

Athens recovered Samos from the Persians in 365 BCE, when Timotheus took the island after a siege (Isocrates, *Antidosis* 111), and sent citizens to settle it, a cleruchy that drove out many of the Samians (see [[samos-ionia]] and [[samians]]). Cydias is known only from Aristotle's report of his speech.
