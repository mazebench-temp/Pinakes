Τρῆχόν τʼ αἰχμητὴν Αἰτώλιον, “Trechus, the Aetolian spearman” (5.706; see [[spearmen]]). The Aetolians came to Troy under Thoas (2.638–644; see [[aetolians]]), and Trechus is the first of them to die in the poem. Later in the book Ares himself kills Periphas, “by far the best of the Aetolians” (5.842–843; see [[periphas]]). The name looks like τρηχύς (“rough, rugged”). He is named only here, and the poem does not say whether Hector or Ares killed him (see [[hector]] and [[ares]]).
