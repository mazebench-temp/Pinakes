Ταλαϊονίδης = Ταλαός + -ιονίδης, "son of Talaus". He is the grandfather of [[Euryalus]], so the Argive entry of the Catalogue reaches back three generations.

## Outside Homer

In later genealogies Talaus was the son of Bias and of Pero, daughter of [[Neleus]] and sister of Nestor, and the father of Adrastus, of Mecisteus and of Eriphyle, the wife of Amphiaraus (Apollodorus 1.9.13).
