{{quote:eefc86e8-e679-525e-8fa3-b48fc30dcb7b}}

Φάλκην δʼ Ἀντίλοχος καὶ Μέρμερον ἐξενάριξε, “and Antilochus slew and stripped Phalces and Mermerus” (14.513; see [[antilochus]], [[phalces]] and [[stripping-the-dead]]). He is named only here. The name is the adjective μέρμερος, “grim, baneful”, which the Iliad uses of the deeds of war, μέρμερα ἔργα (8.453, 10.289, 10.524; see [[grim-deeds]]). In the Odyssey another Mermerus is the father of Ilus of Ephyra (Μερμερίδαο, *Odyssey* 1.259).
