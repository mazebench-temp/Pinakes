**One of ten.** Hector, in Achilles' armor, urges on the leaders of the allies, “and Asteropaeus and Deisenor and Hippothous” among them (17.217; see [[hector]] and [[trojan-allies]]). Walter Leaf notes that these names are mostly taken from the Catalogue (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 17.216); Deisenor is not in it, and the poem gives him no father, people or deed. The name, found nowhere else in Homer, ends in -ήνωρ, from ἀνήρ, “man”, as do Agenor and Hyperenor (see [[agenor]] and [[hyperenor]]).
