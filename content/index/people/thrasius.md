**The fifth of seven.** The Paeonians who had come to Troy with Asteropaeus are still fleeing along the river when Achilles, who has just killed their leader, comes after them (21.205–208; see [[asteropaeus]], [[paeonians]] and [[achilles]]). Θρασίον τε, “and Thrasius”, is the fifth of the seven men he kills there (21.210). He is named nowhere else.

**The name.** Θρασίος seems to be formed on θράσος, “boldness”, as are the names of Thrasymedes, Nestor's son, and of Thrasymelus, whom Patroclus kills (see [[thrasymedes]] and [[thrasymelus]]).
