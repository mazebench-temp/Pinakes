## The name

Στρατίος is the adjective στράτιος, “of the army”, from στρατός, “army”, made a name. The Carians, Herodotus says, worshipped Zeus under that title, as “Zeus of Armies” (Herodotus 5.119.2).

## Outside Homer

Apollodorus' list of the seven sons of Nestor by Anaxibia, daughter of Cratieus, has Στράτιχος, Stratichus, in his place: Perseus, Stratichus, Aretus, Echephron, Peisistratus, Antilochus and Thrasymedes (*Library* 1.9.9).
