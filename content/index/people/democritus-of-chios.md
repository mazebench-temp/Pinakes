greek: Δημόκριτος ὁ Χῖος
kind: person
tags: [performer]
summary: Democritus of Chios, a musician of the late fifth century BCE, contemporary of the philosopher of the same name, who mocked the long preludes of Melanippides.
---

Diogenes Laertius lists him second among the men named Democritus: “a musician of Chios, of the same time” as the philosopher (9.49).
