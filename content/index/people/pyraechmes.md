The name means "fire-spear" (πῦρ, "fire" + αἰχμή, "spear point"). When Patroclus enters the battle in Achilles' armor, his first spear strikes Pyraechmes in the right shoulder, and the Paeonians scatter in panic (16.284–292). The lines that name him there echo the Catalogue: he "led the Paeonian chariot-fighters from Amydon, from the broad-flowing Axius" (16.287–288).
