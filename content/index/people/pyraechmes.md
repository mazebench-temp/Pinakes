The name means "fire-spear" (πῦρ, "fire" + αἰχμή, "spear point"). He leads the Paeonians in the Trojan catalogue, and he is named again only at his death, the first man Patroclus kills when he comes out in Achilles' armor (16.284–292; see [[patroclus]]). The verse that names his town and river there is the Catalogue's, with one word changed (16.288 ≈ 2.849).
