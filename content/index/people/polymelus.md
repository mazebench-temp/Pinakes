καὶ Ἀργεάδην Πολύμηλον, “and Polymelus, son of Argeas” (16.417), is the last name in the list of nine men whom Patroclus kills one after another, with no blow told (16.415–418; see [[erymas-killed-by-patroclus]], [[argeas]] and [[patroclus]]). With Tlepolemus, son of Damastor, he is one of the two in the list who are given a father (see [[tlepolemus-son-of-damastor]]).

His name is the adjective πολύμηλος, “rich in flocks”, said of Orchomenus in Arcadia (2.605), of Iphiclus (2.705) and of Phorbas, the father of Ilioneus (14.490; see [[rich-in-flocks]]). Its feminine is the name of Polymele, the mother of Eudorus, in the catalogue of the Myrmidons earlier in this book (16.180; see [[polymele]]). Polymelus is named only here.
