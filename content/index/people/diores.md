## Elsewhere in Homer

He is among the first Achaean leaders to die. Peiros, the leader of the Thracians, strikes him with a jagged stone on the right ankle and crushes the tendons and bone. Diores falls on his back, stretching out his hands to his companions, and Peiros runs up and spears him beside the navel. Thoas the Aetolian kills Peiros at once, and the two leaders lie side by side in the dust, "one of the Thracians, the other of the bronze-armored Epeians" (4.517–538; see [[peirous]] and [[thoas]]).
