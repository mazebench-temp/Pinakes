---
title: Draco
greek: Δράκων
kind: person
tags: [statesman, athenian, law]
summary: The Athenian lawgiver of about 621 BCE whose laws were remembered for their severity; his name means “serpent”, and his laws, it was said, were not a man's but a serpent's.
aliases: [Dracon]
---

Aristotle says that Draco made laws for an existing constitution and that there is nothing in them worth mentioning except their harshness, because of the size of the penalties (*Politics* 2.12, 1274b15–18). Solon repealed all his laws except those on homicide; the orator Demades said that Draco had written his laws in blood, not ink (Plutarch, *Solon* 17). See [[solon]].
