The name is the adjective χαροπός, "bright-eyed" or "fierce-eyed", which the Odyssey uses of lions (11.611). With [[Aglaea]], "splendor", he makes a fitting pair of parents for the handsomest man in the army. Unusually, the Catalogue names Nireus' mother before his father. Homer names Charopus only here.
