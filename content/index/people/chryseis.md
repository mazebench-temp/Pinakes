## Etymology

Χρυσηΐς (Chrysēïs): the -ίς ending marks a daughter or a woman "of" a place or father, so the name means "daughter of Chryses" or "the girl from Chryse". Homer never gives her another name.

{{notes}}

## Outside Homer

In medieval retellings of the Trojan War, her name merged with Briseis': Boccaccio's Criseida, Chaucer's Criseyde and Shakespeare's Cressida all descend from the pair. See [[from-briseis-to-cressida]].
