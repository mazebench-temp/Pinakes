summary: Son of Telephus, who brought the Ceteians to Troy's aid in the last year of the war and was killed by Neoptolemus; the subject of a tragedy Aristotle lists from the Little Iliad.
aliases: [Eurypylos son of Telephus]
relations:
  - father: telephus
---

“Many of his companions, the Ceteians, were killed around him, because of a woman's gifts” (*Odyssey* 11.519–521): Priam had bribed Eurypylus' mother with a golden vine. He is not the Achaean Eurypylus of the Iliad (see [[eurypylus]], [[neoptolemus]] and [[eurypylus-play]]).
