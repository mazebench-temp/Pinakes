Penelope is the daughter of Icarius (*Odyssey* 1.329) and the mother of Telemachus (see [[telemachus]]). In the Bath, convinced by the stranger's description of her husband's clothes, she infers what is false (see [[the-bath]] and [[paralogismos]]). The critics' difficulty over her father is the subject of [[the-icarius-problem]].
