The Trojan Catalogue named Acamas with no father (2.844). His death supplies one: he is “the son of Eussorus, Acamas, tall and brave” (6.8). A father's name is one of the usual details of a Homeric killing (see [[androktasia]]), and often the only one. Nothing else is told of Eussorus.
