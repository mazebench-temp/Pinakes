kind: person
tags: [demigod, child-of-zeus, crete]
summary: Rhadamanthus, son of Zeus and brother of Minos, famed for his justice, who after death judges the dead or dwells in the Elysian plain.
aliases: [Rhadamanthys]
---

In the Iliad Zeus names him among his sons, with Minos (14.322); in the Odyssey he dwells in the Elysian plain (4.564) and is carried by the Phaeacians to Euboea (7.323). Plato makes him one of the judges of the dead (*Gorgias* 523e–524a). See [[minos]].
