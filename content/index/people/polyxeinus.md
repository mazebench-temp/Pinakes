θεοειδής "godlike" ([[godlike]]). The name joins πολύς, "many", and ξεῖνος, "guest, host": "of many guests". Through his father [[Agasthenes]] he is the grandson of [[Augeas]], the old king of the Epeians, and he leads the fourth division of their ships. He does not appear again in the Iliad.

## Outside Homer

Apollodorus lists him among the suitors of Helen (3.10.8).
