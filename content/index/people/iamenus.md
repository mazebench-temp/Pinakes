{{quote:b737cb42-e11d-5c88-aefc-f1a299f98566}}

Iamenus is one of five men named around Asius when his company rushes the gate on the left of the ships, holding their oxhide shields high (12.137–140; see [[asius]], [[the-gates-of-the-wall]] and [[the-fight-at-the-wall]]). The Lapiths Polypoetes and Leonteus hold the gate, and Leonteus kills him after Hippomachus, Antiphates and Menon:

{{quote:bad3e3c9-f23a-5df2-98f5-380ae121c759}}

The verse of his death ends as the verse that brought him in, καὶ Ἰαμενὸν καὶ Ὀρέστην, “and Iamenus and Orestes” (12.139, 12.193): the two come on together and die together (see [[orestes-the-trojan]]). The last line, “all of them, one after another, he brought down to the bountiful earth”, also closes Teucer's list of victims (8.277) and one of Patroclus' (16.418; see [[leonteus]] and [[whom-first-whom-last]]).
