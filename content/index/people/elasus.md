αὐτὰρ ἔπειτʼ Ἔλασον, “and then Elasus” (16.696): Elasus opens the last verse of the list of nine men whom Patroclus kills when the narrator asks him “whom first, whom last” (16.692–697; see [[adrastus-killed-by-patroclus]], [[whom-first-whom-last]] and [[patroclus]]). He is named only here.
