# Ἀργεάδην (16.417), "son of Argeas", occurs only here in Homer and counts for son and father (polymelus).
grc: [Ἀργεάδην]
relations:
  - child: polymelus (16.417)
---

καὶ Ἀργεάδην Πολύμηλον, “and Polymelus, son of Argeas” (16.417), is the only mention of him (see [[polymelus]]). Of the nine men whom Patroclus brings down one after another in this list, only Polymelus and Tlepolemus, son of Damastor, are given fathers (16.415–418; see [[erymas-killed-by-patroclus]], [[tlepolemus-son-of-damastor]] and [[damastor]]).
