Chromius is the third of Nestor's five captains, with [[Pelagon]], [[Alastor]], [[Haemon]] and [[Bias]] (4.295–296). Nothing else is said of him.

Χρομίος is one of the commonest minor names in the Iliad. Besides this Pylian there are a son of Priam whom Diomedes kills with his brother Echemmon (5.159–160), a Lycian whom Odysseus kills in the same line as an Alastor (5.677), a Trojan killed by Teucer (8.275), and a Trojan ally of Book 17 (17.218, 17.494), probably the [[Chromis]] of the Trojan Catalogue. The Odyssey names a Chromius among the sons of Neleus, Nestor's brothers (Od. 11.286). In the Iliad, however, Nestor says that all his brothers died when Heracles attacked Pylos (11.690–693), and nothing connects that Chromius with the captain of Book 4.
