tags: [lapith, king]
summary: King of the Lapiths, the first man to kill a kinsman, who was purified by Zeus and then tried to seduce Hera; he lay with a cloud in her shape, fathering the Centaurs, and was bound to a burning wheel.
aliases: [Ixion]
refs: [Poetics 1456a1]
---

Pindar tells his story as a warning against ingratitude (*Pythian* 2.21–48; see [[ixion-on-the-wheel]]). In the Iliad Zeus names, among his loves, “the wife of Ixion, who bore Pirithous” (14.317–318; see [[pirithous]]). For the plays see [[ixion-plays]].
