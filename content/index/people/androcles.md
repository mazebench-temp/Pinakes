Thucydides calls him the leading man of the people, “who was most responsible for the banishment of Alcibiades”; the young men of the oligarchic conspiracy killed him in secret in 411 BCE (8.65.2). Plutarch names him among the popular leaders who brought forward witnesses against Alcibiades in the affair of the Herms and the Mysteries (*Alcibiades* 19).
