His home takes more of the verse than his death: “he lived beside the banks of the fair-flowing Satnioeis, in steep Pedasus” (6.34–35). Pedasus was the town of the Leleges in the southern Troad, whose king Altes gave his daughter Laothoe to Priam (21.85–88; see [[pedasus-on-the-satnioeis]] and [[satnioeis]]). He is the only man Agamemnon kills in the list; Agamemnon's second killing in the book is the suppliant Adrastus (6.63–65; see [[adrastus-the-trojan]]). The name returns in the Odyssey for one of the suitors, whom the swineherd kills (Od. 22.267).
