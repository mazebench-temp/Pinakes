## Elsewhere in Homer

Telamonian Ajax holds off Hector at the ships (Iliad 15). His shade will not speak to Odysseus in the underworld, still angry over the armor of Achilles (Odyssey 11.543–564).

## Outside Homer

After Achilles' death Ajax loses the contest for his armor to Odysseus and kills himself. The *Little Iliad* told the story, and Sophocles dramatized it. The other Ajax, the Locrian son of Oileus, drags Cassandra from Athena's altar at the sack of Troy (see [[ajax-son-of-oileus]]).
