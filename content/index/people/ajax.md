## Outside Homer

Telamonian Ajax holds off Hector at the ships (Iliad 15), and after Achilles' death he loses the contest for his armor to Odysseus and kills himself. The *Little Iliad* told the story, and Sophocles dramatized it. His shade will not speak to Odysseus in the underworld (Odyssey 11.543–564). The other Ajax, the Locrian son of Oileus, drags Cassandra from Athena's altar at the sack of Troy (see [[ajax-son-of-oileus]]).
