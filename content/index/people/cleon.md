Cleon dominated the Athenian assembly in the 420s BCE. Thucydides introduces him in the debate on Mytilene as “the most violent of the citizens, and at that time by far the most persuasive with the people” (Thucydides 3.36.6). In 425 he took over the command at Pylos and brought the Spartans captured on Sphacteria to Athens, as he had promised (4.27–41). He was killed in 422 at Amphipolis, in the battle in which Brasidas also fell (5.10). Aristophanes caricatures him in the *Knights*.
