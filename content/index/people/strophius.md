He is named once, as a father: υἱὸν δὲ Στροφίοιο Σκαμάνδριον, “and the son of Strophius, Scamandrius” (5.49), whom Artemis had taught to hunt and whom Menelaus kills. Nothing else is told of him. See [[scamandrius]].
