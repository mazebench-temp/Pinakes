Diomedes kills Abas and his brother Polyidus in the middle of his rampage, after Astynous and Hypeiron and before the sons of Phaenops. The four lines tell their deaths in a single verb, ἐξενάριξε (“slew them and stripped them”, 5.151), and give the rest to their father, the reader of dreams whose art did not save them (see [[eurydamas]]). Ἄβας is the singular of the name of the Euboean Abantes (2.536), but nothing connects him with them.
