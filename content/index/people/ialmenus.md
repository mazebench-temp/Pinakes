Ialmenus never appears without his brother. They are named together in the Catalogue, with the story of their birth to [[Astyoche]] and [[Ares]] (see [[the-birth-of-ascalaphus-and-ialmenus]]), and again among the captains of the night watch (9.82). When Ascalaphus is killed (13.518–520), Ialmenus is not mentioned.

## Outside Homer

Apollodorus lists him with his brother among the Argonauts and the suitors of Helen (1.9.16, 3.10.8).
