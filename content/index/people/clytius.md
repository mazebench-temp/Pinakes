Κλυτίος, from κλυτός, "famous". He sits among the elders at the Scaean gates with his brothers Lampus and Hicetaon, and he is one of the sons of Laomedon in Aeneas' genealogy (3.147 = 20.238; see [[trojan-elders]]). In Book 15 his son Caletor is carrying fire to the Achaean ships when Ajax strikes him with a spear, and Hector sees his "cousin" (ἀνεψιός) fall (15.419–422).
