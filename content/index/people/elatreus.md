## The name

Like most Phaeacian names it is a word of the sea (W. W. Merry, *Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on *Odyssey* 8.111). It belongs with ἐλαύνω, “drive”, the verb of rowing, and with ἐλάτη, the fir of which oars are made: a god gives a fair wind to sailors “when they have grown weary with polished oars of fir, / rowing over the sea”, ἐϋξέστῃς ἐλάτῃσι / πόντον ἐλαύνοντες (*Iliad* 7.5–6; see [[the-fair-wind]] and [[oars]]).
