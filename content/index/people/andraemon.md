Homer names him only as the father of Thoas, here and in later books (7.168, 13.216, 15.281). Later genealogists made him the husband of Gorge, a daughter of [[Oeneus]], which would explain why the command of the Aetolians passed to his son when Oeneus' house died out (Apollodorus 1.8.1).
