title: Lampon
greek: Λάμπων

tags: [seer, athenian]
summary: Lampon, Athenian seer and interpreter of oracles in the age of Pericles, one of the founders of Thurii.
---

Plutarch tells how Lampon read the one-horned ram brought to Pericles as a sign that power would pass to him (*Pericles* 6).
