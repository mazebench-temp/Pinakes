Πείρως Ἰμβρασίδης ὃς ἄρʼ Αἰνόθεν εἰληλούθει, “Peirous son of Imbrasus, who had come from Aenus” (4.520). Ἰμβρασίδης = Ἴμβρασος + -ίδης (“son of”). The poem gives the father's name in the line that gives his son's town, [[Aenus]] on the Thracian coast, and says nothing more about him. See [[peirous]] and [[thracians]].
