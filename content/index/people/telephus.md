Telephus was born at Tegea to Auge, a priestess of Athena, and Heracles, and exposed; he grew up, killed his mother's brothers, and came to Mysia, where he became king. The *Cypria* told how the Greeks, sailing for Troy, landed in Mysia by mistake and Achilles wounded Telephus; told by an oracle that only the wounder could heal him, he came to Argos disguised as a beggar and was healed by the rust of Achilles' spear, and in return guided the fleet to Troy (Proclus; see [[the-silence-of-telephus]]). Euripides' *Telephus* (438 BCE), with its ragged hero, is parodied at length in Aristophanes' *Acharnians*. In the *Poetics* he ends the list of the few houses (1453a21; see [[the-few-houses]]).
