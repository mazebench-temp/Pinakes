“and Perimus, son of Megas” (16.695): of the nine men whom Patroclus kills when the narrator asks him “whom first, whom last”, Perimus is the only one given a father (16.692–697; see [[megas]], [[adrastus-killed-by-patroclus]], [[whom-first-whom-last]] and [[patroclus]]).

His verse is shaped like the last verse of names in the list of Teucer's victims in Book 8. Both end with Melanippus, and in both the man before Melanippus is named with his father: “and Amopaon, son of Polyaemon, and Melanippus” (8.276), καὶ Πέριμον Μεγάδην καὶ Ἐπίστορα καὶ Μελάνιππον (16.695; see [[amopaon]] and [[melanippus-killed-by-patroclus]]). Perimus is named only here.
