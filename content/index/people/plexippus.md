summary: Brother of Althaea and uncle of Meleager. In the later story of the Calydonian boar Meleager killed him when he took the boar's hide from Atalanta.
grc: ["^Πλήξιππ*"]
---

In the version told by Apollodorus, Meleager gave the hide of the boar to Atalanta, and his mother's brothers, thinking it shame that a woman should have the prize while men were present, took it from her; Meleager killed them, and Althaea in her grief burned the brand on which his life depended (*Library* 1.8.2–3; Ovid, *Metamorphoses* 8.425–525). Homer's Meleager kills his mother's brother in the war over the hide, without a name (Il. 9.566–567). See [[meleager]], [[althaea]] and [[the-wrath-of-meleager]].
