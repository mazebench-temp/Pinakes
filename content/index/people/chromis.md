The Catalogue names him only here. In Book 17 Hector urges on “Phorcys and Chromius and Ennomus the augur” (17.218), and the same man, as Walter Leaf takes it, goes with Hector and Aeneas after the horses of Achilles (17.494, 17.534): Chromis and Chromius are two forms of one name (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 2.858). Other men called Chromius, among them a Pylian and a son of Priam, appear elsewhere in the poem (4.295, 5.160).
