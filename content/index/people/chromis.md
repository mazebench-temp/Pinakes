Chromis is named only here. A Chromius whom Hector urges on in Book 17, in a list that also names Phorcys and "Ennomus the augur" (17.216–218), is probably the same man under a slightly different form of the name. Other men called Chromius, among them a Pylian and a son of Priam, appear elsewhere in the poem (4.295, 5.160).
