“and Perimus, son of Megas, and Epistor and Melanippus” (16.695): Epistor is the fifth of the nine men whom Patroclus kills when the narrator asks him “whom first, whom last” (16.692–697; see [[adrastus-killed-by-patroclus]], [[whom-first-whom-last]] and [[patroclus]]). He is named only here.
