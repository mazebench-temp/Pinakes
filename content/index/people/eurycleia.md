Laertes bought her for the price of twenty oxen and honored her like his wife (*Odyssey* 1.429–433). In Odyssey 19 she washes the disguised stranger's feet, knows the scar, and is silenced by Odysseus (*Odyssey* 19.386–490; see [[odysseus-recognized-by-his-nurse]], [[the-bath]] and [[the-scar-of-odysseus]]).
