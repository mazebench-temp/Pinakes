Proclus' summary of the *Cypria* tells that when the Greeks put in at Troy the Trojans tried to keep them from landing and Achilles killed Cycnus, son of Poseidon, and routed them (see [[cypria]]). Pindar names Cycnus among Achilles' victims with Hector and [[Memnon]] (*Olympian* 2.81–83). In Ovid, since no weapon could wound him, Achilles strangled him with the straps of his own helmet, and he was changed into a swan (*Metamorphoses* 12.72–145). See [[achilles]].
