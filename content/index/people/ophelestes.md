ἠδʼ Ὀφελέστην, “and Ophelestes”, ends the first verse of Teucer's list of eight, men otherwise unknown whom he brings down with his arrows (8.274–277; see [[teucer]]).

The same words end another list of victims. When Achilles has killed Asteropaeus at the river, he turns on the Paeonians and kills seven, and the last of them is an Ophelestes (21.205–210).
