Ὄρμενον, “Ormenus”, is the second name in Teucer's list of eight, men otherwise unknown whom he brings down with his arrows (8.273–277; see [[teucer]]).

The name is the participle ὄρμενος, “starting up, rushing on”, from ὄρνυμι, “rouse”: the Trojans' spears stick in Ajax's great shield ὄρμενα πρόσσω, “as they sped forward” (11.572), and a fire that falls on a city is ὄρμενον ἐξαίφνης, “rising suddenly” (17.738). Polypoetes kills another Trojan Ormenus at the wall (12.187). Amyntor, the father of Phoenix, is a son of Ormenus (Ὀρμενίδης, 9.448, 10.266; see [[ormenus-father-of-amyntor]]), and so is Ctesius, the father of Eumaeus (Od. 15.414). Ormenium is one of the towns of Eurypylus (2.734; see [[ormenium]] and [[eurypylus]]).
