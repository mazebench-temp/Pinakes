title: Bryson
greek: Βρύσων
kind: person
tags: [sophist]
summary: Bryson, a sophist of the fourth century BCE, known for an attempt to square the circle and for the claim that no one speaks foully.
---

Aristotle mentions Bryson's squaring of the circle as a proof that does not proceed from the proper principles of geometry (*Posterior Analytics* 1.9, 75b40; *Sophistical Refutations* 11, 171b16). Whether the Bryson of the *Rhetoric* is the same man is likely but not certain.
