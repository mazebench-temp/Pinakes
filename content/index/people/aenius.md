**The sixth of seven.** καὶ Αἴνιον, “and Aenius”, is the sixth of the Paeonians whom Achilles kills along the river after Asteropaeus has fallen (21.210; see [[asteropaeus]], [[paeonians]] and [[achilles]]). He is named nowhere else, and nothing is told of him but his death.

**The name.** Αἴνιος recalls Αἶνος, Aenus, the town on the Thracian coast from which the Thracian leader Peirous had come (Αἰνόθεν, 4.520; see [[aenus]] and [[peirous]]); the poem does not say that Aenius came from there.
