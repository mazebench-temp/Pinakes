summary: Antisthenes (c. 445–365 BCE), companion of Socrates, later counted the founder of the Cynic way of life.
---

Aristotle reports his view that a thing can be spoken of only by its own account, so that contradiction is impossible (*Metaphysics* Δ 29, 1024b32–34). That the Antisthenes of the *Rhetoric* is the philosopher is likely but not stated.
