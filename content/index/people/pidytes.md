Περκώσιον, “of Percote” (6.30), is all the poem says of him: an adjective of place where a father's name might stand. Percote was one of the towns near the Hellespont led by Asius (2.835; see [[percote]] and [[asius]]), and the seer Merops, whose sons led the men of Adrasteia, is Περκώσιος too (2.831; see [[merops]]). The name Pidytes occurs only here.
