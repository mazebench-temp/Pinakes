Φυλὼ δʼ ἀργύρεον τάλαρον φέρε, “and Phylo brought a silver basket” (*Odyssey* 4.125; see [[helen]] and [[handmaids]]). She is named twice: first in the list of the three women who come with Helen, after Adreste and Alcippe (*Odyssey* 4.123–125; see [[adreste]] and [[alcippe]]), and again when the poem has told the history of the basket, τόν ῥά οἱ ἀμφίπολος Φυλὼ παρέθηκε φέρουσα, “This the handmaid Phylo brought and set beside her” (*Odyssey* 4.133; see [[alcandre]]).
