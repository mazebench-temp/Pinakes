In the Iliad Achilles tells Priam her story as an example: even Niobe, whose six daughters and six sons were killed, remembered to eat (24.602–617). Aeschylus and Sophocles wrote tragedies about her (see [[the-children-of-niobe]] and [[niobe-play]]).
