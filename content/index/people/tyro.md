Odysseus sees Tyro among the heroines in the underworld: she loved the river Enipeus, and Poseidon took his likeness and lay with her at the river's mouth, hidden by a wave; she bore Pelias and Neleus (*Odyssey* 11.235–257). Neleus became king of Pylos and father of Nestor, Pelias king of Iolcus (see [[neleus]] and [[pelias]]). Sophocles' *Tyro* told of the twins' exposure and the recognition (see [[tyro-play]], [[the-boat-of-tyro]] and [[tyro-and-her-sons]]).
