His name closes the list of the men Ajax wounds after killing Doryclus: “and wounded Lysander, and Pyrasus, and Pylartes” (11.491; see [[doryclus]], [[pandocus]], [[lysander]], [[pyrasus-the-trojan]] and [[ajax]]). οὖτα, “wounded”, does not say whether he died. Another Pylartes is among the men whom Patroclus kills as he pursues the Trojans toward the city, in a verse that ends as this one does, ἠδὲ Πυλάρτην (16.696; see [[pylartes-killed-by-patroclus]]). The same verse names a Mulius, as Nestor's story in this book does another (11.739; see [[mulius]]). The name is the word πυλάρτης, “gatekeeper”, Hades' title (8.367; see [[gatekeeper]]).
