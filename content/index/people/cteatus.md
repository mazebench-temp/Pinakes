Cteatus is named in the Catalogue only as the father of [[Amphimachus]]. His twin brother [[eurytus-of-elis|Eurytus]] is the father of Thalpius.

## Elsewhere in Homer

Nestor twice recalls the twins. In the war between Pylos and the Epeians he would have killed "the two sons of Actor, the Moliones", had their real father, Poseidon, not hidden them in a mist and saved them (11.709–710, 11.750–752). At the funeral games of Amarynceus they beat him in the chariot race, because there were two of them: one held the reins while the other plied the whip (23.638–642). That is why Poseidon grieves for his grandson Amphimachus (13.206–207).

## Outside Homer

Later poets made the Moliones, named after their mother Molione, a pair of conjoined twins. They fought for Augeas against Heracles, and Heracles ambushed and killed them near Cleonae (Pindar, *Olympian* 10.26–34; Apollodorus 2.7.2).
