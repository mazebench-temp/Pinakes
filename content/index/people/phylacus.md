Φυλακίδης = Φύλακος + -ίδης ("son of"). He shares his name with the family's town, [[Phylace]]. In the Odyssey the seer Melampus lay bound for a year "in the halls of Phylacus" (15.231). With his son Iphiclus and his grandsons Protesilaus and Podarces, the Catalogue gives the house of Phylace three generations in three lines (2.704–706).
