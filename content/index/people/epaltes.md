“Then Erymas and Amphoterus and Epaltes” (16.415): Epaltes closes the first verse of the list of nine men whom Patroclus kills one after another, with no blow told, after Pronous, Thestor and Erylaus (16.399–418; see [[erymas-killed-by-patroclus]] and [[patroclus]]). He is named only here.
