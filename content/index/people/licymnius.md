μήτρως is a mother's brother: Licymnius was the brother of Heracles' mother, Alcmene. ὄζος Ἄρηος, "offshoot of Ares", is a warrior's epithet (see [[offshoot-of-ares]]), and ἤδη γηράσκοντα, "already growing old", makes the killing of an old kinsman the worse. Homer gives no reason for it.

Later sources make him Alcmene's half-brother, a son of Electryon of Mycenae by a Phrygian woman named Midea (Apollodorus 2.4.5). Pindar calls him Alcmene's bastard brother and says that Tlepolemus struck him down at Tiryns (*Olympian* 7.27–30).
