Philip rebuilt the Macedonian army and extended his power over Thessaly, Thrace and the Chalcidice, destroying Olynthus in 348 BCE; Demosthenes' *Philippics* and *Olynthiacs* were spoken against him (see [[demosthenes]]). He ended the Sacred War against the Phocians in 346 and gained a seat on the Amphictyonic Council. In 339 he marched into central Greece, and Athens and Thebes, allied against him, were defeated at Chaeronea in 338. He was murdered at Aegae in 336 while preparing the invasion of Persia. Aristotle was tutor to his son Alexander at Mieza from 343 or 342.
