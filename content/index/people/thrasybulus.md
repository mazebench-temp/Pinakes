Thrasybulus was a leader of the democratic fleet at Samos in 411 BCE (Thucydides 8.73–76). Exiled by the Thirty, he seized the fort of Phyle on the border of Attica with a small band, defeated the oligarchs' forces, occupied the Piraeus, and in 403 led the exiles back into the city (Xenophon, *Hellenica* 2.4.2–43). He commanded again in the Corinthian War and was killed at Aspendus in 388 (*Hellenica* 4.8.25–30). His name means “bold in counsel” (θρασύς, “bold”, and βουλή, “counsel”).
