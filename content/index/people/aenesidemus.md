---
title: Aenesidemus
greek: Αἰνεσίδημος
kind: person
tags: [tyrant, sicily]
summary: A Sicilian ruler of the early fifth century BCE, tyrant of Leontini according to Freese's note, who sent Gelon the prize for a game of cottabus when Gelon enslaved a city he himself had meant to take.
---

Nothing is known of Aenesidemus beyond Aristotle's anecdote. Cottabus was a drinking game, Sicilian in origin, in which the players flung the last drops of wine from their cups at a target; Athenaeus collects much about it in his fifteenth book.
