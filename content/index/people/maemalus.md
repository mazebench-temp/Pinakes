Πείσανδρος ἀρήϊος … Μαιμαλίδης, “warlike Peisander, son of Maemalus” (16.193–194): Maemalus is named only in the title of his son, who leads the third company of the Myrmidons and was the best of them with the spear after Patroclus (see [[peisander-son-of-maemalus]] and [[myrmidons]]). Homer tells nothing else of him. The name may be formed from μαιμάω, “quiver with eagerness”, the verb of a spear that speeds eagerly through a man (5.661, 15.542) and of a fighter's hands and feet, eager for battle (13.75).
