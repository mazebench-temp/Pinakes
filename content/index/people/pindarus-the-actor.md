---
title: Pindarus (actor)
greek: Πίνδαρος
kind: person
tags: [performer, theater]
summary: An actor of the late fifth or fourth century BCE, otherwise unknown, of whom the same was said as of Callippides: that he overdid it. Not the poet Pindar.
aliases: [Pindarus the actor, Pindaros]
---

“For overdoing it, Mynniscus used to call Callippides an ape, and there was the same opinion about Pindarus” (1461b34–1462a1; see [[mynniscus-and-callippides]]). For the lyric poet see [[pindar]].
