This Eurytus is named in the Catalogue as the father of [[Thalpius]]. With his twin [[Cteatus]] he was one of the two sons of Actor, or of Poseidon, whom Nestor remembers from his youth (11.709–710, 11.750–752, 23.638–642); see [[Cteatus]] for their story. He is not the archer king [[Eurytus]] of Oechalia (2.596, 2.730).
