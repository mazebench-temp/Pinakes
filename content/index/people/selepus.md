Σεληπιάδης = Σέληπος + -ιάδης ("son of"). Homer names him only through his son [[Euenus]], in the one line that gives the genealogy of the lords of [[Lyrnessus]]. His grandsons Mynes and Epistrophus died when Achilles sacked the town. Nothing else is known of him.
