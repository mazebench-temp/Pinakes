διοτρεφής, "nurtured by Zeus" ([[zeus-nurtured]]), is the standing epithet of kings. Κεάδαο, "son of Ceas", is the old genitive of the patronymic. The name is the same as that of Troezen in the Argolid ([[troezen]], 2.561), but nothing connects the Thracian with the town.
