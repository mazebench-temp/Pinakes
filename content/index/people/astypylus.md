**The third of seven.** After Asteropaeus, the Paeonians flee along the river, “when they saw their best man in the fierce battle / brought down by force at the hands of the son of Peleus and by his sword” (21.207–208; see [[asteropaeus]] and [[paeonians]]). Achilles goes after them and kills seven, and Ἀστύπυλόν τε, “and Astypylus”, closes the first of the two verses that name them (21.209; see [[achilles]]). He is named nowhere else, and the poem tells nothing of him but his death.

**The name.** Ἀστύπυλος seems to join ἄστυ, “town”, and πύλη, “gate”.
