tags: [performer]
summary: A rhapsode who overdid his gestures in reciting epic; otherwise unknown.
aliases: [Sosistratos]
grc: [Σωσίστρατ*]
---

Aristotle names him to show that overacting is a fault of performance, not of tragedy: “it is possible to overdo the signs in reciting epic too, as Sosistratus did” (1462a5–7; see [[rhapsody]] and [[delivery]]).
