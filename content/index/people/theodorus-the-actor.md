---
title: Theodorus the actor
greek: Θεόδωρος
kind: person
tags: [performer, athenian]
summary: Theodorus, the most famous tragic actor of the fourth century BCE, whose voice seemed to belong to the character he played.
---

Aristotle tells in the *Politics* that Theodorus would never let another actor, even a poor one, come on stage before him, because audiences take to what they hear first (7.17, 1336b27–31).
