In *Iliad* 17 he is named among the allies whom Hector urges on, and Ajax kills him as he stands over the fallen [[Hippothous]] (17.218, 17.312–318). The name also belongs to an old sea god, father of the Graeae and the Gorgons (Hesiod, *Theogony* 270–274) and grandfather of the Cyclops Polyphemus (*Odyssey* 1.71–72), who gives his name to the harbor on Ithaca where the Phaeacians set Odysseus ashore (*Odyssey* 13.96, 13.345; see [[phorcys-the-sea-god]] and [[the-harbor-of-phorcys]]).
