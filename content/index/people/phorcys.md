In Book 17 he is "wise-hearted Phorcys, son of Phaenops". He stands over the body of the fallen [[Hippothous]], and Telamonian Ajax strikes him in the belly through his corselet (17.312–318). The name also belongs to an old sea god, father of the Graeae and the Gorgons (Hesiod, *Theogony* 270–274) and grandfather of the Cyclops Polyphemus (Odyssey 1.71–72).
