## Outside Homer

The Odyssey tells again and again how Clytemnestra, with Aegisthus, murdered Agamemnon on his return (3.262–275, 4.512–537, 11.405–434), a warning set against Penelope's faithfulness. Aeschylus' *Agamemnon* gives her a motive the Iliad does not mention: the sacrifice of her daughter Iphigenia. See [[clytemnestra-and-the-return]].
