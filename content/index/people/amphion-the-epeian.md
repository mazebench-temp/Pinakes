Amphion is named only here, between Meges and Dracius, as a leader of the Epeians (see [[epeians]], [[meges]] and [[dracius]]). In the Catalogue the Epeians came under four leaders, Amphimachus, Thalpius, Diores and Polyxeinus (2.615–624), and Meges led the islanders of Dulichium (2.625–630); by now Diores is dead (4.517–526), and Hector has killed Amphimachus earlier in this book (13.183–187; see [[amphimachus]]). Homer gives Amphion no father, no home and no deed.

Two other men of the name are in the Odyssey's catalogue of heroines. Antiope, who boasted that she had slept in the arms of Zeus, bore Amphion and Zethus, and they were the first to found seven-gated Thebes and wall it (Od. 11.260–265); and Chloris, the mother of Nestor, was the youngest daughter of Amphion son of Iasus, who ruled in Minyan Orchomenus (Od. 11.281–286).
