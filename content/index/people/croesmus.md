αὐτὰρ ὅ γε Κροίσμου στῆθος μέσον οὔτασε δουρί, “but he stabbed Croesmus in the middle of the chest with his spear” (15.523). Polydamas has just killed Otus of Cyllene, Meges' companion, and Meges springs at him; Polydamas ducks aside, for Apollo will not let him fall, and Meges stabs Croesmus instead (15.518–523; see [[meges]], [[polydamas]] and [[otus-of-cyllene]]). “He fell with a thud” (15.524; see [[fell-with-a-thud]]), and as Meges strips the armor from his shoulders, Dolops, son of Lampus, springs at him (15.524–525; see [[dolops-son-of-lampus]]). He is named only here.
