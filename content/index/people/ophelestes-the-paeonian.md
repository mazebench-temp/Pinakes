**The last of seven.** ἠδʼ Ὀφελέστην, “and Ophelestes”, ends the list of the Paeonians whom Achilles kills along the river after their leader Asteropaeus has fallen (21.210; see [[asteropaeus]], [[paeonians]] and [[achilles]]). He is named nowhere else. Achilles would have killed more, had not the river spoken to him in anger from a deep eddy (21.211–213; see [[would-have-had-not]] and [[scamander]]).

**Another Ophelestes.** The same two words end the first verse of Teucer's list of the eight men he shoots from under the shield of Ajax, “Orsilochus first, and Ormenus and Ophelestes” (8.274; see [[ophelestes]] and [[teucer]]). The poem does not connect the two.
