Plato lists Chilon the Lacedaemonian among the seven sages who met at Delphi and dedicated to Apollo the sayings “Know yourself” and “Nothing too much” (*Protagoras* 343a–b; see [[know-yourself]] and [[nothing-too-much]]). Diogenes Laertius tells that he was ephor at Sparta in the middle of the sixth century and collects the sayings given to him (1.68–73).
