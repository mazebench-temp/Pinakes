summary: Hesione, daughter of Laomedon and sister of Priam, whom Heracles saved from a sea monster and gave to Telamon; she was the mother of Teucer.
---

The Iliad does not name her. It knows the sea monster from which, in the later story, Heracles saved her: the Trojans and Pallas Athena had made a high wall on the plain for Heracles, so that he might escape the monster “whenever it chased him from the shore toward the plain” (20.144–148; see [[the-wall-of-heracles]] and [[sea-beasts]]).

{{notes}}

## Outside Homer

Apollodorus tells how Heracles, cheated of his reward by Laomedon, took Troy and gave Hesione to Telamon (2.6.4), and that she bore him Teucer (3.12.7).
