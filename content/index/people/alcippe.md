Ἀλκίππη δὲ τάπητα φέρεν μαλακοῦ ἐρίοιο, “and Alcippe brought a rug of soft wool” (*Odyssey* 4.124; see [[helen]] and [[handmaids]]). She comes with Adreste, who sets the chair, and Phylo, who brings the silver basket (*Odyssey* 4.123–125; see [[adreste]] and [[phylo]]). The name is formed from ἀλκή, “might”, and ἵππος, “horse”.
