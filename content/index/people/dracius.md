Dracius is named only here, the last of the three leaders of the Epeians, after Meges, son of Phyleus, and Amphion (see [[epeians]], [[meges]] and [[amphion-the-epeian]]). The Epeians are one of the five peoples who, with the Athenians and the Phthians under their own leaders, hold Hector off where the wall was lowest (13.685–700; see [[the-battle-at-the-ships]]). Homer gives him no father, no home and no deed, and the name occurs nowhere else in Homer.
