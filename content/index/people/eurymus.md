Τήλεμος Εὐρυμίδης, “Telemus, son of Eurymus” (*Odyssey* 9.509): Eurymus is named only in the patronymic of the seer who grew old among the Cyclopes, and the poem says nothing else of him (see [[telemus]]). The name is formed from εὐρύς, “wide”. Ovid keeps the patronymic, *Telemus Eurymides* (*Metamorphoses* 13.771; see [[ovid]]).
