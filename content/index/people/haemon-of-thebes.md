Αἱμονίδης, “son of Haemon”, identifies Maeon, one of the two leaders of the ambush and the only man Tydeus spared (4.394). Nothing else is said of the father. The same book names a Pylian captain Haemon (4.296; see [[haemon]]).

Theban legend knew another Haemon, the son of Creon, who in Sophocles' *Antigone* is betrothed to Antigone and dies with her. Homer does not connect the two.
