Ἐχεκλῆος κρατερὸν μένος Ἀκτορίδαο, “the strong might of Echecles, son of Actor” (16.189): Actor is named only in the patronymic of the man who married Polymele after she had borne Eudorus to Hermes (see [[echecles]], [[polymele]] and [[the-birth-of-eudorus]]). Ἀκτορίδης, “son of Actor”, occurs only here; the line of Actor of Elis has another patronymic, Ἀκτορίων (2.621, 11.750, 13.185). Nothing else is told of him.

The Iliad knows several men called Actor: Actor, son of Azeus, in whose house Astyoche bore the sons of Ares (2.513); Actor of Elis, father of the Moliones (2.621, 11.750); and the father of Menoetius, Patroclus' grandfather (11.785, 16.14). Menoetius' father is named earlier in the same book, and the poem does not say whether he and the father of Echecles are one man (see [[actor-son-of-azeus]], [[actor-of-elis]] and [[actor-father-of-menoetius]]). The name is usually explained as ἄκτωρ, “leader”, from ἄγω, “lead”.
