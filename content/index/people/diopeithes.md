---
title: Diopeithes
greek: Διοπείθης
kind: person
tags: [statesman, warrior, athenian]
summary: A man to whom the gifts of the Persian king arrived only after his death, Aristotle's example of good fortune that comes too late. He is probably the Athenian general Diopeithes, who commanded in the Chersonese in 343–341 BCE.
grc: ["^Διοπείθ*"]
---

The Athenian general Diopeithes led settlers to the Chersonese and made war on Philip's allies there; Demosthenes defended him against the Macedonian party in the speech *On the Chersonese* (8). Whether he is Aristotle's Diopeithes is not certain.
