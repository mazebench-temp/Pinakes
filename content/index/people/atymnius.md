# Ἀτύμνιον (16.317) is the Lycian son of Amisodarus (atymnius-son-of-amisodarus): excepted for the English name.
grc: [Ἀτυμνιάδην]

ἐσθλὸν Ἀτυμνιάδην, “the brave son of Atymnius” (5.581), is the only mention of Mydon's father. The poem does not say where he came from; his son drove for the Paphlagonian king (see [[mydon]]).

The name returns in *Iliad* 16. Antilochus, who kills Mydon here, spears Atymnius, a son of Amisodarus, the man who reared the Chimaera, and one of Sarpedon's companions (16.317–329; see [[atymnius-son-of-amisodarus]]). They are different men, and the poem does not connect them.
