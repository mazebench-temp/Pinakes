In Sophocles' *Oedipus the King* Oedipus begins the story of his life with him: “My father was Polybus of Corinth, my mother Merope, a Dorian” (774–775). He is not the Trojan Polybus of the Iliad (see [[polybus]]), nor any of the men of the name in the Odyssey (see [[polybus-father-of-eurymachus]], [[polybus-of-thebes]] and [[polybus-the-phaeacian]]).
