summary: Polybus, king of Corinth, who raised the infant Oedipus as his own son.
---

In Sophocles' *Oedipus the King* Oedipus begins the story of his life with him: “My father was Polybus of Corinth, my mother Merope, a Dorian” (774–775). He is not the Trojan Polybus of the Iliad (see [[polybus]]).
