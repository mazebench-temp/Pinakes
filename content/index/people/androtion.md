tags: [statesman, speaker, historian, athenian]
summary: Androtion (c. 410–340 BCE), Athenian politician and orator, said to have been a pupil of Isocrates, who in exile wrote an Atthis, a history of Attica.
---

Demosthenes wrote the speech *Against Androtion* (22) for his prosecution over an illegal decree. The fragments of his *Atthis* are among the chief sources for early Athenian history.
