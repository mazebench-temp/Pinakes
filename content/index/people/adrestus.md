The Greek name is the same as that of Adrastus, king of Sicyon and later of Argos ([[adrastus]], 2.572); the translation spells the Trojan "Adrestus" to keep them apart. It also matches the name of his own town, Adrasteia. See [[the-sons-of-merops]]. Other Trojans called Adrestus die at 6.37–65 and 16.694; the poem does not connect them with this one.
