## The name

Εὐρυδίκη joins εὐρύς, “wide”, and δίκη, “right, judgment”.

## Family

She is the wife of Nestor and the eldest daughter of Clymenus, of whom nothing else is told (*Odyssey* 3.451–452; see [[clymenus]]). The poem names her once and calls her twice more the wife of the house: αἰδοίη παράκοιτις, “revered wife” (*Odyssey* 3.381, 3.451), and ἄλοχος δέσποινα, “the lady his wife” (*Odyssey* 3.403). It does not say whether she is the mother of Nestor's sons and daughters.

## Outside Homer

In Apollodorus Nestor's wife is Anaxibia, daughter of Cratieus, the mother of his two daughters, Peisidice and Polycaste, and his seven sons (*Library* 1.9.9).
