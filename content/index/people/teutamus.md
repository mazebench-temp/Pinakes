Τευταμίδης = Τεύταμος + -ίδης, "son of". The same form turns up in later myth as the name of a king of Larisa in Thessaly. Apollodorus tells how Perseus, competing at funeral games held by Teutamides, killed his grandfather Acrisius by accident with a discus (2.4.4).
