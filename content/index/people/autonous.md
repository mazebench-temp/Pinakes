The name joins αὐτός, “self”, and νόος, “mind”. Autonous is the second name in the list of Hector's victims, after Asaeus (see [[asaeus]] and [[whom-first-whom-last]]), and the Achaean is named nowhere else.

The name has a place in the verse as well as in the list. Hector's victims begin Ἀσαῖον μὲν πρῶτα καὶ Αὐτόνοον καὶ Ὀπίτην, “Asaeus first, and Autonous and Opites” (11.301), and when the narrator asks the same question of Patroclus, his answer begins Ἄδρηστον μὲν πρῶτα καὶ Αὐτόνοον καὶ Ἔχεκλον, “Adrastus first, and Autonous and Echeclus” (16.694). There Autonous is a Trojan. Each list has nine names (11.301–303; 16.694–696; see [[hector]] and [[patroclus]]).
