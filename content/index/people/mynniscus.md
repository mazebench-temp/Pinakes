The ancient *Life of Aeschylus* names Mynniscus of Chalcis as Aeschylus' actor. Aristotle tells his remark about Callippides: “for overdoing it, Mynniscus used to call Callippides an ape” (1461b33–35; see [[mynniscus-and-callippides]] and [[callippides]]).
