The *Little Iliad* and the *Sack of Troy* told of him (Proclus); Virgil's Sinon tells the Trojans a false tale of escape from sacrifice (*Aeneid* 2.57–198; see [[sinon-and-the-wooden-horse]] and [[sinon-play]]).
