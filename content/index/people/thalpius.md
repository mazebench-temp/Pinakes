Thalpius shares the first division of the Epeians with his cousin [[Amphimachus]]; their fathers were the twins Cteatus and Eurytus, "both of Actor's line" (2.621). He is not mentioned again in the Iliad.

## Outside Homer

Apollodorus lists him among the suitors of Helen (3.10.8).
