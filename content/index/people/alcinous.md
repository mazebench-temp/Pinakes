In Odyssey 7–13 Alcinous receives Odysseus in his palace, holds games and a feast, and listens to the tale of his wanderings, books 9–12, known in antiquity as “the tale to Alcinous” (see [[the-tale-to-alcinous]], [[demodocus]] and [[odysseus-set-ashore-on-ithaca]]).
