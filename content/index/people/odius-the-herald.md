# Ὀδίος at 2.856 is the leader of the Halizones (odius), whom Agamemnon kills at 5.39.
grc: [Ὀδίος]
except: [2.856]

Nestor names him when he chooses the envoys: “and of the heralds let Odius and Eurybates go with them” (9.170). Who sent the two heralds is not said. Eurybates is most probably Agamemnon's herald of Book 1 (see [[eurybates]]); the poem gives Odius no master and no other part. The heralds say nothing in Achilles' hut. At the end Odysseus calls on them as witnesses of Achilles' refusal, “Ajax and the two heralds, both wise men” (9.689; see [[heralds]] and [[embassy]]). His name is that of the leader of the Halizones, the first man Agamemnon kills in the Iliad (2.856, 5.38–42; see [[odius]]).
