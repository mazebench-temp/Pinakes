ὄζος Ἄρηος, "offshoot of Ares" ([[offshoot-of-ares]]): a warrior pictured as a branch of the war god's stock. The Achaean catalogue gives the same phrase to Elephenor, Podarces and Leonteus (2.540, 2.704, 2.745); Pylaeus is the only one of Troy's allies to have it. The name may be connected with πύλαι, "gates". He is not mentioned again in the Iliad.
