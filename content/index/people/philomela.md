---
title: Philomela
greek: Φιλομήλα
kind: person
tags: [woman, athenian]
summary: Philomela, daughter of Pandion of Athens, who was raped and mutilated by her sister Procne's husband Tereus and changed into a bird; in the Greek version she becomes the swallow, Procne the nightingale.
---

Apollodorus tells the story and its ending: “Procne becomes a nightingale, Philomela a swallow” (3.14.8). Latin poets often reverse the two.
