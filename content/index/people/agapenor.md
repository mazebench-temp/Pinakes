κρείων "powerful, ruling" ([[powerful]]), an epithet Homer gives most often to Agamemnon. The name seems to join ἀγαπάω, "love, welcome", and ἀνήρ, "man". Agapenor is named only in the Catalogue.

## Outside Homer

Apollodorus lists him among the suitors of Helen (3.10.8). Pausanias tells that a storm drove him to Cyprus on his way home from Troy, and that he founded Paphos there and built the sanctuary of Aphrodite at Old Paphos (8.5.2).
