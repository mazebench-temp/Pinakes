Homer names him only in this phrase, and he does not come to Troy. Later writers made him a son of Poseidon and Astypalaea and king of the Meropes of Cos. When a storm drove Heracles to the island on his way home from Troy, Heracles killed Eurypylus and took the island (Apollodorus 2.7.1), and Eurypylus' daughter Chalciope bore Heracles a son, [[Thessalus]] (2.7.8).
