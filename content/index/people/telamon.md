Τελαμώνιος (“Telamonian”) is an adjective used as a patronymic. It sets the great Ajax apart from the lesser Ajax, son of Oileus: “not so great as Telamonian Ajax” (2.528), and “of the men the best by far was Telamonian Ajax” (2.768). See [[ajax]] and [[ajax-son-of-oileus]].

## Elsewhere in Homer

Telamon is also the father of the archer Teucer, a bastard son whom he raised in his own house (Iliad 8.281–284).

## Outside Homer

Later tradition made Telamon and Peleus sons of Aeacus, driven from Aegina for killing their half-brother Phocus (Apollodorus, *Library* 3.12.6). Telamon sacked Troy with Heracles in the time of Laomedon and received Priam's sister Hesione as his prize (Apollodorus, *Library* 2.6.4).
