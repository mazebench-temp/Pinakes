ὄζος Ἄρηος "offshoot of Ares" ([[offshoot-of-ares]]); Χαλκωδοντιάδης "son of Chalcodon"; ἀρχὸς Ἀβάντων "leader of the Abantes". With him go the forty ships of [[Euboea]].

{{notes}}

## Outside Homer

Plutarch says that Theseus, driven from Athens, sent his sons to Elephenor in Euboea (*Theseus* 35). Apollodorus counts him among the suitors of Helen (3.10.8).
