Astynous and Hypeiron are the first pair Diomedes kills after Athena has answered his prayer, when his fury has tripled like that of a wounded lion (5.136–143). Homer names the two men together and then gives the blows in order: the spear for the first, “struck above the nipple” (5.145), and the great sword for the other (see [[hypeiron]]). Nothing else is told of him. The name joins ἄστυ (“town”) and νόος (“mind”). A second Astynous, son of Protiaon, takes charge of the horses of Polydamas' charioteer when he is killed (15.445–457).
