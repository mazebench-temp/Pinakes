Each side has its Opheltius, and each dies in a list. The Trojan is killed and stripped by Euryalus, named with Dresus in half a verse (6.20; see [[opheltius]] and [[euryalus]]). The Achaean is named with Agelaus in the second verse of Hector's victims, ἠδʼ Ἀγέλαον, “and Agelaus” (11.302), and Agelaus too shares his name with a Trojan killed in an earlier book (8.257; see [[agelaus-the-achaean]] and [[agelaus]]). Nothing more is told of him (see [[hector]] and [[whom-first-whom-last]]).
