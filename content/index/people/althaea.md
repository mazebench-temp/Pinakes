Ἀλθαίη, Attic Ἀλθαία, is also the Greek name of the marsh mallow, a healing plant, from ἀλθαίνω, “heal”; whether the name was meant so is not known. Homer names her once, as “his dear mother Althaea”, with whom Meleager is angry at heart (9.555; see [[meleager]]); she is Oeneus' wife only by implication. The reason follows. Grieving over “the killing of her brother” (κασιγνήτοιο φόνοιο, 9.567), she prayed to the gods below against her own son. Phoenix does not say who killed her brother; the curse makes sense only if Meleager did, in the war over the boar's hide, and later poets said so (see [[the-wrath-of-meleager]]). She prays kneeling and beating the earth, and the Erinys hears her from Erebus (see [[curses]], [[erinyes]] and [[erebus]]). Phoenix has told a father's curse in the same speech, Amyntor's, fulfilled by the same powers, “Zeus of the underworld and dread Persephone” (9.456–457; see [[the-flight-of-phoenix]] and [[persephone]]); his own curse came true, and he leaves hers open (see [[the-death-of-meleager]]). She is also among those who beg her son to fight: “many times his sisters and his lady mother / begged him; but he refused all the more” (9.584–585).

## Outside Homer

Later writers make her the daughter of Thestius and the sister of Leda, the wife of Oeneus, and the mother of Meleager and of Deianeira, the wife of Heracles (Euripides, *Meleager* fr. 515 Nauck; Apollodorus, *Library* 1.7.10, 1.8.1). In the version that prevailed, the Fates came when Meleager was seven days old and said that he would die when the brand on the hearth had burned away; she took it from the fire and kept it in a chest, and when he killed her brothers she burned it (Apollodorus, *Library* 1.8.2–3; Bacchylides 5.136–144). Aeschylus' chorus calls her “the child-destroying daughter of Thestius” (*Libation Bearers* 602–611), and Ovid gives her a long struggle with herself before she throws the brand into the fire (*Metamorphoses* 8.445–525). She killed herself afterward, by hanging in Apollodorus and with a sword in Ovid (*Library* 1.8.3; *Metamorphoses* 8.531–532).
