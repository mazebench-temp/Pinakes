---

The tragic poet [[sophocles]] also served Athens in office, as general with Pericles in the Samian War and as one of the commissioners appointed after the Sicilian disaster; the *Rhetoric* recalls his answer to Pisander about the oligarchy of 411 (3.18, 1419a25–30; see [[pisander-the-oligarch]]). Whether he also pleaded for Euctemon cannot be decided; Freese's note takes this Sophocles to be an orator of the same name.
