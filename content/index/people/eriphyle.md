Odysseus sees “hateful Eriphyle, who took precious gold for her own husband” among the women in the underworld (*Odyssey* 11.326–327). The necklace was Harmonia's, given her by Polynices (see [[the-seven-against-thebes]]). Aristotle: “the stories that have been handed down cannot be undone, I mean such as the death of Clytemnestra at the hands of Orestes, and of Eriphyle at the hands of Alcmaeon” (1453b22–24; see [[alcmaeon-kills-eriphyle]]).
