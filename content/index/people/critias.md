title: Critias

tags: [statesman, athenian, philosopher]
summary: Athenian oligarch, poet and sophist (c. 460–403 BCE), a kinsman of Plato and a member of Socrates' circle, who led the Thirty Tyrants and was killed fighting the democrats in 403.
---

Critias son of Callaeschrus wrote elegies, tragedies and prose works on constitutions, and appears as a speaker in Plato's *Charmides* and *Protagoras*. Exiled by the democracy, he came back after Athens' defeat and became the most violent of the Thirty (Xenophon, *Hellenica* 2.3.15–56), and he fell in the battle at Munychia in 403 BCE (2.4.19). His family was old and famous: in the *Charmides* Socrates says that its house, “the house of Critias son of Dropides”, had been praised by Anacreon, by Solon and by many other poets (157e).
