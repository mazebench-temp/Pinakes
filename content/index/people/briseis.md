## Etymology

Βρισηΐς (Brisēïs): "daughter of Briseus", the same type of name as Chryseis. Homer does not give her a personal name.

{{notes}}

## Outside Homer

Her name, merged with Chryseis', gave the Middle Ages its Cressida. See [[from-briseis-to-cressida]].
