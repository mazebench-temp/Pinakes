υἱὸς δὲ Σφήλοιο καλέσκετο Βουκολίδαο, “and was called the son of Sphelus, son of Bucolus” (15.338). Sphelus is named only in the lineage of his son Iasus, a leader of the Athenians whom Aeneas kills and strips in the rout of Book 15 (15.332, 15.337; see [[iasus]], [[athenians]] and [[aeneas]]), and he is given a patronymic of his own, Βουκολίδης, “son of Bucolus” (see [[bucolus]]). The poem tells nothing else of him, and does not say whether he came to Troy.
