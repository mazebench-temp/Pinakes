Δεξιάδην, “the son of Dexius” (7.15), names Iphinous by his father as Glaucus' spear strikes him (see [[iphinous]] and [[glaucus]]). It is the father's only trace in Homer. Δέξιος looks formed from δεξιός, “right, on the right hand”, whence “dexterous” and “of good omen”.
