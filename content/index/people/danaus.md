---
title: Danaus
greek: Δαναός
kind: person
tags: [argive, king]
summary: The king who fled from Egypt to Argos with his fifty daughters, and ordered them to kill their fifty cousins on their wedding night; only Hypermnestra spared her husband Lynceus. In Theodectes' Lynceus he is killed by the man he meant to kill.
aliases: [Danaos]
relations:
  - son-in-law: lynceus
---

The story is told in Aeschylus' *Suppliants* and foretold in *Prometheus Bound* (853–869); Apollodorus summarizes it (2.1.4–5). The Danaans of Homer take their name from him, in the later genealogies (see [[danaans]] and [[lynceus-and-danaus]]).
