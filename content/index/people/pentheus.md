summary: King of Thebes, grandson of Cadmus, who resisted the worship of his cousin Dionysus and was torn apart by the women of Thebes, his mother Agave among them; his name was read as coming from πένθος, “grief”.
grc: ["^Πενθε*"]
---

In Euripides' *Bacchae* Dionysus lures Pentheus, dressed as a woman, to spy on the maenads on Cithaeron, where his mother and her sisters tear him apart. Tiresias and Dionysus both play on his name (*Bacchae* 367, 508). See [[cadmus]].
