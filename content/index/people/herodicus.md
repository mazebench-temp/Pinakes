Plato's Socrates says that Herodicus, a trainer who fell ill, mixed gymnastics with medicine and so “tortured first himself and then many others”, dragging out a long death under a regimen that left him no time for anything else (*Republic* 3, 406a–b). In the *Protagoras* he is “Herodicus of Selymbria, originally of Megara”, one of those who disguised the sophist's art as gymnastics (316e).
