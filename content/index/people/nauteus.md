## The name

ναύτης is the sailor: a god gives a fair wind “to sailors who long for it” (*Iliad* 7.4; see [[the-fair-wind]]), and in the book that names Nauteus, Euryalus likens Odysseus to “a leader of sailors who are traders” (*Odyssey* 8.162). W. W. Merry counts the Phaeacian names among those “taken from circumstances of seafaring life” (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on *Odyssey* 8.111).
