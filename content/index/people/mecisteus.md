"Son of lord Mecisteus, son of Talaus" (Μηκιστέος υἱὸς Ταλαϊονίδαο ἄνακτος): the title ἄναξ, "lord", belongs to Mecisteus. Ταλαϊονίδης: "son of [[Talaus]]". In Book 23 Homer recalls that he went to Thebes "when Oedipus had fallen" and at the funeral games beat all the Cadmeians (23.677–680). Another Mecisteus, son of Echius, is an Achaean of the Iliad's own generation (8.333, 13.422, 15.339; see [[mecisteus-son-of-echius]]).

## Outside Homer

In later tradition he was a brother of Adrastus and fought in the war of the Seven. Herodotus says that the Theban Melanippus killed him and Tydeus, which is why Cleisthenes of Sicyon brought Melanippus' cult into Sicyon to spite Adrastus (5.67).
