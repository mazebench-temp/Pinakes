## The name

Ἀσφαλίων is formed from ἀσφαλής, “steady, secure”, whose adverb ἀσφαλέως the Iliad uses of a boulder that “runs on surely” down a slope, and of the driver who “keeps them steady” at the turning post (*Iliad* 13.141, 23.325).

## Descriptions

- ὀτρηρὸς θεράπων Μενελάου κυδαλίμοιο, “the ready attendant of glorious Menelaus” (*Odyssey* 4.217), the verse that describes Eteoneus at the door (*Odyssey* 4.23; see [[eteoneus]] and [[ready-attendants]]).
