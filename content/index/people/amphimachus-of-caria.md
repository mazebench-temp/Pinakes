The name means "fighting on both sides" or "fighting all around" (ἀμφί + μάχη). The Achaean catalogue has another Amphimachus, an Epeian leader and son of Cteatus ([[amphimachus]], 2.620). The Carian may be the man who went to war "wearing gold, like a girl" and died at Achilles' hands in the river; the Greek does not say whether that was he or his brother (see [[nastes]]). He is not mentioned again.
