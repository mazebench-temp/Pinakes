In Book 5 Ares rallies the Trojans "in the likeness of Acamas, the swift leader of the Thracians" (5.462). At the start of Book 6 Telamonian Ajax kills him, "the best man among the Thracians, the son of Eussorus, brave and tall", with a spear through the forehead (6.5–11). A son of Antenor bears the same name (2.823).
