# Ἀλεγηνορίδαο (14.503), Promachus' patronymic, counts for father and son; promachus claims it too.
grc: [Ἀλεγηνορίδαο]
relations:
  - child: promachus (14.503)

οὐδὲ γὰρ ἣ Προμάχοιο δάμαρ Ἀλεγηνορίδαο, “for neither will the wife of Promachus, son of Alegenor” (14.503): Peneleos names the father only to name the son, whom Acamas has just killed, and the wife who will not see him come home (14.476–485, 14.501–505; see [[promachus]], [[peneleos]] and [[acamas]]). The poem says nothing else of him.

The name ends in -ήνωρ, from ἀνήρ, “man”, like Agapenor, Prothoenor and Hyperenor; the first part may be ἀλέγω, “heed, care for”.
