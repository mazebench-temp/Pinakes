Antigone's defiance is the subject of Sophocles' *Antigone*; in his *Oedipus at Colonus* she guides her blind father in exile, and Euripides' *Phoenician Women* shows her at the duel of her brothers (see [[antigone-play]] and [[polynices]]). Aristotle names the play, not the woman (1454a1; see [[haemon-threatens-creon]]).
