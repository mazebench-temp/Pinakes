---
title: Callisthenes the general
greek: Καλλισθένης
kind: person
tags: [statesman, warrior, athenian]
summary: An Athenian general of the 360s BCE, condemned to death by the people the day before Ergophilus was tried. He is not Callisthenes of Olynthus, Aristotle's relative and Alexander's historian.
aliases: [Callisthenes]
---

Nothing more is told of him in the *Rhetoric* than his condemnation, which spent the Athenians' anger before the trial of Ergophilus (see [[ergophilus]]).
