---
title: Pylades
greek: Πυλάδης
kind: person
tags: [companion]
summary: Son of Strophius of Phocis, the friend who shared Orestes' exile, helped him kill Clytemnestra and Aegisthus, and went with him to the land of the Taurians; the type of the faithful friend.
---

Homer never names him. In the tragedians Orestes is brought up in Phocis by Strophius, and Pylades, Strophius' son, is his inseparable companion. In Aeschylus' *Libation Bearers* he speaks only once, three lines that remind Orestes of Apollo's command when he hesitates to kill his mother (900–902); in Euripides he marries Electra, and in the *Iphigenia among the Taurians* each friend offers to die for the other (see [[the-recognition-of-orestes-and-iphigenia]], [[electra-recognizes-orestes]] and [[orestes]]).
