Opites ends the first verse of the list, after Asaeus and Autonous (11.301; see [[asaeus]] and [[autonous]]). Like the others, he is given no father, no people and no wound: a name and a death, in a list that answers the question “whom first, whom last” (see [[whom-first-whom-last]] and [[androktasia]]). The narrator calls the nine “the leaders of the Danaans” (11.304), and with them Hector's charge is under way (see [[hector]]).
