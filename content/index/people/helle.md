Ino, the second wife of Athamas, plotted to have Phrixus sacrificed; their mother Nephele sent a golden ram that carried the children through the air; Helle fell into the strait, and Phrixus reached Colchis, where the ram's fleece hung until Jason came for it (Apollodorus 1.9.1; see [[hellespont]] and [[jason]]). A lost tragedy *Helle* is named in the *Poetics* (see [[helle-play]]).
