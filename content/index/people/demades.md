Taken prisoner at Chaeronea in 338 BCE, Demades helped make the peace with Philip and became one of the most powerful men in Athens under Macedonian rule. His sayings were collected in antiquity; Plutarch quotes his remark that Draco wrote his laws in blood (*Solon* 17; see [[draco]]). He was put to death in Macedon in 319. See [[demosthenes]] and [[philip-of-macedon]].
