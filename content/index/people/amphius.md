λινοθώρηξ, "of the linen corselet" ([[with-linen-corselet]]): light body armor of linen. The only other man so described in the Iliad is Ajax son of Oileus (2.529). Diomedes kills the two brothers in Book 11 ([[the-sons-of-merops]]). Telamonian Ajax kills a different Amphius, "son of Selagus, who lived in Paesus, rich in possessions and in grain", in Book 5 (5.612–614). Since Paesus seems to be the Catalogue's Apaesus ([[apaesus]]), the two may go back to one figure of tradition.
