Several men in Homer have this name. Another Antiphus, a son of Talaemenes, leads the Maeonians in the Trojan Catalogue (2.864; see [[antiphus-of-maeonia]]), and Agamemnon kills Antiphus, a son of Priam, in Book 11 (11.101–121). The Odyssey has two more. This one, a Heraclid from Cos, is named only here.
