Xenophon tells the story: the Argives kept claiming a sacred truce whenever Sparta invaded, and Agesipolis, given the right to ignore it by the oracle of Zeus at Olympia, went to Delphi and asked Apollo whether he thought the same as his father about the truce, and Apollo said that he did (*Hellenica* 4.7.2). See [[olympia]] and [[pytho]].
