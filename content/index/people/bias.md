Bias is the last of Nestor's five captains, with [[Pelagon]], [[Alastor]], [[Chromius]] and [[Haemon]]. ποιμὴν λαῶν (“shepherd of the people”) is the formula of a leader ([[shepherd-of-the-people]]), and here it closes the list.

Homer has two other men of the name: an Athenian who leads under Menestheus in the battle at the ships (13.691), and a Trojan whose sons Laogonus and Dardanus Achilles kills (20.460–461). Later genealogy knew a Bias, brother of the seer Melampus, who married Nestor's sister Pero and became the grandfather of Adrastus (Apollodorus 1.9.12–13; see [[talaus]]). The Odyssey tells how Melampus won Pero for his brother without naming the brother (Od. 15.225–242), and nothing in the Iliad connects that Bias with Nestor's captain.
