He is named once, at his son's death: Idomeneus slew “Phaestus, son of the Maeonian Borus, who had come from deep-soiled Tarne” (5.43–44). Μῄων, “a Maeonian”, is the singular of Μῄονες, the people of the country later called Lydia (see [[maeonians]] and [[tarne]]). Nothing else is told of him.

Another Borus, [[borus-son-of-perieres|son of Perieres]], married [[Polydora]], daughter of Peleus, and was called the father of her son [[menesthius-son-of-spercheius|Menesthius]], whom the river [[Spercheius]] had begotten (16.173–178; see [[the-birth-of-menesthius]]).
