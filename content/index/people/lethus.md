Τευταμίδαο, "son of Teutamus", is the old genitive of the patronymic. Lethus himself is known only as a father and a son. With his father Teutamus, the line gives the Pelasgian leaders a pedigree of three generations, like that of Euphemus of the Cicones (2.847).
