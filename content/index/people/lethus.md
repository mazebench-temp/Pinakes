Τευταμίδαο, "son of Teutamus", is the old genitive of the patronymic. Hippothous is "the splendid son of the Pelasgian Lethus" again when he dies in Book 17 (17.288). Lethus himself is known only as a father and a son. With his father Teutamus, the line gives the Pelasgian leaders a pedigree of three generations, like that of Euphemus of the Cicones (2.847).
