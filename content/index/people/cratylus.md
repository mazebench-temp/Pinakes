greek: Κρατύλος

summary: Cratylus, a follower of Heraclitus and, Aristotle says, Plato's first teacher in philosophy; the title figure of Plato's Cratylus.
---

Aristotle says that Plato was familiar in youth with Cratylus and the Heraclitean views (*Metaphysics* Α 6, 987a32–b1), and that Cratylus at last thought one should say nothing, but only moved his finger (Γ 5, 1010a12–15).
