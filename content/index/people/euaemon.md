Εὐαίμων = εὖ ("well") + αἷμα ("blood"): "of good blood". Later books call his son Εὐαιμονίδης, "son of Euaemon" (5.76). Homer names him only as a father and says nothing more about him. His son's realm lay around Ormenium, the spring Hypereia and Mount Titanus (2.734–736).
