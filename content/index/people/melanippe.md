kind: person

summary: Daughter of Aeolus, who bore twins to Poseidon; in Euripides' Melanippe the Wise she defends them with a speech of natural philosophy that Aristotle found unseemly.
aliases: [Melanippē]
refs: [Poetics 1454a31]
---

Her sons Aeolus and Boeotus, exposed and found suckled by a cow, were taken for a portent; Melanippe argued that portents do not exist (see [[melanippe-the-wise]]).
