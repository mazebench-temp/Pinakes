Τεῦκρος δὲ Προθόωνά τʼ ἐνήρατο καὶ Περιφήτην, “and Teucer slew Prothoon and Periphetes” (14.515; see [[teucer]] and [[periphetes]]). He is named only here. The name seems to be formed, like Thoon's, from θοός, “swift”, with πρό, “forward”. Names of the same kind are borne by the Magnesian leader Prothous (2.756) and by the Boeotian Prothoenor, whom Polydamas kills earlier in the book (14.450; see [[thoon]], [[prothous]] and [[prothoenor]]).
