## Etymology

Νεοπτόλεμος (Neoptolemos) = νέος (“new, young”) + πτόλεμος (“war”).

## In Homer

The Iliad names him once, when Achilles, lamenting Patroclus, thinks of the son “who is being reared for me in Scyros” (19.326–327). In the Odyssey Odysseus tells the shade of Achilles that he brought the boy from Scyros himself, and tells of his courage at Troy and his killing of Eurypylus son of Telephus (*Odyssey* 11.505–537).

## Outside Homer

In the *Little Iliad* Odysseus brought him to Troy, and in the *Sack of Troy* he killed Priam at the altar of Zeus (Proclus; see [[neoptolemus-play]], [[eurypylus-son-of-telephus]] and [[sack-of-troy]]).
