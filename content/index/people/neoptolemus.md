Achilles speaks of his son “who is being raised for me on Scyros” (19.326–327), and in the Odyssey Odysseus tells the shade of Achilles of his son's courage at Troy and his killing of Eurypylus son of Telephus (*Odyssey* 11.505–537). In the *Little Iliad* Odysseus brought him to Troy, and in the *Sack of Troy* he killed Priam at the altar of Zeus (Proclus; see [[neoptolemus-play]], [[eurypylus-son-of-telephus]] and [[sack-of-troy]]).
