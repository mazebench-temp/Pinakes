tags: [priest, athenian]
summary: Callias son of Hipponicus (c. 450–c. 370 BCE), the richest Athenian of his day, hereditary torchbearer of the Eleusinian Mysteries and patron of the sophists.
---

Plato's Socrates says that Callias had spent more money on sophists than everyone else together (*Apology* 20a), and the *Protagoras* is set in his house, full of visiting sophists; so is Xenophon's *Symposium*. His family held the office of torchbearer (δᾳδοῦχος) at Eleusis.
