Philocrates proposed the embassy to Philip in 346 BCE and the decree that made the peace and alliance named after him, on which Aeschines and Demosthenes both served as envoys (see [[philip-of-macedon]] and [[demosthenes]]). When the peace came to be seen as a defeat, Hyperides impeached him in 343; he went into exile before the trial and was condemned to death in his absence. Demosthenes' speech *On the False Embassy*, delivered against Aeschines in the same year, makes him the type of the bribed politician.
