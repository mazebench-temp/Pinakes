ὑπερθύμοιο, "high-hearted" (see [[high-hearted]]). Καινεΐδης, "son of Caeneus", is his patronymic. Homer names him only here, between his father, one of the heroes of Nestor's youth, and his son Leonteus, "offshoot of Ares" (2.745). Apollonius counts him among the Argonauts, "Coronus, son of Caeneus", from Gyrton, brave, but not better than his father (*Argonautica* 1.57–58).
