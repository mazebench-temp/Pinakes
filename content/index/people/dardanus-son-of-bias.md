Dardanus and his brother Laogonus, “the two sons of Bias”, die together, thrust from their chariot by Achilles, one by the spear and the other by the sword (20.460–462; see [[laogonus-son-of-bias]], [[bias-father-of-laogonus]] and [[achilles]]). Their deaths are told under [[laogonus-son-of-bias]].

**The name.** Earlier in the book Aeneas began his account of the Trojan royal house with the same name in the same case: “first Zeus the cloud-gatherer fathered Dardanus” (Δάρδανον, 20.215; see [[dardanus]] and [[genealogy-in-battle]]). The son of Zeus founded Dardania, and the Dardanians are named after him (see [[dardania]] and [[dardanians]]); Bias' son is a different man, and the poem tells nothing of him but his death.
