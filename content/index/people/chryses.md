## Etymology

Χρύσης (Chrysēs), "the man of Chryse", named from his town, which in turn is named from χρυσός, gold. His daughter's name, Χρυσηΐς, means "the daughter of Chryses".

## Outside Homer

Plato's Socrates retells the Chryses episode (1.12–42) in plain prose to show the difference between narrative and imitation (*Republic* 3.392e–394a). See [[plato-chryses-diegesis]].
