He is named with Dresus in a half-line, and the verb ἐξενάριξε, “slew and stripped”, serves for both (see [[stripping-the-dead]]). The name returns on the other side: an Achaean Opheltius is one of the nine “leaders of the Danaans” whom Hector kills in Book 11 (11.302–304).
