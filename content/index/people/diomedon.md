title: Diomedon
greek: Διομέδων
kind: person
tags: [athenian]
summary: A tax-farmer who answered criticism of the tax-farmers' trade: if selling is not shameful for you, neither is buying for us.
grc: ["^Διομέδων*"]
---

Nothing more is known of him than Aristotle's report. At Athens the right to collect a tax was sold each year by the city to the highest bidder; the τελῶναι who bought it were the “tax-farmers”. See [[selling-and-buying]].
