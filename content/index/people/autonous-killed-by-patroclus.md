“Adrastus first, and Autonous and Echeclus” (16.694): Autonous is the second of the nine men whom Patroclus kills when the narrator asks him “whom first, whom last” (16.692–697; see [[adrastus-killed-by-patroclus]], [[whom-first-whom-last]] and [[patroclus]]).

The narrator asks the question three times in the Iliad, and twice the answer is nine names in three verses: Hector's victims in Book 11 and Patroclus' here. An Autonous is the second name in each. Hector's is an Achaean, in a verse of the same shape: “Asaeus first, and Autonous and Opites” (11.301; see [[autonous]] and [[hector]]). Patroclus' is a Trojan. The name joins αὐτός, “self”, and νόος, “mind”.
