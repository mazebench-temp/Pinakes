ἐγχεσιμώρους, "fighters with the spear" (see [[fighters-with-the-spear]]), is said of him and his brother. Briseis later calls Lyrnessus "the city of divine Mynes" (19.296), so he was its lord. In the same speech she tells how Achilles killed her husband (19.295–296), and ancient and modern readers have often taken Mynes to be that husband. The text does not say so.
