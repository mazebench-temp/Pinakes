In the speech *Against Leptines* Demosthenes names Leodamas among the advocates of the law and recalls that he had once challenged the grant of honors voted to Chabrias, and lost (20.146). In the *Rhetoric*'s second book he defends himself against a charge brought by Thrasybulus (2.23, 1400a32–36; see [[thrasybulus]]).
