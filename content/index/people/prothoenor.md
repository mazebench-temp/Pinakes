The Catalogue names him only among the Boeotian commanders. The name is a compound of ἀνήρ, "man", like many heroic names in -ήνωρ.

## Elsewhere in Homer

Polydamas spears "Prothoenor, son of Areilycus" through the right shoulder and boasts that the Achaean has taken the spear in his flesh and will lean on it as a staff on his way down to the house of Hades (14.449–457). Peneleos avenges him at once (14.487–505).
