Ἰφιτίδην Ἀρχεπτόλεμον θρασύν, “Archeptolemus, the bold son of Iphitus” (8.128): the patronymic is all the poem tells of him (see [[archeptolemus]]). The name, from ἶφι, “with might”, was borne by others. The Phocian Iphitus, son of Naubolus, is the father of Schedius and Epistrophus (2.517–518, 17.306; see [[iphitus]]), and Iphitus son of Eurytus gave Odysseus the bow with which he killed the suitors (Od. 21.13–41).
