Medon was a νόθος, a son born outside marriage, so Rhene was not Oileus' wife; in Book 13 his wife is Eriopis (13.697). Ὀϊλῆϊ πτολιπόρθῳ, "to Oileus, sacker of cities" (see [[sacker-of-cities]]). Homer names Rhene only here. Her son later lived in exile at Phylace, after killing a kinsman of Eriopis (13.694–697).
