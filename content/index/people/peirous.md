ἥρως, "the hero" ([[heros]]). In Book 4 he is "Peiros son of Imbrasus, who had come from Aenus" (4.519–520). He crushes the ankle of [[Diores]] with a stone and spears him as he lies in the dust. Then Thoas the Aetolian ([[thoas]]) strikes him in the chest, and the two leaders lie side by side, "one of the Thracians, one of the bronze-armored Epeians" (4.517–538).
