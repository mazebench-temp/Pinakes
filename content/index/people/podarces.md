ὄζος Ἄρηος, "offshoot of Ares" (see [[offshoot-of-ares]]). The Catalogue introduces him through his brother: "own brother of great-hearted Protesilaus, younger in birth; the other was both older and braver". His men "did not lack a leader, though they longed for the good man they had lost". He is a substitute, and the poem says so.

The name means "strong in the feet"; as an epithet it belongs to [[Achilles]] (see [[podarkes]]), and Book 2 uses it of him at 2.688. Podarces appears again among the Phthians who defend the ships in Book 13, beside [[Medon]] (13.693–700). In later epic, Penthesilea the Amazon kills him (Quintus of Smyrna, *Posthomerica* 1).
