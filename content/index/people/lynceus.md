Lynceus and Hypermnestra had a son, Abas, father of Acrisius and Proetus; Acrisius' daughter Danae bore Perseus (Apollodorus 2.2.1). He is not the sharp-eyed Lynceus of the Argonauts. See [[lynceus-play]] and [[lynceus-and-danaus]].
