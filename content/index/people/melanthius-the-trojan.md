He closes the list of kills that began with Ajax's (6.5–36): fourteen Trojans and allies in thirty-two lines, killed by ten Achaean leaders, and not one Achaean killed in return (see [[androktasia]] and [[the-war-in-small]]). The next Trojan is taken alive (6.37–38; see [[adrastus-the-trojan]]). In the Odyssey the name belongs to the goatherd Melanthius, son of Dolius, who kicks the disguised Odysseus and later brings arms to the suitors (Od. 17.212–253, 22.135–200).
