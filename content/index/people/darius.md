Darius took the throne after the death of Cambyses and the overthrow of the Magus (Herodotus 3.61–88). His generals were defeated at Marathon in 490 BCE. He was preparing a new expedition when Egypt revolted, in the fourth year after Marathon, and he died the next year, leaving both tasks to his son Xerxes (Herodotus 7.1–4). See [[xerxes]] and [[marathon]].
