Ajax has come up with his shield like a tower, the Trojans around Odysseus have scattered, and Menelaus is leading Odysseus out of the throng (11.485–488; see [[ajax]] and [[odysseus]]). Then Ajax leaps on the Trojans. Doryclus is the first and the only one of the five named men who is said to die (εἷλε, “killed”); the other four are wounded, and Ajax sweeps the plain like a river in winter flood (11.490–497; see [[pandocus]], [[lysander]], [[pyrasus-the-trojan]] and [[pylartes]]).

Πριαμίδην νόθον υἱόν, “a bastard son of Priam” (11.490): Priam's bastard sons fight and die beside their brothers, Democoon (4.499), Isus earlier in this book (11.102), and Cebriones (16.738; see [[nothos]], [[isus]], [[democoon]], [[cebriones]] and [[sons-of-priam]]). Doryclus is named nowhere else.
