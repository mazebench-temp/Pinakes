Aristophon was active in Athenian politics from the restoration of the democracy in 403 BCE to the 340s, and boasted that he had been indicted for illegal proposals seventy-five times and acquitted every time (Aeschines, *Against Ctesiphon* 194). In the Social War he and Chares accused the generals Iphicrates and Timotheus of treason after the defeat at Embata (356 BCE); Iphicrates was acquitted and Timotheus fined. The exchange Aristotle reports probably belongs to that trial. See [[iphicrates]].
