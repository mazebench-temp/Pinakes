## Etymology

Κάλχας (Kalchas). The name has been linked with κάλχη (the purple murex, and a purple dye) and with καλχαίνω ("ponder darkly"), but its origin is unknown.

{{notes}}

## Outside Homer

In the *Cypria* it was he who declared that Iphigenia must be sacrificed. A later story, attributed to Hesiod by Strabo (14.1.27), has him die of grief after losing a contest of prophecy to the seer Mopsus.
