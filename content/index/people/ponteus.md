## The name

πόντος is the sea, above all the open sea (see [[sea]]); the herald of Alcinous, Pontonous, has a name made from the same word (see [[pontonous]]). W. W. Merry, who notes that almost all Phaeacian names “are taken from circumstances of seafaring life”, takes Ponteus and Prymneus as “derived at haphazard from sea-terms”, and Proreus with them (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on *Odyssey* 8.111; see [[prymneus]] and [[proreus]]).
