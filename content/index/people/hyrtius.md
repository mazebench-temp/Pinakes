Ὕρτιον … Γυρτιάδην, “Hyrtius, / son of Gyrtius” (14.511–512), is the first name the narrator gives in answer to his question to the Muses, who was the first of the Achaeans to win the bloody spoils of men when Poseidon had turned the battle (14.508–510; see [[invocation-of-the-muses]], [[bloody-spoils]] and [[poseidon]]). Telamonian Ajax stabs him (see [[ajax]]). He is “leader of the stout-hearted Mysians”, Μυσῶν ἡγήτορα καρτεροθύμων (14.512; see [[mysians]] and [[karterothymos]]); the Catalogue named Chromis and the augur Ennomus as the leaders of the Mysians (2.858; see [[chromis]] and [[ennomus]]). Homer says nothing more of him. His name and his father's, Ὕρτιος and Γύρτιος, differ by one letter.
