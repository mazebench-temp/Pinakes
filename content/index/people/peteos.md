"The son of Peteos" is how the Iliad names Menestheus again when Agamemnon finds him idle (4.327) and when Sarpedon attacks his tower (12.331, 12.355).

## Outside Homer

Plutarch makes Peteos the son of Orneus, son of [[Erechtheus]] (*Theseus* 32). The town of [[Orneae]] was said to take its name from this Orneus.
