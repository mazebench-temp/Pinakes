Talaemenes is known only from this line. The name looks like a compound of ταλα-, "enduring" (as in ταλαίφρων, "enduring-hearted"), and μένος, "might": "of enduring might". His sons' mother is the lake itself, that is, its nymph ([[gygaean-lake]]). The Trojan catalogue names a divine parent only twice: Aphrodite, the mother of Aeneas (2.820), and this lake.
