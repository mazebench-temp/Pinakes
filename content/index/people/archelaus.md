summary: Archelaus, king of Macedon (about 413–399 BCE), who brought Euripides and Agathon to his court; Socrates refused his invitation.
aliases: [Archelaus of Macedon]
grc: ["^Ἀρχέλα*"]
---

In Plato's *Gorgias* Polus holds him up as the happiest of men, a slave's son who murdered his way to the throne (470d–471d; see [[polus]]). Euripides wrote a play named after his ancestor, the *Archelaus*, while living at his court. See [[euripides]] and [[agathon]].
