Odysseus sees Creon's daughter Megara, wife of Heracles, in the underworld (*Odyssey* 11.269). In tragedy Creon is the Theban king in Sophocles' *Antigone* and *Oedipus the King*, and the regent in Euripides' *Phoenician Women*. He is not the Creon of the Iliad, father of Lycomedes (see [[creon]]). For the threat of his son see [[haemon-threatens-creon]].
