## The name

ἐρετμόν is the oar (see [[oars]]), and the oar is the Phaeacians' mark: the Odyssey calls them φιλήρετμοι, “lovers of the oar”, and δολιχήρετμοι, “of the long oars” (*Odyssey* 5.386, 8.96, 8.191; see [[lovers-of-the-oar]] and [[long-oared]]). W. W. Merry counts the Phaeacian names among those “taken from circumstances of seafaring life” (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on *Odyssey* 8.111).
