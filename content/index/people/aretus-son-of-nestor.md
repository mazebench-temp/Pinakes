The name seems to be formed like ἀρητός, “prayed for”, from ἀράομαι, “pray”, as is that of the Trojan Aretus, whom Automedon kills over the horses of Achilles (*Iliad* 17.494–542; see [[aretus]]). Nothing joins the two. Nestor's son is named twice, among his brothers on the polished stones before Nestor's doors and at the sacrifice, where he comes from the chamber with the basin and the basket (*Odyssey* 3.412–415, 3.440–442).

## Outside Homer

Apollodorus names Aretus among the seven sons of Nestor by Anaxibia, daughter of Cratieus (*Library* 1.9.9).
