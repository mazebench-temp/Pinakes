## The name

πολύς, “many”, and νηῦς, “ship”: “of many ships”. W. W. Merry counts the Phaeacian names among those “taken from circumstances of seafaring life” (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on *Odyssey* 8.111). The word for ship also ends the names of Acroneus and Anabesineus in the same list (*Odyssey* 8.111, 8.113; see [[acroneus]] and [[anabesineus]]), and the names of his father and his son are “builder” and “sea-girt” (see [[tecton]] and [[amphialus]]).
