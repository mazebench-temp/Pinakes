{{quote:04e81115-9d6c-5a59-b445-1b4070fb3827}}

αὐτὰρ ἔπειτα Πύλωνα καὶ Ὄρμενον ἐξενάριξεν, “and then he slew and stripped Pylon and Ormenus” (12.187). After Damasus, Polypoetes kills two more men in a single verse, with no wound described (see [[polypoetes]], [[damasus]], [[ormenus-killed-by-polypoetes]] and [[stripping-the-dead]]). The verse is built like the one in which Odysseus kills Thoon and Ennomus, αὐτὰρ ἔπειτα Θόωνα καὶ Ἔννομον ἐξενάριξε (11.422; see [[thoon]] and [[ennomus-the-trojan]]). He is one of eight men the two Lapiths kill at the gate, and of the eight only Iamenus and Orestes are named before they die (12.139, 12.182–194; see [[leonteus]], [[iamenus]] and [[orestes-the-trojan]]).
