He is the third of the four Trojans whom Ajax wounds after killing Doryclus (11.490–491; see [[doryclus]], [[pandocus]], [[lysander]], [[pylartes]] and [[ajax]]). οὖτα, “wounded”, does not say whether he died, and he is named nowhere else. In the Catalogue, Πύρασος is a town of Protesilaus' realm in Thessaly, “flowering Pyrasus” (2.695; see [[pyrasus]]).
