summary: Athenian politician and general of the first half of the fourth century BCE, one of the envoys to Sparta in 371; against Mixidemides he argued from the trial of the Dread Goddesses on the Areopagus.
grc: ["^Αὐτοκλ*"]
---

Xenophon reports the blunt speech Autocles made at Sparta in 371 BCE, on the eve of Leuctra, when he was one of the Athenian envoys sent to make peace (*Hellenica* 6.3.7–9). See [[mixidemides]].
