Αἴας δʼ ἕλε Λαοδάμαντα / ἡγεμόνα πρυλέων Ἀντήνορος ἀγλαὸν υἱόν, “and Ajax killed Laodamas, / leader of the foot soldiers, the splendid son of Antenor” (15.516–517; see [[ajax]] and [[antenor]]). He dies in the same verse as Schedius, whom Hector kills (15.515; see [[schedius-son-of-perimedes]]). πρυλέες are men who fight on foot (11.49, 12.77). Ajax's spear killed another son of Antenor, Archelochus, in *Iliad* 14 (14.463–464; see [[archelochus]]). The name joins λαός, “people”, and the stem of δάμνημι, “tame”.

The Odyssey's Laodamas is another man, the son of Alcinous, who invites Odysseus to the games of the Phaeacians (*Odyssey* 8.143–151).
