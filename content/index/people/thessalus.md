Ἡρακλεΐδαο, "son of Heracles", is his patronymic here. Homer names him only in this line. He never uses the name of Thessaly or of the Thessalians: Θεσσαλός appears in the poem only as the name of this man. In later genealogy his mother was Chalciope, the daughter of Eurypylus, king of Cos (Apollodorus 2.7.8).
