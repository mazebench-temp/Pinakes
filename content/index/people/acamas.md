Ἀκάμας means "tireless" (ἀ-, "not" + κάμνω, "grow weary"). A different Acamas leads the Thracians twenty lines later (2.844). The son of Antenor fights at the Achaean wall (12.98–100) and avenges his brother Archelochus in Book 14, killing the Boeotian Promachus and boasting over him (14.476–485). Meriones kills an Acamas in Book 16 (16.342–344); since the Thracian Acamas died in Book 6, this is usually taken to be Antenor's son.
