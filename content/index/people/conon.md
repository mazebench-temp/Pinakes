Conon was one of the Athenian generals at Aegospotami (405 BCE) and escaped with eight ships to Evagoras (Xenophon, *Hellenica* 2.1.28–29). As commander of the Persian king's fleet with Pharnabazus he defeated the Spartans at Cnidus (4.3.10–12) and in 393 sailed to Athens and helped rebuild the walls (4.8.9–10). See [[evagoras]].
