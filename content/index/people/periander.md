tags: [tyrant, corinthia]
summary: Tyrant of Corinth (c. 627–587 BCE), sometimes counted among the Seven Sages, who arbitrated between Athens and Mytilene in their war for Sigeum.
aliases: [Periander of Corinth]
---

Herodotus tells of his cruelty, of the advice of Thrasybulus of Miletus, who taught him tyranny by cutting off the tallest ears in a field of grain (5.92ζ), and of his quarrel with his son Lycophron (3.50–53). When Athens and Mytilene fought for Sigeum, in the war in which the poet Alcaeus threw away his shield, the two cities chose Periander as arbitrator, and he gave each side what it held (5.94–95; see [[sigeum]] and [[alcaeus]]).
