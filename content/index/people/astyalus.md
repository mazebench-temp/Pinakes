One line holds the whole killing: the victim, the verb ἔπεφνε (“killed”), and the killer with his epithet, μενεπτόλεμος, “steadfast in battle” (see [[steadfast-in-battle]]). Nothing is told of Astyalus but his name, which occurs only here. See [[androktasia]].
