Δαίτορά τε Χρομίον τε, “Daetor and Chromius”, opens the second verse of Teucer's list of eight, men otherwise unknown whom he brings down with his arrows (8.274–277; see [[teucer]] and [[chromius-killed-by-teucer]]). Nothing is told of him but his name and his death.
