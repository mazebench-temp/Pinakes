Later mythographers tie him to the same stretch of coast. Priam's first wife was Arisbe, daughter of the seer Merops; after she bore him a son, Aesacus, Priam gave her to Hyrtacus (Apollodorus 3.12.5). The story links three names from these lines of the Catalogue, [[Merops]], [[Arisbe]] and Hyrtacus. In Virgil, Nisus and Hippocoon are sons of a Hyrtacus (*Aeneid* 9.177, 5.492).
