Three men called Antiphus fight in the Iliad: this Maeonian; an Achaean, son of Thessalus, who leads the islanders of Cos and its neighbors with his brother Pheidippus ([[antiphus]], 2.678); and a son of Priam, whom Agamemnon kills in Book 11 (11.101–121). The Maeonian is not mentioned again.
