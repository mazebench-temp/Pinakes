title: Idrieus
greek: Ἰδριεύς

tags: [king, caria]
summary: Idrieus, probably the dynast of Caria (351–344 BCE), brother and successor of Mausolus and Artemisia.
---

Diodorus records his rule in Caria after Artemisia (16.45, 16.69). When he had been in chains, as Androtion's simile implies, is not known, and the identification is not certain.
