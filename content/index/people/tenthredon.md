{{quote:33d9dcf5-a1c2-5aef-8d8b-1908a9419f79}}

Nothing else is known of him. Homer names him only in the formula that introduces his son, "Prothous, son of Tenthredon". The word τενθρηδών is also the Greek name of a kind of wasp. His son is the last leader named in the Catalogue (2.756–759).
