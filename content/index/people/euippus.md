“Ipheus and Euippus” (16.417): Euippus is the eighth of the nine men whom Patroclus kills one after another, with no blow told (16.415–418; see [[erymas-killed-by-patroclus]] and [[patroclus]]).

The name joins εὖ, “well”, and ἵππος, “horse”. Euippus is named only here.
