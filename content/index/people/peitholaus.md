---
title: Peitholaus
greek: Πειθόλαος
kind: person
tags: [tyrant, thessaly]
summary: Peitholaus of Pherae, who with his brothers Tisiphonus and Lycophron and their sister Thebe killed the tyrant Alexander of Pherae (c. 358 BCE) and then ruled the city until Philip of Macedon expelled them.
---

Plutarch tells the murder of Alexander by Thebe and her brothers (*Pelopidas* 35). A Peitholaus “the Thessalian”, probably the same man, was made an Athenian citizen and lost the grant by a decision of the court ([Demosthenes] 59.91).
