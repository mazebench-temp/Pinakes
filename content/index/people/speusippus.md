---
title: Speusippus
greek: Σπεύσιππος
kind: person
tags: [athenian]
summary: Speusippus, a man disabled by a stroke whom Polyeuctus mocked; perhaps Plato's nephew and successor at the Academy (d. 339 BCE), who was paralyzed in his last years, though the identification is uncertain.
---

Diogenes Laertius says that the philosopher Speusippus was crippled by paralysis in old age (4.3).
