ἀντίθεος = ἀντί (equal to) + θεός (god): godlike. The name means "much spoken of, famous" (πολύς + φήμη).

## Outside Homer

Apollonius includes a Polyphemus son of Elatus among the Argonauts, an old Lapith who had fought the Centaurs in his youth (*Argonautica* 1.40–44). The Cyclops of the Odyssey is a different figure with the same name (see [[polyphemus-the-cyclops]]).
