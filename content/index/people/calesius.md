θεράπων, “attendant” (6.18), is the man who serves a warrior and often drives his horses (see [[attendants-of-ares]]). Calesius was then his master's ὑφηνίοχος, a word found only here: ὑπό (“under”) + ἡνίοχος (“charioteer”), the driver who holds the reins for the fighter beside him. The two die together, and the verb is dual: “the two of them went down beneath the earth” (6.19). His name occurs only here.
