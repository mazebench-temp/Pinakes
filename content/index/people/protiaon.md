Ἀστυνόῳ Προτιάονος υἱέϊ, “to Astynous, son of Protiaon” (15.455): the father is named only to name the son, to whom Polydamas gives the horses of his fallen charioteer (see [[astynous-son-of-protiaon]] and [[polydamas]]). The name occurs nowhere else in Homer.
