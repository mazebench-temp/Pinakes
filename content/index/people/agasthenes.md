"Son of lord Agasthenes, son of Augeas" (υἱὸς Ἀγασθένεος Αὐγηϊάδαο ἄνακτος): the title ἄναξ, "lord", belongs to Agasthenes. Αὐγηϊάδης: "son of [[Augeas]]". The name joins ἄγαν, "very", and σθένος, "strength". Through him the fourth leader of the Epeians descends from the old king of Elis.
