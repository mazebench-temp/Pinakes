The poem names him twice, both times at his son's death: Ajax “struck the son of Anthemion” (Ἀνθεμίωνος υἱόν, 4.473), and “so was Simoeisius, son of Anthemion, stripped of his armor” (Ἀνθεμίδην, 4.488). Ἀνθεμίδης is a shortened patronymic, formed as Idomeneus, son of Deucalion (Δευκαλίων), is called Δευκαλίδης (13.307). The name is formed from ἄνθεμον (“flower”).

He is one of the “dear parents” whom his son did not live to repay for his rearing (4.477–478). His wife is not named. Her parents kept flocks on Ida, and she bore their grandson by the Simoeis as she came down from the mountain (4.474–477). See [[simoeisius]].
