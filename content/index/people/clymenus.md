Κλύμενος is the participle κλύμενος, “famed”, of κλύω, “hear”, whose root is that of κλέος, “fame” (see [[kleos]]). The Odyssey names him once, in the genitive, Κλυμένοιο θυγατρῶν, “of the daughters of Clymenus” (*Odyssey* 3.452): he had more than one daughter, and Eurydice was the eldest (πρέσβα). Homer does not say who he was or where he ruled.
