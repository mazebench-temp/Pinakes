Αὐτόφονος = αὐτός (“self, one's own”) + the root of φόνος (“killing”). Later Greek used the adjective αὐτοφόνος of one who kills with his own hand, or kills his own kin. Father and son both have names made from the word for killing (see [[polyphontes]]). Nothing else is known of him.
