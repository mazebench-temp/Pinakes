## Outside Homer

Andromache tells Hector how Achilles killed her father and her seven brothers but did not strip Eetion's body. He burned him in his armor and heaped a mound over him, and the mountain nymphs planted elms around it (Iliad 6.414–420). See [[sack-of-thebe]].
