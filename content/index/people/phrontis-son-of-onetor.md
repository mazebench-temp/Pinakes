## The name

The name is the noun φροντίς, “thought, care”, which Homer does not otherwise use. The Iliad gives it to a woman, the wife of Panthous and mother of Euphorbus and Hyperenor (*Iliad* 17.40; see [[phrontis]]). Nothing joins the two. His father's name, Ὀνήτωρ, is also borne by a Trojan priest of Zeus in the Iliad, another man (*Iliad* 16.604; see [[onetor-father-of-phrontis]] and [[onetor]]).
