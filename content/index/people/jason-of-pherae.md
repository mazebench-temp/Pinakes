Xenophon has Polydamas of Pharsalus describe him to the Spartans: tireless, master of his appetites, with six thousand picked mercenaries, and planning to make Thessaly the first power in Greece and then to turn on the Persian king (*Hellenica* 6.1.4–16). Chosen *tagos*, commander of all the Thessalians, he was assassinated in 370 BCE while preparing to preside over the Pythian games (6.4.27–32). Aristotle quotes another of his sayings in the *Politics*: he went hungry whenever he was not a tyrant, for he did not know how to be a private man (3.4, 1277a24–25). He is not the Jason of the Argonauts (see [[jason]]).
