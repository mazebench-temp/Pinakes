## In Book 1

{{quote:12f862e3-d8b3-53d6-b3d2-addcb2b37d7d}}

He is listed with Ajax and Odysseus as a "man of counsel" (ἀνὴρ βουληφόρος) fit to lead the embassy.

## Outside Homer

Idomeneus leads eighty ships from Crete (Iliad 2.645–652) and has a book of glory in the fighting at the ships (Book 13). He boasts there of his descent from Zeus through Minos and Deucalion (13.449–454).
