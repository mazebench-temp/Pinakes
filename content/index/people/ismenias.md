Ismenias, the rich and powerful leader of the anti-Spartan party at Thebes, was arrested when the Spartans seized the Cadmea in 382 BCE and put to death after a trial (Xenophon, *Hellenica* 5.2.25–36). Aristotle does not say that the Ismenias of the paternity case is he. See [[stilbon]], [[thettaliscus]] and [[dodona]].
