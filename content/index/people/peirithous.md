## Outside Homer

Peirithous is a son of Zeus by the wife of Ixion (Iliad 14.317–318). His son Polypoetes leads the Lapiths to Troy, born "on the day when Peirithous punished the shaggy beasts" (2.740–744). The Odyssey places the quarrel with the Centaurs in his house (21.295–304). With Theseus he later tried to carry off Persephone from the underworld; Odyssey 11.631 names the two together among the heroes of old.
