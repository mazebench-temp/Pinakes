Γυρτιάδην, “son of Gyrtius” (14.512), is all that Homer says of him: his son Hyrtius, “leader of the stout-hearted Mysians”, is the first man Telamonian Ajax kills when Poseidon has turned the battle (14.511–512; see [[hyrtius]], [[ajax]] and [[mysians]]). Father and son have names that differ by one letter, Γύρτιος and Ὕρτιος.
