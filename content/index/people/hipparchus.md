---
title: Hipparchus
greek: Ἵππαρχος
kind: person
tags: [tyrant, athenian]
summary: Son of Pisistratus and brother of the tyrant Hippias, killed by Harmodius and Aristogeiton at the Panathenaea of 514 BCE. The Athenians remembered him as the tyrant; Thucydides insists that Hippias was.
grc: ["^Ἵππαρχ*"]
---

Thucydides tells the story to correct the common version: Hippias was the eldest son and the ruler, and Hipparchus was killed because of a love affair, after which the tyranny grew harsher (6.54–59). See [[harmodius]], [[aristogeiton]] and [[pisistratus]].
