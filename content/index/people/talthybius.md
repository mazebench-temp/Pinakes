## Outside Homer

Talthybius was honored as a hero at Sparta, where the Talthybiadae, a family of heralds, claimed descent from him. Herodotus tells how "the wrath of Talthybius" (Ταλθυβίου μῆνις) fell on the Spartans after they killed the Persian king's heralds (7.134–137). He uses the same word, μῆνις, as the Iliad's first line.
