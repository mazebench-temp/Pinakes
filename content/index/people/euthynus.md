title: Euthynus
greek: Εὔθυνος
kind: person
tags: [athenian]
summary: A man of whom Isocrates said that it would be strange if Euthynus had learned something and he himself could not discover it.
grc: ["^Εὔθυν*"]
---

Nothing in Isocrates' surviving works matches the remark. One of his court speeches is against a Euthynus, whom Nicias accused of keeping back money deposited with him (21, *Against Euthynus*); whether Aristotle's Euthynus is the same man is not known. See [[isocrates]].
