Λάμπος, from λάμπω, "shine". He sits among the elders at the Scaean gates with his brothers Clytius and Hicetaon (see [[trojan-elders]]). The line that names the three returns in Aeneas' genealogy, where they are sons of Laomedon with Tithonus and Priam (3.147 = 20.238). In Book 15 his son Dolops, "whom Lampus, son of Laomedon, fathered, the best of his sons", attacks Meges, and Menelaus kills him (15.525–544).

The name is also given to one of Hector's horses (8.185) and to one of the horses of Dawn (*Odyssey* 23.246).
