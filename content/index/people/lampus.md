Λάμπος, from λάμπω, "shine". He sits among the elders at the Scaean gates with his brothers Clytius and Hicetaon (see [[trojan-elders]]). The line that names the three returns in Aeneas' genealogy, where they are sons of Laomedon with Tithonus and Priam (3.147 = 20.238). His son Dolops is “the strongest son that Lampus, / son of Laomedon, fathered” (15.526–527; see [[dolops-son-of-lampus]]).

The name is also given to one of Hector's horses (8.185) and to one of the horses of Dawn (*Odyssey* 23.246).
