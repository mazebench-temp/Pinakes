Theagenes is one of the early tyrants who rose as champions of the people. Aristotle says in the *Politics* that he gained the trust of the Megarians by slaughtering the cattle of the well-to-do, which he caught grazing by the river (5.5, 1305a). Thucydides tells that Cylon, an Athenian who had won at Olympia, married his daughter and with troops from him seized the Acropolis of Athens; the attempt failed, and the killing of Cylon's men at the altars brought a curse on the Alcmaeonids (1.126.3–12).
