summary: Athenian general (d. 357 BCE), victor over the Spartan fleet off Naxos in 376, whose statue stood in the agora; Leodamas challenged the honors voted to him.
---

Chabrias commanded Athenian fleets and armies in the 370s and served as a general for hire in Cyprus and Egypt. He defeated the Spartans at sea off Naxos in 376 BCE (Diodorus 15.34–35). Nepos says that his statue in the agora showed him in the stance he had ordered his men to take against Agesilaus, kneeling with shield on knee and spear held forward (*Chabrias* 1). He was killed in the harbor of Chios at the beginning of the Social War, in 357, when he would not leave his ship (Diodorus 16.7; Nepos, *Chabrias* 4).
