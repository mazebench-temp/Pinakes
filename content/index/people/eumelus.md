## Etymology

Εὔμηλος = εὖ ("well") + μῆλον ("sheep, flock"): "rich in flocks", a name that suits a family of Thessalian breeders of horses and sheep.

## Family

{{quote:45774d85-3917-5018-9f00-2ec27fb17279}}

Son of Admetus and Alcestis, "most beautiful in form of the daughters of Pelias". Φηρητιάδης, "descendant of Pheres" (2.763), names him after his grandfather [[Pheres]], the father of Admetus. In the Odyssey his wife is Iphthime, the sister of Penelope, and he has his home in Pherae (*Odyssey* 4.797–798; see [[iphthime]]).

## The mares

{{quote:3d3eb9d9-432c-5e1a-8b5b-b5977726e84c}}

When the narrator asks the Muse who were the best of the men and of the horses, the horses come first, and they are Eumelus' (see [[the-mares-of-eumelus]]). The answer is qualified at once: the best of all were Achilles and his horses (2.769–770; see [[best-of-the-achaeans]]).
