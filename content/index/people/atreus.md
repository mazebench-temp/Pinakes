## In Book 1

{{quote:90fba18f-532e-5cd7-977c-9bf5c6530598}}

Ἀτρεΐδης = Ἀτρεύς + -ίδης (son of). Book 1 uses the patronymic for Agamemnon more often than his name.

{{quote:7d45b576-9b13-50b6-8ecf-6fb47604499e}}

Ἀτρεΐδα δύω (dual), Ἀτρεΐδαι (plural): the two sons of Atreus together. See [[atreidae]].

## Outside Homer

The Iliad names Atreus in the descent of Agamemnon's scepter (2.105–107). Later tragedy made his house a byword for crime: he fed his brother Thyestes his own children (Aeschylus, *Agamemnon* 1583–1602). Homer says nothing of that.
