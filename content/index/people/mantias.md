summary: Mantias of Thoricus, Athenian politician of the early fourth century BCE, who was forced by an oath of the woman Plangon to acknowledge her son Boeotus as his own.
grc: ["^Μαντίᾳ"]
---

The story is told in two speeches of the Demosthenic corpus against Boeotus, written for Mantias' legitimate son Mantitheus. Plangon, whom Mantias had not married, claimed that her sons were his; he challenged her to swear to it, expecting her to refuse, and she swore, so that he had to enroll them (Demosthenes 39.3–4, 40.10–11).
