Dresus and Opheltius share half a line: a name each and one verb, ἐξενάριξε, “slew and stripped” (see [[stripping-the-dead]]). The two men Euryalus kills next are given a mother, a father and a grandfather (6.21–26; see [[bucolion]]). The name Dresus occurs only here.
