The name is probably related to σχεδόν, "near", as in σχεδίην, "at close quarters".

## Elsewhere in Homer

In Book 15 Hector kills "Schedius, son of Perimedes, leader of the Phocians" (15.515–516). In Book 17 he kills "Schedius, son of great-hearted Iphitus, far the best of the Phocians, who lived in famous [[Panopeus]] and ruled over many men" (17.306–308). Either the poem knows two Phocian leaders of this name, or it has slipped; readers ancient and modern have taken both views. The man of the Catalogue is the son of Iphitus.

## Outside Homer

Apollodorus lists Schedius and his brother among the suitors of Helen (3.10.8).
