Ἀμαρυγκεΐδης: "son of Amarynceus". Nestor recalls the day when "the Epeians buried lord Amarynceus at [[Buprasium]], and his sons set out prizes for the king". Nestor won the boxing, the wrestling, the footrace and the spear throw, and lost only the chariot race, to the twin sons of Actor (23.629–642).
