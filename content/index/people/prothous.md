Πρόθοος θοός: "swift Prothous". The epithet repeats the second half of the name, which means something like "running ahead" (πρό "before" + θοός "swift"). Homer sometimes plays on the meaning of a name in this way. He names Prothous only here, as the last of the Achaean leaders (see [[catalogue-of-ships]]).
