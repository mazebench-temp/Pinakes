Ἀγλαΐη means "splendor, beauty" (compare ἀγλαός, "splendid"). In Hesiod it is the name of the youngest of the Graces, whom Hephaestus married (*Theogony* 945–946). The handsomest man in the army has fitting parents: his father [[Charopus]] is "bright-eyed". Homer names her only here.
