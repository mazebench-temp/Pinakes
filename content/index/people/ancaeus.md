"The son of Ancaeus, powerful Agapenor": Homer names this Ancaeus only as a father. Another Ancaeus, "of Pleuron", wrestled the young Nestor at the funeral games of Amarynceus and lost (23.635; see [[ancaeus-of-pleuron]]).

## Outside Homer

Later writers made Ancaeus the son of Lycurgus of Tegea. Apollonius has him join the Argonauts wearing a bearskin and carrying a double axe (*Argonautica* 1.164–171). He was one of the heroes of the Calydonian boar hunt, and the boar killed him (Apollodorus 1.8.2; Ovid, *Metamorphoses* 8.391–402).
