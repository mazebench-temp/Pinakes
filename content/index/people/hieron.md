Hieron succeeded his brother Gelon at Syracuse and defeated the Etruscans off Cumae in 474 BCE; Pindar's first Pythian ode celebrates him (see [[pindar]]). Xenophon's dialogue *Hiero* sets him talking with Simonides about the unhappiness of a tyrant (see [[simonides]] and [[gelon]]).
