Ergophilus commanded for Athens in the Hellespont in 363/2 BCE and was brought to trial after his command. Demosthenes, listing the men the Athenians had punished for betraying the city, names him with Cephisodotus, Timomachus, Ergocles and Dionysius, who were “sentenced to death or fined large sums” (*On the False Embassy* 180).
