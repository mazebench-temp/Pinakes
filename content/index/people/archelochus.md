μάχης εὖ εἰδότε πάσης, "well skilled in every kind of fighting", is in the dual: the praise belongs to both brothers. The name means "leader of the company" or "of the ambush" (ἄρχω, "lead" + λόχος, "ambush, armed band"). The same lines return at 12.98–100. In Book 14 Telamonian Ajax throws at Polydamas, misses, and kills Archelochus instead, "for the gods had planned his death" (14.463–464). His brother Acamas avenges him (14.476–485).
