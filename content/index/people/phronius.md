Φρονίοιο Νοήμονα φαίδιμον υἱόν, “Noemon, the shining son of Phronius” (*Odyssey* 2.386): Phronius is named only with his son, here and twice in Book 4 of the Odyssey, υἱὸς Φρονίοιο Νοήμων, “Noemon, son of Phronius” (*Odyssey* 4.630, 4.648; see [[noemon-son-of-phronius]]). The poem says nothing else of him, though when Noemon has told the suitors about his ship he goes off πρὸς δώματα πατρός, “to his father's house” (*Odyssey* 4.657). The name is formed from φρονέω, “think”, as his son's is the adjective νοήμων, “discerning”.
