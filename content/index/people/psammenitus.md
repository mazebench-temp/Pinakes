Psammenitus reigned six months after his father Amasis and was defeated by Cambyses. In Herodotus' story Cambyses tested him: he saw his daughter carrying water in slave's clothes and his son led to execution, and did not weep, but when he saw one of his old companions, now a beggar, he wept and struck his head. Asked why, he answered that his own sorrows were too great for tears, but his friend's misfortune deserved them (3.14). See [[egypt]].
