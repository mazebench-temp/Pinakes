Πειραΐδης, “son of Peiraeus”, is all that Homer tells of him: the grandfather's name completes the attendant's pedigree. In the Odyssey a different Peiraeus, son of Clytius, is the companion of Telemachus to whom he entrusts the seer Theoclymenus (Od. 15.539–546).
