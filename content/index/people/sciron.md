---
title: Sciron
greek: Σκίρων
kind: person
tags: [megarid]
summary: Sciron, the brigand of the cliffs on the road from Megara to the Isthmus, who kicked travelers into the sea until Theseus killed him.
---

Plutarch tells how Theseus, on his way from Troezen to Athens, killed Sinis the pine-bender and then Sciron, throwing him from his own cliffs (*Theseus* 8, 10).
