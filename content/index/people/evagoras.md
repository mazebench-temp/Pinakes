After the defeat at Aegospotami in 405 BCE Conon fled with eight ships to Evagoras in Cyprus (Xenophon, *Hellenica* 2.1.29). Isocrates' *Evagoras*, a prose encomium written after his death, tells his rise and reign and praises him for receiving Conon (51–57). See [[conon]] and [[isocrates]].
