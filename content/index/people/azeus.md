Ἀζεΐδης = Ἀζεύς + -ίδης, "son of". The patronymic is his only trace in Homer. It gives the sons of Ares a mortal ancestry on their mother's side, through three generations: Azeus, Actor, Astyoche. The name does not occur again in Homer.
