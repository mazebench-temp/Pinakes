Xerxes succeeded his father Darius, who had died while preparing a second expedition against Greece; in the second year of his reign he marched against the Egyptians, who had revolted, and subdued them (Herodotus 7.1–7). In 480 BCE he crossed the Hellespont, forced the pass of Thermopylae and burned Athens, and was defeated at sea at Salamis (Herodotus 7–8). See [[darius]].
