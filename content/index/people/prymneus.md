## The name

πρύμνη is the stern of a ship, where the helmsman steers (see [[stern]]). W. W. Merry, who notes that almost all Phaeacian names “are taken from circumstances of seafaring life”, takes Prymneus and Ponteus as “derived at haphazard from sea-terms”, and Proreus with them (*Commentary on the Odyssey*, 1886, on *Odyssey* 8.111; see [[ponteus]] and [[proreus]]).
