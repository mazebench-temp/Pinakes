μεγάθυμος "great-hearted" ([[great-hearted]]) is all the praise the Catalogue gives him; the phrase is repeated when his son dies (17.306). Ναυβολίδης: "son of [[Naubolus]]".

## Outside Homer

Apollonius makes him one of the Argonauts: Iphitus, son of Naubolus, who had once been Jason's host when Jason went to Pytho to consult the oracle about the voyage (*Argonautica* 1.207–210); Apollodorus also lists him (1.9.16). Another Iphitus, son of [[Eurytus]] of Oechalia, gave Odysseus the bow that he later strings against the suitors (Od. 21.13–41).
