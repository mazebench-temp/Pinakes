## Family

Son of Phronius: Φρονίοιο Νοήμονα φαίδιμον υἱόν, “Noemon, the shining son of Phronius” (*Odyssey* 2.386; see [[phaidimos]]). Book 4 of the Odyssey names him υἱὸς Φρονίοιο Νοήμων, “Noemon, son of Phronius”, both times he speaks (*Odyssey* 4.630, 4.648). Nothing more is told of his father (see [[phronius]]).

## The name

Νοήμων is the adjective νοήμων, “discerning” (*Odyssey* 2.282), and Φρόνιος is formed from the root of φρονέω, “think”, as φρόνις, “wisdom”, is (*Odyssey* 3.244, 4.258): both names are words of the mind. The Iliad has two men of the name, the Lycian whom Odysseus kills (5.678; see [[noemon-of-lycia]]) and a companion of Antilochus (23.612; see [[noemon-companion-of-antilochus]]).
