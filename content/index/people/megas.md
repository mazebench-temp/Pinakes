# Μεγάδην (16.695), "son of Megas", occurs only here in Homer and counts for son and father (perimus).
grc: [Μεγάδην]
relations:
  - child: perimus (16.695)
---

καὶ Πέριμον Μεγάδην, “and Perimus, son of Megas” (16.695), is the only mention of him. His son is the one man of the nine whom Patroclus kills under the narrator's question, “whom first, whom last”, who is given a father (16.692–697; see [[perimus]] and [[whom-first-whom-last]]). The name is the adjective μέγας, “great”.
