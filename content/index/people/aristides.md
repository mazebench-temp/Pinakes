Herodotus calls him “the best and most just man in Athens”, and tells how, recalled from ostracism, he crossed from Aegina to bring Themistocles word that the Greek fleet at Salamis was surrounded (8.79). He commanded the Athenians at Plataea (9.28), and the assessment of the allies' tribute was entrusted to him (Thucydides 5.18.5 names “the tribute of Aristides”). See [[themistocles]].
