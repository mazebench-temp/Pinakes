---
title: Pisander
greek: Πείσανδρος
kind: person
tags: [statesman, athenian]
summary: Pisander, Athenian politician, the chief organizer of the oligarchic revolution of the Four Hundred in 411 BCE.
---

Thucydides tells how he persuaded the assembly to change the constitution and set up the Four Hundred (8.53–54, 8.65–68). He is not the Peisander of the Iliad (see [[peisander-son-of-antimachus]]).
