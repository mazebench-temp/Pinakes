# Ἰθαιμένεος (16.586) occurs only here in Homer.
grc: [Ἰθαιμένεος]
relations:
  - child: sthenelaus (16.586)
---

Σθενέλαον Ἰθαιμένεος φίλον υἱόν, “Sthenelaus, the dear son of Ithaemenes” (16.586): the father is named at his son's death, and nothing more is told of him (see [[sthenelaus]]). Walter Leaf reports Fick's remark that the first syllable of Ἰθαιμένεος cannot be short, which led Leaf to propose Σθένελον, “Sthenelus”, for the son's name (*Commentary on the Iliad*, 1900, on 16.586).
