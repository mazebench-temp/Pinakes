summary: Niceratus, an Athenian who competed in reciting Homer; perhaps Niceratus son of Nicias, who in Xenophon's Symposium knows the whole Iliad and Odyssey by heart.
---

In Xenophon's *Symposium* Niceratus says that his father made him learn all of Homer (3.5). Whether he is the Niceratus beaten by Pratys is not known.
