# Φιλητορίδην (20.457) occurs only here: the patronymic counts for the son (demuchus) and the father.

Δημοῦχον δὲ Φιλητορίδην, “and Demuchus, son of Philetor” (20.457): Φιλητορίδης, “son of Philetor”, is the only trace of him in Homer, in the list of the men Achilles kills after Apollo has saved Hector (20.455–489; see [[demuchus]] and [[achilles]]). The poem says nothing else of him. The name seems to join φιλέω, “love”, and the agent ending -τωρ of ἡγήτωρ, “leader”, said of Iphition a little before (20.383; see [[iphition]]).
