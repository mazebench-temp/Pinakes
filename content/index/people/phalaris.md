tags: [tyrant, sicily]
summary: Tyrant of Acragas in Sicily (about 570–554 BCE), remembered for roasting his victims in a bronze bull. In Aristotle's story Stesichorus warned the people of Himera against giving him a bodyguard.
grc: ["^Φάλαρ*"]
---

Pindar sets the hateful name of Phalaris, “who burned men in a bronze bull with his pitiless mind”, against the kindly fame of Croesus (*Pythian* 1.95–98). In the *Politics* he is one of the tyrants who rose from high office (5.10, 1310b28). See [[stesichorus]] and [[himera]].
