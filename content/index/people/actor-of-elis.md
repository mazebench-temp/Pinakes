Ἀκτορίωνε is a dual patronymic, "the two of Actor's line". Elsewhere Homer uses it of the twins [[Cteatus]] and [[eurytus-of-elis|Eurytus]] themselves (11.750, 23.638). Here the dual seems to agree with their sons, [[Amphimachus]] and [[Thalpius]], and the translation renders it "both of Actor's line". Nestor adds that the twins' real father was Poseidon (11.751).

He is not [[actor-son-of-azeus|Actor, son of Azeus]], in whose house the sons of Ares were born (2.513), nor Actor the grandfather of Patroclus (11.785).

## Outside Homer

Their mother was Molione, from whom the twins took the name Moliones. Pausanias says that Actor founded the town of [[Hyrmine]] and named it after his own mother (5.1.11).
