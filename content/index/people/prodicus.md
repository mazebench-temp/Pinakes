---
title: Prodicus
greek: Πρόδικος
kind: person
tags: [sophist]
summary: Prodicus of Ceos, sophist of the late fifth century BCE, known for his fine distinctions between words of like meaning and for the fable of Heracles at the crossroads.
aliases: [Prodicus of Ceos]
---

Plato parodies his distinctions of synonyms (*Protagoras* 337a–c), and Socrates says he heard only Prodicus' one-drachma lecture on the correctness of names, not the fifty-drachma one (*Cratylus* 384b). Xenophon retells his story of the choice of Heracles (*Memorabilia* 2.1.21–34).
