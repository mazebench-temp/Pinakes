The Odyssey calls Penelope “daughter of Icarius” (*Odyssey* 1.329) and makes Icarius and her brothers press her to marry (*Odyssey* 15.16–18). Later mythographers made him a Spartan, brother of Tyndareus (Apollodorus 3.10.6). See [[the-icarius-problem]] and [[icadius]].
