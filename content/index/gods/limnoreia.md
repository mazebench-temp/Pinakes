## Etymology

The name begins with λίμνη, “lake, pool”, a word Homer also uses of the sea: Poseidon's house is “in the depths of the sea”, βένθεσι λίμνης (13.21). What the second part means is uncertain.

## In Homer

She is named once in Homer, in the catalogue of the Nereids who gather around Thetis in the depths of the sea when Thetis hears Achilles groan for Patroclus (18.37–49; see [[nereids]]).

## Outside Homer

Hesiod does not name her (*Theogony* 240–264). Apollodorus ends his list of the Nereids with her (*Library* 1.2.7).
