## In Book 1

{{quote:72b61a08-65ca-5c70-8710-9cf513607ba4}}

The sun sets on the Achaeans at Chryse.

{{quote:9c68ae1e-7d7c-5d68-bda2-611f78ed3376}}

Hephaestus falls all day and lands on Lemnos "as the sun was setting". The sun measures the length of the fall.

{{quote:baab8eec-aa74-5f52-a055-f6f56071d1e6}}

The bright light of the sun sets on the gods' feast, and the book ends.

## Outside Homer

Helios is invoked as a witness to oaths "who sees all things and hears all things" (Iliad 3.277). His cattle on Thrinacia are the ruin of Odysseus' crew (Odyssey 12).
