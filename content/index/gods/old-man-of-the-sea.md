## Outside Homer

In the Iliad the old man of the sea is Thetis' father, and so Nereus; the Nereids are named as a group at 18.38–49. The Odyssey gives the same title to other sea gods, Proteus (4.365) and Phorcys (13.96). Hesiod calls Nereus "the old man" because he is truthful and gentle (*Theogony* 233–236).
