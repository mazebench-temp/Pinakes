title: Phorcys (sea god)
greek: Φόρκυς
kind: god
tags: [sea-deity, primordial]
summary: An old god of the sea, son of Pontus and Earth, father with Ceto of the Graeae, the Gorgons and other monsters; Aeschylus' Daughters of Phorcys was about the Graeae.
aliases: [Phorkys, Phorcus, old man of the sea]
---

Homer calls him “the old man of the sea”, father of the nymph Thoosa, mother of Polyphemus (*Odyssey* 1.71–72), and gives his name to a harbor of Ithaca (*Odyssey* 13.96). Hesiod makes him and Ceto the parents of the Graeae, gray from birth, the Gorgons, and the serpent that guarded the golden apples (*Theogony* 270–336). He is not the Phrygian Phorcys of the Iliad (see [[phorcys]], [[daughters-of-phorcys]] and [[perseus-and-the-graeae]]).
