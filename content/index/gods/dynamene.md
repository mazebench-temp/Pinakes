## Etymology

Δυναμένη is the present participle of δύναμαι, “be able, have power”: “she who has power”.

## In Homer

She is named once in Homer, in the catalogue of the Nereids who gather around Thetis in the depths of the sea when Thetis hears Achilles groan for Patroclus (18.37–49; see [[nereids]]). Her verse is repeated word for word in the *Theogony* (18.43 = *Theogony* 248; see [[doto]]).

## Outside Homer

Hesiod names her among the daughters of Nereus and Doris in the same verse (*Theogony* 248), and Apollodorus lists her (*Library* 1.2.7).
