## Etymology

The meaning of the name is unknown.

## In Homer

She is named once in Homer, in the catalogue of the Nereids who gather around Thetis in the depths of the sea when Thetis hears Achilles groan for Patroclus (18.37–49; see [[nereids]]). Another Maera is among the heroines whom Odysseus sees in the underworld, named with a Clymene and with Eriphyle: Μαῖράν τε Κλυμένην τε ἴδον στυγερήν τʼ Ἐριφύλην (*Odyssey* 11.326; see [[eriphyle]]). In the Iliad's catalogue a Clymene stands in the verse before (18.47; see [[clymene-the-nereid]]).

## Outside Homer

Neither Hesiod nor Apollodorus names a Nereid Maera (*Theogony* 240–264; *Library* 1.2.7).
