Pan is the son of Hermes in the *Homeric Hymn to Pan* (19). Herodotus tells that he met the runner Philippides on Mount Parthenium before Marathon and asked why the Athenians gave him no worship, and that afterwards they founded his shrine under the Acropolis (6.105). Pindar called him the companion, “dog”, of the Mother of the gods (fr. 96), whose shrine stood by Pindar's house (*Pythian* 3.77–79). See [[hermes]].
