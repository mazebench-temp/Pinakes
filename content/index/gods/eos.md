## In Book 1

{{quote:c4ee75fe-aa17-56e2-914f-e4d007b6e72a}}

ῥοδοδάκτυλος = ῥόδον (rose) + δάκτυλος (finger): rosy-fingered, the first light spreading like fingers. ἠριγένεια = ἦρι (early) + γίγνομαι (be born): early-born. The whole line recurs as a formula, twenty times in the Odyssey alone.

{{quote:e43044a0-d532-5a19-b287-6f0e23c39ef8}}

The twelfth dawn after Thetis' visit, when the gods come home. See [[time]].

## Outside Homer

Eos is a daughter of Hyperion and Theia (Hesiod, *Theogony* 371–374). She rises each morning from the bed of her husband Tithonus (Iliad 11.1–2).
