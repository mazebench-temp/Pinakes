In Homer she rises each morning from the bed of her husband [[tithonus|Tithonus]] (11.1–2 = *Odyssey* 5.1–2).

{{notes}}

## Outside Homer

Eos is a daughter of Hyperion and Theia (Hesiod, *Theogony* 371–374).
