## Etymology

Ὠκεανός (Ōkeanos). The etymology is unknown and probably not Greek. Our word "ocean" comes from it.

{{notes}}

## Outside Homer

In Hera's deceptive speech Oceanus is "the origin of the gods" (Iliad 14.201) and "the origin of all things" (14.246). Achilles says all rivers, the sea, springs and wells flow from him (21.195–197). Hesiod makes him the eldest son of Uranus and Gaia (*Theogony* 133) and husband of Tethys (337–370).
