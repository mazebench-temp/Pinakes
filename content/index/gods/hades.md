## Etymology

Ἀΐδης (Aïdēs), dative Ἄϊδι. Ancient and modern readers have taken it as "the unseen one" (ἀ- "not" + ἰδεῖν "see"). The derivation is attractive but uncertain.

{{notes}}

## Outside Homer

Hades is the brother of Zeus and Poseidon, and the dead are his share when the world is divided (Iliad 15.187–193). The Odyssey's eleventh book takes Odysseus to the edge of his realm.
