---
title: Calliope
greek: Καλλιόπη
kind: god
tags: [muse]
summary: One of the nine Muses, in Hesiod the most excellent of them, who attends on kings.
---

Hesiod names her last of the nine and says she is “the foremost of them all” (*Theogony* 79–80). The Iliad does not name the Muses one by one (see [[muses]]).
