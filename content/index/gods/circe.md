Homer calls her “Circe of the lovely hair, a dread goddess with human speech”, sister of Aeetes and daughter of the Sun and of Perse, daughter of Ocean (*Odyssey* 10.135–139). She gave Odysseus' men a drugged drink and turned them into pigs with a stroke of her wand; Odysseus, protected by the herb moly that Hermes gave him, forced her to restore them, and stayed with her a year. She sent him to consult Tiresias in the house of Hades and, on his return, warned him of the Sirens, of Scylla and of Charybdis (*Odyssey* 10.133–12.150; see [[odysseus-passes-scylla]]). Hesiod names Agrius and Latinus as her sons by Odysseus (*Theogony* 1011–1013); the Telegony made her the mother of Telegonus (see [[telegonus-kills-odysseus]]).
