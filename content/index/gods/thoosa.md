## Name

Θόωσα (Thoōsa) seems to be formed from θοός, “swift”, like the name of the Nereid Thoe (Θόη, *Iliad* 18.40; see [[thoe]]).

## Family

{{quote:ad8a8a30-fde5-5e81-be18-d99f13a91350}}

Daughter of the sea god [[phorcys-the-sea-god|Phorcys]], “who rules over the barren sea”, and mother, by [[Poseidon]], of the Cyclops [[polyphemus-the-cyclops|Polyphemus]] (*Odyssey* 1.71–73). She is called νύμφη, “nymph” (see [[nymphs]]), and she lay with the god ἐν σπέσσι γλαφυροῖσι, “in the hollow caves”, the words used of the caves where Calypso holds Odysseus (*Odyssey* 1.15; see [[hollow-caves]]).
