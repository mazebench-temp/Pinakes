## In Book 1

{{quote:4fe0a215-4fd6-5749-8db4-f0fca6ad0c3a}}

The poem's first answer to "which god?" is a genealogy: Apollo is "the son of Leto and of Zeus".

{{quote:b196aeb1-d192-59e0-a569-670e09c2d8b7}}

ἠΰκομος = ἠΰ (well) + κόμη (hair): of the lovely hair. Chryses names Apollo's mother as he begins to pray.

## Outside Homer

Hesiod makes Leto a daughter of the Titans Coeus and Phoebe (*Theogony* 404–408), and Zeus' union with her one of his marriages (918–920). The *Homeric Hymn to Apollo* tells how she wandered in search of a place to give birth, until Delos took her in. There she bore Apollo, and in later tradition Artemis as well.
